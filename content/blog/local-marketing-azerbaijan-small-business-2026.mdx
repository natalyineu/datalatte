For local marketing in Azerbaijan, Instagram and TikTok carry more ad reach than Facebook. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 4.25 million people reachable by Instagram ads, 7.61 million adults on TikTok and 1.65 million on Facebook, in a country of 10.4 million. This page covers those numbers, the economy and the advertising rules we could verify.

## How Big Is the Online Audience?

Azerbaijan had 9.27 million internet users in October 2025 (89.0% of 10.4 million people), according to DataReportal. Social media user identities reached 7.61 million, up 1.2 million (19.2%) in a year. Cellular connections stood at 12.3 million, 118% of the population.

About 58.5% of people live in urban areas and the median age is 33.6. A young, mostly online market suits social channels, though identities may include duplicates.

## Which Platform Fits Which Business?

Ad reach in late 2025 was: Instagram 4.25 million (40.8%), Facebook 1.65 million (15.8%), LinkedIn 1.2 million members, Messenger 438 thousand. TikTok reported 7.61 million, which is 98.7% of adults, a figure large enough that it likely includes duplicate or inflated accounts, so do not plan around it.

Gender splits differ. Facebook's reach is 35.7% women and 64.3% men among adults, while Instagram is closer to even (46.4% women, 52.4% men). A beauty salon and a car-parts shop will not find the same audience on the same app.

## What Is the Economy Like for Local Spending?

The World Bank says real GDP grew 1.4% in 2025, with non-oil growth at 2.7% and oil and gas output down 2%. Inflation was 5.5% in 2025, with food at 6.9%, and the central bank cut its policy rate from 7.25% to 6.75% late in the year. The World Bank projects 1.2% growth in 2026.

For a small business this means modest growth and rising prices. Keep ad budgets small and tied to bookings you can count.

## What Do Advertising Rules Require?

Reports of the April 2025 amendments say the Law on Advertising now covers digital platforms, apps, SMS and websites aimed at consumers in Azerbaijan, and that people who post ads on social media must label them and be registered taxpayers. Fines for influencers range from AZN 30 to 2,000, per secondary reporting (Jam News, Caspian Legal Center). Advertising must also follow the State Language law, which affects ad text. The official law text was not opened for this page, so confirm with a local lawyer before launching.

## FAQ

### Is Instagram or Facebook better in Azerbaijan?

By ad reach, Instagram (4.25 million) is about 2.6 times Facebook (1.65 million). Test both with a small budget.

### Are there ad price benchmarks for Azerbaijan?

None that could be verified. Be wary of any table that lists exact CPC values for the country.

### Do I need to use Azerbaijani in ads?

The advertising law refers to the State Language law. Use Azerbaijani for public-facing text and check the details locally.
