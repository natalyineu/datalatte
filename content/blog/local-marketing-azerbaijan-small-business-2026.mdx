Local marketing in Azerbaijan starts with two facts: most registered businesses are very small, and the size category a firm falls into decides which tax and support rules apply. The Azerbaijani Agency for Development of Small and Medium Businesses reported that as of January 1, 2021, 92.1% of registered business entities were micro-businesses, 5.3% were small and 2.1% were medium-sized. Those figures are from 2021, so check current registers before planning. This guide covers size rules, reported state support, and a budget that a small firm can test.

## How Many Businesses in Azerbaijan Are Small?

The Agency told Trend that about 99 percent of business entities in Azerbaijan are micro, small and medium-sized businesses ([Trend via AzVision](https://en.azvision.az/news/143185/enhancing-role-of-small,-medium-businesses-in-azerbaijani-economy-is-one-of-priorities,-agency-says.html), secondary). The same statement gives the split: 92.1% micro, 5.3% small and 2.1% medium-sized as of January 1, 2021.

The number of individual entrepreneurs registered without creating a legal entity exceeded one million people during the reporting period. Most local competitors are therefore likely to be very small operators. A clear local offer, such as a fixed-price service or same-week booking, often matters more than a large media budget.

## Who Counts as a Micro, Small or Medium Business?

The criteria were approved by a Cabinet of Ministers resolution dated December 21, 2018, and the Agency restated them in its 2021 statement. A firm must be checked against both employee count and annual income, because it can meet one test and fail the other.

| Category | Employees | Annual income | Marketing implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro | Up to 10 | Up to 200,000 manat ($117,647) | Owner-led channels, free listings, bookings tracked by hand |
| Small | 11 to 50 | 200,000 manat to 3 million manat ($1.8 million) | Dedicated staff time, small paid tests, a simple tracking sheet |
| Medium-sized | 51 to 250 | 3 million manat to 30 million manat ($17.6 million) | Budget for multiple channels, formal reporting |

Category decides which tax and support rules apply, so confirm yours before reading the next section.

## Which State Support Applies to Small Firms?

The Agency reported that 75 percent of income is exempt from tax for individual micro-entrepreneurs and for legal-entity micro-entrepreneurs. Micro-entrepreneurs are also exempt from property tax, except where fixed assets have a residual value above 1,000,000 manat at the start of the year.

Micro and small businesses can be exempt from income tax for three years from the date they receive a Startup Certificate, which the Agency issues. Tax rules change, so confirm the current position with a local accountant. The 2021 statement is a secondary report, not the tax code itself.

## Which Free Agency Services Help Marketing?

The Agency said it provides financial support for research on the domestic market, free participation in specialised exhibitions to expand sales, and free training and consulting. Its "Friend of SMEs" network operates in 24 cities and districts. Development Centers are located in Baku, Sumgayit, Khachmaz, Sheki and Yevlakh.

In 2022, the Minister of Economy Mikayil Jabbarov said that the development of small and medium-sized businesses is a priority for the ministry ([AzVision, 2022](https://en.azvision.az/news/163889/news.html), secondary). A firm that cannot afford an agency can use free training to learn basic tracking and use exhibitions to meet nearby buyers. Ask the Agency which services are open in your district, since the statements reviewed do not confirm current availability.

## How Should You Choose Channels Without Reach Data?

The sources reviewed for this page do not verify platform reach or ad prices for Azerbaijan, so no audience percentages are given here. Testing with your own booking records is more reliable. The steps below keep the test simple:

1. Pick one goal for the month, such as bookings or walk-ins.
2. Set a fixed test budget for 30 days and do not change it mid-test.
3. Ask every new customer how they found you and record the answer.
4. Keep a channel only if its cost per booking stays below what a booking is worth to you.
5. Schedule posts with a tool you can manage alone. The [Hootsuite vs Sprout Social comparison](/blog/hootsuite-vs-sprout-social-small-business-local-business) shows which fits a one-person team.

Record spend in a simple ledger. The [FreshBooks vs QuickBooks guide](/blog/freshbooks-vs-quickbooks-small-business-local-business) explains which accounting tool suits a small firm. For appointment-based businesses, the [Jobber vs ServiceTitan guide](/blog/jobber-vs-servicetitan-small-business-local-business) explains how booking systems can record the source of each job.

## What Does a Small Monthly Budget Return?

Assumption: a barbershop in Baku has 6 staff, which places it in the micro category by employee count. Assumption: annual income is 150,000 manat, which is also within the micro income limit. Assumption: the owner sets aside 2% of income for marketing, which is 3,000 manat a year or 250 manat a month. These inputs are invented to show the method.

Assumption: 150 manat goes to paid social ads and 100 manat to free listings and printed flyers. Assumption: the paid ads produce 10 bookings at 15 manat of ad spend each, and each booking is worth 40 manat in revenue. The ad spend then returns 400 manat of revenue for 150 manat spent, which is 2.7 manat of revenue per manat of ads. This is revenue, not profit, because staff time and materials are not deducted.

## FAQ

### Which size category does a small business in Azerbaijan fall into?

The 2021 criteria use employee count and annual income. Micro-businesses have up to 10 employees and income up to 200,000 manat. Check both measures, because the category depends on meeting the tests set in the resolution.

### How much of my income is exempt from tax as a micro-business?

The Agency reported that 75 percent of income is exempt from tax for micro-entrepreneurs. Confirm the current rule with a local accountant, since the source is a 2021 statement relayed by a news agency.

### Are there published ad prices or reach figures for Azerbaijan?

Not in the sources checked for this page. Use your own booking data as the benchmark, and be cautious with any table that lists exact cost figures for the country.

### Can a small firm bid for government contracts in Azerbaijan?

The Agency reported that public procurement of goods, works or services worth about $3 million or less is carried out through e-procurement with small and medium businesses, using an open tender method. Check the current procurement rules before bidding.
