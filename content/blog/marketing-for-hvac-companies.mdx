An HVAC company should build marketing around urgent searches: a verified Google Business Profile, Local Services Ads or Search ads for repair calls, and a steady review habit. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks list Home and Home Improvement at $8.33 per click and $90.92 per lead, the closest published category. HVAC is an "urgent" category in Google's screening.

Expect background checks and insurance proof before Local Services Ads go live.

This guide is for residential HVAC firms in the US. The benchmark covers home services in general, not HVAC alone.

## What Marketing Works for HVAC Companies?

When a system fails, people search and call the first credible result. The foundation is findability.

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence. Verify the profile, fill in every field, keep hours current and reply to reviews. As a service-area business you can hide your address and list up to 20 service areas, kept within roughly 2 hours of driving.

BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 97% of consumers read reviews, 68% require four or more stars, and 74% only consider reviews from the last three months.

## What Does Local Services Ads Screening Require?

Google's screening page calls HVAC an urgent category with an extra level of screening. Its lists include a business check and an owner check, general liability insurance, state-level business and owner licences where local law requires them, and a public, verified Business Profile. Not every item applies to every advertiser, so check your own account.

You pay for leads. Google says your bid is the most you will pay per lead, and higher-quality profiles may rank higher and pay lower costs per lead.

Google's migration page lists HVAC among phase-one verticals for moving Local Services campaigns to Performance Max with pay-per-lead goals, starting August 2026 for select US providers. See our [Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-services-ads-complete-guide).

## How Do You Stay Booked Year-Round?

Demand swings with weather, so plan. Use an ad schedule to choose days and hours, and bid adjustments for times that perform. Google paces campaigns toward 30.4 times the average daily budget each month.

No source here gives HVAC demand by month, so use your own call log. Build off-peak work: maintenance plans, tune-ups and follow-ups to past customers who agreed to hear from you. Add a call-back text so no ad call goes unanswered. The [home services guide to Facebook ads](/blog/facebook-ads-for-home-services) and [Google Ads for contractors](/blog/google-ads-for-contractors) cover planned work.

## The Math: What Does $1,500 Buy?

All inputs below are **assumptions** except the benchmark.

| Step | Assumption or source | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget | Assumption | $1,500 |
| Cost per lead | $90.92 (benchmark) | about 16.5 leads |
| Leads who book | 40% (assumption) | about 6.6 jobs |
| Average first ticket | $450 (assumption) | about $2,970 |
| Cost per booked job | $1,500 divided by 6.6 | about $227 |

The first ticket may not cover the cost, but replacement jobs and maintenance plans can. If only 20% book, cost per job doubles to about $455. Answer fast: an unanswered urgent call goes to the next result.

## How Do You Ask for Reviews the Right Way?

Ask every customer after the visit, then reply to each review. The FTC's rule on fake reviews took effect on 21 October 2024 and bars offers of rewards that require a positive review. Read [reviews strategy for home services](/blog/reviews-strategy-for-home-services) and the [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## FAQ

### How much should an HVAC company spend on marketing?
No published figure fits every firm. Size a test from the benchmark of $90.92 per lead and adjust to your close rate.

### Is HVAC allowed on Local Services Ads?
Yes in the US. It is an urgent category with extra screening, including background checks and general liability insurance.

### Can I hide my address?
If customers do not visit your premises, Google says to remove the address and list service areas.

### How do I handle seasonal swings?
Use an ad schedule and your own call history. No source here gives monthly HVAC demand.

### What reviews matter most?
Recent ones. BrightLocal found 74% only consider reviews from the last three months.
