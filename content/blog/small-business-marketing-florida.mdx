## Who Are Florida Small Businesses Selling To?

Residents and visitors, in proportions that differ by town. In the third quarter of 2025, Visit Florida estimated 34.339 million visitors, up from 34.239 million a year earlier, and 507,000 Canadian visitors, down from 597,000. WLRN notes that more than nine in ten visitors come from elsewhere in the US, and that Canada typically sends upwards of 3 million visitors a year.

If you sell to tourists, such as a coffee shop near a beach or a studio near hotels, watch how your own sales moved against these national swings. For resident-led businesses, tourism data matters less than local reviews and repeat visits. For a city example, see [marketing for Miami businesses](/blog/marketing-for-miami-businesses).

## How Should Florida Businesses Handle Hurricane Season?

NOAA's National Hurricane Center says the season runs from June 1 to November 30, with a peak on September 10 and most activity between mid-August and mid-October. Storms can also occur outside those dates.

Marketing steps that follow from that:

- Know how you will update customers if you close. Google Business Profile lets you set special hours for holidays or temporary periods, or mark the business closed.
- Keep a short message template for closures and reopenings.
- Pause or reduce ads if you cannot serve customers, so budget is not spent on clicks you cannot answer.

Ready.gov offers a hurricane-specific Ready Business Toolkit for continuity planning. It publishes no marketing advice.

## How Do You Show Up Locally on Google?

Complete your Business Profile, keep hours exact, and ask real customers for reviews. Our [Google Business Profile optimization checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers the fields. Hours matter in Florida because closures and seasonal schedules change often.

Edit hours in the profile editor on Google Search or in the Google Maps app (Business, then Edit profile). Google reviews changes before they go live, so do not leave an update to the last hour.

## What Does Google Ads Cost for Florida Businesses?

No Florida figure is published in the sources reviewed. The 2026 WordStream by LocaliQ benchmarks, updated June 1, 2026, give US averages across all advertisers of $5.42 per click, a 6.64% click-through rate and $66.69 per lead. Categories differ widely: Restaurants and Food $2.05 per click, Animals and Pets $4.06, Beauty and Personal Care $4.62, Health and Fitness $6.17 and Home and Home Improvement $8.33.

Use location targeting to limit spend. Google's help says radius targeting needs at least 1 km, and you can target cities and ZIP codes. Test "Presence" if you only serve people who are physically in your area.

## Should You Run Ads in Spanish?

It depends on your customers, and the setting is changing. Google says that starting September 2026, Search campaigns no longer use a campaign-level language setting, and ads match users based on the language of the ads. When a search term clearly uses one language, ads in that language are prioritized.

So a Spanish ad group needs Spanish ads, keywords and a landing page in Spanish. This article found no Florida-specific data on Spanish-language search, so test with a small budget. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) helps size the test.

## What Does a 30-Day Plan Look like?

1. Week 1: audit the Business Profile, hours and categories.
2. Week 2: prepare a closure and reopening template and a review request routine.
3. Week 3: launch one small, local Search campaign with call tracking.
4. Week 4: review search terms, add negatives, compare leads to sales.

## FAQ

### What is the best marketing for a small business in Florida?

No source ranks channels for Florida. A complete Google Business Profile and genuine reviews cost little and suit most local businesses. Add paid search once you can measure leads.

### When is hurricane season in Florida?

June 1 to November 30, with a peak on September 10, according to NOAA's National Hurricane Center.

### Is tourism up or down in Florida?

Visit Florida reported 109.782 million visitors from January to September 2025, up 0.1%, with Canadian visitors down 15.5%. A full-year figure was not found in a primary source.

### How much should I budget for Google Ads in Florida?

There is no standard. At US averages of $4 to $8 per click for local categories, $1,000 buys roughly 120 to 250 clicks a month. Start small and track leads.

### Do I need Spanish-language ads?

Only if your customers search in Spanish. Google matches ads by the language of the ad, so you must write them in Spanish.
