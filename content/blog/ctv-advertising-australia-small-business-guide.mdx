An Australian small business can buy streaming TV advertising mainly through broadcaster video on demand (BVOD) such as 9Now, 7plus and 10 Play, and through programmatic or self-serve tools that sell the same screens. BVOD is now 19.1% of Total TV viewing minutes in Australia, according to OzTAM's VOZ report for the first half of 2026. Prices and minimum budgets for these platforms are not published in the sources checked, so get written quotes before you plan.

This guide separates what is verified from what you must ask a seller. The budget example is an assumption, not a market rate.

## How Many Australians Watch Streaming TV?

OzTAM's VOZ Total TV Viewing Report for 28 December 2025 to 27 June 2026 found that Total TV, which combines free-to-air broadcast and BVOD, reached 86.7% of Australians each month. BVOD made up 19.1% of viewing minutes, up from 16.2% in the second half of 2025. Free ad-supported channels (FAST) were 4.5% of BVOD viewing.

The 16 to 39 age group had the highest BVOD-only monthly reach at 19.9%, which means about one in five young viewers were reached by BVOD and not by broadcast. The TV set is still the dominant screen. VOZ does not cover subscription streaming such as Netflix or Stan, which are covered by OzTAM's separate Streamscape report.

## Which Platforms Sell Ads to Small Business?

BVOD from the free-to-air networks is the most documented route. Nine's Nine Ad Manager, launched with DanAds in November 2023 according to a trade press release, is a self-serve platform for small and medium businesses that started with 9Now. It lists targeting by age, gender and location down to postcode, plus Nine's 9Tribes audience segments. The release does not state a minimum budget, and it is a secondary source from 2023, so confirm current terms with Nine.

For 7plus and 10 Play, no self-serve terms were found in the sources checked. Pricing for ad-supported tiers of Binge, Stan and Kayo is also not published here. Ask each seller for a rate card and the exact inventory on offer.

## How Does Location Targeting Work?

Where a seller offers postcode or state targeting, you can keep ads close to your shop. Nine's tool lists postcode-level location, according to its launch release. Ask every platform three questions: what is the smallest area you can target, how is location determined (household or device), and does the report show where ads were delivered.

If a platform can only target a state, a single suburb café will pay for reach it cannot use. Compare that with search ads, where you can control a radius. Our [guide to the best CTV platforms](/blog/ctv-advertising-platforms-compared-2026) shows how to compare sellers.

## What Creative and Seasonality Should You Plan?

A TV ad should work on a large screen with sound often on: a clear shot of the shop, the product, the address or suburb, and one offer. Keep the call to action simple, like a short web address or a code. See the [CTV creative specs guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026) for file formats.

OzTAM's data shows how live sport drives BVOD. The FIFA World Cup and State of Origin contributed 4.8 billion BVOD minutes in the week of 14 to 20 June 2026, the highest weekly volume since VOZ started in December 2021. Sport weeks are busy, so inventory may cost more then. Check with the seller.

## FAQ

### How much does streaming TV advertising cost in Australia?

No rates are published in the sources checked. Ask for a rate card from each network or platform.

### Can a small business advertise on 9Now?

Nine launched a self-serve tool for small and medium businesses in 2023, according to a trade press release. Confirm the current minimum and terms with Nine.

### What is BVOD?

Broadcaster video on demand: the streaming services of free-to-air networks, such as 9Now, 7plus and 10 Play.

### Does VOZ include Netflix or Stan?

No. VOZ covers free-to-air broadcast and BVOD. OzTAM's Streamscape report adds subscription streaming.

### How do I know if a TV ad worked?

Use a unique code, link or phone number and compare against weeks without the ad.
