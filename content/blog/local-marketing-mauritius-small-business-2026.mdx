A Mauritian small business should combine Facebook, which reaches 886 thousand people (69.9% of the population), a complete Google Business Profile and, if it serves visitors, English-language search visibility. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report also shows 418 thousand reachable on Instagram (33.0%) and 630 thousand LinkedIn members (49.7%). Tourist arrivals in 2025 were about 1.44 million, per a government statement reported by India Outbound.

No source checked publishes Mauritian ad prices, so there are no CPC figures.

## How Big Is the Mauritian Online Market?

DataReportal counts 1.27 million people, 1.01 million internet users (79.5%) and 2.25 million mobile connections, 178% of the population. Social media user identities are 886 thousand, 69.9%, up 5.3%. The population is small, so reach matters less than relevance.

Facebook and the whole social media count are identical because DataReportal's method may reflect only the largest platform. For another island market, compare [Madagascar](/blog/local-marketing-madagascar-small-business-2026) and [the Maldives](/blog/local-marketing-maldives-small-business-2026).

## Which Platforms Reach Mauritians?

| Platform | Ad reach | Share of population | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 886 thousand | 69.9% | up 5.3% |
| LinkedIn (members) | 630 thousand | 49.7% | up 16.7% |
| Instagram | 418 thousand | 33.0% | up 9.2% |

LinkedIn is unusually large here, which suits professional services, but it counts registered members, not active users. Facebook remains the widest consumer channel. DataReportal does not cover TikTok for Mauritius in this report.

## How Much Do Tourists Matter?

India Outbound, citing a Mauritian government press statement of 14 January 2026, reports about 1.44 million tourist arrivals in 2025, up 3.9% from 1.38 million. The article gives 1.43 million in its headline and 1.44 million in its text, so treat it as roughly 1.4 million. Air arrivals rose 4.7%, while sea arrivals fell 27.6%.

India was the fastest-growing source market, up 33.5%. France, the United Kingdom and Germany declined. For hotels, tours and restaurants, visitors are a large audience relative to a population of 1.27 million. This is a secondary report; the government release itself was not opened.

## How Do You Show Up on Google Maps in Mauritius?

Google says local ranking uses relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Reviews and ratings can help prominence. Visitors read reviews before booking, so reply to each one. ## Worked Example: A Tour Operator Facebook Test

All numbers are **assumptions**.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget, 30 days | $200 |
| Enquiries | 20 |
| Share that book | 15% |
| Bookings | 3 |
| Margin per booking | $90 |

Three bookings give $270 margin against $200 spend, a gain of $70. With two bookings margin is $180 and the test loses $20. Count enquiries and bookings by source.
