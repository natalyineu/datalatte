Google lists Ireland among 28 countries for the Google TV network, Virgin Media Television sells AdSmart addressable ads to up to 500,000 Sky households, and RTÉ sells advertising on RTÉ Player. ## How Big Is the Online Audience in Ireland?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 5.32 million people in October 2025 and 5.26 million internet users at the end of 2025, or 98.9% of the population. Social media user identities stood at 4.26 million (80.1%).

YouTube's ad reach is 4.26 million, 80.1% of the population. Facebook and Instagram each show 2.60 million. These are advertising reach estimates, not active user counts. The report has no TV or streaming viewing figures.

## Can an Irish Small Business Advertise on the TV Screen?

Yes, through several routes. The ones documented in sources opened for this guide:

- **YouTube on the Google TV network** (self-serve in Google Ads).
- **AdSmart from Virgin Media Television**, addressable TV by household.
- **RTÉ Player ads**, sold through RTÉ.

Prices are not published for any of them in the pages checked, apart from one dated RTÉ figure covered below. There is no public Irish CTV CPM in the sources, so none is quoted here.

## Is the Google TV Network Available in Ireland?

Yes. Google Ads Help lists IE among the 28 supported countries. The network lets advertisers extend YouTube video campaigns to TV screens, and in-feed ads on the Google TV homescreen run as part of Video Reach campaigns with the "Efficient reach" goal. Audience and location targeting are supported. Creative must be 1920 x 1080 pixels at 16:9.

Google's Reach Planner has connected TV data only for the United States, per its help page, so do not expect a built-in Irish CTV forecast. YouTube TV, the US live-TV service, is not available outside the US.

## What Is AdSmart From Virgin Media?

Virgin Media describes AdSmart as addressable advertising: households watching the same programme can see different ads. Targeting uses location and lifestyle attributes based on the latest Census, alone or combined. The page presents it as suited to low-cost, local and Smart TV campaigns and says "TV advertising is now even more accessible for SME's".

The page says it targets up to 500,000 households in Ireland that are Sky TV customers, and that it would extend to Virgin Media homes from mid-2021. That wording is dated, so ask whether the extension happened. The page lists no price or minimum spend. The enquiry address given is adsmart@virginmedia.ie.

Sky has also offered airtime schemes to first-time TV advertisers. A marketing.ie article, undated and apparently from 2020 or 2021, describes one worth €20,000 of airtime for businesses that had not used TV before. Treat it as history, not a current offer.

## What Does Advertising on RTÉ Player Cost?

Only an old figure is public. Today FM reported on 10 June 2022 that, according to RTÉ figures, the cost of a 30-second advert on RTÉ Player starts at between €80 and €122, and that the player earned €4.9 million in advertising in 2020. The article does not explain whether the price is per ad slot or per thousand views, and it does not mention targeting.

This is a secondary source from 2022. Do not budget from it. RTÉ's advertising page redirects to its corporate site, and RTÉ sells ads through its media sales team, so request a current rate card.

## What Advertising Rules Apply in Ireland?

The Advertising Standards Authority for Ireland administers the Code of Standards for Advertising and Marketing Communications in Ireland. Its site says the Code covers broadcast, print, sales promotions and online content that promote goods or services.

The ASAI offers pre-publication copy advice that is "confidential, non-binding and free of charge". Its download link is labelled 7th Edition, Revision 2021, while a document list shows the 7th edition from 2016, so check which version is current. Coimisiún na Meán's rules for broadcasters and on-demand services were not checked for this guide, so confirm any rules for your category before you buy.

## Worked Example: A Café Test Budget

The inputs are **assumptions** for illustration, not benchmarks. A café in Cork wants to test an addressable TV ad aimed at its local postcodes.

| Step | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget | €2,000, as a ceiling to negotiate against |
| Goal | New customers who use a printed offer code |
| Gross margin per new regular over six months | €90 |
| Customers needed to break even | 2,000 / 90 = about 23 |
| Decision rule | Fewer than 12 redeemed codes, do not repeat |

Whether the money buys anything depends on the quote. The point is to set the break-even number before you talk to a seller, so the quote can be judged.

## What Should You Ask Before You Buy?

1. What is the minimum spend, in euro, and does it include creative production?
2. Which households, counties or Eircodes can you target, and how is the audience matched?
3. Which channels, apps and devices will carry the ad?
4. What report will you receive, and what counts as a view?
5. Has the ad been checked against the ASAI Code?

A seller who answers these in writing is worth a test. For smaller budgets, fix your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) and Search ads first.

## FAQ

### Can small businesses in Ireland advertise on streaming TV?

Yes. Google lists Ireland for the Google TV network, Virgin Media sells AdSmart, and RTÉ sells Player ads. Minimums are not published in the pages checked.

### How much does CTV advertising cost in Ireland?

No current public price list was found. One dated figure puts a 30-second RTÉ Player advert at €80 to €122 (Today FM, 2022), but its basis is unclear. Ask for a quote.

### Does AdSmart reach every Irish household?

No. Virgin Media's page says up to 500,000 Sky TV households at launch, with an extension to Virgin Media homes planned for mid-2021. Confirm current coverage with the seller.

### Is there a planning tool for Irish CTV?

Google's Reach Planner offers connected TV data only in the United States. Use seller reports and your own offer codes to measure results.
