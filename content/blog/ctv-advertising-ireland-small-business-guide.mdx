Yes. Small businesses in Ireland can advertise on streaming TV (connected TV, or CTV) through broadcaster players such as RTÉ Player and Virgin Media Player, through ad-supported streaming platforms, and through programmatic buying. Budgets are far lower than for traditional broadcast TV, and you can target by area, but prices, minimums and inventory vary, so get current quotes from the platform or an agency before you plan.

Ireland is a small, English-speaking market with strong broadcaster streaming services, so local advertisers can reach households with TV-quality video without a national broadcast budget. All figures below are indicative; confirm the current position with sellers.

## The Irish Streaming Landscape

**RTÉ Player** is the national broadcaster's streaming service, carrying content from RTÉ One, RTÉ2 and other channels. For many local businesses it offers a trusted, culturally familiar environment. Digital advertising on RTÉ Player is sold through RTÉ's commercial team and, in some cases, programmatic channels. Ask about current minimums, regional targeting options and available audience data.

**Virgin Media Player** streams Virgin Media's channels, with entertainment, reality and sports content. Its audience profile may skew somewhat younger and more urban than RTÉ's, but ask for current audience data. Advertising is available via managed sales and programmatic routes.

**TG4 Player** carries Irish-language and Irish-interest content. It can be a useful, less crowded option for businesses in Irish-speaking areas or with a cultural link to the language.

**Amazon Prime Video and other global platforms.** Ad-supported tiers on global services have expanded in Europe. Availability of ad inventory and local targeting in Ireland varies, so check whether your area is supported. See our [Amazon Prime Video ads guide](/blog/amazon-prime-video-ads-small-business).

**FAST channels (free ad-supported streaming TV)**, such as Pluto TV, are often the lowest-cost way to try CTV, and are typically bought programmatically.

## Advertising Rules and Compliance

- **Broadcast and online rules:** Ireland's media regulator, Coimisiún na Meán, oversees broadcasting and online media. Broadcasters such as RTÉ and Virgin Media have their own advertising acceptance processes, so the platform will normally review your creative before it runs. Allow time for approval.
- **ASAI Code:** the Advertising Standards Authority for Ireland (ASAI) administers the self-regulatory code. Claims must be honest and not misleading, testimonials must be genuine and prices should be shown clearly, including VAT where applicable.
- **Sector rules:** alcohol, health, weight loss and financial services are subject to extra restrictions and scheduling rules. If you are in a regulated sector, check the current code before you create the ad.
- **GDPR:** campaigns targeting people in Ireland must comply with GDPR. Ask your platform or agency how audience data is sourced and what data processing agreements are in place.

These notes are a general orientation, not legal advice.

## Geographic Targeting in Ireland

- **Dublin:** the largest and most competitive market, with higher prices than elsewhere. Ask whether your platform supports targeting down to postal districts or suburbs, and whether income or interest segments are available.
- **Cork:** the second city, often with lower competition. Suburban areas can suit family-oriented services.
- **Limerick, Galway, Waterford and other cities:** regional targeting is often less granular, so a national-minus-Dublin buy can be an efficient option. Ask what each platform supports.
- **Rural areas:** national or multi-county buys, or FAST and programmatic inventory, can be the most cost-effective way to reach households in smaller towns.
- **Northern Ireland:** some platforms are available across the island, with different currency and rules to consider, including UK advertising regulation.

## Budget Guidance

CTV is priced by CPM (cost per thousand impressions). Prices differ by platform, audience, time of year and geography, and Dublin is typically higher than the rest of the country. A practical approach:

1. **Start with a test:** a modest monthly budget on a programmatic or FAST platform to learn what delivery and cost look like
2. **Scale what works:** if you see an uplift in enquiries or branded searches, move budget to broadcaster platforms with better targeting
3. **Time your bursts:** concentrate spend around your peak season and promotions rather than spreading it thinly

To estimate impressions, divide your budget by the quoted CPM and multiply by 1,000. For example, EUR 1,500 at a hypothetical EUR 25 CPM would be about 60,000 impressions, spread across households and frequency.

## Measuring CTV Results

CTV rarely produces direct clicks, so plan measurement before you start:

- A dedicated landing page, QR code or phone number for the campaign
- Changes in branded search and Google Business Profile views, calls and direction requests in your target area
- A "how did you hear about us?" question at booking or checkout
- A comparison to a period with no CTV activity

## FAQ

**Q: How is RTÉ Player different from RTÉ broadcast TV for advertisers?**
A: Linear TV is typically bought through broadcast sales houses with larger minimums, while digital streaming inventory may be available at lower thresholds through managed or programmatic routes. Ask for current minimums and options.

**Q: Do I need Irish-specific creative?**
A: It is not required, but local references, neighborhood names and an Irish voice can build familiarity. Use creative that feels local to your community.

**Q: Can a business in Northern Ireland advertise on Irish CTV platforms?**
A: Many platforms serve the island, and some allow targeting of Northern Irish areas. Check the currency, regulations (UK vs Irish) and what your buying route supports.

**Q: What is the minimum budget for CTV in Ireland?**
A: It varies. Programmatic and FAST routes tend to be the most accessible for small budgets, while managed broadcaster campaigns can have higher minimums. Request current quotes.

**Q: Do I need approval before running my ad?**
A: Broadcaster platforms typically review ads for compliance before they run. Plan extra time for approval and check any sector-specific rules.
