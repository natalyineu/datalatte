Shopify suits a local business that sells products, wants online orders with in-store pickup or local delivery, and wants one system for online and counter sales. It is a weaker fit for a business that only needs a brochure site or bookings. Shopify's US pricing page (checked October 31, 2026) lists Basic at $39 a month, or $29 a month billed yearly, plus card fees of 2.9% + 30 cents online and 2.6% + 10 cents in person.

This guide covers what the plans cost, what Shopify does for local sales, where it costs more than people expect and a monthly example with assumed sales. Prices change, so confirm on Shopify's page.

## What Does Shopify Cost for a Local Shop?

Shopify lists four plans in the US: Basic ($39 monthly, $29 yearly), Grow ($105, $79), Advanced ($399, $299) and Plus (from $2,300). The trial is 3 days free, then $1 a month for 3 months, according to the page.

| Plan | Monthly price | Online card | In-person card | Third-party gateway fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $39 ($29 yearly) | 2.9% + 30c | 2.6% + 10c | 2% |
| Grow | $105 ($79 yearly) | 2.7% + 30c | 2.5% + 10c | 1% |
| Advanced | $399 ($299 yearly) | 2.5% + 30c | 2.4% + 10c | 0.6% |

Most single-location shops start on Basic. The page says Shopify Payments is required for some features such as built-in fraud analysis, and a third-party payment provider adds a transaction fee on top of that provider's own charges.

## How Does Shopify Handle Local Pickup and Delivery?

Shopify's help center says pickup in store needs at least one location that fulfills online orders and the latest checkout version. You set a processing time, mark an order ready for pickup to send an email, then mark it picked up. By default, pickup is available only when the whole order is in stock at that location. Orders from the Facebook & Instagram channel do not get the ready-for-pickup email, so contact those customers yourself.

Local delivery lets each location have up to 10 zones, defined by radius (up to 100 miles) or postal codes. Each zone can set a minimum order and its own price, and 0 means free delivery. Customers need an address Google can verify. The help page does not say which plans include local delivery, so check before you commit.

## Is Shopify POS Included?

Basic in-person selling is included in every paid plan, with limited staff access and simple customer profiles. POS Pro is an add-on at $89 a month per location, billed yearly according to Shopify's POS page. It adds unlimited staff access, roles and permissions, loyalty insights, inventory management and returns across locations.

A one-person shop may never need POS Pro. A multi-staff store with several locations probably will. Add that cost to your plan price when comparing with other systems. See our [Shopify versus Square Online comparison](/blog/shopify-vs-square-online-for-local-shop) for a head-to-head view.

## What Are the Main Pros and Cons?

Pros, based on what we verified:

- One system for online orders, in-store pickup and counter sales.
- Local delivery zones with minimum orders and per-zone prices.
- Card rates fall as you move up plans.

Cons:

- Monthly cost plus card fees on every sale, and a transaction fee if you use a third-party gateway.
- Some features depend on Shopify Payments.
- Local delivery plan availability is unclear in the help page we opened.
- It is built for selling products. A service business that only needs booking may do better with a simpler site.

Whether it is worth it depends on volume. We did not find a published figure for how much an average local shop earns from online orders, so run your own numbers.

## What Does a Month Cost? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**: a small retail shop on the Basic plan paid monthly.

| Item | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan, paid monthly | Shopify pricing | $39 |
| Online orders (assumption) | 100 orders at $80 | $8,000 |
| Online card fees | 100 x ($80 x 2.9% + $0.30) | $262 |
| In-store sales (assumption) | 200 sales at $20 | $4,000 |
| In-person card fees | 200 x ($20 x 2.6% + $0.10) | $124 |
| Total platform and card cost | $39 + $262 + $124 | $425 |
| Share of $12,000 sales | $425 / $12,000 | about 3.5% |

On these assumptions the system costs about 3.5% of sales before apps, a domain or a theme, which we did not price. Card fees are the biggest part, and you would pay similar fees on most card systems. The question is whether online orders add sales you would not have had. If they replace counter sales, you gain little.

## How Do You Get Local Customers to the Store?

A shop site does not bring visitors by itself. Fill in your Google Business Profile with the same hours, category and website link, and send pickup customers to a page that shows stock and hours. The [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists the fields to complete.

Pair the store with a small Search campaign aimed at your area once the checkout works, and start with a short test. Our [Instagram shopping guide](/blog/instagram-shopping-guide) covers selling through social.

## FAQ

### How much is Shopify for a small local shop?

Basic is $39 a month, or $29 a month billed yearly, on Shopify's US page, plus card fees. Add POS Pro ($89 per location per month) only if you need its features.

### Does Shopify support local pickup?

Yes. Help center instructions say you need a location that fulfills online orders and the latest checkout, then turn on pickup per location.

### Can Shopify handle local delivery?

Yes, with up to 10 zones per location by radius or postal code. The help page does not state which plans include it.

### Is there a free trial?

The pricing page shows 3 days free, then $1 a month for 3 months.

### Is Shopify better than Square Online?
