A good local landing page has one offer, one action, a headline that matches the ad or search that brought the visitor, a visible phone number or button, and proof such as reviews. It also loads fast. Unbounce's Conversion Benchmark Report puts the median landing page conversion rate across industries at about 6.6% (Q4 2024 data, 41,000 pages), so that is a fair yardstick for a first test.

This checklist is for a coffee shop, salon, groomer, studio or trade that sends paid or local traffic to a page. Figures that are examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Is a Landing Page and When Do You Need One?

A landing page is the page a visitor reaches after clicking an ad, a link or a search result, built for one action. You need one when you pay for traffic or run a specific offer, such as a first class, a free quote or a booking.

Your home page tries to serve everyone. A landing page serves one promise. Google's Ads help defines landing page experience as "how relevant and useful your landing page is to people who click your ad", and rates it as above average, average or below average against other advertisers. Dedicated pages tend to be easier to keep relevant for that reason.

## What Should Be Above the Fold?

Put four things in the first screen on a phone: a headline that repeats the promise from the ad, a short line on who it is for and where, a call button or booking button, and one proof point such as your star rating and review count.

Skip the site menu on paid pages so people have fewer ways to leave, but keep your business name, address and phone number visible somewhere. Google's destination policy also requires that sites be easy to navigate and that phone numbers in ads be in service in the targeted location and relate to the advertised company.

## How Fast Should a Landing Page Load?

Google's web.dev guidance gives these targets, measured at the 75th percentile of page loads: largest contentful paint (LCP) within 2.5 seconds, interaction to next paint (INP) of 200 milliseconds or less, and cumulative layout shift (CLS) of 0.1 or less.

Check your page with PageSpeed Insights or the Core Web Vitals report in Search Console. The usual causes of a slow small business page are oversized photos, too many tracking scripts and an unneeded slider. Our [website speed guide](/blog/website-speed-optimization-small-business) covers the fixes.

## How Many Fields Should the Form Have?

Ask only for what you need to follow up. For most local offers that is name, phone or email, and one detail about the job. No source here gives an ideal field count, so test two versions instead of copying a rule.

Offer a phone number next to the form, because many local customers prefer to call. Put the number in a tap-to-call link, and track calls separately, as described in [how to get more phone calls from your website](/blog/how-to-get-more-phone-calls-from-your-website).

## What Proof Belongs on the Page?

Use real reviews with names or initials the customer agreed to share, your Google rating, years in business if true, and photos of your own work. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults found 97% read online reviews, 68% require at least 4 stars and 74% only consider reviews from the last three months.

That means recent reviews on the page beat old ones. Never invent testimonials or ratings. See [trust signals for your website](/blog/trust-signals-website) and, for layouts, [local landing page examples](/blog/local-business-landing-page-examples-that-convert).

## How Do You Test a Landing Page?

Change one thing at a time and keep the test running until you have enough conversions to compare, then keep the winner. Start with the headline, then the button text, then the form.

Small businesses often do not have enough traffic for a statistical test. In that case, compare two periods and write down what changed. The worked example below shows the arithmetic.

## What Do Google Ads Policies Say About Landing Pages?

Google's destination requirements say your ad destination must work on common browsers and devices, must be crawlable by Google's AdsBot, and must match the display URL's domain. Pages must offer unique value and cannot be built mainly to show ads. Google also says it warns advertisers at least 7 days before suspending an account for a violation.

Test every ad link on a phone before publishing, including the call button.

## FAQ

### How long should a landing page be?

No source sets a length. Make it as long as it takes to answer the visitor's questions about price, location, proof and next step, and no longer.

### What is a good landing page conversion rate?

Unbounce's Q4 2024 median across industries is about 6.6%. Rates differ by industry and traffic source, so compare yourself with your own earlier results first.

### Can my home page be my landing page?

It can, but a page built for one offer is easier to keep relevant to the ad. Google rates landing page experience by how relevant and useful the page is to people who click.

### Do I need a landing page builder?

No. Any site platform that lets you control the layout and load speed works. Pick the tool you can keep updated.

### How fast does the page need to load?

Google's targets are LCP within 2.5 seconds, INP of 200 ms or less and CLS of 0.1 or less, measured at the 75th percentile.
