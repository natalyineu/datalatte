A Connecticut small business should aim its marketing at two audiences: residents who buy all year and the large visitor market the state reports. Connecticut's tourism study for 2025 counts 72.2 million visitors, $12.3 billion in business sales supported by traveler spending and 125,167 jobs, according to the state's economic development department (DECD).

No source publishes a Connecticut-specific click price. The WordStream by LocaliQ 2026 all-industry average is $5.42 per click. Use it as a starting range for cafes, studios, salons and service firms in Hartford, New Haven, Stamford and smaller towns.

## How Big Is the Visitor Market in Connecticut?

The DECD page for the study lists $20.1 billion in total impact, $1.6 billion in state and local taxes and 125,167 jobs.

The figures describe the whole state. They do not give your town's share or your category's share, so compare them with your own sales by month before you spend on visitor-focused ads.

## How Do You Target Towns, Not the Whole State?

Connecticut customers often live in one town and work in another. Google Ads lets you target a radius or named towns. Google describes "Presence or interest" as reaching people in or interested in your location, and "Presence" as reaching only people physically there. A local service firm needs "Presence". A venue that draws weekend visitors from New York can test the first option.

Review the search terms report weekly and add negative keywords for services you do not offer.

## How Do You Win the Map Listing?

Google says relevance, distance and prominence decide local results and that you cannot pay for a better ranking. Complete every field, add photos, choose accurate categories and reply to reviews. Dense areas such as New Haven County put many businesses within the same radius, so completeness and reviews matter.

## FAQ

### How much should a Connecticut business spend on Google Ads?

No state benchmark exists. At the national average of $5.42 a click, $400 a month buys about 74 clicks. Start small and measure cost per customer.

### Does tourism matter for a small Connecticut business?

Statewide it is large: 72.2 million visitors in 2025. Whether it matters for you depends on whether visitors buy from you, so check your own seasonal sales.

### Should I target the whole state?

Usually no. Target the towns within a short drive of your location and widen only if the data supports it.

### Is Google Business Profile worth it?

Yes. It is free and sits where people compare nearby options. Keep hours and categories accurate.
