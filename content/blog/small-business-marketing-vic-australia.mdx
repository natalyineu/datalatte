## Which marketing channels should a Victorian small business start with?

Start with the channels that capture people already searching for what you sell. For most trades, cafes, studios and retailers in Victoria, that means your Google Business Profile first, then Google Ads and local search. Meta and email are useful, but they work better once someone already knows your name.

The [Australia small business marketing guide](/blog/local-marketing-australia-small-business-2026) covers the national picture. This Victorian version focuses on suburb-level targeting, since a plumber in Dandenong and a studio in Fitzroy face different search patterns and different competitors.

Keep the first phase small. One profile, one search campaign, one retargeting audience, and one way to track calls. Adding more channels before these work usually spreads budget too thin to learn anything.

## How should Victorian businesses use Meta ads?

Use Meta mainly for awareness and retargeting. People who already visited your website or booking page are the most efficient audience, because they have shown interest once. A specific offer on that audience usually performs better than a general brand message.

Keep creative local. Show your storefront, your team, or a real job in a suburb your customers recognise. A short phone video often does more than a polished studio shoot, and it costs far less to produce.

Scheduling tools can help you keep posts consistent across platforms. The [social media tool comparison](/blog/hootsuite-vs-sprout-social-small-business-local-business) explains the trade-offs between two popular options, which matters if you run posts across several Victorian suburbs.

## Does seasonality change when Victorian businesses should spend?

Yes, but plan timing from your own booking history rather than from a fixed calendar. Look at the last 12 months of calls and bookings by month, then reduce spend in quiet periods and raise it before busy ones. Many trades see demand move with weather, so track it yourself.

Large local events can also lift searches in specific suburbs. Check event dates from official sources, then decide whether a campaign fits your offer. Avoid pushing a generic discount during an event if your service does not suit a rush of visitors.

Record what each season delivers in cost per job. After one full cycle you will know which months justify extra budget and which are better for retention work like reminders and reviews.

## What mistakes do Victoria businesses make with local marketing?

The most common mistake is targeting all of Melbourne when the business only serves a handful of suburbs. Wide targeting spends money on people who will never drive to you. Set a radius around your service area, then exclude suburbs you cannot serve.

The second mistake is ignoring calls. Many service bookings still come by phone, so track calls from ads and your profile. Without call tracking, you cannot tell which keywords produce real customers.

The third mistake is letting booking slip through the cracks. A campaign that brings in calls is wasted if the office misses them. For trades, compare scheduling options in the [field service software comparison](/blog/jobber-vs-servicetitan-small-business-local-business) before scaling spend.

## Your 30-day plan for a Victorian business

Use this checklist to launch without overbuilding:

1. Week 1: Complete your Google Business Profile with photos, every service listed, a suburb and Victoria in the description, and replies to all reviews.
2. Week 2: Launch a search campaign with a radius around your service area and exact or phrase match keywords for your service plus suburb.
3. Week 3: Set up call tracking and conversion tracking, and confirm both record correctly.
4. Week 4: Start a Meta retargeting audience of recent website visitors with one specific offer, then review cost per booked job after 60 days.

Each step is low cost and easy to measure. Adjust budget only after you have enough calls and bookings to judge.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much should a small business in Victoria spend on Google Ads?

Start with an amount you can sustain for at least 60 days. The worked example above used A$550 per month as an assumption. Scale only when cost per booked job stays below your margin per job.

### Is Google Business Profile enough for local marketing in Victoria?

It is a strong starting point, but not a complete plan. A complete profile helps local discovery, while paid search and retargeting reach people who have not found you yet. Use both, and track which one produces bookings.

### How do I compare Melbourne marketing agencies?

Compare the scope, reporting frequency and outcomes tracked, not just the starting price. Ask for a written list of deliverables and check whether they commit to results without guarantees.

### Does Meta advertising work for Victorian local businesses?

It can, especially for retargeting people who already visited your website. Keep the offer specific and the creative local, and judge performance by cost per booked job rather than by impressions.
