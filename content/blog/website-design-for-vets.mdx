A vet clinic website should let a worried pet owner find your hours, location, services and a way to book or call in a few seconds on a phone. Put the phone number, hours and a booking or request button at the top, list services and new-client steps in plain words, and use VeterinaryCare structured data so search engines know what you are.

The audience is large. The American Pet Products Association says 95 million US households own a pet, according to its 2025 National Pet Owners Survey. This guide covers the pages, the technical basics and a worked example.

## What should be on a vet clinic home page?

NN/g's homepage principles apply directly: say who you are and what you do, put the most important content above the fold, and show your real offer. For a clinic that means:

- Clinic name, town and species you treat.
- Phone number that taps to call, and today's opening hours.
- A primary button: book, request an appointment or call.
- What to do out of hours. State your emergency arrangement or the referral clinic you use, if you have one.
- A real photo of your team or building.

BrightLocal found 54% of consumers visit a business's website after reading positive reviews, so put your Google rating and a recent review near the button.

## Which pages does a clinic site need?

| Page | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Hours, phone, booking, emergency arrangement |
| Services | Wellness, vaccinations, dental, surgery, diagnostics: one section each |
| New clients | What to bring, how to transfer records, first visit steps |
| Team | Names, roles and photos, which builds trust |
| Contact and location | Map, parking, public transport, hours |
| FAQ | Prices, payment, cancellations, species seen |

Add one page per service you want to rank for. Our [guide to Google Ads keywords for veterinarians](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-veterinarians-in-2026) shows what owners search.

## Should a vet website show prices?

Show what you can. NN/g reports that prospects rank price as their top information need on any site and that hiding costs reads as evasive. Vet bills vary with the animal and findings, so show "from" prices for routine items such as a wellness exam, and explain what changes the total. See our [pricing page guide](/blog/website-pricing-page-local-business). Check any rules your veterinary board has on advertising fees.

## How do you tell Google what your clinic is?

Google's LocalBusiness structured data guide says to use the most specific subtype possible. Schema.org defines VeterinaryCare as "a vet's office", under MedicalOrganization. Google's page does not list it by name, so test your markup in the Rich Results Test before relying on it.

Include name, address, phone and opening hours; Google supports openingHoursSpecification and a priceRange field. Keep it consistent with your Business Profile. See our [local business schema guide](/blog/local-business-schema-markup-rich-snippets-google).

## How fast and accessible should the site be?

Owners often search from a phone in a stressful moment. Google rates a Largest Contentful Paint of 2.5 seconds or less as good, with INP at 200 ms or less. Never lazy-load the first-screen image. Text should meet the WCAG contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1. See our [speed guide](/blog/website-speed-optimization-small-business) and [accessibility guide](/blog/website-accessibility-small-business).

## Worked example: what does online booking add?

All inputs are **assumptions**.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly website visitors | 1,200 |
| Visitors who book or request today (phone only) | 2% |
| Visitors who book or request with an online option | 3% |
| Value of a new-client first visit | $150 |

Today: 1,200 x 2% = 24 new requests, or $3,600. With online booking: 1,200 x 3% = 36 requests, or $5,400. The difference is $1,800 a month. The 1-point lift is invented. Your own phone logs and booking form submissions show whether it holds. Also check that staff can handle the extra requests.

## FAQ

### How much does a vet website cost?

It depends on the builder and features. Squarespace's published plans run from $19 a month (Basic, billed annually) to $99 a month (Advanced), but booking software, design help and extras may cost more. Get quotes.

### Do I need online booking?

It is not required. If you offer it, link it from the home page and your Google Business Profile. Google reports bookings in Profile insights only when handled through a booking provider.

### What schema should a clinic use?

VeterinaryCare is a schema.org type for a vet's office; confirm it validates in Google's Rich Results Test.

### How do I handle emergencies on the site?

State what you do and where owners should go when you are closed. Keep it at the top of the home page and on the contact page.

### How do I get more clinic reviews?

Ask every client the same way and never offer incentives. See our [Google reviews guide](/blog/google-business-profile-reviews).
