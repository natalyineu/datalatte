In Italy, Instagram reaches 29.9 million people in ad tools, just ahead of Facebook at 28.5 million, and TikTok reaches 22.0 million adults. DataReportal counts 59.1 million people and 89.9% internet penetration, so a local Italian business can reach most customers online but must choose carefully among crowded platforms.

This guide is for salons, cafes, studios, groomers and service firms in Italy. It uses published figures, labels every assumption, and ends with a break-even formula you can run on your own numbers. Facts were checked on 1 November 2026. The platform data are DataReportal's Digital 2026 figures, mostly from October 2025.

## Who Are You Marketing To in Italy?

Italy had 59.1 million people in October 2025, down 0.3% over the year. 72.7% live in urban areas and the median age is 48.2. Internet users number 53.1 million (89.9%), and social media identities total 41.2 million (69.7%).

Cellular connections number 67.7 million (114%), down 13.7% over the year. With a median age of 48.2, a Facebook or search-led plan can reach older customers who may not use TikTok.

Instagram and Facebook are close in reach, but Instagram is growing (+4.2%) while Facebook shrinks (-2.6%). TikTok reaches 43.5% of adults and is still growing. Snapchat reaches only 8.6% of the population, so skip it for most Italian local businesses.

Local Italian platforms and messaging apps are not covered by DataReportal's article, so this guide gives no figures for them.

## How Should Google Ads Language and Location Work in Italy?

Google's Ads Help says that from September 2026 the campaign-level language setting is being removed for Search campaigns, and Search ads will match on the language of the ads themselves. For searches with a clear language, Google prioritises ads in that language. Write ads and pages in Italian for local customers. In tourist areas, add a separate English ad group and landing page if visitors are part of your demand.

For location, Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km and recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting as best practice for Search, while presence-only fits when you want to avoid people outside your target area. Set a radius around your premises, then read the report to see which areas convert.

No Italy-specific click costs were found in the sources checked, so every cost figure below is an assumption.

## How Should You Split a Small Budget?

Start with Google Search for service plus town, add Instagram or Facebook for people who have not searched yet, and run TikTok as a small test of about 10 to 15% of paid spend (a suggestion, not a platform rule). Compare cost per booked appointment after four to six weeks.

## What Does a Test Budget Return? The Math

These rows are **assumptions** for illustration, in US dollars. They are not Italy benchmarks. Replace each with your own data after a month.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget (assumption) | $300 | $300 | $300 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | $0.80 | $0.50 | $0.35 |
| Clicks | 375 | 600 | 857 |
| Click to enquiry rate (assumption) | 3% | 5% | 7% |
| Enquiries | 11 | 30 | 60 |
| Enquiry to booking rate (assumption) | 30% | 40% | 50% |
| Bookings | 3.4 | 12 | 30 |
| Ad cost per booking | $89 | $25 | $10 |
| First-visit value (assumption) | $35 | $35 | $35 |
| Revenue minus ad spend | -$182 | +$120 | +$750 |

Counts use unrounded values. Revenue is not profit: staff, product and rent come out of it. A first visit often does not pay back by itself, which is why repeat visits matter.

Your break-even click price is the first-visit value times the enquiry rate times the booking rate. At $35, 5% and 40%, that is 35 x 0.05 x 0.40 = $0.70 per click. If your clicks cost more on average, the first visit loses money and you need repeat bookings to justify it. The biggest lever is usually the booking rate: answer messages and calls within minutes.

## FAQ

### Is Instagram bigger than Facebook in Italy?
Slightly, by ad reach: 29.9 million against 28.5 million in late 2025, per DataReportal.

### How many Italians are online?
53.1 million, 89.9% of the population, in October 2025.

### Is TikTok worth it for a local Italian business?
It reaches 22.0 million adults (43.5%), so a small test is reasonable. Judge it on booked appointments.

### Why do mobile connections exceed the population?
Many people hold more than one connection, and some are voice or SMS only, DataReportal notes.
