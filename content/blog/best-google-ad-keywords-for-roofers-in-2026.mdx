Exact search volumes and click prices for these terms are only visible in your own Google Ads Keyword Planner, so none are listed here. The nearest published benchmark, Home and Home Improvement in WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 data, is $8.33 per click.

## Which Keyword Groups Should a Roofer Start With?

Build ad groups by job type, because each needs different ad text and a different landing page. Replace [city] with the places you serve.

| Group | Example searches | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repair | roof repair [city], roof leak repair near me | Urgent, ready to call |
| Emergency | emergency roof repair, storm damage roof repair [city] | Urgent, price-insensitive |
| Replacement | roof replacement [city], new roof quote | High value, slower decision |
| Inspection | roof inspection [city], roof inspection before buying | Lower value, can lead to repair |
| Material | metal roofing installer, shingle roofing [city] | Specific, lower volume |
| Trust | licensed roofing contractor [city], best roofer [city] | Comparison stage |

Check the volume and cost of each in Keyword Planner for your location. The numbers vary a lot between a large metro area and a rural county.

## Why Do Place Terms Matter?

Searchers for local services usually name a town or use "near me", and Google can use the searcher's location as well. Adding the city to the keyword does not replace location targeting. Set targeting to the areas you serve, so ads do not run in places you cannot reach. Google requires a radius of at least 1 km and recommends "Presence or interest" for Search as a general default, while "Presence" suits businesses that only want people physically in the area.

Claims that "near me" keywords convert at a set multiple of others are not backed by a public source, so measure it in your own account.

## What Negative Keywords Does a Roofing Campaign Need?

Negatives stop paid clicks from people who will never hire you. Add these before launch and review the Search terms report weekly:

- Jobs: jobs, careers, hiring, salary, apprenticeship
- DIY: diy, how to, tutorial, at home
- Product and trade: wholesale, supplier, shingles for sale, equipment, school
- Areas you do not serve

Google explains that negative keywords do not match close variants, so add singular and plural forms yourself, and that they follow their own match types. Keep a spreadsheet of added negatives.

## Which Match Types Should You Use?

Google defines three types. Broad match can show ads for related searches that do not contain your keyword's direct meaning. Phrase match matches searches that include the meaning of the keyword. Exact match matches the same meaning or intent, gives the most control, and reaches the fewest searches.

Google's help says Smart Bidding is critical with broad match. A practical start for a small account is phrase and exact match with a strong negatives list, then test broad match with conversion tracking once you have data. This is a recommendation, not a measured result.

## How Do Reviews and Landing Pages Affect Results?

Ads send people to a page, and weak pages waste the click. Show your license details where your state requires them, a phone number at the top, a short form and real photos of finished roofs. ## FAQ

### What is the best Google Ads keyword for roofers?
There is no single best term. Repair and emergency searches with a city name are the usual starting point because the searcher needs a roofer soon, but confirm cost and volume in Keyword Planner.

### How much does a roofing click cost?
No roofing figure is published. LocaliQ's 2026 benchmark for Home and Home Improvement is $8.33 per click, which is a range to test against.

### Should roofers use broad match?
Google says Smart Bidding is critical with broad match. Start with phrase and exact, add negatives, and test broad once conversion tracking works.

### What negative keywords should a roofer add?
Jobs, DIY, supplier and wholesale terms, plus any area you do not serve. Add plural forms because negatives do not match close variants.

### How do I stop ads outside my service area?
Use radius or location targeting for the places you serve, and exclude the rest.
