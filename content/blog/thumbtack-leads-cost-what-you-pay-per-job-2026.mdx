Thumbtack has no fixed price per lead. Its Help Center says prices vary with the type and size of the job, how many pros are available and your market, and you can set the price you will pay for each service ([Thumbtack Help: lead prices](https://help.thumbtack.com/article/set-lead-prices), checked Oct 2026). Higher-value jobs such as contracting, HVAC and electrical work generally cost more per lead than cleaning or handyman requests. Because Thumbtack changes its model from time to time, check the price shown in your own account before you spend, and judge the platform by cost per won job.

## How Thumbtack Pricing Works

Thumbtack charges professionals per lead instead of a flat subscription, so you pay for the lead whether or not you win the job. Thumbtack's help center separates two things: your lead price (what you pay for one lead) and your weekly budget (the most you will spend on all leads in a week). Lead price is one of several factors that determine how many leads you receive. Others include your profile, star rating and reviews, how quickly you respond and local demand ([Thumbtack Help: how to set lead prices](https://help.thumbtack.com/article/set-lead-prices), checked Oct 2026).

Charging rules, credits and fees have changed over time, so read the current terms in Thumbtack's pro help center instead of relying on older articles, including this one.

## What Drives the Cost of a Thumbtack Lead

**Trade and job size.** Thumbtack says price follows the type and size of the job, and bigger jobs usually carry higher lead prices.

**City and competition.** Your market and the number of available pros affect the price, so large metro areas generally cost more than smaller markets.

**Season and demand.** Local demand moves with the season, such as landscaping in spring or HVAC in summer and winter, and prices can shift with it.

**Your price settings.** Because you set the price you are willing to pay per service, a lower setting can mean fewer leads.

**Your profile and responsiveness.** A complete profile with reviews and fast replies can improve your results and the share of leads you win, even when the price per lead stays the same.

## What Is a Good Cost Per Lead?

There is no universal answer. The right number depends on your close rate and average job value.

Hypothetical math (not a Thumbtack price): if a plumbing lead costs $35, you win 1 in 4 leads, and the average job brings in $400, your cost per customer is $140 against $400 revenue. Whether that works depends on your margins. If you win 1 in 10, the same lead would cost $350 per customer and probably not pay.

Track these four numbers for every platform you use:

1. Leads purchased
2. Leads that replied
3. Jobs won
4. Revenue per job

## How to Get More Value From Every Lead

**Respond fast.** The first professional to reply often wins the job. Turn on app notifications and reply within minutes where you can.

**Quote only jobs you can win.** Skip jobs outside your service area, price range or skill set.

**Build reviews.** After each completed job, ask for a review with a direct link. Reviews help conversion on every platform. Our guide on [getting more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-5-star-google-reviews-local-business) has scripts.

**Set a weekly budget.** Thumbtack lets you set a weekly budget that caps what you spend on all leads. Review results every week and adjust after about a month.

**Ask for a refund on invalid leads.** A Thumbtack staff reply in its community forum says a lead that violates the Terms of Use qualifies for a refund, while a customer who simply stops replying is not covered ([Thumbtack Community](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/comment/5974), checked Oct 2026). Check the current policy in the app before you request one.

**Automate follow-up.** Quick templated replies and reminders help you not lose warm leads. See [AI agents for lead generation](/blog/ai-agent-for-lead-generation-local-business).

## Is Thumbtack Worth It?

It can be, for project-based service businesses where one job pays for many leads, and where you can reply quickly. It is usually a weaker fit for businesses built on repeat visits, such as salons, fitness studios and cafes, where building your own Google presence and email list pays off more. A balanced approach is to add Thumbtack to your own channels, not rely on it alone, and keep your own Google Business Profile strong. ## A Simple Way to Test Thumbtack

1. **Set a test budget** you can afford to lose for about a month, and use the weekly cap in your account.
2. **Complete your profile** with photos, services, service area and a clear description before you start.
3. **Log every lead** in a spreadsheet: date, job type, price paid, replied or not, quote sent, job won and revenue.
4. **Calculate cost per won job** after the test: total lead spend divided by jobs won.
5. **Compare it** with your other sources, such as Google Ads, referrals and Local Services Ads, and put more budget behind the cheapest profitable source.

If your cost per won job is higher than the profit on an average job, change your targeting, pricing or response speed, or stop. Tracking this is more reliable than any average quoted in an article.

## Signs a Lead Is Worth Paying For

- The customer gave a clear description, timing and location.
- The job is in your service area and matches your skills.
- The budget is realistic for the work described.
- You can respond quickly, ideally with a phone call or message right away.

Leads missing these details often waste money, so skip them where the platform lets you choose.

## FAQ

**Q: How much do Thumbtack leads cost?**
A: It depends on your trade, location and the request. Thumbtack says it varies with job type and size, number of pros and your market, so check the price in your account instead of trusting averages.

**Q: Does Thumbtack charge me even if I don't get the job?**
A: Yes, you pay for the lead, not for winning the job, so a non-responsive customer is generally not refundable. That is why your conversion rate matters.

**Q: Can I get a refund on a bad lead?**
A: Possibly. A Thumbtack staff reply says leads that violate the Terms of Use qualify for a refund, but non-responsive customers are not covered. Check the current policy in the app and submit requests promptly.

**Q: How long does it take to see results on Thumbtack?**
A: Allow several weeks. A complete profile, reviews and fast replies improve your chances over time.

**Q: Should I use Thumbtack and Google Local Services Ads together?**
A: Many service businesses do, because they reach customers at different stages. Compare the cost per won job for each, then put more budget behind the winner.
