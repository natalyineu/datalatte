A small business in Montenegro should start with Instagram and Facebook, add TikTok if the customer is under 35, and keep a Google Business Profile for people searching on a phone. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report puts Instagram ad reach at 445,000, Facebook at 329,000 and TikTok at 395,000 adults, in a country of about 630,000 people.

This guide uses published audience data only. No public Montenegrin ad-price data (CPC or CPM) was available from a source that could be opened, so this guide has no price benchmarks. Treat any price you see elsewhere as unverified until you see it in your own ad account.

## How Big Is the Online Audience in Montenegro?

DataReportal reports 566,000 internet users at the start of 2026, a penetration of 89.7%, and 445,000 social media user identities, equal to 70.6% of the population. The country is small: 69.3% of people live in cities, and the median age is 40.

World Bank data lists Montenegro's 2025 GDP at US$9.23 billion, GDP per capita at US$14,816.8 and annual growth at 2.7%. The market is small, mostly online and mid-income, so one campaign can cover most of the country.

DataReportal warns that ad reach is not a count of active users and may include duplicates. Use it to compare platforms, not as an exact headcount.

## Which Platforms Reach the Most People?

Instagram leads on reach in DataReportal's table, followed by TikTok among adults and Facebook. LinkedIn and X are small.

## Can Montenegrin Businesses Run Google Ads?

Yes. Google's Ads terms page lists Montenegro in its Europe, Middle East and Africa table with the language English. Google's country restriction page names only six sanctioned locations where Google Ads is unavailable (Crimea, Cuba, the so-called Donetsk and Luhansk People's Republics, Iran and North Korea), and Montenegro is not among them.

No Google Ads Help page states Montenegrin language support for ad text. Check the language settings in your account before you build campaigns in Montenegrin.

## What Currency and Legal Rules Apply?

Montenegro pays in euros. The Central Bank of Montenegro says the German mark became the only legal tender in January 2001 and that the euro became the official means of payment in March 2002. Montenegro is not in the eurozone: the bank notes it entered this regime before the ECOFIN Council adopted its position against unilateral euroisation. Your ad account can bill in euros.

For email, SMS and retargeting, the Montenegrin framework needs consent. Karanovic and Partners, a law firm and a secondary source, says personal data may be used for marketing only with the subject's consent and that the Agency for Personal Data Protection and Free Access to Information supervises.

Sources disagree on whether a newer data law has replaced the 2008 law. Confirm the current text with the agency or a local lawyer before you send marketing messages.

Advertising content rules, for example for alcohol or health claims, are unverified here. No Montenegrin regulator page on them was opened.

## FAQ

### Is Facebook or Instagram better for a Montenegrin small business?

On DataReportal's ad reach, Instagram (445,000) is ahead of Facebook (329,000). Facebook still reaches 58.1% of internet users, so the better choice depends on your customer's age. Test both with the same offer.

### Does TikTok work in Montenegro?

It reaches 395,000 adults, 69.8% of internet users, and was the fastest-growing platform in the report. No Montenegrin conversion data is public, so run a small test before you commit.

### How much do ads cost in Montenegro?

No public benchmark could be verified. Set a small budget, read your account's actual CPC or CPM, and use that.

### Is the euro safe to use for ad billing?

The euro has been the official means of payment since March 2002, per the Central Bank of Montenegro. Check the billing options shown in your own Google Ads or Meta account.
