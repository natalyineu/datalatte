## How Does Instagram Decide Which Reels to Show?

Instagram's ranking explainer says Reels work like Explore: it finds Reels you may like, then ranks them by how interesting they seem to you, and most come from accounts you do not follow. The key predictions are how likely you are to reshare a reel, watch it all the way through and like it.

The signals, in rough order, are your own activity, your history with the creator, information about the reel such as audio and visuals, and information about the creator. For a studio, the practical read is simple: a short clip that people send to a friend beats a long clip nobody finishes.

## What Makes a Reel Less Likely to Be Shown?

Instagram states it aims to make some Reels less visible: low resolution, watermarks, muted audio, borders, mostly text, and content already posted on Instagram. That affects common habits. Re-uploading a TikTok with its logo, filming a class through a window or stacking text over a still image all work against you.

Film vertically on a phone, in good light, with sound. A Reel of a coach showing one movement mistake and its fix fits the stated signals without needing any trick.

## Which Metrics Should a Studio Track?

Meta's developer documentation defines the Reels metrics. Reach is the number of unique users who saw the reel at least once. Views count total plays. Saved and shares count how many people saved or sent it. Total interactions adds likes, saves, comments and shares, minus unlikes, unsaves and deleted comments. Average watch time shows how long people stay.

Meta marks some of these as still in development, so definitions can change. Track reach, saves, shares and average watch time weekly in a simple sheet, and note which Reels led to a trial-class booking.

## What Do Reels Ads Cost for a Fitness Studio?

Meta does not publish a Reels-only price. LocaliQ's September 2026 Facebook benchmarks, which cover Meta placements broadly, show Health and Fitness lead campaigns at $1.84 per click, a 3.09% click-through rate, a 7.98% conversion rate and $27.11 per lead. The all-industry lead average is $27.39. Traffic campaigns in Health and Fitness average $0.64 per click.

For creative, Meta's specifications call for full-screen 9:16 video under 90 seconds, with the bottom 35% kept free of text and logos. Organic reels used as ads also must not be shared to Facebook, and must avoid third-party copyrighted music.

## What Should You Post?

Pick formats that are easy to share and finish. Ideas that match Instagram's stated signals include a 20-second form fix, a coach answering one beginner question, a class warm-up routine, a before-class setup showing the room, and a member-approved walkthrough of a first visit.

## FAQ

### How often should a fitness studio post Reels?

No source we opened gives a posting frequency that Instagram rewards. Choose a pace you can keep, such as one or two a week, and judge it by reach, saves and trial bookings.

### Do hashtags increase Reels reach?

The ranking signals Instagram lists in its explainer do not mention hashtags. Use them for context if you like, but do not count on them for reach.

### Can I run an organic Reel as an ad?

Yes, with conditions. Meta says the reel must not be shared to Facebook and must avoid third-party copyrighted music, GIFs, interactive stickers and camera filters.

### How long can a Reels ad be?

Meta's specification says less than 90 seconds, in a 9:16 vertical format.

### How do I know if Reels bring in members?

Add a link or code to each Reel, ask new leads how they found you, and compare trial bookings with reach. See the [website conversion guide for fitness studios](/blog/website-cro-for-fitness-studios) for the landing page side.
