YouTube suits a local business when the service benefits from being seen: before-and-after work, a tour of the premises, or a 30-second explanation of a confusing offer. Skippable in-stream views are commonly reported between $0.01 and $0.05 in 2026 secondary benchmarks, and Google Ads sets no fixed minimum daily budget.

A view is charged when a viewer watches 30 seconds, finishes a shorter ad, or interacts, on YouTube's published rules checked in November 2026. That pricing rewards a strong first 5 seconds; most local ads should name the service and suburb before the skip button appears.

## What do YouTube ads cost a local business?

There is no rate card. Secondary 2026 benchmarks put skippable CPV between $0.01 and $0.05, in-feed clicks higher, and non-skippable formats on CPM pricing. Competitive categories in large metros sit above those ranges; a single suburb with modest competition often sits inside them.

Practical tests start around $10 a day. Below that, postcode-level targeting delivers too few views to judge creative within a month. A $300 first month usually answers the only question that matters: cost per call, direction request or booking from the video's landing page.

**Assumption example.** Assume $300 buys views at an assumed $0.03 CPV, or 10,000 charged views. If 1% visit the site and 5% of visits book, the test yields 5 bookings at $60 each. Every rate is an assumption; replace CPV and conversion with the campaign's first two weeks of real data.

Compare that with pure audio before splitting budgets. [Audio ads vs display ads](/blog/audio-ads-vs-display-ads-local-business) prices the same $300 test across formats, and [Peacock live sports ads](/blog/peacock-live-sports-advertising-local-business) covers the premium streaming end of video.

## How should a local business target YouTube?

Start with postcode radius around the premises, then layer content targeting: channels and topics the customer already watches. A plumber gains more from home-repair content near the trade area than from broad demographic settings across a metro.

Separate brand and generic creative. A search-adjacent video answering "how much does X cost in [suburb]" catches active research. A 20-second tour builds familiarity. Blending both jobs into one advert usually produces a video that does neither.

Send viewers to a page that matches the video's promise, with call and direction buttons visible without scrolling. [Google Display Network guide](/blog/google-display-network-guide) covers the postcode discipline that applies to both formats.

## What makes a local YouTube ad work?

Name the business, suburb and service in the first 5 seconds, before the skip. Show the real premises and staff; stock footage wastes the one advantage a local advertiser has over national chains. End with one action: call, get directions, or book.

Keep one idea per video. A café advertising breakfast, catering and events in 30 seconds gives the viewer nothing to remember. Three short videos, one per offer, let the budget follow whichever produces calls.

## FAQ

### How much should a local business spend on YouTube ads?

There is no platform minimum; practical local tests start near $10 a day, or about $300 a month, on 2026 guide consensus. Judge the test on cost per call or booking, not cost per view. Views are cheap almost by design; bookings decide whether a second month happens.

### Are YouTube ads cheaper than Meta ads?

Per completed view, usually yes: secondary 2026 benchmarks put skippable CPV at $0.01 to $0.05. Per booking, it depends on the offer's need for demonstration. Visual, considered services favour video; impulse and reminder offers often convert cheaper on Meta. Run both against the same booking page to compare honestly.

### What video does a small business need to start?

One phone-shot 20 to 40 second video per offer, filmed in the real premises with the service and suburb named in the first 5 seconds. Captions are essential; much viewing is muted. Production polish matters less than the match between the video's promise and the landing page behind it.

### Can YouTube ads target one suburb?

Yes. Google Ads supports postcode and radius targeting for video campaigns, so a single-site business can limit delivery to its trade area. Combine location with content targeting on relevant channels and topics. Tight geography keeps the modest daily budget concentrated instead of spread across a metro.
