YouTube ads suit a local business when the service benefits from being seen first: before-and-after work, a tour of the premises, or a short explanation of a costly service. Skippable in-stream views are reported at about $0.01 to $0.03 each in 2026 pricing guides, and Google Ads sets no fixed minimum budget. A practical local test starts near $10 a day, roughly $300 a month, and should be judged on calls, direction requests and bookings rather than views. All figures are in US dollars.

## What do YouTube ads cost a local business?

There is no published rate card. AMW's 2026 pricing guide reports average cost per view at $0.010 to $0.030 and CPM at $5 to $20, and it puts a single produced video between $1,000 and $10,000 or more ([AMW](https://amworldgroup.com/pricing/youtube-ads-cost), secondary). These are vendor figures for the platform in general, not for any one suburb. Competitive categories in large metros tend to sit at the top of the range, while a single suburb with modest competition often sits inside it.

Production is the cost people underestimate. A short video filmed in your own premises costs far less than the top of that production range, so most local tests begin with simple footage. Budget the media spend separately from filming, and keep the first test large enough to produce a usable answer within a month.

## How does YouTube charge for a view?

Google counts a view when a viewer watches 30 seconds of an ad, watches a shorter ad to the end, or interacts with it ([Google Ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2375464)). Skippable in-stream ads can be skipped after five seconds, so the opening decides most of the outcome. A viewer who skips should still have seen your business name and service ([Heights Digital](https://heightsdigitalmedia.com/pages/blog/youtube-ads-for-local-business), secondary).

Bumper ads run six seconds and cannot be skipped, which makes them reminders rather than explanations ([Heights Digital](https://heightsdigitalmedia.com/pages/blog/youtube-ads-for-local-business), secondary). Non-skippable formats are priced by impression, so their cost logic differs. For a small budget, skippable in-stream is usually the format to test first, because you pay only when a viewer commits to watching.

## What does a $300 test buy for a local business?

The inputs below are assumptions for illustration. Replace them with your campaign's data after two weeks.

Assume a $300 monthly test, a cost per view of $0.03 (assumption, inside the $0.010 to $0.030 range above), 1% of viewers visiting the website (assumption), and 5% of those visits booking a job (assumption).

Views = $300 / $0.03 = 10,000. Visits = 10,000 × 1% = 100. Bookings = 100 × 5% = 5. Cost per booking = $300 / 5 = $60.

The same calculation at other assumed view prices:

| Assumed cost per view | Charged views from $300 | Visits (1%, assumption) | Bookings (5%, assumption) | Cost per booking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $0.01 | 30,000 | 300 | 15 | $20 |
| $0.02 | 15,000 | 150 | 7.5 | $40 |
| $0.03 | 10,000 | 100 | 5 | $60 |

Cost per booking, not cost per view, decides whether a second month runs. A $60 booking may be cheap for a roof repair and expensive for a haircut, so set the threshold against your own job value before the test starts. Fractional bookings in the table are rounding artefacts of the assumed rates, not real outcomes.

## How should a local business target YouTube ads?

Start with location. Set a radius or a postcode list around the premises, using presence targeting so the ad reaches people physically in the area. Heights Digital notes that YouTube supports ZIP code and radius targeting ([Heights Digital](https://heightsdigitalmedia.com/pages/blog/youtube-ads-for-local-business), secondary). Layering many conditions onto a small area shrinks the audience until delivery drops, so add content targeting cautiously.

Separate the jobs each video does. A 20-second tour builds familiarity, while a video answering a cost question catches people already researching. Blending both into one advert usually produces a video that does neither. The [step-by-step YouTube ads guide](/blog/how-to-run-youtube-ads-for-local-business-step-by-step) walks through campaign setup in order, and [Google Local Services Ads](/blog/google-local-service-ads-worth-it-local-business) cover the search side for people ready to book now.

## What makes a local YouTube ad work?

Name the business, the suburb and the service in the first five seconds, before the skip. Show the real premises and staff, because stock footage removes the one advantage a local advertiser has over national chains. End with a single action: call, get directions or book.

Heights Digital recommends 15 to 60 seconds for most local ads ([Heights Digital](https://heightsdigitalmedia.com/pages/blog/youtube-ads-for-local-business), secondary). Keep one idea per video. Three short videos, one per offer, let the budget follow whichever produces calls. ## FAQ

### How much should a local business spend on YouTube ads?

There is no platform minimum. Practical local tests start near $10 a day, or about $300 a month, according to 2026 guide consensus. Judge the test on cost per call or booking rather than cost per view, since views are cheap by design.

### Do viewers use YouTube to decide on a purchase?

AMW reports that 70% of viewers say YouTube helps them decide ([AMW](https://amworldgroup.com/pricing/youtube-ads-cost), secondary). Treat that as a vendor figure, not an independent study, and confirm it with your own booking data.

### Can YouTube ads target one suburb?

Yes. Google Ads supports postcode and radius targeting for video campaigns, so a single-site business can limit delivery to its trade area ([Google Ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2375464)). Tight geography keeps a modest daily budget concentrated instead of spread across a metro.

### Does a small business need professional video to start?

Not necessarily. AMW's production range of $1,000 to $10,000 or more per ad describes polished productions ([AMW](https://amworldgroup.com/pricing/youtube-ads-cost), secondary). A practical start is one short phone-shot video per offer, filmed in the premises with the service and suburb named early, and kept within 15 to 60 seconds.
