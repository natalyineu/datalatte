A local business can start on TikTok with a business account, a few short videos showing the place, the people and the product, and a clear way for viewers to find you. Paid ads come after that, once you know which videos people respond to. Pew Research Center's 2026 survey found 31% of US adults use TikTok, rising to 65% of those aged 18 to 29 and falling to 11% of those 65 and over, so check that your customers are in the age groups it reaches.

TikTok has real limits for local reach, listed below. This guide covers who is on it, how to start organically, what paid ads require and a worked example with assumed numbers.

## Is TikTok Right for a Local Business?

It suits businesses whose customers are under 50 and who have something worth filming: food, hair, pets, fitness, makers. Pew's fieldwork ran from January 30 to June 18, 2026, with 5,511 US adults and a margin of error of 1.8 points. Facebook (70%), Instagram (51%) and YouTube (84%) reached more US adults overall.

Reach is not the same as local reach. A video can be seen far from your shop. Treat TikTok as a way to build recognition and show your work, and keep Google Business Profile and Search as the way people find your address. Our [Instagram versus Facebook comparison](/blog/instagram-ads-vs-facebook-ads) shows how the older platforms compare.

## How Do You Start Posting?

Create a business account, fill in the profile with your area and hours, and post short videos of real work: a cut being finished, a drink being poured, a groom being completed, a class warming up. Keep the first seconds clear about what the video shows. Add your town in the caption so viewers know it is local.

One rule is easy to miss. TikTok says businesses cannot use its general music library for commercial use and should use the Commercial Music Library, a catalog of roughly one million tracks, for organic posts, video ads and branded content. Check the music source before you post.

## How Does Local Targeting Work for TikTok Ads?

TikTok's Ads Help lists these location types in the US: country, state, city, county, DMA region and zip code. The page does not mention radius targeting. It says you can select up to 3,000 locations per ad group, that zip codes help when you need finer targeting than a DMA or city, and that excluding locations is not supported.

That means a small shop targets by zip codes or city rather than a drawn circle. Zip codes can include people who would never visit, so watch whether the sign-ups and calls come from nearby. ## What Does TikTok Advertising Require?

TikTok's budget help says the daily campaign budget must exceed $50 and the ad group daily budget must exceed $20. For a lifetime ad group budget, the minimum is $20 multiplied by the number of days, so a 31-day ad group needs at least $620. These minimums set a floor that is higher than some owners expect.

Promote, TikTok's in-app boost tool, offers goals such as growing followers or LIVE viewers, and after integration with Ads Manager it can use website conversion goals. The help page we opened does not state a minimum budget for Promote. We did not find a published TikTok CPM or CPC benchmark we could verify, so measure your own. ## What Does a First Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**, except the $20 minimum daily ad group budget from TikTok's help page.

| Item | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily ad group budget | TikTok minimum is above $20; assumption | $21 |
| Days (assumption) | Chosen | 7 |
| Test budget | $21 x 7 | $147 |
| Cost per landing-page visit (assumption) | Chosen; no published TikTok benchmark | $1.00 |
| Visits | $147 / $1.00 | 147 |
| Visit to booking or coupon claim (assumption) | Chosen | 3% |
| Bookings | 147 x 3% | about 4 |
| Cost per booking | $147 / 4 | about $37 |

If the cost per visit were $2.00, the cost per booking would double to about $74. The point of the test is to learn your own number, not to hit a target. Use a unique offer or landing page so you can count results, and compare the cost per booking with what a customer is worth.

## What Mistakes Should You Avoid?

Common ones are posting once and stopping, copying trends that do not fit your brand, using unlicensed music, and boosting a video before you know whether anyone cares about it. Another is judging results by views. Count calls, direction requests, bookings and coupon claims.

Before you spend, post for a few weeks, see which videos get saved or shared, and boost only one of those. See the [step-by-step coffee shop TikTok ads guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-for-coffee-shops-step-by-step-guide) for a test with a set budget.

## FAQ

### Is TikTok good for local businesses?

It can be if your customers are in the age groups that use it. Pew finds 65% of US adults aged 18 to 29 use TikTok, versus 31% of all adults.

### How much does TikTok advertising cost?

TikTok requires daily ad group budgets above $20 and campaign budgets above $50. We did not find a verified CPM or CPC benchmark, so run a small test and measure.

### Can I target a radius around my shop?

TikTok's location targeting page lists country, state, city, county, DMA and zip codes, and does not mention radius.

### Do I need a special music library?

Businesses should use the Commercial Music Library, according to TikTok. The general music library is not for commercial use.

### Should I start with organic posts or ads?

Organic posts first. They cost time, not budget, and show which videos are worth boosting.
