## Which Facebook ad format fits a local business?

The right format depends on the action you want. AdEspresso counts seven basic ad formats, and the ones most relevant to a local shop are image, video, slideshow and Stories. Each format has a trade-off that matters for a small team with limited time and budget.

| Format | Best for | Watch-out from the sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single image | One offer, one product, a clear location | Only one image to carry the message, so it struggles to demonstrate a process ([AdEspresso](https://adespresso.com/guides/facebook-ads-beginner/facebook-ads-types/)) |
| Video | Showing a space, a service or a process | Time-consuming to make; a simple image may be more efficient for a simple message ([AdEspresso](https://adespresso.com/guides/facebook-ads-beginner/facebook-ads-types/)) |
| Slideshow | Low-effort motion for a short offer | Uses 3 to 10 images or one video; needs music you have rights to use ([AdEspresso](https://adespresso.com/guides/facebook-ads-beginner/facebook-ads-types/)) |
| Stories | Brand presence in full-screen vertical format | Not placed in feeds, and needs formatting different from feed ads ([AdEspresso](https://adespresso.com/guides/facebook-ads-beginner/facebook-ads-types/)) |
| Carousel | Several services or menu items | Only useful when each card has a distinct item; a single offer does not need it (assumption based on the format's purpose) |

Use the table as a filter, not a ranking. A café wanting walk-ins has different needs from a salon wanting bookings, and the format should follow that goal.

## How long should a Facebook video ad be?

AdEspresso reports that Facebook suggests keeping video ads short, under 15 seconds. The same guide advises starting with a strong opening so the viewer stays engaged. For a local business, this usually means one moment that shows the space, the product or the result, followed by a clear call to action.

Video drives high engagement, according to the same guide, but it is slower to produce than an image. A barber or dental clinic can film a short clip on a phone, but the production budget should be counted in hours, not minutes. If you only need to say one thing, a well-lit single image may do the job faster.

## When does a slideshow ad beat a video?

Slideshow ads use three to ten images or a single video that plays as a slideshow. AdEspresso reports that they use five times less data than video ads, which is why Meta positions them for markets with limited internet connections. For a local business, the practical benefit is lower production effort and a format that still includes motion.

Slideshows work best as a stepping stone. If you already have a strong video, you can turn its best frames into a slideshow. Avoid building a slideshow around a weak image set, because the format depends on high-quality images and a clear sequence. Confirm your music rights before you publish.

## Are Stories ads worth the extra production?

Stories ads are full-screen, vertical experiences shown between Stories in Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp. AdEspresso notes that they allow effects, emojis and augmented reality, which gives room for brand personality. The same source warns that Stories are not placed in feeds, so they are not a complete replacement for feed ads.

Stories also require unique formatting. If you cannot make vertical content regularly, the extra work may not pay back. A practical approach is to run feed ads first, then add a Stories version only when you have an existing vertical clip that works without rebuilding it. Stories suit brand awareness better than a single offer that needs a fast response.

## How should a local business plan a format test?

Testing beats guessing because no single format wins for every local business. The example below uses assumed inputs. None of the figures are benchmarks, and you should replace them with your own numbers after the first two weeks of data.

All inputs are **assumptions**:

- Total test budget: $450 for 30 days
- Test A: single 4:5 image to a lead form, $150
- Test B: 15-second vertical video to a lead form, $150
- Test C: click-to-message ad using the stronger creative from A or B, $150
- Enquiries that became booked appointments: Test A 3, Test B 5, Test C 4

Cost per booked appointment: Test A is $150 divided by 3, which is $50. Test B is $150 divided by 5, which is $30. Test C is $150 divided by 4, which is $37.50. In this assumed case, the video to lead form wins, so the next month shifts most of the budget toward it. The decision rule matters more than the numbers: count only enquiries that became appointments, not form fills.

For budget ranges across the month, read [how much to spend on Facebook ads for a local business](/blog/facebook-ads-budget-local-business-how-much-to-spend). The $450 test above is small, so treat it as a learning budget rather than a steady-state plan.

## What budget do local businesses start with?

ActGlobalMarketing, an agency, reports in its own guide that local businesses often invest between $800 and $2,500 per month. The same guide reports cost per lead between $8 and $25 in its Florida campaigns, and a 90-day Orlando case with a $1,500 monthly budget that generated $96,000 in revenue. These are secondary, agency-reported figures, drawn from one agency's campaigns, and should not be treated as a benchmark for your market.

The useful lesson is the structure: fix a test budget, split it across a small number of formats, and judge them on the same outcome. A test that runs for two weeks with clear tracking teaches more than a month of unmeasured spend.

## FAQ

### What is the best Facebook ad format for a small local business?
No single format wins in every case. Start with one single-image or short-video ad that has a clear call to action, then test a lead form or message ad against it for two weeks.

### How long should a Facebook video ad be?
AdEspresso reports that Facebook suggests keeping video ads under 15 seconds. Lead with the most important visual in the first few seconds.

### Should a local business use Stories ads?
Only if you can produce vertical content regularly. Stories are not placed in feeds, so they work best as an addition to feed ads rather than a replacement.

### How much should I spend to test Facebook ad formats?
There is no universal figure. The $450 example in this guide uses assumed inputs. Pick a test budget you can sustain for at least two weeks and judge formats on cost per booked appointment.
