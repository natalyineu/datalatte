For most local businesses, start with one single-image or short-video ad in a 4:5 feed ratio plus a 9:16 vertical version for Stories and Reels, and send people to a call, a message or a lead form. Carousel and collection ads only pay off when you have several products or services to show.

Meta does not publish a ranking of formats for local businesses, so test two formats against each other before scaling.

This guide compares the main Meta ad formats using Meta's own specifications, checked on 2026-11-01.

## Which Facebook Ad Format Is Best for a Local Business?

There is no single best format. Pick by the action you want. A café wanting walk-ins needs a clear photo and a location. A salon wanting bookings may use a lead form or a message. A studio launching a class series can use a short vertical video.

| Format | Best for | Meta spec to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single image | Simple offers, one product | 4:5 for Facebook Feed, 1:1 for Instagram Feed |
| Video | Showing a space, a service, a process | 4:5 feed; 9:16 for Stories and Reels |
| Carousel | Several services or menu items | 2 to 10 cards; up to 3 cards in Instagram Stories |
| Collection | Product catalogs | Catalog product set of at least 4 products |
| Lead ad (instant form) | Quotes, enquiries, sign-ups | At least one question; a privacy policy is required |
| Click to message | Questions and bookings by chat | Opens Messenger, Instagram or WhatsApp |

## What Image and Video Sizes Does Meta Recommend?

Meta says Feed placements support both 1:1 and 4:5 for images and videos. It recommends 4:5 for single-image ads on Facebook Feed and 1:1 for Instagram Feed. For Stories, Status and Reels it recommends 9:16, because most people hold phones vertically. In-stream video should be 16:9.

If you upload only one ratio, Meta may crop or pad it to fit other placements. Check the auto-generated crops before publishing so text and faces are not cut off. Meta's Ads Manager can also take up to 10 images or videos in one ad, and you can add 9:16 versions when Stories matters.

## How Do Carousel Ads Work?

A carousel shows two or more images or videos in a single ad, each with its own headline, description, link and call to action. On Facebook the limit is 10 cards. On Facebook, Meta accepts 1:1, 4:5 or 9:16 cards, with a recommended 1024 x 1024 px for 1:1 images and a 500 x 500 px minimum.

Copy limits on Facebook are 125 characters of text, 40 characters of headline and 25 of link description. Videos in a carousel autoplay as people swipe, and play without sound unless the viewer turns it on. Meta recommends 15 seconds for carousel video.

Use a carousel for a menu with six drinks, a salon with six services, or a studio with six class types. Do not use one for a single offer.

## When Do Collection Ads Make Sense?

Collection ads pair a cover image or video with a grid of products below. Tapping opens a full-screen Instant Experience. Meta requires a catalog product set of at least 4 products. That makes them useful for retail stores with a catalog and mostly irrelevant for a barber or a yoga studio with no product feed.

## Should You Use Lead Ads or Click-to-Message?

Meta says lead ads with an instant form collect information without people leaving Facebook or Instagram, and contact details such as name, email and phone may be prefilled. A form needs at least one question and a privacy policy. Forms have types, including more volume, higher intent and rich creative. Meta says phone number verification by one-time password is available to filter fake numbers.

Click-to-message ads send people into a chat with your business on Messenger, Instagram or WhatsApp. They suit businesses that already answer messages quickly. Meta does not publish a cost comparison between the two on its help pages, so test both.

## Worked Example: Splitting a $450 Monthly Test Budget

All inputs are **assumptions**, not benchmarks.

- Budget: $450 for 30 days (assumption)
- Test A: 4:5 single-image ad to a lead form, $150
- Test B: 9:16 15-second video to a lead form, $150
- Test C: click-to-message with the better of the two creatives, $150
- Decision rule: keep the format with the lowest cost per real enquiry (a call or a booked slot, not a form fill)

Run each for at least two weeks before judging. Count only enquiries that became appointments. See [how much to spend on Facebook ads](/blog/facebook-ads-budget-local-business-how-much-to-spend) for budget ranges.

## FAQ

### What is the best Facebook ad format for a small local business?
There is no proven winner. Start with a single image or short video and a clear call to action, then test a lead form or message ad.

### What size should a Facebook Feed image ad be?
Meta recommends a 4:5 ratio for single-image ads on Facebook Feed and 1:1 for Instagram Feed.

### How many cards can a Facebook carousel have?
Between 2 and 10. Instagram Stories carousels allow up to 3 cards, and Instagram Feed up to 10.

### Do I need a product catalog for collection ads?
Yes. Meta requires a catalog product set of at least 4 products.

### Are lead ads or message ads better?
Neither is better in general. Lead ads capture details quickly. Message ads suit businesses that reply fast.
