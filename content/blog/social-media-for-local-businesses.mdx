A local business should post original photos and short videos of real work, pick one or two platforms, and send people to a booking or contact link. Pew Research Center's 2026 survey shows 70% of US adults use Facebook and 51% use Instagram, so those two cover most local customers.

## Which Platform Should a Local Business Start With?

Choose where your customers already are. Pew's 2026 data: YouTube 84%, Facebook 70%, Instagram 51%, Pinterest 37%, LinkedIn 33%, TikTok 31%, X 25%. Most local services should begin with Facebook and Instagram, using one set of photos for both.

Add a second channel only when the first runs on a schedule. A business that posts weekly on two platforms beats one that posts in bursts on five. Our [social media strategy comparison of organic and paid](/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media) helps with that choice.

## The Ten Tips

1. **Post original content.** Instagram says reposted, watermarked content and accounts that regularly reshare others' work are less likely to be recommended.
2. **Use keywords.** Instagram recommends relevant keywords in captions, bio and hashtags so people find you through search.
3. **Show the work.** Photos and short videos of finished jobs, with the neighbourhood named.
4. **Make Reels short.** Instagram notes recommended Reels can run no longer than three minutes.
5. **Reply to comments and messages.** Feed ranking weighs comments and shares, per Instagram's explainer.
6. **Tag partners with Collabs.** Instagram suggests Collabs for shared posts.
7. **Check Insights monthly.** Look at how much reach comes from non-followers.
8. **Keep your Google Business Profile current.** It is free and shows posts, reviews and booking options.
9. **Ask for reviews.** Google lets you share a link or QR code.
10. **Link every post to one action.** Book, call or visit.

## How Does Instagram Decide What to Show?

Instagram has said it has no single algorithm. For Feed, its 2023 explainer lists five predictions it weighs most: time spent on a post, comments, likes, shares and taps on the profile photo. For Reels, the key predictions are watching to the end, resharing, liking and visiting the audio page.

For Stories, the main inputs are viewing history, engagement history and closeness to the author. The explainer is dated May 2023, and Instagram has not published exact weights, so treat it as direction.

## Do Reviews Matter More Than Posts?

For discovery they often do. BrightLocal's 2026 Local Consumer Review Survey reports that 97% of consumers read online reviews. Google's help says you can ask customers to visit a Google link or scan a QR code, and prohibits offering free or discounted goods in exchange for reviews.

## What Does a Realistic Weekly Plan Look Like? A Worked Example

Inputs are an **assumption** for a shop owner with three hours a week.

| Day | Task | Time (assumption) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Shoot 6 photos and 2 short videos | 60 min |
| Tuesday | Post one Reel, reply to comments | 30 min |
| Thursday | Post one photo with a booking link | 20 min |
| Friday | Post to Stories, ask one customer for a review | 30 min |
| Sunday | Check Insights, note best post | 20 min |

That is 2 hours 40 minutes. After a month, keep the post type with the most saves and shares and drop the weakest.

## FAQ

### How often should a local business post?
No platform publishes an exact figure. Pick a number you can keep, such as three posts and a few Stories a week, and review monthly in Insights.

### Is Instagram or Facebook better for a local business?
Pew shows Facebook reaches 70% of US adults and Instagram 51%. Your own customers decide, so ask five of them where they look first.

### Do hashtags still matter?
Instagram's creator guidance lists hashtags next to captions and bio as places for relevant keywords. It does not publish a hashtag count.

### Should I pay for social ads?
Only after the organic posts and profile work. See the [social media advertising cost guide](/blog/social-media-advertising-cost-guide).

### How do I get more reviews?
Share your Google review link or QR code and ask in person. Do not offer discounts for reviews, which Google prohibits.
