## What Is Programmatic Video Advertising?

Programmatic means ads are bought by software in an auction, in real time, instead of through a fixed booking. Video is one inventory type. For a local business, the most direct way in is Google Ads on YouTube and Google's video partners, where you set the audience, location and budget yourself.

Larger demand-side platforms also sell video and connected TV placements, but their minimum spends and fees vary by vendor and were not verified here. For a plain-English overview, read [how programmatic buying works for local business](/blog/how-programmatic-buying-works-local-business) and the [programmatic CPM benchmark guide](/blog/how-much-does-programmatic-advertising-cost-real-cpm-benchmarks-2026).

## Which Video Ad Format Should You Pick?

Google Ads Help describes four YouTube formats. Billing depends on the bidding strategy you pick.

| Format | Length and behaviour | How you pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skippable in-stream | Viewer can skip after 5 seconds | CPV bidding: when a viewer watches 30 seconds (or the whole video if shorter) or interacts. Other strategies: per impression |
| Non-skippable in-stream | Cannot be skipped; 60 seconds or shorter per Google's page | Target CPM: per impression |
| Bumper | Non-skippable, 5 to 6 seconds | Target CPM: per impression |
| In-feed | Thumbnail and text in search results, next to related videos or on the mobile homepage | Usually when someone clicks to watch; in Video View campaigns, when the ad autoplays and is watched for at least 10 seconds |

For awareness of a single offer, bumper ads keep the message short. For a service people must understand, such as a dentist or a gym, skippable in-stream gives you time to explain and charges you only for engaged views under CPV.

## How Do You Keep Video Ads Local?

Use location targeting. Google's help states that radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km and that very small areas may show only intermittently or not at all. Google also recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting for Search and says "Presence" fits when you want people physically in the area.

Keep language and location settings consistent, as Google advises. A neighbourhood business may be better off choosing a city or postcode-sized area than a tiny radius.

## What Should the Video Say?

With a skip button at 5 seconds, the first five seconds carry the message: who you are, where you are and what to do. Show the place, the service or the finished result, and end with one action such as a call or a booking link. Short is fine: bumper ads last 5 to 6 seconds.

No source verified here gives a best-performing length, so test two versions against each other. Our [programmatic vs Google Display comparison](/blog/programmatic-vs-google-display-ads-whats-the-difference) shows where video fits among other formats.

## What Does a $500 Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All numbers are an **assumption**, because Google does not publish an average CPV. Suppose a salon sets a $500 budget and a CPV of $0.05 for skippable in-stream.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $500 |
| Maximum CPV | $0.05 |
| Paid views, at most | 10,000 |
| Viewers who book (assumed 0.2%) | 20 |
| Cost per booking | $25 |

If the real CPV is $0.10, the same budget buys 5,000 views and 10 bookings at $50 each. Replace every assumed figure with your account data after the first two weeks, and stop if the cost per booking exceeds what a new client is worth.

## FAQ

### How much do YouTube ads cost for a local business?
Google's help pages describe how you are charged but do not publish an average price. Set a small budget and read cost per view or per thousand impressions in your account.

### Is programmatic video worth it for a small business?
It can be when the offer is visual and the area is defined. Test with a budget you can lose, and track calls or bookings.

### What is the difference between skippable and bumper ads?
Skippable ads can be skipped after 5 seconds and, under CPV bidding, charge when a viewer watches 30 seconds or interacts. Bumper ads last 5 to 6 seconds and are billed per impression.

### Can I target only my city?
Yes. Use location targeting with a radius of at least 1 km or a named area.

### How many people use YouTube?
Pew's 2026 survey found 84% of US adults use it.
