Digital marketing in Angola works through a smaller audience than most guides assume. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 17.6 million internet users in October 2025, which is 44.8% of the population, and 5.50 million social media user identities. So most advertising reaches the urban, connected minority first. This guide uses only figures opened at the source and marks gaps where no public data exists.

## How Big Is Angola's Online Market?

Angola had 39.3 million people in October 2025 according to DataReportal, citing UN data, and 70.0% lived in urban areas. Mobile connections reached 30.6 million, equal to 77.8% of the population, though some may be voice or SMS only. The World Bank lists 2025 GDP at about $122 billion and GDP per capita at about $3,130.

The online audience is smaller: 21.7 million people (55.2%) were offline. Internet users grew by 3.0% in a year, but the adoption rate stayed flat. For a small business, that means your online customers are concentrated in cities, and the median age of 16.6 years means many of the population are children.

## Which Platforms Can You Advertise On?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 figures for ad audiences in Angola show where paid reach exists:

| Platform | Ad audience | Year change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 5.50 million | +12.2% |
| TikTok (18+) | 3.95 million | +10.7% since end 2024 |
| LinkedIn (members) | 1.40 million | +27.3% |
| Instagram | 1.03 million | +18.1% |

Facebook reaches 31.2% of internet users. Instagram reaches 5.8%, so it suits a narrow urban audience. TikTok grew by 597 thousand between July and October 2025 alone. DataReportal warns that these are ad audiences, which may not equal unique people.

No reliable public figure for WhatsApp or YouTube users in Angola was found, so this guide gives none. Many older articles quote them without a source.

## Who Are You Actually Reaching on Social Media?

Social media user identities in Angola are 57.8% male and 42.2% female, although the population is 50.5% female. Facebook's adult ad audience is 57.3% male and 41.8% female. TikTok skews further, with 64.5% male.

If your business sells to women, such as beauty or fashion, expect Facebook ads to reach fewer of them than the population share suggests. Test creative aimed at women on Instagram, where the adult audience is 46.2% female. Check the split in your own ad account after a few weeks.

## Can You Sell Through WhatsApp?

Meta lists WhatsApp Business as a way to give customers a business profile, greet people when they start a chat, and respond when you are away. Meta also lists ads that click to WhatsApp as a product, which open a chat when tapped.

Because no verifiable public WhatsApp user data for Angola was found, treat WhatsApp as a channel to test, not assume. Ask ten recent customers how they prefer to message you. ## Does Internet Speed Limit Video and Landing Pages?

Median fixed download speed was 20.39 Mbps in the Ookla data DataReportal cites, up 9.7% over twelve months to August 2025. That figure covers fixed connections, which are not how most Angolans go online, so keep pages light for phones.

Compress images, avoid auto-playing video, and put your phone number, address and opening hours at the top of the page. A short TikTok or Reels clip works better than a long video that needs a strong connection.

## What Would a Test Campaign Look Like?

This is a planning example. Every input is an **assumption**, and Meta sets real prices in an auction.

| Input | Assumption |

| Goal | Messages to your business |

## FAQ

### How many people use the internet in Angola?

DataReportal counts 17.6 million internet users in October 2025, a penetration rate of 44.8%. About 21.7 million people were offline.

### Which social platform is biggest for ads in Angola?

Facebook, with a 5.50 million ad audience, followed by TikTok at 3.95 million adults. Instagram has 1.03 million.

### Is WhatsApp the main channel for Angolan businesses?

It may be, but no reliable public user count for Angola was found. Test it with your own customers before building a plan around it.

### What currency and language should ads use?

Portuguese is the official language, and prices should be in kwanza. This guide does not source an exchange rate, so check the current rate before setting prices.

### Do I need a website?

Not to start. A Google Business Profile, a WhatsApp Business profile and a Facebook page can handle first contact while you test demand.
