A local business can run Instagram Reels ads by making a vertical 9:16 video under 90 seconds and choosing Reels as a placement in Meta Ads Manager. No Reels-only price was found in the sources checked. LocaliQ's September 2026 Meta benchmarks show $0.60 per click for traffic campaigns and $1.80 per click for lead campaigns across all industries. Use those as a starting range, then measure your own cost per enquiry.

## What Are the Technical Requirements for a Reels Ad?

Meta's developer documentation asks for a full-screen 9:16 vertical video of less than 90 seconds. It advises keeping the bottom 35% of the frame free of text, logos and key elements so the Reels interface does not cover them, and keeping calls to action clear of that area too.

If you repurpose an organic reel as an ad, the documentation says it must not already be shared to Facebook and must be free of third-party copyrighted music, GIFs, interactive stickers and camera filters. Reels placements support awareness, traffic, engagement, leads, sales and app promotion objectives. File size and resolution are not specified in that document, so check Ads Manager's preview.

## How Do You Target Only Nearby People?

Set the location in Ads Manager to your town or a radius around the business, and choose Reels as a manual placement or leave placements automatic. Meta's documentation shows manual placements for Instagram Reels and profile reels. If you leave placements unset, delivery considers all default positions, so your video may appear in the feed or Stories as well.

Use manual placement only if you have made a video designed for Reels. Otherwise automatic placement lets Meta spread the budget, and you can read results by placement afterwards.

## What Should a Local Reels Ad Show?

Meta publishes no list of winning formats, so treat these as tests:

- The product or service being made, in the first two seconds
- One clear offer or action, such as "Book a table"
- Text large enough to read with sound off
- Your location named on screen

## What Does a $300 Test Look Like?

Inputs are **assumptions** using LocaliQ's all-industry lead campaign figures. Your results will differ by category.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $300 |
| Cost per lead | $27.39 |
| Leads | about 11 |
| Leads who become customers (assumption) | 25% |
| New customers | about 2.7 |
| Cost per customer | about $110 |
| First-visit value (assumption) | $60 |
| Revenue from first visits | about $164 |

At these inputs the first visit does not cover the ad, and the test pays only if customers return. Use a booking link or a code so you can match customers to the ad. If cost per customer stays above what a customer is worth after three months, change the offer or stop.

## FAQ

### What is the best video length for a Reels ad?

Meta specifies under 90 seconds. It does not name an ideal length, so test short and slightly longer versions.

### Can I boost an organic reel?

You can repurpose one if it meets Meta's conditions, including no third-party copyrighted music.

### What is the minimum budget?

Meta's help page was not available to check, so confirm the minimum in Ads Manager for your currency.

### Do Reels ads cost less than feed ads?

No Reels-only benchmark is published in the sources checked.

### Should I choose traffic or lead objectives?

Choose the one that matches your goal. Lead campaigns cost more per click but aim at enquiries.
