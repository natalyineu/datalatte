A small business in mainland China reaches local customers mainly through WeChat, Douyin, Meituan and Xiaohongshu, not Google or Meta. Start with a WeChat presence and payments, add a Meituan or Dianping listing if you sell food, beauty or fitness, then use Douyin and Xiaohongshu for discovery. Legal setup comes first: a Chinese entity is usually required for commercial WeChat features and an ICP licence.

This guide is for owners operating inside mainland China: cafes, salons, studios and shops. ## Which Platforms Matter Most for a Small Business in China?

Pick by what you sell and who you want. Each platform does a different job, so you rarely need all five.

| Platform | Job | Best for | Verified data |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WeChat | Messaging, booking, payment, loyalty | Every business | 1,439M combined Weixin and WeChat users (Tencent, 30 June 2026) |
| Meituan and Dianping | Search, deals and reviews for local services | Food, beauty, fitness | Meituan Q2 2026 core local commerce revenue RMB 71.5B (secondary) |
| Douyin | Short video discovery, group-buy deals | Food, beauty, entertainment | No current user count verified |
| Xiaohongshu | Search and reviews | Beauty, cafes, lifestyle | About 245M monthly users (QuestMobile via 36Kr, secondary) |
| Baidu | Search and maps | Services, B2B | Market share not verified |

Douyin user counts and Baidu's search share could not be confirmed from an opened source, so they are left out.

## How Do You Set Up a Business Presence on WeChat?

Open an Official Account, then decide whether you need a Mini Program. Third-party guides say a Subscription Account can send one message a day, while a Service Account sends four a month into the main chat list and unlocks payment features. Tencent's own documentation was not opened, so check the limits when you register.

The legal side matters more than the account type. A 2026 China Briefing guide says a Chinese entity, such as a WFOE or joint venture, is needed for an ICP licence. A separate FDI China guide says a commercial WeChat account requires a mainland WFOE. Older sources say foreign documents were enough, but those are dated around 2018.

If you are a local Chinese business with a business licence, this is simpler. If you are a foreign owner, talk to a China-based adviser before building anything.

## What Is the Difference Between an ICP Filing and an ICP Licence?

An ICP filing is a registration required for every website, app or Mini Program hosted on mainland servers. An ICP licence is a commercial permit required on top of it when you make money online, such as selling products or running ads.

According to China Briefing (22 April 2026):

- Filing takes about 3 to 4 weeks. A police (PSB) filing is due within 30 days of the filing number.
- A commercial licence typically takes 2 to 6 months.
- A cross-province licence has a registered capital minimum of RMB 10 million; single-province RMB 1 million.
- Informational sites without a revenue model need the filing only.

For many shops and salons, running everything through WeChat and a Meituan listing avoids owning a website at all. Confirm with an adviser, as rules differ by business type.

## How Do You Get Customers From Meituan and Dianping?

If you sell food, beauty or fitness services, a listing here is often the first thing a local customer sees. Customers search by district and category, compare ratings, and buy group-buy vouchers.

Practical steps:

- Claim and complete your listing with photos, hours and a menu or price list.
- Ask satisfied customers for reviews and reply to complaints quickly.
- Offer one clear first-visit deal rather than five.
- Track redemptions by voucher so you know what worked.

Meituan's results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 show revenue of RMB 104.6 billion, up 14.4%, with the Core Local Commerce segment at RMB 71.5 billion (secondary reports of the HKEX filing). Reports also say Douyin's local life business competes with Meituan on group-buy deals, but no verified 2026 Douyin figures were found.

## How Do Douyin and Xiaohongshu Work for Local Shops?

Douyin and Xiaohongshu are discovery channels. Customers find you in video or notes, then check ratings elsewhere before visiting.

**Douyin** supports short videos with a location tag and group-buy deals. Post what the customer will experience: preparation, before and after, prices on screen. Avoid guessing ad prices; check the ad dashboard for current minimum budgets.

**Xiaohongshu** works as a search engine for reviews, so posts that list address, price and a clear photo help. Treat creator fees as a test: pay one small creator, track a unique code, and measure bookings.

## What Are the Advertising Rules You Cannot Ignore?

China's Advertising Law bars superlatives such as "best", "No. 1" and "highest" in ads. Law-firm summaries report fines from RMB 200,000 to RMB 1 million and cases against small sellers; these sources date from 2018 or earlier, so check the current text. Platforms such as Baidu and WeChat reportedly reject ads containing the character for "most".

Write claims you can prove: "open until 22:00", "free tasting on Saturday", "800 reviews on Dianping". This is also better copy.

## How Do Payments Work for a Small Business?

Set up WeChat Pay and Alipay QR codes at the counter. One payments comparison site lists WeChat Pay's standard rate as starting from 0.6% per transaction (secondary; your agreement may differ).

You must still accept cash. The People's Bank of China has said no entity may refuse renminbi cash, and enforcement in 2024 included fines on large chains (PYMNTS and Cash Matters reports, secondary). No rule requiring acceptance of foreign cards was found, so foreign visitors may need cash or international card support.

## Worked Example: A Cafe With RMB 10,000 a Month

All inputs are **assumptions** for planning, not benchmarks.

| Line | Assumption | RMB per month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meituan or Dianping deal and promotion | 40% | 4,000 |
| Xiaohongshu creator test | 20% | 2,000 |
| Douyin content and local promotion | 20% | 2,000 |
| WeChat member offers and reminders | 10% | 1,000 |
| Reserve for the best channel | 10% | 1,000 |

Review after 4 weeks. Move the reserve to the channel with the lowest cost per redeemed voucher or booking. If no channel works, check the offer and the listing before spending more. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to split a budget in general.

## FAQ

### What is the best marketing platform for a small business in China?

WeChat for communication and payment, plus Meituan or Dianping for local discovery if you sell food, beauty or fitness. Douyin and Xiaohongshu add reach.

### Can I use Google Ads or Facebook in China?

Not as your main channels. This guide focuses on domestic platforms. Check current access rules with an adviser.

### Do I need an ICP licence?

Every website or Mini Program on mainland servers needs an ICP filing. Commercial sites also need an ICP licence, per China Briefing.

### How many people use WeChat?

Tencent reported 1,439 million combined Weixin and WeChat monthly active users at 30 June 2026.
