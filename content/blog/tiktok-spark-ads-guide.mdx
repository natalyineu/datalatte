A Spark Ad is a TikTok ad built from an organic post, yours or a creator's. To run one, pick a post, authorize it, add it to a campaign and set a budget. TikTok's published minimums are $50 at campaign level and $20 at ad group level, so plan your spend before you boost.

This guide is for local businesses such as cafes, salons and studios. Facts come from TikTok's own help pages, opened in October 2026. TikTok does not publish average costs per view or click on those pages, so none are quoted here.

## What Are TikTok Spark Ads?

TikTok describes Spark Ads as a native format that lets you use organic posts as ads. You can use your own posts or, with authorization, a creator's. Videos up to 10 minutes can be sparked.

The main difference from a standard ad is where the engagement goes. TikTok says all views, comments, shares, likes and follows gained from boosting the video are credited to the original organic post.

For a local business, this suits a post that already earned real reactions from nearby customers, because the ad's name and text reflect that post.

## How Do You Authorize a Post?

Authorization is how an account owner allows a post to run as an ad. TikTok documents a quick method and a full method.

- **Quick method:** open the video in the TikTok app, tap the three dots, tap the Ad settings icon, and tap Generate to get the post code.
- **Full method:** allow authorized posts in profile settings, open the post's Ad settings, agree to the Advertising Content Terms of Service, turn on Ad authorization, generate a code and choose 7, 30, 60 or 365 days.

The advertiser then enters the code in Ads Manager under Tools, Creative, Creative library, Spark ads posts, Apply for Authorization. Duets and Stitched posts need codes from both creators. Choose the length by how long you plan to run the ad, since the authorization expires.

## How Do You Set Up a Spark Ad Campaign?

TikTok's help page for Smart+ campaigns lists two ways to add a post.

In "Pull", you pick a post from your linked account on the TikTok Post tab, click Add Post to authorize it, confirm, finish the ad settings and submit. In "Push", you pick or upload a creative in Your Library, connect a TikTok account, confirm and submit.

Before you submit, note the limits TikTok lists:

- You cannot edit the caption after authorization, so finish it first.
- A private video becomes public once it is used in a campaign.
- A code can only be deleted after all ads using that video are deleted.
- One Ads Manager account supports up to 10,000 Spark Ads.

Manual campaigns use a separate flow. TikTok's page links to it but the steps there were not available to check.

## What Does a Spark Ads Budget Look Like?

TikTok's help pages say the minimum budget is $50 at the campaign level and $20 at the ad group level. Its daily budget page states that ad group daily budgets must exceed $20, and gives an example where lifetime budgets are $20 multiplied by scheduled days, which is $620 for 31 days.

**Worked example (assumptions).** Say a cafe runs one ad group for 31 days at the $620 minimum. Assume an average order of $12 and a 65% margin, which is $7.80 per order. The ad needs about 80 extra orders in the month, roughly 2.6 a day, to cover the spend. That is a useful test of whether a minimum-budget campaign suits your shop.

## Which Posts Should You Boost?

Boost posts that already show your place and your product to a local audience. Compare views, comments and saves across your organic posts, and pick the ones with real replies from customers. Avoid a post that needs context, such as a joke about an event that has passed.

Because the caption is locked after authorization, check the spelling, hours and address first. If you want a different call to action, publish a new post and spark that one.

## How Do You Measure Results?

TikTok separates organic and paid metrics for Spark Ads. Paid metrics include paid clicks (on the call to action button and caption), music clicks, paid profile visits, and paid likes, shares, comments and followers within one day of someone seeing the ad.

Track the result that matters to your shop: a coupon redeemed, a phone call, a map direction tap, or a new customer who says TikTok brought them in. Compare against what you spent. ## FAQ

### Do Spark Ads cost more than regular TikTok ads?
TikTok's help pages do not publish a price difference. The budget minimums are $50 at campaign level and $20 at ad group level.

### Can I boost a post from another account?
Yes, with authorization. The creator generates a code with a validity of 7, 30, 60 or 365 days, and you apply it in Ads Manager.

### Can I edit the caption on a Spark Ad?
No. TikTok says you cannot edit a post's caption after it is authorized as an ad, so finalize it first.

### Do likes from the ad count on my post?
TikTok says views, comments, shares, likes and follows gained from boosting the video are attributed to the organic post.

### How long can the video be?
TikTok states that videos up to 10 minutes can be sparked.
