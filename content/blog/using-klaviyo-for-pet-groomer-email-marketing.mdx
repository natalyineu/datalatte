A pet groomer can start on Klaviyo's free plan, which Klaviyo lists at up to 250 active profiles and 500 email sends per month. Build two automations first: a welcome email for new clients and a rebooking reminder based on a date you store for each pet. Klaviyo flows can start from a list, a segment, an action or a date property, so a "last groom" date can trigger a reminder. Klaviyo is built around online-store events, so check whether your booking software connects before you commit.

## Is Klaviyo a Good Fit for a Pet Groomer?

It can be, with limits. The free plan is enough for a small client list, but 500 sends a month disappears fast: a list of 200 clients mailed twice uses 400. Klaviyo's help describes event data, such as orders and product views, from installed integrations, and says event variables work only in flows triggered by a metric.

That design suits shops. A groomer with a booking tool may have no such events. If your tool does not connect, you can still import a client list and use a date property. Compare alternatives in our [Mailchimp vs Klaviyo guide](/blog/mailchimp-vs-klaviyo-small-business-which-is-better).

## What Data Should You Collect About Each Client?

Keep it small. For each client collect email, pet name, breed or size, and the date of the last groom. Add consent for marketing at the time of booking.

Klaviyo's flow help lists "date property" as a trigger: a profile enters when a date-based property matches, such as a birthday. Store the pet's birthday and the last appointment date as properties. The birthday gives you a yearly message and the last groom date drives the rebooking reminder. Do not collect more than you use.

## Which Two Flows Should You Build First?

Build these before any newsletter:

1. **Welcome flow.** Trigger: a profile is added to your "new clients" list. Send one email right away with parking, what to bring, vaccination rules and how to reschedule. Add a second a few days later if the first has not been opened.
2. **Rebooking reminder.** Trigger: a date property. Send a short email asking if it is time to book the next groom, with one booking button.

Klaviyo explains that flows can include time delays and branches. Keep yours short: one send, one delay, one follow-up. ## What Are the Legal Basics?

In the US, the FTC lists CAN-SPAM rules: accurate headers and subject lines, a clear ad disclosure, a valid postal address, a working opt-out, and honouring opt-outs within 10 business days. Each separate violating email can carry penalties of up to $53,088. Opted-out addresses cannot be sold.

Google's sender guidelines add rules for anyone who sends more than 5,000 messages a day to Gmail, including one-click unsubscribe and a spam rate under 0.30%. A groomer will not hit that volume, but the habits help. In the UK and EU, consent rules are stricter, see our [GDPR email guide](/blog/gdpr-compliant-email-marketing). This is not legal advice.

## Should You Add SMS?

Klaviyo's free plan lists $5 of mobile messages a month, noted as region and carrier dependent, and the page shows no per-message rates. Text reminders are popular with appointment businesses, but they need separate opt-in consent. ## The Math: What Is a Rebooking Reminder Worth?

All inputs are **assumptions**, not benchmarks.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Clients on the list | 200 |
| Reminder emails sent per month | 40 (clients due for a groom) |
| Share who book after the email | 10% |
| Extra bookings per month | 4 |
| Average groom price | $70 |
| Added revenue per month | 4 x $70 = $280 |
| Plan cost | $0 (free plan, 40 sends) |

Under these inputs the reminder adds $280 a month. Even if only 2 clients booked, it adds $140. Track bookings by asking new bookings how they heard, or use a unique booking link in the email.

## FAQ

### Is Klaviyo free for small businesses?
Klaviyo lists a free plan with up to 250 active profiles and 500 email sends per month. Paid plan prices were not shown on its pricing page when checked.

### What is a date property flow?
A flow that starts when a date on a profile matches, such as a birthday. Klaviyo lists it as one of four trigger types.

### Do I need a store integration to use Klaviyo?
Not strictly. You can import contacts and use properties. Event-based personalisation needs an integration, per Klaviyo's help.

### Can I email clients without permission?
In the US, CAN-SPAM allows marketing email with rules such as an opt-out. UK and EU rules require consent. Get consent at booking.

### How many emails should a groomer send?
No neutral benchmark exists. Start with automated emails plus one monthly message, then adjust to unsubscribes.
