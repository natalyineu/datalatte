Marketing a Montreal business starts with language. Statistics Canada's 2021 Census data for the Montreal metropolitan area shows 65% of residents speak mainly French at home, 17% English and 12% another language. Among workers, 80% were at least bilingual. So build French first, add English where your customers need it, and check Quebec's French-language rules before you print signs or ads.

This guide covers language, Google Business Profile, ads, email rules and a test budget. It gives no Montreal-specific CPC because no source read for it publishes one.

## Which Language Should Montreal Marketing Use?

Use French as the default and English as a second version. The census figures show a French-speaking majority at home and a largely bilingual workforce: 69% of workers were English-French bilingual and 28% were at least trilingual. Statistics Canada says 70% of workers mainly used French at work, 21% English and 8% both equally.

| Audience | What the data suggests | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| French speakers | Largest group at home, 65% | French first on site, profile and ads |
| English speakers | 17% at home | English version of key pages and ads |
| Other languages | 12% at home | Add a language only if your customers ask |

The figures describe residents and workers, not your customers. Check your own analytics and bookings before you split a budget.

## What Do Quebec's Language Rules Require?

The Charter of the French Language governs public signs and commercial advertising. Law firm summaries, not the primary texts, report that another language may appear alongside French if French is "markedly predominant," meaning the French text has a much greater visual impact, and that the stricter standard for trademark signs visible from outside took effect on June 1, 2025.

The Office québécois de la langue française (OQLF) pages and the regulation were not opened for this article, so confirm the details with the OQLF or a Quebec lawyer before spending on signs. Equal-size English and French is a common mistake that summaries say does not meet the test. Check each case.

## How Should You Set Up Google Business Profile in Montreal?

Write the business description in French first and English second, or use the main language of your neighbourhood. Keep your name, address and phone number the same everywhere. If you visit customers, Google says to hide the address and set service areas of up to 20 cities or postal codes, within about two hours of driving.

## How Do You Run Bilingual Google and Meta Ads?

Make a separate French and English ad group or ad set, each with its own landing page. That keeps the language of the ad, the page and the booking form consistent, and lets you see which language converts. Do not rely on machine translation for headlines. Have a French speaker read the copy.

Start each language with a small test. Compare cost per enquiry between them after a month, then shift the budget. For setup basics see [local marketing in Canada](/blog/local-marketing-canada-small-business-2026) and [Canada local business marketing](/blog/local-business-marketing-canada-guide-2026).

## Can You Email and Text Montreal Customers?

Only with consent. Under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation, section 6, a commercial electronic message cannot be sent unless the recipient consented, express or implied. The message must identify the sender, give a way to contact them, and include an unsubscribe mechanism. Contact details must stay valid for at least 60 days after sending.

Penalty amounts were not in the text read here, so check them with the Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission or a lawyer. Send emails in the language the customer chose when they signed up.

## Worked Example: A CA$800 Bilingual Test

All numbers below are **assumptions**: CA$800 a month, split 60% French and 40% English to reflect the census mix, a cost per click of CA$2.50 in both, and a 4% enquiry rate.

| | French | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | CA$480 | CA$320 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | CA$2.50 | CA$2.50 |
| Clicks | 192 | 128 |
| Enquiry rate (assumption) | 4% | 4% |
| Enquiries | about 7.7 | about 5.1 |
| Cost per enquiry | CA$62.50 | CA$62.50 |

The assumed rates are equal on purpose. In real accounts they will differ, and the point of the test is to find the difference. After a month, move budget to the language with the lower cost per enquiry.

## FAQ

### Do I have to advertise in French in Montreal?

Quebec's Charter generally requires French in commercial advertising and public signs, per law firm summaries. Confirm your case with the OQLF or a Quebec lawyer.

### What share of Montreal speaks French?

Statistics Canada's 2021 Census shows 65% speaking mainly French at home in the Montreal metropolitan area, 17% English and 12% another language.

### Should my website be bilingual?

Most Montreal businesses benefit from French and English versions. The data shows a bilingual workforce, but a French-speaking majority.

### How much do Montreal Google Ads cost?

### Can I send marketing emails?
