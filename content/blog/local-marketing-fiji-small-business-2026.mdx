A small business in Fiji should treat Facebook and Messenger as the main paid and organic channels, and use Instagram as a second channel for visual products. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report for Fiji puts internet users at 741,000 (79.3% of the population) and Facebook ad reach at 569,000, or about 61% of everyone in the country. This guide uses that report, World Bank figures and Fiji's payment regulator to show what is known and what is not.

It is written for shops, cafes, salons, tour operators and trades in Suva, Nadi, Lautoka and Labasa. It does not give ad prices, because no public Fiji price benchmark was found.

## How Many People in Fiji Can You Reach Online?

DataReportal (data from Kepios, the UN and GSMA Intelligence) counts 934,000 people in Fiji, 59.8% of them urban, with a median age of 28.1. There are 1.42 million mobile connections, which is 152% of the population, because many people hold more than one SIM. Internet users number 741,000, so about 193,000 people (20.7%) are offline.

Social media user identities total 569,000, or 60.9% of the population. DataReportal warns that ad reach is what each platform's own tools report, not a count of active users, and one person can hold several accounts. Treat the figures as a ceiling for targeting, not a customer list.

## Which Platforms Matter Most for a Fiji Small Business?

Facebook reaches 569,000 people, which is 90.1% of adults aged 18 and over in DataReportal's estimate. Messenger reaches 466,000, so a business that posts on Facebook can also take enquiries by Messenger. Instagram reaches 284,000 and grew by 53,900 (23.4%) between October 2024 and October 2025. LinkedIn shows 280,000 members, which matters mainly for B2B, recruitment and professional services.

TikTok and YouTube are not included on the Fiji page, so this guide gives no figures for them. If a video-first idea fits your business, test it with a small organic budget and check the numbers in your own page analytics, not in a general claim.

## Does Google Ads Work in Fiji?

Google's help page on country restrictions lists the places where Google Ads is blocked because of US sanctions: Crimea, Cuba, the so-called Donetsk and Luhansk People's Republics, Iran and North Korea. Fiji is not on that list, and the page shows no update date. Whether a Fiji business can pay for ads in a given currency depends on the billing options Google offers for its country and currency, so check them inside the account before planning a budget.

No public source found for this guide gives a cost per click for Fiji. Do not copy benchmarks from the US or Australia. Run a small test, then use your own cost per enquiry.

## How Do Fijians Pay, and Why Does That Change Your Ads?

Mobile money is part of everyday payment in Fiji. M-PAiSA, run by Vodafone Fiji, launched in June 2010. The Fiji Times reports that the ATH Group annual report for the year to 30 June 2025 shows more than $6 billion processed through M-PAiSA (secondary source, currency not stated in the article). It also says the Reserve Bank of Fiji licensed M-PAiSA as a payment service provider under the National Payment System Act 2021.

For an advertiser the point is simple. If customers pay through a wallet or a bank transfer, put the accepted methods in your ads and your Messenger auto-reply. Digicel's MyCash page says it moves money between banks and wallets in real time, with a maximum wallet balance of FJD 10,000.

## What Does the Fiji Economy Look Like for Pricing?

The World Bank lists Fiji's 2025 GDP at US$6.2 billion, GDP growth of 3.2% and GDP per capita of US$6,642. Tourism affects many businesses, but this guide has not verified visitor numbers, so none are quoted here. A business near hotels or the Nadi corridor can still segment ads by language and location in Meta's tools without relying on a visitor statistic.

## How Should a Small Fiji Business Test Facebook Ads?

Start with a Page that has opening hours, a map pin, a phone number and a Messenger button. Then run one local ad with a message objective, aimed at a radius around your shop. DataReportal's Fiji page gives no WhatsApp figure, so Messenger is the channel with documented reach here.

## FAQ

### How many people in Fiji use Facebook?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 569,000 people in Facebook's ad reach in Fiji in late 2025. That is 60.9% of the population and 90.1% of adults.

### Is Google Ads available in Fiji?

Fiji is not on Google's sanctions-based restriction list. Payment options still depend on your billing country and currency, so check them in your Google Ads account.

### How much do ads cost in Fiji?

No public Fiji cost-per-click or cost-per-message benchmark was found for this guide. Use a small test and your own results.

### Should a Fiji business use Instagram?

Instagram reaches 284,000 people and grew 23.4% in a year, so it suits visual products such as food, beauty and events. Facebook has the larger audience.

### Which payment methods should I show in ads?

Show the ones your customers use, such as bank transfer or a mobile wallet like M-PAiSA or MyCash, and confirm the limits with your provider.
