## How Much Should a Long Beach Salon Spend on Meta Ads?

Bookeo's [social media marketing guide for beauty salons](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/) suggests starting Facebook and Instagram ad spend at USD 750 to USD 1,500 per month. That is a starting test range, not a guaranteed result, so judge it after several weeks of data.

Adwave's [channel guide for small businesses](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-choose-advertising-channels) puts a standalone Meta buy at roughly $300 to $1,000 per month. Its [veterinary guide](https://adwave.com/resources/veterinary-advertising) gives $300 to $1,000 per month for most practices. These figures come from other local service categories, so treat them as reference points for a low-cost test rather than salon benchmarks.

Plan for weeks, not days. Adwave's [channel comparison](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-small-business-2026) notes that Meta takes weeks to optimise, and its [dental guide](https://adwave.com/resources/dental-advertising-get-more-patients) lists a 2 to 4 week timeline for results from Meta spend. Keep the budget steady through that window before changing the offer.

## Which Ad Formats Work for Hair Salon Campaigns?

Adwave cites Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, which says short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram ([Adwave electrician guide, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/electrician-advertising-reaching-homeowners)). For a salon, that points toward short clips of real cuts, colour processes and stylists at work, filmed at your own chair.

Meta lets advertisers set goals such as traffic or reservations across Facebook, Instagram or both ([The Fork Manager, secondary](https://www.theforkmanager.com/en/blog/introduction-paid-ads-restaurants)). For a salon, choose the goal closest to a booked appointment, not a like or a view. Carousels can show a service menu, but test them against a single video before drawing conclusions.

Keep the same creative for the full test and watch for falling engagement. If clicks drop while spend holds steady, a fresh clip is more useful than a new audience.

## Who Should You Target Within 5 to 10 Miles?

Bookeo advises setting a 5 to 10 mile radius around the salon for Facebook and Instagram ads. For a Long Beach location, start at the wider end of that range and narrow it only if the cost per booking stays high after several weeks.

Use Facebook and Instagram Insights to see when followers are online. Bookeo notes this is available through the Insights tab, provided you have a business account ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). Schedule posts and check ad delivery around those hours.

Build a custom audience from your own past clients, so existing visitors can be reached again with rebooking offers. Keep the audience split by service type, such as colour, cuts and barber services, so each ad speaks to one need.

## How Do You Track Bookings From Meta Ads?

Meta Ads Manager covers Facebook, Instagram and Messenger ([Turrboo, secondary](https://turrboo.com/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media)). Adobe describes it as a central point to run ads across Instagram and Facebook ([Adobe Express, secondary](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-advertising)).

Check these before spending:

1. Confirm you have a business account, which also unlocks Insights.
2. Install the Meta Pixel on the booking confirmation page, not just the homepage.
3. Set a conversion event for a completed booking or a submitted booking form.
4. Add UTM parameters to every ad URL so your analytics can separate Meta traffic.

Measure cost per booking, not impressions. The [marketing automation guide for hair salons](/blog/marketing-automation-for-hair-salons) covers how to route booking data into follow-up messages.

## Which Mistakes Waste Meta Ad Budget?

The most common error is measuring the wrong thing. Impressions and reach look healthy while bookings stay flat. Tie every campaign to a booking event before launch.

Other frequent problems are a missing pixel, a radius wider than the salon can serve, and one ad running unchanged for months. Each one makes the cost per booking harder to read, so fix tracking first.

Finally, start with one service and one location. Adding services, radii and audiences at once makes it impossible to see what drove the result.
