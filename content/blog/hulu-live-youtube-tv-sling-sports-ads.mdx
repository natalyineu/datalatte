## What is a vMVPD and why do advertisers care?

A vMVPD (virtual multichannel video programming distributor) streams live TV channels over the internet, such as Hulu + Live TV, YouTube TV, Sling TV and Fubo. Advertisers care because live sports and news stay valuable while cable shrinks.

Scale, from third-party reports:

- **YouTube TV:** analysts estimated roughly 11 million subscribers after adding about 750,000 in Q3 2025, per [CordCuttersNews](https://cordcuttersnews.com/youtube-tv-added-750000-subscribers-in-the-3rd-quarter-of-2025-according-to-a-new-report/) citing MoffettNathanson. It is an estimate, not a company figure.
- **Pay TV overall:** the same report says the sector gained 303,000 net subscribers in Q3 2025, its first quarterly gain since 2017, with a base near 43.2 million.
- **Fubo and Hulu + Live TV:** [TheWrap reported](https://www.thewrap.com/fubo-earnings-q3-2025-subscribers/) that Fubo had 1.63 million North American subscribers after the Hulu + Live TV merger, creating a service with nearly 6 million subscribers.

## Who sells ads on each service now?

| YouTube TV | Google's help page for ads on TV screens does not mention YouTube TV. |
| Sling TV | AdWave says it is owned by DISH Network, that direct deals need relationships and $10,000+, and that you reach Sling viewers inside broader programmatic campaigns, not Sling-only ones. Vendor claims. No official self-serve terms found. |

If a rep or platform promises "YouTube TV" or "Sling live sports" inventory, ask which exact channels, which placements and which contract terms apply.

## Can you buy Hulu ads from $500?

Per the [Disney Campaign Manager FAQ](https://www.disneycampaignmanager.com/resources/product-updates/faq/getting-started/), you can launch Hulu and Disney+ campaigns with a $500 minimum per campaign. Targeting includes ZIP code, age, gender, interests and program genre. Signup is free, and billing starts when the ad runs. This is the clearest low-cost way to test the Disney audience. It is not confirmed to include live channels, so ask support before assuming sports placements.

For platform comparisons see [CTV advertising platforms compared](/blog/ctv-advertising-platforms-compared-2026).

## Does Google Ads put your ad on YouTube TV?

Not that Google says. [Google's help page](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10147229?hl=en) states ads can appear in the YouTube app on TV and that videos should run at least 10 seconds, with 15 seconds recommended. It does not mention YouTube TV, the live subscription service. A separate [Google Ads page](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16903036?hl=en) says CTV QR codes "aren't available on YouTube TV or Google TV inventory", which shows Google treats those inventories separately.

Practical step: if you want YouTube TV, ask Google or a reseller in writing which placements are included.

## How do vendor figures compare?

Some ad platforms publish numbers for vMVPD buying. [AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/mvpd-vs-vmvpd) claims programmatic buys can start at $50, with CPMs of $18-30 for general inventory and $30-50 for live sports, and says direct deals often need $5,000 or more. These come from a vendor that sells the service and cite no outside source, so treat them as a negotiating reference only.

Worked example (assumption): $1,000 at a $25 CPM buys 40,000 impressions. If a household sees each ad about three times, that is about 13,000 households, a figure AdWave also uses. Your target ZIP codes may deliver fewer.

For CPM detail see [what CTV advertising costs](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data).

## Which businesses should test live TV streaming ads?

Choose it only if you can answer three questions:

1. **Do you know the offer?** A short, trackable offer beats a brand message.
2. **Can you measure?** Use a unique landing page, QR code or phone number.
3. **Can you afford a 30-day test?** Budget at least the platform minimum, and set a stop rule in advance.

Matching ads to games is a separate decision, covered in the [NFL CTV advertising guide](/blog/nfl-ctv-advertising-local-business).

## What does local TV ad insertion look like?

Local stations are adding household-level ad replacement. [TV Technology](https://www.tvtechnology.com/business/from-spots-to-audiences-how-advanced-advertising-is-reshaping-local-tv) reports that Hearst's WLWT in Cincinnati replaces selected inventory for eligible viewers through an MVPD partner. A Hearst executive said it needs greater scale across MVPDs "and eventually vMVPDs". So station-level addressable buying on vMVPDs is not yet standard.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I advertise on YouTube TV with Google Ads?**

Google documents ads on the YouTube app on TV, but not YouTube TV. Confirm placements in writing.

**Q: Is there a self-serve option for Hulu?**

Yes, Disney Campaign Manager has a $500 minimum per campaign for Hulu and Disney+. Live TV is not mentioned.

**Q: What happened to Fubo and Hulu + Live TV?**

They merged in 2025, and TheWrap says Fubo's sports inventory and ad sales team move into the Disney ecosystem in Q1 2026.

**Q: Are vendor CPMs reliable?**

They are a starting point only. Ask for a written insertion order with placements.

**Q: How should I judge results?**

Count calls, bookings, redemptions and new customers from a tracked offer.
