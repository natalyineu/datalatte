## What does Zapier cost a small business in 2026?

Prices below come from [Zapier's pricing page](https://zapier.com/pricing). Check it before you buy, because tiers change.

| Plan | Tasks per month | Price per month | Multi-step Zaps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 100 | $0 | No, two-step only |
| Pro (entry tier) | 750 | $19.99 annual, $29.99 monthly | Yes |
| Pro | 1,500 | $39.00 annual, $58.50 monthly | Yes |
| Pro | 2,000 | $49.00 annual, $73.50 monthly | Yes |

The free plan also polls for new data every 15 minutes and excludes Filters, Paths, Formatter and webhooks. New accounts get a 14-day Professional trial with no credit card.

## What counts as a task?

Only action steps use tasks. According to [Zapier's task usage guide](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496196837261-How-is-task-usage-measured-in-Zapier), triggers, Filter steps, Paths steps, Formatter and Delay never count, and neither do steps that error or are skipped by a filter.

Worked example (assumption, not a measured result): a salon gets 40 bookings a month. The Zap is Calendly trigger, then a Google Sheets row, then a Gmail confirmation. That is two action steps, so 80 tasks a month.

It fits under 100 tasks, but a two-action Zap is a multi-step Zap, so it needs a paid plan. At $19.99 a month the 750-task tier leaves room for about 375 bookings of this kind.

## Which Zapier automations help bookings and reminders?

Zapier lists the triggers below on its app pages, so these are all supported out of the box.

1. **Log every booking in a sheet.** Calendly's "Invitee Created or Rescheduled" trigger can add a row to Google Sheets. You get one master list of clients and dates.
2. **Flag cancellations and no-shows.** Calendly also offers "Invitee Canceled" and "Invitee No Show Created" triggers. Send yourself a Gmail or Slack alert so you can refill the slot.
3. **Mirror Square appointments.** Square offers a "New Appointment" trigger that can write to Google Sheets or post to Slack.
4. **Create a Square appointment from another system.** Square has a "Create Appointment" action, useful if bookings arrive by form.

Tip: put a Delay step before reminder emails. Delay steps do not use tasks.

## Which automations help sales and stock?

5. **Record every sale.** Square's "New Payment" trigger plus a Google Sheets "Create Spreadsheet Row" action gives you a daily sales log without exporting reports.
6. **Alert the team about big orders.** Square's "New Order" trigger can post to a Slack channel with the "Send Channel Message" action.
7. **Add new customers to a mailing list.** Square's "New Customer" trigger pairs with Mailchimp's "Add/Update Subscriber" action. Only add people who agreed to email, and follow your local consent rules.
8. **Adjust inventory.** Square has a "Change Inventory" action for stock counts. Teams with a sheet-based stock count can drive it from Google Sheets rows.

## Which automations help with leads and feedback?

9. **Send Facebook lead form leads to your email tool.** Facebook Lead Ads has a "New Lead" trigger, and Mailchimp can add that person as a subscriber. You reply while the lead is still warm.
10. **Follow up on feedback forms.** Google Forms has a "New Form Response" trigger, and Gmail can "Send Email" or "Create Draft". Use drafts if you want to personalise each reply.

## Is Zapier or another tool better for you?

Zapier suits owners who want the widest app list and a simple editor. If you expect high volume, compare it with [Make](/blog/make-automation-for-small-business). [Make's pricing page](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) lists a free plan with 1,000 credits a month and a Core plan at $12 a month for 10,000 credits, where each module action counts as one credit. For a wider view of workflows, read [workflow automation for small business](/blog/workflow-automation-for-small-business) and [local business marketing automation](/blog/local-business-marketing-automation).

Decision guide:

- Under 100 action steps a month and only two-step Zaps: stay on Free.
- Need a confirmation plus a log entry per booking: Pro at 750 tasks.
- Bulk email or SMS blasts: use a dedicated email or SMS tool and let Zapier only add contacts to the list.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the Zapier free plan enough for a small business?**

Sometimes. Free gives 100 tasks a month and only two-step Zaps, so one trigger and one action. It works for a single log-to-sheet automation at low volume. Anything with a second action needs Pro.

**Q: Do triggers use up my task allowance?**

No. [Zapier states](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496196837261-How-is-task-usage-measured-in-Zapier) that triggers, Filter, Paths, Formatter and Delay steps do not count as tasks, nor do steps that error or are skipped.

**Q: What happens when a Zap fails?**

A failed run shows a Stopped status in your Zap history. According to Zapier's [replay documentation](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496241726989), you can replay it manually, or enable autoreplay for Stopped runs. One API document says a Zap is paused automatically if 95% of its runs in the last 7 days failed. Check your plan's autoreplay availability on the pricing page.

**Q: Can Zapier replace my booking or POS software?**

No. It moves data between tools such as Square, Calendly, Google Sheets and Gmail. You still need the underlying booking and payment apps.

**Q: Should I send bulk email through Zapier?**

No. Use Zapier to add contacts to Mailchimp or a similar tool, then send from that tool.
