## How Big Is Ontario's Small Business Market?

ISED's Key Small Business Statistics 2025 report lists 410,154 small employer businesses in Ontario as of December 2024. ISED defines a small business as one with 1 to 99 paid employees. Canada had 1.10 million employer businesses, of which 1,079,188 were small, so Ontario holds about 38% of them (our calculation).

These counts cover firms with employees only. Sole traders with no staff are not included, so the real number of small operators is higher. For a local owner, the practical point is competition: in a big city, many rivals compete for the same map results, so a complete profile and steady reviews matter before ad spend.

## Which Marketing Channels Should You Use First?

Start with free local visibility, then add paid channels once you can measure results. The order below is a recommendation, not a ranked study.

| Step | Channel | Cost | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Google Business Profile | Free | Shows you on Search and Maps |
| 2 | Reviews and photos | Free | Helps customers choose between nearby options |
| 3 | Google Ads, local Search | Ad budget | Reaches people searching now |
| 4 | Meta Ads retargeting | Ad budget | Re-reaches website visitors |
| 5 | Email and SMS list | Low | Repeat sales, with consent |

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. More reviews and positive ratings can help prominence.

## How Much Does Google Ads Cost in Ontario?

No published source gives an Ontario figure. The closest public reference is LocaliQ's US 2026 benchmark: $5.42 average cost per click across industries, from $2.05 for Restaurants and Food to $8.33 for Home and Home Improvement, in US dollars. Canadian costs, in Canadian dollars, will differ.

Use Google's Keyword Planner for your own keywords once your account is set up, then run a two-month test. Our [Google Ads cost breakdown](/blog/how-much-does-google-ads-cost-for-small-businesses-real-2026-pricing-breakdown) explains how to read the numbers.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local in Ontario?

Google allows radius targeting from 1 km upward and recommends "Presence or interest" for most Search campaigns. It says "Presence" fits when you only want people physically in the area.

A Toronto business serving one neighbourhood should not target the whole Greater Toronto Area. Draw a radius around the places customers actually come from, and add negative keywords for jobs, DIY and free. Google says negative keywords do not match close variants, so add plurals yourself.

## Can You Offer a Discount for a Google Review?

No. Google's contributed content policy lists offering "payment, discounts, free goods and/or services" in exchange for reviews as fake engagement. You may ask for a review, with a direct link, right after a visit. See our [guide to getting more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for scripts.

## Do Meta Ads Work in Ontario?

They can, mainly for retargeting people who already visited your website and for visual businesses such as salons and studios. LocaliQ's US 2026 Facebook benchmarks list lead-campaign results of $27.11 per lead for Health and Fitness and $50.91 for Beauty and Personal Care, with $1.84 and $2.97 average CPC. These are US figures, not Ontario.

Run one offer to a small radius and compare cost per booking with Google. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) covers how to split spend between channels.

## What Does CASL Require for Email and SMS?

Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) prohibits sending a commercial electronic message unless the recipient has consented, expressly or impliedly (section 6). Subsection 20(4) sets the maximum penalty per violation at $1,000,000 for an individual and $10,000,000 for any other person.

Collect sign-ups with a clear checkbox at checkout or booking, keep a record of when and how each person agreed, and honour unsubscribes. A legal adviser can confirm what applies to your list.

## When Should Ontario Businesses Run Seasonal Campaigns?

Check event dates each year with the organizers. Conflicting dates for large Toronto events turned up in the sources checked for this guide, so none are quoted here. Plan around your own sales calendar: compare each month's bookings over the past year, then raise offers before your slow months.

This is a planning method, not measured Ontario demand data.

## FAQ

### How many small businesses are there in Ontario?

ISED counts 410,154 small employer businesses (1 to 99 employees) in Ontario as of December 2024. Businesses without employees are not in that figure.

### How much should an Ontario small business spend on Google Ads?

No source publishes a standard Ontario figure. Pick a budget you can hold for two months, estimate click cost in Keyword Planner, and work backwards from the value of a customer.

### Does Meta advertising work in Ontario?

It can for retargeting and visual businesses. No published source gives Ontario costs, so test a small daily budget.

### Is Google Business Profile free?

Creating and managing a profile costs nothing. You need to claim and verify the business to appear on Search and Maps.

### What is the CASL penalty?

Up to $10 million per violation for a business and $1 million for an individual, per subsection 20(4) of the Act.
