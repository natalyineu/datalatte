Alberta has more small businesses per person than the Canadian average, so being found in local search matters from day one.

## How Big Is Alberta's Small Business Market?

Alberta had 34.3 small businesses per 1,000 people in 2024, against 31.2 for Canada, according to an Independent Contractors and Businesses Association (ICBA) analysis of Government of Alberta data. Alberta counts businesses with 1 to 49 paid employees as small, and the federal definition uses 1 to 99. Employers of that size provide 35% of private-sector jobs in the province.

Construction is about 15% of small businesses. Professional, scientific and technical services is the largest group. Many local rivals means customers compare options online first, so your profile, reviews and website need to be in order before you spend on ads.

## Which Marketing Channels Should You Use First?

Start with free local visibility, then add paid channels once you can measure results. The order below is a recommendation, not a ranked study.

| Step | Channel | Cost | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Google Business Profile | Free | Shows you on Search and Maps |
| 2 | Reviews and photos | Free | Helps customers choose between nearby options |
| 3 | Google Ads, local Search | Ad budget | Reaches people searching now |
| 4 | Meta Ads retargeting | Ad budget | Re-reaches website visitors |
| 5 | Email and SMS list | Low | Repeat sales, with consent |

Do not skip step 1. Google says you must add or claim your business and verify it to show on Search, Maps and other Google services. If you change your business name or move, you may need to verify again.

## How Do You Set Up Google Ads for an Alberta Business?

Use local Search campaigns tied to your service area. Google allows radius targeting from 1 km upward and recommends "Presence or interest" for most Search campaigns. It says "Presence" fits when you only want people who are physically in the area.

For a plumber in Edmonton's south side, a radius around the neighbourhoods you serve works better than the whole city. Add negative keywords for jobs, DIY and free. Google says negative keywords do not match close variants, so add plurals yourself. Our [Google Ads for small business setup guide](/blog/how-to-set-up-google-ads-for-your-small-business-in-2026-step-by-step-with-screenshots) walks through the steps.

Start small and measure calls and form fills. Google's Keyword Planner shows estimated monthly searches and average cost for your own keywords once you complete account setup.

## What Does a Worked Example Look Like?

| Input or result | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | CA$600 |
| Cost per click | CA$3.00 |
| Clicks | 200 |
| Conversion rate (call or form) | 6% |
| Leads | 12 |
| Cost per lead | CA$50 |
| Leads who become jobs | 35% |
| New jobs | about 4 |
| Cost per job | about CA$143 |
| Average job value | CA$400 |

Four jobs at CA$400 bring in CA$1,600 against CA$600 of ads. If your real click cost is double, the cost per job doubles too. Run the test for at least two months before judging it, and record how each lead found you.

## When Should You Run Seasonal Campaigns?

Alberta's calendar is shaped by events. The 2026 Calgary Stampede drew 1,411,954 guests over 10 days, down from 1,470,288 in 2025 and a record 1,477,953 in 2024, per organizers as reported by Global News. Any Calgary café, restaurant, bar or retailer near the grounds or downtown can plan Stampede-week offers, longer hours and staffing.

For other events, check organizers' own sites for current dates before you plan spend. A simple year plan:

- **January to March:** New-year offers for gyms and studios, indoor services, winter warm-up menus
- **April to June:** Spring renovation, landscaping and tune-up services
- **July:** Stampede in Calgary; plan staff and stock early
- **September to December:** Back-to-school, fall services and holiday gift cards

This calendar is a planning suggestion based on typical business types, not measured demand.

## What Does CASL Require for Email and SMS?

Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) prohibits sending a commercial electronic message unless the recipient has consented, expressly or impliedly. Each message must identify the sender, give contact information and include an unsubscribe mechanism. The maximum penalty per violation is CA$1,000,000 for an individual and CA$10,000,000 for any other person, according to section 20 of the Act.

Collect sign-ups with a clear checkbox at checkout or booking, keep a record of when and how each person agreed, and honour unsubscribes. A legal adviser can confirm what applies to your list.

## Does Alberta's Tax Position Change Your Marketing?

Alberta's small business corporate income tax rate is 2% and the general rate is 8%, the lowest general rate among provinces, according to the Government of Alberta. Personal income tax is collected through the Canada Revenue Agency, with a lowest rate of 8% in 2026.

This does not set your ad budget. It does mean that after-tax profit per job is a better yardstick than revenue when you decide how much to spend per lead. Ask your accountant how marketing costs are treated in your case.

## FAQ

### How much should an Alberta small business spend on Google Ads?

No source publishes a standard Alberta figure. Pick a budget you can hold for two months, estimate your click cost in Keyword Planner, and work backwards from the value of a job. The worked example above shows the arithmetic with assumed numbers.

### Does Meta advertising work in Alberta?

It can, mainly for retargeting people who already visited your website and for lifestyle businesses that sell visually. No published source gives Alberta cost per thousand impressions. Test a small daily budget and compare cost per lead with Google.

### Do I need French content in Alberta?

Not for most businesses. Add French only if your customers ask for it, and check which languages your reviews and enquiries use. No source supports a rule that French copy raises results in Alberta.

### Is Google Business Profile free?

Creating and managing a profile does not cost money. You need to add or claim the business and verify it to show on Search and Maps.

### What is the CASL penalty?

Up to CA$10 million per violation for a business and CA$1 million for an individual, per section 20 of the Act. Get consent first and keep records.
