A local business needs to track four things: calls, direction requests, form or booking leads, and which campaign sent each. Google Business Profile reports calls, directions and website clicks. Google Analytics 4 records leads as key events. UTM tags on your links show the source. Together they answer which marketing brings people through the door.

This guide is for owners of shops, clinics, salons and studios with a website and a Google Business Profile. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Should a Local Business Track?

Track actions that lead to revenue, not page views. For most local businesses that means:

- Phone calls from the Business Profile and the website
- Direction requests
- Booking, quote or contact form submissions
- The source of each: search, maps, ads, email, social

Start there before adding anything else. Our [data analytics guide for small business](/blog/data-analytics-for-small-business) goes into reporting habits once the basics are in place.

## What Does Google Business Profile Report?

Google's help says performance data is available only for verified profiles. It reports views (unique people, counted once per day), searches that brought people to your profile, direction requests, calls (clicks on the call button, which needs a phone number on the profile) and website clicks. Directions are adjusted to filter out repeated taps, cancelled requests and spam.

Other metrics such as messages, bookings, menus and offers appear when they apply to your business type. Read our [Business Profile analytics guide](/blog/google-my-business-analytics) for how to read each one. Note that a call-button click is not the same as an answered call.

## How Do You Set Up Key Events in GA4?

Google defines a key event as an event that measures an action particularly important to the success of your business. Any event you collect can be marked as one. Typical choices for a local site are form submit, click on a phone number, click on a booking button and a thank-you page view.

Key events then appear as a column in reports such as Landing pages and User acquisition, and can be turned into Google Ads conversions. For setup steps, follow our [GA4 guide for small business](/blog/google-analytics-4-for-small-business), or use [Google Tag Manager](/blog/google-tag-manager-for-local-website-analytics) to fire events without editing site code.

## How Do UTM Links Show Which Campaign Worked?

Add campaign parameters to links you control, such as emails, social bios and ads. Google says you should always use utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign. Values are case-sensitive, so utm_source=google and utm_source=Google are recorded as different sources. Missing parameters show as "(not set)" in reports.

Pick one lower-case naming scheme and write it in a shared sheet. Example: utm_source=newsletter, utm_medium=email, utm_campaign=november-offer. Do not tag links inside your own site, only links that come from outside.

## Can You See Business Profile Data in Google Analytics?

Yes. Google Analytics Help describes a link under Admin, Product links, Google Business Profile links. You need the Editor or Administrator role on the property and Owner or Manager permission on each profile. The shared metrics are interactions, website clicks, calls, directions, messages, bookings and menus.

Limits apply. Only the last six months are available, multiple linked profiles are combined and cannot be split, and the data cannot be used in custom explorations. Export monthly figures to a sheet if you want longer history.

## Which Settings Protect Your Data History?

GA4 lets you choose event data retention of 2 months or 14 months on standard properties, and the page does not state the default. Check the setting under Admin, then Data settings, and pick 14 months if you want year-over-year views in explorations. Shortening retention deletes older data in the next monthly process.

For on-page behaviour, Microsoft's Clarity documentation lists session recordings and heatmaps, which show where visitors click and scroll.

## How Do You Calculate Cost per Lead? A Worked Example

All numbers are **assumptions** for a salon spending on a local ad campaign.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly ad spend | $400 |
| Calls from Business Profile and site | 18 |
| Form leads | 12 |
| Total leads | 30 |
| Cost per lead | about $13.33 |
| Share of leads who book (assumption) | 50% |
| Cost per booking | about $26.67 |

If a booking is worth $60 in gross profit, each one leaves about $33 before overheads. Compare this to your Business Profile calls in the same month to see whether ads add leads or only claim ones you would have won anyway. ## FAQ

### What is the most important metric for a local business?

Leads that turn into customers. Count calls, direction requests and form submissions, then track how many become paying customers.

### How long does GA4 keep my data?

Event data retention can be set to 2 months or 14 months on standard properties. Age, gender and interest data always use 2 months.

### What happens if I forget UTM tags?

Traffic from those links shows "(not set)" for the missing values, so you cannot tell which email or post sent it.

### Can I track phone calls?

You can see call-button clicks in Business Profile and track phone-number clicks on your site as key events. Calls made after copying a number need call tracking software, which this guide does not cover.
