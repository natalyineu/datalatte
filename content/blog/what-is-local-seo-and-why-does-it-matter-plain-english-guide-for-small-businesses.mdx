## How Much Does Local SEO Cost for a Small Business?
The cost of local SEO can vary depending on the size of your business, the complexity of your website, and the level of competition in your industry. Here are some estimated costs:
* **DIY local SEO**: If you're willing to put in the time and effort, you can improve your local SEO without spending a fortune. Estimated cost: $0 - $100 per month.
* **Local SEO agency**: Working with a local SEO agency can provide more comprehensive results, but it can also be more expensive. Estimated cost: $500 - $2,000 per month.
* **Local SEO tools**: There are many local SEO tools available that can help you improve your online visibility. Estimated cost: $20 - $100 per month.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does local SEO take to work?**

Three to six months for noticeable results, assuming you're starting from scratch. The first month is fixing your Google Business Profile and getting citations consistent. Month two is review generation and photo updates. Month three is when you should see a traffic bump. If you're in a competitive market like NYC or Los Angeles, add two months. If you're in a smaller city, it can happen faster. Anyone promising results in two weeks is selling you something.
