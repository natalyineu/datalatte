Starbucks runs email on top of a loyalty program: its Rewards members were 59% of spend at US company-operated stores in Q3 FY26, and 35.8 million people in the US had been active in the previous 90 days. Starbucks does not publish email open rates, click rates or send schedules. What it does publish is how the program works, including that members see their level by email. Each fact below becomes a tactic a small cafe can copy.

## What has Starbucks actually confirmed about its loyalty emails?

Two primary sources cover it:

- The [Starbucks Card, Loyalty and Mobile dashboard](https://s203.q4cdn.com/326826266/files/doc_financials/2026/q3/Q3-FY26-Digital-IR-Dashboard.pdf), updated July 7, 2026, shows 35.8 million US 90-day active members in Q3 FY26 (33.9 million in Q3 FY25) and Rewards members at 59% of tender dollars at US company-operated stores.
- Starbucks' [January 29, 2026 press release](https://investor.starbucks.com/news/financial-releases/news-details/2026/Starbucks-Unveils-Reimagined-Loyalty-Program-to-Deliver-More-Meaningful-Value-Personalization-and-Engagement-for-Members/default.aspx) describes the updated program, which launched March 10, 2026.

From the release: there are three levels (Green, Gold, Reserve). Gold needs 500 Stars and Reserve 2,500 in a 12-month period. Green Stars are valid for six months, extended a month by a qualifying monthly activity; Gold and Reserve Stars never expire. Green members get a free birthday beverage or food item, with a seven-day redemption window for Gold and 30 days for Reserve. Green members receive personalized offers, and members see their level in the app and by email.

## Should a cafe show customers where they stand?

Starbucks shows members their level in the app and by email. People work harder toward a goal they can see.

**Copy it:** a 10-stamp card is a level system. Send a monthly email such as "You're 3 visits from a free drink." If your POS exports balances, merge the number in. If not, "Collect 8 stamps by 30 November" works. See [coffee shop loyalty ideas without an app](/blog/coffee-shop-loyalty-program-ideas-without-an-app).

## Does an expiry date make people come back?

Green Stars expire after six months unless the member stays active. The lever is a deadline plus an easy action.

**Copy it:** put an end date on every offer and say what to do: "Free cookie with any drink, this Friday only." Do not invent fake urgency, because customers who feel tricked unsubscribe.

## Is a birthday email worth sending?

Starbucks gives Green members a free birthday item and gives higher levels longer to redeem it.

**Copy it:** ask for a birthday month (not the year) on your sign-up form, then automate "Happy birthday, a coffee is on us this month." Most email tools can send on a date field. Make the treat cheap for you and generous for them.

## How do I personalize without Starbucks' data?

Green members receive personalized offers. A small shop can run three groups:

| Segment | What defines it | Message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New | Joined in the last 14 days | Welcome and first-visit offer |
| Regulars | Visited in the last 30 days | New menu items, loyalty progress |
| Lapsed | No visit in 45 to 90 days (suggested window) | A reason to come back |

Add one question at sign-up: "What's your usual order?" Our [welcome email series template](/blog/welcome-email-series-local-business-5-email-template) shows the setup.

## How do I get more people on the list?

Starbucks' members come through its app. You have a till. Put a QR code on the counter and receipt, have staff say one sentence ("Want a free drink on your birthday? Scan here"), and offer something small and real. Our [list-building guide](/blog/coffee-shop-email-list-building) has more ideas.

Every email should carry one offer, a deadline, your address and your hours, so it is trackable ("Show this email for a free cookie this Friday"). Never buy a list: it raises spam complaints, which is the fastest way to stop reaching inboxes.

## What email rules apply to a small cafe?

- **CAN-SPAM (US).** The FTC's [compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) requires accurate header information, non-deceptive subject lines, a clear ad disclosure, a valid postal address and a clear opt-out, and says you must honor an opt-out within 10 business days. Each violating email can be penalized up to $53,088. UK, EU and Australian rules differ, so check them if you email there.
- **Gmail.** Google's [sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126) require SPF or DKIM for all senders and ask that spam rates stay below 0.10%, never reaching 0.30%. Senders of 5,000 or more messages a day to Gmail also need DMARC and one-click unsubscribe. A cafe is nowhere near that volume, but your email tool should handle authentication, so switch it on.

## Do This Today

1. Put a sign-up QR code on the counter and receipt with a one-line reason to join.
2. Add birthday month and usual order to your sign-up form.
3. Write and automate one welcome email: thanks, your best-seller, one dated offer, address and hours.
4. Create a lapsed segment (45 to 90 days) and draft one win-back email with a deadline.
5. Confirm every message has your postal address and an unsubscribe link.

## FAQ

**Does Starbucks publish its email open rates?**
Not in the sources checked. It publishes loyalty figures such as active members and Rewards share of spend, not email metrics. Be wary of articles that quote them as fact.

**Is email marketing worth it for a cafe with a small list?**
It can be, because a usual-order tag lets you send relevant messages. The example above shows a modest return that depends on your redemption rates, so test and measure.

**Do I need an app to run a loyalty program?**
No. A stamp card, a POS loyalty feature or a spreadsheet plus a monthly email covers the idea. For texts, see our [SMS marketing guide for coffee shops](/blog/sms-marketing-for-coffee-shops-how-to-start).

**What is a good email open rate?**
No number is quoted here, because sources disagree and some email apps load images automatically, which inflates opens. Compare your own emails with each other and track visits.
