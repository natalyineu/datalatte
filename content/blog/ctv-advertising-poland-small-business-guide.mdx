Connected TV (CTV) advertising in Poland lets a local business show video ads on streaming services watched on television sets. Polish viewers use local-language platforms such as Polsat Box Go, Player.pl, TVP VOD and Canal+ online. A small business should plan a short, measurable test, get written CPM quotes before committing, and treat any benchmark as a sense check rather than a forecast. This guide covers the platform options, a worked PLN budget example, the available benchmarks and their limits, and the steps to take before buying inventory. The source set covers Google's display advertising and general search benchmarks, not Polish TV audience data, so Polish-specific CPM and reach figures are left out and must come from platform quotes.

## Which Polish streaming platforms can a local business buy?

The main platforms named for Polish CTV are Polsat Box Go, Player.pl (TVN and related channels), TVP VOD, Canal+ online, and Samsung TV+ in Poland. Each has a different viewer base, so the choice should follow the customer profile rather than the platform's size. A premium service business usually weighs audience quality over raw reach, while a regional business may need a platform with local-region targeting.

Inventory can be bought through a platform's own sales team or through a demand-side platform (DSP) that lists Polish streaming inventory. Ask each seller to confirm in writing which targeting options (city, district, device, household) are available, and request a CPM quote for each option before committing. Rate cards from older articles, including earlier versions of this guide, should not be used for planning.

## How much can a small Polish budget buy?

The worked example below uses assumption inputs only. Replace each assumed value with a quote from the platform or DSP before setting spend. The CPM is the input that changes the plan most.

- Monthly budget: 3,000 PLN (assumption)
- CPM, cost per 1,000 impressions: 60 PLN (assumption, placeholder for a real quote)
- Target frequency per household per month: 3 (assumption)
- Campaign length: 3 months (assumption)

Impressions per month equal 3,000 ÷ 60 × 1,000 = 50,000 (calculated from assumptions). Over three months that is 150,000 impressions. Estimated unique households at frequency 3 are 50,000 ÷ 3 ≈ 16,667 per month. The table shows how two budgets and two CPM placeholders interact.

| Scenario (all inputs assumption) | Monthly budget (PLN) | CPM (PLN) | Impressions per month | Est. households at frequency 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Test, low CPM | 3,000 | 60 | 50,000 | 16,667 |
| Test, high CPM | 3,000 | 90 | 33,333 | 11,111 |
| Core, low CPM | 5,000 | 60 | 83,333 | 27,778 |
| Core, high CPM | 5,000 | 90 | 55,556 | 18,519 |

If a seller quotes 90 PLN where the placeholder was 60, the same budget delivers about a third fewer impressions, so the quote should be checked before the budget is approved.

## What do the search and display benchmarks tell you?

The WordStream benchmark covers Google Ads search and display, not CTV, so it cannot predict a CTV campaign's results. It is still useful as a sense check on the rest of a paid mix. The page reports an all-industry average search click-through rate of 3.17% and a display rate of 0.46%, and it notes that both averages were higher than a couple of years earlier ([WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks), secondary).

The same source gives an all-industry average search cost per click of $2.69 and display cost per click of $0.63, both slightly above the figures from a couple of years before ([WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks), secondary). Google's help page describes the Display Network as reaching people across 35 million websites and apps, including YouTube and Gmail ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190), primary). That figure describes Google Display, not Polish streaming TV. For a local business, a useful comparison is a [Google Ads services](/services/google-ads) search test run alongside CTV, measured on the same cost per enquiry.

## What compliance checks should a Polish CTV advertiser make?

This article does not cite Polish or EU data-protection, broadcasting or advertising law, because the source set does not cover them. Before launch, confirm with the platform or DSP which consent framework they use for any audience targeting. Ask a local lawyer to check claims, pricing statements, and any restrictions on regulated product categories.

Keep the claims in ad creative consistent with the landing page and the offer terms. If a promotion has conditions, state them in the creative or in a clear disclosure the platform accepts.

## How should a local business in Poland start?

A short, controlled test is the safest first step. The checklist below keeps the first campaign measurable.

1. Define one service and one city or district, for example a single Warsaw café or a Kraków salon.
2. Request written CPM quotes from at least two sellers, with targeting options listed.
3. Set a test budget that can be absorbed over one to three months.
4. Use a unique landing page or promo code for CTV so enquiries can be traced to the campaign.
5. Record weekly impressions, site visits and enquiries, then compare cost per enquiry with other channels.
6. Scale only after two full cycles show a cost per enquiry that can be sustained.

## FAQ

### Is Polsat Box Go the best platform for every Polish small business?

No. Polsat Box Go may suit broad local reach, but the right platform depends on the target customer, city, and budget. Compare written CPM quotes and targeting options from each seller before choosing one.

### Can the 3.17% search CTR benchmark be used to forecast CTV results?

No. That figure is an all-industry average for Google Ads search. It is a sense check for search campaigns only and cannot forecast CTV click or enquiry rates.

### Is Polish-language creative needed for a CTV campaign in Poland?

Most Polish CTV audiences watch Polish-language services, so Polish-language creative is the default unless the offer requires otherwise. Test one version first, then compare results before producing more.

### How long should a first CTV test run?

One to three months is a practical range, because it gives enough weeks to see patterns in enquiries. Set the length and budget before launch, and do not extend the test without comparing cost per enquiry with other channels.
