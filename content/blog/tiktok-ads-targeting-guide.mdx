TikTok ads targeting has five categories: demographics, audience targeting, advanced targeting, device type and smart targeting. For a local business, the settings that matter most are location, age and one or two interests. TikTok combines choices inside a category with OR and choices across categories with AND, so every extra filter shrinks your audience. Keep the setup simple and let the first 14 days show you what works.

## What targeting options does TikTok Ads Manager offer?

TikTok's [ad targeting help article](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/ad-targeting) lists the categories, all set at the ad group level while you create a campaign. Which options appear depends on your market.

| Category | What it does | Local relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Demographics | Location, age, gender, language | High: this is where you set your area |
| Audience targeting | Interests, behaviors, custom and lookalike audiences | Medium: use one or two interests |
| Advanced targeting | Extra filters offered in Ads Manager | Low at first |
| Device type | Phone and OS filters | Low |
| Smart targeting | AI toggle that widens reach to similar people | Test later |

TikTok's [targeting guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/guides/targeting-advertising-guide) adds that smart targeting never overrides key filters such as age or location.

## How does TikTok combine your targeting choices?

Within one dimension, selections use OR. Across dimensions, they use AND. TikTok's own example: choosing the interest Beauty or the hashtag makeup includes people who match either one, while choosing Female and 25 to 44 includes only people who match both.

For a small shop, this means stacking filters costs you reach fast. A barber who picks a neighborhood, ages 18 to 34, men, and three narrow interests is multiplying restrictions. Start with location and age, then add a single interest group once you see delivery numbers.

## What is the difference between interests and behaviors?

TikTok describes interests as long-term passions, such as beauty, gaming, travel or food. Behaviors reflect recent activity, such as people who watched, liked or shared related videos in the last 15 days ([TikTok targeting guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/guides/targeting-advertising-guide)).

Local rule of thumb: pick an interest that matches what the customer wants today. A pet groomer might choose a pets interest and the behavior of recent pet-video engagement. A fitness studio might choose fitness. Check results after a week, because interests that sound right can still deliver poorly.

## How do you target a specific local area?

TikTok lists location types that include city, county, DMA region, state, urban district and zip or postal code ([About location targeting](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/location-targeting)). Three rules matter for small shops.

- Exclusions are not supported, so select the places you want rather than excluding others.
- Small areas can cut impressions and clicks and may raise cost per result.
- You can select up to 3,000 locations per ad group where available.

Our full walkthrough on [targeting local customers on TikTok](/blog/how-to-target-local-customers-on-tiktok) shows how to pick zip codes around a shop.

## What about custom and lookalike audiences?

Custom audiences are built from uploaded customer lists, TikTok Pixel site visitors, and people who engaged with your ads or content. Lookalike audiences start from a custom audience and find new people with similar traits ([TikTok targeting guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/guides/targeting-advertising-guide)).

For a business with an email list, upload it as a custom audience, either to reach existing fans or as the seed for a lookalike audience. TikTok does not publish a minimum list size in the pages we reviewed, so test with what you have and check the audience size Ads Manager shows.

## What does a sample targeting setup look like?

This is an assumption-based example. A hair salon runs a 14-day test at $21 a day (the ad group floor is above $20, per [TikTok's budget help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget)), a total of $294.

- Location: the salon's city, or 8 to 12 zip codes nearby
- Age: 22 to 44 (assumed customer base; change to match your clients)
- Gender: all, unless the service is gender-specific
- Interest: beauty
- Smart targeting: off for the first week, on for the second week as a comparison

After 14 days, compare cost per click and bookings between the two weeks. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I target by radius around my shop?**
TikTok's location help lists city, county, DMA, state, urban district and zip or postal code. A radius option is not listed, so use zip or postal codes instead.

**What is the minimum budget to test targeting?**
An ad group's daily budget must exceed $20, so about $21 a day. A campaign-level daily budget must exceed $50.

**Should I use broad or narrow targeting?**
Start with location and age only, then narrow. TikTok warns that small location sets can reduce delivery and raise cost per result.
