Meta ads on Facebook and Instagram can bring new clients to a pet groomer in Boston, MA, but budget and targeting decide whether they pay off. One groomer marketing guide suggests starting at $10 to $20 per day, and raising spend only after identifying high-performing ads. Typical Meta cost per click runs $1 to $3 and CPM runs $5 to $15, so a small budget can still test several audiences and offers before committing to more.

## Are Facebook or Instagram Ads Worth It for Pet Groomers?

For most groomers who want bookings from people nearby, yes. Instagram ads run through the same system as Facebook ads, Meta Ads Manager, because [Instagram is owned by Meta](https://adwave.com/resources/instagram-advertising-for-local-business), and one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook or both. That shared setup means you do not need separate tools to test both apps.

Meta's ads can also be set to a specific goal. [Restaurant campaigns can target traffic or reservations across Facebook, Instagram or both](https://www.theforkmanager.com/en/blog/introduction-paid-ads-restaurants), and a groomer can pick a traffic or booking goal the same way.

Choosing between the two apps is mostly a question of where your clients spend time. The [comparison of Instagram and Facebook ads](/blog/instagram-ads-vs-facebook-ads) covers the trade-offs in more detail, and the [pet groomers guide to Instagram marketing](/blog/pet-groomers-guide-to-instagram-marketing) covers the organic side that supports paid results.

## How Much Should a Pet Groomer Spend on Meta Ads?

The groomer guide's starting range of $10 to $20 per day works out to roughly $300 to $600 a month over 30 days. For context, AdWave estimates [$500 to $2,000 per month for most single-location restaurants](https://adwave.com/resources/restaurant-advertising-complete-marketing-guide), and its [restaurant comparison lists Meta at $300 to $1,500+ monthly](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-restaurants). Those are other industries, so treat them as a ceiling check rather than a groomer benchmark.

Run your own version with your real booking rate and ticket price before deciding a monthly budget.

## Which Audience Should a Boston Groomer Target?

The groomer guide suggests [targeting users within a 10 to 15 mile radius](https://barklead.com/post/using-facebook-ads-for-groomers) of your location, and [pet owners aged 25 to 60 who are likely to have disposable income](https://barklead.com/post/using-facebook-ads-for-groomers). Those are starting points, not rules. Boston's density varies by neighbourhood, so a tighter radius may suit dense areas better. Test two radii, such as a short drive and a longer one, and keep the one that produces bookings rather than clicks.

Retargeting is worth a separate ad set. [Meta retargeting is listed at a typical CPM of $5 to $15](https://adwave.com/resources/retargeting-ads-small-business), which is close to the general range, so people who already visited your booking page or follow your account can be reached without a large premium. The [pet groomers Instagram guide](/blog/pet-groomers-guide-to-instagram-marketing) explains how to build that audience from organic followers.

## Which Ad Formats Work Best for Pet Groomers?

Short video is the strongest format to test first. [According to Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, as cited by AdWave, short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images](https://adwave.com/resources/electrician-advertising-reaching-homeowners) on Facebook and Instagram. A groomer can shoot a clip of a clean, dried dog and show the before and after in a few seconds.

Offers matter as much as the visual. The groomer guide's sample ad offers [10% off a customer's first grooming session](https://barklead.com/post/using-facebook-ads-for-groomers) with a "book today" call to action. Use one offer per ad set so you can see which message drives bookings. If you plan to build short clips, the [Reels approach in this fitness studio guide](/blog/fitness-studio-marketing-with-instagram-reels) shows the same format decisions in a service business.

## How Long Does It Take for Meta Ads to Bring Bookings?

Expect a learning period. [AdWave's Meta comparison lists results in 1 to 4 weeks](https://adwave.com/resources/restaurant-advertising-complete-marketing-guide), and another AdWave guide notes that Meta [takes weeks to optimize](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-small-business-2026) while offering good targeting. Judging a campaign after three days will usually mislead you.

Set a review date four weeks out. Before then, change only one variable at a time, such as the offer or the radius, so you know what caused any change in results.

## What Should Be Set Up Before Spending Money?

Verify these steps before your first ad goes live:

1. Confirm you have access to Meta Ads Manager, the [central place to run ads across Instagram and Facebook](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-advertising), and that billing is set up.
2. Install Meta's tracking code on your website and confirm it fires on the booking confirmation page. Meta's help pages explain the current setup steps.
3. Configure one booking or lead conversion event so the platform optimises for appointments, not clicks.
4. Add UTM parameters to every ad URL so your analytics can separate paid traffic from organic.
5. Start at $10 to $20 per day and hold it steady until the review date.
6. Record each campaign's spend, bookings and revenue in one sheet so the next budget decision uses real numbers.

Meta also offers free training. [Meta Blueprint has free Facebook and Instagram advertising courses with certification on completion](https://sascom247.com/digital-marketing-for-beginners-in-nigeria/), which can help a groomer who wants to run the account in-house before hiring help.

## FAQ

### How much should a pet groomer spend on Meta ads per month?

The groomer guide suggests starting at $10 to $20 per day, which is about $300 to $600 over 30 days. Increase spend only after you find ads that produce bookings.

### Is Facebook or Instagram better for pet groomers?

Both run from the same Meta Ads Manager, so you can test them side by side. Choose the placement that brings bookings at the lowest cost per booking, not the one with the most impressions.

### Do I need a large audience to run Meta ads?

No. A groomer guide recommends a modest budget and a local radius of 10 to 15 miles. A small, local audience can produce bookings if the offer and creative are clear.

### Is there free training for Meta advertising?

Meta Blueprint offers free Facebook and Instagram advertising courses with certification on completion, according to a secondary summary of the program.
