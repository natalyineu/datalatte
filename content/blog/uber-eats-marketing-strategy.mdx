To market a restaurant on Uber Eats, first check that each order still earns a profit after the marketplace fee, then spend on Sponsored Listings only for dishes with a healthy margin. Uber's US pricing page lists a 20% fee on Lite, 25% on Plus and 30% on Premium, so a promotion that cuts your price can turn an order into a loss.

This guide covers US restaurants and cafes. Uber's pages were opened on 1 November 2026, and fees can differ by city. Uber's pages we opened do not give typical ad results, so none are quoted. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Do the Uber Eats Plans Include?

On the US merchant pricing page, Lite charges a 20% Marketplace Fee and lists discoverability when customers search for your store. Plus charges 25% after a 30-day 0% intro and adds "ads and offers" to boost visibility in the app. Premium charges 30% after the intro, adds the same ads and offers, and lists ads credit matching of up to $100 per month based on the prior month's ad spend ([Uber Eats for Merchants](https://merchants.ubereats.com/us/en/pricing/)).

The page also says every plan has access to in-app marketing tools and the option to buy ads. Pickup is 7% with validated in-store pricing and 10% on Lite without it. Some cities have lower fees, for example 15% for delivery in Seattle on Lite.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Premium | 30% after 30-day 0% intro | Yes | Up to $100 a month, matched to prior-month spend |

Source: Uber Eats US merchant pricing, 1 Nov 2026.

## How Do Sponsored Listings Work?

Uber's help page says Sponsored Listings promote your restaurant when people are already interested in ordering. Campaigns run weekly from a budget you set in Uber Eats Manager, and you can pause them there. The page does not say whether you pay per click or per order, and lists no minimum budget ([Uber help](https://help.uber.com/merchants-and-restaurants/article/sponsored-listings-faq?nodeId=b76c227a-9850-42cd-a419-163adae83fed)).

Because the billing basis is not stated, read it in Uber Eats Manager before you launch. Check where the ad spend shows in your payment statement, and compare it with orders from the same week.

## Which Items Should You Promote?

Promote items that earn the most after the fee, not the most popular ones. Work out the contribution per order: price, minus food cost, minus the marketplace fee, minus packaging. Then pick two or three items with a clear margin and a good photo.

Offers cost margin too. A discount you fund comes off the same price that the percentage fee is charged on, so check how your menu price and discount interact in your own statements. Uber's pages we opened do not explain this in detail.

## The Math: Does a $100 Ad Week Pay?

All inputs are **assumptions**. The 25% fee is Uber's Plus rate; everything else is invented for illustration.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Item price | $16.00 |
| Food and packaging cost | $6.00 |
| Marketplace fee (25%) | $4.00 |
| Contribution per order | $6.00 |
| Ad budget for the week | $100 |
| Extra orders from the ad (assumption) | 20 |
| Extra contribution | $120 |
| Net after ad spend | $20 |

At 20 extra orders the week earns a little. At 10 extra orders it loses $40. The break-even is about 17 extra orders at this margin. That is why untracked promotion is risky: compare orders with and without the campaign week, and drop it if the lift is smaller than the break-even.

Calculate the contribution per order for your top five items using your plan's fee. Promote only items where it is clearly positive.
</Callout>

## How Do You Keep Customers Beyond the App?

Uber Eats owns the customer relationship in the app, so build your own channels. Put your direct ordering link and phone on your Google Business Profile, ask for Google reviews, and add a card or QR code to delivery bags if the platform's rules allow it. Check Uber's merchant terms before you do so.

## FAQ

### How much does Uber Eats charge restaurants?
On Uber's US merchant page, the Marketplace Fee is 20% on Lite, 25% on Plus and 30% on Premium, with 0% for the first 30 days on Plus and Premium. Pickup is 7% with validated in-store pricing. Rates vary by city.

### Do I need a paid plan to advertise on Uber Eats?
Uber says every plan can buy ads, while Plus and Premium list ads and offers as plan features.

### What does Sponsored Listings cost?
The help page does not give a price or minimum. You set a weekly budget in Uber Eats Manager.

### Is Uber Eats marketing worth it?
Only if the extra orders cover the fee and the ad spend. Calculate the break-even per item first.

### Can I get a refund of fees on a slow month?
Premium lists fee refunds for months with fewer than 20 orders for the first 6 months, if conditions are met. Read the full terms.
