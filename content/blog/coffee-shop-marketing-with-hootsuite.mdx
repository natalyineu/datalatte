For most single-location coffee shops, Hootsuite is more tool than they need. Its Standard plan starts at $99 per user per month and covers 10 social accounts, with Professional at $199 and Advanced at $399 per user. A café run by one owner, posting to Instagram, Facebook and perhaps TikTok, can usually schedule posts by hand or with a cheaper tool. Hootsuite earns its price when several staff post, posts need approval, or one team manages many accounts.

## How Much Does Hootsuite Cost for a Coffee Shop?

Hootsuite offers four paid plans priced per user, plus a free trial, according to a [comparison of social media AI agents](https://sintra.ai/blog/ai-social-media/best-social-media-ai-agents) (secondary). Standard is $99 per user per month and manages up to 10 accounts with unlimited scheduling. Professional adds unlimited accounts and workflow automation at $199 per user. Advanced is $399 per user and adds approval workflows, message routing and team collaboration.

Enterprise has custom pricing rather than a published price. For larger businesses, a [HubSpot guide to AI agents for social media](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/ai-agents-for-social-media) (secondary) says Hootsuite's Heyday product requires contacting the company for pricing and is aimed at mid-to-large businesses. A single café is not that buyer.

Because pricing is per user, the bill grows with each person who logs in. A [Turrboo comparison](https://turrboo.com/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media) (secondary) puts three seats on Hootsuite Standard at $297 per month on annual billing. Check the current figures on Hootsuite's own pricing page before you buy, since third-party guides can lag.

## Is Hootsuite Worth It? A Worked Time-Saving Example

Every input below is an **assumption** for one owner running three channels. Replace them with your own numbers.

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Posts per week across 3 channels (assumption) | 9 |
| Minutes to post each manually (assumption) | 6 |
| Minutes to post each when batched in a scheduler (assumption) | 2 |
| Minutes saved per week | 36 |
| Minutes saved per month (4.33 weeks) | about 156, or 2.6 hours |
| Value of owner time per hour (assumption) | $25 |
| Value saved per month | about $65 |
| Hootsuite Standard, one user | $99 per month |
| Hootsuite Standard, three users | $297 per month (annual billing, secondary) |

On these inputs, one Standard seat costs $34 more per month than the time it saves. To break even at $25 per hour, you would need to save about 4 hours a month, or roughly 55 minutes a week. Three seats cost $297, which the same time saving does not come close to covering.

The maths changes if posting is a bottleneck, for example if you run ten accounts across several sites, or if an agency approves posts before they go live. Those cases are where Hootsuite's approval and team features start to matter.

## How Often Should a Coffee Shop Post?

Scheduling only matters once you know how much to schedule. The [Restaurant HQ coffee shop guide](https://www.therestauranthq.com/operations/coffee-shop-marketing/) (secondary) recommends posting on social media at least once per week, ideally one to two times per day. That is a wide range, so the right number depends on how much time you can keep up.

Hootsuite's own research, as reported in a [salon marketing article](https://salonbizsoftware.com/blog/daily-salon-social-media-marketing-ideas-how-to-engage-your-audience/) (secondary), suggests Instagram at 2 to 7 times per week and Facebook at 1 to 2 times per day. Those ranges are a starting point for testing, not a guarantee of results.

A simple weekly rhythm works for most cafés. Plan one new drink or seasonal item, one behind-the-counter video, one staff or regular customer feature with permission, and one offer or event. Batch them in a single hour, then let the scheduler release them. ## Which Platforms Take the Most Daily Time?

The [Bellwether Coffee guide](https://bellwethercoffee.com/blog/social-media-marketing-for-coffee-shops) (secondary) lists a daily time investment for each platform: about 30 minutes for Instagram, 15 minutes for Facebook, 5 minutes for Google Business and 1 hour for TikTok. Those are estimates from one brand's guide, not measured times, but they show where the hours go.

Instagram, Facebook and Google together come to about 50 minutes a day, or around 4 hours a week. That is the range where a scheduler and batching can meaningfully cut time. Adding TikTok roughly doubles the load, which is when a tool starts to pay off for a busy shop.

Google Business Profile does not sit in a social scheduler's usual workflow. A [BrewRound guide](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/) (secondary, dated 2020) notes that creating a listing is free. The [Per Diem guide](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide) (secondary) recommends answering common questions in the profile's Q&A section before customers ask, such as whether you offer free Wi-Fi, dairy-free options or dogs on the patio. For the full setup, use the [Google Business Profile optimization checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## Is a Free Tool Enough to Start?

For one location, usually yes. Hootsuite is named alongside Buffer and Sprout Social as a tool that helps plan and manage posts, according to a [Raklet marketing article](https://www.raklet.com/blog/membership-marketing-strategies-for-success/) (secondary). A [Thinkific review](https://www.thinkific.com/blog/marketing-automation-platforms/) (secondary) lists Hootsuite as best for entry-level social media needs, which suggests the product can fit small teams. That does not mean a café has to pay for it.

Start with the free option that covers your channels, and track the minutes you spend posting for two weeks. If the total stays under about 30 minutes a week, a paid scheduler will not repay its cost. If you are regularly losing an hour or more to posting, or a second person now needs to approve content, run a Hootsuite free trial and measure the difference against the worked example above.

## FAQ

### Is Hootsuite worth it for one coffee shop?

Usually not at the Standard price of $99 per user per month. The worked example shows the time saved for one owner is worth about $65 a month on assumed inputs. It becomes worth considering when several people post or approvals are needed.

### Does Hootsuite have a free plan?

The sources used here list a free trial for Hootsuite's paid plans, but do not describe a permanent free plan. Check Hootsuite's current pricing page for the trial length and any free tier.

### How many accounts does Hootsuite Standard include?

Standard manages up to 10 social accounts, with unlimited scheduling and AI content generation, according to the comparison in a [Sintra article](https://sintra.ai/blog/ai-social-media/best-social-media-ai-agents) (secondary).

### How often should a coffee shop post on social media?

A [Restaurant HQ guide](https://www.therestauranthq.com/operations/coffee-shop-marketing/) (secondary) recommends at least once per week, ideally one to two times per day. Start with a rhythm you can keep for a month, then adjust based on what gets responses.
