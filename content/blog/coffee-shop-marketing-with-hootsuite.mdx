For most single-location coffee shops, Hootsuite is more tool than they need. Its cheapest plan starts at $99 a month and allows 10 social accounts, while Buffer's free plan covers 3 channels and its paid plan is $5 per channel per month. Hootsuite makes sense when several people post, or you need one place to handle posts, messages and reviews across many accounts.

This guide compares the published prices, shows what a coffee shop actually needs to schedule, and gives a worked time-saving example labelled as an assumption. Prices come from vendor pricing pages opened on 31 October 2026.

## How Much Does Hootsuite Cost?

Hootsuite's plans page lists Standard from $99, Professional from $199 and Advanced from $399, each described as "starting at" prices. Standard includes 10 social accounts, Professional and Advanced allow unlimited accounts, and Enterprise is custom. There is no free plan; paid plans have a 14-day trial, and trial accounts are limited in daily posts and have no bulk scheduling.

The page does not show a separate monthly-billing figure, and third-party guides disagree on the top tier, so confirm the price at checkout. Currency is not stated on the page; the amounts are shown in dollars.

## Hootsuite or Buffer for a Coffee Shop?

A café usually has an Instagram, a Facebook page and perhaps a TikTok. That is three channels.

| | Hootsuite Standard | Buffer Free | Buffer Essentials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | from $99 per month | $0 | $5 per channel per month |
| Accounts | 10 | up to 3 channels | priced per channel |
| Post limit | trial limits only listed | 10 scheduled posts per channel | not stated on the page we read |
| Trial | 14 days | free forever | 14 days |

Three channels on Buffer Essentials would be $15 a month on the quoted rate, against $99 for Hootsuite. ## Which Networks Can Hootsuite Publish To?

Hootsuite says it supports Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, WhatsApp and Pinterest. Google Business Profile is not listed by that name on the integrations page we opened. The page mentions a "Google My Business" app that can monitor reviews, message customers and publish updates, but does not say it is the same product. Check this before you rely on Hootsuite for Google posts or review replies.

For a coffee shop, your Google profile often drives more walk-ins than social does. Keep it up to date directly using our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## What Should a Coffee Shop Schedule?

A scheduler does not create content, it only releases it on time. Batch one hour a week to plan the next seven days. A simple weekly rhythm, which is a suggestion and not a tested formula:

1. A new or seasonal drink, with a clear photo
2. A behind-the-counter short video (see [Reels ideas for coffee shops](/blog/coffee-shop-instagram-reels-ideas-that-get-views))
3. A staff or regular customer feature, with permission
4. An event, offer or opening hours change
5. A story-style reminder about your busiest quiet time

Plan the angle for each platform, not just copy-paste. The [Instagram strategy guide for coffee shops](/blog/coffee-shop-instagram-marketing-strategy) covers formats.

## Is It Worth the Money? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for a one-shop owner.

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Posts per week across 3 channels | 9 |
| Minutes to post each manually, at the time | 6 |
| Minutes to post when batched in a scheduler | 2 |
| Minutes saved per week | 36 |
| Saved per month (4.33 weeks) | about 156 minutes, or 2.6 hours |
| Value of owner time (assumption) | $25 per hour |
| Value saved per month | about $65 |

On these inputs, Hootsuite at $99 costs more than the time it saves. Buffer at $15 is under the saving. The conclusion flips if you have three baristas posting, ten accounts, or an agency client approving posts. Use your own minutes and rate.

## FAQ

### Is Hootsuite worth it for one coffee shop?

Usually not. The price from $99 a month is aimed at teams and multiple accounts. A free or per-channel scheduler covers the needs of one shop.

### Does Hootsuite have a free plan?

No free plan is listed on the plans page. Paid plans include a 14-day trial.

### Can Hootsuite post to TikTok and Instagram?

Hootsuite's integrations page says it supports both. Check the format limits in your trial.

### Does scheduling improve engagement?

No verified data shows that a scheduler raises engagement. It saves time and helps consistency. Engagement depends on the content.

### What should I use instead?

For one location, start with Buffer Free or the native tools inside Instagram and Facebook, then upgrade only if you hit a limit.
