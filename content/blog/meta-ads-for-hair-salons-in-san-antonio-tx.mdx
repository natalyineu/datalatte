## Which Campaign Setup Fits a Hair Salon?

Meta Ads Manager can run ads across Instagram and Facebook from one central point, and [Turrboo](https://turrboo.com/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media) notes that it also covers Messenger. Choose a campaign goal that matches the action you want, which is a booking. The Fork Manager notes that restaurants can pick traffic or reservation goals across Facebook, Instagram or both. A salon can apply the same logic and pick a goal tied to appointments.

Set the radius to 5 to 10 miles around the salon, as Bookeo recommends. Keep one ad set per radius so you can see which area books. The [Meta ads setup guide for hair salons](/blog/using-meta-ads-for-hair-salon-marketing) walks through the account structure step by step.

## Which Ad Formats Should a Salon Test First?

According to Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, as cited by [AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/electrician-advertising-reaching-homeowners), short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram. Start with short clips of real services, such as a cut, a colour process or a finished style, filmed in the salon.

Stories disappear after a set time, so they suit same-week openings and last-minute slots, as [Associated Hair Professionals](https://www.associatedhairprofessionals.com/updates/blog-posts/power-social-media-your-hairstyling-brand) describes them. Bookeo says most salon owners find three to four Instagram posts and one to two Facebook posts a week sustainable. Pair ads with an organic plan, as covered in [Instagram marketing for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons).

## How Do You Target a San Antonio Audience Without Guessing?

Peak follower activity is visible in the Insights tab, but only if you have a business account, according to Bookeo. Check Insights first and use the active hours to schedule posts and set ad delivery windows. Without a business account, you lose this view of when local followers are online.

Build age and gender ranges from your own booking data rather than from assumed city profiles. This guide does not supply San Antonio demographic figures, so treat any audience a vendor describes as a hypothesis to test. Past clients can form a custom audience for repeat-visit offers. For follow-up after the first booking, see [marketing automation for hair salons](/blog/marketing-automation-for-hair-salons).

## What Does a Realistic Return Look Like?

The worked example below uses one sourced figure and two labelled assumptions. The $750 budget is Bookeo's starting point. The cost per booking and the first-visit value are assumptions for illustration, not benchmarks.

- Monthly ad budget: $750 (sourced, Bookeo starting point)
- Cost per booked appointment: $45 (assumption)
- Average first-visit value: $85 (assumption)

Bookings: $750 ÷ $45 = 16.7, so about 16 bookings. Revenue: 16 × $85 = $1,360. Revenue per ad dollar: $1,360 ÷ $750 = about 1.8. This is revenue, not profit, and it leaves out product costs and staff time. Replace the assumptions with your own tracked numbers before making a budget decision.

## What Should You Check Before Signing an Agency Contract?

PetPath, a pet-care agency, states that ad spend goes directly to Meta, that its management fee is separate and fixed monthly, and that its Meta ads plans have no lock-in contract. It also says customers can pause or change a plan with 15 days' notice. These are one vendor's terms, so use them as a checklist and compare them with any agency you consider.

Ask who is billed for ad spend, whether the management fee is fixed or variable, how much notice is required to pause, and what the monthly report measures. Software Pro Digital says it reports on bookings and orders rather than reach, which is the measure a salon should expect.

## How Should You Track Bookings From Meta Ads?

Track bookings, not impressions or reach. Connect the account so reports pull together in one place. DashThis lists Meta Ads, Facebook Insights and Instagram among its reporting connectors, which is one way to combine ad data with booking figures.

1. Confirm the Instagram and Facebook accounts are business accounts with Insights access.
2. Add a booking-confirmation tracking event to the website so each booking is recorded against the campaign.
3. Compare cost per booking against your first-visit value every month.

## FAQ

### How much should a hair salon spend on Meta ads each month?

Bookeo suggests starting Facebook and Instagram ad spend at $750 to $1,500 a month. Adjust the figure after several weeks of tracked bookings, since AdWave says optimization takes weeks.

### Does a salon need a business account to use Insights?

Yes. Bookeo says peak follower activity is shown in the Insights tab, provided the account is a business account.

### How long before Meta ads show results?

AdWave's channel table lists 1 to 4 weeks for Meta ads across its vertical guides, and says optimization takes weeks. Expect the first month to be a learning period.

### What radius should the ads target?

Bookeo recommends a 5 to 10 mile radius around the salon for Facebook and Instagram ads. Narrow or widen the range based on where your booked clients actually live.
