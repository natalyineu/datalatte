To use digital out-of-home (DOOH) screens around a local event, book screens near the venue and the routes to it, schedule the ads for the hours before and during the event, and measure with a store-visit or code-based method. StackAdapt, a DOOH vendor, says CPMs run from $2 to $15. An IAB UK guide says some media owners set minimum orders of £2,500 to £5,000.

No source publishes event-specific price premiums.

This guide explains how event timing works, what lead time to allow, how impressions are counted and what a small test might look like.

## How Does Event-Based DOOH Work?

You choose screens close to an event, such as roadside or transit screens on the routes to a venue. Then you run your ad in the hours that matter. IAB UK explains that campaigns can be targeted by environment, time of day down to the hour, and geography, even down to a business "100m down the road".

Its example is a café running a breakfast offer only during the weekday commuter rush.

The IAB's 2025 measurement guide adds that contextual targeting can respond to conditions such as weather or time of day. Event listings come from the venue or city calendar, so you plan from public dates.

## When Should You Book?

The IAB UK guide says creative typically needs to reach the media owner at least five working days before launch, but a campaign can start the same day if the ad is ready, because everything is delivered remotely. That guide dates from October 2020 and describes UK media owners, so check lead times with your US seller.

For a recurring event such as a weekly market, book a trial burst early and compare against a week without ads. For a one-off event, confirm the screen schedule and creative specifications before the date is close.

## What Will It Cost?

StackAdapt states CPMs for DOOH range from $2 to $15 depending on screen type and campaign scope, with large iconic screens at the top and smaller screens in busy places at the bottom. It gives no source for the range and no minimum budgets. IAB UK said some UK media owners set minimum orders of £2,500 to £5,000 for a burst of a few weeks.

In that 2020 guide, about £5,000 bought six to eight screens for two weeks in a town. These are UK figures and old, so treat them as a scale check, not a quote.

## How Are DOOH Impressions Counted?

Not as people reached. The IAB's 2025 guide says that for static DOOH, an impression multiplier is applied to account for the average number of people in the viewing range during each play. The multiplier comes from audience data such as foot traffic and dwell time.

So an impression is a modelled estimate. The IAB also describes attribution that uses geofences around screens to verify exposure and link it to behaviour such as footfall. Ask your seller how impressions and visits are measured and who verifies them.

## How Do You Measure an Event Campaign?

Give the campaign its own offer code, QR code or landing page, so redemptions can be tied to the screens. Compare event-week sales with a similar week without ads, noting that the event itself raises traffic.

Because the event lifts sales on its own, the ad's effect is hard to isolate without a control. ## The Math: What Does $1,000 Buy?

All inputs are **assumptions** using the StackAdapt CPM range. Impressions are modelled, not unique people.

| Input | Low CPM | High CPM |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | $1,000 | $1,000 |
| CPM | $2 | $15 |
| Impressions | 500,000 | about 66,700 |
| Redemptions needed to cover cost at a $6 margin per redemption (assumption) | about 167 | about 167 |

At a $6 margin per redemption, $1,000 needs about 167 redemptions to break even, whatever the CPM. The CPM only decides how many impressions you get for that spend. Aim the ad where the event crowd is dense, and use a code so you can count redemptions.

## FAQ

### Can a small business afford DOOH?
Minimums vary by seller. IAB UK listed £2,500 to £5,000 minimums for some UK media owners in 2020. Ask sellers for current US minimums.

### How far ahead should I book?
IAB UK says at least five working days for creative, with same-day starts possible. Confirm with your seller.

### What creative works on screens?
IAB UK says ads typically show for 10 seconds, and advises a simple, readable headline and a clear logo.

### Can I measure store visits?
The IAB describes geofence-based attribution, but it depends on data partners. Use codes as a backup.
