## How Big Is Brazil's Digital Audience?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 185 million internet users in Brazil in October 2025, or 86.9% of the population, and 150 million social media user identities. Instagram's advertising reach is 147 million, up 8.0% in a year. TikTok's reach among adults is 131 million, up 17.6%. Facebook's reach is 109 million and fell 1.3%.

These are advertising audience figures, not unique active users, and one person can appear on several platforms. DataReportal's Brazil report gives no WhatsApp user count, and this guide does not quote one from unverified sources. Treat WhatsApp as a channel you must test yourself: ask new customers how they contacted you.

## Which Channels Should a Local Business Start With?

Start with the free ones, then add paid reach. The table gives a sensible order for a salon, café, gym or pet shop. The order is a recommendation, not a measured ranking.

| Channel | What it does | Cost to start | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | Shows you on Maps and local search | Free | Add hours, photos, WhatsApp link, services |
| WhatsApp Business | Bookings, questions, quotes | Free to use the app | Platform messaging is billed per message |
| Instagram | Shows results and atmosphere | Free | 147M ad reach in Brazil |
| Click to WhatsApp ads | Sends ad clicks into a chat | Ad budget | Free reply window after the click |
| Google Search ads | Catches people searching now | Ad budget | No published CPC for Brazilian local niches |
| TikTok | Short video; fastest-growing reach | Free to post | 131M adult ad reach |

Work down the table. A complete Google profile and a fast WhatsApp reply do more for a small shop than a large ad budget pointed at a slow inbox.

## How Does WhatsApp Pricing Work for Businesses?

Meta bills the WhatsApp Business Platform per delivered message since 1 July 2025, and never charges for messages a customer sends to you. Template messages (marketing, utility, authentication) can be sent at any time. Service messages are replies inside the 24-hour customer service window, which restarts each time the customer writes.

Each business phone number gets 1,000 free service messages per month, and Meta charges from the 1,001st.

Meta's page says charging for service messages beyond the free tier resumed on 1 October 2026, and that accounts without a payment method may stop delivering them. Meta publishes rates per market in a downloadable rate card. It also offers Brazilian businesses billing in reais (BRL). The rate card amounts are not in the page text, so check them before you budget.

The free WhatsApp Business app, used by many small shops, is a separate product from the Platform that agencies and software providers use. The pricing above applies to the Platform.

## What Do Click to WhatsApp Ads Do?

A Click to WhatsApp ad runs on Meta's apps and opens a chat with your business when someone taps it. Meta says that when a customer messages you from one of these ads, your reply opens a free entry point window of up to 7 days. During that window Meta does not charge for marketing, utility, authentication or service messages (Meta Business Agent messages are still charged).

This suits businesses that sell by conversation: salons, clinics, trainers and groomers. Send the person a short greeting, ask what they need, and offer two time slots. Meta publishes no Brazilian cost per conversation for local niches, so test with a small budget and record your own number.

## Is Pix Important for Local Businesses?

Yes. Pix is the Brazilian central bank's instant payment system, and its volume is large. A Let's Money article citing Banco Central data reports 22.93 billion Pix transactions worth R$ 10.499 trillion in the second quarter of 2026, and a single-day record of 318 million transactions on 4 September 2026 (secondary source). The same article puts credit card volume at 6.049 billion transactions in the quarter.

For a small business, this means you should accept Pix at the counter and in WhatsApp chats. Add your Pix key or a payment link to your replies and your catalog. A customer who has to ask how to pay may simply not book.

## How Should You Use Instagram in Brazil?

Use Instagram to show proof: before and after photos with permission, short Reels of the work, Stories of the day, and a bio with a WhatsApp link. Mention your bairro (neighbourhood) in captions and on your profile, since local customers search by area.

No source supports a claim that a particular music style or post format wins in Brazil, so test your own. Look at which posts bring WhatsApp messages, not only likes. ## What Does a Worked Example Look Like?

This example uses **assumptions** only, because no published Brazilian benchmark covers local niches. A hair salon receives 300 new WhatsApp chats a month and replies about three times in each.

| Input or result | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| New customer chats per month | 300 |
| Service messages sent per chat | 3 |
| Total service messages | 900 |
| Free service messages per number | 1,000 |
| Chats that book | 25% |
| Bookings | 75 |
| Average ticket | R$ 120 |
| Revenue from these bookings | R$ 9,000 |

At this size the salon stays inside the free tier of service messages on the Platform. If chats double, it passes 1,000 messages and Meta charges from the 1,001st at the rate on its card. The business should check that rate before launching a broadcast, because marketing template messages are priced separately.

## What Should You Do First?

1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile, with a WhatsApp link and your hours.
2. Switch on WhatsApp Business, write a greeting message, and set opening hours.
3. Post proof on Instagram three times a week and put your WhatsApp link in the bio.
4. Add Pix to every payment step.
5. After a month, test one Click to WhatsApp ad with a small budget and measure cost per booking.

## FAQ

### Do I need WhatsApp Business to sell in Brazil?

DataReportal publishes no WhatsApp user count for Brazil, so there is no measured share to quote. In practice, customers expect to message a business directly, and a WhatsApp number removes friction. The free WhatsApp Business app is enough for a small shop.

### How much does it cost to send WhatsApp messages as a business?

On the Platform, Meta charges per delivered message, with 1,000 free service messages per phone number per month. Rates depend on the message type and country and are published in Meta's rate card. The free Business app has no per-message charge but fewer automation options.

### Is Instagram or TikTok better for a Brazilian local business?

Instagram has the larger ad reach (147 million against 131 million adults on TikTok) and suits showing finished work. TikTok reaches younger audiences and grew faster in the last year. Pick the one your customers use and measure messages received.

### How much do Google Ads cost in Brazil?

No source publishes click costs for Brazilian local niches. Use Google's Keyword Planner for your city and keyword, and set a small test budget.

### Should I use Pix or cards?

Offer both, but make Pix easy. Banco Central data cited by Let's Money shows Pix handles far more transactions than credit cards each quarter.
