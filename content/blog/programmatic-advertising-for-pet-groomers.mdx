Most pet groomers should start with Google Search and a complete Business Profile, then add programmatic display or retargeting once those work. LocaliQ's 2026 benchmark for Animals and Pets shows $4.06 per click, a 7.49% click-through rate and a 16.22% conversion rate on Search, because those searchers already want a groomer.

Programmatic advertising buys display and video ads by software, to reach people before they search. This guide covers where it helps a grooming salon, what platforms state about minimums and a worked budget. Figures are from the sources below.

## Should a Pet Groomer Use Programmatic Ads at All?

Search comes first. Someone typing a grooming query is close to booking, and the Animals and Pets category converts at 16.22% on Google Ads according to LocaliQ, with a cost per lead of $31.50. Compare that with all industries at $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead. Read our [Google Ads for pet groomers guide](/blog/google-ads-for-pet-groomers-guide) and [common mistakes](/blog/pet-groomer-google-ads-mistakes) first.

Programmatic display and video add value in a few cases: reminding people who visited your site, reaching new movers or promoting a seasonal offer. It does not replace Search.

## How Big Is the Market Around You?

The American Pet Products Association puts the "other services" category, which includes boarding, grooming, insurance, training, pet sitting and walking, at $14.3 billion for 2025, with $14.9 billion projected for 2026. It does not split out grooming alone. The page gives 95 million US households owning a pet. These are national figures and say nothing about demand in your postcode.

## What Can Programmatic Do for a Groomer?

- **Retargeting:** show a reminder to people who viewed your booking page. AdRoll states no minimum spend and CPM pricing, with rates that vary with demand.
- **Customer Match:** Google lets you use your own customer data to reach those customers on Search, YouTube, Gmail and Display. Lists stay eligible when at least 100 members are added or updated within 540 days.
- **Local display:** target a radius around your salon. Google's minimum radius is 1 km.

If the average groom is worth more than $50 in profit, the test pays. A programmatic display test of the same size would be judged differently: count visits and later branded searches, not direct bookings.

## What Mistakes Should You Avoid?

Do not run display before your Business Profile and booking page are ready; see our [Google Business Profile for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-for-pet-groomers). Do not target a whole region when clients come from a few miles around. Do not judge the test in a week, because bookings follow the grooming cycle.

## FAQ

### How much should a pet groomer spend on programmatic ads?

No standard amount exists. Start with a Search budget you can measure, and add a small display or retargeting test only after bookings flow.

### Is programmatic better than Google Ads for groomers?

For ready-to-book customers, Search converts better. Programmatic builds awareness.

### What is a good conversion rate for pet services?

LocaliQ's 2026 figure for Animals and Pets is 16.22% on Google Ads. Your result will differ.

### Can I use my client list for ads?

Google Customer Match allows it with consent and policy compliance. Check the policy first.
