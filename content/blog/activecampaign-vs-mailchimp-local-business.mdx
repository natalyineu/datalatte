Pick Mailchimp if you have a small list and want simple newsletters, or want to start free. Pick ActiveCampaign if you want automations that react to what a customer does, such as a rebooking reminder after a haircut. Mailchimp's free plan stops at 250 contacts and 500 sends a month. ActiveCampaign has no free plan, only a 14-day trial with no credit card.

This guide compares the plans as the vendors describe them on their own pages. ActiveCampaign does not show prices on its public pricing page, so its dollar figures below come from a secondary source and are marked as such.

## What Does Each Plan Include?

Mailchimp lists four plans. Free is $0 with up to 250 contacts, 500 emails a month and a daily cap of 250. Essentials starts at $13 a month and allows up to 4 steps in a flow. Standard starts at $20 a month and allows up to 200 flows. Premium starts at $350 a month and is the only plan the pricing page lists with multivariate and A/B testing. The Mailchimp page shows promotional first-year pricing, so check the price at checkout.

ActiveCampaign lists Starter, Plus, Pro and Enterprise. All are quoted for 1,000 contacts billed yearly, but the public page shows no dollar amounts.

## How Do Automations Differ?

This is the main reason to pay more. On ActiveCampaign, Starter allows multi-step automations with 5 actions each, email only. Plus and above allow unlimited actions across channels, landing pages and sales automation. Pro adds predictive sending and conversion tracking.

On Mailchimp, the pricing page ties flow size to the plan. Essentials allows 4 flow steps, which fits a welcome email and one follow-up. Standard raises the limit to 200 flows. For a salon that wants a booking reminder, a review request and a win-back message, count the steps before you choose a plan.

## What Does ActiveCampaign Cost?

ActiveCampaign's pricing page says "no setup costs or hidden fees" and offers a 30-day refund guarantee, but it does not print prices. A secondary review site, Email Software Insights, lists these prices for 1,000 contacts: Starter $19 monthly or $15 on annual billing, Plus $59 or $49, Pro $99 or $79. The page is dated October 2025 and the figures may have changed.

Two add-ons matter for local shops. SMS and the enhanced CRM are sold separately on ActiveCampaign, and the SMS add-on is not available on Starter. The prices for both are not shown.

## How Does Mailchimp Count Contacts?

Mailchimp counts every person in your audience, including unsubscribed and non-subscribed contacts. Archived, cleaned and deleted contacts do not count. If you pass 250 contacts on the free plan, live and test sending is held until you upgrade or reduce the list.

On paid plans the send limit is a multiple of your contact limit: 10 times on Essentials, 12 times on Standard, 15 times on Premium. If you go over the contact limit, service continues but an extra charge appears on the bill. Keep the list clean, because every dormant address can raise the price.

## Which One Fits Which Local Business?

A coffee cart sending one weekly offer to 300 regulars is a Mailchimp case, even if it must leave the free plan at 250 contacts. A hair salon or fitness studio that wants different messages for new clients, lapsed clients and members is closer to ActiveCampaign. Segmentation and multi-step logic matter more when customers book repeatedly.

Neither tool replaces a booking system. Whichever you pick, connect it to the list you already own. ## Is the Upgrade Worth It? A Break-Even Test

The inputs below are **assumptions**, not benchmarks. Use your own numbers.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Plan | ActiveCampaign Plus, annual billing, 1,000 contacts (secondary price) |
| Yearly cost | $49 x 12 = $588 |
| Average visit value (assumption) | $60 |
| Visits needed to cover the plan | $588 / $60 = 9.8, so 10 extra visits a year |
| Starter for comparison | $15 x 12 = $180, or 3 extra visits |

If an automated reminder brings back even one extra client a month, the plan pays for itself under these assumptions. If you cannot name the automation you would build, stay on the cheaper plan. ## FAQ

### Is Mailchimp free for small businesses?

Yes, up to 250 contacts and 500 sends a month, with a daily limit of 250 emails, one audience and one seat. Above 250 contacts, sending is held until you upgrade or cut the list.

### Does ActiveCampaign have a free plan?

No. It offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card and a 30-day refund guarantee, according to its pricing page.

### How much is ActiveCampaign per month?

The official page shows no prices. A secondary review lists $15 to $79 a month at 1,000 contacts on annual billing, depending on plan. Check the live page before buying.

### Which is easier for a beginner?

Mailchimp is usually simpler for a newsletter. ActiveCampaign takes longer to set up, but its automations are less limited on higher plans.

### Can I switch later?

Yes. Both let you export contacts. Keep your list clean and consent records ready so the move does not stall.
