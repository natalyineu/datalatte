A local citation is any online mention of your business name, address and phone number, usually in a directory or listing. For most small businesses the right strategy is short: perfect your Google Business Profile, claim the main map and search listings, add a few directories that fit your trade, and keep every detail identical. Whitespark's 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors say citations have lost weight over a decade, but still matter for AI search visibility.

This guide covers the order of work, what Google actually says, and a time estimate. Estimates are labelled as assumptions.

## Do Citations Still Help Local Rankings?

A little, and less than reviews and your profile. Whitespark's 2026 edition (published November 2025) says citations lost about half a percentage point of weight and that review and behavioural signals gained importance. In its local pack list, "Consistency of Citations on Primary Search Engines" ranks #28 with a score of 124, and "Quality/Authority of Structured Citations" ranks #49 with a score of 105.

For AI search visibility the picture differs. Whitespark says three of the top five AI search visibility factors are citation factors, with "Quality/Authority of Unstructured Citations" ranked #4. That is a survey of local SEO professionals, not a Google statement, so treat it as expert opinion.

## What Does Google Say About Ranking?

Google's help says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and prominence. Prominence depends partly on how many websites link to you and how many reviews you have. It also says businesses with complete and accurate information are more likely to show up, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Google does not mention citations by name on that page. So the safe reading is: accurate information everywhere helps people and machines trust your details, but it does not replace reviews or a complete profile.

## Which Listings Should You Build First?

Work from the most important source outward.

| Order | Listing | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Google Business Profile | Local results on Google Maps and Search |
| 2 | Apple Business | Apple says you can claim your location on Maps and control hours and images |
| 3 | Bing Places | Check its current requirements on Microsoft's page; this guide could not read it |
| 4 | Your own website contact page | Same details, same format |
| 5 | Two to five trade or area directories | Choose ones your customers actually use |
| 6 | Local chamber of commerce or association | Often links to your site |

Do not pay for mass submission to hundreds of directories. No source here shows that volume beats a handful of accurate listings.

## How Do You Keep Name, Address and Phone Consistent?

Pick one exact version of your name, address and phone number and copy it everywhere. Google's guidelines say your name should reflect your real-world name, with no extra keywords, taglines, store codes or hours added. Addresses must be precise; P.O. boxes and virtual offices are not eligible for a profile, and service-area businesses should generally hide their street address.

Write the master version in a spreadsheet: name, address, phone, website, hours, categories, short description. When you move or change numbers, update that sheet first, then each listing. ## How Long Does It Take? A Worked Example

All times are **assumptions**.

| Task | Time per item | Items | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Build the master sheet | 30 min | 1 | 30 min |
| Claim or create a main listing | 30 min | 3 | 90 min |
| Add a trade or area directory | 20 min | 5 | 100 min |
| Quarterly check of all listings | 5 min | 9 | 45 min |

That is about 3.7 hours to set up and under an hour a quarter to maintain. Spending the same hours asking customers for reviews is likely to do more for rankings. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults found 97% of consumers read online reviews, and 74% only consider reviews from the last three months.

## How Do You Fix Wrong Listings?

Search your phone number and your old addresses and see what comes back. Claim the listing if the directory allows it, correct the details, and note the date in your sheet. Duplicates on the same directory should be merged or removed through that directory's support process; the steps differ by site.

If you also run a site, keep the contact page in the same format. Our posts on [schema markup for local business websites](/blog/schema-markup-local-business-website) and [local business website GDPR compliance](/blog/local-business-website-gdpr-compliance) cover the markup and legal side.

## FAQ

### What is a local citation?

An online mention of your business name, address and phone number, usually in a directory, map service or website. It may or may not include a link.

### How many citations do I need?

No source gives a number. Start with Google, Apple and Bing, your own site and a few directories your customers use, then keep them accurate.

### Do citations help with AI search?

Whitespark's 2026 study says so: three of the top five AI search visibility factors are citation factors. It is expert opinion, not a statement from Google or an AI company.

### Should I pay for a citation service?

Only if it saves time and you can still control the master details. Check whether you can edit or remove listings if you cancel.

### What if my business has no storefront?

Google says service-area businesses should generally hide their street address. Use the same service area wording everywhere.
