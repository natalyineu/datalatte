For most local businesses with one to three social profiles, Buffer is the cheapest: $5 per channel per month on its Essentials plan, and a free plan for up to 3 channels. Later starts at $18.75 a month with annual billing and bundles a link-in-bio page. Hootsuite starts at $99 a month and has no free plan, so it only makes sense if you need its team and inbox tools.

Prices below come from each vendor's pricing page, checked on 2026-10-25. Vendors change plans often, so confirm before you buy.

## How Much Do Hootsuite, Buffer and Later Cost?

| | Hootsuite | Buffer | Later |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cheapest paid plan | Standard, $99 | Essentials, $5 per channel per month | Starter, $18.75 per month |
| Pricing basis | Per user, annual billing | Per connected channel | Per plan, annual billing |
| Free plan | No (14-day trial) | Yes, up to 3 channels | No (14-day trial) |
| Free trial | 14 days, no card needed | 14 days on paid plans | 14 days |
| Accounts included on entry plan | 10 social accounts | Pay per channel | 1 social set (8 profiles) |
| Scheduled posts | Unlimited | Free: 10 per channel. Paid: unlimited, fair-use cap of 5,000 per channel | Starter: 30 per profile |
| Users on entry plan | 1 or more | 1 (Team plan: unlimited) | 1 |

Hootsuite's page shows prices "based on annual billing" and does not publish monthly-billing prices. Its mid tiers are $199 (Professional) and up to $399 (Advanced). Later lists Growth at $37.50 and Scale at $82.50 a month. Buffer's Team plan is $10 per channel per month.

## Which Tool Is Cheapest for a Single Local Business?

Buffer, in most cases. You pay only for the channels you connect, so a shop with Instagram and Facebook pays for two. The Free plan covers 3 channels but limits you to 10 scheduled posts per channel at a time, which suits light posting.

Later's Starter plan costs more than Buffer for two or three channels but includes a link-in-bio page and a set of 8 profile slots, one per platform. Its 30-post limit per profile is the thing to check against your posting rate.

Hootsuite costs several times more. Its page lists unlimited scheduled posts and AI post generation, but a single-location business rarely uses what the price pays for.

## Which Platforms Does Each Tool Support?

Buffer lists Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest, Google Business, Mastodon, X and Substack. Google Business matters for local shops because it lets you post updates to your profile from the same queue.

Later's pricing page says a social set covers Instagram, Facebook, Threads, Pinterest, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube and Snapchat. It does not list Google Business there.

Hootsuite's plans page names Facebook Pages, Instagram profiles and X as examples but does not publish a full list, and its platforms page returned an error, so confirm your channels in the free trial.

## Which Tool Has the Features a Small Business Needs?

- **Analytics:** Later Starter includes platform analytics for up to 3 months, Growth up to 1 year and Scale up to 2 years. Buffer's Essentials plan lists unlimited insights history, while Free keeps 30 days.
- **Inbox:** Later includes a social inbox on Growth and Scale, not Starter. Hootsuite lists one inbox from Standard.
- **Team approvals:** Buffer Team and Hootsuite Advanced list approval workflows. Later's page shows 2 users on Growth and 4 on Scale.
- **Link in bio:** included on all Later plans.

Pages do not publish how well each tool's analytics match native Instagram or Facebook numbers, so no accuracy claim is made here.

## Which Should You Choose?

- Pick **Buffer** if you post on up to three channels, want to start free and keep costs near $15 a month.
- Pick **Later** if Instagram and TikTok are your main channels and you want link in bio and visual planning in one plan.
- Pick **Hootsuite** if several staff share inboxes, you need approval steps, or you manage many accounts.

## FAQ

### Is there a free version of Hootsuite, Buffer or Later?

Only Buffer. Its Free plan allows up to 3 channels and 10 scheduled posts per channel. Hootsuite and Later offer 14-day trials but no free plan on their pricing pages.

### Which is best for Instagram?

Later is built around Instagram and TikTok planning with link in bio on every plan. Buffer also schedules Instagram. Neither page publishes Instagram-specific performance data.

### Can I post to Google Business Profile from these tools?

Buffer lists Google Business among its channels. Later's pricing page does not list it, and Hootsuite does not publish a full list. Check in a trial.

### Do the prices include taxes?

No. Hootsuite states that its prices exclude applicable taxes, and the others show list prices only.

### Are the monthly prices lower with annual billing?

Hootsuite says annual billing gives a lower per-user monthly rate but does not publish the monthly-billing price. Later's listed prices are for yearly billing, with a yearly discount offer shown on the page.

### Do I need a paid scheduler at all?

Not always. Buffer's Free plan covers up to 3 channels, which may be enough to start. The pages above do not measure how much time any tool saves, so test one on a free plan or trial first.
