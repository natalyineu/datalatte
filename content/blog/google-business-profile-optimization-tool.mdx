A single-location business does not need a paid Google Business Profile tool to optimise its listing. Google's own profile editor covers the essentials, and a review link or QR code is free. Paid tools help with rank tracking, citations, scheduled posts and review requests. On 1 November 2026 the entry prices were $41 a month for BrightLocal (1 location, monthly billing), $39 for Localo (billed annually) and from $14 for Whitespark's rank tracker.

This page compares what those vendors publish on their pricing pages. Prices change, so check before you buy. Worked examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Can You Do for Free?

Before paying for anything, use what Google gives you. You can edit your hours, categories, photos and replies in the profile itself. Google says you can ask customers to visit a Google link or scan a QR code to leave a review, and customers must be signed in to a Google Account. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists every field to complete.

Whitespark also lists free tools: Review Checker, Google Review Link Generator, Free Local Ranking Checker and Freshness Distance Calculator. A free review link plus a sensible [review routine](/blog/google-business-profile-reviews) covers many small businesses.

## How Much Does BrightLocal Cost?

BrightLocal lists three plans for one location. Track is $41 a month ($369 a year, about $31 a month). Manage is $54 ($485 a year, about $40 a month). Grow is $65 ($584 a year, about $49 a month). All three include the Local Rank Tracker with up to 100 keywords and four competitors per location, and a GBP audit.

Only Grow includes review monitoring across multiple sites and review requests by email, SMS and in-store. Manage adds GBP post scheduling and AI Insights. A 14-day free trial needs no credit card. Prices rise with the number of locations: for 2 to 5 locations, Grow is $87 monthly.

## How Much Does Localo Cost?

Localo lists Single Business at $39 a month (regular $49), billed annually and excluding VAT. It covers one profile with position tracking, review management, scheduled Google posts, weekly guided tasks and competitor insights. Pro is $149 a month (regular $169) for multiple profiles and is built for agencies.

There is a 14-day free trial without a card and a 30-day money-back guarantee. The page is inconsistent: the FAQ says annual billing gives a 20% discount, while the pricing table says two months free. Confirm the real total at checkout.

## How Much Does Whitespark Cost?

Whitespark's pricing page lists Local Citation Finder plans from $33 to $149 a month and Local Rank Tracker plans from $14 to $200 a month. Plan details beyond those ranges were not readable on the page, so check what each tier includes. Citation finders suit businesses cleaning up listings across directories, a task that matters more after a move or rebrand.

## FAQ

### Do I need a paid tool to optimise my Business Profile?
No. Google's profile editor and a free review link cover the basics. Paid tools add tracking and automation.

### What is the cheapest paid option?
On 1 November 2026, Whitespark's Local Rank Tracker started at $14 a month, and Localo's Single Business plan at $39 a month billed annually. Features differ.

### Do these tools have free trials?
BrightLocal offers 14 days with no credit card. Localo offers 14 days without a card and a 30-day money-back guarantee.

### Does a tool improve my ranking?
The vendor pricing pages do not publish ranking results. Tools track and manage. The profile itself and your reviews do the work.

### Which BrightLocal plan includes review requests?
Only Grow, per BrightLocal's pricing page.
