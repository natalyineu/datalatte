The best food delivery platform for advertising in Europe is usually the one with the most active customers in your own city, not the biggest brand overall. Glovo is strongest in parts of Southern and Eastern Europe, Wolt in the Nordics, Germany and Central and Eastern Europe, Deliveroo in the UK, Ireland and parts of Western Europe, and Uber Eats across many large cities. Check which apps your customers already use, then compare commission, ad costs and order volume on that platform.

Market presence changes often because these companies expand, exit markets and merge, so confirm which platforms operate in your area before you commit. All fees, ad formats and features below are typical and should be verified in each platform's current merchant dashboard or with your account manager.

## Platform Overview

| Platform | Typically strongest in | Common ad and promo tools | Worth checking |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glovo | Spain, Italy, Portugal, parts of Eastern Europe and beyond | Promoted placements, discounts, limited-time offers, subscription-member offers | Availability in your city and current commission tiers |
| Wolt | Nordics, Germany, Baltics, Central and Eastern Europe | Boosted or sponsored placement, discounts, menu highlights | Local ad formats and whether bids are per click or per order |
| Uber Eats | Many large cities across the UK and Europe | Sponsored listings, promotions, featured placement | Markets where it operates, and ad pricing in your category |
| Deliveroo | UK, Ireland, France and other select markets | Promotions, sponsored visibility tools, offers for subscribers | Current ownership, product changes and local availability |

Platform details change quickly. Ask each platform's sales or support team what is currently available for your restaurant, cafe or bakery.

## How to Choose the Right Platform

Use these factors to compare platforms in your city:

1. **Customer overlap.** Ask your regulars which delivery app they use, or check your own order history and Google reviews.
2. **Commission and fees.** Platform commissions are commonly in the range of roughly 15 to 30 percent depending on country, plan and whether you use the platform's couriers. Confirm your exact rate.
3. **Delivery area.** Make sure the platform covers the neighborhoods where your best customers live.
4. **Ad options and minimums.** Compare cost models, minimum spend and reporting.
5. **Contract terms.** Check exclusivity, notice periods and promotion obligations.

<Callout type="tip">Read the merchant terms carefully before signing up. Some platforms limit when and how you can promote prices elsewhere, or tie promotions to commission tiers.</Callout>

## Cost per Click vs. Cost per Order

Delivery platforms sell visibility in a few different ways, and the model can change your math:

- **Cost per click (CPC):** you pay when someone taps your listing or ad, whether or not they order.
- **Cost per order (CPO or CPA):** you pay when an order is placed through the promoted placement.
- **Flat fee or percentage uplift:** you pay more commission or a fixed fee for featured placement.

Not every platform or market offers every model. To compare them, use the same simple formula:

**Ad cost per order = ad spend divided by orders attributed to the ad.**

Then compare that to your profit per order after food cost, packaging and commission. If your ad cost per order is higher than the profit you make on that order, the ad loses money on that sale, even if it brings new customers who may come back. Run a two-week test with a small budget on one platform, record spend and orders, and then decide.

## Free Ways to Win More Delivery Orders

Before you pay for ads, optimize what platforms already reward:

- **Photos:** use bright, clear photos for your best sellers.
- **Menu:** keep names clear and descriptions short, and hide items you cannot reliably prepare.
- **Ratings and reviews:** reply to reviews and fix recurring issues quickly.
- **Prep time and availability:** stay accurate on opening hours and avoid cancellations.
- **Offers:** use platform promotions, such as a small discount or free item on a minimum order, during quiet periods rather than all day.

## Measuring Success

Track these numbers weekly for each platform:

| Metric | How to calculate | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Ad cost per order | Ad spend divided by ad-attributed orders | Whether ads are profitable |
| Profit per order | Order value minus food, packaging, commission and discounts | Your margin ceiling for ad spend |
| Conversion rate | Orders divided by listing visits or clicks, if the platform reports it | Whether your menu and photos convert |
| Repeat orders | Customers who order again within 30 to 60 days | Whether ads are building loyalty |
| Share of paid vs. organic orders | Compare weeks with and without ads | Whether you are paying for orders you would get anyway |

<Callout type="warning">Track customer acquisition cost against lifetime value. If paid delivery orders never repeat and your margin is thin, shift effort to direct ordering and local search instead.</Callout>

## Reduce Dependence on Delivery Platforms

Commission-based platforms are useful for discovery, but they can squeeze margins. Many restaurants and cafes also:

- Build direct ordering on their own website and encourage repeat customers to use it.
- Keep their [Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile) complete so people can find them in Maps and search.
- Run local search ads with [Google Ads management](/services/google-ads) for people searching "order [food] near me".
- Use [local SEO](/services/local-seo) to rank for cuisine and neighborhood searches.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the best food delivery platform for a small restaurant in Europe?**
A: It depends on your city and customers. Check which apps dominate locally, compare commission rates, and run a short test on the platform with the most active customers in your area.

**Q: How much does food delivery advertising cost?**
A: Costs vary by platform, country and category. Besides ad spend, platforms charge commission on orders, often somewhere in the range of 15 to 30 percent, depending on the plan. Ask for your exact rate and ad pricing.

**Q: Can I use multiple food delivery platforms at once?**
A: Often yes, but check each contract for exclusivity clauses. Using several platforms can widen reach, but it adds menu management and fees, so track profit per platform separately.

**Q: Are sponsored placements on delivery apps worth it?**
A: They can be if your ad cost per order stays below your profit per order and customers come back. Test with a small budget for two weeks and compare to periods without ads.

**Q: How can I get more delivery orders without paying for ads?**
A: Improve photos, menu clarity, ratings and prep times, use targeted discounts in slow periods, and promote direct ordering to repeat customers.
