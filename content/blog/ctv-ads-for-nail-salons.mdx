A nail salon can run connected TV (CTV) ads through self-serve platforms such as Roku Ads Manager, which lets advertisers target by ZIP code. AdWave's July 2026 review puts the blended US CTV CPM at about $26, with most campaigns between $25 and $35. At $26, a $1,000 budget buys roughly 38,000 ad impressions.

No source we opened publishes nail-salon CTV results, so treat CTV as a test, not a proven channel.

CTV works best as awareness for a salon that already has a strong Google Business Profile and a way to book online. If those are missing, fix them first.

## How Much Do CTV Ads Cost for a Small Salon?

CPM means cost per 1,000 impressions. AdWave lists free ad-supported streaming at roughly $15 to $25, standard programmatic on premium services such as Hulu, Peacock and Prime Video at roughly $25 to $45, and addressable buys at $45 to $85. It calls these benchmarks, not rate cards.

Vibe estimates Roku at $20 to $60 per 1,000 impressions, but says Roku does not publish fixed rates. Vibe sells competing ad services, so confirm prices inside the platform before you plan. Our [CTV cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data) compares more sources.

## How Do You Target Local Clients?

Roku's help center says location targeting can use states, postal or ZIP codes, or designated market areas, and that you choose one geographic type per selection. It advises using residential ZIP codes only for ZIP targeting.

For a salon, list the ZIP codes within a short drive, then check each one against where your current clients live. Your booking software and Google Business Profile insights can show that. We did not find a source on the best age or gender targeting for nail services, so start broad within the area and review results.

## What Creative Do You Need?

You need a finished video, not a static image. Roku's and Amazon's requirements change, so read the current specs before you commission anything; our [CTV creative specs guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026) lists common ones. Keep the message plain: salon name, neighbourhood, one service and a booking link or QR code.

Production can cost more than the media for a small test. A phone-shot clip of real work, with permission from clients shown, is a cheap start. Avoid health or safety claims you cannot support.

## Is CTV Worth It? A Break-Even Check (Worked Example)

Every figure here is an **assumption** apart from the $26 CPM benchmark. A salon spends $1,000 on a one-month test.

| Item | Assumption | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | $1,000 | |
| CPM | $26 (AdWave blended) | about 38,500 impressions |
| Margin per new client over a year | $150 (assumption) | |

Seven new clients from about 38,500 impressions is the bar. You will not know the real number until you test. Ask every new client how they found you, and add a unique booking link or QR code on the ad.

## FAQ

### How much do CTV ads cost?

AdWave puts the 2026 blended US CPM at about $26, with a typical range of $25 to $35. Roku-specific prices are not published; Vibe estimates $20 to $60 per 1,000 impressions.

### Can a nail salon target only nearby ZIP codes?

On Roku, yes. The help center lists ZIP code targeting as one of three location types, with a note to use residential ZIP codes only.

### What is the minimum budget on Roku?

It is unclear. One source says Roku publishes no minimum and another mentions about $500 without a citation. Confirm in the platform.

### Does Amazon have a minimum for streaming TV ads?

Amazon's streaming TV page says there is no minimum spend requirement, and also lists a $10K recommended campaign minimum for self-service packages. Ask Amazon which applies to you.

### Should I try CTV before Google Ads?

Search and your Google Business Profile reach people already looking for a nail salon, so most salons set those up first. Add CTV when the basics convert.
