A service business should film the work itself: a before and after, a short answer to a question customers ask, and the people doing the job. Pew's 2026 survey says 31% of US adults use TikTok, and 45% of adults aged 30 to 49 do, so the audience includes homeowners and parents, not only teenagers. Use a business account, but note that TikTok limits those accounts to its Commercial Music Library when you add sounds.

This guide is for plumbers, cleaners, salons, groomers, trainers and similar local services. Reach and follower numbers for small accounts are not published by neutral sources, so none are given. Facts come from the sources at the end.

## Is TikTok Worth It for a Service Business?

It depends on who your customers are. Pew's survey puts YouTube at 84% and Facebook at 70% of US adults, ahead of Instagram at 51% and TikTok at 31%. Among adults 65 and older, TikTok use is 11% in the same survey. If your clients are mostly retirees, Facebook is a better first channel.

If your clients are 18 to 49, TikTok is worth a small test. Women use it more than men in the survey, 43% against 31%, which may matter for salons, groomers and cleaners. Judge it by bookings, not views. Our comparison of [TikTok and Instagram for local businesses](/blog/tiktok-vs-instagram-ads-local-business-2026) goes deeper.

## What Behind-the-Scenes Videos Work for Services?

Pick formats that show the result and the process. Five that fit most trades:

1. **Before and after.** A dirty oven, a messy lawn, a haircut, in under 20 seconds.
2. **One question, one answer.** "Why does my drain smell?" answered in 30 seconds.
3. **A day of the job.** Arrive, assess, finish, no voiceover needed.
4. **Tools and tips.** What you use and why, hands only if you prefer not to show your face.
5. **Pricing explained.** What affects the quote, without naming a number you cannot keep.

No neutral study ranks these formats, so test them. Our [TikTok content ideas for local business](/blog/tiktok-content-ideas-for-local-business) has more prompts.

## Which Sounds Can a Business Account Use?

TikTok's help says that on a Business Account, when you tap Add Sound you will only see sounds from the Commercial Music Library, called Commercial Sounds. Trending pop tracks may not appear for you.

Avoid the problem by speaking on camera, using plain captions, or choosing a Commercial Sound. Do not download a track from another source. If you want the full setup, see our [TikTok for Business account guide](/blog/tiktok-for-business-account-guide).

## What Do You Need to Disclose?

Filming a customer's home or pet needs their permission, and customer reviews you repost should be real. The FTC's endorsement guidance says free products require disclosure however they were provided, and that a discount tied to a review should be disclosed by the reviewer.

It also says "Gifted" alone is ambiguous, and that disclosures should be in the video itself, not only in the description. Tying a discount to positive reviews is risky, since it can bias them. Invite all customers to review honestly. This is not legal advice.

## How Often Should You Post?

No neutral source gives a best frequency for a service business. A schedule you can keep matters more than a high number. Film several clips in one visit, edit them together later, and post on fixed days. Track what each video brings: calls, messages, booking-form hits.

## The Math: What Does a Weekly Routine Cost?

All inputs are **assumptions**, not benchmarks.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Filming time per week | 1 hour (3 clips) |
| Editing and posting per week | 1 hour |
| Your hourly value | $40 |
| Weekly cost | 2 x $40 = $80 |
| Monthly cost (4 weeks) | $320 |
| Average job value | $150 |
| Jobs needed to break even | $320 / $150 = about 2.1, so 3 jobs |

If three new jobs a month come from TikTok, the routine pays under these inputs. If you have none after three months, stop and move the time to Google Business Profile reviews. Check how customers found you on every booking.

## FAQ

### Do I need to show my face on TikTok?
No. Hands, tools and results can carry a video. Voiceover or captions explain it.

### Can a business account use any song?
No. TikTok's help says Business Accounts see only Commercial Music Library sounds when adding sound.

### Who uses TikTok in the US?
Pew's 2026 survey: 31% of adults, 45% of ages 30 to 49 and 11% of ages 65 and over.

### Do I need to disclose free services given for a video?
Yes, per the FTC's endorsement guidance, free products or services need disclosure, ideally in the video itself.

### Is TikTok better than Facebook for services?
Not by reach. Pew's 2026 data shows Facebook at 70% of adults against 31% for TikTok. Choose by your customers' age.
