To retain pet grooming clients, book the next appointment before they leave, send a reminder timed to the coat's interval, and track how long each client goes between visits. Published guidance puts professional grooming at roughly every 4 to 12 weeks depending on coat. A client who moves from a 12-week gap to a 7-week gap adds about three visits a year.

This guide gives a retention routine you can run with a notebook or booking software, the rules on texts, emails and reviews, and a worked example. No client results are quoted, because none could be verified. Prices in the example are assumptions.

## How Often Do Dogs Need Professional Grooming?

There is no single interval. Woofie's, a pet-services franchise, lists every 8 to 12 weeks for double-coated dogs, every 4 to 6 weeks for long or silky coats, and every 4 to 8 weeks for curly or wiry coats. For short coats it says professional grooming is not typically required for haircuts. This is secondary guidance from a company that sells grooming, so confirm with your own breed notes.

Use it to set each client's rebooking window by coat type. A poodle mix due at six weeks should not get the same reminder date as a Labrador due at ten.

## Why Does Rebooking at Checkout Work?

The client is standing at the counter, the dog looks good, and the next appointment is a calendar entry rather than a decision. You do not need a statistic to see that this removes the step where clients forget. It also lets you fill your calendar before it is empty.

Give a specific date and a fallback: "Max will be due around the 14th. I have Tuesday at 10 or Thursday at 2." If they decline, record their preferred day and time in the client profile, so your reminder offers a slot they would take.

Track the share of clients who rebook at checkout every month. It is the single easiest retention number to measure.

## What Reminder Schedule Should You Use?

Build the schedule from each dog's interval. A simple pattern, for a client due at six weeks:

- **Week 4:** a short text or email, "Max is due in about two weeks, here are openings"
- **Week 6:** a due-date message with two slots
- **Week 8:** a last nudge, then pause for a few weeks

Send from your own business name so the message looks personal. Marketing texts need the customer's consent, and the US rules on texting have changed recently, so check the FCC's current requirements or ask your booking software provider before you start. Email is covered by the FTC's CAN-SPAM guide: accurate headers, a clear ad label if it is promotional, a postal address and a working opt-out honoured within 10 business days. ## What Should a Client Profile Hold?

Keep it short and useful, so staff will fill it in at the till.

- Dog name, breed, coat type and interval
- Last groom date and next due date
- Preferred day and time
- Handling notes, such as nervous with the dryer
- Contact preference and consent date

Booking software can store this and send reminders. See our guide to [AI scheduling for pet groomers](/blog/ai-scheduling-for-pet-groomers) and [marketing automation for pet groomers](/blog/marketing-automation-for-pet-groomers). A notebook works until you have around 100 active clients.

## How Do Reviews Help Retention?

Reviews bring new clients and let current ones feel good about recommending you. Google Maps policy bars offering payment, discounts, free goods or services for posting, revising or removing a review, and bars soliciting only positive reviews. You may ask customers for genuine reviews.

So ask everyone, reply to each review and skip the "free nail trim for a five-star review" offer. Keep your profile current too: see [Google Business Profile for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-for-pet-groomers).

## FAQ

### How often should I tell clients to come back?

Set it by coat. Franchise guidance puts long and silky coats at 4 to 6 weeks, curly or wiry coats at 4 to 8 weeks and double coats at 8 to 12 weeks. Confirm with your own experience.

### Is text or email better for reminders?

The sources checked do not give a verified comparison. Use the channel each client chose, and log their consent.

### Can I give a discount for a Google review?

No. Google Maps policy prohibits offering discounts, free goods or services for reviews.

### How do I measure retention?

Track the share who rebook at checkout and the average weeks between visits. Both come from your appointment records.

### How many pet owners are there?

APPA reports about 95 million US households own a pet, and about 71 million own a dog.
