The best Google Ads keywords for plumbers are urgent, local, job-specific searches such as "emergency plumber near me", "water heater replacement" and "clogged drain repair", set to phrase or exact match with a long negative list. No source publishes a plumber-only click price. The nearest benchmark, LocaliQ's 2026 Home and Home Improvement average, is $8.33 per click and $90.92 per lead.

This guide groups keywords by job value, lists negatives and explains when Local Services Ads may suit a plumber better than Search ads. Keyword examples are starting ideas to check in Keyword Planner, not search-volume claims. Budget examples are labelled as assumptions.

## Which Keyword Groups Should a Plumber Start With?

Separate keywords by how urgent and how profitable the job is, because they need different budgets and landing pages.

| Group | Example keywords | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency | "emergency plumber near me", "burst pipe repair", "24 hour plumber" | High urgency, call-ready |
| Common repair | "leaking faucet repair", "clogged drain", "toilet running" | Steady demand, smaller jobs |
| Big ticket | "water heater replacement", "tankless water heater install", "repipe house" | Larger invoices |
| Inspection | "sewer line camera inspection" | Planned jobs, less price pressure |
| Brand | [your company name] | Protects your name |

Add your city or service area to these in a second pass, for example "emergency plumber" plus the city. Check each term's estimated bid in Keyword Planner. Google defines the estimated top of page bid as the bid you likely need to appear among the top ads, based on Quality Score and competition, and says it is not guaranteed.

## Which Match Types Work Best?

Google's help says broad match is the default and the most comprehensive, phrase match covers searches that include your keyword's meaning, and exact match gives the most control but reaches the fewest searches. It also says it is critical to use Smart Bidding with broad match.

With a small budget, start on phrase and exact for your emergency and big-ticket groups. Move to broad match only when you have conversion data and use Smart Bidding. This approach is a recommendation based on Google's descriptions, not a published rule.

## What Negative Keywords Should Plumbers Add?

Negative keywords stop your ads showing for searches you do not want. Google says negatives do not match close variants, so add plural and singular forms yourself.

Start with these and add more from your search terms report each week:

- Job seekers: `jobs`, `hiring`, `salary`, `apprentice`, `career`
- Training: `course`, `school`, `license exam`, `certification`
- Do-it-yourself and parts: `diy`, `how to`, `parts`, `supplies`, `wholesale`
- Free and cheap: `free`, `cheap` if you do not compete on price
- Services you do not offer, such as `pool`, `septic` or `commercial` if that applies

Review the search terms report weekly. Block anything that does not match a job you would take.

## Are Local Services Ads Better Than Search Ads?

Possibly. Google's help says Local Services Ads charge for each valid lead, with prices varying by location, job type, lead type and bidding mode. Invalid or low-quality leads are not charged, and repeat contact from the same number within 15 days is not charged again.

Plumbers face extra screening, including business and owner background checks, insurance and licensing where required by state law, and a verified Google Business Profile. Google also says Local Services Ads are moving into Google Ads as Performance Max campaigns with pay-per-lead goals, starting in August 2026 for select US home service advertisers such as plumbing, HVAC and roofing. Check which version your account has before you budget.

## The Math: What Could $1,500 Do?

Inputs from LocaliQ are the $8.33 click and 8.05% conversion rate. Everything else is an **assumption**.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget (assumption) | $1,500 |
| Cost per click | $8.33 |
| Clicks | about 180 |
| Conversion rate (call or form) | 8.05% |
| Leads | about 14.5 |
| Cost per lead | about $103 |
| Share of leads who book a job (assumption) | 50% |
| Jobs | about 7.2 |
| Cost per job | about $207 |
| Average invoice (assumption) | $350 |

The calculated $103 per lead differs from LocaliQ's $90.92 average because the two figures are averaged differently. At a $350 invoice, a $207 ad cost per job leaves little margin unless the customer returns or the job is a water heater replacement. This is why many plumbers bid harder on big-ticket groups and keep cheap repair terms tight.

## FAQ

### What is the average CPC for plumbers on Google Ads?

No plumber-specific figure was found. LocaliQ's 2026 Home and Home Improvement average is $8.33 per click, the nearest category. Keyword Planner shows estimates for your own terms.

### Should plumbers use broad match?

Only with conversion data and Smart Bidding. Google says Smart Bidding is critical with broad match. Start with phrase and exact.

### How much should a plumber spend on Google Ads?

No standard figure exists. Work backwards from jobs wanted, booking rate and cost per lead, as in the example above.

### Is Local Services Ads cheaper than Search?

Not necessarily. Lead prices vary by location and job type, and no national plumber price is published. Test both against cost per job.
