Midjourney has four paid plans: Basic at $10 a month, Standard at $30, Pro at $60 and Mega at $120. Billed yearly they cost $8, $24, $48 and $96 a month. Standard, Pro and Mega include 15, 30 and 60 Fast GPU hours. Basic is limited to about 200 image jobs a month, and only Pro and Mega include Stealth mode, which keeps your images out of public view.

Prices and features checked on 7 November 2026.

This guide is for owners and marketers of cafés, salons, grooming studios and gyms who want AI-made visuals for social posts, menus or packaging ideas. It covers plans, what "GPU time" means, privacy and licence terms. Prices come from Midjourney's pricing page; its help pages returned an error, which is stated where it matters.

The pricing page lists four plans. Yearly prices are per month, billed once a year.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $10 | $8 | Not listed, about 200 image jobs | No | No | 3 / 1 | SD only |
| Standard | $30 | $24 | 15 hours | Unlimited images | No | 3 / 3 | SD and HD |
| Pro | $60 | $48 | 30 hours | Unlimited images and SD video | Yes | 12 / 6 | SD and HD |

## What Is GPU Time and What Is Relax Mode?

Midjourney charges in GPU time, not in a count of pictures. The FAQ on the pricing page says: "Each Image Prompt usually consumes about one minute of GPU time, and video prompts consume about eight minutes."

Fast hours are the quick lane. Relax mode, offered from Standard, queues jobs behind others and does not use Fast hours. Standard and up list unlimited Relax images, and Pro and Mega add unlimited SD video in Relax.

Using the vendor's rough figures, 15 Fast hours is 900 minutes. That is about 900 image prompts or about 112 video prompts, if every job takes the stated average. This is an assumption derived from "usually about", so real counts vary with settings.

## What Is Midjourney and How Does It Work?

Midjourney generates images from text descriptions, and on paid plans also short videos. You describe a scene, get four images back, and then vary or upscale one. Its pricing page also offers an Editor for images you upload. Members work on midjourney.com; the pages opened did not state other platforms, so check the product before buying.

For a local business the main skill is prompt writing and picking a consistent style. Output is a picture, not a finished ad: text such as prices and opening hours is better added in a design tool afterwards. ## What Are the Rules and Risks?

From Midjourney's pricing page FAQ:

- **Public by default.** "By default every image and video you create can be found on the Explore page." Stealth mode "keeps your images and videos hidden from other members on the website" and is available only on Pro and Mega.
- **Ownership.** "You own all the images and videos you create, even if you decide to cancel your subscription."
- **Commercial use.** "Feel free to use them commercially, with a few exceptions." The page links to the Terms of Service for general commercial terms.

The Terms of Service and help articles on docs.midjourney.com returned a 403 error when opened, so exceptions cannot be listed from the source. A search summary of those pages mentioned a Pro or Mega requirement for larger companies and a broad licence that Midjourney receives over content users create. Treat both as unconfirmed: read the Terms yourself, especially if your business is large. No revenue figure is given here.

Other risks:

- **Private ideas.** Do not make unreleased packaging or a confidential rebrand on a plan with public images.
- **Likeness.** Do not generate real people without their permission.
- **Claims.** A picture of a perfect cut or a finished dish can mislead customers.
- **Credit sizing.** Video uses about eight minutes of GPU time per prompt, so it drains Fast hours faster than images.

## FAQ

### How much does Midjourney cost per month?

Basic is $10, Standard is $30, Pro is $60 and Mega is $120 on monthly billing. Yearly billing brings them to $8, $24, $48 and $96 a month.

### Does Midjourney have a free plan?

The pricing page lists no free plan and does not mention a trial. The cheapest option is Basic at $10 a month, limited to about 200 image jobs.

### What are Midjourney Fast hours?

Fast hours are GPU time at full speed. The vendor says one image prompt uses about a minute and a video prompt about eight. Standard has 15 hours, Pro 30 and Mega 60.

### Can I use Midjourney images commercially?

The pricing page says you may use them commercially, with a few exceptions, and that you own what you create. The exceptions are in the Terms of Service, which did not open here, so read them first.

### Are Midjourney images private?

Not by default. Images and videos can appear on the Explore page. Stealth mode hides them from other members and is available only on Pro and Mega.
