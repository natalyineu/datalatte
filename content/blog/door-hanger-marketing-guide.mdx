Door hanger marketing means leaving a printed offer on the front doors of homes near a business. Vendor-published figures put printing at $0.08 to $0.25 per piece, and the Data and Marketing Association reports a 4.4% response rate for direct mail, a broader channel than door hangers. Treat both as starting points. Measure your own drop with a unique code for each batch, and plan around the low end of the response range until your own results say otherwise.

## Why Do Door Hangers Get Noticed?

A door hanger has to be taken off the doorknob before anyone can use the house, so the recipient almost always sees it. Master Printing and Mailing says this physical placement is hard to ignore in a way that email and a postcard in a stack of mail are not. It lists four advantages: guaranteed visibility, hyper-targeted local exposure, high tactile impact and low cost compared with postage-paid direct mail ([Master Printing and Mailing](https://masterprinting.ink/door-hanger-marketing-guide/), secondary).

Those advantages only matter if the hanger is read. Universal Mailworks notes that most people only glance at a door hanger, so every word has to earn its place ([Universal Mailworks](https://www.universalmailworks.com/blog/door-hanger-marketing-design-costs-distribution), secondary). Use one clear image, a headline that states a benefit, and a single call to action. A headline such as "Enjoy a Pest-Free Summer" works better than a company name alone.

Size is another lever. Power Direct notes that most door hanger designers use a 3.5 by 8.5 inch format, and that its own standard size is 5.5 by 17 inches, which it describes as mini-billboards that are harder to overlook ([Power Direct](https://powerdirect.net/door-hanger-marketing-blueprint/), secondary). A larger format costs more to print, so test it against the standard size on one street before committing a full drop.

Print quality also shapes the message. Universal Mailworks recommends a stock that feels substantial, because flimsy hangers blow away and look cheap. For outdoor drops, it suggests UV or aqueous coating for weather resistance.

## How Much Does a Door Hanger Campaign Cost?

Universal Mailworks gives a printing range of $0.08 to $0.25 per piece and a price table that falls as quantity rises. For 1,000 pieces on 14pt C2S with four-colour printing it lists $0.25 to $0.40 per piece, or $250 to $400 in total. At 10,000 pieces the range drops to $0.08 to $0.15 ([Universal Mailworks](https://www.universalmailworks.com/blog/door-hanger-marketing-design-costs-distribution), secondary).

Printing is only part of the budget. Distribution labour is the other cost, whether it is staff, family members or a paid distributor. Master Printing and Mailing describes door hangers as budget-friendly to print in bulk and as avoiding postage rates, but its page does not give a per-door distribution price ([Master Printing and Mailing](https://masterprinting.ink/door-hanger-marketing-guide/), secondary). Get two quotes that include delivery to your street before committing.

## What Response Can You Expect?

The clearest published figure is the DMA's 4.4% response rate for direct mail, set against 0.12% for email. Universal Mailworks cites it from its 2023 data ([Universal Mailworks](https://www.universalmailworks.com/blog/door-hanger-marketing-design-costs-distribution), secondary). That figure covers direct mail in general and is not a door hanger benchmark, so it should not be treated as a promise for any one street.

Other vendor sources give higher and lower numbers without a clear method. Power Direct says front door placement reaches almost every person who finds something on the porch, but it does not publish a response rate ([Power Direct](https://powerdirect.net/door-hanger-marketing-blueprint/), secondary). That is a claim about exposure, not sales.

Timing and offer change results more than the paper. Master Printing and Mailing suggests matching distribution to a need, such as lawn care before spring or takeaway food later in the week ([Master Printing and Mailing](https://masterprinting.ink/door-hanger-marketing-guide/), secondary). Its example calls for a clear offer, such as "Scan this QR code for an instant 20% discount on your first order," which gives the recipient a specific next step.

## How Do You Track Door Hanger Results?

Without tracking, a business will credit a door hanger with sales it did not make. Each batch needs its own way to be counted.

1. Print a different offer word on each street or batch, and ask customers to say it at the till.
2. Use a QR code that goes to a page only that batch uses, so the visit can be traced.
3. Record which street and batch each new customer came from in the sales or booking tool.
4. Compare revenue from each batch against its printing and distribution cost, not against total sales.

Service businesses that already track jobs can add the batch name to the lead source field. ## What Does a 1,000-Door Drop Look Like?

Every input below is an **assumption**, not a result. Printing uses the low end of the Universal Mailworks range for 1,000 pieces, and distribution is assumed to be included in the total.

| Input (assumption) | Low case | High case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hangers delivered | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| Total campaign cost | $250 | $250 |
| Response rate | 1% | 3% |
| Responses (new customers) | 10 | 30 |
| Cost per response | $25 | about $8.33 |
| Average first-visit spend | $20 | $20 |
| First-visit revenue | $200 | $600 |

At 1%, first-visit revenue of $200 falls short of the $250 cost, so the drop pays only if those customers return. At 3%, first-visit revenue of $600 covers the cost with $350 to spare, assuming the spend figure holds.

The unknown is repeat visits. Track how often a new customer returns, using till or booking records. Put a repeat-visit offer on the hanger, such as a discount on a second booking, so that return can be measured directly.

## When Are Door Hangers the Wrong Channel?

Door hangers suit businesses whose customers live close by and respond to a visible offer, such as a salon, a pizza shop or a home cleaning service. They are weak where customers travel from across a city, or where a sale depends on a long consideration period. If a 1% response would not be profitable, test a smaller drop on two streets before scaling.

Check the local rules on leaflets and building access before distribution, particularly in apartment blocks and gated estates where managers may need to approve delivery.

## FAQ

### How much do door hangers cost?

Universal Mailworks gives a printing range of $0.08 to $0.25 per piece, and $250 to $400 for 1,000 pieces at the 14pt C2S tier. Distribution is an extra cost, so get quotes that include delivery.

### What response rate do door hangers get?

No door hanger-specific study was found in the sources. The closest figure is the DMA's 4.4% for direct mail, cited by Universal Mailworks. The offer and street choice will decide the actual rate.

### Should door hangers go in mailboxes?

No. Hang them on the door handle, not in the mailbox, and check local postal and city rules before distribution.

### How do I know if a door hanger worked?

Give each batch a different offer word or QR destination, and ask customers at the till or in booking forms where they heard about the business.

### Do door hangers work for coffee shops and salons?

They can, if customers live nearby. Run one small test drop and compare the revenue from it against a normal week.
