Door hanger marketing means leaving a printed offer on the doors of homes near your business. Printing and distribution vendors report response rates of about 1% to 5% and an all-in cost of $0.10 to $0.40 per household. Those numbers come from companies that sell the service, with no named study behind the range, so treat them as an upper-end guide and measure your own drop.

This guide covers costs, the one legal point to check, tracking with codes and UTM links, and a 1,000-door example. No case results are quoted, because none could be verified. Inputs in the example are labelled as assumptions.

## How Much Does Door Hanger Marketing Cost?

4OVER4, a printer that sells door hangers, puts printing at $0.05 to $0.25 per piece and distribution at $0.05 to $0.15 per door. That makes $100 to $400 for a 1,000-piece drop. The same page lists its own door hangers from $290.41 for 250, which is far above its generic range, so get real quotes before you budget.

Vistaprint offers door hangers in 3.5 by 8.5 inch and 4.5 by 11 inch sizes with matte, glossy and plastic options, and optional perforated tear-off tabs. Its page does not state a clear unit price in the text. Distribution is the other cost: you can hang them yourself, use staff, or hire a distributor. Direct to Door Marketing says its pricing depends on coverage area and quantity and gives no per-piece figure.

## What Response Rate Can You Expect?

The 1% to 5% range appears on 4OVER4's page as an industry benchmark without a named study. The same page cites the Data and Marketing Association for 2.7% to 4.4% on direct mail to house lists, which could not be checked against the DMA itself.

Direct to Door Marketing says strong offers generate 2 to 4 times higher response and warns that its published case studies represent exceptional results, not typical outcomes. It gives timing differences too, such as a gym in January against July at 3 times. Both vendors agree on the main point: the offer and the targeting move the result more than the paper.

Plan for the low end. If a 1% response is not profitable for you, a door hanger is probably not your channel.

## Is It Legal to Hang Flyers on Doors?

Door hangers go on the door, not in the mailbox. Federal law, 18 U.S.C. 1725, prohibits knowingly depositing mailable matter with no postage paid in a mailbox approved by the Postal Service, with intent to avoid paying postage. Each offence carries a fine. So never put hangers inside a mailbox or on its flag.

Cities and housing associations may add their own rules on soliciting, leafleting and gated buildings. No national source covers these, so check your city's rules and ask building managers before you drop in apartments.

## How Do You Track Door Hanger Results?

Give every batch a way to be counted. Without it you will credit the door hanger for sales it did not make.

- **Offer code per batch:** print a different word for each street or batch, and ask each customer at the till.
- **Dedicated phone number:** only if you will keep it long enough to be worth printing.
- **QR code to a landing page:** Google Analytics asks for utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign on a tagged link. For example, utm_source=doorhanger, utm_medium=print, utm_campaign=spring-offer. Values are case sensitive, so keep them lowercase.

## What Does a 1,000-Door Drop Look Like?

All inputs are **assumptions**, not results. Costs come from the middle of the vendor ranges.

| Input | Low case | High case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hangers delivered | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| Cost per household | $0.25 | $0.25 |
| Campaign cost | $250 | $250 |
| Response rate | 1% | 3% |
| Responses | 10 | 30 |
| Cost per response | $25 | about $8.33 |
| First-visit spend per customer | $20 | $20 |
| First-visit revenue | $200 | $600 |

At 1%, the first visit does not cover the cost, so the drop only pays if those customers come back. At 3% it covers the cost on the first visit. The number to find out is how often a new customer returns for your business, from your own till records. Put a repeat-visit offer on the hanger to test it.

## When Do Door Hangers Beat Digital Ads?

They suit businesses whose customers live within a short distance and who answer to a visible offer, such as a pizza place, a groomer or a cleaning service. They are weak where customers come from across a city. ## FAQ

### How much do door hangers cost?

Vendors report $0.10 to $0.40 per household all in, or about $100 to $400 per 1,000. Quotes vary by size, stock and distribution, so get two before you decide.

### What response rate do door hangers get?

Vendors cite 1% to 5%, with no named study. Your offer and your street selection will decide where you land.

### Can I put door hangers in mailboxes?

No. Federal law prohibits unstamped mailable matter in Postal Service mailboxes. Hang them on the door and check local rules.

### How do I know if a door hanger worked?

Use a different code or QR link for each batch, and ask customers at the till.

### Do door hangers work for coffee shops and salons?

They can, if the customers live near you. Test one small drop and compare it to your normal week.
