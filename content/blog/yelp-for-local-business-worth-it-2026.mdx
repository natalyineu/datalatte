Category decides most of the answer. Restaurants, home services and personal services are browsed on Yelp with buying intent in many US metros. Retail and professional services usually see more discovery through Google. The listing should be complete either way, because an abandoned profile with old hours actively loses customers.

## When does Yelp matter for a local business?

Yelp matters where its app and search presence are strong and the purchase is review-led: a first visit to a restaurant, a plumber during an emergency, a salon before a wedding. In those moments a complete profile with recent photos and answered reviews converts research into calls.

Check two signals before paying. First, search the business category plus suburb on Yelp and see whether competitors buy placement. Second, ask new customers how they found the business for a month and count Yelp mentions honestly. Both checks are free and take less time than a sales call.

Google usually deserves the first hour regardless. The profile there drives Maps discovery for most local trades, and the review workflow overlaps. Reputation work on the [Google Business Profile service](/services/google-business-profile) page covers that foundation, and Yelp should mirror whatever it establishes.

## What should a business do on Yelp for free?

Claim the listing, then complete every field the profile allows: hours including holidays, services, attributes, and recent photos of real work. Yelp's recommendation software surfaces reviews by its own rules; complete profiles give it and readers more to work with.

Answer reviews without arguing. A short, factual reply that names the fix and invites direct contact reads well to the next hundred readers, which is who the reply is for. Never offer incentives for reviews and never write or buy them; Yelp's policies prohibit soliciting reviews, per its business guidance checked in November 2026. [AI-powered local SEO tools](/blog/ai-powered-local-seo-tools) covers review drafting help with the same human gate.

**Assumption example.** Assume a home-services firm completes its free profile in 2 hours valued at $40 an hour, or $80 of time. If the finished profile produces one extra $250 job a quarter that the owner can attribute through "how did you find us", the free work pays for itself many times over. The job figure is an assumption; the attribution habit is the actual system.

## When are Yelp Ads worth testing?

Test ads when the category search on Yelp shows competitors advertising, the ticket value is high enough to absorb auction clicks, and the profile already converts profile views into calls. A weak profile advertised harder mostly buys more people seeing the weakness.

Set a small, fixed test budget and judge on calls and direction requests tracked in Yelp's dashboard plus the business's own booking notes. Compare cost per job with [Google ads and Meta routes](/blog/using-meta-ads-for-hair-salon-marketing) for neighbouring trades; the cheapest click is rarely the cheapest customer. End the test on schedule if the numbers do not clear the alternative.

Businesses whose customers mention Yelp unprompted can reasonably scale what worked. Businesses guessing should stay free. That asymmetry (free completeness always, paid reach only on evidence) is the whole 2026 answer for most single-site firms.

## FAQ

### Is Yelp free for businesses?

Claiming and completing a listing is free on Yelp for Business, checked in November 2026. Paid products such as Yelp Ads and profile upgrades are optional and auction- or package-priced without a published CPC rate card. A business can get most of Yelp's discovery value from the free, complete profile and answered reviews.

### Should a business ask customers for Yelp reviews?

Yelp's own guidance says not to solicit reviews, and its recommendation software filters accordingly. The safe route is neutral: make the service good, make the profile easy to find, and answer every review that arrives. Incentivised reviews risk filtering and penalties, and readers increasingly recognise the pattern.

### Yelp or Google first for reviews?

Google first for most local trades, because Maps and Search drive the majority of discovery and the same response discipline applies. Yelp second, with the profile kept accurate and current. Businesses in Yelp-strong categories and metros, especially restaurants and home services, should maintain both from the start.

### How much do Yelp Ads cost?

Yelp Ads are auction-priced with advertiser-set budgets and no published rate card, on Yelp's advertising pages checked in November 2026. Exact costs depend on category and metro competition, so treat third-party average CPC figures as estimates. Test with a capped budget and judge on tracked calls against the cost of the same job from Google or Meta.
