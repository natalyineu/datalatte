## How Much Does Google Ads Cost a Florist?

No source we opened gives a florist-specific cost per click. LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks list Shopping, Collectibles and Gifts at $4.14 per click, 8.28% click-through rate, 4.01% conversion rate and $49.40 per lead. The all-industry average is $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead.

Gift shopping is the closest match, but flower orders are more date-driven than most gifts. Treat these figures as a starting range and replace them with your own account data after the first month.

## How Big Are the Valentine's Day and Mother's Day Peaks?

The National Retail Federation (NRF) forecast record spending for both. Its January 2026 survey of 7,791 US adults, run January 2 to 8, projected $29.1 billion for Valentine's Day, including $3.1 billion on flowers, with 41% of shoppers planning to buy flowers.

For Mother's Day, NRF's April 2026 survey of 7,877 adults projected about $38 billion in total and $3.2 billion on flowers. About 75% of shoppers planned to buy flowers, and they planned to spend an average of $284.25 on gifts. These are US survey forecasts, not your sales.

## Which Keywords Should a Florist Start With?

Start with searches from people who want flowers delivered or collected. Examples: "florist near me", "flower delivery" plus your city, "same day flowers" plus your city, and "wedding florist" plus your city. Wedding clients search weeks or months ahead, so keep them in a separate campaign with its own budget.

Our [florist keyword guide](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-florists-in-2026) lists more terms by intent. Check the Search terms report weekly and add negatives for jobs, courses and plants you do not sell.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local and Block Junk Clicks?

Google says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km, and that very small radiuses can make ads show only intermittently or not at all. It recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting for Search, while "Presence" suits advertisers who want only people physically in the area.

For negative keywords, Google states they do not match close variants. Excluding "flowers" does not block "red flower", so add singular, plural and synonym forms yourself. Too many negatives can reduce reach, so review the list rather than only adding to it.

## Should You Bid on FTD or 1-800-Flowers?

You can. Google's trademark policy says Google Ads will not restrict the use of trademarks as keywords. It does restrict using a direct competitor's trademark in the ad text, and ads that use a trademark in a confusing or misleading way.

So you may target a competitor's name as a keyword, but your ad should not contain their name. Competitor searches come from people who already chose a brand, so no source says they convert well. Test with a small budget and keep them in their own ad group.

## How Should You Set Up Seasonal Budgets?

Google's average daily budget can spend up to two times your daily budget on one day, with a monthly cap of 30.4 times the daily budget for most campaigns. Plan for that around peak days.

Google's seasonality adjustments tell Smart Bidding about an expected change in conversion rate, and are meant for short events of 1 to 7 days. They work with Target CPA or Target ROAS on Search. Performance Max supports any bid strategy. Google warns they may work less well beyond 14 days.

## Should You Track Phone Calls?

Yes, if customers ring to order. Google says clicks on a call asset cost the same as headline clicks. For call tracking you set a minimum call length, and only calls at least that long count as conversions. Forwarding numbers are available only in certain countries.

Pair that with a note at the till: ask phone and walk-in customers how they found you. Keep a simple tally of orders from "Google" each week.

## FAQ

### How much should a florist spend on Google Ads?

No standard figure is published. At $4.14 per click, a $450 month buys roughly 109 clicks. Start with a test month, measure conversion rate, then raise the budget for Valentine's Day and Mother's Day.

### Is it better to run ads year-round or only at holidays?

NRF's forecasts show large spikes at Valentine's Day and Mother's Day, but no source we opened gives florists' ad results by month. A small always-on campaign plus a short seasonal boost lets you compare both with your own data.

### Can I bid on a competitor's name?

Google allows trademarks as keywords, but restricts a direct competitor's trademark in your ad text. Keep their name out of the ad itself.

### Do calls count as conversions?

They can. You set a minimum call length, and calls at least that long are counted. Forwarding numbers are available only in some countries.

### Do I need a Google Business Profile?

It is not required to run ads, but it supports them. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-in-2026-the-complete-guide-25-point-checklist) covers hours, photos and categories.
