## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Pet Groomer?

The AdTurbo page gives dog groomer clicks of about $2–$8 and warns that a few wasted clicks add up fast. It also reports that most advertisers spend around $800–$5,000 a month. That is a wide range, so treat your first month as a test rather than a forecast.

Seasonality matters too. Groomer to Groomer, quoted on Adwave's pet grooming advertising page, notes that November and December are traditionally a very busy time for groomers, sometimes too busy. Plan extra budget for those months only if your diary can absorb the extra bookings.

If you are comparing paid search with a pay-per-lead model, read the [Google Local Services Ads guide for pet groomers](/blog/google-local-services-ads-for-pet-groomers) before committing either budget.

## How Should a Memphis Groomer Set the Target Area?

Memphis has a city limit and a wider metro area, and customers do not stop at city boundaries. Targeting only the city name can cut off nearby clients who live a short drive away, while still paying for clicks from people on the far side of town.

A radius gives you more control. A Barklead guide written about Facebook ads suggests targeting users within a 10–15 mile radius. Use that as a rough starting point for Google too, then narrow or widen based on where bookings actually come from. Check Google's current radius rules on its live help pages before you set one.

Mobile groomers should centre the radius on the area they cover each week, not on a home base. Marketing 360's 2023 mobile groomer case used Facebook and Google together, so consider whether a second channel fits your model.

## Which Keywords Should a Memphis Groomer Use?

Start with terms that show a booking need. The AdTurbo page lists "dog groomer near me," "dog grooming near me," and "puppy grooming near me" as keywords worth bidding on. Test those with Memphis added, and compare cost per booking against location targeting alone to see which setup performs better.

Negative keywords matter just as much. Groomer Genie, a vendor, says its proprietary negative keyword list contains more than 500 groomer negatives. A ready-made list helps, but review your own search terms report every week. Searches from people looking for jobs, courses or free information rarely lead to a booking, so block them as they appear.

## What Should Count as a Conversion?

AdTurbo names booking a grooming appointment as the primary conversion to track. Make that the main goal in your account. Phone calls and form submissions can support it as secondary signals, but judge the campaign by bookings, not by clicks.

Set up call tracking and check the live Google Ads help pages for how short calls are counted, so wrong numbers do not inflate your results. For online booking forms, mark the confirmation page as the conversion so that every completed request is counted once.

Record which keyword produced each booking, not only the click. A monthly report that joins ad data with your booking records shows this clearly. The [AI-driven local SEO reports guide](/blog/ai-driven-local-seo-reports-with-google-data-studio) describes one way to build that kind of report.

## FAQ

### How much does Google Ads cost for a pet groomer in Memphis?

No Memphis average is published here. The AdTurbo benchmark for dog groomer clicks is about $2–$8, and most advertisers spend around $800–$5,000 a month. Your own account data is the best local figure.

### What budget should a Memphis groomer start with?

Choose a budget you can sustain long enough to see bookings, usually at least a month. The worked example uses $600 as an assumption, which buys about 120 clicks at an assumed $5 cost per click.

### Should I target the whole city or a radius?

A radius around the salon gives you more control over where ads appear. A Barklead guide suggests 10–15 miles for Facebook ads, which is a reasonable starting point for Google too. Adjust it based on where bookings come from.

### How do I track bookings?

Make the booking appointment your primary conversion, then add call tracking and form submissions as supporting data. Check the live Google Ads help pages for current settings on short calls.
