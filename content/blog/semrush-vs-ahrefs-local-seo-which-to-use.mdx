For a single-location local business, Semrush is usually the better fit because it sells local tools by location: Local Base is $30 a month per location and Local Pro is $60, with map ranking heatmaps, listings and review tools. Ahrefs is stronger for backlinks and content research, and starts at $129 a month on Lite. If you only need free basics, Ahrefs Webmaster Tools costs nothing for sites you own.

## How do Semrush and Ahrefs compare on price?

Semrush has two price lists: the main SEO plans and a separate Local toolkit priced per location. Ahrefs has one list for everything. The table shows monthly list prices as shown on each pricing page.

| Tool and plan | Monthly price | What the page lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Semrush SEO | $139 ($117.33/mo billed annually) | 5 websites, 500 keywords tracked daily |
| Semrush Starter | $199 ($165.17/mo billed annually) | 5 websites, 500 keywords, 50 AI prompts |
| Semrush Pro+ | $299 ($248.17/mo billed annually) | 15 websites, 1,500 keywords |
| Semrush Local Base | $30 per location | Map Rank Tracker (375 credits), GBP AI agent |
| Semrush Local Pro | $60 per location | Listings (70+ directories), review tools, 1,225 map credits |
| Ahrefs Starter | $29 | Keyword and competitor research |
| Ahrefs Lite | $129 | 750 tracked keywords, 5 non-verified projects, 1 user |
| Ahrefs Standard | $249 | 2,000 tracked keywords, 20 non-verified projects |
| Ahrefs Advanced | $449 | 5,000 tracked keywords, 50 non-verified projects |

Ahrefs Enterprise is listed at $1,499 and needs an annual commitment. Semrush Advanced and Enterprise are custom quotes. The older plan names and prices that circulate in many articles (Guru, Business) no longer match Semrush's current page.

## Which tool has better local features?

Semrush has more tools built for a business with a storefront. Its Local toolkit page describes Listing Management (pushes your details to 70+ US directories and others), a Map Rank Tracker that shows Google Maps rankings as a heatmap across your area, and Review Management with review requests by SMS and email.

Ahrefs is a general SEO suite. Its Rank Tracker covers 190+ locations down to city and ZIP code and lists the local pack among the 19 SERP features it monitors. Its page does not say that it reports a separate map-pack position the way a map heatmap does. Ahrefs has no listing distribution or review request tool.

## Which is better for backlinks and content research?

Ahrefs. Its Site Explorer shows which sites link to you and your competitors, and the free Webmaster Tools tier includes it for verified sites, limited to 1,000 backlinks and keywords visible at once. Site Audit on the free tier runs 5,000 crawl credits per project per month.

Semrush also has backlink and content tools, and its higher plans add historical data and content optimization. For a shop that wants to find who links to a local competitor, either tool works. The gap matters more for a business publishing a lot of content.

## What does it cost for one coffee shop? A worked example

This example uses **assumption** inputs: one location, no agency, a goal of tracking the shop's map position and keeping listings consistent.

| Option | Monthly cost | Annual cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Semrush Local Pro, one location | $60 | $720 |
| Semrush Local Base, one location | $30 | $360 |
| Ahrefs Lite | $129 | $1,548 |
| Ahrefs Webmaster Tools only | $0 | $0 |

Local Pro covers the listing and review work for a quarter of the cost of an Ahrefs Lite plan. If the shop's main need is a map heatmap, Local Base is enough. If it only wants to see its own site's keywords and links, the free Ahrefs tier does that. Whether either pays off depends on what you change after reading the data.

## Do you need both tools?

Not for one location. A small business rarely uses the full limits of either plan. Agencies and businesses with several locations or heavy publishing may justify both: Semrush Local for listings and maps, Ahrefs for links and research.

The cheaper path for a first year is to claim and fix your Google Business Profile (free), use Ahrefs Webmaster Tools, and add Semrush Local Base or Pro only when you have a specific question to answer. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers the free work first.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Choose Semrush Local** if you have a storefront, care about Google Maps position and want listings and reviews in one dashboard.
- **Choose Ahrefs Lite or Standard** if you publish content or build links and want large keyword and backlink data.
- **Start with Ahrefs Webmaster Tools** if your budget is zero and you own the site.
- **Skip both for now** if your Business Profile is incomplete. That fix is free and comes first.

## FAQ

### Is Semrush or Ahrefs cheaper for a local business?

For one location, Semrush Local Base at $30 or Local Pro at $60 per location is cheaper than Ahrefs Lite at $129. Semrush's main SEO plan starts at $139 a month, so it is the Local toolkit that makes the difference.

### Do Semrush and Ahrefs offer a free trial?

Semrush's pages say you can try it free for seven days. Ahrefs' pricing page does not state trial terms, so check at signup. Ahrefs Webmaster Tools is a separate free product for sites you own and verify.

### Can Ahrefs track local rankings?

Yes. Its Rank Tracker supports city and ZIP-level locations and lists the local pack among the SERP features it tracks. It is not a map heatmap tool.

### Which tool is better for Google Maps?

Semrush, through its Map Rank Tracker, which shows rankings as a heatmap across your area. You still need to manage your Business Profile itself.

### Are keyword volumes accurate for small towns?

Treat them as estimates. Both vendors model search volume, and small local terms are often rounded or missing. Use the trend and your own Search Console data to check.
