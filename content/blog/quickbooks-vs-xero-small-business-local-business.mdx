## What Does QuickBooks Online Cost?

The QuickBooks pricing page listed four paid plans with a struck-through regular price and a 50% off promo for 3 months. Simple Start is $38 regular ($19 promo) for 1 user. Essentials is $85 ($42.50) for 3 users. Plus is $140 ($70) for 5 users. Advanced is $340 ($170) for 25 users. Each plan also shows access for 2 accountants (3 on Advanced).

The page also showed a Free plan at $0 for 1 user and mentioned a 30-day trial, but it did not clearly say which plan the trial covers. It also did not state the promo's fine print, or whether the prices are billed monthly or annually. Confirm those at checkout.

## What Does Xero Cost in the US?

Xero uses plans by activity rather than by user count. Early is $2.70 a month for the intro period, then $27. Growing is $5.90, then $59. Established is $9.70, then $97. The page said there are no per-user license fees.

Early is limited: quotes and 20 invoices, 5 bills, with auto-reconcile as a $5 a month add-on. Growing and Established list invoices, bills and reconciliation with no limit shown.

Payroll is separate. Xero Payroll was listed at $36 a month plus $6 per employee or contractor.

## Who Should Pick QuickBooks and Who Should Pick Xero?

These are rules of thumb, not vendor claims.

- **Pick Xero** if you have many users (a bookkeeper, a manager, you) and a modest invoice volume. No per-user fee keeps the bill flat, and Growing at $59 covers unlimited invoices and bills per the page.
- **Pick QuickBooks** if your accountant already uses it. Both pages show accountant access, but familiarity saves hours.
- **Skip Xero Early** if you send more than 20 invoices a month or pay more than 5 bills.
- **Choose by the regular price**, not the promo. Compare $38 against $27, or $85 against $59.

Ask your bookkeeper or accountant before switching, because migrating history takes time.

## What Should You Check Before You Sign Up?

Open the checkout page and read three things the pricing pages did not make clear on 24 October 2026: whether the price is billed monthly or annually, what the price becomes when the promo ends, and how you cancel. Then export a sample of your own data, such as last month's invoices, and see whether the tool imports it cleanly.

Also confirm whether your bookkeeper or accountant has a login on the plan you pick. QuickBooks showed accountant seats on every paid plan, and for Xero you should ask the accountant directly. A switch mid-year creates extra work at tax time, so the best moment to change is the start of a new financial year or quarter.

Finally, put the promo end date in your calendar. A price that doubles or rises tenfold after the intro period is the most common surprise with both tools.

## FAQ

### Is Xero cheaper than QuickBooks?

On the regular prices listed on 24 October 2026, yes at each tier. Early is $27 against $38 for Simple Start, but Early has invoice and bill limits.

### Does QuickBooks charge per user?

Plans include a set number of users: 1, 3, 5 or 25 on the four paid plans. Extra users mean moving up a plan.

### Does Xero charge per user?

The US page says there are no per-user license fees. Payroll is priced per employee separately.

### How long do the intro discounts last?

QuickBooks showed 50% off for 3 months. Xero showed 90% off the first 6 months for new US customers through 2 November 2026.

### Is there a free trial?

QuickBooks mentioned a 30-day trial but did not make clear which plan it applies to. Xero's page did not mention a trial.
