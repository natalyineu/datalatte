## Who Can Advertise on Instacart?

The first test is whether you own a product that is sold through the platform. Instacart ads are built around items that shoppers can add to a basket, so the advertiser is usually the brand behind a packaged food, drink, pet or household product. Retailers and brands manage campaigns in the same ad system, but the ad types open to brands are not the same as the ones open to a store.

A café, salon, gym or barber shop that sells a service has no product in the basket to promote. A restaurant that sells only prepared food in its own kitchen is in a similar position. For these businesses, the better question is which local channel reaches people searching nearby, such as search ads or a Business Profile, rather than which grocery platform to join.

Confirm eligibility in the platform's own help pages before you plan spend, because account rules change.

## Which Formats and Bidding Models Should You Compare?

Most grocery and delivery ad platforms sell at least two billing models. Cost per click (CPC) charges when someone clicks. Cost per thousand impressions (CPM) charges for each thousand times an ad is shown. CPC suits a product with a clear buying decision and a known margin. CPM suits awareness, where you care about reach more than the single click.

Compare the formats on the table below before you choose, and check current names and billing in the ad manager. This list is a checklist of what to confirm, not a record of Instacart's current offer.

| Question to confirm | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Which formats open to your account type | Some formats may be brand-only |
| Billing for each format (CPC or CPM) | Sets how you measure cost per sale |
| Minimum bid and minimum budget | Decides whether a small test is viable |
| Where the ad appears | Search results and category pages differ in intent |
| Review time before a campaign goes live | Affects how early you can start |

Set a maximum bid per click once you know your margin. A bid above the gross profit on one unit means each sale loses money before any other cost.

## What Would a Small Test Cost?

All inputs below are **assumptions** to show the arithmetic. They are not platform benchmarks, and your figures will differ.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Daily budget | $20 |
| Average CPC | $0.60 |
| Clicks per day | about 33 |
| Click-to-purchase rate | 10% |
| Units sold per day | about 3.3 |
| Price per unit | $12 |
| Gross margin per unit | 40% |

Revenue is about $40 a day against $20 of spend, a return on ad spend (ROAS) of 2.0. Gross profit is about $16, so the test loses roughly $4 a day before other costs. Break-even ROAS equals 1 divided by the gross margin, which is 2.5 at a 40% margin.

The general lesson holds for any grocery product. A low-margin item needs a lower CPC, a higher conversion rate or repeat purchases to pay back. Test one product with real data before scaling, and track cost per sale as well as clicks.

## How Do Delivery-Only Local Brands Fit In?

A local food business that operates only for delivery has a different question: how it appears on Google. Google's guidelines say delivery-only food brands are permitted if they have distinct branded packaging and a distinct website. Multiple virtual brands from one location are permitted but face additional verification steps. Delivery-only brands must add their service areas and hide their address on their Business Profile ([Google Business Profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177)).

These rules apply to the Google listing, not to Instacart ads. They matter when a local kitchen is considering a second brand or a delivery-only concept, because the verification and address rules affect how customers find the business.

## How Do You Start a Test?

Work through these steps in order. Each one reduces the chance of spending on a product that cannot pay back.

1. Confirm that you own a product listed through the platform, and check eligibility for each ad format in the account.
2. Calculate gross profit per unit, then set a break-even ROAS and a maximum CPC bid below your profit per sale.
3. Pick your highest-margin product and set a small daily budget.
4. Review search terms and orders after a few weeks, and remove terms that bring clicks but no sales.
5. Fix the product listing first. Images, size and price shown on the page affect conversion more than a small bid change.

## FAQ

### Can a local restaurant or coffee shop advertise on Instacart?

Probably not through brand-focused ad formats unless it sells a packaged product through the platform. Check the eligibility rules in the ad account before planning spend.

### How much do Instacart ads cost?

No price is quoted here, because the pages used for this article did not give a verified Instacart price. Costs depend on your bid, your category and your margin. Check the live Ads Manager help pages and your account for current bid and budget rules.

### Is there a minimum budget?

This article does not state one. Set a daily budget you can afford to lose for a few weeks, and use the break-even calculation above to size it.

### Can I target my neighborhood?

Grocery retail media is organised around products and shoppers, not map radius. For a local service business, location targeting in a search ad platform is the more direct tool.
