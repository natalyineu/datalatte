Instacart Ads let a brand pay to show its products to people shopping on Instacart. The self-serve format, Sponsored Products, is billed per click, and Instacart's documentation marks it, along with display and video, as available to brands only. A café, salon or gym cannot buy these placements for itself. A food or pet-product brand that sells through Instacart retail partners can.

Instacart does not publish a price list or a minimum budget. This guide covers what is documented, labels every example input as an assumption and says plainly where a number is not public.

## Who Can Advertise on Instacart?

Account setup is open to retailers and brands. Most ad formats are not. Instacart's Ads Manager documentation says sponsored products, display ads and the other paid formats are "available to brands only", while retailer promotions are retailer-only.

In practice this means the advertiser owns a product sold through an Instacart retail partner: packaged food, drinks, pet food, household goods. A local business that sells services, or a restaurant with no packaged product, has no self-serve way in. A third-party guide from Perpetua also says Instacart Ads run in the United States and Canada.

For a local business that is not a product brand, a local channel such as Google Ads or a Business Profile usually fits better. See our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## Which Ad Formats Does Instacart Offer?

Instacart's Ads Manager lists these formats:

| Format | Billing | Where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sponsored products | Per click (CPC) | Homepage, search results, browsing |
| Display ads | Per 1,000 impressions (CPM) | Storefronts, departments, aisles |
| Shoppable display | CPM | Search results, brand imagery with a bundle of items |
| Shoppable video | CPM | Search results, video with a product carousel |
| Inspiration ads | CPM | Recipes and curated out-of-aisle ideas |
| Pages | Free | Shoppable brand destination with its own URL |
| Brand promotions | Not listed | Spend-and-save and free gift offers |

Perpetua reports that Sponsored Products is the only fully self-serve format. Hero banners, coupons and delivery promotions go through an Instacart representative. Instacart's own page does not say this, so treat it as secondary and check in your account.

## How Does Instacart Bidding Work?

Sponsored products use a second-price auction on cost per click. You set a maximum CPC bid per ad group. Instacart says the winner pays $0.01 more than the next-best competitor's bid, not necessarily the maximum, and ranks ads using the bid, product conversion rate and other factors.

Perpetua reports a minimum bid of $0.15. That is a third-party figure, not in Instacart's documentation. Instacart publishes no average CPC and no price range, so any "typical Instacart CPC" figure you see elsewhere is an estimate.

Shoppable display works differently. Bidding is by CPM, you can pick up to 40 products from your product library, and the Ads Quality team reviews each campaign within 3 business days. Match types are exact, narrow and broad, with broad as the default.

## How Do Budgets Work?

You choose between two budget profiles for sponsored products:

- **Lifetime budget:** needs a start date and a total. The campaign enters every eligible auction until the money runs out, and Instacart warns it does not pace spending.
- **Daily budget:** spend averages the daily figure over time. It can exceed the daily amount on busy days, but Instacart says it never spends more than double the daily budget on one day. Without a lifetime budget, monthly spend is capped at 30.4 times the daily budget.

Instacart does not state a minimum campaign budget in these pages. Plan around your margin, not around a platform minimum.

## What Would a Small Test Cost? A Worked Example

All inputs below are **assumptions** to show the arithmetic. They are not Instacart benchmarks.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Daily budget | $20 |
| Average CPC | $0.60 |
| Clicks per day | about 33 |
| Click-to-purchase rate | 10% |
| Units sold per day | about 3.3 |
| Price per unit | $12 |
| Gross margin per unit | 40% |

Revenue is about $40 a day against $20 of spend, a return on ad spend (ROAS) of 2.0. Gross profit is about $16, so each day loses roughly $4 before any other cost. Break-even ROAS is 1 divided by the margin: at 40% it is 2.5.

The lesson is general. A low-margin grocery item needs either a lower CPC, a higher conversion rate or a repeat-purchase effect to pay back. Test one product with real data before scaling. Our [CPM vs CPC guide](/blog/cpm-vs-cpc-which-is-better) explains the two billing models.

## What Results Does Instacart Claim?

Instacart's ads page makes several claims, all with caveats on the page. It says its ad ecosystem reaches "55+ million consumers" (Comscore, deduplicated unique visitors over 12 months), cites a 21% in-store halo sales lift (Circana, Q2 2024, exposed users versus a control group) and an incremental sales lift of 25% from a previously published test. These are vendor claims. The page itself notes that prior results do not guarantee future outcomes, and none of them is an expected result for a small brand.

## How Do You Start?

1. Confirm your products are listed through Instacart retail partners and that you are the brand owner.
2. Create an account through Instacart's Ads Manager and pick a campaign objective. Sponsored products sit under "Maximize sales" and "Build your own".
3. Add your best-margin products, set a daily budget and a default CPC bid.
4. Review search terms and orders after a few weeks, then add keyword bids for the winners.
5. Fix product pages first. Images, size and price shown in the listing affect conversion.

## FAQ

### Can a local restaurant or coffee shop advertise on Instacart?

Not with the self-serve brand formats unless it sells a packaged product through Instacart retail partners. Instacart marks sponsored products and display ads as brand-only.

### How much do Instacart ads cost?

Instacart does not publish prices. Sponsored products are billed per click in a second-price auction, and Perpetua reports a minimum bid of $0.15. Display and video formats bill per 1,000 impressions. Your real costs appear in the account.

### Is there a minimum budget?

The pages reviewed do not state one. You set a daily or lifetime budget, and a daily budget can overspend up to double on a single day.

### Can I target my neighborhood?

Instacart's documentation reviewed here does not describe neighborhood targeting. If you need a one or two mile radius, Google Ads location targeting is built for that.

### Is Instacart Ads better than Amazon Ads?

No published comparison supports a claim either way. Perpetua says category CPCs can be much lower than on Amazon, but gives no figures.
