Most local businesses should test Facebook (Meta) ads first and add TikTok ads second. Facebook has published benchmarks you can plan around: WordStream by LocaliQ lists $0.60 per click for traffic campaigns. TikTok publishes no price list, and its Ads Manager needs more than $50 a day per campaign and more than $20 a day per ad group. TikTok makes sense when you already have short video to show.

This guide is for local service and retail businesses such as cafes, salons, groomers and studios. Every number has a source at the end. Where a number is not published, the text says so.

## What Do Facebook Ads Cost for a Local Business?

WordStream by LocaliQ updated its [2026 Facebook benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/facebook-advertising-benchmarks/) on 23 September 2026. Across industries, traffic campaigns average a 1.93% click-through rate and $0.60 per click. Lead campaigns average $1.80 per click, an 8.54% conversion rate and $27.39 per lead.

Local categories differ. For traffic campaigns the page lists $0.45 per click for Restaurants and Food, $0.50 for Beauty and Personal Care, $0.64 for Health and Fitness and $0.51 for Animals and Pets. Beauty lead campaigns cost $2.97 per click and $50.91 per lead; fitness lead campaigns cost $1.84 per click and $27.11 per lead. 

The page has text that disagrees with its own tables, so these figures come from the tables.

## What Do TikTok Ads Cost?

TikTok does not publish average prices. No TikTok CPC or CPM is quoted here because none could be checked at a primary source.

What TikTok does publish is its floor. Its [daily budget help page](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-daily-budgets) says campaign budgets must exceed $50 and ad group daily budgets must exceed $20. A lifetime ad group budget is $20 times the scheduled days, so a 31-day run needs at least $620.

TikTok's [bidding methods page](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/bidding-methods) says you may be billed per impression (CPM or oCPM), per qualifying video view (CPV) or per click (CPC). Our [TikTok ads cost guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-guide) explains each and labels the second-hand ranges.

## Which Platform Reaches Local Customers Better?

TikTok's [location targeting page](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/location-targeting) lists cities, DMAs, counties, states and zip codes, with up to 3,000 locations per ad group. It does not support excluding locations. It warns that small areas or small sets of zip codes can reduce impressions and clicks and may raise CPA.

That matters for a single shop with a three-mile catchment. A small audience can limit delivery on TikTok, so test a wider city-level audience first. For a deeper look at TikTok targeting, see our [TikTok ads targeting guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-targeting-guide). No Meta targeting claims are made here because the Meta help page could not be read.

## How Much Should You Budget to Test Each?

TikTok's [bidding strategy guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/best-practices-for-bidding-strategies), updated August 2026, recommends a daily budget of 10 times your cost per result so the campaign can pass the learning phase. At the $20 ad group floor, that supports a cost per result of about $2. A booking rarely costs that little, so judge early TikTok tests on cheaper actions such as clicks and trace bookings separately.

A practical split for a first month, labelled as advice rather than a benchmark:

- Under $300 a month: use one platform, and make it Facebook.
- About $600: one TikTok ad group at $20 a day, or a Facebook campaign at a similar spend, not both.
- $1,200 or more: run both with the same offer and compare cost per booking.

## Worked Example: What Click Price Can You Afford?

All inputs are **assumptions** for a grooming salon. They are not benchmarks.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Margin per booking (assumption) | $35 |
| Share of clicks that book (assumption) | 3% |
| Margin per click | $35 x 0.03 = $1.05 |
| Break-even CPC | $1.05 |

Facebook traffic clicks for Animals and Pets average $0.51 per LocaliQ, so on these assumptions that would leave about $0.54 margin per click. For TikTok there is no published CPC to compare, so you only know after a test. If your TikTok clicks cost more than the break-even figure, stop. If the booking rate is 1.5% instead, break-even halves to about $0.53 and Facebook only just covers it.

## FAQ

### Is TikTok or Facebook cheaper for a local business?
Only Facebook has a published benchmark: $0.60 per click for traffic campaigns (LocaliQ 2026). TikTok publishes no average price, so compare cost per booking from your own test.

### What is the minimum budget for TikTok ads?
TikTok's help page says campaign budgets must be above $50 and ad group daily budgets above $20. Lifetime ad group budgets are $20 times the days scheduled.

### Can I run both at once?
Yes, with the same offer and a separate tracking link for each, so you can compare bookings. Start with one if your budget is under about $600 a month.

### Do I need video for TikTok?
Yes, TikTok ads are video-led. Facebook accepts images and video, which is easier if you have no footage.

### How long should a test run?
TikTok ties the learning phase to budget: 10 times your cost per result per day. Run until you have enough bookings to compare, not a fixed number of days.
