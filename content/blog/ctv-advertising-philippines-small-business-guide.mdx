Connected TV (CTV) advertising places video ads on streaming screens. For a Philippine small business, the cost depends on the platform, the targeting and the length of the test. Published benchmarks are available, but they are US-dollar figures, not Philippine peso rate cards. This guide explains what the sourced numbers do and do not show, then sets out a worked peso budget for a café. Every peso figure in the example is an assumption, so request current local quotes from each platform before committing money.

## What does CTV advertising involve for a Philippine small business?

CTV ads run on televisions connected to the internet, usually through streaming apps or free ad-supported services. For a local business, the main difference from search is the format: a full-screen video shown in a household setting. Search ads reach people who are already looking, while CTV suits building awareness before a customer searches for a product.

Google's documentation describes Display campaigns reaching people across 35 million websites and apps, along with Google-owned properties such as YouTube and Gmail. That figure covers Display inventory, not CTV specifically, so it should not be read as a Philippine CTV audience count. Each CTV provider publishes its own reach data, and that is the number to compare.

Platform choice matters more than headline reach. A single-location café gains little from national inventory, so the buying decision should start with where its customers live and which apps they use. The [Indonesia CTV guide for small business](/blog/ctv-advertising-indonesia-small-business-guide) covers another Southeast Asian market and shows how regional inventory and pricing can differ from a single-city plan.

## How do published benchmarks translate to a peso budget?

WordStream's benchmark table reports an average search click-through rate of 3.17% and a display rate of 0.46% across all industries. The average search cost per click is $2.69 and the display figure is $0.63. The dataset is US-focused, and its own author notes that it is updated regularly, so check the current version before using any figure.

Industry matters too. In the same table, Legal ($6.75) and Consumer Services ($6.40) sit at the top of the search cost-per-click ranking. A café's closest category may differ by platform, so confirm which category your provider assigns. Either way, the numbers are in dollars, and they tell you how costs are structured rather than what a peso CTV buy will cost.

Google's Display documentation also states that Display campaigns optimize within days. That is a Display statement, not a CTV one, but it is a reasonable reason to give any test enough weeks to learn from.

## Which steps should a café take before a CTV test?

A test is useful only when the rules and the measurement are settled first. Follow this sequence before spending money.

1. Request each platform's current Philippine CPM and minimum spend in writing.
2. Set one fixed test budget and one success measure, such as cost per reached household or visits tracked with a promo code.
3. Target one city or district rather than the whole country.
4. Produce one video in the language your customers use, with the address and opening hours clearly visible.
5. Check price claims and any health or food claims against local advertising rules with the platform's review team and a local adviser.
6. Keep a comparison area with no CTV ads, so any lift in visits can be judged against a baseline.

For a lower-cost awareness format to compare against, read the [native advertising guide for small business](/blog/native-advertising-guide-small-business).

## What should a business verify before buying CTV in the Philippines?

Several figures often quoted for Philippine CTV are not supported by the sources used for this guide. These include platform-by-platform peso CPM ranges, national population counts and specific regulator rules. Do not budget against them until a current platform rate card, an official population source and a legal reference confirm them.

Verification is inexpensive. A rate card, a government statistics page and a written note from a local adviser replace guesswork with dated, checkable numbers. Keep those documents with the campaign file so the test can be repeated later with the same method.

Judge the test on the success measure chosen in step 2, not on impressions alone. A campaign that reaches many households but produces no tracked visits has not yet proven its value. If you want help designing the measurement, the [free audit](/free-audit) is a practical starting point, and [Google Ads management](/services/google-ads) can cover the search layer that captures people once awareness builds.

## FAQ

### Are the WordStream benchmarks valid for Philippine ads?

No, not directly. The dataset is US-focused and reports costs in dollars. Use it to understand how cost metrics are structured, then replace the numbers with current Philippine quotes from your platforms.

### How much should a small business budget for a CTV test?

No sourced minimum budget is available for the Philippines. The ₱30,000 example in this guide is an assumption used to show the arithmetic. Start from the platform's minimum spend and the CPM quote you receive in writing.

### Does Google's 35 million figure apply to CTV?

No. Google's documentation gives that reach figure for Display campaigns across websites, apps and Google-owned properties. CTV inventory is a separate buy, so ask each provider for its own reach numbers.

### Is CTV better than search ads for a local café?

They do different jobs. CTV suits awareness and brand recall in a household setting, while search ads capture people already looking for a product. A test can pair a small CTV budget with search and measure whether awareness raises branded search volume.
