Maine Office of Tourism data show 14.15 million visitors in 2025, down 4.4%, but direct spending up 1.4% to $9.37 billion. Fewer visitors are spending more each. Fix your Google Business Profile first, then run small Search tests in the weeks that matter.

## What Does the Maine Market Look Like?

Small and tight. BLS lists Maine's September 2026 unemployment rate at 3.3% (preliminary, seasonally adjusted), with 661.7 thousand nonfarm jobs, up 0.1% over 12 months. Leisure and hospitality jobs number 71.7 thousand, up 0.7%.

The Census Bureau estimates Maine's population at 1,414,874 in 2025, up 6,436 (0.46%). Deaths (16,802) exceeded births (11,783) by 5,019, so all growth came from migration: 7,406 domestic and 4,040 international. A small, ageing local base is why many Maine businesses depend on visitors.

## How Is Maine's Visitor Season Changing?

Visitors fell but spent more. The Maine Office of Tourism's 2025 report, as covered by the Portland Press Herald, counts 14.15 million visitors, down 4.4%, and $9.37 billion in direct spending, up 1.4% before inflation. Canadian visitors were 3.6% of the total, down from 5.4% in 2024. About 18% were first-time visitors and nearly 20% arrived by plane.

Summer, from May to August, brought 7,293,400 visitors, down 6% from 7,756,000. The report ties the drop mainly to day trippers. Room nights fell 5.8%, while the average daily rate rose 7.9% to $242.64. Among summer visitors, 80% were from out of state and 20% were Maine residents.

## Where Are Visitors Going Instead?

Inland. Compared with summer 2024, the share of summer visitors going to Aroostook County rose from 3% to 7%, Kennebec Valley from 9% to 12% and the Lakes and Mountains region from 16% to 20%. For a business inland, that is a reason to advertise earlier than before. For a coastal business, it suggests focusing on higher-spending overnight guests over day trips.

The report ties lower visitor numbers to fewer lodging units and hotel occupancy, and links lower spending to travellers being more price-conscious. Treat the shift as a trend to watch, not a promise.

Revenue of $2,450 against $1,500 in ads leaves $950 before food and labour costs, so the test only pays if gross margin on those visits is above about 61%. This is why seasonal tests should aim at repeat visits and higher-value bookings, not one-off meals. If your real visit rate is 30%, the test loses money.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local in Maine?

Set a radius around the business and leave out places you cannot serve. Google's help page says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km. It recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting as the best practice on Search, and says "Presence" only suits cases where you want to exclude people who are elsewhere but interested in your offer.

Maine has a few compact towns and long rural distances. In Portland a 2 to 4 mile radius may fit, which is an assumption to check against customer addresses. For a lakes or mountains business, target the regions where your visitors start their trip and test a wider radius.

Add negative keywords weekly from the Search terms report: jobs, free, DIY and services you do not offer. Google notes that negative keywords do not match close variants, so add plurals and synonyms yourself.

## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Maine?

Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. It also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

List seasonal hours exactly and update them each year. Upload photos from the season you are promoting, and reply to every review. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes through each field.

## Can You Offer a Discount for a Google Review?

No. Google's contributed content policy says merchants may not offer incentives, such as payment, discounts, free goods or services, in exchange for posting a review. You may ask customers for a review. You may not reward them for one. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for ways to ask.

## Where Can Maine Owners Get Free Advice?

Maine SBDC offers no-cost, confidential advising to entrepreneurs and small businesses across the state, according to search results. It is a program of the US Small Business Administration, the Maine Department of Economic and Community Development and the University of Southern Maine, with advising available virtually. ## What Mistakes Do Maine Owners Make?

These are recommendations, not study findings.

- Spending evenly all year when sales are concentrated in a few weeks.
- Chasing day trippers when overnight guests spend more.
- Forgetting locals in the off-season. A [local marketing budget](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) should include them.
- Not updating hours for each season.

## FAQ

### How many tourists visit Maine?

The Maine Office of Tourism counted 14.15 million visitors in 2025, down 4.4% from 2024, with direct spending of $9.37 billion, up 1.4%.

### Is summer tourism in Maine growing?

No. Summer 2025 visitors fell 6% to 7,293,400, mainly because of fewer day trippers, while accommodation spending rose 5.0%.
