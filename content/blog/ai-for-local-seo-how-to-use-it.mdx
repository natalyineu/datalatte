A weekly AI routine for local SEO takes about 90 minutes when the assistant drafts and a person decides. Use it for repetitive jobs: comparing listing details, drafting review replies, outlining one useful page, checking markup and summarising reports. A person stays responsible for every fact a customer will read, including prices, opening hours and claims. Google's guidance favours accurate, helpful information over tricks, so the routine focuses on consistency and real answers rather than volume.

## What Does the 90-Minute Weekly Routine Look Like?

The week splits into five short slots. Each slot has one job for the assistant and one job for the owner, and the human step is never skipped.

| Slot | Task | AI's job | Your job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 min | Consistency check | Flag mismatches in name, address, phone, hours | Fix them at the source |
| 20 min | Review replies | Draft replies | Edit, add one detail, post |
| 25 min | One page or post | Outline and first draft | Add real prices, photos, process |
| 15 min | Markup check | Draft structured data | Match it to the visible page |
| 15 min | Reporting | Summarise the numbers | Read the numbers yourself |

Keep the same order every week so the routine becomes habit. Ninety minutes is a planning figure, not a benchmark, so adjust it to your volume of reviews and calls.

## How Should AI Handle Consistency and Review Replies?

Start with consistency. Paste the Business Profile details, the website contact page and two or three directory listings into an assistant. Ask for a table of every difference in business name, address, phone and hours. Comparing messy text is tedious for people and easy for a model. Then correct the differences in the original listings, not in the assistant's table.

Google's [guidelines for representing your business](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) ask businesses to use the name as it appears on signage, stationery and branding. Taglines, store codes or opening hours added to the name are not permitted and can lead to suspension. The assistant should never be allowed to "improve" the name.

For review replies, let the assistant draft and then edit each one. Mention one concrete detail the customer gave, avoid repeating the same sentence across replies, and write replies to negative reviews yourself. Never offer discounts or freebies in exchange for reviews, and never have AI write reviews.

## Which Content Can AI Draft Without Creating Risk?

Choose one real question customers ask, from calls, emails or Search Console queries, and write one page that answers it. The assistant can build an outline from your notes and draft a first version of a service page or FAQ. Then add what only the owner can supply: prices, method, team, photos and neighbourhood details.

Delete any claim that cannot be proven, such as awards, years in business or certifications. Google's [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) says no secret technique will automatically rank a site first, and that the work is about helping users and search engines understand content. One page that answers a question well does more than ten near-identical pages with a town name swapped.

The business description should stay factual too. Google's guidelines say it should describe services, mission and history, and avoid promotions, prices and links.

## What Should You Avoid?

- Publishing raw AI output across a site in one go.
- Near-identical suburb pages where only the place name changes.
- Discounts, gifts or AI-written reviews to boost ratings.
- Adding keywords, taglines or hours to the business name.
- Trusting AI summaries of rankings or traffic that have not been checked in your own dashboards.

## What Should You Do This Week?

1. Paste the Business Profile and website contact details into an assistant and ask for a table of every mismatch. Fix each one at the source.
2. Draft replies to the last five unanswered reviews, edit each with one concrete detail, and post them personally.
3. Write down five questions customers ask. Pick one and draft a single page with real prices and photos.
4. Check the business name, description and categories against Google's guidelines, and remove anything that is not the real-world name.
5. Put a recurring 90-minute block in the calendar and make the first line of the routine: "A person checks every fact."

## FAQ

### Do I need AI for local SEO?

No. Every step can be done by hand. AI saves time on drafting, comparing and summarising, which matters when the owner runs the business alone.

### Can AI reply to my Google reviews?

It can draft replies, and some tools post them. A person should still check each one, and negative reviews deserve a human-written answer. Never offer discounts in exchange for reviews.

### Can I use AI to write many location pages?

It can help draft a page, but publishing many near-identical pages with only the place name changed adds little for users. Write each page around a real question and real local facts.

### How long until I see results?

Google's starter guide says changes can take effect within hours or take months, and suggests waiting a few weeks before judging them. Track calls, direction requests and Search Console clicks over several months.
