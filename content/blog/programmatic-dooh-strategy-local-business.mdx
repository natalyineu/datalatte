Programmatic DOOH means buying ad plays on digital billboards and other screens through a platform, by place, time and audience, instead of booking one board for months. For a local business the strategy is simple: pick screens near your customers, run short bursts around your busiest times, and measure visits against a comparison period. OAAA data says US out-of-home spend reached $9.46 billion in 2025, DOOH was 36% of it, and programmatic was 30% of DOOH.

Published minimums are scarce, and the example below is labelled as an assumption.

## How Big Is Programmatic DOOH?

OAAA's 2025 summary gives total OOH ad spend of $9.46 billion, up 3.6%, with DOOH up 10.5%. By category, transit rose 9.8%, street furniture 5.6%, billboards 2.3% and place-based 0.2%. Multiplying the 36% DOOH share by $9.46 billion gives roughly $3.4 billion, our own arithmetic, not an OAAA figure.

Forecasts of programmatic spend alone differ by publisher and definition. Secondary reports attribute about $1.23 billion for 2026 to eMarketer, but we could not open the original chart. Use trends, not a single number.

## Which Screens Can a Local Business Buy?

| Screen type | Audience context | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roadside digital billboard | Drivers on a route | Restaurants, services along commuter roads |
| Street furniture and transit | Pedestrians, riders | Shops, cafes, gyms in town centers |
| Place-based screens (gyms, malls, offices) | People already at a venue | Targeting by venue type |
| Moving screens (taxi, bus) | Wider reach, longer dwell time inside vehicles | Broad awareness |

IAB's July 2025 DOOH measurement guide says static placements allow more predictable traffic modeling, while moving formats reach more widely. It also says interior moving screens give longer dwell time and make attribution easier. Pick the type that matches how your customers move, not the biggest network.

## How Do You Target the Right Audience?

The UK IAB guide lists three simple controls: context (rail, retail, roadside), time of day down to the hour, and geography, even for a business 100 meters from a screen. These controls work without personal data.

Audience segments built from mobile location data are a second layer. The IAB measurement guide says privacy rules such as GDPR, CCPA and CPRA should be treated as non-negotiable. Ask what data a vendor uses, whether it is aggregated, and how segments are defined. Do not assume a segment like "dog owners" is accurate without seeing the method.

## How Should the Creative Look?

The UK IAB small business guide says many digital ads show for about 10 seconds, so the headline must be readable at a glance. Put your name and one offer on the screen. Add a QR code or short URL only where people can stop, such as transit stops or venue screens.

Creative specs vary by network. Ask for the file formats and slot lengths before you design. For event timing, see our [DOOH event marketing guide](/blog/dooh-event-marketing-local-business).

## How Do You Measure Whether It Worked?

IAB's guide separates impression types: opportunity to see counts people in the exposure zone, and likelihood to see requires evidence they could actually view the screen. It also lists footfall and sales lift as outcome measures and recommends incrementality testing, such as synthetic control or matched markets.

For a small business, a practical version is to compare visits or sales in the target area against a similar area without ads. A store with 200 weekly visitors cannot detect a small lift, so keep expectations modest. Our [coffee shop DOOH page](/blog/dooh-advertising-for-coffee-shops) covers a niche example, and the [CTV and DOOH guide](/blog/ctv-dooh-omnichannel-strategy-local-business) covers combining channels.

## What Does a Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**. A dental clinic runs a two-week test.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $2,500 |
| Screens | 6 digital street-level and roadside screens within 3 miles |
| Hours | Weekdays 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. |
| Creative | One 10-second ad, one offer |
| Comparison | Bookings from the same postcodes in the 4 weeks before |
| Decision rule | Continue only if new bookings exceed what the budget would buy elsewhere |

Cost per new booking is $2,500 divided by new bookings in the test area, minus the baseline you would have seen anyway. No price or CPM is published for these screens, so the vendor quote sets the real numbers.

## FAQ

### What is programmatic DOOH?

It is the automated buying of ad time on digital out-of-home screens through a platform, by location, time and audience.

### How much does it cost?

No standard price is published. A UK IAB guide cites minimum orders of about 2,500 to 5,000 pounds for a burst of a few weeks. US platforms do not publish minimums on the pages read for this article.

### Is programmatic DOOH better than a regular billboard?

It is more flexible. You can change the creative, dates and hours. It is not automatically cheaper, so compare quotes.

### How do I know if people saw my ad?

Impressions are estimates, according to IAB's guide. Test results come from visits and sales compared with a control area.

### Does it work for a single-location shop?

It can if screens are close to the shop. Start with a short test and one offer.
