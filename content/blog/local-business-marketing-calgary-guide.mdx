## How Big Is the Calgary Market?

The City of Calgary's population table lists 1,509.8 thousand people in April 2024, 1,558.7 thousand in 2025 and a forecast of 1,583.2 thousand for 2026, completed in January 2026. The 2024 and 2025 figures are marked "Estimate (no Civic Census)". An official civic census has not been run for years, so treat these as estimates.

Growth means newcomers who have not chosen a regular barber, groomer or cafe yet. Our [Canada local marketing guide](/blog/local-business-marketing-canada-guide-2026) and the [Alberta guide](/blog/small-business-marketing-alberta-canada) cover the wider market.

## Why Does the Stampede Matter for Marketing?

CTV News Calgary reported that official attendance at the 2026 Stampede was more than 1.4 million. The article did not give the dates, a 2025 comparison or a record claim, so use it only as a sense of scale. Restaurants, bars, salons and gyms near the grounds and downtown see a visible seasonal spike.

Plan the profile updates early: special hours, a Stampede offer post and fresh photos. Avoid claiming a precise lift. No published figure was available for how much Stampede week raises demand for any single business type.

## How Do You Show Up Near Me in Calgary?

Google says more reviews and positive ratings can help local ranking, and that you cannot pay for a better local ranking. Adding or claiming your Business Profile costs nothing. Choose the right primary category, list your service area if you travel to customers, and add exterior photos so drivers can find the entrance.

Ask for reviews with a Google link or QR code, and never offer a reward for them, which Google's policy prohibits. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists every field to complete.

## How Should You Run Google Ads in Calgary?

Google's help says radius targeting needs at least 1 km, and recommends Presence or Interest for Search, with Presence only for sensitive cases or to exclude people elsewhere. Calgary is large, so set a radius around your premises or name your quadrants, and check the Search terms report to see which neighbourhoods really produce calls.

Add negative keywords for jobs, DIY and courses, and include plural forms because negatives do not match close variants. Benchmarks from WordStream by LocaliQ are US-only, so read your CPC from Google's Keyword Planner. Our [Google Ads setup guide](/blog/how-to-set-up-google-ads-for-your-small-business-in-2026-step-by-step-with-screenshots) walks through the account.

## What Are the Rules for Email and Text Marketing?

Canada's anti-spam law, CASL, applies to commercial electronic messages. Government guidance says express consent means someone has agreed, verbally or in writing, to receive your messages. Implied consent applies only in certain cases, such as a recent purchase or inquiry, and is time limited: up to 2 years, or 6 months for inquiries.

Every message must identify you with a name and mailing address plus a phone, email or website, and give clear unsubscribe instructions. Requests must be handled within 10 business days. Keep records of who agreed, when and how. This summary is not legal advice.

## FAQ

### How many people live in Calgary?

The City of Calgary estimates 1,558.7 thousand in April 2025 and forecasts 1,583.2 thousand for 2026. These are estimates, since there is no civic census.

### How much does Google Ads cost in Calgary?

No Calgary-specific price is published in the sources we opened. Use Keyword Planner for your service and start with a small test.

### Can I email customers after they buy?

Implied consent can apply to recent purchases for a limited period, but you must identify yourself and offer an unsubscribe. Get express consent where possible.

### Does the Stampede help small businesses?

Attendance was above 1.4 million in 2026. No source we read measures the effect on individual businesses.

### Do I need a website?

Your Business Profile can link to one, and ads need a landing page. A simple page with hours, services and a booking link is enough to start.
