Podcast advertising in Ireland is worth a small test for a local business with a clear offer and a reachable audience, but it is not a first channel. RED C's Listen Up Ireland 2026 study for IAB Ireland found that 76% of Irish adults aged 16 and over listen to digital audio in an average week, and 41% of those listeners use podcasts weekly. Treat it as a way to build awareness next to Google Business Profile and Search, not instead of them.

No source we could open publishes Irish podcast ad rates. The only price benchmarks below come from US-based networks, quoted in dollars, so use them to size a test, not to predict an invoice.

## How Big Is Podcast Listening in Ireland?

The 2026 study surveyed 1,032 adults online from 5 to 15 March 2026 and was published on 30 April 2026. Weekly digital audio reach was 76%, about 3.09 million people, unchanged from 2025. Podcast weekly use among those listeners was 41%, up from 22% in 2019.

The attention and action figures are claimed, not measured. Podcasts had the highest claimed ad attention of the digital audio formats, 35%, and ad trust was 36%. These are survey answers, so expect real response to be lower.

Reuters Institute's 2026 Digital News Report for Ireland adds context: it describes a "vibrant podcasting culture," with established news brands hosting most of the popular news podcasts. That suggests the biggest shows are national, which matters for a local shop.

## Which Podcasts Make Sense for a Local Business?

A national show wastes most of its reach on people who cannot visit you. Better fits are local-interest, county or city shows, and niche shows whose audience matches your customer, such as a running podcast for a physio clinic.

Ask every show for download or listen counts, location data and the date range they cover. If a host cannot show location data, price the ad as if only a fraction of listeners is nearby.

## What Does Podcast Advertising Cost?

Podcast ads are priced by CPM, the cost per 1,000 listens or downloads. Libsyn Ads lists baked-in host-read rates of $18 to $22 CPM for 30-second spots and $24 to $26 for 60-second spots, with Health & Fitness at $27. Acast's guide gives $15 to $30 for pre-recorded ads and $25 to $40 for host-read sponsorships.

These are US-oriented networks quoted in dollars, and the Acast page has an unclear date and an inconsistent FAQ range. Irish local shows may charge a flat fee per episode instead. Always ask what the CPM is based on: downloads in 30 days, or listens.

## Can You Buy Audio Ads Through Spotify in Ireland?

Possibly, but check. Spotify Ads says audio and video campaigns start at $250 and that you can create a voiceover for free inside Ad Studio. Its location-targeting help page lists options for the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Mexico, Spain and New Zealand and does not mention Ireland, so it does not confirm availability here.

A 2020 trade report listed Ireland among the markets where self-serve Ad Studio launched, but that is old. ## What Are the Disclosure Rules?

Ireland's advertising code is administered by the ASAI. In a ruling about an influencer promoting their own podcast on Instagram, the ASAI upheld the complaint because the posts were not labelled as advertising. It cited code 3.31, which requires that marketing communications be clear that they are marketing communications.

For a sponsor, the safe approach is a clear spoken line such as "this episode is sponsored by." If a host reads your ad as personal opinion, the unlabelled endorsement can create a complaint risk for both of you. Check the current ASAI code before you approve a script. The FTC guide on disclosure is a US source, so use it only as a comparison.

## What Does a Test Cost? A Worked Example

Every input is an **assumption**, using US dollar CPMs because no Irish rate is published.

| Item | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listens per episode (assumption) | Local show | 3,000 |
| CPM, host-read 60 seconds (assumption) | Within Acast and Libsyn ranges | $25 |
| Cost per episode | 3,000 / 1,000 x $25 | $75 |
| Episodes in test (assumption) | Chosen | 4 |
| Test cost | $75 x 4 | $300 |
| Response rate with offer code (assumption) | Chosen | 0.1% |
| Redemptions | 12,000 listens x 0.1% | 12 |
| Cost per redemption | $300 / 12 | $25 |

Whether $25 per redemption works depends on what a customer is worth. Change the response rate to 0.05% and the cost doubles. Use a unique offer code or landing page so you count real results, and run at least four episodes before judging.

## FAQ

### Is podcast advertising worth it for a small Irish business?

It can be, for businesses with a clear offer and a local or niche show. Claimed action is 19% among digital audio listeners in the 2026 survey, but that is self-reported. Run a small tracked test.

### How much does a podcast ad cost in Ireland?

No Irish rate card was found in the sources we opened. US networks list $15 to $40 per 1,000 listens depending on format.

### Do I have to label a podcast ad as an ad?

Yes. The ASAI code requires marketing communications to be recognisable as such. Ask the host to say it is sponsored.

### How do I measure results?

Give each show its own offer code, phone number or landing page URL. Ask how long after release the downloads are counted.

### Is Spotify Ad Studio available in Ireland?

Spotify's pages we opened do not confirm it. Check the sign-up page directly.
