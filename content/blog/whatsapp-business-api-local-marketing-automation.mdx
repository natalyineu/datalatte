## What Is the Difference Between the App and the API?

The WhatsApp Business app is the free app for one phone. The API (the WhatsApp Business Platform) connects WhatsApp to software, so a system can send reminders, route messages to staff, or run an AI assistant. Pricing, templates and rules described here apply to the platform.

To begin, Meta's quickstart asks for a Facebook or managed Meta account, developer registration, a business portfolio and a WhatsApp-enabled device for tests. A temporary access token expires quickly, so Meta tells you to create a system user for a permanent token. The quickstart page does not describe business verification, so check Meta's current requirements for your account.

## How Does WhatsApp API Pricing Work?

Meta's pricing page says businesses pay only for delivered messages, and messages from users to businesses are free. Rates depend on the message category and the recipient's country code.

| Type | What it is | Can be sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing template | Promotions | Any time, with opt-in |
| Utility template | Transactional updates | Any time |
| Authentication template | Identity codes | Any time |
| Service message | Reply to a customer | Inside the 24-hour window |

When a customer writes first, a 24-hour customer service window opens, and each new customer message resets it. Outside it you can only send templates. If a customer starts a chat from a Click to WhatsApp ad, a free entry point window of up to 7 days can open after your reply, and messages other than Meta Business Agent ones are not charged during it. ## What Changes on October 1, 2026?

Meta lists three changes. Each business phone number gets 1,000 free service messages per month, with no rollover, and charges start with the 1,001st. Utility messages sent inside an open customer service window, which were free from July 1, 2025, become chargeable. Rate card updates also take effect. Meta updates pricing only on the first day of a quarter.

The page also notes rate changes in several markets in 2026, for example a higher UK marketing rate from July 1, 2026. It gives no per-message figure for the US or UK, so read the downloadable rate cards. Our [WhatsApp marketing guide](/blog/whatsapp-business-marketing-local-business) covers the free app.

## What Does a Message Provider Charge?

Many small businesses connect through a provider. Twilio's page lists a fee of $0.005 per message for inbound and outbound messages, a $0.001 fee for messages that fail, and Meta's pass-through template fees. Utility and authentication messages start at $0.0034 each from Meta, according to Twilio, and utility messages in an open window carry no Meta charge on that calculator.

Prices change, so check the provider's page on the day you sign up. Ask what the fee is for a failed message and for inbound replies.

## What Are the Compliance Rules?

WhatsApp's business policy says you may contact people only if you have opt-in permission, and must honor opt-outs. Outside the 24-hour window you may only send approved templates, and WhatsApp can review, pause or reject them. You may use automation within the window, but automated responders must provide a clear escalation path, such as a human agent, phone, email or in-store visit.

Do not ask customers for full card numbers or other sensitive identifiers in chat. Keep a record of when and how each customer opted in. ## What Does a Month of Reminders Cost? A Worked Example

Inputs are **assumptions**. A clinic sends one utility reminder to 1,200 patients, receives 300 replies, and uses Twilio.

| Item | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Provider fee, outbound | 1,200 x $0.005 | $6.00 |
| Provider fee, inbound | 300 x $0.005 | $1.50 |
| Meta utility fee | 1,200 x rate for your country | Not stated here, see the rate card |
| Possible Meta utility floor | 1,200 x $0.0034 | $4.08 (Twilio's "starting at" figure) |

The Twilio fees add up to $7.50. Adding the "starting at" Meta fee gives roughly $11.58, a floor, not a quote, because Meta's rate varies by country. Compare it with the cost of a no-show before deciding whether to automate.

## FAQ

### Do I need the API for a small shop?

Not always. The free WhatsApp Business app covers low volume. The API suits automation, shared inboxes and larger lists.

### Can I send promotions to anyone?

No. WhatsApp requires opt-in permission, and marketing templates must be approved.

### What is the 24-hour window?

It opens when a customer messages you. Within it you can send non-template replies, and each new customer message resets it.

### Is WhatsApp automation allowed?

Yes within the window, as long as there is a clear way to reach a human.

### How much does a WhatsApp marketing message cost?

It depends on category and country. Meta publishes the rates in its rate cards, not on the pricing page.
