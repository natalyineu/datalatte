CTV advertising is typically priced by CPM, meaning the cost per 1,000 ad views, and commonly quoted CPMs for connected TV run well above those for social or display ads, often in the tens of dollars per thousand. For a local business, that usually means a test campaign starts in the hundreds of dollars per month, with a few thousand dollars per month for sustained reach. Exact prices depend on the platform, targeting, season and how you buy, so always request a current quote before you plan.

This guide explains how CTV is priced, what pushes costs up, and how to turn a CPM into a realistic cost per customer.

## What Is CTV Advertising?

**CTV (Connected TV)** refers to smart TVs, streaming devices such as Roku, Fire TV and Apple TV, and game consoles that stream video over the internet. Unlike traditional TV, CTV allows digital targeting and reporting. If you are unsure how it differs from OTT, read [CTV vs OTT: what's the difference](/blog/ctv-vs-ott-whats-the-difference-and-which-should-you-buy). In short, CTV is the screen, and OTT is the content delivered over the internet.

## How CTV Ads Are Priced

Most CTV inventory is sold on a CPM basis. To estimate how many people you can reach, divide your budget by the CPM and multiply by 1,000.

| Budget | If the CPM is $25 | If the CPM is $35 | If the CPM is $45 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $500 | about 20,000 views | about 14,300 views | about 11,100 views |
| $1,000 | about 40,000 views | about 28,600 views | about 22,200 views |
| $2,500 | about 100,000 views | about 71,400 views | about 55,600 views |

These are arithmetic examples, not predictions. Your actual CPM depends on the factors below. Note that "views" are impressions, not unique people, so the same household can account for several.

## What Affects CTV Ad Pricing

1. **Platform and inventory.** Premium streaming services and live sport generally cost more than free ad-supported or FAST channels. Platforms such as Hulu, Roku, Amazon and YouTube all price differently, and prices change often.
2. **Targeting.** Narrow location, demographic or audience targeting typically raises CPM because fewer impressions qualify. Tight local targeting also means smaller reach, so check estimated audience size.
3. **Ad format and length.** Common formats are 15- and 30-second video ads. Cost can differ by placement and format.
4. **Buying model.** Direct deals offer predictable placements but can have higher minimums. Programmatic buying, which uses real-time bidding, is more flexible. See [real-time bidding explained](/blog/real-time-bidding-explained-for-non-technical-business-owners).
5. **Seasonality.** Prices usually rise in busy periods such as the end of the year and big sports events.

## How Much Should You Spend on CTV Ads?

There is no universal figure. Platforms and ad partners set their own minimums, and some self-serve options have low minimums while managed services often require more. Ask each vendor for its current minimum before you plan.

A sensible way to decide is to start from your total marketing budget. Search intent channels such as [Google Ads](/services/google-ads) and your Google Business Profile usually deserve funding first, because they capture people already looking for you. CTV works better as an awareness layer on top. Many small businesses put a minority share of their budget into CTV, then adjust based on results.

<Callout type="coffee">Rule of thumb: if you cannot afford to run CTV for several weeks with enough frequency to be remembered, spend that money on search ads first and come back to CTV when your basics are working.</Callout>

### Worked Example: Turning a CPM into a Cost per Customer

The numbers below are an illustration of the math, not a real campaign.

Suppose a coffee shop spends $600 in a month at a $35 CPM:

- Impressions = 600 / 35 x 1,000 = about 17,000 views
- If those views reach roughly 5,000 households, each sees the ad around 3 or 4 times
- If the campaign leads to 20 new customers (tracked through a promo code), the cost per new customer is $30

If an average new customer spends $15 on a first visit but returns regularly, you can compare $30 with the customer's lifetime value rather than with the first sale. The point is to estimate your own break-even: how many new customers must the campaign bring in to pay for itself?

## How to Keep CTV Costs Under Control

### Use tight local targeting

For location-based businesses, limit delivery to the area your customers actually travel from, such as a few miles around your shop. Extra targeting layers can add cost, so test whether they improve results.

### Cap frequency

Set a frequency cap so the same household does not see your ad dozens of times in a week.

### Test your creative

Compare 15-second and 30-second versions, and different offers. Make your business name clear, say or show the web address, and make one call to action.

### Track conversions

Use a promo code, a dedicated landing page or QR code, and Google Analytics and conversion tracking. Ask every new customer how they heard about you. If you cannot prove a return, you cannot justify the spend.

## FAQ

**Q: How much does CTV advertising cost per month for a small business?**
A: It depends on the platform, targeting and the minimum spend. Self-serve and programmatic options can allow a test in the hundreds of dollars per month, while managed services typically need more. Request current quotes from a few vendors.

**Q: What is a good CPM for CTV ads?**
A: CTV CPMs are generally higher than for social or display ads and vary by platform and targeting. A "good" CPM is one that delivers customers at a cost per customer you can afford, so judge results by that, not CPM alone.

**Q: Can I run CTV ads on a small budget?**
A: Yes, but a small budget buys fewer views, so it works best with tight local targeting or retargeting people who have already visited your website. Expect to run it for several weeks before judging.

**Q: How do I know whether a CTV viewer visited my business?**
A: Use a unique promo code, a QR code to a landing page you control, a tracked phone number, and a "how did you hear about us?" question. Watching branded search volume in Google Search Console can also show a lift.

**Q: Is CTV better than Google Ads for local businesses?**
A: They do different jobs. Google Ads captures people actively searching, while CTV builds awareness. For most local businesses, Google Ads and a strong Google Business Profile come first.
