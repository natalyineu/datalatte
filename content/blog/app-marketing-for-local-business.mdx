Most local businesses do not need their own mobile app. A mobile-friendly website, a complete Google Business Profile and a booking or loyalty tool you rent cover what customers ask for. A custom app makes sense only when people already come back often, such as a coffee shop with a daily regulars base, and when you can pay for store fees and updates every year. Pew's 2026 survey shows 91% of US adults own a smartphone, so the audience exists. The question is whether an app is the best way to reach it.

This guide is for owners of cafés, salons, studios and shops in English-speaking markets. Figures come from the sources at the end. A typical custom-development price is not published by any neutral source, so none is given here.

## What Does It Cost to Publish an App?

The store fees are small and published. Apple lists the Developer Program at $99 a year. Google Play charges a one-time $25 registration fee, and personal accounts must also complete app testing requirements before an app can go live.

The fees are not the main cost. Design, development, testing, updates for new phone software and support are not priced by Apple or Google, and no neutral source publishes an average. Get at least two written quotes and ask what the yearly cost is after launch. Budget for that ongoing cost, not just the build.

## Will Apple Accept a Simple Business App?

Not always. Apple's review guideline 4.2 says an app should include features, content and UI that "elevate it beyond a repackaged website". Guideline 4.2.2 adds that, other than catalogs, apps should not primarily be marketing materials, advertisements, web clippings or a collection of links.

Guideline 4.2.6 also says apps created from a commercialized template or app generation service are rejected unless the provider of the content submits them directly, with an exception for aggregated "picker" apps. If you plan to use a cheap app builder, ask the vendor how they handle this rule before you pay.

## Which Cheaper Options Reach the Same Customers?

Try these before commissioning an app:

| Need | App-free option | Source of the claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Be found nearby | Complete Google Business Profile with hours, photos, review replies | Google Business Profile Help |
| Work on any phone | Responsive website | Pew: 91% smartphone ownership |

| Bookings, orders, points | Rented booking or loyalty tool with its own customer app | Vendor pricing pages |

Google's help describes the profile as the place for address, hours, attributes, photos and review replies. A Progressive Web App, per web.dev, is a website with modern capabilities and installability from one codebase. ## When Does a Custom App Make Sense?

Consider an app when three things are true. Customers already return at least weekly. You have a feature a website cannot give you, such as stored payment, a points balance or pre-ordering. And you can fund updates for years.

Coffee shops are the clearest case, because repeat visits are frequent. Even then, test demand first. ## The Math: How Many Downloads Pay Back an App?

All inputs are **assumptions** for a café, not benchmarks.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Build cost | $15,000 |
| Yearly upkeep and store fees | $3,000 |
| Extra profit per active app user per year | $60 |
| Break-even in year 1 | ($15,000 + $3,000) / $60 = 300 active users |

Active users are people who open the app and order, not downloads. If your shop serves 150 regulars, a custom app cannot pay back in year one under these inputs. If the same shop rents a loyalty tool for a few hundred dollars a year, the break-even drops sharply. Replace each number with your own quote and margin.

## FAQ

### Do local businesses need their own app?
Usually not. A fast website and a complete Google profile reach most customers. An app helps when customers return often and you need features like stored payment or points.

### How much does it cost to publish an app on the stores?
Apple's Developer Program is $99 per year and Google Play has a one-time $25 fee. Development costs are not published by a neutral source, so request quotes.

### Can Apple reject my app?
Yes. Apple's guidelines reject apps that are only a repackaged website or a collection of links, and apps made from templates unless the content provider submits them.

### What is a Progressive Web App?
Per web.dev, it is a website that uses modern APIs to offer better reliability and installability across devices from a single codebase.

### What should I try first?
Fix your Google Business Profile and website, then add a rented loyalty or booking tool. Build an app only if repeat demand is proven.
