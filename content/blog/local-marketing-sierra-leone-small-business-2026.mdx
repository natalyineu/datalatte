Facebook is the main online channel for a Sierra Leone business, but the online audience is small. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 1.85 million internet users, 20.8% of 8.86 million people, and a Facebook ad reach of 1.3 million. Most customers are offline, so online marketing works best next to word of mouth, signage and mobile payments.

This guide uses DataReportal, the World Bank and payment-system reports. No public ad price data for Sierra Leone, so there is no CPC or CPM here. Examples marked as assumptions are illustrations.

## How Many People in Sierra Leone Are Online?

Few. DataReportal lists 1.85 million internet users in October 2025, up 53 thousand from a year earlier, with 7.02 million people offline. There are 8.94 million mobile connections, 101% of the population, and 83.4% of them are classed as broadband.

Social media user identities total 1.30 million, 14.7% of the population and 70.4% of internet users. That number grew 25.3% in a year. The report warns that identities may not equal unique people, so treat it as an upper estimate.

## Which Platforms Can You Advertise On?

Advertising audience estimates from DataReportal, late 2025:

| Platform | Ad reach | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 1.30 million | 14.7% |
| Messenger | 305 thousand | 3.4% |
| LinkedIn (members) | 210 thousand | 2.4% |
| Instagram | 127 thousand | 1.4% |
| X | 34.2 thousand | 0.4% |

Facebook is roughly ten times Instagram. For consumer businesses such as shops, salons and food sellers, a Facebook Page is the logical base. The report gives no WhatsApp or TikTok figures for Sierra Leone, so we make no claim about them.

## What Is the Economy Doing in 2026?

The World Bank says real GDP grew 4.8% in 2025 and projects 4.0% in 2026. Inflation reached a low of 4.4% in December 2025, then rose to 15.7% in August 2026, from 6.4% in January. Rising prices squeeze customers and raise your own costs.

Price your offers in a way you can keep for a few weeks, and check supplier costs before you promise a discount. Keep ad budgets small and review weekly while inflation is moving this fast.

## Can Customers Pay You by Mobile Money?

Often, yes, and it matters for a business with few card payments. The International Growth Centre named Orange Money and Afrimoney as the mobile network operators' providers, and reported that 24% of adults had an account at a financial institution or mobile money provider in 2019, 12% with banks only. That figure is dated.

A national instant payment switch was launched on 13 February 2025, with 7 banks and 2 mobile operators connected and an integration deadline of 1 April 2025, according to Finance for Development. It allows real-time transfers across banks and mobile money. We could not verify current user counts.

## Can You Run Google Ads in Sierra Leone?

Treat it as uncertain. Google's payment settings page lists Sierra Leone among countries and territories where prepay is not available. We opened that page, but it may be out of date, and it does not say whether you can run ads with other payment methods.

Google search volume is also limited when only one in five people use the internet. Start with a Facebook Page and a Google Business Profile, as in our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist). Paid search can wait until you have confirmed billing.

## How Do You Mix Online and Offline?

Use the channels customers already use. Put your Page name and payment number on the shop sign and receipts. Ask each customer how they found you and note the answer.

Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) explains how to split a small budget, and [do Facebook ads work for local businesses](/blog/do-facebook-ads-actually-work-for-local-businesses-data-backed-answer) reviews the evidence, mostly from other countries. Use them as method guides, not Sierra Leone benchmarks.

## Worked Example: A Small Facebook Test

All inputs are **assumptions**, because we have no local ad cost data.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget (assumption) | Whatever you can lose, such as one week of takings from a slow day |
| Duration | 14 days |
| Offer (assumption) | One product with a pickup time and a mobile money payment option |
| Success measure | Messages that become paid orders |
| Tracking | Ask every customer where they heard of you |

After two weeks, divide the spend by paid orders. If the result is above your profit per order, change the offer before spending more. For ad budget logic, see [Facebook ads budgets for local businesses](/blog/facebook-ads-budget-local-business-how-much-to-spend).

## FAQ

### What percentage of Sierra Leoneans use the internet?

DataReportal reports 20.8% in October 2025, or 1.85 million people.

### Is Facebook or Instagram better for a Sierra Leone business?

Facebook reaches 1.3 million against Instagram's 127 thousand, so Facebook is the larger audience.

### Is there a CPC benchmark for Sierra Leone?

There is no public, sourced benchmark. Measure your own cost per paid order.

### Can I rely on Google Ads?

Not until you confirm billing. Google lists Sierra Leone among places without prepay, per a help page that may be dated.
