A coffee shop website can be one page: opening hours, address with a map, a menu in plain text, one button for ordering or calling, and a few real photos. Make it load fast on a phone. Google's Core Web Vitals call a page good when the largest element loads in 2.5 seconds or less, input response is 200 milliseconds or less and layout shift is 0.1 or less, measured at the 75th percentile of visits.

This guide is for independent cafés in English-speaking markets. Every number comes from a source listed at the end. No conversion lift is promised, because no neutral source publishes one for coffee shops.

## What Does a Coffee Shop Homepage Need?

Start with what a hungry person nearby wants. Put these items above the fold on a phone, in this order:

- Open now or closing time, plus holiday hours
- Address with a tap-to-open map
- One main action: order ahead, call, or get directions
- A menu as real text, not only a PDF or photo
- Two or three photos of your actual shop and drinks

Research from Nielsen Norman Group found that about four in five test users scanned every new page and only about one in six read word by word. Short lines and clear headings beat long copy. See our [online menu setup guide](/blog/online-menu-website-restaurant-setup) for the menu part.

## How Should You Handle the Menu?

Publish the menu as HTML text on the page. Text is readable on small screens, can be searched and can be updated in a minute. A PDF is slow to open on a phone and hard to edit.

Google also lets a food business give one menu link on its Business Profile through Edit profile, per Google's help. Point that link at your web menu so both places match. If your prices change, update the page and the profile on the same day.

## How Do You Make the Site Show Up Locally?

Keep your name, address and phone identical on the site and the Business Profile. Add LocalBusiness structured data. Google's documentation lists name and address as required and opening hours, menu URL and cuisine as recommended. Violations of its guidelines can lead to a manual action, so mark up only what visitors can see.

Then work on the profile itself, which is where most local map traffic starts. Our [coffee shop Business Profile guide](/blog/coffee-shop-google-business-profile-optimization) covers it, and the [schema markup article](/blog/schema-markup-local-business-website) shows the code.

## Where Should the Order or Booking Link Go?

Place one primary button near the top and repeat it after the menu. Google's profile help says you can add up to 10 links for each transaction type, such as food orders, and mark one as "Business preferred". Use the same link on your site so customers see one clear path.

Avoid a stack of equal buttons. A visitor on a phone wants one answer: order, call or come in. Test that the button works on a real phone every month.

## How Fast Should the Site Be?

Use Google's thresholds as the target: LCP 2.5 seconds or less, INP 200 milliseconds or less, CLS 0.1 or less, at the 75th percentile. The usual causes of slow cafe sites are oversized photos and heavy sliders. Compress photos, set image width and height so the layout does not jump, and drop auto-playing video. Our [website speed guide](/blog/website-speed-optimization-small-business) lists the fixes.

## FAQ

### Does a coffee shop need a website if it has Instagram and Google Maps?
A site gives you a menu, hours and order link you control. Google's profile can link to it. Social posts are harder to search and keep current.

### What should the homepage show first?
Hours, address, one main action and a text menu. Photos support these, they do not replace them.

### How fast should the site load?
Google's good targets are LCP 2.5 seconds, INP 200 milliseconds and CLS 0.1 or better at the 75th percentile of visits.

### Do I need structured data?
It is optional. Google lists name and address as required for LocalBusiness and hours, menu and cuisine as recommended.

### How much does a coffee shop website cost?
Prices vary by builder and change often, and no neutral benchmark exists for cafés. Compare current plan pages and your own time before deciding.
