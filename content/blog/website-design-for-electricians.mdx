An electrician's website should put a tap-to-call number and your license details on the first screen, give each core service its own page, and offer a short request or booking form that works on a phone. Field-service tools that add online booking start at $29 a month (Jobber Core, annual) and $59 a month (Housecall Pro Basic, annual).

This guide is for electricians and small electrical contractors. Prices were checked on 31 October 2026 on the vendors' own pages. Examples are labelled as assumptions; no conversion benchmark for electrician sites was found.

## What Should an Electrician's Home Page Show?

Visitors usually need one of three things: urgent help, a quote for planned work, or proof you are licensed. Build the first screen around those.

- A phone number that is tappable on mobile.
- The area you serve, by city name.
- Your license information. Rules differ by state; the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation says non-exempt electrical work must be done by licensed people and through a licensed electrical contractor. Check your own state board for how to display a number.
- One main button: call, request a quote or book.

Do not promise response times you cannot meet. A "2-hour response" line that you miss twice does more damage than no promise.

## Which Service Pages Should You Build First?

Give each high-value job its own page, such as panel upgrades, EV charger installation, rewiring, lighting and emergency repairs. Each page should state what the job involves, what the customer should prepare, how you quote, the areas you cover and the next step. Our [website design for plumbers](/blog/website-design-for-plumbers) shows the same structure for a neighbouring trade.

Do not invent prices. If you cannot give a fixed price, say what affects it (access, panel age, permit needs). Our [local business website checklist](/blog/local-business-website-checklist) lists the other basics.

## Which Booking Tool Should You Add?

If you already use field-service software, check whether its online booking works on your site. Published prices:

| --- | --- | --- |
| Jobber Core | $29/mo annual, $39/mo 1-year, $49/mo no commitment; 1 user | Listed: "Book and schedule jobs online" |
| Jobber Connect | from $99/mo annual, 1 user | Included |
| Housecall Pro Basic | $59/mo annual, $79/mo monthly; 1 user | Included |
| Housecall Pro Essentials | $149/mo annual, $189/mo monthly; 5 users | Included |
| Calendly Standard | $10 per seat per month (yearly billing) | A scheduling link, not a field-service tool |

Calendly only schedules; Jobber and Housecall Pro also handle quotes, jobs and invoices. Square Appointments pricing could not be loaded when checked, so it is not quoted.

## Should You Use Google Local Services Ads Too?

Local Services Ads are a separate paid listing. Google's help page says you pay only for leads related to your business and only when a customer gets in touch from the ad. Electricians are on the list of eligible categories, but availability depends on your area, and new advertisers must complete Google's screening and verification process to earn the Google Verified badge. Google does not publish a lead price on that page; costs vary by area, so test with a small budget. Your site still matters because many people check it before calling. See our [Google Ads guide for contractors](/blog/google-ads-for-contractors).

## How Fast Should the Site Be?

Google's Core Web Vitals targets are LCP within 2.5 seconds, INP of 200 ms or less and CLS of 0.1 or less at the 75th percentile. Google indexes the mobile version, which should hold the same content as desktop. Test your page for free in PageSpeed Insights and compress job photos before uploading.

## Worked Example: What Does a Booked Job Cost in Software?

All inputs are **assumptions**.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Tool | Housecall Pro Basic, $59/mo annual |
| Online requests that become jobs | 8 a month |
| Average job value | $350 |
| Software cost per won job | $59 / 8 = $7.38 |
| Monthly job revenue from online requests | 8 x $350 = $2,800 |

The software is about 2% of that revenue. Your close rate and job size will differ. Track where each call came from to know whether the site is the reason.

## FAQ

### How much does an electrician website cost?

A hosted builder starts at $19 a month on Squarespace annual billing (see our [personal trainer site guide](/blog/website-design-for-personal-trainers) for the builder table). Booking software is extra. Custom work is quoted by the designer.

### Do I need online booking?

Not always. For emergencies, a tap-to-call number is faster. For planned jobs, a request form works.

### Can I use Calendly?

Yes, for appointment slots, at $10 per seat on yearly billing. It does not handle quotes or invoices.

### Does my site help me rank on Google Maps?

It supports your Business Profile but does not replace it. See [marketing for electricians](/blog/marketing-for-electricians).

### Should I show prices?

Show what affects price, and a starting price only if you can honour it.
