For most local businesses, Yahoo search ads are not a separate channel to set up. Microsoft's own ad pages say Microsoft Search ads can appear on Bing and on search partner sites such as Yahoo, AOL, DuckDuckGo and Ecosia. You buy them in Microsoft Advertising, usually by importing a working Google Ads campaign. Yahoo is small: StatCounter shows 2.63% of US search in September 2026.

This guide explains what you can verify, what to test, and what cannot be stated because no source publishes it, such as typical Yahoo click costs.

## Where Do Yahoo Search Ads Come From?

Microsoft's search ads page lists Yahoo, AOL, DuckDuckGo and Ecosia as search partners where ads can appear, in addition to Bing itself. Microsoft Advertising community answers describe the network as Bing, AOL and Yahoo properties plus syndicated partner sites, and say you can narrow distribution in your account settings. Those community answers are secondary, so check the options in your own account.

The practical result: one Microsoft Advertising campaign can reach Bing and Yahoo users. Microsoft Advertising also offers a separate display placement called the Microsoft Audience Network, covered in our [Microsoft Audience Network guide](/blog/microsoft-audience-network-local-business).

## Is Yahoo Big Enough to Matter?

Alone, no. Worldwide StatCounter data for September 2026 shows Google at 89.94%, Bing at 5.29% and Yahoo at 1.36%. In the US, Google has 85.19%, Bing 9.91% and Yahoo 2.63%. Bing and Yahoo together are about 12.5% of US search.

That share can still matter for a business whose customers skew toward desktop users or older searchers, but the sources used here do not give audience age data for Yahoo. Treat that idea as a hypothesis to test, not a fact.

## What Does It Cost?

Neither Microsoft's search page nor the Yahoo advertising site gave a current click price or minimum budget when checked on 31 October 2026. The Yahoo advertising page returned a server error. Any figure like "30 to 60% cheaper than Google" is not published, so do not plan around it.

You set your own daily budget and bids in Microsoft Advertising. Microsoft says you do not need a large budget to start. Our [Bing Ads cost guide](/blog/bing-ads-microsoft-advertising-cost-local-business) covers what Microsoft Advertising costs in more detail.

## How Do You Test Yahoo and Bing Cheaply?

1. Take one proven Google Ads campaign for a service with clear value, such as emergency repair or a first-visit offer.
2. Import it into Microsoft Advertising and keep the same location targets.
3. Keep the same call and form tracking, so results are comparable.
4. Set a small daily cap you can lose.
5. Review after 60 days using cost per lead, not clicks.

Our guide to [Microsoft (Bing) Ads for local business](/blog/bing-ads-microsoft-advertising-local-business) walks through the import step.

Worked example, all inputs are assumptions: a locksmith spends $150 over 60 days and gets 6 calls, which is $25 per call. On Google the same service costs $40 per call. The test passes on cost, but with six calls the sample is small, so the owner runs another 60 days before moving budget.

## What About Yahoo DSP?

Yahoo DSP is a separate programmatic buying platform, not search ads. This article did not verify its minimum spend, fees or local targeting options, so none are quoted. If you want to understand how programmatic buying differs, read our [programmatic vs Google Display guide](/blog/programmatic-vs-google-display-ads-whats-the-difference).

## FAQ

### Is Yahoo Ads dead?

Yahoo still has a measurable share of search: 2.63% in the US in September 2026 per StatCounter. Its search ads run through Microsoft's network, so the decision is really about Microsoft Advertising.

### Can I choose to run only on Yahoo?

Community answers say you can narrow distribution to Bing, AOL and Yahoo sites or to partner sites, but not to a single site. Confirm the settings in your account.

### Is Yahoo cheaper than Google?

No source checked here publishes a Yahoo click price, so there is no verified comparison.

### How much budget should I test with?

There is no official minimum in the sources used. Pick an amount you can lose and plan for at least 60 days, as in the example.

### Can I track calls?

Use call tracking in Microsoft Advertising or a third-party tool. Check the current feature list in your account.
