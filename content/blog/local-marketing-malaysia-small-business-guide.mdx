A Malaysian local business should start with a complete Google Business Profile, a WhatsApp Business number for bookings, and one social platform where its customers already are, then add paid ads once those work. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 35.4 million internet users in Malaysia (98.0% of the population) and 30.7 million social media user identities. Language is the main local decision: Malay, English, Chinese and Tamil audiences often need different copy.

## Which Platforms Reach Malaysian Customers?

DataReportal's late-2025 advertising reach estimates are 23.6 million for YouTube, 23.0 million for Facebook, 16.1 million for Instagram and 30.7 million for TikTok users aged 18 and over. These are ad reach figures, not active user counts, and Meta and Google say they are not proxies for monthly or daily users. The TikTok number covers adults only, so it is not directly comparable.

What the numbers say: Facebook and YouTube reach about two thirds of the population by ad reach, Instagram less than half. Pick the platform where your customers are, not the biggest. A hair salon in an urban area may do better on Instagram and TikTok. A neighbourhood restaurant may reach more people through Facebook and Google Maps.

## Which Language Should You Use?

Use the language your customer uses to search and chat, and test it. We could not open a current official source with Malaysia's language shares, so we do not quote percentages here. The practical approach is to look at who already buys from you and which language they write in.

A sensible start is English plus Bahasa Malaysia on your Business Profile and website, then add Chinese or Tamil where your area and customers call for it. Do not machine-translate offers without a native speaker reading them. Run separate ad groups per language so you can see which one brings bookings.

## How Do You Use WhatsApp for Bookings?

WhatsApp Business gives a business profile, tools to present items and prices, and automatic greeting and away messages, according to its product page. The page does not state whether the app is free, so check the app store listing.

Put the booking number on your Business Profile, ads and website, and answer quickly. Our [WhatsApp automation guide](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-local-marketing-automation) covers replies at scale. For rules on collecting numbers and sending promotions, read Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act guidance: secondary law-firm summaries say individuals can refuse direct marketing, but confirm details with the official regulator or a lawyer.

## What About Halal Certification?

If you sell food or drink, customers may look for halal status. Malaysian press reported in December 2024 that the Housing and Local Government Minister said the federal government encourages food and beverage operators to apply for halal certification but does not force them, and that councils should consult JAKIM first. Local council rules can differ, so ask your own council.

Applications go through JAKIM's MYeHALAL system according to business guides, which are secondary. Fees and steps change, so use the official portal before you act. Never display a halal logo you have not been issued.

## FAQ

### Which platform should a Malaysian small business start with?

Google Business Profile and WhatsApp Business first, since they cost little. Then choose Facebook, Instagram or TikTok based on where your customers are.

### Should I advertise in Malay, English or Chinese?

In the language your customers use. Start with the one or two you can do well, and add others when you can have a native speaker review.

### How much do Google Ads cost in Malaysia?

No Malaysian industry benchmark was found in the sources we opened. Set a small test budget and measure your own cost per click and per booking.

### Do I need halal certification to advertise food?

Reports say federal policy encourages but does not require certification, while local rules may differ. Do not use a halal logo unless it has been issued to you.

### Is TikTok worth it in Malaysia?

DataReportal estimates 30.7 million TikTok ad reach among adults, so the audience is large. Test organic videos first, then small paid tests.
