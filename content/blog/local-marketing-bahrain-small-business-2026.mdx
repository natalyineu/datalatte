## Who Are You Marketing To in Bahrain?

Bahrain had 1.65 million people in October 2025, up 2.2%. 90.2% live in urban areas, the median age is 33.4, and the population is 62.0% male and 38.0% female. Internet users number 1.64 million (99.0%).

Cellular connections number 2.61 million (158%). Social media identities total 1.31 million (79.4%). A small, almost fully online, mostly urban market means one tight area covers most customers.

## Which Platforms Reach People in Bahrain?

DataReportal takes platform reach from each platform's own advertising tools. It says these figures are not monthly active users and may not be unique people, so use them to compare channels, not to count customers.

| Platform | Ad reach, late 2025 | Share of population | Change over the year |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram | 1.10M | 66.6% | +76.0k (+7.4%) |
| Snapchat | 1.10M | 66.3% | +30.0k (+2.8%) |
| TikTok (18+ only) | 1.31M | 101.4% of adults (flagged) | +159k (+13.7%) |
| Facebook | 773K | 46.8% | +10.6k (+1.4%) |

Instagram and Snapchat are level in reach. Both fell from July to October 2025 (Instagram -4.3%, Snapchat -5.8%), so treat the short-term movement as a signal to test, not a trend. TikTok's figure is above 100% of adults, which DataReportal flags as an anomaly, so do not rely on it. Facebook is the smallest of the four and grew only 1.4%.

YouTube is not shown in this guide's table. Local platforms and messaging apps are not covered by DataReportal's article, so no figures are given for them.

## How Should Google Ads Language and Location Work in Bahrain?

Google's Ads Help says that from September 2026 the campaign-level language setting is being removed for Search campaigns, and Search ads will match on the language of the ads themselves. For searches with a clear language, Google prioritises ads in that language. Bahrain is multilingual in practice, so write separate Arabic and English ad groups, each with its own headlines and landing page, and let your results show which converts better.

For location, Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km and recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting as best practice for Search, while presence-only fits when you want to avoid people outside your target area. Set a radius around your premises, then read the report to see which areas convert.

No Bahrain-specific click costs were found in the sources checked, so every cost figure below is an assumption.

## What Does a Test Budget Return? The Math

These rows are **assumptions** for illustration, in US dollars. They are not Bahrain benchmarks. Replace each with your own data after a month.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget (assumption) | $300 | $300 | $300 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | $1.00 | $0.60 | $0.40 |
| Clicks | 300 | 500 | 750 |
| Click to enquiry rate (assumption) | 3% | 5% | 7% |
| Enquiries | 9 | 25 | 53 |
| Enquiry to booking rate (assumption) | 30% | 40% | 50% |
| Bookings | 2.7 | 10 | 26.3 |
| Ad cost per booking | $111 | $30 | $11 |
| First-visit value (assumption) | $40 | $40 | $40 |
| Revenue minus ad spend | -$192 | +$100 | +$750 |

Counts use unrounded values. Revenue is not profit: staff, product and rent come out of it. A first visit often does not pay back by itself, which is why repeat visits matter.

Your break-even click price is the first-visit value times the enquiry rate times the booking rate. At $40, 5% and 40%, that is 40 x 0.05 x 0.40 = $0.80 per click. If your clicks cost more on average, the first visit loses money and you need repeat bookings to justify it. The biggest lever is usually the booking rate: answer messages and calls within minutes.
