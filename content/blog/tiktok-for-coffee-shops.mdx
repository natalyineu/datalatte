Start with free organic videos, tag your location and use only licensed business music. Add paid ads later, once videos bring people in.

## Who Is on TikTok?

Pew's 2026 fact sheet, from a survey of 5,511 US adults between 30 January and 18 June 2026, finds YouTube used by 84% of adults, Facebook by 70% and Instagram by 51%. TikTok's share is smaller overall but strong with the young: 65% of those 18 to 29. Instagram reaches 79% of the same age group.

If your regulars are office workers aged 30 to 50, Instagram and Facebook may reach more of them. If you sit near a campus, TikTok may match your customers. Ask your counter staff where customers say they saw you.

## What Does TikTok Give a Local Business?

In February 2026 TikTok announced a Local Feed tab for nearby content, including posts about restaurants, shopping and small businesses. Posts show based on location, topic and posting time. Precise GPS sharing is off by default and being introduced gradually across the US, so reach varies by user.

TikTok also lets you add a location to a post by tapping Location on the post screen, though availability depends on region. Tag your shop on every video so people can find the address.

## Which Music Can a Coffee Shop Use?

Only the Commercial Music Library. TikTok says businesses cannot use the general music library for commercial use and should use the Commercial Music Library, a pre-cleared library of about 1 million songs, free for businesses, for organic content and ads alike. Using other users' sounds may require a licence.

Check that each video uses a track from that library. The page does not describe penalties, so confirm in the library before posting. Our [coffee shop TikTok organic playbook](/blog/coffee-shop-tiktok-marketing-guide) covers formats and setup.

## The Math: How Many Visits Pay for the Time?

All inputs are **assumptions** for an illustrative café, not benchmarks.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Videos per week | 3 |
| Minutes per video (film, edit, post) | 45 |
| Hours per month | about 9.75 |
| Value of your time per hour | $20 |
| Monthly time cost | about $195 |
| Average ticket | $6 |
| Gross margin on a ticket | 65% ($3.90) |
| Visits needed to cover time cost | 50 |

Fifty extra visits a month at a $3.90 margin cover $195 of time. That is about two a day. Count visits by asking customers or by giving a TikTok-only code, and stop if you cannot reach the number in three months.

## FAQ

### Do I need a lot of followers?
No source sets a follower threshold for local results. Local Feed shows posts by location, topic and time, so tag your shop and post consistently.

### Is TikTok better than Instagram for coffee shops?
It depends on your customers' ages. Pew shows TikTok stronger for 18 to 29 and Instagram stronger overall (51% of adults).

### Can I use trending songs?
Businesses should use the Commercial Music Library. Other sounds may need a licence.

### How do I know if it works?
Use a code, a link or a counter question. Do not rely on views alone.
