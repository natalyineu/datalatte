The best Google Ads keywords for a veterinary practice are urgent and local: "vet near me", "emergency vet [town]", "vet open Saturday [town]" and "new puppy vet [town]". Put them in phrase or exact match, and block jobs, school and "how to treat at home" searches. LocaliQ's 2026 Animals and Pets category averages $4.06 per click and a 16.22% conversion rate.

That category covers pet businesses broadly, including groomers and pet stores, so it is a loose guide for a clinic. This article gives the keyword method, match types and negatives, with the numbers sourced at the end.

## Which Keyword Groups Should a Vet Build?

Group searches by urgency and service. The phrasings below are examples to test, not measured volumes.

| Group | Example searches | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent | emergency vet [town], vet open now, 24 hour vet near me | Very high: call now |
| Find a vet | vet near me, veterinarian [town], animal hospital [town] | High |
| New pet | puppy vaccinations [town], kitten vet, new pet check up | High, planned |
| Service | dog dental cleaning [town], pet spay neuter [town] | Medium to high |
| Brand | your practice name | Defend your own name |

Urgent searches need the phone number at the top, correct opening hours and an ad schedule that matches when someone answers. A call to a closed clinic wastes the click. See our [pet groomer keyword guide](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-pet-groomers-in-2026) for the neighbouring service.

## What Does a Click Cost?

LocaliQ lists Animals and Pets at $4.06 per click, 7.49% click-through, 16.22% conversion and $31.50 per lead. The all-industry average is $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead. Physicians and Surgeons, a human-medicine category, is $4.76 per click.

No source breaks out veterinary clinics. Emergency terms may cost more in a competitive town, but no published figure supports a specific number, so check Keyword Planner and your account.

## Which Match Types Suit a Practice?

Google describes broad match as the default, reaching related searches without the literal words. Phrase match carries the keyword's meaning. Exact match gives the most control and the fewest searches.

For a clinic with a small budget, start with phrase and exact for the urgent and find-a-vet groups. Google's help notes that Smart Bidding works best with broad match, so test it later when conversion data exists.

## Which Negative Keywords Should a Vet Add?

Google says negative keywords do not match close variants, so add singular and plural forms yourself.

- Careers: vet tech jobs, veterinary assistant, salary
- Training: vet school, degree, course
- Home advice: home remedy, how to treat, symptoms, DIY
- Products: pet food, supplements, for sale
- Wrong service: grooming, boarding, training, if you do not offer them
- Free intent: free, free vet advice

Read the Search terms report weekly. Symptom searches are often research, not booking, so decide whether you want that traffic.

## Do Health Audience Rules Apply to Pet Clinics?

Google's personalized advertising policy lists Health as a sensitive category and says remarketing, Customer Match and lookalike segments are not supported for it. The page does not say whether veterinary care is covered, so check your account for policy notices before building audience lists.

Keyword Planner help also notes that some sensitive keywords return no data. If a pet-health term shows nothing, test it with a small budget.

## FAQ

### What are the best Google Ads keywords for veterinarians?
Urgent and local terms: "emergency vet [town]", "vet near me", "vet open Saturday [town]". Add new-pet and service searches in separate groups.

### How much does a click cost for a vet?
No clinic-specific figure is published. LocaliQ's Animals and Pets category averages $4.06 per click in 2026.

### Should a vet advertise emergency keywords?
Only if someone answers the phone when the ad shows. Use an ad schedule that matches staffing.

### Can vets use remarketing?
Google's policy says remarketing is unsupported for sensitive categories such as Health. It does not state how veterinary care is treated, so check your account.

### How many keywords do I need?
Start with 10 to 20 tightly themed keywords across three ad groups and grow from the Search terms report.
