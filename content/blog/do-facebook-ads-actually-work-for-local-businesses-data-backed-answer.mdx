Facebook ads can work for local businesses, but the published data measures clicks and leads, not sales. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks, based on 1,892 US campaigns, show an average traffic click of $0.60 and an average lead cost of $27.39 on lead campaigns. Whether that is profitable depends on how many leads become paying customers, which no benchmark can tell you for your shop.

So the honest answer is yes for a business that can turn a lead into a booking within a day, and unclear for one that cannot follow up. The rest of this page shows the numbers, where they differ by industry, and how to test with a small budget.

## What Do the 2026 Benchmarks Say?

The report covers US campaigns from April 1, 2025 to June 30, 2026. Its averages are medians, to reduce the effect of outliers. Traffic campaign CPC fell to $0.60 from $0.70 the year before. Lead campaign CPC fell 6.25% to $1.80, conversion rate rose 10.62% to 8.54%, and cost per lead moved only 0.98% down to $27.39.

The report also states that Google Ads CPC is more than double the average Meta CPC. It frames the two as complementary: Google tends to catch buying intent, and Meta can stretch the budget.

## Does the Cost Change by Industry?

Yes, a lot. The report lists these lead campaign figures.

| Industry | Cost per lead | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All industries | $27.39 | Median |
| Real Estate | $13.74 | Second lowest |
| Home and Home Improvement | $42.95 | Conversion rate 5.32% |
| Beauty and Personal Care | $50.91 | CPC $2.97 |
| Dentists and Dental Services | $61.56 | Highest |

Health and Fitness had a lead conversion rate of 7.98%. Restaurants and Food were among the cheapest for traffic clicks at $0.45. A lead for a salon costs about twice the all-industry average, so a salon should plan its test budget on that basis.

## Who Is Actually on Facebook?

Pew Research's 2026 social media fact sheet, from a survey of 5,511 US adults fielded January 30 to June 18, 2026, shows Facebook used by 70% of US adults, behind YouTube at 84%. Rural adults are more likely to use Facebook (76%), and among adults 65 and older 59% use it.

For a neighbourhood business that means the audience is there. Reach is not the constraint. Message and follow-up are.

## How Do You Target Only Nearby People?

Meta lets you target a radius around a location and choose people who live in the area, were recently there or are travelling there. Use a radius that matches how far your customers really travel. Put the offer, the neighbourhood and the next step in the first line of the ad. ## What Should a Test Look Like?

The example is an **assumption**, built on the published lead cost. A lead is a form or message request.

| | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget (assumption) | $300 |
| Cost per lead (WordStream, all industries) | $27.39 |
| Leads | about 11 |
| Leads who book (assumption) | 30% |
| New customers | about 3 |
| Cost per new customer | about $91 |
| First-visit revenue (assumption) | $60 |
| Repeat visits in 6 months (assumption) | 3 |

At these inputs the first visit does not cover the $91 acquisition cost, and the test pays only if customers return. That is the real question for a local business: lifetime value, not leads. If you do not know it, track repeat visits for three months before you scale.

## When Do Facebook Ads Fail?

Ads fail when nobody replies for a day, when the landing page is slow on a phone, or when the offer is generic. Benchmarks also move: the report notes cost per lead rose for some categories, such as Personal Services (up 24.6%) and Home and Home Improvement (up 4.1%), while 12 of 14 industries saw a decrease. ## FAQ

### How much should a local business spend on Facebook ads?

No benchmark sets a right figure. Start with an amount you can afford to lose for 30 days and compare cost per new customer with what a customer is worth.

### Is a lead the same as a customer?

No. WordStream measures cost per lead. How many leads book depends on your reply speed and offer. The 30% in this page's example is an assumption.

### Are Facebook ads cheaper than Google ads?

Per click, yes. WordStream says Google Ads CPC is more than double the average Meta CPC. Google searchers often have more intent, so cost per customer can differ.

### Which objective should a local business pick?

Lead campaigns give you contact details. Traffic campaigns are cheaper per click but send people to a page that must do the work.

### Do older customers use Facebook?

Pew's 2026 survey found 59% of adults 65 and older use it, and 70% of all US adults.
