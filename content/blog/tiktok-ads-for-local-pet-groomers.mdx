Are TikTok ads worth it for a local pet groomer? They can be, if you can afford the minimum test and know your break-even cost per new client first. TikTok requires ad group daily budgets above 20 USD, which is roughly 600 USD for a 30-day test of one ad group. In the worked example below, a groomer needs to win a new client for under 117 USD to break even over a year. Below that line TikTok pays. Above it, Google Business Profile and Search ads are the safer spend.

Every platform figure here comes from TikTok's help pages, Pew Research, APPA or LocaliQ, all linked. Cost and margin figures in the example are labelled assumptions.

## Who Can You Reach With TikTok Ads?

Pew Research found that 37% of US adults use TikTok, up from 21% in 2021. Use is highest among younger adults: 63% of those under 30, 44% of ages 30 to 49, 30% of ages 50 to 64 and 12% of those 65 and older ([Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/03/02/8-facts-about-americans-and-tiktok/)).

For a groomer, the audience matters because dog ownership is broad. APPA reports that 53% of US households, about 71 million, owned a dog in 2025 ([APPA](https://americanpetproducts.org/news/americas-dog-owners-are-becoming-smarter-spenders)).

The same APPA report carries a warning. It says spending on salon grooming and mobile grooming declined in 2025, and that 51% of owners groom their dogs at home. So the pitch is not "your dog needs grooming". It is a reason to choose a professional over a bathtub: matting, de-shedding, a nervous dog, a breed that is hard to clip.

## How Much Do TikTok Ads Cost to Test?

TikTok's help center sets the floor. For daily budgets, both campaigns and ad groups have minimums: campaign daily budgets must exceed 50 USD and ad group daily budgets must exceed 20 USD ([TikTok Ads Manager: About daily budgets](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-daily-budgets)). A separate help article gives a lifetime example: a 31-day ad group needs 620 USD (20 times 31) ([TikTok Ads Manager: About budgets](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget)).

That shapes the plan:

- **One campaign, one ad group** is the cheapest valid test.
- **About 600 USD for a month** is the realistic entry price, not 100 USD.
- **You cannot switch** between daily and lifetime budgets after launch.

Compare that with search. LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put median cost per lead on Google and Microsoft search at 31.50 USD for Animals and Pets, with a 16.22% conversion rate ([LocaliQ](https://www.localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/)). Search reaches people already looking for a groomer. TikTok reaches people who are not.

## Worked Example: Does a 600 USD Test Pay Back?

Every number here is an **assumption**. Swap in your own.

- Average groom price: 65 USD.
- Contribution after groomer time and supplies: 60%, so 39 USD per groom.
- A new client visits 3 times in 12 months, worth 117 USD in contribution.
- Test spend: 600 USD for one month.

| Cost per new client | Clients from 600 USD | Net after first visit | Net after 12 months |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 40 USD | 15 | -15 USD | +1,155 USD |
| 80 USD | 7.5 | -308 USD | +278 USD |
| 150 USD | 4 | -444 USD | -132 USD |

How to read it: the break-even cost per client over a year is 117 USD. At 150 USD you lose money even with repeat visits. The 12-month column only holds if clients actually rebook, which is why the booking follow-up matters as much as the ad. If you cannot track new clients back to the ad, you cannot know which row you are in.

## How Do You Target Local Pet Owners on TikTok?

TikTok's location targeting offers city, county, DMA, state and zip code levels, among others. Its help page adds three cautions ([TikTok Ads Manager: About location targeting](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/location-targeting)):

- Delivery and accuracy are not guaranteed in every situation.
- Very small areas or sets of zip codes can noticeably reduce impressions and clicks and may raise CPA.
- TikTok does not support excluding locations or zip codes.

TikTok's help pages do not document a mile-radius option, so plan around zip codes or cities instead. A practical set-up for a shop: choose the zip codes within a normal drive of your salon, then widen if delivery is too thin. For a mobile groomer, list only the zip codes on your route.

Interest targeting is not covered by the sources used for this guide, so test it rather than assuming it works.

## Which Ad Format Fits a Groomer?

Three options are worth knowing, all documented by TikTok:

1. **Spark Ads** turn your organic posts into ads. TikTok says engagement from the boost is attributed to the original organic post, and you can customise how long an authorization code lasts ([TikTok Ads Manager: Spark Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/spark-ads)). Groomers already film before-and-afters, so this is the lowest-effort route. See our [Spark Ads guide for local business](/blog/tiktok-spark-ads-local-business-guide).
2. **Instant Forms** collect enquiries inside TikTok. The forms are built in Leads Center and can be linked to an ad account ([TikTok Ads Manager: Instant Forms](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-create-and-share-instant-forms-in-leads-center?lang=en)). Useful if your booking page is slow on a phone.
3. **Customer file audiences** let you target an uploaded list. The catch for small shops is size: TikTok requires a minimum audience of 1,000 to target a custom audience in an ad group ([TikTok Ads Manager: Managing custom audiences](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/manage-custom-audience)). A client list of a few hundred will not qualify, and the matched count is the number that counts, not the rows you upload.

<Callout type="warning" title="Before You Spend">
Make sure every ad ends in one trackable action: a booking, a call or a form. If you cannot count it, the 600 USD test cannot tell you whether TikTok worked.
</Callout>

## What Should You Measure and When Should You Stop?

Track three numbers per month:

- **Cost per new client** from TikTok, counted only when a booking is confirmed.
- **Rebooking rate** of those clients after 8 to 12 weeks.
- **Cost per client from other channels**, for comparison.

Set a stop rule before launch. For example, with a 117 USD break-even, pause if the cost per client is above 150 USD after the first 600 USD. Do not add budget until one ad has beaten your break-even line. TikTok's pages do not give a fixed number of days a campaign needs, so use your own spend threshold as the test.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need a TikTok following to run ads?**
A: TikTok's help center does not state a follower requirement for running ads in the pages used here. Spark Ads do boost posts from an existing account, so a few strong videos help.

**Q: What is the minimum I can spend?**
A: TikTok says ad group daily budgets must exceed 20 USD and campaign daily budgets must exceed 50 USD. A 31-day ad group at the minimum needs 620 USD in lifetime budget.

**Q: Can I target a few miles around my shop?**
A: TikTok's help pages list city, county, DMA, state and zip code targeting. They do not document a radius option. Very small areas can reduce delivery and raise CPA.

**Q: Can I upload my client list?**
A: Yes, in CSV or TXT format, but TikTok requires at least 1,000 matched users to target a custom audience. A small list will not qualify.

**Q: Is TikTok better than Google Ads for groomers?**
A: They do different jobs. Search catches owners already looking, at a 31.50 USD median cost per lead in LocaliQ's benchmark. TikTok introduces your shop to people who have not searched. Start with search and your Google Business Profile if budget is tight.
