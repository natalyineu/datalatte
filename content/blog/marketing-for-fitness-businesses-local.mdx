Google clicks in the Health and Fitness category average $6.17 and leads $67.36 (LocaliQ 2026), but the number that matters is cost per paying member.

This guide is for gyms, studios and personal trainers in the US. Sources are at the end. Examples built on guesses are marked as assumptions.

## How Big Is the Fitness Market for a Local Studio?

The Health & Fitness Association reports 81 million Americans held a fitness facility membership in 2025, up 5.2% from 2024 and 26.1% of people aged six and older. Visits reached an estimated 7 billion. Gen Z adults aged 18 to 24 had the highest penetration at 35.5%.

The share of members who never used their membership fell from about 10% to 4.6%. That is a national figure for all facility types, so it does not predict your own churn. The useful read is that more people already pay for fitness, so your pitch is why your place beats the one they already know.

## What Do Google Ads Cost for a Gym?

LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks for Health and Fitness show $6.17 per click, 5.81% click-through rate, 6.94% conversion rate and $67.36 per lead. The all-industry averages are $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead, so fitness leads cost about the same as the average advertiser.

A lead here may be a trial sign-up or a call, and the share who become paying members depends on your sales process. Our [Google Ads for gyms](/blog/google-ads-for-gyms) guide shows keywords and tracking in detail. Google's Local Services Ads help page also lists fitness among its categories, so check whether your area is supported.

## Does Meta Advertising Work for Studios?

It can, and it suits offers that need a picture: a first class, a challenge, a new schedule. Meta's health and wellness policy sets limits. Weight-loss ads must be targeted to people 18 or older, should clearly indicate the time taken to achieve noticeable results, and may not show a close-up of a body area being pinched.

Read the policy before you build creative, because rules change. The version we checked lists a most recent update of July 22, 2026. Our [Facebook ads for gyms and studios guide](/blog/facebook-ads-for-gyms-fitness-studios-complete-guide) covers offers and audiences.

## How Should a Fitness Business Use Google Business Profile?

Local search is where people compare nearby gyms. Fill in categories, services, hours, photos and the booking link. Google's guidelines say the name should be your real-world name, with no keywords, taglines or hours added.

BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 68% of consumers will only use a business rated 4 stars or higher and 47% will not use one with fewer than 20 reviews. Ask members after a milestone, using a Google link or QR code. Google bans offering free or discounted services in exchange for reviews. ## Why Does Retention Belong in Marketing?

Each member you keep reduces the number you must buy back with ads. No single verified churn benchmark applies to all studios, so track yours: members who joined per month, members who left, and average months stayed.

Welcome emails, a second-week check-in, and a message when someone misses two weeks are simple tools. Our [member retention strategy](/blog/fitness-studio-member-retention-strategy) guide lists tactics. Seasonal timing also matters, covered in year-round fitness studio marketing.

## FAQ

### How much should a gym spend on marketing?

No verified percentage applies to all gyms. Set the limit from cost per new member and average months stayed. At LocaliQ's $67.36 average cost per lead, $1,000 buys roughly 15 leads before your own conversion rates.

### Which channel is best for a new studio?

Start with your Business Profile and reviews, which cost time rather than ad spend, then test one paid channel with a first-visit offer. Add others after you know your cost per member.

### Do before-and-after photos work on Meta?

Meta's policy permits general cosmetic before-and-after depictions but limits weight loss and body imagery. Check the current policy first, since it was updated in July 2026.

### Should I run a January promotion?

Many gyms do, but no verified data in this guide shows how much it lifts sign-ups. Compare it with your own past months.
