Yelp Ads cost a pet groomer as little as about $5 a day, and self-serve advertisers have no term contract, so you can pause or cancel at any time. Yelp does not publish a price per click. It is set by an auction and shown as an estimate when you build the campaign, so the honest answer to "what will I pay per click" is: check the estimate for your city.

This guide corrects a common claim that Yelp requires a 12-month contract. Yelp's own advertising page says otherwise for self-serve ads. Every figure below comes from a source listed at the end. Worked examples are labelled as assumptions.

## Does Yelp Require a 12-Month Contract?

No, not for self-serve ads. Yelp's advertising page states there are "no term contracts with self-serve advertisers," and answers the question about cancelling with "You are free to cancel at any time." It also says you can edit or cancel ads whenever you like and change your budget as often as you want.

Earlier versions of this article said Yelp typically requires 12 months. That was not supported by Yelp's published terms and has been removed.

Two caveats. Yelp also sells larger programs through a sales team, and the page does not describe those contract terms. And if an agency runs your ads, its management agreement is a separate contract with its own notice period. Read both before signing (secondary source: WebFX, August 2026).

## How Much Do Yelp Ads Cost for Pet Groomers?

Yelp states you can spend "as little as $5/day on average" and set your own maximum. WebFX lists a starting point of about $150 a month. It also reports that Yelp's optional Upgrade Package is $180 a month on its own and a bundle starts at $270 a month (secondary source, August 2026).

The price per click is not published. WebFX writes that there is no reliable average cost per click for Yelp Ads. A September 2026 analysis from SuperDupr says Yelp publishes no click price and that the auction depends on competing advertisers, inventory in your market and weekly consumer interest (secondary source). Its $5 to $25 range covers home-service contractors, not pet groomers, so do not read it as a grooming benchmark.

The practical step: start the campaign builder, read the estimate for "pet groomers" in your service area, and compare it with the Google figures below.

## Do Yelp Ads Pay Off? A Break-Even Example

Every input here is an **assumption**, not a Yelp or industry figure. Replace them with your own.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
|---|---|
| Monthly Yelp budget | $150 |
| Cost per click | $4 |
| Clicks | about 37 |
| Clicks that become a booking | 10% |
| New bookings | about 3.7 |
| Cost per booking | about $40 |
| First groom price | $70 |
| Visits per year if the client stays | 8 |

At these inputs a first visit covers the ad cost. Change the booking rate to 3% and you get about 1.1 bookings, a cost of roughly $135 each, so a $70 first groom loses money unless the client returns. The result depends almost entirely on the click price and the booking rate, and both are unknown until you test. A small budget with a clear stop date is the cheap way to find out.

## When Are Yelp Ads Worth Testing?

Yelp makes sense when people in your area already search Yelp for groomers. Check your Yelp profile's own traffic first: if it gets few views a month without ads, paid clicks may have little to buy.

Ads send people to your profile, so the profile does the selling. Yelp does not publish a minimum review count for ads. Our [comparison of Yelp, Google and Facebook review platforms](/blog/review-platform-comparison-yelp-google-facebook-tripadvisor) and our [general guide to whether Yelp Ads are worth it](/blog/yelp-ads-worth-it-local-business-2026) help you decide where reviews matter in your area.

A sensible test is one budget, one service area, and a fixed review date. Count calls, requests and bookings that name Yelp, and cancel if the cost per booking is above what a first groom earns.

## Can You Ask Clients for Yelp Reviews?

Not directly. Yelp's content guidelines say "Businesses should never ask customers to write reviews." Earlier versions of this article suggested asking every happy client. That conflicts with Yelp's policy and has been removed.

What you can do: complete your profile, add clear photos, respond to reviews you receive, and make sure your Yelp page is easy to find. For Google, where asking is allowed, see our [Google Business Profile guide for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-for-pet-groomers).

## FAQ

### Can I cancel Yelp Ads if they are not working?

For self-serve ads, yes. Yelp says there are no term contracts and you are free to cancel at any time. Contract-based programs sold through a sales team may differ, so ask for the cancellation terms in writing before agreeing.

### How much does a click cost on Yelp for a groomer?

Yelp does not publish it. The price comes from an auction and Yelp shows an estimate during setup. Third-party guides give wide ranges that are not specific to pet grooming.

### Are Yelp Ads better than Google Ads for groomers?

There is no published comparison. Google's 2026 Animals and Pets averages are a $4.06 click and a $31.50 lead, but those are category averages. Test with a small budget and compare your own cost per booking.

### Does Yelp hide my reviews if I do not advertise?

Yelp says its recommendation software is automated. This article did not verify any link between ads and review filtering, so no claim is made either way.
