A local business can run Roku ads through Roku Ads Manager, the self-serve platform Roku launched on September 18, 2024. You upload a video, pick states, ZIP codes or DMAs, set a budget and wait 2 to 3 business days for creative review. Roku does not publish a minimum spend.

About $500 is the figure most third-party guides use, and CPMs are estimated at $20 to $60. Confirm both inside the sign-up flow before you plan.

This guide covers what Roku ads cost, who they suit, the exact creative specs from Roku's help center, and how to judge a test. Numbers that Roku does not publish are labelled as estimates.

## What Are Roku Ads and Who Can Buy Them?

Roku sells video ads that play on its streaming platform, including The Roku Channel and other ad-supported channels. Roku Ads Manager is the self-serve buying tool. Roku's launch announcement, as reported by Advanced Television, described it as a direct self-service CTV performance solution with a buying experience similar to search and social, and with Shopify-based shoppable campaigns.

For scale, Roku reported 89.8 million streaming households worldwide at the end of December 2024 (The Desk). It stopped reporting households from 2025, so there is no newer verified count, and the figure is global, not US-only. Treat any "85 million US households" claim as unverified.

## How Much Do Roku Ads Cost?

Roku does not publish rates. Industry guides estimate a CPM (cost per 1,000 ad views) of $20 to $60 in 2026, depending on targeting and placement. Each extra targeting layer narrows inventory and can raise the price.

| Budget | CPM $20 | CPM $30 | CPM $60 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $500 | 25,000 views | 16,667 views | 8,333 views |
| $1,000 | 50,000 views | 33,333 views | 16,667 views |
| $3,000 | 150,000 views | 100,000 views | 50,000 views |

Views are ad plays, not people. The same household can see your ad several times.

### Worked example: a yoga studio with $1,000

All inputs are **assumptions**, not Roku data.

- Budget: $1,000 for a 4 week flight; CPM $30 (midpoint of the estimate).
- Ad views: 1,000 / 30 x 1,000 is about 33,333.
- Assumed average frequency of 4 views per household.
- Households reached: about 8,300.
- If the ZIP codes you pick hold fewer households than that, frequency rises and you are paying to repeat yourself.

Check the number of households in your target ZIP codes before launch; Roku's help center says to use residential ZIP codes only.

## How Does Roku Compare With Other Ad Channels?

| | Roku Ads Manager | Google Ads Search | Meta lead ads |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who sees it | People watching TV | People searching now | People scrolling |
| Format | Video, 6 to 92 seconds | Text | Image or video |
| Price signal | CPM, estimated $20 to $60 | CPC, $5.42 average (LocaliQ 2026) | CPC about $1.80 average (LocaliQ) |
| Best for | Awareness and recall | Ready-to-buy demand | Offers and leads |
| Measurement | Pixel, reach, completion | Calls, forms, clicks | Leads, clicks |

Search finds people who already want what you sell. Roku creates awareness in an environment where people cannot easily skip. If search is not set up yet, fix that first; our [CTV overview for local business](/blog/connected-tv-ads-local-business-how-to-start) explains where TV fits after that.

LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put the all-industry Google Ads average at $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead, and the all-industry Meta lead-campaign average at $1.80 per click and $27.39 per lead.

## What Do You Need to Launch a Roku Campaign?

You need a finished video file, a landing page, and a way to measure. Roku's help center lists these specs for video:

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Format | .mov or .mp4 (no YouTube links) |
| Length | 6 to 92 seconds |
| Aspect ratio | 16:9 (horizontal) |
| Frame rate | 23.98, 25.00 or 29.97 fps |
| Bit rate | 6 Mbps minimum |
| Audio | 2-channel stereo, 48 kHz, PCM preferred, AAC accepted |
| File size | 1 GB maximum |

Roku's advertising guidelines are reported to ask for 15 or 30 second ads (unverified, the page did not load). The help center also lists English audio as a rule (English with Spanish subtitles is allowed), and a valid landing page URL that matches the business. The help center says the most common upload failures are low bit rate or resolution.

## How Do You Set Up Roku Ads Step by Step?

1. **Create an Ads Manager account** at Roku's advertising site and add billing.
2. **Set the budget and dates.** Pick a total budget and a flight; the help center places the budget step before locations.
3. **Choose locations.** Roku offers three types: states, postal/ZIP codes, and DMAs. You can use only one type per include or exclude list.
4. **Choose an audience** or let Roku target the best-performing demographic for your objective. Improvado's overview lists age, gender, income, content genre and device type as options (secondary source).
5. **Upload creative** and allow 2 to 3 business days for manual review. Rejections restart the review.
6. **Install the Roku pixel** on your site if you want site actions tracked (secondary source).

## Which Local Businesses Should Try Roku Ads?

Roku suits businesses that sell something visual, repeat, or considered, and that already have a working search presence. Good tests include fitness and yoga studios, restaurants and cafes, and home-service firms. A pure emergency service, such as a locksmith, gets more from search ads.

## How Do You Know If Roku Ads Worked?

Do not judge a TV test on clicks. Pick two or three signals before you launch and compare them to the four weeks before the flight:

- Branded searches for your name in Google Search Console.
- Direct visits and calls.
- A unique offer code or phone number used only in the ad.
- Ask new customers how they heard about you.

Run the test for at least 4 weeks and keep the rest of your marketing steady. Those windows are a recommendation, not a Roku rule.

## FAQ

### What is the minimum budget for Roku ads?

Roku does not publish one. Third-party guides commonly say campaigns start around $500, but that figure is an estimate; confirm it in Ads Manager.

### How much do Roku ads cost?

Roku does not publish rates. Industry estimates put CPM between $20 and $60 per 1,000 views for 2026.

### Can I target my ZIP code on Roku?

Yes. Roku Ads Manager allows state, ZIP code and DMA targeting, one type per list. ZIP targeting is not available when a Credit, Housing or Employment category is declared.

### Do I need a professional video?

You need a file that meets the specs: 16:9, at least 6 Mbps, and a length between 6 and 92 seconds. Professional production is not required, but a low bit rate causes rejections.

### Are Roku ads skippable?

Standard video ads are described as non-skippable by third-party guides; Roku's own pages opened for this guide do not state it, so treat it as unverified.

### Are Roku ads better than Google Ads?

They do different jobs. Search captures existing demand; Roku builds awareness. Start with search, then test Roku.
