## Which Google Business Profile settings matter before you run ads?

Creating a Google Business Profile is free, and it requires a Google account, which can also be registered for free, according to [BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/) (secondary). Google verifies the location before the listing goes live. [Per Diem](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide) (secondary) describes this as usually a postcard with a code mailed to the business address, though some businesses can verify by phone or email. BrewRound adds that the profile does not appear in results until it is verified.

Category choice matters too. Per Diem recommends choosing "Coffee shop" or a related term rather than something broad like "Restaurant." BrewRound notes that only one category can be selected at creation, with more added later, so set the primary category first.

Two features are worth configuring before paid traffic arrives. Per Diem recommends answering common questions in the Q&A section in advance, such as whether you offer free Wi-Fi, dairy-free options or dogs on the patio. Google also lets users message businesses directly from the listing. For a fuller setup, see [Coffee Shop Google Business Profile Optimization](/blog/coffee-shop-google-business-profile-optimization). BrewRound's 46% figure comes from a 2020 post that cites no source, so treat it as dated context rather than a current benchmark.

## Which keywords and negative keywords should Seattle coffee shops use?

Start with phrases that signal someone wants to visit soon. A localized phrase with a place name is likely to have less competition than a generic term like "coffee shop," which can lead to a lower cost per click, according to [seoplus+](https://seoplus.com/paid-ads/the-ultimate-guide-to-multi-location-ppc-advertising-on-google-ads/) (secondary). The table below shows a starting set. For a fuller keyword list, see [Best Google Ad Keywords for Coffee Shops in 2026](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-coffee-shops-in-2026).

| Keyword | Searcher intent | Suggested use |
|---------|-----------------|---------------|
| coffee shop near me | Visit now | Core exact-match term |
| best coffee Seattle | Choosing a shop | Core exact-match term |
| café near me | Visit now | Test in its own ad group |
| coffee shop Capitol Hill | Neighborhood visit | Phrase match, one group per neighborhood |
| coffee shop open now | Visit now | Test, and keep hours accurate in the profile |

Add neighborhoods you actually serve, such as Capitol Hill, Fremont or Ballard, as separate phrase groups. Keeping one ad group per neighborhood lets the ad copy name that area directly.

Negative keywords stop clicks that rarely turn into visits. Useful exclusions include:

- Job terms such as "jobs," "hiring," "career" or "salary"
- Learning terms such as "how to," "course," "training" or "certification"
- Trade terms such as "wholesale," "supplier" or "franchise"
- Place names outside your service area

## How much budget does a Seattle coffee shop need to test?

The sources used here do not include a verified Seattle cost per click, so the example below uses labeled assumptions. Replace each input with live data from your own account before making a budget decision.

Inputs, all assumptions:

- Monthly budget: $1,000
- Average cost per click: $3.00
- Click-to-visit rate: 3%
- Average first-visit spend: $6.50

| Step | Calculation | Result |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Clicks | $1,000 ÷ $3.00 | 333 |
| Visits | 333 × 3% | 10 |
| Cost per visit | $1,000 ÷ 10 | $100 |
| Cost per visit at an 8% visit rate (assumption) | $1,000 ÷ (333 × 8%) | About $37.50 |

At these inputs, one first visit worth $6.50 does not cover the $100 cost per visit. Even at the higher 8% visit rate, the cost per visit stays well above a single first purchase. The campaign only pays back if the shop earns repeat visits, higher spend per customer, or a lower cost per click. That is why tracking matters more than the headline budget. Tighter keywords and radius targeting lower cost per click, and a complete profile supports a higher visit rate.

## What should you track and check before launch?

Set up conversion tracking for these actions, so you can compare each one against the cost per visit above:

1. Phone calls from ads, using a call tracking number
2. Direction requests from the Google Business Profile
3. Booking or contact form completions
4. Clicks on "book now" or contact buttons as a secondary signal

Before the first ad goes live, confirm that the profile is verified and shows the right category, that the Q&A section answers the common questions, that neighborhood and radius targeting is in place, and that the negative keyword list is loaded. Plan for upkeep as well. Bellwether Coffee lists Google Business at about 5 minutes per day of time investment, per its [social media marketing guide](https://bellwethercoffee.com/blog/social-media-marketing-for-coffee-shops) (secondary). Once visitors arrive, [marketing automation for local coffee shops](/blog/marketing-automation-for-local-coffee-shops) can help follow up with new customers.

## FAQ

### Is Google Ads worth it for a coffee shop in Seattle?

It depends on what each visit is worth to the business. Run the cost-per-visit calculation above with your own cost per click, visit rate and margin. If the cost per visit stays above what a customer is worth, tighten the keywords and radius before increasing spend.

### Do I need to verify my Google Business Profile first?

In practice, yes. Google verifies the listing before it goes live, and BrewRound notes the profile does not appear in results until it is verified. Verification usually involves a mailed postcard with a code, according to [Per Diem](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide) (secondary).

### Which category should a coffee shop choose?

Per Diem recommends "Coffee shop" or a related term rather than a broad category such as "Restaurant." BrewRound notes that one category is selected at creation and more can be added later.

### How long before Google Ads show results?

These sources do not give a timeframe. Check the platform's live help page for current guidance on learning periods, and judge a campaign on tracked visits over at least a full month of data.
