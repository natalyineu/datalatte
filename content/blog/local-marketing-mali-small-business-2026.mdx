A small business in Mali should start with a Facebook Page and WhatsApp Business. DataReportal counts 2.40 million people reachable by Facebook ads in Mali, against 247 thousand for Instagram. Only 35.1% of Malians were online in October 2025, so word of mouth, signage and phone calls still carry most local trade. Google Ads is listed as available, but no Mali click prices were found.

This guide is for shops, cafes, salons and service firms in Mali. Every figure comes from a source listed at the end, checked on 1 November 2026. Where no source exists, the text says so.

## How Many Malians Can a Small Business Reach Online?

Fewer than four in ten. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report lists Mali's population at 25.4 million and internet users at 8.91 million, or 35.1%. Cellular mobile connections stand at 23.2 million, 91.6% of the population, so most people own or use a phone that is not necessarily used for the internet.

| Measure | Mali, late 2025 | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Population (UN) | 25.4 million | n/a |
| Cellular mobile connections | 23.2 million | 91.6% |
| Internet users | 8.91 million | 35.1% |
| Facebook ad reach | 2.40 million | 9.5% |
| Instagram ad reach | 247 thousand | 1.0% |
| Messenger ad reach | 220 thousand | 0.9% |
| LinkedIn members | 470 thousand | 1.9% |
| TikTok, YouTube, Google ad reach | Not reported | n/a |

Facebook's 2.40 million equals roughly 27% of Mali's internet users (2.40 divided by 8.91). Meta says its audience size is not a proxy for active users, and DataReportal warns that platforms revise their numbers.

## Which Platform Should a Malian Business Start With?

Facebook, because it is the only paid social platform with a large reported audience in Mali. At 2.40 million it is almost ten times Instagram's 247 thousand, so an Instagram account is a nice extra, not a place for budget. Messenger's 220 thousand shows that messaging inside Facebook is a smaller channel than the Page itself.

WhatsApp is the practical place to take orders and bookings. WhatsApp Business describes ads on Facebook and Instagram that open a chat with your business. Whether that ad format is available to advertisers in Mali was not verified, so check in Ads Manager before planning around it.

## Can a Malian Business Run Google Ads?

Yes, on paper. Google's list of billing countries includes Mali. In that list the account currency and payment method columns read "All", but Google says the options you see depend on your billing country, currency and payment setting, and that prepay is not offered in many countries.

Open the billing page in your own account to see what is available. DataReportal does not report Google ad reach for Mali, and none of the sources checked publishes click prices for the country.

With only 35.1% of people online and no Mali price data, a small Search test is the sensible way to learn. Set a ceiling you can afford to lose, run it for several weeks and compare it with Facebook enquiries. The [Google Ads cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-google-ads-cost-for-small-businesses-real-2026-pricing-breakdown) explains how click costs and conversion rates combine.

## What About Mobile Money and Cash?

No verified current figure for mobile money use in Mali was found, so this guide quotes none. DataReportal does not cover payments, and the part of Google's payment methods page that was checked lists cards and bank accounts, not mobile money.

For your own sales, ask ten recent customers how they paid and how they found you. Then show the payment methods you accept on your Page and in your WhatsApp greeting. That costs nothing and tells you more than a national estimate.

## What Does a First Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for a small cafe that takes orders on WhatsApp. They show the method, not Malian results. Currency is "units" of your local money.

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Gross profit per new customer over a year (assumption) | 40 units |
| Enquiries that become customers (assumption) | 25% |
| Most you can pay per enquiry and still break even | 40 x 25% = 10 units |
| Test budget (assumption) | 100 units |
| Enquiries needed to break even | 100 / 10 = 10 enquiries |

If the Facebook campaign delivers ten or more WhatsApp enquiries for 100 units, it paid for itself. If it delivers three, fix the offer or the reply speed before adding money. Compare this with a monthly plan from the [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide).

## How Do You Get Found on Google Maps in Mali?

Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. It also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Fill in every field, use real photos, reply to every review and keep the name, address and phone number identical everywhere. Because few Malians are online, also put your map pin, opening hours and WhatsApp number on the shop sign and on printed cards. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes through each field.

Do not offer a discount or a free item in return for a review. Google's contributed content policy says merchants must not offer incentives, such as payment, discounts, free goods or services, in exchange for posting a review. Asking is fine. Rewarding is not. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for ways to ask.

## What Mistakes Do Malian Small Businesses Make?

These are recommendations, not findings from a study.

- Spending on Instagram first, when its reported audience is a tenth of Facebook's.
- Ignoring the 65% of people who are offline. A sign, a flyer or a referral still reaches them.
- Slow replies on WhatsApp. An enquiry that waits a day is often lost.
- Running one big campaign instead of a small test with a break-even number.

## FAQ

### How many people use Facebook in Mali?

DataReportal reports a Facebook ad reach of 2.40 million in late 2025, 9.5% of the population. That is the audience Meta's ad tools estimate, not a count of active users.

### Is Google Ads available in Mali?

Mali appears in Google's list of billing countries. No source checked publishes Mali click prices, so test with a small budget and read your own cost per enquiry.

### Should a Mali business use Instagram?

Instagram's reported ad reach is 247 thousand, or 1.0% of the population. Keep a profile if your customers use it, but put paid budget on Facebook first.

### Does mobile money work for advertising payments?

Not verified. The part of Google's payment methods page that was checked lists cards and bank accounts, not mobile money, and Meta's pages were not confirmed for Mali. Check the billing screen in your account.

### How much should a small shop in Mali spend on ads?

No Mali-specific figure exists in the sources checked. Pick an amount you can lose, then use the break-even method above to decide whether to continue.
