Teeth whitening Google Ads can start small. Geotargetly, a secondary source, says location-targeted pay-per-click campaigns can start at $10–$50 a day, and competitive industries may need higher budgets. A $1,500 month works out to about $50 a day, the top of that range. The number that decides whether ads pay is not the click price but the cost per paying client. That depends on how many clicks become enquiries and how many enquiries become treatments.

This guide is for dental practices and cosmetic whitening studios in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. Whitening is a searched-for, elective treatment, so a Search campaign usually fits better than display or video.

## Can You Legally Offer Whitening Where You Operate?

Check this before any budget decision. Rules on who may provide tooth whitening differ by country and by state or province, and they can change. This article does not state those rules.

Ask your national or state dental regulator, in writing, whether a non-dentist or a kit-only business may offer whitening, and whether advertising it is allowed. If the answer is no, spend the budget elsewhere. Ads cannot fix a licensing problem.

## How Do Reviews Fit Into Whitening Ads?

Reviews carry real weight in healthcare. NRC Health, cited by Weave, reports that 83.3% of patients value online reviews more than personal recommendations when choosing certain healthcare providers. Weave's page also says 9 out of 10 people check reviews before choosing a business, but that banner names no source.

Adwave's photo guide says dental practices that show smile makeovers have seen inquiries rise by up to 40%. That figure has no named source either. Test any before-and-after photos against your own results before you use them in an ad.

Respond to every review, and never write or pay for them. For practices with many reviews to answer, the [AI agent for Google reviews management](/blog/ai-agent-for-google-reviews-management) guide covers an automated reply workflow.

## How Should the Search Campaign Be Built?

Build one Search campaign per location. Search catches people who are already looking for whitening, which is the intent you want. Smart campaigns automate more of the setup and show less keyword detail, so a standard Search campaign is easier to audit on a first run. The [Google Ads smart campaigns](/blog/google-ads-smart-campaigns) guide covers that trade-off.

Group keywords by intent:

- Ready to book: "teeth whitening near me", "book teeth whitening", and your town name
- Professional: "dentist teeth whitening", "in chair whitening"
- Event: "teeth whitening before wedding"
- Price: "teeth whitening cost" with your town
- Brand: your practice name, exact match

Negative keywords matter here. Kit brands target shoppers with terms such as "Whitening Strips" and "Teeth Bleaching Kits", as a [vendor's keyword list for kit brands](https://www.mbadv.agency/resources-ecommerce-industry/10-keywords-every-teeth-whitening-kits-brand-needs-in-their-google-ads-strategy) shows. A clinic should exclude home-kit and DIY terms, such as "diy", "home remedy", "baking soda", "charcoal", "pen", "cheap" and "free", unless it sells kits.

## How Do You Run Several Studios From One Account?

Google's [manager account page](https://business.google.com/en-all/ad-tools/manage-accounts/) says one email address can be used for up to 20 Google Ads accounts. You can also create multiple campaigns in the same account without creating a new one.

Managers can use labels, automated rules, alerts and email notifications to monitor linked accounts. The name you give a manager account is the name clients see in their client account, so choose one that reads well to them. Shared managers can be given Administrative or Standard access, and their level can be changed if their role changes.

## What Does the Budget Look Like in Practice?

The model below uses one monthly budget and two scenarios. The $50 a day equals $1,500 over 30 days, the top of Geotargetly's starting range. Every other input is an assumption. Replace them with your own account data after one month.

| | Base | Tough |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $1,500 (assumption, 30 days at $50) | $1,500 (assumption) |
| Cost per click | $6.00 (assumption) | $10.00 (assumption) |
| Clicks | 250 | 150 |
| Click to enquiry | 8% (assumption) | 8% (assumption) |
| Enquiries | 20 | 12 |
| Enquiry to paid treatment | 50% (assumption) | 50% (assumption) |
| New whitening clients | 10 | 6 |
| Ad cost per client | $150 | $250 |
| Treatment price | $400 (assumption) | $400 (assumption) |
| Ad cost as share of treatment price | 37.5% | 62.5% |

To set a ceiling, work backwards. Maximum cost per click equals the most you will pay per client, multiplied by the click-to-enquiry rate, multiplied by the enquiry-to-paid rate. If $150 is your ceiling, $150 × 0.08 × 0.50 = $6.00 per click.

If the paid rate drops to 40% in the base case, cost per client rises to $187.50. That is why the front desk matters as much as the ad.

## How Should You Track Enquiries Without Sharing Health Data?

Count enquiries, not patient records. Track booking form submissions on a thank-you page, calls from the ad, and calls from your website. Keep patient names, treatment details and health information out of URLs, form fields passed to ad platforms, and any conversion uploads.

Ask every new client how they found you and log the answer in your own system. That log is the most reliable record of which channel works.

Some agencies sell tracking setup as a service. One [vendor's pricing](https://www.mbadv.agency/resources-ecommerce-industry/10-keywords-every-teeth-whitening-kits-brand-needs-in-their-google-ads-strategy) lists a $500 tracking setup covering server-side tracking, Consent Mode and GA4. Whether you need that depends on your site and your privacy obligations.

## What Should the Landing Page and Front Desk Do?

Send clicks to a page that matches the search. It should name the treatment, say how long it takes, state who should not have it, show a price or a "from" price, list your qualifications, and include a booking button with a tap-to-call number.

If calls are your main goal, a call-only format may suit a service business better. The [call-only campaigns guide](/blog/google-ads-call-only-campaigns-service-businesses) explains when that works.

Front desk speed affects every enquiry. A reply within minutes that includes a booking link is a simple test. Log reply times for a month and compare them with enquiries that wait a day.

## How Often Should You Review and Adjust?

Each week, read the search terms report, add negatives, and check which hours calls go unanswered. Pause keywords that keep spending without producing enquiries. Test one headline or offer every two weeks.

For a single view of search, call and local data, the [AI-driven local SEO reports guide](/blog/ai-driven-local-seo-reports-with-google-data-studio) shows how to build one. Give the account at least a month before judging cost per client.

## What Should You Do First?

1. Confirm in writing with your regulator that you may offer whitening and advertise it.
2. Pull your last 50 whitening clients and map their locations to set a radius.
3. Set a maximum cost per client and calculate your maximum cost per click with the formula above.
4. Build one Search campaign with the five ad groups, a negative list, and no reliance on remarketing.
5. Set up enquiry and call tracking before the ads go live, and add "how did you find us" to your booking form.

Improving your Google Business Profile at the same time helps local visibility. The [AI for Google Business Profile optimization](/blog/ai-for-google-business-profile-optimization) guide covers that work.

## FAQ

### Can teeth whitening be advertised on Google Ads?

Ad platform rules change and differ by sector, so check the current policy pages for your sector before launch. Separately, confirm with your regulator that you may offer whitening at all. An ad cannot make an unlicensed service legal.

### How much should a whitening campaign cost per month?

This guide has no verified figure. Geotargetly gives a $10–$50 a day starting range for location campaigns, and $1,500 a month is about $50 a day. The right budget depends on your maximum cost per client.

### Should I use remarketing for whitening?

That depends on the rules for your sector and the audience tools your account offers. Build the account so it works on Search keywords alone, then treat any other audience as a bonus.

### How long before ads bring in clients?

Calls and form enquiries can start within days of launch. Judge cost per client after a full month, using the table above to set your thresholds.

### Can one account manage several studios?

Yes. Google's manager account page says one email address can be used for up to 20 Google Ads accounts, and multiple campaigns can sit within one account.
