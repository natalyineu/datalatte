Instagram ads can bring delivery orders to a restaurant, but only if the cost of an order stays below what the order earns you. Meta's ads cost little per click: WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put restaurants and food at a median $0.45 per click in the US, against $0.60 across all industries. The harder part is the click-to-order step and the profit per order, so work out break-even before you spend.

This guide is for independent restaurants that sell delivery or takeaway through their own ordering link or through apps. The cost data is from US campaigns because no European restaurant benchmark was found. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## How Big Is the Instagram Audience for a Restaurant?

Large enough in most European cities. DataReportal's Digital 2026 reports, both published 5 November 2025, show Meta's ad tools reaching about 31.3 million people on Instagram in Germany (37.2% of the population) and 35.5 million in the United Kingdom (50.9%). These are advertising reach estimates, not monthly active users.

Your own market is smaller. A restaurant that delivers within four kilometres cares about the people inside that circle, and Ads Manager shows an estimated audience for any area you choose. If the estimate is tiny, widen the radius or accept that the ad will repeat to the same people.

For the wider picture of free Instagram content, see our guide to [Instagram marketing for restaurants](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-restaurants).

## What Do Instagram Ads Cost for a Restaurant?

WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 Facebook ads report analysed 1,892 US campaigns running between April 2025 and June 2026. For traffic campaigns, the median cost per click was $0.60 across all industries and $0.45 for restaurants and food, one of the lowest. The median click-through rate was 1.93% overall and 2.68% for restaurants and food.

For lead campaigns the median cost per click across industries was $1.80, with a median cost per lead of $27.39. The report publishes no cost per order for restaurants.

Treat these as a starting guess for the US only. Prices differ by country and season, and the report notes its figures are medians. Your first two weeks give the real number. Our [comparison of Instagram and Facebook ads](/blog/instagram-ads-vs-facebook-ads) explains how the two placements differ.

## Why Do Clicks Not Always Become Orders?

A click only sends someone to your page. The order depends on what happens next: how fast the page loads on a phone, whether the menu is easy to read, whether the delivery area and fee are clear, and whether checkout is short. A visitor who has to search for the menu leaves.

LocaliQ's report makes a related point from its experts: metrics are not independent, and a cheaper click can come with a lower share of people converting, so cost per result only improves if the click saving outweighs the conversion drop. Judge ads by cost per order, not cost per click.

Send ad traffic to a page that matches the ad. If the ad shows a lunch deal, link to the lunch menu with the order button visible without scrolling.

## Should You Send Orders to Your Own Page or to Delivery Apps?

Your own page keeps the customer data and avoids platform commission, but it needs a working ordering system. Delivery apps bring their own audience and take a fee. Our [comparison of food delivery advertising in Europe](/blog/food-delivery-platform-advertising-comparison-europe) covers platform ad formats and fee examples.

A sensible split is to use the app for discovery and your own page for repeat customers. Print a card in each delivery bag with a code for your own ordering link. Check the app's terms first, in case they restrict promoting direct ordering.

Only advertise a dish you can deliver well. A burger that arrives cold turns an ad click into a one-star review.

## What Is the Break-Even Maths?

All inputs below are **assumptions** for an imaginary restaurant. Replace them with your numbers.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Ad budget | €140 (€10 a day for 14 days) |
| Cost per click | €0.45 |
| Clicks | about 311 |
| Click-to-order rate | 3% |
| Orders | about 9 |
| Average basket | €22 |
| Contribution margin after food and packaging | 30% |
| Profit per order | €6.60 |
| Total profit from orders | about €61 |
| Result against ad spend | about −€79 |

To break even at €0.45 per click, each click must earn at least €0.45, so the click-to-order rate must reach about 6.8% at a €6.60 profit per order. If the order rate is 3%, the cost per click would need to fall to about €0.20 or the profit per order rise. That is why many restaurants start with offers to existing followers and run ads only for dishes with a strong margin.

Repeat orders change the sum. If an average customer orders three times in a year, the first order does not have to pay for the ad alone.

## How Do You Run a Fair Two-Week Test?

Set one goal, one radius and one offer. Run two or three images or short videos and keep the one that gets orders, not the one that gets likes. Record orders by asking a code word at checkout or using a dedicated link.

Stop the test if cost per order after the first week is more than twice your break-even figure. Do not change several things at once, or you will not know what worked. Our [Facebook ads guide for restaurants](/blog/facebook-ads-for-restaurants) has more campaign options.

## FAQ

### How much do Instagram ads cost for a restaurant?
WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 US benchmark is a median $0.45 per click for restaurants and food in traffic campaigns. No European figure was found, so test with a small budget.

### Are Instagram ads worth it for delivery?
They are worth it only if profit per order exceeds your cost per order. Use the break-even table above with your own numbers.

### How many people can I reach on Instagram in Europe?
Meta's tools showed 31.3 million in Germany and 35.5 million in the UK in late 2025, as estimates of ad reach.

### Should I send ad clicks to a delivery app?
It is easier to measure and keep customers if you send them to your own page, but apps may bring more discovery. Check each platform's terms.

### How long should I run a test?
Two weeks is enough to see order counts without overspending. Judge cost per order, not clicks.
