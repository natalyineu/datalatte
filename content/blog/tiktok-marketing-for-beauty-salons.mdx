A beauty salon can start TikTok marketing for free by posting short videos from a business account. Paid ads are optional and have a floor: TikTok's help center says a campaign budget must exceed $50 and an ad group daily budget must exceed $20. Ads can be aimed at a city or district, so you pay to reach people near the salon, not the whole country.

TikTok does not publish average click costs by industry in the sources checked for this article, so no TikTok price per booking is quoted here. Where a number is missing, the text says so. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Does It Cost to Advertise a Salon on TikTok?

The published floor is the budget rule. For a daily budget, the campaign must exceed $50 and each ad group must exceed $20. For a lifetime budget, the campaign must exceed $50 and the ad group minimum is $20 multiplied by the number of days. A 31-day run therefore needs at least $620 at ad group level.

TikTok also warns that a campaign budget set below the ad group budget can cause delivery problems. Once an ad is live you can change the amount, but you cannot switch between daily and lifetime budgets.

Treat the minimum as a test budget, not a plan. A salon with a short booking calendar may prefer a slower spend on Google or Meta, where benchmarks exist (see the table below).

## How Do You Target Local Clients on TikTok?

TikTok's help center lists city, urban district, district, county, state and postal code among its location types, with up to 3,000 location selections per ad group where available. The page says accuracy is not guaranteed in every situation, because location relies on various signals.

Radius targeting appears in third-party blogs, but not in the official help article, so check the Location section of your own Ads Manager before planning around it. For a single salon, a city or a district is the practical unit. Our guide on [how to target local customers on TikTok](/blog/how-to-target-local-customers-on-tiktok) walks through the steps.

## Which Music Can a Salon Use in Videos and Ads?

Use the Commercial Music Library through a TikTok Business Account. TikTok states that the library holds more than 600,000 tracks pre-cleared for royalty-free use in branded content and ads. It also says songs in the General Music Library are not available for use by any brand or business, and that using them to promote a business risks copyright infringement.

A trending sound from a personal account is therefore a risk for a salon. For paid ads, TikTok points to separate Commercial Music Library guidelines on usable placements, so read those before you boost a video.

## What Should a Beauty Salon Post?

TikTok publishes no ranking of salon formats, so these are working ideas to test, not proven winners:

- A short transformation, with the client's written permission
- One technique explained in under 30 seconds, such as how to prepare hair for a colour appointment
- A quick look at a booking slot filling up, ending with the booking link in the profile
- A stylist or technician introducing themselves
- A product shown in use, not just on a shelf

Post on a steady schedule for a month, then keep the three videos that held attention and make more like them. Check TikTok's current ad policies before running ads that show cosmetic results, because this article did not verify those rules.

## What Does a $620 Test Look Like?

All inputs are **assumptions**, not benchmarks. The salon runs one ad group at the minimum $20 a day for 31 days, which is $620 in total.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Spend | $620 |
| Booking requests from ads | 12 |
| Cost per booking request | about $51.67 |
| Requests that become paid visits | 60% (about 7) |
| Cost per paid visit | about $88.57 |
| Average first visit | $85 |
| Share who rebook once | 40% |

At these inputs the first visit does not cover the ad cost. The test only pays if rebooking and add-on sales close the gap, so track which clients came from TikTok at the front desk. Change the assumptions to your own numbers before you spend.

## FAQ

### Do beauty salons need paid ads on TikTok?

No. Organic posting from a business account costs nothing. Paid ads add reach in a chosen area, with a budget floor of $50 per campaign and $20 per ad group a day.

### Can a salon target only people nearby?

Yes, at city or district level, and by postal code where available. TikTok notes that location accuracy is not guaranteed in every situation.

### Can I use any trending song in a salon video?

Not safely. TikTok says the General Music Library is not available for brands, and points businesses to the Commercial Music Library.

### How much does a TikTok booking cost?

TikTok does not publish an industry average in its help pages. Run a small test and calculate your own cost per booking request.

### Is TikTok better than Google for a salon?

They do different jobs. Google Search reaches people ready to book, with LocaliQ listing a $39.25 cost per lead for beauty. TikTok builds awareness, and has no published benchmark.
