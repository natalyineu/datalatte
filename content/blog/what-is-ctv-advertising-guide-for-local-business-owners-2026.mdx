CTV advertising means video ads shown on a television screen through streaming apps and devices, such as Hulu, Disney+, Roku or a smart TV. The Media Rating Council defines CTV by the device: nonlinear video delivered to a TV set. Unlike broadcast spots, you buy it by the thousand views, can limit it to chosen areas, and can start at $500 on self-serve platforms like Roku and Disney Campaign Manager.

This guide explains the terms, the buying options and a realistic first test for a local business. Prices below come from platform pages or are marked as secondary estimates.

## What Is CTV Advertising?

CTV is advertising on a TV screen delivered over the internet. AdExchanger explains that the Media Rating Council adopted this device-based definition in August 2021: CTV is nonlinear video delivered to a TV screen, including streaming devices, smart TVs and gaming consoles. Ads play before or during streamed shows, the same way they do on a phone, but on a large screen with the sound on.

The format matters for a local business because the buying is digital. You choose a budget, an audience and a schedule in a dashboard, instead of negotiating with a station.

## Is CTV the Same as OTT?

Not exactly. After the 2021 change, OTT became a content-based reporting category: video watched on any device, including computers and phones. All CTV is OTT, but OTT is wider. In practice, when a vendor says OTT they usually mean streaming video, and when they say CTV they mean streaming on the TV set.

For buying, the difference shows up in reporting. Ask whether a number refers to TV screens only. Our [CTV vs OTT explainer](/blog/ctv-vs-ott-advertising-explained) covers the terms in more detail.

## How Does CTV Advertising Work?

Most CTV ads are sold on a CPM basis, meaning a price per thousand ad views. Roku states that its pricing is dynamic and based on audience demand and inventory, and that you can set a maximum bid. You pay as the campaign delivers.

The steps are short:

1. Open an account. Disney Campaign Manager says verification typically takes 24 to 48 hours.
2. Set a budget of at least $500 per campaign.
3. Pick an audience. Disney lists ZIP code, age, gender, interests and genre.
4. Upload a video. Roku accepts .mov or .mp4 files of 6 to 92 seconds, up to 1 GB.
5. Wait for review. Roku says manual content review takes 2 to 3 days.

Tubi, owned by Fox, announced a self-serve platform on May 6, 2025. The announcement does not state minimums or targeting.

## How Much Does CTV Advertising Cost?

Roku publishes no CPM. Vibe, an ad vendor, estimates $20 to $60 per thousand for Roku and says most campaigns start around $500. AdWave, a reseller citing eMarketer, reports a 2026 blended US CTV CPM of about $26, with most campaigns between $25 and $35. Both are secondary sources and estimates, not rate cards.

## What Can a $600 Test Buy? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $600 |
| CPM | $30 |
| Ad views | 20,000 |
| Average views per household | 4 |
| Households reached | about 5,000 |
| Visits that mention the ad | 10 |
| Profit per visit | $40 |

Under these assumptions the test costs $60 per tracked visit and returns $400 in first-visit profit, so it does not pay back on first visits alone. It might pay back if customers return. CTV is an awareness channel. Judge it over repeat visits and alongside the rest of your marketing.

## Is CTV Right for Your Business?

It suits a business with a clear offer, a defined area and a short path to action, such as a booking link or QR code. Roku notes that interactive end cards and QR codes are available through a partner. It is a poor first move if your Google Business Profile is incomplete or your website cannot take bookings.

## FAQ

### What does CTV stand for?
Connected TV. It means a TV set that gets video over the internet, through a smart TV, streaming stick or game console.

### Can a small business afford CTV ads?
Roku requires a lifetime budget of $500 or more per campaign, and Disney Campaign Manager lists a $500 minimum. Whether that is enough to reach your area depends on the CPM you receive.

### Do I need a TV commercial?
Not necessarily. Roku says existing social videos can be reused if they meet its file rules.

### Can I choose who sees my ad?
Disney lists ZIP code, age, gender, interests and program genre. Roku's pages checked do not describe geography, so check the account.

### How do I know if it worked?
Track new customers by source and add a short link or QR code. No platform page checked promises sales results.
