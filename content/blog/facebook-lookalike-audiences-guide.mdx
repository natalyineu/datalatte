A Facebook lookalike audience is a group of people Meta picks because they resemble a source list you supply, such as past customers. Meta's help page says the source must include at least 100 people from one country, and the audience size runs from 1% to 10%. A local business should use its best customers as the source, test the narrow 1% setting first, and judge by cost per lead.

This guide covers what to upload, which size to test and what to expect. Meta's own help pages were only partly readable, so some details come from a named secondary source and are marked. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Is a Lookalike Audience?

You give Meta a source audience, and Meta finds people with similar characteristics. The size setting sets how close the match is. Meta's page gives a range of 1% to 10%, and a lower percentage is a narrower, closer group, according to the secondary source below.

Quality of the source matters more than size. A list of customers who spent the most beats a list of everyone who ever liked your page. Our [custom audiences guide](/blog/how-to-create-facebook-custom-audiences-local-customers) shows how to build the source.

## What Should You Use as the Source?

Meta requires at least 100 people from a single country. Jon Loomer, an established Meta ads trainer (secondary source, last updated March 2025), says Meta recommends between 1,000 and 5,000 people. Treat both as limits to check in Ads Manager, because Meta changes them.

Useful sources for a local business:

- Customers who booked or bought more than once
- Highest-spending customers, if you have spend data
- People who submitted a lead form and showed up

Avoid mixing buyers with people who only clicked. Remove staff, suppliers and duplicates before upload.

## Which Size Should You Test First?

Start with 1%, the closest match. Widen to 2% or 3% if delivery stalls, and only then go broader. This order is Loomer's advice rather than a Meta rule.

For a local business the audience is built by country or region, and Loomer says at least one region or country must be selected. Set the ad set's location to your service area so the ad does not reach people who cannot visit. Check this in Ads Manager before you spend.

## Do Lookalikes Still Work in 2026?

Loomer's page covers Advantage Lookalike, an option that lets Meta expand beyond the percentage you chose. It does not describe how Advantage+ audience settings relate to lookalikes, and the Meta help text available for this article did not state it. Meta's interface changes often, so test on a small budget and check the audience you selected is the one that delivered.

LocaliQ's benchmark report, updated September 23, 2026, gives context for results: across industries, Meta lead campaigns average $1.80 per click, 8.54% conversion and $27.39 per lead. Beauty and Personal Care lead campaigns average $2.97 per click and $50.91 per lead. Health and Fitness average $1.84 per click and $27.11 per lead.

## When Should You Skip Lookalikes?

Skip them if your list is under 100 people from one country, if it is mostly cold leads or if you cannot track what happens after the form. A list of 60 customers is below Meta's 100-person minimum, and a bigger percentage does not fix a weak source.

In those cases, run a simple local campaign first, collect leads for a few weeks and then build the source. Retargeting website visitors is another low-cost step, covered in our [retargeting guide](/blog/retargeting-ads-for-local-business).

## FAQ

### How big does a lookalike source need to be?
Meta says at least 100 people from one country. A secondary source says Meta recommends 1,000 to 5,000.

### What lookalike percentage should I choose?
Meta allows 1% to 10%. Start at 1% and widen only if delivery is poor.

### Can I use lookalikes for a local shop?
Yes, if you have a source list. Build it for your country or region and limit the ad set location to your service area.

### Do lookalikes beat broad targeting?
No source proves that for local business. Test both with the same budget.

### What does a lead cost on Meta?
LocaliQ's September 2026 benchmark shows $27.39 per lead across industries, and varies by sector.
