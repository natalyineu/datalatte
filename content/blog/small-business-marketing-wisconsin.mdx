## How Big Is the Wisconsin Visitor Market?

Gov. Tony Evers and the Department of Tourism reported 2025 as the fourth straight record year: $27 billion in total impact (up from $25.8 billion in 2024), 117.9 million visits (up 3.5 million), more than $1.7 billion in state and local tax revenue and more than 183,000 jobs supported. The figures come from a state press release republished by Urban Milwaukee.

Those are state totals. They do not say how much any one cafe or salon gains. If you serve visitors, compare your own monthly bookings with last year before you shift budget.

## How Do You Keep Ads Inside Your Own Town?

Google's help says radius targeting shows ads to people within a set distance of a location, with a minimum radius of 1 km. It describes "Presence or interest" as reaching people in or interested in your location, and "Presence" as limited to people physically there.

A Door County cafe may want visitors who search the area before they drive up, so use "Presence or interest". A Milwaukee neighbourhood barber that only serves locals may prefer "Presence". ## How Do You Get Found on Google Maps in Wisconsin?

Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to pay for a better local ranking. You can control relevance and prominence: complete the profile, add photos, collect reviews and reply to them.

For storefronts, Google's hours guidance says to use special hours for holidays or temporary changes instead of editing regular hours. That matters for seasonal Wisconsin businesses that close or shorten hours in winter. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers each field.

## Does Facebook Matter More in Rural Wisconsin?

Possibly. Pew's 2026 national survey found Facebook used by 76% of rural US adults against 67% of urban adults, while Instagram is the reverse (40% rural, 58% urban). Pew does not publish Wisconsin-only figures.

For a business in a small town, start with a Facebook Page and local posts. In Milwaukee or Madison, test Instagram too. Our guide to [Meta ads for local businesses](/blog/local-business-meta-ads) covers paid options.

## FAQ

### How much should a Wisconsin small business spend on Google Ads?

No state figure exists. At the all-industry average of $5.42 a click, $500 a month buys roughly 92 clicks. Start with an amount you can afford to lose while you learn.

### Is tourism a good reason to advertise?

Only if visitors buy from you. State tourism totals are large, but they do not show your share. Compare your own seasonal sales first.

### Is Google Business Profile worth the effort?

Yes. It is free, and Google says reviews, links and complete information affect how prominent you are in local results.

### Does Facebook beat Instagram in Wisconsin?

Nationally Facebook is stronger in rural areas and Instagram in cities. Test both with a small budget.
