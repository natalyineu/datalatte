Programmatic advertising is priced per 1,000 impressions, called CPM, and the final bill depends on format, inventory quality and the minimum you commit to. Programmatic DOOH CPMs range from $3 to $20 according to [AdQuick](https://www.adquick.com/answers/dooh-campaigns/how-much-does-dooh-digital-out-of-home-advertising-cost), and those campaigns generally need $10,000 to $50,000 of minimum spend. For small businesses, one [programmatic guide](https://www.adpersonam.io/blog/small-business-advertising/programmatic-advertising-strategies-for-small-businesses) recommends at least $1,000 to get meaningful reach. Platform fees come on top, and the CPM alone does not show what a lead costs.

## What Does a CPM Actually Buy?

CPM stands for cost per mille, the price of 1,000 ad impressions. To estimate reach, divide your budget by the CPM and multiply by 1,000. The DOOH example from [AdQuick](https://www.adquick.com/answers/dooh-campaigns/how-much-does-dooh-digital-out-of-home-advertising-cost) makes this concrete: a $10,000 budget at a $5 CPM yields approximately 2 million impressions.

An impression is not a visit. Nobody has to click, so a low CPM says nothing about whether anyone acted. Programmatic buying uses automated technology to purchase and serve ads in real time, matching inventory to the criteria the advertiser sets, according to [Paramount's ad manager glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions). Targeting controls who sees the ad, not what they do next.

For a local business, the useful number is cost per lead, which needs click rate and conversion rate as well.

## Is There a Minimum Budget for Programmatic Advertising?

Yes, and the figure depends on the format. A small-business guide recommends at least $1,000 per month for programmatic to reach a meaningful audience ([AdPersonam](https://www.adpersonam.io/blog/small-business-advertising/programmatic-advertising-strategies-for-small-businesses), 2024). Programmatic DOOH is a much larger commitment, with campaigns generally requiring $10,000 to $50,000 ([AdQuick](https://www.adquick.com/answers/dooh-campaigns/how-much-does-dooh-digital-out-of-home-advertising-cost)).

Ask every provider for its minimum before you sign. Minimums are set by the vendor, and the sources above are vendor pages rather than rules, so the figure you are quoted may differ. A budget below $1,000 a month is hard to test properly, because too few impressions arrive to show what works.

## How Much Do DOOH and CTV Programmatic Campaigns Cost?

Connected TV is priced differently again. In the UK, [The TV Agency](https://thetvagency.com/insights/connected-tv-ads-in-the-uk/) says a regional CTV test could easily cost less than £10k, while a much larger national campaign could easily cost £50k or more. A [Netflix example from AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/netflix-advertising-small-business) puts a $200 to $400 monthly test at a $40 programmatic CPM. Dividing $200 and $400 by $40 gives 5,000 to 10,000 impressions.

Video is also priced on completed views. [Amazon Ads](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/connected-tv-advertising) notes that cost per completed view measures the price of each completed ad, alongside video completion rate. For more detail, read [how much CTV advertising costs](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data).

A vendor case study reports that HP's Trade Desk campaign cost per unique household came in 23% below its previous campaign, which had used programmatic guaranteed and private marketplace buying ([AI Digital](https://www.aidigital.com/blog/ctv-advertising-examples), 2024). That is one brand's result, not a benchmark.

## Which Fees Sit on Top of the CPM?

Platform fees are the cost most owners miss. [ClicksGeek](https://clicksgeek.com/best-advertising-platforms/) states that The Trade Desk charges platform fees on top of media spend, so total programmatic cost includes both. Agency or managed-service fees can add another layer.

Ask for the total cost per 1,000 impressions, including every fee, in writing. Treat vendor dashboards with care too. A [BidWaves page](https://www.bidwaves.com/what-we-do/small-business-digital-marketing-services/programmatic-advertising) shows a streaming performance figure of −28%, but it is labelled as a campaign illustration, not a verified client result.

## What Does a Local Budget Look Like? A Worked Example

The inputs below are assumptions for a med spa testing one programmatic campaign for a month. The value per customer uses the low end of the $3,000 to $7,000+ case value that [Adverge](https://advergemedia.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks-2026) cites for dental, orthodontic and med spa leads.

| Step | Value | How it is calculated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $1,000 (assumption) | Input, near the recommended minimum |
| CPM | $10 (assumption) | Input |
| Impressions | 100,000 | $1,000 ÷ $10 × 1,000 |
| Click-through rate | 0.3% (assumption) | Input |
| Clicks | 300 | 100,000 × 0.3% |
| Cost per click | $3.33 | $1,000 ÷ 300 |
| Booking page conversion | 3% (assumption) | Input |
| Leads | 9 | 300 × 3% |
| Cost per lead | $111 | $1,000 ÷ 9 |
| Value per customer | $3,000 (assumption) | Low end of cited case value |
| Close rate | 25% (assumption) | Input |
| Expected value per lead | $750 | $3,000 × 25% |

On these inputs the expected value per lead is about 6.8 times the cost per lead. Change one assumption and the result moves. At a 0.1% click rate, you would get 100 clicks, a $10 cost per click, 3 leads and a cost per lead of about $333. Replace every assumption with your own test data before you decide.

## How Does Programmatic Cost per Lead Compare With Search?

Search is a different channel, so these figures are not like-for-like. A grooming-industry page cites LocaliQ's 2026 search benchmarks for Animals and Pets, which averaged $4.06 per click, a 16.22% conversion rate and $31.50 per lead ([Perform Pets](https://performpets.com/how-much-does-grooming-salon-marketing-cost/), secondary).

Search leads look cheaper in that category because people are already typing a need. Programmatic reaches people who have not searched yet, which is why it suits awareness and retargeting. Compare channels on cost per lead for your own offer, not on CPM.

## When Should a Small Business Skip Programmatic?

Skip it for now if your budget is under about $1,000 a month, if you cannot track leads from the ads, or if customers already find you by searching. For Google Ads, Focus Digital estimates a minimum viable monthly budget of $3,800 for real estate ([Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/minimum-google-ads-budget-by-industry-2025-guidelines/), 2025), so search can also need more money than owners expect. Read [what Google Ads costs for small businesses](/blog/how-much-does-google-ads-cost-for-small-businesses-real-2026-pricing-breakdown) before deciding.

Many salons, for example, get more from search and their listing than from display. The [salon marketing guide](/blog/best-marketing-for-salons-in-2026-what-actually-works-with-real-examples) covers what tends to work. Social ads are another cheaper option to compare, and [Facebook ad costs](/blog/how-much-do-facebook-ads-cost-for-small-businesses-2026-benchmarks) show how they stack up.

## FAQ

### How much does programmatic advertising cost?

Programmatic DOOH CPMs range from $3 to $20, and a recommended small-business minimum is $1,000 a month. Platform and agency fees are extra, so the real total is higher than the media price.

### What is a good CPM for programmatic?

No universal figure exists. A low CPM can still produce expensive leads, so judge the campaign by cost per lead and by whether leads convert into customers.

### Is programmatic cheaper than Google Ads or Meta?

Per impression, it can be. Per lead, the answer depends on click and conversion rates, and the worked example above shows how much those rates matter.

### Do quoted CPMs include fees?

Not always. The Trade Desk charges platform fees on top of media spend, so ask providers for the all-in cost per 1,000 impressions in writing.
