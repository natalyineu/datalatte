A European small business should set its marketing budget from what one customer is worth, not from a fixed percentage of revenue. This guide could not find an official or well-sourced percentage for European small firms, and the US Small Business Administration page it checked gives none either, so any figure quoted without a source is a guess. What you can do is cap paid spend with Google's rules, put free channels first, and test small.

Eurostat reports that 63.57% of EU enterprises used social media in 2025 (60.59% of small enterprises with 10 to 49 people), so your competitors are already visible online.

## How Much Should a European Small Business Spend on Marketing?

Start with a number you can lose while you learn, then judge it by customers won. A practical method has three steps. Work out the gross profit of an average customer. Decide how many extra customers you want a month. Divide the money you can afford by the customers it must bring in to break even.

For example, if a customer brings you EUR 120 gross profit over a year (an assumption), spending EUR 60 to win one is sensible and EUR 200 is not. The worked example below applies this. No source here supplies a typical percentage of revenue for Europe, so treat anyone who gives one without a source with caution.

## What Should Come First: Free or Paid?

Free channels first, because they raise the return on paid ones. A complete Google Business Profile, a mobile-friendly site and a steady flow of reviews cost time, not media money. Google says there is no way to pay for a better local ranking in its free results, and that local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence.

BrightLocal's 2026 survey (1,002 US adults, so use with care in Europe) found 97% read online reviews and 68% require at least 4 stars. ## How Do Google Ads Budgets Actually Work?

Google's help says your average daily budget is what you are willing to spend each day on average. On a given day spend can reach up to twice the daily budget, and Google will not charge more than the monthly charging limit, which is 30.4 times the average daily budget. Its example: a 10 per day budget gives a maximum monthly charge of 304.

So a daily budget of EUR 15 caps a month at about EUR 456. Set the daily figure from the monthly amount you can afford, divided by 30.4. Click prices vary by country and trade; the LocaliQ 2026 benchmark of $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead across industries is for US advertisers in dollars, so use it only as a rough reference. See [Google Ads for European businesses](/blog/google-ads-for-european-businesses).

## What Else Costs Money in a European Marketing Budget?

List the fixed items before the ad spend. They are easy to forget and they differ by country.

- Website hosting, domain and updates.
- Email or SMS tool subscriptions.
- Photography or video of your own premises and work.
- Any freelancer or agency fee.
- Time: yours or staff, at a rate you set.

Current European pricing for email tools could not be verified (Brevo's pricing page returned no figures), so get quotes in your own currency. Consent and privacy rules for email and texts vary by country, and this guide did not verify them; check your national regulator before sending marketing messages.

## Worked Example: A EUR 600 Month

All figures are **assumptions** for an imaginary hairdresser; they show the method, not a recommendation.

| Item | Amount (EUR) |
| --- | --- |
| Google Ads, search only | 300 |
| Review request tool or printed cards | 30 |
| Photos of recent work | 100 |
| Email tool and website upkeep | 70 |
| Reserve for testing a second channel | 100 |
| Total | 600 |

Suppose EUR 300 of ads brings 5 new customers (assumed). That is EUR 60 each. If each brings EUR 120 gross profit over a year, the ads return EUR 600 on EUR 300 before other costs. If it brings only 2 customers, the cost per customer is EUR 150 and you should stop, change the keywords or fix the website.

## How Do You Review the Budget Each Month?

Keep a one-page sheet: spend, enquiries, customers and cost per customer, by channel. Ask every new customer how they found you. After 60 to 90 days, move money from the channel with the highest cost per customer to the lowest.

## FAQ

### What percentage of revenue should a small business spend on marketing?

No official figure was found for European small business. The US SBA page checked gives none either. Base the budget on customer value and test results.

### How much should a small business spend on Google Ads per month?

Whatever you can afford to lose while testing. Google's monthly cap is 30.4 times your average daily budget, so a daily budget of EUR 10 caps a month at EUR 304.

### Is social media free?

Posting costs time, not fees. Eurostat says 63.57% of EU enterprises used some form of social media in 2025, so competition for attention is high.

### Are US ad benchmarks usable in Europe?

Only as a rough reference. LocaliQ's figures are US dollar averages for US advertisers. Use your own account data after the first month.

### When should I raise the budget?

When a channel gives a cost per customer below what a customer is worth and you have capacity to serve more.
