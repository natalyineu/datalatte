You can no longer create new Google call-only ads. Google's help page says that from February 2026 "all options to create new call ads will be removed," and "existing call ads will stop receiving impressions" in February 2027. The replacement is a Responsive Search Ad with your phone number added as a call asset. Clicks on call assets cost the same as headline clicks.

This guide is for service businesses that rely on phone enquiries: plumbers, cleaners, locksmiths, electricians and similar trades. It explains what changed, how to switch and what to measure.

## Do Call-Only Campaigns Still Work in 2026?

Existing call ads can still run until February 2027, but you cannot build new ones. Google's Ads API notice dates the end of creation to January 2026 and says existing call-only ads "will stop serving in February 2027." An ad group whose only ad is a call ad will stop showing then.

Google says to move to call assets. If you still run a call-only campaign, add a responsive search ad with a call asset to every ad group now, then compare results before the old ads stop.

## What Replaces Call-Only Ads?

A call asset adds your phone number to a Search ad. Google's help says call assets run on Search and Smart campaigns, and that clicks on mobile call assets and desktop "Call us" buttons are billed at the same rate as headline clicks.

| | Call-only ad (retiring) | RSA with call asset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can you create it now? | No | Yes |
| Click goes to | Starts a call from the device | Website or the phone call, user's choice |
| Billing | Not stated on the Google page read | Same CPC as a headline click |
| Scheduling | Not stated on the page read | Asset schedule by day and hour |
| Status | Stops serving Feb 2027 | Supported |

The main difference is that people can choose your page or the phone. That helps when callers want to check reviews or prices first, and it costs you some call-only exclusivity.

## How Do You Set Up a Call Asset?

1. Add a phone number that follows E.164 formatting. Google does not accept vanity, premium or fax numbers, and the exact number must appear in your site's source code or your domain must be verified in Search Console.
2. Turn on call reporting if you want calls counted as conversions. Google says this is available on the Search Network and uses Google forwarding numbers, which exist only in certain countries.
3. Use the asset schedule to show the number only when someone answers.
4. Add the asset to campaigns or ad groups that already use a responsive search ad.

Check the country list before planning around forwarding numbers. If they are unavailable where you operate, track calls with your phone system or CRM instead and import them.

## How Do You Measure Calls?

Google lists five conversion types: calls from ads, calls to a website number, taps on a number on a mobile site, clicks on call ads and assets without a forwarding number, and imported call conversions. Taps on a number do not prove a call took place, and clicks without a forwarding number are Google's estimate.

For calls from ads and website calls you set a minimum call length, and every call at least that long counts. Choose a length that matches a real enquiry. The help page gives no default. The [hardware store guide](/blog/google-ads-for-hardware-stores) shows the same call-length setting in a retail example.

## What Should a Call Cost?

Nobody publishes a cost per call for service trades. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks give $5.42 CPC and $66.69 cost per lead across all industries. Home and Home Improvement averages $8.33 CPC, 8.05% conversion and $90.92 cost per lead. Plumbing is not listed.

| Category (LocaliQ 2026) | CPC | Conversion rate | Cost per lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All industries | $5.42 | 8.18% | $66.69 |
| Home and Home Improvement | $8.33 | 8.05% | $90.92 |
| Automotive repair, service and parts | $4.35 | 15.51% | $29.96 |
| Attorneys and legal services | $9.87 | 5.55% | $131.63 |

These are averages across all lead types, not calls only. Use them as a range.

## Worked Example: A Weekly Call Budget

Inputs are **assumptions**, built on the Home and Home Improvement benchmark: a $1,000 monthly budget, $8.33 CPC and 8.05% conversion rate, where a conversion is a qualified call.

| Step | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Clicks ($1,000 divided by $8.33) | about 120 |
| Conversions (120 times 8.05%) | about 9.7 |
| Cost per conversion | about $103 |
| Share of calls booked (assumption) | 50% |
| Cost per booked job | about $206 |

If an average job earns more than about $206 in profit, the campaign pays. If not, tighten keywords and the service area before raising the budget. ## FAQ

### Can I still create a call-only campaign?

No. Google's help page says options to create new call ads were removed in February 2026.

### When do existing call ads stop?

Google says existing call ads will stop receiving impressions in February 2027.

### Do call assets cost more than call-only ads?

Google says clicks on call assets cost the same as headline clicks, a standard CPC. A comparison of old call ad prices is not published.

### How do I count a call as a conversion?

Turn on call reporting for calls from ads or website calls and set a minimum length. Imported call conversions work for calls tracked in a CRM.

### What is a good cost per call?

Google and LocaliQ publish no cost per call for service trades. Use cost per booked job from your own account.
