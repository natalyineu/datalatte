A local business should send its first welcome email immediately after signup, then follow with a short series. Constant Contact's 2026 post says welcome emails get open rates of 68% to 84% depending on the study, against about 30.8% for its standard marketing email. Those figures are vendor data and inflated by Apple Mail Privacy Protection, so use them as direction only.

This guide gives a five-email template for cafes, salons, groomers and studios, based on what the sources say about timing and length. It also covers metrics, the legal basics and a worked example labelled as an assumption.

## How Many Emails Should a Welcome Series Have?

Fewer than most templates suggest. Stripo Research summarises GetResponse data: a single welcome email gets the highest per-email open rate, short series of one to three emails hold the strongest opens and clicks, and series of five or more show tapering opens. A series earns more total revenue than a single email, but returns diminish.

So treat the five-email template below as a menu. Send emails 1 to 3 to everyone, and send 4 and 5 only to people who clicked. Stripo's report is a secondary source summarising other vendors, and it labels most figures as directional. Our own sequence has no tested results behind it.

| Source | Welcome open rate | Welcome click rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GetResponse (via Stripo) | 83.63% | 16.60% |
| Omnisend (via Stripo, directional) | about 35% | not stated |
| Constant Contact 2026 post | 68-84% | about 14-16% |

## What Goes in Each of the Five Emails?

Each email has one job and one link.

1. **Immediately: the promise.** Deliver what the signup offered, such as a discount code or guide. Constant Contact advises one clear next action. Say who you are and where you are.
2. **Day 2: the story.** Why the business exists, with a real photo. Use true facts only.
3. **Day 4: the best-seller.** Show the product or service customers book most, with price and a booking link.
4. **Day 7: proof.** Quote a real Google review with the reviewer's permission, or link to your profile. See our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).
5. **Day 10: the invitation.** Ask for a first visit or booking, and mention a reply address.

The day gaps are a suggestion, not a tested schedule. The sources do not publish an ideal delay beyond sending the first email at once. ## Which Metrics Should You Judge It By?

Do not rely on open rates. Apple Mail Privacy Protection preloads images, so opens are overstated, and welcome emails are hit hardest. Stripo advises judging them on click-to-open rate, conversion and revenue per recipient. GetResponse reports a click-to-open rate of 19.85% for welcome emails, which is a vendor figure.

For a local business, conversion means a booking, a visit with a code, or a reply. Track the code on redeeming. If you cannot track it, ask new customers at the till how they heard about you.

## What Does the Law Require?

In the US, CAN-SPAM requires accurate header information, a non-misleading subject line, a clear ad disclosure, a physical postal address and a working opt-out honoured within 10 business days. The FTC says each violating email can carry a penalty of up to $53,088.

In the UK and EU, get consent first. Our [GDPR guide](/blog/gdpr-compliant-email-marketing) explains it. A welcome series is only for people who signed up, so keep the sign-up form clear about what they will get.

## A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**, not benchmarks.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| New subscribers a month | 80 |
| Welcome email click rate | 10% |
| Clickers who redeem the offer | 15% |
| Average first spend | $12 |

80 subscribers at 10% gives 8 clickers. At 15% redemption, that is about 1 redemption, or $12 in first spend. That is small, and honest. The value of a welcome series is that it runs without extra work, and the same list can later get seasonal messages, see [holiday email campaigns](/blog/holiday-email-marketing-campaigns). ## FAQ

### How long should a welcome email series be?

GetResponse data summarised by Stripo suggests one to three emails perform best. A five-email series is fine if later emails go only to people who engaged.

### When should the first welcome email send?

Immediately. Constant Contact says immediate delivery converts better than delayed sends, though its 4x claim is not backed by named data.

### Do welcome emails need a discount?

No source says they must. Constant Contact advises delivering whatever the signup promised. If you promised nothing, send a useful guide or booking link.

### What is a good open rate?

Published figures range from about 35% to 84%. Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates them, so judge on clicks and conversions.

### Can I send a series to people who did not sign up?

In the US you must still follow CAN-SPAM. In the UK and EU you need consent. Welcome series should go only to those who opted in.
