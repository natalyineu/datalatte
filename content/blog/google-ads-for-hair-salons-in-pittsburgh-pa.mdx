## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Hair Salon?

LocaliQ, updated 1 June 2026, shows Beauty and Personal Care at $4.62 per click, a 6.75% click-through rate, a 10.35% conversion rate and a $39.25 cost per lead. All industries average $5.42 per click, 6.64% click-through, 8.18% conversion and $66.69 per lead.

The category includes other beauty businesses, so a salon's own numbers may differ. The cost per click in your area depends on how many salons bid on the same searches. Use your first month of data as the real benchmark.

## How Does Pittsburgh's Layout Affect Targeting?

Wikipedia (secondary source) gives Pittsburgh 302,971 residents in the 2020 census and a metro area of 2,429,917. It names neighbourhoods such as Shadyside, Squirrel Hill, Lawrenceville, South Side and the Strip District, and says the University of Pittsburgh and Carnegie Mellon University sit in the East End, among 68 colleges and universities.

A salon draws mostly from nearby areas. Google says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km, and "Presence" fits when you only want people in your locations. Start near the salon, check the location report weekly and widen only if booking cost holds.

## Which Keywords Should a Salon Start With?

Begin with services you want more of, paired with place: for example "balayage Pittsburgh" or "haircut Lawrenceville" if you offer them. Google's match types are broad, phrase and exact; exact gives the most control and the fewest searches. A new account usually suits phrase and exact first.

Add negatives from the start: jobs, courses, wigs, hair products and "diy". Google says negatives do not match close variants, so add singular and plural forms yourself. Review the Search terms report weekly. Our [Google Ads for hair salons guide](/blog/google-ads-for-hair-salons-complete-guide) covers account structure in more detail.

## How Do You Track Bookings?

Count the actions that lead to a chair being filled: booking button clicks, calls and form submissions. Google says clicks on a call asset cost the same as headline clicks, and calls become conversions when you turn on call reporting. If your booking software sits on another domain, check that it passes data back, or conversions will go unrecorded.

Cross-check against free data. Business Profile performance shows calls, directions, website clicks and, when you use a booking provider, completed bookings. See [Google Analytics for hair salons](/blog/google-analytics-for-hair-salons) for tracking setup.

## How Much Control Do You Have Over Spend?

Google defines average daily budget as the amount you set per campaign per day. A day can spend up to two times that amount, and the monthly limit is 30.4 times the daily budget for most campaigns. A $20 daily budget can therefore reach about $608 in a month, and no more.

Review the budget as demand changes. Before you raise it, fix your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist), because a complete profile helps every channel.
