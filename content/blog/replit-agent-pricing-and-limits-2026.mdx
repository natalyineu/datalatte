Replit Agent is included in Replit's plans: Starter is free, Core costs $20 a month ($18 a month billed annually) and Pro costs $100 a month ($90 billed annually). Beyond a free allowance, Agent work is charged by effort, so a small edit costs less than a large build, and costs come out of your plan credits. Publishing, databases and storage draw on the same credits.

Prices and features checked on 7 November 2026.

This guide is for owners and marketers of local businesses who want to know what Replit Agent is, what it costs in practice and where the limits are. Numbers come from Replit's own pages, listed in Sources. Where a page did not state a figure, the guide says so.

## How Much Does Replit Agent Cost?

Replit's pricing page showed Core, Pro and Enterprise cards; the documentation describes Starter as the free plan.

| Plan | Price | What the vendor pages say |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Free | Daily Agent credits capped monthly, lite build only, one free published app that goes down after 30 days, "Made with Replit" badge, no collaboration seats |
| Core | $20 a month, or $18 a month billed annually | "For personal projects and simple apps." "$20 towards more powerful models", up to 30 hours of chat on Free Mode, up to 60 projects on Free Mode, unlimited workspaces |
| Pro | $100 a month, or $90 a month billed annually | "For commercial and professional builds." Everything in Core plus more Free Mode usage, "$100 credits", up to 15 collaborators, database rollback up to 28 days, premium support |

The documentation adds that Core includes full build, Plan Mode, third-party connectors and removal of the badge. It gives Core 5 collaboration seats and 1 background task, and Pro 15 seats and 10 background tasks. ## How Does Effort-Based Pricing Work?

Replit's billing documentation says paid Agent work uses effort-based pricing. Agent creates a checkpoint when it completes work on your request. Simple requests, such as bug fixes or small changes, typically cost less than complex builds. Complex builds are bundled into one checkpoint, which may cost more to reflect the total effort.

Two safeguards are stated. Replit asks for confirmation before a paid action starts, and costs per checkpoint show in the Agent tab. Usage alerts and budget limits are set in Settings, under Billing. Dashboard data can take up to 30 minutes to appear.

Some Agent features call outside AI services such as Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini. Those are billed at the provider's public API rate and deducted from your credits. The pages do not give a dollar cost per checkpoint, so no per-task price is stated here.

## What Else Uses Credits?

Credits cover more than Agent. According to the documentation, they also pay for published apps, storage and databases. Publishing is metered in three ways: outbound data transfer, Autoscale compute units and requests. Core includes a 100 GiB outbound transfer allowance.

Databases follow separate rules. Development databases are free and only production databases are billed, on compute time and storage. The production storage limit is 100 GiB on paid plans and 1 GiB on Starter. A database suspends after five idle minutes and counts compute time while it receives requests.

For a small site that rarely changes, the monthly hosting-style cost therefore depends on traffic and database use, not on the plan price alone. Published prices for requests are on Replit's pricing page and were not shown in the text opened.

## What Is Replit Agent and How Does It Work?

Replit describes Agent this way: "Tell Replit Agent your app, website, or agent idea, and it builds it through chat." You refine the result by giving feedback. Agent integrates with a built-in Database and user authentication, plus services such as Stripe and OpenAI. It tests the app in a browser, reports issues and fixes them, and for harder requests you can turn on extended thinking and high-power models.

It sits with [Lovable](/blog/lovable-pricing-and-credits-2026) and Bolt.new as a chat-to-app builder, unlike editor-style tools such as [Windsurf](/blog/windsurf-editor-pricing-and-setup-2026).

## What Can a Small Business Use It For?

These are possibilities, not results. The product page lists apps, websites and automations that connect to tools such as Slack or email. For a local business that could mean:

- A one-page site for a café menu with opening hours.
- A booking-request or class-enquiry page that collects names and emails.
- A simple internal tool, such as a rota or stock checklist for a salon.
- A small client form for a pet groomer that stores answers in the built-in database.

Each of these still needs human checks: forms, payments and stored customer details deserve a careful test. If the goal is just a site, compare with the [Cursor route](/blog/cursor-ai-build-local-business-website-no-code)..

## What Are the Risks and Limits?

- **Variable cost.** Effort-based pricing means the cost of a request is not fixed in advance, though confirmation is requested first.
- **Free app expires.** On Starter, the one published app goes down after 30 days and carries a Replit badge.
- **Credits do not last forever.** Pro's tiered monthly credits roll over for two months, per the documentation.
- **Payment failures.** Continued failures may suspend published apps.
- **Review.** Test forms and stored data before real customers use them.

This guide did not open Replit's terms on data use, ownership or export of code, so those are not stated here. Read them before building anything with customer data.

## FAQ

### Is Replit Agent free?

There is a free Starter plan with daily Agent credits capped monthly. Per the documentation it is lite build only, with one published app that goes down after 30 days. Full build requires Core or Pro.

### How much does Replit Agent cost per month?

Core is $20 a month, or $18 a month billed annually. Pro is $100 a month, or $90 billed annually. Agent work beyond free allowances is charged by effort and deducted from credits.

### What is effort-based pricing on Replit?

Agent creates a checkpoint when it finishes your request. Simple changes typically cost less than complex builds, which are bundled into one checkpoint. Replit asks for confirmation before a paid action starts.

### Can I publish a website with Replit?

Yes, according to the documentation. Starter allows one free published app for 30 days. Core and Pro allow more published apps. Hosting is metered in data transfer, compute units and requests.

### Is Replit Agent worth it for a small business?

It can suit simple tools and pages if you are willing to review the result and watch credits. Start on Starter, then try Core for a fixed period. Ongoing hosting costs depend on traffic and databases.
