In South Korea, KakaoTalk has 49.1 million monthly users (95.1% of the population), Instagram reaches 25.6 million in ad tools, and Facebook reaches only 7.05 million. A small Korean business should plan around KakaoTalk, Instagram and local search, not Facebook.

This guide is for cafes, salons, studios and service firms in South Korea. It uses published figures, labels every assumption, and ends with a break-even formula you can run on your own numbers. Facts were checked on 1 November 2026. The platform data are DataReportal's Digital 2026 figures, mostly from October 2025.

## Who Are You Marketing To in South Korea?

South Korea had 51.7 million people in October 2025, down 0.1%. 81.6% live in urban areas and the median age is 45.6. Internet users number 50.6 million (97.9%), and social media identities total 49.3 million (95.4%).

Median download speeds from Ookla are 224.54 Mbps on mobile and 233.15 Mbps on fixed lines in the 12 months to August 2025, so heavy video and image pages load fast. Only 1.09 million people are offline.

Facebook is a weak channel here: 7.05 million and falling 7.8%, with a 61.7% male adult audience. ## How Should Google Ads Language and Location Work in South Korea?

Google's Ads Help says that from September 2026 the campaign-level language setting is being removed for Search campaigns, and Search ads will match on the language of the ads themselves. For searches with a clear language, Google prioritises ads in that language. Write ads and landing pages in Korean for local customers. Add an English version only if you serve foreign visitors.

For location, Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km and recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting as best practice for Search, while presence-only fits when you want to avoid people outside your target area. Set a radius around your premises, then read the report to see which areas convert.

No South Korea-specific click costs were found in the sources checked, so every cost figure below is an assumption.

## How Should You Split a Small Budget?

Start with local search and map listings where your customers look, add Instagram for visual services, and use KakaoTalk to keep customers you already have. Treat TikTok as a small test of about 10 to 15% of paid spend (a suggestion, not a platform rule), and skip Facebook unless your own data shows it works.

## What Does a Test Budget Return? The Math

These rows are **assumptions** for illustration, in US dollars. They are not South Korea benchmarks. Replace each with your own data after a month.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget (assumption) | $300 | $300 | $300 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | $1.00 | $0.60 | $0.40 |
| Clicks | 300 | 500 | 750 |
| Click to enquiry rate (assumption) | 3% | 5% | 7% |
| Enquiries | 9 | 25 | 53 |
| Enquiry to booking rate (assumption) | 30% | 40% | 50% |
| Bookings | 2.7 | 10 | 26.3 |
| Ad cost per booking | $111 | $30 | $11 |
| First-visit value (assumption) | $40 | $40 | $40 |
| Revenue minus ad spend | -$192 | +$100 | +$750 |

Counts use unrounded values. Revenue is not profit: staff, product and rent come out of it. A first visit often does not pay back by itself, which is why repeat visits matter.

Your break-even click price is the first-visit value times the enquiry rate times the booking rate. At $40, 5% and 40%, that is 40 x 0.05 x 0.40 = $0.80 per click. If your clicks cost more on average, the first visit loses money and you need repeat bookings to justify it. The biggest lever is usually the booking rate: answer messages and calls within minutes.

## FAQ

### Is Facebook useful in South Korea?
Reach is small: 7.05 million in ad tools and falling 7.8% over the year, per DataReportal.

### How big is KakaoTalk?
49.1 million monthly active users, 95.1% of the population, according to Kakao earnings cited by DataReportal.

### Is Instagram worth it for a Korean cafe or salon?
It reaches 25.6 million and grew 13.1%, and 55.2% of its adult audience is female, so it fits visual services.

### Does this guide cover Naver?
Only a 2021 Smart Place press release. DataReportal does not cover Naver, so no reach figures are given.
