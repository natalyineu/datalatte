There is no single European social media market. In late 2025 Facebook had the biggest ad reach of the main Meta apps in the UK and France, Instagram led in Spain, and in Germany YouTube and Instagram were far ahead of Facebook. A local business in Europe should check the numbers for its own country before it picks a platform. This page collects the published figures for four large markets.

All reach numbers come from DataReportal (Kepios) "Digital 2026" country reports, published 5 November 2025. They are advertising reach estimates from each platform's own ad tools. DataReportal says they are not monthly active users, so treat them as a guide to audience size, not exact headcounts.

## How Many Europeans Use Social Networks?

Eurostat reports that in 2025, 67% of people aged 16 to 74 in the EU took part in social networks. That is the EU average, and country figures differ. A secondary aggregator, Trading Economics, lists Denmark near 90% and Cyprus near 87% for 2025, with Croatia near 61% at the bottom of its top-ten table. Check Eurostat's own table for your country before you rely on those.

The point for a local business is simple: most adults in the EU are reachable on social media, so the real question is which platform, not whether to be on one. The next sections compare platforms in four markets.

## Which Platforms Reach the Most People in the UK, Germany, France and Spain?

The table shows ad reach as a share of the total population, from DataReportal's country reports. TikTok is the exception: DataReportal reports it as a share of adults aged 18 and over, so do not compare it directly with the other columns. A dash means the figure was not in the report.

## Is Facebook Still Worth Using in Europe?

Yes in some countries, much less in others. Facebook reaches 55.6% of the UK population and 47.2% of France, but 27.1% of Germany. DataReportal also reports year-on-year changes: Facebook ad reach fell 11.5% in Germany and 5.2% in Spain between October 2024 and October 2025. DataReportal warns that part of any decline can be a platform data correction.

For a hairdresser in Lyon or a café in Leeds, a Facebook page and local groups still reach a large share of residents. In Germany the same effort often does better on Instagram or YouTube, where reach is higher. Test with a small paid budget before you commit.

## Where Is TikTok Strongest?

DataReportal reports TikTok ad reach of 52.0% of Spanish adults, 48.4% of UK adults, 43.9% of French adults and 33.9% of German adults. In Spain, TikTok reach grew 15.9% from end-2024 to late 2025; in Germany it grew 8.5% over the same period.

That makes TikTok worth testing for businesses whose customers are under 40, such as salons, cafés and gyms. It does not tell you your local customers are there. ## How Should a Local Business Split a Small Social Budget? (Worked Example)

All inputs below are **assumptions** for illustration, not benchmarks. A café in Madrid has a social budget of 300 euros a month.

| Step | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Starting point | Spain: Instagram 55.0% of the population, Facebook 42.3%, TikTok 52.0% of adults |
| Month 1 split | 150 euros Instagram, 100 euros Facebook, 50 euros TikTok boosts |
| What to record | Direction requests, calls, and "how did you find us" answers at the till |
| Month 2 decision | Move budget toward the platform with the cheapest recorded visit, not the most likes |

The split follows reach, but the decision after month one follows your own results. If two platforms cost about the same per visit, keep both. Page reach figures never replace your own counts.

## FAQ

### Which social media platform is most popular in Europe?

It depends on the country. In late 2025 DataReportal showed Facebook ahead in the UK and France among Meta apps, and Instagram ahead in Spain and Germany. YouTube had the largest reach in all four markets, but it behaves more like a video library than a local discovery tool.

### Is TikTok big enough in Europe for a local business?

DataReportal shows TikTok reaching roughly a third to a half of adults in the UK, Germany, France and Spain. Start with organic posts and one small paid test before spending seriously.

### Do these numbers show how many people use each platform every month?

No. They are advertising reach estimates from the platforms' ad tools. DataReportal says they are not active user counts, and LinkedIn figures are registered members.

### Should I use the same platform mix in every European country?

No. The same page can do very different jobs in Germany and Spain, as the table shows. Check the figures for your own market.
