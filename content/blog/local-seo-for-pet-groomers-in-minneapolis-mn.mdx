## What does a Minneapolis pet groomer need on Google Business Profile?

Google Business Profile is a free tool for managing your presence across Google Search and Maps, so the cost of setup is time, not money. [Google's sign-up page](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/) describes it as a free profile. Claim it, verify it, then complete every field. For a deeper walkthrough, see the [Google Business Profile guide for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-for-pet-groomers).

1. Choose a primary category that matches your main service. Adwave reports that the primary category is the top ranking factor for the Local Pack, though it does not name the study behind that claim. Check the top 3-5 map results for your service search to see which categories competitors use.
2. Write a description of up to 750 characters. Lead with the service, the Minneapolis neighbourhood, and what sets you apart.
3. Make your hours, phone number, and address 100% accurate. Adwave treats this as a basic requirement.
4. Upload at least 15 high-quality photos. Adwave recommends this number for a profile that gets more clicks and calls.
5. Post at least once a week. Google shows a post publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live, so check the status after publishing.
6. If you travel to clients or work from home, hide your street address and set a service area. Google treats such profiles as Service Area Businesses.

## How many reviews does a pet groomer need to rank?

Google states that reviews appear next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search, so each review is visible to a searcher at the moment of decision. Adwave cites a Blogging Wizard analysis of 2 million profiles that found businesses with over 200 reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack. For a single grooming shop, treat that figure as a benchmark rather than a near-term target.

Another blog states that businesses with over 50 reviews and a 4.7+ average rating dominate top map pack spots, but it cites no source, so treat that claim as unverified. Ask for reviews after each appointment, and invite customers to describe the service they used in their own words.

## Which citations should you check first?

Citations are listings of your name, address, and phone number on other websites. Adwave advises that your name, address, and phone number must be exactly the same on your website, your Business Profile, and major directories such as Yelp, Apple Maps, and Bing Places. Fix errors in existing listings before adding new ones, because a wrong listing repeated across sites adds confusion rather than signal.

Small differences count. Adwave notes that the listing must match down to punctuation and whether you write "St." or "Street". Apple Business Connect is also worth claiming: a maps-only comparison reported by Adwave shows it has already captured a 7% share and is growing. It reports Profile Views, which counts how many people saw your business card in Apple Maps.

## Does your website affect Minneapolis map pack rankings?

Your website supports the signals your profile sends. Google's location asset documentation says that data for Google Ads location assets comes directly from Google Maps and Business Profile, so fixes you make there flow into linked ad accounts. If the Business Profile option does not work, advertisers can select up to 10 Maps locations to link with an Ads account, but those locations must be yours.

Add a Google Maps embed on your contact page, as explained in the [guide to embedding a map on your website](/blog/google-maps-embed-local-business-website). Paid search can also work alongside organic efforts. A 2023 Marketing 360 case study describes a mobile pet groomer that advertised on Facebook and Google. Compare the two paid channels in the [Google Ads vs Facebook Ads comparison](/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-local-business-which-wins).

## What does a worked budget look like for a Minneapolis groomer?

Because the profile is free, the real cost is the hours spent on upkeep. The example below uses assumption-labelled inputs. Replace them with your own numbers from GBP Insights.

Inputs (all assumptions):
- Calls, direction requests, and website visits from your profile per month: 60
- Share of those contacts that become bookings: 25%
- Average booking value: $70
- Hours per month spent on profile upkeep, photos, and posts: 4

Calculation: 60 x 25% = 15 bookings per month. 15 bookings x $70 = $1,050 in monthly booking value. Divided by 4 upkeep hours, that is about $262 of booking value per hour under these assumptions. If your real contact numbers are lower, the value per hour falls in proportion, so measure before you scale.

The steps below keep the upkeep manageable:
1. Week 1: claim, verify, and complete every profile field.
2. Week 2: check the top 3-5 competitors' categories and update yours.
3. Week 3: audit citations and correct any mismatched name, address, or phone entries.
4. Weeks 4-6: upload photos and publish one post per week.
5. Ongoing: ask for a review after each appointment.
6. Monthly: record the metrics below and compare them with the previous month.

## How do you track map pack progress?

Adwave names six local search metrics worth tracking: Business Profile impressions, map pack views, clicks to call, direction requests, website visits from the profile, and branded search lift over time. Record these monthly. Adwave also says a business can begin ranking for its own name a few weeks after verification, which it presents as an estimate.

Check your position for your top five keywords each week in a private browser window, and note the date of each check so you can connect ranking changes to profile changes.

## FAQ

### Is Google Business Profile free for a pet groomer?

Yes. Google describes its Business Profile as a free profile on Search and Maps. Your costs are the hours you spend keeping it current.

### How long before a new Minneapolis groomer ranks for its own name?

Adwave says you will often start ranking for your own business name a few weeks after verification. This is the vendor's own estimate, not a guarantee.

### Can a mobile groomer hide its home address?

Yes. If you work from home or serve customers at their location, you can hide your street address and be designated a Service Area Business, which Adwave describes.

### What should I fix first if my profile is weak?

Start with accuracy. Make your hours, phone number, and address exactly correct, then match those details on your website and directories. Photos, posts, and reviews come next.
