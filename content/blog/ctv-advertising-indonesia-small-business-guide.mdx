The most practical way for an Indonesian small business to start with connected TV advertising is a YouTube video campaign in Google Ads with "TV screens" included in the device settings, aimed at your city. Vidio, a leading local streaming service, sells ads through its sales team, and it publishes no rate card. No public source checked gives a reliable CPM for Indonesian CTV, so budget from a test, not from a quoted average.

This guide covers the audience size, the buying routes, the cost maths and what to check before you spend. Every figure comes from the sources at the end. The illustration is labelled as an assumption.

## How Big Is the Streaming Audience in Indonesia?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 230 million internet users in Indonesia, 80.5% of the population, and 151 million people reachable with YouTube ads, equal to 65.5% of internet users. Google's figure is ad reach, not monthly active users, and it covers all devices, not only TV sets.

For streaming services, Media Partners Asia data reported by Good News From Indonesia in February 2026 puts paid streaming accounts at 26.9 million and says Vidio's watch time grew 24% in Q4 2025. The same article says connected TV adoption is growing quickly in Indonesia but gives no number. No public figure was found for the share of Indonesian households with a connected TV.

## Where Can You Buy CTV Ads in Indonesia?

| Route | How it works | Minimum spend | Rates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube via Google Ads | Video campaign, device targeting includes TV screens, CPV or CPM bidding | Google states no minimum in its help page | Set by auction, not published |
| Vidio | Contact ad sales | Not published | Not published |
| Global streamers (Netflix, Prime Video and others) | Usually through agencies or programmatic buying | Not published | Not published |

Google Ads lists computers, mobile, tablets and TV screens as device categories, and says the TV screens option, which covers smart TVs and gaming consoles, is available only for Display and Video campaigns. Video campaigns can be bought on a cost per view or cost per thousand impressions basis, and you set either a daily or a total budget. Google's own Google TV network is limited to the US, so it is not an option here.

Vidio published a claim in April 2026, carried on the AVIA site, that Nielsen's survey of 11 urban areas ranked it first among OTT platforms by cumulative audience reach, with more than 10.9 million unique viewers in 2025. This is Vidio's own press release and the Nielsen report was not available to open, so read it as a sales claim.

## Do You Need Special Approval for Ads in Indonesia?

This guide does not give legal advice on content rules. We could not verify from an official source which Indonesian bodies regulate streaming ads, or what the rules on alcohol, health and religious content say today. Check each platform's ad policy and ask a local adviser before you run claims in those areas.

On tax, ASEAN Briefing describes the statutory VAT rate as 12% applied to an 11/12 base for non-luxury goods and services, which gives an effective 11%. Ask your platform or agency whether your ad invoice includes VAT.

## What Does a First Test Cost? A Worked Example

Rates are not published, so every input below is an **assumption** for illustration only, not a quote.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget for four weeks | Rp 3,000,000 |
| CPM | Rp 40,000 |
| Impressions bought | 75,000 |
| Average views per person | 4 |
| People reached | about 18,750 |
| Share who contact the business after seeing the ad | 0.2% |
| Contacts | about 38 |
| Cost per contact | about Rp 79,000 |

If the real CPM turns out to be Rp 25,000, the same budget buys 120,000 impressions. If it is Rp 60,000, it buys 50,000. The test exists to find your actual CPM and your actual contact rate. Give people one way to respond, such as a WhatsApp number or a short URL on screen, and ask every new customer how they heard about you.

Compare the result with a search campaign. Google Ads captures people already looking for a coffee shop or a gym, which usually costs less per contact than TV-style reach. See our [CTV cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data) for how to read CPM numbers.

## Which Local Businesses Should Try CTV First?

CTV fits businesses where a new regular customer is worth much more than the cost of reaching them. Restaurants with delivery, coffee shops with a loyalty offer, gyms with monthly memberships and salons with repeat bookings are the clearest cases. A shop that sells a single low-price item is a harder fit.

Start with one city. Indonesia is spread over a very large number of islands, so a campaign aimed at one city, with one offer and one way to respond, is easier to read than a national one. ## FAQ

### How much does CTV advertising cost in Indonesia?

No platform checked publishes a CPM for Indonesia. Earlier versions of this page quoted an average of Rp 42,000, but it had no source, so it was removed. Run a small test and read the CPM from your campaign report.

### Can I run CTV ads on YouTube in Indonesia?

Yes, through a Google Ads video campaign. Google's device targeting includes TV screens for Display and Video campaigns. Check the device report afterwards to see how much of your spend actually ran on TV screens.

### Is there a minimum budget?

Google's Video campaign page states no minimum. Vidio does not publish one, so ask its sales team. Treat any minimum quoted for other countries as irrelevant.

### Do I need Indonesian-language creative?

Use the language your customers speak. No source checked makes it a legal requirement for these platforms, but a message in the viewer's own language and a local address is easier to act on.

### Is CTV better than social media ads?

They do different work. TV-style ads build awareness on a large screen, and social or search ads handle direct response. A small budget usually does better starting with search and your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).
