Responsive search ads (RSAs) work best when you supply 15 distinct headlines and 4 descriptions, pin as little as possible, and judge results by conversions rather than the ad strength label.

Google's own data, from 15 to 20 August 2025, links moving ad strength from Poor to Excellent with about 15% more clicks and conversions on average. This guide gives 10 tips, a sample set of headlines, and a rule for when to ignore the score.

## What are the RSA limits and how does ad strength work?

Google's [RSA requirements page](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7684791) sets these limits:

- Up to 15 headlines of 30 characters each.
- Up to 4 descriptions of 90 characters each.
- A minimum of 3 headlines and 2 descriptions to create an ad.

Google assembles combinations and tests them over time. Ad strength is a rating of Incomplete, Poor, Average, Good or Excellent. Per [Google's ad strength page](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9921843), it does not directly affect whether your ad can serve. It is a guide for finding improvements, not a quality score or a ranking signal.

## Tips 1 to 4: How do you write better headlines and descriptions?

1. **Fill all 15 headlines and 4 descriptions.** More unique assets means more combinations to test.
2. **Make every headline different.** Google advises against repeating words, phrases or ideas; each asset should add a new selling point or call to action.
3. **Put your keyword in some headlines.** Google recommends including at least one keyword in your headlines.
4. **Write the first three headlines to read well together.** Google says to do this because they often appear side by side.

Sample set for a hair salon (an assumption for illustration; the character counts are exact):

| Type | Headline | Characters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keyword | Hair Salon in Austin | 20 |
| Offer | Book Your Cut Online | 20 |
| Proof | Rated 4.8 by 300+ Clients | 25 |
| Call to action | See Open Slots Today | 20 |
| Price | Cuts From $45 | 13 |

Only use claims such as ratings, prices and review counts that are true on the landing page.

## Tips 5 to 7: How should you handle pinning and sitelinks?

5. **Pin sparingly.** Google says pinning reduces the combinations available and can affect ad strength. If every position is pinned, unpinned assets will not show.
6. **If you pin, pin two or three unique options per position.** Pinning identical or near-identical text to one slot blocks testing, per Google.
7. **Add at least 6 sitelinks.** Google says ads need 6 or more sitelinks across ad group, campaign or account level to reach Good or Excellent.

When must you pin? Google suggests pinning text that has to appear in every ad, such as a legal disclaimer, to Headline 1, Headline 2 or Description 1. Assets in Headline 3 or Description 2 positions are not guaranteed to show.

## Tips 8 to 10: How many ads per group, and when to ignore the score?

8. **Run at least 2 RSAs per ad group.** Google's internal data associates adding a second RSA to an ad group with one RSA with a 6.6% increase in conversions at a similar cost per conversion, and a third RSA with a further 3.7%. Give each ad a unique final URL where it makes sense.
9. **Group keywords tightly.** Separate ad groups by service (colour, bridal, men's cuts) so headlines match the search.
10. **Judge by conversions, not the label.** Because ad strength does not decide eligibility, an Average ad that brings bookings at a good cost beats an Excellent ad that does not. Do not rewrite a converting ad only to chase the score.

## What does a monthly RSA review look like?

A simple routine for a small account:

1. Check each ad group has 2 or more RSAs and the rating is at least Good.
2. Replace headlines that repeat an idea with one new angle.
3. Review the search terms report and add negatives.
4. Compare cost per conversion before and after changes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need all 15 headlines?**
A: Google recommends providing up to 15 so it can build more combinations. The minimum to create an ad is 3.

**Q: Does a low ad strength stop my ad showing?**
A: No. Google says ad strength does not directly influence serving eligibility.

**Q: Is pinning bad?**
A: Not always. Use it for must-show text, but each pinned position reduces combinations.

**Q: How many RSAs per ad group?**
A: Google recommends at least two, each with Good or Excellent ad strength.

**Q: Can I use dynamic text like location?**
A: Google recommends opting into text customization to help ad strength; review generated variants for accuracy.
