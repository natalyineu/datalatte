## What Goes in a One-Page Pet Groomer Marketing Plan?

Copy these six headings into a document and answer each in one or two lines.

1. **Goal.** One number and a date, for example "40 new clients booked by 31 March".
2. **Target client.** Dog or cat owners within a set distance or service area, and the coat types or sizes you want more of.
3. **Offer.** What you sell first: a first-groom price, a package, or a mobile route slot.
4. **Channels.** Pick three from the list below.
5. **Budget.** A monthly amount in dollars.
6. **Tracking.** The five numbers you will check monthly.

The SBA's market research steps suggest gathering demographics about your customers and assessing competitors by service and market segment. For a groomer, that means checking which groomers appear in the map results for your area, and what they charge.

## Which Three Channels Should You Pick?

Choose by cost and control. This table lists what published sources say about each.

| Channel | Published fact | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | Local results are based on relevance, distance and prominence; no one can pay for a better ranking | Free |
| Reviews | More reviews and positive ratings can help local ranking | Free |
| Website with call button | Core Web Vitals target: main content in 2.5 s or less | Your time or a developer |
| Google search ads | Animals and Pets: $4.06 per click, $31.50 per lead (LocaliQ 2026) | Budget you set |
| Social posts | 70% of US adults use Facebook, 51% Instagram (Pew 2026) | Free, plus time |

A common order is profile and reviews first, then the website, then ads. Our [Google Business Profile guide for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-for-pet-groomers) and [Google Ads budget guide](/blog/how-much-should-a-pet-groomer-spend-on-google-ads) cover the first and fourth rows.

## How Much Should You Budget?

The SBA says many businesses use a percentage of revenue as a guide, and that the percentage varies widely, so it suggests checking trade associations for benchmarks. Its figures: an average of 7.9% of revenue on marketing in 2018 across businesses, with B2C service companies at 11.8% (Web Strategies), and an average of 1.08% on advertising alone (Small Business Trends).

These are third-party numbers repeated by the SBA, and none is specific to groomers. No source we checked gives a pet grooming budget share. The SBA adds that startups and younger businesses typically need larger budgets than established ones.

Treat the percentage as a starting point and adjust after three months of tracked results.

## Worked Example: A Monthly Plan

All inputs are **assumptions** for a salon with $12,000 monthly revenue.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly revenue | $12,000 |
| Marketing share | 7.9% (SBA's cited average) |
| Monthly budget | about $950 |
| Google search ads | $400 |
| Review request cards and QR code | $30 |
| Photos, design and printing | $120 |
| Reserve to test one new idea | $400 |

At LocaliQ's $31.50 cost per lead for Animals and Pets, $400 in ads buys about 12 to 13 leads, if your account matches the category average, which is not guaranteed. If half book, that is about 6 new clients. At an assumed $70 first groom, first-visit revenue is $420, so rebooking is what makes the plan pay.

## How Do You Track Results?

The SBA says tracking can be as simple as asking new customers where they learned about you. For digital channels it points to the analytics built into email, social and ad tools.

Use five numbers: profile calls, direction requests, website clicks, booked grooms by source, and ad cost per booked groom. Our [pet groomer analytics guide](/blog/pet-groomer-analytics-tracking) shows how to set each up. Review the plan every quarter; the SBA recommends keeping marketing plans current at least annually.

## FAQ

### How much should a pet groomer spend on marketing?
No source gives a groomer-specific figure. The SBA cites a 7.9% average of revenue across businesses (2018) and says the share varies widely.

### What is the cheapest marketing for a groomer?
A complete Google Business Profile and a steady stream of honest reviews cost no ad spend.

### Do I need a written marketing plan?
A one-page plan is enough. Write a goal, three channels, a budget and the numbers to check.

### How often should I update the plan?
Quarterly for the numbers. The SBA recommends keeping marketing plans current at least annually.
