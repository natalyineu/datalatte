An accounting practice should email its own clients and warm leads a few times a year, with deadline reminders, plain-language updates and one clear next step, and it should check consent rules before using tax return information for marketing. Mailchimp's benchmarks (last updated December 2023) put the Business + Finance average at a 31.35% open rate, a 2.78% click rate and a 0.15% unsubscribe rate. Mailchimp does not list accounting separately.

This guide is for solo accountants, bookkeepers and small firms in the US. It covers rules first, because tax preparers face stricter limits than most businesses. The worked example uses assumed numbers.

## What Rules Apply to Accountants' Marketing Emails?

Two sets of rules matter. CAN-SPAM covers any commercial email. The FTC guide says headers must be accurate, subject lines cannot be deceptive, the message must be identified as an ad, and it must include a valid physical postal address. Recipients need a clear way to opt out, and you must honor a request within 10 business days. Each violating email can carry penalties of up to $53,088.

The second set applies to tax return preparers. Under the IRS regulation 26 CFR 301.7216-3, a preparer may not disclose or use a taxpayer's tax return information without prior written consent, unless an exception applies. The consent must be knowing and voluntary, signed and dated by the taxpayer, and describe the particular use. To solicit unrelated products, it must identify each type of product or service.

We opened only the consent section, not the exceptions in 301.7216-2. Ask your own tax adviser which exceptions cover emails to existing clients before you build a list from tax files.

## Who Should You Email?

Start with people who have asked to hear from you: current clients, people who requested a quote, and newsletter sign-ups collected with a clear form. Keep a record of how and when each person joined. Do not buy a list.

Separate clients by what they need. A sole trader needs different reminders from an S corporation owner. Two or three segments are enough. A form on your website that says what you will send and how often keeps expectations clear, and it raises the chance people open the email.

## What Should You Send Through the Year?

Send fewer emails with more purpose. A practical calendar:

- A short deadline reminder before each filing or payment date that applies to the segment. Use the IRS or your state's published dates, not memory.
- A "what to bring" checklist ahead of the busy season.
- One plain-language update when a rule changes that affects your clients.
- A year-end note on records to gather.
- A quiet check-in with an offer to book a planning call.

Each email should have one link or reply request. Write the subject line as what it is, such as "Documents we need before your meeting." ## How Do You Stay Out of the Spam Folder?

Google's sender guidelines apply to anyone sending 5,000 or more messages a day to Gmail, but its authentication advice helps smaller senders too. It says to set up SPF and DKIM for your domain plus DMARC, to support one-click unsubscribe with a clearly visible link, and to keep the spam rate in Postmaster Tools below 0.30%, with 0.10% as a stricter target.

A small practice rarely sends 5,000 a day, so the thresholds may not bind you, but the habits are cheap. Send from your own domain, set up the records your email tool asks for, and remove people who unsubscribe or never open. ## FAQ

### Can I email my clients' tax information to market other services?

Not without checking. 26 CFR 301.7216-3 requires written, signed and dated consent for a preparer to use tax return information, with a description of the use. Exceptions exist, so ask a tax professional.

### Do clients have to be able to unsubscribe?

Yes. CAN-SPAM requires a clear opt-out and honoring requests within 10 business days. Every marketing email also needs your physical postal address.

### How often should an accountant email clients?

No published source sets a standard frequency for accountants. A calendar tied to real deadlines, with a small number of other emails, avoids fatigue.

### What open rate is good?

Mailchimp's Business + Finance average is 31.35% from December 2023 data. Use it as a rough guide and watch clicks and replies as well.

### Is Google Ads better than email for new clients?

They serve different people. Ads reach strangers, and email keeps existing contacts. See our [Google Ads keyword guide for accountants](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-accountants-in-2026) for the paid side.
