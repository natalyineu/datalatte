Here is a number to hold onto. Ahrefs' cheapest paid plan with the full toolset, Lite, is listed at $129 a month. A single-location tool like BrightLocal's Track plan is listed at $41 a month. Run both for a year and you have spent about $2,000 before you have changed a single page on your site. For one coffee shop, that only makes sense if the data leads to a handful of extra customers. For a company publishing content in a competitive city, it can.

This guide is for owners of local businesses in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. It covers what Ahrefs offers a local business (including two things many older guides say it does not do), what it still does not do, what the alternatives cost, and a plain-numbers way to decide. Prices are US dollars from vendor pricing pages, checked in October 2026. They change often, so confirm before you buy.

## What Ahrefs Does for a Local Business

Ahrefs is a general SEO platform built around organic search and backlinks. The parts that matter locally:

- **Keywords Explorer.** Type your service ("dog groomer") and look at Matching and Related terms for city, neighbourhood and "near me" variants. Local volumes for small towns are often missing or rounded, so treat them as relative, and cross-check against Search Console. Our local keyword research guide walks through the process.
- **Site Explorer.** Enter a nearby competitor's domain to see the pages and keywords that bring them organic traffic, and who links to them.
- **Site Audit.** Crawls your own website for technical and on-page issues such as missing titles, broken links and slow pages.
- **Rank Tracker.** Tracks your pages' positions for chosen keywords in a chosen place.

### Two Things Older Guides Get Wrong

**1. Rank Tracker is not national-only.** Ahrefs' [Rank Tracker page](https://ahrefs.com/rank-tracker) states "Location tracking. From country, to city, to ZIP code," lists 190+ locations and includes "Local pack" among the SERP features it covers. That is not the same as a map grid (see the limits below), but it does mean you can watch whether a local pack appears for your keyword in your city.

**2. Ahrefs does have a Google Business Profile tool.** Ahrefs' help centre describes [GBP Monitor](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/13249082-about-google-business-profile-gbp-monitor) as a free tool for subscribers on the Lite plan or higher. It logs changes to your profiles (made by you, your team or Google), sends email alerts, lets you revert unwanted edits, and after you connect your Google account shows views and interactions such as calls, bookings, directions and website clicks. Setup means giving Ahrefs Google permission to make changes on your behalf, so think about who else has access before you connect it. Ahrefs' [GBP Monitor product page](https://ahrefs.com/gbp-monitor) says it is "available on all paid plans while in beta", which conflicts slightly with the help article, so confirm the terms in your account.

## What Ahrefs Still Does Not Do Well

- **Map grids.** The Ahrefs pages opened for this article list "Local pack" as a SERP feature but do not describe a geographic grid of Maps rankings. BrightLocal, by contrast, lists a geo-grid map on its Track plan (see below). If "where do I show on the map from each corner of my service area" is your question, use a local tool.
- **Citation management.** The Ahrefs pages opened here do not list a citation audit or builder. BrightLocal and Whitespark both sell citation products.
- **Review requests and responses.** Ahrefs' help centre mentions managing reviews inside GBP Monitor, but the review-request workflows (email, SMS, in-store) that BrightLocal lists on its higher plan are not described on the Ahrefs pages checked.
- **Accurate small-town volumes.** Every keyword tool is rough here.

### The Free Option

[Ahrefs Free](https://ahrefs.com/webmaster-tools) (formerly Ahrefs Webmaster Tools) gives you web analytics, Site Audit and Site Explorer for websites you verify. Per Ahrefs, limits are 5K crawl credits a month per project, up to 1,000 backlinks and 1,000 keywords visible at once, and unlimited verified websites. That covers a lot of what a single-location business needs: technical fixes on your own site, your own keywords and who links to you. Ahrefs says extended access to Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer and its other tools requires a paid plan, so expect deeper competitor research to sit behind a subscription.

## A Sensible Stack by Situation

**You have one location and a small budget.** Free tools first: Ahrefs Free, Google Search Console and your Google Business Profile. Our [Search Console guide](/blog/google-search-console-guide) and [GBP optimisation checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) cover setup. Spend nothing until you have fixed the basics.

**You compete in a crowded city and publish content.** Buy one month of Ahrefs Lite as a sprint: keyword research, a competitor review, a backlink prospect list. Export everything, act on it, cancel.

**Your customers mostly come from Maps.** Skip Ahrefs for now. A local tool with a geo-grid and citation monitoring (BrightLocal, Whitespark) tells you more. Our [guide to ranking in the local pack](/blog/how-to-rank-in-google-maps-local-pack) shows what to fix first.

**You run several locations.** GBP Monitor's change log and alerts are worth considering, since several people editing profiles is where accidents happen.

## The Math

How many extra customers do the tools need to produce to pay for themselves? Every figure below besides the vendor prices is an **assumption**: a new customer is worth $200 in gross profit.

| | Free stack | Ahrefs Lite, one-month sprint + BrightLocal Track, 12 months | Ahrefs Lite, 12 months + BrightLocal Track, 12 months |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ahrefs cost | $0 | $129 | $1,548 |
| BrightLocal Track (12 x $41) | $0 | $492 | $492 |
| Year-one cost | $0 | $621 | $2,040 |
| Gross profit per new customer (assumption) | $200 | $200 | $200 |
| Extra customers needed to break even | 0 | 3.1 | 10.2 |

Monthly billing prices only; annual billing is cheaper. The sums use the listed monthly prices multiplied by 12.

How to read it: a tool earns its cost only if you act on what it shows. If a $621 year buys you three more customers from a fixed page or a better listing, it paid. If the reports sit unread, even the cheap stack lost money. For a very low-value customer such as a $4 coffee, the break-even is hundreds of customers, which is why free tools are the right start for a café.

## Do This Today

1. Verify your website in Ahrefs Free and Google Search Console, and run Site Audit. Fix the top five issues it flags.
2. Open your Google Business Profile insights and note last month's calls, directions and website clicks as your baseline.
3. Write ten ways a customer might search for what you sell in your town. Check them in Search Console, then decide whether paid keyword research would add anything.
4. Work out your gross profit per new customer, and use the table above to set a tool budget you could justify.
5. If you test a paid tool, put a cancellation reminder in your calendar and a written list of the three actions you will take from it.

## FAQ

**Q: Does Ahrefs track local rankings?**
A: Partly. Ahrefs says Rank Tracker covers locations from country down to city and ZIP code, and includes the local pack as a SERP feature. It does not describe a Maps rank grid, which BrightLocal lists on its Track plan.

**Q: Does Ahrefs show Google Business Profile data?**
A: It can, through GBP Monitor. Once connected, Ahrefs says you can see views and interactions such as calls, directions and website clicks, plus a log of profile changes. Your own Business Profile dashboard shows the same type of data without a subscription.

**Q: Is Ahrefs worth it for one small shop?**
A: Usually not as a permanent subscription. Use the free tools first, then consider a one-month sprint if you publish content or face strong competition.

**Q: What is cheaper, BrightLocal or Whitespark?**
A: It depends on the product. At the entry level, Whitespark's rank tracker starts at $14 a month and BrightLocal Track at $41, but they do different things. Compare what you need, not the headline price.

**Q: Ahrefs or Semrush for local SEO?**
A: Both are general platforms with similar limits. See our [Semrush vs Ahrefs for local SEO comparison](/blog/semrush-vs-ahrefs-local-seo-which-to-use).
