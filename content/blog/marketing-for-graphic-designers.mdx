A graphic designer builds a client base by making the portfolio easy to find and easy to judge: project pages with descriptive names and alt text, a few detailed case-style pages, reviews or testimonials that follow FTC rules, and direct outreach to the clients you want.

The market is not growing fast. The US Bureau of Labor Statistics projects graphic designer employment to decline 2% from 2025 to 2035, and 20% of designers are self-employed, so standing out matters.

This guide is for freelancers and small studios. Paid-ad numbers are from the closest category benchmark, Business Services.

## What Is the Market Like for Graphic Designers?

BLS data shows a mature field. Median pay was $62,960 a year ($30.27 an hour) in May 2025, and there were 253,100 jobs in 2025. The projected change of minus 2% from 2025 to 2035 is about 4,200 jobs fewer. One in five designers is self-employed.

BLS figures describe all designers, not freelance rates. They show competition for work, not what you should charge. Choose a specialty, such as packaging for food brands, and say so on your homepage.

## How Do You Get Your Portfolio Found?

Google's image guidance applies directly to portfolios. It calls alt text the most important image attribute, advises informative alt text that fits the page and warns against keyword stuffing. It recommends short, meaningful file names such as my-new-black-kitten.jpg over IMG00023.JPG, and says Google draws image subject matter from page content, so place images near related text.

For each project page, state the client type, the brief, your role and the result, using only facts you can show. Submit an image sitemap if you host images on a CDN. Our [website speed guide](/blog/website-speed-optimization-small-business) explains why heavy image files hurt.

## Should a Designer Run Google Ads?

Sometimes, for a clear niche. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 report has no design category. Business Services, the closest, averages $5.87 per click, a 4.85% conversion rate and $93.69 per lead. Treat it as a rough ceiling check.

A designer who works from home can still use a Business Profile as a service-area business. Google says to remove your address if customers do not visit you. Ranking depends on relevance, distance and prominence, and cannot be bought.

| Business Profile | No ranking fee | Local clients |
| Search ads | $5.87 per click (Business Services) | A narrow, high-value service |
| Direct outreach | Your time | Specific target clients |

## How Do You Use Testimonials Safely?

Use real ones with permission. The FTC's rule on fake reviews took effect on 21 October 2024. It bars fake or false testimonials, buying reviews, and insider reviews without clear disclosure. Do not write testimonials for clients and do not offer discounts that require a positive review.

BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses and 89% expect owners to respond. Reply to every one. See our guides to [managing online reviews](/blog/managing-online-reviews-small-business) and [Google reviews](/blog/google-business-profile-reviews).

## FAQ

### How do graphic designers get clients?
Through a findable portfolio, a clear specialty, reviews or testimonials and direct outreach. No source here ranks channels by results.

### Is graphic design a growing field?
BLS projects employment to fall 2% from 2025 to 2035.

### How do I optimize portfolio images for Google?
Use descriptive file names, informative alt text and place images near related text.

### Do freelance designers need a Business Profile?
Only if you want local visibility. Home-based designers can hide their address.

### Can I write my own testimonials?
No. The FTC's rule bars fake or false testimonials.
