A small business in Mexico should combine a complete Google Business Profile for people searching nearby, Meta ads aimed at a defined area, and WhatsApp for answering customers. Facebook reaches 93.5 million people through advertising in Mexico, and 86.1% of the population aged 6 and over used the internet in 2025, so the audience is large. Ad prices for Mexican small businesses are not published by Meta, Google or INEGI, so this guide gives no CPC figures.

Data comes from INEGI (Mexico's statistics institute), DataReportal, Meta and Google. Examples are marked as assumptions.

## How Many Mexicans Are Online?

INEGI's 2025 household technology survey (ENDUTIH, released June 16, 2026) found 104.9 million internet users aged 6 and over, which is 86.1% of that population. Use was 88.9% in urban areas and 75.2% in rural areas. 78.3% of households had internet, against 39.1% in 2015, and 84.6% of people used a mobile phone.

Mexico City led household access at 90.5%, Nuevo León followed at 89.9% and Baja California at 89.1%. Chiapas was lowest at 53.9%. A business in a small southern town reaches fewer online customers than one in Monterrey, so check your own area first.

## Which Platforms Reach Mexicans?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report for Mexico uses advertising audience reach, not monthly active users. Treat the numbers as estimates.

| Platform | Ad reach in Mexico | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 93.5 million | 70.7% |
| YouTube | 85.0 million | 64.3% |
| Instagram | 53.6 million | 40.5% |
| TikTok (18+) | 99.0 million | 105.7% of adults 18+ |

DataReportal warns that the TikTok figure above 100% may be anomalous. The WhatsApp figure is not in the report, so no user count for WhatsApp is given here. Facebook is the widest paid reach for a general local audience, and Instagram skews to a narrower group.

## How Large Is the Small Business Market?

INEGI's 2024 Economic Census timely results (published January 2025) count 7,056,499 establishments and 36,793,604 employed people. By activity, 44.7% of establishments are commerce and 42.1% are services. INEGI classes micro units as 0 to 10 people and small units as 11 to 50.

The census covers fixed establishments, and it does not count street vendors. For a restaurant, salon or shop, this means competition is local and dense. Winning a nearby search matters more than a national reach figure. Our [Latin America overview](/blog/small-business-marketing-latin-america-2026) compares neighboring markets.

## How Should You Use WhatsApp for Customers?

Many Mexican customers expect to message a business. Meta's pricing page explains the costs for the WhatsApp Business Platform. Since July 1, 2025, it charges per delivered message, with rates by message category and recipient country code. Messages from customers to you are not charged.

Meta also says marketing, utility, authentication and service messages are free during a free entry point window, which opens when a customer messages you from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad on the Android or iOS app and may stay open up to 7 days. From October 1, 2026, service messages beyond the free monthly tier and utility messages inside the service window are charged. Meta's page does not state a Mexico rate in the text; use its rate card. Setup basics are in our [WhatsApp Business guide](/blog/whatsapp-business-marketing-local-business).

## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Mexico?

Google says local results rely on relevance, distance and prominence, and that you cannot pay for a better local ranking. Complete your Business Profile, verify it, add photos, and reply to reviews. Use Spanish names for services and neighborhoods ("colonia") that customers actually type. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists the fields.

If you advertise on Google Search, keep the radius tight and add negative keywords. Prices vary by city and keyword, so use Keyword Planner in your own account.

## When Does a WhatsApp Ad Pay for Itself?

You cannot know your cost per chat before you test. You can set the most you can afford to pay. All numbers below are **assumptions**, in Mexican pesos.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Average sale | MXN 450 |
| Gross margin | 50% |
| Profit per customer | MXN 225 |
| Chats that become customers | 20% |
| Break-even cost per chat | MXN 45 (225 x 20%) |

If Meta shows a cost per message conversation above MXN 45 after a fair test, this offer loses money on first sales. Improve the reply speed, the offer or the close rate before raising the budget. If the cost is well below MXN 45, the ad has room to scale. Repeat customers improve the result, but count them only once you track them.

## FAQ

### How many people use the internet in Mexico?

INEGI reports 104.9 million users aged 6 and over in 2025, or 86.1% of that population.

### Which social platform has the most ad reach in Mexico?

DataReportal lists Facebook at 93.5 million, ahead of YouTube at 85.0 million and Instagram at 53.6 million.

### Is WhatsApp free for business?

Customer messages to you are free. Business-sent messages on the WhatsApp Business Platform are charged per delivered message, with free periods such as the free entry point window. Check Meta's rate card for Mexico.

### How much do Meta ads cost in Mexico?

No official small-business benchmark is published. Run a small test and read the cost per result in Ads Manager.

### Do I need ads in Spanish?

Yes, for most local customers. Use the Spanish terms and place names your customers use.
