## What Nextdoor Ads Actually Are

Nextdoor is a verified neighborhood social network — to join, you have to confirm your address, so the audience is genuinely local. Businesses can advertise through several formats:

**Local Deal:** A sponsored offer that appears in neighborhood feeds. Best for promotions with a specific offer (10% off, free first class, etc.)

**Neighborhood Sponsorship:** Your business appears as a sponsor in your chosen neighborhoods — similar to a display banner with your branding, hours, and contact details.

**Nextdoor Ads (self-serve):** The standard paid placement that appears in feeds. Targets by neighborhood, zip code, or custom radius. This is the primary format for most local businesses.

The key difference from Google Ads: Nextdoor ads are awareness-driven, not intent-driven. Someone on Nextdoor isn't searching for "hair salon near me" — they're scrolling neighborhood news. Your ad needs to create the desire, not capture existing demand.

## Step-by-Step: Setting Up Your First Nextdoor Ad

### Step 1: Create a Business Page

Before running ads, you need a free Nextdoor Business Page at **business.nextdoor.com**.

Required for setup:
- Business name and address (must be a real, verifiable address)
- Business category
- Phone number, hours, website URL
- At least one photo

Tip: Upload 6–10 photos — exterior, interior, products/services, and your team. Nextdoor business pages with photos get 3–5x more profile visits than text-only listings.

### Step 2: Access Nextdoor Ads Manager

Go to **ads.nextdoor.com** and sign in with your Business Page credentials. The self-serve ad interface is straightforward — similar in structure to Facebook Ads Manager but simpler.

### Step 3: Choose Your Campaign Objective

Nextdoor offers three objectives:

- **Awareness:** Maximize reach in your target neighborhoods
- **Engagement:** Drive clicks to your website or Business Page
- **Leads:** Collect phone number or email via a lead form in the ad

For most local service businesses starting out, **Engagement** is the right choice — you want people to click through to your booking page, not just see your name.

### Step 4: Set Your Targeting

This is Nextdoor's competitive advantage. Options:

**Geographic targeting:**
- By neighborhood (Nextdoor's neighborhood boundaries)
- By zip code
- By radius from your address (1–25 miles)

**Recommended for most local businesses:** Target by neighborhood, selecting the 5–10 neighborhoods within 1–2 miles of your location. This is more precise than zip code targeting and aligns with how Nextdoor users actually think about "nearby."

**Audience filters (optional):**
- Homeowners vs. renters (important for home services)
- Household type (families, couples, singles)
- Age range

For a children's hair salon, filtering for households with children makes sense. For a fitness studio or café, broad demographic targeting within your radius is usually better — you don't want to over-filter a small audience.

### Step 5: Set Your Budget

**Minimum daily budget:** $1/day (effectively ~$30/month minimum)

**Practical minimum to see results:** $150–200/month

Nextdoor's CPM runs $2–6 for most local categories — significantly lower than Facebook ($8–15 CPM) or Google Display ($3–10 CPM). But the audience is smaller, so raw impression volume is lower even at a fair CPM.

Recommended starting budgets by business type:

| Business Type | Suggested Monthly Budget | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Café / coffee shop | $150–250 | High frequency needed for walk-in habit |
| Hair salon / beauty | $200–350 | Longer consideration cycle |
| Pet groomer | $150–250 | Lower search volume, Nextdoor fills gap |
| Fitness studio | $250–400 | Competing for committed-buyer intent |
| Home services (cleaning, handyman) | $300–500 | High LTV justifies more spend |
| Restaurant | $200–400 | Directional + awareness combination |

### Step 6: Create Your Ad

**Ad formats available:**
- Single image (most common)
- Carousel (multiple images)
- Video (15–60 seconds)

**Image specs:** 1200×628px or 1080×1080px (square). Use high-quality photography — blurry or stock-looking images underperform significantly on Nextdoor's feed.

**Copy structure that works:**

```
Headline (up to 50 characters): "[Business Name] — [One-line value prop]"
Body (up to 150 characters): Specific offer or reason to visit, plus nearest landmark or intersection
CTA: Book Now / Learn More / Call Now
```

Example for a hair salon:
- Headline: "Glow Hair Studio — New Clients Welcome"
- Body: "First colour service 20% off. 2 blocks from Central Park. Online booking open."
- CTA: Book Now

### Step 7: Set Campaign Duration and Review

Nextdoor campaigns run as continuous or scheduled. For testing, run for a minimum of 30 days before evaluating — the platform's neighborhood-based targeting takes time to build frequency with the right audience.

## What Good Nextdoor Ad Creative Looks Like

Nextdoor's audience is in a different mindset than Google searchers or Instagram scrollers. They're in "neighborhood mode" — thinking about community, local recommendations, and what's near them.

**Creative that performs well:**
- **Real photos of your location:** exterior shot, interior, the team. Not stock photography.
- **Local references:** mentioning a nearby landmark ("near Central Station") makes the ad feel relevant rather than generic
- **Specific offers:** "Free consultation this week" beats "Come see us"
- **Social proof:** "Recommended by 200+ neighbors" if you have Nextdoor recommendations

**Creative that underperforms:**
- Generic stock photography (looks like every other brand)
- Vague copy ("best service in town")
- No clear call to action
- Complex design with small text (Nextdoor feed is mobile-first)

## Organic Nextdoor Strategy: What to Do Before Paying

Running Nextdoor ads works significantly better if you have organic presence on the platform first. Here's why: Nextdoor's algorithm surfaces business pages that have activity, and users who see your ad can click through to your profile — which either validates you or does nothing, depending on what's there.

**Before launching paid ads:**

1. **Claim and optimize your Business Page** — complete every field, upload 8+ photos
2. **Get Nextdoor recommendations** — ask 5–10 of your best local customers to leave a Nextdoor recommendation (similar to Google reviews but neighborhood-specific)
3. **Post to your neighborhoods 3–4 times** — these are organic posts in neighborhood feeds, not ads. Introduce your business, share a special, answer a community question
4. **Respond to neighborhood mentions** — if anyone mentions needing a service you offer, respond helpfully

Businesses that run paid ads alongside active organic profiles typically see 20–30% lower cost per click than those with empty profiles behind the ads.

## Tracking Results from Nextdoor Ads

Nextdoor's attribution is less precise than Google Ads. You won't see a clean "10 bookings came from this ad." Here's how to track meaningfully:

**Method 1: Unique promo code**
Include a Nextdoor-specific discount code in your ad. Track how many times the code is used. Works well for retail and restaurants.

**Method 2: UTM-tracked landing page**
Link your ad to a page URL with `?utm_source=nextdoor&utm_medium=paid`. Your website analytics will show traffic and conversions from this source.

**Method 3: "How did you hear about us?"**
Simple intake question. Underestimates Nextdoor impact (people often don't remember exactly where they saw something) but gives directional signal.

**Method 4: Foot traffic lift**
Run Nextdoor ads for one month, pause for one month, repeat. Compare busy/slow periods. Crude but effective for businesses without online booking.

## Nextdoor vs. Facebook vs. Google: Where Does It Fit?

| Attribute | Nextdoor | Facebook/Meta | Google Ads |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience intent | Awareness | Awareness + interest | High intent (searching now) |
| Geographic precision | Neighborhood-level | Radius/zip | Radius/zip |
| Cost (CPM) | $2–6 | $8–15 | $3–10 (display) |
| Best for | Trust-building with neighbors | Visual, retargeting | Capturing search intent |
| Typical start budget | $150/month | $300/month | $500/month |
| ROI timeline | 2–4 months | 4–8 weeks | 2–4 weeks |

**The honest answer on priority:** For most local businesses, Google Ads comes first (captures people actively looking for you), then Meta Ads (retargeting + lookalike audiences), then Nextdoor (neighborhood awareness layer). Nextdoor alone is rarely enough — but it can meaningfully amplify other channels by building neighborhood name recognition.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much should I spend on Nextdoor ads to see results?

A minimum of $150–200/month to generate enough impressions to measure anything meaningful. Below that, you may run your entire budget in 2 weeks and not have enough data to make decisions. For home service businesses with higher average job values, $300–500/month is a more useful starting point. The key is running the campaign for at least 60 days before evaluating performance.

### Can I target only homeowners on Nextdoor?

Yes — Nextdoor's audience filters include homeowner vs. renter status. This is particularly useful for home services (cleaning, painting, lawn care, HVAC) where homeowners are the relevant decision makers. For most other business types (salons, cafés, fitness studios) this filter unnecessarily restricts your audience.

### Is Nextdoor better than Facebook Ads for local businesses?

Neither is universally better — they serve different purposes. Facebook Ads has stronger creative formats, retargeting capabilities, and higher volume. Nextdoor has stronger neighborhood trust and often lower CPM. For businesses where neighborhood credibility matters (home services, local food, community-oriented businesses), Nextdoor's trust premium is real. For businesses targeting a specific demographic or running complex retargeting sequences, Facebook is more powerful. Many local businesses run both with separate budgets.

### How do I get more Nextdoor recommendations?

Ask your best local customers directly — explain that Nextdoor recommendations help your business show up in the neighborhood feed. Send an email or text with a direct link to your Nextdoor Business Page and a simple request. Don't incentivize recommendations (against Nextdoor's terms), but asking directly is completely fine. Aim for 10+ recommendations before launching paid ads — it significantly improves your profile's credibility.

### Can I run Nextdoor ads without a website?

Yes. Your Nextdoor ad can link to your Business Page, Google Business Profile, Facebook page, or a phone number (for call ads). Many local businesses with no website run successful Nextdoor campaigns. If you want to track conversions precisely or collect email leads, a simple landing page helps — but it's not required to get started.

### How long before Nextdoor ads show results?

Nextdoor advertising builds awareness, not immediate search intent. Expect to run campaigns for 6–8 weeks before drawing conclusions. Foot traffic and booking lift tends to appear in weeks 3–5 as neighborhood frequency builds. Don't judge the channel on a 2-week test — the audience needs repeated exposure before they act.
