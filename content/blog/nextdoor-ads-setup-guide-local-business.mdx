To set up Nextdoor ads, claim your free Nextdoor Business Page, open the Ads tab, choose a goal (website visits, a sale or discount, or messages), pick your target area by radius or ZIP code, set a daily budget, then upload a photo-led ad with one clear local offer. Nextdoor says its current ad flow replaced per-neighborhood pricing with a daily budget and lets you target a radius of up to 30 miles around your business ([Nextdoor Ads update](https://business.nextdoor.com/local/localdealstransition), checked Oct 2026). Results take a few weeks to judge, so plan a test before changing anything.

Nextdoor reaches an audience Google and Meta struggle to target precisely: people in your immediate area who are already reading local news and recommendations. It is not the highest-volume platform, but for neighborhood-anchored businesses such as cafes, salons, pet groomers and yoga studios it reaches people who could walk to your door today. Menu names, minimums and formats change, so check the Ads tab for the current options.

## What Nextdoor Ads Actually Are

Nextdoor is a neighborhood social network. Nextdoor's current ad setup offers three goals ([Nextdoor Ads update](https://business.nextdoor.com/local/localdealstransition), checked Oct 2026):

- **Increase website visits:** image-based ads that send people to your site.
- **Promote a sale or discount:** the updated Local Deal format, built around a specific offer such as 10% off or a free first class.
- **Get more messages:** text-based ads that encourage people to contact you directly.

The key difference from Google Ads: Nextdoor ads are awareness-driven, not intent-driven. Someone on Nextdoor isn't searching for "hair salon near me". They're scrolling neighborhood news. Your ad needs to create the desire, not capture existing demand.

## Step-by-Step: Setting Up Your First Nextdoor Ad

### Step 1: Claim Your Business Page

Before running ads, claim your free Business Page at **business.nextdoor.com**. Nextdoor describes it as a free way to build an online presence, collect recommendations and post unlimited free updates ([Nextdoor Business Page](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/getting-started/business-page), checked Oct 2026). Nextdoor pulls initial business information from third-party public data, so search for your business by name and location and correct any wrong details.

Complete every field you can: category, phone, hours, website and photos of the exterior, interior, products or services, and your team. Pages with real photos look more credible than text-only listings.

### Step 2: Open the Ads Tab

Sign in to your Business account and open the Ads tab, where you can create new ads, manage existing ones and see a performance dashboard ([Nextdoor Ads update](https://business.nextdoor.com/local/localdealstransition), checked Oct 2026).

### Step 3: Choose Your Goal

Pick one of the three goals above. For most local service businesses starting out, **Increase website visits** or **Get more messages** fits best, because you want people to book or contact you, not only see your name. Use **Promote a sale or discount** when you have a time-limited offer.

### Step 4: Set Your Targeting

This is Nextdoor's competitive advantage. Nextdoor says you can set the target area with a slider up to 30 miles around your business, or choose ZIP codes within your radius ([Nextdoor Ads update](https://business.nextdoor.com/local/localdealstransition), [ZIP code targeting](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/small-business/resources/blog/zip-code-targeting-on-nextdoor), checked Oct 2026). Nextdoor says older neighborhood-based targeting was converted to ZIP code targeting.

For most local businesses, start with a tight area around your location, such as the ZIP codes or radius from which customers realistically walk or drive a few minutes. Nextdoor's agency product also advertises targeting by demographics and interests ([Nextdoor for public agencies](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/public-agency/ads), checked Oct 2026), but the small-business filters may differ, so confirm what your Ads tab offers.

For a children's hair salon, filtering for households with children makes sense if the option exists. For a fitness studio or cafe, broad targeting within your area is usually better, since over-filtering a small audience limits delivery.

### Step 5: Set Your Budget

Nextdoor now uses a daily budget and duration, and offers three recommended starter plans, so you can choose a plan or customize ([Nextdoor Ads update](https://business.nextdoor.com/local/localdealstransition), checked Oct 2026). We could not confirm a published minimum or CPM, so check the figures in your own Ads tab. A very low daily budget will run, but it may not produce enough impressions to judge results, so pick a test budget you can sustain for several weeks. Compare cost per customer, not just cost per impression.

Suggested starting approach by business type (illustrative, not fixed prices):

| Business Type | Starting approach | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Cafe / coffee shop | Small, steady budget | Frequency builds the walk-in habit |
| Hair salon / beauty | Moderate budget | Longer consideration cycle |
| Pet groomer | Small to moderate | Lower search volume, Nextdoor fills the gap |
| Fitness studio | Moderate budget | Competing for committed-buyer intent |
| Home services | Higher budget | High customer value justifies more spend |
| Restaurant | Moderate budget | Awareness plus directional intent |

### Step 6: Create Your Ad

Nextdoor's creative specs list newsfeed, For Sale and right-hand-rail placements. Images can be square (1080 x 1080 pixels) or rectangular (1080 x 540 pixels), in JPG or PNG up to 30 MB, and headlines can be up to 120 characters. Nextdoor advises that lifestyle photography performs better than images with words ([Nextdoor creative specs](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/small-business/resources/ads-creative-specs), checked Oct 2026). Specs vary by placement, so check the current page before you design.

**Copy structure that works:**

```
Headline: "[Business Name]: [One-line value prop]"
Body: Specific offer or reason to visit, plus nearest landmark or intersection
CTA: Book Now / Learn More / Call Now
```

Example for a hair salon:
- Headline: "Glow Hair Studio: New Clients Welcome"
- Body: "First colour service 20% off. 2 blocks from Central Park. Online booking open."
- CTA: Book Now

### Step 7: Set Campaign Duration and Review

Run a test for at least a month before evaluating. Neighborhood targeting takes time to build frequency with the right audience, and a two-week test is usually too short. You can pause or cancel an ad with a toggle and edit the target area, copy, photo, call to action and daily budget from the dashboard.

## What Good Nextdoor Ad Creative Looks Like

Nextdoor's audience is in a different mindset than Google searchers or Instagram scrollers. They're in "neighborhood mode": thinking about community, local recommendations and what's near them.

**Creative that performs well:**
- **Real photos of your location:** exterior shot, interior, the team. Not stock photography.
- **Local references:** mentioning a nearby landmark ("near Central Station") makes the ad feel relevant rather than generic.
- **Specific offers:** "Free consultation this week" beats "Come see us".
- **Social proof:** "Recommended by neighbors" if you have real Nextdoor recommendations (use your actual count).

**Creative that underperforms:**
- Generic stock photography
- Vague copy ("best service in town")
- No clear call to action
- Complex design with small text, since the feed is mobile-first

## Organic Nextdoor Strategy: What to Do Before Paying

Running Nextdoor ads works better if you have an organic presence first, because people who see your ad can click through to your profile, which either validates you or does nothing, depending on what is there.

**Before launching paid ads:**

1. **Claim and complete your Business Page.** Fill every field and upload real photos.
2. **Collect Nextdoor recommendations.** Ask your best local customers to leave one. Nextdoor says businesses with just one recommendation see 3x more page views and 4x more messages ([Nextdoor recommendations guide](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/blog/small-business-guide-to-getting-nextdoor-recommendations), checked Oct 2026). In a Nextdoor survey, 72% of members said a business recommendation had influenced them ([Nextdoor report](https://about.nextdoor.com/press-releases/nextdoor-releases-new-insights-report-revealing-the-power-of-neighbor-recommendations), checked Oct 2026).
3. **Post to your neighborhoods a few times.** These are organic posts, not ads. Introduce your business, share a special, answer a community question.
4. **Respond to neighborhood mentions.** If anyone mentions needing a service you offer, respond helpfully.

## Tracking Results from Nextdoor Ads

Nextdoor's attribution is less precise than Google Ads, so you may not see a clean "10 bookings came from this ad." Here is how to track meaningfully:

**Method 1: Unique promo code.** Include a Nextdoor-specific discount code and count redemptions. Works well for retail and restaurants.

**Method 2: UTM-tracked landing page.** Link to a URL with `?utm_source=nextdoor&utm_medium=paid`. Your website analytics will show traffic and conversions from this source.

**Method 3: "How did you hear about us?"** A simple intake question. It underestimates Nextdoor's impact but gives a directional signal.

**Method 4: Foot traffic lift.** Run ads for one month, pause for one month, repeat, then compare busy and slow periods. Crude but useful for businesses without online booking.

## Nextdoor vs. Facebook vs. Google: Where Does It Fit?

| Attribute | Nextdoor | Facebook/Meta | Google Ads |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience intent | Awareness | Awareness + interest | High intent (searching now) |
| Geographic precision | Radius or ZIP | Radius/zip | Radius/zip |
| Best for | Trust-building with neighbors | Visual content, retargeting | Capturing search intent |
| Typical timeline | Slower, builds with frequency | Weeks | Often the fastest to show results |

For most local businesses, Google Ads comes first (it captures people actively looking for you), then Meta Ads (retargeting and lookalike audiences), then Nextdoor as a neighborhood awareness layer. Nextdoor alone is rarely enough, but it can amplify other channels by building neighborhood name recognition.

## FAQ

**Q: How much should I spend on Nextdoor ads to see results?**
A: Enough to gather meaningful data over several weeks. Nextdoor offers recommended starter plans and custom daily budgets, so check the options in your Ads tab and pick a test budget you can sustain for at least a month. See our breakdown in [Nextdoor ads cost 2026](/blog/nextdoor-ads-cost-2026).

**Q: Can I target only homeowners on Nextdoor?**
A: Nextdoor has offered audience filters such as homeowner versus renter, which suit home services such as cleaning, painting and lawn care. For salons, cafes and fitness studios, extra filters often shrink your audience too much. Confirm the current options in your Ads tab.

**Q: Is Nextdoor better than Facebook Ads for local businesses?**
A: Neither is universally better. Facebook offers stronger creative formats and retargeting, while Nextdoor offers neighborhood trust. Many local businesses run both with separate budgets. See [Nextdoor vs Facebook local ads](/blog/nextdoor-vs-facebook-local-ads).

**Q: How do I get more Nextdoor recommendations?**
A: Ask your best local customers directly with a text or email that links to your Business Page. Check Nextdoor's current rules before offering any incentive.

**Q: Can I run Nextdoor ads without a website?**
A: Yes, for some goals. The "Get more messages" goal is built around text-based ads that encourage direct contact. A simple landing page still helps with tracking and lead capture.

**Q: How long before Nextdoor ads show results?**
A: Nextdoor builds awareness rather than search intent, so plan for several weeks before judging the channel.
