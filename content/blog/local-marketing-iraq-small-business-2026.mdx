An Iraqi small business can reach a young, mostly mobile audience through YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 39.6 million internet users, 83.8% of the population, with ad reach of 24.7 million on YouTube, 21.5 million on Instagram and 20.8 million on Facebook. The same report shows large gender gaps on several platforms, which matters for who you target.

## How Many Iraqis Are Online?

Iraq has 47.3 million people, 72.2% in urban areas, with a median age of 20.8 (UN data in DataReportal, October 2025). Cellular connections total 50.8 million, 108% of the population, and 86.2% of them are 3G, 4G or 5G. Internet users grew by 1.8 million, or 4.7%, in the year to October 2025, and 7.66 million people remain offline.

DataReportal notes that one person can hold several connections and that internet figures can lag reality. Median mobile download speed is 56.34 Mbps and fixed is 41.52 Mbps (Ookla, 12 months to August 2025). Compress your images and keep landing pages light.

## Which Platforms Reach Iraqi Customers?

DataReportal gives late 2025 ad reach figures:

- YouTube: 24.7 million (52.3% of the population)
- Instagram: 21.5 million (45.4%)
- Facebook: 20.8 million (44.0%)
- Snapchat: 19.3 million (40.7%)
- Messenger: 14.9 million (31.5%)
- X: 2.74 million, LinkedIn: 2.70 million, Threads: 1.60 million

DataReportal lists TikTok at 40.1 million, above the internet-user count, and says such figures are published unaltered because of source methods. We do not use the TikTok number as a reach estimate. Ad reach is also not the same as monthly active users.

## Who Is Reachable? The Gender Gap

Gender splits are uneven. Facebook's ad audience is 27.8% female and 72.2% male. Instagram is 40.1% female and 59.6% male. Snapchat is closest to balance at 45.0% female and 53.4% male, and YouTube is 46.8% female and 53.2% male.

For a business selling to women, such as a salon or a clothing shop, this changes the plan. Snapchat and YouTube show a more balanced audience than Facebook in these figures. Test each channel with a small budget and compare cost per message, not only reach. Platform gender data is based on what users declare or what systems infer.

## What Is the Economy Like?

The World Bank data page puts Iraq's 2025 GDP at US$254.37 billion, GDP per capita at US$5,409.7, and growth at -2.2%. Inflation was 0.3% and unemployment 15.5% (ILO modelled estimate). The country overview says oil is about 53% of real GDP, 88% of government revenue and 91% of merchandise exports in 2025 estimates.

The overview also reports that non-oil GDP growth slowed to 1.5% in the first nine months of 2025, citing water and electricity shortages and a liquidity squeeze. Customers may delay spending, so lead with value and clear prices.

## Can You Pay for Google Ads in Advance in Iraq?

No. Google Ads Help lists Iraq, under Middle East, among the countries and territories where prepay is not available. It says postpay, or monthly invoicing if you are eligible, are the only supported payment settings. That means you pay after ads run, so cap your daily budget.

No verified click or impression prices for Iraq were found, so none are given. We also did not verify Google Ads availability by city or language.

## How Do Customers Pay, and What Rules Apply?

The Central Bank of Iraq said in September 2025, as reported by Channel 8, that it aims to end cash payments across government and other institutions by July 2026. This is a secondary report, and we did not verify whether the deadline was met. For a shop, it is a reason to ask your bank about accepting cards or wallets.

**Unverified:** rules for advertising and influencers. The Communications and Media Commission (CMC) publishes regulations for digital platforms, but its page blocked our access, so we cannot describe them. Check cmc.iq or ask a local lawyer. Regional context, including the Kurdistan Region, may differ, and we have no verified data on it.

A: No. Google lists Iraq among countries where prepay is not available. Postpay is the supported setting.
