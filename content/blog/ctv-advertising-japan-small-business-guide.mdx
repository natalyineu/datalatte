A small business in Japan can buy connected TV ads mainly through TVer, the free streaming service run by the major commercial broadcasters, or through YouTube on TV screens via Google Ads. TVer offers a self-serve plan with no stated minimum and a managed plan that was reported at a ¥500,000 minimum in 2023. Treat that minimum as old and confirm it with TVer or an agency before planning a budget.

## How Big Is Japan's Online and TV Ad Market?

Dentsu's figures, reported by Jiji through Adnkronos, put 2025 advertising spend in Japan at ¥8,062.3 billion, up 5.1%. Internet advertising reached ¥4,045.9 billion, up 10.8%, and passed 50% of the total for the first time, at 50.2%. TV, newspaper, magazine and radio together fell 1.6% to ¥2,298 billion.

The article does not give a TV-only figure. The takeaway for a local shop is that budgets keep moving to online video, which is where CTV sits.

## What Platforms Can a Local Business Use?

TVer is the main free ad-supported service. A secondary source, Boundless, reports 44.7 million monthly unique browsers in January 2026 (browsers, not people), 630 million monthly video plays, and CTV at 38.6% of viewing. The same source reports ABEMA, also ad-supported, at 28.47 million weekly active users in September 2025.

Netflix and Prime Video are subscription services with about 10 million and 19.3 million subscribers in Japan according to the same source. It does not describe their ad options, so do not plan around them without checking directly.

## How Do You Buy TVer Ads?

TVer announced its own programmatic ad platform in December 2020, with audience targeting and frequency control on connected TV (Advertimes, trade press). A 2023 blog by KWM describes two plans. The managed plan reportedly required ¥500,000 minimum, with targeting by prefecture and municipality. The self-serve plan, launched in April 2023, reportedly had no minimum, auctioned CPM and targeting by prefecture only.

Those details are secondary and from 2023. Confirm today's minimums, targeting levels and review period with TVer or an agency. One source mentions a pre-delivery review of the advertiser and creative before ads run.

## Can You Run CTV Ads Through Google?

Yes, in part. Google Ads lists "TV screens" as a device option for Video campaigns, covering smart TVs, game consoles and devices like Chromecast. The option is only available for Display and Video campaigns. That lets a small business show YouTube ads on TV screens with the same location targeting it uses elsewhere.

Google's help page lists Japan among the 28 countries where the Google TV network is available. It extends YouTube video campaigns to in-feed and Masthead inventory on the TV screen.

## What Rules Apply to Japanese Ads?

The Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations, enforced by the Consumer Affairs Agency, prohibits false or misleading claims about the quality and price of goods or services. Superlatives and price claims need evidence you can show.

JARO, a public-interest advertising review association, handles complaints about ads and reported a record 12,589 complaints for 2025. Its site also covers the Pharmaceutical and Medical Device Act, which matters for health and beauty claims. Check your claims before filming.

Consumption tax is 10% standard and 8% reduced for food and drink excluding dining out, per the National Tax Agency. If your ad shows a price, make sure it is stated clearly and with tax as required.

## Which Local Businesses Fit CTV?

CTV works best when the audience is nearby and the purchase is repeat or high value. Salons, fitness studios, dental clinics and restaurants are typical, but there is no verified Japanese data on which category performs best. Test one offer in one area.

## Is a ¥500,000 Test Worth It? A Break-Even Check

All inputs below are **assumptions**. TVer does not publish CPMs in the sources opened for this article.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | ¥500,000 |
| CPM (assumption) | ¥3,000 |
| Impressions | 500,000 / 3,000 x 1,000 = about 166,700 |
| Margin per new customer (assumption) | ¥6,000 |
| New customers to break even | 500,000 / 6,000 = about 84 |

If you cannot see 84 new customers coming from 166,700 impressions, a smaller test through Google's TV screens option may be a safer start. ## FAQ

### How much does CTV advertising cost in Japan?

No official CPM was found. TVer's managed plan was reported at a ¥500,000 minimum in 2023; the self-serve plan reportedly had none. Confirm both with TVer.

### Is TVer good for local targeting?

The 2023 KWM description says managed plans could target by prefecture and municipality, and self-serve by prefecture. Check current options.

### Can I advertise on YouTube on TV screens in Japan?

Google Ads lists TV screens as a device option for Video campaigns, so yes, through a Video campaign.

### Do I need a lawyer for ad claims?

Not always, but keep proof for any "best" or price claim. The Consumer Affairs Agency enforces the Premiums and Representations Act.
