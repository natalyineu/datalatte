Turkey has 77.5 million internet users (88.3% of 87.7 million people), and Instagram reaches 62.3 million through Meta's ad tools, more than any other platform in DataReportal's Digital 2026 report.

For a Turkish small business, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok are the main places to find customers, and sponsored influencer posts must follow a published advertising guide. Annual inflation was 29.73% in September 2026, which affects any ad budget set in lira.

## How Many People in Turkey Are Online?

DataReportal gives Turkey's population as 87.7 million in October 2025, with 81.9 million mobile connections (93.3% of the population). Internet users numbered 77.5 million, leaving 10.3 million (11.7%) offline. Social media user identities were 62.3 million (70.9%), up 8.3% in a year.

Internet growth was only 0.2%, so reaching new customers means winning attention from people who are already online.

| Facebook | 34.7M | 39.5% |
| LinkedIn (members) | 21.0M | 23.9% |

Instagram grew 10.3% in a year and TikTok 14.8%, while Facebook grew 1.0%. DataReportal notes that these are ad-tool figures that platforms revise, so use them for comparison, not exact counts. No WhatsApp figure for Turkey in the sources we opened.

## Can You Run Google and Meta Ads From Turkey?

Google lists Turkiye as a billing country for Google Ads with all currencies and payment methods shown as available, and Turkish-language terms. Meta lists the Turkish lira (TRY) among supported ad account currencies. So lira billing is possible on Meta, and Google lists all currencies for the country. We could not find a public source for average click prices or CPM in Turkey, so we give none.

## How Does Inflation Change Your Ad Budget?

The Central Bank page, relaying TURKSTAT data, shows annual CPI inflation of 29.73% in September 2026, with monthly inflation of 1.84%. The World Bank forecast inflation of 18% by end-2026 and 15% by end-2027, in a forecast made before this reading. Prices, wages and rents are moving, so a lira budget set in January loses buying power by autumn.

Review the budget monthly and measure cost per message or booking in lira each month. Do not carry last quarter's results forward.

## What Rules Apply to Influencer and Sponsored Posts?

The Ministry of Trade published the Advertising Board's guide for social media influencers on 7 May 2021. It says covert advertising is banned and disclosure must be noticeable at first glance. Rewards such as money, free or discounted goods must be disclosed, and influencers may not promote a product they have not experienced. On Stories, tags such as "#Reklam" must be used. Advertisers, agencies and media each carry responsibility.

If you pay a local creator, brief them on these rules in writing. For ideas, see [influencer marketing for local restaurants and cafes](/blog/influencer-marketing-for-local-restaurants-and-cafes).

## How Do You Check a Test Budget?

These inputs are **assumptions** for an imaginary hair salon, not Turkish benchmarks.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly test budget | TRY 15,000 |
| Average ticket | TRY 1,200 |
| Gross margin | 50% |
| Enquiries that become bookings | 40% |
| Bookings needed to cover the budget | 25 |
| Enquiries needed | about 63 |
| Maximum cost per enquiry | about TRY 240 |

With monthly inflation near 1.84%, raise the ticket and the budget inputs as prices change. Compare your actual cost per enquiry with the maximum.

## FAQ

### How many people in Turkey use Instagram?

DataReportal reports an Instagram ad reach of 62.3 million in late 2025, or 70.9% of the population.

### Is Facebook still worth using in Turkey?

Ad reach is 34.7 million (39.5%) and grew 1.0%. It is smaller than Instagram or YouTube, but still large for older audiences.

### What does Google Ads cost in Turkey?

No public source we could open gives a Turkish average. Use Keyword Planner and a small test.

### Do I have to label influencer posts as ads?

Yes. The Ministry of Trade's guide bans covert advertising and requires a clear disclosure.

### Which currency should I use for ad billing?

Meta lists TRY as supported, and Google lists Turkiye as a billing country. Check your account for current options.
