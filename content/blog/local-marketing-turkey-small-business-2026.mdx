## What Do the Sources Say About Turkish Ad Reach?

The sources do not provide a verified figure for Turkish ad reach on any platform. A Turkish influencer agency describes the country as having "a large, young and highly social audience, with tens of millions of people on social platforms", but gives no count. [House of Lein](https://www.houseoflein.com/en/influencer-marketing-agency-turkey) is a commercial agency, so its description is treated as secondary.

The large numbers in the StatRow come from campaigns outside Turkey, such as a 2019 count of Instagram ads and an essay-service campaign on YouTube. They show the scale that influencer work can reach, not what a Turkish shop should expect. Before setting a budget, check audience estimates inside each platform's own ad tools, and record the figures with the date they were pulled.

Reach matters only if it turns into enquiries. Keep a simple log of where each enquiry came from, so the numbers reflect your customers rather than a platform's estimate.

## Which Platforms Should a Turkish Small Business Test First?

The same agency reports that most influencer collaborations in Turkey run on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, and that LinkedIn is increasingly used by B2B brands. For a local salon, café or repair shop, that points to starting with Instagram and TikTok for short visual content. YouTube can follow if you can produce longer demonstrations.

Hyperiser, another Turkish influencer agency, reported in a March 2025 post that brands integrating naturally into humour creators' Reels produced high engagement in its review of the previous year. That is a single agency's finding, so treat it as a hypothesis to test rather than proof that the same format works for your category.

Test one platform for at least four weeks before adding another. Splitting a small budget across three platforms at once makes it impossible to tell which one produced the enquiries. ## What Disclosure Rule Applies to Sponsored Posts?

According to House of Lein's summary, posts made in return for any benefit must carry the word "Reklam" (advertisement) or "Tanıtım" (promotion), together with the advertiser's name. The label must be visible at first glance. The same summary says gifted products and invitations also count as a benefit, so a free service for a creator falls under the rule too.

Brief every creator in writing before a post goes live. Include the exact label, where it must appear (on Stories as well as feed posts), and what the creator may say about the product. Ask for the draft before publishing and check that the label is visible without expanding the caption.

Do not rely on the creator to know the rules. The advertiser remains responsible for the label on its own campaign, so build disclosure into the contract and the approval step. Check the current legal text with a qualified adviser before relying on this summary.

## How Do You Budget Ads When Prices Move?

The sources reviewed do not include Turkish inflation data, so this article gives no inflation rate. Before setting a lira budget, check current price data from an official source. Ingredient, rent and wage costs can change within a single quarter, and a budget set in January can lose buying power by autumn.

A practical approach is to set a fixed monthly test budget in lira, calculate the maximum cost per enquiry you can afford from your margins, and review both every month. If your average ticket rises, recalculate the ceiling instead of carrying last quarter's numbers forward.

Use a spreadsheet with one row per month showing spend, enquiries, bookings and cost per enquiry. Compare each month against the ceiling rather than only against the previous month. If you prefer one system for spend records and invoices, an accounting tool such as [FreshBooks or QuickBooks](/blog/freshbooks-vs-quickbooks-small-business-local-business) can hold them.

## How Do You Check a Test Budget?

The inputs below are assumptions for an imaginary hair salon, not Turkish benchmarks. Replace each one with your own figures.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly test budget | TRY 15,000 |
| Average ticket | TRY 1,200 |
| Gross margin | 50% |
| Enquiries that become bookings | 40% |
| Bookings needed to cover the budget | 25 |
| Enquiries needed | about 63 |
| Maximum cost per enquiry | about TRY 240 |

The logic is simple. At a 50% margin on a TRY 1,200 ticket, each booking leaves TRY 600 of gross profit, so 25 bookings return the TRY 15,000 spend. If 40% of enquiries become bookings, the salon needs about 63 enquiries, which caps the cost per enquiry at about TRY 240.

If the actual cost per enquiry stays above that ceiling for two consecutive months, stop the channel or change the offer rather than raising the budget. If it stays below the ceiling, there is room to test a second platform or a larger creator. For scheduling posts across Instagram and TikTok, a planning tool such as [Hootsuite or Sprout Social](/blog/hootsuite-vs-sprout-social-small-business-local-business) can keep the calendar consistent.

## FAQ

### Which platforms do influencer campaigns use most in Turkey?

A Turkish influencer agency reports that most collaborations run on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, with LinkedIn increasingly used for B2B brands. This is secondary information from a single agency, not an official measure.

### Do sponsored posts in Turkey need a label?

Yes, according to a local agency's summary. Posts made for any benefit must carry "Reklam" or "Tanıtım" and the advertiser's name, visible at first glance, under a regulation in force since 1 August 2026. Confirm the current text with a legal adviser.

### Are there verified Turkish ad-reach figures in these sources?

No. The sources reviewed do not give verified Turkish ad-reach numbers. Check the audience estimates inside each platform's ad tools and record the date you checked them.

### How do I know if my lira budget is working?

Calculate the maximum cost per enquiry from your margin and average ticket, then compare your actual cost per enquiry each month. Use your own numbers, not benchmarks from another market.

### Should a small business pay a creator or use its own content?

Test your own content first, since it costs less to produce. Add a paid creator once you have a clear offer and a way to track enquiries, and brief the creator on the labelling rule in writing.
