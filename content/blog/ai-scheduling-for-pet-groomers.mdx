For a pet groomer, "AI scheduling" mostly means grooming software that takes bookings online, texts reminders and holds a card on file. The published prices are modest: MoeGo's mobile grooming plans start at $49 a month, Time To Pet at $25 and Gingr's grooming plan at $109. A 2016 meta-analysis in BMJ Open found digital reminders cut no-shows from 21% to 15% in clinics, which is the best-sourced evidence for the main benefit.

This guide covers what these tools actually do, the pricing pages as we read them, and a worked example for a solo groomer. Examples labelled assumption are not data.

## What Does AI Scheduling Mean for a Groomer?

The label covers four jobs: online booking that respects how long each breed takes, automatic reminders by text or email, rules that protect your day (deposits, card on file, waitlists) and routing for mobile vans. Some vendors market these as AI. The pricing pages we opened for MoeGo, Time To Pet and Gingr do not list AI features by name, so judge a tool by the functions, not the label.

A separate group of products are chatbots and voice agents that answer calls and book appointments. We have not verified any of those with published prices, so this guide leaves them out. For a broader look, see our piece on [marketing automation for pet groomers](/blog/marketing-automation-for-pet-groomers).

## Do Reminders Really Reduce No-Shows?

Evidence exists, but not from grooming. Robotham et al. (BMJ Open, 2016) pooled 21 studies of electronic text notifications in healthcare. Patients who got them were 25% less likely to miss appointments, with no-shows of 15% versus 21%. Multiple notifications beat a single one.

Clinics are not grooming salons, and nobody has published an independent no-show rate for pet groomers that we could verify. Vendor blogs suggest 5% to 15%, which is a vendor estimate. Measure your own rate for a month before you buy anything.

## What Is the Return? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for a solo groomer. The reminder effect copies the clinic result above, which may not transfer.

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Appointments per week | 30 |
| Average ticket | $80 |
| No-show rate before | 12% |
| No-show rate after (a drop of a quarter, like the clinic data) | 9% |
| No-shows avoided per week | 0.9 |
| Revenue recovered per week | about $72 |
| Per month (4.33 weeks) | about $312 |
| Software cost (MoeGo Basic) | $49 |

On these inputs, the tool earns about six times its fee, if the freed slot is refilled and not left empty. A missed appointment is only recovered when the slot gets used again, so a waitlist matters. If your no-show rate is already 3%, there is little to recover.

## How Should You Set It Up?

1. **Count your baseline** as above.
2. **Pick the tool by your model**: a van needs routing, a shop needs time slots and a front desk.
3. **Add a written policy**: card on file, a deposit, and a cancellation window, accepted at booking. A grooming-software vendor notes that no-show fees are allowed when clients agree in advance, but check your local rules.
4. **Send at least two reminders**, such as 48 and 24 hours before. The meta-analysis found multiple notifications worked better than one.
5. **Keep a waitlist** so a cancelled slot is filled.

## FAQ

### Is AI scheduling worth it for a solo groomer?

If you lose more than a few appointments a month, yes. A $49 plan pays for itself when it recovers one $80 appointment a month. Test it on a monthly plan first.

### Can scheduling software take deposits?

Gingr lists deposits and prepayments under Integrated Payments; MoeGo's Basic plan lists card-on-file, prepayment and pre-authorization under no-show protection. Confirm the exact setup with the vendor.

### Do reminders work for pet grooming?

The best-sourced evidence is from healthcare: reminders cut no-shows by about a quarter. No independent grooming study was found.

### What about AI that answers the phone?

We did not verify pricing or results for AI receptionists in grooming, so we do not recommend a specific one here.

### Is a deposit better than reminders?

A vendor claims 58% fewer no-shows with deposits, but gives no source, so we do not use it. Use both and track your own result.
