## Which Google Business Profile settings matter most for a coffee shop?

Start with the category. Per Diem recommends choosing "Coffee shop" or a closely related term rather than a broad option such as "Restaurant" ([Per Diem guide](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). BrewRound's 2020 walkthrough says you can select one primary category at setup and add more later ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary).

Verification comes next. BrewRound reports that Google mails a letter containing a code, and the profile does not appear in results until it is verified. Per Diem adds that without verification, updates will not show publicly and customers may not find the business. Check your verification status before spending time on anything else.

For a complete walkthrough of these fields, see [the coffee shop profile optimization guide](/blog/coffee-shop-google-business-profile-optimization).

## How should a coffee shop use Q&A, messaging, and posts?

Answer the common questions yourself. Per Diem recommends seeding the Q&A section with answers to whether you offer free Wi-Fi, dairy-free options, or dogs on the patio ([Per Diem guide](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). Your own answers control the wording customers see first.

Google also lets users message a business directly from its listing, so check that the messaging option is reachable and that someone answers it. Per Diem cites Think Coffee, an independent brand, which says it now has thousands of app downloads after placing its app download link in its Google profile (secondary).

Posts only work when they are live. Google's help page says a post shows on Search and Maps only when its status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts stay hidden ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en), primary). The Restaurant HQ recommends posting at least once per week, ideally one to two times per day ([The Restaurant HQ](https://www.therestauranthq.com/operations/coffee-shop-marketing/), secondary). For posting ideas, see [the Google Business Profile tips for coffee shops](/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-tips).

## How do reviews affect a San Antonio coffee shop's map visibility?

Google states that reviews show next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search ([Google review tips](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122), primary). Reviews therefore sit in the most visible part of your listing, and their volume and recency are worth tracking every week.

The frequency of requests matters more than bursts of activity. The worked example below uses assumption-labelled inputs to estimate a realistic weekly review count. Replace each assumption with your own till data before relying on the result.

**Worked example (all inputs are assumptions):**
- Customers per day: 80 (assumption)
- Trading days per week: 6 (assumption)
- Share of customers sent a review text: 10% (assumption)
- Share of texted customers who leave a review: 20% (assumption)

Calculation: 80 × 6 = 480 customers per week. 480 × 10% = 48 review requests. 48 × 20% = about 10 reviews per week.

Ten reviews a week, spread across the month, is a steadier signal than 40 reviews in one weekend. Reply to each review within a day and mention your service type and neighbourhood where it fits naturally.

## What citation and consistency steps support the profile?

Name, address, and phone number must match across every directory. Google's Google Ads help explains that location data used in Google Ads comes directly from Google Maps and Google Business Profile, and corrections made there flow into linked ad accounts ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en), primary). Fixing your profile therefore helps paid and organic listings at the same time.

Start with the listings you already have. Correct mismatched addresses and phone numbers on those before creating new listings, because duplicates spread the same error. Abbreviations such as "St." and "Street" are worth standardising to one form across every directory.

Embedding a map on your own contact page reinforces the same location signal. A step-by-step guide is available in [the Google Maps embed guide](/blog/google-maps-embed-local-business-website).

## What does a 90-day local SEO plan look like?

The plan below is a practical sequence, not a guaranteed timeline. Each step can be checked off in your profile dashboard.

1. Confirm the profile is verified and the category is "Coffee shop," with related terms added where relevant.
2. Complete the business hours, description, and service list, including terms such as "latte art" or "pour-over" if you serve them.
3. Seed the Q&A with the five questions customers ask most, including Wi-Fi, dairy-free options, and seating.
4. Publish one post per week and confirm each shows a Live status after review.
5. Send a review text after each visit window, and reply to every review within a day.
6. Correct citation mismatches on existing directories before adding new ones.
7. Check profile performance every 30 days and adjust the post and review cadence.

## How do you measure whether local SEO is working?

Track three things each month. First, check profile views and direction requests in your Business Profile dashboard. Second, track impressions and clicks from search in Google Search Console, page by page. Third, check your position for your top five search terms, using a private browser window to reduce personalised results.

Record the baseline before you start. Without a starting number, you cannot tell whether a change to categories, posts, or reviews made a difference. For broader context on how Maps listings compare, see [how coffee shops dominate Google Maps](/blog/coffee-shops-dominate-google-maps).

## FAQ

### Does a Google Business Profile cost anything?

Google describes its Business Profile as free, with the headline "Stand out on Google with a free Business Profile" ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/), primary). Setup needs a Google account, which BrewRound says can be registered for free (secondary).

### Can a coffee shop choose more than one category?

BrewRound's 2020 guide says you can select one category at setup and add more later (secondary). Check the category list in your dashboard, because options can change over time.

### Why isn't my Google post showing?

Google only displays posts publicly when their status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts do not show on Search or Maps ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en), primary).

### Do reviews appear next to my listing?

Yes. Google states that reviews show next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122), primary).
