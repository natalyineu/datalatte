Programmatic buying is the automated purchase of ad space, where each ad impression is put up for bid in a real-time auction. IAB Tech Lab, which maintains the OpenRTB standard, describes it as an open protocol for the automated trading of digital media and calls real-time bidding a "programmatic on-the-spot auction". For a local business, that means software, not a sales rep, decides which website, app or video your ad appears on and what you pay.

This guide explains the pieces, who sells access to a small budget and what to test first. Prices vary by platform, and most vendors do not publish them, so none are claimed here.

## What Happens When Someone Loads a Web Page?

The flow, in plain terms:

1. A person opens a page or app that has an ad slot.
2. The publisher's system offers that slot, with signals such as location and device, to an ad exchange.
3. Buyers' systems, called demand-side platforms (DSPs), decide in an instant whether to bid for that person, and how much.
4. The highest bid wins and its ad loads.

OpenRTB is the shared language that lets these systems talk. IAB Tech Lab says real-time bidding supports addressable advertising, meaning ads served to individuals based on demographic, psychographic or behavioral attributes. Its page does not give timing figures.

## Who Can Buy Programmatic With a Small Budget?

You usually do not buy from an exchange directly. You use a platform that has done it for you.

## How Does Targeting Work for a Local Business?

Three signals matter locally:

- **Location.** Set a radius around your business or a list of areas. Google says radius targets must be at least 1 km, that very small areas may serve intermittently, and that location targeting is best effort, not exact.
- **Context.** Show ads on pages about topics related to your service.
- **Your own customer list.** Google's Customer Match lets you upload email addresses to target or exclude your customers. Google's help says memberships expire after 540 days, and a list needs at least 100 members added or updated within that window to stay eligible. Uploads can take up to 48 hours.

Using a customer list requires permission to use that data under Google's policies. Do not upload addresses you collected without consent.

## What Does It Cost, and What Is a Fair Test?

No platform in the table publishes a typical CPM or CPC for small advertisers. StackAdapt lists pricing models but not rates, and Google Display prices depend on the auction. That means you can only learn cost by testing.

The worked example below uses a made-up price to show the arithmetic. Every input is an **assumption**.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget | $500 for 30 days |
| Cost per 1,000 impressions (CPM) | $6 |
| Impressions | about 83,000 |
| Click-through rate | 0.5% |
| Clicks | about 417 |
| Landing page conversion rate | 3% |
| Enquiries | about 12 |
| Cost per enquiry | about $40 |

Change the CPM and CTR to your own numbers after the first week. Our [programmatic cost benchmarks article](/blog/how-much-does-programmatic-advertising-cost-real-cpm-benchmarks-2026) discusses what the published ranges do and do not tell you.

## Should a Local Business Use Programmatic at All?

Often not first. Programmatic ads reach people who were not searching, so they build awareness more than they capture demand. If your Google Business Profile, Search ads and review count are weak, fix those first. Programmatic makes sense when you have a tested offer, working conversion tracking and a reason to reach a wider audience, such as a new location or a seasonal event.

Allow time for learning: Google says a new Display campaign typically needs 5 to 7 days and suggests 7 to 14 days to stabilize after changes before judging performance. ## FAQ

### What does programmatic mean in advertising?

It means ad buying done by software. Each impression is offered in an automated auction and the winning ad loads in a fraction of a second.

### Is programmatic only for big brands?

No. StackAdapt states it has no minimum advertising spend, and Google Display campaigns can run on a modest daily budget. Results at small budgets depend on tracking and creative, not on the platform alone.

### How is programmatic different from Facebook ads?

Meta ads run inside Meta's apps. Programmatic ads run across many websites, apps and video or TV services through an exchange.

### Can I use my customer list?

Yes, on platforms that offer it, such as Google Customer Match, if you have permission to use the data and meet the platform's size and consent rules.
