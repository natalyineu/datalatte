Google Ads offers six YouTube video formats: skippable in-stream, non-skippable in-stream, in-feed video, bumper, masthead and Shorts. For most local businesses the choice is between skippable in-stream (you pay when someone watches 30 seconds or interacts) and bumper (5 to 6 seconds, paid per 1,000 impressions). Masthead is sold by reservation and is not aimed at small budgets.

Everything about formats and billing below comes from Google Ads Help, opened on 2026-10-25. Google does not publish average prices per format there, so any price in the examples is a labelled assumption.

## What Are the Main YouTube Ad Formats?

Google's help page lists each format with its length, skip rule, billing model and placement. The table below condenses it.

| Format | Length | Skip rule | Billing | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Skippable in-stream | No maximum (under 3 minutes recommended) | Skip after 5 seconds | CPV: 30 seconds watched or an interaction. Impressions if you bid tCPM, tCPA or Maximize conversions | YouTube watch pages, Google video partners |
| Non-skippable in-stream | 15 to 60 seconds depending on campaign subtype | Cannot be skipped | Target CPM (impressions) | YouTube videos, Google video partners |
| In-feed video | No maximum | No skip control; the viewer chooses to click | When someone clicks to watch | YouTube Search, Watch Next, Home feed |
| Bumper | 6 seconds (5 seconds minimum) | Cannot be skipped | Target CPM (impressions) | YouTube videos, Google video partner sites and apps |
| Masthead | No maximum (over 10 seconds recommended) | Autoplays without sound | Reservation, CPM or cost per hour | Top of the YouTube Home feed |
| Shorts | Under 60 seconds recommended | Viewer swipes away at any time | CPV (10 seconds or end of ad) or tCPM | YouTube Shorts feed |

Google's page gives two length ranges for non-skippable ads, so check your campaign settings before you film.

## How Do Skippable Ads Work and Cost?

Skippable in-stream ads let viewers skip after 5 seconds. With view-based bidding (CPV), Google says you pay when a viewer watches 30 seconds of your video or interacts with it. If you bid on target CPM, target CPA or Maximize conversions, you are charged for impressions instead.

That billing rule shapes the creative. The first 5 seconds are free reach if the viewer skips, so put your business name, location and offer there. Google does not publish an average CPV on this page. Our [YouTube ad cost guide](/blog/how-much-do-youtube-ads-cost-real-cpv-cpm-data) covers how pricing works and how to read your own forecast.

## When Should a Local Business Use Bumper Ads?

Bumper ads are 6 seconds or shorter and cannot be skipped, billed per impression. They suit a single message: a name, a place and one reason to remember you. Use them after a longer video has already told the story, to remind people who watched it.

On their own, 6 seconds is short for a new customer who has never heard of you. A salon can say "Haircuts, 5 minutes from Main Street, book online". It cannot explain a package. ## What Is the Difference Between Non-Skippable and In-Feed Ads?

Non-skippable in-stream ads run 15 to 60 seconds, depending on the campaign subtype, and viewers cannot skip them. You buy impressions at a target CPM, which means you pay to be seen whether or not the viewer cares. That is expensive reach for a business with a small radius.

In-feed video ads, which older guides often call discovery ads, look like a search result or a suggested video. Nobody is forced to watch: Google says you are charged when someone clicks to watch. They fit a business whose audience searches YouTube for answers, such as "how to clean a cast iron pan" for a kitchenware shop or "stretching for back pain" for a physio.

## Which YouTube Ad Format Fits Which Goal?

This is a decision guide, not a Google rule. Match the format to what you want the viewer to do.

| Goal | Format to test first | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get a click to a booking page | Skippable in-stream | You can pay only for engaged views |
| Be remembered in a small area | Bumper after a longer video | Cheap repeat exposure, one message |
| Reach people searching for help | In-feed video | Charged only on click to watch |
| Reach people scrolling Shorts | Shorts | Under 60 seconds, viewers can swipe away at once |
| Mass reach | Masthead | Reservation only, not for small budgets |

If you have never run video ads, start with one skippable in-stream campaign and one video. Add bumpers later.

## What Does a Test Budget Buy? A Worked Example

All prices in this table are **assumptions** used to show the arithmetic. They are not Google figures, and your forecast in Google Ads will differ.

| Input | Skippable in-stream | Bumper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget (assumption) | $300 | $300 |
| Price (assumption) | $0.05 per view | $10 per 1,000 impressions |
| What you get | $300 / $0.05 = 6,000 views | $300 / $10 x 1,000 = 30,000 impressions |
| Viewer outcome | Watched 30 seconds or interacted | Saw 6 seconds, no choice |

Swap in the estimate Google shows when you build the campaign. A view and an impression are different things, so do not compare the two outputs as if they were equal. Judge each by what happens next: visits to your booking page, calls, and bookings.

## How Do You Pick Between Formats Without Wasting Budget?

Change one thing at a time. Run a skippable video for a month, then compare views, view rate and bookings. Add a bumper cut from the same video and watch whether branded searches or direct visits move.

Keep the video focused on one service and one area. ## FAQ

### Can viewers skip every YouTube ad?

No. Skippable in-stream ads can be skipped after 5 seconds. Non-skippable in-stream, bumper and masthead formats have no skip option, and Shorts ads can be swiped away immediately.

### How long is a bumper ad?

Google's table lists 6 seconds with a 5-second minimum, while its text says between 5 and 6 seconds. Plan for 6 seconds or shorter.

### When am I charged for a skippable ad?

On view-based bidding, when a viewer watches 30 seconds of the video or interacts with it. On target CPM, target CPA or Maximize conversions, you are charged for impressions.

### Are discovery ads still a YouTube format?

Google's current list uses the name in-feed video for ads shown in YouTube Search, Watch Next and the Home feed. Older articles and this page's earlier title used the name discovery.

### Which format is cheapest?

Google does not publish prices per format on its format page. Cost depends on your auction, audience and bid, so read the estimate in Google Ads before you launch.

### Do I need a long video?

No. Skippable ads have no maximum length, with under 3 minutes recommended by Google, but a clear 15 to 30 second video with the offer in the first 5 seconds is a sensible start for a local business.
