Google is the review platform to build first for almost every local business. In BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US consumers, 71% said they read Google reviews, and 45% had written a Google review in the past 12 months, ahead of Facebook at 34%, Yelp at 24% and Tripadvisor at 16%. 

Add the others only where your customers use them: Yelp for restaurants and services, Tripadvisor for travel and tourist spots.

This guide compares the four on usage, the rules for asking for reviews, and where each one fits for coffee shops, salons, groomers and fitness studios. Platform rules change, so each claim is dated and sourced at the end.

## How do the four review platforms compare?

The survey also found that 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses, 41% say they always do, and people use an average of six review sites. Google's reading share fell from 83% in 2025 to 71%, so shoppers are spreading across more sites.

| | Google | Facebook | Yelp | Tripadvisor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wrote a review, past 12 months | 45% | 34% | 24% | 16% |
| Asking customers for reviews | Allowed; share a link or QR code | Not directly addressed in the guide checked | Discouraged; may hurt your rating (secondary source) | Encouraged, with free tools (secondary source) |
| Incentives for reviews | Prohibited | Prohibited | Prohibited | Prohibited |
| Best fit | Every local business | Community and social proof | Restaurants, home and personal services | Hotels, attractions, tourist-area food |

## Why does Google come first?

Google reviews appear where people search and on Google Maps, and Google lets you ask. Its help page says you can share a review link or QR code, that reviews must reflect a genuine experience, and that offering discounts or free items for reviews is strictly prohibited. Customers need a Google account to post.

Reply to reviews too. Google advises short, specific, polite replies with no deals inside them, and says to flag reviews that break its content policies.

## What is different about Yelp?

Yelp takes a stricter line. According to BrightLocal's summary of Yelp's guidelines, which is a secondary source because Yelp's own help pages returned errors when opened, Yelp says proactively asking for reviews may hurt your rating. Yelp's software may move solicited reviews to a "not recommended" section that does not count toward the star rating. The same source says Yelp bans asking for reviews after a survey or contact form.

In practice, put a Yelp badge or link on your site, claim your profile, and do not run a Yelp review campaign. Whether paid Yelp ads are worth it is a separate question, covered in our [Yelp ads guide](/blog/yelp-ads-worth-it-local-business-2026).

## What does Facebook add?

Facebook replaced star ratings with yes-or-no recommendations in 2018, according to BrightLocal and other coverage, which are secondary sources. A recommendation can include text and photos and appears on your Page. Meta's help article for it returned an error when opened, so check your Page settings for the current behavior.

Facebook's value for a local business is social proof next to your posts and ads. The BrightLocal figure of 34% writing Facebook reviews shows people still do it, so keep your Page active and reply.

## When does Tripadvisor matter?

Only if your customers are travelers. A restaurant near a hotel district, a tour, a cafe in a tourist area or an attraction should claim its Tripadvisor listing. Tripadvisor's restaurant page says replying to reviews is free once you claim the listing, according to a search summary of the page, which returned 403 when opened. Its review request tool, Review Express, was free at its 2013 launch, but current terms could not be confirmed.

A salon, groomer or gym in a residential area will see little return. At 16%, Tripadvisor has the lowest reviewing share of the four.

## Which platform should each business prioritize?

This is a recommendation based on the survey and rules above, not a measured result.

- **Coffee shops:** Google first, then Yelp as a claimed profile. Add Tripadvisor only in a tourist area. See [how coffee shops win Google Maps](/blog/coffee-shops-dominate-google-maps).
- **Hair salons:** Google first, Facebook second for social proof, Yelp claimed.
- **Pet groomers:** Google first, Facebook second.
- **Fitness studios:** Google first, Facebook second, Yelp claimed.

## FAQ

### Which review site matters most for a local business?

Google. In BrightLocal's 2026 survey, 71% of US consumers read Google reviews and 45% had written one in the past year, the highest of the four.

### Can I ask customers for Yelp reviews?

Yelp discourages it. According to a secondary summary of its guidelines, asking may hurt your rating because solicited reviews can be filtered out. Check Yelp's current support pages before you act.

### Can I offer a discount for a review?

No. Google's help page says offering free or discounted goods or services for reviews is strictly prohibited, and BrightLocal's summary reports the same rule on the other three platforms.

### Do I need all four platforms?

No. Build Google first, claim the others so the details are right, and invest only where your customers actually look.

### How often should I reply to reviews?

Google advises replying promptly. Make it a weekly habit at minimum, keep replies specific and short, and do not discuss private details.
