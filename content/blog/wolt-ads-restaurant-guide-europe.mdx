## What Is Wolt Ads and Where Do Restaurant Ads Appear?

Wolt launched Wolt Ads on April 10, 2024 after a beta, in two lines: Wolt Ads for Merchants and Wolt Ads for Brands. The merchant line is for local restaurants and shops. At launch Wolt reported more than 140,000 merchant partners, 86% of them small independent businesses.

Wolt's merchant help center describes Ads as sponsored listings that put your venue in top positions on the Restaurants, Stores, Categories, Search and Discovery surfaces. The launch release names dedicated positions in the Search, Stores and Restaurants tabs and on the Discovery page. Availability depends on your market.

## How Does Wolt Charge for Ads?

Wolt bills Ads by results. Its help center says you only pay for ads that result in a sale, and its results guide repeats that you pay only when your ads result in actual sales. Views and clicks are free.

Two things are not published in the pages reviewed: how a sale is attributed to an ad, and what rate Wolt charges per attributed order. Ask your account contact, or read the terms you accept when creating the campaign. Do not assume the rate matches your regular commission. ## How Do You Set Up a Wolt Ads Campaign?

The Merchant Portal flow, per Wolt's help center:

1. Open **Campaigns** in the left menu.
2. Click **New campaign** and choose **Ads**, then **Get started**.
3. Enter your **weekly budget** and set the campaign schedule.
4. Accept the terms and select **Create campaign**.

Wolt does not state a minimum weekly budget in these pages. Wolt also offers multi-venue campaigns, so one campaign can run across several locations, which matters for chains.

Ads are different from Promos. Promos are discounts, such as free delivery, an order value discount or an item promotion. Wolt says the two can be combined, so test them one at a time to see which moved orders.

## Which Results Does Wolt Report?

Under Campaigns in the portal you can see a summary of Ads and Promos for a date range. The Ads metrics Wolt lists are:

| Metric | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue per euro spent | Return on ad spend (ROAS) |
| Total sales | Revenue earned during the campaign |
| Total spent | Amount invested |
| Total orders from ads | Orders placed through the ads |
| Avg. cost per order | Estimated cost of each order |
| Avg. order value | Typical basket |

Wolt's guide does not mention impressions or clicks as metrics. It also does not show a new versus returning customer split, so you cannot tell from this report alone whether the orders came from new people.

## Do Wolt Ads Pay Back? A Worked Example

Wolt's help center cites an average return of 6 EUR for every 1 EUR spent on Ads, and says 70% of customers buy from the top 10 venues. Both are Wolt's own figures with no method given, so do not plan around them.

Use your own numbers. All inputs below are **assumptions**.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly Ads budget | 100 EUR |
| Reported ROAS | 4.0 |
| Sales from ads | 400 EUR |
| Average order value | 25 EUR |
| Orders from ads | 16 |
| Cost per order | 6.25 EUR |
| Margin after food and packaging, before ad cost | 35% |

Margin on 400 EUR of sales is 140 EUR. After 100 EUR of ad spend, 40 EUR remains. Break-even ROAS is 1 divided by the margin, about 2.9 at 35%. At ROAS 2.0 the same week loses money.

Run the test for four weeks and compare against a baseline week without Ads. Because the report has no new-customer split, also track how many of those customers order again. Our [delivery app marketing strategy](/blog/delivery-app-marketing-strategy-restaurants) covers repeat orders.

## How Can You Get More From Wolt Ads?

Ads raise your placement, but conversion still depends on your listing. Fix these before you raise the budget:

- Photos and descriptions on your top sellers, covered in our [Wolt visibility tips](/blog/how-to-rank-higher-on-wolt-visibility-tips).
- Price points that survive platform fees.
- Ratings and delivery times, which customers see next to the placement.

Wolt Ads also announced off-app options in October 2025 with partners Koddi, Magnite, Skai and StackAdapt, including social, search, streaming and out-of-home formats. Those are aimed at brands and agencies, and the release gives no pricing.

## FAQ

### How much do Wolt Ads cost?

Wolt does not publish a price in the pages reviewed. You set a weekly budget and pay only when an ad leads to a sale. The per-order rate is in the terms you accept in the Merchant Portal.

### Is there a minimum budget?

No minimum is stated in Wolt's help pages reviewed. The portal asks for a weekly budget.

### What is the difference between Ads and Promos?

Ads raise your placement in the app. Promos are customer discounts: free delivery, order value discounts or item promotions.

### Where do I start a campaign?

In the Merchant Portal, under Campaigns, then New campaign, then Ads.

### Can I tell whether Ads bring new customers?

The reported metrics cover sales, spend, orders, cost per order and order value. A new versus returning split is not listed.
