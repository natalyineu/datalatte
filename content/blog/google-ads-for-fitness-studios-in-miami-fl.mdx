## How much should a Miami fitness studio spend on Google Ads?

Budget depends on studio size. The same [Vertical Impression guide](https://www.verticalimpression.com/guides/how-to-market-health-wellness-business-2026) gives $600–$1,200 a month for a small independent studio with under 200 members, and $1,500–$2,500 for a mid-size gym with 200–600 members.

For a well-optimised campaign at $1,000–$2,000 a month, the guide projects 300–600 clicks, with 3–6% of those clicks converting to trial sign-ups. Treat that as a planning aid, not a promise for any one neighbourhood.

It also sets two targets to judge results against: a cost per trial lead under $30, and a cost per converting member under $150–$200. If a campaign misses them after a full month of data, review the keywords and the booking page before adding spend.

## Which keywords should a Miami studio bid on?

Start with searches that name the service and the place. The [Association of Fitness Studios](https://member.afsfitness.com/content/how-make-your-fitness-business-stand-out-google) notes that Google's Map Pack lists relevant local businesses for queries such as "personal training studios near me". Searchers who see the Map Pack are often choosing between nearby options, so the ad and the Google Business Profile work as a pair.

Useful keyword patterns combine the service with a local modifier:

- Service plus "near me", for example "yoga classes near me"
- Service plus city, for example "fitness studio Miami"
- Service plus neighbourhood, for example "personal trainer Wynwood", where the studio is within reach of that area

Use exact and phrase match on terms that already convert. Keep broad match for later, once there is conversion data to judge it. The [best Google ad keywords for fitness studios](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-fitness-studios-in-2026) guide covers keyword selection in more depth.

At the low end, the studio misses both targets. At the high end, it meets both: the cost per trial is under $30, and the cost per member is under $150. The gap comes from click volume and trial rate, not from the budget alone. Before raising spend, test the landing page and booking flow, since they decide which end of the range a studio reaches.

## How do you keep wasted clicks out of a Miami campaign?

Add negative keywords for searches that rarely turn into bookings:

- Job searches, such as "jobs", "hiring" or "fitness studio salary"
- DIY and course searches, such as "how to" or "certification"
- Places outside the studio's service area
- Commercial terms that do not fit a studio, such as "franchise" or "wholesale"

Radius targeting around the studio address keeps the audience near the location members can realistically attend, instead of spreading spend across the whole metro. The [fitness studio Google Ads guide](/blog/fitness-studio-google-ads-guide) walks through campaign setup step by step.

## What should you track once people click?

Set conversion tracking for actions that lead to a booking: calls from the ad, booking form completions, direction requests from the Google Business Profile, and clicks on "book now" or contact buttons. The Association of Fitness Studios describes the profile as a free Google tool that shows hours, phone numbers, addresses, reviews and photos directly in search results, so it deserves the same attention as the campaign. The [Google Business Profile optimisation guide](/blog/fitness-studio-google-business-profile-optimization) covers the setup.

Speed matters after the enquiry. [Mariana Tek](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/) advises following up within 24 hours with an automated welcome sequence, because interest fades fast after a pop-up ends. The [marketing automation guide for fitness studios](/blog/marketing-automation-for-local-fitness-studios) explains how to set that up.

Reviews are the slower lever. Mariana Tek states that Google review volume can start influencing visibility within a few weeks, and its table gives a 6 to 12 month payoff timeline for local SEO and Google reviews.

## FAQ

### How much should a fitness studio in Miami spend on Google Ads?

The benchmark guide cited above puts a small independent studio with under 200 members at $600–$1,200 a month, and a mid-size gym with 200–600 members at $1,500–$2,500. Start within the band that fits your size, then adjust after a full month of tracked data.

### How long until a Google Ads campaign shows results?

The sourced timelines cover reviews and local SEO rather than paid ads. Review volume can start influencing visibility within a few weeks, while local SEO and reviews typically take 6 to 12 months to pay off. Judge a paid campaign on at least one full month of tracked conversions.

### What cost per lead should a fitness studio aim for?

The benchmark target is a cost per trial lead under $30, and a cost per converting member under $150–$200.

### Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. The Association of Fitness Studios describes it as a free Google tool that displays business hours, phone numbers, addresses, reviews and photos in search results.
