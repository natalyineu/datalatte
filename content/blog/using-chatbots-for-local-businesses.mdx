A chatbot for a local business can run on a website widget or on WhatsApp, and the cost depends on the channel. Tidio's website chat lists a free plan with 50 billable conversations a month, then $24.17 (Starter) and from $49.17 (Growth) per month. WhatsApp charges per delivered message, with 1,000 free service messages per phone number a month until 1 October 2026. Google's own Business Profile chat ended in July 2024.

This guide compares the channels, shows what each charges, and gives a worked break-even example. Prices were read from vendor pages on 31 October 2026 and change often.

## Where can a chatbot live?

Three channels matter for a local business:

- **Your website.** A chat widget answers opening hours, prices and booking questions. Vendors such as Tidio sell this.
- **WhatsApp.** Customers message your number and a bot replies. Meta bills each delivered message.
- **Google Business Profile.** Not available. Google removed the Chat feature: customers could no longer start new chats after 15 July 2024, and it was removed from Business Profiles on 31 July 2024. Google suggested a phone number or Reserve with Google instead.

A bot can also sit on Instagram or Facebook Messenger, but those fees and rules were not checked for this guide.

## What does website chat software cost?

Tidio's pricing page, which shows monthly prices, lists:

| Plan | Price per month | Billable conversations | Lyro AI conversations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 50 | first 50 free, lifetime |
| Starter | $24.17 | 100 | 50 one-off |
| Growth | from $49.17 | from 250 | upgradable to 50 to 1,000 a month |
| Plus | from $300 plus usage | custom | custom |

The Lyro AI agent add-on starts at $32.50 a month for 50 conversations and is sold separately. Tidio offers monthly or annual billing, with 2 months free on annual billing. The other chat vendors were not compared here, so this is one example, not a ranking.

## How does WhatsApp charge?

Meta's developer pricing page says businesses are charged per delivered message, not per conversation, and that rates vary by message category and by the recipient's country calling code. Messages from users are free. Categories are marketing, utility and authentication (templates), plus service messages that can only be sent inside an open 24-hour customer service window.

Two dates matter. On 1 July 2025 pricing moved from conversation-based to per-message. The same page says that from 1 October 2026, service messages beyond the free tier of 1,000 per phone number per month are charged, and utility messages inside an open service window are charged too. Meta's marketing page for the platform describes service messages as free within the window, so it differs from the developer docs. Check your own rate card before you budget.

The rates themselves sit in Meta's rate card and calculator. Pick your country and category there.

## Will a chatbot replace your front desk?

No source checked gives a share of enquiries a bot resolves for local businesses, so none is claimed here. What can be said: a bot is best at repeatable questions with one correct answer, such as hours, address, price list and parking. It should hand over to a person for complaints, custom quotes and anything with a booking conflict.

## Worked example: when does a $49.17 plan pay for itself?

All figures here are **assumptions**, not benchmarks.

A dental clinic assumes 40 website enquiries a month after hours, 5% of which would book a $120 visit if answered immediately.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| After-hours enquiries per month | 40 |
| Share that book when answered fast | 5% |
| Extra bookings | 2 |
| Revenue per visit | $120 |
| Extra revenue | $240 |
| Chat plan (Tidio Growth, from) | $49.17 |
| Setup time, one-off | 3 hours of staff time at $20 = $60 |

At a 50% margin (also an assumption), 2 extra visits earn $120. Month one: $120 − $49.17 − $60 = $10.83. From month two, with no setup cost, the gain is $120 − $49.17 = $70.83. With only 1 extra booking ($60 margin), month two gives $10.83. The break-even is therefore about one extra booking a month at this price and margin.

## What should you set up first?

Start with the free plan on your website and write answers for your ten most common questions. Add a handover to email or phone, and review the transcripts weekly. Only pay once you hit the conversation limit. For WhatsApp, read the rate card for your country before you open a number. For reaching people who find you on Google, keep a complete [Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) with a phone number and booking link, since its chat feature is gone.

## FAQ

### Does Google Business Profile still have chat?

No. Customers could not start new chats after 15 July 2024, and the feature was removed on 31 July 2024.

### How much does a website chatbot cost?

Tidio lists a free plan with 50 billable conversations, Starter at $24.17 and Growth from $49.17 per month. Prices shown are monthly, and annual billing gives 2 months free.

### Is WhatsApp Business free?

Replying to customer messages has a free tier of 1,000 service messages per number per month until 1 October 2026 according to Meta's developer docs. Template messages are billed per delivered message.

### Do I need an AI chatbot or will rules do?

Tidio sells its Lyro AI agent as a separate add-on from $32.50 a month for 50 conversations, so AI answers are an extra cost on top of the plan. Whether you need them depends on how varied your questions are.

### Can a chatbot book appointments?

Some tools can connect to a calendar. Check the vendor's integration list for your booking system.
