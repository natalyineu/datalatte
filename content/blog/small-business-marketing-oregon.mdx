## How big is the Oregon market for local businesses?

Oregon had 4,273,586 residents on July 1, 2025, up 8,262 (0.19%) from 4,265,324 a year earlier, according to the Census Bureau's Vintage 2025 state file.

Natural change was negative at -3,764, as deaths outnumbered births. Net migration was +11,864, made up of +9,636 from abroad and +2,228 from other states. So Oregon's growth depends on people arriving, and a new resident looking for a gym or a hairdresser is a realistic customer. Check your own county before assuming a boom.

## Which Oregon metro areas are growing?

The Census Bureau publishes metro population estimates. Changes below are July 2024 to July 2025.

| Metro area | Population, July 2025 | Change | Change, % |
|---|---|---|---|
| Portland-Vancouver-Hillsboro (OR-WA) | 2,542,282 | +11,037 | +0.44% |
| Salem | 445,814 | +2,391 | +0.54% |
| Eugene-Springfield | 381,584 | -497 | -0.13% |
| Bend | 266,376 | +2,169 | +0.82% |
| Medford | 221,795 | +448 | +0.20% |

Bend grew fastest at 0.82%, while Eugene-Springfield shrank slightly. The Portland metro crosses the state line into Washington, so its figures include Clark County. A Bend studio and a Eugene studio should not use the same growth assumption.

## How do you keep Google Ads local in Oregon?

Google Ads lets you target regions, cities or postal codes depending on the country, or a radius of at least 1 km around a location ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722043)). Portland, Salem, Eugene and Bend are separate catchments. Give each town its own campaign and radius instead of one Oregon-wide setup.

Google recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting for Search, but says "Presence" may suit advertisers who only want people physically in the area. A studio in Bend may prefer Presence so that it does not pay for clicks from people who are only interested in the area, such as visitors planning a trip. Run a separate campaign for each town you serve so budget and results stay readable.

## Why does Google Business Profile come first?

A free profile appears in map results without a per-click cost. Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your ranking ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)).

Fill in every section, pick the most specific category and keep hours current. Ask customers for reviews with a link or QR code. Google prohibits offering free or discounted goods in exchange for a review ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)). Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers the full setup.

## The math: what does a $500 test return?

Inputs are **assumptions** built on the LocaliQ benchmark for this industry, applied to a fitness studio (Health and Fitness benchmark).

- Budget: $500 a month. CPC: $6.17. Clicks: 500 / 6.17 = about 81.
- Conversion rate: 6.94%, so about 5.6 leads. Cost per lead: about $89.
- Assumption: 40% of them become new members at $140 each, giving about 2.2 new members and $315 in first-sale revenue.

On first visits alone the test earns less than it costs. Repeat business decides the result, which is why a rebooking or reminder system matters more than click price. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to plan for that.

## How do you win new residents and keep customers?

Newcomers need a first reason to try you. Offer a clear first-visit or trial class, and capture an email or phone number at sign-up. Retention matters more than click price: a member who stays for months is worth more than the first sale. See our fitness marketing guide for seasonal ideas.

## FAQ

### How much should a Oregon small business spend on marketing?
There is no Oregon standard. Start with free channels and a test budget you can afford to lose, then scale on cost per customer. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) covers the method.

### Are Google Ads cheaper in Oregon?
Unknown. The benchmark source publishes US averages by industry, not by state. Check your own cost per click in the first month.

### Which Oregon metro is growing fastest?
Among Oregon metros in the Census file, Bend grew 0.82% (+2,169), Salem 0.54% and Portland-Vancouver-Hillsboro 0.44%. Eugene-Springfield fell 0.13%.

### What comes first, SEO or ads?
Set up your Google Business Profile first because it is free. Add ads when you know what a new customer is worth.
