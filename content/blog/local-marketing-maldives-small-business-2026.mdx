## How Big Is the Maldivian Online Audience?

DataReportal counts 530 thousand people in October 2025, with 777 thousand mobile connections (147% of population). Internet users numbered 449 thousand, and 81.2 thousand (15.3%) were offline. Social media user identities were 383 thousand, 72.3% of the population, up 6.7%.

With so few people, a Malé café can reach most local social users with a small radius campaign. The numbers are identities, not unique people.

## Which Platforms Reach People in the Maldives?

DataReportal's late 2025 ad reach:

| Platform | Ad reach | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 383K | 72.3% |
| Instagram | 293K | 55.3% |
| Messenger | 238K | 44.9% |
| LinkedIn (members) | 210K | 39.6% |
| X | 118K | 22.2% |

Facebook reaches 93.0% of adults 18+, and Instagram 71.2%, according to the same page. Instagram grew 10.0% in a year. DataReportal flags the Reddit and X figures as possibly unreliable. No verified WhatsApp or TikTok figure for the Maldives, so we omit them.

Local businesses can start with a Facebook page, Instagram and Messenger replies. Our [Facebook Ads for restaurants](/blog/facebook-ads-for-restaurants) guide shows a simple offer structure.

## How Important Is Tourism?

The World Bank overview says tourism is about 21% of GDP (year not stated). In the first quarter of 2025 arrivals rose 9.4% but shorter stays held real GDP growth to 2.5%. Public debt reached US$9.5 billion, or 126.9% of GDP, by end-June 2025, and reserves covered about 1.8 months of imports.

For a guesthouse or dive centre, this means demand tracks overseas travel trends. A news search put 2025 arrivals near 2.25 million, but we could not open the ministry source, so we do not rely on it.

## How Do You Reach Overseas Travellers?

Tourist-facing businesses reach travellers when they plan trips, not while they are in the islands. That means search ads, Instagram and listing sites in the traveller's own market. Our guides on [Instagram marketing for hotels](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hotels) and [Google Ads for travel agencies](/blog/google-ads-for-travel-agencies) cover the tactics.

Google lists the Maldives as a billing country for Google Ads, with all currencies and payment methods shown as available and English terms. Meta's currency table does not list the Maldivian rufiyaa, so expect to pay in another currency and confirm in Ads Manager. No verified click cost for any Maldives keyword, so test with a small budget.

## How Do You Check a Test Budget?

These inputs are **assumptions** for an imaginary guesthouse, not Maldivian benchmarks.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly test budget | USD 600 |
| Average booking value | USD 900 |
| Gross margin | 30% |
| Enquiries that become bookings | 15% |
| Bookings needed to cover the budget | about 2.2 |
| Enquiries needed | about 15 |
| Maximum cost per enquiry | about USD 40 |

If your ad enquiries cost more than USD 40 under these inputs, improve the offer or landing page first. Direct enquiries also save platform commissions, which is the real gain.
