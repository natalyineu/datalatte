Local businesses can reach live sports audiences through local spots bought from a TV station, or through self-serve streaming platforms such as Roku Ads Manager (minimum $500 lifetime budget) and Amazon streaming TV ads (recommended $10,000 for self-service). Audiences are large: the 2025 NFL regular season averaged 18.7 million viewers per game. Prices for local game spots are not published.

This guide lays out where live-sports ads can run, what the platforms publish about minimums, and how to test small. Audience figures come from ESPN and Sports Media Watch (Nielsen data). Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## How Big Are Live Sports Audiences in 2026?

The 2025 NFL regular season averaged 18.7 million viewers per telecast window, up 10% on 2024 and the highest since 1989, according to Sports Media Watch's report on Nielsen and Adobe Analytics data. The most-watched game was Chiefs-Cowboys on Thanksgiving with 57.3 million. Amazon's Thursday Night Football averaged 15.33 million.

ESPN reports the 2026 NBA Finals averaged 19.6 million viewers across ABC and ESPN through four games, using Nielsen Big Data + Panel. Treat year-over-year comparisons with care: Nielsen changed methodology in September 2025, and Sports Media Watch notes that this could account for much of the NFL's increase.

## Why Does a Local Business Care About Live Sports Audiences?

Because live games are watched in real time, and a local business can buy a slice of that attention without a national budget. The catch is that national audiences are not local. A game with 18 million viewers might reach a few thousand households in your service area, so check geographic targeting before you pay for a sports slot.

Sports ads fit businesses with a broad customer base and a simple offer: restaurants, auto services, home services and gyms. They fit poorly when the buying decision is complex and needs a long click path. ## Where Can You Buy Live Sports Ads?

Four routes exist, and only two publish a minimum. Each has a different entry cost and control.

| Route | What the source says | Entry cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roku Ads Manager | Lifetime budget required on every campaign, at least $500; billed only for delivered spend | $500 lifetime budget |
| Amazon streaming TV ads | Can run alongside live sports including Thursday Night Football; self-service packages have a $10K recommended campaign minimum, managed packages $50K | $10K recommended (self-service) |
| Local TV station spots | Stations sell local ad time; rates depend on market and game | Not published; request a rate card |
| Live TV streaming bundles (Hulu Live, YouTube TV, Sling) | Not verified for this article; see our guide linked below | Not verified |

Amazon's page also says streaming TV has no minimum spend requirement in one section while the FAQ sets minimums by package, so confirm in the buying flow. Amazon also says supply varies by locale outside the US.

## Does Roku Show Ads in Live Sports?

Roku's help center confirms the budget rules but, in the pages read for this article, does not say which live sports events its self-serve inventory includes. Do not assume your ad will air in a specific game. Ask what inventory a campaign can target, and whether you can pick sports content, before you commit the $500 minimum.

## What About March Madness and College Sports?

College events run on national networks and carry strict trademark rules. The NCAA publishes advertising and promotional guidelines (the 2026 edition is dated July 1, 2026), but the document could not be read when this article was checked, so no rule is quoted. Do not use "March Madness" or "Final Four" in your creative without checking with the NCAA or a lawyer.

Safer wording is "college basketball season" or "tip-off special". Read our [March Madness CTV advertising guide](/blog/march-madness-ctv-advertising-local-business) and [college football CTV guide](/blog/college-football-ctv-advertising-local-business) for planning.

## How Do You Measure Live Sports Ads?

Use a promo code, a unique URL or QR code, and a "how did you hear about us" question. Add a call tracking number if phone calls matter. Compare the two weeks of the test against the two weeks before. Sports schedules move with the season, so also compare with the same period last year if you have the data.

Do not judge a campaign on impressions alone. Count the actions that make money: bookings, orders or visits.

## Worked Example: A $500 Test

Inputs are **assumptions**, because neither Roku nor Amazon publishes a CPM for local sports.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget (Roku minimum) | $500 |
| CPM | $30 |
| Impressions | about 16,700 |
| Average frequency | 3 |
| People reached | about 5,500 |
| Share who act | 0.1% |
| Customers | about 5 |
| Value per new customer | $120 |

At these inputs the test returns about $600 on $500 of spend. If the action rate were 0.05% the same test returns about $300. The point is not the output, it is that you can write down the break-even action rate before you spend: $500 divided by $120 is about 4.2 customers.

Write down the break-even number of customers for a $500 test, using your own average customer value. If you cannot reach it in a plausible scenario, spend the money on search ads first.

## FAQ

### How much does it cost to advertise during live sports?

No public rate card exists. Roku sets a $500 minimum lifetime budget, and Amazon recommends $10,000 for self-service packages. Local station spots are priced by the station.

### Can a small business advertise on Thursday Night Football?

Amazon's page says streaming TV ads can run alongside live sports including Thursday Night Football. It recommends a $10,000 minimum for self-service. Confirm eligibility in the buying flow.

### Are NFL audiences really up 10%?

The NFL's 18.7 million average is up 10% on Nielsen's numbers, but Sports Media Watch notes the new Big Data + Panel method could explain much of the rise.

### Can I use the words March Madness in my ad?

Do not without checking. The NCAA publishes advertising standards that restrict its marks. Use neutral wording instead.

### Should I run sports ads or Google Ads first?

If people are already searching for what you sell, search ads usually come first. Sports ads build awareness.
