Lovable has a Free plan with 5 build credits a day, up to 30 a month, and paid Pro plans that start at $25 a month for 100 credits ($21 a month on annual billing). Higher Pro tiers add credits in steps, from 200 credits at $50 up to 10,000 at $2,250. Business starts at $50 for 100 credits. A credit is the unit Lovable charges for building.

Prices and features checked on 7 November 2026.

This guide is for owners and marketers of local businesses who want an AI tool that builds a web page from a description. It covers plans, how credits work, what the Free plan cannot do and what a small project might cost. All numbers come from Lovable's own pages, listed in Sources.

## How Much Does Lovable Cost?

Lovable's documentation lists four plans. Prices for Pro and Business depend on the monthly credits you choose.

| Plan | Price | What the documentation says |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 5 build credits a day, capped at 30 a month, daily chat allowance, no rollover, no top-ups |
| Pro | From $25 a month for 100 credits | Same daily credits, code editing and download, custom domains, design systems, rollover, top-ups, email support |
| Business | From $50 a month for 100 credits | Everything in Pro plus governance features, single sign-on, role-based access, priority support, unlimited invited publishing |
| Enterprise | Contact sales | Volume-based credits, audit logs, SCIM, dedicated support |

All plans include unlimited workspace members, Git sync (GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket) and custom connectors, per the same page. Verified students and teachers can get 50% off Pro with 100 credits a month for up to 12 months.

## How Do Lovable Credits Work?

Lovable prices by credits, not by projects or seats. The pricing page says plan credits are added monthly and can cover building, Cloud and AI features for apps you build. Default Mode costs vary by task complexity, while Plan Mode costs 1 credit per message. The pages do not state a credit count for a typical page build, so none is assumed here.

Rules that matter for budgeting:

- **Daily grants** refresh at 00:00 UTC and expire at the end of each day.
- **Monthly plan credits** roll over while the subscription is active. They expire two months after issue on monthly plans, or one month after the annual period ends.
- **Top-up credits** on Pro and Business expire 12 months after purchase.
- **Cloud, AI and connector grants** (20, 4 and 1 credits a month) do not roll over and are described as temporary and subject to change.

The documentation also gives a legacy conversion rate of $0.25 per credit on Pro and $0.50 on Business, used to convert older balances. It is not a price list for new credits.

## What Is Lovable and How Does It Work?

Lovable calls itself "a full-stack AI development platform for building, iterating on, and deploying web applications using natural language." You describe an app and it generates a working project with frontend, backend, database, authentication and integrations, "all backed by editable code". Each project holds its chat, preview, code and version history, and can be published to a live URL.

The documentation says you can "keep full ownership of your code" and sync to Git. The same plan table, however, says code editing and download are Pro features, not Free. Compare with Bolt.new and [Replit Agent](/blog/replit-agent-pricing-and-limits-2026), which work the same way.

## What Does the Free Plan Not Include?

According to the plan table, Free cannot edit or download code, use custom domains or design systems, and publishes "to invited people only, up to 10 people per project". The same table lists the 10-person limit for Pro too, and unlimited invited publishing for Business.

That wording is hard to read for a business that wants a public website. The pages opened do not say plainly whether a public page is possible on Free or Pro, so check the live pricing page and Publish settings before paying. Custom domains, which most businesses want, are listed from Pro.

## What Would It Cost? A Worked Example

This is an assumption using only published prices. A pet groomer wants a small site built and tweaked over two months.

| Line | Assumption | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Free plan | Try it with 5 daily credits | $0 |
| Pro, 100 credits, monthly billing | Two months at $25 | $50 |
| Pro, 200 credits for one month | If the 100 credits run out | $50 |
| Pro, 100 credits, annual billing | 12 months | $250 |

Two months on the lowest tier is $50. Whether 100 credits cover a site depends on the work, and the pages give no per-page figure. Because monthly credits roll over for two months, unused credits are not lost at once.

## What Are the Risks and Limits?

- **Credits run down.** Complex requests cost more than simple ones, and Plan Mode messages are 1 credit.
- **Publishing limits.** Check who can view a published project on your plan.
- **Expiry.** Rollover credits expire after two months on monthly plans.
- **Ownership and export.** The page says you keep ownership of your code. Download is a paid-plan feature.
- **Review.** Test forms, payments and stored data before launch.

This guide did not open Lovable's privacy or data-training terms or hosting details, so they are not stated here.

## FAQ

### Is Lovable free?

Yes, the Free plan gives 5 build credits a day, up to 30 a month, plus a daily chat allowance. It has no rollover, no top-ups, no code download and no custom domains, per the documentation.

### How much does Lovable cost per month?

Pro starts at $25 a month for 100 credits, or $250 a year ($21 a month). Higher tiers go up to $2,250 a month for 10,000 credits. Business starts at $50 a month.

### What are Lovable credits?

Credits are the unit Lovable charges for building. Cost depends on task complexity, and Plan Mode is 1 credit per message. Monthly plan credits roll over for two months on monthly plans.

### Can I use Lovable for a business website?

Lovable lists websites and landing pages among what it builds. Custom domains and code download need a paid plan. Check the publishing rules for your plan before relying on it.

### Is Lovable worth it for a small business?

It can suit simple pages and tools if someone reviews the result. Test on Free first, then pay for one month of Pro to see how fast credits are used.
