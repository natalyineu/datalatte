Glovo promoted listings are worth paying for only when the ad cost of each extra order is lower than the profit that order leaves you after commission and food cost. Glovo's own terms say partners pay "the amount actually consumed" by a campaign, deducted from the invoices Glovo issues under the Partner Agreement, and they publish no click price or minimum budget. So the test is your own break-even, shown in the worked example below.

This guide is for restaurant owners on Glovo. The menu names differ by country, and Glovo says the options a partner sees depend on its business model and agreement. Facts come from Glovo's terms for the Promotion Tool and Self-Service Advertising (last updated August 2025) and a launch report. Nothing here is a Glovo price list.

## What Are Glovo Promoted Listings and Partner Ads?

Glovo Ads launched in 2023. A report from CIO Africa describes Partner Ads as designed for small and medium-sized shops and restaurants, sold on a pay-per-click basis "with any budget", with placements in searches, homepage listings and sponsored spots through the customer journey. That report covers the launch in Kenya and is secondary. Another launch article could not be opened (403) and was not used.

Glovo's current terms describe two Manager Portal tools: the Promotion Tool, where you set the campaign name, stores, cities, dates, budget and maximum number of orders, and Self-Service Advertising, where you set a maximum monthly spend and a start date.

## How Does Glovo Charge for Ads and Promotions?

The terms do not mention a cost per click or minimum spend. Budgets are caps you set, not minimums. Glovo deducts campaign costs from its invoices to you and shows them as "marketing promotions" or similar.

| Item | What the terms say |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | Set by the partner as a cap |
| Stop rule | At 90% of the agreed budget Glovo may stop the campaign |
| Overspend | Glovo charges the difference between initial budget and final spend |
| Cancelling | Allowed any time, but costs already incurred are owed and not refunded |
| Flash Offers | Cost-per-order fee based on the value of each qualifying order; non-refundable |
| Tool fees | Currently included in the Partner Agreement price; Glovo may add one with at least 20 calendar days' notice |
| Tax | Ad costs are charged with applicable VAT; tool prices exclude VAT |

Campaigns run until you cancel them, so check active campaigns weekly. The terms are in Spanish on Glovo's site, and a country page may differ.

## What Can You Promote Besides Ads?

The terms list partner-funded promotions such as free or fixed delivery, percentage or fixed discounts, spend X get Y, 2-for-1, minimum order values, targeted audiences and Flash Offers. Which ones you see varies. Product discounts and 2-for-1 offers are charged without VAT, and delivery costs include VAT.

A discount costs margin on every order, including orders that would have come anyway. An ad costs money only when the budget is consumed. Treat them as different tools and test one at a time. ## Are Glovo Promoted Listings Worth It for You?

Run the break-even test on your own numbers. Divide the ad spend by your profit per order, and compare it with the extra orders you can credit to the campaign.

- Glovo's commission is not published in the terms checked, so use the rate in your own contract.
- Count only extra orders. A regular customer who would have ordered anyway is not a gain.
- Compare with other channels. See [Glovo versus Wolt](/blog/glovo-vs-wolt-which-is-better) and the [Wolt ads guide](/blog/wolt-ads-restaurant-guide-europe) for the alternative.

## Worked Example: What Is the Break-Even on a 300 Euro Campaign?

All inputs below are **assumptions**. Replace them with your contract and cost data.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign budget cap | EUR 300 |
| Average basket | EUR 22 |
| Platform commission | 30% of basket |
| Food and packaging cost | 30% of basket |
| Profit per order | EUR 22 x (1 - 0.30 - 0.30) = EUR 8.80 |

Break-even is EUR 300 divided by EUR 8.80, about 34 extra orders. If the campaign brings 20 extra orders, it loses about EUR 124. If it brings 50, it earns about EUR 140. Labour and delivery packaging are ignored here, so real break-even is higher. If Glovo stops the campaign at 90% of budget, only EUR 270 is spent in this example.

## How Should You Measure Results?

Record orders for the same weekdays in the four weeks before the campaign and during it. Note any menu, price or weather change. Do not rely on the dashboard alone, because it cannot tell you which orders were extra. Ask new customers how they found you if you also take direct orders. ## FAQ

### How much do Glovo promoted listings cost?

Glovo's terms give no price or minimum. You set a budget cap and pay the amount consumed. Launch coverage described Partner Ads as pay per click, with no stated floor.

### Can I stop a Glovo campaign?

Yes, at any time, but you owe costs already incurred and spent amounts are not refundable.

### Do promoted listings guarantee more orders?

No source reviewed makes that promise. The old claims of fixed percentage gains on this page had no source and were removed.

### Is the commission part of the ad cost?

Not according to the terms. Campaign costs are deducted separately on the invoice, and tool fees are currently included in the Partner Agreement price. Check your own contract.

### Should a dark kitchen use promoted listings?

It can, with the same break-even test. See [Glovo for dark kitchens](/blog/glovo-for-dark-kitchens-advertising-guide).
