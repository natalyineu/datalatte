## How Big Is the March Madness Audience?

Sports Video Group's April 2026 roundup of Nielsen-based figures reports a 2026 tournament average of 10.9 million viewers across TBS, CBS, TNT and truTV, up 7% on the year before. The Final Four averaged 14.2 million, up 11% against 2024. The championship game, Michigan over UConn, averaged 18.3 million on TBS, TNT, truTV and HBO Max and peaked at 20.4 million between 11 and 11:15 p.m. ET.

That is the most-watched title game since 2019, according to the same report. The figures are national. They do not tell you how many viewers sit inside your delivery radius, so ask any vendor for a local impression estimate.

## Where Can You Watch, and Where Do Ads Run?

In 2026 the games aired on CBS, TBS, TNT and truTV. Streaming sources report that TBS, TNT and truTV games stream on HBO Max, and Paramount+ says every CBS game streams live on its Premium plan. The NCAA March Madness Live app carries games from all four networks, according to those reports.

| Where | What it is | What a local buyer can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CBS, TBS, TNT, truTV | Linear broadcast and cable | Ask the local cable or broadcast sales rep for availability |
| Paramount+ and HBO Max | Streaming of the same games | Reach through a CTV platform or DSP that carries them; ask which games |
| Other CTV and FAST apps | Sports and general inventory | Buy by geography and interest, not by game |

Ad availability on a given stream changes, and vendor claims about "March Madness inventory" are hard to verify. Ask for placement reports by app. Our [CTV platform comparison](/blog/ctv-advertising-platforms-compared-2026) lists the ways to buy.

## What Does a March Madness CTV Campaign Cost?

No source read for this page prints a local CPM for the tournament. AdWave's 2026 article puts the blended CTV CPM at about $26, with most campaigns at $25 to $35 and the full market range at $15 to $45. It lists premium direct and live sports inventory at about $45 to $65, as a benchmark estimate, not a rate card.

Treat those as planning ranges only. Our [CTV cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data) shows how to turn a CPM into a cost per customer.

## When Does the Tournament Run?

In 2026 the First Four was on March 17 and 18, the first round began March 19, Selection Sunday was March 15, the Final Four was April 4 and the championship April 6 at Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis, according to press listings. The 2027 dates were not confirmed in the sources read, so check the NCAA schedule before planning a flight.

Viewing built through the event: Sports Video Group says every Sweet 16 and Elite Eight window rose on the year before, with the Friday Sweet 16 early primetime window averaging 14.2 million. If you want reach, plan a flight that includes the second weekend.

## How Do You Plan the Campaign? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**, not quotes. A sports bar budgets $3,000 for a three-week flight and assumes a $35 CPM, inside AdWave's quoted $25 to $35 working range.

| Item | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | Assumption | $3,000 |
| CPM | Assumption | $35 |
| Impressions | $3,000 / $35 x 1,000 | about 85,700 |
| Households reached at 3 views each | 85,700 / 3 | about 28,600 |

Whether 28,600 households yield 120 visits depends on targeting, the offer and the creative. None of these rates are published, so run a small test first. ## FAQ

### Can a local business advertise during March Madness games?

Yes, through CTV platforms, DSPs or local cable sales, but the tournament's national ad time is sold by the networks. Local buyers usually reach viewers by geography and content category.

### Where do March Madness games stream?

In 2026, HBO Max carried TBS, TNT and truTV games, Paramount+ Premium carried CBS games, and the NCAA March Madness Live app carried all four networks, per press and Paramount+ listings.

### Is there a published CPM for March Madness ads?

Not for local buyers in any source read for this page. General CTV benchmarks and a vendor quote are the only guide.

### When should I start planning?

Selection Sunday set the bracket on March 15 in 2026. Agree on creative and a target area before the bracket is set so the campaign can start quickly.

### Is CTV better than a Google Ads campaign for a bar?

They do different jobs. Search captures people who already want a place to watch; CTV builds awareness. Test one before expanding.
