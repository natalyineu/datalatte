## What Are the Limits for a Google Search Ad?

Google's help page says a responsive search ad can have up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions, with a minimum of 3 headlines and 2 descriptions. Headlines allow 30 characters and descriptions 90, and the two path fields allow 15 each. Google assembles the text into combinations that avoid redundancy and tests them over time.

Text pinned to Headline 1, Headline 2 or Description 1 always shows. Headline 3 and Description 2 are not guaranteed. Google says pinning is not recommended for most advertisers and can affect ad strength. Give an AI tool these limits in the prompt, then count characters yourself, because tools often miscount.

## How Do You Prompt AI for Ad Copy?

Give the tool facts it cannot invent: service, location, hours, price you are willing to publish, and the action you want. Ask for varied angles, not rewrites of one line. A prompt that works as a template:

- Business type and city
- Three services in priority order
- One offer you can honour
- Hours and booking method
- Forbidden claims (for example, no "best" or "cheapest" without proof)
- Limits: headlines 30 characters or fewer, descriptions 90 or fewer

Then ask for 20 headlines and 6 descriptions in separate lists. Delete the ones that repeat each other, since Google's editorial policy prohibits gimmicky or unnecessary repetition of words or phrases across assets in the same ad group, campaign or account.

## What Must You Check Before Publishing?

Google's help page for generative AI in Performance Max states you should review any generated or suggested assets and check them for accuracy, misleading claims and compliance with advertising policies and applicable laws. It also says assets can be disapproved in combination with other assets or because of local regulations.

The FTC adds that advertising must be truthful and non-deceptive, and that a company needs a "reasonable basis" for its claims before an ad runs. Customer testimonials alone usually do not substantiate claims that need objective evaluation. So delete claims such as "10,000 happy customers" or "fastest in town" unless you can prove them. Google also bars incorrect capitalization, misused punctuation and symbols.

## What Does Good Local Ad Copy Include?

Google recommends including at least one keyword in your headlines, writing the first three headlines so they read well together, and offering many unique headlines. For a local business, add:

- The service and the place ("Hair Salon in Austin")
- A real next step ("Book Online")
- A fact that separates you, such as hours or a specialty, that you can prove

Google's data says advertisers who improved Ad Strength from Poor to Excellent saw 15% more clicks and conversions on average. That is Google's internal figure on its own help page, so treat it as indicative. Use at least two responsive search ads per ad group with Good or Excellent ad strength, each with a unique final URL.

## Worked Example: One Salon Ad

The business and copy are an **illustrative assumption**. Replace every claim with something true for your salon.

| Type | Draft text | Characters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headline 1 | Hair Salon in Austin | 20 |
| Headline 2 | Book Online Today | 17 |
| Headline 3 | Colour and Cut Specialists | 26 |
| Headline 4 | Open Tuesday to Saturday | 24 |
| Description 1 | Cuts, colour and styling by appointment. Book online in two minutes. | 68 |
| Description 2 | Read our Google reviews, then pick a time that suits you. | 57 |

All lines fit within 30 and 90 characters. The example does not claim a rating, price or ranking, because none is substantiated. Add the keyword "hair salon" to the ad group and send clicks to a page that matches, such as a booking page, not the homepage. ## Should You Use Google's Built-In AI Tools?

Google's help page for Performance Max says you enter the landing page URL, Google AI summarises your products or services and suggests assets, and you can edit the summary and add your own text prompts. You can discard generated assets that do not match the business. The tool does not remove the review step.

Compare any generated copy with your own before it goes live. ## How Do You Test AI Copy Against Your Own?

Run the AI version and your current version in separate ads in the same ad group for at least several weeks, with the same landing page and budget. Compare conversions, not clicks. Google publishes no minimum test length for this, so choose a period, note it, and keep it fixed. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) helps you size the test.

## FAQ

### How many headlines can a Google ad have?
Up to 15 headlines, each up to 30 characters, with a minimum of 3. Descriptions: up to 4, each up to 90 characters, with a minimum of 2.

### Does Google approve AI-written ad copy automatically?
No. Google says generated assets are not guaranteed to be approved and can be disapproved in combination with other assets.

### Can I use claims like "best in town"?
Only with proof. The FTC says a company needs a reasonable basis for its claims before running an ad.

### Does AI copy convert better?
No source in this guide shows that. Test it against your existing ads.

### Should I pin headlines?
Google says pinning is not recommended for most advertisers and can affect ad strength.
