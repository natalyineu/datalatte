Programmatic advertising means ad space is bought and sold by software instead of by phone calls and contracts. An advertiser sets a target audience, budget and price, and a platform bids for each ad view as a page loads. The IAB, the digital ad industry body, says all four categories of ad buys it describes are now handled largely by software.

For a small business, the most familiar example is Google's Display network, which Google says can reach people across 35 million websites and apps. Other platforms exist, but many have costs and rules that suit larger budgets.

## How Does Programmatic Advertising Work?

When a person opens a web page or app, the page has empty ad slots. In an auction, advertisers' software bids for each slot, and the highest eligible bid wins and its ad loads. The IAB calls this mechanism real-time bidding and notes it is often used as another name for open auctions.

You do not watch each auction. You choose the audience, the ad, the budget and the goal. The platform bids on your behalf. Our guide on [how programmatic buying works for local business](/blog/how-programmatic-buying-works-local-business) goes into the steps.

## What Are the Types of Programmatic Buying?

The IAB groups buys by whether prices are fixed and whether space is reserved:

| Type | What it means in plain English |
|---|---|
| Open auction | Any approved buyer can bid; price set by the auction |
| Invitation-only auction | Only invited buyers bid |
| Fixed-price, unreserved | A set price, but no guaranteed space |
| Programmatic direct (automated guaranteed) | Price negotiated with one publisher; space reserved |

For local businesses, open auction through a self-serve platform is the usual starting point.

## Is Google Display Programmatic?

Yes in the sense that it is software-bought and auction-based. Google's help page says Display campaigns use responsive display ads by default and optimized targeting by default, and that you can remarket to people who visited your site. Many professionals use "programmatic" for buying through independent platforms such as demand-side platforms. Our article on [programmatic vs Google Display ads](/blog/programmatic-vs-google-display-ads-whats-the-difference) explains the difference.

## Is It Worth It for a Local Business?

It can fit when you want to remind people who visited your website, or reach a defined area with images or video. It is a weaker fit when people search with clear intent, such as "emergency plumber near me", where search ads usually come first.

Costs vary by platform and market. We keep current ranges in our [programmatic cost and CPM article](/blog/how-much-does-programmatic-advertising-cost-real-cpm-benchmarks-2026), and our guide on [programmatic advertising for small business](/blog/programmatic-advertising-for-small-business-is-it-accessible-now) covers access and minimums.

Worked example, all inputs are assumptions: a bakery has 3,000 website visitors in a month. It sets up remarketing to show a seasonal pastry box ad to those visitors in a 10-mile area for 30 days with a $150 budget. It counts online orders and calls linked to the campaign. If 5 orders at $35 come in, revenue is $175 against $150 in ad spend, before ingredients and labor. The bakery would not scale this until it knew its margin.

## What Should You Check Before Starting?

1. Track the action you want, such as calls or orders, before spending.
2. Ask any vendor for a written list of fees and minimums.
3. Start with a small budget and a 30 to 60 day test.
4. Check where ads appeared, using placement reports.
5. Compare cost per lead with your search ads.

## FAQ

### Is programmatic advertising the same as Google Ads?

Not exactly. Google Ads includes Search and Display. Display is auction-based and software-bought, so it overlaps with programmatic. Search ads are usually not called programmatic.

### Do I need a big budget?

The sources used here do not publish a typical local spend. Vendor minimums vary, so ask each vendor and see our small business guide.

### What does real-time bidding mean?

The IAB describes it as the mechanism behind both open and invitation-only auctions: each ad view is auctioned as the page loads.

### Can I do it myself?

Self-serve platforms exist. You need conversion tracking and time to review reports weekly.

### Is it safe for my brand?

Ads can appear on sites you did not pick. Use placement reports and exclusions, and review them regularly.
