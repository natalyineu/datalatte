A referral program rewards existing customers for sending you new ones. To build one: pick a simple reward, make it easy to share, track who referred whom and follow the disclosure rules. It works because people trust people they know. A Nielsen survey from 2015 found 83% of global respondents completely or somewhat trust recommendations from friends and family.

## What Is Referral Marketing?

It is a structured way to get word of mouth. A customer shares your business with a friend, the friend becomes a customer, and you thank the referrer. The structure matters: without a clear offer, a way to share and a way to tell who sent whom, referrals still happen but you cannot repeat or measure them.

The Nielsen number is old (2015), so treat it as direction, not a current benchmark. It supports the idea; it does not predict how many referrals you will get.

## What Should the Reward Be?

Choose a reward that fits your margin and is easy to explain. Common formats are a credit for the referrer and a first-visit offer for the friend. A salon, for instance, can reward without discounting, as shown in our [salon referral program guide](/blog/salon-referral-program-without-discounts).

Pay the reward after the friend completes a first paid visit, not at sign-up. That keeps the cost tied to actual customers and reduces self-referral.

## Can You Offer Rewards for Google Reviews?

No. Google's Maps policy says businesses may not offer incentives such as payment, discounts or free goods in exchange for posting a review, and such reviews can be removed. You may ask for genuine reviews. Keep review requests and referral rewards separate. See our [Google Business Profile reviews guide](/blog/google-business-profile-reviews).

The FTC's reviews rule has been in effect since October 21, 2024. Its guidance says you cannot suggest that a review must be positive to earn an incentive.

## Do You Have to Disclose Referral Rewards?

When someone is paid or given something of value to recommend you publicly, the FTC's Endorsement Guides say the connection should be disclosed clearly and conspicuously. That applies mainly to public posts, such as a customer sharing an affiliate-style link on social media. Add a line to your referral terms telling participants to say they get a reward when they post about you.

The FTC notes its guidance is not definitive and depends on context. For a specific scheme, ask an adviser.

## What Do Referral Tools Cost?

For online stores, ReferralCandy lists four plans: Basic $39 a month with a 10.5% success fee, Grow $79 with 3.5%, Scale $249 with 1.5% and Enterprise $799 with 0.25%. The fee applies to the purchase subtotal of net-new customers' first three orders. A 7-day free trial is offered, and sales during the trial carry no success fee.

For a local service business, a spreadsheet and a unique code per customer can do the same job at no cost. Link rewards to your CRM or booking tool if it supports it.

## The Math: Does a Referral Pay Back?

All inputs are **assumptions**; replace them with your own.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Average first-visit spend | $60 |
| Repeat visits in 12 months | 3 more visits at $60 |
| Reward to referrer | $15 credit |
| Offer to friend | $10 off first visit |

First-year revenue from one referred customer: $60 - $10 + (3 x $60) = $230. Subtract the $15 reward and you keep $215 before your own costs of service. The same logic shows why a reward larger than your margin on a first visit can still pay back, if repeat rates hold. ## How Do You Launch in a Week?

1. Write the offer in one sentence.
2. Create a code or link per customer.
3. Add it to receipts, your email footer and your booking confirmation.
4. Tell your best customers first, by message or in person.
5. Log every referral and the first-visit date.
6. Review after 60 days and adjust the reward.

Email is the easiest channel for step 3; our [email marketing for ecommerce](/blog/email-marketing-for-ecommerce) guide covers the basics.

## FAQ

### How do I track referrals without software?

Give each customer a unique code and log it at checkout or booking. A spreadsheet with referrer, new customer, date and reward status is enough to start.

### Should the reward be cash, credit or a discount?

Credit is simple for service businesses because it brings the referrer back. Pick what fits your margin.

### Is it legal to reward referrals?

Generally yes, but disclosure rules apply when people promote you publicly, and Google bans incentives for reviews. Check local rules.

### How many referrals should I expect?

No reliable benchmark was found for local businesses, so measure your own rate over 60 to 90 days.
