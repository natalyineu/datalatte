Connected TV (CTV) advertising lets a Las Vegas, NV business show video ads on streaming services such as Hulu, Peacock and Tubi, aimed at households in chosen ZIP codes. It builds brand familiarity rather than clicks, so it works best alongside search ads. The sources behind this guide do not include CTV pricing, so no CPM figures appear here. The budget example uses labelled assumptions, and the search benchmarks come from Google-linked data.

## What is CTV advertising for a Las Vegas business?

CTV ads run on the television screen through streaming apps, not on a phone or desktop. The practical difference for a local business is targeting. Households can be selected by location and demographics rather than buying a slot on a national broadcast. A salon, gym or restaurant in Las Vegas can focus on the ZIP codes its customers actually live in.

Las Vegas has a large resident base alongside its visitor traffic. Household-level targeting helps a local business reach residents who can return to the shop or studio, rather than paying for visitor impressions that cannot convert into regular visits.

## How should you set ZIP-code targeting?

ZIP-code targeting is the main lever for a local business. Start with the ZIP codes where existing customers already live. A booking system or CRM usually holds customer postcodes, and the top five or so areas make a sensible first list. A tighter list concentrates spend on the households most likely to return.

Demographic filters come second. A fitness studio might narrow to adults in a target age band, while a family salon might focus on households with children. Keep the first campaign simple so the results are readable. Set a frequency cap in the platform settings, and review it after two weeks of data, since repeated exposure builds recall but too much wastes spend on the same homes.

## What creative specs apply to CTV?

Specifications vary by platform, so check the requirements in each ad manager before uploading. The checklist below covers points most platforms share:

1. Export a 16:9 landscape video, which suits most television screens.
2. Keep the file within the length the platform accepts. Fifteen and 30 seconds are common, but confirm the limit.
3. Include audio, because television ads play with sound on.
4. Put the business name and location early, and repeat the phone number or address at the end.
5. Test two cuts, one focused on the service and one on a customer outcome, before committing the full budget.

Keep the message simple. A single clear offer is usually easier for a viewer to remember than several competing claims. ## How do you measure a CTV campaign?

CTV rarely produces a direct click, so measurement relies on indirect signals. Track these during the campaign and for a period afterwards:

- Searches for the exact business name in Google
- Direct visits to the website
- Branded search clicks in Google Ads, if the business runs them
- Answers to "how did you hear about us?" on new enquiries

Record a baseline for four weeks before launch so the comparison is fair. Continue the same tracking after the campaign ends to see whether any lift holds. Without a baseline, it is hard to tell whether a change came from the television ads or from seasonal demand.

## FAQ

### Is CTV advertising worth it for a small Las Vegas business?

It can be, if the business serves customers within a reasonable drive and can measure indirect signals such as branded searches and direct visits. Run a baseline first and a trial of defined length before committing a larger budget.

### Which CTV platform should a business start with?

Start with one platform that matches the customer profile. The table in this guide is a fit guide, not a performance ranking. Check current minimums and inventory with each platform before buying.

### How long before CTV shows results?

The sources here do not give a timeline for CTV results. Plan for a multi-week window, record a baseline beforehand, and judge the campaign on indirect measures after the trial period.

### Can CTV be combined with Google Ads?

Yes. CTV builds awareness, and search captures the people who then look up the business. Keep the search budget focused on the business name and core services so traffic lands on relevant pages.

### What if CTV pricing is not yet known?

Request quotes from the platform's self-serve ad manager or an authorised reseller. Put the quoted rate into the budget calculation above in place of the assumption figures.
