## Who Are Your Customers in Manchester?

The ONS reports Manchester's usual resident population grew by 9.7% between the 2011 and 2021 censuses, from about 503,100 to 551,900. That is faster than the North West (5.2%) and England (6.6%). The median age rose from 29 to 31 but remains the lowest in the North West, and private renting rose from 28.4% to 32.3% of households.

For a business owner this points to a young, mobile, renting population that finds places on a phone and moves often. Reviews and map listings matter more than a leaflet. The census took place during the pandemic, so ONS notes some results may have been affected.

## How Many Businesses Compete With You?

The Department for Business and Trade estimated 5,690,265 private sector businesses in the UK at the start of 2025. Of these, 4,272,535 had no employees. A regional count for Greater Manchester from the release was not available in the pages checked, and a chamber of commerce figure of 105,755 enterprises could not be opened, so it is not used here.

The practical point is that most competitors are very small. Many will not have kept a Google Business Profile current. That is your opening.

## What Should You Fix First on Google?

Google says local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. Prominence is influenced by links to your business and by reviews. Google also says there is no way to pay for a better local ranking.

Start with the profile: correct categories, hours, services, photos and a booking link. Then build a steady review routine. Ask each customer at the point of sale and reply to every review. Our [Google Business Profile reviews guide](/blog/google-business-profile-reviews) shows a routine, and the [GBP optimisation checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists the fields. If you run a café, read [coffee shop marketing in Manchester](/blog/coffee-shop-marketing-manchester) too.

## How Do You Keep Google Ads Local in Manchester?

Google's location targeting lets you show ads within a radius of at least 1 km around your business, or in chosen areas. Use it to match how people really travel to you, and avoid a blanket "Greater Manchester" setting unless you serve it all. Add negative keywords for jobs, courses and free offers. Google notes negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals yourself.

Keep copy tied to the place: name the district, the street or the nearest tram stop. Write it only if it is true for your business. No UK or Manchester average click price was found in an official source, so work from your own Keyword Planner view and first-month results.

## When Are the Busy Periods?

Plan around dated events you can confirm. Manchester City Council spokesman Pat Karney confirmed, as reported by Manchester's Finest, that the 2026 Christmas Markets open on 6 November across 10 city centre locations with more than 210 stalls. Most sites close on 22 December, while Albert Square and Cathedral Gardens stay open to 3 January.

That is a clear window for city centre cafés, gift shops and late opening hours. Outside the centre, use your own sales records to find your busy weeks, and run offers a fortnight ahead.

## FAQ

### What is the best marketing for a small business in Manchester?

A complete Google Business Profile with regular reviews, because it costs nothing and local searchers see it first. Add a small, tightly located Google Ads campaign next.

### How much do Google Ads cost in Manchester?

No official Manchester or UK average is published. Use Keyword Planner for your keywords and start with a budget you can lose.

### Should I target all of Greater Manchester?

Only if customers travel from across it. Otherwise use a radius or chosen districts around your premises.

### Is Manchester a good place to start a local business?

The city is growing, with 9.7% more residents between 2011 and 2021, and it is young. Competition is also high, so reviews and a clear offer matter.

### When should I plan Christmas marketing?

The city centre Christmas Markets open on 6 November 2026. Start offers and opening-hours updates before that.
