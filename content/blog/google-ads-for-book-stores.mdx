An independent bookstore should run Google Ads in two small campaigns: one for people searching for a book or a bookshop nearby, and one that promotes events such as signings and book clubs. LocaliQ has no bookstore category. Its nearest figures are $4.14 per click for Shopping, Collectibles and Gifts and $1.63 per click for Arts and Entertainment, so use them as a range, not a promise.

## How Many Independent Bookstores Are There Now?

The ABA's 2025 annual report, released May 28, 2026, says membership grew 19%. The association describes itself as supporting over 3,400 independent bookstores with almost 3,800 locations, and says 605 new independent bookstores opened in the US in 2025. These figures come from a press release as printed by a local newspaper, so treat the exact counts as secondary.

For you, the point is that readers in many towns now have more than one local choice, and online sellers are still a click away. Ads are one way to say what makes your shop different. They do not replace the free tools below.

## Which Campaigns Fit a Bookstore?

Run two small campaigns, not ten.

- **Local intent:** searches for "bookstore near me" and for specific titles or genres you stock, with a landing page showing hours and a map.
- **Events:** ads for signings, children's story hours and book clubs, with a clear date and a sign-up link.

Add negative keywords for searches you cannot serve, such as "free pdf", "audiobook download" or "jobs". Review the Search terms report weekly. If you sell online too, keep that campaign separate so the local budget is not spent on shoppers far away.

## Can You Show Books on Hand in Google?

Possibly. Google Merchant Center describes local inventory ads as an add-on that lets you showcase your products and store information to nearby shoppers searching on Google. Free local listings are the no-cost option. Both need physical stores in supported countries, which include the US, UK and Canada.

This suits a store with a point-of-sale system that can export stock. A small shop with a handwritten stock list may find it too much work. Start with free local listings, and consider the paid option only if you sell a large number of titles that people search for by name.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local?

Google's radius targeting requires a radius of at least 1 km. For a neighbourhood bookshop, a radius of a few miles is usually a sensible start. Broad "Presence or interest" targeting can show ads to people who are interested in your area, so Google suggests "Presence" when you only want people physically there.

Your free Business Profile helps too. Google says local ranking depends on relevance, distance and prominence, and that it cannot be bought. Add your events as posts and answer reviews. See our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## Which Dates Should a Bookstore Plan For?

Independent Bookstore Day is on the last Saturday in April: April 25, 2026 was the 13th annual event, and the next is April 24, 2027, according to the ABA. The ABA's page says more than 2,000 bookstores take part nationwide.

Build an event campaign around that date, plus your own author events. Use a short budget for the two weeks before the event, with a sign-up link. Pair it with email and text reminders: our [SMS marketing for retail](/blog/sms-marketing-for-retail) piece covers consent and timing.

## FAQ

### How much should a bookstore spend on Google Ads?

No bookstore benchmark exists. Start with an amount you can lose, such as $300 to $400 for a month, and measure visits and sales.

### Is Google Ads better than free listings?

Free listings and your Business Profile come first. Ads add reach on top of them.

### Can I advertise events?

Yes. Use a separate campaign with a date, place and sign-up link.

### Can I show in-store stock?

Local inventory ads can show nearby shoppers your products and store information, in supported countries.

### When is Independent Bookstore Day?

April 24, 2027, the last Saturday in April, per the ABA.
