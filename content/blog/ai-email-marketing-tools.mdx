## Which AI Email Tool Fits a Local Business?

Pick by what you already use. A shop with a point-of-sale or booking system that connects to Klaviyo can use its data for segments. A business starting from a spreadsheet of customers is fine with Mailchimp or HubSpot's free tier. The table shows only what each vendor states on its own pricing page.

|---|---|---|---|
| Mailchimp | 250 contacts, up to 500 sends a month or 250 a day | Generative AI features included at no extra cost on Standard and Premium, not on Free or Essentials | Which plan has subject line help or send time optimization |

Paid prices change with contact count and promotions. The Mailchimp page showed promotional first-year prices, so check the live page before budgeting.

## What Can AI Actually Do in Email?

The vendor pages describe three jobs: writing subject lines and body drafts, building campaigns or flows from a short instruction, and reading performance data. All three save drafting time. None replaces a decision about who to email or what to offer.

Keep the human parts: the offer, the local detail (a new team member, a seasonal item), and the final proofread. The publishers do not release a time-saving figure for small businesses, so treat any "save X hours" claim as unverified. Measure your own drafting time for two weeks instead.

## How Do You Test AI-Written Subject Lines?

Run a simple split test on a list large enough to see a difference. Most tools can send two subject lines to a small share of the list and the winner to the rest. If your list is under a few hundred people, differences are mostly noise, so compare over several sends instead.

Worked example, all inputs are assumptions: a salon has 800 subscribers and sends a monthly offer. It writes one subject line itself and asks the AI tool for two more. It sends each version to 200 people and the best to the remaining 200. If one version gets 40 opens from 200 (20%) and another 30 (15%), the gap is 10 opens. That is thin evidence. Repeat the test for three months before changing your standard style.

## What Rules Apply to Marketing Email?

AI tools write copy, but the legal duties stay with the sender. The FTC's CAN-SPAM guide lists the main requirements for commercial email in the US:

- No false or misleading header information or subject lines
- Say clearly that the message is an advertisement
- Include your valid physical postal address
- Give a clear way to opt out, and honor opt-outs within 10 business days
- You remain responsible for email sent on your behalf by others

## FAQ

### Will AI make my emails sound robotic?

It can. Generic prompts produce generic copy. Give the tool specifics such as your service names, prices and neighborhood, then edit the draft so it sounds like you.

### How much time will AI save per week?

No vendor publishes a verified figure for small businesses. Time your drafting before and after for two weeks and use your own numbers.

### Do I need a paid plan to use AI?

Not always. Klaviyo lists an AI subject line generator on its free plan. Mailchimp's generative AI features are listed for Standard and Premium only. Check the plan table before upgrading.

### Does AI handle CAN-SPAM compliance for me?

No. The unsubscribe link and address are usually built into templates, but the sender is responsible for the content and for honoring opt-outs.

### How many subscribers do I need to A/B test?

There is no fixed number in the sources used here. Small lists give noisy results, so repeat tests and look for a consistent pattern.
