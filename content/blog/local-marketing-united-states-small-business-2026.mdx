For most US local businesses the order is Google Business Profile first, reviews second, Google Search ads third and Meta ads for offers and events. BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 71% of US consumers use Google for local recommendations, down from 83% in 2025, so Google is still first but no longer alone. Yelp and other directories matter where your customers use them, and Yelp's ad pricing is not published on the pages checked.

This guide compares the paid and free channels with sourced benchmarks. Worked numbers are labelled as assumptions, and anything not published is said plainly.

## Where Do US Customers Look for Local Businesses?

BrightLocal's 2026 Local Consumer Review Survey reports that 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses and 41% always do, up from 29% the year before. Google is the most-used source at 71%, down from 83%, which suggests people spread across more sources.

The survey also shows what they look for: 74% only consider reviews from the last three months, 32% want reviews from the last two weeks, and 47% will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews. Review count and recency are therefore part of marketing, not an extra.

## What Does Google Advertising Cost?

LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks give Google Search averages by category. The all-industry figures are $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead.

| Category | CPC | Conversion rate | Cost per lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beauty and Personal Care | $4.62 | 10.35% | $39.25 |
| Restaurants and Food | $2.05 | 8.05% | $30.57 |
| Health and Fitness | $6.17 | 6.94% | $67.36 |
| Animals and Pets | $4.06 | 16.22% | $31.50 |
| Home and Home Improvement | $8.33 | 8.05% | $90.92 |
| Attorneys and Legal Services | $9.87 | 5.55% | $131.63 |

These are US averages for the advertisers LocaliQ measures. Your city and competitors change them, so test with a small budget. Google's help says its average daily budget can spend up to twice the daily figure on a day, and the monthly limit is near 30.4 times the daily budget.

## What Do Meta Ads Cost?

LocaliQ's Facebook benchmarks, updated September 23, 2026, show lead campaigns at $1.80 per click, 8.54% conversion and $27.39 per lead across industries. Traffic campaigns average $0.60 per click.

By category: Beauty and Personal Care lead campaigns cost $2.97 per click and $50.91 per lead, and Health and Fitness cost $1.84 per click and $27.11 per lead. Meta leads are cheaper per lead than Google leads, but the intent differs: people on Meta were not searching for you. Our [guide to Facebook ads budgets](/blog/facebook-ads-budget-local-business-how-much-to-spend) covers how to size them.

## Are Local Services Ads Worth Testing?

For eligible service categories, yes. Google's help says you pay for leads related to your business, when a customer gets in touch from the ad, and customers can sometimes book through it. Availability is limited by category and area, and Google provides a "Check eligibility" link.

Advertisers go through screening and verification before the Google Verified badge is issued. Google also discontinued its Money Back Guarantee: requests were accepted for services booked before December 7, 2025.

## What About Yelp and Nextdoor?

Yelp: its advertising page says "Reach more customers looking for your services" but gives no pricing, budget minimum or performance figure, and the pricing pages could not be read. Do not plan a Yelp ad budget from an invented CPC. Ask Yelp for a quote, and compare cost per lead with Google.

Nextdoor: no figures were verified for this article, so none are given. Whatever the channel, the rule from Google's review policy applies: do not offer rewards for reviews.

## How Should You Split $1,500 a Month?

This is an **assumption**-based comparison using all-industry benchmarks. Lead-to-customer rates are invented for illustration, because no public source gives them.

| Input | Google Search | Meta leads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | $1,000 | $500 |
| Cost per lead | $66.69 | $27.39 |
| Leads | about 15 | about 18 |
| Share who become customers (assumption) | 30% | 15% |
| New customers | about 4.5 | about 2.7 |
| Cost per customer | about $222 | about $182 |

On these assumptions Meta looks cheaper per customer, but a small change in the close rate reverses it. If Meta leads closed at 10%, cost would be about $273 per customer. Measure the close rate of each source for two months before moving money. ## What Should Come Before Paid Ads?

Verify your Google Business Profile, because Google says you must add or claim the business and verify it to appear in Search and Maps. Then collect reviews steadily and add call and location assets to ads. ## FAQ

### What is the best marketing channel for a US local business?
For most, a verified Google Business Profile with recent reviews, then Google Search ads. Meta ads suit offers and events.

### How much do Google Ads cost for a US small business?
LocaliQ's 2026 average is $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead across industries. Categories range from $2.05 to $9.87 per click in the table above.

### Are Meta ads cheaper than Google ads?
Per lead, yes: $27.39 against $66.69 on all-industry averages. Per customer it depends on how many leads buy.

### How much do Yelp ads cost?
Yelp's pages checked for this article did not publish prices. Ask Yelp for a quote.

### Do Local Services Ads work?
They are pay-per-lead for eligible categories. Check eligibility in your area.
