Connected TV (CTV) advertising lets a Seattle local business run video ads on streaming services such as Hulu, Peacock and Tubi, aimed at households in chosen ZIP codes. The sources behind this guide do not publish CTV rates, platform minimums or Seattle viewing data. The figures below come from Google's Display documentation and WordStream's Google Ads benchmarks, and the budget example uses labelled assumptions. Confirm current CPMs and minimum spends on each platform's rate card before committing budget.

## What Do the Sources Confirm About CTV and Display?

Google's Display documentation covers Google's own inventory, not CTV. It states that Display campaigns can reach people across [35 million websites and apps, plus Google-owned properties such as YouTube and Gmail](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190). That reach figure describes Google's display network, so it does not tell a Seattle business what a Hulu or Peacock spot will cost.

The same page explains that Display campaigns use AI for targeting, bidding and creative assembly, and that [Smart Bidding strategies can be recommended based on the advertiser's goals](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190). These mechanics apply to Google's display ads, so treat them as a reference point when comparing channels, not as a description of streaming inventory.

## How Do Google Ads Benchmarks Compare With CTV Pricing?

WordStream's [Google Ads benchmarks report averages across industries](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks) for click-through rate, cost per click, conversion rate and cost per action. Its all-industry averages are [3.17% for search and 0.46% for display CTR](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks), and [$2.69 for search and $0.63 for display CPC](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks). Legal advertisers pay the most per search click in that table, at $6.75.

These numbers measure clicks, and CTV is not sold on clicks. A streaming spot is priced per thousand impressions, so a CPC comparison cannot show whether CTV is cheaper than search for a given business. The table below sets out what each channel measures and what to verify before buying.

| Channel | What it is priced on | Sourced figure | What to verify before buying |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Search Ads | Cost per click | $2.69 all-industry average CPC ([WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks)) | Your own industry CPC in the current benchmark PDF |
| Google Display | Click and bidding options | $0.63 all-industry display CPC ([WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks)) | Reach in your area, since the 35 million figure is network-wide |
| Hulu, Peacock, Tubi (CTV) | Cost per thousand impressions | No sourced figure; $25 CPM is an assumption | Current CPM, minimum spend and ZIP targeting on the platform rate card |

The benchmark page is updated regularly, so check the current PDF before using any figure in a budget.

## How Should a Seattle Business Set Up ZIP Targeting?

ZIP-code targeting is the main lever for local CTV. Start with the ZIP codes where existing customers already live, using booking or point-of-sale records. Keep the target area small enough that the budget can buy real frequency, rather than spreading impressions thinly across the whole metro.

Use this checklist before launch:

1. Export the last 12 months of customer ZIP codes from the booking system or point-of-sale.
2. Rank ZIP codes by customer count and select the cluster that covers most revenue.
3. Confirm each platform's ZIP or radius targeting options in its ad manager or with a sales contact.
4. Set a frequency cap and write down the reason for the number chosen.
5. Set a spend ceiling per platform so one channel cannot absorb the whole budget.
6. Record a baseline for branded search volume and direct website traffic before the first impression runs.

Platforms set their own minimums and targeting options, so each step needs a check against the platform chosen. Hulu, Peacock and Tubi are the platforms named in the brief, but this guide has no sourced figures for their minimums or CPMs.

## What Does a Seattle CTV Budget Look Like?

The example below uses assumed inputs to show the arithmetic. Replace each assumption with a figure from a platform quote before relying on it.

- Monthly budget: $600 (assumption)
- CPM, cost per 1,000 impressions: $25 (assumption, check against rate card)
- Average frequency per household per month: 4 (assumption)

Impressions equal the budget divided by the CPM, multiplied by 1,000. With these inputs, $600 ÷ $25 = 24, and 24 × 1,000 = 24,000 impressions per month. Dividing by a frequency of 4 gives 24,000 ÷ 4 = 6,000 households reached in that month.

If the platform quotes a CPM of $40 instead, the same $600 buys 15,000 impressions, or about 3,750 households at frequency 4. The result is sensitive to the CPM, so obtain two or three quotes before choosing a platform.

## How Should a Business Measure a CTV Campaign?

CTV builds brand familiarity over time. It does not produce a same-day click, so a short test shows little. Measure against the baseline recorded before launch, and allow enough weeks for a pattern to appear.

Track these three signals:

- Branded search volume in Google Ads, if search campaigns already run, compared with the same weeks before launch.
- Direct website visits to the homepage and booking page during flight dates.
- A consistent "how did you hear about the business?" question on every new-customer form, so answers can be counted.

Branded search is the closest signal available, but it cannot prove CTV caused a change. Read the trend alongside other channels rather than as a single verdict. ## FAQ

### Does CTV advertising work for a small business in Seattle?

The sources here do not measure CTV results for Seattle businesses, so no performance claim can be made from them. A small test with a recorded baseline and a 60 to 90 day window is the practical way to check results for a specific customer base.

### How much should a Seattle local business spend on CTV?

No sourced budget range exists for Seattle CTV. The worked example shows how to calculate impressions from a budget and a CPM using assumed inputs. Set the budget from platform quotes and what the margins can support.

### Is CTV better than Google Ads for local leads?

The two channels measure different things. Google Ads reports clicks and cost per click, while CTV is priced on impressions. Use branded search and direct traffic to see whether CTV lifts demand for search campaigns.

### Which CTV platform should a business start with?

The sources do not compare Hulu, Peacock or Tubi on cost or audience. Choose the platform whose ZIP targeting and minimum spend fit the budget, and check its current terms before buying.
