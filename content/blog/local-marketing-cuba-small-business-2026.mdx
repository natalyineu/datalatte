A private business in Cuba cannot rely on paid search ads. Google states that Google Ads is not available to advertisers in Cuba and that Cuba cannot be targeted, because of US sanctions. What remains is organic work: a clear profile on the messaging and social apps your customers already use, and content that is light on mobile data.

This guide is for owners of private businesses in Cuba and for outsiders marketing to Cuban customers. Cuba is a hard market to research. Where a fact could not be verified, the text says so and lists it under "What we could not verify".

## How Many Cubans Are Online?

DataReportal's Digital 2025 report counts 7.81 million internet users in Cuba in January 2025, a penetration of 71.3%. It also lists 6.58 million social media user identities, equal to 60.1% of the population. DataReportal warns that identities may not be unique people, so treat the social figure as a ceiling.

Cuba's population is about 10.94 million according to the World Bank (2025). Roughly 3.15 million people were offline in the DataReportal count. A single number does not show how often people can afford to connect, which is the bigger limit, covered below.

## Can You Run Google Ads or Meta Ads in Cuba?

Google Ads: no. Google's help page on country restrictions lists Cuba among the places where Google Ads is not available to advertisers. It says sanctioned countries cannot be targeted, and that someone physically in a sanctioned territory cannot sign in to Google Ads.

Meta Ads: unknown. No Meta page confirming or denying ad targeting in Cuba could be opened for this article. Do not assume it works. Check inside Meta Ads Manager with a real account before planning any budget around it.

For a business outside Cuba selling to visitors, the rule is different. Ads aimed at travellers in their home country use that country's targeting, not Cuba's. Whether your own business address in Cuba blocks that is a question for Google's policy team, and it is not settled here.

## What Does Mobile Data Cost?

Data price decides how much content a business can post. Havana Times reported in June 2025 that ETECSA, the state telecom, limited peso top-ups to 360 pesos every 30 days, which the article equates to 6 GB. A 15 GB plan in pesos cost 11,760 pesos, about twice the average monthly salary. ETECSA also sold dollar packages at $10, $20 and $35.

These are one reporter's figures from mid-2025, and ETECSA changes plans. The practical lesson is stable: heavy video is expensive for you and your customers. Short text, compressed photos and voice notes cost far less.

## What Does an Update Cost in Data? A Worked Example

The inputs below are **assumptions** built from the Havana Times prices above. Check ETECSA's current rates before you use the result.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Photos in a menu or catalogue update (assumption) | 20 |
| Size per compressed photo (assumption) | 5 MB |
| Total data per update | 100 MB |
| Cheap peso top-up rate, 360 pesos for 6 GB | 60 pesos per GB |
| Large peso plan rate, 11,760 pesos for 15 GB | 784 pesos per GB |
| Cost of one update at the cheap rate | about 6 pesos |
| Cost of one update at the large-plan rate | about 78 pesos |

The same update costs about 13 times more on the large plan. Customers face the same maths when they open your photos, so compress images and avoid auto-playing video.

## Which Channels Do Customers Use?

DataReportal gives no per-app counts for Cuba that could be verified here, so the old platform table in earlier versions of this article was removed. Reliable app-level numbers are not public. What can be said is that social media identities reach 60.1% of the population, and that business owners report using messaging apps and Facebook to take orders.

A reasonable start is one complete profile on the app where your own customers already contact you. Ask ten recent customers how they found you and how they message. Their answers beat any national statistic.

## How Do Private Businesses Get Paid?

Cuba has strict rules for private firms. The latest rule found for this article is older. Havana Times reported in August 2023 that private businesses could not withdraw cash from their fiscal account, had a 5,000 peso daily maximum per banking operation, and that businesses selling goods or services must offer electronic payment through gateways or POS services.

Transfermóvil is the Central Bank's payment app. A 2022 report said MLC accounts could be converted to pesos in the app at 1 to 120. Both items are dated, and rules since 2023 may differ. Current limits, and how foreign card payments from visitors work, were not verified.

## How Many Private Businesses Exist?

Cuba legalised private small and medium enterprises (MIPYMES) in 2021. ONEI data reported by OnCuba shows 9,550 private MIPYMES at the end of March 2025, up from 9,236 at the end of 2024. Later reports cite other totals, and they conflict, so use ONEI's quarterly releases for a current count.

This matters for marketing because a young private sector means customers are still learning who is out there. A profile with address, hours, prices and a working contact point is already a competitive edge.

## What Should a Small Business Do First?

Without ads, the order of work is simple:

- Write one profile on your main messaging app with products, prices, hours and the area you serve.
- Keep price lists in text, not image-only posts, so they load on small data balances.
- Keep a single phone number for orders and answer questions the same day.
- Save customer questions and turn the most common into a fixed reply.
- Ask satisfied customers to share your profile with friends.

If you also sell to people outside Cuba, a web page or social profile in English costs little data for you to maintain. ## What We Could Not Verify

- Whether Meta Ads can be bought or targeted for Cuba.
- Per-app user numbers for WhatsApp, Facebook, Telegram, Instagram and YouTube in Cuba.
- Whether Google Maps, TripAdvisor or Airbnb listings can be freely managed by hosts in Cuba today. Airbnb has faced US sanctions enforcement; check its current rules.
- Current limits on business bank transfers and cash, and how foreign visitors can pay.
- Current ETECSA prices. The numbers above are from June 2025.

## FAQ

### Can I advertise on Google from Cuba?

No. Google lists Cuba as a place where Google Ads is not available to advertisers, and says accounts in newly sanctioned places are suspended. Signing in from inside Cuba is blocked.

### Can foreign companies target customers in Cuba with ads?

Google says sanctioned countries cannot be targeted. For Meta, the answer could not be verified. Test in Ads Manager and read Meta's current advertising terms.

### How much does mobile data cost in Cuba?

In June 2025 Havana Times reported a 6 GB peso top-up cap per 30 days at 360 pesos, a 15 GB plan at 11,760 pesos, and dollar packages of $10, $20 and $35. Prices change, so check ETECSA.

### How many private businesses are there?

ONEI figures reported by OnCuba list 9,550 private MIPYMES at the end of March 2025. Later totals reported elsewhere differ.

### What is the cheapest way to start marketing?

A complete profile on the app your customers already use, with text prices and compressed photos. It costs only the data you spend.
