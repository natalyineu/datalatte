Promote a pop-up shop in four weeks: check permits first, announce the dates and address everywhere you already have an audience, run a small ad campaign with a tight radius and an end date, and collect emails at the counter. Google lets you set special hours for temporary events, but its Business Profile rules favour staffed, signed locations, so a pop-up usually belongs on your existing profile, not a new one. Retail search clicks cost $4.14 to $4.44 on average in WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 data.

This guide is for owners opening a short-term shop in a market, mall unit, vacant store or shared space. Local permit rules differ by city, so check with yours. Sales figures for pop-ups are not published by neutral sources, so the example uses labelled assumptions.

## What Should You Sort Out Before You Promote?

Check licensing before you announce anything. The US Small Business Administration says most small businesses need licenses and permits from federal and state agencies, and that the local ones depend on your activity. It lists retail and restaurants among the activities commonly regulated by cities and counties. It adds that a physical property must conform to local zoning.

Ask the city office, the landlord or the market organiser about permits, insurance, signage and sales tax for temporary sellers. Do this first, because a shop shut down on day two cannot be rescued by marketing.

## How Do You List the Pop-Up on Google?

Google's Business Profile guidelines do not mention pop-ups. They say businesses that show an address should be staffed by their team during stated hours and keep permanent fixed signage at that address. A short-term shop may not meet that, so check before creating a new listing.

For an existing business, the guidelines say you can set special hours for holidays, temporary closures or other events. Use that, plus a profile post with dates and the address, and tell people where to find you. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) shows how to keep the rest of the listing accurate.

## Which Channels Work for a Four-Week Plan?

| Week | Task | Channel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 weeks out | Announce dates, address, one reason to visit | Email list, Instagram, Business Profile post |
| 3 weeks out | Recruit one or two neighbouring businesses to share the news | Local partners |
| 2 weeks out | Start a small search campaign with an end date | Google Ads |
| 1 week out | Remind, show product teasers | Email, SMS, Stories |
| Opening week | Daily stock and hours updates | Social, profile |

No neutral study ranks these channels for pop-ups, so treat this as a plan to test. Our [SMS marketing for retail](/blog/sms-marketing-for-retail) guide covers texting your existing customers, and the [local boutique TikTok article](/blog/tiktok-marketing-for-local-boutiques) covers short video.

## How Should You Set Up a Short Ad Campaign?

Use radius targeting. Google's help says it lets you show ads to customers within a certain distance from your business, with a radius of at least 1 km. Pick a radius that matches how far people will walk or drive for your goods, and set an end date so the ads stop when the shop does.

Expect retail clicks to cost roughly $4 to $5. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks list $4.14 for Shopping, Collectibles and Gifts and $4.44 for Apparel, Fashion and Jewelry, with conversion rates of 4.01% and 4.50%. Use them as a range, because pop-up searches are narrower than a permanent store's. See our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) for how to split spend.

## How Do You Keep Customers After It Closes?

A pop-up ends, so capture contact details while people are there. Offer a sign-up for the next drop, a restock alert or a small discount on an online order. Get clear consent for email or text, then send one message within a week.

Track what you can: visitors counted at the door, sales, and the email sign-ups. Ask each buyer how they heard about you and tally the answers. That tells you which channel to repeat for the next pop-up.

## FAQ

### How far ahead should I promote a pop-up shop?
Four weeks is a sensible plan: announce early to your list, then remind in the final week. No neutral study gives a best lead time.

### Do I need a permit for a pop-up shop?
Often, yes. The SBA says local requirements depend on your activity and zoning, so check with your city.

### Can I put a pop-up on Google Maps?
Google's guidelines favour staffed, signed locations. Use special hours and a post on your existing profile, and check the rules before adding a new one.

### Should I run Google Ads for a pop-up?
Only if demand exists. Retail clicks average $4.14 to $4.44, so run a small, time-boxed test with a tight radius.

### How do I measure success?
Count sales, visitors and email sign-ups, and ask each buyer how they heard about you.
