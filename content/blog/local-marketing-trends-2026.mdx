The best-supported local marketing trends for 2026 are that people check reviews before they buy, they are spreading searches across more places, and AI summaries are cutting clicks to websites. BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 97% of consumers read local reviews, but only 71% use Google for them, down from 83%. Pew found users clicked a result link in 8% of Google visits with an AI summary against 15% without. Local businesses should keep profiles accurate, collect reviews and own their customer lists.

## Are Reviews Still the Biggest Local Trend?

Yes, and expectations are rising. BrightLocal's Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 (1,002 US adults, published February 11, 2026) found 97% read reviews and 41% always do. 74% only care about reviews from the last three months, and 32% look for reviews from the last two weeks, up from 20%.

Rating thresholds moved up: 31% will only use businesses rated 4.5 or higher, up from 17%. 89% expect owners to reply. So recent, answered reviews matter more than a high count from years ago. See our [guide to managing online reviews](/blog/managing-online-reviews-small-business).

## Is Google Still the Only Place Customers Look?

Not quite. BrightLocal reports 71% use Google for reviews, down from 83% in 2025, and 40% trust AI platforms for business recommendations. The survey does not say which platforms took the share, so treat it as a sign to check your listings across the web.

Pew's 2026 social media fact sheet says YouTube and Facebook are the most widely used platforms in the US, about half of adults use Instagram, and 31% use TikTok, including 65% of adults 18 to 29. Pick channels by who your customers are, not by what is new.

## How Are AI Summaries Changing Search?

Pew tracked the browsing of 900 US adults in March 2025 and 68,879 Google searches. Users clicked a traditional result link in 8% of visits when an AI summary appeared and in 15% when it did not. That is data from one month in 2025 and covers all searches, not only local ones.

For a local business, it suggests that a Business Profile with accurate hours, services and reviews may be seen without a site visit. ## What Is Changing in Paid Search and Email?

Two rule changes are confirmed by Google:

| Change | What Google says |
| --- | --- |
| Language targeting | From September 2026, Search campaigns no longer have a campaign-level language setting; ads match users by the language of the ads |
| Email sender rules | Since February 1, 2024, all senders to Gmail need SPF or DKIM and a spam rate under 0.3%; bulk senders also need one-click unsubscribe |

The 2026 LocaliQ benchmarks (updated June 1, 2026) give averages of $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead across industries, with category figures from $2.05 per click for Restaurants and Food to $8.33 for Home and Home Improvement. This guide did not compare them with earlier years, so it makes no claim that costs are rising.

## What About Fake Reviews and AI Content?

The FTC's final rule bans fake or false reviews and testimonials, including AI-generated ones, and allows civil penalties against knowing violators. Google's guidance on AI content says to manually fact-check AI-generated content before publishing, and warns that generating many pages without adding value may violate its spam policy.

That favors small businesses that publish real photos, real answers and real reviews. Use AI for drafts, such as in [email for hair salons](/blog/ai-powered-email-marketing-for-hair-salons), and review every piece.

## FAQ

### What is the biggest local marketing trend in 2026?

Reviews. 97% of consumers read them, and recency and replies matter more than before, according to BrightLocal.

### Is local SEO still worth it?

Yes, but AI summaries may reduce clicks. Pew found 8% of visits with an AI summary led to a result click against 15% without. Keep your Business Profile and reviews strong so you appear either way.

### Should small businesses be on TikTok?

It depends on your customers. 65% of US adults 18 to 29 use it, against 11% of those 65 and older (Pew, 2026).

### Are ad costs going up?

This guide did not compare years. The current averages are in the LocaliQ benchmarks.

### Do I need to change my Google Ads language settings?

Google says no action is required. Search campaigns will match by the language of your ads.
