A local business can combine CTV and DOOH by buying both in the same ZIP codes and weeks, using the same offer and visuals, then comparing sales in exposed and unexposed areas. CTV gives you a full-screen story at home. DOOH repeats it near the shop. Roku and Disney Campaign Manager both list a $500 minimum for CTV, so a first test is affordable. Nothing here is a promise of results.

This guide is for owners who already know the basics of each channel. It covers when the pairing makes sense, how to split a small budget, and how to measure without enterprise tools. Where a number is not published, the text says so.

## Does Combining CTV and DOOH Make Sense for a Local Business?

It can, if the shop has one clear offer and a defined trade area. The IAB's July 2025 DOOH Measurement Guide says DOOH works best when aligned with other digital channels such as mobile, display and connected TV, and calls it a key upper-funnel channel when synchronized with other media. That is guidance for planners, not proof that the pairing pays off for a single store.

Out-of-home is mostly local money. OAAA reports local sales were 65% of US OOH ad sales in 2025, and DOOH grew 10.5% that year. A shop is not alone on the boards.

If the budget is under roughly $1,000 a month, pick one channel first. Our [CTV platforms comparison](/blog/ctv-advertising-platforms-compared-2026) and [programmatic DOOH strategy](/blog/programmatic-dooh-strategy-local-business) help you choose.

## How Do the Two Channels Differ?

| | CTV | DOOH |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where people see it | On the TV at home | On screens in public places |
| Buying | Self-serve possible, such as Roku Ads Manager or Disney Campaign Manager | Often through programmatic platforms or media owners |
| Published minimum | $500 on Roku and Disney Campaign Manager | Not published by Vistar Media on its homepage |
| Targeting | Disney lists ZIP code, age, gender, interests and genre | By screen location and audience data |
| Frequency control | Per household in the platform | Limited, because screens are public and broadcast (IAB) |

CTV can cap how often one household sees an ad. The IAB guide notes that frequency capping in DOOH is limited, but DOOH exposure data can inform decisions in more controllable channels like mobile or CTV.

## How Should You Sequence the Two Channels?

Run them in the same weeks, not one after the other. The aim is overlap in the same area. Pick the three or four ZIP codes within a short drive of the shop. Book CTV to those ZIP codes where the platform allows it, and book screens that sit on the routes people use to reach the shop.

Keep the creative consistent. Use the same offer, the same headline and the same shop name on both. On CTV, Roku accepts videos of 6 to 92 seconds in .mov or .mp4, so a 15 or 30 second spot is fine. For DOOH, a still or a short loop with one line of text and a place name is easier to read at a glance.

## How Do You Split a Small Budget?

Start with a split you can defend, then change it after four weeks. The numbers below are an **assumption** for planning, not a benchmark.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly test budget | $1,500 |
| CTV share | 60% = $900 |
| DOOH share | 40% = $600 |
| Roku or Disney minimum | $500 each, so one CTV platform only |
| Exposed ZIP codes | 4 |
| Comparison ZIP codes | 4, no ads |

With $900 on CTV you clear the $500 minimum on a single platform. Do not spread $900 across two platforms. For DOOH, ask the seller for the price per thousand plays and the multiplier they use, because rates are not published on the pages checked for this article. The aim of this table is the structure, not the prices.

## How Do You Measure Without Enterprise Tools?

Compare exposed and unexposed areas. The IAB guide describes matched store testing as comparing stores with DOOH exposure to similar stores without it, and it describes indirect validation as comparing sales during the campaign against historical data or control markets while accounting for seasonality. A single shop can copy the idea at ZIP-code level using its booking or till data.

Ask every new customer how they heard of you and record the answer. Add a vanity URL or a QR code to the DOOH creative. Roku mentions interactive end cards or QR codes through a partner on its cost page.

Be careful with impressions. The IAB guide separates Opportunity to See from Likelihood to See, and it says DOOH impressions rely on a multiplier built from foot traffic and dwell time. No standard method is set for how sellers calculate that. Ask which one is used.

Write down the four ZIP codes where 70% of your customers live. Those are your exposed area, and four similar ZIP codes with no ads are your comparison area.

## FAQ

### How much does a CTV and DOOH campaign cost?
Roku and Disney Campaign Manager each list a $500 minimum for CTV. DOOH prices are not published on the sources checked, so request a quote. A first test of about $1,500 a month is a planning assumption, not a standard.

### Can a small shop buy DOOH without an agency?
Programmatic platforms such as Vistar Media list programmatic OOH as a product, but its homepage does not state whether it has self-serve access or minimums. Ask the seller before planning around it.

### Which channel should come first?
If you can only afford one, choose the channel you can measure. CTV reports per household through the platform. DOOH needs location data or a sales comparison.

### How long should the test run?
The IAB guide notes that comparisons should account for seasonality. A simple rule is to run at least four weeks and compare with the same weeks a year ago if you have the data.

### Does DOOH exposure data reach CTV?
It can. The IAB guide says exposure data can be used in DSPs to retarget across mobile, display and CTV, but not all DSPs can take it in, and CTV identifiers often need ID mapping.
