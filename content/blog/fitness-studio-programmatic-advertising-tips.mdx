A fitness studio should treat programmatic advertising as a second channel, after search and a complete Google Business Profile. Use it to remind people who already visited your site, to reach nearby people who have not searched yet, and to keep your name in front of leads. LocaliQ's 2026 search benchmarks put Health & Fitness at $6.17 per click and $67.36 per lead, so search is costly, and display or programmatic can be a cheaper way to stay visible. No source publishes a fitness-specific programmatic CPM, so do not trust anyone who quotes one.

## What Is Programmatic Advertising for a Studio?

Programmatic means ads are bought through automated auctions across many websites and apps, not booked one by one. For a studio, the practical forms are display banners, video and retargeting. Platforms differ in who they serve. Google's Display & Video 360 help page describes partners as agencies, trading desks or large advertisers and requires certified staff for company certification, so it is not a small-studio tool.

Self-serve tools exist. AdRoll's pricing page says it has no minimum spend and prices on dynamic CPM, with no rates listed. Our cost guide, [how much programmatic advertising costs](/blog/how-much-does-programmatic-advertising-cost-real-cpm-benchmarks-2026), covers the CPM ranges and their weak sources.

## Tip 1: Should You Run Search First?

Yes. People searching for a studio nearby are further along than people who see a banner. LocaliQ's Health & Fitness average of 6.94% conversion means roughly 1 click in 14 becomes a lead. Our guides to [Google Ads for gyms](/blog/google-ads-for-gyms) and [programmatic for fitness studios](/blog/programmatic-advertising-for-fitness-studios) explain how the channels fit.

## Tip 2: What Should the Goal Be?

Pick one measurable action, such as a trial class booking. Reach and impressions alone do not pay rent. Count a booking as the conversion and ignore clicks as a success measure.

## Tip 3: Why Is Retargeting the Safest Place to Start?

Retargeting shows ads to people who already visited your site. Google's help describes this as segments of past visitors reached on the Display Network, plus customer lists you upload (Customer Match). The audience is small and warm, so a small budget goes a long way. Google's page did not state minimum list sizes, so check your platform's current requirements.

## Tip 4: How Do You Keep Spend Local?

Use a tight radius around the studio. Google Ads allows radius targeting from at least 1 km (Google location targeting help). Check each programmatic platform's own location controls, since they differ and were not reviewed here. Exclude areas past realistic travel time.

## Tip 5: How Do You Stop Ads Following People Everywhere?

Set a frequency cap. Google Ads lets Display campaigns cap impressions per user per day, week or month, at campaign, ad group or ad level. No source gives the best number for a studio, so start low and review after two weeks.

## Tip 6: What Should the Landing Page Do?

Send people to a page that shows the offer, the schedule and one booking button. Do not send them to a home page. Track bookings as conversions so the platform can report cost per booking.

## Tip 7: How Do You Capture Leads You Can't Book Today?

Offer something specific, such as a trial week or a beginner guide, in exchange for an email, then follow up. See our [fitness email guide](/blog/email-marketing-fitness-studio). The offer needs to be one you can deliver without staff overload.

## Tip 8: How Do You Judge Results?

Measure cost per trial booking and cost per new member, not CPM or clicks. Compare them with search. If search brings a member for less, move the budget there.

## FAQ

### Is programmatic worth it for a single studio?

It can be, as retargeting on a small budget. For a new studio, search and the Business Profile usually come first.

### What does programmatic cost?

No fixed rates are published. AdRoll prices on dynamic CPM and has no minimum spend. Ask any vendor for all fees in writing.

### Can I use Google's Display & Video 360?

Google describes DV360 partners as agencies, trading desks or large advertisers, so a single studio would normally go through a self-serve platform or an agency.

### How do I stop wasting impressions?

Use a tight radius, a frequency cap and a landing page that matches the ad.
