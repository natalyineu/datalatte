## How Many Canadians Listen to Podcasts?

The Canadian Podcast Listener study, reported in a September 17, 2025 press release, surveyed more than 4,600 adults. It found 46% listened to a podcast in the past month, up 19% year on year, and weekly listening up 16%, with about a third of adults listening weekly. Among French Canadians, 33% listened in the past month, up from 24%. Among newcomers, 76% listened monthly.

This is a secondary source (a Podnews reprint of the press release). Triton Digital's 2026 report could not be opened, so its figures are not used.

## Where Can a Small Business Buy Podcast Ads?

Three routes work for a small budget:

- **Spotify Ads Manager:** Spotify's help page says you can launch with a minimum budget of 250 in your local currency, with audio or video ads across music, podcasts or both. Its page for Canada also notes that host-read sponsorships need a Spotify representative.
- **Direct sponsorship:** Message the host of a local show, such as a neighbourhood food, sports or parenting podcast, and ask for a rate card and download numbers.
- **Podcast ad networks:** These sell across many shows. Minimums vary and are not published in the sources checked.

A 2018 report said Spotify's self-serve tool launched in Canada that year. Check that your account sees Canada as a market before you plan around it.

## How Do You Keep Podcast Ads Local?

Podcasts are national by default. Spotify says its targeting uses behavioural, demographic and contextual signals, and a 2018 launch report listed location among the options. For a single-city business, a local show with a loyal audience is often better than broad targeting because the listeners already live nearby.

Give listeners a reason to act that you can count: a vanity URL, a code or a phone line used only in the ad. Without this, a podcast ad is almost impossible to measure.

## How Should a Business Test It?

The example is an **assumption**, because no CPM is published here.

| | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget (assumption) | C$250 (Spotify minimum) |
| Offer | First-visit code PODCAST (assumption) |
| Run length | 4 weeks (assumption) |
| Success measure | Number of redemptions of the code |
| Decision rule (assumption) | Keep if cost per redemption is below one customer's first-visit margin |

Because the rate is unknown, the test tells you your cost per redemption, not a benchmark. Run one creative, change nothing for four weeks and record codes weekly. ## What Will the Ad Say?

Keep it short and specific: who you are, where you are, one offer and one way to respond. Spotify allows audio created with its tools, but a host reading your copy usually sounds more natural. Match the language to the audience, since French Canadian listening is a separate, growing segment.

## FAQ

### How much does podcast advertising cost in Canada?

Rates are set per show and are not published in the sources checked. Spotify Ads Manager lists a minimum campaign budget of 250 in local currency.

### How many Canadians listen to podcasts?

46% of adults listened in the past month in the 2025 Canadian Podcast Listener study, reported secondhand.

### Can I target one city?

A 2018 launch report listed location as a Spotify targeting option; confirm it in Ads Manager. A local show may reach your city more reliably than a national one.

### How do I measure a podcast ad?

Use a unique code, a vanity URL or a dedicated phone number in the ad copy.

### Should I advertise in French?

French Canadian listening is growing, with 33% listening monthly. If you serve a French-speaking area, test French-language shows.
