An AI customer service agent for a local business is a chatbot that answers routine questions (hours, prices, booking, directions) on your website or messages, and hands anything else to a person. Published prices are low: Tidio's standalone Lyro AI agent starts at $32.50 a month for 50 conversations, and Intercom's Fin charges $0.99 per outcome. The risk is quality: a Gartner survey found 64% of customers would prefer companies did not use AI for customer service.

This guide covers what these tools cost, where they help a small business, the main risk, and a worked example labelled as an assumption. All prices were read on vendor pages on 31 October 2026.

## What Can an AI Agent Handle for a Local Business?

The sensible scope is repeatable questions with one correct answer: opening hours, service menu and prices, parking, cancellation rules, how to book, and order status if you sell online. These come up daily and a bot can answer at 11 pm.

Keep out anything that needs judgment: complaints, refunds, price negotiation, medical or legal advice, and special requests. Those should route to you with the conversation attached. For a comparison of simple chat tools and agents that take actions, see [AI agent vs chatbot](/blog/ai-agent-vs-chatbot-whats-the-difference-local-business) and [AI chatbots for local business booking and FAQs](/blog/ai-chatbot-for-local-business-booking-and-faqs).

## What Is the Biggest Risk?

Wrong answers and no route to a person. Gartner's survey of 5,728 customers (fielded December 2023, published July 2024) found 64% would prefer companies not use AI in customer service and 53% would consider switching. The percentages come via a secondary report because Gartner's own page returned an error. Gartner's advice is to let customers reach a human when self-service runs out and to hand over the conversation without making them start again.

A legal point also applies. In Moffatt v. Air Canada (2024 BCCRT 149), a British Columbia tribunal held the airline liable for wrong information from its website chatbot and rejected the argument that the bot was a separate entity. It is one tribunal decision in Canada, not a rule everywhere, but the lesson is practical: you answer for what your bot says. Load it only with policies you have written down.

## A Worked Example: What Does It Save?

All inputs are **assumptions** for a small clinic-style service business.

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Routine enquiries per month | 200 |
| Share the bot resolves | 60% (assumption, not a benchmark) |
| Resolved by the bot | 120 |
| Intercom Fin charge at $0.99 | about $119 |
| Staff time per routine enquiry | 4 minutes |
| Time saved | 120 x 4 = 480 minutes, or 8 hours |
| Staff cost | $20 per hour |
| Value of time saved | $160 |

The saving is about $160 against about $119 in per-outcome fees, before the cost of a helpdesk seat and setup. That is thin. At a 30% resolution rate it turns negative. The gain from an agent often comes from answering after hours and not missing a booking, which this example does not count. Measure your own resolution rate on a trial before committing.

Set a visible "talk to a person" option and test 20 real questions from your inbox before going live. Remove any answer the bot gets wrong from its knowledge base.
</Callout>

## How Do You Set One Up?

1. List your 15 most common questions and write the correct answer for each.
2. Choose a tool with a free or low-cost trial. Tidio's lifetime 50 free Lyro conversations are enough to test.
3. Load only your own text: FAQs, price list, policies.
4. Turn on handoff to email or phone for anything outside that list.
5. Review the conversations weekly for a month and fix wrong answers.

## FAQ

### How much does an AI customer service agent cost?

Published starting prices are $32.50 a month for 50 Tidio Lyro conversations and $0.99 per outcome for Intercom Fin. Total cost depends on conversation volume and whether you need a helpdesk seat.

### Will customers accept a bot?

Many say they prefer a person. Gartner's 2023 survey found 64% would rather companies did not use AI for service. Offer an easy route to a human.

### Is the business responsible if the bot is wrong?

In one 2024 Canadian tribunal case the airline was held liable for its chatbot's wrong answer. Rules differ by country, so ask a lawyer for anything high stakes.

### Which questions should I keep away from the bot?

Complaints, refunds, health or legal questions and price exceptions.

### Do I need an agent that books appointments?

Only if bookings are a main enquiry. Many booking tools include reminders and online booking without AI.
