Connected TV (CTV) advertising in Columbus, OH lets local businesses run full-screen video ads on streaming services such as Hulu, Peacock and Tubi, targeted by ZIP code and household. The main decisions are which platform to buy, how tightly to target the ZIP codes around a business, how long the ad runs and how much budget to test with. The sources reviewed for this guide contain no CTV pricing or Columbus market data, so every example figure below is labelled as an assumption.

## What Do Local Businesses Need to Know About CTV Costs?

Published CTV cost figures for Columbus were not available in the sources reviewed, so no CPM for the market is stated here. Platform minimums and rates change, and they differ by buying route. Check them in the ad manager you plan to use before setting a budget.

Search and display benchmarks give a reference point for comparing channels. The [WordStream Google Ads benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks) are a vendor publication and report all-industry averages, not CTV prices. CTV does not produce clicks in the same way, so judge it on reach, recall and downstream searches rather than click metrics.

## Which CTV Platforms Suit a Columbus Business?

Hulu, Peacock, Tubi and Paramount+ (with Pluto TV) are the streaming services most often named for local buyers. The right choice depends on the audience. A broad, value-focused audience suggests a free ad-supported service. A household audience that watches live sports suggests a service that carries those events.

Before committing, ask each platform for its current audience data, ZIP-code targeting options, creative specifications and minimum spend. A business that already runs paid search may find that one dashboard covering several platforms is easier to manage than separate buys.

## How Should a Business Target Households by ZIP Code?

ZIP-code targeting is the main reason to use CTV for a local business. It reaches households in the neighbourhoods where customers live, rather than the wider audience a broadcast channel reaches. Start with the business address and a radius the business can serve, then narrow to the ZIP codes where existing customers already live.

Demographic filters such as age, income and household composition can narrow the audience further. A tight filter cuts reach, and a narrow audience can make a test too small to read. For a first campaign, a geographic filter alone is often enough to judge whether the channel works.

Frequency is the other setting to decide. Choose a cap that keeps the ad in front of each household enough times to be remembered, without repeating it so often that viewers tire of it. Review the frequency report during the campaign and adjust the cap if one group of households sees the ad far more than others.

## How Do You Measure a CTV Campaign?

CTV is measured through indirect signals. Track branded search volume in Google Ads and Google Business Profile searches for the business name during the campaign weeks, and compare them with a period without the campaign. Ask new customers how they found the business, and record the answers in the same way every time.

Allow enough time before judging the result. A test of a few weeks may show early movement, but brand recall usually builds over a longer period. Set the success measure before launch, such as a defined rise in branded searches or a defined number of new customers who mention the ad, so the result is not read after the fact.

## What Should a 15-Second CTV Ad Include?

The sources reviewed here do not list CTV creative specifications, so check the current format requirements in the chosen platform before producing files. A common structure is a short ad with the brand shown early, the core offer in the middle and a clear call to action at the end.

Keep the call to action simple: one phone number or one web address, and one location. Viewers watch on a television, often from a distance, so text should be large and sparse. If the offer changes between campaigns, produce a separate version for each offer rather than editing one file for several purposes.

## What Steps Should a Columbus Business Follow to Launch?

1. Confirm the business area: list the ZIP codes where current customers live, using your booking or point-of-sale records.
2. Pick one platform with ZIP-code targeting and check its current minimum spend and creative specifications.
3. Set a test budget for several weeks, using the assumption structure in the worked example above.
4. Record baseline branded search volume and new-customer answers for at least two weeks before launch.
5. Define the success measure, such as a set rise in branded searches, and write it down before the campaign starts.
6. Run one offer and one ad version, then review the frequency report midway and adjust the cap if needed.

## Should CTV Run Alongside Search or Display?

CTV and search do different jobs. CTV builds awareness among people who are not yet looking for a service. Search captures people who already are. When both run at the same time, search may pick up demand that CTV creates, which is why branded search should be part of the measurement.

Google's display campaigns reach across a large network of sites, apps and Google-owned properties, including YouTube, according to [Google's Display campaign help page](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190). That is a different channel from CTV, but it is another way to reach people who are not searching. A business that already runs search may test display or CTV as a second channel rather than both at once with a small budget.

## FAQ

### Does CTV advertising work for a small business in Columbus?

It can, if the business serves customers within a reachable area and is willing to measure brand effects rather than clicks. The sources reviewed do not include Columbus-specific CTV results, so run a small test and measure branded search and new-customer mentions before committing a larger budget.

### What is the easiest way to start?

Choose one platform that offers ZIP-code targeting, set a test budget that can run for several weeks, and define the success measure before launch. Keep the first campaign to one offer and one ad version so the result is easier to read.

### How is CTV different from Google display ads?

CTV runs video on streaming services on a television screen. Google display ads run across sites, apps and Google-owned properties such as YouTube. Both can build awareness, but they differ in format, audience and pricing. Check each platform's help pages for current terms.

### How long should a CTV test run?

The sources reviewed do not give a recommended duration, so set one based on the budget and the volume of branded searches already seen. Compare the campaign period with a period without it, and allow enough time for brand recall to show before drawing conclusions.
