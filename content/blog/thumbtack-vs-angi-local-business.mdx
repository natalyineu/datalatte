Thumbtack and Angi differ most in what they cover and how leads reach you. A 2024 comparison by 7ten Marketing (secondary) says Angi centres on about 720 home improvement and repair services, while Thumbtack covers more than 900 services across 13 industries, including events, personal care and fitness. So a roofer and a wedding DJ may land on different platforms.

## How Do the Two Platforms Find You Customers?

7ten describes Angi as working like a directory. Businesses list their services, customers search for providers and then contact the one they like, so a complete profile and visible listing matter.

Thumbtack works as a matching system. Pros build a profile and say which jobs they will take, and Thumbtack matches them with customers who fit. The pros then receive requests aligned with their skills instead of waiting to be found. This is the source's description, written in 2024, so check how each product works today.

## Who Owns Angi, and Why Does It Matter?

Angi Inc. operates the brands Angi, Angie's List, HomeAdvisor and Handy, according to its annual report ([Angi Inc. Form 10-K, FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1705110/000170511026000011/angi-20251231.htm)). Thumbtack is a separate company.

That affects how you buy. With Angi Inc. you may be offered more than one product for similar leads. Ask which product, contract and lead type you are signing. Our [HomeAdvisor vs Angi guide](/blog/angi-ads-vs-homeadvisor-contractors) explains the split.

## Who Tends to Pick Which Platform?

7ten says Angi tends to attract established professionals with years of experience and strong reputations. Competition can be fierce, and standing out may take ad spend. Thumbtack's wider marketplace includes newer businesses as well as veterans, which can mean more price competition in crowded categories.

These are the source's observations, not measured data. Use them as questions to test: how many other pros does a lead reach, and how fast do you need to reply to win?

## How Does Pricing Differ at a Glance?

The 2024 source says Angi is free to join but charges fees to advertise and become more visible, with pricing negotiated around your advertising needs. It says Thumbtack uses credits that pros buy to contact leads, with the credit cost varying by job complexity, demand and location, and no flat annual fee.

Treat both as unverified until you see your own quote. Angi's filing says pros pay fees for consumer matches regardless of whether they do the work. See also our [Thumbtack lead cost guide](/blog/thumbtack-leads-cost-what-you-pay-per-job-2026).

## Which Should You Start With?

- **Home repair or renovation trade:** Angi's focus on home services fits, if you can manage a visible profile.
- **Events, personal care, fitness or mixed services:** Thumbtack's wider category list makes it the first one to check.

## FAQ

### Is Angi or Thumbtack better for home services?

Angi focuses on home services, but a better fit depends on your trade and area. Test both and compare cost per signed job.

### Is Thumbtack only for home services?

No. The 2024 source says Thumbtack covers 13 industries, including events, personal care and fitness.

### Is Angi owned by HomeAdvisor?

They share a parent. Angi Inc. says it operates Angi, Angie's List, HomeAdvisor and Handy.

### Do I pay if I do not win the job?

Generally yes on pay-per-lead products. Angi Inc.'s filing says pros pay fees for matches regardless of whether they do the work.
