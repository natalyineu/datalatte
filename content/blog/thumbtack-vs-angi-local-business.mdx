Thumbtack and Angi differ most in what they cover and how leads reach you. A September 2024 comparison by 7ten Marketing ([secondary](https://7ten.marketing/angie-vs-thumbtack/)) says Angi lists about 720 home improvement and repair services, while Thumbtack covers more than 900 services across 13 industries. Angi works more like a directory, where customers search and then contact you. Thumbtack matches pros with requests. Choose by trade fit first, then test cost per signed job over 30 days.

## How Do the Two Platforms Find You Customers?

7ten describes Angi as a directory. Businesses list their services, customers search for providers, and then they contact the one that suits them. A complete profile and a visible listing matter most in this model.

Thumbtack works as a matching system. Pros build a profile and specify the jobs they will take, and Thumbtack sends them requests that fit their skills. According to the same source, professionals receive direct requests instead of waiting to be found. The source was written in 2024, so confirm how each product works today before you commit.

## Which Platform Fits Your Trade?

Angi's focus is home improvement, repair and maintenance. If your business is a roofer, plumber, electrician or landscaper, the listing categories match your work. A 2024 analysis also reports that Angi pre-screens and background-checks professionals, which can help homeowners who compare providers carefully feel more confident.

Thumbtack covers events, personal care, fitness and business services alongside home work. A wedding DJ, personal trainer or mobile hairdresser has a category there that Angi does not list. For mixed-service businesses, the wider range can reach customers that a home-only directory would miss.

## How Does Competition Differ?

7ten says Angi tends to attract established professionals with years of experience and strong reputations. That can support premium rates, but competition for the top listings can be fierce, and standing out may take ad spend.

Thumbtack's marketplace includes newer businesses as well as veterans. The same source notes this can create price competition in crowded categories. These are observations, not measured data, so treat them as questions to test: how many other pros does each lead reach, and how quickly must you reply to win the job?

## How Does Pricing Differ at a Glance?

The 2024 source says Angi is free to join, but businesses pay fees to advertise and become more visible. Pricing is negotiated around advertising needs, with no flat annual fee for basic functions. It says Thumbtack uses credits that pros buy to contact leads, and the credit cost varies with job complexity, demand and location.

Treat both descriptions as unverified until you see your own quote. Because neither source gives a per-lead price, the only reliable way to compare is to run a test. ## How Do You Test Both Fairly?

The table below is a worked example. Every input is an **assumption** used to show the maths, not real pricing or results. Replace them with your own figures.

| Input or result | Platform A (assumption) | Platform B (assumption) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Test budget, 30 days | $600 | $600 |
| Average cost per lead | $40 | $60 |
| Leads bought (budget divided by lead cost) | 15 | 10 |
| Close rate | 10% | 20% |
| Jobs won (leads times close rate) | 1.5 | 2 |
| Cost per signed job (budget divided by jobs won) | $400 | $300 |

In this illustration, the platform with the pricier lead wins, because it closes more often and lowers the cost per signed job. Your own numbers may point the other way.

Log each lead's price, reply time, outcome and average job value for the full 30 days. Then compare cost per signed job rather than cost per lead, since a cheap lead that never converts costs more than it appears.

## Which Should You Start With?

- Home repair or renovation trade: start with Angi if you can maintain a detailed, visible profile.
- Events, personal care, fitness or mixed services: start with Thumbtack, since its category list is wider.
- Unsure: run both for 30 days on a small budget, and log every result.

## FAQ

### Is Angi or Thumbtack better for home services?

Angi focuses on home improvement and repair, so it is the more specialised fit for those trades. The better choice still depends on your area and reply speed, so test both and compare cost per signed job.

### Is Thumbtack only for home services?

No. The 2024 source says Thumbtack covers 13 industries, including events, personal care, fitness and business services.

### Is Thumbtack a large company?

A 2024 analysis reports Thumbtack annual revenue of US$750M as of June 2024, with a valuation of US$3.2B in its last funding round. Revenue size does not tell you lead quality, so judge the platform by your own results.

### Do I pay if I do not win the job?

It depends on the product, and neither 2024 source gives a clear per-lead rule. Thumbtack uses pay-as-you-go credits to contact leads, so you pay to reach a customer whether or not the job closes. Check the exact billing terms before you buy.
