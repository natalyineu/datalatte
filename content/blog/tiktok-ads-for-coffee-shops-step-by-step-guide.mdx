To run TikTok ads for a coffee shop, create a TikTok Ads Manager account, make a short, real-looking video of your drinks or baristas, target a tight radius around your shop, and run a small test with a trackable offer, such as a promo code or a "show this video" deal. Start with in-feed ads, not expensive takeover formats, and judge success by redeemed offers, not views.

TikTok users skew younger than other platforms, which can suit a café, but local targeting and costs vary by market, so treat the first campaign as an experiment.

---

## Why Coffee Shops Should Consider TikTok Ads

TikTok is not only for trends. For a café it offers:

* **Short, visual video** that suits latte art, baristas and new menu items
* **Location targeting** so you can focus on people near your shop
* **Flexible budgets** so you can test cheaply before committing

It will not suit everyone. If your customers are mostly older commuters, Google and your [Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile) will likely do more for you first. See our broader take in [is TikTok advertising worth it for local businesses](/blog/is-tiktok-advertising-worth-it-for-local-businesses-2026).

## Step 1: Set Up TikTok Business Tools

1. Create a TikTok account for your shop, using your business name and a clear photo or logo.
2. Switch to a business account in the settings, and write a bio that says what you are and where: "Specialty coffee and pastries in [Neighborhood]. Open daily 7 to 3."
3. Create a TikTok Ads Manager account at ads.tiktok.com and complete the business verification and billing steps requested.

Post a handful of organic videos first, so people who check your profile after seeing an ad find something real.

---

## Step 2: Choose the Right Ad Format

* **In-feed video ads** appear between regular videos. This is the best fit for most coffee shops: show a drink being made, a new menu item or your space.
* **Spark Ads** promote an organic post you already published, which keeps comments and likes attached and often feels more native.
* **Brand Takeover and TopView** are premium full-screen placements that are usually priced well above a small café's budget. Check current requirements and costs before considering them.

---

## Step 3: Set Up Your Campaign and Targeting

### Pick an objective

TikTok offers objectives such as Reach, Traffic, Video Views and Conversions (the exact list changes). For a café, a practical approach is:

| Goal | Typical objective |
|---|---|
| Let more locals know you exist | Reach or Video Views |
| Send people to your menu or order page | Traffic |
| Drive trackable online orders or bookings | Conversions, if you have the TikTok pixel set up |

### Choose your audience

* **Location:** target a radius around your shop. Smaller radii (a few miles) usually make more sense for a walk-in business than a large one. Check which location options your account offers.
* **Age and gender:** match your actual customers rather than assuming. Look at your POS or loyalty data.
* **Interests and behavior:** coffee, cafés, baking, study or remote-work interests are common starting points.
* **Custom audiences:** if you have an email list or website visitors with the TikTok pixel installed, you can reach them or build lookalike audiences.

## Step 4: Create Compelling Ad Content

Your ad competes with entertainment, so it needs to feel native.

* **Show, don't tell:** latte art, steaming milk, fresh pastries, the first sip.
* **Hook in the first two seconds:** start with the action, not your logo.
* **Use captions:** many people watch without sound.
* **Add a specific call to action:** "Show this video for a free cookie with any drink."
* **Film vertically** with natural light and clear audio. Phone footage is fine if it is steady and well-lit.

Use TikTok's Commercial Music Library for audio you are allowed to use in ads. Trending sounds from the general library may not be licensed for business use. ## Step 5: Set Your Budget and Schedule

TikTok charges on a CPM, CPC or optimized basis depending on your objective. Minimum budgets exist at the campaign and ad-group level, and the exact amounts change, so check what your account requires.

* **Test small and short:** a modest daily budget for one to two weeks is enough to learn whether the creative and offer work.
* **Test one thing at a time:** run two or three video variations against the same audience.
* **Schedule around demand:** morning and lunch for a commuter café, afternoons for a study spot.
* **Increase only after you see redemptions,** not just views.

For cost context, see [how much TikTok ads cost](/blog/how-much-do-tiktok-ads-cost-real-2026-pricing-benchmarks).

## Step 6: Monitor and Optimize

Check TikTok Ads Manager regularly:

* **Cost per result** (per click, per redemption, per order)
* **Click-through rate** and **video completion**
* **Comments:** common questions point to missing information in your ad

Pause ads that spend without results and put the budget behind the ones that perform. Refresh creative every few weeks, because audiences tire of the same video.

**Track real visits:** use a unique promo code, a "show this video" offer, or ask new customers how they found you. These are imperfect but much better than views.

## FAQ

**Q: I don't want my shop to look like a TikTok video. Will these ads seem unprofessional?**
A: Native-style video is not the same as low-quality video. Use good natural light, steady framing and clear audio, and show real baristas and drinks. Over-polished commercials often perform worse than authentic clips.

**Q: How do I know if people came in because of my ad?**
A: Use a unique code or offer that staff can record in the POS, and ask new customers how they found you. Compare weekday traffic during the test to your usual pattern.

**Q: My customers are older. Is TikTok worth it?**
A: Possibly, if you target the age range of your customers and focus on offers they care about. Test cautiously, and consider Google and Facebook first if your customer base is older.

**Q: Can I just boost organic posts instead of creating a campaign?**
A: Promoting a post (Spark Ads) can work well, but a full campaign in Ads Manager offers more control over targeting, objectives and tracking.

**Q: What if someone leaves a negative comment on my ad?**
A: Don't delete it unless it is spam or abusive. Reply politely, offer to fix the problem, and let other viewers see how you handle it.

**Q: I tried TikTok ads before and got no results. What should I do differently?**
A: Narrow your radius, add a specific offer, film something real rather than a slideshow, and track redemptions. If it still fails, check whether the issue is your hours, pricing or location rather than the ads.
