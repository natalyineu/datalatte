TikTok ads for a coffee shop are worth testing only if you can afford the minimum and measure walk-ins. TikTok's [budget help page](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget) says a daily ad group budget must exceed $20 and a daily campaign budget must exceed $50. A 14-day test at the ad group minimum therefore costs just over $280.

The question is whether that test can earn its money back, and the math below shows when it can.

This guide is for café owners in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. TikTok lists its minimums in US dollars and does not say how they change in other currencies, so check your own Ads Manager. Everything here is a plan for one disciplined test: one offer, one video, one tracked outcome.

If your regulars are mostly commuters over 50, a strong [Google Business Profile](/blog/coffee-shop-google-business-profile-optimization) and Google Ads will likely do more than TikTok. If they are students and remote workers, TikTok is a fair test.

## How Do You Set Up TikTok Ads for a Coffee Shop?

Three steps come before any spend.

1. Create a TikTok account for the shop with your name, logo and a bio that says what you are and where.
2. Open a TikTok Ads Manager account and finish the business and billing steps it asks for.
3. Post three to five real organic videos first, so people who tap your profile after an ad find a living account.

Then pick the format. TikTok's [Spark Ads page](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/spark-ads) says you can publish ads using your own account's posts, and that engagement from the boosted video is attributed to the organic post.

The same page says the ad's display name and text match the chosen post and cannot be edited during ad creation, though another section mentions editing text for a push. Because the page is inconsistent, finalise the caption before boosting.

## Can You Target a Radius Around Your Café?

The official location targeting page does not mention radius targeting. TikTok's [location targeting page](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/location-targeting?lang=en) lists these levels: city, country or region, county, department, district, DMA regions, province, state, urban district and zip code. For Canada it says to use only the three-character postcode prefix. It adds that targeting finds people "located in or regularly in the selected location."

In practice:

- Choose the zip or postal codes within walking or short driving distance, not a whole city.
- Check which options your account shows, since availability varies by market.
- Expect waste. A zip code is a blunt tool.

For the audience, start broad within those locations and let the video do the filtering.

## What Should the Video Look Like?

Your ad competes with entertainment, so it has to feel like something a person would choose to watch.

- **Open on the action.** Steam wand, pour, first sip. Not your logo.
- **Show one offer.** "Show this video for a free cookie with any drink" gives you something to count.
- **Add captions**, since many people watch without sound.
- **Film vertically** in natural light with steady phone footage.
- **Use cleared audio only.** TikTok's [Commercial Music Library page](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/commercial-music-library) says businesses cannot use the general music library for commercial usage, and describes the Commercial Music Library as a pre-cleared library of 1 million songs.

A trending sound outside that library is not safe for an ad. For ideas, see our [Reels ideas for coffee shops](/blog/coffee-shop-instagram-reels-ideas-that-get-views) and the [organic TikTok guide for coffee shops](/blog/coffee-shop-tiktok-marketing-guide).

## How Much Should a TikTok Test Budget Be?

Per the [TikTok budget page](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget):

| Budget type | Campaign level | Ad group level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | Must exceed $50 | Must exceed $20 |
| Lifetime | Must exceed $50 | Total days (including past days) x the minimum daily budget |

A 31-day lifetime run therefore needs at least $620 at ad group level. For a first test, use one campaign, one ad group and two or three videos for 14 days at just over $20 a day: roughly $280 to $300.

<Callout type="warning" title="Do Not Judge By Views">
A video can get 50,000 views and zero redemptions. Decide before launch what counts: a redeemed code, a till tally of "saw it on TikTok", or new loyalty-card sign-ups.
</Callout>

## How Do You Track Real Visits From TikTok?

TikTok reports impressions and clicks. A café needs walk-ins, so use one or more of these:

- **A unique offer** that staff ring in as its own POS button, such as "TikTok cookie".
- **A question at the till**, "Where did you hear about us?", with a tally sheet.
- **A before and after** comparison of new-customer counts, accepting that weather and school terms blur it.

This is imperfect but far better than views. If you take online orders, use a TikTok pixel and a conversion objective to count orders directly.

## What Does a $280 Test Return? A Worked Example

All rows marked (assumption) are illustrative, not TikTok or café benchmarks. The Base CPM and click-through rate come from Lebesgue's [TikTok ads benchmarks](https://lebesgue.io/tiktok-ads/tiktok-ads-benchmarks), a second-hand vendor page built on ecommerce data (a $4.80 average CPM and a 0.61% CTR for conversion campaigns). Local café numbers will differ.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Test budget | $280 | $280 | $280 |
| Cost per 1,000 views | $8.00 (assumption) | $4.80 (Lebesgue) | $4.00 (assumption) |
| Impressions | 35,000 | 58,333 | 70,000 |
| Click-through rate | 0.4% (assumption) | 0.61% (Lebesgue) | 1.0% (assumption) |
| Clicks | 140 | 356 | 700 |
| Click to redeemed offer | 2% (assumption) | 5% (assumption) | 8% (assumption) |
| Redemptions | 2.8 | 17.8 | 56 |
| Cost per redemption | $100 | $15.73 | $5.00 |
| Gross profit per redeemer (assumption) | $16.80 | $16.80 | $16.80 |
| Gross profit returned per $1 of ads | $0.17 | $1.07 | $3.36 |

The $16.80 assumes 4 visits in 90 days at a $6 ticket and 70% gross margin (4 x $6 x 0.70). Base roughly breaks even, Tough loses most of the budget, and Strong is a result not to plan around. Some redeemers are existing regulars, so true new-customer return is lower.

If your test lands below $16.80 per redemption and the redeemers are mostly new faces, scale slowly. If it lands above, change the video or offer before spending more.

## Where Does TikTok Fit Next to Other Channels?

TikTok should sit beside the channels that catch people already searching: an optimised Business Profile, Google Ads for "coffee near me", and email to bring new customers back. ## Do This Today

1. Pull age groups from your loyalty data, or tally the next 100 customers by eye.
2. Choose one offer you can ring in as its own POS button and brief staff on the "where did you hear about us?" question.
3. Film three vertical 15 to 25 second videos using Commercial Music Library audio.
4. Set up one campaign, one ad group, nearby zip codes and a daily budget just over $20 for 14 days.
5. On day 14, divide spend by redemptions and compare it with your break-even figure.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I target a one-mile radius on TikTok?**
A: TikTok's location targeting page lists city, county, DMA, state and zip or postal code levels and does not mention a radius. Check your own account and use nearby zip codes if no radius appears.

**Q: What is the minimum a TikTok ad test costs?**
A: A daily budget must exceed $20 at ad group level and $50 at campaign level. A 14-day test at about $20 a day is roughly $280 to $300.

**Q: My customers are older. Is TikTok worth it?**
A: Pew reports that roughly half of US adults aged 18 to 29 use TikTok daily, against 5% of those 65 and older. Test it only if a meaningful part of your customer base is younger.

**Q: Can I boost a post I already made?**
A: Yes, that is a Spark Ad. Engagement from boosting is credited to your organic post, and TikTok says the text cannot be edited during ad creation, so finalise the caption first.

**Q: Can I use any trending song in an ad?**
A: No. TikTok says businesses cannot use the general music library commercially and should use the Commercial Music Library.
