## How Do European Nail Salons Get New Clients?

Salons use four routes: being found on Google Maps, Instagram, a booking marketplace, and word of mouth that is captured in a client list. The first two cost time rather than commission. Marketplaces cost a share of the first booking but bring people who were not searching for you by name.

Our [Google Maps ranking guide](/blog/how-to-rank-in-google-maps-local-pack) covers the free route. Google's performance report counts calls, directions, website clicks, messages and bookings from your profile, so you can see what the listing earns.

## What Do Booking Marketplaces Charge?

Treatwell's UK partner page lists 35% plus VAT on a client's first marketplace booking, charged once, with no minimum. A client counts as new if you have not served them through Treatwell in the last 365 days. Repeat clients and your own channels, including the Book Now button on Google and social profiles, are 0%. Online prepayments carry 2.5% plus VAT.

Fresha lists a 20% one-time commission on new marketplace clients with a $6 minimum, and free direct, Facebook and Instagram bookings. Its page shows USD and no UK or EU price. A Fresha Independent plan is $19.95 a month. Check your own country's page before deciding.

## Which Is Cheaper: Marketplace or Your Own Booking Link?

For clients you already have, your own link costs nothing in commission. For strangers, a marketplace is paid acquisition with a fixed price, so compare it with what you would pay for ads. The decision depends on repeat rate: a new client who returns pays commission once.

This worked example uses **assumptions**, not market data. A salon charges a first-visit price of £40, a typical client returns four times a year, and the visit price stays at £40.

| | Treatwell | Own channel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First booking price (assumption) | £40 | £40 |
| Commission on first booking | 35% = £14 plus VAT | £0 |
| Repeat bookings in a year (assumption) | 4 x £40 = £160 | 4 x £40 = £160 |
| Commission on repeats | £0 | £0 |
| Year revenue from one client | £200 | £200 |
| Marketplace cost as share of year revenue | 7% plus VAT | 0% |

If the client stays, the commission is small against a year of visits. If most new clients do not return, it is a large cost. Track how many marketplace clients rebook, and remember the 365-day rule can make a lapsed client count as new again.

## How Should You Use Instagram?

Use it to show finished work, prices and availability, with a link to your booking page. Fresha's page lists Instagram bookings as free for the salon. DataReportal reports that 55.1% of online adults aged 16 and over use Instagram each month (GWI, global, not Europe-specific).

## Can You Send Marketing Texts and Emails?

In the UK, PECR generally requires prior consent for marketing emails and texts. The ICO allows a soft opt-in for existing customers who bought a similar service and were offered a simple opt-out when their details were first collected. Every message must give a way to unsubscribe and must not hide who sent it.

EU member states apply their own national versions, so check your country's regulator. For paid search, see our [nail salon keyword guide](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-nail-salons-in-2026).

## FAQ

### How much does Treatwell charge a nail salon?

The UK partner page lists 35% plus VAT on a new client's first marketplace booking, and 0% on repeat clients and your own channels. Software plans start at £9.99 a month excluding VAT, and online prepayments cost 2.5% plus VAT.

### Is Fresha cheaper than Treatwell?

Fresha lists 20% on new marketplace clients with a $6 minimum, but its page shows USD pricing and no European rates. Compare the current local pages and your own repeat rate.

### Do I need consent to send marketing texts in the UK?

Generally yes. The ICO allows a soft opt-in for existing customers who bought a similar service and were offered an opt-out. Other EU countries have their own rules.

### Is Instagram or Google better for nail salons?

Google captures people searching now, while Instagram shows your work. Most salons need both, and neither charges commission.

### What should I track?

Track new clients per channel and how many rebook. Your Business Profile reports calls, directions, website clicks and bookings.
