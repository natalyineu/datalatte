A Tunisian small business should start with Facebook, which reaches 7.80 million people (63.1% of the population), and test TikTok, which reaches 67.6% of adults aged 18 and over, according to DataReportal's Digital 2026 report. Instagram is smaller at 4.05 million (32.8%). Add a Google Business Profile so people searching nearby can find you.

No source checked publishes Tunisian ad prices, so there are no CPC figures. The budget example is an assumption.

## How Big Is the Tunisian Online Market?

DataReportal counts 12.4 million people, 10.4 million internet users (84.3%) and 15.5 million mobile connections (125%). Social media user identities total 7.83 million, 63.3% of the population, up 9.5%. Internet use is high, so most customers can be reached online.

Mobile connections exceed the population because one person can hold several. For North African comparisons, see our guides to [Morocco](/blog/local-marketing-morocco-small-business-2026) and [Egypt](/blog/local-marketing-egypt-small-business-2026).

## Which Platforms Reach Tunisians?

| Platform | Ad reach | Share | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 7.80 million | 63.1% of population | up 9.1% |
| TikTok (aged 18+) | 6.00 million | 67.6% of adults | up 6.7% |
| Instagram | 4.05 million | 32.8% of population | up 15.7% |
| LinkedIn (members) | 2.70 million | 21.8% of population | up 12.5% |

Facebook is the widest paid channel by headcount, but TikTok's share of adults is higher than Facebook's share of the population. Instagram grew fastest. DataReportal notes that platform corrections can distort year-on-year changes and that ad reach is not active users.

## Which Channel Should a Tunisian Business Test First?

Choose by customer. Facebook suits neighbourhood businesses that want phone calls and messages. TikTok suits salons, cafes and gyms whose work looks good on video. Instagram suits a younger, image-led audience.

Test one channel for 30 days and then another, so you can compare. Our [local business Meta ads guide](/blog/local-business-meta-ads) covers setup, and our [TikTok guide for salons](/blog/tiktok-marketing-beauty-salons-europe) shows content ideas.

## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Tunisia?

Google says local ranking uses relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Reviews and ratings can help prominence. Add photos, hours and a phone number, and reply to reviews. See the [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## Worked Example: A Salon Test Split

All numbers are **assumptions**.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget, 30 days | $100 on Facebook, $100 on TikTok |
| New clients, Facebook | 7 |
| New clients, TikTok | 4 |
| Cost per new client | $14 and $25 |
| Profit per visit | $12 |

Facebook costs $14 per new client against $12 profit on the first visit, close to break-even on one visit and positive once clients return. TikTok costs $25 per client and needs about two return visits to cover itself. Judge by repeat visits, not by the first booking alone.
