A local sporting goods store should start with a small Google Ads Search budget aimed at its own area, add local inventory so shoppers see what is on the shelf, and track calls and direction taps. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put the average click at $2.77 for Sports and Recreation and $4.14 for Shopping, Collectibles and Gifts. Neither category is a sporting goods store exactly, so use them as a range.

This guide is for independent stores in the US, UK, Canada and Australia that compete with big chains and online sellers. Every number has a source at the end, and examples marked as assumptions are illustrations. LocaliQ does not publish a sporting goods retail row.

## How Much Does Google Ads Cost for a Sporting Goods Store?

LocaliQ's 2026 data gives an all-industry average of $5.42 per click, a 6.64% click-through rate and $66.69 per lead. Sports and Recreation averages $2.77 per click, 8.75% click-through, 7.69% conversion and $44.26 per lead. Shopping, Collectibles and Gifts averages $4.14 per click, 8.28% click-through, 4.01% conversion and $49.40 per lead.

Sports and Recreation is largely about services such as clubs and classes, while Shopping is closer to retail. A store sits between them, so judge your first month against both, then trust your own account data.

## What Should a Store Advertise First?

Pick products people buy at a known time and want today. Seasonal gear, team equipment and repairs such as racket stringing or skate sharpening suit a local store because shoppers check stock and distance first. Avoid competing on a single branded item that chains and marketplaces discount.

Group keywords by theme, such as one campaign for team sports and one for a service. Keep each ad group to a tight set of terms so the ad text can name the product. For account setup, see our [Google Ads setup guide](/blog/how-do-i-set-up-google-ads-for-my-small-business-complete-guide-for-2026).

## Can You Show In-Store Stock on Google?

Yes, in supported countries. Google Merchant Center describes free local listings, where in-store products can appear at no cost across Google, and local inventory ads, which can show store pickup options. Local inventory ads are available in the US, UK, Canada and Australia among others.

Google reports that retailers using local inventory ads alongside Shopping ads saw a 21% increase in store visits and a 9% increase in online conversions for in-store products (Google data, July 2023 to July 2024). That is Google's own figure, not an independent test. Our [Shopping ads guide](/blog/google-shopping-ads-for-small-business) explains the feed.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local and Cut Waste?

Google says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km, and that very small areas may show ads only intermittently or not at all. It recommends "Presence or interest" for Search in general, while "Presence" suits cases where you only want people physically in the area. Add the country or state when you enter a city name.

Then use negative keywords. Google says they do not match close variants, so add plurals and synonyms yourself. Typical blocks for a store include "jobs", "free" and product types you do not stock. Review the Search terms report weekly.

## The Math: What Does $800 a Month Buy?

All inputs are **assumptions** built on the benchmarks above. A conversion is a call or a direction tap, not a sale.

The spread is nearly three to one, which is why a store should not budget from one benchmark. If your average basket is $70 with a 35% gross margin (both assumptions), a $259 cost per sale loses money on the first purchase, while $90 is close to break-even. ## FAQ

### How much should a sporting goods store spend on Google Ads?

There is no standard figure. At LocaliQ's 2026 click costs of $2.77 to $4.14, $800 a month buys roughly 190 to 290 clicks. Start small and raise the budget when you know your cost per sale.

### Are local inventory ads worth it for a small store?

Google reports a 21% rise in store visits for retailers using local inventory ads with Shopping ads. That is Google's data. Free local listings cost nothing and are a low-risk start.

### Which keywords work for a local sporting goods store?

Product plus a local intent, such as a sport and your town, plus services like repairs. Use Keyword Planner for estimates. Google says it suggests keywords and estimates searches and cost.

### Should I use radius targeting?

Yes, if customers drive to you. Radius must be at least 1 km, and small areas may deliver unevenly.

### How do I stop wasted clicks?

Add negative keywords and check the Search terms report weekly. Negatives do not match close variants.
