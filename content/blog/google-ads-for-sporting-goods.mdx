## How Much Should a Sporting Goods Store Budget for Google Ads?

Adwave's channel comparison lists Google Shopping at $500-$2,000 a month, with 2-4 weeks to results and ROAS as the main measure. The same page suggests that stores just starting out, at $500 a month or less, begin with Google Shopping and email. Adwave writes for online sellers, so treat this as a starting point for a store that also sells online, not a local benchmark. Sporting goods is not a row in the table.

Another Adwave guide puts early-stage Shopify stores at $500-$2,500 a month on Google Ads. A chiropractor campaign guide by Mike Ncube recommends a $500 monthly starter budget, raised as performance improves. That is a different niche, but the pattern is the same: start near $500 and increase only when the numbers justify it.

For a parallel look at how Google Ads is set up for local businesses in another market, see the [Germany guide](/blog/local-marketing-germany-small-business-google-ads). Judge the first month by cost per sale, not cost per click, because a cheap click that never becomes a sale still costs money.

## Which Products Should a Store Advertise First?

Pick products that people buy at a known time and want the same day. Seasonal gear and team equipment suit a local store because shoppers check stock and distance first. Avoid bidding on a single branded item that chains discount, since a small store cannot win that auction on price alone.

Seasonality also affects when money should move. Adwave's summer TV page gives a $2,000-$10,000 monthly range for sporting goods and outdoor brands, with May to July as the heavy spend window and family targeting. That is a television budget, not a search budget, so use the timing as a planning signal rather than a Google Ads benchmark.

Group keywords by theme. One ad group per product line keeps the ad text specific, so a search for a racket or a pair of skates meets an ad that names that product. Keep each group to a tight set of terms and review which ones bring calls or direction taps each week.

## What Are Big Chains Running in Your Category?

The vendor Sprites reports 106 Big 5 Sporting Goods ads in the Meta Ad Library as of October 2, 2026, with 97 still running. It states the median ad ran 101 days and the longest was still up at 164 days. The page was updated November 12, 2026, and the vendor read and explained 30 of the 106 ads.

Run length is a weak signal. An ad can stay live for reasons that have nothing to do with return, such as a standing seasonal offer. Still, a store can search the public Meta Ad Library for its own competitors and note which offers keep running. That shows what a chain is willing to repeat, and that is useful input for a small store choosing what to test.

## How Do You Keep Local Reviews Within Google's Rules?

Reviews sit on your Google Business Profile and influence how a store looks in local results. Google states that offering incentives, such as free or discounted goods or services, in exchange for customers to post, change or remove reviews is strictly prohibited. A store can ask every happy customer for a review, but it cannot attach a discount or a free item to that request.

Build a simple routine instead. Ask at the till or after a repair is finished, and send one follow-up with a direct link to the profile. ## What Does an $800 Monthly Budget Buy?

The store needs repeat buyers, a larger basket, a lower cost per click or a higher share of conversions that turn into sales. Replace each assumption with your own figures after the first month. ## FAQ

### How much should a sporting goods store spend on Google Ads?

Adwave lists Google Shopping at $500-$2,000 a month, and suggests $500 a month or less for stores just starting out. Begin at the low end, then raise spend once you know your cost per sale.

### Does Google Shopping need product data?

Yes. Shopping ads are built from product information, so a store needs clean product details before it can run them. Start with a handful of best sellers rather than the full range.

### Can a store offer a discount for reviews?

No. Google states that offering free or discounted goods or services in exchange for customers to post, change or remove reviews is strictly prohibited. Ask for reviews without attaching a reward.

### Which channel should a new store test first?

Adwave suggests Google Shopping and email for stores starting out with $500 a month or less. Test one channel at a time so you can read the results clearly.
