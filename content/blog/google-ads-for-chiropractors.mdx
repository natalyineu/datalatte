## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Chiropractor?

LocaliQ's 2026 table has no chiropractic row, so use two neighbours as a range. Physicians and Surgeons: $4.76 per click, 6.61% click-through, 12.43% conversion rate, $40.04 per lead. Health and Fitness: $6.17 per click, 6.94% conversion rate, $67.36 per lead. The all-industry average is $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead.

Dentists and Dental Services sit higher at $8.00 per click. None of these are chiropractic averages, and a "lead" is whatever the advertiser counted. Your account data after the first month is the real benchmark.

## Which Keywords Should a Clinic Start With?

Start with people who are ready to book: "chiropractor near me", "chiropractor [town]", "back pain chiropractor [town]", "same day chiropractor". Add one ad group per service you actually offer. Keep broad symptom searches in a separate, smaller test.

Google's help page says exact match gives the most control over who sees your ad, and broad match reaches the most searches. For a small budget, begin with phrase and exact match. Our [chiropractor keyword guide](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-chiropractors-in-2026) lists groups to build.

One caveat from Google: Keyword Planner help says very low-volume and some sensitive keywords cannot be discovered or forecast, and that health topics often return no results. Do not rely on its volume numbers alone.

## What Can You Not Do With Health Ads?

Google's personalized advertising policy lists Health as a sensitive interest category. For those ads, Google says your own data segments (remarketing), Customer Match and audience expansion or lookalike segments are not supported. In-market and affinity segments, demographics, life events and location targeting are still allowed.

In practice a clinic cannot retarget website visitors who read about back pain. The page does not say whether chiropractic counts as Health, so check your account for warnings and read the linked Health policy. Do not target people by an assumed condition.

## Should You Use Local Services Ads?

Local Services Ads are pay-per-lead: Google's help says you pay for leads related to your business, when a customer gets in touch from the ad. In March 2023 Google announced that the programme added health care and wellness categories among five new groups.

The announcement does not name chiropractors. Google's help says eligibility is limited by category and location, with a "Check eligibility" link. Use that link before planning around LSA. Ads also need Google's screening and verification before the Google Verified badge is issued.

## How Do You Track Calls and Bookings?

Calls are the main conversion for most clinics. Google says clicks on a call asset cost the same as headline clicks. Numbers must be in E.164 format, and the number must appear in your site code or your domain must be verified. Call reporting uses Google forwarding numbers and can count calls as conversions.

Lead form assets are an alternative: Google requires a privacy policy link, and leads are downloadable for 30 days and stored for 60. Do not collect health details in a form. Ask for name and phone number, and discuss symptoms on the call. See our [Google Ads extensions guide](/blog/google-ads-extensions-guide) for the setup.

## What Should the Landing Page Include?

Send ads to a page about the service searched, not the home page. Show the address, phone number, opening hours, how to book, what the first visit involves and the practitioners' names. Our [chiropractor website guide](/blog/website-design-for-chiropractors) covers the layout.

Reviews help conversion. BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 47% of consumers will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews, so tidy up your [Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) before paying for clicks.

## FAQ

### How much should a chiropractor spend on Google Ads?
No source publishes a standard budget. Clicks in the nearest category average $4.76, so $1,000 buys roughly 210 clicks. Start at an amount you can test with, and scale once cost per new patient is known.

### Can chiropractors use remarketing on Google?
Google's policy says remarketing and Customer Match are not supported for sensitive categories including Health. Check your account and the Health policy for how it applies to your clinic.

### Are Local Services Ads available to chiropractors?
Google added health care categories in 2023 but does not name chiropractors in its announcement. Use the eligibility check in your account and location.

### Which is better, Google Ads or SEO?
They do different jobs. Ads start immediately and stop when you stop paying. Map listing and reviews take longer but cost nothing per click. Many clinics run both.

### How do I know the ads are working?
Count calls, forms and booked first visits, and divide spend by new patients.
