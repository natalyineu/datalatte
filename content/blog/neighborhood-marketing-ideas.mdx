## What Is Neighborhood Marketing?

It is marketing aimed at people within a short distance of your door, using channels they already see: map results, local groups, mail, partner businesses and the street itself. It suits businesses where customers travel a few miles: cafes, salons, gyms, vets, repair and home services.

Start with where those people look for you. Our guides on [Google Business Profile optimization](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-for-local-seo) and [managing online reviews](/blog/managing-online-reviews-small-business) cover the basics, so this guide concentrates on the extra steps.

## Why Do Reviews Matter So Much Locally?

BrightLocal's 2026 Local Consumer Review Survey, covering 1,002 US adults, found 97% read online reviews for local businesses and 41% always do. Google's share of consumers using it for reviews fell from 83% in 2025 to 71% in 2026, though it is still the leading platform. 74% look for reviews written in the last three months, and 32% want reviews from the last two weeks.

So recency counts. A shop with 200 old reviews and none this quarter looks inactive. Ask every customer for a review when the visit is fresh, with the same link and no incentive.

## What Are Nine Neighborhood Marketing Ideas?

1. **Complete your Business Profile.** Hours, categories, photos, and the service area you actually cover.
2. **Collect fresh reviews steadily.** A small QR sign at the counter works.
3. **Claim a free Nextdoor Business Page.** Nextdoor says pages, posts and recommendations are free. Its own page claims 1 in 3 US households are on Nextdoor and 76% of neighbors have been influenced by a recommendation, but those are vendor figures. See our [Nextdoor business page setup guide](/blog/nextdoor-business-page-setup-guide).
4. **Ask happy customers to recommend you on Nextdoor.** Use [Nextdoor recommendations](/blog/nextdoor-recommendations-strategy-local-business).
5. **Mail the streets near you with EDDM.** See the cost section below.
6. **Partner with a neighbor business.** Swap flyers or a joint offer with a business whose customers match yours but who does not compete.
7. **Host a small local event.** A class, tasting or open day, posted as a Google Business Profile event.
8. **Sponsor something small.** A youth team or school fair, in exchange for your name and a link.
9. **Run a referral thank-you.** A card with a thank-you for new customers that existing ones can hand out.

Pick two and run them for a quarter.

## How Much Does Every Door Direct Mail Cost?

USPS's EDDM page says Retail USPS Marketing Mail flats are currently $0.26 per piece, with BMEU flats as low as $0.259. The page does not give an effective date, so check the USPS tool before ordering. EDDM Retail needs at least 200 pieces, a maximum of 5,000 per day per ZIP Code, and no bulk permit.

Postage is not the full cost. Add design and printing, which USPS does not set. You choose routes in the EDDM online tool and address the piece to "Postal Customer". Our guides on [local direct mail](/blog/direct-mail-marketing-local) and [direct mail versus digital ads](/blog/direct-mail-vs-digital-ads-local-business-2026) compare it with other channels.

## Worked Example: A 2,000-Piece Mailer

Inputs are **assumptions**, except the USPS postage price.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Pieces | 2,000 |
| Postage at $0.26 | $520 |
| Printing and design (assumption) | $400 |
| Total cost | $920 |
| Response rate (assumption) | 0.5% |
| Responses | 10 |
| Cost per response | $92 |
| Average first order (assumption) | $60 |
| Return visits in a year (assumption) | 3 |

Ten responders at $60 give $600 on the first order. If each returns three times, that adds $1,800 over the year. At a 0.2% response rate there are 4 responders and the mailer loses money in the first month. No public benchmark gives a reliable response rate for your area, so test one route first, with a code on the piece.

## How Do You Know Which Idea Worked?

Give each channel its own code or link, such as a coupon code for the mailer and a page link for Nextdoor. Add "How did you hear about us?" to the booking form. Count new customers, not clicks.

Review monthly and drop what brings nobody. Keep one note per idea with date, cost and new customers.

## FAQ

### What is the cheapest neighborhood marketing idea?

Asking every customer for a review, and keeping your Google Business Profile complete. Both are free apart from your time.

### Is Nextdoor worth it for a small business?

A Business Page is free according to Nextdoor. Nextdoor's page states no ad prices or minimums, so check Ads Manager. The reach statistics on its page come from Nextdoor itself.

### How much does a neighborhood mailer cost?

USPS lists EDDM flats at about $0.26 per piece, plus your printing and design. A 2,000-piece drop is about $520 postage.

### Can I put flyers on cars or doors?

Local rules differ, so check your city's rules before you do. Mail is the safer route.

### How long before neighborhood marketing works?

Reviews and profile updates can take weeks to matter. Judge mail within a month.
