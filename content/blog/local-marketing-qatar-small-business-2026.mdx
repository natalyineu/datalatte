Qatar's National Planning Council counted 3,214,609 residents at the end of December 2025, and DataReportal puts internet penetration at 99%. That means almost every customer you could reach is already online, so the question for a Doha salon, café, studio or groomer is not whether to advertise digitally but where the next riyal does the most work. This guide uses published figures for Qatar, labels everything that is an assumption, and ends with a break-even formula you can run on your own numbers.

It is written for small local businesses. Dates are given because several of the rules below changed in 2026. Facts were checked on 10 October 2026.

## Who You Are Marketing To

Population counts differ by source, so treat any single number as approximate:

- National Planning Council (NPC), as reported by The Peninsula: 3,214,609 residents at the end of December 2025, up 2.3% on December 2024.
- United Nations figure used by DataReportal in October 2025: 3.13 million.
- World Bank, 2025: 2,972,215.

Two details change real decisions. First, 71.0% of the population is male and 29.0% female, so a women-only service sits in a smaller pool than a city of this size suggests, and you should target it tightly instead of broadly. Second, 99.4% of people live in urban areas, so almost all of your reach sits inside Doha and its neighbours, not in dozens of separate towns.

On wealth, the World Bank lists GDP per capita of about US$72,525 for 2025. High spending power is real, but it does not change the arithmetic of your own funnel, which is why the math section below uses your numbers.

## Which Platforms Reach People (and Which Are Growing)

DataReportal lists advertising reach taken from each platform's own ad tools. It states these are not monthly active users and may not be unique people. Use them to compare channels, not to count customers.

| Platform | Ad reach, late 2025 | Share of population | Change over the year |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 2.40M | 76.6% | +150k (+6.7%) |
| YouTube | 2.31M | 73.8% | +50k (+2.2%) |
| LinkedIn (registered members) | 1.80M | 57.5% | +200k (+12.5%) |
| Instagram | 1.70M | 54.3% | +200k (+13.3%) |
| Snapchat | 1.24M | 39.5% | +203k (+19.6%) |

Three readings follow from the table:

1. **Facebook reaches more people than Instagram in Qatar's ad tools**, 2.40M against 1.70M. Many guides assume Instagram leads everywhere. For a pet groomer or a family-oriented service, do not skip Facebook.
2. **Snapchat is the fastest riser** in this table at +19.6%, and from July to October 2025 it grew 6.5% while Instagram's reach fell 2.9%. That is a short window, so treat it as a signal to test Snapchat with a small budget, not as proof that it beats Instagram.
3. **Snapchat is smaller than Instagram in reach**, 1.24M against 1.70M. Older claims that Snapchat dwarfs everything else are not supported by this data.

DataReportal's TikTok line (2.95 million) is identical to its total social media identities figure, so this article does not use it. Check TikTok's own ad tool for current reach before planning spend.

On cellular connections, GSMA Intelligence data via DataReportal shows 4.75 million connections, equal to 152% of the population, and 99.2% are broadband-capable. In practice nearly everyone searches and scrolls on a phone, so check your booking page on mobile first.

## Google Ads and Search: Language Is the Decision

Qatar is multilingual, and Google changed how language works in September 2026. According to Google's Ads Help page on language targeting, for Search campaigns (and the Search Network part of Performance Max) the campaign-level language setting is being removed, no action is needed, and Search ads will match based on the language of the ads themselves. Ads that match the language of the search term get priority.

What this means for a Doha business:

- Write separate ad groups in Arabic and English, each with its own headlines, instead of relying on a language setting to sort traffic.
- Keep the landing page in the same language as the ad that sends people to it.
- Review the search terms report monthly. Each language will have its own click prices and its own conversion rate, and only your account can tell you which is better. Our [Google Ads service page](/services/google-ads) explains how we set up that split.

For location, Google's location targeting help says the default reaches people who are physically in or regularly visit your chosen area, plus those who have shown interest in it, that radius targeting is available for most campaign types, and that location is a best-effort signal. Set a radius around your premises, then check the report to see which areas actually convert.

There are no verified Qatar click-price benchmarks in the sources used here, so every cost figure below is an assumption.

## Snapchat, Instagram and Facebook: How to Split a Small Budget

Because no reliable public Qatar benchmarks exist for click prices or conversion rates, start with a test, not a belief:

1. Put the first month's budget mostly on Search, because people searching "salon near West Bay" or "dog groomer Doha" already want the service.
2. Add one social channel, Instagram or Facebook, for people who have not searched yet. Use Facebook first if your customers are families.
3. Add Snapchat as a small test, perhaps 10 to 15% of the paid budget (my suggestion, not a platform rule), and compare the cost per booked appointment after four to six weeks.

For all three, local presence still depends on the basics: a complete Business Profile, accurate opening hours and a phone number that answers. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers the profile steps.

## Rules to Check Before You Send or Sell (as of 10 October 2026)

This is a summary of official texts the author opened, not legal advice. Confirm with the ministry or a Qatari lawyer before relying on any of it.

**Marketing messages.** Law No. 13 of 2016 on Personal Data Privacy Protection (issued 3 November 2016; the version used here is the National Cyber Security Agency copy, which says it is unofficial and that the Official Gazette text is authoritative) has a chapter on direct marketing. Article 22 says an electronic communication for direct marketing is forbidden except after obtaining the individual's prior consent. The message must identify its sender, state that it is direct marketing, and include a valid address to ask the sender to stop or to revoke consent. Article 23 sets a penalty of up to QAR 1,000,000 for violating Article 22, and Article 26 lets an individual complain to the competent department. In practice: only message people who opted in, keep a record of when, and honour opt-outs. WhatsApp's own Business Policy asks for the same thing, with opt-in permission confirming the person wants to receive later messages, and respect for opt-out requests made on or off WhatsApp.

**Advertising claims.** In an April 2026 notice, the Ministry of Commerce and Industry cited Law No. 8 of 2008 on Consumer Protection, which bars selling or advertising goods with false or misleading information that may deceive consumers. Do not publish "before and after" or "guaranteed" claims you cannot prove.

**Selling online.** On 17 September 2026 the Ministry announced an E-Commerce Licence under Decision No. 25 of 2026. It lets e-commerce activity be set up and run fully online, is issued for the website designated for the activity, covers 194 approved business activities and carries a QAR 500 issuance fee. The announcement does not say how Instagram-only or WhatsApp-only selling is treated, so ask the Ministry whether your activity needs a licence before you sell through a link in your bio.

## The Math

These rows are assumptions for illustration, not Qatar benchmarks. Replace each with your own data after a month.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget (assumption) | QAR 5,000 | QAR 5,000 | QAR 5,000 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | QAR 8 | QAR 5 | QAR 4 |
| Clicks | 625 | 1,000 | 1,250 |
| Click to enquiry rate (assumption) | 3% | 5% | 7% |
| Enquiries | 19 | 50 | 88 |
| Enquiry to booking rate (assumption) | 30% | 40% | 50% |
| Bookings | 6 | 20 | 44 |
| Ad cost per booking | QAR 889 | QAR 250 | QAR 114 |
| First-visit value (assumption) | QAR 300 | QAR 300 | QAR 300 |
| Revenue minus ad spend | -QAR 3,300 | +QAR 1,000 | +QAR 8,100 |

Counts use unrounded values. Revenue is not profit: staff, product and rent come out of it. A first visit often does not pay back by itself, which is why repeat visits matter.

**Your break-even click price** is the first-visit value times the enquiry rate times the booking rate. At QAR 300, 5% and 40%, that is 300 x 0.05 x 0.40 = QAR 6.00 per click. If your clicks cost more than that on average, the first visit loses money, and you need repeat bookings to justify it.

The biggest lever is usually the booking rate, not the click price. Answer WhatsApp and calls within minutes, and the Base column moves toward the Strong one without touching the ad account.

## Do This Today

1. Write down your three numbers: first-visit value, how many enquiries become bookings, and the most you would pay for one booking. Work out your break-even click price with the formula above.
2. Check your consent list. Delete anyone you cannot show opted in to marketing messages, and add a sender name and a stop option to every campaign (Article 22).
3. Build two Search ad groups, one in Arabic and one in English, each with its own landing page in the same language.
4. Run one social test: Instagram or Facebook for the first month, plus a small Snapchat test, with a separate booking link on each so you can see which one produces appointments.
5. Ask the Ministry of Commerce and Industry whether your online selling needs the e-commerce licence, and remove any claim from your ads that you cannot prove.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Snapchat bigger than Instagram in Qatar?**
A: Not by reach in ad tools. DataReportal's late 2025 figures show Instagram at 1.70M and Snapchat at 1.24M. Snapchat is growing faster, up 19.6% over the year, so it is worth a small test.

**Q: What language should my ads be in?**
A: Run Arabic and English ad groups separately and let the data decide. Google says Search ads match on the language of the ad itself from September 2026, so the ad language you write is the targeting.

**Q: Can I send promotional WhatsApp messages to a purchased list?**
A: No. Article 22 of Law No. 13 of 2016 forbids electronic direct marketing without prior consent, and the penalty can reach QAR 1,000,000. WhatsApp's Business Policy also requires opt-in. See our [WhatsApp Business automation guide](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-local-marketing-automation).

**Q: How much should a small business in Doha spend?**
A: There is no official figure. Use the math section: choose the cost per booking you can afford, divide by your funnel rates to get a click ceiling, and size the budget to buy enough clicks to learn from. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) gives a framework.

**Q: Why do population numbers differ between sources?**
A: They use different methods and dates. The NPC reported 3,214,609 for December 2025, the UN figure used by DataReportal is 3.13 million, and the World Bank lists 2,972,215 for 2025. Quote the source and date whenever you use one.
