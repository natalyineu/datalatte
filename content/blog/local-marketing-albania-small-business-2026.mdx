## How Big Is the Albanian Online Market?

DataReportal gives a population of 2.77 million, 2.45 million internet users (88.4%) and 3.49 million mobile connections (126% of the population). Social media user identities are 1.35 million, 48.8% of the population. Mobile connections exceed people because many hold more than one.

Albania is a small market with high internet use, so a city business reaches a large share of locals online. For a neighbouring Balkan market, see our [Montenegro guide](/blog/local-marketing-montenegro-small-business-2026).

## Which Platforms Reach Albanians?

| Platform | Ad reach | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram | 1.35 million | 48.8% |
| Facebook | 1.30 million | 47.0% |
| TikTok (aged 18+) | 1.23 million | not given |
| LinkedIn (members) | 710 thousand | 25.7% |

Instagram edges Facebook in Albania, which is unusual among the markets in this series. TikTok's tool reports only users aged 18 and over. Meta states that audience size is not a proxy for active users, so use the numbers to compare channels, not to count customers.

## How Much Do Foreign Visitors Matter?

A study by ALTax, citing official INSTAT data, counts 12.466 million non-resident arrivals in 2025, up 6.6% from 11.696 million in 2024. Non-resident overnight stays reached 7.137 million, up 37.9%. The INSTAT release itself returned a server error when checked, so these figures are secondary.

For cafes, restaurants, guesthouses and tour operators in coastal and city areas, visitors can outnumber residents in season. Search ads and a complete Google profile in English help reach them. See our [Turkey guide](/blog/local-marketing-turkey-small-business-2026) for a larger regional market.

## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Albania?

Google says local ranking uses relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Reviews and ratings can help prominence. Complete the categories, add photos, set seasonal hours and reply to reviews. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists the fields to complete.

## Worked Example: A Restaurant Instagram Test

All numbers are **assumptions**.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget, 30 days | $200 |
| Table bookings from the ad | 16 |
| Average spend per booking | $35 |
| Gross margin | 60% |
| Margin per booking | $21 |

Sixteen bookings earn $336 in margin against $200 spend, a $136 gain. If only 8 bookings arrive, margin is $168 and the test loses $32. Ask each guest how they heard about the restaurant and judge the test on that count.
