Small businesses can use AI for website conversion rate optimization (CRO) at no cost through Microsoft Clarity, a free tool whose Copilot feature summarizes session recordings and heatmaps in plain language. AI points you to problems; it does not fix them. The biggest known leaks are well documented: Baymard's average cart abandonment rate is 70.22%, and Google's Core Web Vitals set load targets of 2.5 seconds or less.

This guide shows what Clarity's AI does, what to fix first, and how to estimate whether a change paid off. Only features documented by the vendors are covered.

## What can AI actually do for website CRO?

Microsoft says Clarity is free forever, with no traffic limits, and that it records sessions and builds heatmaps. Its Copilot feature adds generative AI summaries:

- **Session insights:** plain-language takeaways from a recording, so you need not watch every one
- **Grouped session insights:** takeaways across many recordings at once
- **Heatmap insights:** summaries across the heatmaps of one page and the three device types
- **Ad campaign insights:** how visitors behave after clicking an ad

Microsoft warns that generative models can misinterpret or produce incorrect information, so check the AI's claim against two or three recordings before changing a page. Clarity keeps recordings for 30 days, and favorites for up to 9 months. Our [free heatmaps guide](/blog/microsoft-clarity-small-business-free-heatmaps) covers setup.

## What should you fix before testing anything?

Fix known problems before running experiments. Baymard's list of reasons shoppers abandon checkout (excluding "just browsing", which was 42% of US online shoppers) is a ready checklist:

| Reason for abandoning | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Extra costs too high (shipping, tax, fees) | 40% |
| Delivery too slow | 20% |
| Distrust of site with card details | 19% |
| Had to create an account | 18% |
| Checkout too long or complicated | 17% |
| Site errors or crashes | 17% |
| Could not see total cost upfront | 12% |

Baymard's 70.22% average comes from 50 studies of online carts. It applies to online sales, not to a salon booking form, but the same causes (surprise costs, forced accounts, long forms) are worth checking on any form.

## How fast should the page load?

Google's web.dev gives three Core Web Vitals with "good" thresholds: Largest Contentful Paint within 2.5 seconds, Interaction to Next Paint of 200 milliseconds or less, and Cumulative Layout Shift of 0.1 or less, measured at the 75th percentile of page loads. A page passes when it meets all three.

Measure your key pages before spending on a redesign. Our [analytics overview](/blog/local-business-analytics-and-tracking) explains what to track first.

## How do you A/B test now that Google Optimize is gone?

Google's Analytics Help states that Google Optimize and Optimize 360 were no longer available from 30 September 2023. Google said it was working on Google Analytics integrations with AB Tasty, Optimizely and VWO. Pricing for those tools was not checked here, and low-traffic local sites often cannot reach a reliable result anyway.

For a site with a few hundred visitors a month, change one thing at a time, wait several weeks, and compare enquiries from the same period. Track the result in GA4, as described in our [GA4 guide](/blog/google-analytics-4-for-small-business) and [Tag Manager guide](/blog/google-tag-manager-for-local-website-analytics).

## Worked example: a gym's trial-signup form

All figures are **assumptions**, not benchmarks.

| Input | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly visitors to the signup page | 600 | 600 |
| Form conversion | 4% | 5% |
| Trial signups | 24 | 30 |
| Trial-to-member rate | 25% | 25% |
| New members | 6 | 7.5 |
| Membership value (first 6 months) | $300 | $300 |

The change, based on a Clarity recording showing visitors dropping at a long form, cut it from eight fields to four. Extra revenue: 1.5 members × $300 = $450 over six months. A one-point lift looks small, but with only 600 visitors it could be random noise. Keep the shorter form, watch the next two months, and see whether the lift holds. ## FAQ

### Is Microsoft Clarity really free?

Microsoft's FAQ says Clarity is a free service forever with no traffic limits.

### Does AI improve conversion rate by itself?

No. Clarity's AI summarizes behavior. A person still decides what to change, and Microsoft notes the summaries can be wrong.

### What is a good conversion rate?

It depends on the industry and the action measured. Compare your own before-and-after figures. Industry ad benchmarks exist for paid traffic but were not used here.

### How long should I run a test?

No standard figure applies to every site. Low-traffic sites need several weeks to avoid reading noise as a result.

### What happened to Google Optimize?

It ended on 30 September 2023, per Google Analytics Help.
