## How Competitive Is the Architecture Market?

BLS counts 124,600 architect jobs in 2025, projects 4% growth from 2025 to 2035 (about as fast as average), and reports that 14% of architects are self-employed while 74% work in architectural, engineering and related services. That means most architects sit inside firms, and a solo or small practice competes with them for local clients.

BLS does not say how many practices advertise or how clients choose. What you can do is make your own choice criteria obvious: location, project type, process, fee approach and proof of finished work.

## What Should an Architect's Website Show?

A project page for each type of work you want more of, with photos, the site problem, the scope, your role, and the outcome. A client who is deciding between three practices wants to see work similar to theirs. Add a short process page, a form for brief enquiries, and a phone number.

Do not use claims you cannot support, such as awards you have not received or budgets you cannot document. Keep each page to one project type and one clear next step.

## Does an Architect Need a Google Business Profile?

Yes, for local searches such as architects near me. Google's guidelines say your name should reflect your real-world name, descriptions cannot contain links, and service-area businesses should hide the address and keep areas within about two hours' drive. If clients visit your studio, show the address.

Pick the primary category carefully; our [Business Profile categories guide](/blog/google-business-profile-categories-guide) explains how. Add services, project photos and hours. Then follow the [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## How Do Reviews and Referrals Work for Architects?

BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses, 68% only use businesses rated 4 stars or higher, and 47% will not use one with fewer than 20 reviews. That is for local businesses in general, and a practice with a few projects a year will gather reviews more slowly.

Ask each client at handover, with a Google link or QR code, and ask builders and engineers you work with. Google prohibits incentives for reviews (see [Business Profile reviews](/blog/google-business-profile-reviews)). No verified data shows how many architecture projects come from referrals, so record the source of every enquiry. ## Should an Architect Run Paid Ads?

Test, with a cap. There is no architecture row in LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks. Business Services shows $5.87 per click, 6.10% click-through rate, 4.85% conversion rate and $93.69 per lead, but it covers many unrelated businesses. Your keywords may cost more or less.

Use narrow searches tied to project types, such as home extension design in your city, and a landing page that asks for a short brief. Track calls and forms in Google Ads. Home-project platforms are another route; see [Houzz ads for contractors](/blog/houzz-ads-home-services-contractors) for how that model works.

## FAQ

### How do architects get clients?

BLS does not report how clients find architects. Common local sources are search, maps, referrals from builders and past clients, and reviews. Track your own source for each enquiry.

### How much should an architect spend on marketing?

No verified percentage applies. Set a cap from your fee per project and the share of enquiries you win.

### Is Google Ads worth it for a small practice?

It can be if your fee is large, but there is no architecture benchmark. Test a small budget and measure cost per won project.

### Do I need many reviews?

BrightLocal's 2026 survey says 47% of consumers will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews, but that applies to local businesses generally.
