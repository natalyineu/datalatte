## How does a Colorado Springs fitness studio show up in the Google Maps pack?

The map pack is the block of local listings Google shows above regular results. According to the Association of Fitness Studios, it lists relevant local businesses for searches such as "personal training studios near me" ([AFS, secondary](https://member.afsfitness.com/content/how-make-your-fitness-business-stand-out-google)). For a studio, that means your listing competes on relevance and proximity, and a complete profile gives Google more to match against.

Google's own documentation shows that location data is shared across its products. Google Ads location assets pull data directly from Google Maps and Google Business Profile, and corrections made there flow into linked Google Ads accounts ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)). A wrong address or hours figure in your profile therefore causes problems in both organic and paid results.

If you also run paid search, the two channels reinforce each other. The [complete Google Ads guide for fitness studios](/blog/google-ads-for-fitness-studios-complete-2026-guide) explains how to pair a paid campaign with a well-maintained profile.

## Why does your Google Business Profile carry the most weight?

In a BrightLocal survey cited by Ahrefs, 36% of SEOs said a Google Business Profile is the most important ranking factor for the map pack in 2021. That share rose from 23% in 2013 ([Ahrefs, secondary](https://ahrefs.com/seo/local-seo)). The survey is several years old, so treat it as a trend signal rather than a current measurement.

Google describes the Business Profile as a free tool that lets businesses manage their presence across Search and Maps ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)). It displays business hours, phone numbers, addresses, reviews and photos in search results ([AFS, secondary](https://member.afsfitness.com/content/how-make-your-fitness-business-stand-out-google)). Those are the fields a prospective member reads before deciding to book a trial.

For a step-by-step walkthrough of the profile itself, see the [fitness studio Google Business Profile optimization guide](/blog/fitness-studio-google-business-profile-optimization).

## Which profile fields should a studio complete, in what order?

Work through the profile in this sequence. Each step is a plain action you can finish in one sitting.

1. Confirm your primary category matches what you do, such as gym or fitness centre, and add secondary categories for specific formats like yoga or Pilates.
2. Add each class type as a service so customers can find it by name.
3. Write a description that names Colorado Springs and your neighbourhood in a natural sentence, without repeating keywords.
4. Enter your full opening hours, including any early-morning or evening class times.
5. Confirm the address, phone number and website exactly as they appear on your street sign and website.
6. Complete verification in your Google Business Profile dashboard so the listing is attached to your business.
7. Seed the Q&A section with the questions prospects ask most, such as whether there is a free trial.

Keep a single copy of your business name, address and phone number in a notes file. Use that exact text everywhere you publish it.

## How many photos and posts does a profile need, and how long will it take?

Vertical Impression recommends uploading at least 15 authentic photos of your facility, classes and staff ([Vertical Impression, secondary](https://www.verticalimpression.com/guides/how-to-market-health-wellness-business-2026)). Posts follow a different rule. Google shows a post publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live, and Pending or Not approved posts stay hidden ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)).

Here is a worked photo plan. The inputs are assumptions, so replace them with your own counts.

- Photos on the profile today: 6 (assumption)
- Target: 15 photos (Vertical Impression, secondary)
- Photos added per week: 3 (assumption)
- Photos still needed: 15 minus 6 = 9
- Weeks to reach target: 9 divided by 3 = 3 weeks

After each post, check the dashboard to confirm it reads Live. A post stuck in Pending does nothing for your listing.

## Do reviews and ratings decide map pack position?

Google states that reviews appear next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)). That makes every review a visible signal to a prospect, whatever its effect on ranking. For how to generate and manage them, the [AI tools for Google Business Profile optimization](/blog/ai-for-google-business-profile-optimization) guide covers workflow options.

Use it as a target to test, not a proven threshold.

## Should a studio build citations or fix existing ones first?

Citations are listings of your name, address and phone number on other websites. Before you add new ones, audit the listings you already have. A mismatch between directories gives search engines conflicting details about the same business. Fix the errors first, then add listings in directories that fit your studio.

Google's Ads documentation makes the same point from a different angle. Locations linked to an account must be yours, or they may be disapproved ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)). If you operate several studios, you can select up to 10 locations from Google Maps to link with your Ads account when the standard option does not work.

## How do you know local SEO is working?

Measure before you change anything. Record your current position for a short list of five to ten searches, such as "pilates near me" or "gym near me Colorado Springs." Check those searches in a private browser window each week and log the results in a spreadsheet. Track profile activity in your GBP dashboard, including calls, website clicks and direction requests.

Check the live Google Business Profile Help pages for the current list of insights metrics, since the labels change over time. Compare your numbers monthly, not daily. Local rankings move in response to reviews, photos and profile edits that take weeks to show up.

## FAQ

### Is Google Business Profile really free?

Yes. Google describes its Business Profile as a free listing for Search and Maps ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)). Paid products such as Google Ads are separate and optional.

### How many reviews does a Colorado Springs studio need?

No source gives a confirmed threshold. One unsourced claim puts the bar at 50 reviews with a 4.7+ average for top spots ([Accountability Now, secondary](https://accountabilitynow.net/ai-google-business-profile-ranking-for-landscapers/)). Track your own review count and rating against the competitors who rank above you.

### Do posts on my profile appear right away?

Not always. A post appears publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live. Posts marked Pending or Not approved do not show ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)).

### Where should I start if I have a small budget?

Start with the profile checklist above, then the photo and review steps. These cost time rather than money, and they work on the fields Google displays to prospects.
