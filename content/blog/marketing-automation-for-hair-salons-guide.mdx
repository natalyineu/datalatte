For a small hair salon, the first automation worth paying for is the appointment reminder, because a missed slot is lost revenue you cannot get back. The cost side is small and published. Fresha lists its Independent plan at $19.95 per month with 100 automated texts included ([Fresha pricing](https://www.fresha.com/pricing), checked October 2026). The benefit side has to be estimated, and the best independent evidence comes from healthcare, not salons.

## Do text reminders reduce salon no-shows?

No independent study of text reminders in hair salons turned up. Vendor case studies exist but are self-reported, so none are quoted here. The closest rigorous source is a [Cochrane review of text message reminders for healthcare appointments](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD007458_mobile-phone-text-messaging-reminders-attendance-healthcare-appointments). Across seven studies and 5,841 participants it found attendance of 67.8% with no reminder and 78.6% with a text reminder (relative risk 1.14), with moderate-quality evidence.

A clinic is not a salon, so treat this as a direction, not a promise: reminders help, and the effect is meaningful but not magic.

## What should a salon automate first?

1. **Booking confirmation and reminder.** Sent by your booking tool automatically. On Fresha, automated email notifications are free and automated texts are 100 per month free, then $0.02 each.
2. **Cancellation or no-show rules.** Square lists cancellation policy and no-show fees under its Plus plan, not the free one ([Square Appointments pricing](https://squareup.com/us/en/appointments/pricing)). Fresha lists no-show and cancellation fees and upfront payment as payment features but does not publish a separate price.
3. **Win-back message.** A single message to clients who have not rebooked. Fresha marketing texts cost $0.08 each, and marketing emails are 50 free per month then $0.02 each.
4. **Review request.** Send the Google review link or QR code. Google prohibits offering free or discounted services in exchange for reviews ([Google review policy](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)), so never attach a discount.

Skip general email tools until you have a list (see the [email marketing segmentation guide for hair salons](/blog/email-marketing-segmentation-for-hair-salons) once you do). Mailchimp's Free plan is limited to 250 contacts and does not include automations; its paid Standard plan starts at $20 per month and includes up to 200 flows ([Mailchimp pricing](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/)).

## What could reminders recover? Worked example for a 3-chair salon

Every input below is an **assumption** for illustration. Replace them with your own.

- 200 booked appointments a month, average ticket $60.
- 10% no-show rate, so 20 missed appointments, or $1,200 of lost bookings.
- Reminders cut missed appointments by about one third. That is roughly what the Cochrane figures imply (no-attendance falls from 32.2% to 21.4%, a drop of about a third). Salons may differ.
- Result: about 7 appointments recovered, around $400 a month.
- Cost on Fresha as a team of 3: 3 x $14.95 = $44.85 per month, with 100 free texts per team member, so 300 reminder texts a month cost nothing extra.

Even if the true effect is half the assumed one, the recovered revenue still exceeds the software cost. The check is simple: count no-shows for 4 weeks before switching reminders on, then count again for 4 weeks after.

## Where does salon automation go wrong?

- **Messaging people who no longer visit.** Clean the list first and send win-backs only to recent clients.
- **Too many marketing texts.** Marketing texts are priced higher ($0.08 each at Fresha), so each blast has a visible cost.
- **No human fallback.** Keep a manual check for the next day's bookings if a message fails to send.
- **Buying tools you do not need.** If your booking software already sends reminders, do not add a second reminder tool.

<Callout type="tip">Before comparing tools, write down your current no-show count, your average ticket and the number of chairs. Those three numbers decide whether a $20 or a $45 plan pays back.</Callout>

## FAQ

### Is automation worth it for a solo stylist?
Usually yes, if no-shows cost you at least one appointment a month. On Fresha's Independent plan at $19.95 per month, one recovered $60 slot covers the subscription roughly three times over.

### Do I need a separate texting tool?
Not at first. Fresha includes 100 automated texts per month per plan or team member, and Square Appointments lists appointment reminders on its Free plan. Check that your plan covers the number of clients you book.

### Can I charge a no-show fee?
Square lists no-show fees on its Plus plan. Fresha supports no-show and cancellation fees and upfront payments. Tell clients about the fee at booking so it is not a surprise.

### How do I measure whether it worked?
Count no-shows for 4 weeks before and 4 weeks after turning reminders on, and compare recovered appointments with the monthly software cost.
