Quality Score is a 1-10 rating Google gives each keyword to show how your ad and landing page compare with other advertisers. Google's help page states that Quality Score "is not an input in the ad auction", so you cannot raise it directly to get cheaper clicks. What it does is point at the real drivers: expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience. Fix those and ad quality, which Google says "can often lead to lower CPCs", improves.

## What Is Quality Score in Google Ads?

Google describes Quality Score as a diagnostic tool that gives "a sense of how well your ad quality compares to other advertisers." It is assigned at the keyword level on a scale of 1 to 10. The score is based on historical data, specifically impressions for exact searches of that keyword, compared over the last 90 days.

A new keyword with few impressions may show no score at all. That is normal and not a sign of a problem.

## Does Quality Score Affect Your CPC?

Not directly. Google says Quality Score is not an input in the ad auction. The auction uses Ad Rank, which Google says is built from your bid, ad and landing page quality, Ad Rank thresholds, the expected effect of assets such as phone numbers, and the search context. Ad Rank is recalculated for each search.

On cost, Google's Ad Rank page says you often pay less than your maximum bid and that "higher quality ads can often lead to lower CPCs." Google gives no formula for the actual CPC, so any claim that a score of 8 saves exactly some percentage is not from Google.

## What Are the Three Components?

Each component is rated Above average, Average or Below average. Google defines them as:

| Component | Google's definition | Where to fix it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expected CTR | The likelihood that your ad will be clicked when shown | Ad text, assets, keyword choice |
| Ad relevance | How closely your ad matches the intent behind a user's search | Ad groups, headlines |
| Landing page experience | How relevant and useful your landing page is to people who click | The page itself |

Google says an Average or Below average status "may signal room to improve." Its page does not list tactics per component, so the fixes in the table's last column are practical reading of the definitions, not Google rules.

## How Do You See Your Quality Score?

In Google Ads, open Keywords under the Campaigns menu, click the columns icon, expand the Quality score section, and add Quality Score, Landing Page Exp., Exp. CTR and Ad Relevance. Select Apply. Historical versions of the same columns show past reporting periods.

Sort by impressions. A score on a keyword with 20 impressions tells you little, while a Below average rating on your top spending keyword is worth an hour of work.

## How Can a Local Business Improve It?

Work from the component that is rated lowest on your biggest keywords.

- **Ad relevance low:** split broad ad groups so each holds closely related keywords, and put the searched service and the city in the headline.
- **Expected CTR low:** write ads around what a nearby customer wants (price, opening hours, same-day booking) and add call and location assets.
- **Landing page low:** send "dog grooming near me" clicks to a page about grooming with the address, hours, price range and a booking or call button, not to the homepage.

Google also suggests using Keyword Planner to find relevant, higher-volume keywords, because exact-match search volume is what produces a score. Match type does not change how the score is calculated.

## What Is a Realistic CPC to Compare Against?

The 2026 WordStream by LocaliQ benchmarks (updated June 1, 2026) give an all-industry average of $5.42 per click, a 6.64% click-through rate and $66.69 per lead. Category figures differ: Animals and Pets $4.06 per click, Beauty and Personal Care $4.62, Health and Fitness $6.17, Restaurants and Food $2.05.

These are averages across advertisers. If your CPC sits well above your category, check relevance and landing pages first, then bids and location settings.

## FAQ

### What is a good Quality Score?

Google does not publish a target. Scores of 7 to 10 are usually treated as healthy in practice, but that is a rule of thumb, not Google's. Look at the three component ratings rather than the number alone.

### Does a low Quality Score stop my ads from showing?

Google says Quality Score is not used in the auction. Ad quality still feeds into Ad Rank and its thresholds, so weak ads and pages can lose position or not show.

### How long does it take to see a change?

Google does not say. The score uses historical exact-search impressions over a 90-day comparison window, so give changes time and enough impressions before judging.

### Why does my keyword show no Quality Score?

Usually there are too few exact-match impressions. Google suggests finding higher-volume keywords with Keyword Planner so enough data builds up.

### Should I chase a 10/10 score?

No. It is a diagnostic. Focus on conversions and cost per lead, and use the score to find weak ads and landing pages. Our [conversion tracking setup guide](/blog/google-ads-mistakes-hair-salons-make-and-how-to-fix-them) shows how to measure that.
