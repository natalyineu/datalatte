## What Does Google Ads Cost for an Escape Room?

LocaliQ's [search advertising benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/) (last updated June 1, 2026) list no escape room category. Arts & Entertainment is the nearest, and it covers theatres, museums and attractions too, so treat it as a rough range.

| Metric | Arts & Entertainment | All industries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost per click | $1.63 | $5.42 |
| Click-through rate | 12.75% | 6.64% |
| Conversion rate | 5.91% | 8.18% (labelled Google Ads) |
| Cost per lead | $26.84 | $66.69 |

A lead in these benchmarks is whatever the advertiser counted, such as a form or call. It is not a paid booking. Your cost per booked group will be higher, and your own account is the only real benchmark after the first month.

## How Much Should an Escape Room Spend to Start?

Google does not set a minimum or a recommended budget for local businesses, and no source gives a standard figure for escape rooms. Choose a number you can lose while you learn, and set a stop rule in advance.

A workable plan: pick a monthly cap, run it for at least four weeks, and judge the campaign on cost per booking, not clicks. Compare that cost with the profit on a typical booking. ## How Do You Set Up Booking Tracking First?

Set up conversion tracking before spending. Google's Maximize conversions bidding [requires conversion tracking](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7381968), and without it you cannot tell a booking from a click.

Count two actions:

- **Completed online booking.** Track the confirmation page of your booking widget. If the widget sits on another domain, check with the vendor how to pass the conversion back.
- **Phone calls.** Google's [call conversion help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6100664) says you set a minimum call length and every call that lasts at least that long counts as a conversion. Pick a length that matches a real booking enquiry.

Google also says clicks on a [call asset](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453991) cost the same as headline clicks. Add one if groups often phone to ask about availability.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local?

Google's [location targeting help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722043) says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km, and that a very small target may show ads only intermittently or not at all. Google recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting for Search, and suggests "Presence" when you only want people physically in your chosen locations.

Most escape room customers travel a drive from home, and tourists search from out of town. Start with your city and nearby suburbs, then read the location report and trim the areas that bring clicks but no bookings. If tourists matter in your area, test "Presence or interest" separately.

Add your address with a [location asset](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182), which can show the store address and a map, and a tap-to-call button on mobile.

## Which Keywords Should an Escape Room Bid On?

Search terms with clear booking intent are the safest first set. Examples to test, not a promise of volume:

- Escape room near me, escape rooms in your city, and the same with "book"
- Group activity, team building and birthday party searches, if you host them
- Your own business name, which is cheap and protects your listing

Add negative keywords for job seekers ("hiring", "jobs"), "free", "online" or "at home" escape games, and product searches such as board games. Google explains that [negative keywords](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453972) do not match close variants, so add plurals and misspellings yourself. Our [negative keywords guide](/blog/google-ads-negative-keywords-stop-wasting-budget) has a longer starter list.

## What Should the Landing Page Show?

Send ads to a page where a group can book in two taps. Show the room names and player counts, the price per group or per person, open slots, the address and a booking button above the fold. Put the phone number at the top for mobile visitors.

Corporate and birthday searches deserve their own page with group pricing and a request form. Do not send them to the general homepage.

## What Does $600 a Month Return? A Worked Example

Every input below is an **assumption** for illustration. Only the $1.63 click, the 5.91% conversion rate and the $26.84 cost per lead come from LocaliQ, and they describe a broader category.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget (assumption) | $600 | $600 | $600 |
| Cost per click | $2.50 (assumption) | $1.63 (LocaliQ) | $1.63 (LocaliQ) |
| Clicks | 240 | 368 | 368 |

| Cost per booking | $86 | $27 | $21 |
| Average group booking (assumption) | $120 | $120 | $120 |
| Revenue | $840 | $2,640 | $3,480 |

The Base column treats a LocaliQ "conversion" as a paid booking, which is generous because the benchmark counts leads. If only half of your enquiries book, cost per booking doubles to about $54. Compare cost per booking with your margin per group, after staff and room costs, before you scale. Break-even cost per booking equals your profit on one booking.

## Should You Use Automated Bidding?

Start with a conversion goal. Google says [Maximize conversions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7381968) uses its AI to set bids to get the most conversions and spends your budget, and that if you have a specific ROI goal you should use Target CPA. Google's page states no minimum number of conversions, so do not assume a threshold.

With very few bookings in the account, the system has little to learn from. Watch search terms weekly during the first month and keep negatives current.

## When Should You Run Ads?

Escape rooms fill on evenings, weekends and holidays. Check your booking system for the days that stay empty, and consider pausing or lowering spend at times you are already full. Spending on a sold-out Saturday buys nothing.

Look at lead time too. A birthday group often books a week or more ahead, so judge campaigns on a four-week window, not a single weekend. For wider channel ideas, see [marketing for escape rooms](/blog/marketing-for-escape-rooms).

## FAQ

### How much does Google Ads cost for an escape room?

No escape room benchmark is published. LocaliQ's 2026 data lists $1.63 per click for Arts & Entertainment against $5.42 across all industries. Your cost depends on competition in your city and on your keywords.

### What conversion rate should an escape room expect?

LocaliQ lists 5.91% for Arts & Entertainment, but that category is wider than escape rooms and counts leads, not paid bookings. Use it as a ceiling to compare against, not a target.

### Do I need phone call tracking?

If groups call to book, yes. Google lets you set the minimum call length that counts as a conversion, so short wrong-number calls do not inflate results.

### Is Google Ads better than social ads for escape rooms?

Search ads reach people already looking for an escape room, while social ads reach people who were not searching. No source compares the two for escape rooms, so test search first and add social for events and promotions.

### How long before I know if it works?

Judge on at least four weeks, because groups often book days ahead. Use cost per booking, not clicks.
