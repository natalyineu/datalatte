DOOH (digital out-of-home) advertising is video or image ads shown on digital screens in public places: roadside billboards, bus shelters, transit stations, malls, gyms and taxis. You buy a share of screen time for a place, a time of day and an audience. OAAA data says DOOH was 36% of the $9.46 billion US out-of-home market in 2025, and programmatic buying was 30% of DOOH.

No US price list exists for small advertisers. The only published minimums found for this guide come from a 2020 UK industry guide: GBP 2,500 to 5,000 for a few weeks. This guide explains the formats, how buying works, how to test cheaply and how to measure. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Is DOOH Advertising?

Out-of-home advertising is, in the IAB UK guide's words, any visual advertising found outside the home, such as posters, bus shelters, airports and train stations. DOOH is the digital version. Screens can change the ad by time of day, location or audience, and one screen can show many advertisers in turn.

OAAA reports that total US out-of-home spend grew 3.6% to $9.46 billion in 2025, and DOOH grew 10.5%. By format, transit grew 9.8%, street furniture 5.6%, billboards 2.3% and place-based screens 0.2%. Multiplying 36% by $9.46 billion gives about $3.4 billion for DOOH, which is our own arithmetic. IAB's 2025 guide cites OAAA for a roughly $3 billion market, so sources round differently.

## Which DOOH Screens Can a Local Business Use?

The IAB's July 2025 measurement guide splits DOOH by how screens behave. Pick the type that matches how your customers move.

| Format | Examples | What the IAB guide says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Static outdoor | Roadside, transit shelters, mall screens | Predictable traffic modeling and more accurate impression measurement |
| Exterior moving | Bus wraps, truck sides | Broad reach and impact, but short dwell time and variable viewability |
| Interior moving | Taxi and transit screens | Longer dwell time, captive audience, attribution easier |
| Retail and venue | Stores, stadiums, gyms | Can link exposure to sales through POS or loyalty data |
| Airports | Terminal screens | High dwell times and captive audiences |

For a shop or clinic, static screens within a few miles of the door are the simplest to reason about. Our coffee shop DOOH guide and [restaurant DOOH guide](/blog/dooh-advertising-for-restaurants) show how this applies to two niches.

## How Do You Buy DOOH: Direct or Programmatic?

Direct buying means contacting a media owner and booking screens for a period. Programmatic means using a platform to buy plays by location, time and audience. The IAB guide says programmatic allows dynamic creative optimization and real-time adjustments, and also advises starting with test campaigns before scaling spend.

Platforms differ on small budgets. ## What Does DOOH Cost?

There is no public US rate card, and CPM figures quoted on third-party sites could not be verified for this guide, so none are given. The IAB UK small business guide (October 2020, pounds) is the only price source opened:

- Many media owners set a minimum order of GBP 2,500 to 5,000 for a burst of a few weeks.
- Roughly GBP 5,000 was described as covering 6 to 8 roadside or portrait digital screens across a town for two weeks.
- Medium budgets of GBP 10,000 to 50,000 and large budgets of GBP 50,000 to 200,000 were described for larger cities.

Those are UK prices from 2020 and are not a quote. Use them as a sign that a meaningful local burst is a four-figure commitment, not a few hundred dollars. A one-hour run on a single screen, the guide notes, would not be seen by enough people.

## How Do You Target and Design the Ad?

The IAB UK guide lists three simple controls: context (rail, retail, roadside, airports), time of day down to the hour, and geography, even for a business 100 meters from a screen. A cafe, for example, can show a breakfast offer only during the weekday commuter rush.

On creative, the guide notes digital ads appear on most screens for about 10 seconds, so the headline must be easy to read fast, with your logo and brand clear. Share the finished file with the media owner, typically at least five working days before the start, though a campaign can start the same day if the ad is ready. For timing around events, see [DOOH event marketing](/blog/dooh-event-marketing-local-business).

## How Do You Measure DOOH?

The IAB guide defines the impression types you will see in reports. Opportunity to See (OTS) counts everyone passing through a screen's exposure zone, without confirming actual viewing. Likelihood to See (LTS) requires that the person was in the zone while the screen was active and viewable. Ask the seller which one a quoted number uses.

For results, the guide lists POS integration, promo codes, mobile geofencing for store visits, and incrementality testing with synthetic control or matched markets. A small business can use a simpler version: a unique offer or QR code on the ad, and a comparison of visits in the target area against a similar area without ads. See [combining CTV and DOOH](/blog/ctv-dooh-omnichannel-strategy-local-business) for measurement across channels.

## Worked Example: A Two-Week Test

Every number is an **assumption** for arithmetic. A gym wants new trial sign-ups.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $3,000 |
| Screens | About 8 roadside and street screens within 3 miles |
| Creative | One 10-second ad with a promo code |
| Baseline sign-ups, same area, 2 weeks before | 20 |
| Sign-ups during the test | 32 |
| Sign-ups using the promo code | 14 |

The gym saw 12 more sign-ups than the baseline, so $3,000 / 12 = $250 per extra sign-up. If a member is worth less than $250 over their stay, the test loses money. The promo code count of 14 is higher than the 12 extra sign-ups, so some code users would have come anyway. Judge on the baseline comparison, not the code count.

## FAQ

### What does DOOH stand for?
Digital out-of-home. It means ads on digital screens in public places.

### How much does DOOH advertising cost?
No standard US price is published. A 2020 UK guide cites minimum orders of GBP 2,500 to 5,000 for a few weeks. Get written quotes for your market.

### Is DOOH better than a static billboard?
It is more flexible: you can change creative, hours and locations. It is not automatically cheaper or more effective.

### Can a small business use programmatic DOOH?
Some platforms offer self-service with no stated minimum, others set high minimums for managed service. Check the terms before committing.

### How do I know people saw the ad?
Reported impressions are estimates. Compare real visits or sales in the target area with a control area.
