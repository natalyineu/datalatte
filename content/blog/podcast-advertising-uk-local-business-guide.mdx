A UK local business can buy podcast ads through Spotify Ads Manager, which lists a minimum campaign budget of 250 in the advertiser's local currency, or by arranging a sponsorship directly with a show. Roughly a quarter of UK adults listen to podcasts in a typical week, but reach is national, so a single-town business should test it cautiously. Whatever you buy, a host-read ad must be clearly labelled as an ad under UK rules.

This guide covers the UK audience, ways to buy, advertising rules, and a budget example. No source cited here publishes UK podcast CPM rates for small advertisers, so the example uses labelled assumptions.

## How Many People in the UK Listen to Podcasts?

RAJAR's MIDAS survey for Autumn 2025, as reported by Podcasting Today, found that 24% of UK adults listened to podcasts in an average week, with weekly listeners averaging 9 hours. That works out to 127 million podcast hours across the UK. Ofcom publishes its own podcast reach data, but its page returned an access error, so it is not quoted here.

RAJAR's Spring 2026 wave, published 28 May 2026, reports that 56 million adults aged 15 and over, or 96%, listened to audio in a week. That figure covers all audio, not just podcasts. The sources cited here do not give a regional or postcode split, so they cannot tell you how many listeners live near your shop.

A regional business should ask before buying whether the offer can be limited to its area, and should compare podcast with a local channel that is easier to target. ## What UK Advertising Rules Apply?

The ASA says podcast ads fall under the CAP Code, the same non-broadcast rules as social media ads. They must be obviously identifiable as ads. For host-read ads, where a brand pays, the ASA says a specific label is likely needed. Its guidance says disclosure should come before the ad starts.

CAP's 2024 research found the clearest wording states that the segment is a paid ad, such as "this is a paid ad for...", and that "sponsored by..." is also acceptable, judged case by case. In a 2022 ruling on a Huel podcast ad, a page-turning sound and the words "Quick one" were not enough.

The ASA says it will likely hold the advertiser and the podcast creator jointly responsible. This is guidance, not legal advice.

## Which UK Local Businesses Should Try It?

Podcast ads suit businesses that sell to a wide area or have a considered purchase: a gym chain with several sites, a salon group, a destination venue, or an online-plus-shop retailer. A single café with a ten-minute catchment is rarely a fit for a medium that reaches people across the country.

If you serve one town, search ads, Maps and social targeting are usually easier to measure. ## What Does a Test Budget Look Like?

CPM, the cost per thousand ad plays, is not published for UK small advertisers in the sources used here. The table shows how the 250 minimum converts to ad plays at three **assumed** CPMs, to illustrate sensitivity, not to quote market prices.

| Assumed CPM | Ad plays for a 250 budget |
| --- | --- |
| 10 | 25,000 |
| 20 | 12,500 |
| 30 | 8,333 |

Only a small share of those plays will be near your business. Assume, for example, that 10% are in your area: at the middle row that is 1,250 local plays. Ask what outcome you need: calls, a promo-code redemption or a landing-page visit.

Track results with a unique code, a short URL and a "how did you hear about us" question. Run the test for several weeks and judge it on cost per customer, not plays.
