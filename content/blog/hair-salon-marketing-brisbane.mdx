Brisbane hair salon marketing works best when three channels cover the path from search to booking: a complete Google Business Profile for people looking for a salon nearby, Instagram content that shows real work, and a small paid budget that you track against bookings rather than clicks. The plan below starts with the channels that feed bookings directly, then works through a worked AUD budget you can adapt. Mobile matters most. Booksy reports that over 80% of salon bookings happen on mobile devices, so every profile, landing page and ad should let a client book from a phone in a few taps.

## How Do Brisbane Clients Find a Hair Salon Online?

Clients who want a salon usually start with a short search. Trafft lists "spa near me", "hair salon near me" and "nail salon in [city]" as common phrases people type into Google, per [Trafft's salon ad guide](https://trafft.com/salon-ads/). For a Brisbane salon, the same pattern works with neighbourhood names, so a profile and landing page that name Fortitude Valley, New Farm, West End or your own suburb give search engines clear local signals.

Start with the Google Business Profile. List every service with a clear price range, keep opening hours accurate, and upload current photos of your work and your space.

Offers on the profile give people a reason to choose you. [Zeely's salon advertising guide](https://zeely.ai/blog/hair-salon-advertising-ideas/) suggests a new-client offer such as "$20 off first color service", promoted each month. Pick one offer, state its terms plainly, and replace it when it stops pulling bookings.

For paid search, a [Google Ads guide for hair salons](https://www.ciim.in/google-ads-for-hair-salons/) recommends a local targeting radius of about 5 to 10 km around the salon. Treat that as a starting point and narrow it if clicks come from outside your service area. More detail on local ranking is in our [hair salon local SEO guide](/blog/hair-salon-local-seo-guide).

## Which Social Platforms Work for a Brisbane Salon?

Australian usage statistics from February 2023, as cited by [Zipzipe](https://www.zipzipe.com.au/blog/local-marketing/) (secondary), list Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn among the most used platforms in Australia. For most salons, Facebook and Instagram are the practical pair because both support paid ads and visual posts.

Within that mix, Instagram suits transformation content. [Salesforce's local marketing guide](https://www.salesforce.com/marketing/local-marketing/?bc=OTH) notes that Instagram tends to skew slightly older than TikTok, with a growing user base of millennials and Gen Xers. That matters if your clients span several age groups.

Zeely recommends service demo videos of 15 to 30 seconds that show one treatment in action, such as foils, toning and the final reveal, with a short caption and a starting price. [Instagram marketing for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons) covers the posting rhythm in more depth.

Stories suit time-limited offers because they disappear. [Associated Hair Professionals](https://www.associatedhairprofessionals.com/updates/blog-posts/power-social-media-your-hairstyling-brand) describes Instagram and Facebook stories as temporary formats. Zeely's example pairs a story poll with an auto-DM code such as POLL15 for $15 off, which turns viewers into booking leads.

## How Much Should a Brisbane Salon Spend on Ads?

None of the sources used here gives a reliable AUD benchmark for Brisbane, so the right budget comes from your own numbers: average booking value, the share of clicks that become bookings, and how many new clients you need each month. Set a test budget you can run for several weeks, then judge it on cost per booking rather than cost per click.

Targeting matters as much as spend. A [GroomSoft example](https://blog.groomsoft.com/growing-your-grooming-business/) for a grooming business targets people within a 10-mile radius who have shown interest in pet care. That example is illustrative, and its reach figures are projections rather than measured results. The principle still transfers: narrow geography plus interest signals. For a Brisbane salon, that means a radius around the salon and interest in hair services.

## What Does a Worked Budget Look Like for a Brisbane Salon?

The calculation below uses placeholder inputs. Each one is an assumption to replace with your own figures.

Monthly ad budget: AUD 600 (assumption)
Average cost per click: AUD 3.00 (assumption)
Click-to-booking rate: 5% (assumption)
Average booking value: AUD 120 (assumption)

1. Clicks: AUD 600 divided by AUD 3.00 per click gives 200 clicks.
2. Bookings: 200 clicks multiplied by a 5% booking rate gives 10 bookings.
3. Revenue: 10 bookings multiplied by AUD 120 gives AUD 1,200.
4. Return: AUD 1,200 divided by AUD 600 gives 2.0 times ad spend, before product, staff and card costs.

Now test one change. Suppose a landing page with a clear booking button and a 10% off incentive, the kind of QR code offer Zeely uses as an example, lifts the booking rate to an assumed 8%. The 200 clicks then produce 16 bookings. Each booking brings in AUD 108 after the discount, so revenue is AUD 1,728, or about 2.9 times spend.

The discount costs AUD 12 per booking (assumption: 10% of AUD 120). Run both versions side by side and keep the one that earns more profit per booking, not just more bookings.

## How Do You Win New Residents Who Have No Salon Yet?

People who have recently moved to a city often have no established salon, which makes a clear new-client offer useful. A first-visit discount, such as the "$20 off first color service" in Zeely's example, gives a new resident a reason to try you. Put the offer on your Google Business Profile, on a landing page, and in the first message after a booking.

Follow-up decides whether a first visit becomes a regular. ## FAQ

### How much should a Brisbane hair salon spend on Google Ads?

No sourced AUD benchmark applies here. Start with a test budget your margin can cover, track cost per booking, and adjust from your own numbers, using the worked example above as a template.

### Do most salon bookings happen on phones?

Booksy reports that over 80% of salon bookings happen on mobile devices. Check that your booking button, landing pages and ads work well on a phone before adding spend.

### Should Instagram be the main channel for a salon?

Instagram suits transformation content, but Facebook is also among the most used platforms in Australia according to 2023 statistics. Use the channel where your clients already spend time, and test both before committing.

### What radius should local ads target?

A Google Ads guide for hair salons suggests about 5 to 10 km around the salon. Adjust that once you can see where your bookings actually come from.
