## How Does ZIP Code Targeting Work for Tampa Bay?

Disney lists zip code among its audience options, so you can choose areas where your customers live instead of buying the whole market. Start with a hand-picked list of ZIP codes around your location, not a county.

Tampa Bay spans several counties and long commutes. A customer who lives 40 minutes away rarely becomes a regular, so a tight area usually beats a wide one for a store, salon or studio. Layer demographics only where they fit the offer, because narrow filters shrink the audience.

## What Should the Creative Be?

## How Do You Measure CTV Results?

CTV builds familiarity more than clicks. Track:

- Branded searches for your business name in Google Search Console and Google Business Profile.
- "How did you hear about us?" answers from new customers.
- Direct website traffic during campaign flights.
- Promo codes or dedicated landing pages tied to the campaign.

Give a campaign at least 60 to 90 days before drawing conclusions. Pairing CTV with Google Ads lets CTV build awareness while search captures people who later look you up.

## What Is a Simple Launch Plan?

1. **Define the area.** List the ZIP codes where your best customers live or work.
2. **Pick one goal.** New customers, a seasonal offer or name recognition, with a single call to action.
3. **Produce one 15-second spot.** Add a 30-second version later.
4. **Choose one or two platforms.** Starting narrow makes results easier to read.
5. **Set up tracking before launch.** A landing page or promo code, plus baseline branded search and Google Business Profile numbers.
6. **Run, review and adjust.** Check delivery weekly and judge results over weeks.

## FAQ

### How much does CTV advertising cost in Tampa?
Two self-serve platforms publish a $500 minimum: Disney Campaign Manager per campaign and Roku Ads Manager as a lifetime budget. Tampa CPMs are not published by those pages, so get a current estimate in the platform before planning.

### Which streaming platform is best for a Tampa small business?
There is no single best. Disney Campaign Manager documents zip code targeting, so it suits a ZIP-level local test. Roku and Tubi are worth checking for targeting detail inside their sign-up flows.

### Can I target specific Tampa ZIP codes?
Disney lists zip code as a targeting option. For other platforms, confirm in your account.

### How do I measure CTV results?
Watch branded search, Google Business Profile views and direction requests, promo codes and customer answers. Expect effects over weeks.

### How long should I run a campaign?
Plan on 60 to 90 days for a core test before judging, or a concentrated burst around a peak season.
