## What do AI social media tools actually do?

They draft captions, suggest posting times, adapt one idea into several formats, sort comments and summarize performance. They do not know your neighborhood, your regulars or your slow Tuesday afternoons. That part still comes from you.

Use AI for the first draft and the logistics. Keep the final wording and customer replies in human hands, because a wrong answer about a booking or a price costs trust.

## What is the best AI social media tool for a small business?

There is no single best tool, but the choice narrows quickly:

- **You post fewer than 12 times a month:** use a free plan. Buffer's free plan or the scheduler built into each platform is usually enough.
- **You manage one business on 3 to 5 channels:** Buffer Essentials or Metricool Starter keep the bill under about $20 a month.
- **You manage several locations or brands:** Sendible Core lists 6 profiles with unlimited users at $35 a month. Metricool's Starter plan lists up to 5 brands for $20.
- **You need a shared inbox and approvals:** this is where Hootsuite and Sprout Social start, at $79 to $99 per user at the entry level.

Test two tools on a free trial with one real task: plan and schedule a full month of posts. If it does not save you hours, do not pay.

## Is a tool worth paying for? A worked example

All inputs are **assumptions**, except the tool prices from the table. Assume your time is worth $30 an hour and a tool saves 6 hours a month.

A $199 seat needs 6.6 hours saved to break even ($199 divided by $30). If you save only 2 hours, worth $60 under the same assumption, anything above $60 a month loses money. Replace the 6 hours and $30 with your own numbers.

## When should you not buy a tool yet?

If you post fewer than 4 times a month, a tool has nothing to automate. Spend the time on a complete [Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile), reviews and a clear offer first. Buy a tool once you have a posting rhythm to speed up.

Do not publish AI-written posts without reading them. Check names, prices, opening hours and claims before you schedule.

<Callout type="warning">Fully automated replies to customers are the riskiest AI feature. Hootsuite lists inbox reply automation from its Professional plan at $199. Test it on a few messages first.</Callout>

## How do you get less generic AI content?

Generic posts come from vague prompts. Give the AI four specifics: your neighborhood, your tone, one real detail (a new seasonal item, a stylist's specialty, an upcoming event) and one call to action. Then rewrite the first and last sentences yourself. Our guide to [ChatGPT prompts for local business marketing](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-local-business-marketing) has examples.

The no-cost route: draft captions in a general AI assistant and schedule with Buffer's free plan or Metricool's free plan. For a single local business, that is often enough.

## Do this today in 45 minutes

1. Count your posts from the last 30 days. Under 4? Skip paid tools for now.
2. Open Buffer's or Metricool's free plan and connect your channels (10 minutes).
3. Ask an AI assistant for 12 caption drafts using the four specifics above (10 minutes).
4. Rewrite the first and last line of each in your voice and schedule them (20 minutes).
5. Put a tracked link or offer code in 3 of them to see which bring a booking.
6. Set a 15-minute review for the end of the month.

## Questions to ask before you pay

- **Which of my platforms are supported?** Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Google Business Profile posts and LinkedIn do not work the same way in every tool. Metricool's free plan, for example, lists all networks except LinkedIn and Twitter.
- **How is pricing counted?** Per user (Hootsuite, Sprout), per channel (Buffer) or per profile bundle (Sendible, Later).
- **Can I cancel easily?** Sendible lists no contract. Check the others before an annual commitment.

## FAQ

### What is the best AI social media management tool for a small business?
On published prices, Buffer and Metricool are the cheapest to start, with free plans. Sendible suits several locations. Hootsuite and Sprout Social fit teams that need a shared inbox and reports. Test two on a free trial.

### Are AI social media management tools worth it?
They can be, if you post regularly and your time is the bottleneck. If you post only occasionally, a free plan plus a general AI assistant is usually enough.

### What is the best AI social media management service?
A service is a person or agency doing it for you, not software. No pricing for one is published in the sources used here, so ask for a written scope and compare at least two. Our [social media management service](/services/social-media) is one option.

### Is there a free AI social media management tool?
Yes. Buffer's free plan covers 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts each, and Metricool's free plan covers 1 brand with 20 posts a month and an AI assistant.

### Can I just use ChatGPT to write posts?
Yes. You get usable drafts at low cost, but you must edit for local details and schedule through a separate tool or each platform's own scheduler.

### How long until I see results?
Track bookings or inquiries from social posts for at least 6 to 8 weeks before judging. This is a practical suggestion, not a measured benchmark.
