DOOH (digital out-of-home) advertising shows your yoga studio's offer on digital screens near homes, offices and shops. It is usually bought by CPM, the price per thousand impressions, and StackAdapt states CPMs range from $2 to $15. No source read publishes a yoga studio sign-up rate or a US minimum budget. Treat DOOH as a small awareness test next to search and your own channels.

This guide covers cost, screen choice, creative limits and how to track intro-offer sign-ups. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## How Much Does DOOH Cost for a Yoga Studio?

StackAdapt describes three pricing models: CPM, a flat rate for a set period, and auction buying, which is common in programmatic DOOH. It says CPMs run from $2 to $15, with big iconic screens higher and smaller screens in busy spots lower. Place, audience, campaign length and holiday periods affect the price.

StackAdapt states no minimum budget. A 2020 UK IAB guide says many media owners set minimum orders of about 2,500 to 5,000 pounds for a few weeks. That is old and British, so use it only as a reason to get a written minimum first.

## Is DOOH a Real Channel or Hype?

The Out of Home Advertising Association of America says US out-of-home ad spend grew 3.6% to $9.46 billion in 2025. DOOH made up 36% of sales, programmatic DOOH was 30% of DOOH, and DOOH grew 10.5%. Local advertisers were 65% of total OOH sales.

These are market totals. They do not show what one studio will get back.

## Which Screens Suit a Yoga Studio?

Studios depend on people who live or work within a short trip. The UK IAB guide says DOOH can be targeted by environment (rail, retail, roadside), time of day down to the hour, and geography. It also says most ads run about 10 seconds.

| Screen type | Reach | Fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Screens in shopping and cafe areas | Daytime local traffic | Weekday class offers |
| Transit and bus-stop screens | Commuters waiting | Short message plus QR code |
| Roadside digital billboards | Drivers | Studio name and neighborhood only |
| Venue screens (offices, gyms) | A routine audience | Lunch or evening class timing |

Run ads in the hours before your classes. For example, a lunchtime screen for an evening class gives people time to book.

## What Should the Ad Say?

Use the studio name, one intro offer and the location. For example, an intro week with a booking link in a QR code. Skip long class lists; people read a screen for seconds. If you use search ads too, our [yoga keyword guide](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-yoga-studios-in-2026) shows the terms that people type.

Send the creative to the media owner by their deadline. The IAB UK guide says at least five working days is typical, though same-day launches are possible when the ad is ready.

## How Do You Track Sign-Ups?

The IAB's July 2025 measurement guide says opportunity-to-see impressions count people in a screen's exposure zone, and likelihood-to-see requires presence while the screen is active and viewable. Neither proves a person noticed your ad. The guide lists first-party data, including gym check-ins, as a way to connect exposure to later action.

A studio can do a simple version. Give the DOOH ad its own booking link or offer code, ask "how did you hear about us" at sign-up, and compare new intro-offer sign-ups near the screens with a similar area. The IAB recommends matched market tests for incrementality.

## How Does DOOH Compare With Search and Facebook?

DOOH is priced per thousand impressions while search and Facebook are priced per click, so compare cost per sign-up. LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks for Health and Fitness show Google Ads at $6.17 per click, 6.94% conversion and $67.36 per lead. On Facebook, lead campaigns show $1.84 per click, 7.98% conversion and $27.11 per lead, and traffic campaigns $0.64 per click.

For organic reach, see our [Instagram guide for yoga studios](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-yoga-studios). Health and Fitness is a broad category, so use these as a range.

## What Should You Do First?

1. Pick one intro offer and one booking link.
2. List five screens within a short trip of the studio.
3. Ask each vendor for minimum spend, formats, self-serve access and reporting in writing.
4. Choose a start date and a comparison area.

## FAQ

### How much does DOOH cost for a yoga studio?

StackAdapt states CPMs run from $2 to $15. Total cost depends on screens, hours and minimum orders, which StackAdapt does not state.

### Is DOOH better than Google Ads for a yoga studio?

It does a different job. Google captures people searching now, DOOH builds awareness near the studio. Compare them on cost per intro sign-up.

### Can a small studio try DOOH?

Possibly, if screens are close and the minimum is low. Keep it to a short test with a comparison area.

### How do I know if a screen brought sign-ups?

Use a unique booking link or offer code and ask at sign-up how people heard of you.

### Should I combine DOOH with other screens?
