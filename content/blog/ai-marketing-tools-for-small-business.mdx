Start with the AI features inside tools you already pay for: your email platform, Google Ads and your booking software. Add a standalone tool only when it removes one repetitive job, such as answering the same customer questions. Free and low-cost plans exist, but prices change, so check each vendor's page before you buy.

The US Census Bureau reports that 19.8% of US businesses used AI as of May 3, 2026, and less than 20% of firms with four or fewer employees did. Small shops are not behind a crowd. They are choosing among tools that are still new.

## Which AI Marketing Tools Are Worth Trying First?

Work from the job, not the tool. Most small businesses have four recurring marketing jobs where AI helps: writing drafts, answering customer messages, running ads and following up by email. The table lists one example per job, with what the vendor's own page says.

| Job | Example tool | What the vendor page says | Check before buying |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Email drafts and automation | Mailchimp | Free plan at $0; paid plans listed from $13 (Essentials) and $20 (Standard) per month on promotional pricing; generative AI is listed for Standard and Premium | The page showed promotional prices and no post-promotion price |
| Website chat and FAQs | Tidio | Free plan at $0 with 50 lifetime Lyro AI conversations; Starter $24.17 per month; Lyro add-on from $32.50 per month | Billing period (monthly or annual) was not stated |
| Google Search ads | AI Max for Search | Targeting and ad-text enhancements inside Google Ads, with final URL expansion | Google's gain figure comes from its own data |
| Replies to reviews | Your own draft, edited | Google guidance covers manual replies only | Do not auto-post |

ChatGPT and Canva are common for drafting and design. Their pricing pages returned an access error when checked on 2026-10-25, so no prices for them appear here.

## Can AI Run Your Email and SMS Follow-Up?

It can draft and schedule, which is the part most owners dislike. Mailchimp's pricing page lists generative AI as a no-additional-cost add-on on Standard and Premium, and none on Free or Essentials. The page lists Standard at up to 6,000 emails per month.

Keep the logic simple: a welcome message, a reminder, a win-back after a quiet period. Read every draft before it goes out. Our [marketing automation tools comparison](/blog/best-marketing-automation-tools-small-business) covers the platforms in more depth.

## Should You Use an AI Chatbot on Your Website?

Only if your customers ask the same questions repeatedly: opening hours, prices, parking, how to book. A chatbot suits those. It does not suit complaints or anything involving a refund.

Tidio's pricing page lists a Free plan with 50 Lyro AI conversations for life, not per month. Paid Lyro limits run from 50 to 1,000 conversations a month, according to the page. Estimate your monthly enquiries before you pick a tier. For setup detail, see our guide to [AI chatbots for booking and FAQs](/blog/ai-chatbot-for-local-business-booking-and-faqs) and the [AI receptionist setup guide](/blog/ai-receptionist-small-business-setup-guide-2026).

## Does AI Help With Google Ads?

Partly. Google describes AI Max for Search campaigns as a set of targeting and creative enhancements: better search term matching, customised headlines and descriptions, and final URL expansion. Google states advertisers who turn it on will typically see 14% more conversions or conversion value at a similar cost per conversion. The note under that figure reads "Google internal data, 2025; for non-Retail advertisers", so treat it as a vendor claim.

For a local business, final URL expansion can send people to pages you did not intend. Check the search terms report weekly and keep your location settings tight.

## What Does a Week of AI Help Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs below are **assumptions**, not benchmarks. Say a salon owner spends 3 hours a week on Instagram captions and email drafts, and 1 hour answering repeat messages.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Hours on drafts per week | 3 |
| Hours on repeat messages per week | 1 |
| Share of drafting time AI removes | 40% |
| Share of repeat messages a chatbot handles | 50% |
| Hours saved per week | 1.2 + 0.5 = 1.7 |
| Value of the owner's hour | $30 |
| Weekly value | about $51 |
| Tool cost per month | $24 |
| Value per month (4.3 weeks) | about $219 |

On these numbers the tools pay back only if the owner uses the saved time for work that earns money. Replace each assumption with your own measured hours after two weeks.

## How Do You Reply to Reviews With AI Without Hurting Trust?

Draft with AI, edit by hand, post yourself. Google's Business Profile help describes replies as coming from the business and says helpful, positive replies show you are responsive. Replies that fail its content policies are held back until you edit them. Checks usually take about 10 minutes but can take up to 30 days. The page says nothing about automated replies, so it gives no clearance for them.

A reply that ignores what the reviewer wrote is worse than no reply. Our [review management guide](/blog/ai-agent-for-google-reviews-management) shows what to automate and what to leave to a person.

## What Should You Not Spend Money On?

Skip any tool you cannot connect to a number you already track: bookings, calls, replies or sales. Skip paid tools that duplicate features in your ad platform or email provider. Skip anything that promises results without naming a measurable job.

## FAQ

### Can AI marketing tools replace a marketer?

Not for a small business. They speed up drafts and routine replies. Choosing offers, handling complaints and knowing your neighbourhood still needs a person.

### How many small businesses use AI?

The Census Bureau's survey put overall US business use at 19.8% as of May 3, 2026. Less than 20% of firms with four or fewer employees reported using AI.

### Is there a free AI marketing tool?

Yes. Mailchimp lists a $0 plan without generative AI, and Tidio lists a $0 plan with 50 lifetime AI conversations. Both pages were checked on 2026-10-25 and can change.

### Should I pay for a tool that optimises my ads?

Check the built-in options first. Google Ads offers AI Max as an option inside Search campaigns, and the performance figure Google gives for it comes from its own data.

### How do I know a tool is working?

Pick one number before you start, such as bookings from email or messages answered without you. Compare it after 30 days.
