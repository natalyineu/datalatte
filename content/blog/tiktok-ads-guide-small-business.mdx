## Budget

TikTok states that campaign daily and total budgets must exceed 50 USD, and ad group daily budgets must exceed 20 USD ([TikTok Business Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-daily-budgets)). A one-ad-group test for 14 days at $21 a day costs $294 (simple arithmetic, not a benchmark).

## Audience

Pew found 37% of US adults use TikTok, with 44% of ages 30-49, 30% of ages 50-64 and 12% of 65 and older ([Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/03/02/8-facts-about-americans-and-tiktok/)).

## Tracking

TikTok describes its Pixel as code on your website that sends events to TikTok so you can measure and optimize campaigns ([TikTok help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-pixel)).

## Decision guidance

Estimate break-even first: first-visit profit divided by your expected cost per customer. If you cannot reach that on a $294 test, spend on [targeting and creative](/blog/tiktok-ads-targeting-guide) before adding budget.
