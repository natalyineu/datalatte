The best online menu for a restaurant website is a plain HTML page with real text, prices and a mobile layout, linked from your Google Business Profile. Keep a PDF only for printing. Then add an ordering link: DoorDash lists 15% to 30% delivery commission on its Marketplace plans, while orders taken on your own storefront pay payment processing fees only.

This guide covers where the menu lives, how Google reads it, how to link ordering, and how to compare direct and marketplace orders. Every number is from a source listed at the end; examples are labelled as assumptions.

## Should a Restaurant Menu Be HTML or a PDF?

Use HTML as the main menu. The Bureau of Internet Accessibility, a vendor that sells accessibility audits, lists the problems with PDF menus: they are often images that screen readers cannot read, tags may announce dishes out of order, and they display poorly on small screens, load slowly and are harder for search engines to read. It recommends HTML first, with a PDF only for download.

That is a vendor view, not a court ruling, so use it as a practical guideline. A text menu is also easier for you to update and for customers to copy a dish name from.

## What Should the Menu Page Include?

Build the page so a hungry person on a phone can answer four questions in seconds: what do you serve, what does it cost, are you open, and how do I order.

- Dish names and short descriptions as text, grouped into sections
- Prices on the page, with allergen or dietary notes where you can support them
- Opening hours, address and a tap-to-call number near the top
- A visible order button or link to your ordering provider
- One photo per section at most, with alt text, so the page stays fast

Keep the URL simple, such as /menu, and avoid putting the menu behind a popup or a login. Our guide to [website design for restaurants](/blog/website-design-for-restaurants) covers layout, and the [local business website checklist](/blog/local-business-website-checklist) covers the basics.

## How Do You Mark Up the Menu for Search?

Add structured data so search engines can read the menu. Schema.org defines a Menu type for a FoodEstablishment, with hasMenuSection for course groups and hasMenuItem for single dishes that can carry price and dietary details. A Restaurant points to its menu with the hasMenu property, which accepts a structured Menu or a URL.

Schema.org is a vocabulary, not a promise of any ranking or display feature. Treat it as clean labelling. Our guide to [schema markup for a local business website](/blog/schema-markup-local-business-website) shows how to add it with a plugin or by hand.

## How Do You Put Your Menu on Google?

Google gives two routes. In the Business Profile menu editor, food and drink businesses choose Edit menu, then add sections and items. Changes can take 24 to 48 hours to appear on Maps and Search. If you would rather not rebuild it, go to Edit profile, Business information, Contact, and enter a Menu link with your menu page URL.

Google also lets you add ordering links. Under Food ordering you can add a link and set a preferred provider for pickup or delivery, and the Accept orders on your profile toggle hides third-party options when it is off. See our [Google Business Profile guide for restaurants](/blog/google-my-business-for-restaurants) for the rest of the profile.

## What Does Ordering Through DoorDash Cost?

DoorDash's merchant page lists three US Marketplace plans with no monthly or signup fee. Delivery commission is 15% on Basic, 25% on Plus and 30% on Premier, and pickup commission is 6% on all three. For direct orders, the page says merchants can accept them without paying DoorDash commission, only standard payment processing fees, and gives no specific processing rate.

Plans change, so confirm the current terms with each provider. Other platforms charge their own rates. Our comparison of [DoorDash and Uber Eats restaurant fees](/blog/doordash-vs-uber-eats-restaurant-fees-local-business) and of [Toast and Square for restaurants](/blog/toast-vs-square-for-restaurants-local-business) goes deeper.

## What Does Direct Ordering Cost Instead?

It depends on your software and card processor. Square's pricing page did not return a usable price table when this article was checked, so Square numbers are not quoted here. Ask each vendor for monthly fee, card rate per order and any fee for delivery dispatch, and write them down side by side.

Also count the work. Direct orders mean you handle customer service, delivery drivers if you offer delivery, and your own marketing. Marketplaces bring discovery, which direct orders do not.

## Worked Example: Marketplace or Your Own Site?

Inputs are **assumptions** to show the arithmetic.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Average delivery order | $30 |
| Orders per month on the channel | 200 |
| DoorDash Plus delivery commission | 25% |
| Direct processing cost (check your processor) | 3% of order |

On DoorDash Plus, commission is $7.50 per order, or $1,500 a month on 200 orders. On your own site at an assumed 3%, processing is $0.90 per order, or $180 a month. The gap is $1,320 a month before software fees and delivery costs. Moving even a quarter of repeat customers to direct orders could matter, but only if they would have ordered anyway.

## How Do You Measure Whether the Menu Works?

Track menu page views, clicks on the order button, and completed orders. A free tool such as Google Analytics can count button clicks as events. Add UTM tags to the menu link on your Google profile so you can see how many visits come from Google. Compare the direct share of orders month to month.

## FAQ

### Is a PDF menu bad for a restaurant website?

It is a weaker main menu. A vendor source lists reading, mobile and search problems. Keep a PDF for printing, but make the HTML page the main menu.

### Can I add my menu to Google without building a menu page?

Yes. Food and drink businesses can use the Business Profile menu editor, or add a Menu link to an existing page. Changes can take 24 to 48 hours.

### How much does DoorDash charge restaurants?

Its US Marketplace page lists 15%, 25% and 30% delivery commission and 6% pickup commission on Basic, Plus and Premier. Terms can change.

### Do I need structured data for my menu?

It is optional. Schema.org offers Menu, MenuSection and MenuItem types, and Restaurant can link to them. It labels content for machines and does not guarantee any display feature.

### How often should I update the menu?

Update it when prices or dishes change, and re-check Google after 24 to 48 hours. Out-of-date prices cause complaints at the counter.
