The best Instagram ad practice for a local business is to pick one objective, limit delivery to the area you serve, test one change at a time and judge results by cost per lead or booking, not clicks. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 Facebook ads benchmarks, which cover Meta placements including Instagram, show a $0.60 average click on traffic campaigns and $27.39 per lead on lead campaigns. Use them as a rough yardstick, not a target.

The ten rules below are working guidance, not Meta rules. Meta's help pages for aspect ratios and location radius returned no readable text when checked, so no Meta specifications are quoted here. Check specs in Ads Manager before you build creative.

## Who Is Actually on Instagram?

Pew's 2026 survey found 51% of US adults use Instagram: 79% of ages 18 to 29, 64% of 30 to 49, 41% of 50 to 64 and 21% of 65 and over. Facebook reaches 70% of adults overall. If your customers are older, test Facebook placements too. Our [Instagram vs Facebook ads comparison](/blog/instagram-ads-vs-facebook-ads) explains the difference.

## What Are the 10 Rules?

1. **Pick one objective per campaign.** Traffic, leads and bookings are billed and optimised differently. Choose the action you want, such as a booking or a message.
2. **Send people somewhere that finishes the job.** A booking page or lead form that loads fast on a phone beats a home page.
3. **Keep delivery local.** Set the area around your business and exclude places you cannot serve. Check the radius options in Ads Manager.
4. **Lead with a real photo or short video.** Show your work, your staff and your space.
5. **Say the offer in the first line.** A price, a free consultation or a limited slot.
6. **Test one variable at a time.** Change the image, or the headline, or the audience, never all three.
7. **Give each test enough budget and time.** Judge after the ads have run for a week or more and have produced a meaningful number of results.
8. **Track outcomes you can bank.** Count calls, bookings and form fills, not likes.
9. **Reply to comments and messages fast.** Slow replies waste the click you paid for.
10. **Cut losers and move budget gradually.** Make small changes rather than doubling spend overnight.

## How Do Lead and Traffic Campaigns Differ in Cost?

LocaliQ's 2026 tables show traffic campaigns average $0.60 per click and a 1.93% click-through rate, while lead campaigns average $1.80 per click, a 2.70% click-through rate, an 8.54% conversion rate and $27.39 per lead. Lead clicks cost more because the objective asks for more effort.

For a local business, cost per lead is the better yardstick. Compare it with the value of a customer, not with another advertiser's figure. Our [Instagram ad cost breakdown](/blog/how-much-do-instagram-ads-cost-2026-pricing-breakdown) goes deeper.

## What Creative Works for Local Businesses?

No verified study ranks formats for local services, so test. Run a short video, a single photo and a carousel against the same offer and keep the one that gets leads at the lowest cost. Keep text short and readable on a phone. Make video work without sound, since many people scroll with it off.

## The Math: What Does a $300 Test Return?

All inputs are **assumptions**, using LocaliQ's all-industry lead benchmark as a starting point.

| | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $300 |
| Cost per lead | $27.39 |
| Leads | about 11 |
| Leads who book | 30% |
| Bookings | about 3.3 |
| Cost per booking | about $91 |
| Value of a new customer, first three months | $150 |

On these inputs, a new customer is worth more than it costs to win. If only 10% of leads book, cost per booking triples to about $274 and the campaign loses money. That is why you should track bookings, not leads alone.

## FAQ

### How much should a local business spend on Instagram ads?

There is no standard. Start with a budget you can afford to lose for a month, then compare cost per lead or booking with customer value. LocaliQ lists $27.39 per lead across industries for Meta lead campaigns in 2026.

### Which objective should I choose?

Choose the action that earns you money: leads or bookings for service businesses, traffic only if your page does the converting.

### How long before I judge an ad?

Wait until it has run for at least a week and gathered a meaningful number of results. Changing ads constantly makes the results hard to read.

### Do I need video?

No. Test a photo, a short video and a carousel with the same offer and keep what gets leads cheaply.

### Are Instagram ads better than Google Ads?

They do different jobs. Google reaches people searching now, and Instagram reaches people while they scroll. Many local businesses start with search and add Instagram.
