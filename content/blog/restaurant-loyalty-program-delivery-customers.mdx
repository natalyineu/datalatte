To build a loyalty program for delivery customers, give people a reason to order direct: a rewards sign-up in every bag, your own online ordering link, and points that also work on pickup and dine-in. DoorDash charges 15%, 25% or 30% commission on delivery depending on the plan, so every order moved to your own channel keeps money you would otherwise pay out.

This guide covers the rules of the marketplace platforms, the costs of the direct route and a worked example. All figures come from the sources at the end.

## What Does a Delivery Order Cost You?

On DoorDash's published merchant plans, commission is a percentage of the order subtotal: 15% on Basic, 25% on Plus and 30% on Premier, with 6% for pickup if menu prices match in-store prices. Higher plans add a wider delivery radius and DashPass access. Orders placed through DoorDash's Online Ordering, its direct channel, carry no commission, only a payment processing fee whose rate the page does not state.

Other platforms set their own rates, so check each one. Our delivery platform fees explainer and the [DoorDash vs Uber Eats comparison](/blog/doordash-vs-uber-eats-restaurant-fees-local-business) cover them.

## Who Owns the Customer Data?

This is the catch. DoorDash's pricing page does not say what customer data merchants can access. DoorDash markets tools that let restaurants see who orders and invite marketplace customers into a loyalty program, but a restaurant should read its own merchant agreement rather than assume it can email every marketplace buyer. Commentary from other sources disagrees on how much data is shared, so treat that as unverified.

The safe plan is to collect contact details yourself, with permission, at the moment the customer is already engaged.

## How Do You Move Delivery Customers to Your Own Program?

Put a short, clear offer where the customer will see it. Options that fit the rules:

- A card or sticker in the bag with a QR code to your rewards sign-up.
- A line on your menu page and Google Business Profile pointing to your own ordering link.
- A reward for the first direct order, funded from the commission you save.

Check each marketplace's terms on promotional inserts before you print anything. Where the terms are unclear, ask the platform in writing.

## What Does a Loyalty Tool Cost?

Square Loyalty advertises one plan for the whole business with a 30-day free trial, without listing a monthly price on the page. Points can be earned per visit, per amount spent, per item or per category, and earn on cash payments, in-store and on a Square Online site. Square's own page cites 2022 global data showing enrolled customers spending 53% more and visiting 40% more often; that is a vendor figure, not an independent study.

Whatever system you use, the program should recognise the same customer across delivery-direct, pickup and dine-in. Compare more reward designs in our [restaurant loyalty program ideas](/blog/restaurant-loyalty-program-ideas).

## The Math: What Does One Direct Order Save?

All inputs below are **assumptions**, using DoorDash's published rates.

| | Marketplace order | Direct order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order subtotal (assumption) | $40 | $40 |
| Commission | 25% = $10.00 | 0% |
| Reward given (assumption) | $0 | $4 (10% of subtotal) |
| Payment processing | not modelled | not modelled (rate not on DoorDash page) |
| Left after commission and reward | $30.00 | $36.00 |

On this order you keep about $6 more even after paying a 10% reward, before processing fees on the direct order. At Basic's 15% the saving shrinks to $2. The math favours direct ordering most when you pay a higher plan rate.

## FAQ

### Can I email customers who order through DoorDash?

Not automatically. DoorDash's pricing page does not describe merchant data access, so read your merchant agreement and the current terms before building a list from marketplace orders.

### How much does DoorDash charge restaurants?

Delivery commission is 15% (Basic), 25% (Plus) or 30% (Premier), and pickup is 6% on the published plans. Online Ordering by DoorDash has no commission but has a payment processing fee.

### Is a loyalty program worth it for a small restaurant?

It depends on your repeat rate and margins. Run the worked example above with your own subtotal, plan rate and reward.

### Does Square Loyalty work for online orders?

Yes. Square says points are earned on purchases made through a Square Online site, and rewards can be redeemed in-store or online.
