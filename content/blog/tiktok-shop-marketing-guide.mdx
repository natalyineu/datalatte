Before you market on TikTok Shop, work out what each sale leaves you. TikTok's public US table lists a 6% referral fee for most categories (5% for some, such as jewelry), and independent guides report a rise to 8% for most non-food categories from 4 August 2026, which TikTok's public table did not show when those guides were written. Creator commissions are set by you, often 10% to 20% according to Printful, and shipping, returns and ads come on top.

TikTok's own Seller University pages could not be opened for this article, so every fee below comes from secondary sources and some conflict. Check your rate in Seller Center before you price anything. The guide ends with a margin example using labelled assumptions.

## What Does TikTok Shop Charge Sellers?

Secondary sources agree on the shape of the fees but not on every number. Printful's October 2026 guide says the referral fee is charged on the item price after seller-funded discounts and before shipping and sales tax, and that opening a shop and listing products has no setup, subscription or listing fee.

| Cost | What secondary sources report | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Referral fee | 6% for most categories, about 5% for some such as jewelry | Two sources agree |
| Reported change | 8% for most non-food categories from 4 Aug 2026 | Unconfirmed (beBOLD) |
| New-seller rate | 3% for 30 days after the first qualifying sale | Printful contradicts itself (also says 2% for 90 days) |
| Payment processing | Included, or about 2% to 2.5% extra | Printful gives both |
| Creator commission | Set by the seller, typically 10% to 20% for open offers | One source |
| Refund admin fee | 20% of the original referral fee, capped at $5 per SKU | One source |

Where sources conflict, the safe approach is to build your price using the higher figure, then update it when Seller Center shows your actual rate.

## Is TikTok Shop Right for a Local Business?

It suits a business that sells shippable products and can fulfil orders reliably. It does not replace a local booking page for services. We could not verify TikTok's current eligibility rules or whether service businesses can sell through the shop, so check Seller Center and Seller University before planning around them. Our wider piece on [TikTok Shop for local businesses](/blog/tiktok-shop-local-business-2026) covers the concept.

If you sell physical goods, the first questions are cost and fulfilment. Printful says Fulfilled by TikTok has per-unit fees starting at $4.28 and 60 days of free storage, while another figure in the same article starts at $2.86. Treat shipping as a real cost line.

## How Do You Market a Product on TikTok Shop?

TikTok has three routes to sales: your own videos with product tags, creators who earn a commission, and paid ads. The sources opened here describe ad spend such as GMV Max and Shop Ads only as "set by the seller", so no cost-per-click or return figure is quoted. Start with organic video and a small creator offer, and measure the margin on each order.

Show the product in use, answer the first question buyers ask and keep your own page ready for questions. ## The Math: What Does One Sale Leave You?

All inputs are **assumptions** for a $30 product, using the fee ranges above.

| Line | At 6% fee | At 8% fee |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $30.00 | $30.00 |
| Product cost (assumption) | $10.00 | $10.00 |
| Referral fee | $1.80 | $2.40 |
| Creator commission at 15% (assumption) | $4.50 | $4.50 |
| Left before shipping, returns and ads | $13.70 | $13.10 |

At these inputs, a $500 ad budget needs about 37 to 39 extra orders just to repay itself, and that is before shipping and returns. If shipping costs $5, the margin falls to about $8.70 or $8.10 and the break-even order count rises to about 58 to 62. Do the same arithmetic with your actual numbers.

<Callout type="tip" title="Do This Today">
Write down your cost to produce, ship and refund one order. If you cannot find a margin after a 15% creator commission and an 8% fee, raise the price or skip creators.
</Callout>

## FAQ

### How much does TikTok Shop take from each sale?

The public table cited by beBOLD lists 6% for most US categories, and independent guides report 8% from 4 August 2026. Neither is TikTok's own page here, so check Seller Center.

### Are there monthly or listing fees?

Printful's October 2026 guide says there is no setup, subscription or listing fee.

### What do creators charge?

Printful says commission is set by the seller and is typically 10% to 20% for open collaborations, higher for targeted deals.

### Can a service business use TikTok Shop?

This could not be verified from official sources. Use TikTok's Seller University or Seller Center to confirm eligibility.

### Do I need ads to sell on TikTok Shop?

No source opened says ads are required. Ad spend is set by you, so test a small amount only after organic sales show margin.
