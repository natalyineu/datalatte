## What Does One TikTok Shop Sale Leave You?

Start with the price the customer pays, then subtract every cost tied to that order. The platform fee is one line. Creator commission, if you use creators, is another. Product cost, packaging, shipping and refunds complete the list. Only what remains can pay for ads, staff time or profit.

Many small sellers price by instinct and skip this step. A product that looks profitable at $30 can leave very little once a fee, a creator cut and postage are taken out. Write the numbers down for one order before spending anything on promotion. Keep the record in a spreadsheet so every new product gets the same test.

Refunds need their own line. A returned order can cost the product, the postage both ways and any commission already paid, depending on the terms you agree. Check the refund rules in Seller Center before assuming a return leaves your margin untouched.

## How Many US Adults Use TikTok?

Pew Research Center's 2026 survey of 5,511 US adults found that 37% say they use TikTok, the same share as in 2025. The same survey found 84% use YouTube, 70% use Facebook and 51% use Instagram. The overall margin of error is plus or minus 1.8 percentage points, and the survey represents all US adults ([Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/fact-sheet/social-media/)).

Reach is only part of the decision. Over a third of adults is a large audience, but the figure does not show who buys your product or whether they buy through a shop tab. Use it to judge whether TikTok belongs in your mix, then test with your own customers. The [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) explains how to split spend across channels before you commit a fixed amount.

Because the survey covers US adults only, sellers in the UK, Australia and Canada should look for equivalent figures for their own markets before drawing conclusions.

## What Checks Should You Run Before Listing?

Use this checklist before promoting any product. Each step can be completed in an afternoon with your own figures.

| Step | What to record | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Confirm your fee rate | Current platform percentage for your category | Seller Center |
| 2. Set the price | Price the customer pays, including any discount you fund | Your store |
| 3. Add product cost | Unit cost, packaging, insert or label | Supplier invoice |
| 4. Add shipping | Postage or fulfilment fee per order | Carrier or fulfilment quote |
| 5. Add creator cut | Agreed commission on each sale | Creator agreement |
| 6. Add refund allowance | Expected share of orders returned | Your last 90 days of data |
| 7. Calculate margin | Price minus all lines above | Spreadsheet |
| 8. Set ad budget | Only the margin left after step 7 | Your ad plan |

If step 7 is negative, raise the price, cut the product cost or reduce the creator commission before spending on ads. Repeat the checklist whenever a fee, a supplier price or a postage rate changes, because a margin that worked last quarter can fail quietly.

## What Does a Worked Margin Example Show?

The inputs below are **assumptions**, not platform figures. Replace each one with your own numbers.

- Price charged: $30 (assumption)
- Product cost: $10 (assumption)
- Platform fee: 8% of price, so $2.40 (assumption)
- Creator commission: 15% of price, so $4.50 (assumption)
- Left before shipping, returns and ads: $13.10
- Shipping: $5 per order (assumption), leaving $8.10

Suppose the seller plans a $500 ad budget (assumption). At $13.10 left per order before shipping, about 39 orders are needed to repay it. At $8.10 after shipping, about 62 orders are needed. If sales fall below that count, the campaign loses money even when the account looks busy.

Meta's help page on A/B testing makes a similar point for paid ads. It recommends using the same budget for both versions of a test, and it warns that informal testing, such as switching ad sets on and off by hand, can lead to inefficient delivery and unreliable results ([Meta Business Help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1738164643098669)). That guidance covers Meta Ads, not TikTok, but a fair test still needs equal budgets and one clear measure, such as cost per result.

## Is TikTok Shop Right for a Local Business?

TikTok Shop suits businesses that sell shippable products and can fulfil orders reliably. It does not replace a booking page or a phone line for services. If customers need an appointment, a video that points people to your local listing may do more than a shop tab.

Confirm them in Seller Center before planning around them. If you also sell through other marketplaces, the [Amazon ads guide for local businesses](/blog/amazon-ads-for-local-business-complete-guide) compares the same margin questions on a different platform.

## How Should You Write Product Content?

Show the product in use, answer the first question a buyer asks and keep the price, size and delivery time visible. Short scripts work better when they start from a list of objections customers actually raise. Record those objections in your order messages or reviews, then write one short answer for each.

Drafting tools can speed this up. The [ChatGPT prompts for local business marketing](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-local-business-marketing) give starting points for scripts, and [AI tools that can replace a marketing assistant](/blog/ai-tools-replace-marketing-assistant-local-business) show how small teams handle repetitive posting. Check every claim against the product, and keep a human review on pricing, stock levels and any health or safety statements.

## FAQ

### How do I find my TikTok Shop fee rate?

Check Seller Center, which shows the rate that applies to your account. This guide does not quote a rate, because the sources reviewed do not include TikTok's fee schedule.

### What share of US adults use TikTok?

Pew Research Center's 2026 survey of 5,511 US adults found 37% say they use TikTok, unchanged from 2025.

### How many orders repay a $500 ad budget?

It depends on what each order leaves after costs. In the worked example, using assumed inputs, about 39 orders repay the budget before shipping and about 62 after shipping.

### Should I use creators or ads first?

Start with organic video and a small creator offer, then add ads only when margin per order covers the ad cost. Test with equal budgets so the result is fair.
