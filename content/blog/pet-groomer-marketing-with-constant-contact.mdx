A solo groomer with one list can start on Lite. A shop with several groomers who each want a login, or that wants separate flows for puppies, seniors and new clients, will hit the limit quickly.

## Which Emails Should a Groomer Build First?

Start with two. A welcome email goes to every new sign-up and sets expectations: how to book, your cancellation rule, and what to bring. A rebooking reminder goes out some weeks after the last visit.

Timing should follow the coat. The American Kennel Club says grooming frequency depends on breed, coat length and type, time outdoors, climate and the style requested. Its examples range from every six weeks to three times a year. A fixed interval for everyone is therefore wrong. Use tags or segments, such as short coat, curly coat and puppy cut, and set a different delay for each.

Use a clear subject line and one button that opens your booking page. Constant Contact's AI Content Generator is included on all three plans if you want a first draft, but edit it so it sounds like your shop.

## How Do You Build a List Without Buying One?

Collect addresses where clients already are: at the front desk, on the booking form, and on a sign-up form on your website. Ask for the dog's name and coat type at the same time, so segmenting later needs no extra work. Do not import a purchased list.

## What Does the Law Require?

The FTC's CAN-SPAM guide lists eight requirements for commercial email. The ones groomers forget most often:

- Include your valid physical postal address in every message.
- Label promotional messages as ads.
- Give a clear way to opt out and honor requests within 10 business days.
- Keep the opt-out working for at least 30 days after you send.

The FTC says each separate email in violation can carry penalties of up to $53,088. Constant Contact adds the footer and unsubscribe link, but the responsibility for the content stays with you. A vendor does not transfer your legal duty.

## What Could a Rebooking Email Earn? A Worked Example

All numbers below are **assumptions** chosen for easy arithmetic. They are not benchmarks.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Clients on the list | 300 |
| Reminder sent to clients due this month | 100 |
| Share who book after the email | 8% |
| Average ticket | $70 |
| Plan cost per month | $12 |

Under these assumptions, 100 reminders produce 8 bookings, or $560 in revenue. That is a cost of $1.50 per booking at the plan price alone, before your time. If only 2% book, you get 2 bookings, $140 in revenue, and still cover the plan. Replace each input with your own numbers after two months of sending.

The point is the order of magnitude: a reminder to people who already know you is cheap. Paid ads reach strangers. For that side, see [Meta ads for pet groomers](/blog/pet-groomer-meta-ads) and [Google Ads keywords for groomers](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-pet-groomers-in-2026).

## What Can You Skip at First?

You do not need a newsletter on a fixed calendar, SMS, or a custom automation builder. Premium's custom segments and ecommerce paths matter if you sell products online. For a service shop, one welcome path plus one rebooking path is enough, and Lite covers exactly one path, so decide which matters more or upgrade to Standard. ## FAQ

### How many automations can a pet groomer run on Constant Contact Lite?

One active path, according to Constant Contact's help article. Standard allows three and Premium has no limit on active paths.

### How often should a groomer send rebooking reminders?

It depends on the coat. The AKC says frequency varies by breed, coat and style, with examples from every six weeks to three times a year. Segment clients by coat type and set a delay for each group.

### Is a purchased email list allowed?

Avoid it. Consent is the safer route, and our guide on building a list from zero covers sign-up forms and incentives. CAN-SPAM still applies to anyone you email.

### What does Constant Contact cost?

The official pricing page could not be opened for this article. Secondary sources quote $12, $35 and $80 a month for Lite, Standard and Premium at 500 contacts. Confirm on the live page.

### Do I need SMS reminders too?

Not to start. SMS is an add-on on Lite and Standard and included at 500 messages a month on Premium. Begin with email and add SMS if clients ignore reminders.
