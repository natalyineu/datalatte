To set up a Performance Max campaign for a local business, confirm conversion tracking first, link the store locations you want to promote, choose a goal such as store visits or leads, set a budget you can keep running, and fill an asset group with headlines, descriptions and images. Google's AI then places ads across Google surfaces from that one campaign. The campaign is goal-based, so the conversions you count decide what it optimizes for. Judge results only after enough conversions have come in, and check the live help pages for current eligibility and asset rules.

## What Is Performance Max and Where Does It Run?

Google describes Performance Max as AI-powered. You set a goal such as sales, leads or store visits, and [Google's Performance Max page](https://business.google.com/en-all/google-ads/) states that Google's AI finds the most profitable customers across Search, YouTube, Maps and other Google surfaces. A restaurant guide from Taller Digital says the campaign automatically shows ads across Google Search, Maps, YouTube and Gmail ([Taller Digital, secondary](https://www.tallerdigital.co.uk/post/a-guide-to-google-ads-for-restaurant-owners-in-2025)).

Performance Max for store goals replaced legacy Local Campaigns and uses machine learning to place ads across Search, Maps, YouTube, Gmail and the Google Display Network ([Wizerunek, secondary](https://wizerunekwsieci.com/local-advertising-on-google-for-small-businesses-ready-to-grow/)). If you are weighing a different format for video or social reach, see the [Demand Gen guide for local business](/blog/google-demand-gen-campaigns-local-business) before committing budget.

## Should a Local Business Start With Performance Max?

Sources disagree. Defined Digital Academy advises starting with Search campaigns, not Performance Max, so the account builds conversion data before scaling ([Defined Digital Academy, secondary](https://www.definedigitalacademy.com/blog/google-ads-for-local-service-based-businesses-5-step-strategy-for-2025)). The Taller Digital guide takes the opposite view, saying its authors often see better results from Performance Max than from their search campaigns.

Insidea's pest control guidance (secondary) recommends separate campaigns by service, specific keywords, a thorough negative keyword list, tracking of calls and form submissions, and regular review of performance data ([Insidea, secondary](https://insidea.com/blog/marketing/pest-control-companies/google-ads-strategy-for-exterminators)). Those basics apply whichever format you choose. If you have little conversion history, the Search-first approach gives you a cleaner baseline. Our [Performance Max guide for local business](/blog/google-performance-max-for-local-business-complete-guide-2026) covers when the format fits.

## What Do You Need Before You Launch?

Work through these six steps before you publish anything.

1. Confirm that conversion tracking counts only outcomes that matter, such as a booking, a call or a store visit. A page view should not count as a conversion.
2. Verify your Business Profile and confirm which locations you want to promote. Location assets on Display inventory are only available through Local Campaigns or Performance Max with store goals ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)).
3. Choose one goal that matches your main conversion: store visits, store sales, local actions such as directions, or online sales and leads.
4. Set a monthly budget you can sustain through a full test. Google says it will never charge more than the monthly budget cap ([Google Business](https://business.google.com/us/support/)).
5. Build the asset group with headlines, descriptions, images and a logo, and plan to review any assets Google generates.
6. Book a monthly review date in your calendar before launch, so the campaign is checked on a schedule.

## How Do You Set Up Store Goals for a Local Business?

Store goals let a Performance Max campaign optimize for store visits, store sales or local actions such as directions. Google's Waze announcement confirms that Waze ads inventory was added to these campaigns, where a business can appear as a "Promoted Places in Navigation" pin on a user's map ([Google Ads announcement, November 2025](https://business.google.com/us/accelerate/announcements/access-new-inventory-and-channel-performance-reporting-in-performance-max-campaigns/)).

For U.S. advertisers using Performance Max for store goals, Google says Waze ads require no additional setup. Google also plans to expand Waze inventory to markets beyond the U.S. in 2026, so check your region's current status. Google's retailer guide notes that Performance Max can be configured to optimize for online sales, store sales or omnichannel sales ([Google retailer best practices](https://business.google.com/us/accelerate/resources/articles/retailer-best-practices-for-ai-powered-performance-max-campaigns/)). Merchant Center feeds optimize for online sales, so a local shop with no online store should choose the store goal.

## How Much Budget Should You Set?

Budget figures vary widely by source. Focus Digital's own study analyzed over 150 Google Ads campaigns from February 5 to May 25, 2025, and recommends a monthly budget of $4,200 for Performance Max ([Focus Digital, secondary](https://focus-digital.co/minimum-google-ads-budget-by-industry-2025-guidelines/)). That figure reflects one vendor's sample, not a local-business rule. Wizerunek states that localized Performance Max campaigns can begin testing with monthly budgets between $200 and $350 ([Wizerunek, secondary](https://wizerunekwsieci.com/local-advertising-on-google-for-small-businesses-ready-to-grow/)).

Treat the budget as a ceiling rather than a target. Google says you never pay more than your monthly cap. Check the budget suggestions in your own account, because they reflect your location and history. Pick an amount that lets the test run long enough to gather conversions.

## What Does a Break-Even Test Look Like?

The table below is a worked example. Every input is an assumption, so swap in your own margin and close rate before you rely on it.

| Input or result | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $200 | Assumption, low end of the range a blog cites |
| Average sale | $60 | Assumption |
| Gross margin | 40% | Assumption |
| Profit per sale | $24 | Calculated: $60 × 40% |
| Sales needed to break even | 9 (8.3, rounded up) | Calculated: $200 ÷ $24 |
| Share of calls that become sales | 1 in 3 | Assumption |
| Calls needed to break even | 27 | Calculated: 9 × 3 |
| Break-even cost per call | about $7.41 | Calculated: $200 ÷ 27 |

If your real cost per call is above this figure, the campaign is not paying back at these inputs. Set a conversion value per call that reflects a realistic sale, so bidding can tell a call from a store visit. For retailers with items at different prices, Google recommends transaction-specific values ([Google retailer best practices](https://business.google.com/us/accelerate/resources/articles/retailer-best-practices-for-ai-powered-performance-max-campaigns/)).

## How Do You Judge Results Over Time?

Dental Rank recommends setting aside time each month to analyze ad performance, test new creatives, adjust targeting and refine bidding ([Dental Rank, secondary](https://dentalrank.com.au/top-5-google-ads-mistakes-cost-dentists-thousands-how-to-fix-them/)). Channel performance reporting is now available in all Performance Max campaigns, which shows where your ads ran ([Google Ads announcement](https://business.google.com/us/accelerate/announcements/access-new-inventory-and-channel-performance-reporting-in-performance-max-campaigns/)). Use it to check whether Maps or YouTube are producing the calls you are paying for.

Avoid chasing the wrong metric. Google states that Quality Score is not a key performance indicator and should not be optimized or aggregated with the rest of your data ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167118)). ## FAQ

### Is Performance Max worth it for a single-location business?

The sources reviewed here do not set a minimum location count. Start with the goal that matches your main conversion, and check the live help page for store goal eligibility. Compare cost per conversion against a Search campaign over the same period.

### How much should I spend on a first test?

Google says it will not charge more than your monthly cap. A vendor study recommends $4,200 per month for its sample, and a blog cites $200 to $350 for localized tests. Choose an amount your margin can absorb through a full test.

### Should I run Search campaigns alongside Performance Max?

Opinions differ. One vendor recommends starting with Search to build conversion data before scaling. Another reports that Performance Max often outperforms its own search campaigns. Test both against the same conversion actions before deciding.

### Does the Waze placement need extra setup?

For U.S. advertisers using Performance Max for store goals, Google says no additional setup is required. Confirm the current status for your country, since Google plans to expand Waze inventory to more markets in 2026.
