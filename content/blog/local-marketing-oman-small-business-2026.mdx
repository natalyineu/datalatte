An Omani small business should put Instagram first. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report shows Instagram ad reach of 2.80 million people (50.5% of the population), Snapchat 2.35 million (42.3%) and Facebook 1.75 million (31.6%). TikTok reaches 2.73 million users aged 18 and over. Oman is also 95.3% online.

## How Big Is the Omani Online Market?

DataReportal counts 5.54 million people, 5.28 million internet users and 6.93 million mobile connections (125%). Social media user identities total 3.44 million, 62.1% of the population. Nearly everyone is online, so channel choice matters more than whether people can be reached.

## Which Platforms Reach People in Oman?

| Platform | Ad reach | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram | 2.80 million | 50.5% |
| TikTok (aged 18+) | 2.73 million | not given |
| Snapchat | 2.35 million | 42.3% |
| Facebook | 1.75 million | 31.6% |
| LinkedIn (members) | 1.30 million | 23.5% |

Facebook is only fourth here. Instagram and Snapchat each reach more people, which suggests visual and short-form content for cafes, salons and gyms. Meta, Snap and TikTok figures are ad audience estimates, not active user counts.

## Is Tourism Growing in Oman?

Oman's Ministry of Finance, reporting National Centre for Statistics and Information (NCSI) data on 10 August 2025, says guests at 3 to 5 star hotels reached 1,142,702 by the end of June 2025, up 9.2% from 1,046,224. This covers only larger hotels in the first half of the year, and not all visitors.

For restaurants, tour operators and shops near hotels, that suggests steady visitor demand. A Google Business Profile with accurate hours and photos is the first step; add Instagram posts showing real products and places.

## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Oman?

Google says local ranking uses relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Reviews and ratings can help prominence. Complete the categories, add photos and reply to reviews. ## Worked Example: An Instagram and Snapchat Split

All numbers are **assumptions**, in US dollars.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $150 on Instagram, $150 on Snapchat |
| Orders from Instagram | 20 |
| Orders from Snapchat | 12 |
| Margin per order | $9 |
| Cost per order | $7.50 and $12.50 |

Instagram earns $180 margin against $150 spend. Snapchat earns $108 against $150 and loses $42 on first orders. At these inputs, shift budget toward Instagram, unless Snapchat customers reorder more often.
