## What Does Mailchimp Cost at Different List Sizes?

Mailchimp's price changes with the contact count, so one headline number misleads. A 2026 pricing breakdown lists Essentials at $13 per month and Standard at $20 per month, both for 500 contacts. It lists Premium at $350 per month for 500 contacts, with multivariate testing and a dedicated IP ([Tutorsbot, secondary](https://tutorsbot.com/blog/mailchimp-pricing-2026-cost-per-contact)).

At 5,000 contacts, the [Email Marketing Tools Pricing Guide](https://www.getpricepulse.com/blog/email-marketing-tools-pricing-guide-2026.html) calculator shows Mailchimp Standard at $75 a month. The same calculator lists Klaviyo Email at $100 and ActiveCampaign Plus at $105 for that size (secondary). A vendor-advertising guide puts email platforms such as Mailchimp or Constant Contact at $20 to $100 a month ([AdWave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-for-restaurants)).

These figures come from different pages and dates, so treat them as a range. Mailchimp's own [pricing page](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/) asks you to select your audience size to calculate the price, so confirm the current figure there.

## Where Does MailerLite Fall on Price?

The Stack Reviewer says MailerLite costs roughly half of Klaviyo's price if you need email campaigns plus basic automations ([Klaviyo Pricing Review 2026, secondary](https://thestackreviewer.com/posts/klaviyo-pricing-review/)). The same review says MailerLite costs roughly half of Klaviyo at 5,000 contacts. It also says MailerLite is 10% cheaper on annual billing, while Klaviyo and Mailchimp have no annual discount, as of October 2026.

The reviewer recommends MailerLite for businesses under 10,000 contacts that do not use ecommerce-specific flows. That fits most local service businesses, such as salons, cafes and studios.

A reviewer's ratio is not a MailerLite price list. The review's Mailchimp figure at 5,000 contacts is $100, while the calculator above shows $75, so the two sources differ by $25 a month. Check MailerLite's own calculator at your contact count before you budget.

## How Do Send Limits and Overages Change the Bill?

Mailchimp sets a monthly email send limit based on your contact count. Per its [pricing page](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/), the limit is 10 times the maximum contact count on Essentials, 12 times on Standard and 15 times on Premium. Applied to a 5,000-contact list, that is 50,000 emails a month on Essentials, 60,000 on Standard and 75,000 on Premium (calculated from those multipliers).

Overages apply if you exceed the contact or email send limit, and the page states that sending will be paused when a limit is exceeded. Check your monthly volume before a large promotion, since a single heavy month can trigger extra charges.

Transactional email, such as booking confirmations or receipts, is a separate cost. Mailchimp's Mandrill add-on is $20 per 10,000 transactional emails for Essentials and Standard users ([Tutorsbot, secondary](https://tutorsbot.com/blog/mailchimp-pricing-2026-cost-per-contact)).

## How Often Should a Local Business Send?

Sending frequency drives email volume, and volume drives the send limit. A MailerLite study cited by Selzy found that most businesses send between one and three emails a month ([Selzy, secondary](https://selzy.com/en/blog/email-cadence/)).

A 2025 MailerLite analysis covering more than 42,000 email accounts found open rates of 33.22% for weekly senders and 30.04% for daily senders. Brands sending one to three emails a month averaged 33.48% ([AdWave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/email-frequency-optimization)). The same dataset found 52% of businesses send at least one email a week, and 29.5% send exactly one a week.

Selzy's summary of the same research says open rates stay stable for monthly, weekly and twice-weekly sends, but drop with daily sends. Click-through rates generally rise with frequency, peaking at twice a week. For a local business, a steady weekly rhythm is a reasonable default. ## Which Platform Fits a Local Business?

- MailerLite suits a business under 10,000 contacts that needs basic automations and no ecommerce flows, based on the reviewer's guidance.
- Mailchimp suits a business that needs a transactional add-on for bookings, or the testing features in its Standard and Premium tiers.
- Klaviyo is the better fit for an online store with ecommerce flows, which is outside this comparison.
- Run the same contact count through each calculator on the same day before you commit.

## FAQ

### Is MailerLite cheaper than Mailchimp?

A reviewer says MailerLite costs roughly half of Klaviyo at 5,000 contacts and is 10% cheaper on annual billing. Mailchimp's calculator lists Standard at $75 a month for 5,000 contacts. Enter your own count into both calculators to compare.

### How much does Mailchimp cost for 500 contacts?

A 2026 pricing breakdown lists Essentials at $13 a month, Standard at $20 and Premium at $350 for 500 contacts ([Tutorsbot, secondary](https://tutorsbot.com/blog/mailchimp-pricing-2026-cost-per-contact)). Confirm the current figure on Mailchimp's pricing page.

### Does Mailchimp have a free plan?

The same breakdown says the free plan covers up to 500 contacts with 2,500 emails a month (secondary). Check the live pricing page for current limits.

### What happens if I exceed my Mailchimp send limit?

Overages apply if you exceed the contact or email send limit, and Mailchimp states that sending will be paused when a limit is exceeded ([Mailchimp pricing](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/)).
