## Why Do Water Damage Searches Convert Fast?

The person searching has a live problem. The Insurance Information Institute reports an average claim of $15,400 for water damage and freezing over 2019 to 2023, and that these claims made up 22.6% of homeowners insurance losses in 2023. The size of the loss explains why an owner calls the first company that answers, not the cheapest one.

That changes the ad. Speed and a human voice matter more than a long page. Your phone number, a promise of how quickly you arrive (only if you can keep it) and a mention of insurance paperwork support are enough on the first screen.

## How Should You Structure the Campaign?

Keep the account small. One Search campaign, three ad groups, and phone calls as the main conversion.

- **Emergency:** "water damage restoration near me", "burst pipe cleanup", "flooded basement help"
- **Service:** "water extraction", "structural drying", "mold after water damage"
- **Insurance:** "water damage insurance claim help"

Use phrase and exact match first. Google explains that exact match shows on searches with the same meaning or intent and offers the most control. Our [guide to keywords for contractors](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-contractors-in-2026) covers match types in more detail.

## When Should Your Ads Run?

Floods do not follow office hours, so only schedule ads you can answer. Google says that with an ad schedule your ads are only eligible to show during the times you specify, and that schedules follow the account time zone. If no one picks up at night, ads at night waste money.

Add a call asset. Google states clicks on a call asset on mobile cost the same as headline clicks. Turn on call reporting so calls count as conversions. Google notes this uses forwarding numbers and works on the Search Network.

## Which Negative Keywords Matter Most?

Many searches about water damage come from people who want a cheap fix, a job or a course. Add negatives for job intent (jobs, careers, hiring, salary), training (certification, course, IICRC class), DIY (diy, how to dry, rent dehumidifier) and unrelated topics (water damage on phone, water damage on wood furniture). Negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals.

Review the Search terms report daily during the first week. For the full routine, see [negative keywords for Google Ads](/blog/google-ads-negative-keywords-stop-wasting-budget).

## Are Local Services Ads Worth Adding?

Google's help lists water damage services among Local Services Ads categories. With these ads you pay for leads related to your business and services, set a budget and can pause the ad. Google also describes a Google Verified badge for businesses that pass its screening, and you need business registration or licence information, billing and reviews that meet category rules to go live.

Google's page gives no lead prices, so test it. Read our [Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-services-ads-complete-guide) for how it compares with regular Search ads.

## FAQ

### How much does a water damage lead cost on Google Ads?

LocaliQ lists $90.92 per lead for Home and Home Improvement in 2026. It has no separate restoration figure, so use it as a rough guide.

### Should I use Google Ads or Local Services Ads?

Many companies test both. Local Services Ads charge for leads, and eligibility depends on category and area. Check Google's eligibility tool.

### Do I need to answer calls 24 hours a day?

If your ads run at night, yes. Otherwise use an ad schedule so ads show only when someone can answer.

### Which conversions should I track?

Phone calls first, then form submissions. Count calls above a minimum length so wrong numbers do not distort results.

### How big is a typical restoration job?

No source here publishes a restoration job value. The Insurance Information Institute figure of $15,400 is the average insurance claim for water damage and freezing, not a company's invoice.
