DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 8.72 million internet users, 91.3% of the population, and ad reach of 7.01 million on YouTube, 5.05 million on Facebook and 5.00 million on Instagram. ## How Digital Is the Israeli Market?

Israel has 9.55 million people, 93.1% of them in urban areas, with a median age of 29.2 (UN data in DataReportal, October 2025). Mobile connections total 10.4 million, 109% of the population. Social media user identities total 7.01 million, 73.4% of the population, and 93.8% of adults 18 and over.

Speeds are high. Ookla data in the same report gives a median mobile download of 60.38 Mbps and a median fixed download of 256.99 Mbps. Pages and video ads load quickly for most customers, but test your site on a mid-range phone anyway.

## Which Platforms Reach the Most Israelis?

DataReportal lists late 2025 ad reach:

- YouTube: 7.01 million (73.4% of the population)
- Facebook: 5.05 million (52.9%)
- Instagram: 5.00 million (52.4%)
- TikTok, adults 18+: 4.49 million (69.2% of adults)
- LinkedIn: 3.10 million registered members
- Messenger: 2.70 million, Snapchat: 1.31 million, X: 881 thousand, Threads: 354 thousand

Audiences differ by gender. Instagram's adult audience is 54.2% female, Facebook's 48.0% female, TikTok's 44.0% female, and X's 24.0% female (inferred by the platform). Pick the channel where your customer actually is.

One warning from the report: TikTok shows a 20.1% drop between July and October 2025, but DataReportal says platform corrections can produce declines that are not real changes in behaviour. Do not read it as a collapse.

## What Does the Economy Look Like?

The World Bank's data page shows Israel's 2025 GDP at US$610.78 billion and GDP per capita at US$60,336.8. Growth was 2.9%, consumer price inflation 3.0% and unemployment 3.5%. A high-income market means customers compare quality and convenience, not only price.

This also means competition for attention is strong in dense areas such as Tel Aviv. Use your location, your hours and your reviews as the first selling points, then pay to put them in front of nearby people.

## Can You Pay for Google Ads in Advance in Israel?

No. Google Ads Help lists Israel, under Middle East, among the countries and territories where prepay is not available. It says postpay, or monthly invoicing if you are eligible, are the only supported payment settings there.

That means Google bills you after clicks happen. Set a daily budget you can afford and review spend weekly. No verified Israeli click prices were found, so this guide gives none. Your own account is the only benchmark worth using after the first month.

## What Does Israeli Spam Law Mean for Email and SMS?

Section 30A of the Communications Law restricts advertising messages. According to an Israeli law firm's 2016 summary, the law prohibits sending advertisements by fax, email, text message and automated call unless the recipient gave prior consent, an opt-in approach. Ads must be labelled "advertisement", and automated calls must offer an opt-out at the start.

This summary is secondary and is from 2016, so the rules may have changed. **Unverified:** current exemptions, penalties and enforcement. Check the current text or ask an Israeli lawyer before building a text-message or email list. Collect consent in writing and store it.

## What Should You Set Up First?

Before spending, make sure customers can find you. A complete Google profile helps every ad, so work through the [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist). Add Hebrew and, where relevant, Arabic or Russian versions of your key pages and ads.

Then connect a messaging path. Our guide to [WhatsApp and local marketing automation](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-local-marketing-automation) covers the technical options. DataReportal's Israel page does not report WhatsApp, so ask a sample of your customers which app they prefer.

## FAQ

A: YouTube, at 7.01 million in late 2025, according to DataReportal. Facebook follows with 5.05 million and Instagram with 5.00 million.

**Q: Can I prepay for Google Ads in Israel?**
A: No. Google lists Israel among countries where prepay is not available, and postpay is the supported setting.

A: 8.72 million, or 91.3% of the population, in October 2025.
