## How big is the California market for local businesses?

California had 39,355,309 residents on July 1, 2025, down 9,465 from 39,364,774 a year earlier, according to the Census Bureau's Vintage 2025 state file. The Census press release lists California among the states that experienced population decline ([Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026/population-growth-slows.html)).

The components differ. In the year to July 2025 natural increase was +109,715 and international migration +109,278, while domestic migration, people moving to or from other states, was -229,077. A business in California is selling to a stable base of residents plus arrivals from abroad. Local figures differ by county, so check your own postcodes before assuming either way.

## Which California metro areas are growing?

The Census Bureau publishes metro population estimates. Changes below are July 2024 to July 2025.

| Metro area | Population, July 2025 | Change | Change, % |

| Los Angeles-Long Beach-Anaheim | 12,844,441 | -62,454 | -0.48% |
| San Francisco-Oakland-Fremont | 4,630,041 | +6,607 | +0.14% |
| San Diego-Chula Vista-Carlsbad | 3,282,248 | -5,294 | -0.16% |
| Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario | 4,769,007 | +21,131 | +0.45% |
| Sacramento-Roseville-Folsom | 2,477,274 | +17,467 | +0.71% |
| San Jose-Sunnyvale-Santa Clara | 1,984,473 | +7,478 | +0.38% |

Growth is uneven. The Los Angeles metro lost 62,454 people while Sacramento (+17,467) and Riverside-San Bernardino (+21,131) grew. A salon in Sacramento and one in Los Angeles face different demand trends, so set budgets by metro and avoid state-wide campaigns.

## How do you keep Google Ads local in California?

Google Ads lets you target regions, cities or postal codes depending on the country, or a radius of at least 1 km around a location ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722043)). California is large, so a state-wide radius wastes money. Build one campaign per city or neighborhood cluster, such as Sacramento and Roseville, rather than one for the state.

Google recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting for Search, but says "Presence" may suit advertisers who only want people physically in the area. A neighborhood salon in Los Angeles may prefer Presence so it does not pay for clicks from people who are only interested in the city. Run a separate campaign for each town you serve so budget and results stay readable.

## Why does Google Business Profile come first?

A free profile appears in map results without a per-click cost. Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your ranking ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)).

Fill in every section, pick the most specific category and keep hours current. Ask customers for reviews with a link or QR code. Google prohibits offering free or discounted goods in exchange for a review ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)). Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers the full setup.

## The math: what does a $500 test return?

Inputs are **assumptions** built on the LocaliQ benchmark for this industry, applied to a salon (Beauty and Personal Care benchmark).

- Budget: $500 a month. CPC: $4.62. Clicks: 500 / 4.62 = about 108.
- Conversion rate: 10.35%, so about 11.2 leads. Cost per lead: about $45.
- Assumption: 50% of them become first appointments at $90 each, giving about 5.6 first appointments and $504 in first-sale revenue.

On first visits alone the test roughly breaks even. Repeat business decides the result, which is why a rebooking or reminder system matters more than click price. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to plan for that.

## How do you win new residents and keep customers?

With domestic migration negative and international arrivals positive, many newcomers are new to California and search for a hairdresser, gym or dentist. A landing page with a clear first-visit offer gives them a reason to choose you. Once they book, collect email addresses at checkout and send reminders. ## FAQ

### How much should a California small business spend on marketing?
There is no California standard. Start with free channels and a test budget you can afford to lose, then scale on cost per customer. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) covers the method.

### Are Google Ads cheaper in California?
Unknown. The benchmark source publishes US averages by industry, not by state. Check your own cost per click in the first month.

### Which California metro is growing fastest?
Among the larger metros in the Census file, Sacramento-Roseville-Folsom grew 0.71% and Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario 0.45%, while Los Angeles-Long Beach-Anaheim fell 0.48%. Smaller metros such as Stockton-Lodi (+0.82%) also grew.

### What comes first, SEO or ads?
Set up your Google Business Profile first because it is free. Add ads when you know what a new customer is worth.
