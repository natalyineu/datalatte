## Which Platform Should a Gulf Small Business Use First?

The table uses DataReportal's Digital 2026 reports, published in November 2025 with data mostly from October 2025.

| Country | Population | Instagram ad reach | Snapchat ad reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | 34.7 million | 18.2 million (52.4%) | 25.3 million (72.9%) |
| UAE | 11.4 million | 8.05 million (70.5%) | 5.13 million (44.9%) |
| Qatar | 3.13 million | 1.70 million (54.3%) | 1.24 million (39.5%) |

## Do You Need a Permit to Advertise on Social Media?

Check this before you hire creators or run content. In the UAE, an Advertiser Permit became mandatory on 1 February 2026 for individuals and businesses that advertise on social media, websites or blogs, paid or unpaid, according to Gulf News citing the UAE Media Council. Content breaches carry fines from AED 5,000 up to AED 1,000,000 depending on severity. Gulf News says personal accounts promoting your own products are exempt, but it is unclear whether a business account promoting its own products qualifies. Ask the Media Council before assuming you are exempt.

In Saudi Arabia, the Mawthooq licence was introduced for content creators who earn revenue from advertising. Arab News reported it in October 2022 at SR15,000 for three years. That is old reporting, and the current authority, fee and scope were not confirmed here, so check with the regulator before paying an influencer.

## When Should You Use Arabic and English?

No source consulted gives a language split for the Gulf, so decide with your own data. Run the same offer in Arabic and in English to the same city and compare cost per booking. Native-language copy written by a native speaker is a reasonable default for Arabic, and the UAE has a large mixed-language audience, but this page does not quantify either.

## How Should You Plan Around Ramadan?

Ramadan is expected to begin around 8 February 2027, with Eid Al Fitr around 9 or 10 March, according to The National. The start depends on moon sighting, so treat dates as a guide, not a schedule. Ramadan 2026 has already passed.

Published figures on how much Ramadan shifts spending or posting times were not found, so this page does not state them. Build the plan from your own history instead: look at last year's sales and message volume by week.

## FAQ

### Is Instagram or Snapchat better in the Gulf?

It depends on the country. Snapchat has the larger ad audience in Saudi Arabia; Instagram does in the UAE and Qatar, per DataReportal.

### When is Ramadan 2027?

It is expected to start around 8 February 2027, subject to moon sighting. Eid Al Fitr is projected for 9 or 10 March.

### Do I need a permit to advertise in the UAE?

An Advertiser Permit has been mandatory since 1 February 2026 for those who advertise online, with a stated exemption for personal accounts promoting their own products. Confirm your case with the UAE Media Council.

### How much do Google Ads cost in the UAE or Saudi Arabia?

Not published in any source consulted. Use Keyword Planner and a small test.

### Should I write in Arabic or English?

Test both for your audience. No source consulted quantifies the split.
