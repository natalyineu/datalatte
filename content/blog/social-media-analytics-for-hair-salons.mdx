A hair salon should track five numbers from social media: reach, profile visits, website or booking-link taps, booking-page visits and actual bookings. The first three come from Instagram Insights, which requires a professional account. The last two come from a tagged booking link and your booking software. Likes and follower counts are not on the list, because they do not show whether anyone booked.

This guide shows where each number lives, how to tag links so Google Analytics can see the traffic, and a worked example of the funnel. Industry benchmark rates for salons are not used, since a reliable published source was not found.

## Which Instagram Metrics Matter for a Salon?

Instagram's help center lists Insights metrics including reach, impressions or views, accounts reached, profile visits, website taps and follower demographics. You need a professional account to see them. For a salon, three of those connect to a booking: reach (how many people saw the content), profile visits (who looked at you) and website taps (who tried to book).

Follower count is not a measure of demand. Hootsuite's 2026 trends research says follower count and engagement rate are no longer reliable indicators of impact, and recommends focusing on audience alignment and return on investment instead.

## How Do You Tell Which Posts Bring Bookings?

Instagram shows taps on your link, but not whether the visitor booked. To close that gap, put a tagged link in your bio and in Stories. Google Analytics 4 reads UTM parameters, and Google recommends always using utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign. Values are case sensitive, so use lowercase every time, and missing parameters appear as "(not set)" in reports.

An example link: your booking page followed by a question mark and utm_source=instagram, utm_medium=social and utm_campaign=november-colour. Use a different campaign name for each promotion so the report separates them. If your booking tool does not use your own website, check whether it supports analytics or a Meta Pixel. ## What About Google Business Profile?

Many people who see your content search your name before booking. Google's help page defines the profile metrics: calls count taps on the call button (only if a phone number is listed), directions count people who requested directions, website clicks count taps on the website link, and bookings count completed reservations through a booking provider. Compare these month to month when you run a promotion. See our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## Should You Ask Clients How They Found You?

Yes, because no tool sees everything. Add a required "How did you hear about us?" field to the booking form, or have the front desk ask and record it for new clients. Use five fixed choices, such as Instagram, Google, a friend, walking past and other, so the answers can be counted.

This is the only way to catch the client who saw a Reel, then searched your name and booked from Google. Keep the question for a full month before judging.

## What Should You Check Each Month?

Use a short routine and one sheet:

1. Export reach, profile visits and website taps from Instagram Insights.
2. Read the Traffic acquisition report in Google Analytics, filtered to your instagram source.
3. Count new bookings from your booking software and the "how did you hear" answers.
4. Note which posts or Stories ran in the best weeks.
5. Decide one thing to repeat and one to stop.

Compare weeks, not single posts. Our [Instagram guide for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons) and [Reels and TikTok guide for salons](/blog/video-marketing-for-salons-reels-tiktok-guide) cover what to post.

## What Does a Month Look Like? A Worked Example

All numbers below are **assumptions** to show the arithmetic, not salon benchmarks.

| Step | Source of the number | Assumed value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accounts reached | Instagram Insights | 6,000 |
| Profile visits | Instagram Insights | 300 |
| Website taps | Instagram Insights | 30 |
| Booking-page visits from instagram source | Google Analytics | 24 |
| New bookings that say Instagram | Booking form field | 5 |
| Average service price | Your price list | $75 |
| Revenue from those bookings | Calculated | $375 |
| Staff time spent on content | Your own log | 8 hours |

Dividing 5 bookings by 300 profile visits gives about 1.7%. The useful comparison is that rate against your own previous month, not against another salon. If revenue is below the value of the time spent, change the content or add a stronger booking prompt. ## FAQ

### What social media metrics should a salon track?
Reach, profile visits, website taps, booking-page visits and bookings. Follower counts and likes show little about demand.

### Where do I see Instagram statistics?
In Instagram Insights, which needs a professional account. It lists reach, profile visits, website taps and follower demographics.

### How can I tell if Instagram brings bookings?
Use a tagged booking link, check the source in Google Analytics, and ask new clients how they found you.

### What is a good engagement rate for a salon?
No reliable published benchmark was found for salons, so compare against your own previous months.

### How often should I review the numbers?
Monthly for the full report and weekly for a quick look at bookings during a promotion.
