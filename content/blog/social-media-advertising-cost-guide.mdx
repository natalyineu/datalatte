Social media ads cost what the auction decides, but published benchmarks give a starting point. WordStream by LocaliQ lists Facebook traffic campaigns at $0.60 per click on average and lead campaigns at $1.80 per click and $27.39 per lead. Platforms also set minimum budgets: LinkedIn $10 a day and TikTok above $20 a day per ad group.

This guide covers what those numbers mean for a small local business, which platforms publish prices, and how to size a first test. Only figures from a checked source are used. Where a platform does not publish an average cost, the page says so.

## How Much Do Social Media Ads Cost?

For Facebook, LocaliQ's benchmarks (updated September 23, 2026) separate two objectives. Traffic campaigns average a 1.93% click-through rate and $0.60 per click. Lead campaigns average 2.70% CTR, $1.80 per click, an 8.54% conversion rate and $27.39 per lead.

Industry rows differ. For lead campaigns, Health and Fitness shows $1.84 per click and $27.11 per lead. Beauty and Personal Care shows $2.97 per click and $50.91 per lead. Home and Home Improvement shows $2.18 and $42.95. For traffic campaigns, Restaurants and Food averages $0.45 per click.

Cheap clicks are not cheap customers. A click costs less than a lead, and a lead costs less than a sale.

## Which Platform Fits a Local Business?

Pick by where your customers already look, not by price. Facebook and Instagram share one buying system and suit appointment, event and offer ads within a set distance. TikTok suits short video for younger local audiences. LinkedIn is mainly for business-to-business and costs more per contact.

## How Do You Set a First Test Budget?

Work backwards from what a customer is worth. Decide the most you could pay per lead and still profit, then divide by the lead cost you expect. A test needs enough leads to judge, so budget for at least a few dozen clicks per ad variation, not a single day of spend.

If a lead costs about $27, a daily budget of a few dollars will collect very few leads. A practical option is to optimise for a cheaper event, such as link clicks or messages, while you learn, then move to leads once the account has data.

## FAQ

### How much should a small business spend on social media ads?

There is no standard figure. Platforms let you start small, but a useful test needs enough clicks to judge. Start with an amount you can lose and compare cost per lead.

### Are Facebook ads cheaper than Google ads?

Per click, yes: LocaliQ lists $0.60 on Facebook traffic campaigns against $5.42 on Google Search across industries. Search shoppers are usually further along, so compare cost per customer, not per click.

### Do Instagram ads cost more than Facebook ads?

Meta runs both from one account. The sources checked do not publish a separate Instagram average, so no figure is given.

### Is boosting a post worth it?

The sources checked do not compare boosts with Ads Manager, so this page does not claim either way. Use the objective that matches the action you want.

### How do I know if my ads work?

Count bookings, calls and visits, not likes. Set up conversion tracking before spending.
