## Does Delaware's Lack of Sales Tax Change Your Marketing?

Only partly. The Delaware Division of Revenue states that Delaware does not impose a state or local sales tax. It does tax the seller instead: business and occupational gross receipts tax rates range from 0.0945% to 1.9914%, with no deduction for costs or labor.

So a price tag in Delaware has no sales tax added at the register, which you can mention in an ad. But the tax shifts onto the business, so check your margins before you build an offer around it. We could not find a published study showing how many shoppers cross the border from Pennsylvania or Maryland, so do not assume it. Ask customers where they travelled from, and let your own data decide.

## How Do You Target Wilmington, Newark or Dover Without Wasting Budget?

Use radius or named-city targeting around the area you can serve. Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km, and that a very small radius may show your ads only intermittently or not at all.

Google also lists two settings. "Presence" reaches people located in your target area. "Presence or interest" also reaches people who have shown interest in it, and Google recommends broad geo targeting as a best practice on Search. For a business that needs people to walk in, check your location report and test "Presence" if you see clicks from places you cannot serve.

Review the Search terms report every week and add negative keywords for services you do not offer.

## How Do You Show Up on Google Maps in Delaware?

Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and prominence. Reviews feed prominence: Google states that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. It also says there is no way to pay for a better local ranking.

That makes the free Business Profile your first job, because it works in a small market like Dover or Milford as well as in Wilmington. Fill in every field, pick accurate categories, add photos, and reply to reviews. ## Are Meta Ads Worth It for a Delaware Business?

Meta ads suit awareness and retargeting more than direct search demand. LocaliQ's 2026 Facebook benchmarks show traffic campaigns across industries average a $0.60 click and a 1.93% click-through rate. For lead campaigns the average click is $1.80 and the conversion rate is 8.54%.

By industry, lead campaigns in Beauty and Personal Care average a $2.97 click and 5.63% conversion; Health and Fitness averages $1.84 and 7.98%. These are national averages, and CPM was not reported.

Retargeting website visitors is cheaper to test than cold audiences because the audience is already small. See our guide to [Meta ads for local businesses](/blog/local-business-meta-ads) for the setup.

## What Does a Starter Budget Look Like? A Worked Example

Every input below is an **assumption**, not a Delaware benchmark. The click price comes from the national Beauty and Personal Care average.

| Input (assumption) | Value |

| Monthly Google Ads budget | $450 |

If a new client spends $90 on a first visit and returns twice a year, the ad cost is recovered inside the first visit. If the share who book drops to 30%, the cost per client doubles to about $150. ## What About Email, SMS and Seasonal Timing?

Email and SMS cost little once you have a list, but we did not verify industry open or conversion rates for Delaware businesses, so none are quoted here. Collect addresses at checkout with consent, and keep appointment reminders short.

For timing, a simple yearly rhythm works: retention campaigns in winter, new-client pushes in spring, steady spend in summer, and gift cards in November and December. This is a planning suggestion, not a measured Delaware pattern. Beach-area businesses should expect their own seasonal swings and can compare last year's bookings by month.

## FAQ

### How much should a Delaware small business spend on Google Ads?

There is no standard figure. At the national Beauty and Personal Care average of $4.62 a click, $450 a month buys roughly 97 clicks. Start there, measure cost per booking for 60 days, then scale what works.

### Does Delaware have sales tax?

No. The Delaware Division of Revenue says the state does not impose a state or local sales tax. It does charge a gross receipts tax on businesses at rates from 0.0945% to 1.9914%.

### Is local SEO or paid advertising better for a Delaware business?

Start with the free Business Profile, since Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence and cannot be paid for placement. Add Google Ads when you need bookings faster.

### How long does local SEO take?

No source gives a reliable timeline, and results vary by competition. Check your Profile views and calls monthly instead of expecting a date.
