CTV advertising in New Zealand means showing your video ad on streaming services watched on the TV, such as TVNZ+, ThreeNow, Neon and Sky Sport Now. A small business can usually start through a platform's sales team or a programmatic ad partner, with a modest budget and regional targeting. Rates and minimum spends are not published for most of these services, so ask each seller for a current rate card before you plan.

New Zealand has about 5.3 million people (Stats NZ's June 2025 estimate), roughly a third of them in Auckland ([Scoop](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/AK2604/S00323.htm)). That means audiences are smaller than in the US or UK and inventory is more limited, but free ad-supported streaming gives local advertisers a way onto the TV screen without the commitments of a linear broadcast campaign.

## The New Zealand streaming landscape

Ownership, audiences and ad products change regularly, so use the list below as orientation and confirm details with each seller (checked Oct 2026).

- **TVNZ+:** The streaming service of state broadcaster TVNZ. A recent platform upgrade added new ad formats (connected-TV login screen creative, branded content belts and sponsored title artwork) and more detailed audience data for advertisers ([B&T](https://www.bandt.com.au/tvnz-rolls-out-major-overhaul-with-new-ad-formats/)). It is usually the first platform to price for broad NZ reach.
- **ThreeNow:** A free, ad-supported streaming service. Sky Network Television completed its acquisition of ThreeNow on 1 August 2025 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ThreeNow)), so Sky now sells it alongside its own services.
- **Neon (Sky):** A subscription service. Sky introduced advertising on Neon in January 2024, with pre-roll ads on the entry-level tier and "Ads on Pause" across tiers, and no ads in kids' content ([Stoppress](https://stoppress.co.nz/news/sky-launches-ad-offering-on-neon/), [Newshub](https://www.newshub.co.nz/home/entertainment/2023/12/neon-nz-to-introduce-ads-into-streaming-service-in-january-2024-hikes-prices.html)).
- **Sky Sport Now:** Sky's sports streaming service, which has also been opened to digital advertising ([MAD Daily](https://www.mad-daily.com/?p=80976)). It suits gyms, physio and other businesses whose customers watch live sport.
- **Amazon Prime Video:** Amazon said it would expand ad-supported Prime Video to New Zealand, among other markets ([NewstalkZB](https://www.newstalkzb.co.nz/news/business/amazon-prime-video-nz-unveils-new-features-and-confirms-ads-are-on-the-way)). Ask Amazon Ads whether local businesses can buy it and what targeting is on offer.

## Regional targeting

Most platform sales teams target by region (for example Auckland, Wellington, Canterbury or Waikato), while programmatic partners can sometimes add finer location data. Availability depends on the campaign, so before you commit, confirm:

- Which regions and postcodes the platform can target
- The minimum audience size for your area
- Whether you can set a frequency cap

## Advertising rules in New Zealand

The Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) is the industry regulator. It sets advertising codes and runs a free consumer complaints process, and it does not pre-vet ads ([ASA](https://www.asa.co.nz/about-us/)). Under the ASA code's truthful presentation principle, ads must not mislead or be likely to mislead consumers, including through exaggeration, omission or unrealistic claims.

Practical points:

- Substantiate claims such as "best" or "cheapest".
- Show prices that include GST. New Zealand's Commerce Commission has taken the view that small print saying prices are GST-exclusive cannot fix a misleading headline price ([Commerce Commission](https://www.comcom.govt.nz/news-and-media/news-and-events/archive/more-action-on-fine-print-cellphone-accessories-fined-6000)).
- Individual broadcasters and platforms may run their own creative approval, so ask your seller for specs and review steps.
- The Privacy Act 2020, in force since 1 December 2020, governs how businesses handle personal information ([Office of the Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-act-2020/)). Ask any targeting partner how they comply.

## Budget guidance

We could not find published New Zealand CPMs or minimum budgets that we can verify, so do not plan from averages. Ask each seller for a current quote and work through the stages below.

| Stage | Approach |
|---|---|
| Test | Buy a single region on the platform with the lowest minimum, enough to run for a few weeks |
| Core local campaign | A managed or programmatic campaign on a main platform such as TVNZ+, with regional targeting and a frequency cap |
| Premium | Add a subscription service such as Neon where your customers are |
| Sports | Time a short campaign around major sport seasons if your customers watch live sport |

## How to set up a CTV campaign in New Zealand

### Step 1: Choose your buying path

- **Direct buy:** Contact TVNZ or another platform's sales team. Good for simple campaigns on one platform.
- **Programmatic:** Work with a partner who buys CTV inventory through a demand-side platform. Useful if you want to combine CTV with digital retargeting.
- **Self-serve:** Ask each seller whether a self-serve booking option exists for small advertisers.

### Step 2: Define your audience

Typical options include region, age range, content genre and device. For a single-city business, target your region, match the age range to your best customers, and set a frequency cap so households do not see the ad too often.

### Step 3: Produce your video creative

Check the platform's current technical specs before you produce anything. A strong local ad shows your business in the first few seconds, includes a recognizable place or neighborhood reference, makes one clear offer, and ends with your website or location. See our [CTV advertising creative specs guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026).

### Step 4: Set up measurement

CTV attribution is more limited than search, so use simple methods:

- **Promo code:** "Mention this ad for 10% off."
- **Dedicated landing page:** A short URL used only in the TV ad.
- **QR code** on a companion banner, if the format supports it.
- **Branded search:** Watch whether searches for your business name rise in Google Search Console.
- **Before and after:** Compare customer counts during and before the campaign.

## FAQ

**Q: How much does CTV advertising cost in New Zealand?**
A: It depends on the platform, region and time of year, and most sellers do not publish rates. Pricing is typically quoted as CPM (cost per thousand views). Ask TVNZ, Sky (for ThreeNow, Neon and Sky Sport Now) or a programmatic partner for a current rate card and any minimum spend.

**Q: Which streaming platform is best for NZ small business advertising?**
A: TVNZ+ is a common starting point for broad reach. Neon and Sky Sport Now suit subscriber audiences and sports fans, and ThreeNow is a free ad-supported option now owned by Sky. Choose based on where your customers watch.

**Q: Can I target specific suburbs in Auckland or Wellington?**
A: Platform-managed campaigns often target at the regional level, and programmatic buying may allow more granular targeting. Ask your partner what is currently available.

**Q: Can a business outside Auckland run CTV ads in New Zealand?**
A: Yes, if the platform offers targeting for your region. Confirm availability and audience size first.

**Q: Do NZ streaming ads need to be pre-cleared?**
A: The ASA does not pre-vet ads and works through complaints, but individual broadcasters or platforms may have their own approval steps. Ask your seller, and make sure your claims are truthful and substantiated.
