CTV advertising in New Zealand means showing your video ad on streaming platforms viewed on the living-room TV, such as TVNZ+, ThreeNow, Neon and Sky Sport Now, and a small business can usually start through a platform sales team or a programmatic ad partner with a modest monthly budget and regional targeting. Rates and minimums vary by platform and season, so ask each seller for a current rate card before you plan.

New Zealand is a compact, digitally connected market, with a population of roughly 5 million. Streaming has become a normal way to watch TV, and free ad-supported services give local advertisers a way to be on TV without the larger commitments traditionally associated with linear broadcast campaigns. The trade-off is scale: audiences are smaller than in the US or UK and inventory is more limited, but advertiser competition outside Auckland is also lighter.

## The New Zealand Streaming Landscape

Audience figures, ad products and ownership change regularly, so treat the descriptions below as an orientation and confirm details with each seller.

- **TVNZ+:** The streaming service of state broadcaster TVNZ, with free ad-supported viewing of much of TVNZ's content. It is usually the first platform to look at for broad NZ reach. Local businesses can often buy through TVNZ's sales team or through programmatic partners.
- **ThreeNow:** The streaming home for content from Three, including news and popular local shows. Its audience has tended to skew younger and more urban than TVNZ+'s.
- **Neon (Sky):** A premium subscription service. Where an ad-supported tier is available, its audience tends to be engaged and higher-income, which can suit premium salons, boutique studios and specialty services.
- **Sky Sport Now:** Live sport such as rugby, cricket and football. It suits gyms, physio and businesses with a strong sports-watching customer base, usually at premium rates.
- **Amazon Prime Video:** Offers ad-supported viewing in some markets; check current availability and targeting options for New Zealand.
- **FAST channels (such as Pluto TV):** Often the lowest-cost way to test CTV, if inventory is available in your region.

## Regional Targeting in New Zealand

New Zealand's population is concentrated: Auckland holds roughly a third of residents, followed by Wellington, Christchurch and Hamilton. Most platform sales teams target at the regional level (for example Auckland, Wellington, Canterbury or Waikato), while programmatic partners can sometimes layer in more detailed location data such as postcodes. Confirm what is available before you build a plan.

- **Auckland:** The largest and most competitive market, so expect higher prices.
- **Wellington:** A compact market with a strong professional and government audience.
- **Christchurch and Canterbury:** Often more cost-efficient because there is generally less advertiser competition.
- **Regional centers (Tauranga, Hamilton, Dunedin, Napier and others):** Lower competition can make it easier to stand out, but check that the platform actually offers regional targeting there.

## Advertising Rules in New Zealand

New Zealand's Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) administers advertising codes that apply to all media, including streaming. In general:

- Claims must be truthful and not misleading, and superlatives such as "best" need substantiation.
- Comparisons must be fair and verifiable.
- Testimonials must be genuine.
- Prices should be shown inclusive of GST.
- Health, fitness and body-image advertising is subject to additional codes.

Some broadcasters and platforms may have their own creative approval steps, so ask your seller about technical specs and review requirements. The Privacy Act 2020 also applies to the use of personal data in targeting; reputable platforms and partners handle this as part of their own compliance, but ask your vendor how they do it.

Prices differ by platform, region and season, so ask for current quotes. As a planning approach:

| Stage | Approach |
|---|---|

| Core local campaign | A managed or programmatic campaign on a main platform such as TVNZ+, with regional targeting and a frequency cap |

## Seasonality

Streaming tends to be higher in the cooler months, when evenings are longer and sport seasons are on, and slightly lower over the summer holidays when people are outdoors. Plan around your own demand: fitness and beauty often peak early in the year, hospitality around holidays and tourism seasons, and gifting toward the end of the year.

## How to Set Up a CTV Campaign in New Zealand

### Step 1: Choose your buying path

- **Direct buy:** Contact TVNZ or another platform's ad sales team. Good for simple campaigns on one platform.
- **Programmatic:** Work with a digital advertising partner who can buy CTV inventory through a demand-side platform. This suits businesses that want to combine CTV with digital retargeting.
- **Entry-level test:** Use the lowest-cost inventory available to learn before committing to a larger buy.

### Step 2: Define your audience

Typical options include region, age range, gender, content genre and device, and sometimes household characteristics. For a single-city local business, target your region, match the age range to your best customers, and set a frequency cap so households do not see the ad too often.

### Step 3: Produce your video creative

Check the platform's current technical specs before you produce anything. Typically you will need a high-definition video file, professionally mixed audio, and a 15- or 30-second length. Keep logos and text away from the edges of the frame. For more on specs, read our [CTV advertising creative specs guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026).

A strong local ad shows your business within the first few seconds, includes a recognizable place or neighborhood reference, makes one clear offer, and ends with your website or location.

### Step 4: Set up measurement

CTV attribution is more limited than for search ads, so use simple methods:

- **Promo code:** "Mention this ad for 10% off."
- **Dedicated landing page:** A short URL used only in the TV ad.
- **QR code on a companion banner,** if the format supports it.
- **Branded search:** Watch whether searches for your business name rise in Google Search Console.
- **Before and after:** Compare customer counts during and before the campaign.

## FAQ

**Q: How much does CTV advertising cost in New Zealand?**
A: It depends on the platform, the region and the time of year. Pricing is typically quoted as CPM (cost per thousand views), and premium sport and subscription inventory costs more than free ad-supported services. Ask TVNZ, ThreeNow, Neon or a programmatic partner for a current rate card and any minimum spend.

**Q: Which streaming platform is best for NZ small business advertising?**
A: TVNZ+ is a common starting point for broad reach. ThreeNow can suit younger urban audiences, Neon suits premium positioning, and Sky Sport Now suits sports-oriented businesses. Choose based on where your customers watch.

**Q: Can I target specific suburbs in Auckland or Wellington?**
A: Platform-managed campaigns often target at the regional level. Programmatic buying may allow more granular targeting, such as postcode groups. Ask your partner what is currently available.

**Q: Can a business outside Auckland run CTV ads in New Zealand?**
A: Yes, if the platform offers targeting for your region. Lower competition in smaller markets can make regional campaigns attractive, but confirm availability and audience size first.

**Q: Do NZ streaming ads need to be pre-cleared?**
A: The ASA operates mainly on a complaint basis, but individual broadcasters or platforms may have their own approval steps. Ask your seller, and make sure your claims are truthful and substantiated.
