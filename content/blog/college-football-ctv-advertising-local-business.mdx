A local business can reach college football fans on connected TV (CTV) until the national championship on Monday 25 January 2027, but the games are spread across ESPN, ABC, Fox, CBS, NBC and several streaming apps. The cheapest route is usually standard CTV scheduled to Saturday game windows and limited to your area, not a named-game package. No public rate card exists, so get written quotes.

This guide is for US businesses with customers near a college town or a big fan base. It covers where games air, the remaining 2026-27 calendar, three ways to buy, and a break-even example. Prices come from a vendor benchmark, flagged as such.

## Where Does College Football Stream in 2026?

Sports Media Watch's schedule page lists the ESPN family (ESPN, ABC, ESPN+, SEC Network, ACC Network), the Fox family (FOX, FS1, BTN), the CBS family, the NBC family, The CW, TNT, truTV, USA Network, MW+ and HBO Max. The page says more windows are added as announced, so the lineup can change.

Streaming access follows the networks. Fox says its games stream on FOX One. Sports Media Watch lists Peacock for NBC simulcasts and exclusive Big Ten and Notre Dame games, and Paramount+ for CBS simulcasts. For you, the question is not which app, but which seller will quote a price and a delivery area.

## What Is Left on the 2026-27 Calendar?

The season started on 29 August. The College Football Playoff schedule published by fbschedules.com lists the first round on 18 and 19 December 2026, the Fiesta Bowl on 30 December, three quarterfinals on 1 January 2027, semifinals on 14 and 15 January and the title game on 25 January 2027 in Las Vegas at 7:30 pm ET on ABC/ESPN.

Network labels for some games differ across sources, so check the final listing before you plan a flight. Notably, several Playoff games are listed on TNT, truTV and HBO Max, so not every big game is on Disney or Fox.

## How Big Is the Audience?

Fox's Ohio State and Texas game drew 16.62 million viewers in Week 1 of 2025, the largest opening-week college football audience on record, according to Sports Media Watch. The record carries a caveat: Nielsen's Big Data + Panel measure dates back only about three years, and the previous Week 1 high of 12.34 million (2017) predates it.

These are national numbers for one marquee game, not your delivery area. Most Saturdays have far smaller audiences. Ask a seller for estimated impressions in your ZIP codes before you commit.

## How Do You Buy College Football Attention?

| Route | What you get | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Named-game or package from a network sales team | Ads in specific games | Most control, highest price; ask for rates and minimums in writing |
| Programmatic live-sports package | Sports content across several streamers | Easier to start; a particular game may not be included |
| Standard CTV, scheduled to Saturday windows | Regular streaming inventory in your area | Cheapest, but it reaches fans by time and place, not by content |

The third option fits a small budget. Set the schedule to the Saturday noon, afternoon and evening windows and restrict delivery to your ZIP codes or radius. ## What Do CTV Ads Cost?

AdWave's Q2 2026 benchmark page puts free ad-supported streaming at about $15 to $25 per thousand impressions (CPM), standard programmatic on premium services at $25 to $45, and premium direct and live sports at $45 to $65. AdWave labels these as industry benchmarks, not rate cards, and notes that sources differ. It does not give college football on its own.

That means no verified college football CPM exists in this guide. Older figures for individual game types should not be used to plan a budget. For general pricing see [what CTV advertising costs](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data).

## What Does a $3,000 Test Need to Earn?

**Worked example (all inputs are assumptions).** The CPMs are midpoints of AdWave's ranges: $35 for standard programmatic and $55 for premium live sports. The $60 order value is an assumption.

| | Standard, scheduled | Premium live sports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | $3,000 | $3,000 |
| CPM (assumption) | $35 | $55 |
| Impressions | 85,714 | 54,545 |
| Households at 4 views each | 21,429 | 13,636 |
| Customers needed to cover cost at $60 | 50 | 50 |
| Share of households that must buy | 0.23% | 0.37% |

Compare that with the households in your actual delivery area. If the table reaches more households than you serve, cut the budget instead of spreading it thin. Premium games may deliver better response, but this guide found no published local-business evidence of that.

## What Should the Creative Say?

Say what is true about your business and your area: a game-day offer, a pickup time, a neighborhood. Use a QR code and a promo code so you can count responses. Avoid team names, logos and conference marks unless you have permission, because those are protected. This is general information, not legal advice; ask an attorney before using them.

Pair the ad with an up-to-date Google Business Profile, since people check hours and directions after seeing an ad. ## FAQ

### When does the college football season end?
The national championship is on Monday 25 January 2027 in Las Vegas, at 7:30 pm ET on ABC/ESPN, per the published Playoff schedule.

### Which streaming services show college football in 2026?
Sports Media Watch lists ESPN plans, FOX One, Peacock and Paramount+, plus live TV services such as Fubo, DIRECTV STREAM, Sling, Hulu + Live TV and YouTube TV. Coverage varies by game.

### Is college football CTV cheaper than NFL?
No verified comparison is published. AdWave gives one range for premium direct and live sports ($45 to $65 CPM), not split by league.

### Can a small business buy a single game?
Possibly, through a network sales team or a programmatic package, but minimums are not published. Ask for a written quote, or schedule standard CTV to game windows.
