A New Brunswick small business should market in the language of its town, keep its Google Business Profile accurate and test a small Google Ads budget. The province had 20,631 employer businesses in December 2024, and 98.2% of them were small (1 to 99 employees), according to Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED).

Language matters more here than in most provinces. In the 2021 census, 30.0% of New Brunswickers had French as their only first official language spoken, and 41.9% could hold a conversation in French. ## How Many Small Businesses Compete in New Brunswick?

ISED counts 20,256 small, 325 medium-sized and 50 large employer businesses in New Brunswick, or 29.2 businesses per 1,000 residents aged 18 and over. Nationally, small businesses employed 5.8 million people in 2024, 46.6% of the private sector workforce.

These counts cover employer businesses only. Self-employed people with no staff are not in them, so the real number of competitors is higher.

## Which Language Should Your Marketing Use?

Statistics Canada shows how different the cities are. French is the only first official language for 93.4% of Edmundston residents and 65.6% of Bathurst, but for 32.6% in Moncton, 6.5% in Fredericton and 3.3% in Saint John. Across the province 34.0% could conduct a conversation in both languages.

A Moncton cafe may want a bilingual website and Google Business Profile. A Fredericton shop can start in English and add French pages if customers ask for them. Google advises keeping language and location targeting consistent, so run separate English and French campaigns rather than one mixed ad.

## FAQ

### How many small businesses are in New Brunswick?

ISED counted 20,256 small employer businesses (1 to 99 employees) out of 20,631 in December 2024. Businesses without employees are not counted.

### Should a New Brunswick business advertise in French?

It depends on the town. French is the first official language of most people in Edmundston and Bathurst but a small share in Saint John and Fredericton.

### What does Google Ads cost in New Brunswick?

No provincial figure is published. The US all-industry average is $5.42 per click. Check your own account for the real cost.

### Is Google Business Profile worth it?

Yes. It is free and sits where people compare nearby options. Keep hours accurate.
