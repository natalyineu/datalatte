## Is TikTok Worth It for a European Salon?

It can be, if you can make short videos on a regular schedule and handle the bookings that follow. The audience is large: TikTok reports 189 million monthly users in the EU for the first half of 2026. UK users are outside the EU figure, and Ofcom's data could not be opened, so no UK number is given here.

What is missing is proof of salon returns. No source opened for this guide gives a per-country conversion rate for salons. Judge TikTok by your own booking source question for the first three months, not by views.

## What Should a Salon Post on TikTok?

Post work that shows a process and a result. These are formats to test, not formats proven to win.

- **Transformations:** a before shot, the process sped up, and the finished look from more than one angle
- **Trend recreations:** a cut or colour people are searching for, done on a client it suits
- **Stylist introductions:** a short video of who does what, so people book a named person
- **Answers to common questions:** how long a colour lasts, how to care for it, what a service costs to maintain
- **Behind the scenes:** setting up the chair, mixing a colour, a tidy-up after the last client

Film vertically on a phone. Keep one spot in the salon with good light. ## Do You Need Client Consent to Film Them?

Yes, get clear permission first. In the UK and EU, a client's face and voice can be personal data. The UK Information Commissioner's Office says valid consent must be freely given, specific, informed and an unambiguous indication of the person's wishes, that consent should be recorded, and that people can withdraw it.

In practice: ask before you film, say where the video will be posted, write down the answer in the booking notes, and delete the video if the client changes their mind. The ICO says its consent guidance is under review because of the Data (Use and Access) Act, so check the current version. Rules differ by country, so check with your national data protection authority. Our guide to [before-and-after photos and the law](/blog/hair-salon-before-after-photos-legal-and-converting) covers release forms.

## How Do You Turn Views Into Bookings?

Make booking a single step from the video. TikTok allows a link in the profile, so point it at your booking page, not the home page. Say in the video how to book. Reply to comments and messages during working hours.

Treat TikTok as a lead source and track it. Add "How did you hear about us?" to your booking form with TikTok as an option, and use a tagged link in your bio so analytics can separate the visits. If a video does well, expect questions in the comments and have a short answer ready that includes your booking link.

Reviews still matter. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US consumers found 97% read reviews of local businesses, so keep your Google profile current too. Our [nail salon marketing guide for Europe](/blog/nail-salon-marketing-europe) covers local search in more detail.

## How Do Spark Ads Work, and What Do They Cost?

Spark Ads are a TikTok ad format that runs an organic post as an ad. TikTok's help page describes it as a native format that lets you use organic posts and their features in your advertising, and says all Spark Ads engagement is attributed to the original organic post. You can set how long an authorisation code lasts, which controls how long a creator's post can be used.

Costs: TikTok's budgeting page lists a minimum daily ad group budget of 30 USD for web conversion campaigns in North America and EMEA. No published minimum was found for every campaign type or for the in-app boost button, so check Ads Manager before you plan. See our [TikTok ads guide for hair salons](/blog/tiktok-ads-for-hair-salons) for the setup.

## What Does a TikTok Month Cost? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**, not benchmarks.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Posts per month | 12 (3 a week) |
| Time per post, filming plus editing | 60 minutes |
| Staff time | 12 hours |
| Value of an hour of stylist time | 30 (local currency) |
| Time cost | 360 |
| Ad test | 7 days at the 30 USD daily minimum, about 210 |
| Value of a new client's first visit | 120 |
| Total monthly cost with ad test | about 570 |
| New clients needed to break even on first visits | about 5 (570 / 120 = 4.75) |

On these assumptions, you need roughly five new clients per month from TikTok to cover time and a one-week ad test on first visits alone. Without the ad test, three clients cover the time cost (360 / 120). Repeat visits are extra. If no clients mention TikTok after three months of steady posting, stop paying for ads and keep the free posts you can make quickly.

## FAQ

### Can TikTok work if I do not want to be on camera?

Yes. You can film hands, hair and finished results without showing your face. The consent rule still applies to clients who appear in the frame.

### How often should a salon post?

No source opened for this guide sets a posting frequency that works for salons. Pick a schedule you can keep for three months, such as two or three posts a week, and judge it on bookings.

### Do I need a different approach in each European country?

Language and local beauty trends differ, and captions in the local language make sense for local clients. No published country-level data was found on salon content performance, so test in your own town and look at your booking source answers.

### Should I post on both TikTok and Instagram?

You can reuse the same video on both. Our [TikTok marketing guide for hair salons](/blog/tiktok-marketing-for-hair-salons) compares both.

### Are TikTok ads worth it for a small salon?

Only after a post has proved itself organically. Spark Ads let you promote that post. Start with a short test, track bookings, and stop if there are none.
