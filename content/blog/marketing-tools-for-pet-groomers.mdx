## Which Marketing Tools Does a Pet Groomer Actually Need?

Four jobs matter more than any app: being found, being booked, being trusted and being remembered.

- **Found:** Google Business Profile. Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is "no way to request or pay for a better local ranking".
- **Booked:** a booking tool with reminders and card on file.
- **Trusted:** reviews. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults found 97% read reviews for local businesses and 47% will not use a business with fewer than 20.
- **Remembered:** email or text reminders for the next groom.

Paid ads come later. Pet-related ads are not expensive to test: WordStream by LocaliQ lists Animals and Pets at $4.06 per click and a $31.50 cost per lead. Google's Local Services Ads list also names pet grooming as an eligible category in the US.

## What Does a Booking Tool Really Cost?

Subscription price is only part of it. Fresha's page lists online card payments at 2.79% + $0.20 and in-person payments at 2.29% + $0.20 per transaction. Texts beyond 100 free per team member a month cost $0.02 each, and marketing texts $0.08 each.

The marketplace fee matters most. Fresha charges new clients found through its marketplace a 20% one-time commission with a $6 minimum, while returning clients and bookings from your own link, Facebook or Instagram carry no fee.

Worked example. All inputs are **assumptions** except the Fresha rates:

- A 3-groomer shop on the Team plan: 3 x $14.95 = $44.85 a month, the page's own example.
- Average groom: $80 (assumption).
- A marketplace new client: 20% of an $80 first booking = $16 (assumption that the fee is based on the first booking value; the page does not say).
- The same client booked through your own Google Business Profile link: $0 in commission.
- Card fee on an $80 online payment: 2.79% x $80 + $0.20 = about $2.43.

Ten new clients a month from the marketplace would cost about $160 in commission under that assumption. Sending the same people to your own link saves it. Compare that with our [Google Business Profile guide for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-for-pet-groomers).

## How Do You Collect Reviews With a Tool, Within the Rules?

Most booking tools can send a review link after the appointment. Send it to every client, not only the happy ones. The FTC's rule on fake reviews took effect on 21 October 2024 and bars offers of incentives that expressly or implicitly require a positive review. Its staff guidance says asking only customers you expect to be satisfied can still violate the FTC Act.

BrightLocal also reports that 89% of consumers expect owners to reply to reviews and 42% are unlikely to use a business that never does. Reply to every review, short and specific. Our [Google reviews guide](/blog/google-business-profile-reviews) has examples.

## Is Email Worth Setting Up for a Groomer?

Yes, once there are repeat clients. Grooming is a recurring service, so a reminder at the right interval brings people back without ad spend. Mailchimp's free plan is enough to start: 250 contacts and up to 500 sends a month. Past that, compare paid tiers on the vendor page, because the page showed first-year discounted prices only.

Text reminders suit groomers too, since most clients answer messages faster than email. See our [SMS marketing guide for pet groomers](/blog/sms-marketing-for-pet-groomers) for how to set it up.

## What Should You Set Up First?

1. Verify and complete your Google Business Profile: address or service area, hours, services, photos.
2. Choose one booking tool and put its link on the profile.
3. Ask every client for a review after the visit.
4. Start an email list from your client records, only with their consent.
5. Test ads last, with a small budget. Read our [pet groomer SEO tips](/blog/pet-groomer-seo-tips) and the [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) before you spend.

## FAQ

### What is the best booking software for a pet groomer?
There is no single best option. Fresha lists plans from $14.95 per team member a month, and MoeGo's mobile grooming plans start at $49. Pick by who you serve: mobile groomers benefit from route features, salons from team calendars.

### Is Google Business Profile enough on its own?
It is the best first step, but not enough alone. Google says complete, verified profiles with reviews are more likely to show up, and you still need a way to book clients.

### How many reviews does a groomer need?
BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 47% of consumers will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews, and 74% only consider reviews from the last three months.

### Do pet groomers need paid ads?
Not at first. If you test them, LocaliQ's Animals and Pets benchmark is $4.06 per click and $31.50 per lead, but your own results will differ.

### Can I use a free email tool?
Yes. Mailchimp's free plan allows 250 contacts and 500 sends a month. Collect addresses with consent.
