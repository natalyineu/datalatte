## How Do You Get Found on Google in Glasgow?

Start with your Business Profile. Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. Relevance is how well your profile matches the search. Distance is how far you are from the searcher. Prominence is how well known you are, including links to your site and review count. Google also says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

So fill in your categories, services, hours and photos, and keep them current. Use the neighbourhood name where it is true, for example in the description and on your website. For the same steps in another UK city, see our [Belfast guide](/blog/small-business-marketing-belfast-uk).

## How Many Reviews Do You Need?

BrightLocal's 2026 Local Consumer Review Survey asked 1,002 US adults, so treat it as a guide to behaviour, not a Scottish measure. It found 97% read reviews for local businesses, 47% will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews, and 74% look for reviews from the last three months. Also, 89% expect owners to reply.

Ask in person and with a link or QR code from your profile. Do not offer discounts for reviews, since Google's policy says incentivized reviews are not allowed and will be removed. ## What Does Google Ads Cost in Glasgow?

No Glasgow-specific figure is published in the sources checked for this page. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks are US averages: $5.42 per click across all industries, and $2.05 for Restaurants and Food. UK agency blogs give a wide range of overall averages that disagree with each other, so none are quoted here.

Use your own account. Run a small campaign for a month and record your real cost per click and cost per lead. Then plan from that data.

## How Do You Keep Glasgow Ads Local?

Google Ads lets you target by region, city or postal code, or by a radius around an address. The minimum radius is 1 km, and very small radii may show ads only intermittently or not at all. Google recommends "Presence or interest" for Search but notes that "Presence" alone may suit sensitive verticals.

For a business that serves one area, test a small radius first, then look at the Search terms report weekly and add negative keywords for searches you do not serve. Keep your language and location settings consistent, as Google advises.

## What Are the UK Rules for Email and Text Marketing?

The ICO says you must not send marketing emails or texts to individuals without specific consent. The exception is a soft opt-in for existing customers: you must have offered a simple opt-out when you collected their details, offer it again in every message, and only market similar products or services. Prospects and bought-in lists are not covered.

Sole traders and some partnerships count as individuals. Companies and other corporate bodies can be emailed without individual consent, though the ICO suggests keeping a list of businesses that object. ## FAQ

### How much should a Glasgow business spend on Google Ads?

There is no published Glasgow figure. Start with an amount you can afford to lose, such as the £300 in the example, and track calls and bookings for a month. Increase only when cost per lead is below what a customer is worth.

### Is Google Business Profile free?

Creating and managing a profile is free. Google also says you cannot pay for a better local ranking.

### Do the US review statistics apply to Glasgow?

The BrightLocal survey covers 1,002 US adults, so they may not match Glasgow exactly. They are still useful evidence that review count, recency and replies matter to many shoppers.

### Can I text customers about offers?

Only with consent, or under the soft opt-in for existing customers with an opt-out in every message. The ICO guidance treats texts the same as emails.
