A small business in Georgia, the country in the South Caucasus, should build on Facebook and Messenger first. DataReportal lists a Facebook ad reach of 3.05 million, 80.1% of the population, and Messenger at 2.50 million. Geostat counted 241,701 active enterprises in 2024, so competition is real. Add Instagram and TikTok for younger customers and Google Business Profile for people searching on maps.

This guide is for cafes, shops, salons and service firms in Georgia. Figures come from the sources at the end, checked on 1 November 2026. No Georgian Google Ads click prices were found, so none are quoted.

## Which Platforms Reach the Most People in Georgia?

Facebook, by a wide margin. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report lists 3.81 million people, 3.12 million internet users (81.9%) and 6.46 million mobile connections, 170% of the population. Social media user identities total 3.05 million, 80.1%.

| Platform | Ad reach, late 2025 | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 3.05 million | 80.1% of population |
| TikTok (adults 18+ only) | 2.57 million | 89.5% of adults 18+ |
| Messenger | 2.50 million | 65.7% of population |
| Instagram | 1.85 million | 48.6% of population |
| LinkedIn (members) | 850 thousand | 22.3% of population |
| YouTube, Snapchat | Not reported | n/a |

These are ad-tool estimates, not active user counts, and platforms revise them. Use the table as a ranking.

## How Many Businesses Compete for That Attention?

Geostat's Business sector in Georgia publication lists 984,510 registered enterprises in 2024, of which 241,701 were active, about one in four. Of the active ones, 167,920 were individual entrepreneurs and 73,781 legal persons.

| Where the active enterprises are (2024) | Count | Share of 241,701 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wholesale and retail trade, vehicle repair | 74,488 | 30.8% |
| Accommodation and food service | 10,474 | 4.3% |
| City of Tbilisi | 107,373 | 44.4% |
| Adjara region | 27,333 | 11.3% |

An enterprise is a registered business, not a shop, so one owner can run several outlets. Still, almost a third of active enterprises sell goods, and nearly half sit in Tbilisi. A retailer there competes with many similar businesses for the same Facebook audience.

## Why Does Messenger Matter So Much?

Because replies drive the sale. Messenger's 2.50 million ad reach is about 82% of Facebook's (2.50 divided by 3.05). People who see your ad often want to ask price, size or availability, and an answer within minutes wins the order.

Turn on instant replies and saved answers for opening hours, prices and directions, and check the inbox during business hours. This costs nothing and often beats spending more on reach.

## Can a Georgian Business Run Google Ads?

Yes. Google's list of billing countries includes Georgia. In that list the account currency and payment method columns read "All", but Google says the options you see depend on your billing country, currency and payment setting, and that prepay is not offered in many countries.

Open the billing page in your own account to see what is available. No source checked publishes Georgian click prices, and DataReportal does not report Google ad reach for Georgia.

Search ads suit services people look for with intent, such as a dentist or a car repair. For a cafe, Facebook and Maps usually come first. The [Google Ads cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-google-ads-cost-for-small-businesses-real-2026-pricing-breakdown) explains how to judge results.

## What Does a First Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for a cafe in Tbilisi that runs a Facebook offer answered through Messenger. They show the method, not Georgian results. Currency is "units" of lari.

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Gross profit per new regular customer over a year (assumption) | 90 units |
| Messenger enquiries that become regulars (assumption) | 15% |
| Most you can pay per enquiry and still break even | 90 x 15% = 13.5 units |
| Test budget (assumption) | 270 units |
| Enquiries needed to break even | 270 / 13.5 = 20 enquiries |

If twenty enquiries arrive for 270 units, the test paid for itself on a one-year view. If ten arrive, either lower the budget or improve the offer. Compare your result with the plan in the [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide).

## How Do You Get Found on Google Maps in Georgia?

Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. It also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Fill in every field, use real photos, reply to every review and keep the name, address and phone number identical everywhere. In Tbilisi and Batumi, where many businesses sit close together, a precise pin and clear category matter more than a long description. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes through each field.

Do not offer a discount or a free item in return for a review. Google's contributed content policy says merchants must not offer incentives, such as payment, discounts, free goods or services, in exchange for posting a review. Asking is fine. Rewarding is not. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for ways to ask.

## What Mistakes Do Georgian Small Businesses Make?

These are recommendations, not study findings.

- Posting on Facebook with no plan for replying to Messenger.
- Copying a competitor's offer in a crowded category instead of showing what is different.
- Boosting every post. Pick one offer and one audience.
- Ignoring Maps while 44.4% of active enterprises compete in the same capital.

## FAQ

### How many people use Facebook in Georgia?

DataReportal reports a Facebook ad reach of 3.05 million in late 2025, 80.1% of the population. It is an ad-tool estimate, not a count of active users.

### How many businesses are active in Georgia?

Geostat lists 241,701 active enterprises in 2024 out of 984,510 registered ones.

### Is Google Ads available in Georgia?

Georgia appears in Google's list of billing countries. No source checked publishes Georgian click prices, so run a small test.

### Is TikTok worth using in Georgia?

DataReportal lists TikTok at 2.57 million adults, 89.5% of adults 18 and over, though the base differs from other platforms. Test short videos if your customers are young.

### Does this guide cover the US state of Georgia?

No. It covers the country in the South Caucasus. Platform figures, business counts and Google Ads availability here all refer to that country.
