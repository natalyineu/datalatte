**How much do YouTube ads cost?** YouTube ads are priced by auction, so Google publishes no fixed rate. AdWave's 2026 estimates put skippable in-stream ads at about $0.01 to $0.10 per view (CPV) and standard video at $6 to $15 per 1,000 impressions (CPM). Another dataset reports an average of $0.026 per view and $3.53 CPM. Treat all of these as vendor estimates and check Google Ads forecasts for your area.

This guide is for local businesses planning a first YouTube test. It explains how Google bills each format, why the published benchmarks disagree, and how to turn a CPM into a cost per booking. Examples use labelled assumptions.

## How Does YouTube Bill for Ads?

Google's help page sets out the billing by format:

- **Skippable in-stream:** with CPV bidding you pay when a viewer watches 30 seconds of your video, or the full ad if it is shorter, or interacts with it, whichever comes first. With Target CPM, Target CPA or Maximize conversions bidding you are billed per impression.
- **Non-skippable in-stream and bumper:** Target CPM bidding, so you pay per impression.
- **In-feed:** you are charged when someone clicks to watch.
- **Shorts:** you are charged for an impression, view or engagement.

Viewers can skip skippable ads after 5 seconds ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2467968)). Formats and billing rules change, so confirm inside Google Ads ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2375464)). Our [guide to YouTube ad formats](/blog/youtube-ad-formats-explained-skippable-non-skippable-bumper-discovery) shows each one.

## What Are Typical YouTube CPV and CPM Ranges?

AdWave, an ad reseller, lists skippable in-stream at $0.01 to $0.10 per view, or $0.10 to $0.30 in competitive verticals such as finance and B2B. It lists standard video at $6 to $15 CPM and non-skippable at $10 to $25 CPM. It says these are industry estimates, not Google rates, and that there is no minimum budget on YouTube itself, suggesting $10 to $20 a day for a test.

Mega Digital, a Google Partner agency, reports an average CPV of $0.026 and CPM of $3.53, citing Google data plus its own client data. Its stated period is January to September 2024, and part of the client data is described as search campaigns, so apply it with care. Its industry rows show Health and beauty at $0.034 CPV and $3.40 CPM, Restaurants at $0.037 and $3.70, and Healthcare and Insurance at $0.071 and $7.10.

## Why Do the Benchmarks Disagree?

They measure different things: different years, formats, countries and advertisers. A $3.53 average and a $6 to $15 range can both be right for their samples. For a local business the only reliable number is your own, from a small test in your city. Narrow audiences, small towns and weak creative push cost per view up.

Factors that move price:

1. **Location and competition.** Large metro markets typically cost more.
2. **Format.** Skippable, bumper and non-skippable ads bill differently.
3. **Audience.** Very narrow targeting can raise cost per impression.
4. **Bid strategy.** Manual bids give control, and automated strategies are less predictable until there is conversion data.

## How Do CPM and CPV Relate?

The formula is cost per view = CPM divided by 1,000 times the view rate. These inputs are **assumptions**: a $10 CPM and 20% of impressions becoming paid views. Then 1,000 impressions cost $10 and produce 200 views, so each view costs $0.05. If only 10% of impressions become views, the same CPM gives $0.10 per view.

That is why creative quality matters as much as budget: fewer people watching for the same money raises cost per view. Hook viewers in the first five seconds, since they can skip then.

It depends on the goal. Search captures people ready to book, and YouTube builds awareness. ## FAQ

### Can I run YouTube ads with a $200 budget?
Yes, but reach is small. At the assumed $0.05 CPV, $200 buys about 4,000 views, enough to test a creative and offer but not to dominate a market.

### Should I run YouTube ads or Google Search ads?
They solve different problems. Search captures people already looking, while YouTube builds awareness. Start with search and add YouTube when you have a baseline.

### How long does it take for YouTube ads to work?
Delivery data appears within a day or two. Plan on a few weeks for meaningful results while the campaign learns.

### Can I target people near my shop?
Yes, by location or radius, though very small areas may not deliver enough impressions.

### Do I need a professional video?
Not necessarily. A clear phone video with good audio and an honest message often works for local services. Fix creative before increasing spend.
