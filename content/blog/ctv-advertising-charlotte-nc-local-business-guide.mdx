CTV advertising in Charlotte, NC lets local businesses run full-screen video ads on streaming services such as Hulu, Peacock, Tubi and Amazon Prime Video, aimed at households in the ZIP codes where customers live. CTV builds brand familiarity rather than clicks, so it works best as a planned trial of four to eight weeks, measured against existing search and Google Business Profile numbers. Platform rate cards and minimums change, so confirm current figures in each ad manager before committing budget.

## Which Charlotte streaming platforms should a local business test first?

Hulu, Peacock, Tubi and Amazon Prime Video are the platforms most often named for Charlotte CTV buying. Start with one platform and one clear goal rather than spreading a small budget across all of them. A self-serve platform with ZIP-code targeting keeps the first test simple to set up and easy to read.

The table below summarises how each platform is typically used. It is a planning aid, not a performance ranking, and the sources used for this guide do not cover streaming audiences or rate cards.

| Platform | Typical use for a local business | What to check before buying |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hulu | General starting point with ZIP-level targeting | Current minimum spend in Hulu's ad manager |
| Peacock | Reaching households that watch live sports | Whether your customers watch the content you can buy |

Ask each platform for its current minimum spend and rate card before comparing. The same testing method applies in other markets, such as [Detroit](/blog/ctv-advertising-detroit-mi-local-business-guide) or [Minneapolis](/blog/ctv-advertising-minneapolis-mn-local-business-guide).

## How should a Charlotte business set ZIP targeting and frequency?

ZIP-code targeting is the main lever. Enter the business address with a radius, or choose specific ZIP codes where existing customers live. Check the customer list first. A handful of postcodes often account for most sales, and those are the ones worth selecting by hand.

Demographic filters such as age and household composition can narrow the audience further. A fitness studio might target a working-age band, while a family-focused business might target households with children. Keep the first test to one or two filters so that any change in results can be traced to a specific setting.

Frequency needs a deliberate setting too. Set a cap on how often one household sees the ad each week, then review reports for signs of fatigue, such as falling completion rates or viewer complaints. Use the platform's recommended cap as the starting point and adjust after about two weeks of data.

## What do CTV budgets look like for Charlotte? A worked example

The inputs below are assumptions for illustration, not sourced figures. Replace them with the numbers the platform quotes.

- Cost per 1,000 impressions (CPM): $25 (assumption)
- Unique households reached in the target ZIP codes: 4,000 (assumption)
- Trial budget: $500 over four weeks (assumption)

Impressions equal budget ÷ CPM × 1,000. Average frequency equals impressions ÷ households. The table applies the same formula to three planning scenarios.

| Scenario | Budget (assumption) | Length | Impressions (assumption) | Average frequency per household |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trial | $500 | 4 weeks | 20,000 | 5 over the period |
| Core campaign | $600 per month | 3 months ($1,800) | 72,000 | 18 over the period |
| Seasonal burst | $1,000 | 8 weeks | 40,000 | 10 over the period |

The trial shows how the numbers work. Five impressions per household across four weeks is a light test, which suits a first run. For a cost comparison, the average Google Ads search cost-per-click across all industries is $2.69 ([S1]). The same $500 would buy roughly 186 clicks at that rate. CTV produces impressions, not clicks, so compare spend and reach, not outcomes.

A trial can follow this checklist:

1. Export the last 12 months of customer ZIP codes and pick the top clusters.
2. Choose one platform and set a trial budget that is acceptable if the test teaches little.
3. Produce one 15-second spot: brand in the first seconds, one clear benefit, then a call to action with a phone number or location.
4. Record a four-week baseline before launch: Google Business Profile searches, branded search clicks and direct website visits.
5. Launch, review at week two, and adjust targeting without changing the creative mid-test.
6. Compare results with the baseline and plan a longer run before judging brand effects.

## Which signals show a CTV campaign is working?

CTV does not generate clicks like search ads. It generates brand familiarity, which shows up in indirect signals. Track these against the baseline:

- Google Business Profile searches for the exact business name
- Branded keyword clicks in Google Ads, if campaigns run
- Direct website visits during the flight period
- Answers to "how did you hear about us?", logged at every new customer

Look for movement during the campaign window and a slower decline afterwards. Compare each week with the pre-launch baseline rather than the previous week, since weekly figures move for many reasons. A simple log makes the trend visible at review time.

## Should CTV run alongside Google Ads?

CTV and search do different jobs. CTV introduces the business to households in the target ZIP codes. Search captures people who are already looking. Running both lets people who saw the ad find the business when they need the service, which is the logic behind pairing the two channels.

Before adding CTV, confirm that search campaigns are set up properly. Review [Google Ads management](/services/google-ads) or compare platform options in the [Bing Ads guide for local businesses](/blog/bing-ads-microsoft-advertising-local-business). Clean conversion tracking matters more than channel mix.

## FAQ

### Is CTV advertising worth it for a small business in Charlotte?

It depends on the goal. CTV suits brand familiarity built over weeks, not immediate sales. Test it with a fixed budget, a baseline and clear signals before deciding.

### Can CTV ads be targeted to one ZIP code?

Several platforms offer ZIP-code or radius targeting. Confirm the exact options in the platform's ad manager before planning around them.

### How long should a CTV test run?

Four weeks is a trial length. Brand effects usually need a longer window, so plan more time before judging results.

### Do CTV ads replace Google Ads?

No. CTV builds awareness and search captures intent. Most local businesses should treat them as complementary channels.
