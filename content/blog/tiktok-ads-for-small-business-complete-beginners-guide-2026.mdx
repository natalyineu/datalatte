To run TikTok ads you create an Ads Manager account, pick one objective, set a location, and fund the campaign above TikTok's minimums: more than $50 a day at campaign level and more than $20 a day per ad group, as of the February 2025 help page. That makes the cheapest real test about $600 over 30 days. TikTok is worth testing when your customers are under 50, since Pew's 2026 survey shows 65% of US adults aged 18 to 29 use it, against 11% of those 65 and over.

This guide covers the first campaign only. It uses TikTok and Pew sources, and any example numbers are labelled as assumptions.

## Who Is on TikTok, and Are Your Customers There?

Pew's survey, fielded 30 January to 18 June 2026, found 31% of US adults use TikTok. By age: 65% of 18 to 29s, 45% of 30 to 49s, 31% of 50 to 64s and 11% of those 65 and over. Those are US figures, so check local data if you serve the UK, Australia or Canada.

If your core customers are over 50, such as many dental or mortgage clients, TikTok is a weaker bet. For salons, cafes, studios and gyms with a younger base it is a reasonable test. Compare it with other platforms in our [TikTok vs Instagram ads comparison](/blog/tiktok-vs-instagram-ads-local-business-2026).

## What Is the Minimum Budget?

TikTok's help page on daily budgets, last updated February 2025, says both daily and total budgets at campaign level must exceed 50 USD, and ad group daily budgets must exceed 20 USD. A lifetime ad group budget is 20 USD multiplied by the scheduled days, so a 31-day schedule needs at least 620 USD. You cannot change the budget type after publishing.

So "I will try TikTok ads with $5 a day" is not possible through the standard Ads Manager flow. Plan a fixed test, for example 30 days, and decide beforehand what result justifies continuing. Our [TikTok ads cost guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-guide) covers price ranges and where they come from.

## How Do You Set Up Your First Campaign?

Follow this order and change one thing at a time.

1. **Create the account.** Set up a TikTok Business account and Ads Manager access. Our [business account guide](/blog/tiktok-for-business-account-guide) lists the steps.
2. **Choose one objective.** Traffic, leads or conversions each bill differently, so pick the action that matches your goal: a booking, a call or a form.
3. **Set location.** TikTok supports targeting by country, state, DMA, county, city and zip code, up to 3,000 selections per ad group. Its help page warns that very small areas or sets of zip codes can reduce impressions and clicks significantly and may raise cost per acquisition.
4. **Choose the audience.** Start broad inside your location, then add age bands only if your offer needs them.
5. **Add creative.** Use a short vertical video that shows the service or product, with the offer in the first seconds.
6. **Set the budget and launch.** Respect the minimums above.

For deeper audience choices, read the [TikTok ads targeting guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-targeting-guide).

## Should You Boost an Organic Post with Spark Ads?

Often yes. TikTok describes Spark Ads as a native format that lets you use organic TikTok posts as ads, including posts from other creators with their authorization. Views, comments, shares, likes and follows from the boosted video are credited to the organic post. That means you can test a post organically first and put budget behind the ones that already get a response.

This does not guarantee results, since TikTok publishes no average uplift. See our [Spark Ads guide](/blog/tiktok-spark-ads-guide) for the steps.

## How Do You Know If the Test Worked?

Decide the success number before you spend. Cost per booking is the figure that matters, not views or likes. TikTok publishes no average cost per lead for local services, and benchmark sites vary, so use your own results.

All numbers below are **assumptions** for a test.

| | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget | $620 (20 USD x 31 days) |
| Bookings from ads | 10 |
| Cost per booking | $62 |
| Value of a new customer, first visit | $45 |
| Repeat visits over 6 months | 3 more at $45 |
| Customer value over 6 months | $180 |

On these inputs the test pays back if those customers return. If only the first visit counts, the test loses money. With zero bookings, stop and review the offer and landing page before spending again.

## FAQ

### How much does it cost to start TikTok ads?

TikTok's help page says campaign daily budgets must exceed $50 and ad group daily budgets must exceed $20. A 31-day lifetime ad group budget needs at least $620.

### Do I need a lot of followers to advertise on TikTok?

No source we checked requires a follower count to run ads in Ads Manager. Spark Ads use organic posts, but you can also run standard ads with video you upload.

### What is a Spark Ad?

A native ad format that uses an organic post, yours or a creator's with permission, and credits engagement to the original post.

### Can I target only my city?

Yes. TikTok supports city, county, DMA and zip code targeting. Very small areas can reduce delivery and raise cost, per TikTok.

### Is TikTok good for older customers?

Pew's 2026 data shows 11% of US adults 65 and over use TikTok, against 65% of those aged 18 to 29. Older audiences are usually reached better elsewhere.
