A hair salon can run social media with a free scheduler and a few AI-assisted drafts: Buffer's free plan covers up to 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts each, and Later's Starter plan costs $18.75 a month billed yearly. Hootsuite's Standard plan starts at $99 per user a month on annual billing. AI helps with captions and scheduling. It cannot take the photos of your work, which are the content that books chairs.

## What Does AI Actually Do in a Social Media Tool?

In the plans reviewed, AI features are mostly drafting help. Buffer lists an AI Assistant on all three of its plans, including Free. Later counts AI credits: 5 a month on Starter, 50 on Growth and 100 on Scale. Hootsuite's page says its Standard plan can generate posts and images with AI.

That means a salon can draft captions, reword a post for another channel, or get ideas for a week of content. It does not mean the tool knows your clients, your colour work or your pricing. Read every draft before it goes out, and keep your own details such as stylist names, booking link and hours accurate.

A one-chair salon with one Instagram account and one Facebook page needs two channels. On Buffer Free that costs nothing, and on Essentials it would be $10 a month by the listed per-channel rate.

## Should a Salon Pay for a Tool at All?

Meta Business Suite is described by third-party guides as a free tool to schedule Facebook and Instagram posts, reply to messages and view analytics. Meta's own pages could not be opened for this update, so treat that as secondary and confirm it inside your account.

For one salon on two platforms, a free tool is often enough. Pay when you need a visual calendar, more profiles such as TikTok or Pinterest, or several team members posting. ## What Should You Not Automate?

Automate the schedule, not the relationship. Do not use AI to write fake client stories, invent before-and-after results or post reviews you did not receive. In the US, the FTC's rules on endorsements require that testimonials reflect honest opinions, and in the UK the ASA treats before-and-after photos like testimonials that need signed and dated proof. Use real photos with client permission.

Reply to comments and messages yourself, or with short saved replies you wrote. Automated replies to booking questions should link to your booking page and nothing more.

## How Do You Set Up a Weekly Routine?

A routine that fits a salon owner:

1. Photograph finished work during the week, with permission, and save it in one folder.
2. Once a week, spend 30 minutes drafting captions with the AI assistant and editing them.
3. Schedule posts for the next seven days.
4. Check the booking page link on every post.
5. Review which posts led to enquiries at the end of the month.

For ideas that tie to bookings rather than likes, see boosting hair salon bookings with Instagram. If you want an agent to handle messages, read about [AI agents for hair salons](/blog/ai-agents-for-hair-salons).

## FAQ

### What is the best free social media tool for a hair salon?

Buffer's free plan offers up to 3 channels and 10 scheduled posts per channel, and Meta Business Suite is described as free for Facebook and Instagram. Which suits you depends on the platforms you use.

### Can AI write my salon's Instagram captions?

Yes, as drafts. Buffer, Later and Hootsuite all list AI features. Edit each caption so it matches your salon and has the right booking link.

### How much does Later cost?

Later's Starter plan is $18.75 a month billed yearly and Growth is $37.50, on the page checked 1 November 2026.

### Is it safe to use client photos?

Get permission first. In the UK, before-and-after photos need signed and dated proof they are genuine, and in the US testimonials must reflect honest opinions.

### Do I need Hootsuite?

Probably not for one salon. Its Standard plan starts at $99 per user a month on annual billing, which suits agencies and multi-location groups.
