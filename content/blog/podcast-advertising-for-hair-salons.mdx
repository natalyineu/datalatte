For most hair salons, podcast ads are a second-step test, not a first channel. Acast's 2026 planning guide puts pre-recorded podcast ads at $15 to $30 CPM (cost per 1,000 downloads) and host-read sponsorships on individual shows at $25 to $40 CPM. Those prices buy reach across a whole audience, and only a small share of listeners live near one salon. Start with a local show that serves your city, track with a unique offer code, and keep the first test small.

## Are Podcast Ads a Good Fit for a Hair Salon?

They fit when you can reach people near you. A salon draws customers from a few miles, so a national show wastes most of the audience. Acast notes that each added targeting attribute raises the CPM, which cuts against narrow local targeting. That is why a local or city-focused show with a host who will read your ad is usually a better match than a broad network buy.

Compare with what you already have. Google Search catches people looking for a stylist now, and your Business Profile and reviews convert them. ## What Do Podcast Ads Cost?

CPM means cost per thousand downloads of the episode the ad runs in. Acast, a podcast ad platform and so a seller, lists these ranges for 2026:

| Format | Acast range | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-recorded, network buy | $15 to $30 CPM | Basic country-level targeting about $15 |
| Host-read sponsorship, single show | $25 to $40 CPM | Read in the host's voice |
| Business, finance, B2B shows | $40+ CPM | Not relevant to a salon |

The page states no minimum campaign budget. It also suggests broad produced campaigns at about a $15 to $20 CPM, and notes post-roll is cheapest while mid-roll costs a premium. Sell-side guides tend to run higher than actual deals, so ask any show for its current rate card.

## What Can a Salon Buy With a Small Budget?

Spotify Ads Manager says pricing starts at $250. Podcast ads there can only be selected alongside music ads in a campaign, and the page does not describe location targeting, so confirm in the dashboard whether you can target your area. For direct buys, small local podcasts often sell sponsorships by the episode, and rates are set by each show, not published in a standard source.

Offers help a small advertiser measure. Give each show its own code or landing page, such as a first-visit discount, and count redemptions at the front desk.

## Worked Example: A $300 Local Sponsorship Test

All inputs are **assumptions**. They are not benchmarks for salons.

| Item | Assumption or calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sponsorship price | Chosen | $300 |
| Downloads in the first month | Assumption | 8,000 |
| Implied CPM | $300 / 8 | $37.50 |
| Listeners who are local | Assumption | 25% (2,000) |
| Share who redeem the code | Assumption | 0.5% (10) |
| Cost per new client | $300 / 10 | $30 |
| Average first-visit revenue | Assumption | $90 |

The implied $37.50 CPM falls inside Acast's host-read range. At a redemption rate of 0.2%, the cost per new client would be $75, and the first visit would barely pay. The point of the test is to find your own redemption rate. Repeat only if the cost per new client is below what a repeat customer is worth.

## How Does It Compare With Other Channels?

Podcast ads build awareness slowly, and they are hard to attribute. Search and Maps work at the moment of need. Local Instagram creators sit closer to podcast sponsors in effect. See our [guide to micro-influencer marketing for local business](/blog/micro-influencer-marketing-local-business) and the broader [podcast advertising guide for local business](/blog/podcast-advertising-for-local-business-guide) for formats, plus [Facebook ads for hair salons](/blog/facebook-ads-for-hair-salons) for a cheaper way to reach nearby people.

If you buy a host-read ad, the host should say it is sponsored. The FTC's influencer guidance says material connections should be disclosed clearly, in plain words like "ad" or "sponsored." Its page speaks about social posts, so ask the show how they handle it.

## FAQ

### How much do podcast ads cost?

Acast's 2026 guide lists $15 to $30 CPM for pre-recorded network ads and $25 to $40 for host-read sponsorships on single shows. Spotify Ads Manager starts at $250.

### Can I target podcast ads to my city?

Not reliably through the sources we opened. Spotify's page does not say whether location targeting is available, and Acast says extra targeting raises the CPM. A local show is the practical route.

### Are podcast ads better than Instagram ads for a salon?

No source compares them. Instagram and Facebook ads target by radius, which suits a salon. See the [Facebook ads guide](/blog/facebook-ads-for-hair-salons).

### How do I know if podcast ads work?

Use a unique code or landing page per show and count redemptions. Acast mentions free pixel attribution through its partner Podscribe, which is mainly for online conversions.

### How long should I run a test?

No standard is published in the sources we checked. Run at least one episode cycle, then compare cost per new client with your Google Ads figure.
