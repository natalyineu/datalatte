## What Does QuickBooks Payroll Cost?

The QuickBooks page showed three bundles that pair payroll with an accounting plan. Workforce Payroll plus Simple Start was $88 regular and $44 under a 50% off promo for 3 months. Workforce Payroll plus Essentials was $125 regular and $62.50 promo. Workforce Premium plus Plus was $203 regular and $101.50 promo.

Per-employee fees were $6.50 on the first two bundles and $10 on the third. The promo applied to the base price only, not the per-employee fee. The page did not show the promo footnote, did not label the unit on the regular base price, and showed no contractor price. It links to a separate payroll-only page that was not read.

## Which Should a Small Business Choose?

With one side unverified, use rules that do not depend on Gusto's price.

- **Pick QuickBooks payroll** if you already use QuickBooks Online for your books, since the bundle puts both in one place.
- **Get a Gusto quote** if your books are in Xero, Wave or a spreadsheet, or if you only want payroll without an accounting plan.
- **Compare total monthly cost**, not the base fee: base plus per-person fee times headcount, plus contractor fees.
- **Ask your accountant** which system they can read, because that saves their hours and your fees.

## What Else Does Payroll Cost Beyond the Base Fee?

The listed base and per-employee fees are only part of the bill. Ask each vendor about state tax filing, new-hire reporting, year-end forms such as the W-2 and 1099, and any charge for off-cycle runs or corrections. The QuickBooks page did not show these, and Gusto's pages could not be read, so none are quoted here.

Also check how contractors are paid and priced, because the QuickBooks page linked to a separate product with no price. If you employ seasonal staff, ask whether you are billed for employees who are paid only in some months.

Get the answers in an email you can keep. Payroll mistakes lead to penalties, so support quality and who is responsible for filings matter as much as a few dollars a month.
