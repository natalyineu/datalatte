On vendor pricing checked in November 2026, that stack costs $0 to $40 a month. ChatGPT, Canva, Mailchimp and a website chatbot cover most local marketing tasks between them.

Every additional subscription needs a named weekly task. A tool nobody opens is not a stack; it is a donation. The sections below match tools to tasks, then show how to test the whole stack for one month before paying for depth.

## Which AI tool fits which marketing task?

Writing and research suit a general assistant. ChatGPT drafts emails, rewrites service pages and summarises reviews on a free plan; Plus at $20 a month raises limits, on OpenAI pricing checked in November 2026. Output still needs a human edit for prices, dates and claims.

Design suits Canva. The free plan includes a small AI allowance; Pro at $144 a year for one person adds Magic Write, Magic Resize and the background remover, on Canva pricing checked in November 2026. That covers social posts, flyers and menus for most single-site businesses.

Email suits the AI features inside the sending platform rather than a separate writer. Mailchimp Essentials starts at $13 a month at 500 contacts, on Mailchimp pricing checked in November 2026. Subject-line and send-time helpers only help once a list sends regularly, at least twice a month.

Customer questions suit a chatbot or an agent connected to real booking data. A bot that answers opening hours is a weekend project; one that books and reschedules needs the calendar attached. [AI chatbot for local business booking and FAQs](/blog/ai-chatbot-for-local-business-booking-and-faqs) separates those two builds.

## What should a small business buy first?

Buy in task order. First, the tool that removes the most repeated hour: usually drafting replies and posts. Second, design, if graphics are the bottleneck. Third, automation depth, once messages already send on a schedule.

**Assumption example.** Assume an owner spends 4 hours a week drafting posts and replies, valued at $30 an hour, or about $480 a month in time. A $20 Plus plan plus $12 a month for Canva Pro billed yearly totals $32. The pair only needs to save a little over one hour a month to cover itself. Those inputs are assumptions for the arithmetic; substitute real hours before subscribing.

## When does an AI tool become an AI agent?

A tool waits for a prompt. An agent watches a trigger, such as a new review or a missed call, then acts inside set limits. That difference decides price and risk: agents need connected accounts, spending caps and a human review step for anything customer-facing.

Most local businesses should run tools for a quarter before agents. The prompts, templates and approval habits built in that quarter become the agent's instructions later. ## FAQ

### What is the best free AI tool for small business marketing?

ChatGPT's free plan is the most flexible starting point for drafting and research, and Canva's free plan covers basic design with a small AI allowance, on vendor pricing checked in November 2026. Both are enough to test whether AI output fits a business's voice before any subscription.

### How much should a small business spend on AI tools?

A working stack of one writing assistant, one design tool and entry email software costs roughly $32 to $53 a month on the vendor prices cited here. Spend more only when a named task, such as after-hours booking, returns more than the subscription. Review every tool quarterly and cancel anything unused for a month.

### Can AI tools replace a marketing assistant?

They replace drafting and formatting hours, not judgement. Prices, offers, review replies and anything naming a customer still need a person. Businesses that treat AI output as a first draft usually save time; businesses that publish it raw usually spend the saved time fixing errors instead.

### Which AI tool should a local business avoid?

Avoid platforms that promise to run every channel at once for one low fee. Bundled suites usually have weaker email builders, thinner automation and slower support than the specialist tools they replace. Two or three connected specialists almost always outperform one magic dashboard at this scale.
