To manage social media for several locations, use one brand plan, one scheduling tool and a short local layer for each site. Decide what head office posts for everyone and what each location owns, such as its own offers, staff and events.

For five locations on three channels each, scheduling tools can run from about $75 a month on Buffer's per-channel pricing to $199 on Hootsuite's Professional plan, using the vendors' published starting prices.

This guide covers the content model, tool pricing as listed today, the Google Business Profile rules for chains, and a worked cost example where the assumptions are labelled.

## How Should Multi-Location Content Be Organised?

Split content into two layers so nobody starts from a blank page.

**Brand layer (head office, about 60% of posts, your choice).** Seasonal promotions, new products, brand story and anything that must be consistent. Write it once, schedule it to every location.

**Local layer (each site, about 40%).** Staff introductions, local events, regulars, neighbourhood photos and location-specific offers. A short template keeps it quick: one photo, one sentence, one call to action.

The ratio is a starting point, not a rule. If locations vary a lot by neighbourhood, shift toward local. If your brand is strict about presentation, shift toward the brand layer.

## Should Each Location Have Its Own Account?

Often yes for Facebook and Google, and sometimes no for Instagram. Separate location pages let customers find the right address and leave reviews for the right site. But many small accounts each need their own content. A common compromise is one brand account on Instagram and separate Facebook pages and Google profiles per location.

Decide by asking whether customers search by brand or by neighbourhood. For coffee shops and salons, they often search by neighbourhood. For a chain with similar offers everywhere, a single brand account may be enough.

## What Do Scheduling Tools Cost?

Prices change, so confirm them on each vendor's page.

- **Buffer** ([pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing)): Free for up to 3 channels; Essentials $5 per channel per month; Team $10 per channel per month. Channels above 10 cost less per channel, but the page does not list the rate. Paid plans include a 14-day free trial.
- **Hootsuite** ([plans](https://www.hootsuite.com/plans)): Standard starts at $99 a month with 10 social accounts; Professional starts at $199 with unlimited accounts. The page says prices are based on annual billing and exclude taxes.

## How Do Google Business Profiles Work for Chains?

Google's help page on [bulk verification](https://support.google.com/business/answer/4490296) says you need 10 or more locations of the same business. Duplicate, suspended or disabled profiles do not count toward the minimum, and service-area businesses are not eligible. Agencies with more than one business in an account cannot bulk verify.

The steps are: sign in to Business Profile Manager, choose Verifications, then Chain, then Start, fill in the form and submit. Uploading a spreadsheet alone does not send a request. With fewer than 10 locations, verify each profile individually and keep a log of who owns each one.

Keep your profile details consistent with your social pages. See our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) and [franchise local SEO strategy](/blog/franchise-local-seo-strategy).

## What Does It Cost? A Worked Example

All numbers below are **assumptions** for a five-location café group posting to Facebook, Instagram and one other channel per site.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Locations | 5 |
| Channels per location | 3 |
| Total channels | 15 |
| Buffer Essentials at $5 per channel | $75 a month |
| Buffer Team at $10 per channel | $150 a month |
| Hootsuite Professional (unlimited accounts) | $199 a month |
| Time saved by scheduling in one place | 4 hours a week |
| Value of an hour of staff time | $20 |

Buffer's page says channels above 10 cost less per channel, so the real Buffer bill could be lower than $75 for 15 channels. Four hours saved a week is about $320 a month at $20 an hour, which exceeds any of the tool prices above. The saving only counts if the time is actually freed. Track it for one month before you commit to annual billing.

## How Do You Keep Quality Consistent?

- Write a one-page style guide: tone, spelling, hashtags and what never to post.
- Name one approver for brand posts and one local owner per site.
- Review each location's posts monthly.
- Keep a shared folder of approved photos and logos.
- Reply to comments and messages within a working day.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need a separate account for every location?**
A: Not always. Use separate Facebook pages and Google profiles for local discovery, and consider one brand account on Instagram.

**Q: What is the cheapest way to start?**
A: Buffer's Free plan allows up to 3 channels. Beyond that, pricing is per channel on paid plans.

**Q: Can I use AI to write posts?**
A: For first drafts, yes. Have a person review every post for accuracy and tone.

**Q: How many locations do I need for Google bulk verification?**
A: Google's help page says 10 or more locations of the same business.

**Q: Who should control the accounts?**
A: Use business-owned logins, not personal ones, and record who has access.
