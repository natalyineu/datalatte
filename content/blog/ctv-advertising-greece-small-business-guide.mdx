## What do CTV ad costs look like in planning terms?

No Greek CPM figure was verifiable from the research base, so this guide does not quote one. Any CPM on a platform rate card should be confirmed with the vendor, in euros, and for the exact targeting you plan to use. A CPM is a price per thousand impressions, so total cost depends on how many impressions you buy and how often each household sees the ad.

The closest sourced benchmarks come from Google's search and display ads. [WordStream's Google Ads benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks) report an average search click-through rate of 3.17% and a display click-through rate of 0.46% across all industries. The average display cost per click is $0.63, and the average search cost per click is $2.69.

These are click-based figures, not CTV figures. They show that display inventory is cheap to reach, but they do not predict what a streaming TV impression will cost in Greece.

## Which streaming platforms should a Greek small business test?

The research did not confirm which streaming platforms sell local inventory in Greece, their audience sizes, or their rates. Platform names and rate cards should come from each vendor's current media kit. When you compare options, ask every vendor for the same measures: CPM in euros, minimum spend, geographic targeting depth, and whether frequency caps are available.

Google's Display network is the one platform in this guide with a sourced reach figure. [Google's Display campaign help page](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190) states that Display campaigns can reach people across 35 million websites and apps, plus Google-owned properties such as YouTube and Gmail. That reach is not CTV-specific. It is still a useful yardstick when you judge whether a streaming vendor's reach claim is realistic for your area.

## How do you size a 6-week test in Greece?

A test should answer one question: does the channel reach the right households at a cost you can justify? Build the budget from your own inputs, not from a platform's suggested spend. Every figure in the table below is an assumption except the formula, so replace the CPM and frequency with vendor quotes before you approve anything.

The table shows how three test budgets behave at two CPMs. Frequency is held at 3 views per household (assumption) throughout.

| Test budget (assumption) | CPM (assumption) | Impressions bought | Households reached | Cost per household reached |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| €600 | €12 | 50,000 | 16,667 | €0.036 |
| €600 | €20 | 30,000 | 10,000 | €0.060 |
| €900 | €12 | 75,000 | 25,000 | €0.036 |
| €900 | €20 | 45,000 | 15,000 | €0.060 |
| €1,500 | €12 | 125,000 | 41,667 | €0.036 |
| €1,500 | €20 | 75,000 | 25,000 | €0.060 |

Impressions equal budget ÷ CPM × 1,000, and households equal impressions ÷ frequency. Cost per household reached equals CPM × frequency ÷ 1,000. The table makes one point clear: the CPM quote matters more than the budget size when you compare vendors, because it sets the cost of every household reached.

## Which local businesses fit CTV best?

The research did not include sourced data on which local categories convert best from CTV in Greece, so this section stays qualitative. CTV suits businesses where a short video can show the product, the location, and the atmosphere, such as restaurants, coffee shops, and salons. These businesses depend on customers who live close enough to visit in person, which makes geographic targeting useful.

Search ads fit customers who already want a service now. The sourced benchmarks show search click-through rates well above display rates, so a business with clear search demand should usually fund search first. It can then test CTV with what is left over and use it to build awareness among people who do not yet search.

## What compliance checks apply before launch?

Earlier versions of this topic named specific Greek laws, regulators, time restrictions, and VAT rates. None of those details were verifiable in the research base, so they have been removed rather than repeated. Do not rely on any such detail until you have checked it yourself.

Before launch, read the ad policies of the platform you buy from. Confirm any claims about pricing, food, or health with a Greek adviser or the relevant authority. Price claims should match what the customer will actually pay, including any local tax, so check how your creative presents prices before you submit it for review.

## Does CTV beat search and display ads for a local business?

They do different jobs. Search captures demand that already exists. Display and CTV create awareness. The sourced benchmarks show the gap: a 3.17% average search click-through rate against 0.46% for display, and a $2.69 search cost per click against $0.63 for display. Those numbers come from US-led data, so the gap in Greece may differ.

A practical split is to keep a core search budget running and use a small CTV test to measure whether awareness lifts branded search later. Compare both channels on the same measures over the same period. A local business that wants this comparison built on its own account data can review [Google Ads management](/services/google-ads). Other formats are covered in the [native advertising guide for small businesses](/blog/native-advertising-guide-small-business).

Their rules and rates differ from Greece, so do not transfer their figures here.

## FAQ

### How much does CTV advertising cost in Greece?

This guide cannot give a sourced Greek figure. Ask each streaming vendor for its CPM in euros, then multiply by the impressions you plan to buy. The table above shows how the calculation changes at different CPMs.

### Is CTV better than search ads for a local business?

Search ads usually capture existing demand, while CTV builds awareness. Sourced Google benchmarks show search click-through rates far above display rates, so most small businesses should fund search first and test CTV with a smaller budget.

### What is the smallest test I can run?

The research did not establish a minimum spend for Greek inventory. The worked example uses €900 over six weeks as an assumption. Confirm the minimum with each vendor before you plan around it.

### Do I need Greek-language creative?

The research did not test this question. Local language is a reasonable default for consumer ads, but confirm your approach with a local creative partner and check the results of your own test.
