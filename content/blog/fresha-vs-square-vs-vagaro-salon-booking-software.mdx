Here is a number most comparison pages skip. Take a four-chair salon that runs $20,000 a month through card readers, in 235 transactions of about $85. Using the vendors' own published in-person rates, card fees come to roughly $505 on Fresha, $513 on Vagaro and between $515 and $555 on Square depending on the plan. The subscriptions are close to each other. The spread comes from processing, from which plan unlocks cancellation fees, and from marketplace commissions. This article compares those three things.

It deliberately does not repeat the marketplace and Boost analysis from our [Booksy vs Vagaro vs Fresha comparison](/blog/booksy-vs-vagaro-vs-fresha-best-booking-app-salons-2026), and it leaves reminder automation to [AI automation for hair salons](/blog/ai-automation-for-hair-salons). Here the question is narrower: if Square is on your shortlist, what does it cost and what do you have to upgrade to get?

One honest limit first. Square's US pricing page returned a rate-limit error every time it was requested, so the US Square monthly plan prices are unverified in this article. Square's own UK and Canadian pages and its US help article on processing fees did open, and those figures are used and attributed below. All prices were read in October 2026. Fees change and differ by country, so confirm on the vendor page before you sign.

## What Each Vendor Publishes

### Fresha

According to [Fresha's pricing page](https://www.fresha.com/pricing), the Independent plan is $19.95 a month for one bookable team member, and the Team plan is $14.95 per bookable team member per month. The page shows prices in dollars without naming a currency, and rates exclude local sales taxes. Processing is listed as:

- In person: 2.29% + $0.20 per transaction, plus $0.10 per Tap to Pay authorization
- Online: 2.79% + $0.20
- Manually entered cards: 3.30% + $0.20
- Texts: 100 free a month, then $0.02 each; marketing texts $0.08 each

New clients who find you through the Fresha marketplace carry a one-time 20% commission with a $6 minimum. Returning clients and bookings from your own link, Facebook or Instagram are free. No-show and cancellation fees appear as a payments feature, but the page states no amount.

### Vagaro

[Vagaro's pricing page](https://www.vagaro.com/pro/pricing) shows $30.00 and a promotional $23.99 a month "for the first 6 months", plus $10 per bookable calendar, up to seven; after seven, more employees are added at no extra cost. The page layout does not make clear which figure applies to which column, so treat $30 as the list price and confirm at checkout. Unlimited notifications and appointment reminders are included, the marketplace listing is free, and the trial is 30 days.

Processing is published separately on [Vagaro's card processing page](https://www.vagaro.com/pro/card-processing):

- Small merchants (under $4,000 a month): 2.6% + 10 cents for dip and tap, no monthly cost
- Large merchants (over $4,000 a month): 2.29% + 19 cents for dip and tap, with a $10 monthly fee
- Keyed-in: 3.5% + 19 cents for both groups
- Tap to Pay adds 6 cents per transaction

The page does not list online or card-on-file rates, so ask Vagaro for those before relying on them.

### Square Appointments

Square's [UK pricing page](https://squareup.com/gb/en/appointments/pricing) lists three tiers: Free at £0 for a single location, Plus at £29 a month per location and Premium at £69. Its [Canadian page](https://squareup.com/ca/en/appointments/pricing) lists Free, Plus at $35 and Premium at $85, with unlimited calendars and staff on all plans. In the US, the plan prices could not be confirmed here. Third-party roundups quote $29 and $69, but treat that as second-hand until you see it on Square's own page.

The US processing rates come from Square's [help article on processing fees](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/5068-square-appointments-pricing):

| Payment type | Free | Plus | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tap, dip or swipe | 2.6% + 15 cents | 2.5% + 15 cents | 2.4% + 15 cents |
| Online or invoices | 3.3% + 30 cents | 2.9% + 30 cents | 2.9% + 30 cents |
| Manual entry or card on file | 3.5% + 15 cents | 3.5% + 15 cents | 3.5% + 15 cents |

The same article lists an extra 1.5% on international cards. Notice what this means: a higher Square tier lowers your in-person rate, so heavy card volume can partly pay for the upgrade.

## The Feature Gate That Matters Most: Where Square Puts No-Show Fees

On the Canadian and UK pages, the Plus tier is where cancellation policies and fees appear, alongside class booking, waitlists, processing time (useful for colour treatments where you are free during development), Google Calendar sync and custom text notifications. Premium adds resource management for rooms, chairs and stations, plus time tracking, staff scheduling and commissions.

That changes the "free" claim. If a Square Free account cannot enforce a cancellation fee, a salon that wants deposits or late-cancel charges is really comparing Square Plus against Fresha and Vagaro. Check the current tier table in your own country before assuming otherwise, because the plan lists differ by market.

A practical rule: write down the three features you cannot run the salon without (for most owners: reminders, cancellation fees, staff-specific calendars), then find the lowest tier on each platform that includes all three. Compare those prices, not the headline ones.

## Keyed-In Payments: The Fee Nobody Notices

Phone bookings paid by reading a card number over the phone, and cards stored on file, are priced differently from a tap. On the published US rates the keyed-in rate is 3.5% on Square and Vagaro (15 cents and 19 cents respectively) and 3.30% + 20 cents on Fresha. The gap is small per sale. At 20 keyed payments of $85 in a month, the fee is about $60 on Fresha, $62.50 on Square and $63 on Vagaro. Your reply to that is practical: encourage clients to pay in the chair with a tap, and use online deposits at booking where the platform's online rate is lower than keyed.

Vagaro states that its online rate is not published on that page, so its online and card-on-file cost is the one number you must ask for.

## The Math

This is an illustration for a four-chair salon. Every input marked assumption is mine; fee rates come from the vendor pages above. Card sales are assumed to be entirely in person to keep the comparison clean, and online and keyed-in sales will add to each total.

| Line | Fresha (Team) | Vagaro | Square Free | Square Plus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly card sales (assumption) | $20,000 | $20,000 | $20,000 | $20,000 |
| Average ticket (assumption) | $85 | $85 | $85 | $85 |
| Transactions | 235 | 235 | 235 | 235 |
| In-person rate | 2.29% + $0.20 | 2.29% + $0.19 + $10 fee | 2.6% + $0.15 | 2.5% + $0.15 |
| Card fees | $505.00 | $512.65 | $555.25 | $535.25 |
| Subscription, 4 staff | 4 x $14.95 = $59.80 | $30 + 3 x $10 = $60.00 | $0 | $29 (US price unverified; assumption) |
| Total per month | $564.80 | $572.65 | $555.25 | $564.25 |

How to read it:

- At this volume the four options land within about $17 of each other. The in-person processing rate matters more than the plan price.
- Square Free looks cheapest but, per the UK and Canadian pages, may not include cancellation fees. Square Plus is the fair comparison, and it comes out level with Fresha.
- Vagaro's large-merchant rate applies only above $4,000 a month in charges; a salon doing $3,000 would pay the small-merchant 2.6% + 10 cents instead.
- This excludes the marketplace commission, which on Fresha can dwarf all of the above if you buy new clients that way. Run that math in the Booksy comparison linked earlier.

A rough test of whether a platform is worth switching to: 0.1 percentage points on $20,000 is $20 a month. If a competitor saves you $20 but you lose a week migrating client notes, the saving takes a long time to pay back.

## Which One for Which Salon

**Solo chair renter, mostly tap payments.** Fresha Independent at $19.95 is the lowest published subscription here. Square Free has no subscription at all, so calculate whether you need cancellation fees before choosing it.

**Four to seven staff with colour services.** Look at tier features first: processing time and per-stylist calendars. Vagaro prices each extra calendar at $10 up to seven. Square Plus is a flat per-location fee in the UK and Canada, so it gets cheaper per stylist as the team grows.

**Salon that sells retail.** Square's strength is that payments and sales sit in one system, but the pages opened for this article do not list inventory features per tier, so check the feature list in your own account before choosing on that basis. Vagaro's pricing page lists inventory tracking in every plan.

**Barbershop or nail bar with lots of walk-ins.** Keep an eye on tap and chip rates, because nearly all revenue goes through the reader.

## Switching Without Pain

Whatever you pick, take a few safeguards before you move: export your client list as a CSV, screenshot your service menu with durations and prices, and note your cancellation policy wording. Start the free trial the same day as your top rival and book ten fake appointments on both. Vagaro lists a 30-day trial and Fresha 7 days on their pricing pages, so plan the test window accordingly.

Booking software also does nothing for discovery. Link your booking page from a well-built [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-for-hair-salons), and use the follow-up ideas in [email marketing for hair salons](/blog/email-marketing-for-hair-salons) and our guide to [automating salon marketing with Fresha or Square](/blog/how-to-use-fresha-or-square-to-automate-salon-marketing).

## FAQ

**Q: Which of Fresha, Square or Vagaro has the lowest card fees?**
A: On the published in-person rates, Fresha's 2.29% + $0.20 is lowest at a $85 ticket, with Vagaro's large-merchant 2.29% + $0.19 plus a $10 monthly fee close behind. Square's rate falls as you move up its tiers. Online and keyed-in rates differ, so check those too.

**Q: Is Square Appointments free?**
A: Square lists a Free tier at no monthly cost, per its UK and Canadian pages, with processing fees on each sale. Features such as cancellation fees appear on the Plus tier on those pages, so confirm what your country's Free plan includes.

**Q: Why are Square's US plan prices missing here?**
A: Square's US pricing pages could not be opened during research (they returned a rate-limit error), so the US monthly prices are unverified. Use Square's own page for current figures.

**Q: Does Vagaro charge extra for text reminders?**
A: Its pricing page says unlimited notifications and appointment reminders are included. It does not list marketing text pricing, so ask before running campaigns.

**Q: Can I switch later without losing clients?**
A: Plan to export your client list as a CSV and rebuild services manually. Whether appointment history and notes transfer depends on the platforms, so test with a small batch.
