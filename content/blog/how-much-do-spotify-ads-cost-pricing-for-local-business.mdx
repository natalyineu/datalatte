Spotify self-serve campaigns start at a minimum budget of 250 in your local currency, and pricing varies by ad format ([Spotify Ads Manager help](https://help.adanalytics.spotify.com/about-spotify-ads-manager), [Spotify Ads](https://ads.spotify.com/en-NL/how-it-works), checked Oct 2026). Spotify does not publish a fixed CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) for local advertisers, and the figures quoted in many guides are third-party estimates. Ads Manager shows your CPM and budget pacing in real-time reporting, so treat your own account as the source of truth and plan a test of a few weeks rather than a guess.

Spotify advertising works differently from Google or Meta ads. You are interrupting someone's listening, not answering a search or catching someone scrolling. For local businesses, that changes both the creative approach and the realistic expectations for results.

## How Spotify Ad Pricing Works

### Self-Serve (Spotify Ads Manager)

- **Minimum campaign budget:** 250 in your local currency, per Spotify's help center ([source](https://help.adanalytics.spotify.com/about-spotify-ads-manager), checked Oct 2026).
- **Pricing:** Spotify says pricing varies by ad format, and Ads Manager reports CPMs and budget pacing during the campaign ([Spotify Ads](https://ads.spotify.com/en-NL/how-it-works), checked Oct 2026).
- **Goals:** Spotify offers a reach goal (maximize unique listeners) and an impression-delivery goal (more frequency).

We do not quote CPM ranges here because we could not confirm them from Spotify. Your cost will depend on targeting, format and competition.

### Larger and Managed Campaigns

Larger advertisers can work with Spotify's sales team for custom plans and additional formats. For most small businesses, self-serve is the right starting point.

## Ad Formats

### Audio Ads (most common for local business)

- **Length:** Spotify accepts music ads up to 30 seconds, with longer versions in select markets ([Spotify audio ad specs](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/ad-experiences/audio-ads-specs), checked Oct 2026).
- **Files:** MP3, WAV or OGG up to 50 MB, plus a companion image (square, at least 600x600 pixels, JPEG or PNG, up to 200 KB) per the same spec page.
- **Behavior:** Spotify describes audio ads as non-skippable, sound-on ads that run between songs and podcasts ([Spotify audio ads](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/ad-experiences/audio-ads/), checked Oct 2026). Spotify has also tested letting free users skip ads in some markets, so check current behavior.
- **Who hears them:** Spotify Premium includes ad-free listening ([Spotify Premium](https://www.spotify.com/us/premium/), checked Oct 2026), so audio ads reach the ad-supported listeners.

An audio ad can be good or bad. A weak one is worse than no ad because it creates negative associations.

### Video Ads

Spotify says video ads are served only when users are actively engaging with the app, in-stream between content or in-feed, for example in the Now Playing view ([Spotify video ads](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/ad-experiences/video-ads/), checked Oct 2026). Because many people listen with the screen off, video reaches fewer listeners than audio. For most local businesses, audio makes more sense than video on Spotify. If you produce video, Meta or YouTube are usually better homes for it.

### Podcast Advertising

Spotify also sells podcast ad placements, and its audio spec covers podcast ads inserted at up to 30 seconds. Host-read ads can transfer some of a host's trust to your brand, which suits service businesses where credibility matters. Prices are negotiated or vary by show, so request quotes.

## Working Out a Test Budget

Instead of relying on CPM tables, use this formula with your real CPM from Ads Manager:

**Impressions = (budget / CPM) x 1,000**

For example, at a hypothetical CPM of 20, a budget of 250 buys about 12,500 impressions. Use the CPM Ads Manager shows for your targeting.

Plan a budget you can sustain for several weeks. A campaign at the 250 minimum is usually only a light awareness test, so do not expect to draw firm conclusions from one small flight.

## Targeting Options for Local Businesses

**Geographic targeting.** Spotify's location targeting uses IP addresses. In the United States you can target by country, state, city, postal code or DMA (a metro area). The UK and Canada offer country, city and postal code, and Australia adds state ([Spotify location targeting](https://ads.spotify.com/en-CA/help-center/location-targeting), checked Oct 2026). The help page does not list radius targeting, so you cannot draw a 5-mile circle around your address. Postal-code targeting is the closest option, but check what Ads Manager offers in your market.

**Audience and context.** Spotify says you can reach listeners by location, interests and listening behavior ([Spotify Ads](https://ads.spotify.com/en-NL/how-it-works), checked Oct 2026). Targeting options change, so confirm the current list in Ads Manager.

## Who Spotify Ads Work For (and Who They Don't)

### Good fit

**Fitness studios and gyms.** Workout listeners are in a fitness mindset, so a short audio ad with a first-class offer is contextually relevant.

**Coffee shops and cafes.** A morning-commute ad with a consistent offer can support foot traffic around a defined area.

**Restaurants and bars.** Evening and weekend listeners suit dining and nightlife promotions.

**Businesses with a younger customer base.** If your customers are heavy music or podcast listeners, Spotify reaches them in a different context than search or social.

### Not a great fit

**Home services (plumbers, electricians, roofers).** These are searched when needed, not prompted by awareness advertising. Google Search ads are usually more efficient here.

**Medical and dental.** Advertising rules can limit what you can say, and these are mostly search-intent purchases.

**Hyper-local businesses.** If you need to reach people within a couple of miles, Meta or local search gives you more precise targeting.

## Creating a Spotify Audio Ad

Spotify's Ad Studio lets you upload a finished audio file, or write a script and have Spotify's voice actors record and produce it with licensed background music and mixing at no cost, per Spotify ([Creating audio ads](https://ads.spotify.com/en-NL/creating-audio-ads), checked Oct 2026). Spotify suggests 55 to 70 words for a 30-second script and a direct call to action.

**What makes a good audio ad:**
- Open with your city or neighborhood name, to establish local relevance immediately.
- State your offer clearly in the first few seconds.
- Use one call to action only.
- Avoid ending on a long website address. Listeners cannot write it down, so use the companion image or a memorable phrase they can search.

**Example 15-second script for a salon:**

> "Hey [City], [Salon Name] on [Street] is booking new clients for the summer. First color appointment, 20% off. Book in 60 seconds at [SimplifiedURL.com]. [Salon Name], [neighborhood]'s neighborhood salon."

## Spotify Ads vs. Other Channels for Local Business

| Channel | Intent | Local precision |
|---|---|---|
| Google Search | High (search intent) | High (radius and location targeting) |
| Meta/Instagram | Medium (interest) | High (radius targeting) |
| Spotify audio | Low (awareness) | Medium (city or postal code, no radius) |
| CTV (streaming TV) | Low (awareness) | Often ZIP-level |
| DOOH (digital outdoor) | Low (awareness) | Very high (specific screens) |

Don't start with Spotify. Build your foundation in Google Search (capture existing demand) and Meta (create new demand) first. Add Spotify as a third channel once your core targeting is saturated, since it extends your reach into audio listening.

## FAQ

**Q: How much do Spotify ads cost for a small business?**
A: Self-serve campaigns start at a minimum budget of 250 in your local currency, and pricing varies by format. Spotify does not publish a fixed CPM, so check the CPM shown in Ads Manager for your targeting.

**Q: Does Spotify show my ad to premium subscribers?**
A: No for audio ads in general, because Spotify Premium includes ad-free listening. Your audio ads reach ad-supported listeners.

**Q: Can I target people near my business location on Spotify?**
A: Spotify lists city and postal-code targeting in the US, UK, Canada and Australia, and DMA in the US, but its help page does not list radius targeting. If tight radius targeting matters, Meta or local search may fit better.

**Q: How do I measure whether Spotify ads are driving results?**
A: Ads Manager reports delivery metrics such as impressions and reach. Spotify cannot directly measure in-store visits or phone calls, so use a dedicated URL or promo code and track redemptions, or compare foot traffic and bookings across on and off periods.

**Q: Is the free voiceover service good enough, or should I hire a voice actor?**
A: For initial testing, Spotify's included production is usually enough. Once you see results, consider a professional voiceover artist and request quotes, since prices vary.
