Spotify does not publish a fixed cost per 1,000 impressions (CPM) that applies to local advertisers, so there is no reliable price to quote from the sources available here. The best available benchmark is a 2017 Nielsen Catalina study, which found Spotify ads drove revenue of $24 per 1,000 impressions against a Nielsen benchmark of $19.64 ([Campaign US](https://www.campaignlive.com/article/spotify-ads-25-percent-effective-average-study-says/1436976), secondary). That study covered national consumer brands, not local shops. To estimate your own cost, set a test budget, a CPM from your Ads Manager account and a break-even per customer, then check whether the numbers work before you commit.

## What does a Spotify ad actually cost a local business?

No public Spotify price list was available in the sources for this update, so this article does not quote a minimum budget or a CPM. Spotify's cost depends on format, targeting and competition for the same listeners, and those inputs are visible only inside your own Ads Manager account. Treat any CPM figure in a third-party guide as an estimate until you see your own quote.

Cost comparisons with other channels are easier to anchor. WordStream's all-industry averages put Google Ads search cost per click at $2.69 and display at $0.63 ([WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks), secondary, data from its 2016 update). Those are click prices, not Spotify prices, so they only show the range of what local advertisers usually pay per action elsewhere.

## What did the Nielsen study show about Spotify ads?

The study looked at 11 major consumer packaged goods (CPG) brands over a 12-month period. It found incremental sales per 1,000 impressions were 25 percent higher than the benchmark average ([Campaign US](https://www.campaignlive.com/article/spotify-ads-25-percent-effective-average-study-says/1436976), secondary, 2017). Spotify's own representative described the result as $24 of revenue per 1,000 impressions.

Two findings matter for a small budget. Campaigns combining audio and display performed best, and campaigns that ran on both mobile and desktop saw 39 percent higher incremental sales than single-device campaigns. Audio works well when people listen passively, so a single audio ad is a reasonable starting point rather than a full campaign.

The study is three or more years old and measured packaged goods, so it does not prove a barber or bakery will see the same return.

## How do you estimate cost per customer?

Use this formula: impressions equal budget divided by CPM, multiplied by 1,000. Then divide impressions by how often one listener hears the ad to estimate unique listeners. The worked example below uses assumed inputs only. Replace every assumption with your Ads Manager CPM, your own conversion data and your real average sale.

| Input or result | Assumed value (not Spotify pricing) |
|---|---|
| Test budget | $250 (assumption) |
| CPM from Ads Manager | $30 (assumption) |
| Impressions (250 / 30 x 1,000) | about 8,333 |
| Average times one listener hears the ad | 2.5 (assumption) |
| Unique listeners | about 3,333 |
| Share of listeners who act | 0.5% (assumption) |
| People who act | about 17 |
| Share of actors who buy | 40% (assumption) |
| Paying customers | about 7 |
| Cost per customer ($250 / 7) | about $36 |
| Revenue at a $45 average ticket | about $315 |

On these assumptions the test roughly breaks even on first-visit revenue, before margin. A customer who returns would change the answer. A weaker ad or a higher CPM can push cost per customer well above $36, which is why creative quality matters as much as budget.

## Which Spotify ad formats fit a local business?

Audio ads are the default choice for most local advertisers because they match the passive listening that the Nielsen study described. Spotify's own agency partner describes sponsored playlists as more expensive than simple audio ads, and its Premium subscribers hear no ads at all ([Soda Spoon Marketing](https://www.sodaspoon.com/blogs/resources/unlocking-the-potential-of-spotify-advertising), secondary). Confirm current format prices in your account before choosing.

Video and display formats add reach for people actively using the app. Spotify's agency source notes that display ads appear during active engagement and podcast ads can target niche listener groups, so they suit businesses with a clear audience segment.

Google's own display network is a useful comparison point. Google says its Display campaigns reach people across 35 million websites and apps, plus YouTube and Gmail, and use optimized targeting that draws on keywords from your landing page ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190), primary). If your goal is reach at scale, that network may cover more ground than Spotify for the same budget.

## Who should skip Spotify ads?

Spotify fits best when your customer is already in a listening mindset and you can make a memorable 30-second pitch. It fits poorly for services people search for when they need them, such as emergency plumbing or dental work. For those, search ads capture existing demand instead of asking people to notice something new.

If you run a service business, compare your options first. The guide to [how much Google Ads costs for small businesses](/blog/how-much-does-google-ads-cost-for-small-businesses-real-2026-pricing-breakdown) shows the search side of the same budget, and [Jobber vs ServiceTitan](/blog/jobber-vs-servicetitan-small-business-local-business) covers the software you need to track leads once they arrive.

Spotify also reaches listeners across the whole platform, so it is hard to limit it to a few blocks. Local businesses in Australia face the same targeting limits, so the [Australia local marketing guide](/blog/local-marketing-australia-small-business-2026) is worth reading for regional context.

## How should you plan a Spotify test budget?

Start with a test you can repeat for several weeks, not a single flight. One week of spend rarely produces enough customers to judge.

1. Write your break-even cost per customer: average sale times margin, divided by the share of sales that repeat.
2. Pick a test budget you can afford to lose and set the CPM and listener assumptions in a spreadsheet.
3. Create a unique promo code or landing page so each sale can be traced.
4. Choose the target area and run the test for at least four weeks, with the same offer throughout.
5. Compare redemptions and bookings against the weeks with no ads running.

Keep a written record of every assumption so the next test starts from real numbers.

## Should a small business use Spotify at all?

Spotify belongs after a base of search and social advertising, not before. Build demand capture first with search ads, then test Spotify once you know your cost per customer on the cheaper channels. The [social media comparison of Hootsuite and Sprout Social](/blog/hootsuite-vs-sprout-social-small-business-local-business) can help you plan the organic side of that base.

If you want help building the numbers for a test, [Google Ads management](/services/google-ads) can show how your search results compare before you spend on audio.

## FAQ

### How much does a Spotify ad cost for a small business?

No fixed local price was confirmed in the sources for this article. Check the CPM and minimum budget shown in your own Spotify Ads Manager, because pricing depends on format and targeting.

### Does Spotify show ads to Premium subscribers?

Spotify Premium is an ad-free subscription. Audio ads reach listeners on the free, ad-supported tier, so your audience is narrower than total listeners.

### Do Spotify ads work for local businesses?

The best evidence is the 2017 Nielsen study, which covered national packaged goods brands and found sales per 1,000 impressions above benchmark. It does not measure local shops, so run your own test with a promo code.

### How do I measure whether a Spotify ad brought customers?

Use a unique promo code or dedicated landing page for the campaign, then compare redemptions and bookings during the campaign with weeks when no ads ran.

### Is Spotify better than Google Display for reach?

Google says its Display network reaches across 35 million websites and apps, plus YouTube and Gmail. Spotify reaches a listening audience with less ad clutter. Choose based on whether you want broad reach or a listening habit.
