Spotify self-serve campaigns start at a minimum budget of 250 in your local currency, and pricing varies by ad format ([Spotify Ads Manager help](https://help.adanalytics.spotify.com/about-spotify-ads-manager), [Spotify Ads](https://ads.spotify.com/en-NL/how-it-works), checked Oct 2026). Spotify does not publish a fixed CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) for local advertisers, and the figures quoted in many guides are third-party estimates. Ads Manager shows your CPM and budget pacing in real-time reporting, so treat your own account as the source of truth.

Picture the listener. She is on a run, earbuds in, a playlist going. Between two songs your ad plays. You have about 30 seconds. Spotify suggests 55 to 70 words for a 30-second script. That is your whole pitch, and she cannot tap, cannot scroll back and cannot write down a web address. That is the real price of Spotify: a small minimum budget (250), and a very hard creative brief.

Spotify works differently from Google or Meta. You interrupt a listener instead of answering a search or catching a scroller. That changes the creative and the expectations for results.

## How Spotify ad pricing works

- **Minimum campaign budget:** 250 in your local currency, per Spotify's help center ([source](https://help.adanalytics.spotify.com/about-spotify-ads-manager), checked Oct 2026).
- **Pricing:** Spotify says pricing varies by ad format, and Ads Manager reports CPMs and budget pacing during the campaign ([Spotify Ads](https://ads.spotify.com/en-NL/how-it-works), checked Oct 2026).
- **Goals:** a reach goal (maximize unique listeners) and an impression-delivery goal (more frequency).

We do not quote CPM ranges because we could not confirm them from Spotify. Cost depends on targeting, format and competition. Larger advertisers can work with Spotify's sales team on custom plans, but for a small business self-serve is the place to start.

## The math: what 250 can buy

Impressions = (budget / CPM) x 1,000. Spotify gives no public CPM, so this is a worked example with invented inputs.

**Example only, not Spotify pricing or a market average. Swap in your own numbers.** Budget 250 (in dollars for the example). Assume each listener hears the ad 2.5 times, a $45 average ticket, and 40% of the people who act become paying customers.

| Assumption | Low CPM, strong ad | Higher CPM, average ad | Higher CPM, weak ad |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPM (assumed) | $20 | $30 | $30 |
| Impressions (250 / CPM x 1,000) | 12,500 | 8,333 | 8,333 |
| Listeners (impressions / 2.5) | 5,000 | 3,333 | 3,333 |
| Share who act (assumed) | 0.5% | 0.5% | 0.2% |
| People who act | 25 | about 17 | about 7 |
| Paying customers (40%) | 10 | about 7 | about 3 |
| Cost per customer | $25 | about $37.50 | about $94 |
| Revenue at $45 | $450 | about $300 | about $120 |

Two lessons. First, a bad ad and a pricier CPM can triple your cost per customer, so creative is half the budget. Second, one flight at the 250 minimum is a light awareness test. A handful of customers is noise, not proof. Plan a budget you can repeat for several weeks.

## Ad formats

### Audio ads (the usual pick for local business)

- **Length:** music ads up to 30 seconds, with longer versions in select markets ([Spotify audio ad specs](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/ad-experiences/audio-ads-specs), checked Oct 2026).
- **Files:** MP3, WAV or OGG up to 50 MB, plus a companion image (square, at least 600x600 pixels, JPEG or PNG, up to 200 KB) per the same spec page.
- **Behavior:** Spotify describes audio ads as non-skippable, sound-on ads that run between songs and podcasts ([Spotify audio ads](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/ad-experiences/audio-ads/), checked Oct 2026). It has also tested letting free users skip ads in some markets, so check current behavior.
- **Who hears them:** Spotify Premium includes ad-free listening ([Spotify Premium](https://www.spotify.com/us/premium/), checked Oct 2026), so audio ads reach ad-supported listeners only.

A weak audio ad is worse than no ad. It teaches people to tune you out.

### Video ads

Spotify says video ads are served only when users are actively engaging with the app, in-stream between content or in-feed, for example in the Now Playing view ([Spotify video ads](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/ad-experiences/video-ads/), checked Oct 2026). Many people listen with the screen off, so video reaches fewer listeners than audio. If you produce video, Meta or YouTube are better homes for it.

### Podcast ads

Spotify also sells podcast placements, and its audio spec covers podcast ads inserted at up to 30 seconds. Host-read ads can borrow some of a host's trust, which helps service businesses. Prices are negotiated or vary by show, so request quotes.

## Targeting

**Geography.** Spotify's location targeting uses IP addresses. In the United States you can target by country, state, city, postal code or DMA (a metro area). The UK and Canada offer country, city and postal code, and Australia adds state ([Spotify location targeting](https://ads.spotify.com/en-CA/help-center/location-targeting), checked Oct 2026). The help page does not list radius targeting, so you cannot draw a 5-mile circle around your address. Postal-code targeting is the closest option; check what Ads Manager offers in your market.

**Audience.** Spotify says you can reach listeners by location, interests and listening behavior ([Spotify Ads](https://ads.spotify.com/en-NL/how-it-works), checked Oct 2026). Options change, so confirm the current list in Ads Manager.

## Who it works for

**Good fit**

- **Fitness studios and gyms.** Workout listeners are in the mindset. A short ad with a first-class offer is relevant.
- **Coffee shops and cafes.** A morning-commute ad with one offer can support foot traffic around a defined area.
- **Restaurants and bars.** Evening and weekend listeners suit dining and nightlife promotions.
- **Businesses with younger customers** who are heavy music or podcast listeners.

**Poor fit**

- **Home services (plumbers, electricians, roofers).** People search when they need one. Google Search ads are usually more efficient.
- **Medical and dental.** Advertising rules can limit what you can say, and these are mostly search-intent purchases.
- **Hyper-local businesses.** If you need people within a couple of miles, Meta or local search targets more precisely.

## Writing the audio ad

Ad Studio lets you upload a finished audio file, or write a script and have Spotify's voice actors record it with licensed background music and mixing at no cost, per Spotify ([Creating audio ads](https://ads.spotify.com/en-NL/creating-audio-ads), checked Oct 2026). Spotify suggests 55 to 70 words for a 30-second script and a direct call to action.

- Open with your city or neighborhood name.
- Put the offer in the first few seconds.
- One call to action only.
- Do not end on a long web address. Use the companion image or a short phrase people can search.

**Example 15-second script for a salon:**

> "Hey [City], [Salon Name] on [Street] is booking new clients for the summer. First color appointment, 20% off. Book in 60 seconds at [SimplifiedURL.com]. [Salon Name], [neighborhood]'s neighborhood salon."

## Do this today

1. Write the script and read it out loud with a timer. Over 30 seconds? Cut words until it fits, aiming for 55 to 70.
2. Create a promo code or a dedicated short URL so you can count responses.
3. Write down your break-even: average ticket times margin, then the number of customers a 250 test must bring to cover itself.
4. Choose the target area (city or postal codes) and the weeks the test will run before you press go.
5. Add a reminder to compare bookings and code redemptions in the on-weeks against the off-weeks.

## Spotify versus other channels

| Channel | Intent | Local precision |
|---|---|---|
| Google Search | High (search intent) | High (radius and location targeting) |
| Meta/Instagram | Medium (interest) | High (radius targeting) |
| Spotify audio | Low (awareness) | Medium (city or postal code, no radius) |
| CTV (streaming TV) | Low (awareness) | Often ZIP-level |
| DOOH (digital outdoor) | Low (awareness) | Very high (specific screens) |

Do not start with Spotify. Build the base in Google Search (capture existing demand) and Meta (create new demand), then add Spotify as a third channel once those are working.

## FAQ

**Q: How much do Spotify ads cost for a small business?**
A: Self-serve campaigns start at a minimum budget of 250 in your local currency, and pricing varies by format. Spotify does not publish a fixed CPM, so check the CPM shown in Ads Manager for your targeting.

**Q: Does Spotify show my ad to premium subscribers?**
A: No for audio ads in general, because Spotify Premium includes ad-free listening. Your audio ads reach ad-supported listeners.

**Q: Can I target people near my business location on Spotify?**
A: Spotify lists city and postal-code targeting in the US, UK, Canada and Australia, and DMA in the US, but its help page does not list radius targeting. If tight radius targeting matters, Meta or local search may fit better.

**Q: How do I measure whether Spotify ads are driving results?**
A: Ads Manager reports delivery metrics such as impressions and reach. Spotify cannot directly measure in-store visits or phone calls, so use a dedicated URL or promo code and track redemptions, or compare foot traffic and bookings across on and off periods.

**Q: Is the free voiceover service good enough, or should I hire a voice actor?**
A: For initial testing, Spotify's included production is usually enough. Once you see results, consider a professional voiceover artist and request quotes, since prices vary.
