## How Big Is the Belizean Online Market?

DataReportal counts 424 thousand people, 307 thousand internet users (72.4%) and 349 thousand mobile connections (82.3%). Social media user identities total 238 thousand, 56.1% of the population. The market is very small, so a local ad campaign reaches thousands, not millions.

TikTok's 234 thousand adults is almost equal to Facebook's reach, so video is worth testing. Meta says audience size is an estimate rather than a count of active users.

## How Much Do Visitors Matter in Belize?

The Belize Tourism Board's release of 2 February 2026 gives preliminary 2025 figures: 551,698 overnight arrivals, up 0.8% from 547,370 in 2024, and 967,214 cruise passenger arrivals, up 8.1% from 894,405. Both 2025 numbers are projections in the release, not final counts.

Cruise passengers outnumber the 424 thousand residents. Overnight visitors stay longer and spend on tours, food and lodging. A tour operator, restaurant or shop in a visitor area depends on how those two groups find it, so set up Google profile details early. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists the fields.

## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Belize?

Google says local ranking uses relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Reviews and ratings can help prominence. Visitors compare reviews before booking, so reply to each one and keep hours current, including for cruise ship days.

## Worked Example: A Tour Operator Facebook Test

All numbers are **assumptions**.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget, 30 days | $200 |
| Message enquiries | 25 |
| Share that book | 12% |
| Bookings | 3 |
| Margin per booking | $100 |

Three bookings give $300 margin against $200 spend, a $100 gain. With two bookings, margin is $200 and the test breaks even. Count enquiries and bookings by source to see which channel works.
