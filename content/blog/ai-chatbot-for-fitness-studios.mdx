An AI chatbot lets a fitness studio answer the same questions all day, such as class times, prices, parking and what to wear, and move interested people toward a trial booking when nobody is at the desk. Tools start cheap: Tidio's free plan covers 50 billable conversations a month, and WhatsApp does not charge for service replies sent within its 24-hour window. The chatbot is worth adding only if people already message you.

The benefit is capture, not magic. A bot cannot create demand, and it can lose a lead if it answers wrongly. Plan a handoff to a person.

## What Can a Chatbot Do for a Fitness Studio?

It handles repeat, low-risk questions: opening hours, class schedule, membership options, intro offers, location and cancellation rules. It can collect a name and phone number, offer trial slots, and pass the conversation to staff.

Demand exists for it to serve. The Health and Fitness Association reports that 81 million Americans belonged to a gym, studio or other fitness facility in 2025, 5.2% more than in 2024, or 26.1% of people aged six and over. A studio competes for these members, and a quick answer helps a visitor who is comparing options.

## Which Chatbot Platform Should You Use?

Pick by channel first. If prospects write on your website, use a website chat tool. If they message on Instagram or Facebook, use a tool that connects to Meta. If your clients use WhatsApp, check WhatsApp's own rules and rates.

## What Are the Messaging Rules on Instagram and Facebook?

Meta's Messenger Platform policy sets a standard messaging window, which "for most user actions" lasts 24 hours. After it closes, message tags cover approved update types, and one-time notifications let a user ask for one follow-up. Meta says one-time notifications and sponsored messages are not available for Instagram.

That affects trial reminders. If a prospect messages at 9 pm and you reply the next afternoon, you may still be inside the window. If you reply three days later, you may not be allowed to message them at all, so answer fast.

## How Does WhatsApp Pricing Work?

WhatsApp says it charges per delivered message, based on recipient, category and market, with four categories: marketing, utility, authentication and service. A user message opens a 24-hour window in which service replies are free, and messages sent within 72 hours after a customer messages from a click-to-WhatsApp ad are not charged.

Rates are listed by market on WhatsApp's page and are not copied here because they differ by country. Marketing messages, such as promotions, are charged separately from replies.

## How Should You Set Up the Trial Flow?

Keep the flow short and fixed. A simple order works:

1. Greet and ask what the visitor wants: classes, prices or a trial.
2. Answer from a short approved list written by you, not improvised.
3. Offer two or three trial slots and take a name and phone number.
4. Send the details to the front desk or your booking system and confirm by text.
5. Offer a person at any point, and always after a complaint or a medical question.

Do not let a bot give health or injury advice, and keep health details out of chats. ## FAQ

### How much does a fitness studio chatbot cost?

Tidio lists a free plan with 50 billable conversations a month, Starter at $24.17 and Growth from $49.17. WhatsApp charges per message by category and market.

### Will a chatbot replace my front desk?

No. It answers routine questions and captures contact details. Complaints, billing issues and health questions need a person.

### Can a chatbot book a trial class?

Yes, if it connects to your calendar or booking system. Otherwise it can collect a name and number for staff to confirm.

### Are WhatsApp replies free?

Service replies inside the 24-hour window are not charged. Marketing messages are charged by market, and the rates are on WhatsApp's page.

### What happens after the Meta messaging window closes?

Meta allows message tags for approved use cases. One-time notifications and sponsored messages are not available for Instagram.
