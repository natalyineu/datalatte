## Does Instagram Reach Spa Clients?

Probably, but not for every age group. Adobe Express, citing DataReportal, reports that the largest Instagram age group is 25 to 34, followed by 18 to 24. The platform's users are reported as 48% female and 52% male, with data for other genders unavailable. Instagram says it reaches over 20% of the world's population.

A vendor, Bookeo, states that around 4 in 5 beauty shoppers use Instagram daily, but it does not name a source, so treat that figure with caution. Its general point is that Facebook and Instagram can reach audiences and build trust without spending anything on the platform itself.

For a spa, the practical question is whether your current clients are on Instagram at all. Ask them at booking, and check which age group your treatments already attract before adding Instagram to a budget that is already stretched.

## How Often Should a Spa Post on Instagram?

Hootsuite's research, as cited by Salon Biz Software, recommends 2 to 7 Instagram posts per week. A coffee shop guide from Bellwether Coffee recommends three to five feed posts per week plus daily Stories, and it estimates about 30 minutes per day of time investment for Instagram. Those figures come from other businesses, but they give a realistic starting range.

Pick a number you can keep. Three feed posts a week and daily Stories is a sensible first target for a small spa team. Consistency matters more than volume, because a schedule you abandon after two weeks produces no data at all.

If time is tight, a scheduling tool or an AI agent can draft posts. Sintra's Soshie, for example, produces platform-specific posts and can build a content calendar, according to the vendor, but every post should still be reviewed before it goes live. ## What Should a Spa Post Instead of Chasing Trends?

Trends burn out quickly. Infegy reports that GRWM (Get Ready With Me) video content took off in early 2023 across TikTok and Instagram, then engagement and post volume declined as content shifted toward lifestyle storytelling. A spa that copies a trend may get a short spike and then nothing.

Storytelling holds up better. Show the real treatment room, a therapist explaining what a first visit involves, or a seasonal menu with prices and a booking link. Instagram and Facebook are both listed by Trafft as places to showcase your work, run targeted ads and offer promotions.

Stories disappear after 24 hours, so use them for time-sensitive content such as last-minute openings. Keep evergreen material, like treatment explanations and prices, in a feed post or Highlight that people can find later.

## How Do You Turn Profile Visits Into Bookings?

Make the profile do the selling. Instagram and Facebook let a business add a Book Now button to its profile, according to Appointy, so use it and link it to the treatments you promote most. Include your area and main treatments in the bio so visitors know immediately whether you serve them.

Off Instagram, your Google Business Profile does similar work for local searches. Google allows businesses to post their own contact information, such as social media handles, email and phone number, on their own profile. Google's profile editor also lets you add one social media link per platform, so link your Instagram account there.

Keep the booking method consistent everywhere. If the Instagram bio says book online but the Google profile says call only, some enquiries will be lost. ## Should a Spa Spend on Instagram Ads?

Only as a small test with tracking. Adobe Express, citing AdRoll's 2021 figures, reports that Instagram ads range from $0.20 to $2 per click on average. That range is old, and costs vary by market and offer, so check the platform's live figures in Meta Ads Manager before you commit. Meta Ads Manager can run ads across Instagram and Facebook from one place, and Turrboo lists Messenger as well.

Worked example. Every input below is an assumption, not a measured result:

Budget: USD 750 a month, which sits at the low end of the vendor's suggested starting range.

Cost per click: $1.00 (assumption, within the $0.20 to $2 range).

Clicks: 750 ÷ 1.00 = 750 clicks.

Average first visit: $120 (assumption).

Bookings needed to break even: 750 ÷ 120 = 6.25, so about 7 bookings.

Share of clicks that must book to break even: 6.25 ÷ 750 = 0.83%.

Bookings at a 2% click-to-book rate (assumption): 750 × 0.02 = 15 bookings, worth 15 × $120 = $1,800 on first visits alone.

The breakeven is low, but the assumption carries the result. If your click-to-book rate is closer to 0.5%, the campaign loses money on first visits. Track each booking back to its source before you decide to scale. A grooming business's Facebook and Instagram example, which is hypothetical and not measured, projects 200 to 300 new clients from a 2% booking rate on reached users, which shows how sensitive these numbers are.

## What Does a Weekly Spa Instagram Routine Look Like?

Use this checklist as a starting point. Each step uses only the time and platform guidance covered above.

1. Update the profile: area, main treatments, a current Book Now link, and your Google profile link.
2. Plan three feed posts and a few Stories for the week, using real treatments and staff.
3. Draft posts with a tool if needed, then review each one yourself. HubSpot's guidance is to approve every AI-drafted post manually for the first one to two months, and a review should take only a few seconds per post once the agent is trusted.
4. Check comments and direct messages daily, and reply to booking questions the same day.
5. Once a month, note which posts led to a booking or enquiry, and drop the formats that did not.
6. Only after two or three months of steady organic results, test one ad with a single offer and a fixed budget.

## FAQ

### How often should a spa post on Instagram?
Hootsuite's research, as cited by Salon Biz Software, recommends 2 to 7 posts per week. Start with three and adjust based on which posts lead to enquiries.

### Do Stories or feed posts work better for spas?
They do different jobs. Stories disappear after 24 hours, which suits last-minute openings. Feed posts and Highlights keep treatment menus and prices available for people who visit your profile later.

### How much should a spa spend on Instagram ads?
A vendor suggests USD 750 to USD 1,500 a month as a starting point. Test a small amount with one offer and track bookings before increasing spend.

### Can a spa use Instagram without paying for ads?
Yes. Trafft and Bookeo both describe Instagram and Facebook as useful for showcasing work and building trust without paying for the platform itself. Time is the main cost, according to Marketing 360.

### Do I need to disclose free treatments given to influencers?
This article does not cover influencer disclosure rules. Check the current guidance from your country's advertising regulator before working with influencers.
