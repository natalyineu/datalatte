A spa should use Instagram as a booking channel: post original Reels and photos of real treatments, make the profile say where you are and how to book, and test a small ad budget only after organic posts bring in enquiries. Instagram is a good fit for spa audiences, since Pew's 2026 survey found 56% of US women and 64% of adults aged 30 to 49 use it.

Spa-specific Instagram data is not published by Instagram or Pew. This guide uses platform rules, Pew usage data and LocaliQ's Meta ad benchmarks for the closest category, Beauty and Personal Care. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## Does Instagram Reach Spa Clients?

Yes, for most spa age groups. Pew's 2026 fact sheet says 51% of US adults use Instagram: 79% of ages 18 to 29, 64% of 30 to 49, 41% of 50 to 64 and 21% of those 65 and over. Women are at 56% and men at 45%.

That means a spa aimed at women in their thirties and forties is well covered, while clients over 65 are mostly elsewhere. Reach them with a Google profile and email, not Instagram alone. Pew is a national survey, so it does not tell you how many people in your town follow local businesses.

## What Should a Spa Post on Instagram?

Instagram says each part of the app has its own ranking. In its ranking explainer, Reels favour content people find entertaining and predict whether viewers will watch to the end, reshare or like. Feed ranking looks at the post, the poster and a person's history with that account.

Instagram's creators site adds that Reels make up over 50% of time spent on the app. In January 2025 it also said Reels up to three minutes can be recommended to non-followers.

Content that fits a spa:

- A short Reel of a treatment step, filmed with client permission
- A tour of the room, products and what to expect on a first visit
- A therapist or esthetician introducing a treatment and who it suits
- Seasonal menus and gift-card announcements, with price and booking link

Post original material. Instagram's creator guidance says to avoid reposted content and that it is less likely to recommend watermarked clips.

## How Do You Turn Followers Into Bookings?

Make the profile do the selling. The bio should state the area, the main treatments and the booking method. Instagram also says including relevant keywords in your content, caption, bio and hashtags helps people find you through search.

Use Stories for last-minute openings and Highlights for treatment menus, first-visit steps and prices. Keep the booking link current and match it to what you promote.

For discovery in your town, a complete Google profile matters as much. See the [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## Should a Spa Work With Local Influencers?

Possibly, with rules. The FTC says free or discounted products or services are a material connection that must be disclosed, in the content itself and not buried in a group of hashtags. It names "ad" or "sponsored" as clear wording and advises avoiding vague terms like "collab" or "thanks."

Instagram's Collabs feature creates one post shared to both accounts' followers, which suits a spa and a local boutique or café. Read [micro-influencer marketing for local business](/blog/micro-influencer-marketing-local-business) before paying anyone.

## What Does Instagram Advertising Cost for a Spa?

WordStream by LocaliQ's September 2026 Meta benchmarks give Beauty and Personal Care a traffic CPC of $0.50 and, for lead campaigns, a CPC of $2.97, a 5.63% conversion rate and $50.91 per lead. These are Facebook ad benchmarks, not Instagram-only or spa data.

Worked example. All inputs are **assumptions** built on those benchmarks:

- Budget: $300 a month
- Cost per lead: $50.91 (benchmark)
- Leads: 300 / 50.91 = about 5.9
- Share who book: 40% (assumption), so about 2.4 bookings
- Average first visit: $120 (assumption)
- Revenue: about $283, below the $300 spend

Ads pay off here only if clients return. With three visits per new client, the same bookings bring about $850. ## FAQ

### How often should a spa post on Instagram?
Instagram does not publish a required frequency. Its guidance is to post original content consistently. Choose a number you can keep up, such as three posts a week.

### Do Reels work better than photos for spas?
Instagram says Reels make up over 50% of time spent on the app, so they have more reach potential. Photos and Highlights still help converting visitors who reach your profile.

### How much should a spa spend on Instagram ads?
No standard figure exists. Beauty and Personal Care leads cost $50.91 on average in LocaliQ's data, so even $300 a month buys only around six leads.

### Do I need to disclose free treatments given to influencers?
Yes, according to the FTC, free or discounted services count as a material connection.

### Can I use client photos?
Get written permission first. This article cannot give legal advice for your state or country.
