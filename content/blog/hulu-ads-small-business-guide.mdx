Ads run on Disney+ and Hulu, you pay by credit card, and billing starts after the ad begins running. ## How Do You Advertise on Hulu as a Small Business?

Hulu's old self-serve tool, Hulu Ad Manager, is now part of Disney Campaign Manager. Disney's FAQ says sign-up is free, there are no setup or subscription fees, and most advertisers are verified within 24 to 48 hours. The platform is for brands and agencies of all sizes.

Steps:

1. Sign up and wait for verification.
2. Upload one video ad that meets the specs.
3. Choose targeting. Disney lists demo, location, interests and show genres. Its homepage notes that options depend on the ad type.
4. Set the budget, at least $500 per campaign.
5. Submit for review, which takes at least three business days.

Third-party DSPs can also buy Hulu inventory, but those routes have their own minimums that Disney does not publish.

## What Are the Hulu Video Ad Specs?

Disney's FAQ says video ads must be 15 to 30 seconds and in MP4 or MOV. Disney's separate tech specs page lists 5 to 90 seconds, so the two pages disagree. Plan for 15 or 30 seconds and confirm in the upload tool.

| Spec | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Aspect ratio | 16:9 only. Files in 4:3, 3:4 or 2.39:1 are not accepted |
| Resolution | 1920x1080 preferred, 1280x720 accepted |
| Codec | Apple ProRes 422 HQ preferred, H.264 accepted |
| Max file size | 1.9 GB |
| Audio | 2-channel stereo, 128 Kbps or higher, 44.1 or 48 kHz |

Disney's video commercial page adds that clickable-looking buttons such as "Learn More" are not allowed, while URLs, hashtags and QR codes are.

## How Much Do Hulu Ads Cost?

Disney publishes the $500 minimum but no CPM. AdWave's Q2 2026 article, a vendor benchmark, puts premium programmatic services such as Hulu at $25 to $45 per thousand impressions and the whole CTV market at $15 to $45. It cites eMarketer without linking the data, so treat it as secondary.

Your actual price depends on targeting and competition. Ask for the delivery estimate inside the platform before you commit. Our [CTV cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data) explains the ranges.

## What Does $1,000 Buy? A Worked Example

All numbers are **assumptions**. The CPM is a vendor estimate, not a Disney rate.

- Budget: $1,000 for one campaign
- CPM: $30
- Impressions: 1,000 / 30 x 1,000 = about 33,000
- Households in the targeted area: 15,000
- Average frequency: about 2.2 views per household
- Value of a new customer: $400
- Break-even: 1,000 / 400 = 2.5 new customers

If you cannot tie at least three new customers to the campaign, the test did not pay back. Use a promo code, a unique phone number or a "how did you hear about us" field, because viewers rarely click TV ads.

## Is Hulu Better Than Google Ads or Meta Ads?

They serve different purposes. Search ads reach people already looking, and Meta ads are easy to measure by click. Hulu puts a full-screen video in front of a viewer during a show, which builds familiarity but is harder to trace.

## FAQ

### What is the minimum budget for Hulu ads?
Disney Campaign Manager lists $500 per campaign, for Disney+ or Hulu.

### Can I run Hulu ads outside the US?
The Disney pages checked do not list available countries. Check availability during sign-up.

### How long until my ad goes live?
Verification usually takes 24 to 48 hours, and ad review takes at least three business days, per Disney.

### Do I need a professional video?
Disney requires HD, 16:9 and stereo audio. It does not say a studio shoot is needed, but low-quality video can be rejected under platform policies.

### Can I target my town?
Disney lists location targeting. It does not say how small the area can be, so check the targeting screen.
