A fitness studio can use podcast ads, but only as a small, tracked test on a local or niche show with an intro offer people can claim. Libsyn Ads lists a host-read CPM of $27 for the Health & Fitness category, which means $27 per 1,000 downloads in the first 30 days. At that price, a show with 2,000 downloads per episode costs about $54 per ad, before any production.

Podcasts build familiarity, not instant bookings. If your Business Profile is thin or your site has no trial offer, fix those first. This guide covers prices, targeting, rules and a worked example. The inputs of the example are labelled as assumptions.

## Do Podcast Ads Work for a Local Fitness Studio?

They can reach people who are open to a routine change, but only a fraction of any show's audience lives within reach of your studio. Edison Research's Infinite Dial 2026, as reported by Tubefilter, found 80% of Americans aged 12 and over have listened to or watched a podcast. That is a US figure for any podcast, not for local fitness response.

So reach is not the problem. Geography is. A national show sends most of your money to people who cannot walk in. A city or neighbourhood show, or a show for runners, cyclists or new parents in your area, wastes less.

## What Does Podcast Advertising Cost?

Libsyn Ads gives host-read CPMs of $18 to $22 for 30-second spots and $24 to $26 for 60-second spots, and $27 for Health & Fitness. It does not state a minimum spend. Acast's guide lists $15 to $30 for pre-recorded ads and $25 to $40 for host-read sponsorships, and says CPM is per 1,000 listens.

Acast's page has an unclear date and an inconsistent FAQ range, so treat it as a rough guide. Small local shows often sell a flat fee per episode instead of a CPM. Convert any quote to a CPM by dividing the fee by listens, then multiplying by 1,000, so you can compare shows.

## What Should the Ad Say?

Keep it to one offer, one place and one action. A first-class pass, a trial week or a free assessment works better than a membership price. Give a short URL or code unique to the podcast, such as a page called "podcast-trial", so you can count joins.

Avoid promises about results that you cannot back up. Describe the class, the coach and the schedule instead. We did not find a source we could open that gives a typical response rate for fitness podcast ads, so plan to learn your own.

## Do You Have to Say It's an Ad?

Yes. The FTC's guidance says to disclose any financial, employment, personal or family relationship with a brand, and that terms like "ad," "advertisement" and "sponsored" are acceptable. Vague tags such as "collab" or "thanks" are not enough on their own. The guide does not address podcasts directly, so ask hosts to say "sponsored by" clearly, close to the endorsement.

If you are outside the US, your own advertising code applies. See our [Ireland podcast guide](/blog/podcast-advertising-ireland-local-business-guide) for the ASAI, and the [UK guide](/blog/podcast-advertising-uk-local-business-guide) for the UK code.

## What Does a Test Cost? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**, built on the Libsyn rate.

| Item | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Downloads per episode (assumption) | Local show | 2,000 |
| CPM (assumption) | Libsyn Health & Fitness host-read | $27 |
| Cost per episode | 2,000 / 1,000 x $27 | $54 |
| Episodes (assumption) | Chosen | 6 |
| Test cost | $54 x 6 | $324 |
| Trial sign-ups (assumption) | 0.3% of 12,000 downloads | 36 |
| Trial-to-member rate (assumption) | Chosen | 25% |
| New members | 36 x 25% | 9 |
| Cost per new member | $324 / 9 | $36 |
| Member value in first six months (assumption) | 6 x $60 | $360 |

On these assumptions the test pays back. If sign-ups were 0.05% instead, there would be six trials and about 1.5 members, and the cost per member would be about $216. The response rate is the number to measure. Run six episodes before deciding, since one ad rarely builds recall.

## FAQ

### How much does a podcast ad cost for a gym?

Libsyn lists $27 per 1,000 downloads for host-read Health & Fitness spots. A show with 2,000 downloads would cost about $54 per ad. Local shows may charge a flat fee.

### Is a podcast better than Instagram or Google for a studio?

Google Search captures people already looking for a class. Podcasts build awareness. Start with Search and your profile, and add audio once you can track joins. See [Instagram ads versus Facebook ads](/blog/instagram-ads-vs-facebook-ads) for the social option.

### How many episodes should I buy?

Plan on several. A single ad rarely tells you anything. Six is an assumption for a test, not a published rule.

### Can I make claims about results?

Stay with facts about your classes, such as schedule, coach and price. Avoid promises about weight loss or body change that you cannot back up.

### How do I track joins from a podcast?

Use a unique landing page, phone number or offer code per show. Ask each new member how they heard about you at sign-up.
