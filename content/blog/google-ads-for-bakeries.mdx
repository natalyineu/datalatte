A bakery should use Google Ads for the orders that need planning, such as custom, birthday and wedding cakes, and leave daily walk-in trade to Google Business Profile and local search. Run a small Search campaign near the shop, send people to an enquiry form or call button, and judge it by orders. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put Restaurants and Food at $2.05 per click and $30.57 per lead, the closest published category.

Neither LocaliQ nor Google publishes bakery-specific numbers. This guide uses the nearest category as a range, shows how to build the campaign, and marks every illustrative number as an assumption.

## How Much Do Google Ads Cost for a Bakery?

LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks list Restaurants and Food at $2.05 per click, a 6.83% click-through rate, an 8.05% conversion rate and $30.57 per lead. Across all industries the averages are $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead. Shopping, Collectibles and Gifts, which fits gift cakes and boxes, is $4.14 per click.

Your clicks may cost more or less than the food average. Competition in your town matters more than the national figure. Treat the benchmark as a starting range and replace it with your own data after one month.

## Which Bakery Products Should You Advertise?

Pick products where a customer plans ahead and where the order is large enough to justify paying for a click. Daily bread and pastries rarely do. Better candidates:

- Custom and birthday cakes
- Wedding and event cakes, with tasting bookings
- Corporate orders, such as breakfast platters or branded cookies
- Seasonal boxes for holidays

Split them into separate campaigns or ad groups so each has its own budget, landing page and message. Our [bakery keyword guide](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-bakeries-in-2026) lists search phrases for each group.

## How Do You Keep Ads Close to the Shop?

Use radius or named-area targeting for the distance people will drive or you will deliver, and check your delivery rules before promising it. Google requires a radius of at least 1 km for radius targeting and recommends "Presence or interest" on Search as a default, so test "Presence" if you only want people physically in your area.

Wedding customers may search from outside town, so consider a separate wedding campaign with a wider area than your birthday-cake campaign.

## What Should the Ad and Landing Page Say?

State the product, the area and the next step in plain words, for example custom cakes, local pickup and an enquiry form. Add a call asset: Google says taps on it are billed like headline clicks.

The landing page should show photos of your actual cakes, flavours, a lead-time note and one clear form with date, serving size and budget. Avoid pointing every ad to your home page. Match each page to the search, and test one page against another.

## When Should You Run the Ads?

Cake orders have deadlines, so schedule the spend to match them. Google's ad schedule lets you limit ads to certain days or hours, or adjust bids by time. Increase the budget in the weeks before busy dates, such as Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, graduation and wedding season, and give customers realistic lead times. Review the Search terms report each week and add negative keywords for recipes, jobs and baking supplies. Google notes negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals.

## Should You Use Ads or Organic Local Search?

Use both, in different jobs. A complete Business Profile with photos, hours and products helps you appear on the map for walk-in searches without paying per click. Ads help when a customer is comparing cake makers for a date. Read our [Instagram guide for bakeries](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-bakeries) for the free side.

## FAQ

### How much should a bakery spend on Google Ads?
No standard exists. At the $2.05 food benchmark, $400 a month buys roughly 195 clicks. Start with an amount you can run for at least two months.

### Are Google Ads worth it for a small bakery?
For custom and wedding orders they can be, because the order value is higher. For daily walk-in sales, a good Business Profile is usually more efficient.

### What keywords work for cake orders?
Use product plus place phrases, such as custom cakes or wedding cakes in your town, and add negatives for recipes and supplies.

### Can I advertise only to people near my shop?
Yes. Use radius or named locations, and test the Presence setting if you only want people physically in the area.

### How do I track cake orders from ads?
Use a call asset and a form with a source field, then record which enquiries become paid orders.
