A photographer should start with one Facebook lead campaign aimed at a tight local radius, a clear offer and a budget that can pay for at least 15 to 20 leads a month. LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put the all-industry average for lead campaigns at $1.80 per click and $27.39 per lead. Photography has no category of its own, so treat those numbers as a range, not a promise.

This guide is for portrait, family, newborn and wedding photographers selling to local clients. Every figure comes from a source listed at the end. Where a number is not published, the article says so. Worked examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Do Facebook Ads Cost a Photographer?

LocaliQ's Facebook benchmarks, last updated September 23, 2026, give overall averages for two campaign types. Traffic campaigns average a 1.93% click-through rate and $0.60 per click. Lead campaigns average a 2.70% click-through rate, $1.80 per click, an 8.54% conversion rate and $27.39 per lead. The page does not state the data period.

There is no photography or wedding row. These three published categories are the closest guides:

| Category (lead campaigns) | CTR | CPC | Conversion rate | Cost per lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arts and Entertainment | 3.34% | $0.88 | 15.31% | $14.59 |
| Personal Services | 2.24% | $1.62 | 6.61% | $38.09 |
| Beauty and Personal Care | 1.35% | $2.97 | 5.63% | $50.91 |

The spread is wide, from about $15 to $51 per lead. A photographer selling a $1,500 wedding package can live with a high cost per lead. One selling a $120 mini session cannot.

## Should You Run Traffic or Lead Campaigns?

Use lead campaigns when the goal is an enquiry. Use traffic campaigns only if your website already turns visitors into enquiries. The benchmarks show why: a traffic click is cheap at $0.60, but LocaliQ publishes no cost per lead for that objective, because it measures clicks.

A lead campaign costs more per click at $1.80 but is built around the form submission. For a solo photographer, a short form (name, phone, date, type of session) is easier to keep up with than a website funnel. Reply fast. LocaliQ does not publish how quickly leads go cold in photography, so set your own rule, such as replying the same day.

## Who Should You Target?

Start with geography, because it is the setting you fully control. Choose a radius around your studio or the area you travel to, and keep it to places you will actually drive. A wedding photographer may accept a wider area than a newborn photographer who works in a home studio.

Meta's own help pages could not be opened for this article, so this guide cannot confirm which interest or life-event options (for example, engagement or new parents) are available today. Open the audience section in Ads Manager and check what it offers before you build around a specific option.

Two audiences are safe to plan for: people near you, and people who already know you, such as past clients and website visitors. Our [wedding industry Facebook guide](/blog/facebook-ads-for-wedding-industry) covers the wedding side in more detail.

## What Should the Ad and the Offer Say?

Show your work and state one clear next step. The ad should name the session type, the area and what happens after the person responds. Use your real photos, not stock images, because the buyer is judging your style.

Examples of a single offer per ad: a booking-enquiry form for a wedding date, a limited number of spring mini sessions, or a free consultation call. Do not mix them in one ad. Test two or three images against each other and keep the same offer, so you can tell what changed the result.

LocaliQ does not publish click-through rates for photography creative, so there is no benchmark for a good image. Compare your own ads to each other.

## What Does a Booking Cost? A Worked Example

All inputs below are **assumptions** built on LocaliQ's published lead-campaign averages. Your close rate and prices will differ.

If your average booking is worth $1,200 (assumption), the middle column returns roughly $2,950 in bookings on $450 of ads, before your time and editing costs. If your average booking is $150, the same ad cost per booking is a loss. Work out your own break-even before you spend.

## How Much Should You Spend?

There is no published budget for photographers. A practical way to set one is to start from the leads you need. If you want about 15 leads a month and the all-industry average is $27.39, the budget is roughly $410. Round up, run it for at least a month, and judge by your own numbers.

Meta decides how many people see the ad, so a very small budget may give you too few leads to learn from. Do not change the audience, image and offer all at once. Change one thing, wait, then compare. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to place ads alongside the rest of your marketing.

## How Do You Measure Bookings?

Count what pays you, not what Facebook reports. Keep a simple sheet with four columns: lead date, source, whether they replied, and whether they booked. Ask every new client how they found you.

Review it monthly. Compare cost per lead to the benchmark in the table above, then compare cost per booking to your break-even. If leads are cheap but few book, the problem is the form, the reply speed or the price, not the ad.

## FAQ

### How much do Facebook ads cost for photographers?
LocaliQ does not publish a photography figure. Its 2026 all-industry average for lead campaigns is $1.80 per click and $27.39 per lead. Arts and Entertainment averages $14.59 per lead and Personal Services $38.09.

### How many leads will $300 a month produce?
At the all-industry average of $27.39 per lead, $300 buys about 11 leads. At $14.59 it buys about 21, and at $38.09 about 8. These are arithmetic on averages, not a forecast.

### Are lead ads better than sending people to my website?
Lead campaigns are built to collect enquiries and have a published cost per lead. Traffic campaigns are cheaper per click at $0.60 but have no published cost per lead. Choose by what your website can convert.

### Can I target engaged couples?

No source publishes this for photography. Plan for at least a month of data and compare cost per booking to your own break-even.
