TikTok sets the entry price itself. In Ads Manager, a campaign daily budget must be above $50 and each ad group daily budget above $20, so the cheapest real test runs about $600 over 30 days. What you get for that money varies: published planning ranges are $4 to $13 per 1,000 impressions (CPM) and $0.30 to $1.50 per click (CPC).

This guide separates the official minimums from second-hand benchmarks, shows what $600 can buy, and says when a local business should skip TikTok ads.

## What Is the Minimum Budget for TikTok Ads?

TikTok's [daily budget help page](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-daily-budgets), last updated February 2025, sets two floors:

- **Campaign level:** a daily or total budget must be more than $50.
- **Ad group level:** a daily budget must be more than $20. A lifetime budget is $20 multiplied by the scheduled days.

Many guides still say a campaign needs $500. The official page does not say that. Treat it as unconfirmed and check the budget fields in your own account.

## How Does TikTok Charge for Ads?

You pay by one of four methods, and the campaign objective decides which one applies, according to TikTok's [bidding methods page](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/bidding-methods) (updated August 2025):

| Method | You pay for | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPM | 1,000 impressions | Reach |
| oCPM | 1,000 impressions, optimized toward an action | Conversions, leads, follows |
| CPV | A view of 6 seconds or more, or an early interaction | Video views |
| CPC | A click | Traffic, conversions, profile visits |

So "TikTok CPC" only applies if you choose a click-based objective. Most conversion campaigns are billed per impression.

## What Do TikTok Ads Cost per Impression and per Click?

TikTok does not publish a price list, because the auction sets the price. The numbers below are second-hand. The WordStream page could not be opened directly, so they come from [AdManage's 2026 summary](https://admanage.ai/blog/tiktok-ads-cost) of several benchmark sources:

| Metric | Planning range | Examples cited |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPM | $4 to $13 | $4 to $10 (WordStream, April 2026); $9.16 (WebFX, Oct 2025); $13.26 (Triple Whale, ecommerce) |
| CPC | $0.30 to $1.50 | $0.30 to $1.50 (WordStream); $0.20 to $2.00 (Lebesgue) |
| CPV | $0.01 to $0.07 | WordStream, April 2026 |

These are mostly ecommerce and national-advertiser figures. A local radius is a small audience, so your own prices can differ. Treat the ranges as a planning range, not a promise.

## What Can a $600 Test Buy?

The figures below are **assumptions** built on the ranges above. One ad group at $20 a day for 30 days is $600, and it is a bit above the $20 floor in practice.

| Item | Expensive case | Cheap case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spend | $600 | $600 |
| Impressions at $13 or $4 CPM | about 46,000 | 150,000 |
| Clicks at $1.50 or $0.30 CPC | 400 | 2,000 |
| Bookings at an assumed 3% of clicks | 12 | 60 |
| Cost per booking | $50 | $10 |

If a booking is worth $35 in margin, the expensive case loses money (12 bookings earn $420 against $600 spent) and the cheap case nets about $1,500 after ad spend. The 3% rate is a guess. Replace it with your own booking-page conversion rate before you spend.

## How Much Should a Local Business Budget?

TikTok's [bidding strategy guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/best-practices-for-bidding-strategies) (updated August 2026) says to set the daily budget at 10 times your average daily cost per result so the campaign can leave the learning phase. Do the arithmetic for a local shop:

- A $20 daily budget supports a cost per result of about $2.
- A booking will rarely cost $2.
- So at the minimum, optimize for cheaper actions such as clicks or profile visits, and judge success on bookings you can trace.

Choose by situation:

1. **Under $300 a month to spend:** skip TikTok ads. Fix your Google Business Profile first.
2. **$600 for a 30-day test:** run one ad group, one offer, one landing page.
3. **$1,500 or more a month:** you can test objectives for bookings and need a pixel or booking tracking.

## What Else Affects the Price?

- **Objective:** it sets the billing method.
- **Audience size:** a narrow radius leaves fewer people to bid on, which can limit delivery.
- **Creative:** TikTok's auction rewards ads that people watch, so weak video costs more per result. Compare formats in our [Spark Ads guide](/blog/tiktok-spark-ads-guide).
- **Competition and season:** prices move, so compare month against month.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the minimum you can spend on TikTok ads?**
A: More than $50 a day at campaign level and more than $20 a day per ad group, per TikTok's help page. Check Ads Manager, because limits can change.

**Q: Is $10 a day enough?**
A: Not in Ads Manager. The ad group floor is above $20 a day. Lower entry points exist in boosting tools, but they offer less control.

**Q: How much does a TikTok click cost?**
A: Planning ranges are $0.30 to $1.50, according to second-hand 2026 benchmarks. Your cost depends on your audience and creative.

**Q: Do I pay per click or per impression?**
A: It depends on the objective. Reach and conversion campaigns bill per 1,000 impressions, and traffic campaigns can bill per click.

**Q: How long should a test run?**
A: Long enough to finish the learning phase and collect a meaningful number of bookings. TikTok ties this to budget, not days.
