NBA games are sold by the networks that hold the rights: Disney (ABC and ESPN), NBCUniversal (NBC and Peacock) and Amazon (Prime Video). A local business can reach them through Amazon's self-service ad platform, where the recommended campaign minimum is $10,000, or through local broadcast affiliates. NBA-specific CPMs are not published by any of the three, so any price table you see for "NBA CTV" is an estimate.

This guide lists who carries what in the 11-year deal that began in 2025-26, how the buying routes differ, and how to judge whether the cost makes sense for a local shop. Prices are marked as not published where that is the case.

## Where Can You Watch the NBA in 2026-27?

According to the NBA's announcement of its media agreements, the deal runs through 2035-36 and splits games across three partners:

- **Disney (ABC and ESPN):** 80 regular-season games a year, more than 20 on ABC and up to 60 on ESPN, all five Christmas Day games, and the NBA Finals, which stay exclusively on ABC.
- **NBCUniversal (NBC and Peacock):** up to 100 regular-season games, more than half on NBC, a weekly Monday doubleheader on Peacock and regionalized Tuesday games on NBC affiliates.
- **Amazon (Prime Video):** 66 regular-season games, including Thursday and Friday doubleheaders and a Black Friday game, all six Play-In Tournament games and about one third of first and second round playoff games.

Amazon also becomes the third-party destination for NBA League Pass. TNT is not named in the NBA's release, so older guides that list it as a partner are out of date.

## Which Conference Finals Go Where?

NBCUniversal and Amazon rotate one Conference Finals series each year: NBC has one in 6 of the 11 years, starting 2025-26, and Amazon has one in 6 of 11, starting 2026-27. Disney has one of the two series in 10 of the 11 years. For a local advertiser this matters only if you plan to buy playoff inventory, which is the most expensive and most competitive.

## How Can a Local Business Buy NBA Ads?

There are three realistic routes, and they differ in budget and control.

1. **Amazon Ads self-service (Prime Video, including NBA).** Amazon's page lists a $10,000 recommended campaign minimum for self-service packages and $50,000 for managed service. Supply varies by locale, and the page does not mention targeting by city or DMA, so confirm local targeting with Amazon before committing.
2. **Local broadcast affiliates.** NBC's regionalized Tuesday games run on NBC affiliates, and ABC and NBC affiliates sell local spots. Contact the station's sales team for rates, which are not public.
3. **Other CTV buys near NBA dates.** Targeting basketball fans on other streaming inventory is possible, but it does not place your ad inside the game.

## Which Local Businesses Should Consider NBA Ads?

Businesses with high lifetime value per customer and a wide service area fit best: car dealerships, home service companies, gyms, restaurants with multiple locations, and sports bars. If your customer is worth under $100 and lives within a few miles, local search ads or a [comparison of CTV platforms](/blog/ctv-advertising-platforms-compared-2026) is a better place to start.

## FAQ

### Where are NBA games streamed in 2026-27?
On Peacock (every NBC game plus Monday doubleheaders), Prime Video (66 regular-season games), and ESPN (up to 60 games, plus more than 20 on ABC), as laid out in the NBA's media agreements announcement.

### How much do NBA CTV ads cost?
Rates are not published. Ask the platform or station for a quote and treat any generic NBA price table as an estimate.

### Can a small local business advertise on Prime Video NBA games?
Amazon lists a $10,000 recommended minimum for self-service packages. Small budgets are possible but may not buy enough impressions.

### Is TNT still showing NBA games?
TNT is not named among the NBA's partners in the media agreements release for the deal starting 2025-26.
