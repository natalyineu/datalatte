A local business can use Meta Advantage+ if its goal is sales or leads and it is willing to let Meta choose the audience, placements and budget split inside a location you set. It is a poor fit if you need to control exactly who sees the ad or where. Meta publishes no benchmark that shows Advantage+ beating manual campaigns for local businesses, so run a side-by-side test before moving your whole budget.

This guide uses Meta's developer documentation, read on 1 November 2026. Meta's marketing page and several help pages did not load for us, so claims such as percentage savings are not repeated. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Is Meta Advantage+?

Meta's documentation describes Advantage+ campaigns as a way to run campaigns with automation turned on for three levers: Advantage+ audience, Advantage+ campaign budget and Advantage+ placements. A campaign counts as Advantage+ only when all three are enabled. The goal can be sales, leads or app installs ([Meta Marketing API docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/advantage-campaigns)).

Meta also says it is replacing the separate workflows for manual campaigns, Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns and Advantage+ App Campaigns. As of API version 25.0, developers can no longer create the old shopping campaigns with the previous field ([Meta Marketing API docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/advantage-shopping-campaigns)). In Ads Manager you will see this as Advantage+ sales, leads and app campaigns.

## What Do the Three Levers Do?

| Lever | What Meta automates | What you keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience | Finds people likely to act, beyond a fixed interest list | Locations (geo targeting) and other limits you set |
| Budget | Spreads campaign budget across ad sets | The campaign budget and bid strategy |
| Placements | Uses all eligible placements | Nothing, if you exclude placements the lever turns off |

The documentation says the recommended bid strategy is lowest cost without a cap, and that cost cap, bid cap and minimum ROAS strategies are also supported. It notes that the full range of ad tools remains available. For a local business, the audience and location setting is the most important control.

## When Does Advantage+ Suit a Local Business?

It suits a business with a clear conversion event Meta can count, such as online bookings, a lead form or online orders. It needs conversion data to learn from, so a shop with no tracking will give the system little to optimise. Meta does not publish a minimum number of conversions in the pages we opened, so check Ads Manager's guidance for your account.

It suits less well when your goal is walk-in awareness for a single neighborhood, because you set the location but Meta decides who within it sees the ad. For a plain overview of Meta for local shops, read our [Meta Ads guide for local business](/blog/local-business-meta-ads).

## How Do You Keep Spend Local?

Set locations first and check them. The documentation says you can enable Advantage+ audience with no targeting other than geographic locations, so the location is the main boundary you give Meta. Use a radius around the business, or named postcodes or neighborhoods, depending on your country.

Review the placement and location reports after the first week. If most results come from places you cannot serve, tighten the area. Meta's documentation does not state a minimum radius for Advantage+, so test in your own account.

## How Do You Protect Existing Customers From Being Targeted?

Past advertisers used a cap on existing-customer spend in Advantage+ Shopping. The Meta developer page says that to replicate an existing-customer budget split, you can create two ad sets, one that targets existing customers and one that excludes them. Upload your customer list as a custom audience for the exclusion.

For a salon or café this matters, because paying to reach people who already book is a poor use of a small budget. Our [retargeting guide](/blog/retargeting-ads-for-local-business) shows when reaching past customers is worth it.

## The Math: How Would You Test It?

All numbers below are **assumptions**. A real test needs your own conversion data.

| Input | Manual campaign | Advantage+ campaign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $300 | $300 |
| Run time | 4 weeks | 4 weeks |
| Locations | Same 5-mile area | Same 5-mile area |
| Creative | Same 3 ads | Same 3 ads |
| Goal | Booking form submits | Booking form submits |
| Result to compare | Cost per booking | Cost per booking |

Run both for the same weeks, and judge by cost per booking, not cost per click. If the Advantage+ campaign wins by a clear margin over at least a month, move more budget. If results are within noise, keep the cheaper one to manage. Do not make the call after a few days, because learning takes time. ## What Are the Risks?

Automation reduces control. You cannot easily say why one placement or audience won. Costs may move with the season and auction, and Meta publishes no local-business cost benchmark we could verify. Treat any article that quotes a percentage saving without a method, including older versions of this one, with caution.

Keep a spreadsheet with results by week, and compare against your total new customers, not just Meta's reported conversions. For a spend plan, see the [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide).

## FAQ

### Is Meta Advantage+ worth it for a small local business?
Maybe. It can reduce set-up work if you have conversion tracking and a clear goal. Test it against a manual campaign with the same budget and area before deciding.

### What is the difference between Advantage+ and a manual campaign?
In Advantage+, Meta automates audience, campaign budget and placements. In a manual setup you choose those yourself.

### Can I limit Advantage+ to my city?
Yes. The documentation shows Advantage+ audience working with geographic locations as the only targeting you set. Check reports to confirm delivery.

### Does Advantage+ lower my cost per lead?
No independent local-business benchmark was found in the sources opened. Measure your own cost per booking.

### Is Advantage+ Shopping still available?
Meta says it is replacing separate Advantage+ Shopping workflows with Advantage+ campaigns for sales, app and leads.
