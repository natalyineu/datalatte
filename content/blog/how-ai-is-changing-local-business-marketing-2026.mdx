AI is changing local marketing in three places: how customers find businesses, how they judge them, and how ads are built. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults found 45% use ChatGPT and other generative AI tools for local business recommendations, up from 6% in 2025. Pew Research found people clicked a regular result in 8% of Google visits that showed an AI summary, against 15% without one. The practical response is to keep your listings, reviews and website accurate.

## How Are Customers Finding Local Businesses With AI?

BrightLocal's Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 reports that 45% of consumers use ChatGPT and other generative AI tools, compared with 6% in 2025, which makes AI the third most popular source for business recommendations in its results. It also reports that 40% trust AI platforms for business recommendations, and 42% trust them as much as traditional reviews. The panel was 1,002 US adults surveyed through SurveyMonkey.

That is a survey of stated behaviour, in one country, so it shows direction, not the size of your own AI traffic. Check how your business appears when you ask an AI tool for "best [your service] near me", and note whether the answer is accurate.

## Do AI Summaries Reduce Clicks to Websites?

Pew Research analysed browsing by 900 US adults in March 2025, covering 68,879 unique Google searches, 12,593 of which produced an AI summary. Users clicked a traditional result in 8% of visits with a summary and 15% without one. Clicks on links inside the summary happened in 1% of visits. Users left the site or ended the session after 26% of summary pages, versus 16% of pages without.

The study covers general searches, not local searches, and it predates 2026. It does not say whether the people who do click are worth more. Still, it supports putting key facts such as hours, prices and services where a summary can read them. ## Have Reviews Become More Important?

BrightLocal's 2026 data suggests the bar is rising:

| Finding | 2026 | 2025 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumers who use Google for reviews | 71% | 83% |
| Will only use a business rated 4+ stars | 68% | 55% |
| Will only use a business rated 4.5+ stars | 31% | 17% |
| Will only use a business rated 5 stars | 10% | not stated |
| Look for reviews from the last three months | 74% | not stated |
| Read AI-generated review summaries | 82% | not stated |

Fewer people start on Google, yet more demand a high rating, and most read AI summaries of reviews. A recent, steady flow of honest reviews counts more than a single burst. Our [Google review guide for coffee shops](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) shows how to ask. Google also says more reviews and positive ratings can help local ranking.

## How Is AI Changing Ads?

Google's Performance Max can generate headlines, descriptions and images from your landing page and prompts. Google's help page says you should review any generated or suggested assets for accuracy, misleading claims and policy compliance, and that assets created by generative AI are not guaranteed to be approved by Ads Policy.

That makes AI a drafting tool, not an approver. Our guide to [AI-written ad copy](/blog/how-to-use-ai-to-write-ad-copy-local-business) covers the review steps, and the [Performance Max guide](/blog/google-performance-max-for-local-business-complete-guide-2026) explains where Google's automation sits.

## Can AI Replace Your Marketing Help?

Parts of it, not all. Drafting posts, summarising reviews and sorting inbox questions are tasks AI tools can speed up. Judgement about offers, pricing, staff capacity and what to say to a customer remains with you. Our [AI vs human marketing comparison](/blog/ai-vs-human-marketing-local-business-honest-comparison) lists tasks on each side. No source in this guide measures time saved, so test one task and time it yourself.

## Worked Example: A One-Hour AI Visibility Check

The steps are a suggested routine. The business and counts are **assumptions**.

| Step | Time (assumption) | What you record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask three AI tools for "best hair salon in your city" | 10 min | Whether you appear and what they say |
| Check your Business Profile hours, services, categories | 15 min | Errors to fix |
| Read your latest 10 Google reviews | 15 min | Recent rating, response gaps |
| Search Google for your main service and look for an AI summary | 10 min | Who is cited |
| Write three fixes | 10 min | A to-do list |

Repeat monthly and keep screenshots, because AI answers vary between asks. The [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) gives a fix list for the profile.

## FAQ

### How many consumers use AI to find local businesses?
BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 45% of US consumers use ChatGPT and other generative AI tools for local business recommendations, up from 6% in 2025.

### Do AI summaries reduce clicks on Google?
Pew found 8% of visits with an AI summary included a click on a regular result, against 15% without one.

### Do people trust AI recommendations?
BrightLocal found 40% trust AI platforms for business recommendations, and 42% trust them as much as traditional reviews.

### Should I still collect Google reviews?
Yes. 74% of consumers in BrightLocal's survey look for reviews from the last three months, and 68% only use businesses rated four stars or higher.

### Can I let AI write my ads?
You can draft with it, but Google says to review generated assets for accuracy and policy compliance, since approval is not guaranteed.
