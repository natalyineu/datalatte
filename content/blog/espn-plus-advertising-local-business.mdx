Yes, a local business can advertise on ESPN streaming, but usually through Disney's advertising sales team, a partner agency or a programmatic platform (a DSP) rather than a self-serve dashboard like Google Ads. Smaller budgets generally go the programmatic route, where you choose a sports content category, set geographic targeting for your area and pay on a CPM basis. Rights, product names and ad packages change often, so confirm current details with Disney Advertising or your agency before you plan a campaign.

A note on naming: Disney has been restructuring how ESPN's streaming products are packaged and sold, so "ESPN+" may be called something different, or bundled differently, by the time you buy. Ask your rep or DSP what inventory is currently available and how it is labeled.

## What Live Sports Content Does ESPN Streaming Carry?

ESPN's portfolio has historically included a broad mix of live sports. Depending on current rights deals and packaging, that has included:

- **College football and basketball:** a large share of college sports airs across ESPN's networks and streaming, with regular-season games, rivalry weekends and bowl-season events
- **NFL Monday Night Football:** carried on ESPN and ABC, with streaming availability that depends on the current arrangement
- **UFC and combat sports:** historically a major draw on ESPN streaming
- **MLB, NHL, soccer and tennis:** a mix of league games and major tournament coverage
- **NBA:** Disney has had NBA rights through its current media deal

Which of these are available as ad inventory for a small local buy depends on the current rights and how Disney packages them. Ask what is available in your market and what the minimum spend is.

## How Disney's Advertising Platform Works

Disney owns ESPN, ABC and Hulu, among other brands, and sells advertising across them. For a local business that can mean:

1. You can potentially run ads across sports and entertainment streaming through one partner
2. Disney can combine its viewing data with third-party data for audience targeting
3. You can sometimes buy sports adjacency and entertainment reach together

### Direct Sales or Partner Agencies

Disney Advertising works with advertisers and agencies on larger campaigns. Minimums for direct buys are typically higher than for programmatic. A local CTV agency with Disney access can buy against specific sports content for your DMA. Ask for "local streaming sports adjacency" or the specific event you have in mind.

### Programmatic Platforms (Usually Best for Smaller Budgets)

Some Disney streaming inventory has been available through programmatic platforms such as The Trade Desk and others. A typical setup looks like this:

1. Work with a DSP or an agency that has access
2. Create a CTV campaign with sports content as the context
3. Set geographic targeting to your metro area, or ZIP codes, if supported
4. Schedule around the live events that fit your customers
5. Ask whether you can specify Disney or ESPN-owned inventory, or whether it is bundled with other sports supply

Your reach depends on your budget, the CPM, the market and the content, so ask for a delivery estimate before you commit.

## What Does It Cost?

Live sports is typically the most expensive CTV content because viewers are attentive and demand is high. Exact CPMs for ESPN content vary by event, season, targeting and buying route, and they change from year to year, so request current quotes and compare. In general:

| Content type | Relative cost |
|---|---|
| Major live events, playoffs, marquee games | Highest |
| Regular-season sports and college games | Higher |
| Niche sports and fight nights | Often lower per impression |
| Non-sports library content | Lowest |

To estimate impressions, divide your budget by the quoted CPM and multiply by 1,000. For example, $2,000 at a hypothetical $40 CPM would be about 50,000 impressions, spread across many households and frequency levels.

## Planning a Year-Round Strategy

Because ESPN covers many sports, you can plan around the calendar: football and college basketball in the fall and winter, basketball and hockey playoffs in spring, baseball and summer events through the warmer months. A smaller, steady buy can maintain brand presence, while bursts around key events can be added when the budget allows. Match the sport to your customers: fitness around fight nights, restaurants around marquee games, and home services during high-viewership events.

## How to Measure Results

CTV does not generate many direct clicks, so build in measurement:

- Use a dedicated landing page or phone number for the campaign
- Watch branded search volume and Google Business Profile actions in your target area
- Ask new customers how they heard about you
- Compare results to a period with no campaign

## FAQ

**Q: If I buy ESPN streaming ads, do they also run on regular cable ESPN?**
A: Not automatically. Cable and streaming are different inventory, though Disney can sell cross-platform packages. Programmatic CTV buys mainly reach streaming viewers.

**Q: Is ESPN streaming cheaper than Amazon Prime Video for NFL games?**
A: Prices depend on the game, the package and the buying route, and the rights landscape changes. Ask for current quotes for each platform and compare cost per thousand impressions and the expected audience.

**Q: Can a small local business afford ESPN streaming ads?**
A: Programmatic routes can allow smaller budgets than direct buys, but live sports is premium inventory. Ask your DSP or agency about minimums and expected delivery.

**Q: Can I target my city or ZIP code?**
A: Many CTV buys support DMA and ZIP code targeting. Confirm what is available for the specific inventory you want.

**Q: Do I need a special video ad?**
