## How Big Is Tasmania's Visitor Market?

Tourism Tasmania's March 2026 release says visitors reached an annual record of 1.4 million in the year ending March 2026, 6.0% above 2019. Spend rose 7.8% to $3.8 billion, and international spend hit a record $758.6 million, up 36.2%. The release does not report nights.

These are state totals. They do not show how much any one business gains. If visitors buy from you, compare your own monthly sales with last year before moving budget. The page does not break out seasons, so use your own records.

## How Should You Target Visitors and Locals?

Google's location help says radius targeting needs at least 1 km. "Presence or interest" reaches people in or interested in your location, and "Presence" limits ads to people physically there.

A Hobart cafe near the waterfront may test "Presence or interest" to catch pre-trip searches. A Launceston tradesperson who works only locally should use "Presence". Our [Google Ads for hair salons guide](/blog/google-ads-for-hair-salons-complete-guide) shows a radius setup.

## How Do You Win the Map Listing in Tasmania?

Google says local ranking depends on relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to pay for a better ranking. Fill in every field, add photos and reply to reviews. For holidays and seasonal closures, use special hours rather than editing regular hours.

Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers each field. For another Australian example, see [South Australia](/blog/small-business-marketing-sa-australia).

## What Does the Spam Act Require for Email and Text?

LegalVision's summary of the Spam Act 2003 (secondary source: law firm) says a commercial message is likely spam if it lacks the receiver's express or implied consent, does not identify the sender, or has no unsubscribe facility. The Act covers mobile phone messaging as well as email. Consent must be provable.

The article gives no unsubscribe time limit or penalty amounts. Check the Australian Communications and Media Authority before you send. See [SMS vs email](/blog/sms-vs-email-marketing-local-business-which-is-better).

## FAQ

### How much should a Tasmanian business spend on Google Ads?

No Tasmanian figure is published. At $5.42 a click, $400 a month buys about 74 clicks. Start with an amount you can afford to lose.

### Do record visitor numbers mean more sales for me?

Not automatically. Compare your own sales by month with visitor patterns before shifting budget.

### Does the Spam Act apply to text messages?

LegalVision says the Act covers mobile phone messaging. Get consent, identify yourself and include an unsubscribe option.

### Is Google Business Profile worth it?

Yes. It is free, and reviews, links and complete information affect your prominence.
