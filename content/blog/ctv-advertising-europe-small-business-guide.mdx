A small business in Europe can buy connected TV (CTV) ads without an agency, but only in some markets and on some platforms. Amazon says self-service Fire TV ads have no minimum spend where offered, against $10,000 for managed service, and lists Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands and the UK among its markets. No European CTV price list is public, so you set your own CPM expectations from a test.

This guide covers how to start, what GDPR changes, and how to size a first test. It does not quote average European CPMs, because no published source for them exists that we could verify. A worked example below uses an assumed CPM, clearly labelled.

## How Big Is CTV in Europe?

IAB Europe's AdEx Benchmark for 2025 puts European digital advertising at €131bn across 30 markets, up 10.5%. Video grew 19.6% to €34.0bn and now makes up more than half of display spend. Reporting on the study names CTV as a growth area but gives no separate CTV figure, so treat any "CTV is X% of Europe" claim without a source with caution.

For a local business the useful point is simpler. Money is moving to video and streaming, but the market is split by country and language. You usually plan one country at a time.

## Where Can a Small Business Buy CTV Ads in Europe?

Amazon is the clearest self-serve route. Its Fire TV ads page lists availability in the UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain and the Netherlands, among other countries, and says managed service needs $10,000 while self-service has no minimum "where available". Its DSP announcement of 9 July 2024 added Fire TV supply in the same European markets and says it removes minimum spend barriers for managed-service customers.

Other platforms, such as national broadcasters' streaming apps, are mostly sold through sales teams or DSPs. No public minimums or rate cards were available for them, so ask for a written quote.

## Can You Target Locally Under GDPR?

GDPR lets you process personal data only on a legal basis such as consent or legitimate interests, and consent must be free, specific, informed and unambiguous, according to the European Data Protection Board's SME guide. That mainly affects behavioural and third-party data targeting.

Geographic targeting, such as a city or postcode area, needs less personal data than interest profiles. This is not legal advice, and your platform's terms and national rules still apply. Ask the seller what data a segment uses and who collected consent.

IAB Europe's Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) is a voluntary standard used by publishers, vendors and consent platforms. Version 2.3 became the current version, with an adoption deadline of 28 February 2026. A DSP or publisher handles it, not you.

## What Creative Rules Apply in Europe?

Broadcasters in Europe commonly work to the EBU R 128 loudness recommendation, which sets a programme target of -23.0 LUFS. Version R 128-2023 allows a tolerance for live content. Ask each platform for its own spec sheet, because file size, length and audio rules differ by seller.

Language matters more than format. Run creative in the local language, and keep a separate version per country. Border regions need tight location settings so the wrong language does not reach viewers.

## How Much Should a First Test Cost?

Because CPMs are not public, test small and read the price from your delivery report. The example below uses an assumed CPM only to show the arithmetic.

**Worked example (all inputs are assumptions, not market data):**

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget | €1,000 |
| CPM paid | €20 |
| Impressions | 50,000 |
| Share of impressions on viewers in your target area | 80% |
| Local impressions | 40,000 |
| Local households reached at 4 views each | 10,000 |

If your real CPM is €30, the same budget buys about 33,000 impressions. Use your invoice, not this table. Decide the result you need first, such as promo-code redemptions or new-customer calls, and compare cost per result with your other channels.

## What Are Common Mistakes?

Buying in a market where your seller does not offer self-service, then discovering a high minimum. Using one language across several countries. Targeting too wide, so most impressions fall outside your delivery area. Judging the test on views only, with no offer code, landing page or call tracking to count responses.

## FAQ

### Do I need a European company to buy CTV ads in the EU?
No source we checked requires it for Amazon self-service. Account and billing rules differ by platform, so check the seller's terms, and take advice on GDPR if you handle customer data.

### What is the average CTV CPM in Europe?
No current public source gave one, so this guide does not quote it. Ask sellers for a quote in your target area and compare it with your test result.

### Is there a minimum budget?
Amazon states no minimum for self-service Fire TV ads where available, and $10,000 for managed service. Other sellers set their own terms.

### Which country should I start in?
Start where you have customers. Amazon lists Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands and the UK, so a business in those markets has a self-serve route to try.
