## Why "Near Me" Keywords Still Win

For independent residential cleaning companies, "near me" and "[service] near me" terms convert at 2–3x the rate of generic service keywords, because the searcher has already decided they need a cleaning service and is choosing between nearby options. These searchers are bottom-of-funnel — bid aggressively here, especially on mobile.

<Callout type="tip">
Increase your mobile bid adjustment by 20–30% for "near me" keywords. Mobile searchers for local services convert at significantly higher rates than desktop, particularly during business hours.
</Callout>

Search volume for cleaning services follows a predictable annual pattern. Plan your budget around it:

## Negative Keywords Every Cleaning Service Campaign Needs

Add these immediately to avoid paying for clicks that will never convert:
- "free", "diy", "how to", "at home", "tutorial"
- "jobs", "career", "salary", "hiring", "training"
- "wholesale", "supplier", "for sale", "equipment"
- "cleaning products", "cleaning supplies", "bleach"
- "car cleaning", "car wash", "auto detailing" (unless you offer it)
- "pool cleaning", "gutter cleaning" (unless part of your services)
- near me + a city you don't actually serve (use location exclusions, not just negative keywords)

A clean negative keyword list typically cuts wasted spend by 15–25% within the first 30 days.

## Match Type Strategy

Start with **phrase match** on your top 10 keywords — broad enough to catch variations, tight enough to avoid irrelevant traffic. Move to **exact match** for your 3–5 best performers once you have 30+ days of conversion data. Avoid **broad match** entirely until you have a strong negative keyword list of 50+ terms; broad match without guardrails is the fastest way to burn a budget on irrelevant clicks.

Separate campaigns by service type to control bids and budgets by priority:

```
Campaign 1: Core Residential — "Near Me" & City Terms
├── Ad Group 1: House Cleaning Near Me

Campaign 2: Move-Out & Specialty
├── Ad Group 1: Move-Out Cleaning
│   Keywords: "move out cleaning near me," "end of lease cleaning [City]"
├── Ad Group 2: Airbnb / Vacation Rental
│   Keywords: "airbnb cleaning service [City]," "vacation rental turnover cleaning"
└── Ad Group 3: Post-Construction
```

This structure lets you pause commercial campaigns when at residential capacity, increase move-out bids during peak season without inflating all budgets, and track conversion rates separately by service type.

## Landing Page Must-Haves for Cleaning Services

Your ad should send traffic to a service-specific page — not your homepage. A dedicated landing page for "house cleaning [City]" needs:

1. **Headline that matches the ad:** "Professional House Cleaning in [City]" — not your company tagline
2. **Price or price range visible above the fold:** People exit pages that hide pricing
3. **Trust elements:** license number, insurance badge, review count, years in business
4. **Booking form or phone number high on the page:** Don't make people scroll to contact you
5. **Service area map or city list:** Customers need to know you serve their neighborhood
6. **Clear guarantee:** "100% satisfaction or we re-clean free" removes purchase hesitation

A dedicated landing page with these elements converts at 8–15%. A generic homepage converts at 2–4%.

## Setting Up Conversion Tracking

Google Ads can't optimize toward what it can't measure. Set up all three before your first dollar is spent:

**1. Form submission tracking:** Install the Google Ads conversion tag on your "booking confirmed" thank-you page. Every form submission that reaches this page is a conversion.

**2. Phone call tracking:** Enable call extensions in Google Ads. Use Google's free forwarding numbers — every call from an ad is tracked automatically. For cleaning companies, a large share of bookings happen by phone, especially among older customers.

**3. Mobile tap-to-call tracking:** Add a click-to-call conversion for your phone number on the website. Mobile users who tap your number directly (without going to the landing page) are high-intent customers you'd otherwise miss in your data.

**Weekly checks:**
- Search terms report: add new negatives for irrelevant searches
- Cost per conversion by campaign: move budget from high-CPA campaigns to low-CPA ones
- Quality Score by keyword: anything below 5 needs landing page or ad copy improvements

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best Google Ads keywords for independent residential cleaning companies?

The highest-converting keywords combine service + intent or service + location: "house cleaning near me", "deep cleaning service [City]", and similar near-me or urgent-intent phrases. Broad single-word terms like "cleaning service" have high volume but poor conversion without tight geo-targeting. Move-out and same-day cleaning keywords consistently outperform general terms because intent is specific and urgent.

### How much does it cost to advertise a cleaning service on Google Ads?

Expect an average CPC around $5.60 in 2026. Most independent residential cleaning companies budget $500–$1,500/month depending on market size and competition. Track cost-per-booking rather than cost-per-click to judge real performance.

### What negative keywords should independent residential cleaning companies use?

Start with "free", "diy", "how to", "at home", "jobs", "career", "wholesale", "cleaning products", "cleaning supplies", and "for sale". These block searches from people who will never become paying customers. Check your search terms report weekly and keep adding to the list.

### Should independent residential cleaning companies use phrase match or broad match?

Use phrase match for your core keyword list. Reserve broad match for keyword discovery campaigns only, and only once you have a negative keyword list of 50+ terms — otherwise broad match wastes significant budget on irrelevant traffic.

### How do I track whether Google Ads is generating actual bookings?

Install the Google Ads conversion tag on your booking confirmation page, enable call extensions with Google's forwarding numbers, and add click-to-call tracking for your website phone number. Without all three, you're missing a significant portion of actual conversions. Most cleaning companies discover they're generating 2–3x more leads than incomplete tracking suggested.

### Can I run Google Ads for a cleaning company on a $300/month budget?

Yes, in smaller markets or with a very tight focus. At $300/month with a $5.50 average CPC, you get roughly 55 clicks. At a 10% conversion rate, that's 5–6 booked cleans. At $120 average first clean value, that's $600–$720 in revenue from $300 in spend. It works if you focus on move-out and same-day keywords (higher intent) and use a dedicated landing page.
