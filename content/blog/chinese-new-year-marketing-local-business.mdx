## How Large Is the Chinese Travel Market?

Chinese travelers made 145 million outbound trips in 2024, spending 5.75 trillion RMB, a 17% year-on-year increase, according to [Shanghai Jungle](https://www.shanghaijungle.com/blog/tourism-marketing-china-guide) (secondary). Tourism Tiger reports that Chinese travelers spent $215 billion on outbound tours in the year before its article was written ([Tourism Tiger](https://tourismtiger.com/blog/4-ways-for-tour-operators-to-target-the-chinese-market/), secondary).

These figures describe outbound travel, not demand in your neighbourhood. They matter most if you run a hotel, restaurant, tour or shop that serves visitors. If your customers are mostly local, the more useful question is who lives near you. The guide to [marketing to Chinese tourists](/blog/marketing-to-chinese-tourists-local-business) covers the visitor side in more detail.

## What Is Your Marketing Timeline?

Count backwards from the festival date you confirmed. The weeks below are relative to that date, so the plan works for any year.

| When | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Week minus 8 | Decide the offer, the budget and who will check the translations |
| Week minus 5 | Update your Google Business Profile and website, open pre-bookings |
| Week minus 4 | Launch emails, social posts and any paid ads |
| Week minus 2 | Send a reminder and push the last available slots |
| Final days before the holiday | Check hours, stock and staffing |
| Weeks 1 to 2 after the holiday | Send a thank-you and a rebook offer |

Treat the table as a starting point. A shop with a short booking window may need to compress the first two steps, while a service business with a long lead time can spread them out.

## How Should You Plan the Budget by Month?

The guide from Savoir Management advises looking at last year's calls and bookings by month, then planning budgets around those patterns instead of spending evenly all year ([Savoir Management](https://www.savoirmanagement.com/google-ads-for-local-businesses), secondary). If your records show a February lift, put more of the spend there. If they show a quiet period, move money away from it.

If you do not have enough history, start small. Adwave suggests that most dog trainers send two to four direct mailings per year tied to seasonal peaks, which gives a sense of how many planned touchpoints a seasonal business might use ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/dog-training-advertising), secondary).

## What Does a Holiday Ad Budget Return?

The example below uses assumed inputs for a salon. Replace them with your own cost per lead and booking rate before you decide anything.

Assumptions: ad budget $500, cost per lead $50, half of leads book, average haircut $80. All four are assumptions, not market data.

| Step | Figure | How it was calculated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad budget | $500 | Assumption |
| Leads | 10 | $500 divided by $50 (assumption) |
| New bookings | 5 | 50% of leads (assumption) |
| First-visit revenue | $400 | 5 times $80 (assumption) |
| Cost per booking | $100 | $500 divided by 5 |
| Visits needed per new customer to cover the cost | 1.25 | $100 divided by $80 |

On the first visit alone, the campaign brings in $400 against $500 of spend, a $100 gap. Each new customer needs to return on average about 1.25 times to cover the ad cost in revenue terms. This ignores product costs and margin, so a salon with thin margins would need more repeat visits. The lesson is that a rebooking offer matters as much as the ad itself.

## Which Promotions Fit the Holiday?

Choose offers that match what people already do around the holiday. Each idea below works best when it gives a reason to book or buy before the holiday rush starts.

Salons and barbers can offer pre-holiday appointment slots, opened early so that people can plan ahead.

Restaurants and cafes can offer a set menu or a group booking option for family meals.

Gift shops and florists can offer bundles that customers can buy as gifts.

Any business can sell a gift voucher that customers can give to family or friends.

Fitness and wellness studios can offer a starter pass for people who want a fresh start in the new year.

## How Do You Keep Customers After the Holiday?

A holiday campaign only pays back if customers return. Adwave describes a good service-business post-purchase sequence as including a follow-up note after the job, a simple education email, a satisfaction check-in, and a later maintenance, renewal, referral or seasonal touchpoint ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/loyalty-email-sequences-keeping-customers-engaged-post-purchase), secondary). Build that sequence before the holiday starts, so it runs automatically afterwards.

For promotions between holidays, the U.S. Small Business Administration suggests asking customers for permission to send a regular email, such as once a month, to promote off-season specials or build anticipation for the next season ([Adwave, citing the SBA](https://adwave.com/resources/seasonal-sales-strategies-promotional-planning-that-works), secondary). Ask for consent at the point of booking so the list is ready when the next promotion comes.

For drafting these messages quickly, the guide to [ChatGPT prompts for local business marketing](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-local-business-marketing) includes starting points you can adapt, though every draft still needs a fluent speaker to check any Chinese text.

## How Do You Update Your Google and Yelp Presence?

Update your opening hours before the holiday so customers can plan around any closures. Check your platform's live help page for how to set special or holiday hours on your Google Business Profile, since menu labels and steps change over time. Adwave's sample Seasonal Business Hours campaign runs from December 20 through January 2, which gives an example of how long a holiday hours notice can run ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/year-end-marketing-push-2025), secondary). Adjust the dates to your own holiday.

If you are active on Yelp, Yelp Connect lets businesses publish updates, completed projects, seasonal promotions, educational content and company announcements directly to followers and prospective customers ([iCatch Marketing](https://www.icatchgroup.com/google-ads-vs-yelp-ads/), secondary). A short seasonal promotion there is a low-cost way to reach existing followers.

## How Do You Avoid Cultural and Language Mistakes?

Ask a fluent speaker to review any Chinese text before it is published, including ads, menus and emails. Machine translation can miss tone and meaning, and Chinese is written in Simplified and Traditional forms, so decide which one your customers read and use it consistently.

Keep claims specific and avoid implying that the holiday is for one group only. A simple, accurate message about your offer does more than decorative symbols. If you are unsure whether a design element carries a meaning you do not intend, ask your reviewer before you use it.

## Which Channels Reach Chinese Customers?

Email and text work well for people who already know your business, since you have their consent. For wider reach, short-video platforms can help, and the comparison in [Douyin vs TikTok for local business](/blog/douyin-tiktok-local-business-chinese-market) explains the differences. For in-person reach, a booth or community event can give people a reason to visit, as covered in [event marketing for local business](/blog/event-marketing-for-local-business).

Pick one or two channels you can run well. A small business with a clear offer and a working reminder schedule will usually outperform one that spreads a small budget across many platforms.

## FAQ

### When is Chinese New Year in 2027?

It is a lunar holiday, so the date changes each year. Confirm the exact festival dates on a current lunar calendar before you set your marketing timeline.

### How early should I start marketing for it?

The plan in this guide starts about 8 weeks ahead, launches promotions about 4 weeks ahead and sends a reminder about 2 weeks ahead. Adjust the weeks to match your booking window.

### Should I advertise in Chinese?

If your customers read Chinese, it can help. Have a fluent speaker review all text, and decide between Simplified and Traditional Chinese based on the readers you want to reach.

### How do I know if the campaign is worth the cost?

Work out your cost per booking and the revenue from one visit, then estimate how many visits a new customer makes. The worked example above shows the method, but replace the assumed inputs with your own numbers.
