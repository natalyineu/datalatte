A UK small business can now buy streaming and TV ads from ITV, Channel 4 and Sky through one self-serve platform, Comcast's Universal Ads, which launched in the UK on 23 June 2026. Its UK site states "£0 minimum spend". Ads on these broadcasters still need Clearcast approval, so plan about two to three weeks before launch. None of the broadcasters publishes a rate card, so any CPM you see quoted is an estimate.

This guide covers where to buy, what Clearcast requires, how to target a local area and how to cost a first test. Every figure comes from a source listed at the end. Where a number is our own illustration it is labelled as an assumption.

## Where Can a UK Small Business Buy CTV Ads?

There are three routes, and they differ in effort and control.

| Route | Who it suits | Minimum spend | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Universal Ads (ITV, Sky, Channel 4) | First-time TV advertisers | £0 stated | Self-serve, card payment, launched 23 June 2026 |
| Channel 4 programmatic (private marketplace) | Advertisers with a DSP or agency | None stated | Channel 4 told VideoWeek it has no minimum spend requirements |
| Sky AdSmart direct | Advertisers wanting postcode or drive-time TV targeting | Not published | Older sources cite £3,000, none from Sky in 2026 |

Universal Ads lets you choose a budget, pick an audience, upload creative and launch across ITV Media, Sky Media and Channel 4 Sales. The coverage describes campaigns going live in minutes, but approval of the ad itself is a separate step (see below). Pricing per thousand impressions is not shown in the launch coverage or on the UK page.

ITV's older self-serve platform, Planet V, launched in October 2020 and is, per a June 2024 report, the only route for selling ads on ITVX. ITV said it had added over 1,100 new advertisers since launch. That report gives no minimum spend.

## Do You Need Clearcast Approval for Streaming Ads?

For the broadcasters, yes. Clearcast's help centre says its broadcasters "require VOD approval for their Video on Demand (VOD) advertising in the UK", and that the ads must follow the CAP code. Ads of up to five minutes on channels Clearcast clears are paid for by its stakeholders, so a standard clearance carries no separate fee for the advertiser.

Timing is the part that catches people out. Clearcast targets script feedback within three working days, says script approval takes about two weeks on average, and final approval of the finished ad usually takes two working days. It advises allowing two to three weeks in total. A paid Fast Track option guarantees feedback within one working day, but Clearcast's help page does not state the fee.

Platforms outside the broadcaster set, such as Amazon Prime Video or Pluto TV, have their own ad policies. No official source states whether they need Clearcast, so check with the platform before you book.

## What Is Sky AdSmart and Can a Small Business Use It?

AdSmart is Sky's addressable TV product, launched in 2014. It places different ads in the same break for different households. Sky Media's SME toolkit, announced on 28 January 2026, lists targeting by postcode and local authority, drive-time targeting for retailers, hospitality, leisure and service businesses, and deterministic audience targeting.

The toolkit also includes Waymark, which generates 10, 15 or 30 second ads from your website, and the Clearcast approval step. Sky's article states no minimum spend. Third-party sources have quoted £3,000 for years, but that figure is not confirmed by any Sky page, so ask Sky Media or use Universal Ads for a current figure.

## How Do You Target a Local Area?

The UK has two useful geographic tools. Sky says AdSmart can target by postcode, local authority and drive time from a shop. Universal Ads describes audience choice as working "just like social", without listing the options, so confirm the geographic controls inside the platform before you commit.

For a single-site business, a drive-time area around the shop is usually a better fit than a whole ITV region. Start with the catchment you already serve, such as the postcodes in your customer list, and widen only if reach is too low.

## How Big Is the Streaming Audience in the UK?

Ofcom's Media Nations 2026 report, as summarised by ISPreview, found that 70% of UK households had at least one subscription video service in Q1 2026. Broadcaster on-demand reached 46% of people weekly in 2025, up from 38% in 2022, while live and recorded TV fell to 54% from 71%. People spent an average of 4 hours 27 minutes a day on video at home, and broadcaster content made up 53% of that.

YouTube is a large part of the TV screen now: 19 minutes a day on a TV set in 2025, up from 9 in 2022. For a local advertiser this means the audience is split across broadcaster apps, subscription services and YouTube, which is a reason to test one platform rather than spread a small budget across four.

## What Does a First Test Cost? A Worked Example

Rates are not published, so this example uses inputs we made up. Every input is an **assumption**, not a quote.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | £1,500 |
| CPM paid by the advertiser | £25 |
| Impressions bought | 60,000 |
| Average views per household in the month | 4 |
| Households reached | about 15,000 |
| Share of reached households within reach of the shop | 100% (the targeting does the filtering) |
| People who act on the ad | 0.1% of 15,000, about 15 |
| Cost per person who acts | about £100 |

Change the CPM and the whole table moves: at £15 the same budget buys 100,000 impressions, at £40 only 37,500. The point of a first test is to find your real CPM and your real response rate, not to hit these numbers. Track visits with a unique phone number or a "saw you on TV" question at booking.

For a business with a repeat customer worth several hundred pounds a year, a cost per new customer in this range can work. For a £15 haircut it usually does not, unless the ad drives repeat bookings. Compare with [Google Ads](/services/google-ads) search, which captures people who are already looking.

## Which Local Businesses Should Try It?

CTV suits businesses where one new regular customer is worth a lot and a local radius is easy to define. Coffee shops with a loyalty scheme, salons with rebooking, vets and groomers with recurring visits and gyms with membership fees fit best. One-off, low-ticket sales are a harder fit.

## What Mistakes Do UK Advertisers Make?

- **Submitting creative late.** Clearcast says allow two to three weeks. Do not plan a launch for the week you submit.
- **Treating one rate as universal.** Prices come from auctions and vary by audience and timing. Ask for a quote before you plan around a number.
- **Ignoring creative format.** A TV ad is horizontal, has sound and cannot be clicked. Put one phone number or short web address on screen. See the [CTV creative specs guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026).
- **Skipping measurement.** Decide in advance how you will count results.

## FAQ

### Is there a minimum spend for CTV advertising in the UK?

Universal Ads states "£0 minimum spend" on its UK page. Channel 4 told VideoWeek in September 2025 that its programmatic private marketplace has no minimum spend requirements. A realistic test still needs enough impressions to learn from. Sky has not published a current minimum for direct AdSmart buys.

### Do I need Clearcast approval for ITVX, Channel 4 and Sky ads?

Clearcast says its broadcasters require VOD approval for their on-demand ads in the UK. Allow two to three weeks. Standard clearance has no separate fee for ads on channels Clearcast clears.

### How much do CTV ads cost per thousand views in the UK?

No UK broadcaster publishes a CPM for small advertisers, and there is no reliable public figure. Treat any quoted range as an estimate. Run a small test and read the CPM from your own campaign report.

### Can I run the same video I use on Facebook?

Only if it works on a TV screen. Use a horizontal 16:9 video with sound and a clear single call to action. On Universal Ads a partner can convert social assets into TV creative, according to its UK page.

### Is CTV better than Google Ads for a local business?

They do different jobs. Search ads reach people already looking for your service. TV reaches people who may not know you yet. If your budget is small, start with search and the free [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist), then test TV.
