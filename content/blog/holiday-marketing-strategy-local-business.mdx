A local holiday plan has four jobs: update your Google Business Profile hours, email the customers who already know you, make a gift offer that is easy to buy, and pick a few dated promotions instead of running something every day. NRF forecasts November and December 2026 retail sales up 4.2% to $1.013 trillion, and says 43% of holiday shoppers plan to start before November. Your window has already opened.

NRF's forecast covers US retail excluding car dealers, petrol stations and restaurants, in nominal dollars. It is a national figure and says nothing about your town or trade. Sources were opened on 2026-11-01.

## What Do the 2026 Forecasts Say?

NRF's 15 October release defines the season as 1 November to 31 December. Last year's holiday sales rose 3.1% to $972 billion. NRF expects retailers to hire 325,000 to 375,000 seasonal workers, in line with about 356,000 last year, and says retailers are cautious because of gas prices, inflation, interest rates and consumer debt.

On the consumer side, NRF's data page says 56% of holiday shoppers say the economy will affect their spending, and the top gifts people want are gift cards (47%), clothing (44%), books and media (25%) and personal care or beauty (24%). Gift cards suit service businesses, as in our [pet-grooming gift certificate guide](/blog/pet-grooming-gift-certificates-holiday-marketing).

## Which Dates Should You Plan Around?

By the calendar, US Thanksgiving falls on Thursday 26 November 2026, Black Friday on Friday 27 November, the Saturday after on 28 November and Cyber Monday on Monday 30 November. These come from the standard rules (fourth Thursday, following Friday, following Monday), not from a vendor.

Add your own dates: last day for gift orders, last booking day before closing, and reopening day. Customers act on deadlines you state plainly.

## What Should You Fix Online First?

Google's help says to set special hours for holidays or other temporary periods; the page does not say how far ahead you can set them, so enter them as soon as you know them. Wrong hours cost visits you would otherwise have had.

Then add one Business Profile post for the offer. Google lets posts carry a start and end date, and posts older than 6 months are archived unless a date range is set. See our guide to profile posts and the [profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## How Do You Use Email Before the Rush?

Email goes to people who already chose to hear from you. The FTC's CAN-SPAM guide requires accurate sender details, a clear way to opt out and honouring opt-outs within 10 business days, with penalties of up to $53,088 per violating email. Plan three messages: an early announcement, a reminder with the deadline, and a last-chance note. Our [holiday email campaign guide](/blog/holiday-email-marketing-campaigns) has structures to copy, and [building a list from zero](/blog/local-business-email-list-from-zero) helps if yours is small.

## Should You Run Paid Ads?

Only after the free steps. LocaliQ's September 2026 Facebook benchmarks put the all-industry lead-campaign average at $27.39 per lead, so a holiday test of 20 leads is roughly $548. That is a benchmark for lead campaigns, not for sales, and it varies by industry. See the [Facebook ads budget guide](/blog/facebook-ads-budget-local-business-how-much-to-spend) to size a test.

## A Worked Example: A Six-Week Plan

All inputs are **assumptions** for a salon.

| Week | Task |
| --- | --- |
| 1 (from 2 Nov) | Set special hours; publish gift-card page and profile post |
| 2 | Email 1: gift cards on sale; ask staff to mention them at checkout |
| 3 | Email 2: deadline for gifts in time for Christmas |
| 4 (Thanksgiving week) | One dated offer for Friday and Saturday only |
| 5 | Email 3: last-chance bookings |
| 6 | Reopening note and review request |

Track one number per task: gift cards sold, bookings from each email, and offer redemptions. If an email brings no bookings, change the offer before sending another.

## FAQ

### When should holiday marketing start?

NRF says 43% of holiday shoppers plan to start before November, so as soon as you have a clear offer.

### Are the NRF figures for my town?

No. They are national, nominal and exclude restaurants, so use them as context only.

### Do I need ads for the holidays?

Not necessarily. Hours, a profile post and email to existing customers cost little.

### What is the easiest offer for a service business?

NRF finds gift cards are the most wanted gift at 47%, so a gift card or voucher is a natural fit.

### Do I need to change my email footer?

Keep your postal address and unsubscribe link, as the FTC guide requires.
