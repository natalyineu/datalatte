## How Big Is the Nottingham Market?

The ONS Census 2021 puts Nottingham at about 323,700 people, up 5.9% from about 305,700 in 2011. That is slower than England (6.6%) and the East Midlands (7.7%). Nottingham ranked 33rd of 309 English local authority areas by population. These are rounded first estimates from the ONS.

No Nottingham business count was verified for this guide. UK Business Counts has one, but the dataset was not opened. What matters locally is that customers can compare many nearby options on a phone in seconds.

## Which Marketing Channels Should You Use First?

Start with free local visibility, then add paid channels once you can measure results. The order below is a recommendation, not a ranked study.

| Step | Channel | Cost | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Google Business Profile | Free | Shows you on Search and Maps |
| 2 | Reviews and photos | Free | Helps customers choose between nearby options |
| 3 | Google Ads, local Search | Ad budget | Reaches people searching now |
| 4 | Meta Ads, small radius | Ad budget | Reaches people who are not searching yet |
| 5 | Email list | Low | Repeat sales, with consent |

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. More reviews and positive ratings can help prominence.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local?

Google allows radius targeting from 1 km upward and recommends "Presence or interest" for most Search campaigns. It says "Presence" fits when you only want people physically in the area.

A radius around the shop is simpler to manage than a postcode list, but check that it does not reach areas you do not serve. Add negative keywords for jobs, free and categories you do not offer.

## Can You Offer a Discount for a Google Review?

No. Google's contributed content policy lists offering "payment, discounts, free goods and/or services" in exchange for reviews as fake engagement. You may ask for a review with a direct link right after the appointment. See our [guide to getting more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for scripts.

## Do Meta Ads Work in Nottingham?

They can, for visual businesses and clear offers. DataReportal's Digital 2026 UK report gives Facebook ad reach of 38.8 million people (55.6% of the UK population) and Instagram ad reach of 35.5 million (50.9%) in late 2025. It reports no Nottingham-specific costs.

Use Meta for people who are not searching yet. Run one offer to a small radius and compare cost per booking with Google. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) covers the split.

## What Are the UK Email Marketing Rules?

The ICO says that, in general, you need consent before sending unsolicited email marketing to individual subscribers. A narrower "soft opt-in" can apply to your own similar products and services, and it does not apply to bought-in lists. These points are from a search summary of the ICO guidance (the ICO page timed out when opened), so read the guidance before you send.

Give every message an easy opt-out and act on it.

## FAQ

### How many people live in Nottingham?

About 323,700 at the 2021 Census, according to the ONS.

### How much should a Nottingham business spend on Google Ads?

No verified local figure exists. Pick a budget you can hold for two months, estimate click cost in Keyword Planner, and work back from the value of a customer.

### Do Meta ads work in Nottingham?

They can for visual businesses and local offers. DataReportal reports UK-wide ad reach, not Nottingham costs, so test a small daily budget.

### Is Google Business Profile free?

Creating and managing a profile costs nothing. You need to claim and verify the business to appear on Search and Maps.

### Can I email customers without consent?

Generally not for individuals. The ICO describes consent as the default and a limited soft opt-in as the exception. Check the guidance before you send.
