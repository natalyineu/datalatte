A Nigerian small business should build marketing around the channels its customers already use on a phone: WhatsApp for conversations, Facebook and TikTok for reach, and a Google Business Profile for people searching nearby. Internet use is still under half the population, so offline word of mouth stays part of the plan. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 109 million internet users, 45.5% of 239 million people.

This guide uses published platform data and avoids invented ad prices. No source checked publishes average Nigerian click or CPM costs, so test your own.

## How Big Is the Digital Audience in Nigeria?

DataReportal reports a median age of 18.1 and 165 million cellular connections, more than the number of internet users. Meta's ad tools show a potential Facebook ad reach of 38.0 million and an Instagram ad reach of 10.0 million. TikTok's tools show 47.8 million users aged 18 and over.

These are ad-tool estimates, not active user counts, and one person can hold several accounts. Use them to compare channels, not to promise sales.

## Which Channel Should a Nigerian Small Business Start With?

Choose by what the customer does next. A customer who wants a price or a booking will message you, a customer who browses entertainment will watch a video, and a customer who is nearby will search a map.

| Goal | Channel | What the sources confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Talk to customers | WhatsApp | 24-hour reply window, template rules, per-message pricing |
| Reach many people | Facebook and Instagram | Ad reach 38.0M and 10.0M |
| Reach younger adults | TikTok | Ad reach 47.8M aged 18+ |
| Be found nearby | Google Business Profile | Ranks on relevance, distance, prominence |

Google says it ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence, and that no one can pay for a better local ranking. Complete the profile first, then add paid channels. See our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) for splitting spend.

## How Does WhatsApp Pricing Work for Businesses?

Meta moved to per-message pricing on July 1, 2025, and charges for delivered template messages by category: marketing, utility and authentication. When a customer messages you, a 24-hour customer service window opens and each new message resets it. Outside that window you can only send approved templates.

Meta's pricing page says that from October 1, 2026 each business phone number gets 1,000 free service messages a month, and utility messages sent inside an open window become chargeable. Nigeria has its own line on the rate card, but the per-message amount is not printed on the web page, so read the current rate card before budgeting. Our [WhatsApp marketing guide](/blog/whatsapp-business-marketing-local-business) covers the app basics.

## Can You Message Nigerian Customers Without Permission?

No. WhatsApp's business policy says you may contact people only if you have opt-in permission, and you must honor opt-outs. Nigeria's data rules point the same way. A LawPavilion guide says the General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID), issued on March 20, 2025 under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, lists direct marketing among the cases where consent is required, and stresses easy withdrawal. That is a secondary source, so check the Act and the directive.

Practical steps: ask for consent on the order form or in the first chat, record when it was given, and add a simple "reply STOP" instruction. The [Brazil guide](/blog/local-marketing-brazil-small-business-whatsapp-meta) shows a similar WhatsApp-first market.

## FAQ

### Is Facebook or TikTok better for Nigerian small businesses?

Neither is better in general. TikTok reports 47.8 million adult users in its ad tools, Facebook 38.0 million. Test small budgets on both and compare cost per message or sale.

### Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

Not to start. The free WhatsApp Business app handles small volumes. The API suits automation and larger lists, and uses per-message pricing.

### Is internet access a limit?

Yes. Internet penetration is 45.5%, so combine online channels with referrals and signs in the shop.

### Do Nigerian data rules apply to small businesses?

The commentary read for this article does not describe a small-business exemption. Get consent before marketing and keep records.

### What do ads cost in Nigeria?

No average is published in the sources checked. Set a small test budget in Ads Manager and read the result.
