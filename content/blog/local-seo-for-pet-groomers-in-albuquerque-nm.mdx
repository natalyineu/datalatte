## What does the Google Maps local pack reward for a pet groomer?

The local pack is driven mainly by the GBP listing. [Adwave's guide to local pack listings](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-my-lawn-care-business) states that the top three businesses in the map pack are pulled directly from their GBP listings (secondary). The same vendor says that in-depth analysis of millions of profiles shows the primary category is the #1 ranking factor for the Local Pack, though it does not name that analysis (secondary).

A 2025 benchmark cited by [Adwave's multi-location audit](https://adwave.com/resources/multi-location-local-seo-audit) reports GBP signals at 32% of local pack ranking weight (secondary). Treat these figures as directional, since the original sources do not name their data.

Google's AI answering, referred to as Ask Maps, draws on business profile data, website content, and reviews, according to [Accrisoft's transition summary](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-q-and-a) (secondary). The website and reviews therefore feed the same picture the GBP shows.

## Which Google Business Profile fields should a pet groomer fill in first?

Start with the fields that the sources tie to ranking and clicks. Work through them in this order:

1. Primary category. Search your core service on Google Maps and check the top 3 to 5 businesses to see which category they use, as [Adwave recommends](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones) (secondary). Choose the category that matches the service you sell most.
2. Description. The profile allows 750 characters, per [Adwave's GBP to-do guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack) (secondary). Name your service and neighbourhood in the first sentence.
3. Photos. [Adwave recommends at least 15 high-quality photos](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers) and says a robust gallery gets far more clicks and calls (secondary). The same vendor's beginner guide sets a floor of 10.
4. Hours, phone, and address. Make them 100% accurate, as the same Adwave page advises.
5. Q&A. Google launched Q&A in Maps in 2017, so seed your profile with the questions customers ask most.

The full field-by-field walkthrough is in the [pet groomer GBP optimisation checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-for-pet-groomers).

## How many reviews does a pet groomer need to compete?

The most-cited review benchmark is from a third-party analysis of 2 million GBP pages. It found that businesses with over 200 Google reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack, as [reported by Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business) (secondary). Read it as a correlation, not a guarantee that 200 reviews will place a groomer in the top three.

Another vendor page claims that businesses with over 50 reviews and a 4.7+ average rating dominate top map pack spots, but it cites no source, so treat that claim as unverified ([Accountability Now](https://accountabilitynow.net/ai-google-business-profile-ranking-for-landscapers/), secondary).

Google states that reviews show next to a business's profile in Maps and Search ([Google Business Profile help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)). Customers who describe the service they booked in their own words make that text visible to searchers. The [pet groomer GBP guide](/blog/google-business-profile-for-pet-groomers) covers how to ask for reviews without breaking platform rules.

## Does NAP consistency across directories matter?

Yes. Adwave says the name, address, and phone number (NAP) must be exactly the same on the website, the GBP, and major directories such as Yelp, Apple Maps, and Bing Places ([Adwave voice search guide](https://adwave.com/resources/voice-search-optimization-for-local-businesses), secondary). The same vendor's beginner guide says the match must hold down to the comma and whether the listing uses "St." or "Street" (secondary).

One landscaping firm reported an 18% jump in local pack appearances after it cleaned up its NAP data. That is a single anecdote from a vendor article, not a controlled test ([Accountability Now](https://accountabilitynow.net/ai-google-business-profile-ranking-for-landscapers/), secondary). Fix mismatches in existing listings before adding new ones.

Apple Maps also matters for this audit. Apple Business Connect reports Profile Views, which counts how many people saw the business card in Apple Maps ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/apple-business-connect-the-google-business-profile-alternative-youre-ignoring), secondary). Claim the Apple listing with the same NAP as your GBP.

## How often should a pet groomer post and refresh its profile?

Google reviews each Business Profile post. A post appears publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live, and Pending or Not approved posts do not show ([Google Business Profile help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)). Check the status after you publish.

Adwave advises at least one new Google Post per week to keep a profile fresh, and describes posts as free mini-billboards on the profile (secondary). Use them for a seasonal offer, a new service such as mobile grooming, or a local event.

Google also states that setting up a Business Profile is free ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)). The cost is time, not fees.

## Should a mobile or home-based groomer hide its address?

Yes, if you qualify. Adwave says a business that works from home or serves customers at their location can hide its street address in the GBP and is then designated a Service Area Business (SAB) ([Adwave beginner guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary). Set a service area that covers the neighbourhoods you actually drive to.

The same vendor says you will often start ranking for your own business name a few weeks after verification (secondary). That is an estimate, not a guarantee, so track your position weekly rather than waiting on a fixed date.

## Where do paid ads fit alongside local SEO?

Ads and the GBP share data. Google states that location asset data in Google Ads is sourced directly from Google Maps and Google Business Profile, and that successful corrections made there flow into linked Google Ads accounts ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)). Fix the profile first, and the ads inherit the fix.

A Marketing 360 case study describes a mobile pet groomer that advertised on Facebook and Google to choose its channels (secondary). The page does not report results you can use as a benchmark. For a side-by-side view, read the [Google Ads vs Facebook Ads comparison for local businesses](/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-local-business-which-wins).

## How do you track whether local SEO is working?

Adwave lists six local search metrics to track: GBP impressions, map-pack views, clicks to call, direction requests, website visits from GBP, and branded search lift over time (secondary). Record them monthly so you can compare the same period year on year.

Check your own position for your top five keywords by searching in a private window. GBP messages can also be managed from a desktop dashboard, not only the mobile app ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-messaging-should-you-turn-it-on), secondary). Tools for automating the check are covered in the [AI and GBP optimisation guide](/blog/ai-for-google-business-profile-optimization).

## FAQ

### Does a pet groomer need a website to rank in the map pack?

The sources do not say a website is required. They do say that Ask Maps draws on website content as well as profile data and reviews (secondary). A clear service and location page gives that system more material to read.

### Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Google describes its Business Profile as free to set up on Search and Maps ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)).

### How long until a new listing starts ranking?

A vendor estimate says a few weeks after verification for your own business name (secondary). Rankings for broader searches take longer, and no source gives a reliable timeline.

### Can a home-based groomer keep its address private?

Yes, the GBP lets you hide the street address and then designates the business as a Service Area Business (secondary, Adwave).

### Do GBP posts show straight away?

Not necessarily. A post shows publicly only when its status is Live, so check that status after you publish ([Google Business Profile help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)).
