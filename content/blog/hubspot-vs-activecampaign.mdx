## How Much Does HubSpot Cost for a Small Business?

HubSpot's free plan covers up to 2 users with no credit card needed and includes 2,000 email sends a month, with HubSpot branding on the emails ([secondary source](https://www.femaleswitch.com/tpost/hubspot-vs-activecampaign)). Starter is listed at $7 per seat a month on annual billing as a limited-time offer for new customers, with $20 shown as the regular price.

Professional starts at $800 a month on annual billing and includes 3 seats, with a one-time onboarding fee of $3,000. Extra core seats on Professional cost $45 a month each, according to HubSpot's pricing page. Enterprise starts at $3,600 a month for 5 seats, with a $7,000 onboarding fee.

For a single-location shop, the decision point is the jump from free to Professional, not the Starter price.

## Is ActiveCampaign Cheaper Than HubSpot?

The sources used for this comparison do not give verified ActiveCampaign plan prices, so this article will not estimate one. Check the live pricing page and calculate the total at your contact count before choosing.

Email tool costs can rise as a list grows. One 2026 pricing guide says email marketing that starts at $20 a month can reach $500 to $2,000 a month as a subscriber list grows ([secondary source](https://www.getpricepulse.com/blog/email-marketing-tools-pricing-guide-2026.html)). The same guide lists Mailchimp's Essentials plan at $13 a month and Standard at $20 a month for 1K contacts, which gives a reference point for an email-only budget.

ActiveCampaign offers free 1:1 migration on its Pro plan and above, according to a comparison table ([secondary source](https://ecommerce-platforms.com/articles/hubspot-marketing-hub-vs-activecampaign)). That matters if you are moving from another platform, because the move itself has a cost in time.

## Which Has the Better CRM for a Local Business?

HubSpot is built as a CRM first. The article comparison lists its CRM as free, with hubs tiered by users and features ([secondary source](https://ecommerce-platforms.com/articles/hubspot-marketing-hub-vs-activecampaign)). A salon, consultant or studio can keep contacts in one place at no cost and add paid hubs only when a specific feature is needed.

Integrations are a separate question. The same comparison lists ActiveCampaign at 1,000 apps, with good email integrations and heavier reliance on Zapier. It lists HubSpot Marketing Hub at 2,000 apps, with deep two-way data sync to tools such as Shopify, Slack and Stripe. If your booking or payment tools are central to the business, check which ones connect natively before you pick.

## Which Is Better for Email and Messaging Automation?

ActiveCampaign can send SMS or WhatsApp messages directly from its automation builder, according to the same comparison table. That matters for local shops where reminders and follow-ups can do more work than a monthly newsletter. Its integration count is lower than HubSpot's, so the messaging link is its clearest advantage in this comparison.

Automation suits repetitive work. Encompass describes email marketing automation as sending targeted emails to leads and customers automatically ([secondary source](https://360encompass.com/automated-marketing-tools/)). RingCentral lists email follow-up sequences, lead scoring, SMS workflows and campaign reporting among rule-based tasks that can be automated ([secondary source](https://www.ringcentral.com/us/en/blog/10-marketing-automation-tools-every-smb-needs/)).

A secondary source citing a HubSpot Blog study says around 56% of businesses currently use marketing automation tools ([secondary source](https://thebuild.builderall.com/ultimate-list-of-marketing-automation-platforms-for-2023/)). The inputs below are assumptions: 2 users (an owner and one assistant), 1,000 contacts, one monthly newsletter, and two welcome emails for about 40 new contacts each month. That is roughly 1,080 sends a month (assumption), or about 54% of HubSpot's free 2,000-send limit.

## What Hidden Costs Should You Check?

HubSpot's extra costs come from seats, onboarding and tier jumps. On Professional, a fourth user costs $45 a month because 3 seats are included. The one-time $3,000 onboarding fee applies to Professional, and the $7,000 fee applies to Enterprise.

Vendor pricing also changes over time. Mailchimp reportedly raised prices significantly in 2022 and cut its free plan from 2,000 contacts to 500. Its 2024 increase raised paid plan prices by 30 to 45% ([secondary source](https://www.getpricepulse.com/blog/email-marketing-tools-pricing-guide-2026.html)). Build that risk into your budget by checking the live price each year.

Clean the list before you pick a tier so you only pay for contacts you will email. Neighbourhood reach also sits outside the email budget, as covered in [Nextdoor marketing for small business](/blog/hyperlocal-marketing-nextdoor-small-business).

## FAQ

### Is HubSpot or ActiveCampaign cheaper for a small business?

HubSpot's free plan is the lowest-cost start, with 2 users and 2,000 sends a month. Its Starter price is $7 per seat on annual billing for new customers, against a $20 regular price. ActiveCampaign's current prices were not confirmed here, so compare totals at your contact count.

### Does ActiveCampaign have a free plan?

The sources used for this article do not confirm a free ActiveCampaign plan. Check its live pricing page before you plan a budget around it.

### Is HubSpot worth it for a business with one location?

The free CRM is a practical fit for many single-location businesses. Professional at $800 a month plus a $3,000 onboarding fee is a bigger commitment, so it usually makes sense only when a specific feature or team size requires it.

### Can I switch between them later?

Yes, but plan the move. ActiveCampaign offers free 1:1 migration on Pro and above, according to a comparison table. Confirm the migration terms on the live pricing page before you start.
