For a small local business, HubSpot is the better starting point if you need a free CRM and will add paid tools later. ActiveCampaign is the better fit if email automation is the main job. HubSpot's free CRM allows up to 1,000 contacts with no time limit. ActiveCampaign has no free plan but offers a 14-day trial with no card, and its plans are priced by contact count.

This comparison uses the vendors' own pages where they could be read. ActiveCampaign's pricing page did not display prices when opened for this article, so its dollar figures come from a secondary source and are marked.

## How Much Do HubSpot and ActiveCampaign Cost?

HubSpot's CRM page lists Free at $0 for up to 2 users. Starter is "normally priced at $20/month per seat," with new customers offered $7 per seat per month on annual upfront payment or $10 month to month. Marketing Hub Professional is listed at $800 a month (one other figure of $890 appears on the same page) with a $3,000 onboarding fee, which puts it out of reach for most single-location shops.

ActiveCampaign lists four plans, each "billed yearly for 1,000 contacts," with 1 to 5 user seats. A secondary source (Email Vendor Selection, August 2026) reports $15, $49, $79 and $145 per month for Starter, Plus, Pro and Enterprise on annual billing. Treat those as unconfirmed until you see the number in your own checkout.

| | HubSpot | ActiveCampaign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free option | Free CRM, 2 users, 1,000 contacts | 14-day trial, no card |
| Entry paid plan | Starter, $20 per seat normal, $7 to $10 promo for new customers | Starter, about $15 on annual billing (secondary) |
| Pricing basis | Per seat, plus contact tiers on Marketing Hub | By contact count |
| Mid-tier | Marketing Hub Professional, $800 plus $3,000 onboarding | Plus, about $49 (secondary) |
| Email sends included (1,000 contacts) | Not listed on the pages read | 10,000 on Starter and Plus |

## Which One Has the Better CRM?

HubSpot is a CRM first. The free tier connects to Gmail and Outlook, and Starter adds email and chat support, automation, meeting scheduling, payments and e-signatures. That suits a salon, studio or service firm that wants one place for contacts, notes and follow-up.

ActiveCampaign includes a marketing CRM on every plan, according to the secondary source. Sales pipelines are sold as an add-on for $68 a month on Plus and above, per the same source. If you need deal tracking, add that cost to the plan price before comparing.

## Which One Is Better for Email Automation?

ActiveCampaign is built around automations. Its plans list email send limits (10,000 a month on Starter and Plus, 12,000 on Pro, 15,000 on Enterprise) and 1 to 5 seats. For welcome series, abandoned-booking reminders or win-back emails, that structure is simple to price.

HubSpot's strongest automation sits in the paid Marketing Hub tiers. Starter lists automation, but the page does not say how far it goes. If your plan is a few simple sequences, test both on real flows before paying. ## Worked Example: A Studio With 1,000 Contacts

Inputs are **assumptions**: one user, 1,000 contacts, one welcome sequence and a monthly newsletter, billed annually.

| Option | Monthly cost | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HubSpot Free | $0 | CRM, 2 users, limited tools |
| HubSpot Starter, promo | $7 per seat | CRM plus automation, new customers only |
| HubSpot Starter, normal | $20 per seat | Same, after the promotion |
| ActiveCampaign Starter (secondary price) | about $15 | Email automation, marketing CRM |

On those inputs the difference is a few dollars a month, so price should not decide it. Decide by which tool you will open every week. After 3,000 or 5,000 contacts the contact-based pricing and the add-ons change the math, so rerun the comparison then.

## What Are the Hidden Costs?

For HubSpot, watch the jump from Starter to Professional and the onboarding fee. For ActiveCampaign, watch price growth with contacts, because plans are tiered by list size, and the pipeline add-on. Both vendors discount annual billing, and promotional prices end.

Whichever you choose, clean your list first. Paying for contacts who never open an email is the most common waste. ## FAQ

### Is HubSpot or ActiveCampaign cheaper for a small business?

HubSpot is cheaper at the start, because the CRM is free and Starter is $7 to $20 per seat. ActiveCampaign's entry plan is also low (secondary source: about $15). Costs diverge as the contact list grows.

### Does ActiveCampaign have a free plan?

Not on the pricing page read here. It offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card.

### Is HubSpot good for a business with one location?

The free CRM is a reasonable fit. The premium Marketing Hub tiers, $800 a month plus onboarding, usually are not.

### Can I switch later?

Yes, but contacts, tags and automations must be rebuilt or exported. Test your main flow in both before committing.

### Which should an accountant or consultant choose?

Pick the tool that matches the work. If you track client relationships, start with a CRM. Our [email marketing for accountants](/blog/email-marketing-for-accountants) guide covers newsletters.
