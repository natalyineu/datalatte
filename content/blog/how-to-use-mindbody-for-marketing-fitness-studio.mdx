Mindbody's marketing tools are email and text campaigns, automated welcome and win-back messages, lead capture and analytics. According to Mindbody's pricing page, the campaign and automation features are listed only on the Ultimate plan. Starter and Accelerate include booking confirmations and reminders. Plans start at $79 per month per location.

This guide is for owners of yoga, pilates and boutique fitness studios deciding what to use inside Mindbody and what to run elsewhere. We opened Mindbody's pages on 2026-10-25. Prices for each plan are not public, and Mindbody's own claims are marked as such. Example inputs are labelled as assumptions.

## What Marketing Features Does Mindbody Include?

The pricing page splits features by plan. Email and text campaigns, automated campaigns with templates, and the lead management dashboard are listed under Ultimate only. Advanced reporting is listed under Accelerate and Ultimate. Starter includes unlimited booking confirmations and reminders by email. Accelerate and above add unlimited text reminders.

| Feature | Starter | Accelerate | Ultimate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Email and text marketing campaigns | No | No | Yes |
| Automated campaigns and templates | No | No | Yes |
| Lead management dashboard | No | No | Yes |
| Advanced reporting and analytics | No | Yes | Yes |
| Booking reminders | Email | Email and text | Email and text |

Mindbody's marketing page adds that not all features are available in all regions or on all tiers. Check your own account before planning around a feature.

## How Do Automated Campaigns Work?

Mindbody's marketing page says automated email and text campaigns react to client behavior, with examples such as welcome emails, re-engagement follow-ups and promotions. Its FAQ names the Journeys feature for creating them and mentions a drag-and-drop editor, A/B testing and pre-built templates.

The page also says win-back messages can go out when clients miss appointments or have not visited in a while. Mindbody's playbook for salons, spas and wellness describes a 45 Day Reengagement automation that emails clients who have not visited in 45 days. No send-time or frequency settings are published, so check them in your account.

## How Do You Set Up a Welcome and Win-Back Flow?

Start with two messages, because they match behaviors Mindbody describes.

- **Welcome message:** sent after a first purchase. Say what to bring, where to park and how to book the second class.
- **Win-back message:** sent to clients who have lapsed. Mindbody's playbook uses 45 days and pairs the email with an offer such as a class pack.

Keep each message to one action. Which timing or offer works best for studios is not published, so change one thing at a time and compare. Our [fitness studio email guide](/blog/email-marketing-fitness-studio) has message ideas, and the [retention strategy](/blog/fitness-studio-member-retention-strategy) explains why the second visit matters.

## What Does the Mindbody App Cost?

Listing in the Mindbody app is free, according to the pricing page. A 20% fee, capped at $30 USD, applies only to a new client's first purchase when the app brings in someone you would not otherwise have reached. Existing clients, tracked referral links, and clients who say they found you through the app carry no fee. Later bookings are commission-free.

Mindbody states that more than 3 million active fitness and wellness consumers use its app. That is Mindbody's own claim and it says results vary. Whether the app sends you enough clients is shown by your own sign-up source data.

## Does Mindbody Run Retargeting Ads?

The pricing and marketing pages name no retargeting ad tool. The earlier version of this article claimed one, and that claim is removed. If you want ads for past visitors or lapsed members, you run them in Meta or Google Ads, with an audience you build yourself.

Google says Customer Match lets you use your own data to reach customers across Search, Shopping, Gmail, YouTube and Display, and that memberships expire after 540 days. Meta help pages returned no readable text when opened, so nothing is cited about its audiences. ## How Do You Measure Campaigns?

Mindbody says its marketing analytics track opens, clicks, opt-outs and revenue impact. Its FAQ says conversion tracking is coming "soon", so do not assume you can see which email led to a booking.

A simple check is a code or offer name used only in the email. Count how many people use it and compare that with the number emailed.

## FAQ

### Does Mindbody have built-in marketing automation?

Mindbody's pages list automated email and text campaigns, but the pricing page shows them under Ultimate only. Features vary by region and tier.

### Does Mindbody charge for new clients from its app?

Yes, in one case. A 20% fee, capped at $30, applies on a new client's first purchase when the app brings them in. Other bookings carry no fee.

### Can Mindbody run ads for me?

The pages we opened list no ad tool. Run retargeting in Meta or Google Ads. A comparison with another system is in [Mindbody vs Glofox](/blog/mindbody-vs-glofox-local-business).

### Do I need Mailchimp?

Not necessarily. A Mindbody-to-Mailchimp connection exists through the third-party service tray.ai, which is secondary information and not a native feature. Ultimate lists email campaigns inside Mindbody.

### What results should I expect?

No studio benchmark is published. Mindbody says quoted statistics are not guaranteed and results vary.
