**Can a small business advertise on Amazon Prime Video?** Yes, but it is not an easy fit for a single-location local business. Prime Video ads are sold mainly through Amazon's ad platforms and programmatic partners, often with higher minimums and CPMs than other streaming options, and geographic targeting that is usually broader than a neighborhood. It is most worthwhile for product brands, especially Amazon sellers, and less so for a salon or café with a small local budget.

Amazon added ads to Prime Video in early 2024, and the standard Prime Video plan now includes ad breaks unless members pay extra to remove them. That gives advertisers a very large streaming audience and Amazon's shopping data for targeting. Minimums, costs and targeting options change, so confirm current terms with Amazon Ads or your DSP before planning a budget.

## What Prime Video advertising is

Prime Video ads run as video spots within Prime Video content, in a format similar to commercial breaks on traditional TV. What sets it apart from Hulu, Peacock or Roku is the **targeting data**. Depending on the buying route, Amazon may let you target by:

- **Shopping behavior:** people who have bought or browsed products in certain categories
- **Viewing behavior:** audiences based on genres and shows
- **Audience segments:** such as life stage or household characteristics
- **Location:** typically at the market, state or ZIP level, with options varying by route

For consumer brands in categories that Amazon sells, this can be a genuine advantage over demographic-only targeting.

## Who can actually access Prime Video ads

Access depends on how you buy, and requirements change:

| Route | What to know |
|-------|--------------|
| Amazon DSP (managed) | Historically aimed at larger advertisers with high minimums; check current requirements |
| Amazon Ads self-serve | Amazon has been widening access to streaming TV inventory, but minimums and eligibility vary; confirm in the console |
| Third-party DSPs | Some, such as The Trade Desk or DV360, carry Prime Video inventory; minimums depend on the partner |
| Agency or managed service | Can lower the barrier, but you pay for management |

The practical takeaway: Prime Video is not typically designed for a single-location business with a few hundred dollars a month. Plan on a meaningful test budget, and ask each seller about minimums in writing.

## What do Prime Video ads cost?

Prime Video CPMs are generally on the higher end of streaming. You are paying for the quality of the data and the audience, not just the reach. Exact CPMs vary by targeting, season and buying route, so request current rates rather than relying on a published figure.

| Platform | Relative cost and access (general guidance) |
|----------|---------------------------------------------|
| Prime Video | Premium pricing, higher minimums, strong shopping-data targeting |
| Hulu | Premium, with self-serve options and good demographic targeting |
| Peacock | Premium, especially around live sports |
| Roku | Generally more accessible for small advertisers |
| Free ad-supported (FAST) services | Often lower cost, with less targeting depth |

At a higher CPM, a given budget buys fewer impressions, so a small budget can run out quickly. If your goal is local awareness, there are usually more efficient channels.

## When Prime Video ads can be worth it for a smaller business

Despite the higher barriers, there are scenarios where it makes sense:

**You are an Amazon seller with strong reviews.** Combining Prime Video awareness with Amazon Sponsored Products can form a tight loop: someone sees your video, searches Amazon and finds your listing.

**You sell a regional or national product.** A direct-to-consumer brand has a very different return calculation from a single café.

**Your category is one Amazon carries.** Shopping-based targeting is strongest in categories with lots of Amazon purchase activity, such as pet products, supplements, fitness equipment and beauty.

**You want to test CTV broadly.** Running a small parallel test next to Roku or Hulu can show how shopping-based targeting compares to demographic targeting.

<Callout type="tip">
If you are an Amazon seller, Prime Video advertising can be worth testing even at higher CPMs, because Amazon can connect ad exposure to purchases on its own store more directly than most streaming platforms can. Confirm what reporting is available for your campaign type.
</Callout>

## Prime Video vs Hulu vs Roku for a small business

If you want a streaming TV presence on a limited budget, start with a platform designed for self-serve advertisers. Roku and Hulu are common starting points for small advertisers because of their lower barriers and flexible targeting. Consider adding Prime Video when:

- Your monthly streaming budget is large enough to buy meaningful reach at premium CPMs
- You sell on Amazon and want closer purchase attribution
- You specifically want to reach your category's Amazon shoppers on their TV

## FAQ

**Q: Can I target only people within a few miles of my shop on Prime Video?**
A: Usually not that tightly. Targeting is often at the market or broader ZIP level, so a single-location business will likely reach people who live too far away. If you test it, use a strong local offer and a promo code so you can see how many redemptions came from your real service area.

**Q: What is the minimum spend for Prime Video ads?**
A: It depends on the route (Amazon DSP, Amazon Ads self-serve, a third-party DSP or an agency) and it changes over time. Ask for current minimums in writing before you plan a test.

**Q: I run a hair salon with a modest monthly marketing budget. Should I use Prime Video?**
A: Probably not as a first channel. A budget that size usually works harder on [Google Ads](/services/google-ads) for "hair salon near me" searches, a strong [Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile) and your booking platform's own tools. Add streaming TV later, starting with something more accessible.

**Q: How do I measure whether a Prime Video ad brought people in?**
A: Use a unique promo code, a dedicated URL that redirects to your booking page, or a call tracking number. Run the test for at least a couple of months before drawing conclusions.

**Q: Is Prime Video a better choice than Facebook ads?**
A: They offer different signals. Facebook targets interests and behavior on its platforms; Amazon can target based on shopping activity in its ecosystem. For product brands, Amazon's data can be stronger. For service businesses like plumbers or salons, Meta and Google are often more practical; see our [Meta Ads service](/services/meta-ads).
