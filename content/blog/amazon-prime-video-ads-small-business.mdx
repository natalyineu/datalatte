Ten thousand dollars. That is the campaign size Amazon itself recommends for self-service streaming TV ads, according to the FAQ on its [streaming TV ads page](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/streaming-tv-ads). The same page lists a $50K campaign minimum for managed-service packages, and says there is no minimum spend requirement for the placements themselves. So can a small business advertise on Prime Video? Technically yes. Whether it should is a different question, and for most single-location shops the answer is not yet.

This guide is for owners in the US, UK, Australia and Canada who have heard "you can now run TV ads on Prime Video" and want to know what that means for a café, salon, gym or home service company. It covers what Amazon actually says, the three ways in, the one genuinely local feature, and a break-even table you can fill in with your own numbers. Dollar figures are US; if you are in the UK, Canada or Australia, note that Amazon says supply availability differs by locale.

## What Amazon Says (and What It Does Not)

Amazon's own page gives a handful of numbers worth knowing:

Two details matter. First, the 200M+ figure covers Prime Video, Twitch, Fire TV Channels, Amazon Publisher Direct and third-party exchanges combined, so it overstates what a Prime Video-only buy reaches. Second, the page does not mention local or geo targeting at all.

On Prime Video alone, the numbers are older and second-hand. PPC Land, reporting Amazon's January 2024 launch, said ads would reach an estimated 115 million monthly US viewers, and that Amazon would keep ads out of Kids and Family programming. MediaPost reported in November 2025 that Amazon puts Prime Video's global ad-supported audience at 315 million monthly viewers, up 58% in about a year and a half. Neither is a local number, and none of these figures tells you how many people in your zip code are watching.

One more detail from the viewer side. The ad-free upgrade was reported by Adwave (an ad vendor, so a second-hand source) at $2.99 a month, which means some of the audience has paid to avoid your ad. Adwave does not say where its 115M+ monthly viewer figure comes from.

## Three Ways In, and What Each Costs You

| Route | What is confirmed | What to ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon DSP, managed service | Amazon lists a $50K campaign minimum | Is that per campaign or per month in my country? |
| Amazon DSP, self-service | Amazon says no minimum spend requirement on placements and recommends $10K | What do I pay in platform fees on top of media? |
| Third-party DSP or agency | Amazon's [DSP page](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp) lists Prime Video and Thursday Night Football as inventory DSP can buy | Which Prime Video inventory can you actually reach, and at what minimum? |

The DSP page says Amazon DSP has offered both self-service and managed-service options, and that advertisers do not need to sell on Amazon. It lists no pricing. So the honest summary is: the door is open, the floor is soft, and the recommended budget is still $10K. ## The Only Truly Local Feature: ZIP-Code Versions of One Ad

On October 31, 2025, Amazon announced [location-based interactive video ads on Prime Video](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/whats-new/location-based-interactive-video-ads-on-prime-video). One commercial can become "thousands of variants based on zip codes or tens of variations based on states," swapping in local details such as pricing, nearest location or contact, plus a local click-through URL.

Read the fine print before getting excited:

- It is US only (North America, per Amazon).
- It works with first-party ad server (1PAS) video creatives, built through the Amazon DSP Video template.
- It is open to managed-service and self-service advertisers, except those who ticked the "only use Amazon audiences without demographic signals" restriction.

This is built for brands with many dealers or branches, such as a regional chain or franchise system. For one salon it adds little. For a six-location dental group or a franchise owner with a shared national creative, it is the first reason to take Prime Video seriously. Amazon does not state how finely delivery itself can be limited, so ask your rep. ## Who Should and Should Not Test It

**Reasonable fit:**

- Amazon sellers who can pair Prime Video awareness with Sponsored Products and see sales on Amazon itself. Start with [Sponsored Products for local business](/blog/amazon-sponsored-products-for-local-business).
- Multi-location businesses and franchisees with a $10K+ test budget and a finished video spot.
- Product brands whose category overlaps with what Amazon shoppers buy, and who can sell outside your service area.

**Poor fit:**

- A single-location service business with a monthly budget under about $2,000. The response needed to break even (see the math below) is hard to believe at that scale.
- Anyone without a measurement plan: a promo code, a QR code or a dedicated URL.
- Businesses that cannot serve a metro-sized audience.

## The Math: What Response Does $10,000 Need?

Every number below is an **assumption** for illustration. Amazon does not publish CPMs on the pages above, and Adwave's $30 to $50 launch-era CPM range is unsourced. Use the real figure from your written quote.

Assumptions: $10,000 budget, $40 CPM, frequency of 4, average customer worth $120 in first-year revenue, and for the search comparison a $4 click with 4% of clicks becoming customers.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Impressions ($10,000 / $40 x 1,000) | 250,000 | 250,000 | 250,000 |
| Households reached (impressions / 4) | 62,500 | 62,500 | 62,500 |
| Share of households that become customers (assumption) | 0.05% | 0.13% | 0.30% |
| New customers | 31 | 81 | 188 |
| Cost per customer | $320 | $123 | $53 |
| Revenue at $120 each | $3,750 | $9,750 | $22,500 |
| Same $10,000 in search ads ($4 click, 4% buy) | 100 customers at $100 | 100 customers at $100 | 100 customers at $100 |

Break-even on revenue is $10,000 / $120 = 84 customers, or 0.13% of the 62,500 households. That is the Base column. If you doubt that 1 in 770 households in your target area will act, the test will lose money on direct response, and you are buying awareness. That can be a valid goal, but then you need a way to measure it, such as branded search growth or foot traffic.

The search comparison is just as assumption-driven. The point is not that search always wins. It is that you should write down the response you need before you spend, then compare against a channel you can already measure. ## Do This Today

1. Write your break-even number: monthly budget divided by average customer value. Convert it to a share of households using a $40 CPM and frequency 4, or your quoted CPM.
2. Check whether you already own a 15 or 30-second video spot. If not, price one before pricing media.
3. Email Amazon Ads or a partner with four questions: current minimums in my country, fees on top of media, whether ZIP-level delivery is possible for my budget, and what reporting I will receive.
4. Create the tracking now: a promo code, a QR code and a dedicated URL, set up in Google Analytics.
5. Price a search-ads test for the same weeks, using the [Google Ads service](/services/google-ads), so you have a comparison.

## FAQ

**Q: Is there a minimum spend for Prime Video ads?**
A: Amazon's streaming TV page says there is no minimum spend requirement for the placements, lists a $50K campaign minimum for managed-service packages and recommends $10K for self-service. Terms vary by country and change, so confirm in writing.

**Q: Can I target only people near my shop?**
A: Amazon's location-based interactive ads can create ZIP-code versions of one commercial, but the feature is US only and needs specific creative. Amazon's streaming TV page does not state how tightly delivery can be limited, so ask before assuming a neighborhood radius.

**Q: Do I need to sell on Amazon to advertise on Prime Video?**
A: No. Amazon's DSP page says DSP is available to advertisers who sell on Amazon and those who do not.

**Q: Is Prime Video better than Facebook or Google for a local business?**
A: For direct response from a local radius, search and social usually give you cleaner targeting at lower cost. Prime Video is a reach tool. See our [Meta Ads service](/services/meta-ads) and the [Google Ads service](/services/google-ads).

**Q: How long should a test run?**
A: Long enough to reach your planned frequency across the audience, and to see branded search and sales respond. Decide the number that counts as success before launch.
