A local business FAQ page works when it answers the real questions customers ask before they book: price, timing, location, cancellation and what to expect. Write the questions in customers' own words, group them by topic, and link each answer to the page where the visitor can act. Do not add FAQ markup for rich results: Google says FAQ rich results no longer appear in Search from May 7, 2026.

That change does not make FAQs pointless. NN/g says a public FAQ can cut repeat support questions, answer pre-purchase questions and give search engines indexable content. This guide shows how to build one without fluff.

## What changed with FAQ rich results?

Google's FAQ structured data page now carries a notice that the feature "will no longer appear in Google Search starting May 7, 2026", and the rich result documentation was removed on June 15, 2026. Before that, in September 2023, Google had already limited the feature to well-known government and health websites.

For a local business this means FAQ schema will not give you expanded answers in results. You can still publish the page. Do not spend money on a plugin just for markup.

## Why still have a FAQ page?

NN/g's long-standing guidance, written in 2014, lists the value: fewer repeated emails and calls, a place to answer questions that come before a purchase, content that matches how people search, and a way for prospects to judge your candour. Its research is older than the recent Google change, so use it for UX, not SEO claims.

The page also gives you material for your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) and for [chat or booking assistants](/blog/ai-chatbot-for-local-business-booking-and-faqs).

## Where do the questions come from?

Use real ones. NN/g advises basing questions on what users actually ask and using their vocabulary, which also matches search queries. Sources you already have:

- Phone calls and voicemails.
- Emails and website contact forms.
- Direct messages on social media.
- Questions in your Google reviews and replies.
- Questions your staff answer every day.

Keep a tally for two weeks. The top ten repeats become your page.

## How should the page be laid out?

For a short FAQ, NN/g says a traditional list of questions with answers works well. For long ones, list the questions at the top with jump links to the answers. Group by topic: booking, prices, location, policies.

Use standard HTML for accordions so they work with keyboards. NN/g notes some JavaScript accordions fail with keyboards or when scripts do not load. Add a clear call to action under the last answer. See our [conversion rate guide](/blog/website-conversion-rate-optimization).

## How do you write good answers?

Answer in the first sentence, then add detail. Give numbers where you can: "A standard cut takes about 45 minutes" is better than "it depends". If it truly depends, say what it depends on and give a typical range. Link to the booking page, pricing page or policy page. Our [pricing page guide](/blog/website-pricing-page-local-business) covers ranges.

Google's own guidance on helpful content asks whether a page gives original information and helps the reader reach their goal. A FAQ built from real customer questions fits that.

## Worked example: how many calls could a FAQ page save?

All inputs are **assumptions**.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Calls a week asking the same 5 questions | 12 |
| Minutes per call | 3 |
| Share of those calls avoided by the FAQ | 30% |
| Value of staff time per hour | $20 |

Calls avoided: 12 x 30% = 3.6 a week. Minutes saved: 3.6 x 3 = 10.8 a week, or about 47 a month. Value: roughly 0.78 hours x $20 = about $16 a month. The time saving alone is small. The bigger benefit, if any, is that prospects who find prices and policies answered book without calling. That effect is not quantified here; measure it with your own booking data.

## FAQ

### Should I still add FAQ schema?

It is optional. Google says FAQ rich results no longer appear in Search from May 7, 2026, so schema will not produce them. It does not harm a page.

### How many questions should the page have?

Cover the repeats from your own inbox and calls. No source reviewed here sets a number, so let your own repeat questions decide the length.

### Should I put FAQs on service pages too?

Yes for questions tied to that service. Keep one general page for everything else.

### Do FAQs help local SEO?

NN/g says question-style content matches how people search and gives search engines indexable text. No source reviewed here measures a ranking effect.

### How often should I update it?

Review it monthly or whenever a question keeps coming in that the page does not answer.
