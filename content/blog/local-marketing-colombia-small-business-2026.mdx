Small businesses in Colombia can reach a large online audience with TikTok and Meta ads. DataReportal's Digital 2026 country report, as reproduced by AWISEE, lists TikTok ad reach of 37.7 million, which it puts at 93% of adults, and Facebook ad reach of 36.4 million, or 67.9% of the population. Ad reach is an advertising estimate, not a count of active users, so use these figures to size an audience rather than forecast sales. This guide covers platform choice, the disclosure rules for sponsored posts, and a worked budget example.

## How Big Is the Social Audience in Colombia?

DataReportal counts 37.7 million social media user identities in Colombia, equal to 70.4% of the population. These identities measure something different from ad reach, so the platform totals should not be added together. TikTok's ad reach of 37.7 million matches the same headline number, which DataReportal gives as 93% of adults.

Facebook ad reach stands at 36.4 million, YouTube at 31.0 million and Instagram at 21.6 million, or 40.2% of the population. Instagram reaches fewer people in Colombia than in Brazil or Argentina, according to AWISEE, which says it rarely plans Colombia on Instagram alone. Treat all of these as audience sizes for planning, not as forecasts of who will buy.

## Which Platform Should a Small Business Start With?

TikTok has the largest ad reach in the figures reviewed here, and AWISEE reports that it is the favourite platform for watching creators, at 29% in an Ipsos survey cited via Portafolio (February 2025). That makes it a reasonable first test for consumer brands with short, visual offers such as food, beauty or retail.

Facebook is the second-largest reach figure and works well for local offers that link to a map, a booking form or a phone call. YouTube, at 31.0 million, suits reviews and tutorials for products people research before buying. AWISEE describes it that way for products people research first. Instagram is the smallest of the four, and AWISEE recommends Reels and Stories mainly for fashion, beauty, food and travel brands.

A single-location business can run one platform for four weeks, then compare cost per enquiry before adding a second.

## What Disclosure Rules Apply to Sponsored Posts?

The Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC) published a 2020 guide asking influencers to label paid content as advertising, in Spanish, and as visible as the main message. AWISEE describes this as guidance based on Colombia's consumer statute, not a standalone fine schedule. The label should be easy to see on the post itself, not hidden in a caption tag.

Enforcement is real. In 2023 the SIC opened investigations into companies linked to influencers, including cases over undisclosed commercial ties, as reported by Xataka Colombia. A Senate bill would require labels such as "Publicidad" for content creators. It passed its first debate in 2025 and remains pending, according to the Senate of Colombia.

Label every paid, gifted or affiliate post now. Doing so costs nothing and matches what the SIC already asks.

## Does a Creator Campaign Fit a Small Budget?

Forbes Colombia cites an IAB estimate of 650,000 content creators in Colombia (December 2025). That number is a market size, not a price list. No public source in this review gives creator fees, so a small business should set a fixed test fee per creator and measure what it brings.

Start with two or three creators whose audience sits in your own city. Ask each one for a tracked link or a unique discount code, and give every post a clear disclosure label. Scheduling those posts across platforms is easier with a dedicated tool, and the Hootsuite vs Sprout Social guide compares the two for small teams.

## What Should Stay Out of a First Plan?

This review found no public Colombian cost-per-click or cost-per-conversation benchmark, so the budget above is a test, not a forecast. Do not copy a price from another country and treat it as local.

Service businesses that take bookings, such as clinics, salons or repair shops, often need scheduling and job tracking alongside ads. The Jobber vs ServiceTitan guide compares two field-service tools for that case. Keep the first campaign narrow: one city, one offer, one platform.

## FAQ

### How many people are reached by Facebook ads in Colombia?

DataReportal reports Facebook ad reach of 36.4 million, or 67.9% of the population. It is an advertising estimate, not a count of active users.

### Is TikTok bigger than Instagram in Colombia?

In ad reach, yes. DataReportal's figures show 37.7 million for TikTok and 21.6 million for Instagram. The counting methods differ, so compare trends over time rather than single numbers.

### Do I have to label sponsored posts in Colombia?

The SIC's 2020 guide asks influencers to label paid content as advertising, clearly and in Spanish. A Senate bill proposing specific labels was still pending after its first debate in 2025.

### How much should a small business spend on a first ad test?

No public Colombian price benchmark was found. Start with a fixed test budget, such as the COP 600,000 assumption above, and track cost per conversation for at least four weeks.
