CTV (connected TV) advertising lets a fitness studio show video ads on streaming services to people in its local area. Costs are usually quoted per thousand impressions (CPM) and typically land in the tens of dollars on premium inventory, so many small studios start with a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars per month, as long as the creative is strong and the offer is clear. Exact CPMs vary by platform, targeting and season, so get current quotes before you commit.

## Why Fitness Studios Suit CTV

Fitness is a visual category. Energy, results and community are things you can show in 15 to 30 seconds far more powerfully than you can describe in a text ad. CTV gives you video at scale, with local targeting that lets you reach people near your studio.

Many people think about joining a gym for weeks before they actually search. A well-placed CTV ad during that consideration phase can plant your name, so when they later search "yoga studio near me," they already recognize you. CTV is best viewed as a top-of-funnel awareness channel that supports your search and social ads, not a replacement for them.

## Targeting Local Fitness Prospects

CTV platforms offer several kinds of targeting. Availability depends on the platform and partner, so confirm what is available before you plan around it.

**Interest segments:** Health and fitness, yoga, running, weightlifting and wellness segments are commonly offered, built from content and app-usage data.

**Demographics:** If your studio skews toward a certain age group, you can usually target that range.

**Geography:** Set a radius that matches your realistic draw area, tighter in dense cities and wider in suburbs. ZIP-code level targeting can help focus on neighborhoods where your members live.

**Behavioral segments:** Some providers offer segments of people who recently engaged with fitness content or services. Treat these as helpful but imperfect.

## What Makes a Great Fitness CTV Ad

The best fitness ads show what it feels like to be a member.

**A 15-second structure to try:**
- **0 to 5 seconds:** Class footage or a member moment as the hook
- **5 to 12 seconds:** Your differentiator, such as small classes, coaches who know your name or no long contracts, if true
- **12 to 15 seconds:** One call to action plus your location, such as "First class free at [Studio], [Neighborhood]"

**Creative tips:**
- Use real members, with permission, rather than generic stock footage
- Show the community, not just the equipment
- Mention your trial offer if you have one
- Put the offer in on-screen text, since some viewers watch with the sound off

<Callout type="tip">January is the obvious fitness rush, but many studios also find September and spring worthwhile because motivated prospects are plentiful and competition for ad space may be lower. Start planning three to four weeks before the enrollment period you care about.</Callout>

## Budget Planning for Fitness Studios

CTV is usually bought by impressions, so your budget determines how many local households you reach. A reasonable approach:

| Phase | Goal | How to run it |
|-------|------|---------------|
| Weeks 1 and 2 | Test | A modest daily budget with two or three creative variations |
| Weeks 3 and 4 | Optimize | Cut the weakest creative, tighten the radius, add retargeting |
| Weeks 5 to 8 | Scale | Increase spend only if trials and sign-ups justify it |

Avoid spending so little that you collect no meaningful data, and avoid jumping to a large daily budget before you have tested creative. Watch frequency, and refresh creative every couple of weeks so people do not tire of it.

For a single-location studio, plan a first-month budget you can afford to run for at least four to six weeks. A multi-location gym can run one campaign with a separate local ad set per studio.

**Where to run it:** Work with a CTV-focused platform or managed service that offers local targeting and measurement such as website visit or store visit reporting. Ask about minimums before you sign up.

## Integrating CTV Into Your Marketing Funnel

CTV works best as the top of your funnel:

- **Awareness (CTV):** Reach local people and build name recognition.
- **Consideration (retargeting):** Show Instagram and Facebook ads with a specific offer to people who visit your site. See [Meta Ads for local businesses](/services/meta-ads).
- **Decision (search):** Capture "gym near me" searches with [Google Ads](/services/google-ads).
- **Retention (email and SMS):** Keep new members engaged with automated sequences from [email and SMS marketing](/services/email-sms).

<Callout type="tip">Add a phone number or landing page URL that you use only in your CTV ad. This creates a clean path for crediting calls and form submissions to the campaign without complex analytics.</Callout>

## Measuring What Moves the Needle

CTV measurement is less direct than search, so combine several signals:

- **Delivery metrics:** impressions and video completion rate. A high completion rate suggests the ad holds attention, though benchmarks vary by platform.
- **Unique promo codes or URLs:** a simple code such as "TVFREE" shows how many trials trace back to the campaign.
- **Branded search lift:** compare searches for your studio name before and during the campaign using Google Search Console.
- **Call tracking:** a CTV-only phone number captures calls that do not mention a code.
- **Intake form question:** add "how did you hear about us?" to your sign-up flow and review it after 60 to 90 days.

Clicks are naturally low on CTV because people watch on a couch, so judge the channel on trials, sign-ups and branded search, not click-through rate. Our [analytics service](/services/analytics) can help set this up.

Before launching CTV, make sure the foundation is solid:
1. Your [Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile) is complete and has a healthy number of recent reviews.
2. Your website has a fast, clear landing page with your trial offer.
3. You have a working booking system.
4. You have quality video content, even 15 to 30 seconds of class footage.

## FAQ

**Q: How much does CTV advertising cost for a fitness studio?**
A: CTV is typically priced by CPM, and rates vary by platform, targeting and season. Many small studios start with a budget in the hundreds to low thousands of dollars per month. Ask any provider for current CPM ranges and minimums before committing.

**Q: What types of CTV ads work for fitness studios?**
A: Ads that show real members and real classes tend to perform well, with one clear offer such as a free first class. Keep them short, usually 15 seconds, and avoid cramming in several messages.

**Q: How do I target CTV ads to local people?**
A: Use geographic targeting around your studio, then layer on age or interest segments if your provider supports them. Keep the radius realistic for how far members actually travel.

**Q: Can I measure whether CTV is working?**
A: Yes, though indirectly. Use promo codes, a dedicated landing page, call tracking, branded search trends and a "how did you hear about us" question to connect ads to sign-ups.

**Q: Is CTV better than Google Ads or Meta Ads for a gym?**
A: They do different jobs. Search ads capture people ready to join now, while CTV builds awareness before they search. Most studios should get search and a strong local profile working first, then add CTV.
