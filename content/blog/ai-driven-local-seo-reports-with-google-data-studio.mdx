You can build a free local SEO report in Google's dashboard tool by connecting Search Console and Google Analytics, and by adding Business Profile numbers through a sheet or a partner connector. Google's connector catalog (checked 31 October 2026) lists Search Console, Google Analytics, Google Ads and Google Sheets. It does not list a Google Business Profile connector.

Google's documentation now calls the tool Data Studio again, after the Looker Studio name. It describes it as a no-cost tool, and the older names refer to the same product. This guide shows what to put in the report and where each number comes from.

## Which Data Can You Pull Into a Local SEO Report?

Google-owned sources cover web search and site traffic. Search Console gives queries, clicks and impressions for your site. Google Analytics gives sessions and conversions. Google Ads adds paid results if you run them. Google Sheets lets you add anything else, such as monthly Business Profile numbers you copy in.

| Source | Connector status in Google's catalog | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| Search Console | Listed | Search queries, clicks, impressions |
| Google Analytics | Listed | Sessions, conversions |
| Google Ads | Listed | Paid clicks and spend |
| Google Sheets | Listed | Any manual or exported data |
| Google Business Profile | Not listed | Needs a sheet or a partner connector |

Partner connectors exist for Business Profile, but they are third-party products with their own terms and limits. Treat their feature lists as vendor claims and check the current listing before paying.

## What Does Business Profile Report?

Business Profile Help describes a performance report with these items, shown only where they apply: interactions, searches, views on Search and Maps, directions, calls, website clicks, messages, bookings, and product, menu and offer views.

Some counting rules matter in a report. Views count unique visitors, so they can be lower than notification numbers. Search terms update at the start of each month and can take up to five days to appear. Call tracking needs a phone number on the profile. Bookings need a booking provider.

## How Do You Lay Out the Report?

Keep it to four sections that a shop owner can read in two minutes:

1. **Visibility:** Search Console clicks and impressions by month, plus Profile views.
2. **Actions:** Profile calls, direction requests and website clicks.
3. **Website:** Analytics sessions from organic search and form or booking conversions.
4. **Notes:** Two lines on what changed and what you do next.

Start with a single page and monthly date ranges. Add a comparison to the previous period so changes are visible. ## Where Does AI Fit In?

AI is useful for the writing step, not for the numbers. Export the table, paste it into an AI assistant, and ask for a plain summary of what rose, what fell and which numbers are too small to trust. Then check each claim against the table.

Do not paste customer names or private data. Do not let a tool state causes it cannot see, such as "rankings rose because of your new photos". Search Console shows what happened, not why.

Worked example, all inputs are assumptions: a salon sees Search Console clicks go from 120 in September to 150 in October, and Profile calls from 18 to 22. The report shows +25% clicks and +4 calls. With counts this small, one busy week can explain it, so the note says "watch for two more months" instead of claiming a win.

## How Often Should You Send It?

Monthly is enough for most local businesses, because Profile search terms update monthly. Weekly dashboards can stay open for you, but send the client or owner one short monthly page. Set the date range to the last full month so the numbers do not shift after sending.

## FAQ

### Is Google Data Studio free?

Google's documentation describes the base tool as no-cost. A paid Pro version exists for team and administration features. The pricing page was not readable when checked, so no Pro price is quoted here.

### Is it called Looker Studio or Data Studio now?

Google's current documentation page says the tool is called Data Studio, after the Looker Studio name. Older guides use Google Data Studio or Looker Studio for the same product.

### Is there a Google Business Profile connector?

Not in Google's own connector catalog as checked on 31 October 2026. Use a Sheets export or a partner connector, and read the partner's current terms.

### Can AI write the report for me?

It can summarize a table you give it. You still need to check the numbers and avoid claims about causes.

### How many months of data do I need?

The sources here give no minimum. Use at least three months to spot a pattern, and more if your business is seasonal.
