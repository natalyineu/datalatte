A South African small business should start with three free or cheap channels: a complete Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business for customer messages, and a Facebook page with a small ad budget. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counted 51.7 million internet users (79.6% of the population) in October 2025, and Facebook ads could reach 27.9 million people. Reach is potential audience, not active users.

This guide uses published audience data and platform help pages. DataReportal does not publish ZAR click prices, and none were verified, so cost examples are labelled as assumptions.

## Which Platforms Reach South African Customers?

DataReportal's ad reach figures show how many people each platform's ads could reach: Facebook 27.9 million (up 10.3% in a year), YouTube 26.9 million, Instagram 8.6 million (up 19.4%) and TikTok 29.1 million adults 18 and over (up 33.2%). The report warns these are not active user counts, and it does not mention WhatsApp.

Instagram's reach is far smaller than Facebook's, so a business serving a broad age range usually starts on Facebook. A business aimed at under-35s, such as a salon or gym, can add Instagram or TikTok. Our [Instagram ads guide](/blog/instagram-ads-for-local-business-complete-2026-guide) covers the second step.

## How Should You Use WhatsApp Business?

WhatsApp Business lets you keep a business profile, quick replies and customer lists. For broadcasts, WhatsApp's help centre says a list can hold up to 256 contacts, and only people who have saved your number receive the message. It also warns that people may report frequent broadcasts, and accounts reported multiple times can be banned.

So build the list from real customers who asked to hear from you, send little, and make each message useful, such as a booking reminder or a stock update. Put a click-to-chat link on your Business Profile, receipts and posters. Our [WhatsApp Business guide for local business](/blog/whatsapp-business-marketing-local-business) covers setup.

## Does Load Shedding Still Matter for Marketing?

Less than it did. SAnews reported on 4 August 2026 that South Africa had gone 441 consecutive days without load shedding, since 16 May 2025. Eskom continues load reduction, a separate local measure, in some areas, and targets removing it from a seventh province by October 2026 and nationwide in 2027.

So keep your Google Business Profile hours accurate if your area is affected, and say whether you have backup power if that helps customers decide. Do not plan campaigns around a load-shedding schedule that is currently suspended nationally. Check Eskom's current status before relying on it.

## Is Google Business Profile Worth the Time?

Yes. It is free and sits in the results people see when they search for a service nearby. Google's guidelines say the name should be your real-world name without extra keywords or taglines, categories should be as few as possible, and the description should not contain links or promotions. Get these right before spending on ads.

Then collect reviews from satisfied customers in person or by a WhatsApp message with your review link, and reply to each one. See our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) for the full list.

## FAQ

### What is the best marketing channel for a small business in South Africa?

No single channel wins for every business. Facebook has the largest ad reach among the main social platforms in DataReportal's data, WhatsApp handles customer conversations, and Google Business Profile captures local searches. Start with those three.

### How many people use the internet in South Africa?

DataReportal counted 51.7 million internet users in October 2025, which is 79.6% of the population. 13.3 million people were offline.

### Can I send WhatsApp promotions to everyone?

No. A broadcast list holds up to 256 contacts and only reaches people who saved your number. Frequent reports can get an account banned.

### Is load shedding still affecting South Africa?

National load shedding had been suspended for 441 days as of 4 August 2026, according to SAnews. Load reduction continues in some areas.

### How much do Facebook ads cost in South Africa?

No verified average is published in the sources used here. Set a small test budget and read the cost per result in Ads Manager.
