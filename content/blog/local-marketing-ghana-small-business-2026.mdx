Local marketing in Ghana for small businesses comes down to four channels: a Google Business Profile so customers can find you on Maps, WhatsApp Business for orders and bookings, Facebook for community groups and paid reach, and mobile money so customers can pay without leaving the conversation. Accra is the main market for most of these, with Kumasi as the second city. This guide sets out a practical order of work, a worked budget with labelled assumptions, and the checks to make before spending. It does not quote Ghana platform user counts or ad prices, because the sources available for this rewrite do not verify them.

## Which channels should a Ghanaian small business set up first?

Start with the Google Business Profile. A complete listing with accurate map pins, current opening hours, photos and a steady flow of reviews is the cheapest way to appear when someone searches for a service nearby. Pin accuracy matters most where street addresses are harder to read, so check the pin from the customer's side, not only from your desk.

Next, set up WhatsApp Business with a product or service catalogue and saved quick replies for prices, opening hours and booking questions. Many enquiries will arrive here first, so response speed matters more than post frequency. A reply inside a few minutes usually converts better than a polished post that waits all day.

Facebook comes third. Use it for community groups, daily offers and paid reach to nearby customers. Scheduling tools can cut the time spent posting. Before paying for one, compare [Hootsuite and Sprout Social for small businesses](/blog/hootsuite-vs-sprout-social-small-business-local-business), focusing on the platforms and approval steps you actually need.

## How should mobile money fit into the sales process?

Mobile money is the payment step, so it belongs in the sales plan rather than in a separate task list. Ghana's common options include MTN Mobile Money, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo Money and aggregators such as Hubtel. Ask each provider about merchant fees, settlement times and how payments appear in your records before choosing one or more.

Put the payment details where customers already decide: the WhatsApp profile, the Facebook page and the Google listing. A short reply that includes the payment number and the booking time reduces back-and-forth messages. Test the full path once with a small real order before you rely on it.

For a mobile-first comparison, read [local marketing in Kenya for mobile-first small businesses](/blog/local-marketing-kenya-small-business-mobile-first). Its payment flow differs from Ghana's, but the same logic applies: remove steps between enquiry and payment.

## What does a realistic monthly budget look like?

The example below uses a hair salon in Osu, Accra. All inputs are assumptions for illustration, not market data.

Assumptions: total monthly marketing budget of GHS 3,000. Exchange rate of 1 USD = GHS 15, which makes the budget roughly USD 200 (check the current rate before budgeting, since the cedi moves).

The split is GHS 1,200 for Google search ads, GHS 1,100 for Meta and TikTok ads, and GHS 700 for content creation. The three lines add up to GHS 3,000. Each line is tied to a measure the owner can see in their own bookings.

| Channel | Monthly spend (assumption) | Measure to track |
|---|---|---|
| Google search ads | GHS 1,200 | Booking calls and direction requests |
| Meta and TikTok ads | GHS 1,100 | Booking messages on WhatsApp |
| Content creation | GHS 700 | Profile visits and repeat bookings |
| Total | GHS 3,000 | Cost per booking, not clicks |

Judge each line by cost per booking, not by clicks or impressions. A suggested rule (assumption, not from a source) is to pause any line that produces no bookings over two monthly review cycles, then move that money to the channel that does. Keep the booking definition the same every month, so the comparison stays fair.

## Which tools suit a small business with no marketing staff?

A salon owner or café manager usually needs three things: a way to take bookings, a way to send invoices or receipts, and a way to schedule posts. Choose the fewest tools that cover all three. Many owners find that a messaging app plus a spreadsheet works well at first, and that a dedicated tool only pays off once volume grows.

If you invoice corporate clients or track payments across several accounts, compare [FreshBooks and QuickBooks for local businesses](/blog/freshbooks-vs-quickbooks-small-business-local-business) before choosing. Check whether each tool supports the payment methods your customers actually use, including mobile money where it matters.

Once the basic setup is working, a local SEO review can show which listing and review gaps are holding back calls and bookings. [Local SEO services](/services/local-seo) cover this audit step, including Google Business Profile checks and review requests.

## What should a Ghanaian business check before spending?

1. Confirm the Google Business Profile shows the correct pin, hours and category.
2. Set up WhatsApp Business with a catalogue and quick replies for common questions.
3. Test the payment flow end to end with a small real order.
4. Set a monthly budget and split it across no more than three channels.
5. Track cost per booking for each channel, using the same definition every month.
6. Review the results at the end of each cycle and move money from weak lines to strong ones.

## FAQ

### English or Twi for Ghanaian digital marketing?

English is the main language for most business and search content. Adding Twi phrases to captions can help with local connection, but test whether it improves response rates before making it the default.

### How important is Google Maps for a Ghanaian business?

It is one of the most useful free channels for local discovery. Keep the pin accurate, respond to reviews and ask recent customers to leave feedback. Check the listing from a customer's phone, not only from a desktop.

### Which budget should a small business start with?

Start with an amount you can measure. The worked example in this guide uses an assumed GHS 3,000 per month. Scale up only after a channel has a clear cost per booking over several cycles.
