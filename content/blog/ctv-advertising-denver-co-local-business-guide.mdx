A Denver business can run connected TV (CTV) ads aimed at its own neighborhoods and pay by CPM, the cost per 1,000 views. Ad-tech vendors estimate a blended CTV CPM of about $26, in a market-wide range of roughly $15 to $45. None of the sources used here publishes Denver-specific rates, so this guide uses national ranges and shows how to test them locally.

It covers targeting, budgeting and a break-even example. The price figures are vendor estimates, not platform rate cards, and they change often.

## How Does Local Targeting Work for CTV?

Traditional TV was sold by market. MNTN Research (a CTV vendor, secondary) says Nielsen created 210 Media Markets for this, which it calls imprecise because each covers a large area. It says many CTV platforms now let you target by city or zip code, so a business can limit delivery to the area it serves.

AdWave makes a related point on value: a cheap impression shown to someone outside your service area is worth less than a pricier one shown to a likely nearby customer. Limit delivery to the neighborhoods your customers actually travel from.

## What Does CTV Cost, and Is There a Denver Rate?

None of the sources used here publishes a Denver CPM. AdWave puts the blended 2026 CPM at about $26, with most campaigns at $25 to $35. By tier it lists free ad-supported channels at about $15 to $25, premium direct and live sport at $45 to $65, and addressable buys at $45 to $85 ([AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/average-ctv-cpm-q2-2026)).

Vibe estimates Roku and Hulu at $20 to $60, says Roku publishes no fixed rates, and says campaigns typically start around $500 in total ([Vibe](https://www.vibe.co/blog/roku-advertising-cost)). It lists Hulu with higher minimum spend requirements. Ask each vendor for a written quote for Denver zip codes.

## How Do You Turn a Budget Into Views?

Divide the budget by the CPM and multiply by 1,000. At a $26 CPM, $1,000 buys about 38,500 views. At $45, it buys about 22,200. A view is an impression, not a person, so one household can account for several.

Local targeting narrows the pool. Ask the platform for the estimated audience in your zip codes, then check how often each household would see the ad. Our [CTV cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data) has a full table.

## What Is a Realistic Denver Example?

Every input below is an **assumption** for a fictional fitness studio, not a Denver statistic or a real campaign.

| Assumption | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget | $1,000 |
| CPM | $26 (AdWave blended estimate) |
| Views | about 38,500 |
| Households in target zip codes | 6,000 |
| Average views per household | about 6.4 |
| Value of a new member, first year | $600 |
| Members needed to break even | 2 |

Two members are 0.03% of the 6,000 households, which may sound easy. The hard part is attribution: ads on TV do not produce clicks. Use a promo code, a QR code to a landing page and a "how did you hear about us?" question for every new member. Set a review date and a stop rule before you start.

## What Should a CTV Ad Say?

Keep it simple. A CTV creative advisor quoted by Filmkraft (secondary) suggests a clear call to action and a persistent URL, because you may not have 30 seconds of undivided attention. He mentions 15 and 30-second spots as both effective, depending on the message.

Show your business name, neighborhood and one action. Our [creative specs guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026) lists file requirements.

## FAQ

### How much does CTV advertising cost in Denver?

No Denver rate is published in the sources used here. National vendor estimates are about $15 to $45 per 1,000 views, so $1,000 buys roughly 22,000 to 67,000 views.

### Can I target only my neighborhood?

Many platforms let you target by city or zip code, according to MNTN. Check the minimum audience size for the zip codes you want.

### How much should I start with?

Vibe says Roku campaigns typically start around $500. Other platforms set their own minimums, so ask in writing.

### How do I know it worked?

Use a promo code, QR code and a "how did you hear" question. CTV rarely produces clicks, so track new customers, not only views.

### Should I buy CTV or Google Ads first?
