A local business can automate marketing for free or close to it. Start with three jobs: send every new enquiry to one place, queue social posts ahead of time, and email new contacts a welcome message. Zapier's free plan includes 100 tasks a month and Make's free plan 1,000 credits, which is enough to test before paying.

This guide is for owners of shops, salons, studios and cafés with no marketing team. It compares four tools with prices checked on 31 October 2026, shows what to automate first, and ends with a worked example whose inputs are labelled as assumptions.

## What Can a Local Business Automate First?

Choose repetitive jobs where a late or missed step costs you a customer. Three are worth setting up first:

- Route every website form, booking request or message into one inbox or spreadsheet so none is lost.
- Schedule social posts in a batch, then publish at set times.
- Send a welcome email when someone joins your list.

Leave judgement calls, such as replies to angry reviews, to a person. Our [guide to the best marketing automation tools](/blog/best-marketing-automation-tools-small-business) compares more options, and [AI for marketing automation](/blog/ai-for-marketing-automation) covers where AI helps.

## How Do Zapier and Make Differ?

Both connect apps so that an event in one starts an action in another. Zapier counts usage in tasks, Make in credits. Their free plans differ in size, but a task and a credit are not the same unit, so compare them by running your real workflow and reading the usage screen, not by the headline numbers.

A practical test is to build the same flow in both, run it for a week, and see which stays inside the free allowance. For a salon-specific build, see our [Make.com salon automation walkthrough](/blog/how-to-use-make-com-for-salon-marketing-automation).

## Which Social Posting Tools Are Free?

Buffer's free plan allows up to 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts per channel, and a slot opens again once a post publishes. That fits a business posting a few times a week. The paid Essentials plan removes the cap per channel.

Instagram has its own rules for third-party publishing, covered in our guide to [automating Instagram posting](/blog/how-to-automate-instagram-posting-local-business). For a coffee-shop example, see [coffee shop social media automation](/blog/coffee-shop-social-media-automation).

## What Does Automation Save? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**, not benchmarks. Replace them with your own.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Workflows | 3 |
| Runs per workflow each month | 40 |
| Actions per run (counted as tasks) | 2 |
| Tasks used each month | 240 |
| Minutes saved per run (assumption) | 5 |
| Hours saved each month | 10 |
| Value of an hour (assumption) | $25 |

At 240 tasks, the Zapier free plan (100 tasks) is exceeded and the 750-task Professional tier at $19.99 a month fits. That is $239.88 a year. Ten hours at $25 is $250 a month, so the tool pays for itself if the time savings are real. If your runs are fewer than 100 tasks a month, the free plan is enough.

## What Can Go Wrong?

Automations fail silently. A changed form field or an expired login stops a flow, and nobody notices for weeks. Add an alert email for failures and test each flow monthly with a dummy entry.

Also watch consent. Sending marketing email to people who did not ask for it breaks the rules in many countries, and each platform's terms apply. If you collect leads, store only what you need, and measure what the automation changes with the steps in our [Google Analytics 4 guide](/blog/google-analytics-4-for-small-business).

## FAQ

### What is the cheapest way to start marketing automation?

Use the free plans. Zapier offers 100 tasks a month, Make 1,000 credits, and Buffer 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts each. Build one workflow and see whether it stays within the allowance.

### Is marketing automation worth it for a one-person business?

It is when it removes a task you repeat weekly. The worked example above shows how to compare tool cost with time saved using your own numbers.

### Do I need to know how to code?

Not for the tools in this guide. Zapier and Make connect apps through visual builders.

### Can automation replace my social media manager?

It schedules and routes work. Choosing what to post, replying to people and judging results still need a person.

### How do I know if an automation is working?

Check run history weekly, set failure alerts, and compare leads or posts before and after in your analytics.
