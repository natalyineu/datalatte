Wolt does not publish one commission rate for restaurants. Its merchant help page says merchants pay a percentage of the order subtotal and that actual rates are set in the merchant agreement ([Wolt merchant help](https://merchant.wolt.com/en/aut/learning-center/wolt-merchant-fees-and-commissions), checked October 2026). So the first marketing decision on Wolt is arithmetic: what does one order earn after fees?

## What Wolt says about fees

- **Standard delivery:** applies to orders delivered by Wolt couriers.
- **Wolt+:** a slightly higher rate on Wolt+ subscriber orders; in some markets a fixed fee.
- **Takeaway:** a lower rate when the customer collects, since Wolt does not deliver.
- **Other charges, not in every country:** a platform fee, penalties for delayed orders or missing items, device repayment and sales corrections.

The only public figure we found is second-hand. In December 2021 the Finnish hospitality association MaRa said Wolt's commission can go up to 30 percent ([Yle](https://yle.fi/a/3-12223050)). Treat it as a ceiling claim from one market, not a current rate.

## Worked example (assumption): one 25 euro order

Assume a basket of 25 euro, ingredients and packaging costing 9 euro, and a negotiated commission of 25%. These are invented numbers to show the method.

- Commission: 25% of 25 = 6.25 euro.
- Cost of goods: 9 euro.
- Left before labour and rent: 25 - 6.25 - 9 = 9.75 euro.

At 30%, commission is 7.50 euro and 8.50 euro is left. A 5-point commission difference moves 1.25 euro per order, or 250 euro a month at 200 orders. Marketing spend on Wolt only pays if the extra orders each leave more than their share of ad cost.

## What to do first

1. Get your commission, platform fee and penalty terms in writing.
2. Work out margin per order for your 5 best sellers.
3. Run one promotion for a few weeks and measure new customers and profit after commission.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What commission does Wolt charge restaurants?**

It is not published as one rate. Wolt says the rate is set in your agreement, with different rates for standard delivery, Wolt+ and takeaway.

**Q: Is takeaway cheaper than delivery on Wolt?**

Wolt describes takeaway as a lower rate because it does not handle delivery. Check the exact figure in your contract.
