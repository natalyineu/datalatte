## Is Microsoft Clarity Really Free?

Microsoft's FAQ says "Clarity is a free service forever" and that you never face traffic limits or forced upgrades. It says there is no sampling and no limit on sites per account. It works with WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, Google Tag Manager, Wix and other platforms.

Limits to know: Microsoft's FAQ says data is kept for up to 30 days from the time of recording. And in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, explicit consent is required before Clarity cookies are placed. Without it, sessions fragment and features such as session replay are affected. Clarity captures clicks, scrolls and mouse movement, and input boxes are always masked.

## What Do the Paid Tools Add?

Crazy Egg's page lists AI analysis for heatmaps and recordings on all plans, with exports to AI on Pro and Enterprise. Mouseflow lists click, scroll and geo heatmaps plus attention, movement and interactive heatmaps. When a Mouseflow free plan runs out of sessions, tracking stops until the next monthly refill.

Neither page says a paid tool will lift conversions. For a site with a few hundred visits a month, a free plan covers the need.

## How Do You Use a Heatmap on a Local Business Site?

Look at one page at a time, usually the home page or booking page.

1. Check whether the phone number, booking button and address get clicks.
2. Check how far down the page visitors scroll; if the booking button is below that point, move it.
3. Look for clicks on things that are not links, which suggests people expect them to be.
4. Change one thing and compare two weeks before and after.

No tool publishes how many visits are needed for a reliable heatmap, so treat patterns from a few dozen sessions as clues, not proof. Pair the data with [Google Analytics](/blog/google-analytics-4-for-small-business) and the [local SEO KPIs](/blog/how-to-measure-local-seo-success-kpis).

## A Worked Example: Fixing a Booking Button

All inputs are **assumptions** for a dog groomer's site.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly sessions | 600 |
| Share who reach the booking button | 25% |
| Share who click it | 10% of those |
| Booking-button clicks | about 15 |

If the heatmap shows the button sits below where most visitors stop scrolling, move it to the top and add it to the menu. If that lifts the share who reach it to 60% with the same click rate, clicks become about 36. That is a hypothetical, and your own two-week comparison is the real test. ## FAQ

### What is the best free heatmap tool?

Microsoft Clarity is described as free with no traffic limits. Mouseflow's free plan is capped at 500 sessions a month.

### Do I need cookie consent?

Microsoft requires explicit consent for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. If you have no visitors from there, it says there is no impact.

### Does Clarity keep data forever?

No. Microsoft states recordings are kept for the webmaster for up to 30 days.

### How much does Hotjar cost?

This could not be verified. Check its current pricing page.

### Will a heatmap increase bookings?

Only if you act on it. No vendor page promises a lift.
