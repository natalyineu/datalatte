Local marketing in Brunei starts with a small, concentrated market. The Department of Economic Planning and Statistics (DEPS) estimates the 2025 population at 458,600, and 72.5% live in Brunei-Muara district. DataReportal counts about 300,000 Facebook and 297,000 Instagram ad-reach users. So start with a free Google Business Profile, add a small Search and Meta test aimed at a radius around your premises, and judge it by customers, not clicks.

There is no public Brunei benchmark for Google or Meta ad costs, so the cost model below uses labelled assumptions. Facts come from official statistics and data reports checked in October 2026.

## How big is the Brunei market, according to official data?

The [DEPS Population Estimates 2025 release](https://www.deps.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/POP_2025_PR.pdf) gives these figures:

- **Population:** 458,600 in 2025, up from 455,500 in 2024 (0.7% growth).
- **Status:** citizens 76.7% (351,700), permanent residents 5.8% (26,500), temporary residents 17.5% (80,400).
- **Districts:** Brunei-Muara 72.5% (332,700), Belait 14.8%, Tutong 10.6%, Temburong 2.1%.
- **Age:** median 32.8; 19.8% are under 15.

The [World Bank](https://data.worldbank.org/country/brunei-darussalam) lists 2025 GDP per capita at about US$32,235 and population at 466,330. That differs slightly from DEPS because the methods differ; use DEPS for local targeting. The Monetary Authority of Singapore says Brunei and Singapore dollars [can be exchanged at par and without charge](https://www.mas.gov.sg/currency/brunei-singapore-currency-interchangeability-agreement).

## How many people can I reach on Facebook and Instagram in Brunei?

[DataReportal's Digital 2026 report](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-brunei-darussalam) lists 463,000 internet users (penetration capped at 99.0%) and 300,000 social media user identities. Its late-2025 Meta ad reach:

| Platform | Ad reach | Share of population | Adults reached: female / male |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 300,000 | 64.1% | 40.6% / 57.1% |
| Instagram | 297,000 | 63.7% | 53.1% / 45.4% |
| Messenger | 135,000 | 28.9% | not listed |

DataReportal warns that ad reach is not a count of unique people or active users, and advises against year-on-year comparisons with its earlier reports. It does not list WhatsApp, so this guide has no WhatsApp figure.

A recommendation, not a sourced finding: a salon, bakery or wellness studio selling mainly to women may find a stronger skew on Instagram, and a tools or car business on Facebook. Test both.

## Why should I target one district, not all of Brunei?

With 72.5% of residents in Brunei-Muara, a cafe, salon or clinic in Bandar Seri Begawan is chasing about 332,700 residents at most, and in practice those within a short drive. Skip "Brunei-wide" campaigns unless you ship nationally.

Google says its [location targeting](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6317) lets you choose entire countries, areas within a country such as cities, and a radius around a location. Draw a radius around your premises.

Also, 17.5% of residents are temporary residents. If your customers include foreign workers or professionals, publish English alongside Malay on your profile, ads and menu. How much of each to use is a judgment call; test it.

## Do I need halal certification to advertise food in Brunei?

Check before you advertise. A [BizBrunei report from July 2017](https://bizbrunei.com/2017/07/changes-halal-certification-apply-businesses) said all businesses producing, supplying and serving food and beverages must apply for halal certification, each product needs its own permit, and businesses serving only non-Muslims can apply for an exemption.

A [Star report from August 2026](https://thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/08/09/brunei-strengthens-halal-compliance-amid-rising-applications) quotes the Minister of Religious Affairs: the Halal Food Control Division handled 1,004 applications from January to July 2026, with 614 approved, 122 in process, 190 awaiting the Brunei Islamic Religious Council, 23 exempted and 55 rejected.

The 2017 report may be out of date, so confirm current rules with the Ministry of Religious Affairs before making any halal claim in an ad. For beauty, fitness and pet businesses, keep imagery modest and ask a local adviser if unsure.

## Which channel should a Brunei small business start with?

Order matters on a small budget (this order is a recommendation):

1. **Google Business Profile.** It is free. Google says local results rest on [relevance, distance and prominence](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091), and businesses with complete and accurate information are more likely to appear. Fill in hours, phone, photos and category, and ask happy customers for reviews. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers each field.
2. **Google Search ads.** Volume is small but intent is high. Run one campaign for your main service, with English and Malay phrases such as "hair salon Bandar Seri Begawan".
3. **Meta ads.** Use Instagram and Facebook for offers that prompt action, such as a launch or trial class. Send clicks to WhatsApp or a booking page. Our guide to [WhatsApp Business for local marketing](/blog/whatsapp-business-marketing-local-business) covers setup.

Ask every new customer how they found you. In a small market this beats any dashboard.

The table counts revenue, not profit, so real payback is slower. Your ceiling cost per click is the most you will pay per customer times enquiry rate times close rate. Paying BND 25 per customer at 6% and 30% gives 25 x 0.06 x 0.30 = BND 0.45.

Reach check: if Facebook users spread like the population (an assumption), about 217,500 of the 300,000 are in Brunei-Muara, so 1,000 clicks a month is nowhere near saturation.

## Do This Today

1. Complete every field in your Google Business Profile, with at least ten real photos.
2. Write down average spend per visit, yearly return visits and the most you would pay for a new customer.
3. Set up WhatsApp Business with a greeting and quick replies for price, hours and location.
4. Launch one small Search and one small Meta campaign around your premises, with a budget you can afford to lose.
5. Log how every new customer found you, and review it on the first of next month.

## FAQ

**Is Brunei's small population a problem for digital marketing?**
It caps volume, not effectiveness. DEPS counts 458,600 residents and 72.5% are in Brunei-Muara, so a tight radius and a specific offer matter more than broad reach.

**Should I start with Instagram or Facebook?**
DataReportal shows near-equal reach (300,000 and 297,000). Instagram skews female and Facebook male among adults. Start where your main buyer is, then test the other.

**Is Google Ads worth it with small search volume?**
It can be for high-intent services if the campaign stays narrow and you track real enquiries. Run a capped test first.

**Can I copy Singapore or Malaysia tactics?**
Some transfer, such as Business Profile and WhatsApp enquiries, but market size, religion-linked rules and audience differ. Adapt and test. For budgeting, see our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide).
