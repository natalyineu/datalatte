A gym can test Spotify ads with a small local campaign: Spotify's Ads Manager overview says you can launch with a minimum budget of 250 in your local currency, using audio ads of up to 30 seconds. In the US you can target by country, state, city or postal code. Spotify does not publish a price per listener on those pages, so the cost of reaching your members has to come from your own test.

This guide covers what the platform allows, how to write a 30-second script, how to check results and a worked example labelled as an assumption. No source found measures Spotify's effect on gym sign-ups, so none is claimed.

## What Do You Need to Run a Spotify Ad?

Spotify's Ads Manager overview says you can upload your own creative, create an ad with generative AI, or have Spotify's creative team write, voice and produce an ad at no additional cost. You set an average daily or lifetime budget and either choose flight dates or run always on. The overview does not explain the billing model, so read the billing terms in your account.

Audio specs from Spotify: MP3, WAV or OGG files up to 50 MB, a 44.1 kHz sample rate and a loudness of -16 LUFS. The companion image is square, at least 600 by 600 pixels, in JPEG or PNG and under 200 KB. Clickthrough goes to one HTTPS address, so build a landing page for the offer.

| Item | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum budget | 250 in local currency |
| Audio length | 30 seconds maximum |
| File | MP3, WAV or OGG, 50 MB maximum |
| Companion image | 1:1, 600x600 minimum, 200 KB maximum |
| Advertiser name | 25 characters maximum |
| Call to action | 1 of 20 presets, such as Learn More or Visit Site |

## How Do You Target Local Listeners?

Spotify's US location help says advertisers can target by country, state, city and postal code, and that it uses IP addresses to decide who is eligible. Postal code is the closest fit for a gym that draws from a few neighbourhoods. The help page does not mention a radius option, so check your account.

Spotify's troubleshooting page warns that an audience can be too small to spend the full budget, for example with a very specific location or combined genre and fan targeting, and you pay only when the ad plays and never more than your budget. Also, you choose only one of interest, real-time context, genre or fan targeting. Start broad with location and age, then narrow. ## What Should a 30-Second Gym Ad Say?

Spotify's specs give about 65 words as the maximum for a 30-second script. Use roughly 50 to leave room for pauses. One idea only: who the gym is for, where it is, and one offer with one action.

1. **Seconds 0 to 5:** the listener's situation, such as a first class in your area.
2. **Seconds 5 to 20:** one offer, such as a free first class, stated plainly with any limits.
3. **Seconds 20 to 30:** the action and a short web address that is easy to remember.

Do not promise results like weight loss. Say what you offer. Ads can be rejected for not following Spotify's Advertising Terms, for running over 30 seconds or for linking to a competitor, per the troubleshooting page. Edit and resubmit rather than starting over.

## How Do You Know It Worked?

Send listeners to a dedicated page or code so sign-ups can be traced. Spotify says measurement tools are available, and Ads Manager shows an Estimated results tool for likely delivery before you launch. Compare cost per sign-up with the same measure from other channels you run, and see our [Spotify ad pricing guide](/blog/how-much-do-spotify-ads-cost-pricing-for-local-business).

Do not judge on plays alone. Ask each new member how they heard about you for the first month. ## A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**. Spotify does not publish a local cost per impression or per sign-up on the pages checked.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $250 |
| Ad plays reaching listeners | unknown, set by your test |
| Landing page visitors from ad | 60 (assumption) |
| Visitors who claim a free class | 10% |
| Trial attendees who join | 30% |
| Monthly membership | $60 |

60 visitors at 10% gives 6 trial bookings. At 30% joining, that is about 2 new members, worth $120 a month. Against $250 spent, the test breaks even after about 2 months of membership if both stay, and loses money if they leave sooner. The 60 visitors is a guess. Run the test, record the actual number and then decide.

## FAQ

### What is the minimum budget for Spotify ads?

Spotify's Ads Manager overview says 250.00 in your local currency for a campaign.

### How long can a Spotify audio ad be?

Music ads are limited to 30 seconds. Long-form ads up to 60 seconds are available in select markets for Direct campaigns only.

### Can I target people near my gym?

In the US, you can target by country, state, city and postal code. Location uses IP addresses.

### Do I need to record the ad myself?

No. Spotify says its creative team can write, voice and produce an ad at no additional cost, or you can use generative AI or upload your own.

### How much does a listener cost?

The pages checked do not publish a cost per impression. Your own estimated results in Ads Manager will show likely delivery.
