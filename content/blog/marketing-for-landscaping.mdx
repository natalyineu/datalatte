A landscaping company should market in three layers: a service-area Google Business Profile with photos and reviews, Local Services Ads or Search ads for people ready to hire, and a seasonal budget that rises before your busy weeks. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks list Home and Home Improvement at $8.33 per click and $90.92 per lead, the closest published category.

This guide is for residential and light commercial landscapers in the US. The benchmark covers home services in general. No source used here gives landscaping demand by month, so check your own enquiries to time spend.

## What Marketing Works for Landscaping Companies?

Landscaping is visual and local, so start where both show up. A complete, verified Google Business Profile with before-and-after photos of your own jobs tells people what you do and where. Google says complete profiles are more likely to appear in local results, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help ranking.

BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses, 47% will not use one with fewer than 20, and 74% only consider reviews from the last three months. Ask after each job while the result is fresh.

Photo pages also help search. Google advises descriptive file names, useful alt text and placing images near related text.

## Are Local Services Ads Available for Landscapers?

Yes in the US. Google's list includes "Landscaping services" and "Lawn care services." To go live you need a licence or business registration, billing and a budget, and Google says you pay only for leads related to your business. Google's help says your bid is the most you will pay per lead, and higher-quality profiles may rank higher and pay less.

Google's migration page lists Landscaper and Lawn Care among phase-one verticals for moving Local Services campaigns to Performance Max with pay-per-lead goals, starting August 2026 for select US providers. Setup screens may differ from older guides. See our [Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-services-ads-complete-guide).

## How Do You Budget Around the Seasons?

Use an ad schedule and budget changes tied to your own data. Google Ads lets you choose days and hours and set bid adjustments. It paces a campaign toward 30.4 times the average daily budget each month, so a $40 daily budget targets about $1,216 a month.

Raise the budget before your busy weeks, and cut services you cannot staff. No source here sets the right split by season, so compare enquiries by month in your own call log. The [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to allocate.

## The Math: What Does $1,200 Buy in Spring?

All inputs below are **assumptions** except the benchmark.

| Step | Assumption or source | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget | Assumption | $1,200 |
| Cost per lead | $90.92 (benchmark) | about 13 leads |
| Leads who sign | 35% (assumption) | about 4.6 jobs |
| Average first-season contract | $1,500 (assumption) | about $6,900 |
| Cost per signed job | $1,200 divided by 4.6 | about $260 |

Contract size is not a published figure, so use your own. Maintenance contracts renew, so the first sale understates value. If only 15% sign, cost per job rises to about $600; measure your close rate from quotes sent.

## How Do You Keep Customers Between Seasons?

Landscaping can be recurring: mowing, spring clean-up, seasonal planting. Collect a phone number and email with consent at the quote, and send a short reminder before each season. Do not offer rewards for positive reviews. The FTC's rule on fake reviews took effect on 21 October 2024 and bars them. ## FAQ

### How much should a landscaper spend on marketing?
No published figure fits every firm. Use the benchmark of about $90.92 per lead to size a test, then adjust to your close rate.

### Are Local Services Ads worth it for landscaping?
They can be, because you pay per lead. Google lists landscaping services and lawn care services as eligible in the US.

### How do photos help my ranking?
Google advises descriptive file names, informative alt text and images near related text on the page.

### When should I raise my ad budget?
Before your busy weeks. Use your own enquiry history, because no source here gives monthly demand.

### How many reviews do I need?
BrightLocal found 47% of consumers will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews.
