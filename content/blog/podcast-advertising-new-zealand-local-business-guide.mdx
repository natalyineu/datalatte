A New Zealand local business can buy podcast ads two ways: self-serve in Spotify Ads Manager, where Spotify lists New Zealand among the markets for its podcast ads beta, or through a sales team such as NZME's podcast network, which sells single shows, host reads and genre packages. Neither publishes a price list, so the cost per thousand listens (CPM) has to come from a quote or a test. The audience is there: 55% of New Zealanders aged 16 and over listen to podcasts monthly.

This guide covers the audience, the buying routes, the rules for labelling ads and a worked budget example. Every figure comes from a source at the end, and illustrative examples are labelled as assumptions.

## How Many New Zealanders Listen to Podcasts?

Edison Research's Infinite Dial New Zealand 2025, a March 2025 national survey of people aged 16 and over, found 55% listen to podcasts monthly, rising to 71% among under-35s, and 30% listened in the last week. It also found 94% of New Zealanders consume audio every week. The release gives the sample as 1,007 people but elsewhere mentions over 1,300, so treat the exact precision with care.

A separate GfK Commercial Radio Survey released in May 2026 said that 43% of weekly radio listeners also listen to a podcast weekly. That is a share of radio listeners, not of the whole population, so do not compare it with the Edison figure.

## How Can a Local Business Buy Podcast Ads in New Zealand?

| Route | How it works | Minimum spend | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spotify Ads Manager | Self-serve podcast ads, category, age, gender and location targeting | Not stated on Spotify's page | Fixed CPMs, not published |
| NZME podcast network | Sales-led: single show, host reads, network or genre and audience packages | Not published | Not published |
| An independent show | Contact the host or their sales agent | Varies by show | Negotiated |

Spotify's announcement lists New Zealand among 14 markets for self-serve podcast ads, then in open beta. The page is undated, so confirm in your account that the option is live. It says ads run on and off Spotify, with targeting by podcast category, age, gender and location, and fixed CPMs with no hidden fees. It does not publish the CPM or a minimum.

NZME's page says it sells single-show ads and sponsorships, host-read ads, ads across one of its eleven brand networks and genre or audience modules, and reports 6.1 million downloads and over 1.3 million monthly listeners a month on average from January to July 2026 (Triton data, supplied by NZME). Pricing and minimums are not listed, and you contact its media specialists.

## What Are Host-Read Ads and When Do They Make Sense?

A host-read ad is one the presenter reads in their own voice. NZME lists host-reads as a product. Local businesses often prefer them because the message comes from a voice the listener already trusts, but no independent New Zealand data was found comparing host-read results with other formats, so do not assume a lift.

Ask the seller for the show's New Zealand download numbers, how many of the listeners are in your region and what the ad length is. A national show may send most of your spend to people who live too far away to visit you. ## What Are the Rules for Labelling a Podcast Ad?

The Advertising Standards Authority Code's Rule 2(a) says "Advertisements must be identified as such." The ASA's guidance says payment is not required for content to count as an advertisement, that all parties including advertiser, agency and media share responsibility for making it clear, and that "Sponsored content" and "Advertisement" are accepted identifiers. It does not name podcasts or host reads, so the safest approach is for the host to state clearly that the segment is an ad or sponsorship.

The Commerce Commission adds that it is illegal to make a claim about your goods or services without a reasonable basis and that you must have the evidence when the claim is made. Avoid claims such as "best in Auckland" unless you can support them. This is general information, not legal advice.

## What Does a First Podcast Test Cost? A Worked Example

No New Zealand rate is published, so every input here is an **assumption** for illustration only.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget | NZ$750 |
| CPM | NZ$30 |
| Ad impressions | 25,000 |
| Average plays per listener in the campaign | 2 |
| Listeners reached | about 12,500 |
| Share inside your travel area | 20% |
| Local listeners | about 2,500 |
| Share who use your offer code | 1% |
| New customers | about 25 |
| Cost per new customer | about NZ$30 |

The share inside your travel area is the weak link. If only 5% of listeners live nearby, the same budget produces about six customers, a cost of NZ$120 each. Compare that with a [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) and search ads, which only show to people nearby.

Use an offer code or a dedicated phone number, and add "how did you hear about us?" to your booking form. Podcast ads have no click to count, and the estimates only mean something if you can count responses.

## Which Local Businesses Should Try Podcasts?

Podcasts suit businesses where one new customer is worth much more than the cost of reaching them: gyms, groomers, salons and cafes with regular visitors. They suit less well for one-off low-price items. ## FAQ

### How much do podcast ads cost in New Zealand?

Spotify says it uses fixed CPMs but does not publish them, and NZME does not list prices. Request a quote with the show's New Zealand downloads, or run a small Spotify test and read the CPM in your report.

### Is there a minimum budget?

Spotify's podcast ads page does not state one. NZME does not publish one. Ask when you enquire.

### Can I target one city?

Spotify's page lists geographic targeting. Whether it is available at city level for podcasts was not stated, so check in Ads Manager. For a network buy, ask the seller what location data they have.

### Do I have to label host-read ads as ads?

The ASA says advertisements must be identified as such, and that payment is not required for content to count as one. Ask the host to say it is an ad or sponsor message.

### How long should I run a test?

Long enough to count responses reliably. Four to six weeks with a code or phone number is a sensible starting point, but no source sets a standard test length.
