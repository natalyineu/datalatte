## Should a Local Business Claim Its Google Profile First?

Yes, because the sources treat the profile as the base layer. A Leads Ireland post lists a Google Business Profile with the right main category and real photos of your work as one of the basics small firms most often skip. Another local agency post describes setting up and managing the profile so a business appears in map results. Both are vendor statements, so treat them as practice advice rather than proof of results.

Check that the category, hours, service list and photos match your website exactly. Google's own help pages explain how to add or claim a business, and that is the first step before paying for any ads. A profile that is wrong or empty gives paid campaigns a weaker starting point, so complete it before spending.

## Is Google Ads Worth It for an Irish Trade Business?

The sources support Google Ads as a call-generation tool, but they do not show Irish results. Leads Ireland says its goal is more calls and enquiries from Google rather than better rankings, and it lists Local Services Ads alongside call and form tracking. That tells you what to measure, not how well the channel performs.

ePresence, a Cork agency, describes itself as a Google Partner and says it sets budgets to fit each client. Its pages describe Search, Shopping, Performance Max and Retargeting, but give no prices or conversion rates. Ask any agency for client results in writing, and compare its answer with the [Google Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-service-ads-worth-it-local-business) before committing.

## Which Social Platforms Should You Consider?

None of the sources gives Irish reach figures for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or LinkedIn, so no platform ranking is possible from them. The ePresence and Orbis pages both list Meta, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn ad services. That shows agencies sell these channels, but it does not show which ones suit a particular trade.

Check reach inside each platform's own ad manager for your target town before spending. The sources do support YouTube as a large video audience, which ePresence cites as more than one billion hours watched daily, but that is a global figure from a vendor page. If you manage several accounts, a scheduling tool can save time; the [Hootsuite vs Sprout Social comparison](/blog/hootsuite-vs-sprout-social-small-business-local-business) explains the trade-offs between two common options.

## Do Field Service Businesses Need Booking Software Before Ads?

Ads send people to a page, and the business still has to answer the call, confirm the job and send an invoice. If bookings are handled by phone and paper, a surge in enquiries can be lost. Field service software can connect quotes, scheduling and payments, which makes it easier to track which enquiries turn into paid work.

Compare options on price, mobile app quality and whether the tool connects to your phone system before you buy. The [Jobber vs ServiceTitan comparison](/blog/jobber-vs-servicetitan-small-business-local-business) covers two field service platforms in detail. Treat software as a way to respond faster, not as a substitute for a working Business Profile.

## What Should You Check Before Sending Marketing Messages?

The sources contain no Irish rules on email or text marketing, so this guide does not state any. Before building a list, read the current guidance from the Data Protection Commission and take advice from a legal adviser, because consent and existing-customer rules can change how a business may contact people.

Keep a clear record of how each contact joined your list, include an unsubscribe option in every message, and remove people who ask. Treat this as a checklist to confirm with the regulator, not as a summary of the law.

## What Does a Starter Google Ads Budget Look Like?

The example below is for a plumber in Cork. Every input is an assumption, not an Irish benchmark. Replace each one with your own Keyword Planner data and call-tracking results.

| Item | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | Assumption | 600 euro |
| Cost per click | Assumption | 4 euro |
| Clicks | 600 / 4 | 150 |
| Calls or booking requests per 100 clicks | Assumption | 6 |
| Enquiries | 150 x 6 / 100 | 9 |
| Cost per enquiry | 600 / 9 | about 67 euro |

If the cost per click is 2 euro, the same budget gives 300 clicks and 18 enquiries at about 33 euro each. A weaker click rate or higher click price pushes the cost up quickly, so test for at least one full month before deciding. Compare the enquiries with the call logs, not with the number of clicks, because clicks alone do not show whether the phone rang.

## FAQ

### What should a small business in Ireland do first?

Claim and complete the Google Business Profile, since the sources treat it as the base layer. Add paid channels only after calls or form submissions can be tracked.

### Do the sources give Irish search or social media figures?

No. The pages reviewed contain no Irish search share, ad reach or click-price data. Check Keyword Planner and each platform's ad manager for your own town.

### Can I send marketing emails or texts to customers?

Check the current guidance from the Data Protection Commission and a legal adviser before sending. The sources reviewed do not set out the rules.

### How long should I test Google Ads?

The sources give no testing period. A reasonable starting point is one full month with call tracking in place, so enquiries can be compared with spend.

### Is there a government grant to fund this?

The sources reviewed do not list any Irish grant. Ask your local enterprise office directly about current support.
