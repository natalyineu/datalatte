An Irish small business should start with Google, because Statcounter shows Google at 93.47% of search in Ireland in September 2026. Claim your Google Business Profile, then add Facebook and Instagram, which each had an advertising reach of 2.60 million in late 2025. Add Google Ads once your profile and website can turn a click into a call or booking.

No source we could open publishes Irish click prices for local trades, so this guide gives channels and rules, not invented euro figures. The budget example is labelled as an assumption.

## Which Channels Reach Irish Customers?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report for Ireland counts 5.26 million internet users in October 2025 and 4.26 million social media user identities, equal to 80.1% of the population. It warns that ad reach figures are not monthly active users and that platform corrections can distort changes.

| Platform | Ad reach, late 2025 | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 2.60 million | Down 3.7% on a year earlier |
| Instagram | 2.60 million | Up 4.0% on a year earlier |
| YouTube | 4.26 million | Up 3.6% since end 2024 |
| TikTok | 2.59 million (adults 18+) | Up 16.7% since end 2024 |
| LinkedIn | 3.70 million registered members | Up 8.8% |

For a café, salon or fitness studio, Instagram and Facebook are the sensible starting pair. Use TikTok if you can make short video. LinkedIn suits services sold to other businesses.

## How Does Google Ads Work for an Irish Local Business?

Google's Ads help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km, and that Presence or Interest is the recommended setting for Search, with Presence suited to sensitive verticals or to exclude people elsewhere. Use a radius or named towns such as Cork, Galway or Limerick around your premises.

Click costs differ by trade and town. WordStream by LocaliQ publishes US benchmarks only, so ask Google's Keyword Planner for your own town and service before setting a budget. Add negative keywords early. Google says they do not match close variants, so include plural and singular forms. ## Should You Start With Your Google Business Profile?

Yes, because it is free and Google says you can add or claim your business at no charge. A profile that shows correct hours, services and photos helps both Maps and ads. Google also says more reviews and positive ratings can help local ranking.

Add your Eircode-based address exactly as it appears on your website, and ask customers for reviews with a Google link or QR code. Do not offer a discount or gift for reviews, which Google's policy prohibits. See our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) and the [local pack guide](/blog/how-to-rank-in-google-maps-local-pack).

## What Do Irish GDPR Rules Mean for Email and SMS?

The Data Protection Commission says direct marketing is governed by both the GDPR and the ePrivacy Regulations, and covers emails and texts. Its blog says an ongoing or recent business relationship can change whether marketing counts as unsolicited, and that unsubscribe requests must be actioned.

Details of the existing-customer exception, including when you must offer an opt-out, are in DPC and legal guidance that we could only read in secondary form and in a 2019 post. Get consent for every list you build, include a working unsubscribe link, and check the DPC's current page before sending.

## Is There Any Government Help for Going Online?

Local Enterprise Offices ran the Trading Online Voucher Scheme: up to 2,500 euro, with the business paying 50% of costs, for businesses with 10 or fewer employees and turnover below 2 million euro and at least six months of trading. Eligible spend included booking systems and payment facilities on a website.

The LEO page we read says the scheme closed for applications on December 13, 2024, so it appears closed. Contact your county LEO to ask about current supports. ## What Does a Starter Budget Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**, not Irish benchmarks. A salon in Cork tests Google Ads with 600 euro a month and a click price of 2 euro.

| Item | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | Assumption | 600 euro |
| Click price | Assumption | 2 euro |
| Clicks | 600 / 2 | 300 |
| Booking rate from clicks | Assumption | 5% |
| Bookings | 300 x 5% | 15 |
| Cost per booking | 600 / 15 | 40 euro |

If your click price is 4 euro, the same budget gives half the clicks and a cost per booking of 80 euro. Replace the assumptions with your Keyword Planner data and your own booking rate. See the [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) for how to split spend.

## FAQ

### What is the best marketing channel for a small business in Ireland?

Google, via a free Business Profile, is the base, since Google had 93.47% of Irish search in September 2026. Social media adds reach.

### How many people in Ireland use Facebook and Instagram?

DataReportal's late 2025 advertising reach is 2.60 million each. These are ad audience figures, not monthly active users.

### Can I send marketing emails to customers?

Only under the GDPR and ePrivacy rules. Collect consent, offer an unsubscribe and read the DPC's current guidance.

### Is there a grant for my website?

The Trading Online Voucher Scheme closed on December 13, 2024, per the LEO page. Ask your county LEO about current options.

### How much should I spend on Google Ads?

No Irish benchmark is published in the sources we opened. Use Keyword Planner and start small.
