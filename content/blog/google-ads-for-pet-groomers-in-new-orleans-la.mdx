## Is There Enough Demand for Pet Grooming Ads in New Orleans?

New Orleans & Company reported 19.46 million visitors to the city in 2025, up 2% from 2024, with $10.8 billion in visitor spending (release dated 8 May 2026). Tourists rarely need a full groom, so most of that volume is not your audience. It does mean searches near the French Quarter and the hotels are crowded with visitor intent, and residents are the ones to target.

Nationally, APPA says dog ownership reached 71 million U.S. households in 2025, or 53% of households. The same release warns that spending on boarding, salon grooming and mobile grooming all declined in 2025 as owners sought ways to save, and that 51% of owners groom their dogs at home. APPA gives no New Orleans figure. Clear prices and a first-visit offer matter more than broad awareness.

## Which Keywords Should a New Orleans Groomer Use?

Start with searches that name the service and the place. The themes below are starting points; check volumes and costs for New Orleans in Google Keyword Planner, because no source we opened publishes them.

| Theme | Example | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service plus city | "dog grooming New Orleans" | Clear local intent |
| Near me | "dog groomer near me" | Mobile searchers ready to book |
| Neighborhood | "dog groomer Uptown" | Narrow, lower waste |
| Specific service | "cat grooming", "puppy first groom" | Fewer competitors |
| Mobile | "mobile dog grooming New Orleans" | Only if you offer it |

Add negative keywords early: jobs, school, course, DIY, "how to", and supplies. Google's help explains that negatives do not match close variants, so add singular and plural forms yourself. Our [pet groomer keyword list](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-pet-groomers-in-2026) has more themes.

## How Do You Keep Spend Inside Your Service Area?

Google says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km, and recommends "Presence or interest" as the default for Search, with "Presence" suited to businesses that want to reach only people physically in the location. A groomer whose customers drive from nearby can test Presence if the Search terms report shows clicks from places you cannot serve.

Keep the radius on residential areas such as Uptown, the Garden District or Mid-City rather than on the tourist core, if that is where your clients live. Pick a radius from where your bookings really come from, using your own records. No source gives a standard travel distance, so any figure like "three to five miles" is yours to set. ## What Should You Track?

Set up conversion actions for calls and online booking requests. Google's call assets let people call from the ad, and you set the minimum call length that counts, so choose a length that reflects a real inquiry. Watch the Search terms report weekly and cut terms that bring no calls.

A strong Business Profile helps ads, because many searchers compare reviews and hours before they click. ## What Does a Month of Ads Cost? A Worked Example

## FAQ

### How much does Google Ads cost for a New Orleans pet groomer?

No New Orleans-specific groomer CPC is published in the sources we could open. LocaliQ's Animals & Pets average is $4.06 per click and $31.50 per lead.

### How much should I spend?

It depends on capacity. Work backward from how many new clients you can take each week and your cost per request.

### Is Google Ads better than the Maps listing?

They work together. A Maps listing is free, and ads capture searchers who compare options. Start with your profile.

### Should I target all of New Orleans?

Not at first. Start near your salon or route and widen only if the Search terms report shows good calls from farther away.

### How long until I know if it works?

There is no standard timeline in the sources we opened. Judge calls and bookings against cost over several weeks.
