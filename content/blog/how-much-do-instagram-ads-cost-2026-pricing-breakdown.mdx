Instagram ads have no fixed price, because Meta sells them in an auction. The best public benchmarks for 2026 are these: LocaliQ lists $0.60 per click for Facebook traffic campaigns and $27.39 per lead for lead campaigns across industries, and a secondary report of Emplifi data puts Instagram feed at about $5.00 per 1,000 impressions and $0.70 per click. Meta publishes no official average, and no source below is specific to local businesses on Instagram alone.

This guide shows the numbers, explains what each one measures and turns them into a monthly budget you can test. Illustrations are labelled as assumptions.

## What Are the Published Benchmarks for 2026?

LocaliQ's Facebook benchmarks cover 20 industries and do not state a sample size or data period. They cover Facebook ads, not Instagram only, and they come from advertisers who may run on both. Use them as a planning range.

| Campaign type (Facebook) | CPC | CTR | Conversion rate | Cost per lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Traffic, all industries | $0.60 | 1.93% | not reported | not reported |
| Lead, all industries | $1.80 | 2.70% | 8.54% | $27.39 |
| Traffic, Restaurants and Food | $0.45 | 2.68% | not reported | not reported |
| Traffic, Beauty and Personal Care | $0.50 | 2.73% | not reported | not reported |
| Lead, Beauty and Personal Care | $2.97 | 1.35% | 5.63% | $50.91 |

Retail has no row on that page. The nearest, Shopping, Collectibles and Gifts, lists a traffic CPC of $0.59 and CTR of 1.88%. For a deeper look at one trade, see our [hair salon Facebook ads strategy](/blog/hair-salon-facebook-ads-strategy).

## How Much Does an Impression Cost on Instagram?

The Instagram-specific figures are Emplifi's Q2 2026 worldwide medians, reported by AdsUploader. They cover April to June 2026 across all industries. AdsUploader notes Emplifi does not disclose its paid-ad sample size, so treat them as indicative.

| Placement | CPM | CPC | CTR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Feed | $5.00 | $0.70 | 0.40% |
| Stories | $4.50 | $0.70 | 0.40% |
| Reels | $4.00 | $0.70 | 0.25% |

These are worldwide medians, so a US city can sit above or below them. Emplifi's separate entertainment page shows rough Instagram CPC of $0.45 to $0.70 for that sector, which confirms prices move by industry. Third-party sites quote very different averages, so a single "average Instagram CPC" is not reliable.

## What Do CPM, CPC and Cost per Lead Mean?

CPM is the cost of 1,000 impressions. CPC is the cost of one click. Cost per lead is what one form fill, message or booking request costs.

They connect: cost per click equals CPM divided by 1,000, divided by CTR. At $5.00 CPM and a 0.40% CTR that gives $1.25 per click on paper, which is higher than the $0.70 the same report shows. Medians from different samples do not always reconcile, so do not mix benchmarks in one formula. Use your own Ads Manager data once you have a week of spend.

## What Does a Local Business Budget Look Like?

The example uses the all-industry lead benchmark from LocaliQ. Every input is an **assumption**, not a forecast.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $300 |
| Cost per lead | $27.39 (LocaliQ all-industry, Facebook) |
| Leads | about 11 |
| Leads who become customers | 30% |
| New customers | about 3 |
| Cost per new customer | about $91 |

If one new customer is worth more than about $91 to you over a year, the test is worth running. If your cost per lead is nearer Beauty's $50.91, the same $300 buys about 6 leads and 2 customers, a cost near $150 each. ## How Do You Keep Costs Down?

The levers are in your control, not the auction's.

- Choose the objective that matches your goal. Traffic campaigns are cheap per click but report no leads, as LocaliQ notes, so a cheap click is not a booking.
- Use a tight local radius and test one audience at a time.
- Test Reels, Stories and Feed separately, because the medians above differ by placement.
- Give a campaign time before changing it, so you compare like with like.
- Track bookings, not likes. Our [Instagram ads best practices](/blog/instagram-ads-best-practices-10-rules-for-better-performance) covers creative and testing.

## FAQ

### How much should a small business spend on Instagram ads per month?

No source gives a single right number. A test of $300 for 30 days gives a first reading of your own cost per lead, which you can then scale up or cut. The budget table above shows how to turn a benchmark into leads and customers.

### Is Instagram cheaper than Google Ads?

They measure different things. Google Search ads capture people searching now, and Instagram ads interrupt people browsing. LocaliQ's 2026 search benchmark (reported by Digital Applied) lists a $5.42 median CPC for Google and Microsoft search, which is higher per click but captures people searching now, so compare cost per customer, not cost per click.

### Why do sources quote different Instagram CPCs?

They count different things: all clicks versus link clicks, Feed versus Reels, one industry versus all, medians versus means. Always check the method before using a figure.

### Does Meta publish average ad prices?

Not in the sources checked. Meta's Business Help Center has an article on how ad auctions work, but no price list.
