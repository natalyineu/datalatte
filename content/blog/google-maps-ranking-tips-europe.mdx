To rank higher on Google Maps in Europe, verify your Business Profile, choose the most specific primary category, keep your name, address and hours accurate, add photos, and earn recent reviews that you answer. Google says local ranking rests on relevance, distance and prominence, and that you cannot pay for a better local ranking. The rules are the same across countries. Some features are not.

Google does not publish a separate ranking system for Europe, and no source checked here quantifies the effect of any single fix. This guide uses Google's help pages and Whitespark's 2026 expert survey, and labels opinion as opinion. Example numbers are assumptions.

## What Does Google Say Decides Local Rankings?

Google's help page lists three factors. Relevance is how well your profile matches the search, and Google advises giving complete and detailed business information. Distance is how far you are from the person searching, or from the location they named. Prominence is how well known you are, including links from websites and your reviews and ratings.

Google adds practical steps: verify the business, keep information accurate, include special hours, choose categories, respond to reviews, ask for more reviews, and add photos and videos. It states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

You cannot control distance, so Europe's dense town centres reward the businesses that handle the other two well. Our [Maps ranking factors guide](/blog/google-maps-ranking-factors-what-moves-needle-2026) goes deeper.

## What Do Local SEO Experts Rank Highest?

Whitespark's 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors report, published on 6 November 2025, surveyed 47 experts who scored 187 factors. The top ten for local pack and Maps results were, in order:

1. Primary Google Business Profile category
2. Proximity of address to the point of search
3. Keywords in the business title
4. Physical address in the city of search
5. Business open at the time of search
6. High numerical Google ratings
7. Address showing on the profile, not hidden as a service-area business
8. Additional categories
9. Quantity of native Google reviews with text
10. Proper placement of the map pin

This is expert opinion, not Google's statement. Do not add keywords to your business name: Google tells you to enter it as it appears on your signage, and a name change after verification may require you to verify again.

## How Should You Pick Categories?

The first category is your primary one. Google says to choose the most specific category that represents your main business, and that you can add up to 9 more. Do not use categories as keywords or to describe features.

Category names and availability vary by country and language, so open the list in your own dashboard. A business with several locations must use the same primary category at all of them.

## Which Profile Features Differ by Country?

Some Google features are limited by country. Google's guidelines page says Q&A, in-store products, chat, social links and AI-generated descriptions are limited to select regions, languages or categories. In-store products are listed for the US, Canada, the UK and Ireland.

Online ordering for restaurants is offered in Australia, Canada, Germany, Japan, the United Kingdom and the United States, per Google's restaurant page. Featured posts on mobile apply to single-location food and drink businesses in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand, in English. If you are in France, Spain or Italy, do not plan around features that are not listed for your country. Check what your own dashboard shows.

## How Do You Handle Reviews Across Countries?

Google's rules apply everywhere: reviews must reflect a genuine experience, and discounts, free items or other perks in exchange for reviews are prohibited. Share a link or QR code that opens the review form. Customers need a Google Account.

Reply promptly and briefly. Google asks for replies that are conversational rather than promotional, and says not to share private information. In several European languages, answer in the language of the review where you can.

Whitespark's survey rates high ratings and review quantity in its top ten, but the effect size is not published. Aim for steady new reviews. If your market has a legal duty on marketing messages, such as consent rules for emailed review requests, read your national regulator's guidance before you automate the request.

## How Do You Check Whether It Worked?

Use the Business Profile performance report. Google lists metrics such as calls, directions, website clicks, messages and bookings, shown only if they apply to your profile. Directions are counted with adjustments for multiple taps, cancelled requests and spam, and a person is counted once per day for views.

Searches update at the start of each month and can take up to 5 days to appear. Read our [Business Profile analytics guide](/blog/google-my-business-analytics) for each metric, and the [25-point checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) for the fields to complete.

## Worked Example: A Four-Week Fix Plan

All inputs are **assumptions** for a single-location café in a mid-sized European city.

| Week | Task | Time (assumption) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Verify, check category, name, address, hours, special hours | 1 hour |
| 2 | Add 10 photos and review the map pin | 1 hour |
| 3 | Print a QR code for the review link, ask at the till | 30 minutes |
| 4 | Reply to every review, record calls and directions from the report | 45 minutes |

Total about 3 hours 15 minutes. Compare calls and direction requests with the previous month. Under these assumptions, a change smaller than normal month-to-month variation proves nothing, so repeat the check for three months before judging.

## FAQ

### Does Google Maps ranking work differently in Europe?

Google publishes one set of ranking factors, relevance, distance and prominence, without a separate European version. Feature availability does differ by country.

### Can I pay Google to rank higher in Maps?

No. Google states there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Ads are a separate product.

### How many categories can I use?

One primary category and up to 9 more. Choose the most specific primary category.

### Should I put keywords in my business name?

No. Google says to enter your name exactly as it appears on your signage and other branding.

### How do I get more reviews?

Share the review link or a QR code with customers. Do not offer perks for reviews: Google prohibits them.
