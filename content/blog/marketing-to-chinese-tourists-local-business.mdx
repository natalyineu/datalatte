To attract Chinese tourists, a local business needs three things: a way to be paid in the wallets they use, details a visitor can read in Chinese, and a place they can find you before they arrive. Stripe supports Alipay and WeChat Pay for businesses in the US, Canada, the UK, Australia and many other countries, with a minimum payment of 0.50 USD. The US received 159,391 arrivals from China in January 2026, the fifth-largest source market that month, according to NTTO.

Detailed spend-per-trip figures for Chinese travellers could not be confirmed from an official source here, so none are used. Start with payments and your online listing, then measure.

## How Many Chinese Tourists Visit the US?

NTTO reported 159,391 arrivals from China in January 2026, behind Mexico, Canada, the United Kingdom and Brazil. That is one month, not a year, and it says nothing about your city.

A 2024 NTTO profile summary reported by Hotel Online says China made up 8.2% of the 3.3 million overseas visitors who came on package tours, third after Japan and the United Kingdom. The annual 2024 count for China was not available in the sources opened. For your area, check NTTO's country profile monitor, which covers 71 countries and lets you choose China and a year.

## How Can You Accept Alipay and WeChat Pay?

Stripe's documentation says its WeChat Pay and Alipay payments settle in your local currency or CNY, with customers in China paying through their wallet apps. Business locations include the US, Canada, the UK, Australia, Japan, Singapore and many EU countries. The minimum amount is 0.50 USD.

Both wallets bring different rules from cards. Stripe says neither supports chargebacks, refunds must be made within 180 days for WeChat Pay and 90 days for Alipay, and recurring payments are not supported. Your card terminal setup may differ from Stripe, so ask your payment provider which wallets it supports in person. Stripe lists Terminal support for WeChat Pay but not for Alipay.

## What Should Your Listing Say?

Google advises keeping language and location targeting consistent. If you run ads, write the ad in Chinese and target Chinese-language users rather than expecting an English ad to convert. On your website, add a short Simplified Chinese page with your address, hours, prices, payment options and nearest transit.

Keep your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) complete, and answer reviews in the language used where you can. Whether a Baidu Maps listing can be claimed from outside China could not be verified, so this guide does not cover it.

## Which Platforms Do Chinese Travellers Use?

Xiaohongshu and Douyin are the two most discussed for travel discovery; see [Douyin and TikTok for local business](/blog/douyin-tiktok-local-business-chinese-market). We could not source a share of travellers using each, so do not plan around a percentage. Ask new customers how they found you and count the answers for two months.

Peak travel dates matter more than platform: see the [Chinese New Year marketing guide](/blog/chinese-new-year-marketing-local-business).

## What Is the Return on Chinese-Language Setup? (worked example)

All inputs are **assumptions**: a shop sees 400 customers a week, 5% are Chinese visitors, the average ticket is $30, and a translated page and wallet setup add 10% more of those visitors.

| Item | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chinese visitors a week | 400 x 5% | 20 |
| Extra visitors | 20 x 10% | 2 |
| Extra weekly revenue | 2 x $30 | $60 |
| Extra yearly revenue | $60 x 52 | $3,120 |

Compare that with the cost of translation and any payment fees from your provider. If setup costs less than the extra revenue you would see, proceed. The [marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) helps cap the spend.

## FAQ

### Do I need Alipay or WeChat Pay to serve Chinese tourists?

Not required, but they are the wallets Stripe lists for customers in China. Many visitors will also carry cards.

### Can I accept WeChat Pay in my shop?

Stripe lists WeChat Pay for Terminal in supported countries, except Japan. Check with your own provider.

### Should I translate my website into Chinese?

A short Simplified Chinese page with address, hours, prices and payment options is a low-cost start.

### How many Chinese tourists come to the US?

NTTO reported 159,391 arrivals in January 2026. An annual figure was not available in the sources used.
