## What published pricing looks like

One vendor as a reference point: Tidio's [pricing page](https://www.tidio.com/pricing/) lists a free plan with 50 lifetime Lyro AI conversations, a Starter plan at $24.17 per month (100 billable conversations plus 50 one-off AI conversations), Growth from $49.17 per month, Plus from $300 per month plus usage, and Lyro AI Agent as a standalone product from $32.50 per month for 50 conversations. Prices change, so confirm on the vendor page. Other vendors (ManyChat, Chatfuel and so on) price differently and were not verified for this note.

## Break-even example (assumptions)

Assume you pay $49.17 per month and the bot fully resolves a question that would otherwise take a staff member 4 minutes. Staff time costs $20 an hour, so 4 minutes is about $1.33. Break-even is 49.17 divided by 1.33, roughly 37 resolved conversations a month. If the bot resolves fewer than that, it does not pay on time savings alone; it would need to win bookings that would otherwise be missed (after-hours messages, for example). Replace the 4 minutes and $20 with your own numbers.

## Decision guide

- Fewer than about 40 repeat questions a month: use saved replies and a clear FAQ page instead.
- Many repeat questions and after-hours messages: start on a free tier, read every transcript for two weeks, and keep a visible route to a human.
- Messaging on WhatsApp: remember Meta charges for template messages outside the 24-hour window; see [WhatsApp Business marketing costs](/blog/whatsapp-business-marketing-local-business).
- Comparing bot with live chat: [chatbot vs live chat](/blog/ai-powered-chatbot-vs-live-chat-local-business).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does an AI chatbot cost?**
Published plans from one vendor start free (50 lifetime AI conversations) and paid plans at $24.17 to $49.17 per month for small volumes, with higher tiers from $300 per month. Confirm current prices with the vendor.

**Will a chatbot replace my staff?**
This note makes no such claim. The break-even example above only compares the bot's fee with the staff minutes it saves.

**What should I track?**
Conversations per month, how many the bot resolved without a human, and bookings from after-hours chats.

**Can customers reach a human?**
They should always be able to. Put the handoff in the first menu.
