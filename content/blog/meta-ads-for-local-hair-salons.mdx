## Worked example (assumption)

Assume an average first-visit ticket of $90 and that 1 in 3 leads books. At the benchmark $50.91 per lead, that is roughly $153 per booked client. If a new client is worth $90 on the first visit and you expect 4 visits in a year, lifetime revenue is $360, so the ad can pay back within the year. If you expect only 1 visit, it loses money. The decision hinges on repeat visits, not on the ad.

## Decision guidance

- Run a lead or booking-focused campaign, not post boosts, if your goal is appointments.
- Set a stop rule before you start: for example, pause if cost per booked client exceeds half of expected first-year revenue.
- Fix your booking path first. A $2.97 click that lands on a slow page is wasted.
