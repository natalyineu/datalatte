Local SEO software does four jobs: track rankings by postcode, keep listings consistent, collect and answer reviews, and report what changed. AI features now draft replies and posts inside those tools, but the underlying jobs are unchanged. On vendor pricing checked in November 2026, entry costs run from $1 per location to about $41 a month.

The free baseline matters. A claimed, complete Google Business Profile plus Google Search Console already covers the highest-value local work for one location. Paid tools earn their fee by saving repetition across weeks, locations or clients, not by unlocking a secret ranking switch.

## Which local SEO tool fits which job?

BrightLocal is the generalist. Secondary 2026 comparisons put single-location entry near $39 to $41 a month, covering rank tracking, audits, citations and review monitoring in one dashboard. It suits an owner who wants one login and a monthly report worth reading.

Whitespark is the specialist shelf. Its Local Platform lists at $1 a month per location for profile management, with ranking grids, citation finding and reputation tools priced as separate modules, on Whitespark pages checked in November 2026. It suits agencies assembling only the pieces each client needs.

AI drafting sits inside both ecosystems and in general assistants, including the agents priced in [Best AI agent platforms for small business](/blog/best-ai-agents-for-small-business-2026). A generated review reply still needs a check for names, dates and promises before posting. [Best AI tools for small business marketing](/blog/ai-tools-for-small-business-marketing-2026) covers the writing and design side at $0 to $20 a month.

Bigger suites such as Semrush and Ahrefs track local terms among national data. [Semrush vs Ahrefs for small business](/blog/semrush-vs-ahrefs-local-seo-which-to-use) prices that route at $129 to $139.95 a month, which only suits multi-service or multi-location work.

## What should a single location buy first?

Buy nothing until the profile is complete: categories, services, photos, hours and a review request habit. Those are free and move rankings more than any tracker. The first paid step is usually review management, because replies are weekly work with a visible quality difference.

**Assumption example.** Assume an owner answers 12 reviews a month at 10 minutes each, or 2 hours, valued at $30 an hour. That is $60 of time. A $39 BrightLocal month or a $79 Whitespark reputation module breaks even only if drafting cuts the task roughly in half. The inputs are assumptions; time the real task for one month before choosing.

Rank tracking comes second, and only for the postcodes that produce customers. A grid covering a whole metro flatters and frightens in equal measure. Five to ten checks around the actual trade area show whether profile work is moving anything.

## When does AI help local SEO, and when does it hurt?

AI helps on volume tasks with a human gate: first drafts of replies, post ideas from real photos, and summaries of review themes across a quarter. The approval step is the product. Publishing raw drafts risks wrong offers, invented details and replies that name the wrong customer.

AI hurts when it generates location pages or reviews at scale. Thin pages naming suburbs without real content give search engines little to rank and give owners a cleanup job later. One accurate page per real service area beats twenty generated variants.

Businesses running several locations feel the split sooner. Listing consistency and reply coverage become genuine labour, and per-location pricing starts to justify itself. ## FAQ

### What is the best free local SEO tool?

The Google Business Profile dashboard plus Google Search Console, both free. Together they cover the profile, the queries bringing impressions, and indexing problems. Paid tools add tracking history, citation management and review workflows. For one location that sends a monthly report to nobody, free is usually enough.

### How much do local SEO tools cost in 2026?

Whitespark's Local Platform lists at $1 a month per location, BrightLocal entry sits near $39 to $41 a month for one location, and Whitespark's Reputation Builder lists at $79 per location, on vendor pricing checked in November 2026. National suites cost more: $129 to $139.95 a month at the tiers compared in the Semrush and Ahrefs post.

### Do AI review replies hurt rankings?

No ranking penalty attaches to a drafted reply as such; the risk is accuracy and trust. Replies naming the wrong service or promising refunds nobody approved create complaints, not traffic. Draft with AI if volume demands it, then read every reply aloud before posting. That gate keeps the speed without the errors.

### Should a café or salon pay for rank tracking?

Only once the profile is complete and reviews arrive steadily, and only for postcodes that actually send customers. Tracking five to ten nearby areas monthly shows whether work is landing. Tracking a whole city daily mostly produces anxiety and a larger bill, with no extra decision to show for it.
