Dermatologists get patients from Google Ads by running separate Search campaigns for medical needs (skin checks, acne, eczema, mole removal) and cosmetic services (Botox, lasers, peels), sending each to a matching landing page, tracking every call and booking, and following Google's healthcare advertising policies. The key is to work out what a new patient is worth to your practice, then set your budget and target cost around that number. Costs and results vary a lot by city and specialty, so use the examples below as a framework and check your own market.

Dermatology sits at the intersection of two decision processes. A person searching "skin cancer screening near me" has an urgent medical need and wants an appointment soon. A person searching "Botox cost [city]" is researching and comparing, and needs reassurance and clear information. Keeping those audiences in separate campaigns is what separates a profitable account from an expensive experiment.

## The ROI Math: What Is a New Patient Worth?

Before you spend, work out your own numbers:

1. **Patient lifetime value (LTV):** average revenue from a new patient over a year or more. Medical patients may return for annual checks and treatments. Cosmetic patients may come back for repeat treatments every few months.
2. **Maximum acceptable cost per new patient:** many practices aim for a modest share of LTV, but the right percentage depends on margins and capacity.
3. **Conversion path:** clicks to inquiries, inquiries to appointments, appointments to patients.

Here is a hypothetical example, using made-up round numbers only to show the method:

| Step | Example value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget | $1,500 |
| Cost per click | $5 |
| Clicks | 300 |
| Click-to-inquiry rate | 10% |
| Inquiries | 30 |
| Inquiry-to-new-patient rate | 40% |
| New patients | 12 |
| Cost per new patient | $125 |

If a new patient is worth substantially more than $125 to your practice, the campaign is worth continuing. If not, change the offer, the landing page or the targeting before increasing spend. Replace every number with your own data as it comes in.

## Best Keywords for Dermatology Google Ads

Group keywords by intent. Avoid assuming a specific cost per click: check Google's Keyword Planner for your location, because dermatology terms are often among the pricier local healthcare searches.

### Medical dermatology

- dermatologist near me, dermatologist [city]
- skin cancer screening, skin check
- mole removal or mole check
- eczema treatment, psoriasis treatment
- acne treatment dermatologist
- rash evaluation

### Cosmetic dermatology

- Botox [city] or wrinkle treatment
- dermal fillers [city]
- laser hair removal [city]
- chemical peel near me
- microneedling near me
- skin rejuvenation

### Negative keywords to add early

- DIY, home remedy, natural cure
- school, course, certification, become a dermatologist
- salary, jobs, careers
- free, cheap (unless relevant)
- cream, lotion, product (unless you sell them)
- textbook, study, research

## Campaign Structure

### Campaign 1: Medical dermatology

Target patients with a clear medical need. Lead with credentials and availability, such as "Board-Certified Dermatologist in [City]" only if accurate, and mention whether you accept insurance. The landing page should show credentials, a short list of conditions treated, how to book, and whether you take new patients.

### Campaign 2: Cosmetic dermatology

These patients compare practices. Lead with the treatment, location and a consultation offer. Landing pages work best with service-specific information, pricing guidance where permitted, real reviews (following your professional rules) and an easy consultation request. Use before-and-after images only where your state rules and Google's policies allow, with patient consent.

### Campaign 3: Telehealth (if you offer it)

Target people who want virtual appointments, and only in states where you are licensed. Be clear about which conditions telehealth can and cannot address.

### Ad groups

Within each campaign, create separate ad groups per service (for example, Botox, laser hair removal, acne). Tight relevance between keyword, ad and landing page generally improves Quality Score, which can lower your cost per click for the same position.

## Bidding Strategy

- **First phase:** consider Maximize Clicks with a maximum CPC cap or manual CPC while you gather data.
- **After you have steady conversions:** consider conversion-based bidding such as Target CPA, set at the most you can pay for a new patient. Google recommends enough conversion history for Smart Bidding to work well, so check its current guidance.
- **For top-value services:** you can experiment with impression share goals on a few highly relevant terms.

## Google Ads Healthcare Compliance

Google applies special policies to healthcare and medicine advertising, and the rules differ by country and change over time. Read the current policy before launching. In general:

- Cosmetic and medical dermatology services can be advertised, but claims must be accurate. Avoid guarantees such as "guaranteed to clear your acne".
- Be careful with before-and-after images, since they are restricted in some ad formats and by some state boards.
- Prescription treatments have extra rules. Check whether brand names or certain terms require certification or trigger review, and consider neutral wording such as "wrinkle treatment" when needed.
- Personalized advertising and remarketing are restricted for sensitive health categories, so you may have fewer audience tools than in other industries.
- Your state medical board may have advertising rules covering specialty claims, testimonials and credentials.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. If you handle protected health information, ask a qualified professional how analytics and ad tracking can be used on pages involving patient data.

## Set Up Conversion Tracking

Without tracking you cannot tell which keywords produce patients. Set up:

1. **Form submissions:** track the thank-you page or a form-submit event for appointment requests.
2. **Calls from ads:** use call assets with Google forwarding numbers, or a call tracking service if you want more detail.
3. **Click-to-call on mobile:** track taps on the phone number on your site.

Keep tracking away from pages that collect sensitive medical details. Review weekly: the search terms report for new negatives, cost per conversion by campaign, and Quality Score on important keywords. ## Budget by Market

There is no standard budget. Competition and cost per click rise with city size and with the value of a procedure. As a rough approach:

| Market | Approach |
| --- | --- |
| Smaller city | Start small and focus on a few high-intent services |
| Mid-size city | Run separate medical and cosmetic campaigns with a modest test budget |
| Large metro | Expect higher click costs and more competition, so focus on your most profitable services first |

Run a test for at least a couple of months before judging, and increase spend only when cost per new patient is acceptable. Under-built campaigns waste money in any market. See our [Google Ads budget guide](/blog/google-ads-budget-for-small-business) for sizing methods.

## FAQ

**Q: How much should a dermatologist budget for Google Ads?**
A: It depends on your market and goals. Start with a test budget you can sustain for a couple of months, track cost per new patient, then scale campaigns that perform. Large metros typically need more to appear consistently.

**Q: Which keywords tend to deliver the best return?**
A: High-intent, specific searches such as skin cancer screening, mole removal, acne treatment and specific cosmetic procedures in your city. Avoid broad terms like "skin care" alone.

**Q: How do I track appointments from Google Ads?**
A: Track form submissions on your confirmation step, calls from ads and clicks on your phone number. If you use a third-party scheduler, check whether it supports conversion tracking without exposing patient information.

**Q: Can dermatologists advertise Botox and cosmetic procedures?**
A: Generally yes, when done by licensed professionals and in line with Google's healthcare policies and your state rules. Avoid guarantees and check which terms need certification.

**Q: How long until Google Ads produces patients?**
A: Leads can appear within days or weeks, but plan for a test period of about three months to gather enough data, especially for cosmetic services where patients research for longer.
