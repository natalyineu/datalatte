## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Dermatology Practice?

LocaliQ's 2026 search benchmarks draw on thousands of customer campaigns on Google Ads and Microsoft Ads. For Physicians and Surgeons they list a $4.76 click, a 6.61% click-through rate, a 12.43% conversion rate and $40.04 per lead. Beauty and Personal Care, a rough proxy for cosmetic work, shows $4.62 per click, 10.35% conversion and $39.25 per lead. Health and Fitness runs $6.17 per click and $67.36 per lead, and Dentists and Dental Services $8.00 and $72.97.

The all-industry average is $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead. Cosmetic procedures such as laser treatments often cost more than a skin check, so click prices in your city may sit above these numbers. Read Keyword Planner for your own terms. A lead here is a form or call, not a booked patient.

## How Do You Split Medical and Cosmetic Campaigns?

A person searching "skin cancer screening near me" has an urgent medical need and wants an appointment soon. A person searching "Botox cost" is researching and comparing. Keep them in separate campaigns so each gets its own budget, keywords and landing page.

**Medical campaign keywords:** dermatologist near me, skin check, mole check, eczema treatment, psoriasis treatment, acne treatment dermatologist.

**Cosmetic campaign keywords:** Botox in your city, dermal fillers, laser hair removal, chemical peel, microneedling.

Within each campaign, create an ad group per service so keyword, ad and landing page match. Our guides for [beauty clinics](/blog/google-ads-for-beauty-clinics) and [laser clinics](/blog/marketing-for-laser-clinics) show the cosmetic side in more detail.

## Which Negative Keywords Should You Add?

Review the search terms report weekly. Typical negatives for a practice:

- DIY, home remedy, natural cure
- school, course, certification, become a dermatologist
- salary, jobs, careers
- cream, lotion, product, unless you sell them
- textbook, study, research

Add free and cheap only if they do not match an offer you run.

## What Do Google's Healthcare Policies Mean for Dermatologists?

Google's healthcare and medicines policy lists what it restricts or bans: unapproved substances, unauthorized pharmacies, prescription opioid painkillers, speculative or experimental treatments and more. Prescription drug services, health insurance coverage and most telemedicine and online pharmacy ads need certification or accreditation ([Google Ads policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031?hl=en)). Medical dermatology and cosmetic services are not on that banned list, but the page does not address them by name, so read it for your country before launch.

The personalized advertising policy lists Health among sensitive interest categories. Advertisers promoting those categories cannot use advertiser-curated audiences, though predefined Google audiences remain available ([Google Ads policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/143465?hl=en)). That means fewer remarketing options than in a typical industry. Your state medical board may have separate rules on claims, testimonials and before-and-after images. This is general information, not legal advice.

## How Should You Track Calls and Bookings?

Without tracking you cannot tell which keywords produce patients. Track form submissions on the thank-you step, calls from ads through call assets, and taps on the phone number. Avoid placing tracking on pages that collect sensitive medical details. If you handle protected health information, ask a qualified professional how analytics and ad tags may be used.

## FAQ

### How much should a dermatologist budget for Google Ads?
Start with a test budget you can sustain for two to three months. Track cost per new patient and scale what works. Large metros usually need more to appear consistently.

### Which keywords tend to deliver the best return?
Specific searches such as skin check, mole check, acne treatment and named cosmetic procedures in your city. Avoid broad terms like skin care alone.

### How do I track appointments from Google Ads?
Track form submissions, calls from ads and clicks on your phone number. If a third-party scheduler is used, confirm it supports conversion tracking without exposing patient information.

### Can dermatologists advertise Botox and cosmetic procedures?
Generally yes, when done by licensed professionals and in line with Google's healthcare policy and your state rules. Avoid guarantees and check whether any term needs certification.

### How long until Google Ads produces patients?
Leads can appear within days or weeks. Plan on about three months of data, especially for cosmetic services where patients research longer.
