## How does Google choose which pet groomers appear in the map pack?

Google's local pack is built from business listings, and the listing data comes from the profile itself. [Adwave's lawn care marketing guide](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-my-lawn-care-business) states that the top three businesses in the map pack are pulled directly from their GBP listings. For a groomer, that means the profile is the first thing to get right, before any website work.

Adwave's [categories guide](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones) says that analysis of millions of profiles shows the primary category is the number one ranking factor for the Local Pack, though it does not name the study. Ahrefs, citing a BrightLocal survey, reports that 36% of SEOs in 2021 rated the GBP as the most important map pack factor, up from 23% in 2013. Those figures show expert opinion, not measured ranking weight, so treat them as direction rather than a formula.

## Which Google Business Profile fields should a groomer fill in first?

Start with the primary category. Adwave suggests looking at the top 3-5 businesses in the local map results for your core service search to see which category they use. Then complete the description, which Adwave's [local SEO beginner's guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack) says allows 750 characters. Name your city and service type in the first lines, without repeating keywords.

Hours, phone number, and address need to be exact. Adwave's [Google Maps marketing guide](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers) says these must be 100% accurate, and recommends at least 15 high-quality photos. A separate Adwave guide suggests at least 10. A mobile groomer or one working from home can hide the street address and be designated a Service Area Business. Posts also matter: Adwave advises one new post a week, and [Google's help page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en) says a post shows on Search and Maps only when its status is Live. Pending posts are not visible.

## Do name, address, and phone details need to match everywhere?

Yes. Adwave's [voice search guide](https://adwave.com/resources/voice-search-optimization-for-local-businesses) says the business name, address, and phone number (NAP) must be exactly the same on the website, the GBP, and directories such as Yelp, Apple Maps, and Bing Places. The beginner's guide adds that even punctuation matters, including whether "St." or "Street" is used. Check each listing against your GBP before adding new citations.

Apple Maps is worth claiming too. Adwave's [Apple Business Connect article](https://adwave.com/resources/apple-business-connect-the-google-business-profile-alternative-youre-ignoring) says that in a maps-only comparison, Apple Business Connect has captured a 7% share and is growing. It also reports Profile Views, which counts how many people saw your business card in Apple Maps. One landscaping firm reported an 18% rise in local pack appearances after cleaning up its NAP data. That is a single anecdote, not a controlled result.

## How many reviews does a pet groomer need?

No universal number is confirmed by a primary source. Adwave cites a [third-party analysis](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business) of 2 million GBP pages, by Blogging Wizard, which found that businesses with over 200 reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack. That is an association, not proof that reviews cause the result. Another article claims 50+ reviews with a 4.7+ average dominate top spots, but it cites no evidence.

Google's [review guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122) confirms that reviews appear next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search, so each review also works as a listing-level signal for customers. Ask for reviews that name the service, such as a first-time groom or a nervous dog, and keep the flow steady rather than in bursts.

## Which metrics show whether local SEO is working?

Adwave's [local search intent guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-search-intent) lists six useful measures: GBP impressions, map-pack views, clicks to call, direction requests, website visits from GBP, and branded search lift over time. Record them monthly in a simple sheet, along with the appointments your team books from each source, so you can connect profile activity to revenue.

Expect changes in profile views within a month of consistent work. Adwave's beginner's guide notes that a business may start ranking for its own name a few weeks after verification, which is the vendor's own estimate, so treat it as a rough guide.

## What should a Virginia Beach groomer do this month?

1. Confirm the primary category against the top 3-5 map results for your core search.
2. Check hours, phone, and address against every listing, including Apple Maps.
3. Upload photos of the grooming space, finished dogs (with owner consent), and any mobile van.
4. Set a weekly post and confirm each one reaches Live status.
5. Ask recent customers for a review that names their dog's breed or service.
6. Record the six metrics above each month.

## FAQ

### Do Google reviews affect whether a pet groomer appears in the map pack?

A third-party analysis reported in Adwave's review guide found that businesses with over 200 reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack. It measures association, so treat it as a benchmark rather than a guarantee.

### Does a Google Business Profile cost anything?

Google describes its Business Profile as free to set up on Search and Maps. Paid options, such as ads, are separate costs.

### Can a mobile pet groomer hide its home address?

Adwave states that a business working from home or serving customers at their location can hide its street address and is then designated a Service Area Business. Check Google's live help pages for current service-area settings.

### How long does it take to rank for your own business name?

Adwave's own estimate is a few weeks after verification. This is a vendor estimate, not a guarantee, so track it against your own profile data.
