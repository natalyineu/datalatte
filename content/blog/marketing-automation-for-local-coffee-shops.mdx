A coffee shop should automate three messages first: a welcome email after a customer's first purchase, a win-back email for people who stopped visiting, and a reminder for loyalty rewards. Mailchimp and Klaviyo both have free plans, and their limits (250 contacts or profiles, 500 sends or emails a month) decide which one fits. This guide compares those limits, shows what Square Marketing automates and covers the email rules that apply in the US.

Prices change often, so this page lists limits Mailchimp, Klaviyo and Square published when checked on 31 October 2026, not subscription fees.

## Which Automations Should a Coffee Shop Build First?

Start with messages triggered by one customer action, because they run without weekly work.

1. **Welcome message.** Sent after a customer's first purchase or sign-up, it introduces the shop and gives a reason to come back.
2. **Win-back message.** Sent to people who have not visited for a set period, such as 60 days (an assumption, pick your own).
3. **Loyalty reminder.** Sent when a customer is close to a reward.

Square's help center confirms the building blocks: you can set up an ongoing automated email campaign instead of a one-time send, and campaigns about a loyalty promotion need a Square Loyalty subscription and a loyalty program. Square also resends a campaign to people who did not open it five days after the first send, and a coupon campaign is resent seven days before the coupon expires. The exact templates inside Square Marketing vary, so check your dashboard.

Everything else, such as seasonal drink launches, is a one-off campaign and does not need automation. Our [coffee shop email examples](/blog/coffee-shop-email-marketing-examples) show what to write in each message.

## What Do the Free Plans Actually Allow?

| Tool | Free plan limit | Automation on free plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mailchimp | 250 contacts; 500 sends a month or 250 a day | Marketing automation flows not included |
| Klaviyo | 250 active profiles; 500 emails a month; $5 of mobile messages a month | Campaigns and flows can be created |
| Square Marketing | Subscription needed (Plus or Premium) | Ongoing automated campaigns supported |

On Mailchimp's paid plans, the pricing page lists up to 200 flows on Standard and Premium, and up to four flow steps on Essentials. Mailchimp renamed Customer Journey Builder to Marketing Automation Flows in June 2025. Mailchimp's page splits its price digits when read by software, so confirm monthly fees on the live page. Klaviyo's page lists no paid email price.

Square's help page says an ongoing automated email campaign needs a Square Marketing subscription, Plus or Premium, and customer details in your Customer Directory.

## How Do You Check Whether a Free Plan Fits?

Work out sends before you choose. The numbers below are an **assumption** for a single-location shop.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Subscribers | 200 |
| Newsletter emails per month | 2 |
| Welcome emails per month (new subscribers) | 30 |
| Total sends | 200 x 2 + 30 = 430 |

430 sends fits under 500 a month on both free plans. At 300 subscribers and the same schedule you would send 630 and pass the cap, and Mailchimp's 250-contact limit would already be exceeded. For Mailchimp, the pay-as-you-grow decision comes at 250 contacts. For Klaviyo, it comes at 250 active profiles or 500 emails.

The free plans also differ on automation. A shop that wants a welcome flow on a free plan should compare Klaviyo's allowance with Mailchimp's, which excludes flows.

## What Do the Email Laws Require?

In the US, the FTC's CAN-SPAM guide lists eight requirements for commercial email. The key ones for a cafe:

- Headers and subject lines must be accurate.
- The message must say clearly that it is an advertisement.
- It must include a valid postal address.
- It must explain how to opt out, and you must honor an opt-out request within 10 business days.
- Hiring a company to send for you does not remove your responsibility.

The FTC says each separate email in violation can carry penalties of up to $53,088. Automation tools add the unsubscribe link, but the postal address and honest subject line are your job. Rules differ in the UK, Canada and Australia, so check local law if you serve those markets.

## Where Does Automation Go Wrong?

Three patterns cause most problems, and none needs a statistic to see.

- **No data connection.** If your point-of-sale and email tool do not share customer data, triggers never fire. Square users get this inside one system.
- **Too many messages.** Each automation adds sends. Count them against your plan limit and your customers' patience.
- **Nobody reads the replies.** An automated email that invites replies needs a person to answer.

## FAQ

### Can a coffee shop automate marketing for free?

Partly. Klaviyo's free plan allows campaigns and flows up to 250 active profiles and 500 emails a month. Mailchimp's free plan covers 250 contacts and 500 sends a month but does not include automation flows.

### What is the easiest automation to start with?

A welcome email after the first purchase. Once set up it runs without weekly work, and it reaches every new customer while the visit is fresh.

### Do I need Square Loyalty for loyalty emails?

For Square's loyalty promotion campaigns, yes. Square says you need a Square Loyalty subscription and a loyalty program set up. You can run a loyalty idea with a punch card instead; see our [coffee shop loyalty ideas without an app](/blog/coffee-shop-loyalty-program-ideas-without-an-app).

### How long do I have to honor an unsubscribe?

Under US law, within 10 business days, per the FTC. Your email tool normally handles this, but check that unsubscribes sync if you use two systems.
