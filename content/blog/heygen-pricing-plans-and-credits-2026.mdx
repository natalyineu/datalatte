## How Do HeyGen Credits Work?

HeyGen's FAQ says credits are "HeyGen's single currency for video and asset creation". The cost of a video "can vary based on which AI model is used, video duration, and the complexity of the generation". The page gives no credits-per-minute figure, so no per-video price is stated here.

Rollover rules on the pricing page: Creator credits roll over one month, Pro credits roll over, and for annual subscribers credits accumulate until renewal. Business adds top-up credits, one-time credit packs and auto-reload.

The Free plan has no stated credit amount. Its card says "3 videos a month, watermarked", and the FAQ says no credit card is required. It also includes limited trial access to premium features such as Avatar IV, Video Agent and lip-sync translation.

## What Is HeyGen and How Does It Work?

HeyGen is an AI video platform where a digital avatar reads your script. Its pricing page lists stock avatars, custom video avatars built from your own footage, photo avatars and translation into other languages. You type or paste a script, choose an avatar and voice, and generate a video.

A "Digital Twin" is the term HeyGen uses for a video avatar of a real person. Setting one up requires a consent video. For the voice side of AI, including phone-style assistants, see the [ElevenLabs voice AI guide](/blog/elevenlabs-voice-ai-local-business-customer-calls).

## What Are the Rules and Risks?

From HeyGen's terms of service:

- **Ownership on paid plans.** "As between HeyGen and you, you own all rights in your User Input or User Output", and output may be used for commercial purposes.
- **Free plan.** You own your input but receive "a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license" to your output. Free output "may not be sold, sublicensed, redistributed, monetized". That wording limits what a business can do with Free videos, so check the terms before posting one publicly.
- **Likeness and voice.** The terms prohibit "reproducing their likeness or voice, without the required permission". They also say "ownership of a recording alone does not establish permission to clone or use a person's voice."
- **Consent video.** HeyGen's help centre says a consent video under 30 seconds is required for every video-based Digital Twin, recorded by the person featured, at the same time as the avatar footage.
- **Impersonation.** Impersonating somebody else is prohibited.
- **Training.** The terms grant HeyGen a licence to use your content "to train or otherwise improve or modify our artificial intelligence and machine learning models". Do not upload private footage you would not want used that way.

The pages opened do not include a statement on advertising disclosure for AI-generated people. Check local advertising rules before running an avatar as a "spokesperson".

## FAQ

### How much does HeyGen cost per month?

Creator is $29 a month, or $24 billed annually. Pro starts at $49 a month with 1,000 credits and Business is $149 a month with 1,500 credits. Enterprise is custom.

### Is HeyGen free?

There is a Free plan with 3 videos a month, each up to 1 minute, with a watermark. Per the terms, Free output cannot be sold, sublicensed, redistributed or monetized.

### What are HeyGen credits?

Credits are HeyGen's single currency for video and assets. Cost depends on the AI model, video length and complexity, and the pricing page gives no per-minute figure.

### Can I use HeyGen videos commercially?

On paid plans the terms say you own your output and may use it for commercial purposes. On Free, they restrict selling and monetising. Likeness and voice need permission.

### Is HeyGen worth it for a small business?

It can be for short explainers if the avatar style suits the brand. Test the Free plan, then pay for one month of Creator ($29) and count how many videos your credits cover.
