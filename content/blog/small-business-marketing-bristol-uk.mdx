## How Big Is the Bristol Market for Small Businesses?

The ONS Census 2021 counts about 472,000 people in Bristol, up 10.3% from about 428,200 in 2011. England grew 6.6% in the same period. Bristol ranked 11th of 309 local authorities in England and is the most densely populated area in the South West.

Bristol City Council's Economic Strategy 2025 to 2035 says the business base grew by about 11% between 2015 and 2022, mostly through new micro-enterprises, which made up 87% of the base in 2022. The same document reports fewer new business starts in 2024 than in 2023. No Bristol business total for 2025 was found, so this guide does not quote one.

## How Should You Target Bristol Postcodes?

Google Ads allows radius targeting from a minimum of 1 km around a location. Google recommends "Presence or interest" as a Search best practice and says "Presence" fits when you only want people physically in the area. For a café or salon that people walk or drive to, try a radius of a few miles and test "Presence" if clicks come from places you cannot serve.

Add the area to your ad text, such as the neighbourhood or street. Review the Search terms report weekly and add negative keywords for jobs, courses and services you do not offer. Postcode-level claims such as "BS3 beats Bristol South" are not backed by any published test, so treat them as something to try, not a rule.

## What Should a Bristol Business Do on Google Business Profile?

Google says local results rely on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and higher ratings can improve local ranking. It also says you cannot pay for a better position. So the work is: verify the profile, keep address, hours, category and attributes accurate, add photos, and reply to reviews.

One common tip is wrong. Google's guidelines say there should be only one profile per business and you must not create more than one page for each location. A business with separate service areas and separate staff at each location can have one profile each. A mobile groomer covering BS3, BS4 and BS5 gets one service-area profile, not three. ## Does Meta Advertising Work for Bristol Businesses?

It can reach most local people. DataReportal reports Facebook ad reach of 38.8 million, Instagram 35.5 million and TikTok 26.8 million adults in the UK in late 2025. Ad reach is not the same as active users, and these are national numbers, not Bristol ones.

No public source gives a Bristol Meta cost per thousand impressions or return on ad spend, so this guide gives none. A sensible first test is a small retargeting audience of people who visited your website or booking page, with one clear offer. Judge it by bookings, not by clicks.

## What Are the UK Email Marketing Rules?

The ICO says you generally need prior permission before emailing individuals. The soft opt-in lets you email past customers who bought or discussed buying something similar, if you offered a simple way to opt out when you collected their details and offer it in every message. It does not cover bought-in lists.

Every marketing email needs an unsubscribe option, and you must act on requests promptly. Collect the opt-in at booking or at the till and keep a record. For ideas that work for appointment businesses, see [email and SMS marketing for UK salons](/blog/email-sms-marketing-uk-salons).

## Which Mistakes Waste Bristol Budget?

- Targeting all of the UK instead of a radius around the shop.
- Leaving reviews unanswered, when Google lists replies as part of how you stand out.
- Running ads with no call or booking tracking, so you cannot tell which clicks produced customers.
- Emailing people who never opted in.

Google says call asset clicks cost the same as headline clicks, and you set the minimum call length that counts as a conversion. Choose a length that matches a real enquiry.

## FAQ

### How much should a Bristol small business spend on Google Ads?

There is no standard figure. A £300 to £500 monthly test buys roughly 100 to 330 clicks at £1.50 to £3.00 per click (assumption range). Track calls and bookings for 60 days before scaling.

### Is Google Business Profile or Google Ads better for a Bristol café?

Start with the free profile because it supports every other channel. Add ads once hours, photos and reviews are in place.

### Can I create one Google profile per Bristol postcode?

No, not for the same business. Google's guidelines say one profile per business, with a service-area profile for businesses that travel to customers.

### Do I need consent to email Bristol customers?

Generally yes. The soft opt-in covers past customers of similar products if they had a simple way to opt out. Check the ICO guidance for your case.

### Which Bristol events are worth a campaign?

Pick the ones your customers attend, such as the Great Bristol Run for sport and health businesses. Confirm 2027 dates with the organisers first.
