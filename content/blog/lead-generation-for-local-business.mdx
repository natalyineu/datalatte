A local business gets leads from four main sources: its Google Business Profile, Google Search ads, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) lead ads, and customer reviews and referrals. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put the average cost per lead at $66.69 on Google Search and $27.39 on Meta lead campaigns. The two are not equal: a Meta form fill is usually a weaker signal than a search call.

This guide compares the channels, shows what each costs, and gives a way to test them with a small budget. Worked figures are labelled as assumptions.

## Which Lead Channel Fits a Local Business?

Match the channel to how the customer decides. Someone searching "plumber near me" is ready to call. Someone scrolling Instagram is not looking yet.

| Channel | Intent | You pay | Main limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | High | Nothing | You cannot buy a better rank |
| Google Search ads | High | Per click | Click prices vary a lot by industry |
| Meta lead ads | Lower | Per click or impression | Form fills may not answer the phone |
| Reviews and referrals | Trust building | Your time | Slow, steady |

Google's help says local results rely mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Start with the free profile, then add paid channels where it stops.

## What Do Leads Cost on Google Search Ads?

LocaliQ's 2026 all-industry averages for Google Search ads are $5.42 per click, a 6.64% click-through rate, an 8.18% conversion rate and $66.69 per lead. Industries differ widely. Home and Home Improvement shows $8.33 per click and $90.92 per lead. Business Services shows $5.87 and $93.69. Real Estate shows $3.22 and $102.51.

LocaliQ's page has no category for coffee shops, salons, pet groomers or fitness studios, so those rows are not published here. Use your own account after the first month as the benchmark. Our [budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) explains how to split a small monthly amount.

## What Do Leads Cost on Meta Lead Ads?

LocaliQ's 2026 Meta benchmarks show lead campaigns averaging a 2.70% click-through rate, $1.80 per click, 8.54% conversion and $27.39 per lead. By industry, lead campaigns show $42.95 per lead for Home and Home Improvement, $27.11 for Health and Fitness, $50.91 for Beauty and Personal Care and $13.74 for Real Estate.

One note on the source: its narrative text on the highest lead-campaign click prices does not match its own table, so only the table rows above are used here. Meta's developer documentation says lead ads are optimized for lead volume by default. A "higher intent" form adds a step where the person reviews and confirms their answers before submitting.

## Does a Cheaper Lead Mean a Better Lead?

No. Compare cost per customer, not cost per lead. A lead from a short form on a feed is cheap to get and easy to ignore. A call from a search ad costs more and is more likely to book.

Measure each channel at the same two points: how many leads you contacted, and how many became paying customers. That is how a $27 lead can end up costing more per customer than a $67 lead. The worked example below shows the arithmetic.

## Worked Example: Two Channels, Same Budget

All inputs are **assumptions** to show the method, based on the benchmark averages above.

| | Google Search | Meta lead ads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $600 | $600 |
| Cost per lead (benchmark) | $66.69 | $27.39 |
| Leads | about 9 | about 22 |
| Leads who become customers (assumption) | 40% | 10% |
| Customers | about 3.6 | about 2.2 |
| Cost per customer | about $167 | about $273 |

If your close rates are different, the answer flips. At 25% on Meta and 40% on Google, Meta wins at about $110 per customer. Run both for 60 days, record the close rate honestly, and move budget to the cheaper customer.

## How Do You Get Leads Without Ad Spend?

Three free routes work for most local businesses. First, finish your Google Business Profile: hours, categories, photos and services. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers it. Second, ask for reviews. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults found 97% read online reviews, 68% require at least 4 stars and 74% only consider reviews from the last three months.

Third, give people a reason to leave contact details. [Lead magnet ideas for local businesses](/blog/lead-magnet-ideas-for-local-business) lists options, and [how to get more phone calls from your website](/blog/how-to-get-more-phone-calls-from-your-website) covers the site side. Trades such as roofing have their own channel mix, shown in [lead generation for roofers](/blog/lead-generation-for-roofers).

## How Should You Handle a Lead Once It Arrives?

Answer fast and track the source. Google's Local Services help says that if you regularly fail to answer calls or respond to messages, your ad ranking may be affected, which shows how much responsiveness counts on that platform. No source here gives a response-time target, so set your own and check it weekly.

Put the source in a sheet next to each lead: channel, date, contacted yes or no, result. Send ad clicks to a focused page, as in our [landing page best practices](/blog/landing-page-best-practices).

## FAQ

### How much does lead generation cost for a small local business?

It depends on the channel and the industry. LocaliQ 2026 averages are $66.69 per lead on Google Search and $27.39 on Meta lead campaigns. Both are all-industry averages, so your own cost may be far higher or lower.

### Which channel should I start with?

Start with a complete Google Business Profile because it costs nothing. Then add Google Search ads if people in your area already search for your service. Add Meta lead ads when you want to reach people who are not searching yet.

### Are Meta lead forms worth it?

They can be, if you follow up quickly and track who becomes a customer. Meta's documentation says forms are optimized for volume by default, with a higher intent option that adds a confirmation step.

### How many leads should I expect for $500 a month?

It depends on your industry and click prices. At the all-industry Google average of $66.69 per lead, $500 buys about 7 leads. At the Meta lead average of $27.39 it buys about 18, but quality differs.

### How long until I know if a channel works?

No source gives a fixed period. Track at least a full buying cycle for your business, and compare cost per customer, not just cost per lead.
