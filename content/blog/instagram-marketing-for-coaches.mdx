A coach wins clients on Instagram by showing a repeatable process, using short reels to reach new people, and sending viewers to one booking step. Client transformations can be posted, but the FTC says an ad showing unusually good results needs proof that they are typical, or a clear statement of what results people generally get. Fake or bought testimonials are banned outright.

This guide is for life, business, health and fitness coaches who sell to clients in their area or online. It uses Instagram's own guidance and the FTC's rules. No source publishes average conversion rates for coaches on Instagram, so the example is built from labelled assumptions.

## What Should a Coach Post on Instagram?

Post material that shows how you think and how a session works, not only outcomes. Four formats cover most needs:

- Short reels answering one client question
- Carousels (up to 20 items) that teach one framework step by step
- Stories with a poll or question box
- Posts explaining how your programme works and who it suits

Instagram's creator FAQ recommends videos of 3 minutes or less to unconnected audiences, so short reels have the best chance of reaching non-followers. It also asks for original content, so avoid reposting clips with other apps' watermarks. ## Can You Post Client Transformations?

Yes, with limits. The FTC's endorsement guides say endorsements must reflect the endorser's real experience. If an ad features exceptional results and the advertiser lacks proof that they represent what people generally achieve, the ad must make clear what the generally expected results are.

Payment or something of value to the endorser, such as a free session, usually counts as a connection that must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously. Put the disclosure in the post itself, not only in a comment. Our [coach email marketing guide](/blog/email-marketing-for-coaches) covers the email side of the funnel.

## What Does the FTC Say About Reviews?

The FTC's Consumer Reviews and Testimonials rule took effect on October 21, 2024. It prohibits writing, buying or selling fake reviews or testimonials, offering compensation tied to a review with a particular sentiment, and using intimidation or unfounded legal threats to suppress reviews.

Offering an incentive without requiring a specific sentiment is not prohibited by the rule, but platforms such as Google have their own bans. The FTC page does not give a per-violation dollar amount, so none is quoted. Ask every client for an honest review, and never script one.

## How Do You Move Followers to a Booked Call?

Give each post one next step. Common options are a link in your bio to a booking page, a keyword to message you, or a free resource in exchange for an email. Keep the profile bio short: who you help, the result you aim for, and the link.

Test the path on a phone. If it takes more than three taps from the profile to a booking page, shorten it. ## How Do You Track What Works?

Use a separate link for each channel so you can tell where bookings come from. Add UTM tags to the bio link, for example utm_source=instagram, utm_medium=social, utm_campaign=bio. Google says to always set the source, medium and campaign values, and that they are case-sensitive.

Then count three numbers weekly: profile visits, link clicks and booked calls. Instagram Insights shows the first two for professional accounts. Your booking tool shows the third.

## FAQ

### Can a coach post before-and-after results on Instagram?

Yes, if they reflect real client experience. Where results are above average, the FTC says the ad must make clear what results are generally expected unless you can prove they are typical.

### Do I have to disclose a free session given to a client for a testimonial?

Yes. The FTC's guides say payment or anything of value usually counts as a material connection that should be disclosed clearly.

### Is it legal to buy Instagram testimonials?

No. The FTC rule prohibits writing, buying or selling fake or false consumer reviews and testimonials.

### How long should my reels be?

Instagram recommends videos of 3 minutes or less to reach people who do not follow you.

### How do I know if Instagram is bringing clients?

Use a tagged bio link, track link clicks and booked calls, and ask every new client how they found you.
