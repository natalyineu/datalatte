Facebook reaches about 1.40 million people in Botswana's ad tools, while Instagram reaches 244 thousand, so a small Botswana business should start on Facebook and Google, not Instagram. DataReportal counts 2.57 million residents and 2.09 million internet users (81.4%) in October 2025.

This guide is for salons, cafes, studios, groomers and service firms in Botswana. ## Who Are You Marketing To in Botswana?

Botswana had 2.57 million people in October 2025 (UN data), up 1.6% over the year. 74.2% live in urban areas and the median age is 23.4. Cellular connections number 4.22 million, 164% of the population, though some may be voice or SMS only.

Internet users number 2.09 million (81.4%), so 479 thousand people (18.6%) are offline. Social media identities total 1.40 million, 54.4% of the population. A young, mostly urban, phone-first audience suggests testing mobile-friendly booking links before anything else.

## Which Platforms Reach People in Botswana?

Facebook's 1.40 million equals the total social media identities figure, and is about 5.7 times Instagram's 244 thousand. Facebook is where the reach is, with Messenger as the chat route (304 thousand). Instagram is growing at a similar 16.8% pace but from a much smaller base, so treat it as a later test, not the first spend. X fell 47.7%, which DataReportal flags as possibly a platform correction.

TikTok, Snapchat and YouTube are not in DataReportal's Botswana article, so no reach figures are given for them. WhatsApp and local search platforms are also not covered here. Check each platform's own ad tool before planning spend.

## How Should Google Ads Language and Location Work in Botswana?

Google's Ads Help says that from September 2026 the campaign-level language setting is being removed for Search campaigns, and Search ads will match on the language of the ads themselves. For searches with a clear language, Google prioritises ads in that language. If you serve customers in more than one language, write separate ad groups for each language you use and send people to a landing page in the same language as the ad.

For location, Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km and recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting as best practice for Search, while presence-only fits when you want to avoid people outside your target area. Set a radius around your premises, then read the report to see which areas convert.

## How Should You Split a Small Budget?

Because Facebook dominates reach, put social spend there first. Add Google Search for people who are already looking, such as a service plus your town. Keep Instagram as a small test, perhaps 10% of paid spend (a suggestion, not a platform rule), and compare the cost per booked appointment after four to six weeks.

## What Does a Test Budget Return? The Math

These rows are **assumptions** for illustration, in US dollars. They are not Botswana benchmarks. Replace each with your own data after a month.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget (assumption) | $300 | $300 | $300 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | $0.60 | $0.35 | $0.25 |
| Clicks | 500 | 857 | 1,200 |
| Click to enquiry rate (assumption) | 3% | 5% | 7% |
| Enquiries | 15 | 43 | 84 |
| Enquiry to booking rate (assumption) | 30% | 40% | 50% |
| Bookings | 4.5 | 17.1 | 42 |
| Ad cost per booking | $67 | $17 | $7 |
| First-visit value (assumption) | $15 | $15 | $15 |
| Revenue minus ad spend | -$232 | -$43 | +$330 |

Counts use unrounded values. Revenue is not profit: staff, product and rent come out of it. A first visit often does not pay back by itself, which is why repeat visits matter.

Your break-even click price is the first-visit value times the enquiry rate times the booking rate. At $15, 5% and 40%, that is 15 x 0.05 x 0.40 = $0.30 per click. If your clicks cost more on average, the first visit loses money and you need repeat bookings to justify it. The biggest lever is usually the booking rate: answer messages and calls within minutes.

<Callout type="tip" title="Do This Today">
Write down your first-visit value, then divide by 50 to get a rough break-even click price (5% enquiry rate times 40% booking rate). Set up a Facebook Page with a message button and a mobile booking link.
</Callout>

## FAQ

### Is Facebook or Instagram better in Botswana?
By reach in ad tools, Facebook. DataReportal lists 1.40 million for Facebook and 244 thousand for Instagram in late 2025.

### How many people in Botswana are online?
DataReportal puts internet users at 2.09 million, 81.4% of the population, in October 2025. Figures may lag current use.

### What do ads cost in Botswana?
No Botswana click prices were found in the sources checked. Run a small test and use your own cost per booking.

### Does DataReportal count active users?
No. It says ad reach is not monthly active users and may not be unique people.
