A small business in Nicaragua should start with Facebook and TikTok, treat Instagram as a smaller, younger extra, and use YouTube for demonstration videos. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report gives Facebook an ad reach of 4.10 million, TikTok 4.21 million adults, YouTube 3.17 million and Instagram 1.40 million, in a country of about 7 million people.

This guide uses published audience and macro data only. No Nicaraguan ad-price data (CPC or CPM) was available from a source that could be opened, so there are no price benchmarks. Sanctions and payment questions are marked unverified where no official page was read.

## How Many Nicaraguans Are Online?

DataReportal counts 4.89 million internet users, 69.6% of the population, which leaves 2.14 million offline (30.4%). Internet users grew by 443,000 (9.9%) between October 2024 and October 2025. Social media user identities total 4.21 million, or 59.9%.

The population is young, with a median age of 26, and 60.6% live in urban areas. There are 8.89 million mobile connections, 126% of the population, because many people hold more than one SIM.

World Bank data for 2025 lists GDP at US$22.24 billion, GDP per capita at US$3,173.3 and growth of 4.9%. Spending power per head is much lower than in most markets this site covers, so ad budgets and prices should be set on local results.

## Can You Pay for Google Ads in Nicaragua?

Google lists Nicaragua in the Americas table of its Ads terms page, with Spanish and English as the listed languages. Google's country restriction page names only six sanctioned locations where Google Ads is unavailable, and Nicaragua is not on that list.

One payment detail is verified. Google's billing page includes Nicaragua among the countries where prepay is not available, so an account there pays after the fact, by postpay. Check which card or bank options your account offers before launching.

Whether Meta Ads accepts local payment methods in Nicaragua, and whether any US sanctions rules affect a given advertiser, is unverified here. Read the payment page in your own account and ask your bank.

## What Does the Córdoba Exchange Rate Mean for Ads?

The Central Bank of Nicaragua announced that the córdoba's rate of slide against the US dollar stays at 0% a year in 2026, which sets the official rate at 36.6243 córdobas per dollar for the whole year. Foreign-currency ad billing therefore does not move with a crawling peg this year.

Ad platforms may still convert at their own rates or add taxes and bank fees. Compare your invoice with the official rate before you set a córdoba budget.

## What Do the Data Protection Rules Say?

Nicaragua has a personal data protection law, Law 787, and a regulation, Decree 36-2012. The regulation creates the DIPRODAP office and defines the aviso informativo, a document the data controller gives the person before processing, and says consent must be free, among other traits. The decree text opened here does not mention marketing, so rules for promotional messages are unverified. Ask a local lawyer before building an email or WhatsApp list.

## How Should a Small Budget Be Split?

**Worked example (all inputs are assumptions).** A bakery in Granada has 100 US dollars a month for ads. Assumption: 60% to Facebook and Messenger for a daily-order campaign within 5 km, 30% to short TikTok videos of the oven and the counter, 10% to boosting a Google Business Profile post. After four weeks, compare cost per message or visit in your own reports and move money to the winner. The 100 dollars and the split are placeholders, not benchmarks.

## FAQ

### Is Facebook still worth it in Nicaragua?

Yes. Facebook has a reach of 4.10 million, 58.3% of the population, and grew 10.8% in a year. Messenger adds 1.95 million.

### Should a Nicaraguan business use Instagram?

Its reach is 1.40 million, 19.9% of the population. It is useful for visual products, but it is smaller than Facebook, TikTok and YouTube.

### What does advertising cost in Nicaragua?

No verified public benchmark is available. Start with a small budget and read the real cost per result in your ad account.

### Is the córdoba stable against the dollar?

The central bank set the 2026 slide at 0%, so the official rate is 36.6243 per dollar for the year. Market conditions and fees can differ from the official rate.
