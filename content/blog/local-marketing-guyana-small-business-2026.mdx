## How Big Is the Guyanese Online Market?

DataReportal counts 837 thousand people, 684 thousand internet users (81.7%) and 706 thousand mobile connections (84.3%). Social media user identities total 577 thousand, 69.0%, identical to Facebook reach because the method may reflect only the largest platform.

For other Caribbean and South American markets, see our guides to [the Bahamas](/blog/local-marketing-bahamas-small-business-2026) and [Colombia](/blog/local-marketing-colombia-small-business-2026).

## Which Platforms Reach Guyanese People?

| Platform | Ad reach | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 577 thousand | 69.0% |
| Instagram | 193 thousand | 23.0% |
| LinkedIn (members) | 190 thousand | 22.7% |

DataReportal does not list TikTok for Guyana. Facebook is far ahead, so a local shop, salon or restaurant reaches most of its market there. Meta says audience size is an estimate, not active users.

## Is the Guyanese Economy Growing?

The IMF's May 2025 Article IV press release says real GDP grew 43.6% in 2024, driven by oil, and real non-oil GDP expanded over 13%. It projected 2025 real non-oil GDP growth of 12.9% and a medium-term average of 6.75% a year. These are IMF figures and projections, not guarantees.

For a small business, a growing non-oil economy suggests rising spending in construction-linked and consumer services. Test demand rather than assuming it, and watch your own booking numbers.

## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Guyana?

Google says local ranking uses relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Reviews and ratings can help prominence. Fill every field, add photos, and ask satisfied customers for reviews. ## Worked Example: A Salon Facebook Test

All numbers are **assumptions**, in US dollars.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget, 30 days | $90 |
| New clients | 9 |
| Cost per new client | $10 |
| Profit per first visit | $14 |

At $10 per client against $14 profit, the first visit pays back. If only 4 clients arrive, cost per client is $22.50 and the first visit loses $8.50, so retention decides the result. Ask every new client how they found you.
