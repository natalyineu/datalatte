A coffee shop should make short phone videos of the drink being made, the space and the people, and post them where its customers already are. Pew Research's 2026 survey of 5,511 US adults found 84% use YouTube, 51% Instagram and 31% TikTok, with Instagram at 79% and TikTok at 65% among adults aged 18 to 29. Start with one video a week on Instagram, and add the same clip to your Google Business Profile.

This guide covers where video fits, what to film, what a paid boost costs, and a worked example labelled as an assumption. Sources are listed at the end.

## Which Platforms Should a Coffee Shop Use?

It depends on who you want in the door. Pew's 2026 fact sheet gives usage by age. Among 18 to 29s: YouTube 95%, Instagram 79%, TikTok 65%, Facebook 69%. Among 30 to 49s: YouTube 91%, Facebook 78%, Instagram 64%, TikTok 45%.

For a neighbourhood café with students and young workers, Instagram and TikTok reach the right people. If your regulars are 30 to 49, Facebook and Instagram cover more of them than TikTok. One clip can go on all three, so you do not need a separate production for each. These figures show reach on the platform, not how many people nearby will see your video.

## What Should You Film?

Real moments beat polished ones, and a phone is enough. Five ideas that fit a counter:

- A drink being made from start to finish, one take, no talking
- The new seasonal item with the price on screen
- The morning rush, or a quiet moment before opening
- A staff member showing how they make one drink
- A regular's order, with their permission

Keep each clip short, put the hook in the first seconds, and add captions because many people watch muted. No verified platform benchmark for ideal length was found for ideal length, so test yours. ## Can You Put Video on Google?

Yes. Google Business Profile accepts videos up to 30 seconds, up to 75 MB and at least 720p resolution, per Google's Business Profile Help. A short clip of the shop and a pour makes the profile more informative to someone deciding where to go. Video is only one part of your profile, so follow the [Google Business Profile optimization checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) too.

## What Does It Cost to Boost a Video?

LocaliQ's 2026 Facebook benchmarks (updated September 23, 2026) list Restaurants and Food traffic campaigns at $0.45 per click and a 2.68% click-through rate. Coffee shops are not a separate row, so treat this as a guide. A click is not a visit. For the full picture see [Instagram marketing for coffee shops](/blog/coffee-shop-instagram-marketing-strategy), and note that organic posts cost only time.

## Is It Worth It? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for one café. They are not benchmarks.

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Videos per month | 8 |
| Hours per video (film, caption, post) | 1.5 |
| Total hours | 12 |
| Value of the owner's or staff time | $20 per hour |
| Monthly cost of time | $240 |
| Margin per extra visit | $4 |
| Extra visits needed to break even | 60 ($240 / $4) |
| Per day (30 days) | 2 extra visits |

On these assumptions, video pays if it brings two extra visits a day. That is plausible for a busy street, and unlikely for a quiet one. If you cut the effort to 45 minutes per video, break-even falls to 30 visits. Track it with a "saw you on Instagram" question or a reel-only discount code.

## How Do You Measure Results?

Use simple counts. Watch views and saves in each platform's insights, and the clicks or calls on your Google profile. Ask new customers how they found you. Compare weeks with video to weeks without. No reliable public benchmark exists that we could verify for what a coffee shop video should earn, so compare against your own previous posts.

## FAQ

### How often should a coffee shop post video?

Choose a number you can keep up. Once a week is realistic for most owners. No source we opened proves an ideal frequency.

### Do I need professional equipment?

No. A phone, a steady surface and good natural light are enough. Clean the lens and film vertically.

### Which platform is best?

By Pew's data, YouTube has the widest reach, but Instagram and TikTok are stronger among 18 to 29s. Post the same clip on more than one.

### Can I post the same video everywhere?

Yes, and it saves time. Pew's data shows your customers spread across several platforms. Re-export without another app's watermark and adjust the caption for each.

### Should I pay to boost videos?

Only after an organic post performs. LocaliQ lists Restaurants and Food traffic clicks at $0.45 on Facebook, but that is a click, not a customer.
