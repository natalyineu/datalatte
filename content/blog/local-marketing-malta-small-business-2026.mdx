A Maltese small business should combine a complete Google Business Profile, Facebook and Instagram ads aimed at the islands, and, if it serves visitors, English-language search ads for tourists. Facebook reaches 68.7% of Malta's population through advertising and Instagram 50.2%, according to DataReportal. Malta also received 4,022,310 inbound tourists in 2025, about 7 arrivals for each of its 547,000 residents.

Ad prices for Malta are not published by Meta, Google or the national statistics office, so this guide gives no CPC figures. Use your own account for estimates.

## How Big Is the Maltese Market?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report for Malta counts 547 thousand people and 511 thousand internet users (93.5% of the population). Social media user identities total 375 thousand (68.7%), down 4.9% from the year before. Mobile connections number 778 thousand, or 142% of the population.

The market is small, so a local campaign has a limited audience. A salon in Sliema and a salon in Gozo draw on different, small catchments. That makes frequency and relevance more important than reach. ## Which Platforms Reach People in Malta?

All figures are advertising audience reach from DataReportal (late 2025), not monthly active users.

| Platform | Ad reach in Malta | Share | Change vs 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 375 thousand | 68.7% of population | down 4.9% |
| Instagram | 274 thousand | 50.2% of population | up 6.5% |
| TikTok (18+) | 263 thousand | 56.8% of adults | up 13.9% |

DataReportal cautions that platform corrections can distort year-on-year changes. Facebook is still the widest paid reach, while Instagram and TikTok are growing. Match the channel to the customer: Facebook for community and events, Instagram for visual services, TikTok for younger adults.

## How Much Do Tourists Matter?

Business Now, citing the National Statistics Office (NSO), reports 4,022,310 inbound tourists in 2025, up 12.9% from 3,563,618 in 2024. Tourist spending was €3.9 billion, up 18.6%, and the average stay was 6.3 nights. The United Kingdom was the largest market with 841,397 tourists.

This is a secondary source, because the NSO page itself returned an access error when checked. The direction is clear: for cafes, restaurants, boat tours and shops in tourist areas, visitors can outnumber residents in peak months. Search ads in English with place names target them while they decide where to go.

## Which Language Should You Advertise In?

English is widely used in Maltese business, so many local campaigns run in English. Visitors from Italy and other markets may search in their own language, so test a second language only if your bookings show demand. This guide does not give a language-use percentage because no source was checked for one.

Keep names and addresses consistent in English and Maltese place spelling across your Business Profile, website and ads.

## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Malta?

Google says local ranking uses relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Verify the profile, complete the categories, add photos, and reply to every review. For businesses with visitors, reviews in English matter because tourists read them before booking.

Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists the fields to complete. Add opening hours for public holidays; check the government's list of public holidays, which was not available to verify here.

## What Does a Starter Budget Look Like?

The split below is an **assumption** for a single-location business, not a benchmark. Test it for 60 days.

| Item | Monthly EUR (assumption) |
| --- | --- |
| Google Search ads, small area | 300 |
| Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram) | 250 |
| Photos and short video | 150 |
| Total | 700 |

Now a break-even check, also with assumed numbers. A salon with an average ticket of €50 and a 60% gross margin makes €30 per visit. If €250 on Meta ads brings 9 new clients, cost per new client is about €28, which is below one visit of profit. If it brings 3, the cost is about €83 and the first visit loses money. Track new clients by asking how they heard about you. For budget planning, see our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide).

## Should a Maltese Business Use TikTok?

DataReportal shows 263 thousand TikTok users aged 18 and over, up 13.9%, which is 56.8% of adults. Short video suits restaurants, salons and gyms. ## FAQ

### How many people are on Facebook in Malta?

DataReportal reports Facebook ad reach of 375 thousand in late 2025, which is 68.7% of the population.

### Do tourists matter for Maltese small businesses?

NSO figures reported by Business Now show 4,022,310 inbound tourists in 2025, so tourist demand is large relative to a 547 thousand population.

### Should I advertise in English or Maltese?

English is widely used for business, but no verified language-use figure is given here. Test a second language only if data supports it.

### How much do Facebook ads cost in Malta?

No official figure is published. Run a small test and read the cost per result in Ads Manager.

### Do I need a Google Business Profile?

Yes. It is the free way to appear on Google Maps for local searches, and Google says you cannot pay for a better local ranking.
