## What Does the Malta Market Look Like Right Now?

A secondary Instagram post summarising Malta's data says GDP grew 6.4% year on year in Q4 2025, and unemployment was 3.4% in the same quarter. It also says Malta International Airport reported connections to 109 destinations in 2025, with a new direct New York route running June to October 2026. These figures describe the wider economy. They do not forecast demand for any single sector, and the originals should be checked before they appear in a client report.

For a small business, the practical point is that both residents and visitors can be customers. Malta has a small population, so a local campaign reaches a limited number of people. Frequency and relevance usually matter more than total reach. A salon in one town and a restaurant in another draw on different catchments, so each location needs its own targeting and its own budget line.

## Which Channels Should a Maltese Business Start With?

Start with the channel that matches the customer's decision. Google is where people search for a service and a place at the same time. Facebook suits community and event audiences. Instagram suits services where the result is visual, such as food, hair or retail displays. The table below is a starting point, not a benchmark.

| Channel | Best for | Role in the plan | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | Local searches and Maps | Free base, always on | Incomplete or unanswered reviews |
| Google search ads | Visitors and urgent needs | Paid, tied to place names | Broad keywords waste budget |
| Facebook ads | Community, events, offers | Paid, local radius | Weak creative |
| Instagram ads | Visual services, food, retail | Paid, with Reels or photos | Low-quality photos |

Choose one paid channel first and run it for 60 days before adding another. Splitting a small budget across four channels makes results hard to read. A single channel with clean tracking gives a clearer answer than four channels with mixed data.

## Should You Advertise to Visitors in English?

The same secondary Instagram post describes English as an official language alongside Maltese. That makes English-language ads and listings a sensible default for a business that serves visitors, although the post gives no usage percentage. Use English for search ads, the Business Profile, and menu or price pages.

Test a second language only if bookings or enquiries show demand for it. The approach in [the Australia guide](/blog/local-marketing-australia-small-business-2026) for English-speaking markets, where ad copy is written once and checked against local place names, also works for Malta. Keep place names and addresses spelled the same way across every channel.

## How Do You Build a Profile That Ranks on Google Maps in Malta?

Complete every field in the Google Business Profile before running ads. Verify the listing, choose the correct primary category, add opening hours including public holidays, and upload current photos. Reply to every review, in English where customers write in English.

Keep the business name, address and phone number identical across the profile, the website and the ads. Small differences in spelling or formatting make it harder for Google to match the listing to the business. Use the Google Business Profile help page cited below for the current ranking factors.

## Is Branding Needed Before You Run Ads?

Keen, a Malta branding agency, says many marketers believe a strong brand can take between 2 and 5 years to establish fully. Keen also says the initial branding process may take 2 to 4 months. These are vendor claims, not measured outcomes, but they show that a logo alone does not create recognition.

For most small businesses, a clear name, a consistent logo and a finished profile are enough to start. Keep the first ads simple and refine the brand as results arrive. Brand work and paid campaigns can run side by side, but paid results should be judged on cost per new client, not on brand impressions.

## What Does a Starter Budget Look Like?

The figures below are assumptions for a single-location business, not benchmarks. Test them for 60 days and replace them with real numbers from the ad accounts.

| Item | Monthly EUR (assumption) |
| --- | --- |
| Google search ads, small area | 300 |
| Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram) | 250 |
| Photos and short video | 150 |
| Total | 700 |

The break-even check uses assumed numbers too. Assume a salon has an average ticket of €50 and a 60% gross margin, so each visit earns €30 in margin. If €250 of Meta ads brings 9 new clients, the cost per new client is about €27.78, below the €30 margin from one visit. If it brings 3 clients, the cost is about €83.33, and the first visit loses money. Ask every new client how they heard about the business, and record the answers in a spreadsheet alongside spend.

## Can Messaging Help Convert Local Enquiries?

Many local enquiries arrive as messages after an ad or a Maps search, so reply speed affects bookings. [The Brazil guide on WhatsApp and Meta](/blog/local-marketing-brazil-small-business-whatsapp-meta) covers how messaging fits into paid campaigns in a different market. The same logic applies in Malta: connect the ad to a channel where the customer can ask a question and book quickly.

Set a simple rule for who answers messages and how fast. A reply within a few hours is better than a perfect reply the next day. Each reply should include a clear next step, such as a booking link or a call-back time. For broader budget planning across European markets, the [Belgium guide](/blog/local-marketing-belgium-small-business-2026) shows a similar channel-first approach.

## FAQ

### How many people use Facebook in Malta?

No verified reach figure is given here. Check the audience estimate inside Meta Ads Manager for the chosen radius, and record the date of the check.

### Do tourists matter for Maltese small businesses?

For some sectors, yes. A secondary Instagram summary reports airport connections to 109 destinations in 2025, which points to a steady flow of visitors, but no visitor count has been verified for this guide.

### Should I advertise in English or Maltese?

English is an official language in Malta, so it is a sensible default. Test Maltese only if bookings or enquiries show demand for it.

### Do I need a Google Business Profile?

Yes. It is the free listing that makes a business appear in local Google searches and on Maps. Complete it before spending on ads.

### How do I know if my Malta budget is working?

Track cost per new client, not just clicks. Use the break-even check above with your own average ticket and margin, and review the numbers after 60 days.
