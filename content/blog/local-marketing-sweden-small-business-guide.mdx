## Which Platforms Reach Swedish Customers?

DataReportal lists 8.58 million social media user identities, 80.4% of the population. Facebook ad reach is 5.65 million (53.0% of the population), Instagram 5.55 million (52.0%) and Snapchat 4.81 million (45.1%). TikTok's tools show 3.51 million adults, 41.2% of those aged 18 and over.

Ad reach is an estimate from platform tools, not a count of monthly users, and one person can hold several accounts. Use the numbers to compare channels. Facebook and Instagram show the widest reach in this table. The TikTok figure counts adults only, so it is not directly comparable. ## How Do You Get Found Locally in Stockholm, Gothenburg or Malmö?

Start with Google. Its help says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that you cannot pay for a better local ranking. Complete your Business Profile with opening hours, categories, photos and a Swedish-language description, then answer every review.

Next, add a small Google Search campaign with a radius around the shop. Google's radius targeting requires at least 1 km, which suits a city district. ## When Do You Need Consent to Email or SMS Customers?

Under section 19 of the Swedish Marketing Act (marknadsföringslagen 2008:486), marketing to a natural person by email or automatic calling systems requires that the person has consented in advance. There is an exception for email: you may skip consent if you obtained the address when selling a product to that person, the person has not objected, and the marketing concerns your own similar products. You must also give a free and easy way to object when collecting the address and in every later message.

Section 9 requires that marketing is clearly presented as marketing and that it is clear who is responsible. This is a summary of the law, not legal advice.

## Can You Use Sustainability Claims in Swedish Marketing?

Yes, but carefully. CMS, a law firm, reports that the Swedish Consumer Agency treats environmental claims as a priority. Vague words such as "miljövänlig", "grön" or "hållbar" must be explained next to the claim, and a link or QR code alone is not enough. Without an adequate explanation, the advertiser may need to prove every reasonable interpretation.

Evidence must relate to the specific product, and calculations should rely on generally recognised models. Two court cases cited by CMS found climate claims misleading for lacking such evidence. If you cannot prove "klimatsmart" or "hållbar", do not use it. Say something checkable instead, such as "coffee from [named supplier]" or "packaging made from [named material]".

## What Does a Mailing List Check Look Like? A Worked Example

The numbers are **assumptions**. A Malmö shop has 500 email addresses and wants to send an offer.

| Group | Size | May you send marketing? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opted in with clear consent | 300 | Yes, with a way to object |
| Gave address when buying, no objection | 150 | Yes, for your own similar products, with a way to object |
| Bought list or copied from a website | 50 | No, consent is missing |

After the check, 450 of 500 addresses are usable for the offer. Remove the other 50 and record the source and date of every consent. The exception applies to email, so do not assume it covers SMS.

## FAQ

### How many Swedes use Instagram?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report shows an Instagram ad reach of 5.55 million, equal to 52.0% of the population. That is an ad-tool estimate.

### Is TikTok worth it in Sweden?

TikTok's tools show 3.51 million adults. That is smaller than Facebook or Instagram, so test with a small budget.

### Do I need consent to email Swedish customers?

Generally yes for individuals, with an exception for existing customers under conditions.

### Can I say my business is sustainable?

Only with evidence next to the claim. Vague terms need explanation.

### What do Swedish ads cost?

No average is published in the sources checked. Run a test and measure cost per enquiry.
