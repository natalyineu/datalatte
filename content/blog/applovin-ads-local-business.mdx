AppLovin ads can reach local customers, but they are an experimental channel rather than a first choice. AppLovin sells in-app advertising, meaning ads that appear inside mobile games and apps. Access for non-app advertisers has been changing, so check what is available to you today. Most local businesses should have Google Search, a strong Google Business Profile and Meta ads working first, then treat AppLovin as an add-on test.

## What AppLovin Actually Is

AppLovin is a mobile technology company best known among app marketers. Its relevant pieces for advertisers are:

- **An in-app ad network and exchange.** App developers monetize through AppLovin's mediation software, and advertisers buy that inventory.
- **AXON, its AI optimization system.** It automates much of the targeting and bidding based on the goal you set, so you have less manual control than on Meta or Google.
- **Its advertiser platform.** Historically built for app install campaigns, and, according to AppLovin's public announcements, being extended to other advertiser types. Availability, eligibility and minimums for local businesses can change, so confirm them directly or through an agency.

The key difference from Google or Meta is context. Your ad appears inside a game or utility app, where the person is focused on something else. That is a different attention environment from search results or a social feed.

## Who Is Reachable There

Mobile gaming is a mainstream activity across many ages, and casual puzzle, word and strategy games attract a broad adult audience rather than only young gamers. For some local businesses, that means reaching people who spend little time on social platforms. Exact audience composition varies by app and by campaign settings, so ask for reporting rather than assuming.

## AppLovin Ad Formats

- **Interstitial.** Full-screen image or video shown between levels or screens.
- **Rewarded video.** The user chooses to watch a short video in exchange for an in-game reward. Completion rates are typically high because viewing is opt-in.
- **Banner.** Small fixed placement. Cheaper, but easy to ignore.
- **Native.** Formatted to match the app's layout.

<Callout type="tip">Rewarded video is attractive because the viewer opts in and usually watches to the end. Attention is still not intent, so pair it with a simple offer and a trackable next step.</Callout>

## Who Benefits Most

Better fits tend to be businesses with a clear, quick offer and a way to measure the response:

- **Businesses with their own app** for ordering, booking or loyalty, since an in-app prompt can lead directly to an install or order.
- **Restaurants, cafes and food brands** with simple promotions that suit an impulse moment.
- **Entertainment venues** such as escape rooms, bowling alleys and cinemas.
- **Fitness and wellness offers** with a free trial or intro class.
- **Local e-commerce** with a clear geographic delivery area.

Weaker fits include high-consideration services, businesses with a tiny service radius, and anything that depends on search intent. For those, Google Ads is usually a better first spend. See our [in-app advertising guide for local business](/blog/in-app-advertising-local-business-guide).

## How Local Businesses Can Access AppLovin

1. **AppLovin's own advertiser platform**, if your business type is eligible.
2. **Programmatic DSPs.** Some DSPs can buy mobile in-app inventory, including supply that originates from AppLovin's exchange. Check which supply paths your DSP offers.
3. **Agencies** that specialize in mobile or performance advertising and may have different minimums.

Plan on a test budget large enough for the algorithm to learn. If you cannot commit a meaningful amount for at least a couple of months, a more intent-based channel will usually give you more learning per dollar.

## What AppLovin Ads Cost

Pricing depends on format, geography, audience and competition, and it changes. In general, in-app video and display CPMs can sit below premium social or sports CTV rates, while rewarded and interstitial video cost more than banners. We do not quote a single number here because published ranges vary widely. Ask your rep or agency for current pricing in your own geography, then compare cost per result against your other channels. Our [CPM vs CPC guide](/blog/cpm-vs-cpc-which-is-better) explains how to compare them.

## Creative Basics

Specs differ by format and change over time, so confirm current requirements in the platform. As a general approach:

- Keep the message to one idea and one action.
- Show your brand early and again at the end.
- Use bold, high-contrast visuals that read on a small screen.
- Put the offer in the first few seconds of video.
- Use a QR code, short link or unique phone number so you can track response.

<Callout type="warning">Players are in the middle of a game, so ads that feel like a pure interruption get dismissed fast. Lead with a clear, immediate offer rather than brand storytelling.</Callout>

## Measuring Results

Attribution is the hard part. Combine several signals: a dedicated landing page with UTM tags, a unique phone number or promo code, booking system source fields, and a "how did you hear about us" question. Compare new-customer volume in your target area before, during and after a flight. Expect some uncertainty, and do not judge the channel only on last-click conversions. ## FAQ

**Q: Is AppLovin only for app developers?**
A: It was built for app marketers, and access for other advertisers has been expanding. Confirm current eligibility before you plan a budget.

**Q: What budget do I need?**
A: Enough for the system to gather data, which is more than a token amount. Ask the platform or an agency for current minimums and set aside a multi-week test budget you can afford to lose.

**Q: How is it different from Facebook or Instagram ads?**
A: Meta ads appear in feeds where people are browsing, with detailed interest and lookalike options. AppLovin ads appear inside apps and games, with more automated optimization and less manual audience control.

**Q: Can I target only my city?**
A: Geo-targeting is generally available, but inventory in small areas may be thin. Test a metro-sized radius first and narrow it only if volume allows.

**Q: How do I know whether someone booked because of the ad?**
A: Use a unique URL, phone number or code, plus a source question at booking. Perfect attribution is unlikely, so look for a consistent lift in new customers.

**Q: What is the biggest risk?**
A: Spending on impressions you cannot measure. Set frequency caps, rotate creative, and track both online and offline responses.
