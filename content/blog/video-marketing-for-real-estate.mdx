An agent should start with one vertical walkthrough of each listing, a longer horizontal tour for the listing page and a short talking-head video about the neighbourhood. In a Rocket Mortgage survey of over 800 buyers reported by NAR in 2022, 58% rated photo or video quality essential for a good virtual viewing and 51% said the number of images mattered. That is old, narrow data from one lender's survey, so treat it as a direction and test on your own listings.

This guide is for independent agents and small teams in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. Facts come from the sources at the end. Production cost and view numbers for agents are not published by neutral sources, so the worked example uses labelled assumptions.

## What Do Buyers Say About Virtual Tours?

The NAR article summarised a survey of more than 800 home buyers. About 60% said they would buy a home after only a virtual tour. Prerecorded virtual tours gave higher satisfaction than live virtual tours or panoramic photos. Respondents named viewing more homes, convenience and screening before visits as the main gains. They named difficulty seeing details and inability to inspect problem areas as the main limits.

NAR marks the piece as archived, and the sample is mortgage-lender customers. Use it to justify prerecorded tours and plenty of clear images, not to forecast sales.

## Which Videos Should an Agent Make First?

Start with three, in this order:

1. **Vertical walkthrough, 45 to 90 seconds.** Shot on a phone, one room per few seconds, ends with price, area and how to book a viewing.
2. **Full prerecorded tour for the listing page.** Slower, covers every room, shows the street and outside.
3. **Neighbourhood or buyer-question video.** You on camera answering one question, such as what a street is like on a weekday.

Skip fancy effects. The NAR survey respondents cared most about quality and number of images. Steady footage, good light and a tidy house matter more than music. Our [video content strategy guide](/blog/video-content-strategy-small-business) covers the basic kit.

## Where Should You Post the Videos?

Use each place for a different job:

| Place | Best for | Fact to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listing page and your site | Full tour | You control the page and the contact form |
| YouTube | Searchable tours and area videos | Shorts can be up to 3 minutes if square or vertical (YouTube Help) |

YouTube's help says videos uploaded after October 15, 2024 with a square or vertical aspect ratio up to three minutes long are categorised as Shorts. Horizontal tours stay long-form videos. ## What Legal Rules Apply to Real Estate Video Ads?

In the US, the Fair Housing Act makes it unlawful to publish any notice, statement or advertisement about a dwelling that indicates a preference, limitation or discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, handicap, familial status or national origin (42 U.S.C. 3604(c)). That covers spoken narration and on-screen text in a video, not only written ads.

Review the script for phrases that describe the ideal buyer rather than the home. Other countries have their own rules, and platforms add their own housing-ad policies. Check them before paying to boost a video. This article is not legal advice.

## The Math: What Does a Video Cost in Time?

All inputs are **assumptions** for a solo agent with a phone and a basic tripod.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Filming and walkthrough per listing | 1.5 hours |
| Editing a vertical cut and a full tour | 2 hours |
| Listings per month | 4 |
| Total time per month | 4 x 3.5 = 14 hours |
| Enquiries credited to video per month | 2 |
| Hours per video-led enquiry | 14 / 2 = 7 hours |

Under these inputs, video is worth it only if a video-led enquiry is worth more than seven hours of your time. Track the enquiry source for each lead, and compare with a listing without video. If the answer is no, reduce to one vertical video per listing.

## FAQ

### What video should a real estate agent make first?
A 45 to 90 second vertical walkthrough of each listing, shot on a phone, with price and booking details at the end.

### Do virtual tours help sell homes?
A 2022 Rocket Mortgage survey reported by NAR found about 60% of 800+ buyers would buy after only a virtual tour. It is a lender survey, so test on your own listings.

### How long can a YouTube Short be?
Up to three minutes for square or vertical videos, per YouTube Help.

### Can video ads break fair housing rules?
Yes. 42 U.S.C. 3604(c) covers any advertisement that indicates a preference or limitation based on protected classes, so check narration and text.

### Should I pay for video production?
No neutral price benchmark exists. Start with phone video, track leads by source, then decide.
