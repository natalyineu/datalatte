A coffee shop grand opening needs three things in place before the doors open: a verified Google Business Profile, an opening date customers can see, and a way to collect reviews from day one. Then run a small paid test. Google says an opening date can be entered up to a year ahead but shows only 90 days before the date, so set it early. BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 47% of consumers will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews, so reviews are the first goal.

This plan runs from four weeks before opening to four weeks after. Cost figures come from sourced benchmarks, and the worked example is labelled as assumptions.

## What Should Happen Before Opening Day?

Four weeks out, claim and verify the Business Profile. Google says you must add or claim the business and then verify it to appear on Search and Maps. Some businesses must verify before they can edit at all, so start early.

Then add the opening date. Google says only the month and year are needed, and you can enter a date up to a year in the future, though it will not show until 90 days before. Fill in category, hours, menu link and photos. Our [coffee shop Business Profile guide](/blog/coffee-shop-google-business-profile-optimization) lists the fields to complete.

## What Is the 30-Day Plan?

| Week | Focus | Actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 weeks before | Foundations | Verify profile, set opening date, photos, menu, hours |
| 2 weeks before | Local awareness | Start a small Meta awareness test, tell neighbours and nearby offices |
| Opening week | Visits | Opening offer, staff ask every guest for a review, daily posts |
| Weeks 2 to 4 | Repeat visits | Email or SMS sign-up, loyalty offer, answer all reviews |

The order matters. Awareness ads sent before the profile is verified send people to an empty listing. Build the base, then push people to it.

## How Do You Get the First 20 Reviews?

Ask in person, and give people a way to do it in one tap. Google says you can share a review link or QR code with customers. It also says offering free or discounted goods in exchange for reviews is strictly prohibited, so do not run a "free coffee for a review" promotion.

BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 74% of consumers only consider reviews from the last three months, so the opening-week reviews will age. Keep asking after week four. Read replies from Google's guidance: keep them short, professional and specific, and avoid promotional offers in them.

## What Do Ads Cost for a Coffee Shop?

LocaliQ lists Restaurants and Food at $2.05 per click, a 6.83% click-through rate, an 8.05% conversion rate and $30.57 per lead on Google Search. The all-industry average is $5.42 per click. On Meta, Restaurants and Food traffic campaigns average $0.45 per click and a 2.68% click-through rate, per LocaliQ's September 2026 update.

Neither figure is for new coffee shops, and a click is not a visit. Treat them as planning ranges. See our [coffee shop Google Ads guide](/blog/google-ads-for-coffee-shops-complete-2026-guide) and [keyword list](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-coffee-shops-in-2026).

On these assumptions search ads bring about six visitors for $300. They will not pay back on first orders. The case for paid ads at opening is searchers who go on to become regulars, which depends on a repeat rate no source publishes. That is why free steps (profile, reviews, neighbours, email list) come first, and the ads stay small.

## Should You Use Meta Ads for the Opening?

Meta ads can announce the opening to people near the shop. Use a short radius, one clear message (date, address, opening offer) and a link to your profile or menu. Judge by visits, not clicks.

With traffic clicks around $0.45, $200 buys roughly 444 clicks at the benchmark. No public source says how many of those become visitors, so ask opening-week guests how they heard about you. Our [coffee shop Facebook ads guide](/blog/coffee-shop-facebook-ads-guide) goes into targeting.

## How Do You Turn Opening Visitors Into Regulars?

Collect an email or phone number with consent at the counter, and offer a reason to return in the next 14 days. Our [email list building guide for coffee shops](/blog/coffee-shop-email-list-building) shows how.

Then review the month: reviews gained, profile views, calls, directions, ad cost and visits per source. Keep what worked and cut what did not.

## FAQ

### When should a coffee shop start marketing before opening?
About four weeks before. Verification and profile approval can take time, and Google shows the opening date only 90 days ahead.

### How many reviews do I need at opening?
BrightLocal found 47% of consumers will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews, so aim for 20 as a first target.

### Can I offer free coffee for reviews?
No. Google says rewards for reviews are strictly prohibited.

### How much should I spend on ads for the opening?
No source gives a standard figure. Start with a small test and measure visits.

### Do I need a grand opening event?
It helps create a date to promote, but is not required. A clear offer and a verified profile matter more.
