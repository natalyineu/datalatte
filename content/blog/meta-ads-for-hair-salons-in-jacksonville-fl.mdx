## How Much Should a Jacksonville Salon Spend on Meta Ads Each Month?

Bookeo suggests starting Facebook and Instagram ad spend at USD 750 to USD 1,500 monthly ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). Adwave's channel comparison puts a standalone Meta buy at $300 to $1,000 per month for many businesses ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-choose-advertising-channels)). Its dental practice guide lists $500 to $2,000 per month for Meta, which is a cross-industry figure rather than a salon benchmark ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/dental-advertising-get-more-patients)).

A single-location salon can test at the lower end of these ranges. Spend that level for long enough to see how many bookings come from the ads, then decide whether to raise it. Budget size matters less than whether each dollar is tied to a booked appointment you can count.

## What Do Meta Ads Cost Per Click and Per Thousand Impressions?

Adwave lists typical Meta costs of $5 to $15 per thousand impressions and $1 to $3 per click ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-small-business-guide)). Its retargeting guide gives a similar Meta CPM range of $5 to $15 ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/retargeting-ads-small-business)). Adwave also quotes an average Meta cost per install of $1.50 to $4.00, but that figure is for app installs and does not apply to salon bookings ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-mobile-apps)).

These costs vary by audience and season, so use them only to plan. Your own campaign data, once you have a few weeks of it, will tell you more than any national average. Check the current cost estimates inside Meta Ads Manager before you set a daily budget.

## Which Ad Formats Should a Salon Test First?

Adwave cites Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report to say short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/electrician-advertising-reaching-homeowners)). Engagement is not the same as bookings, so run a short video and a still image side by side and compare cost per booked appointment. Film real work from the chair, such as a colour process or a finished cut, rather than stock footage.

Instagram ads run through the same Meta Ads Manager as Facebook ads, and one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook or both ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/instagram-advertising-for-local-business)). Stories are temporary by design, which suits a time-limited offer. For more on building the organic side, see [Instagram marketing for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons).

## Who Should You Target, and How Far Should the Radius Reach?

Bookeo advises setting a 5 to 10 mile radius around the salon for Facebook and Instagram ads ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). A salon in a dense part of the city may want the smaller end of that range, and a salon serving drivers from further out may need the larger end. Test two radii and compare which one produces more bookings per dollar.

Check when your followers are online using the peak-times view in the Instagram or Facebook Insights tab, which requires a business account ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). Use those hours to schedule ads. For a Facebook-focused setup, see [Facebook ads for hair salons](/blog/facebook-ads-for-hair-salons).

## How Often Should a Salon Post Alongside Paid Ads?

Bookeo says most salon owners find three to four Instagram posts weekly and one to two Facebook posts weekly sustainable ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). That cadence is a workload guide, not a performance benchmark. Bookeo also says Facebook and Instagram can be used to reach audiences and build trust without spending a dollar on the platform itself ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)).

Paid ads send people to a page, so that page should already show recent work, prices and availability. A salon with a thin feed will waste clicks regardless of how well the ad is targeted.

## What Does a Worked Budget Look Like for a Jacksonville Salon?

The inputs below are assumptions for illustration, not sourced figures. Replace them with your own numbers.

- Monthly budget: $750 (assumption, the low end of Bookeo's starting range)
- Cost per click: $1 or $3 (the Adwave range, used as two scenarios)
- Click-to-booking rate: 2% (assumption)
- Average booked ticket: $120 (assumption, for example a cut plus a colour service)

| Scenario | Clicks ($750 ÷ cost per click) | Bookings at 2% | First-visit revenue at $120 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost $1 per click | 750 | 15 | $1,800 |
| Cost $3 per click | 250 | 5 | $600 |

At $3 per click, first-visit revenue of $600 is below the $750 spend, so the campaign does not pay back on the first appointment alone. At $1 per click, it returns $1,800 before costs. These figures are gross revenue, not profit, and they ignore repeat visits. The gap between the two rows shows why cost per click and booking rate matter more than the monthly total. ## What Tracking Should Be in Place Before Spending?

Meta Ads Manager can be used as a central point to run ads across Instagram and Facebook ([Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-advertising)). It covers Facebook, Instagram and Messenger ([Turrboo](https://turrboo.com/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media)). Set up the Meta Pixel and a conversion event on the booking confirmation page, and confirm both fire with Meta's current help pages before the first ad goes live.

Add UTM parameters to every ad link so analytics can separate ad visits from other traffic. Track bookings per campaign, not impressions. Once a booking arrives, a follow-up sequence can keep the client returning; see [marketing automation for hair salons](/blog/marketing-automation-for-hair-salons).
