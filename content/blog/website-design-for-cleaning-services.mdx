A cleaning company website should let a visitor request a quote or book a clean in under a minute on a phone, explain what each service includes, and make recurring service the easy choice. Booking tools start at $27 a month (Booking Koala Starter) and $29 a month (Jobber Core, billed annually), plus card processing fees of 2.9% + 30 cents per online card payment on Stripe.

This guide is for house and commercial cleaning companies. Prices were checked on 31 October 2026 on the vendors' own pages. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Should a Cleaning Website Show First?

State the service, the area and the next step: "Home cleaning in Austin. Get a price in 60 seconds." Then show the one button that starts a quote or booking. Put your phone number beside it for people who prefer to call.

Add the practical details visitors check before they let someone into their home: what is included in each clean, what to prepare, whether you bring supplies, your service area and your cancellation terms. Do not publish insurance, bonding or background-check claims unless you hold them and can show proof.

## Should You Use a Quote Form or Instant Booking?

It depends on how standard your jobs are.

| Approach | Best for | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instant online booking with set prices | Standard home cleans by bedrooms and bathrooms | Needs clear pricing rules and add-ons |
| Quote request form | Deep cleans, move-outs, commercial offices | Slower; you must reply fast |
| Call-first | Urgent or complex jobs | Misses visitors who will not call |

Booking Koala's pricing page says customers can pick services and view pricing and availability you set, and lists multi-step forms and daily discounts on slower days under its Premium plan. No independent data was found on which approach converts best, so test one change at a time.

Card fees matter for recurring service. On a $150 clean, 2.9% + 30 cents is $4.65. ACH at 0.8% would be $1.20 on the same amount, per the Stripe page, though customers must set it up.

## How Do You Make Recurring Clients the Default?

Offer weekly, every-two-weeks and monthly options in the booking form, and show the first-visit option second. If you give a discount for recurring service, state it clearly and make sure your margin holds. Ask for card details on file at booking so repeat visits do not need chasing. ## How Do You Get Found?

A site helps people decide; it does not make them find you. Google's Local Services Ads help page lists house cleaning services and carpet cleaning services among eligible categories, says you pay only for leads and only when a customer contacts you, and notes availability depends on your area. See our [keyword guide for cleaning services](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-cleaning-services-in-2026) for search terms to target.

## How Fast Should the Site Be?

Google's targets at the 75th percentile are LCP within 2.5 seconds, INP of 200 ms or less and CLS of 0.1 or less. Before-and-after photos are heavy, so compress them. Google indexes the mobile version, which should hold the same content as desktop. ## Worked Example: A Recurring Client's Value

All inputs are **assumptions**.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Price per clean | $150 |
| Frequency | Every two weeks, 26 cleans a year |
| Client stays | 12 months |
| Revenue per client | 26 x $150 = $3,900 |
| Card fee per clean (Stripe, 2.9% + 30c) | $4.65, so $121 a year |
| Software (Jobber Core annual) | $29 x 12 = $348 a year |

Ten recurring clients bring $39,000 a year in revenue against about $1,210 in card fees and $348 in software, before labour and supplies. Churn will lower this, so track how many clients stay past three months.

## FAQ

### How much does a cleaning company website cost?

Software starts at $27 to $59 a month for booking. A Squarespace site is from $19 a month on annual billing (see our [personal trainer site guide](/blog/website-design-for-personal-trainers) for builders). Custom design is extra.

### Do I need online payments?

Not required, but card on file reduces missed payments. Fees are listed above.

### Is a quote form better than a price calculator?

No benchmark was found. Use a calculator for standard homes and a form for complex jobs.

### Which photos work best?

Real photos of your team and finished work. Avoid stock images that misrepresent your crew.

### Should I list my insurance?

Only if you hold it. Show proof on request.
