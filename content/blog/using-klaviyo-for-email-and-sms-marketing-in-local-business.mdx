The catch for a shop or studio is consent: Klaviyo states that having consent for email does not mean you have consent for SMS, so each channel needs its own opt-in. Paid prices were not published on the pricing page checked, so budget after you see a quote in your account.

This guide covers the free plan limits, how to collect consent correctly, which messages to start with and how Klaviyo credits sales. It does not promise results.

## What Does Klaviyo Cost for a Local Business?

The pricing page lists a free plan with up to 250 active profiles, 500 email sends per month, $5 of mobile messages per month, built-in reporting and email support for the first 60 days. It does not state a price for paid email plans or SMS beyond that $5. It sends you to a plan builder instead. The page carries no update date, only a 2026 copyright.

Check how many contacts you have. A café with 800 loyalty-card emails exceeds the free profile limit. If your list is small, test the free plan. If not, compare with our [Klaviyo vs Mailchimp review](/blog/mailchimp-vs-klaviyo-small-business-which-is-better).

## How Do You Collect Email and SMS Consent?

Collect them separately. Klaviyo says disclosure language must appear before the person opts in, above the SMS checkbox or button, and for every opt-in method including keywords. Use a dedicated checkbox, not a general "agree to marketing" box. Make SMS clearly optional and never force it to complete a purchase.

Klaviyo advises against putting email and SMS consent on the same step, since customers may think SMS is required. A single-step form is allowed only with double opt-in and a clear label such as "Phone number to receive SMS marketing (optional)". Klaviyo gives this as its guidance, not as legal advice. Ask a lawyer to check your wording.

For email, the FTC's CAN-SPAM guide requires honoring opt-outs within 10 business days and a physical postal address in each message, with penalties up to $53,088 per email.

## Which Messages Should You Send First?

Start with automated messages tied to a visit, then add occasional campaigns. Reasonable first flows for a local business are a welcome message for new sign-ups, a reminder before an appointment, a thank-you with a review request after a visit, and a "we miss you" message after a gap. These are common practice rather than Klaviyo-published benchmarks.

Keep each message to one job and one link. For review requests, follow Google's rules and see [managing online reviews](/blog/managing-online-reviews-small-business). For SMS wording, our [SMS marketing for retail](/blog/sms-marketing-for-retail) guide and the [pet groomer SMS guide](/blog/sms-marketing-for-pet-groomers) show how short texts read.

## How Does Klaviyo Credit a Sale to a Message?

Klaviyo uses an attribution window. Its help center says that for new accounts, email opens and clicks and SMS clicks are credited for 5 days, SMS deliveries for 12 hours and push opens for 24 hours. The default model is last touch. You can change attribution settings up to 3 times in 30 days, and changes can take up to 36 hours.

Attribution only covers sales Klaviyo can see. A local business that takes bookings in another system must connect it or track by code. A walk-in who mentions a text is invisible unless staff record it.

## FAQ

### Is Klaviyo free for small businesses?
It has a free plan with up to 250 active profiles, 500 email sends and $5 of mobile messages per month, according to its pricing page.

### Can I text customers who gave me their email?
No. Klaviyo states that email consent does not mean SMS consent. Collect SMS consent separately.

### How fast must I process an unsubscribe?
The FTC's CAN-SPAM guide says to honor opt-out requests within 10 business days and keep the mechanism working for at least 30 days after sending.

### Does Klaviyo track bookings?
It credits sales it can see within the attribution window. Bookings in other systems need an integration or manual tracking.

### What is the SMS price?
The pricing page checked does not list SMS prices beyond the $5 free allowance, so check your account.
