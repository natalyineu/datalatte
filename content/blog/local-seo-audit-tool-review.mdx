A local SEO audit is a check of the things that decide whether nearby customers find you: your Google Business Profile, your name, address and phone number (NAP) across the web, your reviews, your website, its mobile speed and your rankings. You can run one yourself in about an hour with free Google tools. Paid tools mostly save time and track changes.

This guide gives the checklist first, then compares audit tools using each vendor's pricing page, opened on 2026-11-01. Prices are in US dollars unless stated and change often.

## What does a local SEO audit check?

Google says local results are based on relevance, distance and prominence. Relevance is how well your profile matches the search. Distance is how far you are from the searcher. Prominence is how well known you are, which Google ties to links to your business and to your reviews.

You cannot change distance, so an audit looks at what you can change. Google also states there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking, which is why no audit tool or service can promise a position.

## How do you run a local SEO audit in an hour?

Use a spreadsheet with one row per check and a column for the date you fixed it. The time per step is a planning suggestion, not a standard.

### 1. Google Business Profile (15 minutes)

Check each item against Google's [profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177):

- The name matches your real-world name. Taglines, hours, phone numbers, URLs and service descriptions do not belong in it.
- Categories are the fewest that describe your core business, and the most specific ones that fit.
- The address is real and precise. P.O. boxes and virtual offices are not acceptable. Service-area businesses should hide the address and set a service area.
- The phone number connects to your location, not a call center where possible.
- Photos are JPG or PNG, between 10 KB and 5 MB, with 720 x 720 pixels recommended and 250 x 250 as the minimum, per [Google's photo guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6103862).

Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes deeper on each field.

### 2. NAP and listings (10 minutes)

Search your business name and compare the name, address and phone on Apple Maps, Bing Places, Yelp, Facebook and one or two industry directories with your profile. Fix mismatches at the source.

Google's ranking help gives no weight for directory listings, so treat this as hygiene that keeps customers from finding a wrong number. Our guides to [NAP consistency](/blog/nap-consistency-local-seo) and [citation building](/blog/local-citations-building-strategy) cover the clean-up.

### 3. Reviews (10 minutes)

Look at your rating, count, recency and reply rate. Google says more reviews and positive ratings can help local ranking, and gives no target number. Its Maps policy prohibits offering payment, discounts, free goods or services for reviews, and selectively asking only happy customers.

BrightLocal's Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 (1,002 US adults, published 11 February 2026) found 97% read reviews for local businesses, 68% use only businesses rated four stars or higher and 74% look for reviews from the last three months. It is a US survey from a vendor that sells review tools, so read it as a direction. See [Google Business Profile reviews: rules and tips](/blog/google-business-profile-reviews).

### 4. Website on-page (10 minutes)

- Each page has its own title. Google's guidance asks for descriptive, concise, unique titles without repeated keywords.
- Your address, phone and hours appear as text, with your city and service named in plain language.
- If you add [LocalBusiness structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business), Google requires name and address and recommends fields such as telephone, opening hours, geo and url. Google states it does not guarantee that features using structured data will show up.

### 5. Mobile and speed (5 minutes)

Run your homepage through [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/). It reports mobile and desktop results, with real-user data from the previous 28 days when enough exists. Google's Core Web Vitals pass when the 75th percentile is good on all three: Largest Contentful Paint 2.5 seconds or less, Interaction to Next Paint 200 milliseconds or less, Cumulative Layout Shift 0.1 or less ([web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/vitals)).

### 6. Rankings and results (10 minutes)

Search your main service plus your town on a phone in a private window. Note who appears above you and compare their review counts, categories and photos with yours.

Then record your baseline. Business Profile Performance shows searches, views on Search and Maps, directions, calls, website clicks, messages and bookings, where they apply. Search Console shows clicks, impressions, click-through rate and average position, over the past three months by default. Track the same numbers monthly with our [local SEO KPI guide](/blog/how-to-measure-local-seo-success-kpis).

## Is there a free local SEO audit?

Yes, in pieces. No single free tool audits everything.

- **Google Business Profile Performance:** free, shows how people found and used your profile. It reports what happened, not what to fix.
- **Search Console:** free, covers your website's queries and clicks only.
- **PageSpeed Insights:** free, covers speed and Core Web Vitals.
- **Ahrefs Free:** Site Audit scans for 170+ technical and on-page issues, with 5,000 crawl credits per verified project per month and unlimited verified sites.
- **Screaming Frog free version:** crawls up to 500 URLs, enough for most small sites.
- **Whitespark free tools:** its pricing page lists a Review Checker and a Free Local Ranking Checker. The tool pages were not opened, so limits are unknown.
- **BrightLocal:** its audit page describes a local SEO health check running 300+ automated checks, but it sits behind a 14-day free trial sign-up. The page does not say it is free.

## FAQ

### What is a local SEO audit?
A review of your Google Business Profile, listings, reviews, website, speed and local rankings to find what stops nearby customers finding you.

### How long does a local SEO audit take?
The checklist above is planned for about an hour for one location. Multi-location or messy listings take longer.

### What is the best local SEO audit tool for a small business?
Start with the free Google tools. Add BrightLocal if you want audits and tracking in one plan, or Whitespark if listings are the main problem.

### How often should you audit?
The sources opened give no required interval. A monthly look at your Performance numbers and a full pass when you move, change hours or services is a reasonable habit.

### Can a tool guarantee better rankings?
No. Google says rankings cannot be requested or paid for.
