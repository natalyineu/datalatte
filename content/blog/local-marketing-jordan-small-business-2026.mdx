A small business in Jordan can reach most people online, and the audience is split across several platforms. DataReportal lists YouTube at 6.80 million, Facebook at 5.55 million, Instagram at 4.55 million and Snapchat at 4.20 million. Start with Google Business Profile and a Facebook or Instagram page, then test Snapchat if your customers are young and local. Google Ads is available, but no Jordan click prices were found.

This guide is for cafes, restaurants, salons, shops and service firms in Jordan. Figures come from the sources at the end, checked on 1 November 2026.

## Which Platforms Reach the Most People in Jordan?

YouTube leads, then Facebook. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report lists Jordan's population at 11.5 million, internet users at 10.6 million (92.5%) and 10.1 million mobile connections, 87.7% of the population. Social media user identities total 6.80 million, 59.2%.

| Platform | Ad reach, late 2025 | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | 6.80 million | 59.2% |
| Facebook | 5.55 million | 48.3% |
| Instagram | 4.55 million | 39.6% |
| Snapchat | 4.20 million | 36.5% |
| TikTok (adults 18+ only) | 2.34 million | 32.0% of adults 18+ |
| LinkedIn (members) | 2.20 million | 19.1% |

These are ad-tool estimates, not active user counts, and TikTok's base is adults only. Still, Snapchat's 4.20 million is close to Instagram's 4.55 million, which makes it worth a test.

## What Is Different About Jordan's Platform Mix?

Two things stand out in the numbers. Snapchat is nearly as large as Instagram, and TikTok is smaller than both. A business that has only built an Instagram presence may be missing a similar-sized audience on Snapchat.

Facebook at 5.55 million still outranks Instagram, so a Facebook Page remains the safest place for hours, menus and offers. Add Snapchat only if you can make short, local clips regularly. No source checked says which platform converts best in Jordan, so test each with the same small budget.

## Can a Jordanian Business Run Google Ads?

Yes. Google's list of billing countries includes Jordan. In that list the account currency and payment method columns read "All", but Google says the options you see depend on your billing country, currency and payment setting, and that prepay is not offered in many countries.

Open the billing page in your own account to see what is available. No source checked publishes Jordanian click prices, and DataReportal does not report Google ad reach for Jordan.

Search ads suit businesses people look for by need, such as a dentist, an auto repair shop or a florist. Use location targeting for the city you serve and review the Search terms report weekly. The [restaurant keyword guide](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-restaurants-in-2026) shows how to build a keyword list.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local in Jordan?

Google's help page says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km. It recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting as the best practice on Search, and says "Presence" only suits cases where you want to exclude people who are elsewhere but interested in your offer.

Radius targeting uses kilometres, which fits Jordanian distances. A cafe might test 3 km around the shop, which is an assumption to check against your customers' home areas.

Add negative keywords weekly from the Search terms report. Google notes that negative keywords do not match close variants, so add singular, plural and synonym forms yourself.

## What Does a First Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for a cafe running a Snapchat and Instagram test against a Facebook control. They show the method, not Jordanian results. Currency is "units" of dinars.

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Gross profit per new regular customer over a year (assumption) | 50 units |
| Enquiries that become regular customers (assumption) | 30% |
| Most you can pay per enquiry and still break even | 50 x 30% = 15 units |
| Budget per platform for four weeks (assumption) | 150 units |
| Enquiries each platform needs to break even | 150 / 15 = 10 |

Run the same offer code on each platform for four weeks. The platform that reaches ten qualifying enquiries at the lowest spend gets next month's budget. Without a code or a question at the till, you cannot compare them.

## How Do You Get Found on Google Maps in Jordan?

Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. It also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Fill in every field, use real photos, reply to every review and keep the name, address and phone number identical everywhere. Add the neighbourhood name and a landmark so people searching from a phone can place you quickly. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes through each field.

Do not offer a discount or a free item in return for a review. Google's contributed content policy says merchants must not offer incentives, such as payment, discounts, free goods or services, in exchange for posting a review. Asking is fine. Rewarding is not. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for ways to ask.

## What Mistakes Do Jordanian Small Businesses Make?

These are recommendations, not study findings.

- Building only on Instagram when Facebook and Snapchat reach similar audiences.
- Using one creative for every platform. Snapchat and TikTok need short vertical clips.
- Judging by views. Count enquiries and visits.
- Leaving the Google profile unreviewed for months.
