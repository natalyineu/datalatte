A small business in Latin America should start with three things: a Google Business Profile for search, Instagram or Facebook ads that open a WhatsApp chat, and a payment method local customers already use, Pix in Brazil and cash vouchers such as OXXO in Mexico. The two largest markets are different. Brazil speaks Portuguese and is Instagram-heavy, Mexico speaks Spanish and has more Facebook reach than Instagram reach. Ad prices are not published by any platform, so test before you set a budget.

This guide covers Brazil and Mexico, the two largest markets, using sourced audience figures and platform documentation. Illustrative numbers are labelled as an assumption.

## Which Platforms Reach the Most People in Brazil and Mexico?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 reports, using data from late 2025, give these advertising reach figures. Ad reach counts people who can be shown ads, not monthly active users, and the platforms revise it often.

| Platform | Brazil | Mexico |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internet users | 185 million | 110 million |
| YouTube ad reach | 150 million | 85 million |
| Facebook ad reach | 109 million | 93.5 million |
| Instagram ad reach | 147 million | 53.6 million |
| TikTok ad reach (adults 18+) | 131 million | 99 million |

The pattern matters more than the totals. In Brazil, Instagram reach is far larger than Facebook reach, so a salon or cafe there should lead with Instagram. In Mexico, Facebook reach is larger than Instagram reach, so Facebook belongs in the plan. DataReportal notes that Mexico's TikTok figure exceeds the adult population count, so treat it as a rough size, not a precise one.

## Does Google Still Matter for Local Search?

Yes. StatCounter showed Google with 88.41% of search engine share in Brazil and 88.08% in Mexico in September 2026. When someone searches for a gym, a dentist or a salon, a complete Google Business Profile decides whether you appear on the map.

Fill in every field, add real photos, answer reviews and keep your hours correct. Our guide to the [Google Business Profile optimization checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers each step. Use Portuguese for Brazil and Spanish for Mexico in your profile and ads, not translations of each other.

## How Do Click-to-WhatsApp Ads Work?

An ad on Facebook or Instagram can open a WhatsApp chat with your business when someone taps it. WhatsApp's business site lists the setup: in Ads Manager choose Create, pick an objective, select Messaging Apps and then WhatsApp, set dates and a budget, define the audience and publish. It advises running the ad for at least seven days and says an audience of 2 to 10 million people will perform best.

Meta's pricing page explains the message costs. Since 1 July 2025 Meta charges per message, and when a person starts a chat from a click-to-WhatsApp ad on the mobile app, a free entry point window opens in which Meta does not charge for marketing, utility, authentication or service messages, for up to 7 days. The ad itself is billed separately. Your regular ad charges still apply.

For setup and customer service, read our guides to [WhatsApp Business for local business marketing](/blog/whatsapp-business-marketing-local-business) and [WhatsApp Business for customer service](/blog/how-to-use-whatsapp-business-for-customer-service).

## What Does a WhatsApp Ad Test Cost? A Worked Example

No platform publishes a typical click cost for Brazil or Mexico, so every input below is an **assumption** for illustration. Replace each one with numbers from your own Ads Manager report.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget | R$3,000 |
| Cost per click to WhatsApp | R$1.50 |
| Clicks | 2,000 |
| Share who actually start a chat | 15% |
| Chats | 300 |
| Share of chats that become a booking or sale | 10% |
| Sales | 30 |
| Cost per sale | R$100 |

If your average sale is R$60 and the customer never returns, this test loses money. If a customer is worth R$400 over a year, it pays. The most useful number to find in a first test is the share of chats that become sales, which depends on how fast you reply. Answer within minutes and keep a saved reply for the questions you get most.

## Why Is Pix Important for Brazilian Businesses?

The Central Bank of Brazil's Pix report for 2023 to 2025 shows almost 80 billion Pix transactions in 2025, up 25.7% on 2024, with a value above R$35 trillion. More than 920 million keys were registered. About 148 million people and 12.8 million businesses sent or received at least one Pix in 2025, around 86% of the adult population by the report's count.

For a small business this means customers expect to pay by Pix, usually by scanning a QR code at the counter or tapping a link in a WhatsApp chat. The report does not state fees for business receivers, so ask your bank or payment provider what they charge before you quote any figure to customers.

## How Does Cash Payment Work in Mexico?

OXXO is a Mexican convenience store chain that accepts cash for online purchases. Stripe's documentation describes the flow: the customer gets a voucher with a reference number, pays cash at a store, and the business learns of the payment by the next business day. Vouchers expire after 5 days by default and can be set from 1 to 7 days.

Know the limits before you offer it. Stripe lists a minimum of 10 MXN and a maximum of 10,000 MXN per payment, no refunds through the payment method and no chargebacks. You must handle refunds yourself. Stripe also notes funds can take up to four business days to settle. OXXO suits one-off online orders and deposits, not subscriptions.

## Should You Sell on Mercado Libre?

If you sell physical products, a Mercado Libre listing can bring buyers without building a website first. Mercado Libre's Q2 2026 results, as published by a news repost of the company release, reported 89 million unique active buyers in commerce, up 26% on the year, and said advertising revenue grew 73% in US dollars. The company said its share of Latin America's digital advertising market passed 10% for the first time.

Those are company-wide figures across 18 countries, not a guide to what a seller pays. Mercado Ads does not publish an average cost per click, so do not plan a budget from any number you see in a blog post. Start with a small daily budget on your best-selling listing and watch your sales report.

## FAQ

### What is the best advertising platform for a small business in Brazil?

For reach, YouTube (150 million ad reach), Instagram (147 million) and TikTok (131 million adults) are the largest in DataReportal's 2026 data. For a local service business, Instagram ads that open WhatsApp, plus a Google Business Profile, are the practical starting pair.

### Do I need separate Portuguese and Spanish content?

Yes. Brazil uses Portuguese and Mexico uses Spanish. Spanish vocabulary also differs between countries, so have a native speaker check copy for each market.

### How much do Facebook and Instagram ads cost in Latin America?

Meta does not publish a standard price for these countries, and prices depend on audience, season and competition. Run a small test and use your own cost per click and cost per sale.

### Does Pix charge businesses a fee?

The Central Bank's report does not give a business fee figure. Check with your bank or payment provider.
