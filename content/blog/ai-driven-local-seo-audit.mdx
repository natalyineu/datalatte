An AI-driven local SEO audit means using an AI assistant or audit tool to find gaps in your Google Business Profile, reviews, website and competitors, then fixing them yourself. You can run one in about 60 minutes. AI is good at sorting and summarising data you give it. It cannot see your live rankings, and it cannot confirm facts about your real-world business, so you must check every finding.

## What Does Google Use to Rank Local Businesses?

Google's [local ranking help page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091) names three factors: relevance, distance and prominence. Relevance is how well your profile matches the search. Distance is how far you are from the searcher. Prominence is how well known you are, based on factors such as links, review counts and ratings.

You cannot change distance, so an audit focuses on relevance and prominence. Google also states there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

## Step 1: Is Your Google Business Profile Complete and Compliant?

Open your profile and check it against Google's [profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177):

- The business name matches your real-world signage, with no taglines, keywords, hours or phone numbers added.
- The address is a real physical location. Virtual offices and P.O. boxes are not eligible.
- You use the fewest, most specific categories that describe what you are.
- Hours are regular customer-facing hours, with special hours for holidays.
- The description has no links, promotions or prices.
- Only one profile exists per location.

Google says unnecessary information in the business name could result in suspension. Fix any mismatch before doing anything else. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes deeper.

## Step 2: How Do Your Reviews Compare With What Customers Expect?

BrightLocal's [2026 survey](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) of 1,002 US adults found that 97% read local reviews, 68% require four or more stars, 31% want 4.5 or more, and 74% look for reviews written in the last three months. Export your reviews and check:

- Your average rating against the 4.0 and 4.5 lines.
- How many reviews arrived in the last 90 days.
- How many reviews you have not answered.

An AI assistant can summarise themes in the review text, such as parking, wait times or price. Treat the themes as leads and read the originals before acting.

## Step 3: Does Your Information Match Everywhere?

Compare your name, address and phone number on your website, profile, Facebook page and any directories. Inconsistent details are easy to spot in a spreadsheet and slow to fix by hand. Paste the entries into an AI assistant and ask it to flag differences, then verify each one on the live listing. Do not give the assistant customer data.

## Step 4: Does Your Website Support Your Profile?

Check that each location or service has its own page with the same name, address and phone number as your profile, that the page loads on a phone and that the title says what you do and where.

Google's [guidance on generative AI content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content) warns that generating many pages without adding value may violate its spam policy, and says to manually fact-check AI output. Use AI to outline or tidy copy, then add real local detail yourself.

## Step 5: How Do You Compare With Local Competitors?

Search your main service and your area in a private window and note which three businesses appear. Compare their review count, rating, categories and photos with yours. A paid tool automates this. BrightLocal's [pricing page](https://www.brightlocal.com/pricing/) lists its Track plan at $41 a month for one location, or $31 a month billed annually, and says it includes local search and profile audits. ## What Can AI Do in an Audit, and What Can't It Do?

| Task | AI assistant | Needs a human or a tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summarise review themes | Yes | Check original reviews |
| Spot mismatched details in a list | Yes | Verify on live listings |
| Draft review replies | Yes | Edit before posting |
| See live local rankings | No | Rank tracker or manual search |
| Confirm your real-world name and address | No | You |

## What Does a Worked Example Look Like?

This is an **assumption**, not a case study. A salon finds its profile name includes a keyword, it has 12 reviews in the last 90 days and 9 unanswered ones. The fix list is: correct the name, reply to the 9 reviews and ask recent clients for reviews. The audit costs about 60 minutes. Whether rankings improve cannot be promised, because Google does not publish its weights.

## FAQ

**Q: What is an AI-driven local SEO audit?**
A: A review of your profile, reviews, listings, website and competitors, using AI to sort and summarise the data. A person verifies the findings.

**Q: How long does an audit take?**
A: About 60 minutes for one location, using the five steps above.

**Q: Can AI replace an SEO audit tool?**
A: Not fully. AI can summarise data you give it, but it cannot see live rankings.

**Q: Will fixing the audit raise my ranking?**
A: Not guaranteed. Google ranks on relevance, distance and prominence and does not sell better placement.

**Q: Should I let AI write my website content?**
A: Only as a draft. Google says to fact-check AI output and warns against mass-producing pages without added value.
