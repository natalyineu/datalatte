A local business can automate Instagram direct messages, but Meta's platform rules limit what the automation may do. For a comment on a post, reel or ad, you can send one private reply within 7 days of the comment. You can keep the conversation going only after the person replies, and then only inside a 24-hour messaging window. Plan flows around those limits: answer common questions, offer a booking link, and hand anything complicated to a person.

This guide covers the rules from Meta's developer documentation, a simple flow for salons and studios, and a cost example. Rules were checked on 1 November 2026 and can change.

## What Can Instagram DM Automation Do?

Meta's Messenger Platform documentation for Instagram lists supported features such as sending messages with buttons and templates, quick replies, Ice Breakers (starter questions shown in a new chat), a persistent menu, ig.me links, private replies and story mentions, plus handing a chat to a human agent. Automation tools build on these.

For a local business, practical uses are answering opening hours and prices, sending a booking link, and replying to a comment with a keyword such as "BOOK" by sending a private message. Our guide to [AI agents versus chatbots](/blog/ai-agent-vs-chatbot-whats-the-difference-local-business) explains when simple rules are enough.

## What Are Meta's Rules for Private Replies?

Meta's Private Replies documentation says an app can message a person who commented on a professional account's post, ads post, reel or live story. Posts, ads and reels allow a private reply "within 7 days of when the comment was created". For live stories, the reply is allowed only while the broadcast is active.

"Only one message can be sent to the Instagram user who commented." The conversation can continue only after the commenter replies, and then within the 24-hour messaging window. Meta also says apps need Advanced Access, and that Standard Access apps can only access data for people who have a role on the app. Most owners meet this through a tool provider that already holds the access, not by building their own app.

## Which Flows Work for a Salon or Studio?

Keep each flow short and give a clear exit to a person.

| Trigger | Automated reply | Hand off to a person when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comment with a keyword | One private reply with the booking link | The person asks a question you cannot answer |
| New chat | Ice Breaker questions: prices, hours, location | A complaint or special request |
| Story mention | Thank-you message and offer | The person writes back with details |
| "Price" or "how much" | Price list and booking link | A custom quote is needed |

Link the booking page directly so the client does not retype details. Our guide to [reducing no-shows](/blog/reduce-no-shows-appointment-business) shows what to send after the booking, and [filling empty slots fast](/blog/how-to-fill-empty-salon-appointment-slots-fast) covers using offers.

## What Does It Cost?

Tool pricing changes often. ManyChat's own pricing page returned a block when checked, and secondary comparison sites disagreed with each other, so no ManyChat price is quoted here. Check the tool's pricing page before you commit, and note whether it charges by active contact.

Meta's documentation does not say the platform API itself carries a per-message fee for these features. Your cost is the tool subscription plus the time to write and test the flows.

## How Do You Stay Within the Rules?

Do not message people who have not contacted you or commented. Do not send more than the single private reply. Give people a way to reach a human, and tell them they are talking to an automated assistant if the answer could be mistaken for staff. Meta's own page lists Human Agent Escalation among supported features, so build that path in.

## FAQ

### Can I send a DM to everyone who comments?
You can send one private reply per commenter within 7 days of the comment, using an approved tool. You cannot send further messages until they reply.

### How long can I keep a conversation going?
Meta documents a 24-hour messaging window after the user replies. Check Meta's documents for current exceptions, such as human agent handling.

### Do I need a professional account?
Meta's private reply feature is described for Instagram professional accounts.

### Will automation replace staff?
It handles repeated questions. Keep a person for complaints, custom requests and anything involving money.
