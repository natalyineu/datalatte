Yelp does not allow businesses to ask customers for reviews, so the legal way to get more is to make Yelp visible and easy to find without asking. Yelp's own guidance lists five routes: deliver service worth sharing, answer existing reviews, use free Yelp features such as check-in signage, create photo moments, and promote your Yelp page with stickers and embeds.

This guide covers what Yelp's pages say about solicitation, which tactics sit inside the rules, and a practical weekly routine for a café, salon, studio or groomer. Yelp does not publish a review-count target, so none is promised here.

## Can You Ask Customers for Yelp Reviews?

No. Yelp's content guidelines say businesses should never ask customers to write reviews, and its trust page says businesses are not permitted to ask. Offering incentives is also against the policy, according to Yelp for Business.

The reason is stated on Yelp's recommendation software page: reviews written when asked tend to be biased, and businesses tend to ask customers they expect to rate them well. Yelp says its software flags reviews it suspects were requested by an owner or employee and does not recommend them. Non-recommended reviews stay reachable through a link on the page but do not count toward the star rating or review count.

## How Do You Get Yelp Reviews Without Asking?

Yelp for Business gives five routes. Use them together, and keep a record of what you did.

| Tactic | What Yelp says | Practical version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Great service | "Go be five stars, and then the 5-star reviews happen" | Fix recurring complaints first |
| Respond to reviews | 70% of respondents who read reviews say they are more likely to write one when owners respond | Reply to every review, good or bad |
| Free features | Check-in and Request-a-Quote keep Yelp in the customer journey | Put a check-in sign at the counter |
| Photo moments | Create as many photo opportunities as possible | A mural wall, a latte-art counter, a before-and-after board |
| Promote Yelp | "Find us on Yelp" stickers, star rating displays, embeds | Sticker on the door; embed reviews on your site |

Yelp's older advice on soliciting reviews adds that you can tell customers you are on Yelp through signage or email signatures, without pressuring them for a review. Do not send a "please review us" message, even in a friendly tone.

## How Do You Respond to Yelp Reviews?

Yelp's blog recommends replying within 24 hours, in the same tone you would use with a customer in person.

Name the specific thing they mentioned, say what you changed if it was a complaint, and move any dispute to private message. Do not argue about facts in public. ## What Should You Never Do on Yelp?

- Ask customers, friends or staff to review you, or filter who gets asked.
- Offer discounts, freebies or payment for a review.
- Write reviews of your own business or of competitors. Yelp's guidelines say users should not review their own business or employer, or peers in the industry.
- Use AI tools or chatbots to create reviews. The same guidelines say users should not.
- Post promotional material outside a Yelp advertising product on a Business Account.

Yelp's blog says it has placed Consumer Alerts on pages of businesses caught attempting to deceive consumers (the post cites more than 150; it is an older post, so treat the count as dated). It also notes undisclosed compensation for reviews can violate FTC guidelines. A page flagged this way loses far more trust than a slow review count.

## Worked Example: A 90-Day Plan for a Salon

All numbers are **assumptions** for planning, not Yelp data.

- Week 1: claim the page, add hours, 10 photos and services.
- Week 2: order a "Find us on Yelp" sticker and put it on the door.
- Weeks 3 to 12: reply to every review within 24 hours; add one new photo a week.
- Customers per month (assumption): 400. If 2% choose to review unprompted, that is 8 reviews a month, and 24 over 90 days.

The 2% is a guess. Track your own rate and judge the plan against it. If you ask, you break policy and that count can be removed from your rating.

## FAQ

### Is it okay to hand out a card that points to my Yelp page?
Yelp for Business allows promoting your page through "Find us on Yelp" stickers and signage. A card that says "review us" is asking and is against the rules.

### Why do some of my reviews not count?
Yelp's software decides which reviews to recommend. Non-recommended reviews do not affect your star rating or review count, and Yelp says employees cannot override it.

### Can I reply to a bad review?
Yes. Yelp's blog recommends replying within 24 hours.

### Can I use Yelp reviews in my marketing?
Yelp provides embed and sharing tools and brand guidelines for this. Follow those and do not copy text without them.

### Do check-in offers still exist?
Older Yelp posts recommend them, but their current status could not be verified on 2026-11-01 because Yelp's support site was not reachable. Check your Business Account before relying on them.
