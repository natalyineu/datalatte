## What Do Quebec's French Rules Mean for Your Marketing?

Educaloi summarises the rules this way: public signs and commercial advertising must generally be in French, and another language can appear alongside only if French is clearly dominant, meaning French takes at least twice the space and is at least as legible. A business with a Quebec address that sells to Quebecers needs a French website, and e-commerce sites must show all purchase information in French.

Anything written on a product sold in Quebec, its package, or the documents with it must also be in French. Check the details with the Office quebecois de la langue francaise (OQLF) or a lawyer before launching.

## What Changed on June 1, 2025?

DLA Piper reported in June 2025 (secondary source: law firm) that changes to the Charter took effect on June 1, 2025. A recognized trademark, registered or not, may appear in another language in advertising if no French version appears in the Trademarks Act register. Storefront signs visible from outside that show a non-French trademark or business name must also include French terms that are markedly predominant.

Practical step: if your brand name is English, add a French descriptor or slogan that is clearly larger on signs and ads. Confirm the exact test with OQLF.

## What Are the Penalties?

Per Educaloi, the OQLF can first send a warning with a deadline to comply. If you do not comply in time, the fine is $3,000 to $30,000 for each day the offence continues. It doubles for a second offence and triples for any additional one. You have 30 days from receiving a warning to contest it before the Tribunal administratif du Quebec, and a court can order non-compliant signs or ads removed or destroyed.

## Does CASL Affect Email and Text Marketing?

Yes. Canada's anti-spam law asks businesses to get consent before sending commercial electronic messages, and the government's overview says unsolicited text messages fall inside its definition of spam. The overview page does not state identification rules, unsubscribe timing or penalties, so read the full law or the regulator's compliance pages before building a list.

Practical step: collect clear opt-in on forms, record when and how, and send in French to Quebec customers. For channel choice see [SMS vs email](/blog/sms-vs-email-marketing-local-business-which-is-better).

## FAQ

### Do I need a French website to sell in Quebec?

Educaloi says a business with a Quebec address that sells to Quebecers must have a French version of its website. Rules for businesses outside Quebec differ, so ask OQLF or a lawyer.

### Can I advertise in English only?

Generally no: commercial advertising must generally be in French, with another language allowed only if French is clearly dominant. Trademark rules have exceptions.

### How much should a Quebec business spend on Google Ads?

No Quebec figure exists. At $5.42 a click, $600 a month buys roughly 110 clicks. Start small.

### Does CASL apply to texts?

The government overview says unsolicited text messages are covered. Get consent before sending commercial messages.
