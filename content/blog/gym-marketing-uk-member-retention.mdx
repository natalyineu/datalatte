## How Big Is the UK Gym Market in 2026?

The UK Health & Fitness Market Report 2026, reported by Health Club Management on 9 April 2026, counts a record 12.2 million members, 18% of over-16s, in 5,842 clubs. That is up 4.2% on clubs since 2024. Visits reached 679 million, up 10%, and total income £6.5 billion. The report was commissioned by ukactive and analysed by Grant Thornton UK.

LeisureDB's 2025 report used a different method and counted penetration at 16.6% for the 12 months to March 2025. The two are not comparable, so quote one at a time. Neither article gives a churn or retention figure.

## What Is a Realistic Churn Rate?

There is no published UK figure in the sources checked. Figures you may see online, such as "members cancel within 4.7 months", come without a named dataset, so treat them as unverified. Your own data is the benchmark.

Track three numbers monthly:

- **Monthly churn:** members who cancelled divided by members at the start of the month
- **First-90-day churn:** members who left within 90 days of joining
- **Active share:** members who visited at least once in the last 14 days

Keep the first-90-day number separate. If it is much higher than overall churn, your onboarding is the problem rather than your price. For wider tactics see our [fitness studio membership retention guide](/blog/fitness-studio-membership-retention-tactics).

## How Do You Spot Members About to Leave?

Look at attendance, not complaints. Most booking and door-entry systems can list members who have not visited in a set number of days. Pick a threshold that suits your classes, such as 14 days, and review that list weekly.

This is a practical rule, not a published finding. Peer-reviewed studies of gym dropout link low visit frequency to leaving, but the specific papers could not be opened here, and vendor claims about exact thresholds have no primary source. Test your own threshold: after a quarter, check how many members past it later cancelled.

## What Should Happen in the First Month?

Many leavers go early, so design the first weeks on purpose. A simple sequence an independent gym can run by hand:

1. Day 1: a short induction and a booked first session
2. Day 7: a message from a named coach asking how it went
3. Day 14: check whether the member has attended twice
4. Day 30: a goal review and a booked next session

Keep messages personal and short. A phone or WhatsApp message from a coach tends to feel different from a bulk email, though no source here measures the difference.

## Can You Message Members Freely?

Not for marketing. Texts and emails that promote offers fall under UK electronic marketing rules, which usually need consent or a narrow exemption. The Information Commissioner's Office guidance could not be opened during this update because its site was down for maintenance, so read its direct marketing pages before you build any automated campaign. A message that only confirms a booking is different from one that promotes a package.

## What About the New UK Subscription Rules?

The Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 brings a subscription contracts regime with pre-contract information, renewal reminders and cooling-off rights. It is not in force yet. A law-firm update from April 2026 expected spring 2027, and later law-firm commentary points to January 2027, which gov.uk has not confirmed here. These are secondary sources, and the April update does not mention gyms, so check whether your membership terms are covered with a solicitor or on gov.uk before changing contracts.

## Worked Example: What One Fewer Cancellation Is Worth

All inputs are **assumptions** for an independent gym, not benchmarks.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Members | 400 |
| Monthly fee | £35 |
| Monthly churn today | 5% |
| Monthly churn after changes | 4% |
| Members lost per month, today | 20 |
| Members lost per month, after | 16 |
| Average months a member stays (1 divided by churn) | 20 now, 25 after |
| Revenue per member over their stay | £700 now, £875 after |

Cutting churn by one point keeps four more members each month, and each is worth about £175 more over their stay under these assumptions. Four members kept each month at about £175 each is £700 of extra revenue per month of cancellations avoided, before costs. If your real churn differs, redo the sums. The first-90-day figure matters most.

## FAQ

### What is the average gym churn rate in the UK?

No reliable public UK figure was found in the sources checked. Calculate your own monthly churn and your first-90-day churn.

### How many people belong to a UK gym?

ukactive's 2026 report, as reported by Health Club Management, counts a record 12.2 million members, 18% of over-16s. LeisureDB's 2025 report put penetration at 16.6%.

### When do the new UK subscription rules start?

They are not yet in force. Reports conflict between January and spring 2027, and secondary legislation is still awaited. Check gov.uk.

### Can I text lapsed members an offer?

Promotional texts and emails usually need consent or a narrow exemption under UK rules. Read the ICO direct marketing guidance first.
