The most effective local marketing in Mongolia starts with Facebook (business pages and neighborhood groups), Instagram for visual businesses, and a complete Google Business Profile so you appear on Google Maps. Add Google Ads for high-intent searches once those basics work, and make it easy to pay and book with local tools such as QPay and SocialPay. Facebook's advertising audience in Mongolia was about 2.7 million people in late 2025, roughly three quarters of the population ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mongolia), checked Oct 2026), so it is the obvious first channel. One catch for Google Ads: Mongolian is not on Google's list of languages for ad language targeting (see below).

This guide covers the main platforms, what to promote around local holidays, and how to plan a modest budget for a café, salon, gym or tour business in Ulaanbaatar (UB) and beyond.

## Why Mongolia Is Different

Mongolia had about 3.53 million people in October 2025, and 69.5% of them lived in urban areas ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mongolia)). Internet penetration was 83%, and mobile connections equalled 141% of the population. That makes Ulaanbaatar (UB) the natural focus for most local businesses, with online reach mattering even more outside the capital, where towns are small and far apart.

For marketers this means two things: city-level targeting in Ulaanbaatar is where most small businesses will win first, and tourism or export-style businesses should think in English as well as Mongolian.

## Mongolia's Digital Platform Landscape

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report gives these advertising-audience figures for late 2025. User numbers change every year, so check current figures in each platform's ad tool before you plan.

| Platform | Reach in Mongolia (late 2025) | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | 2.70 million, 76.5% of the population | Almost every local business: pages, groups, Messenger chats |
| Messenger | 2.30 million, 65.2% of the population | Bookings and customer questions |
| Instagram | 1.20 million, 34.0% of the population | Cafés, beauty, fashion, restaurants |
| YouTube, TikTok, WhatsApp | Not verified here; check the ad tools | Tutorials and tours, short promos, international enquiries |
| Google | Main search engine for most users (not measured in the report) | Search ads, Google Maps listings |

Source: [DataReportal, Digital 2026: Mongolia](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mongolia).

**Facebook first.** Neighborhood and community groups across Ulaanbaatar are an important place where people ask for recommendations. A helpful, non-spammy presence in relevant groups, plus a well-maintained business page that replies quickly on Messenger, is a low-cost starting point.

**Instagram for visual businesses.** Cafés, restaurants and beauty businesses do well with strong photos, short videos and before-and-after content. If you want a framework, our guide to [Instagram marketing for coffee shops](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-coffee-shops) and [Instagram marketing for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons) translates well.

**Payments matter.** QPay is a QR payment system that lets merchants accept payments from its wallet and from customers of almost all Mongolian banks ([QPay Merchant app listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1501873756)), and SocialPay is a Mongolian digital wallet from Golomt Bank ([Golomt Bank](https://www.golomtbank.com/en/news/8711)). Accepting the payment methods your customers already use removes friction at checkout and when taking bookings.

## Google Ads and Google Maps in Mongolia

Google Ads accounts can be set up with Mongolia as the billing country ([Google Ads terms by region](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16875158?hl=en)). Language is the catch: Mongolian is not on Google's list of languages for ad language targeting, while English and Russian are ([Google Ads Help: languages](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6333734?hl=en)). Test how your Mongolian-language keywords perform with broader language settings, and watch the search terms report closely. Costs per click depend on your industry, keywords and the season, so start with a small budget and use Keyword Planner and your own results rather than rule-of-thumb figures.

Practical steps:

1. **Claim and complete your Google Business Profile** with Mongolian and, where relevant, English names, accurate hours, photos and categories. Our [Google Business Profile optimization checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) applies in any country.
2. **Run search ads for high-intent phrases** with your district or neighborhood in the keyword.
3. **Send ad clicks to a mobile-friendly page** with a clear phone number, map link and booking or messaging option.

### Ulaanbaatar District Keywords (Mongolian Cyrillic)

Examples to adapt and test, with English translations (we have not verified search volume for these phrases, so check them in Keyword Planner):

```
"гоо засалын газар Улаанбаатар Сүхбаатар" (beauty salon UB Sukhbaatar)
"кофе шоп УБ Баянгол" (coffee shop UB Bayangol)
"фитнес клуб Улаанбаатар Хан-Уул" (fitness club UB Khan-Uul)
"үсчин Улаанбаатар" (hairdresser Ulaanbaatar)
"spa Улаанбаатар" (spa Ulaanbaatar)
```

**Key UB districts:** Сүхбаатар (Sukhbaatar, central), Баянгол (Bayangol), Хан-Уул (Khan-Uul, southern), Баянзүрх (Bayanzürkh, eastern), Сонгинохайрхан (Songinokhairkhan, western) and Чингэлтэй (Chingeltei, central north). Use these in ad groups and Google Business Profile descriptions so you match how locals search.

## Sample Marketing Mixes for Three Business Types

These are illustrative planning examples, not real client results. Adjust the split to your own costs and test results.

| Business | Main channels | Suggested focus |
|---|---|---|
| Specialty coffee shop in UB | Instagram, Facebook groups, Google Maps, small Google Ads test | Photos of drinks and space, loyalty offers, QPay |
| Beauty salon in UB | Instagram transformation content, Facebook, TikTok, Google Maps | Before-and-after posts, booking via Messenger or WhatsApp |
| Gobi or steppe tour operator | English-language Instagram, Google Search Ads in English, TripAdvisor, WhatsApp | Keywords like "Mongolia horse trekking" and "Gobi Desert tour" |

A sensible way to budget: start with a small monthly amount split roughly between search ads, social ads and content, then move money toward whichever channel produces the most enquiries. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) explains how to set that split.

## Mongolian Marketing Calendar

| Period | Opportunity |
|---|---|
| Late January to early March | Tsagaan Sar (Lunar New Year); 18 to 20 February in 2026, and the date moves with the lunar calendar |
| June 1 | Children's Day, a public holiday |
| Around July 11 to 13 | Naadam Festival; public holidays around it run roughly July 10 to 15 |
| November 26 | Republic Day, an official public holiday |

Dates from [Wikipedia: Public holidays in Mongolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_holidays_in_Mongolia).

**Tsagaan Sar.** The "White Month" involves family feasts, gifts and traditional dress, so beauty salons, restaurants and gift businesses often see strong demand. Start promotions a few weeks ahead.

**Naadam.** The national festival of wrestling, horse racing and archery brings heavy local and tourist activity in July. Start campaigns early in the month for food, clothing and tourism offers.

Always confirm holiday dates for the current year before scheduling campaigns.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the best marketing platform for small businesses in Mongolia?**
A: Facebook is the usual starting point because about 2.7 million people in Mongolia are reachable there with ads, plus 2.3 million on Messenger (late 2025). Add Instagram if your business is visual, and Google Maps and Google Ads to capture people searching for your service.

**Q: Does Google Ads work in Mongolia?**
A: Yes, Mongolia is a supported billing country, but Mongolian is not on Google's list of ad language targeting options, so test your keywords with a small budget, target your district and measure calls and messages.

**Q: Should I market in Mongolian Cyrillic or English?**
A: Use Mongolian Cyrillic for local customers, which is the standard written form for everyday communication. Use English for tourists and international enquiries.

**Q: How do I get found on Google Maps in Ulaanbaatar?**
A: Claim and verify your Google Business Profile, choose accurate categories, add photos, keep hours up to date and ask satisfied customers for reviews.

**Q: When is the best time to run holiday campaigns in Mongolia?**
A: Tsagaan Sar and Naadam are the biggest moments. Begin promotions a few weeks before and confirm exact dates each year.
