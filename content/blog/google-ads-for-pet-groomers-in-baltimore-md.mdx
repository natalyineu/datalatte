## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Pet Groomer in Baltimore?

The national all-industry average is $5.42 per click, a 6.64% click-through rate and $66.69 per lead. Animals & Pets is cheaper than that at $4.06 per click and $31.50 per lead. Animals & Pets is a broad category, so it also includes shops that sell products, not only service businesses.

Baltimore had 585,708 residents in the 2020 census (Wikipedia, citing the census, secondary), and sits inside the Washington-Baltimore area of 9.97 million people. Neighbourhoods such as Canton, Fells Point, Federal Hill and Hampden are separate shopping districts, so a groomer in one may draw few customers from another. No published pet-ownership figure for Baltimore was located, so this page makes no claim about it.

LocaliQ does not break these averages down by city. Competition in Baltimore may push your clicks above or below the figure, and the only way to find out is to run a small campaign and read the report.

## Which Keywords Should a Pet Groomer in Baltimore Start With?

Start with searches that name the service and the place, plus the "near me" variants. Group them by service so each ad matches what the person typed.

| Keyword idea | Intent | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| dog groomer near me | Very high | Core ad group, exact and phrase match |
| pet grooming Baltimore | High | Core ad group with the city name |
| cat grooming near me | High | Separate ad group if you groom cats |
| mobile pet groomer Baltimore | Medium | Only if you offer mobile service |
| dog grooming prices | Research | Test carefully, many clicks are price checks |

These are starting ideas, not measured volumes. Google does not publish keyword volumes by city in a citable form, so check the Keyword Planner in your own account. Our [keyword list for this niche](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-pet-groomers-in-2026) goes deeper.

## How Do You Keep Ads Inside Your Service Area?

Location settings are the main cost control. Google's help says radius targeting shows ads to customers within a set distance of your business and needs a radius of at least 1 km. Very small radii may show ads "intermittently or not at all", so do not go below that.

Google recommends "Presence or interest" for Search, and says "Presence" alone fits advertisers who only want people physically in the chosen locations. For a local business that people travel to, test "Presence" if your search terms report shows clicks from people who cannot reach you. If your shop sits near the city line, check whether you want Baltimore County residents. Add them by radius, not by guessing.

## What Should You Track?

Set up conversion tracking before launch. Google says you pay for call asset clicks only when the forwarding number in the ad is clicked, and that you choose the minimum call length that counts as a conversion. Pick a length that matches a real booking call, not a wrong number.

Track these four actions: phone calls from ads, online booking submissions, direction requests from your Business Profile and clicks on your booking button. A complete Business Profile supports every ad, so work through our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) first.

## What Mistakes Cost the Most?

Three mistakes show up in most small local accounts. Targeting a whole metro area when customers come from a few miles away wastes clicks. Skipping negative keywords lets irrelevant searches match. Judging the campaign after one week ignores the learning period.

Google explains that negative keywords exclude search terms and do not match close variants, so add singular and plural forms yourself. Review the Search terms report weekly and keep a list of what each negative blocked. ## FAQ

### How much should a pet groomer in Baltimore spend on Google Ads?

There is no fixed figure. At the national category average of $4.06 per click, $800 buys about 197 clicks. That is enough to learn which keywords convert. Scale up only after you know your cost per booked grooming appointment.

### Is Google Ads worth it for a pet groomer in Baltimore?

It can be if the cost per booked grooming appointment is lower than what a customer pays over several visits. The benchmark cost per lead is $31.50, but a lead is not a booking. Run the example above with your own prices.

### How long before results show up?

Google does not publish a standard learning period that could be cited. Plan to review the account weekly and judge it on at least a full month of data.

### Which Baltimore neighbourhoods should I target?

Start with the neighbourhood your shop is in and the ones within a short drive. Use the Search terms report and your booking records to see where customers actually live before you widen the radius.
