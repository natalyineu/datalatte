The best Google Ads keywords for a real estate agent are local, high-intent phrases: a city or neighbourhood plus "real estate agent", "realtor", "sell my house", "home valuation" or "buyer's agent". WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put the average real estate click at $3.22 and the average cost per lead at $102.51, so every wasted click matters.

Google Keyword Planner shows search volume and cost estimates for your own city, and those change by market. This guide therefore gives keyword groups to test, not a table of volumes. Example inputs are labelled assumptions.

## Which Keywords Should a Real Estate Agent Bid On?

Group keywords by what the searcher wants to do, then add your city or area. Test these groups and keep what produces enquiries:

| Intent group | Example keyword patterns (add your city) | Who searches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hire an agent | real estate agent, realtor, buyer's agent, listing agent | Buyers and sellers choosing an agent |
| Sell a home | sell my house, list my home, how much is my home worth, home valuation | Sellers, often early stage |
| Buy a home | first time home buyer agent, buyer's agent for condos | Buyers ready to talk |
| Property type | condos for sale, townhouses for sale, homes for sale | Mostly browsing; test carefully |
| Area expertise | neighbourhood name plus real estate agent | Local searchers |

Listing-style searches such as "homes for sale" are often served by portals and may cost clicks without enquiries. Put your budget first behind "hire an agent" and "sell a home" groups.

None of these patterns has a published volume or CPC in this guide. Check each in Keyword Planner, which Google describes as giving estimates of monthly searches and the average cost to show on a keyword.

## Does "Near Me" Work for Real Estate Agents?

Sometimes. "Realtor near me" and "real estate agent near me" fit the hire-an-agent group. Whether these convert better than city terms is something to measure in your own account, since no benchmark in this guide splits them out.

A simpler rule: keep location in the keyword or in the campaign targeting, not just one of them, and check the Search terms report for places you do not serve.

## Which Match Type Should You Start With?

Google's definitions: exact match shows ads on searches with the same meaning or intent and gives the most control but the fewest searches. Phrase match covers searches that include the meaning of your keyword, including more specific forms. Broad match is the default type and reaches the widest set of searches.

Google says broad match works best with Smart Bidding, and recommends Smart Bidding with all match types. A cautious start for a small budget is phrase and exact on your core terms, adding broad only once you have conversion data and a negative list. That is a practical suggestion, not a Google rule. ## What Negative Keywords Should Agents Add?

Negative keywords stop your ad showing on unrelated searches. Start with groups like these and read your Search terms report weekly:

- Jobs and training: "jobs", "salary", "license", "course", "exam"
- Rentals, if you do not handle them: "apartments for rent", "rent"
- Other property types you do not sell
- Agent-side searches: "commission rate", "become a realtor"

Google notes that negative keywords do not match close variants, so "flowers" does not block "red flower". Add plurals and variants yourself. Broad-match negatives block a search only when it contains all the negative terms. Our [negative keywords guide](/blog/google-ads-negative-keywords-stop-wasting-budget) goes deeper.

## What Targeting Is Restricted for Real Estate Ads?

Google's housing policy covers homes for sale or rental and real estate services. In the United States and Canada you cannot target by gender, age, parental status, marital status or ZIP codes. Radius targeting (at least 1 km), city and country targeting are allowed, and in Canada the first three characters of the postal code are acceptable.

So build campaigns around the cities and radii you serve. Enforcement follows the targeted geography, not where you are based.

## FAQ

### What are the best Google Ads keywords for real estate agents?

Local, high-intent phrases that pair your city or neighbourhood with "real estate agent", "realtor", "sell my house" or "home valuation". Test by intent group and keep what produces enquiries.

### How much does a click cost for real estate?

WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmark lists $3.22 per click, a 7.61% click-through rate, a 3.70% conversion rate and $102.51 per lead. Your market may be higher or lower.

### Can I target by ZIP code?

Not in the United States or Canada. Google's housing policy prohibits ZIP code targeting there. Use radius or city targeting instead.

### Should I use broad match?

Google says it works with Smart Bidding. On a small budget, start with phrase and exact, and add broad when you have conversion data and negatives.

### How many negative keywords do I need?

Google gives no required number. Add terms from your Search terms report weekly. Add plurals separately because negatives do not match close variants.
