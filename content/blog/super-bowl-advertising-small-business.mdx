## How Much Does a Super Bowl Ad Cost?

AdWave reports the 2026 price at approximately $8 million for 30 seconds, citing industry reports, SuperBowl-Ads.com and Nielsen, and says total campaign costs run from $15 million to $50 million. Some outlets report premium slots near $10 million. The sources differ because $8 million is the standard rate and $10 million is the top of the market. Those are national prices for airtime.

The page checked gives no price for local or regional broadcast spots, so this article does not quote one. Ask your local station directly if you want a quote. AdWave is a reseller and its figures are secondary.

## Why Do Local Businesses Care About the Super Bowl?

Because fans spend. The National Retail Federation surveyed 7,791 adults on January 2 to 8, 2026. It projects a record 213.1 million US adults would watch, 121.1 million would host or attend a party and 18.2 million would watch at a bar or restaurant. Projected spending reached $20.2 billion, or $94.77 per person.

That helps food, drink, delivery and party businesses most. NRF numbers are projections from a survey, not sales data for any shop. Fitness studios, salons and groomers can still use the moment through new-year offers, but the source does not measure that.

## Does Game Day Streaming Matter?

It does for reach, and it changes year to year. NBC, Peacock, Telemundo, NBC Sports Digital and NFL+ carried Super Bowl LX, and Nielsen's count combined them.

Do not assume you can place your own ad inside the game. A local test targets viewers on other shows around the game, not the broadcast itself. Roku states pricing is dynamic and based on demand, so a high-demand week may cost more than usual. Check your account for the current price.

## Is It Worth It? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for a pizzeria.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $600 over two weeks before the game |
| CPM | $30 |
| Ad views | 20,000 |
| Offer | Party tray pre-order with a short link |
| Pre-orders credited | 12 |
| Average order | $85 |
| Gross margin | 35% |

Twelve orders at $85 is $1,020 in revenue and about $357 in margin, against $600 in spend. Under these assumptions the test loses money on the first orders. It would break even only with about 21 orders, or with repeat customers. Lower the cost by tightening the area and shortening the flight, and count orders by code.

## FAQ

### Can a small business really advertise during the Super Bowl?
Not in the broadcast on a small budget; it costs about $8 million for 30 seconds nationally. It can advertise on CTV around the game from $500.

### How many people watch the Super Bowl?
Nielsen's final figure for Super Bowl LX was 125.6 million viewers across NBC, Peacock, Telemundo, NBC Sports Digital and NFL+.

### Which local businesses benefit most?
Food and drink businesses, based on NRF's projected party and bar viewing. NRF does not break out spending by category in the pages checked.

### When should the ads run?
Start planning a few weeks before. Roku requires campaigns to start on a future date and review takes 2 to 3 days, so allow time.

### How do I measure results?
Use a code or short link that appears only in the ad, and ask customers how they heard of you.
