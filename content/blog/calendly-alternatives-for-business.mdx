## What Does Calendly Include, and Where Does It Fall Short?

Calendly's free plan lists one event type and one connected calendar, with no payments. Standard is $10 per seat a month billed yearly and adds unlimited event types, Stripe and PayPal payments and automated reminders. Teams is $16 per seat a month billed yearly and adds round-robin scheduling. Enterprise starts at $15,000 a year with a 50-seat minimum.

The gaps for service businesses are not on the page: Calendly's pricing page does not mention deposits, packages or SMS reminders, so confirm those before relying on them.

## Which Alternative Fits Which Business?

Appointment businesses with one practitioner can use Acuity Starter. It lists Stripe, Square, PayPal and Venmo payments and email reminders, with text reminders from Standard.

Team businesses with tight budgets can start with Setmore Free, then move to Pro when they need SMS reminders and two-way calendar sync. Hair and beauty teams that need stylist-level calendars should compare Vagaro and Fresha, and the [salon-specific Calendly guide](/blog/best-calendly-alternative-for-salons-spas) covers them. Home service crews can look at Housecall Pro, which lists Basic for 1 user and Essentials for 5 users at $149 a month on annual billing.

## What Are the Hidden Costs of Scheduling Software?

Look for four things on the pricing page: per-user pricing, caps on bookings, paid text messages and payment processing. SimplyBook.me caps its free plan at 50 bookings a month. Fresha charges $0.02 per text after 100 free and 2.79% + $0.20 on online payments. Vagaro raises its price after the first six months.

A tool that looks $10 cheaper can cost more once card fees are included, so estimate both for one typical month.

## How Do You Switch Without Losing Bookings?

Run the new page alongside the old one for two weeks, then replace the Calendly link in your email signature, Google Business Profile and social bios. Whether a vendor imports your booking history is not stated on the pricing pages used here, so ask support before you cancel. An AI agent for appointment booking can sit on top of any of these tools.

## FAQ

### What is the best free Calendly alternative?

Setmore Free covers up to four users and Cal.com Free covers one user with unlimited event types, per their pricing pages. SimplyBook.me Free allows 50 bookings a month.

### Is Calendly good for salons?

Calendly is built for meetings. A salon needs staff calendars, deposits and client records, which salon tools such as Vagaro and Fresha are designed around.

### How much does a scheduling tool cost?

From $0 to about $50 a month for a small team on the plans checked here. Add card fees on top.

### Does Calendly have SMS reminders?

Its pricing page mentions automated reminders on Standard but does not mention SMS. Check before buying.
