A pet insurance agency or provider should run Google Ads on Search for high-intent terms, check state licensing and disclosure rules before launch, and judge results by cost per policy, not cost per click. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks list Finance and Insurance at $3.39 per click and a 2.64% conversion rate. LocaliQ has no pet insurance row, so treat that as a rough guide.

Only licensed producers can sell insurance, so this guide is for agencies, brokers and insurers. A groomer or vet who wants to refer clients should check their state's rules first. Examples marked as assumptions are illustrations.

## How Big Is the Pet Insurance Market?

The American Pet Products Association reports 95 million US households own a pet (2025 National Pet Owners Survey) and $158 billion was spent on pets in 2024. Insurance is not broken out; it sits within a $14.3 billion "Other Services" category.

NAPHIA's 2026 report, as covered by Insurance Business, puts US penetration at 4.27% of pets (5.99% of dogs, 2.29% of cats), with insured pets up 9% in a year. Insurance Business is a secondary source, since NAPHIA's own page blocked our fetch. Low penetration means most searchers are first-time buyers who need explanation.

## What Does Google Ads Cost for Insurance?

LocaliQ's 2026 Finance and Insurance row shows $3.39 per click, 9.83% click-through, 2.64% conversion and $74.44 per lead. The all-industry average is $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead. The page has no insurance-only or pet-specific figure, and the category is broad, so your own account will differ.

Conversion here is low because buyers compare plans. Expect many clicks before one quote request, and plan the budget around that.

## What Compliance Rules Apply?

Google's financial products and services policy says you must comply with state and local regulations for any location your ads target, and that verification is required to advertise financial services in some locations. Disclosures cannot sit in roll-over text or behind another link or tab.

The policy page does not mention pet insurance specifically, so confirm the current rules in Google Ads before building. NAIC says pet insurance is technically a property and casualty policy and adopted a Pet Insurance Model Act in 2022 covering disclosures and preexisting conditions. States adopt it separately, so check each state you target.

## Which Searches Should You Target?

Start with comparison and cost-intent searches from owners ready to buy, and add the specific pet and breed. Use Keyword Planner for volume and cost estimates; it needs a Google Ads account with billing set up. Match types matter: exact match gives the most control and the least reach, while broad match can trigger related searches.

Add negatives for jobs, free and claims-related searches from existing policyholders. Google says negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals yourself. For keyword method on a neighbouring niche, see our [veterinarian keywords guide](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-veterinarians-in-2026).

## What Should the Ad and Landing Page Say?

Be plain about what the policy covers. NAIC notes that most pet insurance companies exclude preexisting and hereditary or congenital conditions, and many have waiting periods. State both on the landing page. Buyers who find that later are more likely to cancel and complain.

Keep the page to one action, such as get a quote, and put licence details and required disclosures where they are visible.

## FAQ

### How much does a pet insurance click cost?

LocaliQ publishes no pet insurance row. Its closest category, Finance and Insurance, averages $3.39 per click in 2026. Your cost depends on competition and location.

### Can a groomer or vet advertise pet insurance?

Only if licensed to sell it. Insurance is regulated by state, and NAIC's model act covers producer licensing. Check your state department of insurance before advertising.

### Does Google restrict insurance ads?

Google's financial services policy requires compliance with state and local rules and verification in some locations. It does not name pet insurance, so confirm current rules before launch.

### How many pet owners are there in the US?

APPA reports 95 million households with a pet in its 2025 survey.

### What conversion rate should I expect?

LocaliQ shows 2.64% for Finance and Insurance. Treat it as a benchmark, not a promise.
