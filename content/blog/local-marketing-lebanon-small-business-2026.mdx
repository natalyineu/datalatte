Small businesses in Lebanon can either hire a marketing agency or run a small test themselves. Sortlist's Lebanon directory lists 39 agencies, and two of them show starting prices of €3,000 and €5,000 for marketing work. Those figures are agency entry points, not ad costs. This guide compares agency pricing, the services agencies bundle, and a low-cost test budget that can run in-house before a retainer is signed.

The guide does not quote platform reach, national economic figures or legal rules. The sources available for this rewrite did not contain verified figures for those topics, so they were left out rather than estimated.

## How Many Marketing Agencies Serve Lebanon?

Sortlist's Lebanon marketing page lists 39 agencies and consultants. Each profile shows a review score, team size and starting price. The page also lets a business post a brief and receive proposals, which makes it a practical shortlist tool. It is an aggregator, however, and not an independent audit of agency quality.

Profiles are self-reported. Nascode describes itself as a Meta and Google badged partner, and intouch says it has more than 25 years of experience across the Middle East. Any credential should be checked directly with the platform. Meta and Google both publish partner directories that can be searched by company name.

## What Do Lebanese Agencies Charge to Start?

Two profiles on the Sortlist page show starting prices for the marketing category. intouch lists from €3,000 and Nascode lists from €5,000. Neither page states whether the price is monthly, per project or per campaign. Ask each agency for its billing structure in writing before comparing the two figures.

The same profiles show team size. Nascode lists 11 to 50 members and intouch lists 51 to 200. Larger teams usually suit multi-channel campaigns. A business with one location and one main offer may not need that scope, and paying for it can leave the budget thin for ads themselves.

## What Services Do Lebanese Agencies Bundle?

Spirit, a Beirut agency, describes its offer as branding, communication campaigns, media services, digital marketing, online and social media services, and PR and events management. Its site lists social media management and online presence optimisation among its online services.

Bundling can simplify coordination, but it also means paying for services that may go unused. A local business often needs three things first: an accurate Google Business Profile, regular posts on Instagram or Facebook, and a clear way to receive enquiries. Ask each agency which of these it will deliver and which it will hand back to the client.

Service businesses that take bookings have a separate need. The [Jobber vs ServiceTitan guide](/blog/jobber-vs-servicetitan-small-business-local-business) compares scheduling software, which can matter more than an agency's creative work once enquiries arrive.

## Worked Example: Agency Retainer or Test Budget?

All inputs below are **assumptions** for planning. They are not quotes from any agency.

| Option | Basis | Yearly cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agency at intouch listed start | €3,000 × 12 (assumption: monthly billing) | €36,000 |
| Agency at Nascode listed start | €5,000 × 12 (assumption: monthly billing) | €60,000 |
| In-house test | €300 ad budget × 4 months (assumption) | €1,200 |

The in-house test costs about 3.3% of the lower agency option over a year. It will not replace an agency, but it can show whether an offer, page and message convert enquiries before money is committed to a retainer. Track results in a simple spreadsheet with one row per week: spend, enquiries, bookings and cost per enquiry.

## How Should You Vet an Agency Before Hiring?

1. Ask for the billing structure: monthly fee, project fee or campaign spend, and whether ad spend is billed separately.
2. Ask which platform partner badges the agency holds, then check them in the platform's own directory.
3. Request one past client example that can be verified directly with that client.
4. Confirm who owns the ad accounts, pages and customer data at the end of the contract.
5. Agree on the metric the work will be judged by, such as enquiries or bookings, before the first invoice.
6. Set a review date, usually after one to three months, and a clear exit term.

If social posting will be handled in-house, compare scheduling tools in the [Hootsuite vs Sprout Social guide](/blog/hootsuite-vs-sprout-social-small-business-local-business) before buying a plan.

## Can Sortlist Ratings Be Trusted?

The ratings are useful but limited. Nascode shows 4.9 from 12 reviews and intouch shows 4.9 from 8 reviews. Both samples are small, so one unhappy client can move the average noticeably. Read the written reviews, look for specific outcomes, and note whether the reviewer's business resembles yours in size and sector.

## What Does This Guide Not Cover?

The source pages opened for this rewrite included government and labour statistics sites, but their retrieved text did not contain Lebanon-specific figures for internet use, ad reach, GDP, inflation or data protection law. Those topics need a fresh, verified source before any number is quoted. Until then, ad costs and reach should be checked inside the business's own ad account, and a Lebanese adviser should be asked about any legal rules that affect email or SMS marketing.
