Lebanon is one of the most connected small markets in the region: DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 5.38 million internet users, 91.8% of a population of 5.86 million. Facebook has an ad reach of 3.45 million and Instagram 2.95 million. The economy is the hard part, since World Bank data shows GDP fell 7.1% in 2024 and inflation was 14.6% in 2025.

## How Connected Are Lebanese Customers?

Almost everyone is online. Only 483 thousand people (8.2%) are offline, and social media user identities number 4.58 million, or 78.1% of the population. Cellular connections total 4.76 million, 81.3% of the population, which is a lower count than internet users (people can connect over Wi-Fi without a mobile plan).

For a local business, this means reach is not the limit. Competition for attention and customer spending power are the limits, so the work is in clear offers, fast replies and a strong local profile.

## What Does the Economy Mean for Your Budget?

World Bank figures put GDP at US$25.97 billion in 2024 and GDP per person at about US$4,473, with a 7.1% contraction that year. Consumer price inflation was 14.6% in 2025. When prices move this fast, review your pricing and ad spend monthly and keep offers short-dated.

Set a small test budget first and measure in messages, calls and visits. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to size spend against revenue.

## What Does Lebanese Law Say About Marketing Messages?

Lebanon's Law No. 81 of 2018 covers electronic transactions and personal data. According to DLA Piper's country guide (secondary source, last reviewed in 2022), unsolicited marketing emails that use a real person's name and address are prohibited without that person's consent. An exception applies where the sender obtained the address lawfully through a prior engagement. People can also object to the use of their data for marketing.

The same guide says Lebanon has no national data protection authority. Processing generally requires a declaration to the Ministry of Economy and Trade, with exemptions, including processing with the data subject's agreement. The guide may be out of date and we could not read the Arabic law text, so treat these points as **unverified** and ask a Lebanese lawyer before you run bulk email or SMS.

Instagram and Facebook get the largest share because they combine large reach with direct messaging. After four weeks, compare cost per enquiry in each channel and move money to the cheapest. The split is a starting point and your account data replaces it.

## FAQ

### Is Instagram or Facebook better in Lebanon?

Facebook has more ad reach (3.45 million against 2.95 million), but the gap is small. Test both.

### Can I trust the TikTok figure for Lebanon?

No. DataReportal reports 113.4% of adults, a value it flags as possibly implausible.

### Are there verified ad prices for Lebanon?

None could be verified. Use your own account data.

### Can I email customers without asking?

DLA Piper's guide says unsolicited marketing to a named person needs consent, with exceptions. This is unverified, so confirm with a local lawyer.
