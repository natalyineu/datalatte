## What Does FreshBooks Cost?

FreshBooks showed four plans. Lite is $23 a month regular price and $4.60 under an "80% off for 4 months" promo, with 5 billable clients. Plus is $43 regular ($8.60 promo) for 50 clients. Premium is $70 regular ($14 promo) for unlimited clients. Select is custom: its prices were not visible and it says to talk to a consultant.

Extra team members cost $11 a month each. Select includes 2 team member accounts. Payroll is an add-on at $40 a month plus $6 per user, and Advanced Payments is a $20 a month add-on on the three lower plans.

FreshBooks states a 30-day free trial with no credit card. The page has a yearly toggle, but yearly prices were not visible in the content read.

## What Does QuickBooks Cost?

QuickBooks listed Simple Start at $38 regular and $19 promo, Essentials at $85 and $42.50, Plus at $140 and $70, and Advanced at $340 and $170. The promo was 50% off for 3 months. User limits are 1, 3, 5 and 25. The page also lists a Free plan for 1 user.

The page did not give the promo's fine print or say whether prices assume monthly or annual billing.

## Who Should Pick FreshBooks and Who Should Pick QuickBooks?

These are decision rules, not vendor claims.

- **Pick FreshBooks** if you bill under about 50 different clients, work mostly alone or with one helper, and mainly send invoices and estimates.
- **Pick QuickBooks** if you need bills, a bookkeeper or accountant using the same system, or several staff logins.
- **Check your client count first.** A cafe or salon with hundreds of walk-in customers rarely has 50 billable clients; a studio billing a few dozen regular accounts might.
- **Skip both for now** if you only send a handful of invoices a month and the free QuickBooks plan covers it.

Ask your accountant which they already use. That alone often decides it.

## What Should You Test During the Free Trial?

Use the 30-day FreshBooks trial and the QuickBooks trial to run your real month, not a demo. Send three genuine invoices, record five expenses, and connect your bank feed. Then ask your accountant to look at the reports.

Two checks matter most. First, count your active billable clients against the plan limit, since FreshBooks Lite stops at 5 and Plus at 50 per the pricing page. Second, add the add-ons you will really need, such as payroll ($40 a month plus $6 per user on FreshBooks) or Advanced Payments ($20 a month), and recompute the monthly total.

Write the final monthly cost on paper before the promo ends. The promo prices on both pages are temporary, so the regular price is the one to budget for.

## FAQ

### Is FreshBooks cheaper than QuickBooks?

At the regular prices listed on 24 October 2026, yes for small client counts: $23 and $43 against $38 and $85. The comparison changes as clients or users grow.

### What is a billable client in FreshBooks?

It is the plan's client limit: 5 on Lite, 50 on Plus, unlimited on Premium. The page content read did not define it further, so check the FreshBooks help pages for what counts.

### Do both have a free trial?

FreshBooks states 30 days with no credit card. QuickBooks mentioned a 30-day trial without making clear which plan it covers.

### Can I run payroll in either one?

FreshBooks lists payroll as an add-on at $40 a month plus $6 per user. QuickBooks payroll is priced separately.

### Did the pages show yearly prices?

FreshBooks has a yearly toggle, but the yearly prices were not visible in what was read. QuickBooks did not state its billing basis.
