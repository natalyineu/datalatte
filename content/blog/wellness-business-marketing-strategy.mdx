A small wellness business should start with a complete Google Business Profile, a steady flow of reviews and one paid channel, usually Google Search ads, before adding anything else. In WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks, Google clicks cost $4.62 for Beauty and Personal Care, $6.17 for Health and Fitness and $7.17 for Personal Services.

This guide is for studios, practitioners and clinics selling classes, treatments or memberships in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. Every number comes from a source at the end. Where nothing is published, the text says so, and example inputs are labelled as assumptions.

## What Do Google Ads Cost Wellness Businesses?

LocaliQ has no row called wellness, so use the three nearest categories. All-industry Google Ads averages in 2026 are $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead.

| Category (Google Ads, 2026) | CPC | Conversion rate | Cost per lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beauty and Personal Care | $4.62 | 10.35% | $39.25 |
| Health and Fitness | $6.17 | 6.94% | $67.36 |
| Personal Services | $7.17 | 12.34% | $54.60 |

The spread is wide, so the right row depends on what you sell. A yoga class and a one-hour treatment behave differently. Your own account will be the real benchmark after the first month.

## Why Do Reviews Come Before Ads?

BrightLocal's 2026 review survey of 1,002 US adults found that 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses and 41% always do. Some 68% require four or more stars, and 31% will only use a business rated 4.5 or higher.

So ads that send people to a profile with few or weak reviews pay for visits that do not book. Ask every client for a review after a good session and reply to each one. Our [Google Business Profile guide for fitness studios](/blog/fitness-studio-google-business-profile-optimization) shows the setup steps, and the [25-point checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-in-2026-the-complete-guide-25-point-checklist) covers the rest.

## Which Channel Should You Pick First?

Pick one channel and run it for at least a quarter before judging it. These are the options with published data:

- **Google Search ads:** for people already searching for a class or treatment. Costs are above.
- **Facebook and Instagram lead ads:** LocaliQ's 2026 lead-campaign average for Health and Fitness is $1.84 per click and $27.11 per lead, with 7.98% conversion.
- **Free profile and reviews:** no media cost, but results depend on your location and category, and no benchmark is published.

Paid leads are not paying clients. A Facebook lead who fills a form is a weaker signal than a Google searcher who typed your treatment. Compare cost per booking, not cost per lead.

## What Can Wellness Ads Say?

Meta's Health and Wellness policy says weight loss, supplement and cosmetic ads must target people 18 or older. It also bars clickbait claims, such as promised results in a set timeframe without qualifiers, and claims to cure listed incurable conditions. General wellbeing services, such as health clubs, are exempt from the age rule.

Meta's wider ad standards add that ads must not assert or imply personal attributes, such as physical or mental health. Write about the service and what it offers, not about the viewer's condition. We did not check Google's healthcare ad policy, so read it before advertising treatments.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local?

Google says radius targeting needs at least 1 km, and that very small radiuses can make ads show only intermittently or not at all. It recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting on Search, while "Presence" suits advertisers who want only people physically in the area.

A studio that people travel to should start with a radius or city list, then check the Search terms report weekly. Add negative keywords for jobs, courses and free content. For a studio example, see [Google Ads for yoga studios](/blog/google-ads-for-yoga-studios-complete-2026-guide).

## How Do You Track Whether It Works?

Use three counts, once a month: new bookings, how each person found you, and reviews added. Ask "How did you hear about us?" at booking. Compare the count by source with what you spent.

If you use a booking platform, see whether it can tag the source. Mindbody, for example, lists tracked referral links in its pricing terms. Our [marketing automation guide for fitness studios](/blog/marketing-automation-for-fitness-studios) covers follow-up messages.

## FAQ

### What is the best marketing for a wellness business?

No source ranks channels for wellness businesses. Local search, reviews and one paid channel are the safest start because costs and policies for them are published.

### How much should a wellness business spend on ads?

There is no standard figure. At LocaliQ's $6.17 Health and Fitness click cost, $500 buys about 81 clicks. Start small and judge cost per booking.

### Do Facebook ads work for wellness studios?

LocaliQ lists $27.11 per lead for Health and Fitness lead campaigns in 2026. It does not say how many of those leads book.

### Are there ad restrictions for wellness?

Yes. Meta restricts weight loss, supplement and cosmetic ads and bars exaggerated outcomes. Check each platform's current policy for your service.

### How many reviews do I need?

BrightLocal's survey does not give a number. It shows that 68% of consumers require four or more stars, so quality and recency matter.
