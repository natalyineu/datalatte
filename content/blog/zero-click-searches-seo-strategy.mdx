A zero-click search is one where the person gets what they need on the results page and never visits a website. For a local business, that often still produces a phone call, a tap on the map or a request for directions, and those actions can be measured in your Business Profile. One 2026 compilation puts the zero-click rate for local searches such as "near me", address and hours at 72%, as [cited by Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-knowledge-panel-optimization) (secondary). The practical response is to stop judging success by website visits alone, make your profile answer the question, and count the actions that replace the click.

## How Many Local Searches End Without a Click?

Estimates vary because each study uses different data and methods. The 72% local figure comes from a third-party compilation that Adwave cites, so treat it as a direction rather than a precise rate. The wider picture points the same way. Pew Research, as cited by [StackAdapt](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/ai-search-optimization) (secondary), found that about 15% of Google searches without an AI Overview result in a click to a website.

None of these figures are specific to local businesses. What matters for you is what the searcher does next. Queries like "near me", address and hours are the ones the compilation counts as most often answered on the results page, which makes your profile details as important as your website for those searches.

## Which Actions Replace the Lost Website Visit?

Adwave's breakdown of Business Profile actions shows website clicks at 48% of all actions, calls at 21% and directions at 9%, citing Google data. Website clicks remain the largest single action, so a slow or broken site still matters. Calls and directions together are a meaningful share, and they are the actions a zero-click search is most likely to produce.

Adwave also cites RightChoice.ai in reporting that 59% of consumers use Google to research a purchase they plan to make in-store or locally. Vendor estimates for specific categories point the same way. Adwave's florist page puts a well-optimized metro profile at 50 to 200 calls per month, and its insurance page puts a suburban profile at 30 to 120 calls per month. These are vendor estimates, not benchmarks, so check them against your own Performance data before you plan around them.

## Do Photos and Profile Details Change Direction Requests?

Adwave, citing Google's 2024 guidance, reports that businesses with photos receive 42% more requests for directions and 35% more website clicks than those without. The same vendor estimates that a well-optimized cafe profile in a competitive metro can produce 500 to 2,500+ profile views a month, with 10% to 30% of those views converting to walk-ins or directions requests.

Start with photos that show the real place: the exterior so people can find you, the interior so they know what to expect, and the team and recent work. Keep hours current, including holidays. Add services with starting prices where you can. Reviews add to all of this, and the [Google reviews strategy guide](/blog/google-business-reviews-strategy) covers how to ask customers for them and how to reply.

## Can Paid Local Ads Still Produce Calls and Directions?

Yes, if the campaign is built for store goals. A Google Ads local campaign, as described by [BOFU](https://bofu.ca/en/blogs/the-top-of-the-funnel/campagnes-locales-google-ads-visites-en-magasin?srsltid=AYCX9vJS0e15piQqG8y3sEFW3l_0Q3kVp2w3YE0HjIKXL87k0VqSp2r8) (secondary), automates ad delivery optimized to generate directions, phone calls and physical visits, measured with location data. Google's [Ads Help note on Performance Max](https://business.google.com/us/accelerate/announcements/access-new-inventory-and-channel-performance-reporting-in-performance-max-campaigns/) says U.S. advertisers using Performance Max for store goals get Waze ads with no additional setup.

Cost depends on the action. Clicks on search headlines, directions and call buttons are generally billed per click, while branded logo pins on Maps from automated campaigns can be billed per thousand impressions, according to [Wizerunek Wsieci](https://wizerunekwsieci.com/local-advertising-on-google-for-small-businesses-ready-to-grow/) (secondary). Adwave estimates $1 to $5 per click for local retail searches, which it says means $500 to $2,000 a month for meaningful visibility. For comparison, INSIDEA puts high-intent pest control terms at $8 to $30 per click. Choosing between channels is covered in the [Google Ads versus Facebook Ads comparison](/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-local-business).

## How Do You Measure Calls and Directions Without Double Counting?

Measurement is where many small businesses go wrong. According to [Blast Analytics](https://www.blastanalytics.com/blog/google-adwords-tips-location-extensions) (secondary), phone numbers used from location extensions have no forwarding number, so click-to-call tells you a call was started but not the call details you get from call extensions. Plan your reporting around that gap.

INSIDEA recommends counting only calls lasting longer than 60 seconds as call conversions, which filters out wrong numbers and hang-ups. Apple Business Connect reports Direction Requests, defined as how many times someone asked for directions to the storefront, according to [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/apple-business-connect-the-google-business-profile-alternative-youre-ignoring) (secondary). For website actions, set up tracking as described in the [GA4 setup guide for local businesses](/blog/google-analytics-4-setup-local-business).

If you cut your booking rate in half, the revenue halves too, so measure the rate you actually achieve.

## What Should You Do This Month?

1. Open your Business Profile performance report and write down last month's calls, directions, website clicks and messages.
2. Check categories, hours, phone number and booking link, and fix anything wrong or missing.
3. Upload five real, recent photos covering the exterior, interior, team and work.
4. Reply to every unanswered review with a specific detail from the visit.
5. Rewrite the first sentence of your two most important pages so it answers the main question directly. The [blog content strategy guide](/blog/blog-content-strategy-local-business) shows how to plan those answers.

Google's AI features change what appears on results pages, so it is worth tracking how they affect your queries. The [AI Overviews guide for local SEO](/blog/google-ai-overviews-local-business-seo) covers the local side.

## FAQ

### Are zero-click searches bad for local businesses?

Not automatically. If people call, ask for directions or book from your listing, you still gain customers. The risk is relying on website visits as your only measure of success.

### How common are zero-click local searches?

A third-party 2026 compilation, cited by Adwave, reports a 72% zero-click rate for local searches such as "near me", address and hours. Treat it as a directional figure rather than an exact rate.

### What counts as a useful call from my Business Profile?

INSIDEA recommends counting only calls lasting longer than 60 seconds as call conversions. Calls from location extensions lack a forwarding number, so pair this with your own call tracking.

### Do photos really affect direction requests?

Adwave, citing Google's 2024 guidance, reports 42% more direction requests for businesses with photos than without. Add real photos of the exterior, interior and team, and check your own Performance data after you do.
