Connected TV (CTV) advertising lets a Raleigh, NC business run video ads on streaming services and target households by ZIP code. It suits brand building rather than instant clicks. A practical start is a small four-week test, with the budget, frequency cap and audience set before launch, then judged against search and profile signals over 60 to 90 days. The platform minimums and CPMs in this guide are not confirmed by the sources used here, so each platform's own ad manager should be checked before committing money.

## What Is Connected TV Advertising for Raleigh Businesses?

CTV ads run inside streaming content on televisions. Unlike a social post, the viewer did not choose to follow the advertiser's account, and unlike a search ad, nobody typed a query. The ad reaches a household that is watching something it chose, which makes the format useful for becoming familiar to nearby people before they need the business.

For a Raleigh business, the main appeal is geographic control. Targeting by ZIP code keeps spend on households near the location rather than across the whole Triangle. A coffee shop in Cary and a salon in Durham are different businesses with different catchment areas, so the first decision is which neighbourhoods actually produce customers.

CTV is a brand channel. Measure it against recall and branded demand, not cost per click.

## Which Platforms Should a Raleigh Business Test First?

Most streaming inventory is bought through a platform's self-serve ad manager or a demand-side platform, and the rules differ on minimum spend, creative approval and targeting options. Rather than trusting a list of names and prices from a blog, ask each platform the same four questions before buying.

| Question to ask | Why it matters | What to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum campaign budget | Sets the smallest test that is possible | Amount, and whether it is per month or per flight |
| ZIP-code or radius targeting in your state | Determines whether spend can stay near your location | Yes or no, and the targeting levels offered |
| Creative specifications and review times | Affects production schedule and launch date | File format, length, and approval turnaround |
| Frequency cap and its default | Controls how often one household sees the ad | Default value and the maximum you can set |

Write the answers into a single comparison sheet. Two or three platforms that answer all four questions clearly are a better shortlist than one platform with the largest inventory but unclear terms. For comparison with other mid-size markets, the [CTV advertising in Detroit, MI guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-detroit-mi-local-business-guide) and the [CTV advertising in Phoenix, AZ guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-phoenix-az-local-business-guide) show how similar questions are handled elsewhere.

## How Should You Set ZIP Code and Frequency Targeting?

Start with ZIP codes. Pull the postcodes of the last 50 to 100 customers, or the customers who came from a directory listing, and identify where they cluster. Target those ZIP codes first and widen only if the test shows reach outside them. A radius around the address is simpler, but it can include areas where the business has never had a customer.

Frequency is the second lever. A frequency cap limits how many times one household sees the ad in a set period. Set the cap in the platform and record it, because an uncapped campaign can concentrate impressions on a small group of households and waste budget. Decide the cap before launch and keep it fixed during the test, so results can be compared.

Demographic filters are optional. Layer them in only when there is evidence about which age or household profile buys. Adding filters before customer data exists narrows reach without showing that it helps.

## What Does a Raleigh CTV Test Budget Look Like?

A worked example shows how a small budget turns into reach. The inputs below are assumptions, not market data. Replace them with the figures from a platform quote.

Inputs (all assumptions):

- Test budget: $500 for four weeks
- Cost per thousand impressions (CPM): $25
- Target frequency: 4 impressions per household over the test
- Target ZIP codes: 6 neighbourhoods near the shop

Calculation:

- Impressions bought: $500 divided by $25, then multiplied by 1,000, gives 20,000 impressions.
- Unique households reached: 20,000 divided by 4 gives 5,000 households.
- Cost per reached household: $500 divided by 5,000 gives $0.10.

If the real CPM is higher, the same budget reaches fewer households. At an assumed $40 CPM, the test buys 12,500 impressions and about 3,125 households at the same frequency. This is why the platform's quote matters more than any general average.

After the test, decide whether to extend, change ZIP codes, or change the creative. A four-week test is long enough to see whether the platform delivers to the right neighbourhoods, but short enough to limit losses if it does not.

## How Do You Measure CTV Results in Raleigh?

Measure CTV against signals that should move if people are noticing the ad. Set a baseline before launch, then compare the four weeks of the test with the weeks before and after.

1. Google Business Profile searches for the exact business name
2. Branded keyword clicks in Google Ads, if search campaigns run
3. Website direct traffic during the flight, compared with the baseline
4. A consistent "how did you hear about us?" question at the point of sale

Keep a simple log with dates, ZIP codes, frequency cap and budget spent, so changes can be explained later. Do not claim a result that the baseline cannot support. If branded searches rise during the flight, note that and record what else changed that month.

Give CTV 60 to 90 days before judging it. A short test can show whether delivery works. It rarely shows the full effect on recall.

## What Makes a Good Local CTV Ad?

Keep the ad simple. Show the business name or logo early, state the single benefit a customer gets, and close with a clear action such as a phone number, address or website. Check the platform's current format rules before producing the file, because specifications change and each platform sets its own limits.

Audio plays on CTV, so write the voiceover or music with that in mind. A subtitle line can help viewers who watch with the sound down.

## FAQ

### How much does CTV advertising cost for a small business in Raleigh?

Costs depend on the platform, the audience and the CPM quoted for the chosen ZIP codes. The worked example above uses an assumed $25 CPM. Get a quote from each platform or demand-side platform before setting a budget.

### Is CTV better than Google Ads for a local business?

They do different jobs. CTV builds familiarity across households nearby, while Google Ads captures people already searching. Many small businesses use search first for existing demand and test CTV as a brand layer. The [Google Ads service page](/services/google-ads) explains how search campaigns are set up.

### How many times should a household see the ad?

Set a frequency cap before launch and keep it fixed during the test. The right number depends on budget and reach, so use the worked example to estimate impressions per household and adjust after the first two weeks.

### Can I target ads to Amazon shoppers instead of ZIP codes?

Some streaming inventory offers audience data tied to shopping behaviour. The [Amazon Ads guide for local businesses](/blog/amazon-ads-for-local-business-complete-guide) covers that channel. For a first test, ZIP-code targeting is the simpler starting point.
