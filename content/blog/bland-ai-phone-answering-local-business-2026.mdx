Bland AI is a voice AI platform that lets a business attach a phone number to an AI agent that answers inbound calls and places outbound ones. On the free Start plan, Bland's pricing page lists $0.14 per minute and no monthly platform fee. For a cafe or salon, that means paying only for the minutes the agent is on a call.

This guide covers the setup path in Bland's documentation, what it costs, and where a small shop should be careful. Every product fact comes from Bland's own pages, opened on 6 November 2026. Plans and prices change, so check the pricing page before you commit.

## What Does Bland AI Cost for a Small Shop?

Bland lists three public tiers on its pricing page: Start, Build and Enterprise. Its billing documentation also lists a Scale plan and a separate Agent Phone plan. The table shows the figures that matter to a single-location business.

| Plan | Monthly fee | Talk time per minute | Concurrent calls | Daily call cap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start | $0 | $0.14 | 10 | 100 |
| Build | $299 | $0.12 | 50 | 2,000 |
| Scale | $499 | $0.11 | 100 | 5,000 |
| Agent Phone | $19.99 | Unlimited US and Canada minutes | 1 | 50 |

Other charges in the billing docs: $0.02 per SMS, $0.015 minimum for a failed or outbound call attempt on Bland telephony, and transfer minutes at a separate rate ($0.05 on Start). Calls are billed to the second. The pricing page says SMS and an appointment scheduling node are Enterprise features, so a small shop on Start should plan on voice only.

## How Do You Set Up an Inbound Number?

Bland's Dispatch documentation describes the path. The dashboard steps below follow that page.

1. Create an account and build an agent in the agent builder.
2. Open **Phone numbers** in Dispatch. New organizations can activate one free number. A purchased number is available only in the United States and Canada at $15 a month and needs a payment method on file. You can also connect your own Twilio account.
3. Press **Configure** on the number, pick the agent under **Assignment**, tick the confirmation box and press **Save**.
4. Publish and promote the agent. Bland's docs say attaching a number does not put the current agent into service by itself.
5. Call the number yourself and check that the Assignment column shows the agent.

Per-number settings such as language, webhook, transfers and blocked numbers apply to every call on that number.

## What Should the Agent Say and Do?

Bland's docs list pathways as legacy and name the agent builder as the current replacement. Whichever you use, keep the agent to a short list of jobs it can do reliably:

- Give opening hours, address, parking and menu or service highlights from text you supply.
- Take a callback request with name and number.
- Transfer to a person when the caller asks or is upset.

Keep prices, allergen answers and refund decisions with a human. An agent that guesses a menu price creates more work than a voicemail. Write every fact into the agent yourself, and re-check it when hours or prices change.

## Can It Make Reminder Calls?

Yes. Bland's Send Call endpoint (`POST /v1/calls`) takes a destination `phone_number` in E.164 format and a `task` that describes the agent's job. The API reference also lists optional fields such as `voice`, `record` (default off), `max_duration` (default 30 minutes), `transfer_phone_number` and a `webhook` that receives call details when the call ends.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.bland.ai/v1/calls \
  -H "authorization: $BLAND_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "phone_number": "+15125550100",
    "task": "Call to confirm the customer has a haircut booked tomorrow at 2 pm. If they need to cancel or move it, take a note and say the salon will follow up.",
    "max_duration": 2
  }'
```

The number above is a placeholder. This request follows the parameters in Bland's reference, but the sample has not been run against a live account. Test it on your own phone first.

## Is It Legal to Call Customers With an AI Voice?

Outbound AI calls carry legal risk in the United States. In February 2024 the Federal Communications Commission issued a declaratory ruling that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), so calls using them generally need the called person's prior express consent. This is reported by the law firm Cooley (secondary source). The FCC page itself returned a 403 error when opened, so check the ruling directly.

Bland's own call reference describes built-in `guard_rails` for AI disclosure, recording disclosure and opt-out, and says calls to numbers on a Do Not Call list are rejected. It adds that these features are not legal advice. Use reminder calls only for customers who gave their number and agreed to be contacted, and ask a lawyer about your state. Inbound answering carries a different risk profile, but tell callers they are speaking to an automated assistant.

The inputs below are **assumptions**, not measured results. Use your own call log.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Inbound calls per month | 80 |

| Rate (Start plan, Bland pricing page) | $0.14 per minute |

That is about $28 a month for talk time, or $43 with a purchased number. Compare it with what a missed call costs you. If one missed call a month would have become a $60 booking (assumption), the agent pays back if it saves even that one booking. Track call volume for two weeks before you pay for anything.

## Can It Sound Like the Owner?

Bland has a Clone Voice endpoint. Its documentation says about 10 seconds of audio is ideal, the maximum file is 10 MB, WAV is recommended and the name must be 1 to 30 characters. The billing page lists a voice-clone limit of 1 on Start, 5 on Build and 15 on Scale. The docs do not state how long processing takes.

Cloning a real person's voice needs that person's permission. Do not clone an employee or anyone else without a written yes.

## FAQ

### How much does Bland AI cost per minute?

Bland's pricing page lists $0.14 per minute on Start, $0.12 on Build, with a $299 monthly fee, and custom pricing on Enterprise. Its billing docs also list $0.11 on the $499 Scale plan.

### Is there a free plan?

Start has a $0 platform fee and needs no card, according to the pricing page. You then pay per minute. New organizations can activate one free phone number; further numbers cost $15 a month in the United States and Canada.

### Can Bland AI answer calls for my business 24 hours a day?

An agent attached to a published inbound number answers whenever someone calls. Bland's docs describe concurrency limits per plan (10 on Start), so a very busy line may need a higher plan.

### Does Bland AI take bookings?

The pricing page lists an appointment scheduling node under Enterprise. On Start or Build you would take a callback request or use your own webhook, and have staff confirm the booking.

### Do I need consent to call customers with an AI voice?

In the United States, the FCC ruled in 2024 that AI voices fall under the TCPA, which generally requires prior express consent for such calls. Get legal advice for your state and country.
