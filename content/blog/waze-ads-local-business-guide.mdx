Waze ads put your business on the map in front of people who are driving nearby, using formats such as branded pins, promoted search and full-screen takeovers shown when the car is stopped. They work best for businesses that benefit from impulse stops, like coffee shops, quick-service restaurants, gas stations and auto services. Many small businesses can reach Waze through Google Ads campaigns, and Waze also has its own ad tools. Formats, buying options and minimums change, so check the current Waze Ads and Google Ads documentation before you plan a budget.

## What Makes Waze Advertising Different

Waze is a crowd-sourced navigation app, with a large user base of drivers worldwide (check Waze's latest published figures). It reaches people at a specific moment: they are on the road, moving through physical space and often still deciding where to stop.

- **Physical proximity.** A driver a couple of miles from your cafe is a more relevant prospect than someone scrolling at home.
- **Decision moments.** Drivers make micro-decisions such as coffee, lunch or fuel while navigating.
- **Map-native format.** Your brand appears on the navigation screen, not in a feed that people scroll past.

<Callout type="tip">Waze ads suit businesses where an on-the-way stop is plausible. If customers typically book ahead or walk to you, other channels are likely a better first investment.</Callout>

## Waze Ad Formats

Format names and availability evolve, but these are the main ones to know.

### Branded Pins

Your business appears as a branded icon on the map for drivers near your location instead of a generic marker. This gives you ongoing visibility for people passing by.

### Promoted Search

When drivers search for a category, for example "coffee" or "gas station", your business can appear at the top of results within Waze.

### Zero-Speed Takeovers

Full-screen ads shown only when the vehicle is stopped, for example at a red light. They are designed with safety in mind and are not shown while the car is moving.

### Arrows

Directional prompts showing where your business is relative to the driver's current route.

## How to Buy Waze Ads

**Through Google Ads.** Google has integrated Waze placements into some local campaign types, so a campaign aimed at store visits may show on Waze alongside Google Maps and Search. Campaign types and placement options change, so check what is currently available in your Google Ads account, and see our [Google Ads service](/services/google-ads) if you want help.

**Directly through Waze Ads.** Waze also offers its own advertising platform for businesses that want more control over Waze-specific formats. Eligibility and minimums vary by country, so check the current requirements on the Waze Ads site.

## Which Businesses Get the Most From Waze

**Coffee shops and cafes.** Morning commuters passing your location are a natural audience.

**Quick-service restaurants.** Lunch and dinner commute windows are strong moments for food stops.

**Gas stations and auto services.** Drivers often need these when they are already on the road.

**Gyms and fitness studios.** Evening commuters deciding whether to work out could be reached near your studio.

**Pet businesses.** Pet owners often combine errands with driving, so weekend promotions can be relevant.

**Less likely to work:**

- Businesses in pedestrian-heavy city centers where most customers walk
- Appointment-only services that require advance planning
- Businesses targeting people without cars

## What Does Waze Advertising Cost?

Pricing depends on how you buy and where you are. Waze's own platform has historically allowed relatively small budgets compared with many traditional channels, and Google Ads campaigns that include Waze draw from your overall campaign budget. We cannot promise a specific price, so check current minimums and estimates in the platform before you commit. A sensible approach for a single-location business is to run a defined test, such as a few weeks with a modest budget and a tight radius, then compare the results with your other channels.

## How to Measure Waze Ad Results

Waze and Google offer metrics such as impressions, taps on your ad, navigation requests ("take me there"), and estimated store visits where enough data is available. A navigation request is a clear high-intent signal because someone asked the app to route them to you. For stronger tracking, add:

- A unique offer code that drivers mention at the counter
- A dedicated phone number or tracking link
- Weekly checks of your Google Business Profile insights for calls and direction requests

## Tips to Get Better Results

1. **Keep the radius tight.** Target the area from which people can realistically reach you in a few minutes.
2. **Schedule by time of day.** Focus on commute and meal windows when you are open.
3. **Make the offer specific.** "Free pastry with any large coffee before 10am" beats a generic brand message.
4. **Fix your basics first.** Accurate address, opening hours and phone number on your [Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile) matter because navigation depends on them.
5. **Test one variable at a time,** such as offer, radius or schedule.

## A Simple Waze Test Plan

1. **Pick one goal,** such as walk-ins during the morning commute.
2. **Choose a radius** based on how far a driver would reasonably detour.
3. **Create one clear offer** and a way to count redemptions.
4. **Run for several weeks,** long enough to see patterns across weekdays and weekends.
5. **Compare** navigation requests, redemptions and sales with the weeks before the test, and decide whether to scale, adjust or stop.

## FAQ

**Q: How do Waze ads work for local businesses?**
A: They show your business to nearby drivers as a branded pin, in promoted search results or as a full-screen ad when the car is stopped. You can buy them through Google Ads local campaigns or Waze's own ad platform.

**Q: How much do Waze ads cost?**
A: It depends on the format, location and how you buy. Check current minimums and estimates in Waze Ads or Google Ads, and start with a limited test budget.

**Q: Can I track visits from Waze ads?**
A: Partly. Platforms may report navigation requests and estimated store visits. Add promo codes, tracking links or phone numbers for more reliable measurement.

**Q: Are Waze ads good for a business that is off the main road?**
A: They can help, because a branded pin and navigation request can guide drivers to your door. Keep your radius tight and make your address accurate on your profile.

**Q: Waze ads or Google Local Services Ads?**
A: They solve different problems. Local Services Ads reach people actively searching for a service. Waze ads reach drivers on the move, which suits walk-in and impulse businesses. Test whichever matches how your customers buy.
