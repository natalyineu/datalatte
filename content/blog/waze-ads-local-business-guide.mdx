Waze no longer sells its own ads. Google's FAQ says Waze stopped providing Waze Ads after August 31, 2023, and points advertisers to Performance Max for store goals ([Waze Ads wind-down FAQ](https://support.google.com/wazeads/answer/14260169?hl=en), read 3 Nov 2026). Google Ads Help says ads can appear as promoted places on Waze, and only through that campaign type.

So the practical way to advertise on Waze is a Google Ads store-goal campaign, not a separate Waze account. There is no Waze price list, and you cannot buy "just Waze".

## What is Waze good for?

Waze is a navigation app. People open it because they are driving somewhere, and a driver on a route is still making small decisions: coffee, lunch, fuel. A promoted place is a square pin along the route.

- **Proximity.** A driver two miles from you is worth more than someone scrolling at home.
- **Timing.** The ad shows during the trip, not three hours before it.
- **Format.** A pin on the map, not a post in a feed people flick past.

<Callout type="tip">Waze only makes sense if an on-the-way stop is realistic for your customers. If they book ahead or walk to you, skip it for now and spend on search and your Google Business Profile first.</Callout>

## What happened to the old Waze ads platform?

Guides written before 2023 describe branded pins, promoted search and zero-speed takeovers bought straight from Waze. Google's FAQ says Waze would no longer provide Waze Ads after August 31, 2023, and that support for campaigns through DV360 ended on September 1, 2023 ([Waze Ads wind-down FAQ](https://support.google.com/wazeads/answer/14260169?hl=en), read 3 Nov 2026).

If an agency or tool offers "Waze zero-speed takeovers" or a Waze minimum daily spend today, ask one question: what exactly am I buying, and where will it appear? Those offers describe the retired product. Get the answer in writing before paying anything.

## How do Waze ads work now?

Google's Local Ads help page says ads can appear as promoted places on Waze, and that "Waze Ads are currently available only through Performance Max for store goal campaigns". It names the bidding options Store Visits, Store Sales and Local Actions Directions ([Google Ads Help: Local Ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3246303?hl=en), read 3 Nov 2026).

In practice:

- **Waze is one surface of a larger campaign.** You do not pick Waze on its own.
- **Google describes the campaign type** as one that "uses AI to maximize your in-store visits and sales" ([Waze Ads wind-down FAQ](https://support.google.com/wazeads/answer/14260169?hl=en)).
- **Billing is CPM.** The Local Ads page says Performance Max for store goals campaigns charge cost-per-thousand impressions. No CPM amount is published there.
- **Location data is required.** Advertisers must have location assets linked at the account or campaign level, and Google recommends Google Business Profile as the source.
- **The budget is shared.** The campaign decides how to split spend across surfaces, so Waze gets no guaranteed share.

That means a pure Waze test is impossible: you test the whole store-goal campaign. Check in your Google Ads account what is available before planning around it, and see the [Google Ads service](/services/google-ads) for help setting it up.

## Which businesses suit Waze ads?

Good fits, where a driver can plausibly pull over:

- **Coffee shops and cafes.** Morning commuters passing your door.
- **Quick-service restaurants.** Lunch and dinner commute windows.
- **Gas stations and auto services.** Drivers are already on the road.
- **Gyms and fitness studios.** Evening commuters deciding whether to train.
- **Pet businesses.** Weekend errand runs, with a promotion to match.

Poor fits:

- Pedestrian-heavy city centers where most customers walk
- Appointment-only services that need advance planning
- Businesses targeting people without cars

## How many visits does a test need to break even?

There is no Waze price list, so nobody can quote an honest cost per visit. What you can do is work out in advance what a test must deliver.

**Assumption, not data:** a cafe tests a store-goal campaign at $15 a day for 28 days, a customer who walks in spends $7 on average, and about 60% of that is gross margin. These are invented inputs, not a market average.

| Step | Assumption | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Test budget | $15 x 28 days | $420 |
| Margin per visit | $7 x 60% | $4.20 |
| Visits needed to break even | $420 / $4.20 | 100 visits |
| Per day | 100 / 28 | about 3.6 extra visits a day |
| If 1 in 3 visitors returns once more | extra $4.20 per 3 visitors | break-even drops to about 75 visits (about 2.7 a day) |

If roughly 4 extra customers a day from a location campaign is hard to imagine, the test will not pay for itself on the first visit alone. Then the question is whether repeat visits cover the gap. ## How do you measure Waze ad results?

Google Ads says store visits can be measured and optimised, but store visits tracking has eligibility requirements that Google documents separately. Directions are a listed action: users clicking for directions to your location. Add three more checks:

- A unique offer code customers say at the counter
- A dedicated phone number or tracking link
- A weekly look at calls and direction requests in your Google Business Profile insights

## What should you do today?

1. Open your [Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile) and confirm the address, hours and phone number. Location ads depend on them.
2. Write one offer in a single line, such as "Free pastry with any large coffee before 10am".
3. Decide your break-even visits per day using the table above, with your own ticket and margin.
4. Set a calendar reminder for day 14 and day 28 to compare visits, directions and code redemptions with the 4 weeks before the test.
5. Set ad hours to your open hours. Paying for visibility when the doors are shut is waste.

## FAQ

### How do Waze ads work for local businesses?

Waze no longer sells ads directly. Ads can appear as promoted places on Waze through Google Ads Performance Max for store goals campaigns, according to Google Ads Help.

### How much do Waze ads cost?

There is no separate Waze price. Spend comes out of your Google Ads campaign budget, and Google says these campaigns charge CPM. No CPM figure is published, so start with a limited test and work out break-even first.

### Can I still buy zero-speed takeovers or branded pins directly from Waze?

No. Google says Waze stopped providing Waze Ads after August 31, 2023, and recommends Performance Max for store goals instead.

### Can I track visits from Waze ads?

Partly. Google Ads may report store visits where eligibility requirements are met, and directions are a trackable action. Add promo codes, tracking links or phone numbers for more reliable measurement.

### Waze ads or Google Local Services Ads?

They solve different problems. Local Services Ads reach people actively searching for a service. Location ads on Waze reach drivers on the move, which suits walk-in and impulse businesses.
