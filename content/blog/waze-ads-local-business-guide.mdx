Waze no longer sells its own ads. Waze stopped providing Waze Ads after August 31, 2023, and Google now points advertisers to Performance Max for store goals, which can show your business as a promoted pin on Waze ([Waze Ads wind-down FAQ](https://support.google.com/wazeads/answer/14260169?hl=en), checked Oct 2026). So the practical way to advertise on Waze today is a Google Ads campaign built around store visits, not a separate Waze account. This guide explains what that means for a local business, who it suits and how to test it.

## What Makes Waze Advertising Different

Waze is a crowd-sourced navigation app. It reaches people at a specific moment: they are on the road and often still deciding where to stop.

- **Physical proximity.** A driver a couple of miles from your cafe is a more relevant prospect than someone scrolling at home.
- **Decision moments.** Drivers make micro-decisions such as coffee, lunch or fuel while navigating.
- **Map-native format.** Your brand appears on the navigation screen, not in a feed that people scroll past.

<Callout type="tip">Waze ads suit businesses where an on-the-way stop is plausible. If customers typically book ahead or walk to you, other channels are likely a better first investment.</Callout>

## What Changed: The Old Waze Ads Platform Is Gone

Older guides, including many written before 2023, describe Waze's own self-serve platform with branded pins, promoted search and zero-speed takeovers. That platform was wound down. Google's FAQ says Waze would no longer provide Waze Ads after August 31, 2023, and that support for buying Waze through DV360 ended on September 1, 2023 ([Google Waze Ads wind-down FAQ](https://support.google.com/wazeads/answer/14260169?hl=en), checked Oct 2026).

If you see an agency or tool selling "Waze zero-speed takeovers" or a Waze minimum daily spend, ask what exactly you are buying and where it appears. Those offers describe the retired product.

## How Waze Ads Work Now

Google's Local Ads help page says your ads can appear as promoted places on Waze: location-based square pins along a driver's route. It also states that Waze Ads are currently available only through Performance Max for store goal campaigns bidding to Store Visits, Store Sales or Local Actions Directions ([Google Ads Help: Local Ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3246303?hl=en), checked Oct 2026).

What that means in practice:

- **You do not choose Waze separately.** Waze is one of the surfaces a store-goal Performance Max campaign can use.
- **Google describes Performance Max for store goals** as a campaign type that uses AI to maximize in-store visits and sales, bringing location formats across Search, Display, Gmail, Google Maps and YouTube into one product ([Waze Ads wind-down FAQ](https://support.google.com/wazeads/answer/14260169?hl=en), checked Oct 2026).
- **Your budget is shared.** The campaign decides how to spread spend across surfaces, so Waze is not guaranteed a set share.

Because campaign types and placements change, confirm in your Google Ads account what is available before you plan around Waze. If you want help setting this up, see our [Google Ads service](/services/google-ads).

## Which Businesses Get the Most From Waze

**Coffee shops and cafes.** Morning commuters passing your location are a natural audience.

**Quick-service restaurants.** Lunch and dinner commute windows are strong moments for food stops.

**Gas stations and auto services.** Drivers often need these when they are already on the road.

**Gyms and fitness studios.** Evening commuters deciding whether to work out could be reached near your studio.

**Pet businesses.** Pet owners often combine errands with driving, so weekend promotions can be relevant.

**Less likely to work:**

- Businesses in pedestrian-heavy city centers where most customers walk
- Appointment-only services that require advance planning
- Businesses targeting people without cars

## What Does Waze Advertising Cost?

There is no separate Waze price list any more. Spend runs through your Google Ads budget, and the cost depends on your location, competition and goal. We cannot promise a specific figure, so use the estimates and reporting in your Google Ads account. A sensible approach for a single-location business is a defined test, such as a few weeks with a modest budget, then compare results with your other channels.

## How to Measure Results

Google Ads store-goal campaigns report actions such as store visits and direction requests where enough data is available. A request for directions is a clear high-intent signal because someone asked an app to route them to you. For stronger tracking, add:

- A unique offer code that customers mention at the counter
- A dedicated phone number or tracking link
- Weekly checks of your Google Business Profile insights for calls and direction requests

## Tips to Get Better Results

1. **Fix your basics first.** Accurate address, opening hours and phone number on your [Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile) matter because navigation and location ads depend on them.
2. **Make the offer specific.** "Free pastry with any large coffee before 10am" beats a generic brand message.
3. **Focus on open hours.** Only pay for visibility when you can serve people.
4. **Test one variable at a time,** such as offer, creative or schedule.

## Waze Ads vs Other Local Channels

| Channel | Reaches | Best when |

| Waze (via Google Ads store goals) | Drivers near you | Customers can make an on-the-way stop |
| Google Ads (Search) | People searching right now | Customers already know what they want |
| Google Local Services Ads | People hiring a local pro | Service businesses with calls and quotes |
| Meta Ads | People browsing social feeds | Visual offers and local awareness |

## A Simple Test Plan

1. **Pick one goal,** such as walk-ins during the morning commute.
2. **Create one clear offer** and a way to count redemptions.
3. **Run for several weeks,** long enough to see patterns across weekdays and weekends.
4. **Compare** store visits, direction requests, redemptions and sales with the weeks before the test, and decide whether to scale, adjust or stop.

## FAQ

**Q: How do Waze ads work for local businesses?**
A: Waze no longer sells ads directly. Ads can appear as promoted location pins on Waze through Google Ads Performance Max for store goals campaigns.

**Q: How much do Waze ads cost?**
A: There is no separate Waze price. Spend comes out of your Google Ads campaign budget, and results depend on your area and competition, so start with a limited test.

**Q: Can I still buy zero-speed takeovers or branded pins directly from Waze?**
A: No. Google says Waze stopped providing Waze Ads after August 31, 2023, and recommends Performance Max for store goals instead.

**Q: Can I track visits from Waze ads?**
A: Partly. Google Ads may report store visits and direction requests where there is enough data. Add promo codes, tracking links or phone numbers for more reliable measurement.

**Q: Waze ads or Google Local Services Ads?**
A: They solve different problems. Local Services Ads reach people actively searching for a service. Location ads on Waze reach drivers on the move, which suits walk-in and impulse businesses.
