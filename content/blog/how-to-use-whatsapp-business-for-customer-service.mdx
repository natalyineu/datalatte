## How Do You Set Up WhatsApp Business for Customer Service?

Start with the free app on one phone number. Write a business profile with your address, opening hours and booking link. Then set three messages: a greeting for new chats, an away message for closed hours, and quick replies for the questions you answer daily, such as prices, parking and cancellation rules.

Keep each quick reply short. Two lines that end with a next step work better than a long paragraph, because the customer knows what to do. Review the list each month and delete any reply nobody uses.

[Adwave's guidance on service rules](https://adwave.com/resources/customer-retention-strategies-that-cost-less-than-new-acquisition) applies here. It recommends three or four rules followed consistently rather than a long policy document that nobody checks. For a local shop, that could mean confirming every booking in writing, answering within the same working day, and sending refund questions to one named owner.

## What Does WhatsApp Customer Service Cost?

The app is the low-cost starting point. Costs appear when you move to the WhatsApp Business Platform, the API used by larger setups and software. Per-message charges apply there, and the rate depends on your country and message type. Check the rate card on your platform's live help page, because rates and window rules change.

Platform costs are not only the message price. [Message Central's 2026 vendor guide](https://www.messagecentral.com/blog/best-whatsapp-business-api-platform-brazil) says operational fees, setup, integration and change costs add another 40 to 50 percent on top of the per-message price. A budget that counts only message fees will understate the real bill.

For the API route, see the [WhatsApp Business API guide](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-local-marketing-automation) and the [AI bot setup guide](/blog/whatsapp-ai-bot-local-business-setup-guide).

## How Do Routing Tools Keep Customer Replies Fast?

Once several people share an inbox, slow replies usually come from routing rather than from staff effort. Crescendo Lab says its Auto Routing Agent ensures customer inquiries are swiftly directed to the right support agents, which is the function to look for in any shared inbox tool. Vendor claims should still be tested on your own message volume.

Quick replies cover routine questions. Anything about a complaint, refund or change should go to one named owner. The [AI agent for customer service guide](/blog/ai-agent-for-customer-service-local-business) explains when automation should hand off to a person.

Shared inbox costs are modest. Adwave says SMBs can often run better support with existing tools or light software, typically $0 to $150 per month for a shared inbox, chat or ticketing support.

## How Do You Win Back Past Customers Cheaply?

Customers who stopped booking are the cheapest audience to reach. Winback Engine's comparison puts new customer acquisition at $50 to $200 per contact, while reactivation costs $5 to $20 per contact. Phone outreach sits at $3 to $8 per contact in the same guide.

Start with a segment. CloudLaundry describes sorting customers by order frequency, service type and last order date so broadcasts reach the right people. A salon can apply the same logic using last visit date and the service booked.

Timing matters too. DMly describes sending a Google review request through WhatsApp or SMS after an appointment, with an optional delay so it arrives at a better time. The [customer follow-up guide](/blog/how-to-automate-customer-follow-ups-local-business) covers timing rules for reminders and win-back messages.

## What Does a Reactivation Budget Look Like?

The table below lists the per-contact costs and response rates from the sources. A blank cell means the guide did not publish that figure.

| Channel | Cost per contact | Response or open rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New customer acquisition | $50 to $200 | Not listed |

| Email reactivation | $0.01 to $0.05 | 2 to 5% response |

The worked example below uses a salon with a lapsed client list. Every input except the table ranges is an assumption.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Lapsed clients on the list | 300 (assumption) |
| Email cost per contact | $0.03 (assumption, inside the $0.01 to $0.05 range) |
| Reply rate | 3% (assumption, inside the 2 to 5% range) |
| Average booking value | $60 (assumption) |

Total campaign cost is 300 × $0.03 = $9. Reactivated clients are 300 × 3% = 9. Cost per reactivation is $9 ÷ 9 = $1, well under the under-$30 target that Winback Engine's guide lists. Booking value is 9 × $60 = $540 (assumption). These figures exclude staff time and any platform fee. If you send the same message on WhatsApp, use your platform's current rate card instead of the email figure.

## What Should You Audit in the First Week?

Adwave's service plan suggests auditing the last 20 customer issues in week one, looking for delays, repeat complaints and handoff failures. For a local business, pull the last 20 messages or calls and mark each one that waited more than a working day, repeated a question already answered, or reached the wrong person.

Those three marks show which quick replies to write and which routing rule to fix first. ## FAQ

### Is the WhatsApp Business app enough to start?
For one phone number and routine questions, yes. Greetings, away messages and quick replies cover most simple enquiries. Move to the platform when you need automation or shared access across staff.

### How much do platform fees add?
A vendor estimate puts setup, integration and operational fees at 40 to 50 percent on top of the per-message price. Use your platform's live rate card for the base price.

### How much cheaper is reactivation?
Winback Engine's guide says reactivation campaigns typically cost 5 to 10 times less per converted customer than new acquisition.

### What should I check first after launch?
Audit the last 20 customer issues in week one for delays, repeat complaints and handoff failures.
