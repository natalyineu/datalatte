A local business can use WhatsApp for customer service by setting a greeting, away message and quick replies in the free WhatsApp Business app, and by answering inside the 24-hour customer service window. Meta's pricing page says messages from customers to businesses are free, and charges apply per delivered message through the Business Platform from July 1, 2025.

This guide separates the free app from the paid platform and explains the window rules as of October 31, 2026.

## What Is the Difference Between the App and the Platform?

The WhatsApp Business app is built for small businesses. WhatsApp's launch post lists a business profile with description, address and website, quick replies for common questions, greeting messages and away messages, and simple metrics. Its current product page also describes greeting people when a chat starts and replying when you are away.

The WhatsApp Business Platform is the API used by larger setups and software. Meta charges per delivered message there. Our [WhatsApp Business API guide](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-local-marketing-automation) and [AI bot setup guide](/blog/whatsapp-ai-bot-local-business-setup-guide) cover that route.

## What Does Customer Service on WhatsApp Cost?

According to Meta's pricing page, charges are per message, by category and recipient country calling code, and only for delivered messages. Messages from users to businesses are free. A 24-hour customer service window opens when a customer messages or calls, and each new message resets it.

Inside the window you can send non-template service messages. Meta says it publishes rates for 5 categories across 47 markets, in 16 currencies. It states that utility messages inside the window and service messages become charged from October 1, 2026, with the first 1,000 service messages per phone number each month free and not rolling over. Check the rate card for your country.

## How Do Click to WhatsApp Ads Change the Cost?

If a user messages you from a Click to WhatsApp ad on the Android or iOS app, your reply inside the service window opens a free entry point window of up to 7 days. During it, marketing, utility, authentication and service messages are not charged. Desktop and web apps are not supported for this.

This makes ads a way to start conversations at low messaging cost, though ad spend itself is separate and not covered by this page. See [WhatsApp Business marketing: what is free vs paid](/blog/whatsapp-business-marketing-local-business).

## How Do You Set Up Fast Replies?

Set a greeting for new chats, an away message for closed hours and quick replies for questions you answer daily: opening hours, prices, parking, cancellation rules. Put your address and booking link in the business profile.

Write each answer in two lines and end with the next step. Keep the opening hours identical to your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## FAQ

### Is WhatsApp Business free?
The app is free to download, and Meta does not charge for messages from users to businesses. Platform sending is charged per delivered message.

### How long can I reply to a customer?
Meta's customer service window lasts 24 hours from the customer's last message or call.

### What is a free entry point window?
A window of up to 7 days that opens when a customer messages you from a Click to WhatsApp ad and you reply in time.

### Do I need the API?
Not to start. The app handles greetings, away messages and quick replies. The API suits automation and software.

### Where do I find prices for my country?
Meta publishes rate cards across 5 categories and 47 markets on its pricing page.
