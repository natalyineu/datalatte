A small business in Trinidad and Tobago should start with Facebook and Instagram, and a business that sells to other businesses should also use LinkedIn. DataReportal lists Facebook ad reach at 815 thousand, Instagram at 747 thousand, TikTok at 974 thousand adults and LinkedIn at 700 thousand members, 46.3% of the population. The country has 1.51 million people, so each audience is small. Keep campaigns narrow and measure enquiries.

This guide is for shops, restaurants, gyms, salons and service firms in Trinidad and Tobago. Figures come from the sources at the end, checked on 1 November 2026.

## How Many People Can a Small Business Reach in Trinidad and Tobago?

About 1.3 million are online. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report lists 1.51 million people, 1.28 million internet users (84.7%) and 2.04 million mobile connections, 135% of the population. Social media user identities total 974 thousand, 64.4%, up 15.1% in a year.

| Platform | Ad reach, late 2025 | Share and change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok (adults 18+ only) | 974 thousand | 81.8% of adults, up 15.1% |
| Facebook | 815 thousand | 53.9% of population, up 4.1% |
| Instagram | 747 thousand | 49.4% of population, up 11.2% |
| LinkedIn (members) | 700 thousand | 46.3% of population, up 11.1% |
| Messenger | 437 thousand | 28.9% of population, down 2.4% |
| YouTube, Snapchat | Not reported | n/a |

Instagram grew faster than Facebook over the year, but DataReportal warns that platforms revise their numbers and that earlier reports are not directly comparable. Use the table as a ranking.

## Why Does LinkedIn Matter Here?

Because its reported audience is almost as large as Instagram's. DataReportal lists 700 thousand LinkedIn members, 46.3% of the population and 54.7% of internet users. These are registered accounts, not active users.

For an accountant, a trainer, an engineer or a supplier selling to firms, a profile with clear services and factual updates can reach buyers directly. For a restaurant or a gym, Instagram and Facebook fit better.

## How Small Are the Audiences, and What Follows?

Each local audience is below one million, so overlap between platforms is likely. A campaign that targets the whole country on every platform will reach the same people several times and cost more than it needs to.

Pick one primary platform for the customer you want, set a narrow location, and cap how often people see the ad. The [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to start with a small test instead of a large plan.

## Can a Trinidad and Tobago Business Run Google Ads?

Yes. Google's list of billing countries includes Trinidad and Tobago. In that list the account currency and payment method columns read "All", but Google says the options you see depend on your billing country, currency and payment setting, and that prepay is not offered in many countries.

Open the billing page in your own account to see what is available. No source checked publishes click prices for the country, and DataReportal does not report Google ad reach for it.

## What Does a First Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for a small accounting firm that wants enquiries from local companies. They show the method, not local results. Currency is "units" of Trinidad and Tobago dollars.

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Gross profit per new client over a year (assumption) | 9,000 units |
| Enquiries that become clients (assumption) | 10% |
| Most you can pay per enquiry and still break even | 9,000 x 10% = 900 units |
| Test budget for one quarter (assumption) | 4,500 units |
| Enquiries needed to break even | 4,500 / 900 = 5 enquiries |

Five qualified enquiries from 4,500 units would break even in a year. Business services have longer sales cycles, so judge a quarter, not a week. Add a phone call or a form so each lead is counted once.

## How Do You Get Found on Google Maps in Trinidad and Tobago?

Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. It also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Fill in every field, use real photos, reply to every review and keep the name, address and phone number identical everywhere. Name the area, such as the town or district, in the profile description so people searching locally can see where you are. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes through each field.

Do not offer a discount or a free item in return for a review. Google's contributed content policy says merchants must not offer incentives, such as payment, discounts, free goods or services, in exchange for posting a review. Asking is fine. Rewarding is not. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for ways to ask.

## What Mistakes Do Small Businesses Make Here?

These are recommendations, not study findings.

- Running every campaign nationwide when customers come from one area.
- Treating LinkedIn as only for job seekers when 46.3% of the population is registered.
- Posting without a call to action. Add a message, call or booking button.
- Judging results by reach rather than enquiries.

DataReportal reports a Facebook ad reach of 815 thousand in late 2025, 53.9% of the population. It is an ad-tool estimate, not a count of active users.

### How many people use the internet in Trinidad and Tobago?
