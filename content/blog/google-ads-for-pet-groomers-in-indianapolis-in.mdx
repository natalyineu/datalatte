## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Pet Groomer?

The 2026 LocaliQ benchmarks put the all-industry average at $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead. Animals and Pets is lower at $4.06 per click and $31.50 per lead, with a 16.22% conversion rate. Conversion here means the lead action the advertiser set up, such as a call or form, not a paid groom.

No public source gives Indianapolis CPCs for groomers. Keyword Planner inside your Google Ads account shows estimated ranges for your area for free, and your first 30 days of real data will beat any average.

## Which Keywords Should You Start With?

Start with the searches of people ready to book, and add the city name where it helps.

| Keyword idea | Why |
| --- | --- |
| dog groomer near me | Booking intent, location comes from the searcher |
| dog grooming Indianapolis | Booking intent with the city named |
| mobile dog grooming Indianapolis | Only if you offer it |
| cat grooming near me | Only if you groom cats |

Add negative keywords for "jobs", "school", "course", "diy" and "kit". Google says negative keywords do not match close variants, so add plurals too. Our [list of Google Ads keywords for pet groomers](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-pet-groomers-in-2026) goes further.

## How Do You Target Only People Nearby?

Use location targeting as your main cost control. Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km around a location, and that you can also target cities and ZIP codes, including the state in the name (for example "94103, California"). Exclusions let you remove areas you do not serve.

Google recommends "Presence or interest" as a Search best practice and says "Presence" fits when you only want people physically in the selected locations. A shop with a fixed service area can test "Presence". Pick a radius that matches how far your clients drive, and check clicks by location after two weeks.

## What Should You Track?

Set up conversions for phone calls from ads, booking form submissions and direction requests. For calls, Google lets you set a minimum call length that counts as a conversion, so choose a length that reflects a real inquiry. Review the Search terms report weekly and add negatives.

## FAQ

### How much should a pet groomer in Indianapolis spend on Google Ads?

There is no standard figure. At the national Animals and Pets average of $4.06 a click, $800 a month buys about 197 clicks. Start small, measure cost per booked groom, and scale when it works.

### What is the average CPC for dog grooming in Indianapolis?

It is not published. The national category average is $4.06, and Keyword Planner shows estimates for your area.

### Is Google Ads better than Google Business Profile for groomers?

They do different jobs. Ads buy visibility for a fee, while a complete Business Profile earns free map visibility. Most shops should have the profile in order before they spend on ads.

### How long until I see results?

Google does not publish a timeline. Judge a campaign on cost per booked groom after you have enough clicks, not after a few days.
