A small business in Liberia should build around Facebook and Messenger, accept mobile money, and treat paid ads as a small test. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report says 1.73 million Liberians were online in October 2025, only 30.0% of 5.76 million people, and that Facebook's ad tools reach 1.10 million. Most customers are still offline, so word of mouth, signs and WhatsApp-style direct contact matter alongside ads.

This guide uses DataReportal, the World Bank and reporting on the Central Bank of Liberia's new payment system. It gives no ad prices because none were found.

## How Many Liberians Are Online?

DataReportal lists 5.76 million people in Liberia (UN, October 2025), 54.7% urban, with a median age of 18.8. Mobile connections total 5.54 million, 96.2% of the population. Internet users number 1.73 million, up 105,000 (6.5%) on a year earlier, which leaves 4.03 million people (70.0%) offline.

The gap between SIM cards and internet users shows that owning a phone does not mean using data. A promotion that depends on online ads reaches a minority, so pair it with signage, a clear location and a phone number.

## Which Platforms Reach Customers in Liberia?

Facebook reaches 1.10 million people, 19.1% of the population and 63.6% of internet users, up 270,000 (32.5%) in a year. Messenger reaches 428,000, up 126%. Instagram reaches 125,000, LinkedIn 210,000 members and X 18,900. DataReportal flags the X fall and the large Reddit jump as unusual and says ad reach is not active users.

The country page has no figures for WhatsApp, TikTok or YouTube, so none are given here. Our [WhatsApp Business guide](/blog/how-to-use-whatsapp-business-for-customer-service) explains how to run customer chats, but check with your own customers which app they use.

## Can Liberian Businesses Use Google Ads?

Google's restriction page lists Crimea, Cuba, the so-called Donetsk and Luhansk People's Republics, Iran and North Korea. Liberia is not listed, and no update date is shown. Whether a Liberian business can pay in its currency or by a local method is not confirmed here, because Google decides billing options per country and currency inside the account. Check that before spending time on campaigns.

No public cost-per-click data for Liberia was found. A Google Business Profile costs nothing, so start with our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## How Do Customers Pay: What Is Pay Na-Na?

On 16 December 2025 the Central Bank of Liberia launched the Inclusive Instant Payment System, branded Pay Na-Na, according to AllAfrica reporting. It connects MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money, so a customer on one network can send money to someone on the other in real time. It starts with person-to-person and government-to-person payments, with merchant payments, bill payments and tax collection planned for later phases.

The same report says cash-out fees are capped below 1% and that transfers were fee-free from 16 December 2025 to 2 January 2026 (secondary source; the Business Wire copy of the Central Bank release returned a 403 error and could not be opened).

For a shop, the useful step is to list "MTN MoMo and Orange Money accepted" in every post and ad. Merchant payment features are not live yet, so confirm with your provider.

## What Does the Economy Look Like?

The World Bank lists Liberia's 2025 GDP at US$5.25 billion, growth of 5.0% and GDP per capita of US$915.3. With incomes this low, price and trust decide most purchases. Show prices, a photo of your shop and your location in each post, and answer messages quickly.

## How Should a Liberian Business Test Facebook?

Open a Page with address, hours and phone number. Post real photos of products and prices. Then run one small ad with a Messenger objective aimed at the neighbourhood around your shop in Monrovia or your county town. Our [organic and paid social guide](/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media) explains the split between free posts and ads.

Measure only what you can count: messages, calls, and customers who say they saw the ad. Ask every new customer how they found you.

## Worked Example: What Could US$50 Do?

All inputs are **assumptions**, not Liberian data.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget | US$50 |
| Cost per Messenger conversation | US$0.50 |
| Conversations | 100 |
| Share that buy | 10% |
| Customers | 10 |
| Average sale | US$8 |
| Revenue | US$80 |
| Ad cost per customer | US$5 |

At US$1.00 per conversation, the same budget gives 50 conversations and 5 customers, and revenue falls below the ad spend. Only a product with a higher margin or repeat purchases would justify it.

## FAQ

### How many Liberians use Facebook?

DataReportal lists Facebook ad reach of 1.10 million in October 2025, 19.1% of the population. It is not a count of active users.

### Is Liberia online enough for digital marketing?

Only 30.0% of people used the internet in October 2025. Digital marketing works for urban, younger customers, and should be combined with offline marketing.

### What is Pay Na-Na?

It is Liberia's instant payment system, launched in December 2025, linking MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money.

### Can I advertise on Google in Liberia?

Liberia is not on Google's sanctions-based list. Billing options for your currency need to be checked in your account.

### How much do ads cost in Liberia?

No public benchmark was found. Run a small test.
