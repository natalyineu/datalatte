To start Spotify ads for a local business, open Spotify Ad Studio, set a budget of at least $250, target your zip codes, upload or request a 30-second audio ad with a square companion image, and launch. Then decide before you spend how many new customers you need to cover the $250. This guide walks through each step and the math.

## Is Spotify Right for Your Business?

Spotify suits a business that wants people in your area to remember your name, not one that needs searchers to call today. People do not search on Spotify. They listen while running, driving or working, so the ad interrupts rather than answers. Spotify reported [494 million ad-supported monthly users in Q2 2026, up 14%](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001639920/000114036126031044/ef20078867_ex99-1.htm), which is global, not local.

Good fits are coffee shops, studios, salons and groomers in a dense area with a clear offer. If people already search for you on Google, fix that first with the [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) and [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## How Do You Set Up a Spotify Ad Studio Account?

Create an Ad Studio account at ads.spotify.com with your business details and a payment method. Spotify says [audio and video campaigns on Ad Studio start at $250](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/small-business-advertising/).

Budget and prices differ by country and change, so check the live Ad Studio screens before you commit. Our [Spotify Ad Studio minimum budget guide](/blog/spotify-ad-studio-minimum-budget-2026) goes deeper on what the minimum buys, and the [Spotify ad cost guide](/blog/how-much-do-spotify-ads-cost-pricing-for-local-business) explains pricing.

## How Do You Target Local Listeners?

Spotify's small business page says you can target by demographics, interests, device and listening habits, and that [local targeting by zip code is available](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/small-business-advertising/). For a local business, zip codes beat broad cities.

Pick the zip codes within a short drive or walk of your door. A narrower area means fewer listeners but less waste. Add age and interests only if your customer is clear, because each filter shrinks reach.

## What Should Your Audio Ad Say?

Spotify's [audio ad specs](https://ads.spotify.com/en-IN/ad-experiences/audio-ads-specs) set the maximum for standard audio ads at 30 seconds and recommend no more than 65 words. The files must be MP3, OGG or WAV, and the companion image is 640x640 pixels in JPEG or PNG, no larger than 200 KB. Spotify's small business page also says you can have ads made free in Ad Studio with voice actors, with free revisions.

Use one structure: who you are, where you are, one offer, one action. For example: "Looking for a haircut near Oak Street? [Business name] has same-day appointments. Book online at [short address] and mention this ad for a free blow-dry." Keep the web address short because listeners cannot click while running.

## How Do You Track Whether It Works?

Give the ad its own way to be counted. Use a dedicated web page, a promo word at the till or a phone number you use only for the campaign. Without it, new customers will say they "found you online" and you cannot tell Spotify from everything else.

Run the first flight for a few weeks, not days, so listeners hear the ad more than once. Compare new customers from the promo with the break-even number below.

## What Is the Break-Even Math?

Decide the target before you spend. Here is an example where every figure is an **assumption**:

| Item (assumption) | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign budget | $250 |
| Average gross profit per new customer | $30 |
| New customers to break even | 9 ($250 / $30) |
| New customers at a 2x return | 17 |

If you cannot picture nine new customers coming from the zip codes you picked, raise the offer, lengthen the flight or choose another channel. A single $250 test is a signal, not proof; compare it with other options in [Spotify vs Pandora vs iHeart](/blog/spotify-vs-pandora-vs-iheart-best-audio-ad-platform-local-business) and [digital audio advertising costs](/blog/digital-audio-advertising-2026-spotify-pandora-podcast-ads).

## What Should You Do After the Test?

Keep the offer and zip codes the same, change the script only, and test again. If the first flight misses break-even, check the promo count before blaming the channel. A weak offer, a hard-to-remember address or too few days will all look like "Spotify did not work."

## FAQ

**Q: How much does it cost to start Spotify ads?**
A: Spotify states that Ad Studio audio and video campaigns start at $250. Ad spots use bidding, so there is no fixed price per ad.

**Q: Can I target only my city?**
A: Spotify says zip code targeting is available, so you can aim at the areas around your business instead of a whole metro region.

**Q: Do I need to record the ad myself?**
A: No. Spotify says ads can be created free in Ad Studio with voice actors and professional audio mixing, and revisions are free. You can also upload your own file.

**Q: How long can the audio ad be?**
A: Standard audio ads run up to 30 seconds. Spotify recommends no more than 65 words for a 30-second script.
