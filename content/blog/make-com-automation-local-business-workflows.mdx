Make (Make.com) can automate lead replies, review requests, reminders and weekly reports for a local business, and its Free plan includes 1,000 credits a month. Make's help says most apps charge 1 credit per operation by default, so a four-step workflow that runs 100 times a month uses about 400 credits. Plan prices were not readable on Make's pricing page when this was checked, so check the live page for them.

## How Does Make Work and What Does It Cost?

Make runs "scenarios": a trigger followed by actions. Triggers can be instant, for example a custom webhook that runs the scenario as soon as it receives a request, or on a schedule. Credits are Make's billing unit. Make's help says only features triggered by scenario runs, and the AI agent's chat, use credits, and most apps charge a fixed rate of 1 credit per operation. File uploads cost 1 credit. Some AI Content Extractor modules cost 2 or 10.

The Free plan has 1,000 credits a month. Paid tiers add more credits, and Make says annual payment is cheaper than monthly. Per-plan prices were not readable, so do not budget from this page.

## What Do Workflows 1 to 4 Do?

**1. New enquiry reply.** When a contact form is submitted, send a short email that confirms receipt, states your reply time, and links to your booking page. Add a row to a sheet so every lead is logged with its source.

**2. Review request.** When a booking is marked complete, wait a set time, then send a short message with your Google review link. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults found 97% read online reviews, so asking every customer is worth the setup.

**3. Missed-call follow-up.** If your phone system can send a webhook for missed calls, send an automatic text: "Sorry we missed you, how can we help?" Whether your provider supports this varies and was not verified.

**4. No-show follow-up.** Send a polite message offering a new time. Keep wording neutral.

## What Do Workflows 5 to 10 Do?

**5. Birthday offer.** Run daily, filter the sheet for today's birthdays, send an offer. Only use birthdays customers gave you for this purpose.

**6. Cross-posting.** Copy a new Instagram post to a Facebook Page. Check how the apps connect before relying on it.

**7. Review alert.** Email you when a new review arrives, so you can reply fast. BrightLocal found 89% of consumers expect owners to respond.

**8. Lapsed client nudge.** Weekly, find clients whose last visit is older than your usual rebooking gap and send one reminder.

**9. Welcome sequence.** After a first visit, send a thank-you and a tip. One or two messages is enough.

**10. Weekly summary.** Email yourself last week's leads, bookings and reviews from the sheet.

For SMS in workflows 2 to 5 and 8, rules on consent differ by country. This guide did not verify them, so get a clear opt-in before sending automated texts.

## What Can Go Wrong?

Webhooks have limits. Make's help says a webhook with no connected scenario for more than 5 days is deactivated, the queue size depends on your plan, and a full queue rejects new data. Scenario errors stop a scheduled scenario after 3 errors.

Set an email alert for failures, and look at the run history once a week. ## FAQ

### Is Make free for a local business?

Make has a Free plan with 1,000 credits a month. Paid plans add credits; check Make's pricing page for current prices.

### What is a credit in Make?

A credit is Make's billing unit. Most non-AI modules use 1 credit per operation. AI modules can use more, depending on tokens or file size.

### Do I need to code?

No source here covers this directly. Make uses a visual builder, but you may need to copy keys or URLs between apps.

### Can Make send texts?

It lists a Twilio app family, so SMS through Twilio is possible. Check consent rules in your country first.

### What happens if a workflow fails?

Make stops a scheduled scenario after 3 errors, according to its help. Turn on error notifications and review runs weekly.
