For most local businesses, TikTok is the safer first test if your customers are adults aged 18 to 50 and you can spend about $600 a month. Snapchat is the cheaper way to start, with a $5 daily minimum, and fits a young, mobile-first audience. Neither platform publishes an average cost per click or booking, so the winner is the one that produces cheaper bookings in your own account.

This guide compares what the two platforms state publicly as of 2026-10-25: budget floors, location targeting and audience data. It is written for owners of cafés, salons, groomers and studios in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. Gaps are named, and examples are labelled as assumptions.

## How Much Do Snapchat and TikTok Ads Cost to Start?

Snapchat's pricing page says the minimum spend is $5 per day, and recommends $20 to $50 per day so the ad can finish its Exploration Phase, which it says typically lasts four days or more. TikTok's help center says a campaign daily budget must exceed $50 and an ad group daily budget must exceed $20.

The gap is large. TikTok's example: a 31-day ad group needs at least $620 in lifetime budget. Snapchat lets you start far smaller, though a $5 test may end before it teaches you anything. Our [Snapchat ads cost guide](/blog/snapchat-ads-cost-local-business) and [TikTok ads cost guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-guide) go deeper on each.

| | Snapchat | TikTok |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum daily budget | $5 | More than $50 per campaign, more than $20 per ad group |
| Recommended | $20 to $50 per day | Not stated on the budget page |
| New advertiser offer | $75 credit for $50 spend, or $375 for $350 | Not checked for this guide |
| Average CPC or CPM | Not published | Not published by TikTok |

Third-party blogs quote CPM and CPC ranges for both. None of them come from the platforms, so they are left out of the table.

## Which Platform Targets a Local Area Better?

TikTok's location targeting article lists city, DMA region, county, state and zip code (postal code). You can pick up to 3,000 locations per ad group. It does not support excluding locations or zip codes, and it warns that very small areas "can significantly reduce" impressions and may raise cost per acquisition. The pages read do not mention radius targeting.

Snapchat's own site did not load a targeting page for this guide. Radius targeting, which adds an area around an address or pin, was announced in March 2018 (Social Media Today, secondary). Check Ads Manager to confirm what is available now, and whether it fits a shop with a 3 km catchment.

For a business serving one neighbourhood, test the smallest area that still delivers impressions. Expect to widen it.

## Who Uses Each Platform?

Snapchat says 75% of 13 to 34 year olds in 25+ countries use it, that about 80% of its users are over 18 and that nearly 1 in 4 are over 35. Pew's 2025 survey of 5,022 US adults found 37% use TikTok and 25% use Snapchat (the Snapchat figure is as quoted from Pew's text). Pew also reports that adults under 30 stand out on both.

Teens matter only if you serve them. Pew's 2025 teen survey found 55% of US teens use Snapchat and 12% say almost constantly, while 21% say they use TikTok almost constantly, with about six in ten or more using it at all. Teens rarely buy lattes with their own card, but they do book lessons, hair and activities that parents pay for.

## Which Platform Fits Which Customer?

Match the platform to who books, not to who is trendy. This is a judgement based on the audience data above, not a test result.

- **Students and 18 to 24 year olds near a campus:** try Snapchat first, because it is cheap to start and skews young.
- **Adults 25 to 45 who watch short video:** try TikTok first, since its reach among US adults is wider.
- **Customers 50 and older:** skip both and use Google, Facebook and email. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to split a small budget.
- **Parents booking for teens:** test TikTok, then retarget by form or call.

## What Does a Test Need to Earn to Pay Off?

The numbers below are **assumptions**, not platform benchmarks. Say you spend $600 over 30 days on one ad group, and each new booking leaves $35 in margin.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $600 |
| Margin per booking | $35 |
| Bookings to break even | 18 ($600 divided by $35, rounded up) |
| Cost per booking at 18 bookings | about $33, just under the $35 margin |

If your landing page turns clicks into bookings at some rate, you can work backwards. At 1,000 clicks you would need 1.8% to break even; at 300 clicks, 6%. Measure your own click-to-booking rate on a free channel first, then judge each platform against that number.

Do not scale until two things hold for two weeks: the cost per booking is under your margin and you can see the source of each booking.

## FAQ

### Is Snapchat cheaper than TikTok for local business?

At the minimum, yes: $5 per day versus more than $20 per ad group. Neither platform publishes an average cost per booking, so a cheaper entry does not mean a cheaper result.

### Can I target a single zip code on TikTok?

Yes. TikTok lists zip codes among its location options and warns that small areas can cut delivery and raise cost per acquisition. It does not support excluding zip codes.

### Does Snapchat offer free ad credit?

Its pricing and advertising pages list $75 in credit when you spend $50, and $375 when you spend $350. The offer is for first-time advertisers, requires the spend within 14 days of opening the account and the credit expires 30 days after it is applied.

### Which platform has more adult users in the US?

In Pew's 2025 survey, 37% of US adults said they use TikTok and about 25% said they use Snapchat.

### Should a café or salon use both?

Not at first. Run one for 30 days on a capped budget, then compare.
