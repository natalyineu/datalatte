A coach can use webinars to fill a program if the numbers are planned honestly: expect roughly one in three registrants to attend live. Goldcast's 2025 report, covering more than 19,000 B2B webinars in 2024, puts average registration-to-attendance at 33%, with series webinars at 38%. Those are business-to-business figures, so coaching audiences may differ.

This guide covers what published benchmarks say, how to structure a session, the follow-up that matters most and a worked example labelled as an assumption. No source found publishes webinar-to-sale conversion for coaches, so none is quoted here.

## How Many Registrants Actually Attend?

Goldcast reports a 33% average attendance rate for 2024, up from 29%. Webinars that were part of a series reached 38%, and those with "webinar" in the title reached 36%. The average was 238 registrants and 51 attendees per webinar, and average watch time was about 29 minutes, while 45 to 60 minutes was the most common length.

Other vendors report different numbers because they measure different things. Livestorm's 2026 page says its data covers 33,786 sessions and over 7 million registrations from 2025 but does not publish an attendance figure there. Plan with the lower number, and check what a source counts, live only or live plus replay, before comparing.

| Metric | Goldcast, 2024 data |
| --- | --- |
| Average attendance | 33% |
| Series webinar attendance | 38% |
| Average registrants | 238 |
| Average attendees | 51 |
| Most common length | 45 to 60 minutes (58.3%) |
| Average watch time | about 29 minutes |

## Does On-Demand Replay Matter?

Yes, for the people who cannot make it live. Goldcast found 80% of webinars were published on-demand, 9.5% of registrants watched on-demand and 31% of live attendees rewatched. So the recording is part of the offer. Send the replay link to all registrants, not only no-shows.

Since only about a third attend live, plan your follow-up for the other two-thirds. ## How Should a Coach Structure the Session?

Aim for 45 to 60 minutes, because the data says most webinars run that long, and the average viewer stays about 29. That means your key point and offer should arrive before the half-hour mark, not at minute 55.

- **Minutes 0 to 5:** the problem your ideal client has, stated in their words.
- **Minutes 5 to 25:** one framework or lesson they can use today.
- **Minutes 25 to 35:** the offer, with price, who it is for and who it is not for.
- **Remaining time:** questions.

Goldcast's data shows Thursday is the most common day for webinars (28.72%), while Monday had the highest attendance rate (38.21%). That is correlation across B2B industries, so test your own audience rather than copy a day.

## What Tools Do You Need?

You need registration, a way to run the session and a way to email attendees. Zoom's webinar page lists branded registration pages, downloadable reports and CRM connection, priced per user per month, but does not publish the dollar amounts on that page, so check its pricing directly. YouTube allows live streaming if your channel is verified and has no live-streaming restrictions in the past 90 days.

Tool choice matters less than follow-up. Whatever you use, make sure the registration form collects consent. CAN-SPAM requires an accurate sender, a physical address and a working opt-out honoured within 10 business days, per the FTC, with penalties up to $53,088 per email.

## A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**, not published coaching figures.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Registrants | 100 |
| Attendance rate | 33% (Goldcast average) |
| Live attendees | 33 |
| Attendees who book a call | 10% |
| Replay viewers who book a call | 2% of 67 |
| Calls that become clients | 25% |
| Program price | $1,500 |

33 attendees at 10% gives about 3 calls. 67 non-attendees at 2% adds about 1. Four calls at 25% closes one client, worth $1,500. If paid promotion cost $400 for those 100 registrants (assumption), the webinar covers its cost with one client, and loses money with none. Because you cannot know your own rates in advance, run one session and record each step before scaling.

## FAQ

### How many people attend a webinar live?

Goldcast's 2024 data shows 33% on average and 38% for series. Plan for a third, and send a replay to the rest.

### How long should a coaching webinar be?

The most common length is 45 to 60 minutes (58.3% of webinars), and average watch time is about 29 minutes. Make your offer before people drift away.

### Do I need paid software?

Not necessarily. Zoom Webinars is priced per user per month, and YouTube live streaming needs a verified channel. Compare features and cost on each provider's page.

### What day is best?

Goldcast found Monday had the highest attendance rate (38.21%) while Thursday was the most popular day. Treat both as a starting point for testing.

### Do I need consent to email registrants?

Yes in the UK and EU. In the US, CAN-SPAM does not require prior consent but requires an opt-out and your address. Say clearly on the form what you will send.
