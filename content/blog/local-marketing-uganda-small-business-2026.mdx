A small business in Uganda should treat online ads as a way to reach a minority and keep offline channels running. DataReportal counts 11.4 million internet users, 22.0% of 51.7 million people, and a Facebook ad reach of 2.40 million. Start with a Facebook Page and WhatsApp Business, add Instagram only if your customers use it, and keep a sign and referrals working alongside.

This guide covers shops, salons, cafes and service firms in Uganda. Figures are from the sources at the end, checked on 1 November 2026. Where nothing could be verified, the text says so.

## How Many Ugandans Are Online?

About one in five. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report lists 11.4 million internet users out of 51.7 million people, which is 22.0%. Mobile connections number 40.9 million, 79.1% of the population. Phones are common, internet use is not, so an ad campaign reaches a smaller and probably more urban group than the whole market.

| Measure | Uganda, late 2025 | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Population (UN) | 51.7 million | n/a |
| Cellular mobile connections | 40.9 million | 79.1% |
| Internet users | 11.4 million | 22.0% |
| Facebook ad reach | 2.40 million | 4.6% |
| LinkedIn members | 1.80 million | 3.5% |
| Instagram ad reach | 883 thousand | 1.7% |
| X ad reach | 789 thousand | 1.5% |
| Messenger ad reach | 405 thousand | 0.8% |
| TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat | Not reported | n/a |

Facebook's reach equals about 21% of internet users (2.40 divided by 11.4). Instagram is about 37% of Facebook's size.

## Which Platform Fits a Ugandan Small Business?

Facebook first, then WhatsApp for the conversation. Facebook is the largest consumer platform in the DataReportal table, and Messenger adds 405 thousand. Instagram at 883 thousand is large enough for visual trades such as salons and food, but the budget case is weaker.

LinkedIn's 1.80 million are registered members, not active users. It suits firms selling to other businesses, such as accountants and trainers. It is not a channel for a corner shop.

## Can a Ugandan Business Run Google Ads?

Google's list of billing countries includes Uganda. In that list the account currency and payment method columns read "All", but Google says the options you see depend on your billing country, currency and payment setting, and that prepay is not offered in many countries.

Open the billing page in your own account to see what is available. DataReportal does not report Google ad reach for Uganda, and none of the sources checked publishes Ugandan click prices.

Because three in four Ugandans are offline, choose keywords that match what a buyer with internet access and money would type, and set the location to the town you serve. ## How Many Small Businesses Are There in Uganda?

No current official count was opened for this guide. An IFC market analysis from 2021 describes an estimated 1.1 million micro, small and medium enterprises and says most are informal micro-entrepreneurs. That is a secondary reading from a search summary, and the figure is five years old, so treat it as an order of magnitude.

The practical meaning is competition from many tiny sellers with the same Facebook Page and WhatsApp number. A clear offer, a fast reply and a visible location separate you from them more than an extra campaign does.

## What Does a First Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for a hair salon that books on WhatsApp. They show the method, not Ugandan results. Currency is "units" of your local money.

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Gross profit per new client over a year (assumption) | 60 units |
| Enquiries that become booked clients (assumption) | 30% |
| Most you can pay per enquiry and still break even | 60 x 30% = 18 units |
| Test budget (assumption) | 180 units |
| Enquiries needed to break even | 180 / 18 = 10 enquiries |

Track every enquiry in a notebook or spreadsheet with the source the client names. After four weeks, compare ten enquiries with the real number. The [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to split a small budget between channels.

## How Do You Get Found on Google Maps in Uganda?

Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. It also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Fill in every field, use real photos, reply to every review and keep the name, address and phone number identical everywhere. Add the nearest landmark and a plain-language direction in the description, because many customers will search with a place name rather than a street address. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes through each field.

Do not offer a discount or a free item in return for a review. Google's contributed content policy says merchants must not offer incentives, such as payment, discounts, free goods or services, in exchange for posting a review. Asking is fine. Rewarding is not. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for ways to ask.

## What Mistakes Do Ugandan Small Businesses Make?

These are recommendations, not study findings.

- Judging an ad by likes. Count WhatsApp enquiries and booked clients.
- Ignoring the offline majority. A painted sign and a referral card still do most of the selling in many towns.
- Targeting the whole country. Pick the district or town you can serve.
- Leaving messages unanswered overnight.

## FAQ

### How many Ugandans use Facebook?

DataReportal reports a Facebook ad reach of 2.40 million in late 2025, 4.6% of the population. It is an ad-tool estimate, not a count of active users.

### What share of Ugandans use the internet?

11.4 million people, or 22.0%, according to DataReportal for October 2025. Data lag may understate current use.

### Is Google Ads available in Uganda?

Uganda appears in Google's list of billing countries. No source checked publishes click prices for Uganda, so run a small test.

### Should I use mobile money to pay for ads?

Not verified. The part of Google's payment methods page that was checked lists cards and bank accounts, not mobile money. A 2024 survey figure for mobile money ownership was not confirmed from a primary source, so none is quoted.

### Is LinkedIn useful for a Ugandan business?

Only if you sell to other businesses. DataReportal counts 1.80 million LinkedIn members, which is registered accounts, not active users.
