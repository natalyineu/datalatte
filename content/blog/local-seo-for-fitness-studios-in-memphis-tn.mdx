Local SEO for a fitness studio in Memphis, TN means getting your listing into the Google Maps pack for searches like "personal training studio near me." The main levers are a complete Google Business Profile (GBP), consistent business details across the web, a steady flow of recent reviews, and a website that repeats the same service and location signals. Google's Business Profile is free to set up, so the work is mostly time and consistency. The steps below follow that order.

## How does the Google Maps pack choose fitness studios?

The Map Pack is the set of local listings Google shows in its Maps results for queries such as "personal training studios near me." One fitness association describes it as the section that lists relevant local businesses for these searches ([How to Make Your Fitness Business Stand Out on Google](https://member.afsfitness.com/content/how-make-your-fitness-business-stand-out-google), secondary). Your listing is the input Google uses to decide whether you appear there. In a 2021 BrightLocal survey, 36% of SEOs said the Google Business Profile was the most important ranking factor for the map pack, up from 23% in 2013 ([Local SEO: The Complete Guide, Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/seo/local-seo), secondary).

Google's own help material describes the profile as the place where business hours, phone numbers, addresses, reviews, and photos appear directly in results ([Get Listed on Google, Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)). That makes the profile both a ranking input and the first thing a shopper reads.

## Which Google Business Profile fields should a Memphis studio fill in first?

Start with the fields that define what you are. Choose a primary category that matches your core service, then add secondary categories for specific modalities such as yoga, Pilates, or CrossFit. Write your business description around the services you actually sell and the neighborhoods your members come from, and keep the wording natural. Stuffing "Memphis" into every line reads poorly to people and does not help you rank.

Next, set your hours, phone number, and website link, then add your services as individual entries. List each class type you run, such as HIIT, barre, or personal training, so each has its own line in your profile. Check the details monthly. Small mismatches between your profile and your website create confusion for both shoppers and search systems.

## How many photos and posts does a studio need?

The Vertical Impression guide recommends uploading at least 15 authentic photos of your facility, classes in action, and staff ([Gym and Fitness Studio Marketing Guide](https://www.verticalimpression.com/guides/how-to-market-health-wellness-business-2026), secondary). Use real photos from your own floor and coaches rather than stock images. Refresh them when your space or schedule changes.

Google's help page for posts explains that a post shows publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts do not appear ([Create and manage posts on your Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)). After you publish, check the status. A post that is stuck in review is not doing any work for you.

Plan posts around real events: new class launches, trial offers, holiday schedules, and community events. Keep them specific, with a date and a clear next step.

## How many reviews does a Memphis studio need to compete?

Google's review guidance says reviews appear next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search ([Tips to get more reviews, Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)). That makes every review part of your first impression. Ask members for a review after a good session or a milestone, and make the request easy with a direct link to your profile.

A secondary article claims that businesses with more than 50 reviews and a 4.7+ average rating tend to dominate top map pack spots. That claim is not backed by a cited study, so treat it as a target to test rather than a rule. Track your count and average each month and compare against nearby competitors you can see on Maps.

Respond to reviews in your own words, and name your service and neighborhood where it fits. Keep responses short and accurate.

## Do citations still matter for Memphis map rankings?

Citations are listings of your name, address, and phone number on other websites. The point is consistency. Use the same business name, address format, and phone number everywhere. "Street" in one listing and "St." in another creates a mismatch that you should clean up. Start with your GBP, then check the major directories and any industry booking platforms your studio already uses.

Fix errors in existing listings before adding new ones. Adding more inconsistent listings makes the problem bigger. If you use Google Ads as well, note that location data there is sourced directly from Google Maps and Google Business Profile, and corrections made in those places flow into linked Google Ads accounts ([About location assets, Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)). One fix in your profile can therefore help both channels.

## How do your website pages support the map pack?

Your website should repeat the same service and location signals as your profile. Give each major service its own page, with a clear heading that names the service and the city. Link to your service pages from your profile and from your site navigation.

Embedding a Google Map on your contact or location page is an easy supporting step. ## FAQ

### Is a Google Business Profile really free?

Yes. Google describes its Business Profile as a free listing on Search and Maps ([Get Listed on Google](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)). Costs come from time, photos, and any paid advertising you add on top.

### How long does it take to see map pack changes?

No official timeline is given in the sources used here. Track profile views and map position for at least 60 days before judging a change. Ranking movement depends on many factors, including competition and review activity.

They work well together. Paid search gives immediate visibility while organic listings build over time. For setup steps, read [Google Ads for fitness studios: the 2026 guide](/blog/google-ads-for-fitness-studios-complete-2026-guide).
