Bing Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant inside Bing. For a local question such as "best coffee shop near me," it can write an answer that names businesses before the usual links appear. To show up there, a local business needs accurate public information, clear service pages written as direct answers, and optionally a Microsoft Advertising account for a paid test. This guide covers each step, a worked budget example, and the checks to run on Microsoft's own help pages before spending anything.

## What Is Bing Copilot, and Is It the Same as GitHub Copilot?

Bing Copilot answers questions inside Bing search. It is a different product from GitHub Copilot, the coding assistant, and the two are easy to confuse. Licensing for GitHub Copilot runs under separate GitHub and Microsoft terms, depending on how the plan was bought ([secondary: licensing summary](https://wiki.licenseware.io/wiki/github-copilot-licensing/)). Tom's Hardware has also reported that Microsoft is moving GitHub Copilot to usage-based pricing ([secondary](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/github-copilot-customers-suffer-from-sticker-shock-as-microsoft-switches-to-usage-based-pricing-customers-report-up-to-100-fold-price-hikes)). Neither point affects how a local shop appears in Bing.

Search optimization still means the same thing. A definition from Search Engine Land, quoted by the Association of Fitness Studios, describes SEO as improving a website to increase its visibility in Google, Microsoft Bing, and other search engines ([secondary](https://member.afsfitness.com/content/how-make-your-fitness-business-stand-out-google)). Copilot visibility sits inside that broader job. The site has to be findable, readable, and accurate, and the same facts have to appear everywhere a customer or search engine might look.

## What Should You Check on Your Listings First?

Start with the information customers and search engines read most often. Use this checklist before changing anything else:

1. Confirm the business name, address, and phone number match exactly on your website, Google Business Profile, and any Microsoft listing you claim.
2. Open Microsoft's current business listing help page and follow its live steps for claiming and verifying. Third-party tutorials go out of date when interfaces change.
3. On each key service page, write a short, factual answer to the questions customers ask most: typical price range, hours, and how to book.
4. Keep prices and opening hours identical across every page and listing where they appear.
5. Ask recent customers for reviews on the sites they already use, and reply to each one.

Clear, consistent facts are the signals you control. Vague marketing copy gives Copilot less to work with, and contradictions between your pages make the answer harder to trust.

## How Do You Test Microsoft Advertising Without Overspending?

A small test shows whether the numbers hold for your market. Assume a hair salon spends $600 a month on Google Ads, with an average Google CPC of $1.20 (assumption). The salon tests $250 a month on Microsoft Advertising (assumption). Applying the vendor-cited 19% lower CPC as a planning figure gives $1.20 × 0.81 ≈ $0.97 per click (assumption).

| Item | Google-rate basis | Microsoft basis (assumption) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly test budget | $250 | $250 |
| Average CPC | $1.20 | $0.97 |
| Clicks per month | about 208 | about 258 |

At these inputs, the same budget buys about 50 more clicks a month. That result depends entirely on the assumed CPC, so measure the real figure in your own account.

The promotion offers new US customers a $500 ad credit when they spend $250 on advertising charges, excluding taxes ([Microsoft Advertising](https://ads.microsoft.com/)). At the assumed $250 monthly pace, the credit would cover about two months of spend, but only if the account qualifies. Any credit not used within 90 days of award expires and cannot be carried over, and the offer expires June 30, 2027 ([Microsoft Advertising](https://ads.microsoft.com/)). Readers in the UK, Australia, and Canada should check the local offer page before assuming the same terms apply.

## When Does Adding Microsoft Advertising Make Sense?

It makes sense when your Google Ads account is already producing clear results and you want to test a second source of clicks. Start with the setup and cost questions covered in [the Microsoft Advertising cost guide for local businesses](/blog/bing-ads-microsoft-advertising-cost-local-business), then read [the local setup walkthrough](/blog/bing-ads-microsoft-advertising-local-business) before launching.

Display placements are a separate decision. If you consider them, [the Microsoft Audience Network guide](/blog/microsoft-audience-network-local-business) covers how that inventory differs from search. Keep the test small, set a stop date, and compare cost per booked appointment, not just cost per click. ## How Should Customer Questions Feed Your Visibility?

Repeated customer questions are the content Copilot is most likely to draw on. Answer them once, clearly, on the page where people look for them, and keep those answers identical across your listings. A support tool can help you keep replies consistent when the same questions arrive by phone, chat, and email. [AI agents for customer service](/blog/ai-agent-for-customer-service-local-business) covers how that works for small teams, though it does not change how Bing ranks a page.

Review your question list every quarter. Remove answers that are out of date, add the questions customers have started asking, and check that your Microsoft listing still matches the website.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I have to pay to appear in Bing Copilot answers?

The sources behind this article do not explain how Copilot selects businesses, so confirm the current rules on Microsoft's help pages. Paid advertising is a separate product through Microsoft Advertising, with its own offers and terms ([Microsoft Advertising](https://ads.microsoft.com/)).

### Is the $500 Microsoft Advertising credit available outside the US?

The published offer is for new US customers. Check the local offer page for the UK, Australia, or Canada before assuming the same credit applies.

### How long does the credit last once I receive it?

Credit not used within 90 days of award expires and cannot be carried over or restored. The promotion itself ends June 30, 2027 ([Microsoft Advertising](https://ads.microsoft.com/)).

### Is GitHub Copilot the same product as Bing Copilot?

No. GitHub Copilot is a coding assistant with its own licensing and pricing terms, so policy and pricing pages for it do not describe Bing Copilot ([secondary](https://wiki.licenseware.io/wiki/github-copilot-licensing/)).
