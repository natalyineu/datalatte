## How Do Couples Find a Wedding Planner?

No source checked here gives a ranked list of how couples find planners, so test each route against your own enquiry form: ask every enquirer where they heard of you. Typical routes to track are Google search, vendor referrals, past-client recommendations, Instagram and Pinterest, and wedding directories.

Pinterest says its users saved more than 16.7 billion wedding ideas worldwide, which suggests couples collect ideas there. That is platform data, not independent research. ## How Do You Win on Google?

Start with your Business Profile. Google says reviews are part of the prominence factor and that more reviews and positive ratings can help local ranking. It also says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Reply to every review, as Google notes replies show that you value feedback.

Collect reviews honestly. The FTC's rule on consumer reviews, effective 21 October 2024, bans fake reviews, buying positive or negative reviews, and review suppression through threats. It does not prohibit incentives for reviews, as long as they do not require a particular sentiment. Ask each couple after the wedding and never script the verdict. ## Which Vendor Partnerships Bring Referrals?

Venues, photographers, florists, caterers and hair and makeup artists meet couples at the same stage but sell different services. A referral works best when it is reciprocal and specific. Introduce yourself with a one-page summary of the weddings you plan, the budget range you serve and how to reach you.

Keep a simple log of referrals in and out. After six months you will see which partners send couples who book. No verified referral rate exists for planners, so use your own log. ## Should a Wedding Planner Run Paid Ads?

Maybe, once the profile and website convert. WordStream by LocaliQ has no wedding planner category. The closest are Personal Services, with a $7.17 average click and 12.34% conversion rate, and Arts and Entertainment, with $1.63 and 5.91%. Treat them as a wide range. A planner's keywords, such as "wedding planner [city]", are specific, so check bid estimates in Google's Keyword Planner and your own account.

The first month should be a test with a strict cap. ## What Should a Planner's Website Show?

Show full weddings, not just a gallery: the brief, the venue, the timeline and what you handled. Add the services you offer (full planning, partial planning, day-of coordination), your starting price or a pricing range if you are comfortable sharing it, and a short enquiry form with date, location and guest count.

Do not invent testimonials or case studies. Use real quotes with the couple's permission. A fast mobile page with a visible enquiry button does more than extra pages. The Pinterest report also shows trend data you can use for content, such as interest in atmospheric venues and flower bars, but verify any trend before basing a package on it.

One booking in a month covers the spend several times over on these inputs, but if the booking rate falls to 10% of consultations, you get 0.4 bookings a month and the result takes a quarter to show. Weddings sell slowly, so judge ads over three to six months.

## FAQ

### How can a new wedding planner get clients?

Set up a Google Business Profile, publish two or three full weddings or styled shoots with permission, and build referrals from venues and vendors. Ask each enquirer how they found you.

### Are Google Ads worth it for wedding planners?

They can be if your website converts. LocaliQ publishes no wedding category, so use its Personal Services range of $7.17 per click as a cautious guide and track enquiries.

### Can I offer a discount for reviews?

The FTC rule does not prohibit incentives as long as they do not require a particular sentiment. Fake reviews and buying reviews that require praise are banned. Check Google's and your local rules too.

### Is Pinterest useful for wedding planners?

Pinterest reports over 7 billion wedding searches a year, so couples clearly use it for ideas. It is vendor data, and you should measure your own enquiries from it.

### Should I list my prices?

No verified study settles this. A starting price or range filters out mismatched enquiries. Test it on your own site and compare the quality of enquiries.
