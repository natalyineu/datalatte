A barbershop can run CTV ads from $500 on self-serve platforms. Roku Ads Manager and Disney Campaign Manager (Hulu and Disney+) both list a $500 minimum, and Disney lists ZIP code targeting. At an industry CPM near $26 to $30, $500 buys roughly 17,000 to 19,000 ad views. That is awareness, not instant bookings, so a barbershop should treat CTV as a test beside Google Business Profile and Search ads.

This guide covers what the platforms publish, what they leave out, and how to size a test. No barbershop-specific CTV results are published by the sources below, so the example is labelled as an assumption.

## How Much Do CTV Ads Cost for a Barbershop?

Platforms charge per thousand views (CPM) and do not publish rate cards. Roku says pricing is dynamic and based on demand and inventory, and you can set a maximum bid. Vibe, an ad vendor, estimates Roku CPMs at $20 to $60 and says most campaigns start around $500. AdWave, a reseller citing eMarketer, puts the 2026 blended US CTV CPM at about $26, with most campaigns between $25 and $35.

Treat all of those as secondary estimates. Roku does not list a CPM. Your real cost shows in the campaign dashboard after launch. Our [CTV cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data) explains the pricing terms.

## What Should a Barbershop Ad Show?

Show the shop, a chair, and one offer. Roku accepts .mov or .mp4 files from 6 to 92 seconds, up to 1 GB, and says existing social videos can be reused. A 15-second clip of a fade, the street entrance and a booking line is enough.

End with one action: a short web address or a QR code. Roku mentions interactive end cards and QR codes through a partner. A viewer on a sofa will not type a long link, so choose a short one that goes to your booking page.

## Is a Barbershop Test Worth the Money? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**, not published results.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $600 for four weeks |
| CPM | $30 |
| Ad views | 20,000 |
| Households reached (frequency of 4) | about 5,000 |
| New bookings credited to the test | 6 |
| Average first-visit price | $35 |
| Cuts per year per returning client | 10 |
| Share of new clients who return | 50% |

Six new clients cost $100 each. First-visit revenue is $210. If half return and each spends $35 ten times a year, three clients add $1,050. The test pays back in year one only if that return rate holds, and nothing guarantees it. Count bookings that mention TV or a QR code, and compare with a similar period.

## FAQ

### What is the minimum budget for CTV ads for a barbershop?
Roku requires a lifetime budget of $500 or more per campaign, and Disney Campaign Manager lists a $500 minimum. Tubi has not stated a minimum in the source checked.

### Can I target only my neighborhood?
Disney Campaign Manager lists ZIP code targeting. The Roku pages checked do not describe geography, so confirm it in the account.

### Do I need a professional video?
No platform page checked requires one. Roku says existing social videos can be reused if they meet the file rules.

### How many bookings should I expect?
No source publishes barbershop CTV results. Set a test goal, track new clients by source, and judge after a full month.

### Is the CPM the price per booking?
No. CPM is the price per 1,000 ad views. Cost per booking depends on how many viewers act, which you must measure yourself.
