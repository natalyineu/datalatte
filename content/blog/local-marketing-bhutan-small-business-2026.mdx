A small business in Bhutan should treat Facebook and Messenger as the main paid and organic channel. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 485,000 Facebook ad-reach users, 60.8% of a population of about 798,000, and 362,000 on Messenger. Instagram reaches 148,000 and LinkedIn 140,000 members.

This guide uses only sourced numbers. No Bhutan-specific ad price data was found, and DataReportal lists no TikTok or YouTube audience for Bhutan, so this article does not estimate either.

## How Big Is the Online Market in Bhutan?

It is small. DataReportal (Kepios data, October 2025) lists 798,000 people, 45.8% in urban areas, and 706,000 internet users, which is 88.4% penetration. Cellular connections total 911,000, or 114% of the population. About 92,400 people are offline.

The World Bank puts Bhutan's 2025 GDP at 3.58 billion US dollars and GDP per capita at 4,492.8 dollars. The World Bank also reports internet use of 91% in 2024, a slightly different measure from DataReportal's, so quote one source consistently.

For a business, the audience in the whole country is smaller than a mid-sized city elsewhere. Targeting by district matters more than targeting by interest.

## Which Platforms Matter?

Facebook is first. Its 485,000 ad reach equals 68.7% of internet users and grew 7.4% between October 2024 and October 2025. Messenger follows at 362,000, which signals that customers expect to message a business.

Instagram reaches 148,000 and grew 6.3%. LinkedIn counts 140,000 registered members, a count of accounts, not active users. X reaches 14,600.

DataReportal cautions that ad reach is not the same as active users, and platforms revise these counts. Use them to rank channels, not to forecast sales.

## How Do Bhutanese Customers Pay?

Digitally, and mostly by QR code. Bhutan Broadcasting Service reported that in April to June 2025, mobile banking handled nearly 40 million transactions, up about 15% on a year earlier. QR code payments passed 37 million, up 27.86%, and digital wallets passed 2 million.

The same report counts over 900 point-of-sale machines. A shop or cafe that shows a QR code at the till and on its Facebook page matches how customers already pay. These are news-report figures, so they are secondary.

## Should a Tourism Business Market Differently?

Yes, because overseas visitors decide before they arrive. Bhutan charges most international adult visitors a Sustainable Development Fee, which Nation Thailand reports as 100 US dollars per person per night, cut from 200 dollars and scheduled to stay at that level until 31 August 2027. Children aged 6 to 11 pay 50 dollars, and Indian nationals pay a separate rate of 1,200 rupees.

This is a secondary report. I could not open an official Department of Tourism page, so check current fees there before quoting them to guests.

For a guesthouse or cafe, that means the visitor already pays a fixed nightly fee. A clear price, location and booking contact on your Google Business Profile and Facebook page can matter more than a large ad budget.

## What Would a Cautious Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**, not Bhutanese benchmarks.

- Audience size to reach in your district (assumption): 5,000 people
- Posts a week on Facebook (assumption): 3
- Messenger reply target (assumption): same day
- Test length (assumption): 4 weeks

Track three counts each week: new Messenger conversations, bookings or visits that came from them, and walk-ins who mention Facebook. If four weeks produce fewer than a handful of bookings, change the offer or the photos before adding paid reach. With such a small audience, repeat reach wears out quickly.

## FAQ

### How many people use Facebook in Bhutan?

DataReportal counts 485,000 Facebook ad-reach users in late 2025, 60.8% of the population. It is not a count of active users.

### Is Instagram useful for a Bhutanese business?

It reaches 148,000 people, so it suits tourism and lifestyle businesses with strong photos. It is about a third of Facebook's size.

### How much do ads cost in Bhutan?

No verified Bhutan cost-per-click or CPM data was found, so none is quoted here. Test with a small budget and measure your own cost per booking.

### What is Bhutan's tourist fee?

A secondary report puts the Sustainable Development Fee at 100 US dollars per adult per night until 31 August 2027. Confirm with the Department of Tourism.

### Do customers use mobile payments?

Yes. QR payments passed 37 million transactions in April to June 2025, according to Bhutan Broadcasting Service.
