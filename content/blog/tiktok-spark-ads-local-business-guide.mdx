A Spark Ad pays to amplify an existing TikTok post instead of uploading a separate advert. Views, likes, comments and follows stay on the original post, per TikTok's Spark Ads documentation checked in November 2026. TikTok's budget rules require campaign budgets above $50 a day and ad groups above $20 a day, which makes Spark a deliberate test, not pocket change.

That minimum changes the local maths. A $350 week is the smallest honest Spark test at campaign level, so the post being boosted matters more than the targeting panel. Boosting a weak organic post buys a larger audience for the same weakness.

## How do Spark Ads work?

The business links its TikTok account in Ads Manager, or a creator authorises one video. The creator opens the video's Ad settings, turns on authorization, picks 7, 30, 60 or 365 days, and sends the generated code. The advertiser pastes the code into Ads Manager and builds the campaign around that exact post.

Because engagement accumulates on the original post, Spark suits proof-led content: a queue outside the café, a groom transformation, a class in full swing. The comments arriving under the ad remain visible to later organic viewers, which is both the benefit and the moderation duty.

Compare the format honestly with standard TikTok ads before spending. Standard in-feed ads accept uploaded creative without an existing post, while Spark requires content that already lives on an account. ## Which post should a local business boost?

Boost the post strangers already finish. Completion and shares on the organic version are the only free creative test a small business gets; paying to distribute a post people skip at second three wastes the $50 daily floor. Pick the best-performing genuine post from the last month, not the most polished one.

Film the next candidates deliberately: the product made in real time, a customer question answered at the counter, the room at its busiest honest moment. Captions naming the suburb help local viewers place the business before any targeting loads. **Assumption example.** Assume a dessert shop boosts its best post at the $50 campaign minimum for 7 days, or $350. At an assumed $8 CPM that buys roughly 43,000 impressions inside the chosen area. If 0.3% tap through to the profile or directions and 10% of those visit within the week, the test implies about 13 visits. Every rate is an assumption; TikTok reports the first two numbers, and the till reports the third.

## Is Spark right for a small local budget?

Only when organic TikTok already shows a pulse: regular posts, comments from local accounts, profile taps. Spark amplifies existing signal; it does not create one. A business with three posts and no comments should spend a month posting before spending $350 amplifying silence.

Set the campaign inside the real trade area and judge on profile actions that precede revenue: direction taps, calls, bookings where the link supports it. Views will look enormous and mean little. Move budget to a second post only after the first produces actions at a cost the margin can carry. ## FAQ

### How much do TikTok Spark Ads cost?

TikTok requires campaign budgets above $50 a day and ad group budgets above $20 a day, per its Ads Manager rules checked in November 2026. Media is auction-priced beyond those floors, and creator content, if commissioned, is paid separately outside Ads Manager. A 7-day minimum-scale test therefore costs about $350 before any creator fee.

### What is a Spark Ads authorization code?

It is the permission token for one video. The creator enables Ad authorization in the video's settings, chooses 7, 30, 60 or 365 days, and sends the code to the advertiser, who pastes it into Ads Manager. Without a code, someone else's post cannot be boosted. The creator can revoke authorization, which stops the ad.

### Spark Ads or standard TikTok ads for a café?

Spark when a genuine post already earns completions and local comments, because the engagement stays on the business's profile. Standard ads when no post deserves amplification yet, or when the offer needs a built landing experience. Most cafés should post organically for a month, then Spark the winner rather than manufacturing an advert first.

### Can Spark Ads target one suburb?

Yes. Spark campaigns use Ads Manager location targeting, so delivery can be limited to the trade area around the premises. Tight geography protects the $50 daily floor from spreading across a whole metro. Combine the radius with the post's own suburb naming in captions, so viewers can place the business instantly.
