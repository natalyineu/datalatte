Here is the arithmetic that frames a local TikTok test. TikTok's help page says an ad group's daily budget must exceed $20, so a 14-day test at $21 a day costs $294. If a new customer is worth about $30 in gross profit to you (my assumption, replace it with yours), you need about 10 people to walk in because of the ads to break even. Everything below is about making those 10 people more likely to be the right people.

One more fact changes how you set the test up. Many guides tell local businesses to draw a one-mile circle around the shop. TikTok's own location targeting page lists levels such as city, county, DMA region, state, urban district and zip or postal code. It does not mention radius targeting, and it says TikTok does not support excluding locations or zip codes. This guide shows how to work with what is actually there, for businesses in the US, UK, Australia and Canada.

If you run a café, our [step-by-step TikTok ads guide for coffee shops](/blog/tiktok-ads-for-coffee-shops-step-by-step-guide) walks through the whole setup. This article goes deeper on the targeting decisions that apply to any local business.

## What TikTok's Location Targeting Actually Offers

From TikTok's [location targeting page](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/location-targeting?lang=en):

- **Levels:** city, country or region, county, department, district, DMA region, province, state, urban district, and zip or postal code.
- **No radius.** The page does not mention radius targeting. If a guide tells you to "enter your address and pick 1 mile", check whether your own Ads Manager actually offers it before planning around it.
- **No exclusions.** TikTok says it does not support excluding locations or zip codes, so you build your area by choosing what to include.
- **Up to 3,000 location selections** per ad group where available.
- **Zip+4 is not supported.** Uploading it returns an error. For Canada, enter only the first three characters of the postal code, for example "L6V" rather than "L6V 1B4".
- **Who is reached:** targeting is used to find people "located in or regularly in the selected location", and TikTok says delivery and accuracy are not guaranteed in every situation.
- **Market limits:** some options are not available in all countries or regions. Check the choices inside your own Ads Manager.
- **Small areas cost more.** TikTok warns that small geographic areas or small sets of zip codes can significantly reduce impressions and clicks and may result in a higher cost per acquisition.

That last point is the one most local businesses hit. A single-postcode campaign can look perfectly targeted and still starve for impressions. Start with a handful of nearby codes rather than one.

### How to Choose Your Postcodes

1. Export your last 50 to 100 customers' postcodes from your till, booking system or loyalty list.
2. Count them. The top three to five codes are your core.
3. Add neighbouring codes only if your customers regularly cross them: a commute route, a school, a station.
4. Leave out codes that are close on the map but cut off by a motorway, river or no direct route. Distance matters less than whether people actually pass you.
5. Check the estimated audience size in Ads Manager. If it is tiny, widen the area before you spend.

For UK and Australian businesses, confirm in your Ads Manager what postal levels are offered for your market; TikTok says availability varies by where the account is registered.

## Layer Audiences Carefully

TikTok's targeting help page explains the logic: within one dimension, selections combine with OR, so adding options broadens the audience; across dimensions, selections combine with AND, so each added dimension narrows it ([TikTok Ads Help: Ad targeting dimensions](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/ad-targeting)).

For a local business that means:

- You already have one narrow dimension: location. Stacking age, gender and several interests on top of a small postcode set can shrink the audience to a few thousand people.
- Start with location and a broad age range that matches your real customers, and let the video do the filtering. A video that shows a flat white will mostly be watched by people who like coffee.
- Add one dimension at a time, and only if you can say why. "Women 25 to 44" is a guess unless your booking data says so.

## Custom Audiences: Check the 1,000 Rule

Custom Audiences built from a customer file let you target past customers or exclude them from new-customer offers. But TikTok's setup guide says the file must contain at least 1,000 entries, in CSV or TXT format under 1 GB, and that the audience can take 24 to 48 hours to generate ([TikTok Ads Help: Create a Custom Audience with a customer file](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-create-a-custom-audience-with-a-customer-file)). A separate page says accepted identifiers are email, phone number and mobile advertiser ID, hashed or original ([TikTok Ads Help: Customer File audiences](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/customer-file?lang=en)).

Most single-location cafés, salons and studios do not have 1,000 customer emails or phone numbers. If you do not, skip this feature for now and spend your effort on building the list. ## Use Ads Manager, Not Just the Promote Button

TikTok describes Promote as a lightweight, in-app advertising tool, and says linking it to Ads Manager adds "deeper audience targeting" (interest, location, demographics, behaviour), flexible budget management at ad group level, and deeper conversion objectives such as website conversions with Pixel tracking ([TikTok Ads Help: Promote integration with Ads Manager](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-promote-integration-with-tiktok-ads-manager)). The page does not spell out Promote's own location options, so for anything local, set up Ads Manager and confirm the postcode level is available before you commit budget.

## Schedule, Budget and Patience

**Schedule.** TikTok's ad delivery FAQ says that under Budget & Schedule you can set start and stop dates and schedule ads for specific hours and days of the week, and that delivery follows the account time zone, so check that first ([TikTok Ads Help: Ad delivery FAQ](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/ad-delivery-faq?redirected=2)). Many guides give fixed "best times", but we found no TikTok source for them. A better method: run all hours for the first week, then look at when your redemptions arrive and trim.

**Budget.** TikTok requires more than $50 at campaign level and more than $20 at ad group level for daily budgets. For lifetime budgets, an ad group needs at least the number of days (including past days) times $20, so a 31-day run needs at least $620 ([TikTok Ads Help: Budgets](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget)). One campaign with one ad group and a $21 daily budget over 14 days clears these rules at $294. The campaign minimum is a separate figure, so check what your account displays.

**Patience.** TikTok's learning-phase page says the system works out which users respond best, that performance can fluctuate, and that volatility typically declines after about 25 campaign results or 7 days. It advises against pausing and against edits that retrigger learning ([TikTok Ads Help: Learning phase](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/learning-phase)). At $21 a day you may not reach 25 conversions in a week, so judge on your own count of walk-ins rather than waiting for the label to disappear. Do not touch the ad for the first seven days.

## Count Walk-Ins, Not Clicks

Local businesses need a way to link a video to a till or an appointment.

- **A spoken or on-screen offer.** "Show this video for a free upgrade" lets staff tally redemptions. Our [salon](/blog/tiktok-marketing-for-hair-salons) and [bakery](/blog/tiktok-marketing-for-bakeries) guides show how two other trades do it.
- **A link with UTM tags** if people book or order online; see [UTM parameters explained](/blog/utm-parameters-track-every-marketing-campaign).
- **The TikTok Pixel and Events API** for online conversions. TikTok recommends using Events API alongside the Pixel for websites because connectivity issues and browser inconsistency can affect Pixel-reported conversions ([TikTok Ads Help: Events API](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/events-api?lang=en)).
- **Offline events.** TikTok says offline conversions can be sent through the Events API, a partner integration, or a manual upload in Events Manager, and it lists click-through attribution windows of 28, 14, 7 or 1 day and view-through windows of 7 or 1 day ([TikTok Ads Help: Offline conversions](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-offline-conversions?lang=en)). For a small shop, a weekly upload of redemptions is more than enough; a tally on a notepad is where to start.

## The Math

Take the $294 test: one ad group at $21 a day for 14 days. Every row below except the budget is an **assumption**, because we have no verified local TikTok CPM or redemption data. Replace the figures with your results.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Test budget (14 days at $21) | $294 | $294 | $294 |
| CPM (assumption) | $20 | $12 | $8 |
| Impressions | 14,700 | 24,500 | 36,750 |
| Redemptions per 1,000 impressions (assumption) | 0.3 | 0.8 | 1.5 |
| Redemptions | 4.4 | 19.6 | 55.1 |
| Cost per redemption | $66.67 | $15.00 | $5.33 |
| Gross profit per new customer (assumption) | $30 | $30 | $30 |
| Gross profit | $132 | $588 | $1,653 |
| Net after ad cost | -$162 | +$294 | +$1,359 |

Read it this way: the Tough column mirrors TikTok's warning that a small area raises cost per acquisition. The Strong column is a best case. Break-even customers equal $294 divided by your gross profit per customer, about 10 at $30. If your average customer is worth $120 over a year, you need only three. If they are one-off buyers at $12, you need 25 and should not run the test.

## What to Do After the Test

- **At or above break-even:** keep the same postcodes and offer, and raise spend gradually.
- **Impressions are low:** add neighbouring postcodes before you change the video.
- **Impressions are fine but redemptions are low:** change the first two seconds of the video and the offer, one at a time.
- **No redemptions at all:** check the basics first. Does the video show where you are? Can a staff member tell which offer someone means? Is your [Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile) correct for people who search your name next?

## Do This Today

1. Export the postcodes of your last 50 to 100 customers and list the top three to five.
2. Work out your gross profit per new customer and your break-even number at $294.
3. Open TikTok Ads Manager and check which location levels your account offers. Note whether zip or postal codes appear.
4. Plan one video with one offer, and decide how staff will count redemptions.
5. Launch one campaign, one ad group, $21 a day for 14 days, and leave it alone for the first seven days.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I target a one-mile radius on TikTok?**
A: TikTok's location targeting page lists city, county, DMA region, state, urban district and zip or postal code, and does not mention radius targeting. Check the options in your own Ads Manager. If radius is not offered, choose the nearby postcodes instead.

**Q: Can I exclude areas I do not serve?**
A: No. TikTok says it does not support excluding locations or zip codes, so build your area by selecting only the places you want.

**Q: How small can my target area be?**
A: TikTok does not give a minimum, but it warns that small areas or small sets of zip codes can significantly reduce impressions and clicks and may increase cost per acquisition. Start with several nearby codes and widen if delivery is thin.

**Q: Do I need an email list to use TikTok for local marketing?**
A: No. Custom Audiences from a customer file need at least 1,000 entries, which most small local businesses will not have, but standard location and demographic targeting works without one.

**Q: Is the Promote button enough?**
A: For a first boost it can work, but TikTok describes linking it to Ads Manager as the way to get deeper audience targeting, ad group budgets and Pixel-based conversion objectives. For a local test where postcode choice matters, use Ads Manager.
