Small businesses can build recurring revenue with memberships, subscriptions, prepaid bundles or service plans. The shape matters less than two things: customers get something every month that they value, and you can bill them reliably. Stripe Billing, one common tool, charges 0.7% of billing volume on pay-as-you-go, on top of 2.9% + 30 cents per card charge.

This guide compares the main models, explains what to check before you charge on a schedule and gives a worked example. Figures come from the sources at the end. Conversion and churn rates vary widely and no reliable local-business benchmark was found, so none are claimed.

## Which Recurring Revenue Models Fit a Local Business?

| Model | How it works | Fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Membership | Monthly fee for access or a set of visits | Gyms, studios, car washes |
| Subscription | Regular delivery of a product | Coffee beans, food, pet supplies |
| Prepaid bundle | Pay for several visits upfront | Salons, groomers, massage |
| Service plan | Scheduled recurring work | Cleaning, lawn care, maintenance |

Bundles are not strictly recurring, but they pull revenue forward and bring customers back. For pet groomers, a regular grooming schedule can be sold as a plan; see our [pet groomer email guide](/blog/pet-groomer-email-marketing) for reminders.

## What Does Billing Software Cost?

Stripe's published pricing: Billing is 0.7% of billing volume on pay-as-you-go, with no recurring fee. Card charges are 2.9% + 30 cents each, and ACH direct debit is 0.8% capped at $5.00. Higher monthly plans exist for large volumes, starting at $620 a month for up to $100,000 of monthly billing volume.

Square Appointments offers a Free plan with online booking and reminders, plus Plus and Premium tiers. Its pricing page, as retrieved, listed features but not the monthly prices, so check Square for current amounts and whether memberships or packages are included for your account.

## What Rules Apply to Automatic Renewals?

In the US, the FTC's "click to cancel" rule was vacated by the Eighth Circuit on July 8, 2025. According to a law-firm summary, that does not remove obligations: the Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act still applies to online subscriptions, requiring clear disclosure of renewal terms before billing details are collected, express consent and a simple way to cancel. Many states, including California and New York, have their own renewal laws.

This is general information, not legal advice. Outside the US, rules differ. Put the price, billing date and how to cancel in plain words at sign-up.

## The Math: What Is a Membership Worth?

All inputs are **assumptions**.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly membership price | $40 |
| Members | 50 |
| Monthly churn (members leaving) | 8% |
| Card fees | 2.9% + $0.30 per charge, plus 0.7% billing fee |

Monthly revenue: 50 x $40 = $2,000. Card fees per charge: $1.16 + $0.30 = $1.46, and the 0.7% billing fee is $0.28, so about $1.74 per member, or $87 across 50 members. Net of those fees: $1,913. At 8% churn you lose 4 members a month, so you must add 4 just to stay at 50. That is why retention work matters; see our [customer lifetime value guide](/blog/customer-lifetime-value-strategy) and [AI retention strategies](/blog/ai-driven-customer-retention-strategies).

## How Do You Sell a Membership?

Start with your best existing customers. Offer a clear benefit, such as a visit a month or priority booking, and make the first month easy to try. Announce it by email and at the counter. Our [membership site marketing](/blog/membership-site-marketing) guide covers promotion in more detail.

Review sign-ups and cancellations monthly. If many leave after month one, the offer is not delivering enough.

## FAQ

### What is the easiest recurring revenue model to start?

A prepaid bundle or a simple monthly membership, because you can run it with existing booking or payment tools.

### How much does it cost to take recurring payments?

On Stripe, 0.7% billing volume plus 2.9% + 30 cents per card charge on pay-as-you-go. Other processors differ.

### Do I need to offer an easy way to cancel?

In most places, yes. ROSCA and many state laws require clear terms and simple cancellation for online subscriptions. Confirm locally.

### What churn rate is normal?

No reliable benchmark for local businesses was found. Track your own from the first month.
