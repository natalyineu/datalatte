A small business in Egypt reaches most customers through Facebook, YouTube and TikTok. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 98.2 million internet users (82.7% of the population) and a Facebook ad reach of 51.6 million. The same report shows Egypt is also a market where marketing rules are changing, because the personal data law's executive regulations were issued in November 2025.

This guide uses only figures from pages we opened, and each one is listed in Sources. No Egyptian ad-price benchmark (cost per click or per thousand views) could be verified, so none is quoted here. Check prices inside your own Google Ads or Meta account.

## How Many Egyptians Are Online, and Where?

DataReportal puts Egypt's population at 119 million in late 2025, with a median age of 24.5. Only 43.5% of people live in urban areas, so a large share of customers sit outside Cairo and Alexandria. Mobile connections total 121 million, which is 102% of the population, so many people hold more than one SIM.

Internet use grew by 2.3 million people (2.4%) in the year to October 2025. About 20.6 million Egyptians, or 17.3%, were still offline. A business that relies only on online ads misses that group, so shop signage, word of mouth and local groups still matter.

## Which Social Platforms Should an Egyptian Shop Use First?

Ad reach is what each platform's own ad tool says it can show ads to. It is not the same as active users. With that caveat, DataReportal lists these figures for Egypt:

| Platform | Ad reach | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 51.6 million | 43.4% |
| YouTube | 49.3 million | 41.5% |
| TikTok (18+) | 48.8 million | not stated |
| Messenger | 37.0 million | 31.1% |
| Instagram | 21.7 million | 18.2% |
| Snapchat | 20.6 million | 17.3% |

Facebook is the widest net and grew 10.6% in a year. TikTok grew fastest of the big three at 25.2%. Instagram is less than half the size of Facebook, so it suits a business whose customers are young and urban. DataReportal's extract we read gave no WhatsApp figure, so we do not quote one.

## What Does the Economy Mean for Your Pricing and Budget?

World Bank data for 2025 shows GDP per person of about US$3,086, GDP growth of 4.4% and consumer price inflation of 14.1%. Inflation at that level means your costs and your customers' budgets move quickly. Review prices and ad budgets on a monthly schedule, not once a year.

Set ad budgets in the currency your ad account bills in, and watch the exchange rate when you compare months. No verified source for the pound-to-dollar rate used by ad platforms was available, so check it in your account.

## How Do Egyptians Pay, and Why Does It Matter for Ads?

The Central Bank of Egypt's Financial Stability report, as reported by Arab Finance (secondary source), says the InstaPay instant payment network handled 263 million transactions worth more than EGP 1.2 trillion in the first quarter of 2025.

For a local business, that means a customer can pay by instant transfer, so a bank account or wallet link on your Facebook page or Google Business Profile shortens the path from message to sale.

Put the payment options in your posts and profile, so the customer does not need to ask. Our guide to the [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers the profile fields to fill in.

## What Does Egypt's Data Law Say About Marketing Messages?

Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law is No. 151 of 2020. According to a CMS law firm briefing (secondary source), its executive regulations were issued on 1 November 2025 under Ministerial Decision 816 of 2025 and were published on 25 December 2025. A one-year grace period runs from issuance, so enforcement is expected from 1 November 2026.

For marketing, the briefing says electronic direct marketing needs prior consent, a clear sender identity and a free, simple opt-out.

It also says a specific licence from the Personal Data Protection Centre is required, and that privacy notices must be in Arabic. A soft opt-in for existing customers is described for the grace period. We could not open the law text or the regulator's guidance, so treat these points as **unverified** and ask an Egyptian lawyer before running bulk SMS or WhatsApp campaigns.

## How Should a Small Budget Be Split? A Worked Example

All inputs below are **assumptions** for planning, not benchmarks. No ad-price data is used.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |

Facebook gets the largest share because it has the widest reach in the DataReportal table. TikTok gets a real test because its reach is almost as large and still growing. After four weeks, compare the cost per message or call in each channel, then move money toward the cheapest one. The split is a starting point, and your account data replaces it.

## FAQ

### Is Facebook still the best platform for a small business in Egypt?

By ad reach, yes. DataReportal lists 51.6 million for Facebook against 49.3 million for YouTube and 21.7 million for Instagram. Reach is not sales, so test two channels before committing.

### Do I need to worry about the Egyptian data protection law?

Likely yes if you collect customer phone numbers or emails for marketing. Enforcement is expected from 1 November 2026 per a law-firm briefing. Confirm the details with a local lawyer.

### Are there verified ad prices for Egypt?

None could be verified. Use your own Google Ads and Meta account data.

### Can I reach people outside the big cities?

Partly. 56.5% of Egyptians live in rural areas, and 17.3% are offline. Combine ads with local signage and referrals.
