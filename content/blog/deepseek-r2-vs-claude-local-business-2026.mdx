DeepSeek R2 is not an official product: as of 30 October 2026 DeepSeek's own documentation lists no model by that name. Its current API models are deepseek-flash and deepseek-v4-pro. For a local business, the practical comparison is DeepSeek V4 against Claude Sonnet 5.5 or Haiku 5.5. DeepSeek V4 Flash costs less than Sonnet 5.5, but Haiku 5.5 is cheaper than both on list price.

This page compares published prices and data policies only. It does not rank answer quality, because no published test covers local business tasks. Run your own prompts before you switch.

## Does DeepSeek R2 Exist?

Not officially. DeepSeek's release notes list R1-0528 (May 2025), then V3.1, V3.2, V4 Preview (April 2026), V4-Pro general availability (August 2026) and V4.1-Flash (September and October 2026). R2 does not appear.

Many web pages describe R2 benchmarks and release dates that DeepSeek has not confirmed. If a tool or article claims to offer "R2", check DeepSeek's official channels first.

## What Do the Privacy Policies Say?

DeepSeek's privacy policy says it directly collects, processes and stores personal data in the People's Republic of China. Its "User Input" category includes prompts, uploaded files and chat history, and the policy says data is used to train and improve its models, with a right to opt out. Apps built by third-party developers on its platform are not covered by that policy.

Anthropic's help article says API inputs and outputs are deleted from its backend within 30 days by default, with exceptions such as legal requirements. That page does not address training. Do not paste customer names, phone numbers or health details into either service without checking the current terms and your legal duties.

## Which Should a Local Business Use?

Choose by task and risk, not by headline price.

- Short public replies, such as answering a review: a cheap model is enough, and the content is already public.
- Anything with customer data: read the data policy first, and consider an account type that fits your region.
- Long documents: both DeepSeek V4 models list a 1M-token context; Anthropic's pricing page lists a higher rate for Haiku prompts above 100K tokens.

## FAQ

### Is DeepSeek R2 available?
Not according to DeepSeek's documentation. The current API models are deepseek-flash and deepseek-v4-pro.

### Is DeepSeek cheaper than Claude?
DeepSeek Flash is cheaper per token than Claude Sonnet 5.5, but Claude Haiku 5.5 lists lower than DeepSeek Flash for prompts up to 100K tokens.

### Is DeepSeek safe for customer data?
Its policy says data is stored in China and used to improve models, with an opt-out right. Avoid customer personal data unless you have checked the terms.
