For a local business, Wix and Squarespace cost almost the same at the mid tier: Wix Core is $29.77 a month on yearly billing and Squarespace Core is $29. Squarespace is the tidier brochure site with fewer moving parts. Wix gives you more built-in booking and a looser editor. Neither choice will make or break your Google rankings.

This comparison uses the two companies' own pricing and help pages, read in October 2026. Prices are shown in dollars as listed and change by country. Where a page did not state something, this article says so instead of guessing.

## How Much Do Wix and Squarespace Cost for a Small Business?

Both sell four paid tiers that mostly track what you want to sell online.

| Tier | Wix (yearly billing, per month) | Squarespace (annual, per month) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry | Light $17.77 | Basic $19 |
| Mid | Core $29.77 | Core $29 |
| Upper | Business $39.77 | Plus $49 |
| Top | Business Elite $159.77 | Advanced $99 |

Sources: [Wix plans](https://www.wix.com/plans) and [Squarespace pricing](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing). Squarespace's monthly billing is higher: Basic $25, Core $39.

Both include a free custom domain for the first year on annual plans. Wix says its domain voucher must be redeemed within 60 days and covers only a new domain, not renewals. Wix also has a free plan with no card needed, but the plans page does not say whether it includes a custom domain.

## Which Plan Does a Coffee Shop, Salon or Studio Actually Need?

Match the plan to what the site must do, not to the feature list.

- **Brochure site (hours, menu, map, contact form):** Wix Light or Squarespace Basic. Check that Light covers what you need, because the Wix page did not list its commerce or payment features.
- **Selling gift cards or merch:** Squarespace Core removes the 2% store fee that Basic charges. Wix Core lists basic eCommerce and payment acceptance, with fee details not stated on the plans page.
- **Custom tracking code:** Squarespace Basic does not include code injection. Core, Plus and Advanced do.
- **Many staff or collaborators:** Wix lists 2 collaborators on Light, 5 on Core and 10 on Business. Squarespace's page did not give a collaborator count.

Worked example, labelled as an assumption: a one-location coffee shop that sells about $400 a month of gift cards online. Squarespace Basic's 2% fee is about $8 a month, so the $10 a month upgrade to Core does not pay for itself on fees alone. It pays only if you also need code injection.

## Which Is Better for Online Booking?

Wix is the simpler path if you want booking inside the website builder. Its [Bookings page](https://www.wix.com/bookings) lists online booking, reminders by email, SMS or WhatsApp, deposits, classes, memberships and a built-in CRM. It does not say which Wix plan unlocks each feature, so check at checkout.

Squarespace sells scheduling as a separate product, Acuity. Its [scheduling page](https://www.squarespace.com/scheduling) shows three tiers: one bookable calendar, up to six and up to 36. Text reminders start at the second tier. The page showed no dollar prices, so budget for it as an extra.

Decision rule: a solo groomer or stylist can start with one calendar on either. A four-chair salon needs a tool with at least four calendars. If you already pay for Fresha, Vagaro or Square, embed that booking link and skip both add-ons.

## Which Is Better for Local SEO?

Neither platform ranks a local business by itself. Your Google Business Profile, reviews and consistent name, address and phone number matter more. Use our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) and make sure your site details match it.

What each platform does for you:

- **Wix:** its [SEO Setup Checklist](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-creating-your-seo-checklist) asks for your business name, whether you serve a physical address, and three keywords. It does not analyze your blog or Wix Stores pages. Connecting Google Search Console needs a Premium plan and a connected domain.
- **Squarespace:** its [SEO checklist](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002090267-SEO-checklist) recommends a site title under 60 characters and a description of 50 to 300 characters. It builds a sitemap automatically and adds structured data you cannot edit, such as Organization and Blog post.

The work that moves rankings is the same on both: unique page titles, service pages for each service and city, and compressed photos. Our [local SEO service](/services/local-seo) covers that setup.

## What Does It Cost to Leave Either Platform?

Leaving Squarespace is partial. Its [export help](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/206566687-Exporting-your-site) says the file includes layout and gallery pages, one blog with up to 1,000 comments per post, and text and image blocks. It excludes store pages, product blocks, video blocks, drafts, style settings and custom CSS. The Wix export page was not retrieved for this article, so confirm what Wix exports before you commit.

Worked example, labelled as an assumption: you spend 20 hours rebuilding a site by hand at a freelance rate of $40 an hour. That is $800, more than the roughly $9 a year gap between Wix Core and Squarespace Core on annual billing. Treat the exit cost as the real price of your choice and keep original photos and text in a folder you own.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Wix or Squarespace cheaper for a small business?**
A: At the entry tier Wix Light ($17.77) is slightly below Squarespace Basic ($19). At the mid tier they are within $1 a month: Squarespace Core $29 versus Wix Core $29.77.

**Q: Which is better for SEO, Wix or Squarespace?**
A: Both cover the technical basics. Local rankings depend mostly on your Google Business Profile, reviews and unique page content, not the builder.

**Q: Can I take bookings on Wix or Squarespace?**
A: Yes. Wix has a Bookings product with reminders, deposits and classes. Squarespace uses Acuity Scheduling, sold separately, or any booking tool you embed.

**Q: Can I move my site from one to the other?**
A: Only in part. Squarespace exports pages, one blog and some blocks, but not store pages, styles or custom CSS, so expect to rebuild the design.

**Q: Should I use the free plan?**
A: Wix has a free plan, but the plans page did not say whether it includes a custom domain. A branded domain is worth paying for, and both paid plans include one for the first year.
