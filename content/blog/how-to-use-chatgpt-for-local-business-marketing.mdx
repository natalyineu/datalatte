A local business can use ChatGPT to draft social captions, review replies, Google Business Profile posts, email subject lines and FAQ answers, then edit each draft before it goes out. Treat it as a fast first draft, not a publisher. Google's guidance on AI content says to "manually factcheck and review all AI-generated content for accuracy and trustworthiness before publishing", and the FTC's 2024 rule bans AI-generated fake reviews.

This guide is for owners of cafes, salons, groomers and studios. It names no ChatGPT plan prices, because OpenAI's pricing pages returned a 403 error when checked on October 31, 2026. Check the plan you use on OpenAI's site.

## What Should a Local Business Use ChatGPT For?

Start with short, repeatable writing where you already know the facts and only need wording.

1. Social captions from a list of this week's offers.
2. Replies to reviews, which you edit and post yourself.
3. Google Business Profile post drafts from a short note.
4. Email subject lines and first drafts of a customer newsletter.
5. FAQ answers for your website, based on text you paste in.

Avoid asking it for facts about your market, prices or competitors. It predicts likely wording and does not look things up reliably, which is why Google says output can be wrong.

## How Do You Write a Prompt That Works?

Give it a role, your facts, the format and the limits. Paste in real details, and tell it what not to do. A reusable template:

```text
You are the marketing writer for [business type] in [city].
Facts you may use: [opening hours, services, current offer, price if public].
Task: write [3 Instagram captions of under 40 words] about [topic].
Tone: warm and plain. No emojis, no exclamation marks.
Do not invent prices, awards, customer quotes or opening hours.
```

The last line matters. Models fill gaps with plausible details, and a made-up price or award in a public post is your problem. For more ready prompts, see our [ChatGPT prompts for local business marketing](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-local-business-marketing).

## What Rules Apply to AI-Written Marketing?

Three rules are published by the sources below.

| Rule | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Fact-check and review all AI content before publishing, including titles and meta descriptions | Google Search guidance |
| Generating many pages without adding value may violate the scaled content abuse spam policy | Google Search guidance |
| Fake reviews and testimonials, including AI-generated ones, are banned; civil penalties apply to knowing violators | FTC final rule |

Google also suggests considering a note on how content was created when it is generated automatically. The FTC page gives no dollar amount for penalties in the press release. Never ask ChatGPT to write a review "from a customer", and do not post a template review under a real name.

## Can ChatGPT Write Google Business Profile Posts?

Yes, as drafts. Google's help for Business Profile posts says it may remove posts with unverified contact information and suggests a "Call now" button with your verified phone number instead. Businesses are responsible for making sure posted content follows applicable laws.

So tell the model to leave out phone numbers and links, then add the button yourself. Check offers and dates against what your front desk can honour. Our [AI tools for pet groomer marketing](/blog/ai-tools-for-pet-groomer-marketing) piece shows a niche example.

## How Do You Use It for Customer Service?

Use it to prepare answers, not to speak for you live. Paste your own policies (cancellation window, vaccine requirements, deposit rules) and ask for a friendly reply to a typical question. Save the good ones as templates.

Do not paste customer names, phone numbers or pet health records into a chat tool unless you have read the provider's data terms. ## FAQ

### Is ChatGPT free to use for marketing?

OpenAI offers a free tier at times, but this guide could not load OpenAI's pricing page (403), so it states no current prices or limits. Check the pricing page directly.

### Can I post ChatGPT text without editing it?

You can, but Google advises manual fact-checking before publishing, and wrong details such as prices or hours harm trust. Always read and edit.

### Is it legal to have AI write reviews?

The FTC final rule expressly bans fake reviews and testimonials, including AI-generated ones, and allows civil penalties against knowing violators. Reply to real reviews, and never invent them.

### Will Google penalise AI content?

Google's guidance says producing many pages without adding value may violate its scaled content abuse policy. Helpful, reviewed content that adds value is a different case.

### Which task should I try first?

Review replies. They are short, based on facts you already have, and quick to check.
