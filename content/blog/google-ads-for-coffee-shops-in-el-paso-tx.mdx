## What Does Public Data Say About the El Paso Market?

Census Reporter's profile of El Paso, built on the American Community Survey 2024 one-year estimates, lists 681,724 residents, a median age of 35.5, median household income of $59,932 and a mean commute of 23.5 minutes. The language-at-home figure was not available on that page, so no Spanish-speaking share is quoted here.

A shorter commute than big coastal cities means fewer long train rides and more car trips. That suggests drive-through, parking and "open early" details matter in ads, but this is a recommendation rather than a finding from the data.

## How Many Coffee Competitors Are There?

The BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages reports, for El Paso County in 2024, 171 private establishments in NAICS 722515, Snack and Nonalcoholic Beverage Bars, with 2,250 employees and an average weekly wage of $404. The average across all private industries in the county was $890 a week.

This category includes coffee shops and other drink bars, so it is a close cousin of your business rather than an exact count. It is county-wide, not city-only. For your real competition, search "coffee" on Google Maps and count shops within a ten-minute drive of your address.

## How Do You Set Up the Campaign?

Google's help says that for most campaign types you can target a radius around a location, and that location targeting is best effort. Draw your radius from where your regulars live or work: ask for ZIP codes at the counter for a week.

Start on phrase and exact match, which Google describes as giving more control than broad match. Suggested starting groups:

| Ad group | Example keywords | Match |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Near me | "coffee shop near me", [coffee near me], "cafe open now" | Phrase and exact |
| Local | "coffee shop el paso", "specialty coffee el paso" | Phrase |
| Need | "cold brew near me", "breakfast burrito and coffee" | Phrase |
| Brand | [your shop name] | Exact |

Add negatives from day one: `jobs`, `hiring`, `beans`, `wholesale`, `recipe`, `machine`. Google says negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals separately. Our [coffee shop keyword list](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-coffee-shops-in-2026) has more.

## What Does a Click Cost?

LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks list Restaurants and Food at $2.05 per click, a 6.83% click-through rate, an 8.05% conversion rate and $30.57 per lead. There is no coffee-only row and no El Paso row. Your Google Ads Keyword Planner will show current bid estimates for your own keywords, and your first two weeks of data will be more accurate than any benchmark.

## The Math: What Is a Click Worth?

Every input except the LocaliQ click price is an **assumption**. Replace them with your own figures.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Average ticket | $6.50 |
| Margin after ingredients and cups | 65% |
| Visits per regular per year | 75 |
| Gross profit per regular per year | 75 x $6.50 x 0.65 = about $317 |
| Most you would pay for one new regular | $150 (about six months of profit) |
| Click to first visit | 6% |
| First visit to regular | 20% |
| Maximum click price | $150 x 0.06 x 0.20 = $1.80 |

At the LocaliQ restaurant average of $2.05 a click, the numbers do not clear that $1.80 ceiling. That does not mean ads fail in El Paso. It means you need a higher visit rate, a sharper radius or a higher-margin offer before spending.

A $500 budget at $2.05 buys about 244 clicks, roughly 15 first visits and about 3 regulars. That is $171 per regular, above the $150 target. Improving the click-to-visit rate from 6% to 9% would lift that to about 4.4 regulars for $500, or $114 each.

## What Should You Do Before You Spend?

1. Count competitors within ten minutes of your shop on Google Maps.
2. Fill in your Business Profile completely. Our [coffee shop Business Profile guide](/blog/coffee-shop-google-business-profile-optimization) lists what to check.
3. Run one campaign with the groups above, track calls and direction taps, and use a counter offer you can count.
4. Judge it by cost per regular, not clicks. See [how much a coffee shop should spend](/blog/how-much-should-a-coffee-shop-spend-on-google-ads) for budget rules.

## FAQ

### How much does Google Ads cost for an El Paso coffee shop?

No El Paso coffee figure is published. LocaliQ's 2026 national Restaurants and Food average is $2.05 per click. At that price $500 buys about 244 clicks.

### Should I run Spanish-language ads?

It is worth testing, but no source here shows how many El Paso searchers use Spanish. Run a Spanish ad variant against an English one and compare results in your account.

### Should I target all of El Paso?

Only if people travel across town to you. Most coffee trips are local, so a radius around your shop usually wastes less, though that is a rule of thumb.

### How long before I know if it works?

Set a minimum number of clicks and counted redemptions before judging, such as 200 clicks. No official time period applies.
