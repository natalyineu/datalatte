A Queensland small business competes with a lot of other small firms: as at 30 June 2025 the Queensland Government Statistician's Office counted 524,024 businesses in the state, 97.1% of them small (under 20 employees). The practical response is to make your Google Business Profile complete, run a small, tightly targeted Google Ads campaign, and follow the Spam Act when you email or text. No source publishes a Queensland click price, so the only public reference here is the US-wide WordStream by LocaliQ average of US$5.42 per click.

This guide is for cafes, salons, studios and trades in Brisbane, the Gold and Sunshine Coasts and regional towns.

## How Crowded Is the Queensland Small Business Market?

QGSO reports 508,862 small, 14,231 medium and 931 large businesses at 30 June 2025. Small here means fewer than 20 employees, and the fact sheet does not split out sole traders. The takeaway is simple: there are many competitors for local search, so a complete profile and clear offer matter more than a clever slogan.

The Queensland Government's statement for the year to June 2026 puts the visitor economy at $45.6 billion, with 26 million domestic overnight visitors and day-trip spending up 37.5% to $10.3 billion. Those are state totals, not your share.

## How Do You Target Brisbane, the Coast or a Regional Town?

Google's location help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km. It describes "Presence or interest" as reaching people in or interested in your location and "Presence" as limited to people physically there.

A Gold Coast business serving tourists may test "Presence or interest" for pre-trip searches. A Brisbane trades business that works only in set suburbs should use "Presence". Our [Google Ads for hair salons guide](/blog/google-ads-for-hair-salons-complete-guide) shows a local radius setup.

## How Do You Win the Map Listing in Queensland?

Google says local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to pay for a better ranking. Complete every field, add photos and reply to reviews. Use special hours for holidays and temporary closures.

## What Does the Spam Act Require for Email and Text?

LegalVision's summary of the Spam Act 2003 (secondary source: law firm) says a commercial message is likely spam if it lacks the receiver's express or implied consent, does not identify the sender, or has no unsubscribe facility. The Act covers mobile phone messaging as well as email. Consent must be provable, so keep records.

That article gives no unsubscribe time limit or penalty amount. Check the Australian Communications and Media Authority (ACMA) before you send. For channel choice, see [SMS vs email](/blog/sms-vs-email-marketing-local-business-which-is-better).

## FAQ

### How much should a Queensland business spend on Google Ads?

No Queensland figure is published. At a $5.42 click, $500 a month buys about 92 clicks. Start with an amount you can afford to lose.

### Does the Spam Act apply to text messages?

LegalVision says the Act covers mobile phone messaging. Get consent, identify yourself and include an unsubscribe option.

### Is Google Business Profile worth it?

Yes. It is free, and Google says reviews, links and complete information feed prominence.

### Do tourism numbers mean more sales for me?

Not automatically. State totals do not show your share, so compare your own seasonal sales.
