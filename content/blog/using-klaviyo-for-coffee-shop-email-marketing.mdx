## Is Klaviyo a Good Fit for a Coffee Shop?

Klaviyo suits shops that can attach an email or phone number to purchases: online bean sales, pre-orders, a loyalty sign-up or a Square checkout. It is weaker if most customers pay cash and never share contact details, because there is nothing to email.

Decide with three questions:

1. Do you collect emails or phone numbers at the till, online or on a sign-up card?
2. Do you want flows triggered by behaviour, such as a first order or 30 days without a visit?
3. Can you spend two or three hours a month on it?

If you answer no to the first, fix list building before choosing software. See our [coffee shop email list building guide](/blog/coffee-shop-email-list-building).

## What Does the Klaviyo Free Plan Include?

According to [Klaviyo's pricing page](https://www.klaviyo.com/pricing), the free plan includes:

- Up to 250 active profiles
- 500 email sends per month
- $5 of mobile messages per month (varies by region and carrier)
- Web sign-up forms, a drag-and-drop editor and basic segmentation
- Built-in reporting
- Email support for the first 60 days

Paid prices depend on your profile count and are shown only in Klaviyo's plan calculator, so check it with your own number. Exceeding the free limits may restrict or pause sending.

Do the sends math before you rely on the free plan. 250 profiles emailed once a week is about 1,000 sends a month, which is twice the 500-send cap.

## How Do You Connect Square to Klaviyo?

Klaviyo's [Square guide](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/11117215837211) lists the steps:

1. In Klaviyo, open Integrations, choose Explore apps, search for Square and click Install.
2. Enter your store URL and click Connect to Square.
3. Log in to Square, review the permissions and click Allow.
4. Keep the onsite JavaScript option checked if you want sign-up forms.
5. Click Complete setup.

Know the limits. In-store orders only create a profile if the customer shared an email or phone number, and POS events sync every 30 minutes. Only one Square account can connect to each Klaviyo account.

## What Three Emails Should Your Welcome Flow Send?

Klaviyo's [welcome series guide](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002775172) recommends three emails over about a week. Here is a coffee shop version:

| Email | When | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Immediately | Who you are, opening hours, one reason to come in |
| 2 | 3 days later | Social links and a menu favourite |
| 3 | 4 days after email 2 | Your best sellers, beans or a first-visit offer |

Flows start when someone is added to the list you choose. Klaviyo lists default to double opt-in, so people must confirm before the series starts. A flow left in draft will not send, so switch it to Live.

## What Mistakes Does Klaviyo Warn About?

- **Importing an old list into a live flow.** Klaviyo says imported contacts may be scheduled for the first message even if they subscribed months ago. Set the flow to Manual before importing.
- **Expecting a second run.** List-triggered flows default to no re-entry, so each profile enters once.
- **Skipping compliance.** The FTC's [CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) requires a physical postal address and an opt-out honoured within 10 business days, with penalties up to $53,088 per email.

## Will Klaviyo Pay for Itself? A Worked Example

All numbers here are **assumptions**, except the free plan limits.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Profiles | 240 (inside the free plan) |
| Emails per profile per month | 2 |
| Monthly sends | 480 (inside the 500 cap) |
| Extra visits from the emails | 6 a month |
| Average order | $7 |
| Extra revenue | $42 a month |

At these numbers you earn about $42 a month in revenue at no software cost. After the cost of drinks, that is a small profit. The case for paying grows only if your list grows past 250 or you add SMS and win-back flows that bring back lapsed regulars. Measure for 60 days before paying for anything.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Klaviyo free for coffee shops?**
A: There is a free plan for up to 250 active profiles and 500 email sends a month. Exceeding the limits may pause sending.

**Q: Does Klaviyo work with Square?**
A: Yes. Install the Square app from Klaviyo's integrations. In-store orders sync every 30 minutes when the customer shared an email or phone number.

**Q: How many emails should a welcome series have?**
A: Klaviyo recommends three over about one week.

**Q: Do I need double opt-in?**
A: Klaviyo lists default to double opt-in and recommend keeping it on for your main list.

**Q: Is Klaviyo better than Mailchimp for a small café?**
A: Not always. Klaviyo's free plan lists automations, while Mailchimp's does not. Compare paid prices at your own profile count.
