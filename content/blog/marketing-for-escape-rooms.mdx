To fill empty escape room slots, treat weekdays and Sundays as separate products: find the groups who can come then (corporate teams, schools, birthday and family groups, tourists), offer them a clear private-booking page, and keep your reviews fresh. The Room Escape Artist 2025 report says Saturday is the busiest day and Tuesday the slowest, and that games with higher community scores get booked more often.

This guide is for single-site and small escape room operators in the US. Sources are at the end. Examples built on guesses are marked as assumptions.

## What Does the Escape Room Market Look Like Now?

The Room Escape Artist December 2025 report counts just over 2,000 US facilities, unchanged from 2023 and 2024, with about 7,800 rooms (3.8 per facility). It estimates $300 million in annual revenue, based on about 40,000 bookings a week at an average of $150. Fewer than 200 new facilities opened in 2025.

The report says owners report that sales are down, and cites tariffs, inflation and weaker international tourism. The report calls the coming years likely to be "a rough few years," so efficient marketing matters. Single-facility companies are 57% of facilities, which means you compete with both chains and other independents.

## Which Slots Are Hardest to Fill?

The same report says peak demand runs from Friday night through Sunday afternoon, Saturday is busiest and Tuesday slowest. It suggests Monday opening as an opportunity, especially in tourist areas. Your own booking system will show the empty hours that matter.

Pick the two slowest blocks and give each a target group: after-school or evening groups, corporate team events at lunchtime or after work, or weekday tourists. Build one page for each group with the time windows, group size, price and a booking button.

## Do Reviews Change Bookings?

The report cites data from Morty that games with higher community scores are booked more often. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults found 68% of consumers will only use a business rated 4 stars or higher, 47% will not use one with fewer than 20 reviews, and 74% only consider reviews from the last three months.

Ask each group at the end of the game with a Google link or QR code. Google bans offering discounts or free goods in exchange for reviews. Reply to every review, as 89% of consumers expect that. See [Business Profile reviews](/blog/google-business-profile-reviews) and [how to deal with fake Google reviews](/blog/how-to-deal-with-fake-google-reviews).

## How Should You Set Up the Booking Flow?

The report lists the booking software used by escape rooms: Bookeo 36%, Resova 24%, Xola 9%, FareHarbor 7%, Off the Couch 4% and Peek 3%. It also says private booking dominates, with 78% always private. Whatever system you use, make the booking page open in two taps from your Business Profile.

Google's profile guidelines say your name must be your real-world name, descriptions cannot contain links, and hours should be your regular customer-facing hours. Put the booking link in the booking fields, not the name. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists them.

## Do Paid Ads Pay Off for Escape Rooms?

LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks for Arts and Entertainment show $1.63 per click, 12.75% click-through rate, 5.91% conversion rate and $26.84 per lead. These are low costs compared with home services. The closest category is broad, so your cost may differ.

## FAQ

### How do escape rooms get customers?

Through search and maps, reviews, groups booking private games, events and repeat visits. The Room Escape Artist report does not give a channel breakdown, so track how each booking found you.

### What is the slowest day for escape rooms?

The Room Escape Artist 2025 report says Tuesday is the slowest and Saturday the busiest.

### How much should I spend on ads?

There is no verified percentage. Set a cap that is below the revenue from the off-peak bookings you expect, then adjust after a month of data.

### Are corporate groups worth targeting?

They can fill weekday slots, but no verified conversion data exists for escape rooms. Test with one landing page and measure bookings.
