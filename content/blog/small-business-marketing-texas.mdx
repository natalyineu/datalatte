Texas small businesses should start with the free channels people use to find a local business: a complete Google Business Profile and local search. Then add Google Ads limited to the area you serve. No official Texas-wide ad price exists, so use industry benchmarks and your own account data. LocaliQ's 2026 US search benchmarks put the all-industry average at $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead.

Texas is also a fast-moving market. The Census Bureau counted 31,709,821 residents on July 1, 2025, up 391,243 in a year, the largest gain of any state.

## How big is the Texas market for local businesses?

Large and still growing. The Census Bureau's Vintage 2025 estimates show Texas second in population, with growth of 1.2% in the year to July 2025 ([Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026/population-growth-slows.html)).

The metro data names two Texas areas at the top. Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands added 126,720 people and Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington added 123,557, the two biggest numeric gains of any US metro ([Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026/2025-popest-metro-micro-counties.html)).

Growth is not even. Plenty of customers are new to an area, but local figures differ by county, so check your own ZIP codes before assuming a boom.

## How do you keep Google Ads local in Texas?

Texas is large, so location settings control waste more than any other setting. Google Ads lets you target regions, cities or postal codes depending on the country, and radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722043)).

Google recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting for Search, but says "Presence" may suit advertisers who only want people physically in the area. A neighbourhood salon in Houston may prefer Presence so that it does not pay for clicks from people who are only interested in the city.

Run a separate campaign for each city you serve, so budget and results stay readable.

## Why does Google Business Profile matter first?

A free profile appears in map results at no cost per click. Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your ranking ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)).

Fill in every section, choose the most specific category and keep hours current. Ask customers for reviews with a link or QR code, and never offer a reward for one, which Google prohibits ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)). For the full setup, use our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## Which Texas events create marketing windows?

Large events cluster in spring. Use them as planning dates and confirm each year's schedule.

- **SXSW, Austin.** The 2026 festival ran March 12 to 18 as a seven-day event, and organizers expected attendance close to the 309,300 recorded in 2025 ([Spectrum News](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/tx/south-texas-el-paso/news/2026/04/07/report--sxsw-organizers-say-2026-attendance-held-steady)).
- **Houston Livestock Show and Rodeo.** The organizer lists 2,621,765 total attendance for all activities on the grounds in 2026. Its 2027 run is listed as March 2 to 21 ([RodeoHouston](https://www.rodeohouston.com/about-us/attendance/)).

Whether an event helps your business depends on where it sits relative to your customers. No source here measures the effect on small service businesses, so test with a short, geo-limited campaign near the venue and compare to a normal week.

## The math: what does a $500 test return?

Inputs are **assumptions**, using the Beauty and Personal Care benchmarks above for a salon.

- Budget: $500 a month. CPC: $4.62 (LocaliQ). Clicks: 500 / 4.62 = about 108.
- Conversion rate: 10.35% (LocaliQ), so about 11 leads. Cost per lead: about $45.
- Assumption: half of leads book (5 or 6 clients) at a $90 average first visit, giving roughly $500 to $540 in first-visit revenue.

On first visits alone the test roughly breaks even. It pays if clients rebook, which is why repeat visits matter more than click price. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to plan for that.

## How do you win new residents and keep customers?

The Census figures show lots of arrivals, and people who have just moved look for a hairdresser, gym or dentist. A landing page for newcomers with a clear first-visit offer gives them a reason to choose you. Once they book, an owned list keeps them: collect email addresses at the point of sale and send reminders.

## FAQ

### How much should a Texas small business spend on marketing?
There is no Texas standard. Start with free channels and a test budget you can afford to lose, then scale on cost per customer. Our [small business marketing budget guide](/blog/small-business-marketing-budget-guide-2026) covers the planning method.

### Are Google Ads cheaper in Texas?
Unknown. The benchmark source publishes US averages by industry, not by state. Check your own cost per click in your first month.

### Which Texas city is growing fastest?
By numeric growth the Census Bureau ranks Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands first and Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington second among metros for 2024 to 2025.

### Should I advertise during SXSW or the Rodeo?
Only if your customers are near the event. Run a short, location-limited test and compare it with a normal week before committing.

### What comes first, SEO or ads?
Set up your Google Business Profile first because it is free. Add ads when you know what a new customer is worth.
