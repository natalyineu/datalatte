## How Many People in Laos Are Online?

DataReportal, using UN, Kepios and GSMA Intelligence data, reports 7.90 million people in Laos in October 2025, 60.3% of them living in rural areas. Internet users number 5.03 million, or 63.6% of the population, and 2.87 million people are offline. The median age is 24.9.

There are 6.73 million cellular connections, 85.2% of the population. The report warns that one person may hold several connections and that some connections are voice and SMS only. It also says internet figures can lag actual adoption. Treat all of these as estimates.

## Which Platforms Reach Lao Customers?

Facebook dominates. DataReportal gives these ad reach figures for late 2025:

- Facebook: 4.60 million, 58.2% of the population
- Messenger: 3.05 million, 38.6% of the population
- Instagram: 599 thousand, 7.6% of the population
- X: 251 thousand, and LinkedIn: 190 thousand registered members

The page does not report YouTube or TikTok for Laos, so we cannot compare them. Facebook's adult audience is 46.8% female and 53.2% male. Ad reach is not monthly active users, and Meta revised its audience data, so do not compare these numbers with older reports.

For a shop, café or clinic, the practical result is simple: a Facebook Page with working Messenger replies reaches most online adults, and Instagram is a minor channel outside niche audiences.

## What Is the Economy Like for Local Businesses?

The World Bank's data page shows Laos with a 2025 GDP of US$18.3 billion and GDP per capita of US$2,324.8. Growth was 4.5% and consumer price inflation was 7.7%. Unemployment was 1.2% by the ILO's modelled estimate.

The World Bank's country overview adds context: high inflation and foreign-exchange shortages from 2022 to 2024 hurt macroeconomic conditions, and inflation was easing in 2025. For a business, this means prices are changing and customers watch spending. Show prices clearly in your ads and update them when costs move, rather than leaving old figures online.

## Can You Run Google Ads in Laos?

We could not find a Google page that lists countries where ads can run, so we do not state availability. We can confirm one billing fact. Google Ads Help lists "Lao People's Democratic Republic" among the countries and territories where prepay is not available. It says postpay, or monthly invoicing if you are eligible, are the only supported payment settings there.

In practice, you need a card or an eligible invoicing arrangement that Google accepts, and you are billed after spending, not before. Check what your account offers in the billing section before planning a budget. No Lao-specific click prices were found, so none are given.

## How Do Customers Pay in Laos?

The Bank of Thailand and the Bank of the Lao PDR launched a cross-border QR payment link on 3 April 2024. Lao users can scan Thai PromptPay QR codes, and from the end of June 2024 Thai users can scan LAO QR codes at Lao merchants through participating bank apps. The central banks cite about 2 million Thai and Lao tourist trips in 2023.

Six Lao banks take part, including BCEL, ACLEDA Bank Lao and Lao-Viet Bank. If you serve visitors from Thailand, check with your bank whether your shop can show a LAO QR code, and mention it in your Page and ads.

## What Are the Rules for Advertising in Laos?

We could not verify any current rules for online advertising in Laos. Our searches found only secondary summaries about signboards, which we did not open on an official page, so we do not rely on them.

**Unverified:** whether digital or social advertising needs a permit or content approval. Ask the Ministry of Information, Culture and Tourism or a local lawyer before running paid campaigns, especially for alcohol, health or financial products.
