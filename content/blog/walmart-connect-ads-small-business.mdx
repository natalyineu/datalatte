Walmart Connect is worth it for a small business only if you sell physical products that are listed on Walmart.com, either through Walmart Marketplace or as a supplier. If you run a local service business such as a salon, gym or groomer, it is almost certainly the wrong channel, because the ads promote products listed on Walmart, not local services.

## What Walmart Connect Actually Is

Walmart Connect is the advertising arm of Walmart's US business. Retail media, meaning ads sold inside retailers' own websites, apps and stores, has been one of the fastest-growing areas of digital advertising, and Amazon and Walmart are the two largest players in the US. Walmart Connect lets advertisers show up in several places:

**Walmart.com sponsored search:** Products appear in search results when shoppers search for relevant terms. This is Walmart's equivalent of Amazon Sponsored Products.

**Display advertising:** Banner ads on Walmart.com, the Walmart app, and across a wider publisher network.

**In-store digital advertising:** Ads on screens inside Walmart stores. Availability and minimums vary, and this is generally aimed at larger brands.

**Offsite advertising:** Display and video ads served across the web to Walmart shoppers, using Walmart's purchase data for targeting.

The main advantage over many platforms is first-party purchase data. Walmart sees what its shoppers actually buy, not just what they click, so targeting like "people who bought pet food recently" is possible in principle. Exact targeting options change, so confirm what is available in your account.

<Callout type="tip">
Walmart Connect's most distinctive feature is its in-store reach. For consumer packaged goods, putting a brand in front of shoppers in the aisle is something few digital platforms can do.
</Callout>

## Who Walmart Connect Is (and Isn't) For

Walmart Connect is built primarily for **brands that sell products through Walmart**, in store, on Walmart.com, or both. That excludes most pure service businesses.

**Good fit:**
- Consumer packaged goods brands sold on Walmart.com
- Food and beverage brands with Walmart distribution
- Pet product brands with mass-retail distribution
- Health and beauty products on Walmart shelves
- Coffee and specialty food brands seeking national retail exposure

**Poor fit:**
- Service businesses (salons, gyms, plumbers), since shoppers do not look for service providers on Walmart.com
- B2B companies
- Businesses without Walmart distribution or Walmart.com listings

If you sell physical products and have, or want, Walmart.com distribution, Walmart Connect deserves attention. If you are a local service business, put your budget into channels built for local intent.

## Walmart Connect vs Amazon Ads

| | Walmart Connect | Amazon Ads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Brands with Walmart distribution | Brands selling on Amazon |
| Competition | Generally lower, though growing | Generally higher |
| In-store reach | Yes, through Walmart stores | Limited |
| Self-serve tools | Improving, less mature | More polished and documented |
| Data | Online and in-store purchases | Mostly e-commerce purchases |

For most small product brands, a sensible sequence is to get comfortable with one retail media platform first, then expand to a second once you have the systems to manage it.

## What Walmart Connect May Cost

Walmart is less transparent than Google or Amazon about minimums and pricing, and costs vary by category and season. Treat the following as general expectations rather than quotes:

- **Sponsored search** is typically priced per click and is often reported to be cheaper than Amazon in comparable categories, but verify current costs in your own account.
- **Display and offsite** are typically priced per thousand impressions, with premium placements costing more.
- **In-store screens** usually come with larger minimum commitments that are out of reach for most small businesses.
- **Self-serve sponsored search** may have no strict minimum, but you still need enough spend to gather meaningful data, so plan a testing budget you can sustain for at least a couple of months.

Always check the current rates and requirements on Walmart's advertising site before committing.

## How to Get Started with Walmart Connect

**Step 1: Verify your Walmart seller or supplier status.** To run sponsored search ads, your products need to be listed on Walmart.com, whether through Walmart Marketplace or as a direct supplier.

**Step 2: Access the self-serve platform.** Create an account through Walmart's advertising site. The interface is simpler than Amazon's but offers fewer options.

**Step 3: Start with automatic sponsored search campaigns.** Let the platform find search terms that work, and run for several weeks before optimizing.

**Step 4: Review search term reports and build manual campaigns.** Take the terms that convert and target them with more controlled bidding.

**Step 5: Add display for category and competitive targeting.** Once search is producing data, layer in display ads to reach category browsers.

<Callout type="warning">
Don't launch ads until your Walmart.com listings are in good shape: clear images, complete descriptions, competitive pricing and some reviews. Walmart's search surfaces the best-converting listings, and poor listings waste ad spend.
</Callout>

## FAQ

**Q: What's the minimum budget I need to see real results on Walmart Connect?**
A: There is no universal number, but very small budgets generate too few clicks to learn anything. Plan a testing budget you can sustain for at least 60 days, and judge results on profit, not on a handful of early clicks. If you cannot fund a proper test, put the money into Google Ads or your Google Business Profile first.

**Q: Can I target specific cities or zip codes on Walmart Connect?**
A: Targeting options are mainly built around products and shopper behavior rather than precise local radius targeting, and they change over time. Check the current options in your account. If you need strict local targeting, platforms like Google Ads and Meta Ads are usually a better match.

**Q: Do I have to sell my product on Walmart.com to use Walmart Connect?**
A: For sponsored search, yes. Those ads promote items listed on Walmart.com. Some other formats and managed offerings may differ, so confirm with Walmart for your situation.

**Q: How do I know if Walmart Connect is driving profit, not just revenue?**
A: Look beyond the platform dashboard. Subtract Walmart fees, fulfillment, returns and cost of goods from the ad-attributed revenue, then compare against ad spend. If the result is flat or negative, optimize or stop.

**Q: Is Walmart Connect worth it for a low-priced product?**
A: Only if the margin can absorb advertising. If your gross margin per unit is thin after fees and shipping, even a modest advertising cost of sale can erase profit, so run the unit economics before you spend.

**Q: Can Walmart Connect drive foot traffic to my own store?**
A: Not in the way local ads do. Walmart's ads drive purchases of products sold through Walmart. If your goal is local foot traffic, use [local SEO](/services/local-seo) and local ad platforms instead.
