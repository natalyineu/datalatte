Walmart's own campaign setup guide recommends a minimum daily budget of $100 for Sponsored Products. That is roughly $3,000 a month, and it buys you nothing unless you sell a physical product that is already listed on Walmart.com. If you run a salon, gym, groomer or any local service, stop here: Walmart Connect advertises products on Walmart's shelves and site, not services in your town. If you make and sell products, keep reading, because the break-even math is tighter than most guides admit.

This is for small product businesses in the US (and, with caveats, Canada) deciding whether to put ad money into Walmart. Walmart Connect is a US programme; a separate Walmart Connect Canada site exists, but we did not verify its terms, so check them before planning around it. We cover what the platform sells, what you need before you start, what it costs, and a worked example.

## What Walmart Connect Sells

Walmart's [Walmart Connect site](https://www.walmartconnect.com) lists ad products for small businesses: Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Videos and Onsite Display. Enterprise advertisers also get Brand Shop and Shelf, Offsite media and Store ads. Walmart says it reaches "approximately 150 million customers" (a company claim, not an audit) and that you start in Ad Center. It does not state a minimum spend on that page.

For most small sellers, the product that matters is **Sponsored Products**: ads in Walmart.com search results and on product pages, bought by cost per click. Walmart says you only pay when a customer selects your ad, and that the most relevant product with the highest bid wins the placement.

## The Eligibility Test

Walmart's [Marketplace Learn guide to sponsored search](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Advertising/Walmart%20Connect/Advertise-with-Walmart-Connect-sponsored-search) states the requirements:

- **Sponsored Products:** the item must be in stock, winning the Buy Box, and published on Walmart.com.
- **Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Videos:** you must be a brand owner, based in the United States, China, Hong Kong, the United Kingdom or India, a rights owner registered with the United States Patent and Trademark Office, and onboarded to the Walmart Brand Portal.

Walmart's setup page says you reach Ad Center with Seller Center or Supplier Center credentials, or through a preferred ad partner.

So the test is simple. Do you have a product, on Walmart.com, in stock, that wins the Buy Box? If not, Walmart Connect is not your channel yet.

**Plausible fits:** a pet treat brand, a candle maker, a specialty coffee roaster or a skincare line already selling through Walmart Marketplace. **Not fits:** services, B2B, and any product you are not ready to ship at Walmart's service standards. If your customers live within ten miles of you, a local channel such as [Google Ads](/services/google-ads) will almost always beat this.

## Setup, the Way Walmart Recommends It

From Walmart's [campaign setup page](https://www.walmartconnect.com/insights/easy-campaign-set-up):

1. Choose Sponsored Products as the campaign type.
2. Choose Automatic targeting, which Walmart calls the simplest way to start; the system picks keywords and tests search terms.
3. Choose Dynamic Bidding, which adjusts bids in real time.
4. Set a minimum daily budget of $100, which Walmart recommends to stay visible during peak shopping hours.
5. Start with 10 to 15 best-selling items that would sit together in a retail aisle, with strong listings.

Walmart adds that placement depends on relevancy, competitive bids, review count and scores, and the Two Day shipping tag. Translation: your listing quality is half the campaign. Fix photos, titles, reviews and price before you pay for a single click.

It also puts moderate-competition categories (home goods, kitchen, personal care) at $0.95 to $1.50 and says mature campaigns reach 4x to 5x ROAS. Those are the vendor's figures from larger accounts than most readers have; a small seller with fewer reviews will usually start at the weaker end.

Remember Walmart's own cut as well. The [referral fee schedule](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Getting%20started/Onboarding/Referral-fee-schedule-for-contract-categories) (last updated March 9, 2026) shows 15% for Pet Supplies, Home, Kitchen, Decor & Garden, and Toys & Games, and tiered rates for others: Beauty, Health & Personal Care is 8% on a total sales price of $10 or less and 15% above; Grocery is 8% at $15 or less and 15% above. The fee is a percentage of the total sales price, including shipping and handling. Check your own category, because it changes your margin and your break-even.

## The Math

A worked example for a $30 pet treat. Every number below is an **assumption** except the 15% referral fee, which is Walmart's published rate for Pet Supplies. Replace the rest with your own costs.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget (assumption) | $3,000 | $3,000 | $3,000 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | $2.00 | $1.25 | $0.80 |
| Clicks | 1,500 | 2,400 | 3,750 |
| Click to order rate (assumption) | 6% | 10% | 12% |
| Orders | 90 | 240 | 450 |
| Ad cost per order | $33.33 | $12.50 | $6.67 |
| Sale price (assumption) | $30 | $30 | $30 |
| ROAS (revenue / ad spend) | 0.9x | 2.4x | 4.5x |
| Margin per order before ads* | $10.50 | $10.50 | $10.50 |
| Profit after ads | -$2,055 | -$480 | +$1,725 |

*Margin before ads: $30 price, minus $4.50 referral fee (15%), minus $10 product cost (assumption), minus $5 packing and shipping (assumption) = $10.50, a 35% margin.

How to read it:

- **Break-even cost per click = margin per order x click-to-order rate.** Here that is $10.50 x 0.10 = $1.05. The Base case, at $1.25, loses $480 a month. That is not an error: it matches the idea that the first 60 to 90 days are for collecting data, and SellTru's own first-period ROAS range (1.5x to 2.5x) is below the 2.86x ROAS this product needs to break even ($30 / $10.50).
- **Margin is the real gate.** A product with a 20% margin needs a ROAS of 5x to break even. Check yours before you set a daily budget.
- **Ad-attributed sales are not all incremental.** Some buyers would have found you anyway. Compare total weekly sales before and after, not just the dashboard.
- **Organic rank can help.** Ad-driven sales may lift your listing in search, which can change the math over months. We have no verified figure for how much, so do not bake it into a budget.

If you cannot fund roughly two months of testing at your Base-case numbers, the money is safer elsewhere. See our [Amazon Sponsored Products guide](/blog/amazon-sponsored-products-for-local-business) for the same logic on a different marketplace.

## If You Sell Services or Serve One Town

Use channels with real local targeting instead. A [Google Business Profile](/services/google-business-profile) and Google Search ads catch people who are looking now; [geofencing](/blog/geofencing-advertising-for-local-business) and Meta ads handle radius targeting. For a streaming-TV angle, the recommended starting point is [CTV platforms compared](/blog/ctv-advertising-platforms-compared-2026), and for a big-budget, brand-style test see [Amazon Prime Video ads](/blog/amazon-prime-video-ads-small-business). Selling groceries or pantry goods locally instead? Look at [Instacart ads for local business](/blog/instacart-ads-local-business).

## Do This Today

1. Check you pass the test: your product is published on Walmart.com, in stock and winning the Buy Box. If not, pause here.
2. Work out margin per order after Walmart's referral fee, product cost and shipping, then compute your break-even CPC (margin x click-to-order rate) and break-even ROAS (price / margin).
3. Improve the listings for your best 10 to 15 items: photos, title, price, reviews.
4. Open Ad Center and build one automatic Sponsored Products campaign with Dynamic Bidding, with a budget you can sustain for 60 days.
5. After two weeks, read the search term report, then add the converting terms to a manual campaign and cut the ones that spent with no orders.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the minimum budget for Walmart Connect?**
A: Walmart recommends at least $100 a day for Sponsored Products but states no hard minimum on the pages we checked. SellTru puts the practical floor at $1,500 to $3,000 a month. Confirm current rules in Ad Center.

**Q: Can I advertise my local service on Walmart Connect?**
A: No. Sponsored Products promote items published on Walmart.com. Services need a local channel such as Google Ads.

**Q: Do I need a trademark to advertise?**
A: Not for Sponsored Products. Walmart lists in stock, winning the Buy Box and published on Walmart.com as the requirements. Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Videos require brand-owner status, a USPTO-registered trademark and Brand Portal onboarding.

**Q: What ROAS should I aim for?**
A: Aim for your break-even ROAS, which is price divided by margin per order. For the example above that is about 2.9x. SellTru reports 1.5x to 2.5x in the first 60 to 90 days, so expect to optimise before you profit.

**Q: Can Walmart Connect drive foot traffic to my shop?**
A: Not as a local campaign. Sponsored Products drive purchases of items sold on Walmart.com. For store visits, use [local SEO](/services/local-seo) and local ads.
