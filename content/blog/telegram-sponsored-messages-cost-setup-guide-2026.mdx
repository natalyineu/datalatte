Telegram Sponsored Messages are priced by CPM (cost per thousand views), and the price depends on the channels you target, the language and the current auction, so check the live estimate in Telegram's ad platform before you budget. Telegram has historically required a sizeable prepayment for its ad platform, so many local businesses get started more cheaply by buying a pinned post directly from the admins of local channels. Either way, start with a small test and measure clicks with UTM links.

Telegram says it has hundreds of millions of monthly users, but ads are text-only and targeting is limited, so it works best as a low-cost awareness channel next to Google or Meta, not a replacement for them.

## How Telegram Sponsored Messages work

Sponsored Messages are short text ads that appear in large public channels, labelled "Sponsored". Telegram's ad platform has used an auction-style model and, in recent years, has accepted payment through TON, its related cryptocurrency. Rules and minimums have changed more than once, so confirm the current terms on Telegram's official ad site before you commit money.

Key specs (verify before you build creative):
- **Length:** short text, roughly 160 characters
- **Format:** text only, with no images or video
- **Destination:** a Telegram channel, a bot or an external URL
- **Labelling:** clearly marked as sponsored

What you generally cannot do:
- Target by age, gender or other demographic details
- Retarget website visitors
- Run conversion tracking the way Google or Meta do (you rely on UTM links and bot analytics)

<Callout type="tip">If your goal is bookings, link to a Telegram bot with a simple appointment flow. Keeping people inside Telegram removes a browser redirect, which often helps conversion. Test it against a plain website link.</Callout>

## What does Telegram advertising cost in 2026?

There is no single price. Typical factors:

| Factor | How it affects cost |
|---|---|
| Channel category | Finance and business channels usually cost more than local news or lifestyle channels |
| Language and country | Competitive languages and markets cost more |
| Minimum budget or prepayment | Set by Telegram and subject to change, so check before planning |
| Placement type | Self-serve auction vs a direct deal with a channel admin |

We do not publish a fixed CPM here because rates move with demand and, on the TON route, with the token price. As a rule of thumb, text-only channel ads often cost less per thousand views than Meta, but you also get far less targeting, so cost per customer is what matters.

### Route 1: Telegram's own ad platform

You create an ad, choose your destination and target channel topics or languages, then fund the account. This route needs a funded account and, historically, a larger upfront balance than most local businesses want to risk. Read the current requirements on the official ad platform and look at the estimated reach it shows before launching.

### Route 2: Direct channel placement

You can negotiate directly with the admins of local channels. This is common, and for small budgets it is often more practical.

1. Search Telegram for channels that match your city and niche (for example local food, events or fitness channels).
2. Check subscriber counts and real engagement. Many admins list rates in their bio or a pinned post.
3. Agree on a price for a pinned post and a time window, often 12 to 24 hours.
4. Pay by whichever method the admin accepts.

Prices vary a lot by channel size and niche, so ask for a rate card and compare several channels. A test of three to five mid-size local channels is usually enough to learn whether the audience clicks.

<Callout type="warning">Always check a channel's engagement before paying. Tools such as TGStat show view history, and a channel with many subscribers but very few views per post may have inflated its numbers. Genuine engagement matters more than subscriber count.</Callout>

## Setting up a Telegram Sponsored Messages campaign

### Option A: Telegram's ad platform

1. Create or use a Telegram account and open the official Telegram ad platform.
2. Set up the payment method the platform currently requires (this has involved TON). Check what it supports today.
3. Write your message and choose the destination: channel, bot or external URL.
4. Choose targeting options (topics, languages or channels, depending on what is available).
5. Set your budget and review the estimated reach, then submit. Ads are reviewed before going live.

### Option B: Direct channel deals

1. Use Telegram search, or tools such as TGStat or Telemetr, to find relevant local channels. Filter by country, language and size.
2. Compare views per post with subscriber count. A healthy channel gets views from a meaningful share of its subscribers.
3. Message the admin with a short request: "Hi, I run [business type] in [city]. Can you share your ad rates and availability?"
4. Send your copy and ask to review the post before it goes live.
5. Use a unique UTM link per channel so you can see which one performs.

## What to write in a Telegram Sponsored Message

Space is tight. A simple structure is [local hook] + [specific offer] + [clear action].

For a coffee shop in London:
> "East London's flat white, now with a new single-origin. First cup on us this week. Tap to see the menu."

For a hair salon in Sydney:
> "Surry Hills: new-client colour offer at [Salon Name]. Book via the link before Friday."

For a pet groomer in Toronto:
> "Toronto dog owners: summer groom slots are filling. Book online in a minute."

Tips:
- Lead with your city or neighbourhood
- Give a specific offer with a deadline
- Keep emojis to one at most
- End with a clear action such as "Book" or "Tap to reserve"

## Measuring Telegram ad performance

Telegram has no native conversion tracking for external websites, so use workarounds:

- **UTM parameters:** add `?utm_source=telegram&utm_medium=sponsored&utm_campaign=channel-name` to links. See our [UTM parameters guide](/blog/utm-parameters-track-every-marketing-campaign).
- **Unique links per channel:** one short link per channel shows which sends traffic
- **Bot analytics:** if you link to a bot, every start and tap is recorded
- **Promo codes:** give each channel its own code and count redemptions

## Is Telegram advertising right for your local business?

**A good fit if:**
- Your city has active local Telegram channels (search for news, events and food groups)
- Your customers are comfortable with messaging apps
- You operate in a market where Telegram is widely used
- You want cheap awareness alongside Google or Meta campaigns

**Skip it if:**
- Your customers skew older and rarely use Telegram
- There are no active local channels where you live
- You need precise demographic targeting
- You cannot afford even a small test

If you are still choosing channels, compare it with [Meta ads for local business](/blog/meta-ads-for-local-business-2026) and [WhatsApp marketing for local business](/blog/whatsapp-marketing-for-local-business), which suit many local audiences better.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much do Telegram Sponsored Messages cost?**
A: Pricing depends on channel category, language and current demand, and Telegram has changed its minimums over time. Check the estimate in the official ad platform, or ask channel admins for a rate card if you buy directly. Run a small test first.

**Q: Do I need cryptocurrency knowledge to run Telegram ads?**
A: Telegram's ad platform has used TON for payment, so you may need to buy tokens and fund a wallet. If you buy pinned posts directly from channel admins, you can usually pay by bank transfer or PayPal instead, depending on the admin.

**Q: Can I target by location like Google Ads?**
A: Not in the same way. Targeting is mostly by channel, topic or language, so to reach people in a city you pick city-specific channels.

**Q: Is Telegram ad traffic lower quality than Google Ads?**
A: It depends on the channel. Channels with inflated or bot subscribers deliver poor traffic, while well-moderated local channels can deliver real clicks. Google Ads captures people already searching, while Telegram creates awareness, so expect different results.

**Q: What budget should I use for a first test?**
A: Enough to buy several small placements and gather a few hundred views and clicks, so you can compare channels. Keep some in reserve for a second round if the first batch underperforms.
