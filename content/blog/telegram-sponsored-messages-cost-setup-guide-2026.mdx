Telegram Sponsored Messages are text ads of up to 160 characters that show in public channels, and Telegram sells them through its own ad platform, paid for in cryptocurrency ([Telegram Ads](https://ads.telegram.org/), checked Oct 2026). Telegram does not publish a fixed price list on that page, so the cost depends on the channels you pick and the live estimate in the platform. Many local businesses start more cheaply by buying a pinned post directly from the admins of local channels. Either way, run a small test and measure clicks with UTM links.

Telegram's ad page cites 1 billion users and says half of ad revenue goes to the owners of the channels where ads run ([Telegram Ads](https://ads.telegram.org/), checked Oct 2026). Targeting is built around choosing channels, so it works best as a low-cost awareness channel next to Google or Meta, not a replacement for them.

## How Telegram Sponsored Messages work

Sponsored Messages are short text ads shown in large public channels. You choose the specific channels where your ads should appear, so the context is relevant ([Telegram Ads FAQ](https://ads.telegram.org/faq), checked Oct 2026). The ad text is capped at 160 characters, and payment is in cryptocurrency, so you will need a funded crypto account before you can run anything.

Rules, minimum deposits and creative formats have changed more than once, and we could not confirm a current minimum from Telegram's own pages. Read the current terms on the official ad site before you commit money.

What to expect:
- **Length:** up to 160 characters of text
- **Destination:** a channel, a bot or an external link (check what the platform currently allows)
- **Targeting:** channel-based; the public pages do not list age, gender or retargeting options, so do not plan around them
- **Tracking:** no Google-style conversion tracking, so you rely on UTM links, promo codes and bot analytics

<Callout type="tip">If your goal is bookings, link to a Telegram bot with a simple appointment flow, and test it against a plain website link to see which converts better.</Callout>

## What does Telegram advertising cost in 2026?

There is no single price we can honestly quote. We could not find a published CPM or minimum on Telegram's official pages, and the third-party figures that circulate vary widely, so we have left them out. What moves your cost:

| Factor | How it affects cost |
|---|---|
| Channel size and topic | Bigger, more popular channels cost more |
| Language and country | Competitive languages and markets cost more |
| Minimum deposit | Set by Telegram or a reseller and subject to change, so check before planning |
| Placement type | Self-serve auction versus a direct deal with a channel admin |

Because text-only awareness ads give you far less targeting than Meta, compare cost per customer, not cost per thousand views.

### Route 1: Telegram's own ad platform

You write an ad, choose the channels you want to appear in, then fund the account in cryptocurrency. Check the deposit required and the estimated reach the platform shows before launching. If the upfront balance is more than you would risk on an experiment, use Route 2.

### Route 2: Direct channel placement

You negotiate directly with admins of local channels. For small budgets this is often more practical.

1. Search Telegram for channels that match your city and niche, such as local food, events or fitness channels.
2. Check subscriber counts and real engagement. Many admins list rates in their bio or a pinned post.
3. Agree a price for a pinned post and a time window, often 12 to 24 hours.
4. Pay by whichever method the admin accepts.

Prices vary a lot by channel, so ask for a rate card and compare several channels. A test across three to five mid-size local channels is usually enough to learn whether the audience clicks.

<Callout type="warning">Check a channel's engagement before paying. Tools such as TGStat show view history, and a channel with many subscribers but very few views per post may have inflated its numbers.</Callout>

## Setting up a campaign

### Option A: Telegram's ad platform

1. Open the official Telegram ad platform with your Telegram account.
2. Set up the payment method the platform currently requires.
3. Write your message (160 characters maximum) and choose the destination.
4. Choose the channels where it should appear.
5. Set your budget, review the estimated reach and submit.

### Option B: Direct channel deals

1. Use Telegram search, or tools such as TGStat or Telemetr, to find local channels. Filter by country, language and size.
2. Compare views per post with subscriber count. A healthy channel gets views from a meaningful share of its subscribers.
3. Message the admin: "Hi, I run [business type] in [city]. Can you share your ad rates and availability?"
4. Send your copy and ask to approve the post before it goes live.
5. Use a unique UTM link per channel so you can see which one performs.

## What to write in a Sponsored Message

Space is tight, so use [local hook] + [specific offer] + [clear action].

For a coffee shop in London:
> "East London's flat white, now with a new single-origin. First cup on us this week. Tap to see the menu."

For a hair salon in Sydney:
> "Surry Hills: new-client colour offer at [Salon Name]. Book via the link before Friday."

For a pet groomer in Toronto:
> "Toronto dog owners: summer groom slots are filling. Book online in a minute."

Lead with your city or neighbourhood, give a specific offer with a deadline, use at most one emoji, and end with a clear action such as "Book" or "Tap to reserve".

## Measuring performance

Telegram has no native conversion tracking for external websites, so use workarounds:

- **UTM parameters:** add `?utm_source=telegram&utm_medium=sponsored&utm_campaign=channel-name` to links. See our [UTM parameters guide](/blog/utm-parameters-track-every-marketing-campaign).
- **Unique links per channel:** one short link per channel shows which sends traffic.
- **Bot analytics:** if you link to a bot, every start and tap is recorded.
- **Promo codes:** give each channel its own code and count redemptions.

For the bigger picture, see [how to track which marketing is bringing customers](/blog/how-to-track-which-marketing-is-bringing-customers).

## Is Telegram right for your local business?

**A good fit if** your city has active local Telegram channels, your customers already use messaging apps, and you want cheap awareness alongside Google or Meta campaigns.

**Skip it if** your customers skew older and rarely use Telegram, there are no active local channels, you need precise demographic targeting, or you cannot afford even a small test.

If you are still choosing channels, compare it with [Meta ads for local business](/blog/meta-ads-for-local-business-2026) and [WhatsApp marketing for local business](/blog/whatsapp-marketing-for-local-business), which suit many local audiences better.

## FAQ

**Q: How much do Telegram Sponsored Messages cost?**
A: It depends on the channels you choose and the current auction, and Telegram does not publish a fixed rate on its public ad pages. Check the estimate in the official platform, or ask channel admins for a rate card if you buy directly.

**Q: Do I need cryptocurrency to run Telegram ads?**
A: On Telegram's own ad platform, yes: it states that ads are paid for with cryptocurrency. If you buy pinned posts directly from channel admins, you can often pay by bank transfer or PayPal, depending on the admin.

**Q: How long can a Sponsored Message be?**
A: Up to 160 characters, according to Telegram's ad platform.

**Q: Can I target by location like Google Ads?**
A: Not in the same way. Telegram's platform lets you choose channels, so to reach people in a city you pick city-specific channels.

**Q: What budget should I use for a first test?**
A: Enough to buy several small placements and gather a few hundred views and clicks, so you can compare channels. Keep some in reserve for a second round.
