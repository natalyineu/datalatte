## How do Telegram Sponsored Messages work?

Sponsored Messages are short text ads shown in large public channels. You choose the specific channels where your ads should appear, so the context is relevant ([Telegram Ads FAQ](https://ads.telegram.org/faq), read 3 Nov 2026). Each ad needs a Telegram link to promote. Telegram says it does not use private chat contents or contact lists for ad targeting.

Telegram's ad page claims 1 billion users a month and 1 trillion views in public channels, and says 50% of ad revenue goes to the owners of the channels where ads are displayed. These are Telegram's own figures. Targeting is built around choosing channels, so treat it as an awareness channel next to Google or Meta, not a replacement.

What the public pages do and do not say:
- **Length:** up to 160 characters of text.
- **Destination:** a Telegram link (a channel or a bot).
- **Targeting:** channel-based. The public pages do not list age, gender or retargeting options, so do not plan around them.
- **Minimum deposit, CPM, payment details:** not published on the pages read for this article. Check current terms inside the platform before you commit money.
- **Tracking:** no Google-style conversion tracking, so you rely on UTM links, promo codes and bot analytics.

<Callout type="tip">If your goal is bookings, link to a Telegram bot with a simple appointment flow, and test it against a plain website link to see which converts better.</Callout>

## What does Telegram advertising cost in 2026?

There is no single price to quote. No published CPM or minimum appears on Telegram's official pages, and the third-party figures that circulate vary widely, so they are left out here. What moves your cost:

| Factor | How it affects cost |
|---|---|
| Channel size and topic | Bigger, more popular channels usually cost more |
| Language and country | Competitive languages and markets usually cost more |
| Minimum deposit | Set by Telegram or a reseller and subject to change, so check before planning |
| Placement type | Self-serve auction versus a direct deal with a channel admin |

Because text-only awareness ads give you far less targeting than Meta, compare cost per customer, not cost per thousand views.

### Route 1: Telegram's own ad platform

You write an ad, choose the channels you want to appear in, then fund the account in cryptocurrency. Check the deposit required and the estimated reach the platform shows before launching. If the upfront balance is more than you would risk on an experiment, use Route 2.

### Route 2: Direct channel placement

You negotiate directly with admins of local channels. For small budgets this is often more practical.

1. Search Telegram for channels that match your city and niche, such as local food, events or fitness channels.
2. Check subscriber counts and real engagement. Many admins list rates in their bio or a pinned post.
3. Agree a price for a pinned post and a time window, often 12 to 24 hours.
4. Pay by whichever method the admin accepts.

Ask for a rate card from at least 3 channels and compare. A test across 3 to 5 mid-size local channels is enough to learn whether the audience clicks.

## How do you work out the cost per booking?

**Assumption, not data:** the flat rate, views, click rate and booking rate below are invented to show the arithmetic. They are not Telegram prices and not typical results. Swap in the rates admins quote you and your own numbers.

| Step | Assumption | Result |
|------|------------|--------|
| Channels | 4 | |
| Flat rate per pinned post | $40 | 4 x $40 = $160 total |
| Views per post | 1,000 | 4 x 1,000 = 4,000 views |
| Click rate | 2% | 4,000 x 0.02 = 80 clicks |
| Clicks that book | 10% | 80 x 0.10 = 8 bookings |
| Cost per booking | | $160 / 8 = $20 |
| Average ticket | $45 | 8 x $45 = $360 revenue |

Stress it. If the click rate is 1% instead of 2%, the result is 40 clicks and 4 bookings, and the cost per booking doubles to $40. If a channel has 20,000 subscribers but gets 300 views per post, the budget bought an audience that is not watching, so check views before paying.

Revenue is not profit, so compare the cost per booking with what a booking leaves after costs. The [marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to size the test against your monthly budget.

## What should you do before paying for a placement?

1. List 5 local Telegram channels (city plus your niche). Write down subscribers and views on the last 5 posts of each.
2. Drop any channel where average views are far below its subscriber count.
3. Message 3 admins and ask for a rate card.
4. Write your ad in under 160 characters, then count them.
5. Build one UTM link and one promo code per channel before paying anyone.
6. Set the review date for 7 days after the post goes live.

<Callout type="warning">Check a channel's engagement before paying. Tools such as TGStat show view history, and a channel with many subscribers but very few views per post may have inflated its numbers.</Callout>

## How do you set up a campaign?

### Option A: Telegram's ad platform

1. Open the official Telegram ad platform with your Telegram account.
2. Set up the payment method the platform currently requires.
3. Write your message (160 characters maximum) and add the Telegram link to promote.
4. Choose the channels where it should appear.
5. Set your budget, review the estimated reach and submit.

### Option B: Direct channel deals

1. Use Telegram search, or tools such as TGStat or Telemetr, to find local channels. Filter by country, language and size.
2. Compare views per post with subscriber count. A healthy channel gets views from a meaningful share of its subscribers.
3. Message the admin: "Hi, I run [business type] in [city]. Can you share your ad rates and availability?"
4. Send your copy and ask to approve the post before it goes live.
5. Use a unique UTM link per channel so you can see which one performs.

## What should a Sponsored Message say?

Space is tight, so use [local hook] + [specific offer] + [clear action].

For a coffee shop in London:
> "East London's flat white, now with a new single-origin. First cup on us this week. Tap to see the menu."

For a hair salon in Sydney:
> "Surry Hills: new-client colour offer at [Salon Name]. Book via the link before Friday."

For a pet groomer in Toronto:
> "Toronto dog owners: summer groom slots are filling. Book online in a minute."

These templates are illustrative wording, not tested ads. They run 103, 86 and 76 characters, well inside the 160 cap. Lead with your city or neighbourhood, give an offer with a deadline, use at most one emoji, and end with an action such as "Book" or "Tap to reserve".

## How do you measure Telegram ads?

Telegram has no native conversion tracking for external websites, so use these:

- **UTM parameters:** add `?utm_source=telegram&utm_medium=sponsored&utm_campaign=channel-name` to links. See the [UTM parameters guide](/blog/utm-parameters-track-every-marketing-campaign).
- **Unique links per channel:** one short link per channel shows which sends traffic.
- **Bot analytics:** if you link to a bot, every start and tap is recorded.
- **Promo codes:** give each channel its own code and count redemptions.

Pair every test with a way to see which message produced the booking before spending more.

## Is Telegram right for your local business?

**A good fit if** your city has active local Telegram channels and your customers already use messaging apps.

**Skip it if** your customers rarely use Telegram, there are no active local channels, you need precise demographic targeting, or you cannot afford even a small test.

## FAQ

### How much do Telegram Sponsored Messages cost?

It depends on the channels you choose, and Telegram does not publish a fixed rate on its public ad pages. Check the estimate in the official platform, or ask channel admins for a rate card if you buy directly.

### Do I need cryptocurrency to run Telegram ads?

On Telegram's own ad platform, yes: the ad page states that ads are paid for with a cryptocurrency. If you buy pinned posts directly from channel admins, payment depends on the admin, and bank transfer or PayPal are sometimes accepted.

### How long can a Sponsored Message be?

Up to 160 characters, according to Telegram's ad platform.

### Can I target by location like Google Ads?

Not in the same way. Telegram's platform lets you choose channels, so to reach people in a city you pick city-specific channels.

### What budget should I use for a first test?

Enough to buy 3 to 5 small placements and compare channels. Keep a third in reserve for a second round.
