To grow a restaurant on Glovo, use the two tools Glovo documents for partners: Promotool, for offers such as free delivery or discounts, and Self-Service Advertising, where you set a maximum monthly spend. Start with one small offer and one capped ad budget, then compare orders and margin.

Glovo's terms publish no minimum spend, no cost per click and no commission rate, so ask your account manager for the numbers in your contract.

This guide explains what the terms say, how to price an offer, and which tips come only from secondary sources.

## What Marketing Tools Does Glovo Give Partners?

Glovo's terms (last updated August 2025) describe two tools in the Vendor Portal. Promotool lets you create, enable and disable your own offers. You can set a campaign name, cities and stores, start and end dates, a budget and a maximum number of orders. Offer types include free delivery, percentage or amount discounts, Spend X Get Y, 2x1, minimum basket and Flash Offers.

Self-Service Advertising lets you run campaigns with a maximum monthly spend and a start date. Available options depend on your business model and agreement, so what you see may differ from another restaurant's.

## Who Pays for Glovo Promotions?

You do. The terms say the partner is solely responsible for the cost of the campaign budget, and that discounts and delivery subsidies the partner assumes are charged through invoices. For ads, you pay the amount the campaign actually consumed.

Flash Offers use a cost-per-order model: a fee on each fulfilled order's total value. Those fees are non-refundable, and the terms say neither refunds nor credits are granted.

Glovo may stop a campaign at 90% of the agreed budget, and any difference between the budget and the final amount spent is charged to you. Read these clauses before you set a budget.

## How Should You Choose an Offer?

Pick the offer that fits your margin. A percentage discount cuts margin on every order, while a minimum basket or Spend X Get Y raises the basket size you need before the discount applies. Set the maximum number of orders in Promotool so a busy night cannot make the offer unprofitable.

## What Menu Tips Come From Secondary Sources?

UrbanPiper, a restaurant software vendor, published a Glovo marketing article on 26 September 2024. Its advice: feature bestsellers and high-margin items, use professional photos, write ingredient descriptions, and choose dishes that travel well. It also lists partner ads as pay-per-click that can be paused at any time.

Treat this as a secondary, vendor-written source. It claims "up to 5X ROAS" for brand ads with no baseline, sample size or conditions, so do not use that figure for planning.

## The Math: Does a 20% Discount Pay Off?

All inputs are **assumptions** in euros for an illustrative restaurant.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Average basket | €20 |
| Margin before discount | 30% (€6) |
| Discount | 20% (€4) |
| Margin per discounted order | €2 |
| Orders needed to match €6 margin | 3 per old order |

A 20% discount leaves €2 of margin per order against €6 before. If the offer applies to existing customers who would have ordered anyway, you lose €4 on each. It pays only if it brings new customers who return at full price, or lifts basket size. Use a cap on orders and review after one week.

## FAQ

### How much do Glovo ads cost?
Glovo's public terms give no cost per click and no minimum. You set a maximum monthly spend and pay for what the campaign consumes.

### Can I get a refund on a campaign?
The terms say neither refunds nor credits are granted, and Flash Offer fees are non-refundable.

### Is there a Glovo commission I can read online?
Commission rates are in your agreement. Third-party sites list figures, but none was verified for this page.

### Should I run promotions all the time?
Not necessarily. Compare a promotion week with a normal week on orders and margin.
