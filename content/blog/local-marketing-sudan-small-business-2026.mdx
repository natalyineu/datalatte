Local marketing in Sudan starts with a hard fact: 14.9 million people, or 28.7% of the population, were online in October 2025 (DataReportal Digital 2026). Online ads reach a minority, and the war that began in April 2023 has displaced millions. This page gives the numbers that exist, says where no data exists, and suggests low-cost steps that fit.

## How Many Sudanese Can You Reach Online?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 page for Sudan counts 14.9 million internet users and about 37.1 million people offline (71.3%). Social media user identities stand at 6.06 million (11.6% of the population), up 2.5 million over the year. These can include duplicate accounts, so read them as an upper bound on people.

Mobile connections total 17.8 million, but DataReportal warns that this includes voice and SMS-only plans. A connection is not the same as a person who can see an ad.

## Which Platforms Report Ad Reach?

For Sudan, DataReportal lists TikTok at 6.06 million adults (21.9% of adults 18+) and LinkedIn at 710 thousand members. The page does not publish Facebook or Instagram ad reach for Sudan, so this article gives no figure for them. Check the ad planning tool inside your account for current numbers.

Treat these as advertising audiences, not active users. TikTok's ad tools only report people aged 18 and over.

## What Does the War Do to Local Demand?

The World Bank reports that 11,642,505 people had been forcibly displaced as of 16 March 2026, and that 21.2 million people (41% of the population) faced high food insecurity. Real GDP fell an estimated 29.4% in 2023 and 14% in 2024, then rose an estimated 3.1% in 2025 from a very low base. Inflation was 68% in December 2024, the latest figure given.

For a shop or service, this means customers have moved. A location tag from 2022 may point to an empty street, so confirm where your customers live now before you pay for anything.

## Can You Run Google Ads in Sudan?

Google's country restrictions page lists Crimea, Cuba, the so-called Donetsk and Luhansk People's Republics, Iran and North Korea as places where Google Ads is not available. Sudan is not on that list in the version checked on 24 October 2026. That does not confirm that every Sudanese business can open an account or pay.

Local payment methods, currency and sanctions rules for individual advertisers are unverified here, so check them in Google Ads before budgeting.

None of the sources used publishes click or impression prices for Sudan, so none are given.

## What Can a Small Business Do With Little Budget?

Start with free or near-free steps and measure by conversations, not clicks.

- Keep a business phone number and WhatsApp contact on every post. No 2026 Sudan WhatsApp user figure is available from the sources used, so this is a practical choice, not a statistic.
- Post short video on TikTok, where the reach data exists.
- Update your address and opening status on Google Maps so relocated customers can find you. Our [Google Business Profile guide for Europe](/blog/google-business-profile-optimisation-europe) covers the profile fields, which are the same anywhere.
- Ask existing customers for referrals before you buy ads.

## Worked Example: A Test Budget

Assumption: a small food seller sets aside the equivalent of 50 USD for a four-week TikTok test. Assumption: the goal is 20 messages from new customers. If it produces 8 messages, the cost is about 6 USD per message; if 25, about 2 USD. These inputs are made up for the arithmetic.

## FAQ

### Is Facebook advertising available in Sudan?

It is unconfirmed. DataReportal does not list Meta ad reach for Sudan, and Meta's help pages did not load when checked. Check inside Meta Ads Manager.

### Is there Sudan-specific ad pricing?

None of the sources used publishes it. Treat any price table for Sudan as unverified.

### Do sanctions apply to advertisers in Sudan?

Unverified. Google's restrictions page did not list Sudan, but sanctions rules can change. Ask the platform or a local adviser.
