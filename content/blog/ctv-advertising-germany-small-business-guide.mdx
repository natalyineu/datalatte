A German small business can reach connected TV viewers today through Google's YouTube-on-TV inventory, which is bought in Google Ads. Streaming services such as RTL+ or Joyn, and regional addressable TV, are sold mainly through sales houses, and no public price list for small advertisers could be verified. This guide says what is confirmed, what is not, and how to test cheaply.

It contains no CPM or budget benchmarks. Earlier versions of this page quoted market size, growth and cost figures without sources, and they have been removed.

## What Is the Online Audience in Germany?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 78.5 million internet users, a penetration of 93.5%, with 5.45 million people offline. Social media user identities reach 64.7 million, or 77.1% of the population. Meta's tools show Instagram reaching 31.3 million, TikTok 23.7 million adults and Facebook 22.8 million.

The report has no figures on time spent with streaming TV or on CTV households, so this guide does not give a connected-TV audience size. Be careful with any page that quotes one without naming its source.

DataReportal warns that ad reach is not an active user count and that platform corrections can distort year-on-year comparisons.

## How Can a Small Business Buy CTV Ads in Germany?

The route with public documentation is Google. Google Ads Help says that video action campaigns were upgraded to Demand Gen as of May 2026, and that ads can appear in videos in the YouTube app on TV. For QR codes on connected TV, Google says to make sure device targeting includes TV screens, and that QR codes work in Demand Gen and Performance Max campaigns on CTV.

Google's help pages checked here do not state country availability for QR codes or TV-screen targeting. Whether every feature is live in Germany is unverified, so check the options inside your own account. An older Google page says the TV-screens option applied only to Display and Video campaigns, which shows how fast the documentation changes.

## Is There Local TV Targeting for Small Firms?

Yes, in at least one case, though not confirmed for Germany. RTL AdAlliance announced a self-service addressable TV tool for small and mid-sized businesses on 15 September 2025. Ads are placed by postal code and sociodemographics, on 14 channels including RTL, RTLup, VOX, Nitro and ntv. The announcement quotes a "four-digit" budget and no exact price.

That announcement describes a rollout in Austria with heise regioconcept as the first partner. It does not mention Germany. A German small business should ask RTL AdAlliance or a regional marketing partner whether the product is available and what the minimum spend is. The ad channels named in that article are linear channels, so it is not evidence about streaming inventory such as RTL+.

No official ad-price page from Joyn, RTL+, Sky or the public broadcasters was found, so their rates are not quoted.

## What Do German Advertising Rules Require?

The Medienstaatsvertrag (MStV) sets the broadcast framework. Section 8 requires that advertising be easily recognisable and distinguishable from editorial content, and bans subliminal techniques. It forbids ads that violate human dignity, discriminate, mislead consumers or promote behaviour that seriously endangers health, safety or the environment.

Section 8 also addresses regional ads. Paragraph 11 says that distributing broadcast ads regionally within a nationwide programme is allowed only where the law of the relevant state permits it, and private broadcasters need a separate state-law permission. This is one reason local TV buying runs through specialised products.

For online video, section 22 requires that advertising be clearly recognisable and separated from other content. Section 30 bars advertising, apart from product placement, in the online offerings of public broadcasters, so ARD and ZDF streaming libraries are not an ad channel.

Data protection (GDPR and the German cookie and consent rules) also affects retargeting and customer-list matching. That detail was not opened for this guide, so it is unverified. Ask a lawyer before you upload customer lists.

## What Does a Test Look Like?

**Worked example (all inputs are assumptions).** A fitness studio in Hamburg-Altona wants to test CTV before a January campaign. Assumption: the owner sets one Demand Gen campaign limited to a radius around the studio, includes TV screens in device targeting, and uses a 15-second video with a QR code to a trial-class page. In parallel, the owner asks a regional marketing partner for a quote on addressable TV by postal code. The only fixed figure is the plan: one test, one landing page, and a decision date after four weeks. Budget is the owner's choice, since no verified German CTV prices exist.

## FAQ

### How much does CTV advertising cost in Germany?

No public price list for small advertisers could be verified. Google Ads shows costs inside the account, and RTL AdAlliance describes its small-business tool only as a four-digit budget.

### Can I advertise on ARD and ZDF streaming?

Not with ads. Section 30 of the MStV excludes advertising, except product placement, from the online offerings of public broadcasters.

### Do I need a large budget?

Not for YouTube on TV through Google Ads, where you set your own budget. For addressable TV by postal code, ask the seller for the minimum.

### Is CTV targeting by city possible?

RTL AdAlliance's tool targets by postal code in the market where it is live. In Google Ads you set locations in the campaign. Check what your account allows in Germany.
