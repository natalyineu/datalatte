## How Large Is Connected TV Reach for German Audiences?

The clearest German signal in the sources is supply rather than audience size. The March 2025 Sky Media partnership gives advertisers programmatic access to Sky Deutschland's premium connected TV inventory, so premium German screens can be bought through automated platforms. That is a change in how inventory is sold, not a count of viewers.

No German household count for connected TV appears in the sources, so this guide gives none. The forecast that does appear is for the United States. StackAdapt expects US households streaming content through CTV to grow from 115.1 million in 2024 to 123.3 million by 2028, reaching 89.4% of US households.

That trend shows the format is growing in its home market. It does not tell a German café owner how many neighbours will see a spot. Treat any German reach figure you find as something to verify with the seller, and ask for it in writing.

## Which Routes Can a Small Business Actually Use?

The clearest route is programmatic buying through a platform. StackAdapt describes its Sky Media partnership as giving advertisers access to Sky Deutschland's premium inventory, so a business working with that platform can ask about German CTV placements. Minimums and eligibility are not stated in the source, so confirm them before planning a campaign.

Smaller budgets are possible on some platforms. Adwave's published budget ladder opens mobile web, mobile apps, TV, YouTube, Demand Gen, Google Search, Facebook and Instagram at $30 a day. A test can start in one of these channels, but a budget tier is a starting point, not a guarantee of German inventory or delivery.

Adwave also says it extends campaigns across TV and connected TV on 100+ premium channels. Check that claim against the German channels your seller actually offers. The number of channels does not show whether any of them reach your postcode.

## What Do Connected TV Impressions Cost?

Vendors position CTV among the cheaper TV options. According to StackAdapt, CTV is usually the most affordable TV option, with CPMs ranging from $15 to $30 USD. CPM means cost per thousand impressions, so a lower CPM buys more views for the same spend.

Targeted buys cost more. Adwave puts addressable, first-party-targeted connected TV buys at about $45 to $85 CPM in 2026. The higher range applies to audiences built from first-party data, which is a different product from broad CTV inventory.

Neither range is a German price. Use them to judge whether a quote is in a sensible range, then ask each seller for its own rate card and minimum spend.

## Does Attention on Connected TV Hold Up?

Industry benchmarks cited by Teads show CTV ads achieving 95% completion or more, compared with 20 to 40% on YouTube and under 30% on social video. Completion is not a sale, but it suggests a full-length ad on a TV screen is far more likely to be watched to the end than a short clip on a phone.

YouTube already reaches the TV screen. Adwave, citing eMarketer, says more than 44% of US YouTube watch time happens on connected TVs in 2026, up from about 41% in 2022. More than 180 million Americans watch YouTube on a connected TV. Non-skippable in-stream YouTube ads run up to 15 seconds, with a 30-second option for connected TV.

These are US figures. The sources do not give German YouTube TV-screen shares, so test whether German viewers respond before scaling. Set a frequency cap so a small local audience does not see the same spot every day, as explained in the [ad frequency guide for small business](/blog/ad-frequency-small-business-guide).

## Addressable TV or CTV: Which Targets Better?

The two products target differently. Addressable TV enables household-level targeting, while CTV offers greater precision by targeting individual users who may be streaming separately within the same household. For a business that sells to individuals, CTV targeting is the more relevant concept.

Addressable reach has shrunk in the US. Households with a paid TV subscription or set-top box that allows addressable advertising fell from 68.5 million in 2020 to 48.9 million in 2024, a 28.61% decrease. In a 2024 survey by Go Addressable and Advertiser Perceptions, advertisers cited targeting, personalization and incremental reach as the main reasons for using addressable TV.

For a German business, the practical question is which of these products a seller offers at all. Ask that question first. A label such as addressable or CTV means little if the seller cannot place a spot in the postcodes you serve.

## What Does a Four-Week CTV Test Cost?

**Worked example (all inputs are assumptions).** A fitness studio in Hamburg wants to test CTV before a January class push. Assumption: the owner runs the campaign for 28 days at the $30 a day tier from Adwave's ladder, which is $840 in total. Assumption: the owner expects a CPM at the low end of the vendor range, $15, or at the low end of the addressable range, $45.

At $15 CPM, $840 buys about 56,000 impressions (840 divided by 15, times 1,000). At $45 CPM, the same budget buys about 18,700 impressions. The gap is the cost of targeting, and the owner has to decide whether a more precise audience is worth fewer views.

Conversion check (assumption): the studio gets 35 trial sign-ups from its dedicated URL during the four weeks. That gives a cost per sign-up of $24 ($840 divided by 35). Set the decision threshold before launch, for example an assumed maximum of $30 per sign-up. Keep the campaign if it stays under that figure, and stop it if it does not.

## What Steps Set Up a Safe Test?

1. Choose one channel and confirm German inventory, minimum spend and delivery with the platform or seller in writing.
2. Build one 15-second video and a dedicated trial-class URL, so every sign-up can be traced to the campaign.
3. Set the budget, end date and frequency cap before launch, and write down the sign-up threshold.
4. Map the path from first view to sign-up using the [customer journey mapping guide](/blog/customer-journey-mapping-small-business-guide), then ask new members how they heard of the studio.
5. At the decision date, keep, change or stop the campaign based on sign-up cost, not on impressions alone.

Account basics such as budget limits and conversion tracking are covered in the [Google Ads setup guide](/blog/how-do-i-set-up-google-ads-for-my-small-business-complete-guide-for-2026). Have a German media lawyer check the video and targeting before launch.

## FAQ

### How much does CTV advertising cost in Germany?

This guide has no German CTV price list. The vendor range is $15 to $30 CPM for CTV, and Adwave puts addressable, first-party-targeted buys at about $45 to $85 CPM in 2026. Ask each seller for its own German rate card and minimum.

### Can a small business buy addressable TV in Germany?

The sources do not confirm an addressable TV product for small German advertisers. Addressable TV targets households, while CTV targets individual viewers. Ask a seller which of these it actually sells in Germany.

### Is a $30 a day test enough?

It can be enough to learn whether a TV-style video drives sign-ups. Adwave lists $30 a day as the opening tier across several channels. In the worked example, a four-week test at that level totals $840, which is an assumed budget rather than a forecast.

### Do I need a lawyer before launching?

Yes, before running ads that make claims, use customer lists or target by region. German advertising rules apply to these formats, and this guide does not set out what they require.
