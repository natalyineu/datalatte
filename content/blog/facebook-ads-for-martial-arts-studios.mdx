Facebook ads can fill a martial arts studio's trial classes when they target families within a short drive, sell one low-risk trial class, and are followed by a same-day reply to every lead. The ad produces an enquiry; the reply and the first class decide whether it becomes a paying student. Judge the campaign by cost per enrolled student, not by cost per lead.

Meta publishes no martial arts benchmark, and LocaliQ's 2026 Facebook benchmark page returned an access error when opened for this article, so no industry cost figures are quoted. The worked example below uses labelled assumptions.

## Who Should the Ads Reach?

Decide first who signs up: parents of young children, teens, or adults looking for fitness and self-defence. Each group needs a different message. Parents look for safety, discipline and confidence for their kids; adults look for fitness, stress relief and skills.

Build the first campaign around the group that already fills your classes. Meta's [location selection page](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/202297959811696) says you can reach people who live in, recently spent time in or often visit countries, regions, cities and postal codes. It also warns that accuracy cannot be guaranteed, so some impressions and leads may come from outside your settings.

For kids' classes the buyer is a parent. Target adults in a realistic drive radius, then show the children in the creative.

## What Do You Need Before the First Ad?

A Facebook Page, a connected Instagram account, an ad account and a payment method, then an objective, an audience, a budget and creative. For enrolment, a Leads objective usually fits better than reach.

Meta's [lead ads page](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/761812391313386) describes instant forms that collect details without sending people off Facebook or Instagram. A form needs at least one question, a privacy policy is required to run it, and contact details may be prefilled. Types are more volume (the default), higher intent and rich creative. Features such as phone number verification and conditional logic help filter low-quality leads.

If you also run search ads, coordinate them with [Google Ads management](/services/google-ads) so people who search "karate near me" find you as well.

## Is the Meta Pixel Safe to Use for a Kids' Studio?

Use it carefully. Meta's [Pixel page](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/742478679120153) says the pixel measures what people do on your website and can build custom audiences for retargeting. It also says prohibited information must not be shared with Meta, and lists health, financial and children's data among it. Keep children's names and ages out of event names, URLs and parameters, and ask your legal adviser about consent rules in your country.

## Which Offer Works for a Trial Class?

Offer one specific, low-risk first step. Ideas to test:

- A free or discounted trial class or trial week.
- A free introductory lesson for kids.
- A short guide for parents choosing a studio.

A strong offer removes risk (fear of injury or looking foolish), has a real deadline or a limited number of trial spots, and gives something useful early. Do not invent scarcity, and only offer guarantees you will honour. Test two offers at equal budget for a week or two and compare cost per enrolled student.

## How Fast Should You Follow Up?

Within minutes. Many leads are lost after the ad, when someone requests a trial and waits days for a reply. A simple sequence:

1. **Within minutes:** an automatic text or email confirming the request, with a booking link.
2. **Same day:** a call or text from a real person.
3. **A day or two later:** a reminder if they have not booked.
4. **Before class:** what to wear and where to park.

## What Does the Funnel Cost? A Worked Example

Every input below is an **assumption**, not a benchmark. Suppose a studio spends $600 in a month.

| Step | Assumption | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad spend | $600 | |
| Cost per lead | $15 | 40 leads |
| Lead books a trial | 40% | 16 trials booked |
| Trial booker shows up | 70% | 11.2 attend |
| Attendee enrols | 50% | 5.6 students |
| Cost per enrolled student | $600 / 5.6 | about $107 |

Compare $107 with what a student is worth over their time with you. If follow-up cuts the show-up rate to 50%, the same ad produces four students and cost per student rises to $150. The ad did not change; the process did.

## What Mistakes Waste the Budget?

- **Too wide a radius.** Most students will not travel far, so shrink the radius to a realistic 15 to 20 minute drive and compare it with where current students live.
- **Asking for commitment too early.** "Sign up today" with a long contract link loses people who are scrolling in the evening.
- **Running one ad forever.** Rotate creative every few weeks with different angles: instructors, real students with permission, long-term progress, or a specific open day.
- **Counting clicks instead of students.** Check that the booking form works on a phone and that events record trials, not just page views.

## What Should You Track Weekly?

1. **Cost per lead.**
2. **Lead-to-trial rate.** If low, fix the follow-up.
3. **Trial-to-enrolment rate.** If low, look at the first-class experience, price and instructor engagement.
4. **Cost per enrolled student.**

A spreadsheet that the front desk updates weekly, with ad name, spend, leads, trial dates and enrolments, is enough. Add UTM parameters to links so analytics can show which campaign drove each action. The [analytics service](/services/analytics) can help with tracking.

## FAQ

### How much should a martial arts studio spend on Facebook ads?

No verified benchmark exists for martial arts. Start with a budget you can sustain for several weeks and work backward from what an enrolled student is worth, as in the worked example above.

### Does a free trial class help?

A low-risk trial often lowers the barrier for unsure parents, but this article found no published study. Measure how many trial attendees enrol.

### What audience should I target for kids' classes?

Parents in the right age range within a short drive, with kids' classes shown clearly in the creative. Test parent-focused against child-focused messages.

### How long until results show?

Leads can arrive within days, but learning what works takes several weeks. Change one thing at a time.
