For most local businesses, Google Business Profile matters more, because it is free, it feeds Google Maps and local search, and Google was the review source most US consumers used in BrightLocal's 2026 survey. Yelp still matters if your category is one people search there, such as restaurants and home services, but treat it as a second profile to claim and monitor, not the first thing to build.

The data below comes from BrightLocal's Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 (1,002 US adults) and from Google's and Yelp's own pages, opened on 1 November 2026. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## Which Platform Do Consumers Use More?

Google. BrightLocal reports that the share of consumers who read Google reviews fell from 83% in 2025 to 71% in 2026, and that Google is still the most-used source. In the past 12 months 45% of consumers wrote a Google review, 34% a Facebook review and 24% a Yelp review ([BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)).

BrightLocal's page gives no 2026 reading figure for Yelp, so we cannot say what share of consumers read Yelp reviews. The survey is of US adults, so use it as a guide for the US only. Our [review platform comparison](/blog/review-platform-comparison-yelp-google-facebook-tripadvisor) covers other sites.

## How Do Their Rules for Asking for Reviews Differ?

This is the biggest practical difference. Google lets you ask: its help says you can ask customers to visit a Google link or scan a QR code. It prohibits offering free or discounted goods or services in exchange for reviews ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)).

Yelp's content guidelines say "Businesses should never ask customers to write reviews" ([Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/guidelines)). So a QR code on the receipt works for Google and goes against Yelp's stated rule. Plan your review requests around Google, and let Yelp reviews arrive without prompting.

## Which Helps You Rank in Local Search?

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)). You cannot pay for a better free ranking. A complete, accurate profile is the lever you control.

Yelp is a separate search site. No Yelp page we opened describes how its search ranks businesses, so we make no claim on that. What can be said is that Yelp pages are indexed by search engines and may appear for some searches, but no source we opened measured how often.

## What Does Yelp Cost to Advertise?

Claiming a Yelp page is free. Yelp's ads page says you can "spend as little as $5 a day on average," with no term contracts for self-serve advertisers and cancellation at any time. Ads appear in Sponsored Results above and below organic results and on competitor pages. Yelp does not publish an average cost per click on that page ([Yelp Business](https://business.yelp.com/products/yelp-ads/)).

The page also makes marketing claims, such as 4x more leads. They come with no method, so we do not repeat them as results. Read our [Yelp Ads guide](/blog/yelp-ads-worth-it-local-business-2026) for a test plan.

## How Should You Split Your Time?

Start with the free profile that more customers use, then claim the other so the details are right. A reasonable order:

1. Complete your Google profile: category, hours, services, photos. Use our [profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).
2. Ask every customer for a Google review with a link or QR code.
3. Claim your Yelp page, correct the address and phone, and reply to reviews.
4. Test Yelp Ads only if your category already gets Yelp traffic.

BrightLocal also reports that 80% of consumers say they are likely to use a business that responds to all of its reviews, so replying on both sites is worth the time.

## FAQ

### Is Google Business Profile or Yelp better for a small business?
For most, Google, because it is free, feeds Maps and Search, and is used by more US consumers in BrightLocal's 2026 survey. Yelp can matter in some categories, so check where your own customers say they found you.

### Can I ask customers for Yelp reviews?
Yelp's guidelines say businesses should never ask. Google allows asking with a link or QR code, so focus requests there.

### Do I need both?
Claim both so the information is correct. Put most effort into Google first.

### Do Yelp ads help my Google ranking?
No source we opened says so. Google says there is no way to pay for a better local ranking on Google.

### How much do Yelp Ads cost?
Yelp says you can spend as little as $5 a day on average with no term contract for self-serve ads. It does not publish an average cost per click on that page.
