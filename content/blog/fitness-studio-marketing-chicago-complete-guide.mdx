## Is the Chicago Fitness Market Growing?

The national trend points up. The Health and Fitness Association's 2026 consumer report says 81 million Americans belonged to a fitness facility in 2025, a record and 5.2% more than in 2024. Facilities logged an estimated 7 billion visits, above the 2019 peak. The share of members who never used their membership fell from about 10% to 4.6%.

Yoga is the most widely practiced activity, with 17.7 million members. Gen Z adults aged 18 to 24 had the highest membership rate at 35.5%. These are national figures, and the report excerpt read for this article had no Chicago breakdown. Chicago itself had an estimated 2,731,585 residents in 2025, according to Census figures reported by FOX 32.

## What Do Fitness Ads Cost?

LocaliQ's benchmarks give two reference points. On Google Search, Health and Fitness averages $6.17 per click, a 5.81% click-through rate, a 6.94% conversion rate and a $67.36 cost per lead. On Meta, LocaliQ lists lead campaigns for Health and Fitness at $1.84 per click, a 3.09% click-through rate, a 7.98% conversion rate and a $27.11 cost per lead.

Lower cost per lead does not mean better leads. A person searching "pilates studio near me" is looking to book, while a person who tapped a Meta lead form was interrupted. ## How Should a Chicago Studio Use Local Search?

Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence, and says reviews and ratings help prominence. It also says you cannot pay for a better local ranking. So fill out every profile field, choose the right primary category, add class photos and reply to every review.

For ads, Google's radius targeting requires a radius of at least 1 km. In a dense city, a studio can set a radius around its door, or pick the neighbourhoods where members live. Check your member postcodes first, then match the targeting to the real map. ## What Offer Works Best for New Members?

Published data on which offer converts best in Chicago does not exist in the sources checked, so test one offer at a time. Common options are a first-class trial, a short challenge, or a community class. Pick one, run it for a month and compare booked trials, shows and joins.

Keep the sign-up path short: one page, one class time picker, one phone number. Then measure each step separately: lead, booked trial, attended trial, member.

## FAQ

### How much should a Chicago fitness studio spend on ads?

No Chicago figure is published. Start with an amount you can run for 30 days, then judge by cost per new member, not cost per click.

### Is Google or Meta better for a boutique studio?

Google reaches people searching now, Meta reaches people who may not have been looking. Test both with a fixed budget and compare members, not leads.

### How do I target a Chicago neighbourhood?

Use a radius of at least 1 km or named areas in Google Ads, and compare against where current members live.

### Do reviews matter for rankings?

Google says review count and ratings feed prominence, one of three local ranking factors.

### Are yoga and pilates studios counted in the national numbers?

The report names yoga as the most widely practiced activity, with 17.7 million members.
