Pet groomers should start with one automatic text reminder before each appointment, sent from their booking software, and add promotional texts later and only to clients who agreed to receive them. No source publishes a no-show rate for grooming. The best public evidence is from healthcare: a review of 8 randomised trials found text reminders raised attendance by a relative 14% compared with no reminder.

This guide covers what texting can and cannot be expected to do, what groomer software charges, and the consent rules in the US. Prices and rules were checked in October 2026. Examples with made-up numbers are labelled as assumptions.

## Do Text Reminders Reduce No-Shows?

Probably, though the evidence is not from grooming. The SUPPORT summary of a systematic review covers 8 randomised trials with 6,615 participants. Text reminders compared with no reminders gave a risk ratio of 1.14 (95% CI 1.03 to 1.26): attendance rose from 678 to 773 per 1,000 patients. The certainty of evidence is moderate.

The same summary found little or no difference between text and phone call reminders: 795 against 803 attended per 1,000. So a text is not magic. It is a cheap way to do what a receptionist's call does. These were healthcare appointments, so a groomer should measure their own no-show rate before and after.

## What Do You Need to Be Allowed to Text Clients?

In the US, the rule that matters most is the FCC's TCPA rule, 47 CFR 64.1200. For autodialed telemarketing texts to cell phones, it requires "prior express written consent". The definition requires a signed agreement, which can be electronic, that includes the phone number and states that signing is not a condition of buying anything.

For a groomer, the practical step is a checkbox on the booking form: "Text me reminders and offers" with the number, not pre-ticked, and not required to book. Whether a plain appointment reminder counts as telemarketing is a legal question this guide does not answer. Ask an attorney before sending promotions.

## How Do You Handle Opt-Outs?

Honor them fast. The rule says a person can revoke consent by any reasonable method and lists replies such as stop, quit, end, revoke, opt out, cancel and unsubscribe as reasonable per se. You must honor the request within ten business days.

The FCC voted on September 30, 2026 to adopt Order 26-67, which lets senders designate an exclusive opt-out method if they disclose it in each text. A law firm summary (secondary source) says it takes effect 30 days after publication in the Federal Register and that the date of publication was not given. Until you are sure, accept any clear opt-out. Add "Reply STOP to opt out" to promotional texts.

## What Should a Reminder Say?

Keep it short and put the facts a client needs. Include the business name, the pet's name, the date and time, and a way to confirm or reschedule. Example text, written for this guide:

"Hi Sam, this is Paws Grooming. Biscuit is booked for Sat 14 Nov at 10:00. Reply C to confirm or call us to change. Reply STOP to opt out."

Send the reminder with enough notice for you to refill the slot. No source gives the best timing for groomers, so test one reminder two days before and a second the day before, then compare no-shows. ## What Does It Save? A Worked Example

All numbers are **assumptions**. Say a grooming shop books 120 appointments a month, the average ticket is $75, and 12 clients miss their slot. After adding reminders, assume 9 miss.

| | Before | After (assumption) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Appointments booked | 120 | 120 |
| Missed appointments | 12 | 9 |
| Revenue lost at $75 | $900 | $675 |
| Software cost (MoeGo Basic) | $0 | $49 |

That is $225 recovered for $49, a net of $176 a month. Break-even is under one saved appointment ($49 divided by $75 is 0.65). If your reminders save none, you have lost $49. ## Should Groomers Text Promotions Too?

Only after reminders work and only to clients who opted in. Two uses fit grooming: a rebooking nudge when the usual interval has passed, and a short note about a cancelled slot. Do not text more often than you can keep up with.

## FAQ

### Do text reminders reduce grooming no-shows?
No grooming-specific study was found. In 8 healthcare trials, text reminders raised attendance from 678 to 773 per 1,000 compared with none. Track your own no-show rate for a month before and after.

### How much does SMS reminder software cost for a groomer?
MoeGo's mobile grooming plans start at $49 a month with 200 SMS per van. Gingr's Spa plan is $109 a month, with two-way texting as an add-on whose price is not listed.

### Do I need consent to text clients?
For autodialed telemarketing texts to cell phones, the FCC rule requires prior express written consent. Ask an attorney whether a plain reminder falls under it.

### How do I handle STOP replies?
Treat stop, quit, end, revoke, opt out, cancel and unsubscribe as opt-outs and process them within ten business days.

### What is the average grooming no-show rate?
No source found publishes one. Count your own for a month.
