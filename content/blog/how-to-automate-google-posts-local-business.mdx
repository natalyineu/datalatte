You can automate Google Business Profile posts three ways: schedule them inside Google, use a scheduler such as Buffer, or build a connection through the Business Profile API. For most small businesses the first two are enough. Google's help page confirms posts can be scheduled for a future time or set to repeat weekly, monthly or on a custom schedule, and that posts older than six months are archived unless a date range is set.

## Which method should you pick?

| Method | Setup effort | Cost | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google's built-in scheduling | Minutes | Free | One or a few locations |
| Third-party scheduler (Buffer) | About 30 minutes | Check the tool's current pricing | Teams already scheduling social posts |
| Business Profile API | Developer work and Google approval | Developer time | Agencies and many locations |

Start with the built-in option. Move to a scheduler only if you need one calendar for several channels.

## What does Google allow you to post?

Per [Google's posts help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7662907), there are three types:

- **Update:** news with an optional photo, video and action button.
- **Offer:** requires a title, dates and times, and adds a View Offer button automatically.
- **Event:** requires a title, start and end dates and times.

Content rules matter for automation. Google advises against auto-generated or distracting content and recommends posts that look professional and add value. Posts with a phone number in the description may be rejected if the number cannot be verified as connected to the business. Review each queued post instead of letting AI fill a calendar unseen.

## How do you schedule posts with Buffer?

Buffer's [Google Business Profile help](https://support.buffer.com/article/557-using-google-business-profiles-with-buffer) states these requirements and limits:

1. The profile must be verified, and you connect while logged into the linked Google account.
2. It supports What's New, Offer and Event posts, with one optional call-to-action button.
3. Each post takes one image (JPG, PNG or HEIC), recommended at 720 by 720 pixels, minimum 250 by 250.
4. Maximum 1,500 characters, 50 posts per 24 hours.
5. Not supported: phone numbers in text, videos and GIFs, WebP images, alt text, mentions and clickable hashtags.

Buffer also does not offer Google Business Profile analytics, so keep checking Insights inside Google.

## When is the API worth it?

The API suits agencies managing many locations. Google's [API prerequisites](https://developers.google.com/my-business/content/prereqs) require a verified, active profile (60 plus days), a website on the profile, and a request for Basic API Access through the contact form. A quota of 0 queries per minute means pending; 300 means approved.

Per the [posts API reference](https://developers.google.com/my-business/content/posts-data), you can create Event, Call to Action and Offer posts through the localPosts endpoint, but Product posts cannot be created through the API. A one-location business rarely needs this much effort.

## What should a 12-week calendar look like?

This is an example plan, and the 20 minutes a week is an **assumption** about effort, not a measured figure or benchmark. The aim is a fixed rhythm once photos are ready:

- **Weekly:** one Update (a recent job, a team tip, a seasonal reminder) with a real photo.
- **Monthly:** one Offer with an end date, so it expires cleanly.
- **When relevant:** an Event with exact dates.
- **Always:** one clear action button, and the same name, address and service wording as your profile.

Batch-write all 12 weeks in one sitting, schedule them, then spend a few minutes each week replying to reviews. ## Do automated posts help you rank?

Be careful with promises. [Google's local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091) says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and popularity, with prominence meaning how well-known a business is. It does not mention posts. So automate posting to keep your profile current and give searchers an offer or reason to act, and measure calls, direction requests and website clicks in Insights rather than expecting a rankings jump.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will scheduling get my profile suspended?**
A: Scheduling itself is a documented Google feature. Policy issues come from the content, such as unverifiable phone numbers or disallowed material.

**Q: How often should I post?**
A: Google does not publish a required frequency. Pick a rhythm you can sustain, such as weekly.

**Q: How long should a post be?**
A: The Buffer limit is 1,500 characters. Google's help page does not state a character limit, so keep posts short and put the action first.

**Q: Can I post videos or GIFs through a scheduler?**
A: Buffer does not support them for Google Business Profile. Google's native posts can show videos.

**Q: Do multi-location businesses need separate posts?**
A: Posts belong to each location profile, so tailor offers and photos to each branch.
