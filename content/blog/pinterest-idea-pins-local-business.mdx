Idea Pins no longer exist as a separate format. Pinterest's own FAQ says "Idea Pins are now Pins": since the May 2023 merge, a local business creates a standard Pin, which can be a video up to five minutes long. Pinterest reported 661 million global monthly users on October 29, 2026, so the audience is large, but a local shop only benefits if people nearby search for what it sells. Use video Pins for how-to, before-and-after and seasonal ideas, and send them to a page that lets people book or find you.

This guide explains what changed, how local businesses use video Pins, and how to target Pinterest ads by postal code.

## Are Idea Pins Still a Thing?

No. TechCrunch reported on May 11, 2023 that Pinterest was merging Pin and Idea Pin formats into one creation flow, with video Pins, static Pins and tools like stickers and overlay text in one place. Pinterest's community FAQ states that Idea Pins are now Pins and that previously created Idea Pins are preserved and shown as videos.

Features once limited to Idea Pins, such as music, product tagging and stickers, became available for video and image content. Links, scheduling and post-publish editing came to everyone. Uploading several images or videos on iOS and Android stitches them into a video Pin, and Pinterest says it is deprecating pages. Advertisers can still create Idea Ads, Video Ads, Collection Ads and Carousel Ads.

## What Should a Local Business Post as a Video Pin?

Post things people save and search for: a how-to, a style gallery, a seasonal menu or a before-and-after. A groomer can show a coat-care routine. A café can show a seasonal drink recipe. Pinterest does not publish a ranking of what works for local businesses, so test a few formats.

Add a link now that links are allowed for everyone. Send the Pin to a page with your address, hours and a booking or order button. Pinterest community staff gave 2:3 (1000 by 1500 pixels) as the ideal Pin ratio, while another Pin specs page recommends 9:16 for full-bleed video, so test both. ## Can You Target People Near Your Shop?

With ads, yes. Pinterest lists four location levels: country, region, metro area and postal code. Postal codes let you target specific cities and towns, and most countries in its table support them. Metro targeting exists only in some countries, including the US, UK, Canada and Australia. The default location is your business country.

Organic Pins have no local targeting, so your audience will be wide. That is the main reason to pair organic Pins with a small ad test. For costs and setup, see [Pinterest ads cost](/blog/pinterest-ads-cost-2026) and [how to set up Pinterest ads](/blog/how-to-set-up-pinterest-ads-local-business).

## Is It Worth It? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for a home-decor shop.

| Input | Assumption |

Four customers at $80 brings $320 against $180 of time, a small profit under these assumptions. Pins keep circulating after you post, so later months may add visits without new work. Nothing guarantees that. Track clicks to a page with its own address, and stop if the numbers stay low for three months.

## FAQ

### What replaced Idea Pins?
Standard Pins. Pinterest's FAQ says Idea Pins are now Pins, and video Pins can be up to five minutes.

### Can I still see my old Idea Pins?
Yes. Pinterest says previously created Idea Pins are preserved and distributed as videos. Editing one to add a link removes past reactions and comments on that Pin.

### Do I need a business account?
Pinterest says links and scheduling are now open to all Pinners. You need an account to run ads.

### Can I target my town?
Ads can target by postal code in most countries. Organic Pins cannot be limited by location.

### How many people use Pinterest?
Pinterest reported 661 million global monthly active users for Q3 2026, up 10% on the year.
