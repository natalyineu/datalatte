No public South African CTV price list was found, so this guide gives no CPM and no budget promise.

## How Many South Africans Can See Streaming TV Ads?

The best public numbers are partial. Fabric Data, a research firm, reports in a July 2025 article that 42% of South African households own a smart TV, versus 7% with a cable box or receiver. It also reports 95% smartphone possession and says only 14% of households have a fixed internet connection.

Pay TV penetration is 39%, against 69% for online content, and cord-cutters grew from 12% to 14% between the third quarter of 2024 and the first quarter of 2025. These are survey figures from a data vendor, so they are secondary.

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report adds YouTube: 26.9 million ad reach, 52.1% of internet users, out of 51.7 million internet users and 64.9 million people. It does not say how much of that is viewed on a TV.

## Who Sells Streaming TV Ads in South Africa?

Three kinds of seller are visible in public sources.

- **YouTube through Google Ads.** Google's January 2026 Demand Gen announcement says campaigns that include TV screens drive an average of 7% more conversions at the same return, from an internal 2025 global experiment. It lists Shoppable CTV but gives no country list. Whether TV screens can be selected for a South African account was not verified.
- **SABC+.** The broadcaster's streaming service passed 3 million registered users on 3 August 2026, and says advertisers get first-party data on logged-in, consented viewers. It does not state how many of those are active, or any ad price.
- **DStv Media Sales.** Trade press reports it sells advertising across DStv, linear channels and GOtv, and took over ad sales for the Paramount channels from 1 October 2024. A small business would normally need to ask for a quote.

None of these sources lists a rate card.

## What Does Google Say About Running Ads on TV Screens?

Google's own pages are the source to check before planning. The January 2026 announcement says Demand Gen supports Shoppable CTV and that some launches require a Google representative to activate. It says nothing about eligibility by country, minimum spend or device types.

A separate Demand Gen help page lists the standard surfaces as YouTube, Discover, Gmail and the Google Display Network, with no TV placement named. So a South African advertiser should open the campaign setup in their own account, see whether a TV screens option appears, and ask Google support if it does not. Do not assume it is available.

## Which Advertising Rules Apply?

The Advertising Regulatory Board describes itself as South Africa's self-regulation body and administers the Code of Advertising Practice. Consumers, competitors and others can file complaints about misleading, offensive or unethical advertising, and the board adjudicates them.

Its page shows no separate rules for streaming or influencers. Secondary sources, dating from 2019, say the code's social media appendix requires paid or gifted posts to be labelled with markers such as #AD or #Sponsored. That detail is unverified for 2026, so read the current code on the ARB site before you launch.

This guide does not cover broadcast licensing, tax or consumer-protection law. Take legal advice for those.

## Should a Local Business Try It? A Worked Example

The inputs below are **assumptions**, not South African prices.

- Monthly test budget (assumption): R5,000
- Share for a TV-screen or streaming test (assumption): 20%, so R1,000
- Share kept for Google Search and a Business Profile (assumption): 80%, so R4,000
- Goal (assumption): a tracked phone call or booking on a dedicated landing page

Because no price data exists, request a written quote or minimum spend from any seller first. If the minimum is above R1,000, skip streaming TV this quarter and keep spending on search, where intent is clearer. Judge the test by calls and bookings that mention TV, not by views.

## FAQ

### Is CTV advertising available in South Africa?

Streaming TV inventory exists through YouTube, SABC+ and DStv Media Sales. Whether Google's TV screens placement is open to South African accounts was not verified.

### How much does CTV advertising cost in South Africa?

No public price data was found, so none is quoted. Ask each seller for a written quote and minimum spend.

### Which South Africans stream on a TV?

Fabric Data reports smart TVs in 42% of households and a cord-cutter share of 14% in Q1 2025. These are survey figures, not a count of ad impressions.

### What body regulates advertising?

The Advertising Regulatory Board administers the Code of Advertising Practice and handles complaints about misleading advertising.

### Should I start with CTV?

Usually not. Start with search and your Business Profile, and test streaming TV with a small share once you know your cost per lead.
