To run an Instagram Story ad for a local business, create a campaign in Meta Ads Manager, keep the Stories placement, upload a full-screen vertical 9:16 video or image, and add a call-to-action button such as Book now, Get directions or Call now. There is no swipe-up link: Instagram retired it in 2021, and ads now use a button.

Meta does not publish a cost for Story ads alone. LocaliQ's September 2026 Facebook benchmarks, which cover all placements, show a $0.60 average click for traffic campaigns and $1.80 for lead campaigns. Illustrative examples below are labelled as assumptions.

## Is There Still a Swipe-Up Link on Instagram Stories?

No. TechCrunch reported in August 2021 that Instagram was removing the swipe-up link from Stories from August 30 of that year and replacing it with the Link sticker, which can be resized, restyled and placed anywhere on a Story. Viewers can react and reply to a Link sticker.

That report covers organic Stories, not ads. For paid Stories, Meta's ad specification lists call-to-action buttons instead, so build your ad around a button. Earlier versions of this article claimed that swipe-up links raised conversions by 35% and cut cost per acquisition by 23%. Those figures had no source and have been removed.

## How Do You Set Up an Instagram Story Ad?

The steps below follow the general Ads Manager flow. Menu names change, so check your own screen.

1. **Pick an objective** that matches what you want: traffic for website visits, leads for enquiries, or awareness for reach.
2. **Set the audience.** Use a location around your business, and add age or interest filters only if you have a reason.
3. **Choose placements.** Keep Instagram Stories on, and check how the ad looks in the preview.
4. **Upload a vertical creative** and write short text.
5. **Add a call to action** and the destination: your website, booking page or a phone number.
6. **Set a daily budget** and a start and end date, then publish and review after a few days.

## What Creative Specs Do Story Ads Need?

Meta's Ads Guide for Instagram Stories video lists these specs for awareness ads:

| Spec | Meta's stated value |
|---|---|
| Ratio | 9:16, full-screen vertical |
| Resolution | 1440 x 2560 pixels |
| Length | 1 second to 60 minutes; under 16 seconds plays in full, 16 seconds or longer may be split into up to three Stories cards |
| File size | Up to 4 GB |
| Safe zone | Keep about 14% of the top, 35% of the bottom and 6% of each side free of text, logos and key creative |
| Primary text | 125 characters |

Meta's separate help pages gave different length limits when compared, and two of its help pages returned no content when checked. Preview your ad in Ads Manager before publishing, because that is the only place that shows what your account accepts.

## Which Call to Action Should a Local Business Pick?

Meta's Ads Guide lists many options, and says availability depends on your industry. The ones that suit a local business include Book now, Get directions, Call now, See menu, Order now, Send message, Request time, Get quote and Learn more.

Match the button to the next step you want. A salon that books online should use Book now and link straight to the booking page. A cafe that wants foot traffic can use Get directions. A trades business may prefer Call now or Get quote.

Test one change at a time. Our guides for [salon booking Story ads](/blog/instagram-story-ads-for-salons-bookings) and [Instagram ads for local shops](/blog/instagram-ads-for-local-shops) show how two different businesses apply this.

## Do Video Story Ads Beat Images?

Earlier versions of this article said video raises click-through rate by 30%. That claim had no source and was removed. No source opened for this article compares video and image Story ads, so test both on your own audience.

A practical test: run one image and one video with the same text, button and budget for 7 days. Compare cost per result in Ads Manager, not likes. Keep the winner and test a new variation against it.

## What Do Story Ads Cost? A Worked Example

LocaliQ's September 2026 Facebook benchmarks show averages for all placements. Beauty and Personal Care leads average a $2.97 click and 5.63% conversion; Health and Fitness leads a $1.84 click and 7.98%; Restaurants and Food traffic a $0.45 click. They are not Story-only figures.

The example uses **assumptions** with the Beauty and Personal Care lead averages.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
|---|---|
| Daily budget | $10 for 14 days |
| Total spend | $140 |
| Cost per click | $2.97 |
| Clicks | about 47 |
| Conversion rate | 5.63% |
| Leads | about 2.7 |
| Cost per lead | about $52 |

At those inputs, a salon that closes one in two leads at a $90 first visit spends about $104 per new client. Your real numbers will differ, so judge the ad after a test, not before. ## FAQ

### Do I need a certain number of followers to run Story ads?

No source opened for this article sets a follower minimum. Story ads are bought in Meta Ads Manager. Check that your Instagram account is connected to a Facebook Page first.

### How long should a Story ad be?

Meta says videos under 16 seconds play in full, and longer ones may be split into up to three cards. Keep the offer and button visible inside the safe zone.

### Where does the link go now that swipe-up is gone?

Paid Story ads use a call-to-action button with a destination you set when you build the ad.

### Can I retarget people who visited my website?

Yes, with a Meta Pixel and a custom audience, but this article did not verify retargeting results. See our [lookalike audiences guide](/blog/facebook-lookalike-audiences-local-business).

### Are Story ads better than Feed or Reels ads?

No source opened for this article compares placements. Compare cost per result across placements in your own account.
