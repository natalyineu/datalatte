Yes, an AI agent can post to Instagram and TikTok automatically, but each platform sets conditions. Instagram's API publishes only for professional (business or creator) accounts. TikTok's Content Posting API keeps posts from unaudited apps private until the app passes an audit. Most local businesses are better off using a scheduler such as Buffer, which publishes to both for $5 per channel a month, and using AI only to draft captions.

An agent here means a workflow that drafts a post, waits for your approval, then sends it to a scheduler or the platform API. Approval matters because a wrong price or a wrong offer goes live under your name.

## Can an AI Agent Post to Instagram Automatically?

Meta's Instagram content publishing guide covers professional accounts. Posts, Reels, Stories and carousels can be published through the API, with images in JPEG only and media hosted on a publicly accessible server at publish time. Meta says accounts are limited to 100 API-published posts in 24 hours, although another line on the same page says 50, so check the limit with the content_publishing_limit endpoint.

Personal profiles are not supported for automatic publishing. Buffer's pricing page says personal Instagram profiles get reminder notifications only.

## Can an AI Agent Post to TikTok Automatically?

TikTok's Content Posting API offers Direct Post for videos and photos. The documentation says all content posted by unaudited clients is restricted to private viewing mode, and the client must undergo an audit to lift that. The app also needs approval for the video.publish scope, and photo posts require a photo URL on a verified domain.

For a small business building its own integration, that audit is a real barrier. Buffer lists automatic publishing for TikTok business profiles, so a scheduler is the shorter route.

## How Do You Build the Workflow?

A simple agent has four steps: collect the brief (offer, photo, date), draft captions with an AI model, send the draft to you for approval, then pass the approved post to the scheduler. No-code builders such as n8n or Make can run it; see [n8n AI agent workflows](/blog/n8n-ai-agent-workflow-local-business) and [Make.com workflows](/blog/make-com-ai-agent-workflow-local-business).

Keep a human approval step. Do not let the agent invent prices, hours or reviews. For the bigger picture read [AI social media management](/blog/ai-social-media-management-local-business) and [what AI agents are](/blog/ai-agents-for-local-business-what-they-are-and-how-they-work).

## FAQ

### Can I post to Instagram automatically for free?

Yes. Buffer Free allows up to 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts per channel, and refills are allowed, according to its pricing page.

### Can a personal Instagram account use auto-posting?

Not through the API or Buffer. Meta's guide covers professional accounts and Buffer lists reminder notifications only for personal profiles.

### Why are my TikTok API posts private?

TikTok says content from unaudited API clients is restricted to private viewing. The app must pass an audit to lift the limit.

### Should AI write all my captions?

Use it for drafts and review each one. Accuracy of offers and hours is your responsibility.
