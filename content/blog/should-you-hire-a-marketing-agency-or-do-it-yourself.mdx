Do it yourself while your marketing is basic and your budget is small: a Google Business Profile, review requests and a simple email list. Hire help when one channel, usually paid search, needs more skill or time than you have. Published price surveys are thin and mostly come from agencies, so ask for a written quote.

The best public data is a SE Ranking and Duda survey of 260 agencies, published in December 2024. It covers SEO agencies, not every marketing service, and the authors say the sample may be too small for statistical significance.

## How Much Does a Marketing Agency Cost?

It depends on the service and the region. In the SE Ranking survey, the monthly retainer bands for SEO were under $500 (30% of agencies), $500 to $1,000 (34%), $1,000 to $2,000 (20%), $2,000 to $5,000 (13%) and over $5,000 (2%). The most common hourly rate was $50 to $100.

North American agencies charged more than European ones. Over $2,000 a month was 22% of agencies in the US and Canada and 9% in Europe.

Paid ads are priced differently. One UK agency blog (secondary) gives 10% to 20% of monthly ad spend, or a flat £750 to £3,000 a month, plus a one-off setup fee of £500 to £2,500. These are an agency's own claims, not audited data. No source found publishes a full-service average for local businesses, so treat any single price you read, including old figures, with caution.

## What Does DIY Cost You?

Mainly time. Many tools have free or low-cost plans, such as the Google Business Profile, which has no fee. We have not verified a general figure for hours per month, so measure your own for two weeks. Write down every task: replying to reviews, posting, checking ad terms, sending emails.

Then multiply by what an hour of your work earns. If marketing pulls you off paid jobs, the hidden cost can exceed an agency fee. If you have quiet hours anyway, DIY costs little. Our [free marketing tools list](/blog/free-marketing-tools-for-small-business) shows what you can run without paying.

| Total monthly cost | £730 | £1,255 |
| Bookings per month | 4 | 5 |
| Cost per booking | £183 | £251 |

On these numbers DIY wins unless the agency gets noticeably more bookings, or the owner's hour is worth much more. Raise the booking gap to 7 for the agency and its cost per booking drops to about £179. The point is to run your own numbers, not to trust these.

## What Should You Check Before Hiring?

Ask who owns the ad account. Google lets a business link a manager account and remove it later. Google's help says you need administrative access to unlink, so keep at least one admin login in your name. Ask for the fee model in writing, the contract length and the exit terms.

Ask what reports you will get, and how the agency counts a lead: a click, a call or a booking. A Google Partner badge shows an agency meets Google's current thresholds, including a 70% optimization score, $10,000 of ad spend over 90 days and certified staff. It does not predict results for a small local account. Our guide to [choosing a Google Ads agency](/blog/local-business-google-ads-agency-how-to-choose) goes deeper.

## When Should You Switch From DIY to an Agency?

Consider switching when one of these is true. You have run a channel for 3 months and cannot tell what it earns. Your ad spend is high enough that a 10% to 20% fee is small next to the possible waste. You cannot keep up with replies and posting. Work out the budget first with our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide).

A middle path is a one-off audit or setup by a specialist, then you run it. Before that, make sure the basics are done: a complete [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist), and a plan to earn repeat custom, as covered in why [word of mouth alone is not enough](/blog/why-word-of-mouth-alone-wont-grow-your-local-business-anymore).

## FAQ

### Can I hire an agency on a £500 or $500 budget?

You can hire a freelancer or an SEO agency, since 30% of surveyed agencies charged under $500 a month. For ads, one UK source says most agencies will not take accounts under £500 a month of ad spend, before fees.

### Is it cheaper to do marketing myself?

Usually in cash, not always in time. Count your hours at what they would earn elsewhere.

### What do agencies charge for Google Ads?

One UK blog gives 10% to 20% of ad spend or £750 to £3,000 flat. It is a secondary source, so ask for quotes.

### Who should own the ad account?

You. Keep an admin login and link the agency as a manager, so you can remove access if you leave.

### Does a Google Partner badge mean a good agency?

It means thresholds were met on spend, optimization score and certification. It does not show fit for a small local account.
