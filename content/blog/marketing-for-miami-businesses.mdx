A Miami small business should start with a complete Google Business Profile, a steady flow of recent reviews and a small, tightly targeted Google Ads campaign. Add Spanish ad copy only if Spanish searches show up in your data. Paid clicks are not cheap everywhere: WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 US benchmarks run from $2.05 per click for restaurants to $6.17 for health and fitness. They are national figures, not Miami figures.

## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Small Business?

LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put the all-industry average at $5.42 per click, a 6.64% click-through rate, an 8.18% conversion rate and $66.69 per lead. The spread by industry is wide. Click costs depend on what you sell, not only on where you are.

| Industry (US, 2026) | Avg. CPC | Avg. CVR | Avg. cost per lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Restaurants and Food | $2.05 | 8.05% | $30.57 |
| Beauty and Personal Care | $4.62 | 10.35% | $39.25 |
| Animals and Pets | $4.06 | 16.22% | $31.50 |
| Health and Fitness | $6.17 | 6.94% | $67.36 |
| All industries | $5.42 | 8.18% | $66.69 |

LocaliQ does not publish a Miami-only figure in this report. Use the table as a range and replace it with your own account data after the first month.

## How Do You Show Up in Google Maps Results in Miami?

Google says local results rely mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. Relevance is how well your profile matches the search. Distance is how far you are from the searcher. Prominence is how well known you are, which Google ties to links to your site and the number of reviews. Google also states that you cannot request or pay for a better local ranking.

That makes the profile the first job. Fill in every category, service, hour and photo field, keep holiday hours current and answer messages. ## How Many Reviews Do You Need?

BrightLocal's 2026 Local Consumer Review Survey asked 1,002 US adults. It found that 97% read reviews for local businesses and that 47% will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews. Another 74% look for reviews from the last three months, and 68% will only use businesses rated four stars or higher.

The same survey reports that 89% expect owners to reply to reviews. Google lets you ask customers for reviews with a link or QR code, but it bans rewards for reviews. For the full process, see our guide to [automating Google review requests](/blog/how-to-automate-google-review-requests).

## Should Miami Ads Be in Spanish?

Only if your customers search in Spanish, and your Search terms report will tell you. Google Ads Help says the campaign language setting for Search is being removed from September 2026. Ads will instead be matched to the language of the search term, with priority for an ad and landing page in that language.

In practice, if you want Spanish-speaking customers, write Spanish ads and a Spanish landing page. Do not translate the English page word for word. Keep the location and language settings consistent, as Google advises, and use a radius of at least 1 km around your address.

## What Should You Know Before Sending Texts or Emails?

Florida's Telephone Solicitation Act (section 501.059) requires prior express written consent for automated sales calls and messages. A checked box counts as an electronic signature under the statute. The law allows a private claim for the greater of actual damages or $500, and up to three times that for willful violations.

This is a summary, not legal advice. Collect a clear opt-in at the register or booking form, store the record and give an easy way to stop. Our guide to [SMS marketing for coffee shops](/blog/sms-marketing-for-coffee-shops-how-to-start) covers the setup.

## When Should a Miami Business Plan Its Campaigns?

Plan around dates you can check. Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30, according to the National Hurricane Center, so have a closure and reopening message ready on your profile and social pages. Art Basel Miami Beach and Miami Art Week are listed for December 4 to 6, 2026 by the Miami and Miami Beach visitor site.

Neither date is a promise of extra sales for your shop. Check last year's own numbers before you commit budget to an event week. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) helps you set a monthly split.

## FAQ

### How much should a Miami small business spend on marketing?

There is no published Miami figure. Start with an amount you can lose while you learn, such as the $600 in the example, and track calls and bookings. Our [budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) explains how to split it.

### Is Google Ads or Google Business Profile better for a new business?

Start with the free profile, because it supports every other channel. Add ads when the profile is complete and you have a landing page that matches the search. Google says you cannot pay for a better local ranking, so ads and Maps results are separate.

### Do I need a Spanish website?

Not by default. Check your Search terms report and customer questions. If Spanish queries bring real calls or bookings, a Spanish landing page and ads are worth testing.

### Can I give a discount for a Google review?

No. Google's policy says incentivized reviews are not allowed and will be removed. Ask in person, by receipt, QR code or link.
