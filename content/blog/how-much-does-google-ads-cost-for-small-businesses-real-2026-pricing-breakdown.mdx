Google Ads has no published minimum spend. You set a daily budget and pay per click, so the real question is what a click costs in your industry. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put the all-industry average at $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead. Industry averages range from $2.05 for restaurants to $9.87 for legal services. No source publishes a typical monthly bill for a small business, so this guide shows how to calculate yours.

All four figures come from LocaliQ's 2026 search advertising benchmarks (last updated June 1, 2026), built from LocaliQ customers' Google Ads and Microsoft Ads campaigns. They describe accounts managed on that platform, not every advertiser.

## What Does a Click Cost in Different Industries?

The same $1,000 buys very different traffic depending on the category. The table divides $1,000 by each industry's 2026 average click cost. The click counts are arithmetic, not forecasts.

| Industry (LocaliQ 2026) | Cost per click | Conversion rate | Cost per lead | Clicks per $1,000 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Restaurants and Food | $2.05 | 8.05% | $30.57 | about 488 |
| Animals and Pets | $4.06 | 16.22% | $31.50 | about 246 |
| Automotive: Repair, Service and Parts | $4.35 | 15.51% | $29.96 | about 230 |
| Beauty and Personal Care | $4.62 | 10.35% | $39.25 | about 216 |
| Health and Fitness | $6.17 | 6.94% | $67.36 | about 162 |
| Home and Home Improvement | $8.33 | 8.05% | $90.92 | about 120 |
| Attorneys and Legal Services | $9.87 | 5.55% | $131.63 | about 101 |

Cost per lead depends on the conversion rate as much as the click price. Pets cost $4.06 per click, yet leads cost $31.50 because 16.22% of clicks convert. Legal clicks cost more than twice as much and convert at 5.55%.

## How Does Google Charge for a Daily Budget?

You choose an average daily budget per campaign and can change it at any time. Google's help page says actual spend on any given day may exceed the average daily budget by up to 2 times. Monthly charges are capped at 30.4 (the average number of days in a month) multiplied by the average daily budget. A $10 daily budget therefore caps monthly charges at about $304, which is Google's own example.

That cap is why a daily budget is a better planning tool than a monthly figure. Set the daily amount, then check the account weekly in the first month. Google's help page does not mention a minimum spend, and it does not say that you pay only for clicks, so check the billing terms on your own account.

## What Does the Auction Do to the Price?

Click prices are not fixed. Google describes six factors that decide whether and where an ad appears: your bid, the quality of your ads and landing page, the expected impact of your assets and other ad formats, Ad Rank thresholds, the context of the search, and how competitive the auction is.

Google also states that relevant keywords, ads and assets can help an advertiser win a better position at a lower price, even against competitors with higher bids. For a small business, that means a tight local campaign with matching ad text and a clear landing page can pay less per click than a broad one. Google does not publish by how much.

## What Does a Budget Buy? A Worked Example

All inputs here are **assumptions** built on the all-industry averages. They are not a forecast for your business.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $1,000 |
| Cost per click | $5.42 |
| Clicks | about 184 |
| Conversion rate | 8.18% |
| Conversions (calls, forms, bookings) | about 15 |
| Cost per conversion | about $66 |
| Share of conversions that become paying customers | 40% |
| Paying customers | about 6 |
| Cost per paying customer | about $165 |

The 40% close rate is an invented placeholder. Replace it with your own number. If an average customer is worth $120 on the first visit and does not come back, this account loses money. If the customer is worth $600 over a year, it can pay. That lifetime value is the number that sets your maximum cost per customer, not the click price.

## How Much Should You Start With?

No source can name a correct starting budget for your business. A practical approach is to work backwards from what a customer is worth.

1. Estimate the profit from one new customer over a year.
2. Decide how much of that profit you would pay to win the customer.
3. Divide by your close rate to get the maximum cost per lead.
4. Compare that to the industry cost per lead in the table above.

If your maximum cost per lead is below the benchmark for your industry, the campaign will struggle at average prices. Either improve the conversion rate, narrow the targeting, or put the money into free channels first. ## FAQ

### How much does Google Ads cost per month for a small business?
No source publishes a typical monthly spend. You choose the budget. At the 2026 all-industry average of $5.42 per click, $1,000 buys roughly 184 clicks.

### What is the average cost per click on Google Ads in 2026?
LocaliQ reports $5.42 across industries, with averages from $2.05 (Restaurants and Food) to $9.87 (Attorneys and Legal Services).

### Is there a minimum budget for Google Ads?
Google's help page on managing spend does not state a minimum. It explains that daily spend can reach 2 times the average daily budget, with a monthly cap of 30.4 times the daily budget.

### What is a good cost per lead?
LocaliQ's 2026 all-industry average is $66.69. Industry averages range from about $30 to $132 in the table above. A good number for you is one below what a customer is worth after your close rate.

### Why do two businesses pay different prices for the same keyword?
Google's auction weighs bids, ad and landing page quality, expected asset impact, thresholds, context and competition, so the same keyword can cost different advertisers different amounts.
