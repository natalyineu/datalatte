A pet groomer can run connected TV (CTV) ads on streaming services, but it is an awareness channel, not a booking channel. Disney Campaign Manager, the self-serve tool for Hulu, Disney+ and ESPN streaming, has been described with a $500 minimum per campaign, and it lets you target by location and interest. Search ads and your Google Business Profile still catch people ready to book, so test CTV only after those work.

This guide covers what CTV can and cannot do for a grooming salon, what is published about cost, and how to check if it pays back. Figures are sourced at the end. CPM rates for CTV are not published by the platforms, so none are quoted here.

## What Is CTV Advertising for a Pet Groomer?

CTV ads are video ads that play on internet-connected TVs inside streaming apps. They are 15 or 30 second spots that viewers usually cannot skip. For a groomer the appeal is reach: the American Pet Products Association counts 95 million US households with a pet (2025 National Pet Owners Survey), and many of them watch streaming.

The weakness is intent. Someone watching a show is not looking for a groomer. A CTV ad plants your name, and the booking happens later, often through a search for your business or a Maps listing. See the full [CTV advertising guide for local business owners](/blog/what-is-ctv-advertising-guide-for-local-business-owners-2026) for the basics.

## What Does CTV Cost for a Small Business?

There is no published rate card. What is documented:

| Platform | Published fact | Source quality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Disney Campaign Manager (Hulu, Disney+, ESPN) | $500 minimum per campaign, set at launch of the self-serve tool; targeting by location, interest and program genre | Disney's own page; minimum may have changed |
| Google Ads / YouTube | Video campaigns can be shown on a "TV screens" device type, with its own bid adjustment | Google Ads Help, 2018 announcement |
| Roku Ads Manager | Guides cite about $500, Roku lists no official minimum we could verify | Secondary only |

CPM (cost per thousand views) is not published by these platforms and varies by auction, so check your own quote. Our article on [real CTV cost data](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data) collects what can be sourced. A creative also costs money: you need a finished video file.

## How Do You Target Pet Owners Locally?

Disney Campaign Manager says you can target by location, interest and program genre, and mentions 210 DMAs, which are whole TV markets. A DMA covers many towns, so a single salon will pay to reach people far outside its drive time. No Disney page we opened confirms zip-code targeting or a pet-owner segment, so ask before you assume.

Google lets you show YouTube video ads on TV screens and set a bid adjustment for them, with the usual location settings of a Google Ads campaign. That is a way to keep the radius tighter. Whichever you choose, measure with a vanity phone number or a QR code, because a CTV view does not click.

## Is It Worth It? A Worked Example

Every input here is an **assumption**. Use your own numbers.

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign budget (the reported minimum) | $500 |
| Average groom price | $80 |
| Visits per year per client | 6 |
| First-year value per client | $480 |
| Clients needed to break even on revenue | 2 ($500 / $480 = 1.04, rounded up) |
| Gross margin on a groom | 50% (assumption) |
| Clients needed to cover the ad on margin | 3 ($500 / $240 = 2.1, rounded up) |

On these inputs, three new regulars in a year pay back one $500 campaign. That is a low bar if the ad is shown in your area, and a high one if most of the audience lives out of reach of your van or shop. Track new clients who say "I saw you on TV".

## Should You Choose CTV, Search or Social?

For most groomers the order is: complete Google Business Profile, a modest search campaign, then social. See [Google Business Profile for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-for-pet-groomers) and [Google Ads for pet groomers](/blog/google-ads-for-pet-groomers-guide). CTV suits a salon with several locations or a mobile route covering a wide area, where awareness helps and a $500 test is a small share of a larger budget.

## FAQ

### How much does a CTV ad cost for a pet groomer?

Disney Campaign Manager has been reported with a $500 minimum per campaign. CPM rates are not published, so total reach for that budget depends on the auction. Ask the platform for an estimate before you pay.

### Can I advertise on YouTube TV?

Google Ads can show YouTube video ads on TV screens. We could not verify that YouTube TV, the live TV service, is sold through the same self-serve tool, so check with Google before planning around it.

### Can I target only households with pets?

Disney says it supports interest targeting. No source we opened guarantees pet-owner households in your zip code, so treat that claim with caution and ask the platform.

### How do I track results?

Use a dedicated phone number or QR code on the ad, a short "tell us where you saw us" question at booking, and a branded-search check in Google Search Console.

### Do I need a professional video?

You need a finished file that meets the platform's specs. It can be simple, but must pass review. See [CTV creative specs](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026).
