CTV ads for pet groomers work best as an awareness channel, not a booking channel. Connected TV spots play inside streaming apps. The clearest published price point is for free ad-supported streaming, where [Strategus](https://www.strategus.com/blog/free-ad-supported-tv-fast-advertising) reports Pluto TV advertising typically starts around $10 to $30 CPM (cost per thousand impressions). No source reviewed gives a pet-groomer-specific CTV rate, so get a quote from the platform before committing. Test CTV only after search and your Google Business Profile are producing bookings.

## What Does CTV Cost for a Small Pet Business?

Connected TV ads are video spots shown on internet-connected TVs. Ad-supported video-on-demand (AVOD) services are, in the [Paramount Ads glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions), free or lower-cost streaming services supported by ads. Price depends on the service, the audience and the time slot, so the CPM range above is a starting point, not a rate card.

Local TV gives a different reference point. [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-tv-ad-cost-2026), citing 2026 guidance from Comcast Advertising and National Media Spots, puts a local TV ad at a few hundred to several thousand dollars per airing. That figure covers broadcast and cable spots rather than streaming, and the page notes that production is a separate cost.

Budget for the video file too. A finished creative is an extra line item, and none of the sources reviewed prices it for a grooming salon.

## How Do You Target Pet Owners Near the Salon?

Radius is the setting that matters most for a single location. [Trusted TV's 2026 benchmark](https://www.trustedtv.com/blog/small-business-streaming-tv-benchmark-2026) reports that roughly 100% of its campaigns set their own geography, and the most common setting is a 10-mile radius around a ZIP code. That is vendor data for small businesses in general, not for groomers.

For pet owners specifically, [Barklead's guide to Facebook ads for groomers](https://barklead.com/post/using-facebook-ads-for-groomers) suggests targeting within 10 to 15 miles, and pet owners aged 25 to 60 who are likely to have disposable income. CTV platforms may offer different age and interest settings, so confirm them before you buy.

A radius that is too wide spreads a small budget thin. [Groomer to Groomer](https://www.groomertogroomer.com/reaching-more-customers-with-facebook-ads/), writing in 2016, described 150,000 pet owners in a 20-mile radius on a limited budget as yielding an ad frequency typically below 1.5. Viewers see the ad too few times to remember it.

One vendor blog reports that a single client reduced CTV setup time by 85% after switching to AI-automated targeting. [NETINT](https://netint.com/research/blog/the-gen-ai-revolution-in-ctv-advertising/) does not give the client's name or spend, so treat it as a single example. If you run more than one location, check overlap. [SEOplus](https://seoplus.com/paid-ads/the-ultimate-guide-to-multi-location-ppc-advertising-on-google-ads/) warns that overlapping targeting across location campaigns can lead to internal competition and higher cost per click.

## Is CTV Worth It for a Grooming Business? A Worked Example

Every input in this table is an **assumption**, except the arithmetic. Replace them with your own numbers.

| Input | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign budget | $500 | assumption |
| CPM paid | $20 | assumption, near the middle of the $10 to $30 range |
| Impressions bought | 25,000 | $500 / $20 x 1,000 |
| Average groom price | $80 | assumption |
| Visits per client per year | 6 | assumption |
| First-year revenue per client | $480 | $80 x 6 |
| Clients to break even on revenue | 2 | $500 / $480 = 1.04, rounded up |
| Gross margin on a groom | 50% | assumption |
| Margin per client per year | $240 | $480 x 50% |
| Clients to cover the ad on margin | 3 | $500 / $240 = 2.08, rounded up |

On these inputs the campaign buys 25,000 impressions, and three new regulars in a year cover its cost on margin. Impressions are not visits. Count how many viewers live within your real drive time, and track whether new clients mention the TV ad when they book.

A mobile groomer needs a separate check. [AA Center](https://www.aacenter.net/blog/how-much-to-charge-for-mobile-dog-grooming) notes that mobile groomers usually do fewer dogs per day than a busy salon because of drive time, setup and one-on-one handling. Each new client therefore takes more of a fixed day, so use the margin from your actual route rather than a salon average.

## What Should You Check Before Booking a CTV Campaign?

1. Get the current minimum spend and CPM from the platform in writing, since no pet-groomer rate is published in the sources reviewed.
2. Set the radius to your real drive time, starting near the 10-mile setting that [Trusted TV](https://www.trustedtv.com/blog/small-business-streaming-tv-benchmark-2026) reports as most common.
3. Check the audience age and interest settings against the 25 to 60 pet-owner profile from [Barklead](https://barklead.com/post/using-facebook-ads-for-groomers).
4. Put a unique phone number or QR code on the creative so calls and bookings can be traced.
5. Ask every new client how they heard of you, and record the answer at booking.
6. Set a stop date and a target cost per new client before the first impression is bought.
7. Compare the result with search and profile results before the next budget decision.

## Should CTV Come Before Search or Social?

For most groomers, the sensible order is a complete Google Business Profile, a modest search campaign, and then streaming or social. Search captures people already looking for a groomer. CTV builds awareness that may show up later as a search or a Maps visit, which is hard to attribute.

Digital ads can work for a mobile groomer without CTV. [Marketing 360](https://blog.marketing360.com/case-studies/pet-groomer-marketing-case-study-driving-success-with-digital-ads/) reported a 696% increase in clicks and a 134% increase in impressions for a mobile pet groomer against the previous six months, with ads on Facebook and Google. That was a 2023 vendor case study, not a CTV result.

## FAQ

### How much does a CTV ad cost for a pet groomer?

No pet-groomer-specific CTV rate appears in the sources reviewed. Free ad-supported streaming on Pluto TV has been reported to start around $10 to $30 CPM. Ask the platform for a quote that reflects your geography and audience.

### What radius should a groomer target?

Trusted TV reports that a 10-mile radius around a ZIP code is the most common setting for its campaigns. Barklead suggests 10 to 15 miles for Facebook ads for groomers. Match the radius to the drive time your clients actually accept.

### Is CTV better than search for a small groomer?

The sources do not support that claim. Search captures people already looking, while CTV builds awareness. Test CTV after your profile and search campaigns are bringing in bookings.

### How do I know if a CTV ad brought in a client?

Use a unique phone number or QR code, ask new clients how they heard of you, and compare the count against the break-even figures in the worked example above.
