A salon can run online booking for $19.95 to $60 a month for a small team. Fresha starts at $19.95 for one bookable team member, Vagaro lists $23.99 a month for its first six months, Booksy is $29.99 a month, and GlossGenius starts at $28. Square Appointments has a free plan. The bigger cost is usually card processing, not the subscription, so compare both.

Every price in this guide was read from the vendor's own page on 24 October 2026. Where a page did not show a number, the text says so.

## How much do salon booking systems cost?

The table lists what each vendor's pricing page showed. "Per staff" means the extra cost as your team grows.

| System | Base price | Per extra staff | Free trial |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fresha | $19.95/mo, one team member | Team plan $14.95 per team member/mo | 7 days |
| Vagaro | $23.99/mo for first 6 months ($30.00 also shown) | $10 per bookable calendar, up to 7 | 30 days |
| Booksy | $29.99/mo | $20/mo per extra user | 14 days |
| GlossGenius | $28/mo Standard ($24 billed annually) | Gold $56, Platinum $168 | 14 days |
| Square Appointments | Free plan, unlimited staff calendars | Plus and Premium prices not shown on the page | Not stated |

GlossGenius has no free plan. Its Gold and Platinum tiers add features rather than staff. Square's page listed Free, Plus and Premium plans but did not render the monthly prices for the paid ones, so check Square directly before budgeting.

## What are the card processing fees?

Processing is where a busy salon pays most. The rates below are the ones each vendor published.

| System | In-person card | Online or keyed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fresha | 2.29% + $0.20 | 2.79% + $0.20 online, 3.30% + $0.20 manual entry |
| Booksy | 2.49% + $0.10 (card reader), 2.49% + $0.20 (Tap to Pay) | 2.69% + $0.30 mobile and keyed |
| GlossGenius | 2.4% + $0.15 (from the FAQ) | 2.9% + $0.30 online invoicing |
| Vagaro | Not listed on its pricing page | Not listed |
| Square | Not shown on the pages opened | Not shown |

The Vagaro and Square pages opened here did not show processing rates. Ask for them in writing and do not assume they match another vendor.

## What does a four-stylist salon actually pay? A worked example

All inputs are **assumptions**: four bookable team members, $20,000 a month in card sales, 400 transactions at an average of $50, all taken in person.

| Cost line | Fresha Team | Booksy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subscription | 4 x $14.95 = $59.80 | $29.99 + 3 x $20 = $89.99 |
| Card fees | 2.29% x $20,000 + 400 x $0.20 = $538.00 | 2.49% x $20,000 + 400 x $0.10 = $538.00 |
| Total per month | $597.80 | $627.99 |

The subscription is about a tenth of the bill. The two totals sit within about $30 of each other, and the fees are identical.

A salon with $20,000 in monthly card sales can save more by negotiating a processing rate than by switching booking software. Vagaro's $10 per calendar would put four calendars near $60 a month if the base price includes one (assumption), but its processing rate was not on the pricing page, so no total is shown.

## Which fees are easy to miss?

Fresha's marketplace takes a one-time 20% commission on a new client it finds for you, with a $6 minimum. Returning clients and bookings from your own link, Facebook and Instagram carry no fee. Booksy's Boost is free to switch on, then charges 30% of a new Boost client's first visit, capped at $100.

Vagaro lists "exclusions apply" next to its promotional $23.99, and its page shows $30.00 as well. Treat $30 as the likely ongoing price until you see the checkout screen.

## Is Schedulicity still a separate option?

No longer in the same way. Schedulicity's pricing page now redirects to Vagaro's. Press coverage from January 2025 reports that Vagaro acquired Schedulicity, which is a secondary source. Older comparison articles that list Schedulicity pricing separately are out of date.

## What features should a salon check before choosing?

Features matter less than fit. Check these in a trial before you commit:

- Online booking from your Google Business Profile, Instagram and Facebook. Vagaro lists booking on Instagram, Facebook and Apple Maps.
- Deposit and cancellation rules. Square puts cancellation policies and no-show fees on its Plus plan, not Free.
- Reminders. Vagaro lists unlimited notifications and reminders. Fresha's plans include 100 free messages.
- A payout schedule that suits you. GlossGenius charges 1.8% for instant payouts.
- Export of your client list, in case you leave.

Trial lengths differ from 7 to 30 days, so import a real week of appointments and see how clients react. ## FAQ

### What is the cheapest online booking system for a salon?

Square Appointments has a free plan with unlimited staff calendars and online booking, but you pay processing fees on payments. Among paid plans, Fresha Independent at $19.95 a month and Vagaro's $23.99 promotional rate are the lowest listed.

### Do I need a booking system with payments built in?

Not required, but it reduces double entry. Built-in payments also mean you pay that vendor's processing rate, so compare the rate as well as the subscription.

### Do clients need an account to book?

That depends on the vendor and your settings. Check the booking page as a client during your trial.

### Can I switch booking systems later?

Yes, but confirm you can export your client list and appointment history first. Fresha, Booksy and Vagaro all offer trials, so test an export before paying.

### Are the prices the same outside the US?

No. Square's own help pages show different plan prices by country, and other vendors vary too. Check the pricing page for your country.
