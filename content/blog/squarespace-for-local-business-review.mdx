## What Can Be Verified About Squarespace Support?

The Squarespace Help Center topic page for site guides requires a sign-in before its content loads, so its articles could not be read directly. Its visible contact details are still useful. [Squarespace's help center](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/sections/360000121907-Site-topic-guides) lists live chat from Monday to Friday, 4:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST, offered in English only. Email support is listed as available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and Spanish email support is offered separately.

Those hours matter if a site goes down on a busy Saturday. Chat runs in a weekday window set to US Eastern time, so a business in Sydney or Vancouver may wait longer for a live answer. Email is the only channel that covers weekends in the published details.

The same page shows that account recovery can require documents. Recovery requests ask for a bank statement showing the most recent Squarespace charge, and the .AU domain form asks for government ID and proof of Australian address. Keeping billing records and registrant details in one place before a problem arises saves time.

## What Does Google Business Profile Control for Local Visibility?

Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and prominence. Relevance comes from how completely a profile describes the business. Distance is how far each business sits from the searcher. Prominence reflects how well known the business is, including how many websites link to it and how many reviews it has ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)).

Google also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. A website builder cannot buy a higher position, so spending on a platform should be judged on what it lets the owner control: a fast page, clear service pages and details that match the profile.

Google's guidance on profile upkeep is concrete. Keep the business name exactly as it appears on signage, set hours and service area, choose a specific primary category, and respond to reviews ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617)). These edits are free and work on any website, Squarespace included.

## How Many Categories and Phone Numbers Can a Profile Use?

A Google Business Profile takes one primary category and up to 9 more. Google advises choosing a specific primary category and avoiding categories used as keywords or to describe features of the business ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617)). A coffee shop that adds "bakery", "catering" and "wedding venue" should include only those that are true services.

Up to 2 more phone numbers can be listed beyond the main one. They can be mobile or landline, but not fax. The phone number can also be hidden on the profile. Keep the numbers identical to those on the Squarespace site, because mismatched details can weaken how a profile matches searches.

These are free settings. They are a better use of an afternoon than comparing plan features that do not affect ranking.

## How Should a Local Business Choose a Plan?

Work through these steps before paying for anything:

1. List the three things a customer must do on the site: call, book or visit. Those actions decide which pages are needed.
2. Check the live pricing page for the plan that includes custom code, if a tracking tag or structured data is needed, and confirm whether code is listed as an add-on.
3. Estimate monthly online sales, such as gift cards or class packs. Multiply by any store fee on the live page to see the yearly cost.
4. Decide whether booking is a separate tool. If one is already in use, embed its booking link rather than paying for a second calendar.
5. Confirm that the name, address and phone number match the Google Business Profile exactly.

Local visibility also depends on whether people can reach the business easily. If a contact form is added, check the data it collects against the rules in [local business website GDPR compliance](/blog/local-business-website-gdpr-compliance). If a map is added, follow the steps in [how to embed a Google Map on a local business website](/blog/google-maps-embed-local-business-website).

## Does the Platform Change How Customers Find a Business?

Only indirectly. A platform does not decide relevance, distance or prominence. It affects how quickly pages load and how easily the owner can keep them accurate. Google's guidance focuses on profile completeness and reviews, so those are the first tasks to finish ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)).

Paid search follows different rules. Before budgeting for paid placement, read [whether Google Local Service Ads are worth it for local businesses](/blog/google-local-service-ads-worth-it-local-business). Paid placement adds a monthly cost that belongs in the cost model above, separate from the website plan.

For phone and chat inquiries outside opening hours, an automated tool can cover gaps in the schedule. [AI agents for customer service at local businesses](/blog/ai-agent-for-customer-service-local-business) covers what such a tool does and where it stops.

## Where Should a Local Business Start?

Start with the free items: a complete Google Business Profile, matching contact details, one specific primary category, and a routine for responding to reviews. Then build the site on the cheapest plan that covers the three customer actions identified earlier. Keep original photos and text in a folder outside the platform, so a move later does not mean starting over.
