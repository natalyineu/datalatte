A local business has three ways to run an AI bot on WhatsApp: Meta's own Business Agent, a third-party AI provider connected to the WhatsApp Business Platform, or the free app's greeting and away messages with no AI. Meta's pricing page puts Business Agent at $2.00 per million tokens, about 4 to 5 cents per message. The same page estimates a third-party AI solution at about 9 cents per message in a Brazil example.

These figures are Meta's own estimates, not a quote for your country. The cost example below is labelled as an assumption.

## Which WhatsApp AI Bot Options Does a Small Business Have?

| Option | What it does | Cost, as published |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp Business app, no AI | Greets people when they start a chat and replies when you are away | The page prints no price |
| Meta Business Agent | Meta says it can answer questions, capture leads and recommend products | $2.00 per million tokens, about 4-5 cents a message |
| Third-party AI provider on the Business Platform | Your own bot, built by a vendor | Provider fees plus Meta fees; Meta estimates about 9 cents in its Brazil example |

TechCrunch reported on June 3, 2026 that Business Agent also books appointments and qualifies leads, and hands off to a person when needed. The same report says Meta plans to include it in some tiers of its WhatsApp Business Premium subscription. Meta's page prices it by tokens, so check which route applies to your account before relying on either report.

## How Much Does a WhatsApp AI Bot Cost?

Meta's pricing page says a Business Agent message is metered per message, with one per-token charge that covers AI processing and message delivery. A typical message uses roughly 20,000 to 25,000 tokens. Volume tiers and free messages do not apply, and Business Agent messages are still charged inside the free entry point window.

Messages from a third-party AI solution fall into the service category. From October 1, 2026 each business phone number gets 1,000 free service messages a month, but Meta states the service message update does not apply to AI Providers, which follow a separate policy whose details are not on that page. ## Which Bots Does WhatsApp Not Allow?

The business messaging policy page, last updated September 23, 2026, lets you automate replies inside the 24-hour customer service window, but automated responders must offer a prompt, clear, direct escalation path to a human. Examples it lists include an in-chat agent transfer, a phone number, email, web support or an in-store visit. Outside the window you can send only approved templates, and you need the customer's opt-in.

Press reports say the platform terms also bar general-purpose chatbots, meaning open-ended assistants such as ChatGPT, from January 15, 2026, while support and sales bots remain allowed. We did not find that wording on Meta's policy page, so treat it as secondary and read Meta's current terms before choosing a vendor.

## How Do You Set Up a WhatsApp Bot Step by Step?

1. Write down the ten questions customers ask most: hours, prices, parking, booking, cancellation.
2. Start with the free app's greeting and away message, then see which questions still reach you.
3. Decide whether Business Agent is offered on your account, or whether you need a provider.
4. Give the bot only facts you can keep current: price list, hours, service area.
5. Add a human route to every flow: a phone number or a "talk to a person" reply.
6. Read the first 50 real conversations and fix wrong answers.

Do not collect card numbers in chat; send a payment link from your booking or payment system instead. ## FAQ

### Is there a free WhatsApp AI bot?

Meta's Business Agent is paid per message, and the Business app page lists no AI price. The free app can send a greeting and away messages without AI.

### Can I use ChatGPT on my WhatsApp Business number?

Press reports say general-purpose chatbots have been barred from the Business Platform since January 15, 2026. A bot built for your own support and sales is a different category. Confirm with Meta's terms.

### Do I have to tell customers it is a bot?

The policy page we read does not state a disclosure rule, but it does require a clear route to a human. Saying plainly that a bot is answering is safer.

### Can the bot take payments?

Do not ask for card numbers in chat. Send a link to your payment page.

### How long does setup take?

No published figure. The steps above are the work: collecting answers, testing and reading real chats.
