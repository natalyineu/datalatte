A fitness studio can run Spotify audio ads through Spotify Ads Manager with a minimum budget of 250 in local currency, and target listeners by city or postal code. Spotify does not offer radius targeting on its location page. Pricing is an auction with no published CPM, so the number to trust is the impression forecast Ads Manager shows for your area.

This guide covers setup, targeting, creative specs and a cheap first test. Every figure comes from a Spotify page listed at the end. The worked example is labelled as an assumption.

## Why Would a Fitness Studio Use Spotify Ads?

Spotify reaches people while they listen, which can include a commute or a workout. Spotify's advertising page cites 777 million or more monthly active users, a global figure with no city split. It does not say how many listeners use the app to train, so any claim that Spotify "reaches people mid-workout" is a guess about context, not a published number.

Audio is a reminder channel. It works for awareness and for a clear offer such as a trial class. It is weaker than search for someone looking for a studio right now. For that, see our [fitness studio keyword guide](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-fitness-studios-in-2026).

## How Do You Target Listeners in Your City?

Spotify's location targeting page lists the levels by account country: country, state, city, postal code or DMA in the US; country, city and postal code in the UK and Canada; and country, state, city and postal code in Australia. Location is matched by IP address. The page does not mention radius targeting, so use city or a list of postal codes around your studio.

Other targeting comes in two kinds. Demographic targeting uses age, gender, location and platform from Spotify's own data. Advanced options are interest, real-time context, genre and fan base, and Spotify says you can use one advanced option per ad set. Fan base is for promoting an artist or music event, so a studio will not use it. Spotify's page does not list daypart or lookalike audiences, so check the options in your account.

## How Do You Set Up a Campaign?

Registration for Ads Manager is free. Spotify says you can build an audio or video campaign that reaches listeners on music, podcasts or both, with a minimum budget of 250.00 in your local currency.

1. Pick an objective and your city or postal codes.
2. Set a budget of at least 250 and a flight date.
3. Choose a bid strategy: maximum bid, cost per result, or automated bidding.
4. Upload an audio file or request Spotify's voiceover service.
5. Add a companion image and a click-through URL.
6. Submit and wait for review.

Edits to age targeting, location targeting, the audio file, the companion image or the click-through URL need re-approval. Spotify says the ad is paused for 24 to 48 hours while it is reviewed, so avoid editing a live ad the week of a promotion.

## What Are the Audio Ad Specs?

Spotify's audio ad specs for Ads Manager:

| Item | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Length | Up to 30 seconds |
| File types | MP3, WAV or OGG, 50 MB maximum |
| Recommended script | About 65 words for 30 seconds |
| Companion image | 1:1, at least 600x600 px, JPEG or PNG, 200 KB maximum |
| Advertiser name | 25 characters maximum |
| Tagline | 40 characters maximum |

Sixty-five words is short. Name the studio, give one offer, and end with one action, such as a short web address for a trial class. Put the offer in the companion image too, since people may see it later.

## How Much Do Spotify Ads Cost?

Spotify says Ads Manager runs on an auction and its tools "don't come at a fixed cost." You choose a daily or lifetime budget. The pricing page lists no CPM and no cost benchmark. CPM tables in third-party blogs are estimates, so this guide does not use them. ## FAQ

### Can I target only people in my city?

Yes, by city or postal code in the US, UK, Canada and Australia, and by DMA in the US. Location is matched by IP address. Spotify's location page does not mention radius targeting.

### What if I only have 200 a month?

Spotify's Ads Manager help page sets a minimum campaign budget of 250 in local currency, so 200 is under the floor.

### Can I make the audio ad without a studio?

Spotify offers a voiceover service, and the specs allow MP3, WAV or OGG files up to 50 MB. Keep the script near 65 words for a 30-second ad.

### How do I know if the ad worked?

Spotify does not publish a conversion benchmark. Use a dedicated URL or promo code and compare bookings against spend.

### Do edits to a live ad pause it?

For changes to the audio file, companion image or click-through URL, Spotify says the ad is paused for 24 to 48 hours during re-approval.
