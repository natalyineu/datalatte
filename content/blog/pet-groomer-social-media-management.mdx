For a single-location pet groomer, Hootsuite is usually more tool than the job needs. Its Standard plan starts at $99 per user per month and covers up to 10 social accounts. Professional starts at $199 per user per month with unlimited accounts, and Advanced starts at $399. A one-shop groomer with two or three active channels pays for capacity it will not use. Hootsuite makes sense when you manage many accounts, several users, or approval steps. Plan prices below come from third-party comparisons and can change, so confirm them on Hootsuite's live plans page before you buy.

## How Much Does Hootsuite Cost for a Pet Groomer?

Hootsuite's plans are priced per user, not per shop. A comparison of AI social tools lists Standard at $99 per user per month with up to 10 social accounts, unlimited scheduling and AI content generation ([Sintra, secondary](https://sintra.ai/blog/ai-social-media/best-social-media-ai-agents)). The same comparison puts Professional at $199 per user per month with unlimited accounts and workflow automation, and Advanced at $399 per user per month.

Enterprise has no published price. A separate HubSpot guide says Heyday by Hootsuite requires contacting the company for custom pricing and is aimed at mid-to-large businesses ([HubSpot, secondary](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/ai-agents-for-social-media)). A single groomer is far from that tier.

## Which Hootsuite Plan Includes What?

The plan names matter more than the headline price, because each tier adds a different kind of feature. The table below sets out the published features from the secondary comparisons. Two sources disagree on where approval workflows sit, so check the live plans page before choosing a tier that depends on them.

| Plan | Price per user per month | Main limit or feature (as reported) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | From $99 | Up to 10 social accounts, unlimited scheduling, AI content generation |
| Professional | From $199 | Unlimited social accounts, workflow automation, AI trend forecasting |
| Advanced | From $399 | Approval workflows, message routing, team collaboration, performance reporting |
| Enterprise | Custom | Advanced analytics, social listening, SSO, compliance tools |

One comparison says approval workflows, advanced reporting and the content library are available only at Enterprise ([Thinkific, secondary](https://www.thinkific.com/blog/marketing-automation-platforms/)). Another lists approval workflows under Advanced. For a single owner who approves their own posts, this difference does not change the decision.

## How Many Social Accounts Does One Shop Need?

The Standard limit of 10 accounts is generous for one shop. A typical groomer runs one Instagram profile, one Facebook page and perhaps a Google Business Profile, which is three accounts at most. Paying for capacity above that is the main reason a single site overpays.

Count your accounts first. If a salon and a mobile van run under separate brands, each brand adds accounts, and the 10-account Standard limit starts to matter. For groups with several sites, the approach in the [multi-location social media guide](/blog/multi-location-social-media-management) is a better fit than a one-shop setup.

## What Does a Worked Cost Example Show?

The inputs below are **assumptions** for illustration. Hootsuite does not publish time savings, and the hourly value of owner time is a placeholder you should replace with your own figure.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Hootsuite users | 1 |
| Social accounts | 3 |
| Posts per week | 4 |
| Minutes to post manually, per post | 10 |
| Minutes per post when batched in a scheduler | 5 |
| Weeks per month | 4 |
| Owner time valued at, per hour | $25 |

Standard at one user costs $99 per month, assuming the listed rate applies. Manual posting takes 4 × 10 × 4 = 160 minutes a month. Batched scheduling takes 4 × 5 × 4 = 80 minutes, saving 80 minutes, or about 1.33 hours. At $25 an hour, that saving is worth about $33 a month.

The break-even point is $99 ÷ $25 = 3.96 hours saved each month. At these inputs, the scheduler does not pay for itself on time alone. It pays only if the time saved is larger, or if you need features that Standard includes.

## How Often Should a Groomer Post?

Hootsuite's research, as quoted in a salon marketing guide, recommends posting to Instagram 2 to 7 times a week and to Facebook 1 to 2 times a day ([Salon Biz Software, secondary](https://salonbizsoftware.com/blog/daily-salon-social-media-marketing-ideas-how-to-engage-your-audience/)). These are general platform guidelines, not groomer-specific benchmarks.

Timing matters as well. Groomer to Groomer notes that November and December are traditionally very busy for groomers, sometimes too busy ([AdWave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/pet-grooming-advertising)). A batch of posts scheduled in late October can cover the rush without extra work during the busiest weeks. ## Where Does AI Fit in Scheduling?

AI features in these plans mainly draft captions and suggest content. Standard includes AI content generation, while Professional adds AI-powered trend forecasting ([Sintra, secondary](https://sintra.ai/blog/ai-social-media/best-social-media-ai-agents)). A draft still needs your check, especially for prices, opening hours and any health claim about a grooming service.

Be cautious with statistics that appear in AI or template copy. One groomer resource includes a sample caption saying regular brushing reduces shedding by up to 50%, but it cites no source for that figure. Do not post numbers like this without a primary reference. Free-plan limits change often, and no verified figure for a competing free scheduler is included here. Check each vendor's live pricing page before you compare. The three-way comparison in the [Hootsuite, Buffer and Later guide](/blog/hootsuite-vs-buffer-vs-later-best-social-media-tool) is a starting point for the trade-offs.

## FAQ

### Is Hootsuite worth it for a small pet grooming business?
For one location with two or three channels, usually not. Standard is from $99 per user per month, and a single shop rarely needs 10 accounts or several users.

### Does Hootsuite have a free plan?
The sources used here describe a free trial, not a free plan. Check Hootsuite's live plans page for current trial terms.

### How much does Hootsuite cost for three users?
One comparison gives $297 per month for three seats on Standard with annual billing, against a vendor's comparison ([Turrboo, secondary](https://turrboo.com/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media)). That is consistent with $99 per user per month.

### Does scheduling improve engagement?
The sources reviewed do not include a groomer engagement benchmark. A scheduler saves time and keeps posting consistent. It does not guarantee more engagement by itself.

### Which plan should I choose first?
Start with the plan that covers your real account count and user count. If you have one owner and three channels, test a trial before paying for anything above Standard.
