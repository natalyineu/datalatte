For a single-location pet groomer, Hootsuite is likely more tool than needed. Its Standard plan starts from $99 and covers 10 social accounts, while Buffer's free plan covers 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts each, and its paid Essentials plan is $5 per channel per month. Choose Hootsuite only if you manage many accounts, need several users, or want its analytics and inbox in one place.

This guide compares the two on published plan limits and works through a cost example. Hootsuite and Buffer prices were read from their pricing pages on 31 October 2026 and can change. Examples marked as assumptions are illustrations.

## What Does Hootsuite Cost?

Hootsuite lists four plans. Standard is from $99 with 10 social accounts. Professional is from $199 with unlimited accounts. Advanced is up to $399, also with unlimited accounts. Enterprise is custom priced.

Standard, Professional and Advanced offer monthly or annual billing, and the displayed prices assume annual billing and exclude taxes. The page does not list a separate monthly price, so check the checkout page for the monthly rate. A 14-day free trial is offered, with daily posting limits of 10 to 20 posts per organization and no bulk scheduling during the trial.

The page does not say whether the plans are priced per user, beyond "1+" user seats.

## What Does Buffer Cost?

Buffer prices per connected channel. The Free plan is free forever, with up to 3 channels and 10 scheduled posts per channel; a slot reopens after each post publishes. Essentials is $5 per month per channel, or $60 per channel billed yearly. Team is $10 per month per channel, or $120 per channel billed yearly.

A groomer with Instagram, Facebook and Google Business Profile as channels can start free. Our [comparison of Hootsuite, Buffer and Later](/blog/hootsuite-vs-buffer-vs-later-best-social-media-tool) covers more tools.

## Which Plan Fits a One-Shop Groomer?

| Situation | Tool that fits | Published limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two or three channels, a few posts a week | Buffer Free | 3 channels, 10 scheduled posts each |
| Three to five channels, steady schedule | Buffer Essentials | $5 per channel per month |
| 10 or fewer accounts, one team | Hootsuite Standard | From $99, 10 accounts |
| Many locations or brands | Hootsuite Professional | From $199, unlimited accounts |

A single shop rarely needs 10 accounts. If you run a salon and a mobile van with separate brands, count the accounts before paying.

## How Much Time Does a Scheduler Save? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**; neither vendor publishes time savings.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Posts per week | 4 |
| Minutes to post manually, per post | 10 |
| Minutes per post when batched in a scheduler | 5 |
| Weeks per month | 4 |

Manual posting is 4 x 10 x 4 = 160 minutes a month. Batched scheduling is 4 x 5 x 4 = 80 minutes. The saving is 80 minutes, or about 1.3 hours.

At 3 channels on Buffer Essentials the cost would be 3 x $5 = $15 a month. Hootsuite Standard from $99 costs 6.6 times more for the same single-shop workload. Whether $99 is worth it depends on the extra features, not on the 80 minutes.

## FAQ

### Is Hootsuite worth it for a small pet grooming business?
For one location, usually not. Standard is from $99 for 10 accounts, and most small shops use two or three.

### Does Hootsuite have a free plan?
Its plans page lists a 14-day free trial, not a free plan.

### What is the cheapest way to schedule posts?
Buffer's free plan covers 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts per channel.

### Can I schedule to Instagram and Facebook?
The pricing pages we read do not list supported networks, so check each vendor's current network list before you commit.

### Do scheduled posts get more engagement?
No source we checked publishes a groomer engagement benchmark. A scheduler saves time; it does not by itself raise engagement.
