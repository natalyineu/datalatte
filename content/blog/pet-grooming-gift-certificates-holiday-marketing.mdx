## Why Sell Gift Certificates to Pet Owners?

Pet owners are a large group: APPA reports 95 million US households own a pet, and its "other services" category, which includes grooming, boarding, training and pet sitting, was $14.3 billion in 2025. Gift certificates let a client give a groom to a friend or family member, which can bring a new customer to your door.

No reliable data was found on how many groomers sell gift certificates or how many redeem them, so measure your own results.

## What Rules Apply to Gift Certificates?

In the US, Regulation E says the funds' expiry must be at least five years after the certificate was issued, or, for store gift cards, five years after funds were last loaded. Replacing a certificate or card cannot carry a fee unless it is lost or stolen. The rule text has exemptions and state laws can be stricter, so this page does not try to list them.

Put your terms in writing and ask an adviser or your state agency before launch.

## How Do You Sell Them Online?

Square's page says eGift cards are free to set up and carry up to a 2.5% load fee each time funds are added, plus standard processing fees. Redemption has no additional fee. Physical card prices vary with the number ordered, and Square's page gives no price. Other booking tools have their own terms, so compare before you choose.

Place a "Buy a gift certificate" button on your site's top menu and your Business Profile. See our guide to pet groomer website pages for where it fits.

## The Math: What Does a Certificate Sale Net?

| --- | --- |

| Certificates sold | 20 |

| Card processing (assumed 3% on purchase) | $45.00 |

Cash arrives up front, but the work comes later. If 70% of certificates (14) are redeemed in January and February, that is 14 grooming slots filled. The remaining 6 are a liability you must honour for years. Do not spend all the cash as profit. Track redemptions in a sheet.

## What Happens in January?

Offer redeemers a rebooking prompt at checkout: book the next groom at the end of the visit. Collect email consent so you can remind them at the right interval. A holiday certificate is most useful as a first visit for a new client who then returns.

## FAQ

### Can a gift certificate expire?

In the US, funds cannot expire sooner than five years from issue or last load under federal rules. State laws can be stricter, so check yours.

### How much does Square charge for gift cards?

eGift cards are free to set up with a load fee of up to 2.5% plus standard processing fees. Physical card prices vary.

### Should I discount certificates?

A bonus, such as an extra $10 for every $50, is a marketing choice. Do the math on your margin first, and confirm with local rules how any time limit on the bonus is treated.

### Can I sell them without special software?

Yes, but you need a way to track balances and redemptions. A spreadsheet works for a small volume.
