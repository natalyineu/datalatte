## What Does Make.com Do for a Salon?

Make connects apps with visual workflows called scenarios. A scenario starts with a trigger (a new booking, a new customer, a schedule) and then runs steps (send an email, write to a spreadsheet, post a Slack message). It does not replace your booking software. It joins your booking software to everything else.

Make lists a Calendly app whose trigger fires when an event is created or canceled, and a Square app with triggers such as Watch Customers and Watch Payments. Check that your own booking tool has a connector before you plan anything. If it does not, a webhook or a Google Calendar trigger is the usual fallback.

## How Does Make Count Credits?

Make's [credits help page](https://help.make.com/credits) says that by default one operation equals one credit, whatever the size of the data. Some AI features use more. According to the [operations help page](https://help.make.com/operations), a trigger counts as one operation per check, and every other module runs once for each bundle (item) it receives.

Make's own example: a trigger returns 10 form responses, followed by three modules. That is 1 + (3 x 10) = 31 operations in one run. For a salon, the lesson is simple: process one booking at a time, filter early, and avoid loops over your whole client list.

If you run out of credits mid-month, Make's help page says you can upgrade, buy extra credits or turn on auto-purchase. Check paid plan prices and limits on the [pricing page](https://www.make.com/en/pricing), because they change.

## Which Four Salon Workflows Should You Build First?

Build one, test it for two weeks, then add the next.

| Workflow | Trigger | Steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booking confirmation and reminder | New booking | Send confirmation, schedule reminder |
| Review request | Appointment marked complete | Wait a day, send a short message with your review link |
| Lapsed client list | Weekly schedule | Find clients with no visit in 90 days (your choice), add them to a sheet |
| Monday numbers email | Weekly schedule | Pull last week's bookings into a summary and email it to you |

The weekly schedules run once a week, so they use very few credits. The booking and review workflows scale with your bookings. ## How Do You Build a Scenario Step by Step?

1. Write the goal in one line: "Every completed appointment gets a review request the next day."
2. Create a scenario and choose the trigger module for your booking tool.
3. Add a filter so the scenario only continues for the cases you want (for example, completed appointments with a client email).
4. Add the action: an email module, a Google Sheets row or a Slack message.
5. Run it once with test data and check the result.
6. Send a test to yourself, read it as a client, then turn the schedule on.

Keep a simple log sheet of what the scenario sent. If something goes wrong, you will know who received what.

## What Rules Apply to Automated Emails?

The FTC's [CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) lists the main requirements for US marketing email:

- Make header information accurate, and keep subject lines honest.
- Clearly disclose that the message is an ad.
- Include a valid physical postal address.
- Provide a clear way to opt out, and honour requests within 10 business days.
- You remain responsible even if another company sends the email for you.

The guide states penalties of up to $53,088 for each separate email in violation. Other countries have their own rules, so check them if you email clients in the UK, EU, Canada or Australia. Text messages carry separate consent rules. Get legal advice before you automate SMS.

## What Mistakes Should You Avoid?

- Polling a spreadsheet every few minutes. The trigger check itself uses credits.
- Messaging clients who visited yesterday. Exclude recent visits.
- Sending in the middle of the night. Set a send window.
- Skipping consent. Only message people who agreed.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Make.com free for a salon?**
A: Make's pricing page lists a Free plan with 1,000 credits a month. Check the page for current limits and for paid plan prices.

**Q: Do I need to code?**
A: No. Scenarios are built visually, but you still need to understand your booking tool's data and test every step.

**Q: What if my booking tool is not on Make?**
A: Look for a webhook, email or Google Calendar option instead. Otherwise automate in the booking tool itself.

**Q: Can I automate text messages?**
A: Technically yes, but SMS has separate consent rules. Get proper advice first.

**Q: How long does the first scenario take?**
A: Plan a few hours for setup and testing. That is an estimate, not a vendor promise.
