## Is Pinterest Worth It for a Local Photographer?

It can be, if clients plan ahead. Weddings, family sessions and branding shoots are planned, and Pinterest is where many people collect ideas. Pew shows Pinterest at 37% of US adults, behind YouTube at 84%, Facebook at 70% and Instagram at 51%, so it is a smaller channel than the big three.

Treat it as a slow, low-cost source of search traffic. Photographers who already post on Instagram can reuse images. See our [Pinterest worth-it guide for local businesses](/blog/pinterest-marketing-for-local-businesses-worth-it) and [Instagram marketing for photographers](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-photographers) to decide where to spend time.

## How Do You Set Up the Profile and Claim Your Site?

Create a business account, add your service area to the bio and name your boards by client intent, such as "Austin wedding photographer" or "Newborn session ideas". Then claim your website.

Pinterest's help says claiming connects your site to your profile, so your profile picture and a follow option appear on Pins saved from your site, and you can see how often your content is shared. You must own the domain and be able to edit its code. The four methods are a Google Merchant Center link, an HTML tag, an HTML file or a DNS TXT record, and DNS changes can take up to 72 hours.

## What Makes a Good Pin?

Use Pinterest's recommended 2:3 ratio, or 1000 x 1500 pixels. Pinterest says wider images may be cropped in feeds. Add a clear title with the service and place, up to 100 characters, though only about the first 40 show in feeds. Write a description of up to 800 characters in plain words.

Use images you have rights to publish. For client work, get permission in your contract or a release. Put a short text overlay such as "Backyard family session, Austin" on the image so the Pin makes sense without a click. These specs are from Pinterest's ad specifications page, which is the closest published reference.

## Where Should Each Pin Link?

Link each Pin to a page that continues the story, not to your home page. A gallery page for the session type, with a few images, your starting price or package list, your area and an enquiry form, converts better than a generic portfolio. That is a design judgement, not a measured result.

Pinterest also supports Rich Pins that sync details from a page. Article Pins can show headline, description and author, if you add the right meta tags. Our guide to [designing a photographer website](/blog/website-design-for-photographers) lists the pages to build, and [Pinterest SEO basics](/blog/pinterest-seo-free-traffic-small-business) covers keywords.

## How Do You Know It Works?

Track three things monthly: visits from Pinterest in your website analytics, enquiries that mention Pinterest, and which boards bring the visits. Add a "How did you find me?" field to the enquiry form.

Give a new Pin set a few months before judging, because no neutral source gives a typical time to results. If visits come but nobody enquires, fix the landing pages. If no visits come, check your keywords and image format. For paid reach, see [Pinterest ads cost](/blog/pinterest-ads-cost-2026).

## The Math: What Does One Booking Pay?

All inputs are **assumptions**, not benchmarks.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Time per month making Pins and pages | 6 hours |
| Value of your time | $40 per hour |
| Monthly cost | $240 |
| Average session booking | $450 |
| Bookings needed to cover cost | 240 / 450 = about 0.5 |
| Bookings over 6 months | 3 |
| Revenue over 6 months | $1,350 |
| Cost over 6 months | $1,440 |

Under these inputs, three bookings in six months is close to break-even, and the Pins keep working after that. Wedding photographers with higher fees reach break-even faster. Use your own fees and hours.

## FAQ

### How many people use Pinterest in the US?
Pew's 2026 survey found 37% of US adults use it, against 51% for Instagram.

### What size should photography Pins be?
Pinterest recommends a 2:3 aspect ratio, or 1000 x 1500 pixels.

### Do I need to claim my website?
It is optional but useful. It links your profile to Pins from your site and shows how often your content is shared.

### Can Pinterest bring local clients?
It can send traffic, but no neutral source gives local conversion figures. Name your area in titles and pages, and track enquiries.

### Should I pay for Pinterest ads?
Start with organic Pins. Test paid only once your landing pages convert.
