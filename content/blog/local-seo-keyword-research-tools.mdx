For a single-location business, three free sources cover most local keyword research: Google Business Profile search terms, Search Console queries and Google autocomplete. Paid tools help when you need rank tracking across several locations. On vendor pages checked on 2026-11-01, BrightLocal starts at $31 a month billed annually, Semrush at $117.33 and Ahrefs at $129 a month.

The first job is not volume. It is matching what customers type to a service page or a Business Profile category you can actually rank for.

## Which Free Sources Should You Use First?

Your own data comes before any third-party tool. Google says the Searches metric in Business Profile performance shows the search terms people used when your profile appeared. It updates at the start of each month, and performance data is only available for verified profiles.

Google Search Console's Performance report groups clicks, impressions, click-through rate and average position by query. It shows what your site already appears for, including terms you never targeted. Google autocomplete is a quick supplement for phrasing: type your service and town and write down what it suggests.

## What Does Google Keyword Planner Give You?

Google's help page says Keyword Planner gives keyword ideas from your products, services or website, with monthly search estimates and the average cost to target them. You need a Google Ads account with billing information entered to use it, and the page notes that keywords with very low volumes may not be available. It does not say whether the tool is free to use.

Search volume for local terms is often small, so a missing number does not mean nobody searches. Use it to compare phrases, not to size your market.

## How Do You Turn a Keyword List Into Pages?

Group keywords by intent. "Emergency plumber near me" and "how to fix a leaking tap" need different pages. Give each service its own page with the town in the title, and make sure the same service is picked as a Business Profile category.

Check what already ranks. If map results are full of chains, a narrower term such as a neighbourhood or a specific service may be easier. ## What Should You Do With Paid Keywords?

If you also run Google Ads, the search terms report shows what people typed before clicking. ## FAQ

### What is the best free local keyword research tool?

Your Business Profile search terms and Search Console queries show what real customers used, at no charge. Google autocomplete adds phrasing ideas.

### Do I need Semrush or Ahrefs for local SEO?

Not usually. Their pricing pages list tracked keywords and projects but no local-specific features. BrightLocal lists local rank tracking and a geo-grid.

### Is Google Keyword Planner free?

Google's help page requires a Google Ads account with billing information and does not state a fee. Check your account before assuming.

### How many keywords should a local business target?

No source gives a fixed number. Start with one primary term per service page and one for each Business Profile category.
