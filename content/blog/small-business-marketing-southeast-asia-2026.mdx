A small business in Southeast Asia should pick channels by country: Facebook in the Philippines (95.8 million ad reach), LINE in Thailand (56 million monthly users), Zalo in Vietnam (78.3 million monthly users) and TikTok plus Facebook and Instagram in Indonesia. The figures come from DataReportal's Digital 2026 country reports, published in November 2025.

One caution applies to every number below: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube figures are advertising reach estimates, not active users, and DataReportal warns that platforms revise them. This guide leaves out marketplace and super-app advertising, because no checked source was available for it, and it quotes no regional click prices because none was verified.

## What Works in the Philippines?

DataReportal puts Philippine Facebook ad reach at 95.8 million, 81.9% of the population, up 10.3% in a year. Instagram reaches 26.9 million (up 24.6%) and TikTok 64.0 million adults, though TikTok fell 6.9% between July and October 2025, which the report attributes partly to platform corrections.

Practical step: for a salon, cafe or tutor in Manila or Cebu, make a Facebook Page the main shop window and test a click-to-message ad. No source here measures results for such ads, so judge them by your own cost per enquiry. See [Meta ads for local businesses](/blog/local-business-meta-ads) and [social media for local businesses](/blog/social-media-for-local-businesses).

## What Works in Thailand?

LINE had 56 million monthly active users in late 2025, 78.2% of the population, unchanged from October 2024. Facebook ad reach is 51.5 million (71.9%) and TikTok reaches 56.6 million adults. Internet penetration is 94.7%.

For a Bangkok or Chiang Mai business, a LINE Official Account is the natural place to take bookings and send offers, with Facebook for discovery. LINE's plan prices could not be verified for this guide, so check them on LINE's business site before budgeting.

## What Works in Vietnam?

Zalo had 78.3 million monthly active users, 77.0% of the population, according to its parent company VNG as reported by DataReportal (mid-2025, up 0.9%). Facebook ad reach is 79.0 million and TikTok reaches 76.1 million adults. YouTube reaches 62.1 million.

For a business in Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi, the same logic applies: be reachable on Zalo and Facebook. Zalo Official Account pricing could not be verified here, so confirm it on Zalo's business pages.

## What Works in Indonesia?

Indonesia is the largest of the four: 230 million internet users and 180 million social media user identities. Facebook ad reach is 121 million, Instagram 108 million, YouTube 151 million and TikTok 180 million adults (88.9% of adults). TikTok reach rose 15 million in a year but fell 14.3 million in the last quarter, so expect corrections.

With scale comes language and regional variety. No source here gives local-business results, so start with one city and one platform.

## How Should You Test a Small Budget? (worked example)

All inputs are an **assumption**: US$300 a month for a single-location business in one country, split as $150 for Facebook or Instagram ads, $100 for TikTok-style video content, $50 held for content and design.

Run it for four weeks and record enquiries by source. Keep the platform where cost per enquiry is lowest, then move the other budget there.

Decision rule: if no platform produces an enquiry at a cost below a first order's margin, change the offer before you add budget. The [marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to set that margin.

## FAQ

### Is Facebook still the main platform in the Philippines?

By ad reach, yes: 95.8 million, 81.9% of the population. TikTok and Instagram are growing but smaller.

### Do I need LINE in Thailand?

LINE has 56 million monthly users, 78.2% of the population. Most Thai customers can be reached there, so a business account is worth testing.

### Is Zalo only for Vietnam?

DataReportal's figures cover Vietnam: 78.3 million monthly users. This guide has no data on Zalo elsewhere.

### What does Google Ads cost in these countries?

No source checked for this guide publishes local click prices, so none are quoted. Read the cost from Google Ads after a small test.
