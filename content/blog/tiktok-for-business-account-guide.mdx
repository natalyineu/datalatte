## How do you switch to a TikTok Business Account?

TikTok's business page describes the steps: open Settings and privacy in the app, choose Manage account, then Switch to Business Account. You then choose a business category, which TikTok says gives you content, events and solutions tailored to it.

Before switching, decide who owns the login. Use a company email and keep the password in a shared password manager, so the account does not belong to one employee. Then fill in the profile: name, a one-line description of what you sell and where, and the business contact details.

## What does a Business Account give you?

TikTok's business account page names three things: an Analytics feature that shows how your content performs, account growth and audience demographics; creative tools; and features for driving sales and brand presence. The account itself has no fee. Advertising is paid and covered separately.

TikTok's help articles also say a Business Account lets you add business contact information and a website. The exact fields available can change by country and by verification status, so open Edit profile and see what your account shows.

## Which music can a business use?

TikTok's Commercial Music Library is described as a pre-cleared global music library of 1 million songs. Businesses can use the songs at no cost. TikTok says businesses should not use the general music library for commercial purposes and should use the Commercial Music Library instead.

That covers organic content, video ads and branded content, including duets, reacts and stitches with user videos. If you reuse another user's original sound or any other licensed sound, TikTok advises checking with legal counsel that you hold a proper licence. For a salon or café, the safe habit is to pick sounds from the commercial library only.

## What is the minimum you can spend on TikTok ads?

TikTok's budget help (last updated July 2026) sets these minimums in Ads Manager:

| Level | Daily budget | Lifetime budget |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign | Must exceed $50 | Must exceed $50 |
| Ad group | Must exceed $20 | Total days × the minimum daily budget |

TikTok's own example: a 31-day ad group needs at least $620 lifetime. The figures are in US dollars on the US page. Other countries show their own currency minimums, so check Ads Manager.

You do not need ads to use the account. Many local businesses start with a month of organic posts and only add a paid test after they know which videos get saves and profile visits. Our [TikTok ads cost guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-guide) goes through typical spend.

## What should you add before you run ads?

TikTok Pixel is code placed on your website that shares site events with TikTok, so you can measure traffic and campaign performance. TikTok recommends events that follow the customer journey, such as viewing a product page, adding to cart and purchase. For a service business, a booking confirmation page is the event that matters.

TikTok's page does not state a price for the pixel. The help article says it also collects data such as IP addresses and device details, so mention it in your privacy policy and cookie banner.

## Worked example: a 30-day plan for a café

All figures here are **assumptions** to show the arithmetic, not benchmarks.

| Week | Action | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Switch to Business Account, fill profile, pick 10 sounds from the Commercial Music Library | $0 |
| 2 | Post 3 short videos: the menu, the morning rush, one staff face | $0 |
| 3 | Post 3 more and note which get the most saves and profile views in Analytics | $0 |
| 4 | Optional paid test on the best video: one ad group just over $20 a day for 7 days | about $140 |

A 7-day test at $20 a day is about $140 if the daily minimum were exactly $20. Because the rule says the budget must exceed $20, plan slightly above that. The campaign-level rule of more than $50 a day applies when you set the budget at campaign level. If your budget is smaller, the organic-first route is the practical one. ## FAQ

### Is a TikTok Business Account free?

Yes. TikTok describes it as a free account. Ads are paid separately.

### Can I use any song on a Business Account?

TikTok says businesses should use the Commercial Music Library, which is pre-cleared and free to use for businesses, and not the general library.

### How much do I need to start TikTok ads?

According to TikTok's July 2026 help page, a daily campaign budget must exceed $50 and an ad group daily budget must exceed $20 in the US. A 31-day ad group needs at least $620.

### Can I switch back to a personal account?

TikTok's support guidance reported in search results advises against switching back and forth, since a verification may be lost. That page could not be opened directly, so check the wording in the app before you switch.

### Do I need a website?

No for posting. For ads that drive bookings or orders, a website with the TikTok Pixel lets you measure results.
