## How do you set up a TikTok Business Account?

Open your profile in the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy, and look for the account-type option. Switch to a business account, then pick the category that fits your work. Add your business name, a one-line description of what you offer and where, and your contact details. The TikTok Business Help Center has the current steps, so check there if the menus differ from what you see.

Before you switch, decide who owns the login. Use a shared company email and store the password in a shared password manager. That way the account does not depend on one employee. If staff post for you, agree who approves each video before it goes live.

## What does a business account add for a small business?

The main benefit is analytics. Bookeo advises checking when followers are online, using the Insights tab, which needs a business account. That is written for Instagram and Facebook, but the same habit works on TikTok: post when your viewers are active and compare results week to week.

Add your TikTok link to your Google Business Profile as well. Google allows one social media link per platform, including TikTok, so customers searching for you can find your videos. Keep the link pointing to the account they should follow, not a personal profile.

## What music and video rules should you follow?

Check the current length limits and music permissions before uploading. Adwave's guide to short video makes this point for Reels and Shorts, and the same caution applies on TikTok. A sound that works for personal posts may not be cleared for a business account, so confirm which sounds TikTok allows for commercial use in its Business Help Center.

For ads, keep videos short. Adwave says platforms like Facebook, Instagram and TikTok see the best results from video ads of 15 seconds or shorter. Start with one clear message, such as your menu, a service or a booking link, and show it in the first two seconds.

## What does TikTok advertising cost to start?

Adwave's published TikTok option starts at $91 a day. It runs only after you turn it on in Customize and connect a TikTok account, and it is not in the default live mix. Its cross-channel budget starts at $30 a day across Meta, Google, TikTok, Reddit, web and TV. LeadsBridge, cited on Adobe Express, puts TikTok daily campaign budgets at $50. These are vendor and secondary figures, not TikTok's own price list.

When you open Ads Manager, TikTok asks for your country, time zone, billing currency and legal business name before you build a campaign. Check the minimums shown in your currency, because they vary by market. ## How should you plan the first 30 days?

Start slower than you think. Appointy advises not starting with more than two or three posts a day, because high volume early on can curb the reach of earlier posts when you have few followers. Bellwether Coffee lists TikTok at about one hour a day for a coffee shop, which is a useful planning figure for a team with limited time.

The table below uses those figures. The hours are an assumption for a café, not a benchmark.

## Worked example: is a $91-a-day test worth it for a café?

All inputs below are **assumptions** for the arithmetic. The $91 figure is Adwave's published minimum, and your Ads Manager minimum may differ.

- Test length: 7 days (assumption)
- Daily budget: $91 (Adwave minimum)
- Total spend: 7 × $91 = $637
- Average order value: $6 (assumption)
- Orders needed to cover the spend: $637 ÷ $6 ≈ 107 orders
- Orders from the test: 25 (assumption), which brings in $150 and leaves a gap of $487

On these assumptions the test loses money, so the organic-first route is the sensible starting point. If one video earns strong saves and visits, a smaller paid test on that video is easier to justify. Keep the frequency of your ads in check too, since repeated exposure to the same audience can wear out a test. ## FAQ

### Is there a fee for a TikTok Business Account?

The sources used here do not state a fee for the account itself. Check the switch screen in the app. Ads are paid separately, and Adwave lists its TikTok option from $91 a day.

### Can a small business use any song in its videos?

No. Check music permissions before uploading, and confirm in TikTok's Business Help Center which sounds are cleared for business use.

### Should I start with ads or organic posts?

Start with organic posts. Post two or three short videos a day at most, track which get saves and profile visits, and test one winning video with a small paid budget once you have data.

### How long should a TikTok video be for ads?

Adwave advises 15 seconds or shorter for video ads on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Show your offer in the first few seconds.
