## Why Do Nail Clients Check Instagram First?

Clients use Instagram to judge a salon before they book. The [Bookeo guide](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/) also says around 4 in 5 beauty shoppers use Instagram daily, though it gives no source for that figure. Treat it as a vendor claim.

Adobe Express, citing DataReportal, says at least 1.7 billion people use Instagram. It also reports that the largest age group on the platform is 25 to 34, followed by 18 to 24. Those are the ages most likely to book a manicure, so your captions and post style should suit that audience.

## What Should a Nail Salon Post on Instagram?

Post the work itself. [Trafft](https://trafft.com/salon-ads/) describes using Instagram and Facebook to showcase your work, run targeted ads and offer promotions. For a nail salon, that means finished sets, the shade or style named in the caption, and a short note on what the client chose.

Useful post types for a nail salon:

- Finished sets, shown close up in good light
- Short time-lapse clips of one design being applied
- Care tips tied to a service you sell, such as cuticle work or gel removal
- A weekly availability post with the booking link in the caption
- Stories for same-week openings, since [Instagram and Facebook stories are temporary](https://www.associatedhairprofessionals.com/updates/blog-posts/power-social-media-your-hairstyling-brand) and suit quick, time-bound offers

The [Glammatic blog](https://www.glammatic.com/blog) promises six post ideas a salon can use within the week, which is a useful starting list. The [Instagram plan for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons) uses the same logic and can be adapted to nails.

How often to post? A [Hootsuite recommendation, as cited by Salon Biz Software](https://salonbizsoftware.com/blog/daily-salon-social-media-marketing-ideas-how-to-engage-your-audience/), is 2 to 7 posts per week on Instagram (secondary). The worked example below uses three, labelled as an assumption.

## How Do You Connect Instagram to Your Booking Path?

Start with your Google Business Profile. Google's help page says [you can add one social media link per platform](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617) to your Business Profile, and Instagram is one of the platforms listed. Use that slot so people who find you on Google can reach your feed in one tap.

On the Instagram side, [Appointy's salon and fitness guide](https://blog.appointy.com/2020/12/11/social-media-marketing-for-fitness-studio-gym/) states that Instagram and Facebook let you add a Book Now button to your profile (secondary, from a booking software vendor). Check in the app whether the button appears for your account, and whether your booking tool connects to it. If it does not, put your booking link in the bio and in every Story.

Writing and scheduling posts takes time, and some of that can be handed off. Sintra's vendor article claims a small business that once spent a Sunday planning a month of Instagram posts [can build that same calendar in under an hour](https://sintra.ai/blog/ai-social-media/best-social-media-ai-agents) with an agent. That is a vendor claim, not a measured result. For the options, see [an AI agent for social media posting](/blog/ai-agent-for-social-media-posting-local-business).

## How Do You Measure Bookings From Instagram?

Count appointments, not likes. One London agency's [monthly reporting service](https://oneofonegroup.co.uk/social-media-marketing/hair-salons/) covers followers, reach and enquiries, and names enquiries as the most important figure. Follow that order: record enquiries and booking taps each month, then look at which posts produced them.

If you want a dashboard instead of a spreadsheet, compare the tools in [social media analytics for hair salons](/blog/social-media-analytics-for-hair-salons) or [AI-powered analytics for local businesses](/blog/ai-powered-social-media-analytics-for-local-businesses). The same measures apply to nails.

## Should a Nail Salon Run Instagram Ads?

Only after the organic basics work. Meta's Ads Manager, per [Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-advertising) (secondary), is a central place to run ads across Instagram and Facebook. Adobe's figures put average Instagram ad costs between $0.20 and $2 per click, but those come from AdRoll's 2021 data, so check current prices in the ad platform before you commit.

A grooming business example from [GroomSoft](https://blog.groomsoft.com/growing-your-grooming-business/) is a hypothetical: it projects 200 to 300 new clients if 2% of reached users book. Use that 2% only as a starting assumption, and replace it with your own result after a test.

## Worked Example: A Monthly Instagram Budget

Every input below is an assumption. Replace it with your own numbers from Instagram Insights and your booking software.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Posts per week | 3 (assumption) |
| Minutes per post | 20 (assumption) |
| Hours per month | about 4.3 (3 × 20 min × 4.33 weeks) |
| Profile visits per month | 300 (assumption) |
| Visitors who tap the booking link (4%) | 12 |
| Taps that become appointments (25%) | 3 |
| Average ticket | $55 (assumption) |
| Monthly revenue from organic posts | $165 |
| Revenue per hour of posting | about $38, before product costs |

Now test a small ad budget. Assume $150 per month at $1.00 per click, which sits inside the $0.20 to $2 range above. That buys 150 clicks. At a 2% booking rate (assumption, borrowed from the grooming example), that is 3 bookings, or $50 of ad spend per booking. Against a $55 ticket, the ad only breaks even before product costs. Test it for one month, then decide whether the cost per booking is worth keeping.

## FAQ

### How often should a nail salon post on Instagram?

Hootsuite's recommendation, as cited by Salon Biz Software, is 2 to 7 posts per week. Pick a rate you can keep, such as three in the example, and review results monthly.

### Can I link Instagram from my Google listing?

Yes. Google's help page says you can add one social media link per platform to your Business Profile, and Instagram is listed.

### Do I need an Instagram ad budget to get bookings?

No. The worked example shows organic posts alone can produce bookings. Ads are a separate test, and at the assumed rates they only break even.

### Can an AI tool write a month of posts for me?

Vendors claim this is possible, including a month of calendar work in under an hour, but those are vendor claims. Check the output against your own services and prices before posting.
