For most local businesses with a small budget, Instagram ads (bought through Meta Ads Manager) are the easier first test, because the budget floor is lower and the location tools are finer. TikTok's help center requires a daily campaign budget above $50 and an ad group budget above $20, which means roughly $1,500 or more over a month. TikTok can still work if you have video you can produce and the budget to learn.

Neither platform publishes a clean head-to-head cost comparison. This page uses only what the vendors and LocaliQ publish, and says where a number is missing.

## What Do the Two Platforms Cost?

LocaliQ's Facebook advertising benchmarks, updated 23 September 2026, report Meta averages of $0.60 per click for traffic campaigns and $1.80 per click and $27.39 per lead for leads campaigns. The page says "Facebook ads" and "Meta ads" and does not split out Instagram, so treat the numbers as a proxy.

Selected industries in the same report, leads campaigns: Beauty and Personal Care $50.91 per lead, Sports and Recreation $15.49. For TikTok, none of the sources cited here publishes a cost per click or lead for local advertisers, so there is no honest side-by-side figure.

## What Is the Minimum Budget on Each?

TikTok's help article says a daily budget must exceed $50 at campaign level and $20 at ad group level. For a lifetime budget, the ad group minimum is $20 multiplied by the number of days, such as $620 for 31 days. The page shows a dollar sign but does not name the currency.

Meta's budget help pages did not load, so this page does not quote a Meta minimum. Ads Manager shows an error if a budget is too low, which is the check to rely on. A small business can usually start a Meta test with less, but check the current screen for your currency.

## Which Platform Targets Local Customers Better?

TikTok lists location options such as city, county, state, DMA regions and postal code, up to 3,000 locations per ad group. It does not support excluding locations or zip codes, and it warns that very small areas can sharply reduce impressions and clicks. It also says delivery and accuracy are not guaranteed everywhere.

The TikTok page does not mention a radius option. Meta's location help page did not load, so check Ads Manager for the radius options in your account. If you need a tight neighbourhood target, test that before you commit budget. Our guide on [targeting local customers on TikTok](/blog/how-to-target-local-customers-on-tiktok) goes deeper.

## What Does the Money Buy? A Worked Example

The numbers are **assumptions** built on the published floors and benchmarks. A lead is a booking, enquiry or call.

| Item | Instagram via Meta | TikTok |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $600 (assumption) | $1,500 (30 days at the $50 floor) |
| Cost basis | $27.39 per lead (all-industry Meta average) | No published benchmark |
| Leads | about 22 | Unknown until tested |

At $600, the Meta figure gives about 22 leads ($600 / $27.39). The TikTok row shows that the platform floor alone sets the minimum test size. A local café, salon or gym with a $600 budget cannot meet that floor. A business with $1,500 or more to spare can run a TikTok test, but should judge it on cost per booking, not views.

## When Should a Local Business Pick Each?

Choose Instagram first if you already post photos or reels, have a booking link, and want a small test. See our guide to [Instagram ads for local business](/blog/instagram-ads-for-local-business-complete-2026-guide) and [what Instagram ads cost](/blog/how-much-do-instagram-ads-cost-2026-pricing-breakdown).

Choose TikTok if you can shoot short video regularly, your customers are likely on the app, and you can afford the minimums. ## FAQ

### Is TikTok or Instagram better for local business ads?
Neither is better in published data. Instagram has a lower budget floor and TikTok needs more video effort. Test with your own offer and measure cost per booking.

### What is the minimum TikTok ad budget?
TikTok's help center says over $50 a day at campaign level and over $20 a day at ad group level.

### How much does an Instagram lead cost?
LocaliQ's September 2026 Meta benchmark for leads campaigns is $27.39 per lead across industries. It does not separate Instagram from Facebook.

### Can I exclude areas on TikTok?
No. TikTok says it does not support excluding locations or zip codes.
