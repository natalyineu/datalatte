## What Does a Gutter Cleaning Job Pay?

Bob Vila's cost guide lists the typical range as $119 to $228, with a national average of $163. It was last updated in April 2024 and the figures come from Angi and HomeAdvisor, so treat it as secondary and a bit dated. Single-story homes run about $70 to $250 and second or third-story gutters about $100 to $500.

The same guide lists add-ons: downspout cleaning at $50 to $100 per job, minor repairs at $100 to $250 and roof cleaning at $250 to $600. Add-ons raise the value of a visit, which matters for your ad maths below.

## How Should You Use Google Business Profile?

Gutter cleaners usually visit customers rather than serve them at an address. Google defines a service-area business as one that "visits or delivers to customers directly but doesn't serve customers at their business address." If that is you, remove your address and enter only the service area.

Google allows up to 20 service areas by city, postal code or other area, not by radius. The overall area should stay within about two hours of driving time from your base. List the places you actually serve, with a profile per business, not per town. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists the other fields.

## Why Do Reviews Matter for Gutter Cleaning?

Customers cannot judge the work from the ground, so they lean on other people's experience. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults found 97% of consumers read online reviews and 41% always read them for local businesses. Ask each customer for a review after a clean-out, ideally with photos of the cleared gutter.

For a repeatable process, see [Google Business Profile reviews](/blog/google-business-profile-reviews) and [automating review requests](/blog/how-to-automate-google-review-requests).

## Should You Run Google Ads or Local Services Ads?

Both are options, with different billing. Google's help says Local Services Ads let you pay only for leads related to your business, and that they are available only for certain service categories and areas. Check Google's eligibility tool for your ZIP code to see whether gutter work is offered. No official list read for this article named gutter cleaning, so do not assume it.

For Search ads, LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks list Home and Home Improvement at $8.33 per click, 8.05% conversion and $90.92 per lead. Our [Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-services-ads-complete-guide) and [Google Ads for contractors](/blog/google-ads-for-contractors) explain the setup.

## Worked Example: Do Paid Clicks Pay on a $163 Job?

All inputs are **assumptions** built on the benchmarks above. A conversion is a call or form.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget | $1,000 |
| CPC | $8.33 |
| Clicks | about 120 |
| Conversion rate | 8.05% |
| Leads | about 9.7 |
| Cost per lead | about $103 |
| Share of leads who book (assumption) | 50% |
| Cost per booked job | about $207 |
| Average job (secondary source) | $163 |

At about $207 to win a $163 job, the first visit loses money on revenue alone. The fix is not a bigger budget. Sell a package (gutters plus downspouts), a twice-a-year plan or a bundle with another service, and bid only in areas near your base. If your own numbers show a cheaper click or a higher booking rate, the result changes.

## What Free Channels Work for Gutter Cleaning?

Cheap tactics fit a cheap job. Use yard signs and door hangers on streets you have just served, a short note to past customers before each season, and neighbour referrals. These are advice, not measured results, so track how each new customer found you.

## FAQ

### How much should I charge for gutter cleaning?

Bob Vila reports a $119 to $228 range with a $163 average, based on Angi and HomeAdvisor data from April 2024. Your price depends on height, length and condition.

### Do I need an address for Google Business Profile?

Not if you serve customers at their homes. Google says to remove your address and enter service areas.

### Is Google Ads worth it for gutter cleaning?

Only if your cost per booked job sits below your job value. On benchmark numbers it does not for a single $163 visit, but add-ons and repeat plans can change it.

### Are Local Services Ads available for gutters?

Google says they are limited to certain categories and areas. Check the eligibility tool for your ZIP code.

### How many service areas can I list?

Google allows up to 20, by city, postal code or other area, not by radius.
