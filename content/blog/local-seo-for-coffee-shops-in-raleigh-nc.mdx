## How much does the Google Business Profile matter for the map pack?

Ahrefs reports that 36% of SEOs think a Google Business Profile is the most important ranking factor for the map pack in 2021, up from 23% in 2013 ([Ahrefs local SEO guide](https://ahrefs.com/seo/local-seo), secondary). The figures come from a BrightLocal survey of practitioners. They show opinion, not a measured ranking formula, so use them as direction rather than a rule.

The profile also has to be verified before it works. BrewRound says Google mails a letter containing a code, and the profile does not appear in results until it is verified ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary, 2020). Check the verification status first, since every other step depends on it.

For a fuller walkthrough of the listing setup, see [coffee shop Google Business Profile optimization](/blog/coffee-shop-google-business-profile-optimization).

## Which category should a Raleigh coffee shop choose?

Per Diem recommends choosing "Coffee shop" or a related term rather than something broad such as "Restaurant" ([Per Diem](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). A broad category makes it harder for Google to match the shop to coffee searches.

BrewRound notes that only one category can be selected at signup, and more can be added later ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary, 2020). Set "Coffee shop" as the primary category first. Add secondary terms such as café or espresso bar afterwards, once the listing is live and verified.

## What should the profile say, and how should Q&A be used?

Per Diem advises coffee shops to answer common questions proactively in the Q&A section, such as whether they offer free Wi-Fi, dairy-free options, or dogs on the patio ([Per Diem](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). Seeding these answers means the shop controls the first message a customer reads.

Google also lets users message a business directly through its listing ([Per Diem](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). Check the messaging inbox on the same schedule as posts, so a question does not go unanswered. Write the description in plain language and mention the Raleigh neighbourhood only where it reads naturally. For more profile tactics, see [Google Business Profile tips for coffee shops](/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-tips).

## How often should a coffee shop publish posts?

The Restaurant HQ recommends posting on social media at least once per week, ideally one to two times per day ([The Restaurant HQ](https://www.therestauranthq.com/operations/coffee-shop-marketing/), secondary). That guidance is for social media, but it gives a reasonable floor for Business Profile posts too.

A post only reaches people when it is live. Google's help page says a post is public on Search and Maps when its status is Live, and Pending and Not approved posts do not show ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en), primary). After publishing, open the post and confirm it reads Live. A post stuck in Pending is invisible to customers.

## How do reviews affect Google Maps visibility?

Google states that reviews appear next to a business's profile in Maps and Search ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122), primary). A steady flow of recent, specific reviews is therefore visible at the moment a customer is choosing a shop.

Ask customers for reviews in their own words, and reply to each one with the same care. Avoid scripted review requests that push people toward particular phrases, because that can breach Google's review policies. Track the number of reviews each month rather than chasing a fixed target that no source supports for Raleigh coffee shops.

## Do citations and the Google Ads link need attention?

Google Ads location assets pull their data directly from Google Maps and Google Business Profile, and successful corrections made there flow into a linked Google Ads account ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en), primary). A clean profile therefore helps paid search as well as organic results. Paid keyword planning is covered in [the best Google ad keywords for coffee shops in 2026](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-coffee-shops-in-2026).

For citations, keep the business name, address and phone number identical across every directory. Abbreviations such as "St." and "Street" count as differences, so choose one format and apply it everywhere. Fix mismatches on existing listings before creating new ones.

## FAQ

### Is a Google Business Profile free for a coffee shop?

Google describes its Business Profile as free to set up ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/), primary). A Google account is needed, and BrewRound notes that account registration is also free ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary, 2020).

### Which category should I pick first?

Choose "Coffee shop" or a related term rather than "Restaurant" ([Per Diem](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). Add more categories after the profile is live, since only one can be chosen at signup.

### Why isn't my post showing?

A post appears publicly only when its status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts do not show on Search or Maps ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en), primary).

### Does the map pack depend on the Business Profile?

A BrightLocal survey reported in Ahrefs found 36% of SEOs rate the profile as the most important map pack factor ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/seo/local-seo), secondary). Treat this as expert opinion from 2021, not a fixed ranking rule.
