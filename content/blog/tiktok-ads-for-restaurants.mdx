## Is Your Restaurant's Audience on TikTok?

TikTok is used by 37% of US adults in Pew Research Center's 2026 survey, the same share as in 2025 and up from 33% in 2024. The survey covered 5,511 US adults between January 30 and June 18, 2026, with a margin of error of plus or minus 1.8 percentage points. Pew's figures do not cover UK, Australian or Canadian adults.

Platform reach is not the same as local reach. Before spending, check whether your guests already watch short food video. A café near a university may find a younger audience than a steakhouse in a business district. Your own order data, delivery platform reviews and table-booking records will tell you more than a national average.

## How Much Should a Restaurant Spend on a TikTok Test?

Set the test budget first, then decide whether it is affordable. The sources reviewed here do not give TikTok's current minimum daily budgets, so check the figure inside Ads Manager before launching. This guide uses $20 a day as an assumption to show the maths.

A 14-day test at $20 a day spends $280. If the budget rises, the break-even count rises in proportion. Keep the test small enough that a poor result does not damage cash flow. Fixed budgets also make results easier to compare later.

Do not raise the budget while the test is still running. Changing spend mid-test makes the results harder to read. Meta's guidance on A/B testing recommends equal budgets for each version so the comparison stays fair ([Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1738164643098669)).

## How Do You Calculate Break-Even for a Restaurant Ad?

Divide the test spend by the gross profit on one redeemed offer. This tells you how many redemptions the ad must produce to pay for itself. The table uses assumptions throughout, apart from the spend, which follows from the assumed daily budget.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Daily budget | $20 (assumption) |
| Test length | 14 days (assumption) |
| Total spend | $280 |
| Average check per redeemed offer | $28 (assumption) |
| Gross margin on that check | 60% (assumption) |
| Gross profit per redemption | $16.80 |
| Redemptions needed to break even | 17 (rounded up from 16.7) |

On these assumptions, the ad needs 17 redeemed offers in 14 days to cover its cost on the first visit. If the average check is lower or the margin is thinner, the number rises. If the target is out of reach, change the offer or the video before adding budget.

Repeat visits can improve the result, but only count them once they are tracked. A guest who returns with a loyalty code is easier to measure than one who says they saw a video. Delivery commissions also change the margin, so a delivery order may need a higher check to clear the same break-even.

## Should You Measure Orders or Video Views?

Measure orders. A video can reach many people and bring in nobody. Views show whether the content holds attention, but they do not show whether a guest booked a table or ordered food.

Restaurant software vendor GloriaFood recommends adding a link to a website or online ordering page on the profile to increase conversions ([GloriaFood, secondary](https://www.gloriafood.com/tiktok-for-restaurants)). Use a unique offer for the ad, such as a menu item available only through the video, and count redemptions at the till. If guests order by phone, ask them to mention the code.

## How Should a Restaurant Test Two Ad Versions?

Test one variable at a time. Meta's help page on A/B testing is written for Meta ads rather than TikTok, but its logic applies. Each version should be shown to a separate segment of the audience, and both versions should have the same budget ([Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1738164643098669)).

Meta also advises against switching ad sets on and off by hand, because overlapping audiences make results unreliable. Apply the same principle on TikTok. Run two versions of a video with the same budget and audience, and keep the rest of the setup unchanged until the test ends.

Good variables to test are the opening hook, the dish shown in the first three seconds, or the offer itself. Changing the audience and the video at the same time makes the cause of any result impossible to identify.

## What Should the Ad Show?

Short, real footage often suits TikTok better than polished menu photography. GloriaFood describes hashtag challenges, staff-led clips, behind-the-scenes kitchen footage and collaborations with local micro-influencers as common formats ([GloriaFood, secondary](https://www.gloriafood.com/tiktok-for-restaurants)). These are ideas to test, not proven results.

## Is TikTok Better Than Other Channels for a Restaurant?

Pew's 2026 survey shows TikTok at 37% of US adults, compared with 51% for Instagram, 70% for Facebook and 84% for YouTube. The larger platforms still reach more adults, but these numbers say nothing about which channel brings in diners for a specific restaurant.

Compare channels by cost per redeemed offer, not by reach. If most orders come through delivery apps, the platform commission changes the margin and therefore the break-even figure. ## FAQ

### How much should a restaurant spend on TikTok ads?

Start with a fixed test budget that can be lost without harm. The example here uses an assumed $20 a day, which spends $280 over 14 days. Check the current minimum inside TikTok Ads Manager before launching.

### Can a restaurant target only people near its location?

The sources reviewed here do not list TikTok's location options. Confirm the targeting choices in Ads Manager before launching, and compare results by postcode or area if the platform reports them.

### Do TikTok ads guarantee a return?

No source reviewed here shows a guaranteed return for restaurants. Track redeemed offers against spend, and use the break-even figure to decide whether to continue.

### Should I test one ad or several?

Test two versions of one variable at a time, with the same budget and audience. Changing several things at once makes the result hard to interpret.

### Do I need an agency to run TikTok ads?

Not necessarily. Self-serve tools exist, but an agency can help with creative and tracking. Whichever route is taken, set the break-even number before starting.
