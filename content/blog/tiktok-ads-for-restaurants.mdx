A restaurant can start TikTok ads with a daily budget just above $20 per ad group, which makes a 14-day test about $294. TikTok's help page sets that floor: ad group daily budgets must be above $20, and campaign-level budgets above $50. TikTok does not publish a restaurant cost per view or per order, so judge the test by orders and table bookings, not by views.

This guide is for independent restaurants and cafes in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. It does not repeat the often-quoted claim that restaurants earn a set return on TikTok. No source opened for this guide supports one.

## How Much Do TikTok Ads Cost for a Restaurant?

The cost has two parts: the budget floor and the price per result. The floor is known. TikTok says daily and total budgets at campaign level must be above $50, and ad group daily budgets above $20. Lifetime budgets are calculated as $20 times the number of scheduled days.

The price per result is not published for restaurants and varies by city, offer and video. Treat any restaurant CPM or CPC you see in a blog post as unverified. For how the bidding methods work, see our [TikTok ads cost guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-guide).

## Which Objective Should a Restaurant Choose?

Pick one goal. TikTok's food advertising guide gives foot traffic to a restaurant, online orders and brand awareness as separate goals that need different approaches. For a local restaurant, orders or booking clicks are usually easiest to measure.

TikTok's bidding page says conversions and lead generation use oCPM by default, traffic uses CPC, and Reach uses CPM. Choose the objective first, then let the bidding method follow. ## How Do You Target People Near the Restaurant?

TikTok lists location types that include city, county, DMA region, state, urban district and zip or postal code. A radius around the restaurant is not listed on that page. TikTok also says it does not support excluding locations or zip codes, so choose the areas you want rather than removing others.

Very small areas can reduce impressions and may raise cost per result, TikTok warns. For a restaurant in a large city, start with the zip codes within a short drive and widen if delivery is poor. More options are covered in our [TikTok ads targeting guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-targeting-guide).

## What Should the Ad Look Like?

TikTok's food advertising guide says ads that feel authentic and native do best on the platform, and suggests a hook in the first three seconds with a clear call to action. It recommends an 80/20 split: 80% of content gives value, entertainment or education, and 20% is directly promotional.

For a restaurant, value content can be a dish being made, a kitchen moment, or a menu explained in 20 seconds. The promotional 20% is the offer. Spark Ads let you promote organic posts that already perform well, which suits restaurants that post often. Read our [Spark Ads guide](/blog/tiktok-spark-ads-guide) for the steps.

## How Do You Measure Results?

Install the TikTok Pixel and Events API before you spend. TikTok says this lets you track conversions, optimize for actions and calculate return on ad spend. If orders happen by phone or in person, use a unique offer such as a menu item only available through the ad, and count redemptions at the till.

Do not rely on views. A video can reach thousands of people and bring nobody to the door. Compare ad spend with redeemed offers, not with video plays.

## What Does a 14-Day Test Cost, and When Does It Pay?

All figures below are **assumptions** except the budget floor.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Daily budget | $21 (just above the $20 ad group floor) |
| Days | 14 |
| Total spend | $294 |
| Average check per redeemed offer | $28 (assumption) |
| Gross profit on that check | 60% (assumption) |
| Gross profit per redemption | about $16.80 |
| Redemptions needed to cover the test | 18 |

On these assumptions the test needs 18 redeemed offers in 14 days to break even on the first visit. If the average guest returns, the picture improves, but do not count on it before you have tracked returns. If you cannot reach 18 redemptions, change the offer or video before raising the budget.

<Callout type="tip" title="Do This Today">
Write down your average check and gross margin, then divide your planned test spend by the profit per check. That is your break-even number of redemptions.
</Callout>

## Is TikTok Better Than Instagram for a Restaurant?

It depends on where your guests already look for food. TikTok suits short, entertaining food video and discovery. Instagram and Facebook may reach an older audience, and Google Maps captures people already hungry. ## FAQ

### How much should a restaurant spend on TikTok ads?

At least the platform floor: just above $20 per day per ad group, or above $50 at campaign level. A 14-day test at $21 a day is about $294. Scale only after you count redemptions.

### Can I target only people near my restaurant?

Yes, by city, zip code, county or DMA region. TikTok's help page does not list a radius option, and it does not support excluding locations.

### What ad formats work for restaurants?

TikTok's guide favors native-feeling video and Spark Ads, which boost posts that already perform well.

### Do TikTok ads guarantee a return on ad spend?

No. TikTok publishes no restaurant return figure. Track redeemed offers and compare them with spend.

### Do I need an agency?

No. Ads Manager is self-serve. An agency helps with creative and tracking, but you can test alone with the budget above.
