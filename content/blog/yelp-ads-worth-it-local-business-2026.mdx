Yelp Ads are worth a small, time-limited test if your customers already search Yelp for your type of business. Yelp says you can spend as little as $5 a day on average, there are no term contracts for self-serve advertisers, and you can cancel at any time. Yelp does not publish a standard cost per click, so the only honest answer on return comes from your own test, measured in bookings.

This guide uses Yelp's own business pages, opened on 2026-10-25, and marks secondary sources. Examples with invented numbers are labelled as assumptions.

## What Do You Get When You Buy Yelp Ads?

Yelp says ads appear in Sponsored Results sections above and below organic search results, on competitors' pages, and off Yelp when people search elsewhere. You choose your photos and text or let Yelp optimize them, set a goal such as calls, messages, website visits or directions, and target by location and keyword.

Setup has seven steps in Yelp's guide: goal, photo, text, keywords, location, budget and billing information. You can see your budget, average cost per click and a chart of clicks in the Yelp Ads tab, and pause or cancel whenever you like.

Yelp also sells larger products, such as Showcase Ads and Spotlight Ads, with a dedicated account team. Those are not the self-serve ads this guide covers.

Yelp's own pages describe billing two ways. The getting-started guide says you pay when someone clicks. Another Yelp article says you set a monthly budget and Yelp maximizes clicks, leads and projects within it. In both, the monthly maximum is the ceiling. Read the billing terms on your own account before you enter a card.

WebFX also says there is no reliable average cost per click for Yelp Ads and that Yelp shows an estimate for your category, targeting and location. Treat any list of Yelp click prices without a source as a guess.

## Does Your Business Suit Yelp?

Check three things before spending. This is a judgment checklist, not a Yelp rule.

- Search your service and city on Yelp. If competitors have reviews and photos and you do not, ads send people to a weak profile.
- Look at your profile views in Yelp for Business. If few people find you now, ads may add little.
- Count how you get customers today. If most come from Google, test Google first with our [Google Ads vs Facebook Ads guide](/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-local-business) as a comparison.

The BrightLocal 2026 survey results in our [review platform comparison](/blog/review-platform-comparison-yelp-google-facebook-tripadvisor) show how Yelp's reach compares with Google and Facebook for reviews.

## What Does Yelp Claim About Results?

Yelp's ad page says "Get 4x more leads for your business with Yelp Ads" and that over half a million businesses advertise on Yelp. Another Yelp article says 82% of users hire or buy from a business they found on Yelp within a week, and adds that individual results may vary.

These are Yelp's own marketing claims. The pages opened for this article give no method behind them, so do not use them as a forecast. Case studies on the page, such as a stated 90% advertising ROI for a moving company, are single advertisers.

## Can You Ask Customers for Yelp Reviews?

Be careful. Yelp's own help pages returned errors when opened, so this comes from BrightLocal's summary of Yelp's guidelines (secondary). It says Yelp's guidelines state that businesses should not ask for reviews, that proactive requests may hurt your rating, and that Yelp's software may not recommend reviews that appear requested. Those reviews can sit in the "not recommended" section and do not count toward your star rating.

So ads and reviews work against each other if you push for reviews to support the ad. Put a Yelp link on your site, claim your profile and do not run a review campaign. For where to focus instead, read our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## A Worked Example: Is $300 a Month Profitable?

All numbers are **assumptions** for a service business. A real test will differ. Yelp's own estimate at setup replaces the click prices.

| | Lower click price | Higher click price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget (assumption) | $300 | $300 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | $2 | $6 |
| Clicks | 150 | 50 |
| Clicks that become a call or message (assumption) | 10%, so 15 | 10%, so 5 |
| Contacts that book (assumption) | 50%, so 7.5 | 50%, so 2.5 |
| Profit per booking (assumption) | $60 | $60 |
| Profit from ads | $450 | $150 |
| Result against $300 spend | +$150 | -$150 |

The same budget swings from a profit to a loss on click price alone. Before you start, calculate your break-even: spend divided by profit per booking gives the number of bookings you need. Here, $300 / $60 = 5 bookings. Track calls with a dedicated number or ask every new customer how they found you.

## How Should You Run a Test?

Start low, since Yelp lets you set a daily average and cancel freely. Run one goal, one location radius and a small keyword set for 30 to 60 days. Check the dashboard weekly for clicks and average cost per click, and check your booking records for the real result.

Stop if your cost per booking exceeds your profit per booking after two months. Compare with other channels using our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) and, for another review-driven option, [Nextdoor ads](/blog/nextdoor-ads-cost-2026).

## FAQ

### How much do Yelp Ads cost per month?

Yelp says you can spend as little as $5 a day on average. You set a monthly maximum, and Yelp says you will never pay more than it. WebFX, a secondary source, puts the starting point at $150 a month, which is about $5 a day.

### Is there a contract?

Yelp says there are no term contracts for self-serve advertisers and you can cancel at any time without a penalty. Sales-assisted plans may differ, so check your agreement.

### What does a Yelp click cost?

Yelp does not publish an average. It shows an estimated cost per click during setup for your category and area, and says this can change with demand.

### Do Yelp Ads remove competitor ads from my page?

Not by themselves. LocaliQ, a secondary source, lists removing competitor ads from your profile as part of a paid profile upgrade, separate from ads.

### Are Yelp Ads better than Google Ads?

Neither Yelp nor Google publishes a head-to-head comparison for local businesses. Test with a small budget and compare cost per booking.

### Do ads change my star rating?

Yelp's guidelines, as summarized by BrightLocal, are about review solicitation. No source opened for this article says ads change your rating.
