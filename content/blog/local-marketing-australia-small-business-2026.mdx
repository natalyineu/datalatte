An Australian small business should start local marketing with a complete Google Business Profile, then add a small Google Ads Search campaign aimed at a few suburbs, then use Facebook and Instagram to stay visible. With 97.1% of Australians online and about two-thirds living in capital cities, most customers can be reached through these three channels.

This guide covers cafés, salons, gyms, pet services and trades. Figures were checked on 24 October 2026. No Australian Google Ads click prices could be verified from an open source, so the cost examples use clearly labelled assumptions.

## How Big Is the Australian Small Business Market?

The ABS puts Australia's population at 27,921,150 at 31 March 2026, up 1.4% over the year. Capital cities held 18,750,888 people at 30 June 2025, which is about 68% of the national total when added to the regional figure of 8,862,800. That share is a calculation, not an ABS headline, and it means the common claim of 80% in capital cities is too high.

The ABS counted 2,814,778 actively trading businesses at 30 June 2026, of which 996,203 have employees. By subtraction, about 64.6% are non-employing, and 922,512 are small employers with 1 to 19 staff. Entries were 460,461 and exits 375,331 in 2025-26, so competition changes every year.

DataReportal reports 26.2 million internet users and 21.0 million social media user identities in late 2025. Ad reach is not the same as active users, and the figures overlap between platforms.

## How Do You Target Suburbs and "Near Me" Searches?

Google Ads radius targeting has a minimum of 1 km. Google recommends "Presence or interest" for Search and says "Presence" fits when you want only people physically in the area. Start with the suburbs you actually serve and add them to ad text and landing pages.

Add negative keywords for jobs, courses and services you do not offer, and check the Search terms report weekly. Google says negative keywords do not match close variants, so add plurals separately. ## Why Is Google Business Profile So Important in Australia?

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that reviews and links raise prominence. You cannot pay for a better local position. So the work is to verify the profile, keep hours, category and attributes accurate, add photos, and reply to reviews.

Do not create several profiles for the same business to cover more suburbs. Google's guidelines allow one service-area profile for a business that travels to customers, and separate profiles only where there are separate service areas and staff. ## What Do the Spam Act Rules Require?

ACMA guidance, as summarised in its published materials, says commercial emails and texts need three things: consent, clear sender identification, and a working unsubscribe. The unsubscribe should be actioned within 5 working days and stay functional for at least 30 days after sending. You must be able to show evidence of consent if asked.

These rules apply to messages that promote goods or services. The ACMA page itself returned a server error when checked, so details come from ACMA and business.gov.au documents found in search. Confirm the current wording before you send a campaign.

## Which Dates Matter for Australian Local Marketing?

Seasons run opposite to the northern hemisphere, and some holidays fall on different dates from the US and UK.

| Date | Why it matters | Source status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Father's Day, first Sunday in September (6 September 2026) | Gifts, grooming, hospitality | Secondary: listings |
| Melbourne Cup Day, Tuesday 3 November 2026 | Victorian public holiday, event bookings | Secondary: listings |

Melbourne Cup Day is a Victorian public holiday, not a national one, so a national campaign should not assume it. Check each state's public holiday list before you schedule ads. ## What Languages Should Australian Businesses Consider?

The 2021 Census shows Mandarin at 2.7% of people, Arabic 1.4%, Vietnamese 1.3%, Cantonese 1.2% and Punjabi 0.9% as the top non-English languages spoken at home. If your suburb has a large community of one language, a translated Business Profile description or booking page may help. Have a native speaker check any machine translation before you publish.

## FAQ

### Is Google Ads or Meta Ads better for an Australian local business?

Google Search reaches people already looking for your service, so it suits a small first budget. Meta reaches 17.7 million Facebook and 15.2 million Instagram users by ad reach, which suits staying visible. No Australian conversion benchmark could be verified for either.

### How important are Google reviews in Australia?

Google lists reviews and replies as part of local prominence. No verified Australian review-count thresholds exist, so aim to keep collecting and answering reviews.

### Is TikTok growing in Australia?

DataReportal reports TikTok ad reach of 10.9 million adults, up 13.9% since late 2024. That is reach, not active use.

### What are the email and SMS rules?

The Spam Act requires consent, sender identification and a working unsubscribe. Check the ACMA guidance for the current detail.

### How do I market in regional Australia?

About 8.9 million people lived in regional Australia in June 2025. No verified regional click prices exist, so test a small budget and compare with your own data.
