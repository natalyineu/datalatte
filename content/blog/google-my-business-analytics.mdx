Google My Business analytics are now found in the Performance section of your Google Business Profile. Sign in with the Google Account that manages a verified profile, search for your business, click Performance, pick a date range and click Apply. The page reports views, searches, calls, directions, website clicks and, where they apply, messages and bookings. Google counts unique viewers once a day and filters out repeat taps and spam, so the numbers will not match your phone log exactly.

This guide explains what each metric counts, where to find the data in other tools and how to keep a simple monthly record.

## Where Do You Find Your Business Profile Analytics?

There are three places:

- **Search or Maps.** In Google Search, open your Business Profile and click Performance. In the Google Maps app, tap Business, then See more next to Performance. Choose a time period and apply it.
- **Google Analytics.** You can link your profile in Admin under Product links, then Google Business Profile links. You need Owner or Manager on the profile and Editor or Administrator on the property.
- **Download.** In Business Profile Manager you can select profiles, choose Actions, then Insights, pick a timeframe and download a report.

Performance data is available only for verified profiles. If you have not verified yours, start with our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## What Does Each Metric Count?

Google's definitions, simplified:

| Metric | What it counts | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Views | People who viewed your profile on Search and Maps | Unique visitors, counted at most once a day, so the figure can be lower than other places |
| Searches | Search terms people used to find your business | Updated at the start of each month, up to 5 days to appear |
| Calls | Clicks on the call button | Needs a phone number on the profile; counts taps, not answered calls |
| Directions | People who asked for directions | Multiple taps, cancelled requests and spam are filtered out |
| Website clicks | Clicks on your website link | Pair with your site's own analytics |
| Messages | Distinct message conversations | Only appears if it applies to you |
| Bookings | Completed bookings | Needs bookings managed through a booking provider |

Only the metrics that apply to your business appear, and the data covers both organic results and Google Ads. Google's Insights suggestions may not be available for all profiles.

## What Does the Google Analytics Link Add?

The link shares interactions, website clicks, calls, directions, messages, bookings and menu clicks with a GA4 property, and a Google Business Profile collection appears in your reports. Three limits apply, per Google Analytics Help:

- Data is available for the last 6 months only, even if you pick a longer range.
- With several profiles linked, the data is combined, and you cannot filter by one location.
- The metrics cannot be used in custom explorations or comparisons.

So Analytics helps with one location, and with several it is less useful. Download or screenshot monthly numbers if you need history beyond six months. For more on analytics setup, see our [Google Business Profile insights guide](/blog/google-my-business-insights).

## What Changed Recently?

Google removed Business Profile chat and call history on July 31, 2024. Calls from your profile still come through, and web traffic, directions and other engagement metrics continue to be tracked. The BUSINESS_CONVERSATIONS metric is also no longer available in the Performance API. If an older guide tells you to read chat logs, that feature is gone.

Developers using the Performance API can read daily metrics such as desktop and mobile impressions on Maps and Search, direction requests, call clicks, website clicks, messaging clicks and bookings. Impressions by the same unique user in a day count once.

## How Do You Turn the Numbers Into Decisions?

Keep it small. Once a month, copy these into a sheet: views, calls, directions, website clicks and the top five searches. Compare month to month, and note any change you made, such as new photos or a category edit. Our [photos and videos guide](/blog/google-my-business-photos-and-videos) shows one change worth tracking.

An example, with **assumed** numbers: if calls rise from 20 to 26 after you add services and photos, and you answer 70% of calls and book half of those, the extra 6 calls are worth about 2 more bookings. The assumption is yours to replace with your own answer and booking rates. Compare with actual bookings, since Google counts taps, not customers.

## FAQ

### Is Google My Business the same as Google Business Profile?

Google renamed it. Its help pages now use Business Profile, and the performance report is called Performance.

### Why do my calls differ from my phone records?

Google counts clicks on the call button, not completed calls. Customers who find your number elsewhere are not counted.

### How often does the data update?

Google states only the timing for Searches: they update at the start of each month and can take up to 5 days to appear. It gives no schedule for the other metrics.

### Can I see data older than six months?

Not in Google Analytics, which shows the last 6 months. Download reports or record numbers monthly to keep history.
