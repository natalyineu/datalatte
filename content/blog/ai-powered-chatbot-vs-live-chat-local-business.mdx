For most local businesses, use an AI chatbot for the repeat questions (hours, prices, booking links) and keep a person for anything complex. The software costs $0 to about $49 a month on Tidio's list prices, while live chat costs staff time, which is usually the bigger bill. One survey also found that many customers dislike AI-only service, so always offer a route to a human.

This guide compares the two on cost, speed and fit, with a worked example and a decision rule.

## What Is the Difference Between an AI Chatbot and Live Chat?

An AI chatbot answers automatically from the content you give it, around the clock. Live chat connects a visitor to a person, so it only works while someone is available.

| | AI chatbot | Live chat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who answers | Software | A person |
| Hours | 24/7 | Only when staffed |
| Best for | Repeat questions, simple bookings | Complex, high-value or upset customers |
| Main cost | Subscription plus setup time | Staff time |
| Main risk | Wrong or stuck answers | Slow replies when you are busy |

## What Does Each Option Cost?

Tidio's [pricing page](https://www.tidio.com/pricing/) lists these plans:

| Plan | Monthly price | Billable conversations a month | AI conversations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 50 | First 50, lifetime |
| Starter | $24.17 | 100 | 50, one-off |
| Growth | From $49.17 | 250 to 2,000 | Not listed |

Two details matter. The free AI allowance is a one-time 50, not monthly, and Tidio sells its Lyro AI agent separately from $32.50 a month for 50 conversations. The page does not say whether the prices assume annual billing, so confirm at checkout.

Live chat tools can be cheap too, since the Free plan has 10 seats. The real cost is the person answering.

## What Does It Cost in Practice? A Worked Example

Everything below is an **assumption** for a salon with 120 chat conversations a month.

| Item | Chatbot first | Live chat only |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Software | $49.17 (Growth, 250 conversations) | $0 to $49.17 |
| Staff time | 10 hours a month handling escalations at $20 | 15 hours a week at $20 |
| Staff cost | $200 | about $1,300 |
| Total | about $249 | about $1,300 to $1,350 |

Staffed chat for 15 hours a week costs more than five times as much in this example. If 120 conversations are mostly the same five questions, the chatbot wins on cost. If each conversation books a $300 service, a human reply may earn its price. Replace the wage and the conversation count with yours.

## When Should You Choose a Chatbot, Live Chat or Both?

Choose by the type of question, not by the tool:

1. **Mostly hours, prices, parking and booking links:** chatbot.
2. **Consultations, custom quotes or $150 or more services:** live chat or a callback form.
3. **Both kinds of questions:** chatbot first, with a clear Talk to a person button.
4. **Fewer than about 30 chats a month:** skip both and put the answers on your booking page.

Gartner's survey (second-hand, via [Insurance-Canada.ca](https://insurance-canada.ca/2024/07/15/gartner-survey-ai-for-customer-service/)) found 64% of 5,728 customers would prefer companies not use AI in customer service, and 53% would consider switching. The top worry was not being able to reach a human. A visible handoff addresses that.

## How Do You Set Up a Chatbot Without Annoying Customers?

1. Write the 10 questions customers ask most and answer them in plain text.
2. Connect it to your booking link.
3. Add a handoff that passes the transcript to a person.
4. State your human hours on the widget.
5. Read the first 50 conversations and fix wrong answers.

## How Do You Measure Which One Works?

Track three numbers for 30 days: chats started, bookings from chats and the cost per booking (software plus staff time divided by bookings). Keep a record of conversations that ended without an answer. Published chat conversion rates come from other businesses, so test in your own account.

## FAQ

**Q: Is an AI chatbot cheaper than live chat?**
A: Usually, because live chat costs staff time. In the example above, the chatbot setup cost about $249 a month against roughly $1,300.

**Q: Can I get an AI chatbot for free?**
A: Tidio's Free plan includes 50 billable conversations a month, but only 50 AI conversations in total, not per month.

**Q: Will customers accept a chatbot?**
A: Some will not. Gartner's survey found 64% prefer companies not use AI in customer service, so make a human reachable.

**Q: Which converts better?**
A: There is no reliable published figure for local businesses. Test for 30 days and compare cost per booking.

**Q: Do I need a chatbot at all?**
A: Not under about 30 chats a month. Clear answers on your booking page may be enough.
