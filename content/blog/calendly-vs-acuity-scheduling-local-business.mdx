## What Do Calendly and Acuity Cost to Start?

A 2025 comparison guide from GoConverso lists Calendly's starting price at $10 a month, with a free plan limited to one event. The same guide lists Acuity Scheduling at $16 a month and says it has no free plan. Because [GoConverso's guide](https://docs.goconverso.com/en/guides/best-online-scheduling-system) is a secondary source, treat these figures as a starting point, not a quote.

The guide does not say whether the $10 figure is billed monthly or yearly, or whether it applies per user. Those details decide the real total, and prices change, so read the billing line on the live pricing page before you budget.

## Is Squarespace Scheduling the Same as Acuity?

Squarespace has confirmed that Squarespace Scheduling is now officially Acuity Scheduling. If your site was set up with Squarespace Scheduling, the tool you pay for is the same product under its new name, so the comparison in this article applies to you as well.

Squarespace's [help center](https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/sections/360000121907-Site-topic-guides) is the primary source for the rename. Check your current Squarespace billing before assuming the Acuity prices quoted here are what you pay.

## Which Tool Fits a Solo Owner or Consultant?

Calendly fits a solo owner whose main need is a link that clients use to pick a time. A free plan limited to one event means one kind of booking, such as a single consultation type. If you add a second event type, the free plan will likely no longer cover it, and the $10 starting price becomes the number to plan around.

Solo owners who take deposits or sell packages should check what the paid tier includes before choosing. The sources behind this article give price detail for Calendly but not a full feature list, so test a real booking end to end on the free plan first.

## Which Tool Fits a Team That Takes Many Bookings?

Acuity starts at $16 a month and has no free plan, so it is a fixed monthly cost from day one. That suits businesses where appointments are the core product, such as salons, studios and groomers. If you want an assistant to answer routine questions and take bookings, read our guide to [AI chatbots for local business booking and FAQs](/blog/ai-chatbot-for-local-business-booking-and-faqs) before adding one.

Some marketing agencies already work with Acuity. patientMoon, which markets pilates studios, says it integrates with booking systems including MINDBODY, Acuity, Square, Jane and Momence. If an agency runs your website or ads, ask whether it connects to the scheduling tool you choose.

## Does the Booking Flow Work on a Phone?

If clients book from a phone, the mobile flow is the test that matters most. Bookeo, a booking software vendor, recommends simplifying a booking flow that takes longer than 30 seconds or more than three taps. Use those two limits as a pass mark for either tool.

Run the test yourself. Open your booking link on your own phone, time how long it takes, and count the taps from the first screen to a confirmed slot. If it fails either limit, change the form or the event setup before paying for extra features.

## Which One Should You Pick?

Pick Calendly if you run a one-person service, need one event type and value a free starting point more than deposits or packages. Pick Acuity if appointments are the centre of the business and you would rather pay from the first month for a dedicated booking system.

Do not choose on the starting price alone. If you want scheduling bundled with email and marketing automation, compare the total in our [GoHighLevel pricing guide](/blog/gohighlevel-pricing-for-local-business) before deciding.

## What Should You Check Before Choosing?

1. Confirm the billing cycle behind each price on the live pricing page.
2. Confirm whether the price is per person or per account.
3. Book a test appointment on a phone and time it against the 30-second and three-tap limits.
4. Check whether the tool connects to your website, your agency or any chatbot you plan to use. Our guide to [AI agents for appointment booking](/blog/ai-agent-for-appointment-booking-local-business) explains what that connection involves.

## FAQ

### Is Calendly free?

Calendly has a free plan, which a 2025 comparison guide says is limited to one event. Paid plans start at $10 a month. Check the live page for current limits.

### Does Acuity Scheduling have a free plan?

No. A 2025 comparison guide says Acuity has no free plan and starts at $16 a month. Confirm any trial terms on Acuity's own website.

### Is Squarespace Scheduling still available?

Squarespace says Squarespace Scheduling is now officially Acuity Scheduling. It is the same product under the new name.

### Which is cheaper for three people?

It depends on whether Calendly's $10 is charged per person. If it is, three people cost about $30 a month, against Acuity's $16 starting price. If it is not, the answer changes, so check the live pricing page.
