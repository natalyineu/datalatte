## Why does local targeting beat broad reach for a small firm?

Local marketing means promoting products or services to people in a specific geographic area. For a small firm, that area is usually where most customers already come from, so relevance matters more than reach.

Salesforce uses a small bakery to illustrate the point. A $1,000 citywide campaign reached 10,000 people and produced 100 customers, a 1% conversion rate. The same $1,000 aimed at residents within a 5-mile radius reached 2,500 people and produced 200 customers, an 8% rate. These are illustrative figures from a vendor example, not measured UK results, but they show why targeting matters.

Salesforce's research adds that 80% of shoppers rate experience as highly as the product itself. A local firm can deliver that experience through accurate listings, quick replies and visible community ties. The [US local marketing guide for small business](/blog/local-marketing-united-states-small-business-2026) covers many of the same principles for American firms, which is useful for comparison but not a UK source.

## Where should a UK local business start?

Start with the basics that cost nothing to maintain: an accurate listing on the main map and search profiles, correct opening hours, services, photos and a clear phone number. These are the places a nearby customer checks first, so gaps there lose enquiries before any ad is paid for.

Next, make contacting the business easy. Add a messaging link or booking button wherever the phone number appears. Salesforce's guide stresses that the better you know your audience, the more specifically you can tailor offers, so note which questions customers ask most and answer them in the listing itself.

Reviews come after that. Ask recent customers for feedback in a simple, repeatable way, and reply to every review, positive or negative, on a fixed routine. A steady pattern of replies signals an active business to both customers and the owner's own follow-up process.

## How do community campaigns work for small firms?

Birdeye's UK examples show how local firms build interest without large budgets. Signature Brew, a London brewery, responded to pub closures during lockdown with "Pub in a Box", delivering craft beer, glassware, snacks, playlists and virtual pub quizzes to local customers. The campaign relied on packaging worth sharing, social media engagement and a clear reason to order.

The Kingdom of Fife campaign in Scotland took a shared approach. It built a digital directory of more than 200 local businesses, ran a #TasteofFife social campaign and promoted food trails through an app. Birdeye describes these as real stories from family-owned producers. Both examples come from a secondary vendor summary, so treat them as ideas to adapt rather than proven benchmarks.

For trades that book jobs, the software matters as much as the message. Compare options such as [Jobber and ServiceTitan for small local businesses](/blog/jobber-vs-servicetitan-small-business-local-business) before adding a booking flow to your marketing.

## Should you pay for ads or local services first?

Ads work best after the listing is complete, because paid traffic lands on pages that need to convert. If a customer clicks and finds no hours, no reviews and no way to message, the spend is wasted.

Local service listings can be a different route for certain trades. The [guide to whether Google Local Service Ads are worth it](/blog/google-local-service-ads-worth-it-local-business) explains how they differ from standard search ads. Whichever route you choose, start with a small fixed test budget covering one service or one area.

Judge the test by cost per booking, not cost per click. Divide monthly ad spend by the number of bookings it produces, then compare that figure with what an average booking is worth. If the cost per booking is higher than that value, change the offer, the targeting or the landing page before adding budget.

## What does a first-quarter budget look like?

The numbers below are **assumptions** for a one-person service business. They are not UK benchmarks and should be replaced with measured figures after 30 days.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly marketing budget | £300 |
| Listing setup and reviews (month 1) | £0 spend, about 3 hours of owner time |
| Messaging link and quick replies | £0 spend |
| Ads from month 2 | £250 |
| Cost per click | £2.00 (assumed) |
| Clicks per month | 125 (£250 divided by £2.00) |
| Share of clicks that enquire | 5% (assumed) |
| Enquiries per month | about 6 |
| Share of enquiries that book | 50% (assumed) |
| Bookings per month | about 3 |
| Cost per booking | about £83 (£250 divided by 3) |

If an average booking is worth less than £83 and customers rarely return, the ads lose money. If customers come back often, the cost per booking pays back over time. The purpose of the first quarter is to replace each assumption with a measured number before deciding whether to scale.

## How should you handle consent for emails and texts?

Before sending marketing emails, texts or direct social messages, check the UK rules that apply to each channel. The sources reviewed for this guide do not set those rules out, so this step should be completed using the regulator's own guidance rather than assumed.

Keep a record of how each contact opted in, and make unsubscribing simple. Treat those records as part of the marketing plan from the start, because a contact list built without them is hard to repair later.

## FAQ

### Why do local ads often convert better than broad ones?

Salesforce's bakery example shows a narrower audience producing more customers from the same spend. Proximity and relevance make a person more likely to act. The example is illustrative, so your own results will vary.

### Which channel should a UK small business start with?

Start with the listing customers already check, then add a messaging or booking option. Birdeye's examples suggest that a clear reason to engage, such as a bundle, event or shared directory, matters more than the channel alone.

### How do I know if my local marketing is working?

Track enquiries and bookings from each source, then compare the cost per booking with what a booking is worth to the business. Change one thing at a time so you can see what caused a result.

### Do I need to spend a lot to start?

No. Listing accuracy, reviews and quick replies depend mainly on time rather than spend. Paid ads can be tested with a small fixed budget.
