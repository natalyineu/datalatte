Educational businesses such as tutoring centres, music schools and driving schools can use TikTok in two ways: free short videos that show a teacher and a lesson, and paid lead-form ads that collect enquiries inside the app. TikTok's own education guide says enrolment decisions take longer, so plan a longer testing period and judge leads by quality, not volume. Ads need a daily ad group budget above $20 in the US.

This guide covers what TikTok itself recommends for education, what the minimum spend is, and how to work out whether a lead is worth buying. No public benchmark exists for cost per enrolment in local education, so the example uses labelled assumptions.

## What does TikTok recommend for education advertisers?

TikTok's education marketing guide lists four tools: Lead Generation instant forms that capture enquiries in the app, Spark Ads that turn proven organic posts into ads, the Creator Marketplace for tutorial or endorsement content, and interactive add-ons such as polls and countdowns for deadlines and open days.

Its advice is to offer value first, such as a study tip or a mini-lesson, before asking for contact details, and to keep users in the app because in-app forms reduce friction. It suggests short tutorial videos of about 30 to 60 seconds. The case figures in the guide (30% more leads and a 36% lower cost per lead for Universitas Terbuka; more than 500 form submissions for IDP Education) are TikTok's own advertiser stories, so treat them as vendor claims for large institutions, not as results a small school will match.

## What content works for a local school or tutor?

TikTok does not publish engagement rates for education by format, so no format can be called "best" from data. What its guide supports is tutorial-style content and a lead form offering something useful first. For a local school, that translates into a few repeatable video types:

- A 30 to 60 second mini-lesson from one named teacher
- A walk through a typical first class, with the room and the timetable shown
- Answers to one common parent or student question per video
- A reminder of a deadline or open day, using the platform's countdown add-on

Use sounds from TikTok's Commercial Music Library. TikTok describes it as a pre-cleared library of 1 million songs, free for businesses, and says businesses should not use the general library commercially. ## How much does it cost to start paid TikTok ads?

TikTok's Ads Manager help (last updated July 2026) says the daily budget at campaign level must exceed $50, and at ad group level must exceed $20. For lifetime budgets the ad group minimum is the total days times the minimum daily budget, so a 31-day ad group needs at least $620. These are US figures.

No source gives a typical cost per click or per lead for local education, so none is stated here. Run a small test, record your own cost per lead, and compare it with the value of one enrolment. For general ad pricing, see our [TikTok ads cost guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-guide).

## How do you judge lead quality?

TikTok's guide says to judge lead quality, not just volume, by tracking form completion, inquiry type and progression to the next enrolment stage. It also recommends measuring cost per lead, lead-to-application conversion and application-to-enrolment ratio, and using UTM parameters to compare TikTok with other channels.

That means your form should ask the questions that separate a serious enquiry from a curious one: which course, which start date, a preferred contact method. Then someone must call back fast and record the result. Without that record, you only know cost per form, not cost per student.

## Worked example: what can one enrolment justify?

All numbers are **assumptions** for illustration. A music school sells a 10-week course at $400 and keeps $250 after teacher pay.

| Step | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Ad budget for a test | $620 (31 days at $20 a day) |
| Leads from the test | 20 |
| Leads who book a trial | 8 |
| Trials who enrol | 4 |
| Cost per lead | $31 |
| Cost per enrolment | $155 |
| Margin per enrolment | $250 |
| Profit from the test | $380 |

If only 2 trials enrolled, cost per enrolment would be $310, above the $250 margin, and the test would lose money on the first course. Repeat students change that, so the school should also track whether students re-enrol. The numbers will differ for every school. The point is to set your break-even cost per enrolment before spending, using your own margin. For other options to compare, read [TikTok organic versus paid](/blog/tiktok-organic-vs-paid-strategy).

## FAQ

### Can a small school get results on TikTok without ads?

Organic posting is free and needs a Business Account and time. No published data shows how many enquiries it produces for local schools, so track enquiries by asking each new student how they found you.

### What is the minimum TikTok ad budget?

In the US, TikTok's July 2026 help page says campaign daily budgets must exceed $50 and ad group daily budgets must exceed $20.

### What are TikTok instant forms?

TikTok's education guide describes Lead Generation instant forms as in-app forms with custom questions on programme interest, enrolment timeline and contact preferences.

### Are there special ad rules for education?

TikTok's North America ad handbook page lists general policies such as misleading and false content and deceptive practices, but showed no education-specific rules in the visible text. Read the handbook for your country before you claim results or job outcomes.

### How long should I test?

TikTok's guide says education marketing often needs longer testing periods because decisions take longer. Plan weeks, not days.
