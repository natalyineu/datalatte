## Why Does Tourism Shape Marketing in South Dakota?

The South Dakota Department of Tourism released the 2025 figures at the Governor's Conference on Tourism in January 2026. The data was compiled by Tourism Economics, and local station KOTA reported nearly 15 million visitors, $5.16 billion in spending, nearly 60,000 jobs and more than $406 million in state and local taxes. KOTA is a secondary source, so confirm the final figures with the Department of Tourism.

For a cafe, motel, gym or gift shop, this means demand can swing by season and by location. A Sioux Falls dentist and a Keystone gift shop need different campaigns.

## What Does Google Ads Cost for a South Dakota Business?

There is no published South Dakota average. The national LocaliQ 2026 benchmarks show $5.42 per click across all industries, a 6.64% click-through rate and a $66.69 cost per lead. Category averages vary: Arts and Entertainment is $1.63 per click, Health and Fitness $6.17, and Home and Home Improvement $8.33.

## How Do You Reach Visitors Without Wasting Money on Locals?

The location setting decides who sees the ad. Google's help says broad geo targeting, called "Presence or interest", is a best practice on Search because it reaches people who are in a place and people who have shown interest in it. It suggests "Presence" when you only want users physically in the chosen locations.

A Black Hills attraction can use "Presence or interest" to reach people planning a trip. A Rapid City plumber should use "Presence" so the budget stays with people in the service area. Radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km.

## How Do You Get Found on Google Maps?

Google says local results rely mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. Prominence includes review count and ratings, and Google states there is no way to pay for a better local ranking.

That makes your free profile the first job: correct hours, categories, photos and a reply to every review. For a seasonal business, update holiday and summer hours before the season starts. The [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists the fields to complete.

## When Should You Plan Around the Sturgis Rally?

Travel South Dakota lists the 2027 Sturgis Motorcycle Rally for August 6 to 15, 2027, and describes it as drawing hundreds of thousands of enthusiasts to the Black Hills each year. It gives no official attendance figure.

If you sell food, lodging, repairs or merchandise nearby, start the profile updates and any ads at least a month earlier. Ad cost data for the rally period is not published, so watch your own click costs.

## Is Email and SMS Worth It for a Small SD Business?

Yes, because it reaches repeat customers without paying for each click. In the US, the FTC says each separate email that breaks CAN-SPAM can bring a penalty of up to $53,088. The rules include a physical postal address, a clear opt-out and honoring opt-outs within 10 business days.

## FAQ

### How much should a South Dakota small business spend on marketing?

No South Dakota figure is published. Start with a test budget you can afford to lose for 30 days, then scale only the channel with a measurable cost per call or booking.

### Does Google Ads work for rural South Dakota businesses?

It can, if the service area is small enough for the budget. Use a tight radius or named towns, and block irrelevant searches with negative keywords.

### Should I target tourists differently from locals?

Yes. Use "Presence or interest" for visitors planning a trip, and "Presence" for local services.

### When is the next Sturgis rally?

Travel South Dakota lists August 6 to 15, 2027.

### Are the tourism numbers official?

They come from the South Dakota Department of Tourism, reported by KOTA. Treat the figures as approximate.
