Local SEO for a Tampa pet groomer comes down to one goal: appearing in the Google Maps local pack, the top three business listings in local search results. Google pulls those listings from Business Profile data, so the profile is where most of the work happens. This guide covers the fields to fix first, how photos, posts and reviews affect map pack results, how citations must match, and a worked example of what extra calls can be worth.

## What decides whether a Tampa pet groomer appears in the map pack?

Adwave describes the local pack as the top three listings in local search results, and says the top three businesses are pulled directly from their Business Profile listings ([Adwave local SEO audit checklist](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-audit-checklist-15-things-to-fix-today), secondary; [Adwave, how to market a lawn care business](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-my-lawn-care-business), secondary). Your profile is therefore the main surface you control.

Adwave also cites an analysis claiming the primary category is the number one ranking factor for the Local Pack, but names no source for that analysis ([Adwave category guide](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones), secondary). Treat category as a field to set carefully, not a guaranteed lever. Search "dog groomer" in Tampa and note which categories the top three to five businesses use before you edit yours.

## Which Google Business Profile fields should Tampa groomers fix first?

Google states that a Business Profile is free to set up, so cost is not a barrier to starting ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/), primary). The table below pairs each field with what the sources say and what a grooming business should check.

| Profile element | What the sources say | What to check for your grooming business |
|---|---|---|
| Business description | Allows 750 characters ([Adwave beginner guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary) | Name the service, Tampa and your neighborhood, and who you groom. Keep it readable. |
| Photos | Adwave recommends at least 15 high-quality photos ([Adwave Maps marketing guide](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary); another Adwave page sets 10 as its floor ([Adwave beginner guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary) | Post-groom shots with owner consent, your grooming space, and your mobile van if you run one. |
| Posts | Posts show publicly only when their status is Live ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en), primary); Adwave advises at least one new post a week (secondary) | Schedule a weekly offer or seasonal update and confirm it shows as Live. |
| Hours, phone, address | Adwave says these must be 100% accurate (secondary) | Update holiday hours early and confirm the phone number rings to the right line. |
| Service area | A business that works from home or serves customers at their location can hide its street address and be designated a Service Area Business (secondary) | Use this for true mobile groomers. A storefront should show its address. |

Google's Q&A feature has been in Maps since 2017 ([Adwave Q&A guide](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-q-and-a), secondary). Seed it with the questions customers ask most often. For a fuller walkthrough of these fields, see the [pet groomer profile optimization guide](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-for-pet-groomers).

## How do reviews and posts change map pack results?

Adwave cites a third-party analysis of 2 million Business Profile pages, which found that businesses with over 200 Google reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack ([Adwave review guide](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business), secondary). The analysis describes a correlation, so read it as a strong signal rather than a guaranteed result.

Google states that reviews appear next to your profile in Maps and Search ([Google review tips](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122), primary). That makes each review a visible trust signal for a pet owner comparing groomers. Another page claims that 50 or more reviews with a 4.7+ average dominate top spots but names no source, so do not treat it as a threshold. For a broader look at how the profile is built, see [our Google Business Profile guide for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-for-pet-groomers).

## How should Tampa groomers build citations without conflicts?

Citations are listings of your name, address and phone number on other sites. Adwave says the NAP must be exactly the same on your website, your Business Profile and major directories such as Yelp, Apple Maps and Bing Places ([Adwave voice search guide](https://adwave.com/resources/voice-search-optimization-for-local-businesses), secondary). Another Adwave page says even punctuation counts, including whether "St." or "Street" is used ([Adwave beginner guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).

Audit existing listings before adding new ones. Fixing one mismatched listing is cheaper than building ten more with the wrong details. Adwave also says Apple Business Connect has captured a 7% share of maps-only searches and reports Profile Views, which is worth claiming if you want Apple Maps coverage ([Adwave Apple Business Connect guide](https://adwave.com/resources/apple-business-connect-the-google-business-profile-alternative-youre-ignoring), secondary).

## What is an extra booking worth to a Tampa groomer?

The example below uses assumed inputs. Replace them with your own GBP Insights and booking records.

- Calls from your profile per month: 40 (assumption)
- Share of calls that become a booking: 50% (assumption)
- Average groom price: $75 (assumption)

Bookings: 40 × 0.50 = 20 per month. Revenue: 20 × $75 = $1,500 per month.

If better profile work adds 10 calls a month (assumption), that is 5 more bookings and $375 more revenue at the same rates. Track the calls and direction requests in GBP Insights to test whether your inputs hold.

## Should a Tampa pet groomer also run Google Ads?

Google states that location data used in Google Ads comes directly from Google Maps and Google Business Profile, and corrections made there flow into linked accounts ([Google Ads location assets](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en), primary). If a business cannot use its profile for location assets, advertisers can select up to 10 Maps locations, but they must be locations that belong to them ([Google Ads location assets](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en), primary).

A Marketing 360 case study describes a mobile pet groomer that advertised on Facebook and Google, with the channel choice made to fit the business ([Marketing 360 case study](https://blog.marketing360.com/case-studies/pet-groomer-marketing-case-study-driving-success-with-digital-ads/), secondary). For a side-by-side comparison, read [Google Ads vs Facebook Ads for local businesses](/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-local-business-which-wins).

## Which local SEO metrics should a Tampa groomer track?

Adwave lists six metrics it calls the useful ones: Google Business Profile impressions, map pack views, clicks to call, direction requests, website visits from the profile, and branded search lift over time ([Adwave local search intent guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-search-intent), secondary). Check them monthly in GBP Insights and in Search Console.

## FAQ

### How many photos does a Tampa pet groomer need on Google?

Adwave recommends at least 15 high-quality photos, while another Adwave page uses 10 as its minimum. Aim for the higher number and rotate in new shots each season.

### Do Google Business Profile posts need approval before they show?

Google reviews each post after publishing. Posts show publicly on Search and Maps only when their status is Live, and Pending or Not approved posts do not show ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en), primary).

### Can a mobile pet groomer hide its home address?

If you work from home or serve customers at their location, the profile offers an option to hide your street address, and the business is then designated a Service Area Business ([Adwave beginner guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).

### Does a Google Business Profile cost anything?

Google states that a Business Profile is free to set up and appears on Google Search and Maps ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/), primary).
