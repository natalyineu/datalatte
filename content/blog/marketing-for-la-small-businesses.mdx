## How much does a lead cost for an LA small business?

These are US-wide averages from [LocaliQ's 2026 search advertising benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/) (thousands of customer campaigns, last updated June 2026). LA is a pricey auction, so expect your own numbers to be higher in competitive categories.

A lead is a form fill or call, not a paying customer.

## What should I fix before spending on ads?

Start with the free assets, because ads send people to them. [BrightLocal's Local Consumer Review Survey 2026](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) (1,002 US adults) found 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses, and 68% require at least four stars. It also found 80% are likely to use a business that responds to all its reviews, and Google is still the most-used review site at 71%.

A pre-ad checklist:

- Claim and verify your profile, then complete every field (hours, services, photos).
- Ask every happy customer for a Google review and reply to all of them.
- Make one landing page per service with a phone number and booking button above the fold.

## Worked example: what does $1,500 a month buy in LA?

This is an assumption-based calculation using the national benchmarks above. It is not a promise.

- **Coffee shop, $600/month on Google search:** $600 / $30.57 = about 20 leads.
- **Salon, $900/month:** $900 / $39.25 = about 23 leads.
- **Fitness studio, $1,500/month:** $1,500 / $67.36 = about 22 leads. If one in four leads becomes a member worth $100 a month, that is roughly 5 members and $500 a month in new recurring revenue, so the studio needs members to stay for a few months to break even.

Use your real close rate and customer value before committing budget. The ad cost is only half the equation.

## Which channel should an LA business try first?

- **Google search ads:** best when people already search for what you sell ("pet groomer near me"). See Google Ads for coffee shops in Los Angeles and [Google Ads for pet groomers in Los Angeles](/blog/google-ads-for-pet-groomers-in-los-angeles-ca).
- **Meta ads:** best for visual offers and retargeting, such as a first-class deal at a studio. See [Meta Ads for fitness studios in Los Angeles](/blog/meta-ads-for-fitness-studios-in-los-angeles-ca).
- **Local SEO:** slower but cheaper over time. See local SEO for hair salons in Los Angeles.

Pick one paid channel, run it for at least 30 days, and only add a second when you can see which leads become customers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much should a small LA business spend on marketing?

There is no single rule. Use the worked example: pick the number of customers you want, divide by your close rate to get leads, and multiply by the cost per lead for your category.

### Do I need Instagram and TikTok?

Only if your customers discover you there. A coffee shop or studio can benefit from visual content, while most service businesses get more from Google search and reviews.

### How long until ads work?

Plan for at least 30 days of data before judging a campaign, and adjust keywords, locations and landing pages as results come in.

### Should I outsource ad management?

Ads are the easiest place to waste money, so many owners hand them over first. Ask any provider for a monthly report showing spend, leads and booked customers.
