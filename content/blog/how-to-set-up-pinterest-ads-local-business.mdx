To set up Pinterest ads, you need a Pinterest business account, a claimed website, the Pinterest tag on your site, and then a campaign in Ads Manager with an objective, a budget and a bid. Pinterest's help pages describe each of those pieces, and this guide walks through them in order for a café, salon, studio or other local business.

It does not promise results. Pinterest publishes no average cost or return for small local advertisers, so the budget example below is labelled as an assumption. For price ranges, read our [Pinterest ads cost guide](/blog/pinterest-ads-cost-2026).

## Is Pinterest Worth Setting Up for a Local Business?

It can be, if people plan purchases there before they buy. Pinterest reported 640 million global monthly active users in Q2 2026, up 11% from 578 million a year earlier. In the US and Canada the figure was 106 million, up 4%.

Pinterest is a planning tool, so it suits categories where customers collect ideas first: weddings, haircuts, home, food, events. It suits a plumber less. Our comparison of [Pinterest and Instagram ads for local business](/blog/pinterest-vs-instagram-ads-local-business) and the [worth-it breakdown](/blog/pinterest-marketing-for-local-businesses-worth-it) can help you decide before you spend.

## Step 1: How Do You Set Up the Business Account?

Ads require a Pinterest business account, and Pinterest's help says ads are not available in every region, so check your country first. If you already use a personal account, convert it or create a separate business profile, and use your business name and logo.

Fill in your profile with a description and your website. Create a few boards that match what you sell, such as "Wedding hair" or "Latte art". Pin your own photos with links to the matching pages on your site. These organic pins also give your ads something to promote later. See our [Pinterest SEO guide](/blog/pinterest-seo-free-traffic-small-business) for how to write pin titles.

## Step 2: How Do You Claim Your Website?

Claiming connects your site to your profile. Pinterest says that when an image from your site is saved, your profile picture appears with the Pin and viewers can follow you. You also get insight into how often your content is shared.

You must own the domain and be able to edit its source. Pinterest offers four methods:

- Add an HTML tag to the head of your home page
- Upload an HTML file to your server's root folder
- Add a DNS TXT record (changes can take up to 72 hours)
- Verify through Google Merchant Center, for some accounts only

Go to Settings, then Link to Pinterest, then Claim next to Websites. Enter the URL and click Verify. A website can be claimed by only one Pinterest account, and most social accounts and marketplace stores cannot be claimed.

## Step 3: Why Install the Pinterest Tag?

The tag is code on your website that lets Pinterest track what visitors do after they see your ad. Pinterest's help lists what it enables: tracking and optimising conversions, measuring campaign performance, building audiences, and conversion insights.

Installation must be done from a desktop computer, and the tag can take up to 24 hours to appear in Ads Manager. You can use an integration partner if your site platform is supported, email instructions to a developer, or add the base code yourself. For a booking page, decide which action counts as a result, such as a completed booking or a menu click, and track that one.

## Step 4: How Do You Pick a Campaign Objective?

Pinterest's help lists five objectives: Brand awareness, Video completion, Consideration, Leads and Sales. The objective decides how you bid and which ad formats you can promote.

- **Brand awareness:** helps people discover you. Bids can be Performance+ or custom, with a maximum CPM.
- **Consideration:** increases Pin clicks or outbound clicks. Custom bids use a maximum CPC.
- **Leads:** drives actions such as sign-ups on your site, with a budget and target CPA.
- **Sales:** aimed at shopping, with a target CPA. Shopping campaigns attach to this objective.
- **Video completion:** counts views that reach 95% of the video. Performance+ only.

A café or salon with a booking link usually starts with Consideration (clicks to the site) or Leads, once the tag records bookings. Brand awareness makes sense for a new opening with a fixed date.

## Step 5: How Do You Promote a Pin Quickly?

The shortest route is promoting an existing Pin. Log in, open your profile, hover over a Pin and click Promote. You land on Create Campaign, which defaults to the Automated Campaign option. A dropdown in the top-right corner switches you to manual setup.

Only eligible Pins show the Promote button. Pick a Pin that already earns saves or clicks. Pinterest does not state a performance threshold, so judge by your own results.

## Step 6: How Should You Set the Budget?

Pinterest offers three budget types. A lifetime budget is a total for the campaign. A Performance+ daily budget is an average you are willing to spend each day. A fixed daily budget spends the same amount each day.

With a Performance+ daily budget, spend is averaged over a Sunday-to-Saturday week. Pinterest says it tries to spend no more than 125% of your daily budget on a single day. With a $100 daily budget, one day could be $125 and another $75. Editing the budget mid-week resets the average from that point.

Pinterest's help states no minimum budget. A community FAQ says its team typically recommends starting at $100 per day. That is a recommendation, not a rule, and a small shop can run less and wait longer for data.

## Step 7: How Does Bidding Work?

Performance+ bids let Pinterest adjust bids several times a day. Custom bids let you set a maximum and change it yourself. Your bid is a ceiling. Pinterest says you pay only the minimum amount required at any time, so a $3 bid against a $0.50 auction minimum charges $0.50.

Each campaign has a bid floor based on type and country. Pinterest's example: a CPC campaign targeting the US, UK and Canada has a minimum of $0.10. Costs can fluctuate, especially early, so judge a campaign after a few weeks, not days.

## FAQ

### Can I run Pinterest ads for a local business?

Yes, where Pinterest ads are available. The help pages say ads are not available in every region, so check the list for your country first.

### How much do I need to start?

Pinterest's help states no minimum budget. A community FAQ says its team typically recommends $100 per day, which is guidance, not a requirement.

### Which objective should a salon or café pick?

Consideration drives clicks to your site. Leads suits a booking or sign-up goal once the tag tracks it. Choose the one that matches the action you will measure.

### Do I need the Pinterest tag?

You can run ads without it, but the tag is how Pinterest measures conversions and builds audiences, so you will not see results on your site without it.

### How long before I know if it works?

Pinterest says costs can fluctuate, especially early in a campaign. Plan for a few weeks of data before changing the strategy.
