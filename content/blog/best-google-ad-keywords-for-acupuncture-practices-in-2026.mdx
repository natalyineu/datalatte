The best Google Ads keywords for an acupuncture clinic are local and service-led: "acupuncture near me", "acupuncturist [town]", "acupuncture for back pain [town]" and "acupuncture for [condition] [town]". Start in phrase and exact match, and block course, school and job searches. LocaliQ has no acupuncture category; the nearest, Physicians and Surgeons, averages $4.76 per click in 2026.

Two limits matter for health clinics: Google restricts some audience tools for sensitive categories, and Keyword Planner often returns nothing for health terms. This guide covers both. Worked numbers are labelled as assumptions.

## Which Keyword Groups Fit an Acupuncture Clinic?

Group by what the searcher is trying to do. These are example phrasings to test, not measured volumes.

| Group | Example searches | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find a clinic | acupuncture near me, acupuncturist [town], acupuncture clinic [town] | High |
| Condition and service | acupuncture for back pain, acupuncture for migraines, cupping [town] | Medium to high |
| Price and booking | acupuncture cost [town], acupuncture appointment, new patient acupuncture | High |
| Insurance | does insurance cover acupuncture [town] | Medium: answer on page |
| Brand | your clinic name, practitioner name | Defend your own name |

Only bid on conditions you may legally and honestly treat in your jurisdiction. Keep ad text to what the clinic offers. Do not promise a cure. Our [chiropractor keyword guide](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-chiropractors-in-2026) uses the same structure for a neighbouring profession.

## What Does a Click Cost?

There is no acupuncture row in the benchmarks. Physicians and Surgeons averages $4.76 per click, 6.61% click-through, 12.43% conversion and $40.04 per lead. Health and Fitness averages $6.17 per click, 6.94% conversion and $67.36 per lead. The all-industry average is $5.42 per click.

Use the range for planning only. A "lead" in these tables is whatever the advertiser counted, and your town may sit well above or below it.

## Why Does Keyword Planner Show Nothing for Health Terms?

Google's Keyword Planner help says very low-volume and some sensitive keywords cannot be discovered or forecast, and that sensitive categories such as health often return no results because of privacy policies.

So a blank result does not mean nobody searches. Start with a small test budget, let the Search terms report show what people type, and build the keyword list from real data.

## What Audience Limits Apply to Health Ads?

Google's personalized advertising policy lists Health as a sensitive category. For those ads, remarketing, Customer Match and audience expansion or lookalike segments are not supported. In-market and affinity segments, demographics, life events and location targeting remain available.

The page does not say whether acupuncture counts as Health, so check your account for policy notices. Location targeting does most of the work for a local clinic. Our [wellness marketing guide](/blog/wellness-business-marketing-strategy) covers channels outside paid search.

## Which Negative Keywords Should an Acupuncture Clinic Add?

Google's help page says negatives do not match close variants, so add both singular and plural forms.

- Training: school, course, certification, degree, license exam
- Jobs: jobs, hiring, salary, assistant
- Products: needles for sale, kit, supplies
- Pets: animal, dog, horse (unless you treat them)
- Free intent: free, DIY, at home

Check the Search terms report weekly at the start and add terms that spent without an enquiry.

## How Do You Track Calls?

Google says call assets cost the same as a headline click and that call reporting uses forwarding numbers and can count calls as conversions. Numbers must be in E.164 format and appear in the site code, or the domain must be verified. ## FAQ

### What are the best Google Ads keywords for acupuncture?
Local searches such as "acupuncture near me" and "acupuncturist [town]", then condition-based phrases you can treat. Test them in a small campaign, since Keyword Planner often shows no data for health terms.

### How much does a click cost for an acupuncture clinic?
No acupuncture-specific figure is published. The nearest LocaliQ categories average $4.76 and $6.17 per click.

### Can acupuncturists use remarketing on Google?
Google's policy says remarketing is not supported for sensitive categories, including Health. Check your account for how it applies to your clinic.

### Should I use broad match?
Not at first. Phrase and exact match give more control on a small budget.

### Is $600 a month enough to test?
It buys about 126 clicks at $4.76. That is enough to see which searches convert, not enough to judge results with certainty.
