In Malawi, Facebook's ad reach of 2.40 million is more than half of all internet users, while only 18.0% of the 22.4 million population is online. A small Malawi business should treat digital ads as a way to reach urban, connected customers, and keep offline word of mouth for the rest.

This guide is for shops, salons, cafes and service firms in Malawi. It uses published figures, labels every assumption, and ends with a break-even formula you can run on your own numbers. Facts were checked on 1 November 2026. The platform data are DataReportal's Digital 2026 figures, mostly from October 2025.

## Who Are You Marketing To in Malawi?

Malawi had 22.4 million people in October 2025 (UN data), up 2.6% over the year. Only 19.0% live in urban areas, 81.0% are rural, and the median age is 18.1. Cellular connections number 14.1 million, 63.0% of the population.

Internet users number 4.02 million, 18.0% penetration, which leaves 82.0% offline. Social media identities total 2.40 million (10.7%). Online ads can reach only a small share of the population, so choose the town or area where your customers are connected.

## Which Platforms Reach People in Malawi?

DataReportal takes platform reach from each platform's own advertising tools. It says these figures are not monthly active users and may not be unique people, so use them to compare channels, not to count customers.

| Platform | Ad reach, late 2025 | Share of population | Change over the year |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 2.40M | 10.7% | +800k (+50.0%) |
| Instagram | 178K | 0.8% | +34.3k (+23.9%) |
| LinkedIn (registered members) | 420K | 1.9% | +70.0k (+20.0%) |
| Messenger | 62.8K | 0.3% | -8,150 (-11.5%) |
| X | 77.0K | 0.3% | -47.2k (-38.0%) |

Facebook is the only platform with scale here, at 2.40 million or 59.7% of internet users. Instagram reaches 178 thousand, 0.8% of the population, so it will reach very few customers. The Facebook jump of 50.0% over the year may partly reflect Meta's revised reporting, which DataReportal says makes figures not comparable with earlier reports.

TikTok, Snapchat and YouTube are not in DataReportal's Malawi article. WhatsApp and mobile money are also not covered by the sources used here, so this guide makes no claims about them. Check what your own customers use.

## How Should Google Ads Language and Location Work in Malawi?

Google's Ads Help says that from September 2026 the campaign-level language setting is being removed for Search campaigns, and Search ads will match on the language of the ads themselves. For searches with a clear language, Google prioritises ads in that language. Decide which languages your customers search in and write a separate ad group for each, with a landing page in the same language as the ad.

For location, Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km and recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting as best practice for Search, while presence-only fits when you want to avoid people outside your target area. Set a radius around your premises, then read the report to see which areas convert.

No Malawi-specific click costs were found in the sources checked, so every cost figure below is an assumption.

## How Should You Split a Small Budget?

With 18% online, keep paid spend modest. Put most of it on Facebook with a message button, since Messenger reaches only 62.8 thousand and many chats will start through the Page. Add Google Search only if your service is searched for in your town. Judge the test on booked customers, not likes.

## What Does a Test Budget Return? The Math

These rows are **assumptions** for illustration, in US dollars. They are not Malawi benchmarks. Replace each with your own data after a month.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget (assumption) | $300 | $300 | $300 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | $0.50 | $0.30 | $0.20 |
| Clicks | 600 | 1,000 | 1,500 |
| Click to enquiry rate (assumption) | 3% | 5% | 7% |
| Enquiries | 18 | 50 | 105 |
| Enquiry to booking rate (assumption) | 30% | 40% | 50% |
| Bookings | 5.4 | 20 | 52.5 |
| Ad cost per booking | $56 | $15 | $6 |
| First-visit value (assumption) | $10 | $10 | $10 |
| Revenue minus ad spend | -$246 | -$100 | +$225 |

Counts use unrounded values. Revenue is not profit: staff, product and rent come out of it. A first visit often does not pay back by itself, which is why repeat visits matter.

Your break-even click price is the first-visit value times the enquiry rate times the booking rate. At $10, 5% and 40%, that is 10 x 0.05 x 0.40 = $0.20 per click. If your clicks cost more on average, the first visit loses money and you need repeat bookings to justify it. The biggest lever is usually the booking rate: answer messages and calls within minutes.

## FAQ

### How many people in Malawi use the internet?
DataReportal counts 4.02 million internet users, 18.0% of the population, in October 2025. The figure may lag current use.

### Is Facebook the main platform in Malawi?
By ad reach, yes: 2.40 million against 178 thousand for Instagram in late 2025.

### Can I reach customers outside cities with online ads?
Only a few. 81.0% of Malawians live in rural areas and 82.0% are offline, so rural reach online is limited.

### Does this guide cover WhatsApp or mobile money?
No. The sources used do not cover them, so no figures or claims are made.
