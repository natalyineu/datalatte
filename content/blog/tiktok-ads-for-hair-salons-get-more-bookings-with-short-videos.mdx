A hair salon can test TikTok ads from about $600 a month, because TikTok requires a daily budget above $50 per campaign and above $20 per ad group. Use the Lead generation objective with an Instant Form, target your city or postcode, and judge the test on cost per booking, not views. TikTok publishes no price list, so the auction sets your cost.

This guide covers the minimums, the campaign set-up, what to put in the video and a $600 example. No salon case results are quoted, because none could be verified, and every input in the example is labelled as an assumption.

## What Does It Cost to Start TikTok Ads for a Salon?

TikTok's budget help page says campaign budgets must exceed $50 a day and ad group budgets must exceed $20 a day. A lifetime budget at ad group level is the days in the campaign multiplied by $20, so a 31-day run is $620. The page shows dollar signs without naming a currency, so check yours.

That makes about $600 the least you can spend on a month-long test with one ad group. TikTok's bidding guidance adds that the daily budget should be at least 10 times your cost per result so a campaign can leave its learning phase. If a booking costs $25, that points to $250 a day, which is more than most salons can spend. Optimise for a cheaper event first.

Post first. Ads amplify what works, so find which videos earn saves, comments and booking questions before you pay to show them. ## How Do You Set Up a Booking Campaign?

Choose the Lead generation objective. TikTok says tapping the call-to-action on a lead ad can open an Instant Form, send people to your website, or open TikTok direct messages or a messaging app. Instant forms load faster than a typical landing page and can pre-fill fields. Leads can sync to your CRM through Zapier, LeadsBridge or custom APIs.

For a salon the form should ask for name, phone and the service wanted. Keep it short. TikTok's Form Card format allows up to three questions, all personal information (name, email, phone), so asking about the service means a form that starts with a personal-information question (US traffic) or a multiple-choice question. Phone each lead the same day, because a slow reply wastes the ad spend. If you need qualifying questions, send people to your own booking page instead, as TikTok notes a website form may work better there.

## How Do You Target Local Customers?

TikTok's location help lists city, county, district, DMA regions, province, state, urban district and postal code, and notes some options are not available in every country. It does not list radius targeting, so use postcode or city. Our [TikTok targeting guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-targeting-guide) covers audiences.

Start with a broad audience inside your area, then narrow by results, as TikTok recommends. TikTok also suggests launching with three to five ad groups using different targeting and creative, but at the minimum budget you can afford only one or two. Test creative first.

## What Should the Video Show?

Show the service and the result in the first seconds: a transformation, a consultation, a finished style. Name the service and your area on screen, add a clear booking action and keep one idea per video. Check any before-and-after images against our guide to [salon before-and-after photos and the law](/blog/hair-salon-before-after-photos-legal-and-converting), and get clients' permission to use their faces.

Reuse your best organic videos with new captions to test hooks. Keep notes on which video produced booking forms, not likes.

## FAQ

### How much do TikTok ads cost for a hair salon?

TikTok sets minimums of more than $50 a day per campaign and $20 a day per ad group, about $600 a month for one ad group. It publishes no average click or lead price.

### Are TikTok ads better than Instagram ads for salons?

No verified comparison exists for salons. Test with equal budgets and compare cost per booking. Our [TikTok vs Instagram ads comparison](/blog/tiktok-vs-instagram-ads-local-business-2026) shows the method.

### Can I target only people near my salon?

You can target by city, county or postal code. A radius setting is not listed in TikTok's location help.

### What is a Spark Ad?

It is a native ad format that runs an organic post as an ad. TikTok says views, likes, comments, shares and follows gained while boosted are credited to the original post. See our Spark Ads guide.

### How long before I see results?

TikTok's learning phase needs enough results per ad group, so allow several days at least, and judge at the end of a full month.
