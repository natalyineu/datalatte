## How Many Agencies Serve Denmark?

RevenueBase identified 461 company matches for e-commerce advertising agencies associated with Denmark, as of September 2026. It tracks 2,905 professional contacts across them, including 831 with verified work emails. These are counts from a data vendor's company graph. They show how many suppliers exist, not how many local businesses are searching for help.

For a small business, the practical point is that many firms compete for the same agency budgets. A long list does not indicate quality. Ask each provider for the named specialist who will manage the account, a sample monthly report, and the terms of a trial period before signing a retainer. Use the same questions for every firm so that proposals can be compared directly.

## Which Danish Cities Have the Most Agencies?

RevenueBase names Copenhagen as the largest hub. Its top-25 list also includes firms headquartered in Aarhus, Hobro and Albertslund. Precis, listed in Aarhus V, has 201 to 500 employees. Vertica, listed in Aarhus C, has 51 to 200 employees and describes itself as a dedicated e-commerce agency with 150 staff.

Businesses outside Copenhagen are not short of providers, and a firm's location does not limit where it can run campaigns. Company sizes on a vendor list are recorded by the vendor and may be out of date. Check each firm's current case studies and references directly before drawing conclusions from its listing.

## What Do Google Ads Agencies in Denmark Offer?

Ambition, a Copenhagen agency, describes its Google Ads service as search advertising, YouTube, display and Shopping campaigns. Its page says the team consists of senior specialists who follow the numbers closely. That is the agency's own description. The page does not publish prices or results, so proposals should be compared on scope and reporting rather than on marketing claims.

Ask any agency for its fee structure, who manages the account day to day, and which metrics it will report each month. A useful brief names the service area, the target action (a booking, a call or a visit) and the maximum cost per lead the business will accept. Without those three points, a proposal cannot be judged against the business's actual goals.

## How Should a Small Business Set Up Google Ads in Denmark?

Use this checklist before spending money on a campaign:

1. Set a fixed monthly test budget, for example DKK 5,000 (assumption), and do not exceed it during the first 60 days.
2. Write ads and the landing page in Danish if your customers are Danish-speaking.
3. Target a small area around your premises and exclude areas you do not serve.
4. Track calls, direction requests and bookings as separate conversions.
5. Review cost per booking after 30 days, then decide at 60 days which channel gets the next budget.

The local area you choose matters more than the number of keywords. Start with a handful of neighbourhood terms and widen only when you have booking data. The [local service ads guide](/blog/google-local-service-ads-worth-it-local-business) compares pay-per-lead formats, which can be a useful reference point when a campaign is being evaluated against other options.

## Is There a Worked Budget for a Copenhagen Cafe?

The table below uses assumptions only. No source here gives Danish click costs, so these numbers show how to structure a test, not what it will cost.

| Decision rule after 60 days | Keep the channel with the lowest cost per booking or visit |

Suppose the cafe records 40 Google-driven visits in month one (assumption). Cost per visit would be DKK 62.50 (2,500 divided by 40). If social delivers 15 visits (assumption), its cost per visit is DKK 100 (1,500 divided by 15). The next month's budget would then move toward search, and the test would run again. Replace these assumptions with tracked figures from your own account before making any decision.

## FAQ

### Do I need to market only in Danish?

For Danish-speaking customers, write ads and landing pages in Danish. The sources checked do not compare Danish and English ad performance. Test English only if many international visitors are part of your customer base, and compare cost per booking.

### How much should a small business spend on Google Ads in Denmark?

No source checked publishes Danish cost-per-click or budget benchmarks. Start with a fixed test budget, such as the DKK 5,000 example above, and decide on scale only after 60 days of tracked results.

### Is a Danish marketing agency worth hiring?

The agency list shows many providers, so compare them on named specialists, reporting and trial terms. RevenueBase counts come from a vendor list and do not rank quality. The [free audit](/free-audit) can show where a current setup loses leads before any agency is hired.

### Can I offer a discount for Google reviews?

Do not assume the answer without checking Google's current policy directly, since the sources in this guide do not cover review rules. Ask a Danish adviser before running any review incentive.
