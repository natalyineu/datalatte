They suit businesses that sell physical products and keep a feed up to date. LocaliQ's 2026 benchmark for the Shopping, Collectibles and Gifts category is $4.14 per click, a 4.01% conversion rate and $49.40 per lead. If you sell only services, Shopping ads do not apply.

## What Are Google Shopping Ads?

Google describes Shopping ads as product ads that, unlike text-only ads, show a product photo, title, price and store name. Google generates them from your campaign settings and product data. Shopping campaigns show on the Shopping tab, Google Search next to results, Google Images and, if you opt in, Search Partner sites.

Performance Max can also run Shopping ads. Google says those placements extend to Google Search text ads, Display, YouTube, Gmail, Demand Gen and Maps (via local inventory ads).

## How Do Shopping Ads Differ From Search Ads?

| | Search text ads | Shopping ads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What triggers the ad | Keywords you choose | Your Merchant Center product data, not keywords |
| What the shopper sees | Headlines and descriptions | Photo, title, price, store name |
| Pricing | Pay per click | Shopping campaigns pay per click |
| What you need | A landing page | Merchant Center account with a product feed, linked to Google Ads |
| Campaign types | Search | Standard Shopping or Performance Max |

For Performance Max, Google says you pay for performance, such as clicks or CPM. Your product titles and descriptions do the work that keywords do in Search, so write them the way customers search.

## What Do You Need Before You Start?

Google's Shopping ads page lists two requirements: a Merchant Center account with your product feed, and a linked Google Ads account. For free listings, Google's Merchant Center page lists required attributes: ID, title, link, image link, price, description and availability. Condition, brand, and GTIN or MPN apply in specific cases. Apparel needs color, size, age group and gender.

Your site should show a return policy, which Google says may affect whether a customer decides to buy. Shipping settings are required in listed countries, including the US, UK, Canada and Australia. The Needs attention tab flags data issues, so check it before launching.

## Can You Show Products for Free?

Yes. Merchant Center free listings let products appear at no cost, generating organic traffic alongside any paid campaigns, and new accounts are opted in by default. Start there to check that your feed is approved and that products appear correctly, then decide whether paid ads are worth it.

Free listings do not replace ads. They do not give you control over budget or placement, and Google's page does not publish how many clicks they bring. Treat them as a free test of your feed.

## Is Shopping Worth It for a Local Shop?

It depends on whether customers buy online. A shop selling gifts or pet supplies online can use the format. A coffee shop or groomer selling appointments and drinks in person cannot, because there are no products to list. Those businesses get more from Search ads, a Business Profile and reviews. ## Worked Example: What Does $600 Buy?

Inputs are **assumptions** using the LocaliQ category averages above.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $600 |
| Cost per click | $4.14 |
| Clicks | about 145 |
| Conversion rate | 4.01% |
| Orders | about 5.8 |
| Cost per order | about $103 |
| Average order value | $45 |
| Gross margin on an order | 40% |
| Gross profit per order | $18 |

At these assumptions, each order costs about $103 to win and earns $18 gross profit, so the first sale loses money. Shopping ads only pay when the average order is large, margins are high, or customers come back. Work this out for your own products before spending. ## What Should You Check Each Week?

1. Merchant Center Needs attention tab for feed errors.
2. Search terms and product-level results, to find products that take budget but do not sell.
3. Price and availability accuracy, because the feed shows both to shoppers.

Pause products with spend and no orders after enough clicks to judge. Google does not publish how many clicks that is, so pick a threshold and write it down.

## FAQ

### Do Google Shopping ads use keywords?
No. Google says Shopping ads use your existing Merchant Center product data, not keywords.

### What do I need to run Google Shopping ads?
A Merchant Center account with your product feed and a linked Google Ads account.

### How much do Shopping ads cost per click?
LocaliQ's 2026 category average for Shopping, Collectibles and Gifts is $4.14. It is a category average, not a Shopping-format figure.

### Can service businesses use Shopping ads?
No, because Shopping ads promote products with a photo, title and price. Services use Search ads or Local Services Ads.

### Are there free ways to show products on Google?
Yes. Merchant Center free listings show products at no cost and new accounts are opted in by default.
