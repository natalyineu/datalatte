Holiday CTV ads work best for a local business when the flight starts before Thanksgiving, runs in phases through New Year, and promotes one offer per phase: gift cards, holiday appointment slots, then memberships. Streaming has already [surpassed the combined share of broadcast and cable](https://www.elementaltv.com/blog/as-streaming-becomes-the-main-shelf-ctv-becomes-the-holiday-power-play) (Elemental TV, secondary), so the living-room screen is where holiday viewing happens. ## Why does Q4 matter for CTV advertising at a local business?

Two things peak at the same time in the fourth quarter: streaming viewing and shopping. Nielsen reported that streaming captured a record 47.5% of all U.S. TV viewing in December 2025, as [retold by AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/ctv-advertising-2026-update) (secondary). On the shopping side, the National Retail Federation forecasts $1.013 trillion in November and December retail sales, up 4.2%, per [AdWave's compilation of holiday figures](https://adwave.com/resources/holiday-advertising-spending-2026) (secondary).

Adobe's online forecast puts Cyber Monday at $15.1 billion, according to the same compilation, which reports it through Drip (secondary).

Forecasts are not guarantees. NRF predicted 3.7% to 4.2% growth last year, and the final 2025 figure was $972 billion, up 3.1%, short of the range. The compilation recommends reading forecasts as a range of plausible outcomes. For a local business, the takeaway is to plan around the offer and the calendar, not around a single national number.

## How much are advertisers planning to spend on holiday CTV?

Surveys point toward more CTV money, with caveats. An Advertiser Perceptions survey commissioned by Premion, fielded in October 2025, found nearly 70% of CTV advertisers expected to raise CTV spending in 2026, by an average of 17% ([AdWave, citing Marketing Dive](https://adwave.com/resources/holiday-advertising-spending-2026), secondary). These are statements of intent from marketers already using streaming TV, not actual spend.

Market-size estimates differ by definition. The IAB video report estimates 2026 US CTV spend at $29.3 billion, up from $26.5 billion in 2025. eMarketer projects about $38 billion, up from $33.35 billion ([AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/ctv-advertising-2026-update), secondary). Different scopes produce different totals, so neither should be mixed with the other.

Holiday-specific prices are not documented in these sources. Anyone planning a Q4 buy should ask the platform for current rates before locking a budget. The [real CPM data guide](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data) explains what to compare.

## When should a local business start its holiday flight?

Before Thanksgiving. In an October 2025 Circana survey, 48% of consumers browsed before Thanksgiving and 44% planned to shop earlier overall, as [listed in AdWave's holiday roundup](https://adwave.com/resources/holiday-advertising-spending-2026) (secondary). Shoppers who start early are researching gifts, gift cards and appointments weeks before the peak days.

The season also has different end dates depending on who measures it. Mastercard's forecast window ends December 24, while NRF's runs to December 31. For a business selling physical gift cards or pre-booked appointments, the practical deadline sits earlier than December 24. Digital gift cards and online booking can stay live longer.

The same Circana study found consumers spending selectively: planned spend averaged $779, against $796 the year before, with a forecast range from down 1% to up 2.5% (survey of 3,534 consumers, secondary). Selective shoppers respond to a clear, specific offer. A generic "happy holidays" spot gives them no reason to choose one local shop over another.

## What should each type of local business run?

Each business needs one primary offer per phase and one place to send viewers. The table below matches offers to the four business types this guide covers. The offers are recommendations, not benchmarks.

| Business | Early phase (pre-Black Friday to Cyber Monday) | Mid phase (early to mid December) | Final phase (Christmas to New Year) | Where viewers go |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coffee shop | Gift card or bundle offer, such as a card plus a bag of beans | Seasonal drink launch, office order and catering pickup | Back-to-routine loyalty sign-up | Online gift card page, or a QR code to the loyalty app |
| Hair and beauty salon | Gift card for services | Holiday event and party-look booking slots | New Year refresh and first-visit offer | Online booking page with gift card option |
| Pet groomer | Gift card and holiday grooming slot pre-booking | Last holiday grooming slots, with a visible deadline | Winter coat care and first-visit offer | Booking page showing real open dates |
| Fitness studio | Gift pass or class pack | Trial week sign-ups ahead of January | New Year membership offer | Trial or membership page with a clear start date |

The fitness row connects directly to the [CTV ads for fitness studios](/blog/ctv-ads-for-fitness-studios) guide, which covers creative and targeting in more depth.

Whatever the business, the landing page must match the spot. If the ad promises holiday grooming slots, the page should show open dates, not a general homepage.

## What does the Black Friday to New Year calendar look like?

The calendar below is built in relative weeks, so it works whatever the exact dates in a given year. Each week has one job.

- Week 1 (launch, before Thanksgiving week): lock the offer, build the landing page, upload the creative, set the local geography and launch. Check delivery within two to three days.
- Week 2 (week before Thanksgiving): lead with gift cards or early booking. Compare impressions delivered with the plan and fix pacing gaps.
- Week 3 (Thanksgiving and Black Friday): run the strongest budget week of the early phase. Make sure staff know the offer and the page can handle traffic.
- Week 4 (Cyber Monday week): push online gift cards and booking. Rotate in a second creative if the first has run for several weeks.
- Week 5 (early December): move to holiday appointments, events and trial offers. Review the mid-flight report.
- Week 6 (mid December): add deadline language such as "last grooming slots" or "digital gift cards delivered instantly". Gift windows narrow before December 24 for physical cards.
- Week 7 (Christmas week): cut or pause anything that is already sold out. Switch fitness and coffee creative to the New Year message.
- Week 8 (New Year week): run the membership, resolution and first-visit offers. Write down what worked for next year's Q4.

Creative needs to be ready before Week 1. The [CTV creative specs guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026) lists what platforms usually require, which helps avoid launch-week rejections.

## What does a holiday budget look like in practice?

The example below is a pet groomer running a six-week flight. Every input is an assumption for illustration, not a benchmark or a quote from any platform.

Assumptions:

- Total budget: $1,500 over six weeks
- CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions): $30
- Average frequency: 5 impressions per household over the flight
- Conversion: 0.3% of reached households book during the flight
- Average booking value: $85

| Week | Phase | Assumed spend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Launch, gift cards and early booking | $200 |
| 2 | Pre-Thanksgiving ramp | $200 |
| 3 | Black Friday | $300 |
| 4 | Cyber Monday | $300 |
| 5 | Holiday slots, mid December | $250 |
| 6 | Last slots with deadline | $250 |

At a $30 CPM, $1,500 buys 50,000 impressions. At a frequency of 5, that reaches about 10,000 households. A 0.3% booking rate gives 30 bookings, or $2,550 in first-visit revenue against $1,500 in spend.

Break-even is about 18 bookings ($1,500 divided by $85, rounded up). That is 0.18% of the reached households. If the real CPM is higher or the conversion lower, the result changes quickly, which is why the first-week delivery check matters. ## How can delivery be checked before the holidays?

Delivery problems found on Black Friday are expensive. Most can be caught in the first week by checking the following.

- Approval status: confirm the creative passed review and the campaign is live, not pending.
- Geography: confirm the targeted ZIP codes or radius match the real service area.
- Pacing: compare impressions delivered at day three and day seven with the plan. Underdelivery in the pre-holiday weeks means the budget will be compressed into peak days.
- Frequency: check that households are not seeing the spot far more often than planned.
- Landing page: open the URL or QR code on a phone and confirm the offer matches the ad. [New CTV formats such as QR codes and shoppable ads](/blog/new-ctv-ad-formats-2026-pause-ads-qr-codes-shoppable) make this link more important.
- Tracking: tag the landing page so bookings and gift card sales are attributable. The [Google Analytics 4 guide for small business](/blog/google-analytics-4-for-small-business) covers setup.

Measurement should be in place before launch. AdWave's holiday roundup makes the same point: set up how results will be measured before the campaign starts, so the business has its own numbers rather than waiting on industry reports (secondary).

Platform choice also affects reporting. The [CTV platforms comparison](/blog/ctv-advertising-platforms-compared-2026) shows what each option offers.

## FAQ

### When should holiday CTV ads start for a local business?

Before Thanksgiving. An October 2025 Circana survey found 48% of consumers browsed before Thanksgiving and 44% planned to shop earlier overall (secondary). A launch the week before Thanksgiving week leaves time to fix delivery problems before Black Friday.

### Is there a documented holiday price increase for CTV ads?

The sources used for this guide do not document one, so no change in rates should be assumed in either direction. Ask the platform for current rates before setting the budget, and compare them with the CPM guide linked above.

### What should a coffee shop, salon, groomer or fitness studio promote?

Gift cards early, holiday appointments or seasonal products in the middle of the season, and New Year memberships or first-visit offers at the end. The table above gives a version for each business.

### How much should a local business spend on holiday CTV?

No source sets a standard figure. The groomer example uses $1,500 over six weeks, labelled an assumption. A better approach is to start from the number of bookings or sales needed to break even and work backward.
