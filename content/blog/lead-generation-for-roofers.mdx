A roofer gets leads most reliably from three places: Google Business Profile (free), Google Local Services Ads (pay per lead) and Google Search ads (pay per click). WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks, whose closest category is Home and Home Improvement, put a search click at $8.33 and a lead at $90.92. Roofing is not listed separately, so treat those figures as a range, not a quote.

This guide compares the channels, explains what each charges for, and shows how to check the numbers against your own jobs. Illustrative figures are labelled as assumptions.

## Which Lead Channels Work for Roofers?

Compare channels by what you pay for, not by their names. A click, a lead and a free listing behave differently.

| Channel | You pay for | Control | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | Nothing for the listing | Low (Google decides ranking) | Nearby homeowners searching "roofer near me" |
| Local Services Ads | Each valid lead | Medium (budget and bid mode) | Calls from people ready to hire |
| Google Search ads | Each click | High (keywords, locations, negatives) | Specific jobs: repair, replacement, storm damage |
| Referrals and reviews | Your time | High | Raising close rate on all of the above |

Google's own help page says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking in the free listings, so the profile is a base to maintain, not a lever to buy.

## How Do Google Local Services Ads Charge Roofers?

Google's Local Services help says you pay for leads, not clicks. A bid is the most you would pay for a lead, and you pay only when you receive one. You set an average weekly budget; once your monthly maximum is reached, the ad stops for the rest of the month unless you raise it.

Lead prices vary by location, job type, lead type and bidding mode. Google does not publish a roofing price list, so ask your own account what it quotes. Calls count when answered, or when a missed call during business hours stays on the line for more than 20 seconds. The same customer contacting you again within 15 days from the same number or email is not charged twice.

Google says leads judged invalid or low quality are not charged, and charged leads are reassessed for 30 days. Credits are not available to advertisers in EMEA, so UK and European roofers should check the rules for their country.

## Do Search Ads or Local Services Ads Cost Less per Lead?

Nobody can answer that for your town without data. Search ads give you the cost per click, and your own conversion rate turns it into cost per lead. Local Services Ads quote a price per lead directly, but it can change with the job type and competition.

The LocaliQ figures give a sanity check for search. At $8.33 per click and 8.05% conversion, one lead costs about $103 (8.33 divided by 0.0805), a little above the published $90.92 average, since each average is calculated separately. Treat anything near $90 to $105 as the benchmark range and your own account as the truth.

Local Services Ads also depend on how fast you answer. Google says that if you regularly fail to answer calls or respond to messages, your ad ranking may be affected. Bidding can move your ad higher, but responsiveness and reviews still count.

## How Many Reviews Does a Roofer Need?

There is no magic number, but the survey shows what buyers filter on. BrightLocal's 2026 Local Consumer Review Survey (1,002 US adults) found 68% require at least 4 stars, 31% will only use a business rated 4.5 or higher, and 74% only consider reviews from the last three months. It also found 89% expect owners to respond.

That makes review collection a monthly routine, not a one-off push. Ask after each finished job while the roof is fresh in the customer's mind, and reply to every review. Google says prominence depends partly on how many reviews you have. For the profile setup itself, use our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## Which Search Terms Should a Roofer Target?

Start with jobs you want and can book: repair, replacement, inspection and, where relevant, storm damage. Add your city or service area to each. Then block waste. Job seekers, DIY shingle questions, materials-only buyers and roofing suppliers will otherwise take clicks you pay $8 or more for.

Review the Search terms report weekly and add negatives. Our [roofer keyword guide](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-roofers-in-2026) lists starting terms, and the [negative keywords guide](/blog/google-ads-negative-keywords-stop-wasting-budget) shows how to apply them. ## What Is a Lead Worth? A Worked Example

All inputs here are **assumptions**, chosen to show the method, not to predict your results.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget | $1,500 |
| Cost per lead | $100 |
| Leads per month | 15 |
| Share of leads that become quotes | 50% |
| Quotes that become jobs | 25% |
| Average job gross profit | $2,500 |

Fifteen leads give about 7.5 quotes and about 1.9 jobs. That is roughly $4,700 in gross profit against $1,500 of spend, or about $800 per job in acquisition cost. Change one input and the result moves a lot: at a 15% quote close rate, you win about 1.1 jobs and gross profit falls to about $2,800. Measure your real close rate before raising the budget.

## How Do You Track Where Leads Come From?

Use a separate tracked phone number or Local Services Ads call records for each paid channel, and ask every caller how they found you. Log the source in a simple sheet with the date, job value and result. After 60 to 90 days you can drop the channel with the worst cost per job.

## FAQ

### How much does a roofing lead cost?

Google does not publish roofing prices. LocaliQ's 2026 benchmark for Home and Home Improvement is $90.92 per lead, with an $8.33 click. Local Services Ads vary by location and job type, so check the quote in your account.

### Are Local Services Ads better than Google Search ads for roofers?

They suit different goals. Local Services Ads charge per valid lead and rely on reviews and fast answers. Search ads give you more control over keywords and areas but charge per click, including clicks that never call.

### Can I get a refund for bad leads?

Google says invalid or low-quality leads are not charged and can be flagged on the Leads page, with automatic credits for poor-quality, duplicate or invalid leads. Credits are not available to advertisers in EMEA.

### How many reviews do I need?

No official number exists. BrightLocal 2026 says 68% of consumers require 4 or more stars and 74% only read reviews from the last three months, so steady new reviews matter more than a high total.

### Should I run ads in the off-season?

No source in this article tests that. Compare your own cost per job by month and set the budget from your numbers.
