An electrician should start with three things: a service-area Google Business Profile, review requests after every job, and a call-focused Google campaign or Local Services Ads. Google's Local Services Ads list "Electricians" as a category. Paid clicks cost about $8.33 and leads $90.92 on average in the closest LocaliQ category, Home and Home Improvement (2026), so measure calls, not clicks.

This guide is for residential and light commercial electricians in the US. Sources are listed at the end, and examples built on guesses are marked as assumptions.

## How Competitive Is Electrician Marketing?

The Bureau of Labor Statistics counts 821,000 electrician jobs in 2025, with employment projected to grow 9% from 2025 to 2035 and about 72,700 openings a year. Only 7% are self-employed, so most electricians work for firms. That points to many established companies competing for the same local searches.

BLS does not report how many of those firms advertise. What it does show is a trade with steady demand and many players, so a clear local profile and recent reviews matter more than a slogan.

## Are Local Services Ads Worth Testing for Electricians?

Google's Local Services Ads help page includes Electricians among its home service categories. To go live you submit a license, or business registration where no license is required, and set up billing and a budget. Google also offers a Google Verified badge to businesses that pass its screening.

Cost per lead for electricians is not published on that page and varies by city, so test for a month and divide spend by booked jobs. Compare the result with a call-focused Search campaign. [Best Google Ads keywords for electricians](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-electricians-in-2026) lists the search terms to start from.

## How Should You Run Search Ads for Emergency and Planned Work?

Split them. Emergency searches such as a power outage or a tripping panel need a phone number visible first. Planned work such as an EV charger, a panel upgrade or rewiring suits a landing page with a form and examples of finished jobs.

Google says clicks on a call asset cost the same as headline clicks, and call reporting uses Google forwarding numbers so you can measure calls. Add negative keywords for jobs, courses and DIY searches. Google notes negatives do not match close variants, so add singular and plural forms yourself.

## What Should Your Google Business Profile Show?

Google's guidelines say service-area businesses should hide their address, keep service areas within about two hours' drive, and use the real business name without taglines or phone numbers. Add services, photos of finished work, and hours that match how you answer calls.

Reviews carry weight. BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 68% of consumers will only use a business with 4 stars or more, and 89% expect owners to respond. Google allows asking for reviews by link or QR code but prohibits offering discounts for them. ## Does an Electrician Need a Website?

A site gives service pages, license details and a form that you can track. Our [website design for electricians](/blog/website-design-for-electricians) guide covers what to include. Keep one page per main service and make the phone number a tap-to-call link.

Past customers, property managers and builders are the usual sources of repeat work, and a short [referral program](/blog/referral-marketing-guide) formalises that. No verified benchmark exists for electrician referral conversion, so track it yourself.

## FAQ

### How much should an electrician spend on advertising?

No verified percentage exists for electricians. Work backwards from the profit on a booked job and your call capacity. At the $90.92 average cost per lead from LocaliQ, $1,000 buys about 11 leads on average.

### Are Local Services Ads available for electricians?

Yes, Electricians is a listed category. You need a license or business registration, billing and a budget before going live.

### Do I need to hide my address on Google?

If you work from home and customers do not visit, Google says a service-area business should hide its address. A virtual office is not eligible unless staffed during business hours.

### How many reviews do I need?

BrightLocal's 2026 survey says 47% of consumers will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews, and 74% only consider reviews from the last three months.
