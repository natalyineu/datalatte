A local business should plan its holiday emails around five dates: Thanksgiving on November 26, Black Friday on November 27, Small Business Saturday on November 28, Cyber Monday on November 30 and Christmas on December 25, 2026. Send a first email about a week before Thanksgiving, then one or two more.

Put your address and an unsubscribe link in every message, and check your tool's send limits first.

This guide covers dates, legal basics for US senders and a worked send plan. No source we opened publishes reliable holiday open-rate or revenue benchmarks for small local businesses, so none are quoted here.

## Which Holiday Dates Matter for Email in 2026?

Thanksgiving is the fourth Thursday of November, which makes it November 26 this year. The shopping days follow from it. Dates below come from the calendar and were cross-checked against secondary calendar sites, which also list a wider Cyber Week running to December 3.

| Date (2026) | Day | What it means for a local business |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nov 26 | Thursday | Thanksgiving. Most shops avoid sending. |
| Nov 27 | Friday | Black Friday. Good for a one-day offer. |
| Nov 28 | Saturday | Small Business Saturday. Natural fit for a local shop. |
| Nov 30 | Monday | Cyber Monday. Matters mainly if you sell online. |
| Dec 25 | Friday | Christmas. Last-order and gift-card deadlines come earlier. |

Retail sale windows vary by seller, so treat these as anchors, not a rule that every offer must start on the same day.

## How Many Holiday Emails Should You Send?

There is no verified number that fits every business, so build the plan from your list size and your tool's limits. A workable pattern is three messages: an early heads-up about a week before Thanksgiving, a main offer on or just before Black Friday, and a last-chance reminder before your cut-off date for gift cards or orders.

Fewer, clearer emails are easier to keep accurate. Each one should have one offer, one button and one deadline. If you only email twice, make one of them the Small Business Saturday message and the other the final reminder.

## What Does the Law Require in Every Marketing Email?

For US senders, the FTC's CAN-SPAM guide lists the basics. Do not use false or misleading header information, and do not use deceptive subject lines. Include a valid physical postal address. Give a clear way to opt out, make sure it works for at least 30 days after sending, and honour requests within 10 business days. The FTC states penalties of up to $53,088 for each violating email.

If any subscribers live in the EU or UK, stricter consent rules apply. Our [GDPR email marketing guide](/blog/gdpr-compliant-email-marketing) covers consent, records and unsubscribes.

## Do Gmail's Sender Rules Apply to a Small Shop?

Only if you send in volume. Google's rules for bulk senders apply to anyone sending more than 5,000 messages a day to Gmail accounts. Those senders need SPF, DKIM and DMARC, one-click unsubscribe for marketing messages, and a spam rate below 0.30% in Postmaster Tools, with Google advising to stay below 0.10%.

A small local list will usually stay far under the threshold. Authenticating your sending domain and making unsubscribing easy is still sensible, because those habits protect delivery at any size.

## What Does a Holiday Send Plan Look Like? (Worked Example)

All inputs here are **assumptions**. A café has 240 subscribers, which fits inside the Mailchimp free plan's limit of 250 contacts. That plan also caps sending at 500 emails a month, and the pricing page lists automation journeys as not included.

| Item | Assumption | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subscribers | 240 | Under the 250-contact limit |
| Emails in November | 2 | 480 sends, under the 500 cap |
| A third email in November | 1 more | 720 sends, over the cap |
| Gift-card buyers (assumption) | 3% of 240 | about 7 buyers |
| Average gift card (assumption) | $40 | about $288 |

The point is the arithmetic, not the conversion rate. A third November email would break the free plan's monthly cap, so the owner either upgrades, moves one send to December or merges two messages. Replace every assumption with your own numbers after the first send.

## FAQ

### When should I send my first holiday email?

About a week before Thanksgiving works as a starting point. That leaves the Black Friday, Small Business Saturday and final reminder slots free. No source we opened proves a best send day for local businesses, so test one change at a time.

### Can I email customers who gave me their address at the till?

In the US, CAN-SPAM does not require prior opt-in, but it does require an address, honest headers and subject lines, and a working opt-out. Collecting a clear yes at sign-up is still better practice. In the EU and UK, consent rules are stricter.

### Do I need an unsubscribe link in every email?

Yes. The FTC guide requires a clear opt-out method, which must work for at least 30 days after the message is sent, and requests must be honoured within 10 business days. Google also requires one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.

### Is the Mailchimp free plan enough for holiday emails?

For a small list, possibly. At the time of checking it allowed 250 contacts and 500 emails a month and listed automation journeys as not included. Check the current limits before you plan your sends.

### What if my list has EU or UK subscribers?

Apply the stricter rule to everyone on that segment: clear consent, a record of it and an easy unsubscribe. The GDPR guide linked above explains the main requirements.
