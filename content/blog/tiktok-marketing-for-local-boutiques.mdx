A local boutique can start on TikTok for $0: switch to a free Business Account, post short videos of new arrivals and styling, and add paid reach later. TikTok offers three paid routes. Promote runs from the app in about three minutes, Spark Ads turn your own posts into ads inside Ads Manager, and full Ads Manager campaigns require a daily ad group budget above $20 in the US.

No public benchmark shows how many store visits a boutique gets from TikTok, so this guide sticks to what TikTok documents and uses labelled assumptions for the arithmetic.

## What should a boutique post on TikTok?

TikTok does not publish content-type benchmarks by retail category, so there is no data-backed "best video". Practical formats that suit a clothing or gift shop, all free to make on a phone:

- New arrivals shown on a rail or a mannequin, with prices in the caption
- One outfit styled three ways
- A quick tour of a single shelf or window display
- Answers to a question customers ask in store, such as sizing

Post from a Business Account and use sounds from the Commercial Music Library. TikTok describes it as a pre-cleared library of 1 million songs, free for businesses, and says businesses should use it instead of the general library for commercial content. Our [TikTok for local businesses guide](/blog/using-tiktok-for-local-businesses) and [organic strategy](/blog/tiktok-local-business-organic-strategy) cover the weekly routine.

## How do Promote, Spark Ads and Ads Manager differ?

| | Promote | Spark Ads | Ads Manager campaign |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Where you set it up | In the TikTok app | Ads Manager | Ads Manager |
| What you advertise | A video you posted | Your own organic post, or another creator's with their authorisation | Any ad creative |
| Goals TikTok lists | Video views, website visits, followers; also leads and sales in newer guidance | Depends on campaign | Full range |
| Stated minimum | Not stated on the pages checked | Follows the campaign budget rules | Daily ad group above $20, campaign above $50 |

TikTok's Promote page lists targeting options including gender, age, interest and geography, which matters for a shop that only serves one town. Promote's budget minimum was not stated on TikTok's pages when checked. Some third-party guides quote about $3 a day, but they conflict with each other, so read the figure on the in-app Promote screen.

Spark Ads let you run ads from your own account's posts. TikTok's help states a video must be 10 minutes or shorter to be sparked, and one Ads Manager account supports up to 10,000 Spark Ads.

## What does TikTok say about minimum ad spend?

TikTok's budget help (last updated July 2026) says the daily budget must exceed $50 at campaign level and $20 at ad group level. For a lifetime budget, the ad group minimum is the total days times the minimum daily budget; a 31-day ad group needs at least $620. These figures apply to Ads Manager, not necessarily Promote.

For many single-shop boutiques, a $620 test is more than the first month needs. Start with organic posts, then try Promote on the one video that already got the most saves or profile visits. For comparisons of ad cost, see our [TikTok ads cost guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-guide).

TikTok Shop lets sellers list products inside TikTok, but eligibility, fees and country availability vary. ## Worked example: a $0 month, then a small test

All figures are **assumptions** for illustration.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Weeks 1 to 3 | 9 organic videos, $0 |
| Best video | 2,000 views, 60 profile visits |
| Week 4 test | Promote that video with a website-visits goal |
| Test budget | $50 (owner's choice, above any in-app minimum) |
| Visits to site | 80 |
| Visitors who come in or order | 4 |
| Average basket | $45 |
| Revenue from test | $180 |
| Gross margin | 50% |

At a 50% margin, $180 of revenue earns $90 against $50 of spend, a $40 gain before staff time. If only 2 of 80 visitors bought, revenue would be $90 and margin $45, slightly below the spend. The example shows how to set a break-even rate: here, 3 buyers per 80 visits.

## FAQ

### Is TikTok worth it for a small boutique?

There is no published retail-by-category data. The free account costs nothing, so test organic posts first and keep a count of customers who mention TikTok.

### How much does TikTok Promote cost?

TikTok's pages checked do not state a minimum. Third-party guides give conflicting figures, so use the amount shown in the app.

### Can I use any song in my videos?

TikTok says businesses should use the Commercial Music Library, which is free for business use, rather than the general library.

### What is the minimum for Ads Manager campaigns?

In the US, the daily campaign budget must exceed $50 and the ad group daily budget must exceed $20, per TikTok's July 2026 help page.

### Can I turn my best post into an ad?

Yes. Spark Ads use your own account's organic posts, up to 10 minutes long.
