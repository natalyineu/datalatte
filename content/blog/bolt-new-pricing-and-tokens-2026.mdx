## How Much Does Bolt.new Cost?

Bolt's pricing page lists four plans.

| Plan | Price | Tokens | What the page says |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 1M a month, 300K a day | Public and private projects, website hosting, unlimited databases, Bolt branding on sites, 10 MB file upload, up to 333K web requests |
| Pro | $25 a month, billed monthly | Starts at 10M a month, no daily limit | No Bolt branding, private site sharing, 100 MB file upload, up to 1M web requests, tokens roll over, custom domains, AI image editing |
| Teams | $30 per member a month, billed monthly | Not specified | Everything in Pro plus centralized billing, admin controls, sharing within the organization, private NPM registry |
| Enterprise | Custom | Not specified | SSO, audit logs, compliance support, dedicated account manager |

Annual billing is said to "Save up to 28%", but annual prices are not listed on the pricing page. 

Bolt's billing article gives two examples: Pro 10M annual at $216 a year ($18 a month) and Pro 26M annual at $540 a year ($45 a month). It notes monthly plans at $50 and $100 in upgrade examples, and says the token amounts in those examples are inconsistent, so higher tiers are not confirmed here.

## How Do Bolt Tokens Work?

The support center explains that tokens measure the text Bolt reads and writes. Short common words are usually one token, while rarer words can split into several. Bolt uses tokens when it reads, thinks about and builds the project.

Rules from the tokens article:

- **Free tokens** reset on the 1st of each calendar month. The 300K daily limit resets each day and does not carry over. Free tokens do not roll over.
- **Paid tokens** reset on the renewal date. Unused monthly tokens roll over and stay valid for two months from the start of the billing cycle in which they were received. Older tokens are used first.
- **Cancelling** loses all allocated tokens, rollover included, when the billing cycle ends. Resubscribing before they expire restores unexpired rollover tokens.
- **Buying more.** Upgrading is called the most cost-effective option. "Reload tokens" is available only on the highest individual monthly Pro plan or any annual Pro plan, and reloaded tokens do not expire. Prices are shown inside the app.

Bolt also warns that asking Bolt to publish uses tokens, while the Publish button does not. The support center has separate pages on using tokens efficiently and managing project context.

## How Does Hosting Work?

Built-in hosting is included. Per the support center, publishing takes about a minute and the first publish creates a random bolt.host address you can change later. Later edits do not go live until you click Update in the Publish menu.

| Feature | Free | Paid plans |
|---|---|---|
| Address | bolt.host, public | Private publishing allowed |
| Custom domain | Not available | Available, public sites only |
| Bolt badge | "Made in Bolt" | Removed |
| Bandwidth and requests | Up to 10 GB and 333,333 requests a month | Pro lists up to 1M requests |
| Supabase connection | Requires Pro or Teams | Included |

A custom domain can only be used with a public site, so connect it only if you want the page visible to everyone.

## What Are the Risks and Limits?

- **Token burn.** Long chats and big projects use more tokens; the support center has guidance on keeping context small.
- **Free limits.** Bolt branding, a daily cap, public sites on bolt.host and no custom domain.
- **Cancelling.** All allocated tokens, including rollover, are lost when the billing cycle ends.
- **Cards only.** Pro and Teams accept credit and debit cards only.
- **Review.** Test forms and published pages before sharing the link.

This guide did not open Bolt's terms on data use or code ownership, so those are not stated here.

## FAQ

### Is Bolt.new free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 1 million tokens a month with a daily limit of 300,000. Sites are public on bolt.host with Bolt branding, and custom domains are not available.

### How much does Bolt.new cost per month?

Pro is $25 a month for a plan starting at 10 million tokens, and Teams is $30 per member. Annual billing is said to save up to 28%. Higher Pro tiers exist, but their prices are not confirmed on the pages opened.

### What are Bolt.new tokens?

Tokens measure the text Bolt reads, thinks about and writes while building your project. Free tokens reset monthly and do not roll over. Paid tokens roll over for two months.

### What happens when I run out of Bolt tokens?

On Free, the daily limit resets each day, and you can still edit existing code in Code view. On paid plans you can upgrade, or reload tokens on eligible plans. Reloaded tokens do not expire.

### Is Bolt.new worth it for a small business?

It can be for simple pages, if someone checks the result. Start on Free, then try Pro for one month. Watch how quickly tokens are used before committing to annual billing.
