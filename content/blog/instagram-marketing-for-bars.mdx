A bar should use Instagram to show what a night there feels like, then give people one reason to come on a specific night. That means short videos and photos of the room, the drinks and the crowd, a clear event or happy-hour post each week, and a profile that shows hours, address and how to book. Alcohol brings extra rules for paid ads and for age restrictions, covered below.

This guide is for bar owners in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. Benchmarks come from Dash Social's 2026 report. Policy points come from Meta and Instagram pages opened on 6 November 2026. Illustrative figures are labelled as assumptions.

## What Do Benchmarks Say About Posting?

Dash Social's 2026 report covers 3,313 global Instagram handles with at least 1,000 followers, from 1 January to 30 June 2026. Food and beverage accounts averaged a 3.2% engagement rate by views, against 2.6% for all industries, and about five posts a week. The data includes organic, boosted and promoted content but excludes paid ads, and it does not split results by format.

Engagement rates depend on the formula, so you can't compare this figure with a per-follower rate from another vendor. Use it to judge direction, then compare your own posts over time.

## What Should a Bar Post?

Dash Social's page says leading food and beverage brands use product demonstrations, recipes, staff reactions and creator partnerships. For a bar, that translates into a simple weekly mix:

- **The room:** short videos of the bar at your busiest and your best-lit hour. Bars are dark, so shoot where there is light and keep the phone steady.
- **The drinks:** a pour, a garnish, a stir. One signature drink per post.
- **The people:** staff making something, a regular's table, with permission.
- **The plan:** the next quiz, live music night or happy hour, with date, time and price.

Every post needs a next step. "Happy hour 5 to 7, $6 wine" beats "great night." Add your hours and a booking link in your bio. Our guides on Instagram Reels for business and Instagram marketing for restaurants cover the video basics.

## How Do Events Fit In?

Events give people a date to put in their diary and something to share. A weekly format such as a quiz, a tasting or a live set works because it repeats. Post the lineup a week ahead, share a few clips on the night and a recap the next day. Tag guests only where they have agreed.

## What Are the Rules for Promoting Alcohol on Instagram?

Rules differ by country and change, so read the current pages. Three points stand out:

1. **Paid ads.** Meta's advertising standards for alcohol say that, at a minimum, ads may not be targeted to people under 18. Some countries set a higher minimum: the page lists 21 for the United States and 19 for Canada. Ads must follow local laws, industry codes and licences. Ads for venues and events whose primary business model depends on alcohol sales are treated as alcohol ads. Some countries prohibit alcohol ads altogether.
2. **Age restrictions on posts.** Instagram's creator guidance says a minimum age can be set on branded posts, for example 21, which hides the post from everyone under 21. Alcohol is listed among goods that "can only be promoted with restrictions". Gating cannot be added after a post is published. You would remove the post and repost it with the restriction. This guidance covers branded content and affiliate formats, so check how it applies to your own organic posts.
3. **Your licence and local code.** Alcohol advertising codes in your country may limit what you show, such as promotions that encourage heavy drinking. Check with your licensing authority or an adviser before running ads.

If you work with local creators, give them the same rules in writing. Our [guide to Instagram Reels ads for local business](/blog/instagram-reels-ads-local-business-guide) is a starting point for the paid side.

## Does Instagram Marketing Pay Back for a Bar?

## FAQ

### How often should a bar post on Instagram?

Dash Social's 2026 data shows food and beverage brands posting about five times a week on average. Choose a frequency you can keep. Consistent weekly posts beat bursts followed by silence.

### Do I need a professional photographer?

Not necessarily. Phone video works if you shoot in good light and keep the phone steady. A photographer can help with a set of photos for your profile and website.

### Can a bar run Instagram ads?

Yes, within Meta's rules. Alcohol ads must follow local law, cannot target people under 18 (higher in some countries, such as 21 in the US) and are banned in some countries.

### Should I age-restrict my posts?

Instagram lets accounts set minimum ages, and its creator guidance lists alcohol among restricted goods. Review the current options in your account settings and your local rules.

### Photos or videos?

The Dash Social page does not break out results by format. Test both and compare your own reach and saves.
