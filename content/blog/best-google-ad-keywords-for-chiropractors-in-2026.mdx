## What Do Chiropractor Keywords Cost?

Treat every chiropractic cost figure as an estimate. The Valley Marketing Group, an agency that sells Google Ads management, cites LeadGulls for $2.50 to $8.00 per click on local terms and $4.50 to $9.80 on condition terms in major US metros. It cites The Leading Practice for a blended $84 cost per lead. Both are secondary, with no published method.

For an official anchor, WordStream by LocaliQ lists Health and Fitness at $6.17 per click and a 6.94% conversion rate, against $5.42 across all industries. Chiropractic is not its own row. Your own account will tell you more after one month than any benchmark.

## Which Keywords Should a Chiropractor Bid On First?

Group keywords by what the searcher already knows. Someone typing a condition plus a city is closer to booking than someone reading about back pain. These are example patterns, not volume-ranked lists.

| Intent group | Example patterns | Why it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local, ready to book | chiropractor near me, chiropractor in [city] | Searcher wants a nearby clinic |
| Condition plus place | sciatica chiropractor [city], neck pain chiropractor [city] | Specific problem, strong match to a landing page |
| Situation | auto accident chiropractor [city], prenatal chiropractor [city] | Clear need, often a separate offer |
| Insurance and cost | chiropractor that takes [insurer], chiropractor cost | Price-aware; send to a page that answers it |
| Brand-neutral research | best chiropractor [city] | Comparing options; reviews matter more here |

Give each group its own ad group and landing page. A visitor who searched "sciatica chiropractor" should land on a sciatica page with booking and phone, not the home page. Condition keywords can overlap with health-claim rules, so describe services and avoid promising cures.

## How Do Match Types Work for a Small Clinic?

Google describes three types. Broad match can show on searches related to a keyword, including some that do not contain its direct meaning. Phrase match shows on searches that include the keyword's meaning, and can be more specific. Exact match shows on searches with the same meaning or intent and reaches the fewest searches.

For a small clinic, start with phrase and exact match on the groups above. Google says broad match needs Smart Bidding to work well, and that Smart Bidding suits all match types. If you use broad match, do it only with conversion tracking running and a strong negative list, and check the Search terms report weekly.

## Which Negative Keywords Protect the Budget?

Negative keywords stop ads from showing on searches that look similar but mean something else. Google notes they do not match close variants, so add singular and plural forms yourself. It also advises using them selectively, because too many cut your reach.

Starting list, based on the searches that clearly are not patients:

- Jobs and pay: salary, jobs, hiring, career
- Education: school, degree, course, continuing education
- DIY: how to crack your back, at home, tutorial
- Legal: malpractice, lawsuit
- Products: equipment, table for sale, wholesale

Add "free" only if you do not offer a free consultation or screening. Then move to the Search terms report each week and add what you see.

## FAQ

### What are the best Google Ads keywords for chiropractors?

Local and condition-plus-city phrases, such as "chiropractor near me" and "sciatica chiropractor [city]". They show clear need and map to one landing page each.

### How much does a chiropractor click cost on Google Ads?

No official figure exists. An agency summary citing LeadGulls gives about $2.50 to $8.00 for local terms. WordStream lists $6.17 for Health and Fitness overall.

### Should chiropractors use broad match?

Only with conversion tracking, Smart Bidding and a solid negative list. Otherwise start with phrase and exact match.

### How many negative keywords do I need?

Google does not set a number. Start with the jobs, school, DIY and legal terms above and add from the Search terms report.

### Where do I find search volumes for my city?

Use Google Keyword Planner inside a Google Ads account. Volumes change by place, so a national table would mislead.
