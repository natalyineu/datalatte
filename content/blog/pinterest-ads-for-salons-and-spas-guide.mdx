## Do Pinterest Ads Work for Salons and Spas?

They can fit services people plan for, such as bridal hair, colour changes or facials, because users search for ideas before they book. Whether that turns into appointments depends on your offer and landing page. No source verified here publishes a salon conversion rate on Pinterest.

Compare with the intent on Google. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks for Beauty and Personal Care Search show $4.62 per click, a 10.35% conversion rate and $39.25 per lead. Pinterest numbers are not published, so measure your own. See [Pinterest vs Instagram ads for local business](/blog/pinterest-vs-instagram-ads-local-business) for the broader picture.

## How Do You Target Local Clients on Pinterest?

Pinterest's targeting overview lists keywords, interests, audiences, actalike audiences and demographics including location, language and device. You can also choose placements: home feed, search results or related Pins. Performance+ targets people searching for similar keywords or interests.

For a salon, start with keywords for the service ("balayage ideas", "bridal updo") and limit the location to your service area. Upload a customer list as an audience only if you have consent to use it. Setup steps are in our [Pinterest ads setup guide](/blog/how-to-set-up-pinterest-ads-local-business).

## How Does Pinterest Budgeting Work?

Pinterest offers lifetime and daily budgets. A Performance+ daily budget is an average, not a cap: Pinterest tries to spend no more than 125% of it in one day, averaged over a Sunday-to-Saturday week. A fixed daily budget spends the same amount each day.

The budget help page states no minimum, so check the figure shown in Ads Manager when you set up. Editing the budget mid-week resets the weekly average, so change it once, on a Monday if possible. See also [Pinterest ads cost in 2026](/blog/pinterest-ads-cost-2026).

## What Creative Gets Appointments?

Pins should look like inspiration, not like a flyer. Use your own before-and-after photos with client permission, a clear title with the service name and city, and a link to a page where booking takes one tap. Free Pins matter too: our [Pinterest SEO guide](/blog/pinterest-seo-free-traffic-small-business) shows how to earn traffic without a budget.

Keep a tracking tag on the booking page so Pinterest can report appointments. If your booking system hides conversions inside an iframe, track clicks on the booking button instead.

## What Does a $300 Test Cost per Booking? A Worked Example

The figures are an **assumption**, as Pinterest does not publish typical costs.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget for 30 days | $300 ($10 a day) |
| Cost per click | $1.00 |
| Clicks | 300 |
| Booking-page visitors who book | 3% |
| Bookings | 9 |
| Cost per booking | about $33 |
| Average first visit value | $80 |

At those numbers the test returns $720 in first-visit revenue on $300, before product and labour costs. If clicks cost $2.00 instead, bookings fall to 4 or 5 and the cost per booking doubles. Use the real figures after two weeks.

## FAQ

### How much do Pinterest ads cost for a salon?
Pinterest's help pages do not publish a typical price. Start with a small daily budget and read cost per click in Ads Manager.

### Can I target only my city?
Pinterest lists location among its demographic targeting options. Check the available areas in Ads Manager for your country.

### Is Pinterest better than Instagram for salons?
It depends on your clients. Pew shows 51% of US adults use Instagram and 37% Pinterest. Compare in the [hair salon social media guide](/blog/hair-salon-social-media-marketing).

### How long should I run a test?
Pinterest averages daily budgets over Sunday-to-Saturday weeks, so run at least two full weeks before judging.

### Should I also run Google Search ads?
Often yes. LocaliQ's 2026 Beauty and Personal Care benchmark shows a 10.35% conversion rate on Google Search, so compare both on cost per booking.
