## Do Pinterest Ads Work for Salons and Spas?

Pinterest is useful when people search for ideas before they book. Bridal updos, balayage and facial treatments all fit that pattern, because the buyer is comparing looks and prices before choosing a stylist. Whether Pins become appointments depends on your offer, your photos and how fast the booking page loads.

Pew's 2026 fact sheet lists YouTube at 84%, Instagram at 51% and TikTok at 37% of US adults. Its latest table does not report Pinterest, so check current platform data before you budget around reach. For paid search context, WordStream's industry benchmarks are dated: the Consumer Services average search cost per click was $6.40 in its 2016 data, and it has no beauty category. Use that only as a rough signal.

## How Do You Target Local Clients on Pinterest?

Start with one service rather than the whole menu. An ad for "balayage ideas" sent to a single booking page is easier to judge than a broad ad for every treatment. Choose keywords that match how clients search, and limit the location to the area you can realistically serve in a day.

Keep the audience simple. Pinterest's targeting options change, so confirm which location, keyword and interest controls appear in your own Ads Manager before you set up. Pair paid targeting with local search basics such as your Google Business Profile, which the [hair salon local SEO guide](/blog/hair-salon-local-seo-guide) covers in detail. Ads only work if the salon also appears clearly when someone searches for it.

## How Should a Salon Set Up a Pinterest Test?

A short test gives clearer answers than a long, unfocused campaign. Use this checklist:

1. Pick one service with a clear price, such as a colour refresh or a 60-minute facial.
2. Set a fixed daily budget you can afford to lose for 30 days.
3. Create three Pin designs with different photos or titles, all linked to the same booking page.
4. Add tracking to the booking confirmation, or to the booking button if your system blocks tracking.
5. Run the test for at least two full weeks before changing anything.
6. Compare cost per booking with a Google Search test for the same service, using the [Google Ads guide for hair salons](/blog/google-ads-for-hair-salons-complete-guide).

Avoid switching ads on and off by hand to compare them. Meta's guidance on its own platform says informal testing can create overlapping audiences and unreliable results, so split the budget evenly and change one variable at a time.

## What Creative Gets Appointments?

Pins should look like inspiration, not a flyer. Use your own before-and-after photos, and only with client permission. Put the service name and city in the title so the Pin explains itself without a click.

Make the landing page match the Pin. If the Pin shows a silver toner, the booking page should name that service and show the price. A social proof element from real reviews can help, and the [salon marketing ideas guide](/blog/salon-marketing-ideas-25-strategies-that-fill-your-chair-every-week) lists low-cost ways to gather them. Creators can also add reach; the [influencer marketing guide for hair salons](/blog/influencer-marketing-for-hair-salons-how-to-get-free-bookings-from-local-creator) explains how.

## How Much Should a Salon Spend on a Pinterest Test?

A $300 test is a learning budget, not a growth budget. Set it as a fixed share of your monthly marketing spend, so a weak result does not hurt the salon's cash flow. The [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to split a small budget across channels.

Judge the test on bookings and revenue, not clicks. A cheap click that never books is more expensive than a costly one that fills a chair. If the cost per booking stays above your average first-visit value after the test, pause the Pinterest spend and put the money into the channel that books more cheaply.

## FAQ

### How much do Pinterest ads cost for a salon?
Set a small daily budget and read the real cost per click in your own Ads Manager.

### Is Pinterest better than Instagram for salons?
It depends on your clients. Pew's 2026 data shows Instagram at 51% of US adults, while its latest table does not report Pinterest.

### How long should a Pinterest test run?
At least two full weeks. Judge the result on cost per booking, not on click counts from the first few days.

### Should a salon also run Google Search ads?
Often yes. Test both on the same service and compare cost per booking, since intent differs between a search and a browse.
