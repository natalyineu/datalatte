Fresha, Booksy and Vagaro all charge a monthly subscription in 2026, so none of them is a free option any more. Fresha starts at $19.95 a month for one team member, Booksy at $29.99 plus $20 per extra staff member, and Vagaro at $30 (with a promotional $23.99 for new accounts) plus $10 per extra bookable calendar. The right pick depends on how you win clients: Booksy if you want its marketplace, Vagaro if you want the most built-in marketing, Fresha if you want low base pricing and a UK-based vendor. All prices below are US list prices checked in October 2026.

Take one Saturday booking. A brand-new client finds you in a marketplace and books an $85 color. On Fresha, 20% of a new client's first booking is the fee, so you hand over $17. On Booksy, if she came through a paid Boost, the fee is 30% of that first visit, so $25.50 (capped at $100). On Vagaro, the marketplace listing is free. Multiply by ten new clients a month and the monthly subscription starts to look like the small number. That is the real comparison, and the headline prices hide it.

The app you pick handles appointments, client data, payments and automated messages, often for years, and switching later means migrating your client list. Here is a plain comparison of the three platforms most often shortlisted in the US and UK.

## Booksy

Booksy's main draw is its consumer marketplace: clients can discover your business in the app, not just book if they already know you. Its pricing is a single all-inclusive subscription, with extra cost only when you add staff or choose to use Boost ([Booksy pricing](https://biz.booksy.com/pricing)).

**What it does well**
- One plan with all features included, no tiers to compare
- Text and email marketing tools, loyalty cards, automated rebooking reminders and a waitlist, per its [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id725335996)
- Deposits and cancellation-fee enforcement to cut no-shows

**Watch out for**
- Boost's 30% fee on a Boost client's first visit adds up fast on higher-priced services
- Cost climbs by $20 for every extra staff member, the steepest per-seat rate of the three
- Compare its reporting and automation with Vagaro's during the 14-day trial before committing

**Best for:** independent stylists, barbershops and nail salons that want client discovery built in.

## Vagaro

Vagaro is the broadest of the three. It suits multi-service businesses: salons that sell retail products, spas with several treatments and studios that run classes.

**What it does well**
- Inventory tracking, loyalty programs, AI marketing tools, unlimited appointment reminders and payment processing are listed as part of the standard plan ([Vagaro pricing](https://www.vagaro.com/pro/pricing))
- A free marketplace listing and a Daily Deals feature for attracting new clients ([Vagaro](https://www.vagaro.com/learn/vagaro-more-than-booking-software))
- Email and text marketing with ready-made templates
- Cheapest extra staff cost: $10 per calendar, and nothing more after seven

**Watch out for**
- More to learn than Booksy or Fresha
- Deals can attract bargain hunters rather than regulars
- Check which add-ons (for example a website builder or text marketing) cost extra, since total cost can exceed the headline price

**Best for:** full-service salons, spas and barbershops with retail, and anyone who wants marketing and reporting in the same tool.

## Fresha

Fresha is a London-based company that built its name on a free booking tool. That is no longer the case: it now charges $19.95 a month for the Independent plan (one team member) or $14.95 per team member on the Team plan, each with a 7-day trial ([Fresha pricing](https://www.fresha.com/pricing)). The headline price is the lowest of the three for a solo stylist, but fees come on top.

**What it does well**
- Lowest entry price for a single stylist
- Scheduling, point of sale, client management, inventory, gift cards, packages and memberships are included in both plans
- Own-link and Google or social bookings carry no marketplace commission
- Strong UK presence

**Watch out for**
- A 20% commission (minimum $6) on each new client from the Fresha marketplace
- Messaging beyond the free allowance is metered: 100 free messages and 50 free marketing emails per team member each month, then about $0.02 each
- Optional add-ons such as AI Concierge ($99.95 per location per month) and Client Loyalty ($59.95) add up quickly

**Best for:** solo stylists and small teams who take most bookings through their own link, plus UK-based salons.

## The math: what a 4-chair salon pays per month

Prices are the list prices above. The salon, the ten new marketplace clients and the $85 first visit are assumptions for illustration, not a real salon and not a market statistic. Card processing is left out because it depends on your sales. Swap in your own staff count and new-client numbers.

Example: owner plus 3 stylists (4 people). Assume Fresha on the Team plan at $14.95 per person, Vagaro at $30 plus $10 for each of 3 extra calendars, Booksy at $29.99 plus $20 for each of 3 extra staff.

| Line | Booksy | Vagaro | Fresha |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subscription | $29.99 + 3 x $20 = $89.99 | $30 + 3 x $10 = $60.00 | 4 x $14.95 = $59.80 |
| Marketplace fees, 0 new marketplace clients | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Total, 0 new marketplace clients | $89.99 | $60.00 | $59.80 |
| Marketplace fees, 10 new clients at $85 (assumes all via Boost on Booksy) | 10 x $25.50 = $255 | $0 | 10 x $17 = $170 |
| Total, 10 new marketplace clients | $344.99 | $60.00 | $229.80 |

Read it like this. With no marketplace clients, Fresha and Vagaro are within 20 cents of each other and Booksy costs about $30 more. With ten marketplace clients, the commission is almost three times the subscription on both Booksy and Fresha. Whether that is a good trade depends on the clients: ten new clients worth $85 on the first visit is $850 in revenue you may not have had otherwise. If they come back, the fee is a one-time cost on that first visit. If they would have booked through your own link anyway, you are paying for nothing. That is the question to answer before you pick.

## Which should you choose?

- **Choose Booksy** if you run a barbershop or nail studio and want marketplace discovery without a per-booking commission unless you switch on Boost.
- **Choose Vagaro** if you sell retail, want marketing and reporting in one tool, and have several staff.
- **Choose Fresha** if you are a solo stylist or small team, most of your clients come from Instagram or word of mouth, and you want the lowest base price.

A slightly higher subscription often beats a commission model if you rely on the marketplace.

## One gap all three leave open

Booking apps can send review requests, but they will not build your review count for you. In the 50 hair salons we pulled from Google Maps in Austin in Oct 2026, the median shop had 359 reviews and 32 had more than 200. Chicago's 50 had a median of 251. A new client who finds you in a marketplace will still check Google. Earn reviews deliberately: a personal text after the appointment with your direct review link, or a dedicated review-request tool.

## Do this before you pick an app

1. Count your stylists and write the per-seat total for each app (use the table above).
2. Ask your last 10 new clients how they found you. If 0 to 2 say a marketplace, favor the app with no commission.
3. Start the free trial on your top 2 apps the same day: 14 days on Booksy, 30 on Vagaro, 7 on Fresha.
4. Export your client list as a CSV before you leave your old tool.
5. Put the new booking link in your Instagram bio, Google Business Profile and website.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I switch between these platforms without losing my client data?**
A: Usually, yes, with some manual work. Most platforms let you export your client list as a CSV file and import it into the new one. Appointment history is harder to move because each system stores it differently. Switch in a quiet period, give yourself a couple of weeks, and tell clients about the new booking link.

**Q: Is Fresha still free?**
A: No. Fresha now charges $19.95 per month for the Independent plan or $14.95 per team member for the Team plan, plus card processing and a 20% new-client commission (minimum $6) on marketplace bookings ([Fresha pricing](https://www.fresha.com/pricing)). Returning clients and bookings from your own link have no commission.

**Q: Which booking platform is best for a one-chair solo stylist?**
A: Fresha has the lowest base price at $19.95 a month, so it is usually the cheapest start. Booksy is worth the extra $10 a month if marketplace discovery matters in your city. Vagaro has the most features, which a solo stylist may not use.

**Q: Do these platforms work in the UK and Australia?**
A: Fresha is UK-based and well established there. Booksy and Vagaro both operate outside the US and publish regional pricing pages. Prices and payment processing differ by country, so confirm local availability and rates for your market.

**Q: What about clients who prefer to book by phone?**
A: All three let you add appointments manually, so phone clients still fit in. Put your booking link in your Instagram bio, Google Business Profile and website, and let clients move online at their own pace.
