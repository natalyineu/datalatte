A sports club should run a small, local Google Search campaign for trial sessions and membership enquiries, track calls and form submissions, and block irrelevant searches with negative keywords. LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks for Sports and Recreation show $2.77 per click, a 7.69% conversion rate and $44.26 per lead. Those are industry averages, not a forecast for your club.

This guide is for local clubs, leagues, gyms and classes in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. Each figure has a source at the end, and the worked example is labelled as an assumption.

## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Sports Club?

Sports and Recreation is cheaper per click than the all-industry average of $5.42, and its click-through rate (8.75%) and conversion rate (7.69%) are above or close to the average of 6.64% and 8.18%. Cost per lead is $44.26 against $66.69 for all industries.

Your own cost will depend on the sport, the area and the competition. A busy city with several gyms is not the same as a village football club. The report does not split the category by sport or by country, so use your first month of data as your real benchmark.

## How Much Does a New Member Cost? A Worked Example

The inputs below are **assumptions** built on the category benchmarks. A conversion here is a trial booking, call or enquiry form.

| Input | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $500 | Assumption |
| Cost per click | $2.77 | LocaliQ 2026, Sports and Recreation |
| Clicks | about 180 | $500 / $2.77 |
| Conversion rate | 7.69% | LocaliQ 2026 |
| Leads | about 14 | 180 x 7.69% |
| Cost per lead | about $36 | $500 / 14 |
| Lead becomes member | 25% | Assumption |
| Members won | about 3.5 | 14 x 25% |
| Cost per new member | about $144 | $500 / 3.5 |
| Membership value | $320 | Assumption: $40 a month for 8 months |

Under these assumptions the ads pay back. If only 10% of leads join, cost per member rises to about $360 and the same budget loses money. The follow-up call matters as much as the ad.

## Which Keywords Work for a Sports Club?

Bid on searches from people who want to join or try something now: the sport plus "classes", "club", "lessons" or "trial", with your town. Separate ad groups by sport or age group, so the ad matches the search and the page.

Negative keywords stop wasted spend. Google says they exclude search terms from Search ads and do not match close variants, so add plural and singular forms yourself. Start with:

- Jobs, coaching careers and "salary"
- Equipment shopping searches if you do not sell kit
- Scores, fixtures and live-stream searches
- Free-only intent, unless you run a free taster

Check the Search terms report weekly. Our [gym keyword list](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-gyms-in-2026) shows how keyword groups can be organised.

## How Do You Keep Ads Within Travel Distance?

Members rarely travel far, so set the location to the area people will drive or walk from. Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km and that very small targets may show ads only intermittently.

Google recommends "Presence or interest" as a best practice on Search, but it notes that "Presence" can fit when you only want people physically in the area. If you see clicks from people who live too far away, test "Presence" and compare lead quality.

## How Do You Measure Sign-ups?

Count the actions that lead to a membership, not just clicks. Three that work for clubs:

- Call assets. Google bills a click on a call asset at the same rate as a headline click, and you can set a minimum call length for it to count as a conversion.
- Lead form assets, available to eligible advertisers in supported countries. Google stores leads for 60 days, so export them or connect your CRM.
- A form or booking page with a conversion tag.

Ask every new member how they found you and compare the answer with your reports. See also [Facebook Ads for gyms and fitness studios](/blog/facebook-ads-for-gyms-fitness-studios-complete-guide) if you want to compare channels.

## FAQ

### How much should a sports club spend on Google Ads?
There is no standard budget. At the benchmark $2.77 per click, $500 a month buys roughly 180 clicks. Start small and raise the budget only once you know your cost per new member.

### What is a good cost per lead for a sports club?
LocaliQ lists $44.26 for Sports and Recreation in 2026. Compare it with what a member is worth to you, not with other clubs.

### Can I advertise a free trial class?
Yes. Use the trial in the headline and send people to a page where they can book it. Track bookings as the conversion.

### Do I need a website to run Google Ads?
Ads need a landing page, so you need at least one page that explains the offer and has a booking or contact method.
