## How Big Is the South Australian Small Business Market?

The ABS release of 18 August 2026 shows South Australia going from 168,697 businesses at the start of 2025-26 to 174,310 at the end, a rise of 5,613 or 3.3%. Nationally the count rose 3.1% to 2,814,778. In SA, 26,412 businesses entered and 20,685 exited during the year, so about one in eight stopped trading.

The same data tracks the 156,120 SA businesses operating in June 2022. By June 2026, 100,817 of them, or 64.6%, were still operating, against 61.9% nationally. The ABS notes that multi-location businesses are counted in one place only. The figures say nothing about which businesses are small, so they describe competition, not size.

## Why Start with Google Business Profile?

Google's [Business Profile help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091) says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that you cannot pay for a better local ranking. Reviews and links from other sites feed prominence. A suburb cafe or a plumber in the Adelaide Hills competes on all three.

Follow Google's [representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177): use your real business name, choose the fewest and most specific categories, and, if you work at customers' homes, set a service area and hide your address. See our [coffee shop profile guide](/blog/coffee-shop-google-business-profile-optimization) for a field-by-field list that suits most shopfronts.

## How Should You Target Suburbs with Google Ads?

Google's [location targeting help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722043) says a radius must be at least 1 km. It recommends "Presence or interest" for Search campaigns and says "Presence" fits when you only want people physically in the area.

For a metro cafe or salon, start with the suburbs you actually draw from. For a regional trade across a wide area, choose towns or a larger radius, since fewer people live there. Add suburb names to ad text and landing pages. Our [Google Ads vs Facebook Ads comparison](/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-local-business) explains when each fits.

## Which Social Platforms Reach South Australians?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report gives national figures for Australia, not South Australia. Facebook ad reach was 17.7 million (65.3% of the population), Instagram 15.2 million (56.0%) and TikTok 10.9 million adults. These are advertising reach estimates, not active users, and the figures overlap.

No SA-only platform split could be verified. If you use Meta ads, test a narrow radius around your shop and compare cost per enquiry against Google. Our [national Australia guide](/blog/local-marketing-australia-small-business-2026) covers the platform table and the cost caveats in more detail.

## Can You Plan Around Adelaide Fringe?

Yes, if you are in or near the city. InDaily reported on 14 July 2026 that Adelaide Fringe 2026 sold about 1,083,000 tickets and drew more than six million attendances at ticketed and free events. It reported $165.8 million in new expenditure and $238.3 million in total economic contribution. This is a news report of the festival's impact report, so treat it as secondary.

Fringe runs for several weeks, so plan offers, opening hours and a refreshed profile before it starts. The data does not show which kinds of business gain most, so judge by your own sales.

## What Are the Rules for Email and SMS Marketing?

Australian rules are set out on [business.gov.au](https://business.gov.au/online/promoting-your-business-by-email-or-text-messages). You need permission before sending marketing messages, either express (the person knows and accepts marketing) or inferred. Keep a record of who gave permission, when and how.

Unsubscribe requests must be honoured within 5 working days, the option must work for at least 30 days after sending, and you cannot charge a fee or ask for a login. Branded SMS needs a registered sender ID or it shows as "Unverified." For SMS ideas, see our [SMS marketing best practices](/blog/sms-marketing-best-practices).

## Worked Example: What Could A$600 a Month Buy?

All inputs are **assumptions** for a Hills cafe. They are not South Australian benchmarks.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly Google Ads budget (assumption) | A$600 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | A$3.00 |
| Clicks | 200 |
| Click to call or direction rate (assumption) | 5% |

At A$120 per new customer, the ads only pay if a customer is worth more than that over time, which for a cafe usually means regular visits. If your real click cost is A$1.50, the same arithmetic gives A$60 per new customer. Replace each assumption with figures from your own account after 30 days.

## What Mistakes Should SA Businesses Avoid?

- **Targeting the whole state:** a Unley bakery gains little from clicks in Port Augusta. Set a radius or suburb list.
- **Ignoring reviews:** Google says more reviews and positive ratings can help local ranking, and replying can help you stand out.
- **Messaging without consent:** see the rules above, and keep records.
- **Judging by clicks only:** track calls, direction requests and bookings.

## FAQ

### How many businesses are there in South Australia?
The ABS counted 174,310 actively trading businesses at 30 June 2026, up 3.3% in a year.

### How much should a South Australian small business spend on Google Ads?
No verified SA benchmark exists. Start with a small monthly budget you can afford to lose, track enquiries, and scale only when cost per customer is clear.

### Do I need consent to text customers in Australia?
Yes. business.gov.au says you must have permission before sending marketing messages, and must keep a record of who gave it, when and how.

### Does Adelaide Fringe help small businesses?
Reported visitor spending was large, but the figures do not break out small businesses. Plan for it, then measure your own sales.

### Can a mobile or home-based business use a Google Business Profile?
Yes, as a service-area business with the address hidden if customers do not visit.
