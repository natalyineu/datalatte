## What Makes a Good Prompt?

OpenAI's prompting guide gives advice that applies to any chat: give clear, explicit instructions, show examples of what you want, separate sections of the prompt clearly, and say what format you need. For a local business that means five parts: who you are, who you write for, what you want, the facts to use, and the format.

A reusable template:

> You write for [business type] in [town]. Our customers are [audience]. Write [format, e.g. three Instagram captions under 40 words] about [offer or topic]. Use only these facts: [price, hours, dates]. Tone: [friendly, plain]. Do not invent discounts, reviews or statistics.

The last sentence matters. Without it, chat tools often fill gaps with made-up details.

## Which Prompts Help With Social Posts and Content?

- "Write 5 Instagram captions for [business type] in [town] announcing [new item or service]. Facts: [price, start date]. Each under 40 words, one emoji at most."
- "Turn this customer question into a short FAQ answer for our website: [paste question and your real answer]. Keep my facts, 60 words max."
- "Suggest 10 post ideas for [season or holiday] that a [business type] can make with a phone. List what to photograph for each."
- "Rewrite this paragraph in plain English at a 7th grade reading level, keeping all numbers: [paste]."

Reuse one photo across formats by asking for a caption, a story line and a short text for a window sign. See our guide on using ChatGPT for local business marketing for a fuller workflow.

## Which Prompts Help With Email?

- "Write a welcome email for new subscribers of [business]. Include [offer] and one clear link. Under 120 words."
- "Give me 8 subject line options for an email about [offer]. Under 45 characters, no all-caps, no false urgency."
- "Write a reminder text for an appointment on [day]. Include how to reschedule. Under 160 characters."
- "Write a win-back email for customers who have not visited in [number] days. Offer [real offer]. Friendly, no guilt."

US law applies to the finished email, not the tool. The FTC's CAN-SPAM guide requires honest subject lines, a postal address and a working opt-out. Read the tool options in our AI email marketing tools guide.

## Which Prompts Help With Google Business Profile and Reviews?

- "Write a first draft of my Google Business Profile description. Business: [type]. Services: [list]. Area: [neighborhood]. Plain words, no superlatives."
- "Write a polite reply to this 5-star review: [paste]. Mention one detail from it. Under 50 words."
- "Write a calm reply to this 2-star review: [paste]. Apologize for the specific problem, offer to fix it offline, no excuses. Under 80 words."
- "Write a short message asking a customer for a Google review. Include the placeholder [review link]. Do not offer a discount or gift."

That last rule comes from Google: offering incentives such as discounts or free products in exchange for reviews is strictly prohibited. Google recommends giving customers a link or QR code. ## Which Prompts Help With Ads and Landing Pages?

- "Write 10 headlines under 30 characters for a Google Search ad for [service] in [town]. Use only these facts: [facts]."
- "Write 4 descriptions under 90 characters for the same ad."
- "List 25 negative keywords for a [business type] search campaign, grouped by reason: jobs, free, DIY, wrong service."
- "Review this landing page text and list three things a first-time visitor might not understand: [paste]."

Check character limits in the ad platform. Chat tools often miscount. Test several versions with a simple split test, as explained in our A/B testing guide.

## How Do You Check AI Output Before Publishing?

Google's guidance on generative AI content gives a short checklist you can follow: fact-check all of it, including titles, meta descriptions and alt text; make sure it adds value for readers; and be open about how it was made where that matters.

Worked example, all inputs are assumptions: a bakery asks for five captions about a new sourdough loaf. The draft says "baked fresh every morning since 2010" and "voted best in town". The owner opened in 2015 and has no award. She deletes both claims, adds the real price and pickup time, and posts one caption. Time spent is five minutes, and nothing false went live.

## FAQ

### Is it legal to use ChatGPT for business copy?

Nothing in the sources used here bans it. The same advertising rules apply as for any copy: no false claims, honest reviews and compliant emails. The FTC's consumer reviews rule covers fake reviews, so never ask a tool to invent testimonials.

### Will Google penalize AI-written content?

Google's guidance says generative AI can help with research and structure, but the content must meet its spam policies. Producing many pages without adding value can break the scaled content abuse policy.

### How much does ChatGPT cost?

### Should I paste customer data into prompts?

Avoid names, emails and phone numbers. Use anonymous descriptions instead.

### Which prompt should I start with?

Start with a review reply or a welcome email. Both are short, repeatable, and easy to check against facts you know.
