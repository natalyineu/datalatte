Programmatic TV means buying streaming-TV ads through software, with your own audience, location and budget, instead of booking a time slot with a TV station. A local business can start small: Roku Ads Manager requires a $500 minimum lifetime campaign budget, and Disney Campaign Manager states a $500 minimum per campaign.

This guide compares the self-serve options, explains cost and gives a worked budget. Prices move, so figures are as of 1 November 2026 from the pages cited. Anything not published is marked as such.

## What Is Programmatic TV Advertising?

It is the automated buying of video ads shown on connected TVs and streaming apps. You pick who sees the ad by location and other settings, set a budget and pay as the ads run. Roku says you pay as the campaign delivers, with no upfront payment, and can pause or extend at any time.

It differs from traditional TV, where a station sells fixed slots and production costs can be high. Roku's page says a broadcast-quality commercial has cost upwards of $10,000 and that existing social video can be adapted for TV. If you are new to the channel, start with our [connected TV guide for local businesses](/blog/connected-tv-ads-local-business-how-to-start).

## Which Platforms Let a Small Business Buy?

| Platform | What it states | Minimum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roku Ads Manager | CPM pricing, dynamic rates, self-serve | $500 lifetime campaign budget |
| Disney Campaign Manager | Hulu and Disney+ inventory, pay by credit card | $500 per campaign |
| StackAdapt | Lists CTV among its channels | No minimum ad spend stated |
| Amazon DSP | Prime Video, Fire TV, Twitch | No minimum published; contact Amazon |

Roku also says $500 to $1,000 is enough for a first-time advertiser to get a campaign live and begin generating meaningful data. Neither Roku nor Amazon publishes a specific CPM rate. Compare more options in our [CTV platforms comparison](/blog/ctv-advertising-platforms-compared-2026).

## How Much Does It Cost?

Roku's CPM is dynamic and depends on demand and available inventory. One reseller, AdWave, reports a blended US CTV CPM of about $26 in Q2 2026, attributing it to eMarketer, with ranges from $15 to $25 for free ad-supported streaming up to $45 to $85 for addressable buys. That is a secondary source, so treat the numbers as a guide, not a quote.

Your own cost depends on your location, audience and the platform. Get the live figure from the campaign planner before committing.

## The Math: What Does $500 Buy?

All inputs are **assumptions** based on the secondary range above.

| CPM (assumption) | Impressions for $500 |
| --- | --- |
| $15 | about 33,300 |
| $26 | about 19,200 |
| $45 | about 11,100 |

Impressions = $500 divided by CPM, times 1,000. Even the best case is a modest reach, so a $500 test is for learning, not for scale. Define success first: visits to a landing page, direction requests or branded searches. Our [CTV vs OTT explainer](/blog/ctv-vs-ott-advertising-explained) clarifies the terms.

## What Creative Do You Need?

A video ad made for a TV screen. Keep it short, with your business name and location early. Do not reuse a vertical phone video without checking how it looks full screen. Roku mentions tools that adapt social videos to TV format; check what your chosen platform offers.

## How Should You Measure It?

TV ads on a phone-centric funnel are hard to trace. Use a dedicated landing page or QR code, then compare branded searches and direction requests before and after. Treat the first test as a baseline. If you cannot measure a lift, spend on Search instead.

## FAQ

### Is programmatic TV the same as CTV advertising?

They overlap. CTV is the screen and apps; programmatic is the way the ads are bought. Most small-business CTV buying is programmatic.

### What is the cheapest way to try it?

Use a self-serve platform with a stated $500 minimum, such as Roku Ads Manager or Disney Campaign Manager, or ask StackAdapt about its plans.

### Can I target only my city?

Platforms offer location targeting, but options and minimum audience sizes vary. Check the planner before you buy.

### Does programmatic TV work for local business?

No reliable independent benchmark was found. Roku cites one advertiser result, which is a single case. Run a test and measure.
