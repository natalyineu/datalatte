Snapchat suits a local business when its customers are under 35, the offer is visual, and the trade area is small enough for cheap frequency. Self-serve ads start at a $5 daily minimum, on Snapchat pages checked in November 2026, although Snapchat's own guidance puts a working learning budget nearer $20 to $50 a day.

That gap between minimum and workable is the whole decision. Five dollars a day delivers impressions but teaches the auction almost nothing. A two-week test at $20 a day costs about $280 and produces a readable cost per call, swipe or direction request.

## What does Snapchat advertising actually cost?

The floor is $5 per day per campaign in Ads Manager, with no monthly minimum, on Snapchat's self-serve terms checked in November 2026. Secondary 2026 market readings put CPMs roughly between $5 and $14 depending on objective, with a February 2026 Gupta Media reading at $14.05.

Treat CPM comparisons against Meta carefully. Snapchat reach skews younger, so a cheaper thousand impressions can still be the wrong thousand. Judge both platforms on cost per booking action inside the same postcode set. [Instagram retargeting](/blog/instagram-retargeting-how-to-re-engage-website-visitors) shows the measurement setup that makes that comparison honest.

**Assumption example.** Assume a salon tests Snapchat at $20 a day for 14 days, or $280. At an assumed $10 CPM that buys 28,000 impressions. If 0.5% tap through and 5% of taps book, the test yields about 7 bookings at $40 each. Every rate is an assumption; replace CPM and conversion with the account's real first-week numbers.

## Which local businesses fit Snapchat?

Fit is demographic before it is geographic. Student-heavy services, bubble tea and dessert shops, budget fitness, beauty services aimed at younger clients, and entertainment venues match the audience. Services bought mainly over 40, such as most home repairs, rarely do.

Creative fit matters next. Snapchat creative is vertical, fast and native-looking; a repurposed television-style advert usually underperforms a 6-second phone video of the actual product or room. One strong vertical asset beats five polished ones nobody watches to the swipe.

Businesses already running short video elsewhere should compare honestly with [YouTube ads for local business](/blog/youtube-ads-for-local-business) on cost per booking. Businesses already running Meta should compare honestly. [Meta ads for hair salons](/blog/hair-salon-meta-ads) covers the incumbent route for one Snapchat-friendly trade, including the booking objective Snapchat tests should be judged against.

## How should a local business test Snapchat?

Run one campaign, one postcode set, one offer, for 14 days. Use a call or website-booking objective, not raw impressions, and put a unique booking phrase or landing page on the ad. Without that separation, Snapchat results blur into every other channel's.

Set a kill rule before launch: for example, pause if cost per booking exceeds twice the Meta figure after $150 of spend. Small budgets cannot average their way out of a bad first signal; they can only stop early and keep the remainder.

Winners scale by widening postcodes before raising daily budget. Frequency inside a small area saturates quickly, and the same students seeing the same snap eight times stop responding. New postcodes refresh the audience at the same CPM.

## FAQ

### What is the minimum budget for Snapchat ads?

Snapchat's self-serve Ads Manager minimum is $5 per day, with no monthly minimum, on terms checked in November 2026. Snapchat guidance reported in 2026 puts a workable learning budget at $20 to $50 a day. A $5 test proves delivery, not profitability; budget $280 for a readable two-week answer.

### Are Snapchat ads cheaper than Meta ads?

Per thousand impressions, often yes: secondary 2026 readings put Snapchat CPMs near $5 to $14. Per booking, not always, because the audience skews under 35. A business whose buyers are older can pay less per impression and more per customer. Compare cost per booking inside identical postcodes.

### Which businesses should skip Snapchat?

Businesses whose buyers are mostly over 40, whose offer needs long explanation, or whose trade area is rural with thin Snapchat usage. Home services, legal and most B2B trades fit that list. Their budgets usually work harder on search and Meta, where intent and age targeting are stronger.

### How long should a Snapchat test run?

Fourteen days at $20 or more per day is a practical minimum for a local read. Shorter tests end inside the learning phase and report noise as results. Decide the kill rule and the booking target before launch, then judge only on completed bookings or calls.
