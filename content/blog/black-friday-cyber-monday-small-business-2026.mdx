In 2026, Black Friday falls on November 27 and Cyber Monday on November 30, with Small Business Saturday between them on November 28. A coffee shop, salon, pet groomer or fitness studio does not need a deep discount to use that weekend. The margin-safe plays are gift cards with a bonus for later, a pre-sale to the email and SMS list, bundles, capped slots and correct Google Business Profile holiday hours. The national figures below are context, not local forecasts.

## What are the Black Friday and Cyber Monday dates in 2026?

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26. Black Friday is Friday, November 27, Small Business Saturday is November 28, and Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30. Adobe defines Cyber Week as the five days from Thanksgiving to Cyber Monday, so in 2026 the whole stretch sits inside November, according to [PPC Land's report on Adobe's forecast](https://ppc.land/adobe-puts-us-online-holiday-spend-at-275-1-billion-up-6-7/) (secondary).

In 2025, Cyber Monday landed on December 1. That shift matters for planning: last year's results cannot be lined up day for day against this year's calendar, and the follow-up day this year is Tuesday, December 1.

From Monday, November 16 to Wednesday, November 25 there are ten days to prepare. The checklist later in this article uses that window. Anything that needs approval, such as a Google Business Profile hours change, belongs at the start of it.

## What do the national forecasts say, and what do they not say?

NRF predicts November and December retail sales will grow 4.2% over 2025, reaching $1.013 trillion, according to [NRF's winter holiday page](https://nrf.com/research-insights/holiday-data-and-trends/winter-holidays?gmid=5486). Adobe forecasts $275.1 billion in US online holiday spending from November 1 to December 31, up 6.7% ([Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/09/29/adobe-projection-2026-online-holiday-sales-275-billion/amp/), secondary).

All of these figures are national. None describes foot traffic, bookings or demand in a single neighborhood.

## Why do gift cards and pre-sales suit a local business better than deep discounts?

NRF's 2026 survey found 47% of holiday shoppers want to receive gift cards, ahead of clothing at 44%. Personal care or beauty items were named by 24% ([NRF](https://nrf.com/research-insights/holiday-data-and-trends/winter-holidays?gmid=5486)). That favors salons, groomers and studios, whose product is an appointment rather than a box.

The same page says 43% of holiday shoppers plan to start before November and 56% say the economy will affect their spending. A pre-sale to existing customers reaches people before the public weekend, and a bonus gives a visible deal without cutting the base price.

In NRF's 2024 survey, 57% of planned Thanksgiving-weekend shoppers cited deals and discounts as a reason ([Hardware Retailing](https://hardwareretailing.com/holiday-sales-growth-expected-nrf-says/), secondary). A deal can come as a bonus card or bundle rather than a markdown.

## Which offers fit a coffee shop, salon, pet groomer or fitness studio?

The table below sets out one offer per business type. Dollar amounts are deliberately left out; set them from your own cost per service. The only rule is that the extra value arrives later, in a slow period, and is not stacked with other discounts.

| Business | Core offer | Margin-safe structure | Cap or limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coffee shop | Gift card with a bonus card for a January visit | Bonus card is drinks only, weekday mornings or afternoons, one per customer | Bonus issued only on purchases made Nov 27 to Nov 30 |
| Salon | Service gift card with an off-peak bonus card | Bonus valid on slow weekdays only, excluded from the highest-cost services | Appointments per stylist set in advance |
| Pet groomer | Prepaid multi-visit grooming bundle | Visits spaced across the year, price below single visits but above cost | Pre-sale limited to open future slots |
| Fitness studio | Class-pack gift card with a start date after the holidays | Bonus is an extra class, not a free month; no waived fees | Limited by class size and trainer hours |

For groomers, Adobe expects online pet product sales to rise 93% during Cyber Week. That compares Cyber Week with the average September 2026 level, not with last year, and it covers products, not grooming ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/adobe-puts-us-online-holiday-spend-at-275-1-billion-up-6-7/), secondary).

## How do bundles and limited slots protect margin?

A bundle moves the discount from price to volume. A groomer who sells three visits up front collects cash now and secures repeat bookings, while the per-visit price stays close to normal. The customer sees a clear saving, and the business sees a known number of future appointments.

Limited slots work the same way. Chairs, grooming tables and class spots are fixed capacity. A pre-sale capped at the number of open future slots cannot oversell, and a visible cap gives a reason to buy before November 30 without inventing urgency.

Two rules keep the structure safe. First, calculate the cost of every dollar redeemed, including the bonus, before publishing the offer. Second, set a use-by window on bonus cards only, and confirm with an accountant whether use-by dates are allowed on the cards in the relevant state.

## What should the Google Business Profile say over the holiday weekend?

Customers searching on the weekend look for hours first. Google's help page says holiday or temporary hours are set as special hours, and a business can also mark itself closed ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617)).

Google also states it reviews changes before they go live on a profile. Submit special hours for November 26 through December 1 at the start of the checklist, not on Thanksgiving morning.

Keep the business name exactly as it appears on signage. The same page says the name should match real-world signage, so promotional wording such as a sale name does not belong in it. Check the phone number and address while editing.

The full edit routine is in the [Google Business Profile optimization checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist). Because Adobe forecasts 63% of Thanksgiving Day online sales to come from mobile ([Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/09/29/adobe-projection-2026-online-holiday-sales-275-billion/amp/), secondary), open the gift card page on a phone and complete a test purchase.

## What is the day-by-day checklist from November 16 to December 1?

The calendar below assumes one offer per business, a list that has opted in to email or SMS, and a gift card product that can be sold online or at the counter. Longer planning rhythms are laid out in the [12-month local marketing calendar](/blog/local-business-marketing-calendar-week-by-week-12-months).

| Date | Task |
|---|---|
| Mon, Nov 16 | Choose one offer, calculate cost per redeemed dollar, set caps and use-by window for bonus cards |
| Tue, Nov 17 | Build the gift card and bundle product; run a test purchase and a test redemption |
| Wed, Nov 18 | Submit special hours for Nov 26 to Dec 1 on Google Business Profile; check phone and address |
| Thu, Nov 19 | Write email and SMS copy; set up a mobile-friendly booking or purchase page |
| Fri, Nov 20 | Send the pre-sale to the opted-in list, with the end date stated |
| Sat, Nov 21 | Put counter cards and signage in place; brief staff on one sentence about the offer |
| Sun, Nov 22 | Send a short SMS reminder to opted-in contacts |
| Mon, Nov 23 | Count pre-sale sales against slot caps |
| Tue, Nov 24 | Photograph the space, product and team; schedule posts for Black Friday and Saturday |
| Wed, Nov 25 | Confirm staffing, POS and hours; check that special hours show as live |
| Thu, Nov 26 | Thanksgiving: keep posting light, confirm hours, check the mobile page |
| Fri, Nov 27 | Open the public offer by email, SMS and in store; watch slot counts |
| Sat, Nov 28 | Small Business Saturday: feature the team and the neighborhood, ask happy customers for reviews |
| Sun, Nov 29 | Remind people who opened but did not buy |
| Mon, Nov 30 | Cyber Monday: send the last-day message for online gift cards and bundles; close the offer that night |

## What does a gift-card promotion earn? A worked example

This example uses a hair salon. Every input is an assumption, not a benchmark, and all figures ignore payment fees, rent and marketing costs.

Assumptions: the salon sells 60 gift cards at $100 each over the weekend. Each card comes with a $20 bonus card valid on weekdays from January to mid-March. 90% of the $100 balances are redeemed, and 70% of the bonus cards are redeemed. Variable cost (stylist pay and products) is 40% of the service price.

| Line | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Cash collected | 60 cards × $100 | $6,000 |
| Main card services redeemed | $6,000 × 90% | $5,400 |
| Bonus services redeemed | 60 × $20 × 70% | $840 |
| Variable cost | ($5,400 + $840) × 40% | $2,496 |
| Contribution | $6,000 − $2,496 | $3,504 |

For comparison, assume the same 60 customers each book one $100 service at 20% off. Revenue is 60 × $80 = $4,800, variable cost is 60 × $40 = $2,400, and contribution is $2,400.

Under these assumptions the gift card plan collects the cash in November, fills slow weekdays after the holidays and leaves 10% of the main balances unredeemed. The result depends entirely on the redemption and cost inputs, so replace them with the business's own numbers before launch.

## FAQ

### Does a small business have to discount on Black Friday?

No. Adobe forecasts discounts of up to 30% off list price during Cyber Week on retail sites, but that reflects large online retail. A local service business can offer value through a bonus card, bundle or capped pre-sale without cutting its base price.

### Is Small Business Saturday worth planning for?

NRF's holiday surveys cover Small Business Saturday alongside Black Friday and Cyber Monday. In 2026 it falls on November 28, between the two. A short local message, photos of the team and a review request cost little to prepare.

### When should the pre-sale go out?

NRF says 43% of holiday shoppers plan to start before November, so waiting until Black Friday misses early planners. The checklist above sends the pre-sale to the opted-in list on November 20 and opens the public offer on November 27.

### Do the NRF and Adobe numbers predict local traffic?

No. NRF and Adobe figures are national. The 2025 results, including the record 202.9 million shoppers, describe last year across the country. They do not forecast bookings or foot traffic in any single neighborhood.
