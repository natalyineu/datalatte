An independent car wash gets more traffic by being easy to find within a few miles: a complete Google Business Profile, accurate hours, photos of the site, and a small paid search budget aimed at drivers nearby. The International Carwash Association says about 200 companies run ten or more stores, with over 6,000 locations between them, while about 3,000 companies run one or two. You compete on location and convenience, not on advertising scale.

## How Big Is the Competition?

The association's industry page cites a 2020 Census Bureau estimate of 17,584 car washes with paid employees. A separate 2020 study it commissioned estimated about 17,500 conveyor washes, 29,000 in-bay automatic washes and 16,250 self-service washes. Those counts are several years old, and the page says new store construction is estimated to exceed 500 units a year, without a date.

It also says the share of US drivers who most often use a professional wash rose from about 48% in 1994 to nearly 80%, without dating the second figure. The takeaway is simple: most drivers already pay for a wash, so your job is to be the one they choose and return to.

## What Should a Car Wash Fix on Google First?

Google says local ranking depends on relevance, distance and prominence, and that reviews and ratings help prominence. Chains have more locations, but distance still favours the wash that is closest to the driver.

Check these on your profile: the right primary category, accurate opening hours and holiday hours, a phone number, photos of the entrance and bays, and your wash types and prices where the profile allows them. Reply to reviews with specific, brief answers. Google asks businesses not to offer free or discounted services for reviews. ## How Do You Keep Ads Local?

Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km. For a single site, set the radius around the wash and widen it only if the Search terms report shows demand from farther away. Broad "Presence or interest" targeting can reach people who are only interested in an area, so Google suggests "Presence" when you only want people physically in the chosen locations.

Add negative keywords for searches that cannot become customers, such as jobs, equipment for sale or "how to wash a car at home". Review the Search terms report weekly.

## Does a Membership or Repeat Offer Help?

It can, because one extra visit is worth little but a recurring customer is worth much more. No sourced figure on membership uptake for independents was found, so test a simple offer and track sign-ups. Collect an email address or phone number with consent at the counter, then use [SMS and email](/blog/sms-marketing-for-retail) to remind members of the offer. ## What Does a $600 Test Look Like?

Inputs are **assumptions** using the automotive repair benchmarks as a stand-in. A "conversion" is a call or direction request.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $600 |
| CPC | $4.35 |
| Clicks | about 138 |
| Conversion rate | 15.51% |
| Conversions | about 21 |
| Share who visit (assumption) | 30% |
| New customers | about 6.4 |
| Single wash price (assumption) | $14 |
| Revenue from first washes | about $90 |

At a single-wash price, ads cost far more than the first visit brings in. The test only pays if some of those customers join a membership. If 6 of them pay $25 a month for 6 months, that is $900. If none join, stop and rework the offer, or spend the money on the free profile and on reviews.

## FAQ

### How much should a car wash spend on marketing?

No car wash figure is published. Set a test budget for 30 days and judge by new customers, not clicks.

### Is Google Ads or Facebook better for a car wash?

Google reaches people searching now. Meta can remind nearby residents at a lower click cost, but benchmark figures are for all industries, not car washes.

### How do I compete with express chains?

Win on proximity, hours, clear prices and reviews. About 3,000 US companies run only one or two stores, according to the ICA, so independents are common.

### Should I offer discounts for reviews?

No. Google prohibits incentives for reviews.

### What is the first thing to fix?

Your Google Business Profile: category, hours, photos and review replies.
