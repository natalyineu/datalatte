## Which Estonian Market Data Can You Rely On?

The sources reviewed for this guide were a Facebook login page, a Sortlist directory of performance marketing agencies in Tallinn, and pages from the US Small Business Administration and Bureau of Labor Statistics. None of them contained Estonian internet use, platform reach or tourism counts. Earlier market figures on these topics could not be traced to a source in this set, so they have been removed.

Check current figures yourself before you spend. Ads Manager and Google Ads report reach and cost for your own audience, which is more useful for a single town than any national average. Those reports will also show whether your audience is in Tallinn, another city or a rural area, which changes the test you should run.

## Which Channels Should a Small Estonian Business Test First?

Start with the channel where local people already search for your service. For most local trades, that means Google search and a Google Business Profile. Paid social suits businesses that sell something visual or impulsive, such as a gym intro offer, a cafe or a salon.

Directory listings show how agencies price their work. The Sortlist page lists one Tallinn agency with a starting fee of €600 for performance marketing, which is a useful reminder that outsourced management can cost more than a small test budget. Treat that as one listing, not a market average.

## How Should You Split a Small Monthly Budget?

The table below is an example for a service business with an assumed EUR 400 monthly budget. The percentages are a planning choice, not a benchmark.

| Line | Share | Amount (assumption) | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google search ads | 50% | EUR 200 | Capture people already looking for your service |
| Facebook and Instagram ads | 30% | EUR 120 | Test offers with a local radius |
| Test reserve | 20% | EUR 80 | Try one new offer or audience each month |
| Google Business Profile | 0% | Your time | Keep hours, photos and services current |

Move money toward the line that produces joined customers. If a line produces only clicks or enquiries that never convert, cut it after two full test cycles rather than after one weak week. A single week rarely shows whether an offer works.

## How Do You Improve Google Maps Visibility in Estonia?

Complete every field in your Google Business Profile: primary and secondary categories, opening hours, service areas, photos and a clear description of what you sell. Keep those details current when hours or services change, since out-of-date listings are the easiest thing for a customer to notice.

Ask recent customers to leave honest reviews, and reply to them in the language they wrote in. The sources checked for this guide do not explain how Google weighs each local ranking factor, so avoid relying on any specific ranking claim until you have read Google's current help article. ## Worked Example: A Gym Intro-Offer Test

All inputs below are assumptions, in euros, for a small gym in a residential area of Tallinn.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget, 30 days | EUR 300 |
| Trial sign-ups | 20 |
| Share that join | 25% |
| New members | 5 |
| Value of a member over 6 months | EUR 180 |

Five new members at EUR 180 each give EUR 900 of revenue against EUR 300 of spend, so cost per member is EUR 60. If only 2 join, cost per member rises to EUR 150. That is still below the EUR 180 value, but it leaves little room for running costs. Judge the test on joined members, not on sign-ups, because a cheap sign-up that never pays is not a result.

## How Do You Track Leads Without a Large Team?

Tag every enquiry with its source, whether a Google listing, a Facebook ad or a referral, so you can compare channels at the end of each month. A shared spreadsheet is enough at this scale. Record the date, source, offer and outcome in one row per enquiry.

## FAQ

### Is Facebook advertising worth it for a small business in Estonia?
It can be, for offers that suit visual or local audiences. Test a small budget for 30 days and measure joined customers. No source checked gives Estonian Facebook reach or prices, so use your own platform data.

### Do I need Google Ads or a Google Business Profile first?
Start by completing your Google Business Profile, then test search ads if you have a clear offer. Track cost per joined customer for each before increasing spend.

### How much should a small business spend on a local test?
It depends on your margins. The worked example uses an assumed EUR 300 over 30 days. Set your own limit by working out what one new customer is worth to you.

### Should I market in English or Estonian?
Use the language your customers search in. The sources checked do not cover language use in Estonia, so test both in small campaigns and compare the enquiries you receive.

### Is Instagram useful for a service business?
It depends on whether your work photographs well and whether local followers book services. Run one offer for two cycles before deciding.
