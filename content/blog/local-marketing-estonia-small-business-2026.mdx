## How Big Is the Estonian Online Market?

DataReportal counts 1.34 million people, 1.24 million internet users and 1.91 million mobile connections, 143% of the population. Social media user identities total 1.01 million, 75.4%. A small, highly connected market means precise local targeting matters more than reach.

## Which Platforms Reach Estonians?

| Platform | Ad reach | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 703 thousand | 52.5% of population |
| Instagram | 499 thousand | 37.3% of population |
| LinkedIn (members) | 490 thousand | 36.6% of population |
| TikTok (aged 18+) | 412 thousand | 37.9% of adults |

Facebook still reaches the most people, and LinkedIn is large for a country this size, which helps business services. DataReportal counts LinkedIn registered members, not active users, and Meta states that ad audience is not active users.

## How Much Do Foreign Tourists Matter?

Statistics Estonia, in a release of 10 February 2026, reports about 3.7 million tourists accommodated in 2025, up 2%. Of these, nearly 1.95 million were foreign, up 5%, and 1.74 million were domestic, down 1%. Tourists spent 6.7 million nights, with foreign tourists accounting for 3.8 million.

Foreign tourists grew while domestic ones fell, so a cafe or tour in a tourist area may gain more from English-language search than from local ads. A year-round business in a residential area should still focus on locals.

## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Estonia?

Google says local ranking uses relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Reviews and ratings can help prominence. Complete the categories, hours and photos. ## Worked Example: A Gym Intro-Offer Test

All numbers are **assumptions**, in euros.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget, 30 days | EUR 300 |
| Trial sign-ups | 20 |
| Share that join | 25% |
| New members | 5 |
| Value of a member over 6 months | EUR 180 |

Five members at EUR 180 give EUR 900 of revenue against EUR 300 spend, so cost per member is EUR 60. If only 2 join, cost per member is EUR 150, still below EUR 180 revenue but before running costs. Judge the test on joined members, not on sign-ups.
