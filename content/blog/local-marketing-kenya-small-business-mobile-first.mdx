A small business in Kenya should start with three free or cheap channels: a verified Google Business Profile so people find you on Google Maps, a WhatsApp Business chat as the place customers ask and book, and a small Facebook or Instagram ad that opens that chat. Facebook reaches far more Kenyans than Instagram does, so most shops start there. No published price for Kenyan ads was found, so test with a small budget first.

This guide is for shops, salons, cafés, studios and service businesses in Nairobi, Mombasa and other towns. Every number comes from a source listed at the end. Where a figure is not published for Kenya, the text says so.

## How Many Kenyans Are Online, and Where?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report for Kenya (published 8 November 2025) counts 23.4 million internet users, which is 40.5% of the population of 57.8 million. It also counts 77.5 million cellular mobile connections, or 134% of the population, because many people hold more than one SIM.

Meta's ad tools show 17.0 million people reachable on Facebook (29.4%) and 3.95 million on Instagram (6.8%). TikTok shows 18.4 million users aged 18 and over, 56.1% of adults. YouTube shows 12.1 million. DataReportal stresses these are advertising reach estimates, not monthly active users.

The practical reading is simple. Phones are everywhere, but about six in ten Kenyans were not counted as internet users. A purely online plan misses them, so keep a shop sign, a phone number and word of mouth alongside it.

## Which Channel Should a Kenyan Small Business Use First?

| Channel | Reach in Kenya (DataReportal, late 2025) | Best use for a local shop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 17.0M ad reach | Offers, events, local ads that open WhatsApp |
| TikTok (18+) | 18.4M ad reach | Short videos of products, food, before and after |
| YouTube | 12.1M ad reach | Longer how-to or tour videos |
| Instagram | 3.95M ad reach | Visual trades such as salons and food |
| Google Maps and Search | Not published in this report | People searching for a nearby service |

Pick one lane to begin. A café or salon often does well on Instagram and TikTok because customers like pictures. A repair shop or clinic does better on Google Maps because people search with a problem in mind. The [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers the free listing in detail.

## How Do You Get Found on Google Maps?

Create or claim a Business Profile. Google states you can add or claim your business at no charge, but only businesses eligible for a Business Profile can add or claim a location. Google asks you to make sure the address meets its guidelines and to choose a verification option and follow the steps.

Fill in the category, opening hours, phone number, photos and a list of services. Ask happy customers for reviews and reply to each one.
This is the cheapest channel because it has no ad cost. It also works for people already close to you and ready to buy, such as someone searching for a salon or a mechanic nearby.

## How Do WhatsApp and Facebook Ads Work Together?

Meta offers ads that click to WhatsApp. WhatsApp's business site describes them as Facebook or Instagram ads that open a WhatsApp chat with your business. In Ads Manager you click Create, choose an objective, then under Conversions select Messaging Apps and WhatsApp. You set dates and a budget, define the audience, pick a format, write the ad text and customise the automated replies.

WhatsApp recommends running the ad for at least seven days. It also says an audience of about 2 to 10 million people performs best. A single town in Kenya may be smaller than that, so check the audience estimate shown in Ads Manager and widen the area if it is very small.

Set up the chat before the ad. Write a greeting, opening hours, a price list and a reply for the three questions customers ask most. An ad that sends people to an empty chat wastes money. The [guide to Instagram ads versus Facebook ads](/blog/instagram-ads-vs-facebook-ads) compares the two placements.

## What Do Ads Cost?

No Kenyan price was found in a source that could be opened. The only benchmark checked is for the United States. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 report, built on 1,892 US campaigns between April 2025 and June 2026, gives a median Facebook cost per click of $0.60 for traffic campaigns and $1.80 for lead campaigns, and a median cost per lead of $27.39.

Do not use those as a Kenyan forecast. Costs depend on country, season and audience. Treat your first two weeks as the test, and compare your own cost per chat started with what a customer is worth.

## What Would a Two-Week Test Look Like?

All inputs below are **assumptions** for planning, not Kenyan benchmarks. A shop spends 14 days on a click-to-WhatsApp ad and measures chats and sales.

| Input (assumption) | Cautious case | US-benchmark case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily budget | $4 | $4 |
| Days | 14 | 14 |
| Total spend | $56 | $56 |
| Cost per click | $0.30 | $0.60 |
| Clicks | about 187 | about 93 |
| Share who start a chat | 20% | 20% |
| Chats | about 37 | about 19 |
| Share of chats that buy | 25% | 25% |
| Customers | about 9 | about 5 |
| Cost per customer | about $6 | about $12 |

The result shows why order value matters. If an average sale leaves a profit of $15, the cautious case pays and the benchmark case roughly pays. If the profit per sale is $3, neither does. Replace every row with your own numbers after the first week.

## Where Do You Start This Week?

Work in this order, because each step supports the next:

1. Claim your Google Business Profile and add hours, photos and your phone number.
2. Set up WhatsApp Business with a greeting and price list.
3. Post two or three real photos of your work on Facebook.
4. Run one small click-to-WhatsApp ad for seven days or more.
5. Write down every sale that came from a chat.

## FAQ

### How many Kenyans use Facebook?
Meta's ad tools showed 17.0 million Facebook users reachable in Kenya in late 2025, or 29.4% of the population, per DataReportal. This is an advertising reach estimate, not a count of monthly active users.

### Is Instagram worth it in Kenya?
Reach is much smaller than Facebook's at 3.95 million, or 6.8% of the population. It suits visual businesses such as salons and food. Test it with the same posts you use on Facebook before paying for separate content.

### Does Google Business Profile cost money?
Google says you can add or claim your business at no charge. Only businesses eligible for a Business Profile can add or claim a location.

### How much should a Kenyan small business spend on ads?
No Kenyan benchmark was found. Start with a budget you can afford to lose for seven to fourteen days and judge it by cost per customer, not clicks.

### Can I run ads that open WhatsApp?
Yes. WhatsApp describes ads on Facebook or Instagram that open a chat with your business, set up in Ads Manager under Messaging Apps.
