The best Google Ads keywords for an electrician are urgent, service-specific phrases tied to a place: "emergency electrician" plus your city, "panel upgrade" plus your town, "EV charger installation" plus your area. Start with phrase and exact match, add negative keywords before launch, and judge each keyword by the calls and jobs it produces rather than by clicks. Electricians can also use Local Services Ads, which are billed per lead rather than per click. Adwave puts typical electrician Google Ads and LSA spend at $1,000 to $3,000 a month.

## Which Keywords Should an Electrician Bid On?

Group keywords by what the searcher wants, and give each group its own ad group and landing page text. A searcher with a burning smell at an outlet needs a different message from a homeowner planning a new EV charger.

| Intent group | Example keywords | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent repair | emergency electrician, power outage in one room, burning smell outlet | Highest urgency, fastest decision |
| Named service | panel upgrade, outlet replacement, ceiling fan installation, rewiring | The searcher already knows the job |
| Installation projects | EV charger installation, generator hookup, hot tub wiring | Bigger tickets, less price shopping |
| Inspection and compliance | electrical safety inspection, home inspection electrician | Planned jobs, often from landlords or buyers |
| Trust and comparison | licensed electrician, best electrician plus city | The searcher is choosing between firms |

These groups are starting points to test, not a ranked list. Trades with similar mixes of urgent and planned work, such as [contractors](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-contractors-in-2026), often find that the urgent group converts fastest while the installation group carries the larger tickets. Check the volume and cost estimates in Keyword Planner for your own area before committing a budget.

## Should an Electrician Use Local Services Ads or Search Keywords?

Local Services Ads and standard Search campaigns are different products, and the difference is in how you pay. According to [GoMarketing's guide to Google Local Services Ads](https://gomarketing.com/blog/the-ultimate-electricians-guide-to-google-local-services-ads/), electricians do not pay for every click their LSA gets but for every lead it generates. That makes lead cost easier to compare directly with the invoice for a job.

Search keywords give you direct control over the exact phrases you bid on, the negative list and the ad copy. LSAs run under their own profile and lead rules, so check the current Google help page for eligibility before you set one up. Many electricians run both, using LSAs for the urgent calls and Search for named services and installations.

## Which Match Type Should an Electrician Start With?

Start with phrase and exact match. Phrase match covers searches that include the meaning of your keyword, and exact match gives you the most control over which searches trigger your ads. Both are easier to manage in a new account with a small budget.

Broad match can reach a wider set of related searches, which is useful later but harder to control early on. Add it only after conversion tracking is working and your negative keyword list is long enough to catch the irrelevant searches that broad match brings in. Check Google's current Ads Help pages on match types before you change settings, because the behavior of each type is updated from time to time.

## Which Negative Keywords Should You Add First?

Negative keywords stop your ads from showing on searches you do not want to pay for. Add singular and plural forms yourself, along with common misspellings and synonyms, because a single entry may not catch every variant. Start with these groups and adjust from the Search terms report:

- Do-it-yourself intent: "diy", "how to", "tutorial", "video"
- Jobs and training: "jobs", "apprenticeship", "salary", "course", "license exam"
- Products and parts: "wholesale", "supplier", "buy online", "cheap wire"
- Services you do not offer, such as car or appliance repair

Negatives matter more for electricians than for many trades because "electrician" searches often come from people who want to learn the work or buy parts, not hire a firm.

## How Often Should You Review Keywords and Budget?

Check the Search terms report weekly for the first month, then move to a monthly review once the account settles. Every irrelevant search term you find becomes a negative keyword. Pause any keyword that has spent money without producing calls after a fair test, and move the winners to exact match so you can see their results clearly.

If your business is mainly emergency work, set ad schedules to match the hours you can answer the phone. A missed call on a paid click is money spent with nothing to show for it. Service businesses with booked appointments, such as [cleaning services](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-cleaning-services-in-2026), face the same scheduling problem and can use the same approach.

## FAQ

### What are the best Google Ads keywords for electricians?

Urgent and service-specific phrases with a place name work best, such as emergency electrician, panel upgrade and EV charger installation. Group them by intent and test each group separately so you can see which one produces calls.

### How much should an electrician spend on Google Ads?

Adwave states that most electricians need $1,000 to $3,000 per month in ad spend to generate consistent lead flow. Its starting tier runs from $500 to $1,500 a month, and the figure you need depends on how much of your work comes from paid search.

### Are Local Services Ads different from Google Ads keywords?

Yes. GoMarketing notes that electricians pay for each lead an LSA generates rather than for each click. Search keywords give you direct control over phrases and negatives, while LSAs follow their own rules, so many electricians compare the two in their own area.

### Should an electrician use broad match at the start?

Usually not. Start with phrase and exact match, and add broad match only once conversion tracking works and your negative list is long enough to handle the extra searches it brings in.
