The best Google Ads keywords for an electrician are urgent and service-specific phrases that include a place: "emergency electrician" plus your city, "panel upgrade" plus your city, "EV charger installation" plus your town. Start with phrase match, add negative keywords on day one, and judge keywords by cost per call, not cost per click.

WordStream by LocaliQ does not publish an electrician benchmark. Its closest category, Home and Home Improvement, averages $8.33 per click in 2026. Google does not publish search volumes for individual keywords outside your own account, so this guide groups keywords by intent and gives no volume numbers.

## Which Keywords Should an Electrician Bid On?

Group keywords by what the searcher wants. Each group needs its own ad group and its own landing page text.

| Intent group | Example keywords | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent repair | emergency electrician, power outage in one room, burning smell outlet | Highest urgency, fastest decision |
| Named service | panel upgrade, outlet replacement, ceiling fan installation, rewiring | Searcher knows the job |
| Installation projects | EV charger installation, generator hookup, hot tub wiring | Bigger tickets, less price shopping |
| Inspection and compliance | electrical safety inspection, home inspection electrician | Planned jobs, often landlords or buyers |
| Trust and comparison | licensed electrician, best electrician plus city | Searcher is choosing between firms |

These are examples to test, not a ranked list with volumes. Open Keyword Planner in your account to see volume and cost estimates for your area. Google says the tool gives estimates of monthly searches and the average cost for an ad to show, and that very low-volume keywords may not be forecastable.

## Do "Near Me" Keywords Work for Electricians?

Often yes, but you do not need to bid on the phrase "near me" to appear for it. Location targeting decides who sees the ad, and your keyword can be the service itself. Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km, and that broad geo targeting ("Presence or interest") is its recommended best practice on Search.

For a service van that covers set suburbs, the risk is clicks from outside your area. Test "Presence" targeting if the Search terms report shows places you do not serve. Our [Google Ads guide for contractors](/blog/google-ads-for-contractors) covers radius and schedule settings for trades.

## Which Match Type Should You Use?

Google defines three types. Broad match can show on related searches that do not share your keyword's exact meaning. Phrase match covers searches that include the meaning of your keyword, including more specific versions. Exact match covers searches with the same meaning or intent and gives the most control.

For a new electrician account, start with phrase and exact match. Google states that it is critical to use Smart Bidding with broad match, so only add broad match later, once conversions are tracked and the negative list is long.

## Which Negative Keywords Should You Add First?

Negatives stop ads showing on searches you do not want. Google's help is explicit that negative keywords do not match close variants, so add singular, plural and synonym forms yourself. A broad negative blocks a search only when it contains all the negative terms, in any order.

Start with these groups and adjust from the Search terms report:

- Do-it-yourself intent: "diy", "how to", "tutorial", "video"
- Jobs and training: "jobs", "apprenticeship", "salary", "course", "license exam"
- Products and parts: "wholesale", "supplier", "buy online", "cheap wire"
- Services you do not offer, such as car or appliance repair

Review the report weekly for the first month. Every irrelevant search term you find becomes a negative.

## How Often Should You Review Keywords?

Check the Search terms report weekly at first and monthly after the account settles. Pause keywords with spend and no calls after a fair test, and move winning terms to exact match. ## FAQ

### What are the best Google Ads keywords for electricians?

Urgent and service-specific phrases with a place name, such as emergency electrician, panel upgrade and EV charger installation. Group them by intent and test each group separately.

### How much does an electrician keyword cost on Google Ads?

No public benchmark exists for electricians. LocaliQ's 2026 Home and Home Improvement average is $8.33 per click and $90.92 per lead. Your own account will show the real figure.

### Should an electrician use broad match?

Not at the start. Google says Smart Bidding is critical with broad match. Begin with phrase and exact match, and add broad match only once conversion tracking and negatives are in place.

### Do I need negative keywords?

Yes. Negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals and synonyms yourself. Block DIY, jobs and parts searches first.
