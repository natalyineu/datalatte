For most small businesses, the cheapest way to start with AI agents is a no-code platform with a free tier: Zapier Agents is free to try, n8n can be self-hosted free of licence fees, and Lindy offers a free trial before $29.99 a month. Pick by who will maintain it, not by feature lists. This page compares those three on published prices checked on 1 November 2026. Make is covered too, but its pricing page blocked automated access, so no Make prices are quoted here.

An AI agent here means software that reads a message or trigger, decides on a step, and acts in your other tools, such as answering an enquiry or booking a slot. For the basics, see [what AI agents are and how they work for local business](/blog/ai-agents-for-local-business-what-they-are-and-how-they-work).

## Which AI Agent Platform Is Best for a Small Business?

There is no single winner, because the three tools suit different owners. Zapier suits non-technical owners who want pre-built app connections. n8n suits people willing to set up a workflow builder, or pay someone to do it, in return for lower cost per run. Lindy is built around agents that you describe in plain language and pay for in credits.

| | Zapier | n8n | Lindy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entry price | Free (100 tasks, 400 agent activities per month) | Free self-hosted Community Edition; cloud from 20 EUR a month on annual billing | Free trial credits; Team from $29.99 a month |
| Paid tier detail | Pro from $19.99 a month for 750 tasks (annual billing) | Pro 50 EUR a month for 10K executions (annual) | 3,000 credits for $29.99; top-ups $10 per 1,000 credits |
| Unit you pay for | Tasks, plus agent activities | Workflow executions | Credits |
| Best for | No-code owners | Technical help available | Plain-language agents |

All prices were read from each vendor's pricing page on 1 November 2026 and may change.

## What Does Zapier Include?

Zapier's free plan gives 100 tasks a month. Paid Pro plans start at $19.99 a month on annual billing, or $29.99 monthly, for 750 tasks. Zapier Agents is listed as free for all plans, with 400 activities a month on Free and 1,500 on Pro, and agent activities do not use up task allowances. AI by Zapier is included in paid plans but not Free.

Zapier also lists chatbots: 2 on the Free plan, with paid tiers of about 5 and about 20 chatbots at roughly $13.33 and $66.67 a month on annual billing. Check the pricing page for the current rate per AI model tier, as Zapier does not publish it in the plan table.

## What Does n8n Include?

n8n's cloud plans are priced on workflow executions. The pricing page lists Starter at 20 EUR a month (2.5K executions), Pro at 50 EUR (10K) and Business at 667 EUR (40K, self-hosted), all on annual billing, which it labels "Save 17%". Enterprise is by quote. A free, self-hosted Community Edition is available on GitHub.

Self-hosting removes the licence fee but not the work: you run the server, apply updates and handle backups. Our [n8n AI agent workflow guide](/blog/n8n-ai-agent-workflow-local-business) shows what a first build involves.

## What Does Lindy Include?

Lindy sells credits. The Free plan gives $50 in one-time credits that last 7 days. The Team plan is $29.99 a month for 3,000 credits, and higher tiers run from 4,000 credits at $39.99 up to 1,750,000 credits at $9,999.50. Extra credits cost $10 per 1,000. Each active user in a workspace is billed.

Lindy does not state on the pricing page how many credits a given task uses, so measure the credits used on a real week of work before committing to a tier.

## What About Make?

Make's pricing page lists Free, Core, Pro, Teams and Enterprise plans, and 1,000 credits a month on Free, where most actions use 1 credit. Its page returned a security check when reopened for monthly prices, so no paid prices are quoted here. Read them on Make's site, and see our [Make.com AI agent workflow guide](/blog/make-com-ai-agent-workflow-local-business).

## The Math: What Might an Agent Cost a Salon?

All inputs are **assumptions** for a hair salon that wants an agent to answer booking enquiries and send reminders. They are not measured results.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Enquiries handled per month | 200 |
| Steps (actions) per enquiry | 4 |
| Total actions per month | 800 |
| Zapier Pro, 750 tasks, annual billing | $19.99 |
| Zapier Pro, 1,500 tasks, annual billing | $39.00 |
| Staff time saved (assumption) | 3 minutes per enquiry = 10 hours |
| Value of staff time (assumption) | $15 per hour = $150 |

If every step counted as a task, 800 actions would exceed the 750-task tier, so the $39.00 tier would apply. Against $150 of staff time, that is a $111 gap in this example. The result flips if enquiries need more steps or the agent makes mistakes you must fix. Run the platform on a free tier for one month and count actual usage first.

## How Do You Keep an Agent Safe?

Give the agent narrow permissions, and keep a person in the loop for refunds, medical or legal questions and anything that commits money. Log every action it takes. Our guide on AI agents versus chatbots explains where simple rules are enough, and building an agent without code covers the disclosure rules that apply.

## FAQ

### What is the cheapest AI agent platform for a small business?
Self-hosted n8n has no licence fee, and Zapier Agents is free to try. Self-hosting costs time, and free tiers have limits, so compare after a trial month.

### Do I need to code to use an AI agent?
Not for Zapier or Lindy, which are built for no-code setup. n8n is a visual builder but is more technical.

### How is pricing measured?
By tasks and agent activities on Zapier, workflow executions on n8n, and credits on Lindy. The units are not interchangeable, so convert using your own usage.

### Are these prices final?
They are the vendors' published prices on 1 November 2026. Vendors change plans often.
