For a local business, the best TikTok mix is organic first, paid second. Post for a few weeks to learn which videos locals respond to, then put a small paid test behind your best post. TikTok's own help page sets the entry price: an ad group's daily budget must exceed $20, so a 14-day test costs at least $294 at $21 a day. Paid is for reach in your area; organic is what makes the ad worth watching.

## Should a local business start with organic or paid TikTok?

Start organic. Organic posts cost only time, and they show you what to promote. A paid campaign needs a video that already works, and you cannot know that before you post.

Reach also differs by audience. Pew Research Center's 2025 survey found 37% of US adults use TikTok, and roughly half of adults aged 18 to 29 use it daily versus 5% of those 65 and older ([Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/11/20/americans-social-media-use-2025)). If your customers skew older, expect fewer of them on the app, and weigh paid spend accordingly.

Our guide to a [zero-to-10K organic TikTok plan for local businesses](/blog/tiktok-local-business-organic-strategy) covers the posting side in detail.

## What does paid TikTok cost at the minimum?

TikTok publishes hard floors in its [budget help article](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget). The wording matters: budgets must exceed the floor, not just meet it.

| Budget level | Daily floor | Lifetime floor |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign | Must exceed $50 | Must exceed $50 |
| Ad group | Must exceed $20 | Days x $20 (a 31-day ad group needs $620) |

The Promote button inside the app is a lighter route. TikTok describes it as a way to boost a post with a goal, budget, duration and audience, but minimums are not stated in the material we reviewed, so check the live screen before you commit ([TikTok Promote overview](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/promote-101)).

## How do you turn an organic post into an ad?

Use Spark Ads. TikTok describes them as a native format that promotes an organic post, with views, likes and follows credited to the original post ([Spark Ads help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/spark-ads)). That means paid reach also builds the social proof under your own video.

Pick the post to promote by looking at your own account data, not a hunch. Good candidates show saves, shares or comments that mention your area, and a clear next step such as "come in Saturday". Our [Spark Ads guide for local businesses](/blog/tiktok-spark-ads-local-business-guide) walks through the setup.

## What does a worked example look like?

This is an assumption-based example, not a result. Say a café has posted 12 videos in four weeks and one drew far more comments than the rest. The owner promotes it for 14 days at $21 a day, targeted to the city or nearby zip codes.

- Total spend: 14 x $21 = $294
- Assumed value of a new regular customer: $30 gross profit (replace with your own)
- Break-even: about 10 new customers who came in because of the ad

Tell staff to ask new customers how they found you and tally the answers. If you reach the break-even number, raise the budget in small steps. If not, change the video before you change the budget. Targeting choices are covered in our guide on [how to target local customers on TikTok](/blog/how-to-target-local-customers-on-tiktok).

## When is organic enough, and when should you pay?

Use this checklist to decide.

- **Stay organic** if you post fewer than a handful of videos so far, if you cannot spare $294, or if no video has beaten the others.
- **Add paid** if one post clearly outperforms, your offer is ready (a booking link or a clear in-store hook), and you can count the results.
- **Pause paid** if the test ends without reaching break-even and the video was not the issue, for example the offer was weak.

Keep organic running during paid tests. Spark Ads need a steady supply of posts to choose from, and you want a profile worth visiting when a new viewer taps through.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is organic TikTok reach dead for small businesses?**
We found no primary source that shows this either way, so treat claims like that with caution. What you can measure is your own account: compare views on your last ten posts and see whether they trend up.

**How much should I spend on TikTok ads per month?**
The floor for one ad group is above $20 a day. At $21 a day, a 30-day month is $630. Start with a 14-day test at $294 before committing a full month.

**Do I need a TikTok business account to run Spark Ads?**
You need an Ads Manager account to run them. Spark Ads can use your own posts or, with the creator's authorization, other creators' posts ([TikTok help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/spark-ads)).

**Can I run ads only in my city?**
Yes. TikTok lists location types including city, DMA region and zip or postal code. It does not support excluding locations, and small areas can cut impressions and raise cost per result ([TikTok location targeting help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/location-targeting)).
