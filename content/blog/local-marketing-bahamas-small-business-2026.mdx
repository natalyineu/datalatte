A Bahamian small business has two audiences: residents who are almost all online, and a very large flow of visitors. DataReportal counts 381 thousand internet users in January 2025 (94.8% of the population), and the Ministry of Tourism reported 11.22 million visitors in 2024. Reach locals through Facebook, and reach visitors through Google, reviews and travel platforms before they land.

This guide is for salons, restaurants, tour operators and shops in Nassau, Freeport and the Family Islands. Click-cost figures come from US benchmarks because no Bahamas-specific price data was found, and the text flags where that applies.

## How Many Bahamians Are Online?

DataReportal's Digital 2025 report lists 381 thousand internet users, 250 thousand social media user identities (62.0% of the population) and 351 thousand mobile connections (87.3%). Facebook ad reach is 225 thousand, equal to 55.9% of the total population.

Two cautions apply. Identities may not be unique people, and Meta's reach figure is not the same as active users. Even so, more than half the population is reachable on Facebook, which makes it the first place to look for local customers. Verified per-app numbers for Instagram, WhatsApp and TikTok in the Bahamas were not found.

## How Big Is the Visitor Market?

The Bahamas Ministry of Tourism reported 11.22 million international visitors in 2024, up from 9.65 million in 2023, as reported by Travel Market Report. Foreign air arrivals exceeded 1.7 million, about flat on 2023. December 2024 was the best month on record with 1.15 million visitors.

The gap between 1.7 million air arrivals and 11.22 million total visitors suggests most arrive by sea, which is an inference, since the article gives no sea breakdown. The Florida-Caribbean Cruise Association put cruise tourism expenditure at $654.8 million in the 2023/2024 cruise year. A business that serves cruise passengers has a few hours to win each customer, so it needs to be findable on a phone map.

## How Should You Reach Locals?

Residents find businesses through Facebook Pages and groups, friends and Google Maps. With 55.9% of the population in Meta's ad reach, a simple Page with hours, prices, location and a phone number covers the basics.

- Post real photos of work, products and menus weekly.
- Reply to messages the same day; many customers prefer to ask before they visit.
- Pin one post with prices and booking instructions.
- Use a [Meta ad](/blog/instagram-ads-for-local-business-complete-2026-guide) with a small radius around Nassau or your island when you have a clear offer.

Bahamian English phrases in posts can add warmth, but test them on your own audience before building a campaign around them.

## How Should You Reach Visitors?

Visitors search before they arrive and again when they dock or land. Cover three places: your Google Business Profile, review sites, and your own simple page with a booking route.

Google says local ranking depends on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help. Use our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) and ask happy customers for reviews. See also our [review platform comparison](/blog/review-platform-comparison-yelp-google-facebook-tripadvisor), and our [TripAdvisor ads guide](/blog/tripadvisor-ads-restaurants-hotels-2026) if you run a restaurant or tour.

## What Might Google Ads Cost? A Worked Example

No Bahamas CPC data was verified. The example uses WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 US benchmarks as a rough guide: Travel at $2.14 per click and a 5.83% conversion rate, Beauty and Personal Care at $4.62 and 10.35%, Restaurants and Food at $2.05 and 8.05%. Everything else below is an **assumption**.

| Input | Tour operator |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget (assumption) | $800 |

| Clicks | about 374 |
| Conversion rate, US Travel benchmark | 5.83% |
| Leads (inquiries) | about 22 |

| Bookings | about 5.5 |
| Revenue per booking, party of 2 at $120 each (assumption) | $240 |
| Revenue | about $1,308 |

Revenue per ad dollar is not profit. After boat fuel, crew and fees, this example may lose money, which is why it is worth testing with a small budget and tracking bookings by source. A real Bahamas account may behave very differently.

## When Is the Busy Season, and What About Storms?

The Ministry data shows December as the peak month in 2024. Junkanoo parades run in Nassau on December 26 and January 1 around Bay and Shirley Streets, starting about 2 a.m. and finishing about 8 a.m., according to World Nomads. Start times differ between sources, so confirm with official listings before promoting an event.

The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30, with a peak on September 10, according to the National Hurricane Center. Update opening hours and closure notices in your Google profile and Facebook Page quickly during a storm, and keep a pinned post for customers.

## What Should You Do First?

- Complete your Google Business Profile and Facebook Page.
- Collect reviews on Google and TripAdvisor if you serve visitors.
- Track where each booking or order comes from; ask every customer.
- Start with a small ad test only after those basics are done.

## FAQ

### How many people in the Bahamas use Facebook?

Meta's tools show an ad reach of 225 thousand, 55.9% of the population, according to DataReportal for January 2025. It is a reach estimate, not a count of active users.

### How many tourists visit the Bahamas?

The Ministry of Tourism reported 11.22 million international visitors in 2024, up from 9.65 million in 2023, as reported by Travel Market Report. The source gives no sea versus air split beyond foreign air arrivals of over 1.7 million.

### How much do Google Ads cost in the Bahamas?

Not verified. Use US benchmarks as a ceiling guide and test: for example Travel at $2.14 per click, Restaurants and Food at $2.05, and Beauty at $4.62 in the 2026 LocaliQ data.

### Should I advertise to cruise passengers or stay-over guests?

No Bahamas data on their spend per person was verified. Test both with separate offers and track each, then compare the cost per booking.

### When should I plan for Junkanoo?

Parades run December 26 and January 1 in Nassau, per World Nomads. Build campaigns weeks ahead, and confirm times with official sources.
