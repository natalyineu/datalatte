## What Does an AI Receptionist Do for a Studio?

It answers the phone with a synthetic voice, follows a script you write and hands off what it cannot do. Goodcall's site lists the typical jobs: triage callers, qualify leads, book appointments on a calendar, update a CRM, and transfer, take a message or arrange a callback. Mindbody describes its AI Concierge as a front desk assistant that handles missed calls and drives bookings.

For a studio, the useful calls are simple. Class times, prices, intro offers, parking, cancellation policy and "do you have space tonight". The risky ones are billing disputes, injuries and membership freezes. Send those to a person.

## Will It Book Into Mindbody or Glofox?

This is the question that decides the purchase, and the answer varies. Mindbody's own AI Concierge runs inside its platform, which removes the integration problem if you are on Mindbody and a plan that includes it.

For outside vendors, the pages read name other tools. Goodcall names HighLevel, Google Calendar and Zapier. Rosie names Zapier and "direct calendar booking" without naming a calendar. Vapi has Google Calendar tools. None of these pages mentioned Mindbody or Glofox. Ask the vendor in writing whether a booking lands in your class schedule, or only in a separate calendar.

If it cannot book, a fallback still helps. The agent can text a booking link and send you a message.

## What Is the Break-Even for a Studio?

The inputs here are **assumptions**, not studio benchmarks. Replace them from your call log.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Calls missed each month | 40 |
| Share from new prospects | 50% (20 calls) |
| Prospects the agent books into an intro class | 40% (8) |
| Intro attendees who join | 50% (4) |
| Membership | $150 a month |
| AI receptionist plan | $129 a month |

Four new members at $150 is $600 in monthly recurring revenue against a $129 plan. Break-even is one extra member (129 divided by 150 is 0.86). If the agent books nobody, you have lost $129, so ask for a trial period. Many vendors run one, and Retell and Vapi give free credits.

Count missed calls before you buy. Your phone provider's call log shows them for a month at no cost.

## How Do You Set It Up?

Start with a one-page script, then test.

- **List the ten questions** callers ask most and write the exact answers, including price and cancellation terms.
- **Set hand-off rules:** which calls go to your mobile, which become a message.
- **Connect the calendar or booking link** and place three test bookings.
- **Forward calls after hours or on busy lines**, not all day, for the first two weeks.
- **Read every transcript** for two weeks and fix wrong answers.

## What Are the Legal and Trust Risks?

Tell callers they are speaking to an AI. Goodcall says its callers are told so, and that practice avoids surprises. Recording rules differ by state and country, so confirm consent wording with a local adviser.

If you plan outbound reminder or sales calls, the rules are stricter. In February 2024 the FCC ruled that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices under the US Telephone Consumer Protection Act, so prior consent is generally required (Cooley, a law-firm summary; secondary). Keep the agent on inbound calls until your member consent is in order.

Health details belong to a person. Do not let the agent give injury or medical advice.

## FAQ

### How much does an AI receptionist cost for a gym?

Published AI plans run from $49 a month (Rosie, 250 minutes) to $299 a month (Rosie 2,000 minutes, Goodcall 2,000 unique callers). Building your own on Retell or Vapi costs about $0.08 to $0.13 a minute in usage.

### Can an AI receptionist book classes in Mindbody?

Mindbody's AI Concierge is part of its own platform on the Ultimate plan, and an add-on on Accelerate. Other vendors' pages did not name Mindbody or Glofox, so ask.

### Is it better than a human receptionist?

It is cheaper per call and always on. A human handles billing disputes and upset members better. Many studios use the agent after hours and humans in staffed hours.

### Do callers know they are speaking to an AI?

Goodcall says its callers are told. Disclose it in your greeting either way.

### How many missed calls are normal for a studio?

No source found publishes a studio benchmark. Count your own phone log.
