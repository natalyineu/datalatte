For emails and messages to clients, German law (UWG section 7) requires prior consent, with one narrow exception for existing customers. The IVH and ZZF counted 10 million dogs and 15.7 million cats in German homes in 2025, so demand is large. This guide covers what a groomer controls: profile, reviews, ads, messages and the legal basics.

The figures come from a household survey run by the research firm Skopos for the two industry associations (5,000 respondents, phone and online). The press release does not give numbers for grooming salons or grooming prices, so this guide does not either.

## How Big Is the Pet Market for a German Groomer?

According to IVH and ZZF, 33.4 million dogs, cats, small mammals and ornamental birds lived in German households in 2025, plus fish and terrarium animals. At least one pet lived in 43% of households. Cats were the most common pet with 15.7 million animals in 24% of households. Dogs followed with 10 million in about one household in five.

Total sector sales were just under 7 billion euros, almost all of it food and supplies. The associations report that accessories fell 4.6% in 2025 because owners postponed larger purchases. Grooming services are not broken out, so there is no published market size for them in this release. Use these numbers as a measure of the number of potential clients, not of grooming demand.

## Why Start With Your Google Business Profile?

Because a local search is where a dog owner without a regular groomer starts, and the profile is the part of it you control. Fill in the category, opening hours, service area, phone number, photos and a list of services with prices. Write it all in German. The detailed steps are in our [Google Business Profile guide for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-for-pet-groomers).

## How Do You Ask for Reviews the Right Way?

Ask in person at pickup, or send the review link, and ask everyone, not only happy clients. Google's Business Profile help says you can ask customers to visit a Google link or scan a QR code, that reviews must reflect a genuine experience and that offering incentives such as free or discounted services is strictly prohibited. So a discount for a five-star review is out.

Reply to each review in German, short and factual. For a critical review, thank the writer, state what you will change and offer to talk by phone. Keep the reply free of client health or personal details. Our [social media guide for groomers](/blog/pet-groomer-social-media-marketing) covers using posts and photos alongside reviews.

## What Does German Law Require Before You Message Clients?

For advertising by email, consent comes first. Section 7 of the UWG (law against unfair competition) lists advertising by electronic mail without the recipient's prior express consent as an unreasonable nuisance. There is one exception in paragraph 3: you got the address in connection with a sale of a service, you use it for similar services of your own, the client has not objected, and the client was clearly told, when the address was collected and at every use, that they can object at any time at base-rate cost.

In practice, a booking reminder for an appointment already made is a service message. A newsletter with offers needs a consent checkbox, or must fit the exception above. Whether a WhatsApp message counts as electronic mail under this rule is a legal question, so ask a lawyer or your trade association before you use it for promotions. This is general information, not legal advice.

## What Must Your Website Show?

An Impressum. Section 5 of the Digitale-Dienste-Gesetz (DDG) requires providers of commercial digital services to keep certain details easy to find and always available. These include your name and address, a way to contact you quickly including an email address, the register and register number if you are entered in a commercial register, and the supervising authority if your activity needs official approval.

The law text also covers legal form and representatives for companies. Put a link to the Impressum in the footer of every page. Whether a particular grooming qualification is legally required for a German salon was not confirmed from an official source. Check that with your local trade office or chamber (IHK or Handwerkskammer).

## Should a German Groomer Run Google Ads?

Only after the free basics are in place, and only with a tight radius. Google's help says radius targeting must be at least 1 km around a location and recommends the Presence or Interest setting for Search as a best practice. Presence limits ads to people physically in the area, which suits a salon that clients drive to. Our [Google Ads guide for pet groomers](/blog/google-ads-for-pet-groomers-guide) explains negative keywords, call tracking and budgets, and the [budget article](/blog/how-much-should-a-pet-groomer-spend-on-google-ads) shows how to size a test.

## FAQ

### How many dogs and cats are there in Germany?

IVH and ZZF counted 10 million dogs and 15.7 million cats in German households in 2025, based on a survey of 5,000 people by Skopos.

### Do I need consent to email my grooming clients?

For advertising, yes, in principle. UWG section 7 requires prior express consent, with an exception for your own similar services if the client was told they can object. Appointment reminders for booked visits are a separate matter. Check with a lawyer.

### Can I give a discount for a Google review?

No. Google's Business Profile help calls incentives such as free or discounted goods or services strictly prohibited.

### Is an Impressum mandatory for a groomer's website?

If the site is a commercial digital service, section 5 DDG requires the provider details described above to be easy to find. A lawyer can confirm your case.

### Is Instagram worth the time for a German groomer?

It can show your work, but no German source comparing channels for groomers was found. Start with the profile and reviews, then see our [Instagram guide for groomers](/blog/pet-groomers-guide-to-instagram-marketing).
