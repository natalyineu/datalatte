A hair salon gets more bookings from Instagram by posting finished work that people want to save or send to a friend, and by making the next step to book obvious. Instagram's head Adam Mosseri says Explore leans on likes, saves and shares, and that Reels are judged on predictions such as whether people watch to the end, like, or reshare. That favours before-and-after work over generic promo.

## What Does Instagram Reward for a Salon Account?

Instagram says it has no single algorithm. Feed, Stories, Explore, Reels and Search each rank in their own way. Mosseri's explainer (31 May 2023) says Feed leans on your activity and your history with the poster, Stories on viewing and engagement history, and Explore on post popularity, which "matters much more" there.

| Surface | What it rewards (Instagram, 2023) | Salon content that fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feed | Past interaction with the account | Posts that regulars comment on |
| Stories | Viewing history, closeness | Behind-the-chair updates, availability |
| Explore | Likes, saves, shares | Colour transformations, cuts |
| Reels | Watch to the end, likes, reshares, audio | Short transformation clips |

The page is from 2023, so treat the details as a guide, not a rulebook.

## What Should a Hair Salon Post?

Post work that a stranger would save for their next appointment: a colour correction, a cut on a specific hair type, a styling step. Saves and shares are exactly what Explore is said to weigh. Add the service name and your town in the caption so it also reads as a local ad.

Reels suit transformations, because the result at the end rewards people who watch fully. Stories suit the day-to-day: a free slot on Thursday, a new product, a client reaction shared with permission. ## How Do You Turn Views Into Bookings?

Make booking one tap from the profile. Search results describe Instagram action buttons, such as Book now, for professional accounts, with one button shown at a time and a link to a booking partner required. These details come from secondary sources, because Instagram's help page text could not be read by our fetcher, so check the setting in the app.

Put your booking link and area in the bio, pin your best three posts, and answer messages quickly. Start with a clean booking page that shows prices and open slots. ## How Do You Measure It?

Ask every new client how they found you and record the answer. Check profile visits and link taps in Instagram's insights each week. If visits rise but taps stay flat, change the bio and the booking page. If taps rise but bookings do not, the page or availability is the problem.

## FAQ

### How often should a hair salon post on Instagram?

No source we opened gives a number that is proven for salons. Choose a pace you can keep, such as a few Reels and regular Stories each week, and compare results over two months.

### Do Reels or photos work better for salons?

Instagram says Reels are shown mostly to people who do not follow you, with reshares and watching to the end among the signals. That favours Reels for reaching new people. Photos still serve regulars in Feed.

### Does a salon need Instagram ads?

Not at first. Ads cost money per lead, per LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks. Fix your booking path first, then test a small budget.

### How do I add a Book now button?

Use a professional account and add an action button under Edit profile, linked to a booking partner. This comes from secondary sources, so confirm in the app.
