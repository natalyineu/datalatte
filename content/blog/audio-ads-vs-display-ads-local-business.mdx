Neither format wins outright. Audio ads rent attention during drives, workouts and workdays, when screens are off. Display ads rent cheap, clickable reminders near the moment of search. On platform terms checked in November 2026, a first audio test on Spotify Ads Manager needs a $250 campaign minimum; a display test on Google can start with any daily budget.

The right first buy depends on the offer. A business selling a considered service, such as a gym membership, needs repeated hearing before a visit. A business selling an urgent fix, such as a locksmith call, needs to appear at the exact moment of need.

## Which costs less to test, audio or display?

Display costs less to start. Google Display has no campaign minimum, so $5 a day tests creative for a week, although clicks at that spend prove little beyond which image gets tapped. Targeting by postcode keeps a local test from leaking across a whole metro area.

Audio costs more to start but buys finished listening. Spotify's help centre lists a 250 minimum in local currency per Ads Manager campaign, and secondary 2026 pricing guides put typical US audio CPMs around $15 to $25. Narrow city or interest targeting raises that range.

**Assumption example.** Assume a $300 test budget. At an assumed $20 CPM, audio buys about 15,000 impressions. At an assumed $1 cost per click, display buys about 300 clicks. If 2% of clicks become bookings, display yields 6 bookings; audio must lift branded search or walk-ins by the same 6 to tie. Every rate here is an assumption for the arithmetic, not a measured result.

## Which format drives foot traffic better?

Display is easier to credit. A click, a call tap and a direction request can be counted in Google Ads, so cost per direction is a real number within weeks. [Google Display Network guide](/blog/google-display-network-guide) covers the placement and postcode settings that keep local display spend honest.

Audio is easier to remember. A 30-second spot heard on a commute repeats the business name in a way a banner cannot, but the visit arrives days later without a click. Honest measurement uses a unique offer phrase, a dedicated landing page, or a before-and-after check on branded search volume.

For most single-site businesses, the sequence matters more than the format. Display retargeting catches people already looking; audio builds the familiarity that makes them look. [Spotify ads for local businesses](/blog/spotify-ads-for-local-business-how-to-start) walks through the audio setup, while [Podcast advertising for local businesses](/blog/podcast-advertising-for-local-business-guide) covers host-read options.

## When should a local business choose audio first?

Choose audio first when the offer needs explanation or trust and the trade area is tight. Fitness studios, clinics and home services fit: the listener hears the name weekly inside a few postcodes, and frequency stays affordable because the audience is small.

Choose display first when demand already exists and the job is capture. Seasonal offers, event tickets and urgent services fit: the banner or search ad appears while intent is warm, and the click goes straight to a booking page.

Budgets under $250 cannot test Spotify self-serve at all, which settles the question for very small spenders. That money works harder on display or search until the monthly budget clears the audio minimum with room for a second flight. [Programmatic versus Google Display](/blog/programmatic-vs-google-display-ads-whats-the-difference) explains when a DSP enters the picture.

## FAQ

### How much do audio ads cost for a small business?

Spotify Ads Manager lists a 250 minimum in local currency per campaign, with an average daily budget option around $15, on Spotify pages checked in November 2026. Secondary 2026 guides put typical US audio CPMs near $15 to $25, rising with narrow targeting. Host-read podcast spots usually cost more per thousand than self-serve audio.

### Are display ads cheaper than audio ads?

Per click, usually yes, and there is no platform minimum on Google Display. Per remembered impression, the comparison flips: a finished 30-second listen is worth more than a half-seen banner. Judge display on cost per call or direction request, and audio on offer redemptions and branded search lift, not on raw CPM.

### Which is better for foot traffic?

Display produces countable direction requests quickly, so it proves itself first. Audio builds repeat name recognition inside a small trade area, which supports walk-ins over weeks. Most local advertisers get the best return running display for capture first, then adding audio once the booking page converts.

### Can a local business run both on $500 a month?

Yes, with sequencing. Put the first month into display to validate the offer and landing page. From month two, split roughly $250 to a Spotify flight and keep the rest on display retargeting. Review after two flights; move budget toward whichever produced verifiable bookings.
