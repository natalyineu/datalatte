A small business can advertise on podcasts in two main ways: a self-serve campaign in Spotify Ads Manager, or a direct sponsorship, usually a host-read ad, with a show. Spotify states that it reaches over 115 million monthly podcast listeners in the U.S., but most shows have national audiences. A local business should favour a show whose listeners sit in its own town. Every ad should carry a unique code, phone number or link so that responses can be counted.

## Is Podcast Advertising Worth It for a Local Business?

Spotify's guide reports that 71% of daily podcast users say podcast content gets all or most of their attention, and that 70% of its podcast listeners are open to hearing ads in podcasts ([Spotify Advertising](https://ads.spotify.com/en-AU/news-and-insights/podcast-advertising-guide/)). These are vendor-reported survey figures. They are not an independent test of any particular category.

Attention is not the same as sales. The same guide says 42% of Spotify podcast listeners trust their favourite host as much as their friends. That trust is the main reason a host-read ad can work for a local firm, because the host's recommendation carries weight with the audience.

The deciding question is whether the local firm's customers listen to podcasts at all. If they do not, the audience totals are irrelevant.

## What Types of Podcast Ads Can You Buy?

Matinée Multilingual's guide describes three creative types. Host-read ads run 60 to 120 seconds, are recorded by the host, and carry the highest cost per thousand impressions. Produced spots run 15 to 30 seconds, use scripted voice talent, cost the least, and scale through dynamic ad insertion ([Matinée Multilingual, secondary](https://matinee.co.uk/blog/podcast-advertising-guide/)).

Placement also matters. Spotify describes pre-roll ads at the start of an episode as short and punchy, suited to high-intent listeners. Mid-roll ads sit in breaks and build recall through storytelling. Post-roll ads suit longer, non-urgent campaigns ([Spotify Advertising](https://ads.spotify.com/en-AU/news-and-insights/podcast-advertising-guide/)). Matinée Multilingual reports that mid-roll placements deliver the highest engagement because listeners skip them less (secondary).

For a local firm, a host-read mid-roll on a show based in the same town is often the most natural fit. A produced spot is cheaper per placement but usually reaches a broader audience.

## How Do You Buy Podcast Ads Through Spotify?

Spotify Ads Manager is the self-serve route. Advertisers can target by age, gender, location and podcast category, and ads can reach listeners on other apps as well as on Spotify ([Spotify Advertising](https://ads.spotify.com/en-AU/news-and-insights/podcast-advertising-guide/)). Location targeting is the closest tool to a local audience, but its precision should be checked inside the tool before any budget is committed.

Custom sponsorships, such as host-read ads, go through Spotify's sales team. Spotify says these can include presenting sponsorships and branded segments, and that direct buying places a brand alongside the host's credibility. A small firm may move faster by contacting a local show directly, since the sponsor and host can agree terms without a platform intermediary.

Before agreeing to anything, ask the show for its download numbers per episode, listener locations, ad slot length, and whether the host will read a script or use their own words.

## How Do You Write and Track a Local Podcast Ad?

Keep the ad to one idea: who the business is, where it is, one offer and one way to respond. Say the response code aloud twice, once in the middle and once at the end. Long web addresses are hard to remember when heard, so a short code or a phone number works better.

Hosts usually sound more natural when they read two or three facts in their own words than when they read a full script. The business supplies the facts and lets the host write the delivery.

Tracking shows whether the spend worked. Matinée Multilingual lists vanity URLs, promo codes and conversion pixels as the main methods (secondary). For a local firm, a promo code plus a dedicated phone number is usually enough. Responses should be recorded weekly and matched against the code each customer quotes.

## When Is Podcast Advertising a Poor Fit?

Podcast ads are a weak choice when enquiries are needed this week, because listeners rarely act straight away. They also struggle when the offer is generic or when the target customers do not listen to podcasts. In those cases, search and map listings usually deserve the budget first.

## FAQ

### How much does podcast advertising cost for a small business?

Rates are set by each show or network, and the sources used here do not publish a rate card. Ask each show for its rate and download numbers before committing.

### Is podcast advertising good for a local business?

It can work, especially through a show whose listeners are in the same area. It needs a trackable offer and several weeks before the results can be judged.

### Can I target only my city with podcast ads?

Podcasts usually reach national audiences. Spotify Ads Manager allows location targeting, but its precision should be checked in the tool. A local show is the most direct way to reach one town.

### How do I know if a podcast ad worked?

Use a unique code, link or phone number that appears only in that ad, then count how many customers quote it.

### How long should a podcast ad be?

Matinée Multilingual reports host-read ads at 60 to 120 seconds and produced spots at 15 to 30 seconds (secondary). Current formats should be confirmed in Spotify Ads Manager or with the show.
