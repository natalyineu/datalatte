A local business can buy podcast ads two ways: a self-serve audio campaign in Spotify Ads Manager, which lists a minimum budget of 250 in your local currency, or a direct sponsorship with a show your customers already listen to. Choose the sponsorship if your audience is in one town, because most podcasts reach people nationally. Use a unique code or phone number in every ad, or you will not be able to tell whether it worked.

Rate cards vary by show, and no average CPM was verifiable from the pages opened for this guide, so none is quoted. Ask each show for its rate and download numbers.

## Is Podcast Advertising Growing?

The IAB/PwC Internet Advertising Revenue Report for 2025 put US podcast advertising at $2.862 billion, up 17.6% from 2024, inside a digital audio market of $8.4 billion, up 10.2% from $7.6 billion. RBR reported these figures on April 16, 2026, so they are a secondary source.

Revenue growth shows advertisers are spending. It does not show that small local businesses earn a return. That has to be tested with your own offer.

## What Are Your Options to Buy?

| Route | How it works | Fit for a local business |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spotify Ads Manager | Self-serve audio or video ads across music, podcasts or both; 250 minimum in local currency | Quick test, little setup |
| Local show sponsorship | You contact the host and agree a rate and a script | Best match for one city |
| Podcast network | Sold across many shows; minimums unpublished here | Wider reach, higher commitment |

Spotify says custom sponsorships such as host-read ads require contacting a Spotify representative, and that its targeting uses behavioural, demographic and contextual signals. For country-specific notes, see the [Canada guide](/blog/podcast-advertising-canada-local-business-guide), the [UK guide](/blog/podcast-advertising-uk-local-business-guide) and the [Australia guide](/blog/podcast-advertising-australia-local-business-guide).

## Which Shows Should You Look For?

Look for shows where the host already talks to people in your area or your customer type: a local business podcast, a parenting show from your city, a sports show tied to the local team, or a niche show your customers follow. Ask for the number of downloads per episode, where listeners are located and how long the ad slot is.

Listen to three recent episodes. Check how the host reads ads and whether the sponsor slot feels natural. Our [podcast marketing overview](/blog/podcast-marketing-for-local-business) covers hosting your own show as an alternative.

## How Do You Write a Podcast Ad?

Keep it to one idea. Say who you are, where you are, one offer and one way to respond. Use the same line twice, once in the middle and once at the end. Avoid web addresses with many parts. Use a short code that listeners can remember and say out loud.

A host reading your script in their own words usually sounds more natural than a recorded spot. Give the host two or three facts, not a full script.

## What Does a Test Look Like?

The example is an **assumption**, because no rate is published here.

| | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Budget (assumption) | 250 in local currency |
| Channel | Spotify Ads Manager, or a small show sponsorship |
| Run length | 4 weeks (assumption) |
| Tracking | One code, one landing page |
| Decision rule (assumption) | Repeat if cost per redemption is below the margin on a first visit |

After four weeks you know cost per redemption for one creative. Do not change the offer midway, and record codes weekly. If you get no redemptions, check the offer and the call to action before blaming the channel.

## When Is Podcast Advertising a Poor Fit?

It is a weak fit when you need leads this week, because listeners rarely act at once. It also struggles when the offer is generic or when your customers do not listen to podcasts. In those cases a [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) and search ads usually come first. For other audio formats, read [digital audio advertising on Spotify and Pandora](/blog/digital-audio-advertising-2026-spotify-pandora-podcast-ads).

## FAQ

### How much does podcast advertising cost?

Rates are set by each show or network. Spotify Ads Manager lists a minimum campaign budget of 250 in local currency. No average CPM is quoted here.

### Is podcast advertising good for small businesses?

It can be, especially through a local or niche show, but it needs a trackable offer and several weeks to judge.

### Can I target only my city?

Podcasts usually reach national audiences. A local show or a targeting option in Spotify Ads Manager may help, so confirm in the tool.

### How do I know if a podcast ad worked?

Use a unique code, link or phone number only in that ad, and count responses.

### How long should a podcast ad be?

Spotify audio ads run 15 to 30 seconds in older reports; confirm current formats in Ads Manager. Sponsorship reads are agreed with the host.
