CTV advertising lets a local business buy short, full-screen video ads that run inside streaming TV content on internet-connected televisions. Paramount's ad glossary describes CTV ads as non-skippable and typically 15 or 30 seconds long. Programmatic platforms buy and serve these ads automatically against criteria the advertiser sets. For a Fresno business, the real question is whether a small monthly budget can reach enough of the right households to matter. This guide covers the format, the inventory types, the creative and budget math, and how to measure results.

## What Is CTV Advertising for a Fresno Business?

CTV stands for Connected TV, a television that connects to the internet and lets viewers stream content. [Simpli.fi](https://simpli.fi/latest/what-is-ctv) defines CTV advertising as video ads delivered through streaming content on internet-connected screens, including smart TVs and devices such as Roku and Fire TV. [Teads](https://www.teads.com/blog/what-is-connected-tv-advertising/9858/) (secondary) makes the same point more broadly, describing Connected TV as any television that connects to the internet to stream digital content.

The format is what makes CTV distinct from other video. [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions) describes CTV ads as non-skippable, typically 15 or 30 seconds, and shown full-screen. [Amazon Ads](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/connected-tv-advertising) notes that the video completion rate of CTV ads tends to be very high because many streaming services do not allow ads to be skipped.

A local business should expect the reach to be broad and the response to be indirect. Viewers rarely click through from a TV. The effect usually shows up later, in searches, visits, or a customer saying where they heard about you, so the measurement plan matters as much as the creative.

## Which Streaming Inventory Should You Buy?

Streaming ad inventory is not one pool. [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions) defines ad-supported video-on-demand (AVOD) as free or lower-cost streaming services supported by ads, and free ad-supported streaming TV (FAST) channels as streaming channels that are free for viewers and supported entirely by advertising, with Pluto TV given as an example.

[Priority Media Agency](https://prioritymediaagency.co.uk/services/tv-tvod-streaming) adds that, unlike traditional TV, VOD ads appear on streaming services, catch-up TV, and connected devices. The table below sets out how each inventory type differs and what to check before buying it.

| Inventory type | What the source says | What to check before buying |
|---|---|---|
| AVOD | Free or lower-cost streaming services supported by ads (Paramount) | Whether the platform supports the ZIP-level targeting you need |
| FAST channels | Free for viewers and supported entirely by advertising; Pluto TV is the example (Paramount) | Whether the channel mix reaches your likely customers |
| VOD on streaming and catch-up TV | Ads appear on streaming services, catch-up TV and connected devices (Priority Media) | Which apps and devices the buying platform can reach |

Streaming services such as Hulu and Peacock are common buying options. Minimums, CPMs, and audience profiles change, so confirm them on each platform's current ad-buying page before committing a budget.

## How Does Programmatic Buying Put an Ad in Front of Households?

Most CTV for small businesses is bought programmatically. [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions) explains that programmatic advertising uses automated technology to buy and serve ads in real time, with the platform purchasing inventory that matches advertiser-set criteria. [Amazon Ads](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/connected-tv-advertising) confirms that Amazon streaming TV ads are currently purchased the same way, meaning the ads are bought and sold digitally by an automated system.

Scale is the upside. [ClicksGeek](https://clicksgeek.com/best-advertising-platforms/) (secondary) describes The Trade Desk as giving access to inventory across millions of websites, apps, and streaming services. For a Fresno business, breadth is less useful than control. Set criteria that narrow delivery toward your trade area and your likely customers, and check what geographic options the platform actually offers.

Location-based targeting works differently in other channels. The [geofencing advertising guide](/blog/geofencing-advertising-local-business-guide) explains how location triggers reach phones, while mobile placements are covered in the [in-app advertising guide](/blog/in-app-advertising-local-business-guide). CTV reaches the household on the living room screen, which changes both the audience and the creative.

## What Makes a 15-Second CTV Ad Work?

Attention is won early. [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions) recommends a strong opening hook within the first 3–5 seconds to engage streaming viewers. For a local business, that means showing the service, the result, or the problem it solves right away, with the business name following quickly rather than sitting on a logo for two seconds.

Technical specifications are not uniform. [Advertise In Tampa](https://advertiseintampa.com/services/advertising/ctv-ott/), in a CTV services page, states that every streaming giant has its own set of rules regarding resolution, frame rate, bitrate and format. Export one master file, then check each buying platform's spec sheet before uploading. A file that fails one platform's rules can stall a campaign.

Cost is tied to completion. [Amazon Ads](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/connected-tv-advertising) explains that cost per completed view (CPCV) measures the price of each completed ad, and it sits alongside video completion rate in CTV programmatic pricing. A message that loses viewers in the first few seconds therefore affects what each completed view costs, which is why the opening deserves the most editing time.

## What Does a Fresno CTV Budget Look Like?

[Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/netflix-advertising-small-business) gives one example: a $200–400 monthly Netflix test budget at a $40 programmatic CPM delivers approximately 5,000–10,000 impressions across Netflix and other streaming platforms. That is a vendor example, not a Fresno rate. The worked calculation below uses assumptions to show how the numbers fit together.

Inputs, all assumptions:

Monthly test budget: $300, inside the $200–400 range Adwave describes.
Programmatic CPM: $40, taken from Adwave's example.
Completion rate: 90% of impressions watched to the end.
Frequency: 4 impressions per household per month.

Calculations:

Impressions = $300 ÷ $40 × 1,000 = 7,500 impressions, which falls within Adwave's 5,000–10,000 example range.

Estimated households reached = 7,500 ÷ 4 = about 1,875 households.

Completed views = 7,500 × 90% = 6,750.

Cost per completed view = $300 ÷ 6,750 = about $0.044.

A three-month run at the same level would total $900 (assumption). The result is only meaningful if the households reached match the customers you serve, and the actual Fresno CPM will differ from the example. Replace the assumptions with live quotes from your buying platform before deciding.

## How Should You Measure a CTV Campaign?

CTV measurement starts with the platform. [Amazon Ads](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/connected-tv-advertising) names video completion rate and cost per completed view as metrics used in CTV programmatic pricing, so both should appear in the campaign dashboard. Record them weekly so you can see whether the hook and frequency settings are working.

Brand effects need different signals. Track branded searches for your business name, direct website visits, and how new customers say they found you. Compare these during the flight with the weeks before it. A complete framework for tying spend to outcomes is in the [marketing ROI guide](/blog/how-to-track-marketing-roi-local-business-guide).

Allow the campaign enough time for awareness to appear. A two-week test shows delivery and completion. Whether it changed customer behaviour usually takes a longer window, and the test should be judged on that longer view.

## Where Does CTV Fit Next to Google Ads?

CTV and search do different jobs. CTV builds recognition among households who are watching, while search captures people already looking for a service. When a household saw your ad and later searches for your name, the two channels overlap, and that overlap is worth tracking.

Whether this combination pays off in your market is a question for a test, not an assumption. Run the TV flight for a fixed period, watch branded search and paid search volume, and compare against a period without the TV flight. If you want help setting up the search side, the [Google Ads service](/services/google-ads) covers how paid search captures that intent.

## FAQ

### Is CTV advertising worth it for a small business in Fresno?

The sources in this guide do not provide Fresno-specific performance data, so the answer depends on your own test. Run a small, time-limited campaign with defined metrics such as completion rate, branded search and customer self-reports, then decide based on those results.

### What is the difference between CTV and AVOD?

CTV is the category of television that connects to the internet and shows streamed video. AVOD, according to Paramount's glossary, refers to free or lower-cost streaming services supported by ads. An AVOD service is one type of streaming environment where CTV ads can run.

### Are CTV ads skippable?

Paramount's glossary describes CTV ads as non-skippable video advertisements. Amazon Ads notes that many streaming services do not allow ads to be skipped, which is one reason completion rates tend to be high.

### Why does the first few seconds of a CTV ad matter?

Paramount's glossary recommends a strong opening hook within the first 3–5 seconds. Because cost per completed view depends on viewers watching to the end, an opening that holds attention early supports the value of each impression.
