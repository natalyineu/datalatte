## How Much Do Google Ads Cost for a Car Wash?

WordStream by LocaliQ's [2026 search benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/) (last updated June 1, 2026) do not list car washes. The nearest category covers repair shops and parts sellers, who earn far more per customer than a wash, so click costs may be lower or higher for you.

| Metric | Automotive: Repair, Service & Parts | All industries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost per click | $4.35 | $5.42 |
| Click-through rate | 5.56% | 6.64% |
| Conversion rate | 15.51% | 8.18% (labelled Google Ads) |
| Cost per lead | $29.96 | $66.69 |

A lead in these benchmarks may be a call or form fill, not a wash sold. A car wash sells a low-priced, high-frequency service, so click cost matters more to you than to a repair shop. Your own account after four weeks is the benchmark that counts.

## Is Google Ads Worth It for a Car Wash?

Often only for part of the business. A single $15 wash cannot easily repay a $4 click, so ads on generic searches rarely pay unless the customer returns. Ads make more sense for higher-value products: detailing packages, ceramic coatings, fleet accounts and monthly membership plans.

That is a reason to choose the offer before the keywords. See [marketing for car washes](/blog/marketing-for-car-wash) for free channels such as your Google Business Profile, which handles most "near me" demand without click costs, and the [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) for sizing spend. The [Google Ads guide for service businesses](/blog/google-ads-for-service-businesses) covers the wider setup.

## Which Keywords Should a Car Wash Bid On?

People searching for a wash want one close by, now. Test:

- Car wash near me, car wash in your city, and the same with "open now"
- Interior detailing, full detail and wax searches, if you offer them
- Monthly car wash membership or unlimited wash, if you sell one

Add negative keywords for "jobs", "for sale", "equipment", "franchise", "how to wash" and "DIY". Google says [negative keywords](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453972) do not match close variants, so add plurals and misspellings yourself. Our [negative keywords guide](/blog/google-ads-negative-keywords-stop-wasting-budget) has more starter terms.

## How Do You Target a Small Radius?

Distance decides everything for a car wash, because people rarely drive far for a wash. Google's [location targeting help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722043) says radius targeting needs at least 1 km and that a very small target may show ads only intermittently or not at all.

Start with a radius that covers the neighbourhoods your drive-through or bays can realistically serve, then read the location report. Google recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting for Search and suggests "Presence" if you only want people physically in your area. Add a [location asset](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182) so the ad can show your address and a map.

## What Conversions Should You Track?

Track what a visitor does that has value: calls, direction taps and, if you sell memberships online, completed sign-ups.

Google's [call conversion help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6100664) says you set a minimum call length and every call at least that long counts as a conversion. Google also says clicks on a [call asset](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453991) cost the same as headline clicks. Turn on call and location assets, then check results against your own wash count.

## Which Bidding Strategy Fits?

Google says [Maximize conversions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7381968) uses its AI to get the most conversions for your budget and requires conversion tracking. It states no minimum conversion volume. With a few weeks of data and a firm cost-per-conversion goal, Google points to Target CPA instead.

For a first campaign, fixed daily budget plus conversion tracking plus weekly search term review is enough.

## When Should the Ads Run?

Wash demand follows weather and weekends, and no source gives a standard pattern. Check your own sales by day and hour, run ads when you have spare capacity, and lower spend when queues are long. Compare against rainy and dry weeks before judging results.

## FAQ

### How much do Google Ads cost for a car wash?

No car wash benchmark is published. LocaliQ's 2026 data lists $4.35 per click for Automotive: Repair, Service & Parts against $5.42 across all industries. Your cost depends on your city and keywords.

### What is a good conversion rate for a car wash?

LocaliQ lists 15.51% for the nearest automotive category, but it counts leads for repair shops, not washes. Do not use it as a target.

### Should I advertise basic washes?

Rarely. A $4 click is hard to repay with a low-priced wash unless customers return. Detailing, memberships and fleets suit paid search better.

### Do I need call tracking?

If customers phone to ask about prices or hours, yes. Set the minimum call length that counts as a real enquiry.

### Is a Business Profile enough?

For many washes, it covers most map searches without click costs. Ads add reach when your profile is not in the top results.
