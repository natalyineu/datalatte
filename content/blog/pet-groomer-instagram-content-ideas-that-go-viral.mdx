## What Should a Pet Groomer Post on Instagram?

Post what helps a pet owner decide to book. APPA's 2026 Dog Report says 51% of dog owners groom at home, and that spending on salon and mobile grooming fell in 2025. Your posts have to show why a professional groom is worth the price and the trip.

Each idea below fits one of four jobs: show the result, build trust, answer questions, or bring in local people. Try to cover all four every month, not only before-and-after photos.

## Which 20 Post Ideas Work for Groomers?

| Job | Ideas |
| --- | --- |
| Show the result | 1. Before and after of a matted coat. 2. Time-lapse of a full groom. 3. Breed-specific cut explained. 4. The "reveal" at pickup, with the owner's consent. 5. Puppy first groom. |
| Build trust | 6. Tour of the salon and tools. 7. Meet the groomer, with qualifications. 8. How you keep nervous dogs calm. 9. Cleaning and safety routine. 10. Photo of a review you earned (with permission). |
| Answer questions | 11. How often to groom each coat type. 12. What a groom includes and what costs extra. 13. How to book and cancel. 14. Brushing between visits. 15. What to do about matting. |
| Bring in locals | 16. Collab with a vet or pet shop. 17. Neighborhood dog of the month, with owner consent. 18. Holiday booking dates. 19. Adoption event or shelter drive. 20. Poll in Stories: which breed next? |

Do not post a customer's pet without the owner's agreement, and keep addresses out of the frame. The same ideas can run on TikTok; see our [TikTok guide for pet groomers](/blog/tiktok-for-pet-groomers).

## How Long Should Reels Be?

Instagram's creators blog says that from January 21, 2025, creators in the United States can publish reels up to 3 minutes in the Reels editor, and reels up to 3 minutes are eligible to be seen by non-followers in the Reels tab and Explore. Instagram's help pages add that reels can be edited up to 20 minutes, but reels over 3 minutes will not be recommended to new audiences.

For a groomer, 15 to 45 seconds is usually enough for a transformation. Use a longer Reel only for a full how-to. Those lengths are a style choice, not an Instagram requirement.

## Can Trial Reels Help a Small Account?

Trial reels share a reel only with people who do not follow you, and you then decide whether to share it with followers. For a groomer with a small local following, that lets you test a new format on strangers first. Instagram also says that posting original content consistently gives accounts of all sizes the chance to reach new audiences.

Keep the test fair. Change one thing per trial: the first three seconds, the caption, or the cover photo. Then keep what works.

## Is Collaborating with Other Local Businesses Worth It?

It is usually the cheapest way to reach nearby pet owners. A vet, pet shop, dog walker or trainer already has your target audience. Instagram lets one account invite another to co-author a post, and the post then appears on both profiles once the invite is accepted. Check the current steps in the app, because Instagram changes this feature.

## What Does a 12-Post Month Look Like?

The plan below is an **assumption** you can adjust, not a proven result. It is built for a solo groomer with limited time.

| Week | Post 1 | Post 2 | Post 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Transformation Reel | Care tip | Stories poll |
| 2 | Salon tour | Price and timing explainer | Transformation Reel |
| 3 | Partner collab | Care tip | Transformation Reel |
| 4 | Meet the groomer | Holiday booking dates | Transformation Reel |

Now the value. Assume a groom costs $75 and a new client returns six times a year. Five new clients a month from Instagram would be worth about $2,250 over a year (5 x $75 x 6). Those five numbers are assumptions; replace them with your own, and ask each new client how they found you.

## FAQ

### How often should a pet groomer post on Instagram?

No official number exists. Instagram advises posting original content consistently. Three posts a week is a realistic target for a solo groomer.

### Do before-and-after posts still work?

Yes, if each one shows a real result and says what was done. Mix them with trust and question posts so the feed does not repeat itself.

### How long should my Reels be?

Instagram says reels up to 3 minutes can be recommended to non-followers. Most groomer transformation clips need less than 45 seconds.

### Can I use customers' pets in my posts?

Ask the owner first, and keep personal details such as address out of the post.

### Is Instagram or TikTok better for groomers?

Both reward short video. Start where your customers already are, and reuse the same footage on the other. Compare them in our [TikTok guide](/blog/tiktok-for-pet-groomers).
