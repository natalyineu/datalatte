To win back a no-show, send a short, non-judgmental email soon after the missed appointment, with one clear way to rebook. If there is no reply, send a second message a week later. Stop there. No published figure tells small service businesses how many no-shows these emails recover, so this guide gives a template and a way to measure your own result.

The research that does exist is about reminders before the visit. A Cochrane review of text message reminders for healthcare appointments found attendance rose with a risk ratio of 1.14 versus no reminder, and rated the evidence as moderate quality.

## What Is a Win-Back Email for a No-Show?

It is a message to a customer who booked and did not arrive. Its job is to rebook, not to scold. Send it from a real person's name, mention the service they booked, and give one button or reply option to pick a new time.

Prevention comes first. Reminders reduce no-shows, and we cover them in our guide to [reducing no-shows](/blog/reduce-no-shows-appointment-business). Use win-back emails for the customers who still miss.

## What Does the Research Say About Reminders?

The Cochrane review looked at mobile phone message reminders for health appointments. Compared with no reminder, text reminders raised attendance (risk ratio 1.14, 95% confidence interval 1.03 to 1.26, seven studies, 5,841 participants). Compared with phone call reminders, results were about the same (risk ratio 0.99). The authors called the evidence of low to moderate quality.

This is healthcare, not salons or studios, so do not copy the size of the effect. It supports one idea: a timely message helps people show up. Our [SMS vs email guide](/blog/sms-vs-email-marketing-local-business-which-is-better) compares the two channels.

## What Should the Three Emails Say?

| Email | When | Goal | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Within 24 hours of the miss | Rebook | "We missed you today." One link to pick a new time |
| 2 | About 7 days later | Gentle nudge | Same link, mention a policy if you have one |
| 3 | 30 to 60 days later (optional) | Reactivate | Seasonal reason to return |

Keep each under 100 words. Do not guilt the customer. Do not offer a discount to everyone who misses, because that rewards no-shows. If you have a fee or deposit policy, state it plainly and check your booking tool's rules and local law before charging.

Sample text for email 1, with placeholders: "Hi [first name], we missed you at [time] today. Life happens. Here is a link to pick another time: [link]. If something about the booking did not work for you, just reply and tell us."

## Is a No-Show Email a Marketing Email?

It depends on content. The FTC's CAN-SPAM guide treats transactional or relationship messages, such as those that confirm or complete a transaction the customer agreed to, as largely exempt. A message that promotes an offer is commercial. The FTC says a mixed message is judged by its subject line and where the transactional content sits.

The safe approach is to treat win-back emails as commercial: include your postal address, an unsubscribe link, and honor opt-outs within 10 business days. The FTC lists penalties of up to $53,088 per non-compliant email. For SMS rules, check the laws in your country before texting.

## How Do You Measure Whether It Works?

Track three numbers per month: no-shows, win-back emails sent, and rebookings within 14 days of an email. Divide rebookings by emails sent.

Worked example, all inputs are assumptions: a studio has 30 no-shows in a month and sends 30 first emails. Nine people rebook within 14 days. That is 30%, or nine appointments. At an assumed average price of $40, that equals $360. Compare this with the time spent. If the booking tool sends the emails automatically, the cost is mostly setup.

## FAQ

### How fast should I email after a no-show?

Within 24 hours is a sensible default, while the missed visit is fresh. No source used here gives a tested best time.

### Should I charge a no-show fee?

That is a policy choice. If you do, state it before booking and follow your local rules.

### How many follow-ups are too many?

Two or three over two months is plenty. After that, stop and keep the customer on your regular list only if they did not unsubscribe.

### Do text messages work better than email?

For reminders before an appointment, the Cochrane review found texts improved attendance versus no reminder and were about equal to phone calls. It did not compare texts with email.

### Can I use AI to write the email?

Yes for a first draft. Check the facts and keep the tone personal.
