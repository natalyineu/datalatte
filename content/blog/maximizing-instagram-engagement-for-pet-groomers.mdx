## What Does Instagram Reward?

For Feed, Instagram's 2023 explainer says it predicts how likely you are to spend a few seconds on a post, comment, like, share and tap the profile photo. For Reels, the key predictions are watching all the way, resharing, liking and going to the audio page.

Instagram's creator tips add that original content is more likely to be recommended to non-followers than reposts or content with watermarks, and that recommended Reels can run no longer than three minutes. Rival IQ's 2025 benchmark report says Instagram engagement dipped across industries, and it does not cover pet businesses.

## Which Posts Work for a Grooming Salon?

Use formats that show a result and invite a reaction:

- Before-and-after Reels of a coat, with the breed named in the caption.
- A short clip of the full groom, ending on the finished dog.
- A photo post asking a question, such as "What would you name this cut?"
- Stories showing the schedule and open slots.

Ask owners for permission before posting their pet and tag them. Our [pet groomer social media strategy](/blog/pet-groomer-social-media-strategy) and [social media marketing guide](/blog/pet-groomer-social-media-marketing) go deeper on the full plan.

## How Do You Get Found by New Local Customers?

Instagram recommends adding relevant keywords to content, captions, bio and hashtags so people find you through Search. Use words a pet owner types: breed, service and town, for example "doodle grooming Austin". Repeat the same wording on your Google Business Profile.

Collabs let a post appear with a second account, such as a local pet shop. Instagram suggests them for shared posts. Check Insights for how much reach comes from non-followers, and see Instagram's Account Status page if reach drops.

## Should You Also Use Google?

Instagram builds familiarity, while Google catches people who are ready to book. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 Search benchmarks for Animals and Pets show a $4.06 average click, a 7.49% click-through rate, a 16.22% conversion rate and $31.50 per lead. These are US category averages, not groomer-specific.

## How Do You Measure Engagement? A Worked Example

The figures are an **assumption** for one month, used only to show the arithmetic.

| Metric | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Reach of a Reel | 2,000 accounts |
| Likes, comments, shares and saves | 120 |
| Engagement per reach | 6% |
| Profile visits from the Reel | 90 |
| Link clicks to booking page | 12 |
| Bookings | 3 |

Divide interactions by reach for each post and compare your own posts over time. Keep the post type that produces the most shares and booking clicks, because those lead to appointments.

## FAQ

### How often should a groomer post on Instagram?
No source verified here gives an exact number. Pick a rhythm you can keep, for example two Reels and one photo a week, and review monthly.

### What is a good engagement rate for pet groomers?
No published benchmark for groomers was found. Compare each post with your own average.

### Do hashtags help?
Instagram lists hashtags among the places for relevant keywords, along with captions and bio. It publishes no required count.

### Can I repost customers' photos?
Instagram says reposted content is less likely to be recommended. Ask for permission and tag the customer in your own post, or use Collabs.
