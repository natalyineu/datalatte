A sports business should post short, real training and class clips on a TikTok Business Account, use the licensed Commercial Music Library, and only buy ads after a few weeks of organic posts show what people watch. Pew's 2026 survey found 31% of US adults use TikTok, but 65% of adults aged 18 to 29 do and only 11% of those 65 and older. TikTok ads need a daily ad group budget above $20, so plan the spend first.

This guide covers gyms, martial arts and climbing clubs, sports shops and youth programs. It does not repeat the "action content" idea from the earlier version of this page, because no source defines such a content type. All figures are sourced or labelled as assumptions.

## Who Uses TikTok, and Is It Your Audience?

Pew's fact sheet (survey fielded January 30 to June 18, 2026) gives use by age: 65% of adults 18 to 29, 45% of 30 to 49, 31% of 50 to 64 and 11% of 65 and over. By sex, 43% of women and 31% of men use it.

| Age group | Share using TikTok |
| --- | --- |
| 18-29 | 65% |
| 30-49 | 45% |
| 50-64 | 31% |
| 65+ | 11% |

For a gym, climbing club or dance studio aimed at adults under 50, that is a large audience. For a senior fitness program, Facebook may reach more people. Pew's data is national, not local, so your own members matter more than the average.

## What Should a Sports Business Post?

No source gives a formula, so test a few formats and keep what gets saves and messages. Starting ideas that need only a phone:

- A coach showing one drill or fix in 20 seconds.
- A class from the entrance to the final minute.
- A beginner's first session, filmed with their written permission.
- Gear care or sizing tips for a sports shop.
- Behind-the-scenes setup before opening.

Get written consent before filming members, and from a parent or guardian for children. Our [TikTok content ideas for local businesses](/blog/tiktok-content-ideas-for-local-business) has more formats.

## Can You Use Any Music in Your Videos?

No. TikTok's help says "Businesses cannot use the general music library for commercial usage" and points them to the Commercial Music Library, described as a pre-cleared global library of 1 million songs, free for businesses. If you use another user's original sound, TikTok says to consult your legal team about the license.

Set up a Business Account first, since that decides which music you see. Our [TikTok for Business account guide](/blog/tiktok-for-business-account-guide) covers the setup.

## How Much Do TikTok Ads Cost for a Local Sports Business?

TikTok does not publish a typical CPC in the pages checked. What it does publish are floors. Its help states that campaign daily and total budgets must exceed 50 USD and ad group daily budgets must exceed 20 USD.

That means even a small test has a minimum. Read the cost picture in our [TikTok ads cost guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-guide), and see the [fitness studio version](/blog/using-tiktok-for-fitness-studio-marketing) for a sibling niche. Do not borrow CPC numbers from blogs without checking their source.

## Worked Example: A Two-Week Test Budget

All inputs below are **assumptions**, except the minimums from TikTok.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Ad group daily budget | $21 (just above the $20 minimum) |
| Campaign budget | Above $50 per TikTok's rule |
| Length | 14 days |
| Total ad group spend | $21 x 14 = $294 |
| Membership value (assumption) | $60 per month |

At $294, you need about 5 new members paying $60 a month to cover one month's spend, ignoring retention. Whether the ads can deliver that is unknown until the test runs. Count new sign-ups by asking "how did you hear about us?" and set a stop date before you start.

## How Do You Know If It Works?

Track three things weekly: video views, profile visits and enquiries or trial bookings. Views alone do not pay rent. Ask each new member how they found you and write it down.

If after 4 to 6 weeks of posting there are no enquiries, change the content or the audience before adding budget. Many sports businesses also do better with [local TikTok targeting](/blog/how-to-target-local-customers-on-tiktok) than with broad reach.

## FAQ

### Is TikTok good for gyms and sports clubs?

It can be, especially for audiences under 50, where Pew finds 45% to 65% use it. Results depend on your content and location, and no source guarantees leads.

### How much should I spend on TikTok ads?

At least enough to meet TikTok's floors: above $50 for the campaign and above $20 a day for each ad group. Start with a fixed test like the example above.

### Can I use popular songs in my videos?

Businesses cannot use the general music library for commercial usage. Use the free Commercial Music Library.

### Do I need a Business Account?

For music licensing and ads, yes. See our [Business Account guide](/blog/tiktok-for-business-account-guide).

### Should I film children in class?

Only with consent from a parent or guardian, and check your club's safeguarding rules first. This article did not review child-safety law.
