## Which Keywords Bring Contractor Leads?

Pick searches that name a project and, where possible, a place. A homeowner who types "bathroom remodel contractor [city]" is further along than one who types "bathroom ideas".

| Intent | Example keyword pattern | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hire for a project | "kitchen remodel contractor [city]", "basement finishing [city]" | Highest intent, highest cost |
| Local, near me | "general contractor near me", "home renovation [neighbourhood]" | Needs strict location settings |
| Specific job | "deck builder [city]", "roof repair [city]" | Use one ad group per job |
| Quote | "free estimate remodel [city]", "remodeling cost [city]" | Cost searches often come from researchers |

These are patterns to test, not proven winners. Check each in Google Keyword Planner and in your own Search terms report.

## What Do Match Types Mean for a Contractor?

Google describes broad match as the default that can show on related searches, phrase match as searches that include the keyword's meaning, and exact match as searches with the same meaning or intent. Exact gives the most control and the fewest searches.

At $8.33 a click, a loose match type is expensive. Start with exact and phrase for your three or four core jobs. Google says broad match needs Smart Bidding, so leave it until conversions are tracked.

## Which Negative Keywords Protect a Contractor's Budget?

Negative keywords stop ads showing on searches with a term. Google states that negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals and synonyms.

- Job intent: jobs, hiring, apprenticeship, salary, union
- DIY intent: diy, how to, tutorial, youtube, video
- Product intent: materials, wholesale, home depot, supplier
- Out-of-scope work: trades you do not offer, such as plumber or electrician when you only manage remodels

Add negatives at campaign level for terms that never fit. Our [negative keywords guide](/blog/google-ads-negative-keywords-stop-wasting-budget) has a weekly routine.

## Should Contractors Use Local Services Ads Too?

Possibly. Google lists Local Services Ads for certain service categories, and a business pays for leads related to its services, with a budget it can adjust or pause. Eligibility depends on category and area, and Google offers a tool to check. Search ads and Local Services Ads can run together. ## FAQ

### What is the average CPC for contractor keywords?

LocaliQ does not publish a contractor category. Home and Home Improvement averages $8.33 per click in 2026, so expect your own figure to differ by trade and city.

### How many keywords should a contractor start with?

Ten to fifteen exact and phrase match keywords across three or four core jobs. One ad group per job keeps ad text relevant.

### Are cost-research searches worth bidding on?

Test them with a low budget. Searches like "remodeling cost" often come from people comparing prices, so check whether they turn into calls.

### Does broad match work for contractors?

Google says to use Smart Bidding with broad match. Without conversion tracking, start with phrase and exact.

### Where do I see real search volumes?

In Google Keyword Planner, which needs a Google Ads account with billing set up. This page does not publish volumes.
