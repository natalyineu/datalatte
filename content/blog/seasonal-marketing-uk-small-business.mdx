The key dates for UK small businesses between November 2026 and October 2027 are Black Friday on 27 November 2026, Christmas Day on Friday 25 December, Mothering Sunday on 7 March 2027, Good Friday on 26 March, Easter Monday on 29 March, and the Early May and Spring bank holidays on 3 and 31 May. Bank holiday dates below come from GOV.UK. Plan each campaign about two months ahead.

This calendar is for coffee shops, salons, pet services and gyms in the UK. The timing advice is practical guidance, not measured results. Where a number appears, it has a source at the end or is labelled as an assumption.

## How Should You Plan Black Friday and Christmas?

Black Friday 2025 was big on the high street too. Nationwide reported a record 11.9 million customer transactions on 28 November 2025, up 8.7% on 2024. That is card-spend data from one bank's customers, not a measure of small-business sales.

For service businesses, a voucher can protect your margin better than a discount on appointments. You take cash now and deliver the service later. Prepare creative by the first week of November, and set special hours for Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day.

**Worked example (all numbers are assumptions):**

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Voucher face value | £50 |
| Black Friday price | £40 |
| Vouchers sold | 60 |
| Cash collected | £2,400 |
| Share redeemed within 6 months | 85% |
| Value of services delivered | £2,550 |
| Gap if all redeemed (£50 x 60 = £3,000) | £600 |

In this assumed case the £600 discount is the cost of the cash up front. Subtract your delivery cost per £50 of service to see the real margin. Vouchers that are never used are extra margin, but do not plan on it.

## What Should You Run in January and February?

January suits gyms and studios because many people set fitness goals. This is general experience, not a measured figure, so test your own January offer. Coffee shops can promote a warm workspace and non-alcoholic drinks. Salons and groomers can use quieter weeks to fill diary slots.

Valentine's Day 2027 is a Sunday, so promote Friday and Saturday bookings as well. Offers for groups of friends and couples both fit. Keep offers small enough to deliver, such as limited class places or a set number of appointments.

## When Is Mothering Sunday in 2027?

Mothering Sunday is the fourth Sunday of Lent in the UK. It is on 15 March in 2026 and 7 March in 2027. That differs from the US Mother's Day in May, so a template campaign from an American source can go out at the wrong time.

Start promoting gift vouchers and treat packages two to three weeks before. For salons that means vouchers for hair or beauty. Coffee shops can sell gift cards and family breakfast offers. Groomers can sell a voucher as a gift for a pet-owning parent.

## How Do You Handle Easter and May Bank Holidays?

Good Friday falls on 26 March 2027 and Easter Monday on 29 March, so the long weekend is the last weekend of March. The Early May and Spring bank holidays follow on 3 May and 31 May.

Bank holiday checklist:

1. Set special hours on your Google Business Profile. Google's steps: Edit profile, Hours, Special hours, pick a date, then Save. Special hours show only if your hours are set to "Open with main hours".
2. Publish the holiday offer at least a week ahead.
3. Email your list before the break.
4. Check staff rotas against expected bookings.
5. Review ad schedules and budgets before each date, and compare with last year's own account data.

Scotland has different bank holidays, so Scottish businesses should not copy the English list.

## What About Summer and September?

June to August varies by trade. Weddings affect hair and beauty. Gyms often see people travelling. The mistake is to stop all marketing in the quiet period. Use July and August to prepare content for September, because many people restart routines when school terms begin.

School term dates vary by area, so check your local council before timing a back-to-school offer. A coffee shop can launch autumn drinks in early September. See our guide on [seasonal drinks for coffee shops](/blog/how-to-market-seasonal-drinks-coffee-shop).

## How Do You Build the Calendar for Your Business?

Pick the four or five dates that match your trade. Write the launch date, offer, channel, budget and one number you will track for each. Review last year's bookings by week to see where your own peaks fell.

## FAQ

### When is Mothering Sunday in 2027?
7 March 2027. In 2026 it falls on 15 March.

### When are the UK bank holidays in 2027?
For England and Wales: 1 January, 26 March (Good Friday), 29 March (Easter Monday), 3 May, 31 May and 30 August. Scotland and Northern Ireland differ, so check GOV.UK.

### How early should I start a seasonal campaign?
About two months before for planning and creative, and two to three weeks before for promotion on most dates. This is a working rule, not a measured result, so adjust using your own booking data.

### Should I discount or sell vouchers on Black Friday?
Vouchers move the cost to a later date and bring cash in sooner. Check that your discount still leaves a margin after the service cost.
