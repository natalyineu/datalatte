Google Ads can work for custom home builders, but only if you judge it on signed projects rather than clicks. A custom home is a large purchase with a long decision, so a campaign that sends 10 consultation requests a month can pay for itself if one of them signs. The levers are narrow keywords, tight geography and call tracking.

Google does not publish cost per click for home builders, so this guide shows you how to calculate the cost per lead you can afford. It is written for builders in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. Figures marked as assumptions are placeholders for your own numbers.

## Which Keywords Should a Custom Home Builder Bid On?

Group keywords by how far along the buyer is. Someone searching for "custom home builder near me" is shortlisting. Someone searching for "house plans" is browsing and may never hire a builder.

| Buyer stage | Example searches | How to treat them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ready to hire | custom home builder [city], build a house on my lot [city], luxury home builders near me | Core ad group, phrase and exact match |
| Has land, needs a builder | builders for my land, lot clearing and build | Separate ad group, landing page about building on your lot |
| Design-led | design build homes [city], custom home architect and builder | Test with a design-consultation offer |
| Browsing | house plans, floor plans, dream home ideas | Exclude with negatives |
| Jobs and DIY | home builder jobs, how to build a house yourself | Exclude with negatives |

Google's [keyword match types help page](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7478529) describes three types. Exact match, written in square brackets, gives "the most control over who sees your ad" but reaches the fewest searches. Phrase match, written in quotes, reaches more searches than exact but fewer than broad. Broad match is the default and can show on searches that do not share the keyword's direct meaning, which Google pairs with Smart Bidding.

For a new builder campaign with little data, start with phrase and exact match. Add broad match later, only once conversions are tracked and you review the search terms weekly.

Negative keywords, per the same page, exclude your ads from searches containing those terms. Start with plans, floor plans, jobs, salary, kits, diy and free. More on this in our guide to [negative keywords](/blog/google-ads-negative-keywords-stop-wasting-budget).

## How Much Can a Lead Cost Before the Campaign Loses Money?

Start from what a project is worth, not from the click price. Every input below is an **assumption**: Google publishes no builder CPC, and your gross profit, close rate and lead quality are yours.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget (assumption) | $5,000 | $5,000 | $5,000 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | $14 | $10 | $7 |
| Clicks | 357 | 500 | 714 |
| Click to consultation request (assumption) | 2% | 3% | 4% |
| Consultation requests | 7 | 15 | 29 |
| Cost per request | $714 | $333 | $172 |
| Request to signed contract (assumption) | 5% | 10% | 15% |
| Signed contracts per month | 0.4 | 1.5 | 4.3 |
| Gross profit per home (assumption) | $60,000 | $60,000 | $60,000 |
| Ad cost per signed contract | $14,286 | $3,333 | $1,163 |

The break-even cost per request is gross profit per home x close rate. In the Base case that is $60,000 x 10% = $6,000, far above the $333 the campaign pays. In the Tough case it is $60,000 x 5% = $3,000, still above $714. That is why builders can afford leads that look expensive for other trades.

Two cautions. First, the sale takes months, so one month of results says little; track consultations and proposals as well as contracts. Second, a 5% close rate in the table means about 1 contract per 20 requests. If your real close rate is lower or your margin is thinner, rerun the table with your own figures before you raise budget.

## Is Google Local Services Ads Available to Home Builders?

Possibly not under that name. Google's [Local Services Ads category list](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/6224841) lists General contractor, Kitchen remodeling, Bathroom remodeling, Foundations services and others, but no "home builder" category. The list also says availability varies by country and that some categories are limited to certain US states. Check your own eligibility inside the Local Services Ads sign-up flow before you plan around it.

If you qualify, the pricing model differs from search ads. Google's [help page on how leads work](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/7195435) says you pay for valid leads, not clicks. Invalid or low-quality leads at first contact are not charged, and a missed call is charged only if the caller stays on the line for more than 20 seconds during business hours. You set an average weekly budget and the monthly maximum equals that figure multiplied by the average number of weeks in a month.

Google also notes that new advertisers must complete its [screening and verification process](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/7549288) to receive the badge. Our [Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-services-ads-complete-guide) covers setup in more detail.

## How Should You Set Up the Account?

1. Link your Google Business Profile so your address, reviews and phone number can appear with ads.
2. Set up conversion tracking for form submissions and phone calls. Track them as separate actions, so you can see which keywords bring calls.
3. Limit location targeting to the area you actually build in. Check the locations report after two weeks and exclude places that spend without leads.
4. Build a Search campaign first. Add remarketing or other campaign types only after Search is tracked and profitable.
5. Send each ad group to a matching page, not your homepage. A page for "build on my lot" should talk about lot assessment and permits, not only finished homes.

If you also take smaller jobs, see how [Google Ads for contractors](/blog/google-ads-for-contractors) differs: those searchers want a quote this week, while custom home buyers research for months.

## What Should the Landing Page Contain?

The page has one job: a booked consultation. Include a headline that repeats the search, a click-to-call number, a short form (name, phone, lot status, rough timeline), photos of finished homes, your licence details and a few reviews with real names and permission.

Be honest about price. Many buyers want a starting range. If you will not publish one, say what drives cost (lot, size, finishes) and how fast you can give a ballpark. No published conversion rate exists for builder pages, so compare your own results before and after any change.

See [landing page best practices](/blog/landing-page-best-practices) for layout checks.

## How Do You Know Which Keywords Produce Signed Projects?

Ads report leads, not contracts. Keep a simple log: lead source and keyword if known, consultation held (yes or no), proposal sent, contract signed, and contract value. Review it monthly with your search terms report.

If you can, upload offline outcomes such as "consultation held" back into Google Ads, so bidding learns from real outcomes. That is a feature you configure in the account, so check Google Ads Help for the current steps in your country.

## What Mistakes Waste Builder Budgets?

- Bidding on "house plans" and similar terms that attract people who will never hire you.
- Targeting a whole state when you only build within a drive of your office.
- Judging a campaign after two weeks when the sales cycle is months.
- Counting clicks or form fills instead of consultations held.
- Sending all traffic to the homepage.

## FAQ

### Do Google Ads work for custom home builders?
They can, particularly Search ads that catch people looking for a builder in your area. The test is cost per signed contract, not cost per click, and it takes months of data to read.

### How much should a custom home builder spend on Google Ads?
Google does not publish a figure. Use the break-even table above: take gross profit per home, multiply by your close rate, and make sure your cost per consultation request stays well under that number. Check Keyword Planner for estimated click costs in your area.

### Are Local Services Ads available for home builders?
Google's category list shows General contractor but no separate home builder category, and availability varies by country. Check eligibility in your account.

### Which match type is best for builder keywords?
Start with phrase and exact match for control. Move to broad match only with conversion tracking and weekly search term reviews.

### How long until Google Ads produces custom home leads?
Clicks start immediately. Judge lead quality after several weeks and signed contracts after a full sales cycle, which for custom homes can be many months.
