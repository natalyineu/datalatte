For most single-location local businesses, Netflix ads are not a practical first channel. Netflix says its ad plan reaches more than 250 million global monthly active viewers (May 2026), but it publishes no rate card, no CPM and no minimum budget. Pricing is quoted through its sales team or buying partners, so you cannot plan a small test from public numbers.

This guide covers what Netflix says it sells, how you can buy it, what is not published and what to try instead. Every figure is from a source at the end. Older versions of this page quoted CPMs and a self-serve minimum that I could not verify, so they are removed.

## What Do Netflix Ads Offer Advertisers?

Netflix calls its platform the Netflix Ads Suite. At its May 2026 upfront it listed planning tools (an Audience Insights API and a Reach Curve API), AI-assisted media planning, pause ads and vertical video ads on mobile. It also said live and pause ads would be sold programmatically, in the US and Canada first.

Netflix also reports that programmatic audience targeting through Amazon DSP would reach all ad-supported countries by June 1, with Yahoo DSP to follow. Those are announcements aimed at agencies and large buyers. None of the pages I opened describe a postcode or radius option for a corner shop.

## How Much Do Netflix Ads Cost?

Netflix does not publish it. The upfront announcement gives no prices, minimum spends or commitments. The ad-tech vendor Adwave (secondary source) says Netflix's direct and upfront deals are built for large national brands and that small businesses generally cannot buy Netflix directly. It gives no Netflix CPM; its only figure is a general CTV average of roughly $15 to $35.

If you see a blog quoting an exact Netflix CPM or a $2,500 self-serve minimum, ask for the source. I could not find one on Netflix's own pages. For real numbers, contact Netflix advertising sales or a partner agency, and ask for the CPM, minimum, geographic options and creative specs in writing.

## Can You Target Customers Near Your Shop?

Not from anything Netflix has published. The targeting Netflix describes is audience based: demographics, interests, in-market segments and first-party data, plus partner DSP data. The pages I read do not state whether you can limit delivery to a city or ZIP code. Ask before you commit.

Lack of fine local targeting matters for a business whose customers live within a few miles. Impressions outside your service area are paid for and wasted. Channels such as [Google Ads](/services/google-ads) or Nextdoor, covered in our [Nextdoor ads cost guide](/blog/nextdoor-ads-cost-2026), let you narrow by place.

## What Is the Audience Like?

Netflix reports that 44% of members who see an ad on Netflix never saw it on broadcast TV or other streamers, and that purchase intent is 23% above benchmarks versus competitors. Both are Netflix's own claims from its upfront, with no methodology shown. Netflix did not define "monthly active viewers" on that page.

Netflix cited a different, earlier total in 2025, so do not compare it with the 2026 figure to claim growth.

## Worked Example: Why a Small Test Does Not Add Up

All inputs are **assumptions** for illustration, since Netflix publishes no CPM.

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $5,000 |
| CPM (assumption only) | $30 |
| Impressions | about 166,000 |
| Share of impressions in your service area (assumption) | 5% |
| Local impressions | about 8,300 |
| Effective cost per local impression | $0.60 |

With a 5% local share, the effective local CPM is $600, which is twenty times the assumed headline CPM. Change the share and CPM to your quote, and the math tells you whether to proceed. If the sales team can geo-limit delivery, the share rises and the picture changes.

## FAQ

### Can a small business buy Netflix ads?
Yes in principle, through Netflix sales, partner agencies or programmatic DSPs. Netflix has not published a self-serve minimum, so ask for a quote.

### Do Netflix ads work for local businesses?
Netflix's own results are brand-level, such as purchase intent and reach. I found no published local-business case, so I cannot say what to expect.

### What does the Netflix ad plan cost viewers?
Netflix's ads-plan page lists $8.99 per month in the United States. Check Netflix for the current price.

### Is Netflix better than Hulu or Roku for local ads?
Roku and Hulu publish more about self-serve buying; see the guides above. Netflix gives little public detail for small advertisers.
