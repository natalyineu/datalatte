## Do Garage Door Companies Need Verification to Run Google Ads?

Yes, in the United States. Google's policy on other restricted businesses says ads for locksmith and garage door repair services are restricted, and that to advertise you must apply for advanced verification. Google's help page on the process asks for your business details and Google Ads customer ID, professional license details where the law requires a license, and a video call held at your business location with official documents nearby.

Google does not publish how long this takes, so start the application before you plan launch dates. Outside the US, the policy page names locksmith services in Canada, not garage doors.

## What Does a Click Cost for Garage Door Services?

LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks list Home and Home Improvement at $8.33 per click, 6.47% click-through, 8.05% conversion and $90.92 per lead. The all-industry average is $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead. Google does not publish a garage door category, so your own account is the real benchmark after a month of data.

Actual prices depend on your city and the keyword. Emergency terms such as a broken spring usually attract more bidders than installation research, but no source publishes garage door CPCs by keyword. Do not trust a vendor who quotes one without showing an account.

## Should You Use Search Ads or Local Services Ads?

Both are worth testing. Google's Local Services Ads help lists garage door services among supported categories in the US and says you pay for leads, not impressions. It also says pre-badge ads, which let you receive leads while screening finishes, are not available for garage door services. Expect to complete screening before leads arrive.

Search campaigns give you control of keywords, ad text and location. Local Services Ads put you in a separate unit above standard results with a Google Verified badge. Google does not state the per-lead price on its help page, so check the quote in your own account area before you budget.

## How Do You Set Up the Search Campaign?

Start narrow and keep the structure simple:

- **Repair campaign:** terms about broken springs, off-track doors and openers that stopped working, with a call button as the main action.
- **Install campaign:** replacement and new door terms, with a quote form.
- **Locations:** use radius targeting around the area you can reach in a few hours. Google says a radius must be at least 1 km, and a very small one may show intermittently.
- **Schedule:** Google lets you set an ad schedule with up to six time windows a day, based on the account time zone. Run it when someone can answer the phone.

Then build a negative keyword list from the Search terms report each week. ## How Do You Track Phone Calls?

Calls are the main conversion for urgent repair. Google says clicks on a call asset on mobile are billed like headline clicks. For conversion tracking, you set a minimum call length, and every call at least that long counts. Turn on call reporting so Google forwarding numbers are used.

Choose a length that matches a real job enquiry, not a wrong number. Check the Call details report afterward and adjust. For website actions such as form sends, a tag manager helps; see our [Google Tag Manager setup](/blog/google-tag-manager-for-small-business).

## FAQ

### How much should a garage door company spend on Google Ads?

There is no standard figure. At LocaliQ's $8.33 benchmark click for home improvement, $1,500 buys roughly 180 clicks. Start with an amount you can sustain for 2 to 3 months and review cost per booked job. More on budgeting is in our [Google Ads cost breakdown](/blog/how-much-does-google-ads-cost-for-small-businesses-real-2026-pricing-breakdown).

### Why is my garage door ad not running?

If you are in the US and have not passed advanced verification, ads for garage door repair are restricted. Check the policy status in your account and apply through Google's request form.

### Are Local Services Ads better than Search ads?

Not necessarily. Local Services Ads charge per lead and add a verified badge, but need screening first. Search ads give more control. Test both and compare cost per booked job.

### Do I need to track calls?

Yes. Most urgent repair customers call. Enable call reporting and set a minimum call length that fits a real enquiry.

### Can I advertise only in business hours?

Yes. Use an ad schedule so ads show when someone can answer. Google allows up to six windows per day.
