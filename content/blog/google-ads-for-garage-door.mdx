## Can a Garage Door Company Use Local Services Ads?

Yes, if your service area and category are supported. Google's [Local Services Ads help page for the US](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/6224841?hl=en&co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DUS) states that pre-badge ads are not available for garage door services. In practice, that means you should expect the screening step to finish before any leads arrive. Check the current requirements in your own account before you commit a budget.

Google's [comparison article for small businesses](https://business.google.com/us/resources/articles/small-business-marketing/) describes Local Services Ads as designed to be less hands-on, with minimal management after setup. Google Ads requires more active management. For a one-person repair business that cannot watch an account daily, that difference matters more than the headline cost. The [pet groomer Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-services-ads-for-pet-groomers) shows the same lead-based setup in another service category, and the [complete Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-services-ads-complete-guide) covers screening in more detail.

## What Does a Garage Door Lead Cost?

Vendor numbers vary a lot. Built-Right Digital says that across its home services clients the average cost per lead is under $30, though high-ticket window jobs can run higher. That is the vendor's own claim, and it covers a mix of trades rather than garage doors specifically. Admanage's benchmark table puts the average cost per lead for Search Ads in Home Services at $90.92, updated February 2026.

A garage door operator quoted on a [Pipelineon contractor ad examples page](https://pipelineon.com/blog/high-converting-facebook-ad-examples/) reported a $12 cost per call with a 68% answer rate for one Facebook ad pattern, compared with $42 per lead on lead forms. That is a single self-reported result, not a benchmark. Use it to test call-focused ads, not to forecast your budget.

## How Much Budget Should You Start With?

Homeservicedirect's [Google Ads guide for window companies](https://www.homeservicedirect.net/google-ads-ppc-guide-window-replacement/) says a workable starting budget for most local window and door companies is around $2,500 to $5,000 a month. Garage doors are close enough to use as a starting range, but not as a promise. Pick the lower end if your service area is small and you can handle the calls yourself.

Management costs are separate from ad spend. Built-Right Digital lists its Google Ads management for window and door companies at a flat $999 a month, with ad spend paid to Google directly. Compare that fee against what you would otherwise spend on in-house time before deciding who manages the account.

## How Should the Search Campaign Be Structured?

Split repair and installation into separate campaigns so budgets and messages do not compete. Repair terms, such as broken springs or openers that stopped working, should lead to a call. Installation terms should lead to a quote form. Keep both campaigns tied to a service radius you can actually reach in a day.

Review the search terms report every week. Built-Right Digital says it updates its negative keyword list weekly and sets up lists before campaigns launch to block cheap wholesale materials and DIY repair tutorial searches. Those are vendor practices, but the principle holds: a garage door company does not want clicks from people looking to fix their own spring. Build the negative list from real search terms, not guesses.

## FAQ

### How much should a garage door company spend on Google Ads?

Homeservicedirect states a workable starting budget for most local window and door companies is around $2,500 to $5,000 a month. Start in that range only if you can handle the calls, and track cost per booked job from the first month.

### Why would a garage door ad not run?

Check your account's policy status and the Local Services Ads help page for your country. Google's US help page says pre-badge ads are not available for garage door services, so screening may need to finish before leads arrive.

### Are Local Services Ads better than Search ads?

Not in every case. Google describes Local Services Ads as less hands-on, with minimal management after setup. Search ads give more control over keywords and ad text. Test both and compare cost per booked job, not cost per lead alone.

### Do I need call tracking?

For urgent repair, usually yes. Calls often carry the most intent. A free option such as Google My Business Insights may cover the basics, while paid tools add detail across campaigns.

### Is one cost-per-lead figure enough to plan a budget?

No. The sources give a vendor claim under $30 and a benchmark of $90.92 for home services Search ads. Your own account data, filtered to garage door keywords, will be more reliable than either figure.
