## Do Instagram Stories ads work for fitness studios?

They can, mainly because the cost per lead on Meta is far below Google search for the same category. [LocaliQ's 2026 Facebook advertising benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/facebook-advertising-benchmarks/) list a $27.11 cost per lead and a 7.98% conversion rate for Health and Fitness leads campaigns. [LocaliQ's search benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/) show $67.36 per lead for the same category on Google, with a 6.94% conversion rate.

Two cautions. These figures cover Facebook and Instagram lead campaigns in general, not Stories alone. And a lead is a form fill, not a member, so what counts is how many leads show up to the class and join.

## Stories ads or Google search: which should a studio start with?

| Question | Google search ads | Instagram Stories ads |
|---|---|---|
| Who sees it? | People searching "yoga near me" | People who fit your audience, whether or not they are looking |
| Benchmark cost per lead | $67.36 | $27.11 (Meta leads campaigns) |
| Best for | Capturing existing demand | Creating demand with an offer |
| Needs | Landing page, call tracking | Short vertical video, offer, booking link |

A practical order: if you have no reviews and an incomplete profile, fix your Google Business Profile first. Then run Stories ads for a first-class offer while search ads capture people ready to book. ## Worked example: what does $600 a month produce?

This is an assumption-based example using the benchmark above. It is not a result from a real studio.

1. Budget: $600 a month at $27.11 per lead is about 22 leads.
2. Assume 40% of leads attend the first class: about 9 visits.
3. Assume 40% of those join: about 3 new members.
4. Assume each member pays $99 a month and stays 6 months: about $1,780 in revenue.

Ad cost per new member is then about $200 ($600 / 3). If your attendance or join rate is half the assumed rate, the cost doubles to about $400 and the campaign barely breaks even. Measure attendance and joins from week one, because leads alone will mislead you.

## What should the ad say?

- **One offer:** "Free first class" or "2 classes for $20" beats "come visit us".
- **One place:** name the neighborhood in the first line.
- **One action:** a booking link or lead form that works in two taps.
- **Real footage:** a 10 to 15 second vertical clip of an actual class, shot on a phone, with captions because many people watch without sound.

Stories are full-screen vertical (9:16), and Meta's preview in Ads Manager shows which parts of the frame are covered by buttons and profile icons, so keep captions and logos inside the visible area. Instagram replaced the old "swipe up" link with link stickers in 2021, [as reported by PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2021/08/24/instagram-is-replacing-swipe-up-links-in-stories-with-link-stickers), and paid Stories ads use a call-to-action button you choose in Ads Manager.

## How do I set up a Stories ad in five steps?

1. Connect your Instagram account and Facebook Page in Meta Business Suite and install the Meta pixel on your booking page.
2. Choose a leads or sales objective, not "boost post".
3. Target a radius around the studio (start small, around 3 miles in a city) and an age range matching your members.
4. Select placements, keep Instagram Stories, and upload a vertical video.
5. Set a daily budget, run for at least 7 to 14 days without edits, then compare cost per attended class.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum budget for a Stories ad test?

There is no official minimum that guarantees results. A practical test is enough budget to collect several dozen leads, so divide that by your expected cost per lead.

### How long should I run an ad before judging it?

Run it long enough to gather leads and attendance data, usually 7 to 14 days. Changing the ad every day resets Meta's learning.

### Should I use TikTok instead?

For a studio that needs members within a few miles, Instagram's location targeting is simpler to manage. Test TikTok only after Instagram is producing members.

### Can I run the ads myself?

Yes. Keep one audience, one offer and one booking link, then change one variable at a time.

### How do I know a member came from the ad?

Use UTM links and ask every new member how they heard about you. Record both in a spreadsheet.
