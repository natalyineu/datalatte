## Who Can You Reach Online in Madagascar?

Before buying any ads, check current national figures for internet use, mobile use and social media accounts in a recent published digital report. The sources reviewed for this article did not contain those figures, so none are quoted here. Treat any number you find as a starting point rather than a guarantee, because account counts and unique users are different measures.

Internet access also varies between towns and rural areas. A shop in a capital city and a stall in a rural village may not reach the same people online. Plan the mix of channels around where customers actually live and buy, and check that assumption with a few direct conversations before committing money.

## Which Channels Fit a Small Madagascar Business?

The SEED Madagascar example points to community events and direct promotion as a way to build demand for new products. Its project sells mason-built sanitary platforms through local artisans and treats community members as customers who make purchasing decisions for their own homes. The lesson for a local business is that trust often comes from people seeing a product, hearing about it from others and asking questions in person.

Combine that with a simple online presence: a complete business listing, a phone or messaging contact that someone answers quickly, and one paid test channel. Add a new channel only after the first one produces enquiries you can count. Channels that generate no countable enquiries after a fair test should be dropped, whatever their popularity elsewhere.

## Where Should You Target Your Ads?

Fieldwork Africa, a market research company, describes field teams that coordinate from Antananarivo and cover Toamasina, Fianarantsoa, Mahajanga and Antsiranana across Madagascar's regions. That spread is a useful reminder that a single national audience is rarely the right unit for a local business.

Set up campaigns by town and radius rather than by country. Start with the one or two towns where existing customers already live. Keep the first test small, so that a weak audience wastes only a limited budget. If you are comparing methods across neighbouring markets, the [Angola guide to local marketing](/blog/local-marketing-angola-small-business-2026) and the [Botswana guide](/blog/local-marketing-botswana-small-business-2026) use the same budget-first approach.

## How Do You Size a Test Budget?

The table below uses inputs that are all assumptions for an imaginary café. They are not Malagasy benchmarks, so replace them with your own numbers before deciding anything.

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly test budget (assumption) | MGA 200,000 |
| Average order (assumption) | MGA 15,000 |
| Gross margin (assumption) | 40% |
| Share of enquiries that become orders (assumption) | 30% |
| Gross profit per order | MGA 6,000 (15,000 × 40%) |
| Orders needed to cover the budget | about 33 (200,000 ÷ 6,000) |
| Enquiries needed | about 111 (33 ÷ 30%) |
| Maximum cost per enquiry | about MGA 1,800 (200,000 ÷ 111) |

If ads bring enquiries that cost more than the maximum, change the offer or the audience before raising the budget. The same method works in other markets, including [Algeria](/blog/local-marketing-algeria-small-business-2026): start from margin, not from what the platform suggests.

## What Steps Should a Business Take First?

1. Write down the average order value and gross margin, using real till or invoice data.
2. Decide the monthly amount you can lose on a test without harming cash flow.
3. Divide the budget by gross profit per order to find the orders needed, then by your expected order rate to find the enquiries needed.
4. Choose one or two towns and set a radius around each.
5. Run the test for a fixed period, then compare actual cost per enquiry with the maximum.
6. Keep, change or stop the channel based on that comparison.

## Can You Pay for Ads From Madagascar?

The sources reviewed did not confirm which billing countries, currencies or payment methods Google or Meta accept for Madagascar accounts. Do not assume. Open ad account settings before planning a budget, and check which currency the account charges in, since it may differ from the local currency.

Check whether the payment card works, and what exchange-rate fees apply. A test that fails at checkout costs time and can distort planning, so confirm the payment setup with a small first charge before committing the full test budget.

## What Data Rules Should You Check?

The sources reviewed did not include a verified summary of Madagascar's data protection rules, so this article gives no legal position. Before collecting customer phone numbers or email addresses for marketing, a lawyer in Madagascar should confirm the current rules and how they apply to the business.

Until that advice is available, collect only what is needed for a booking or order, and keep a record of how each customer agreed to hear from the business. That habit is useful whatever the rules turn out to be.
