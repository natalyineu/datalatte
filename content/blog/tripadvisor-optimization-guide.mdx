## How Does Tripadvisor's Popularity Ranking Work?

Tripadvisor ranks businesses against others in the same area. Trade coverage of its algorithm update (summarized by [eHotelier](https://insights.ehotelier.com/?p=21941)) quotes three factors:

- **Quality:** bubble ratings from travellers still drive ranking. More 4 and 5 bubble reviews rank higher, all else equal.
- **Quantity:** you need enough reviews for a statistically meaningful comparison, not more than every rival.
- **Recency:** Tripadvisor believes recent reviews are more valuable to travellers than older ones.

That article describes a 2016 change for restaurants, attractions and hotels, so treat the detail as background. Tripadvisor has not published the weight of each factor, and claims that recency is worth a given percentage are guesses.

What it means in practice: 200 reviews from three years ago can lose to a smaller set from this season. Aim for a steady flow, not a one-time push.

## How Do You Claim and Complete Your Listing?

Claiming gives you access to the Management Center, where owners can respond to reviews and, per third-party guides, send review requests. Do these in order:

1. Claim the listing with the business email you control.
2. Make name, address, phone and hours identical to your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).
3. Add current photos of the dining room, menu items and exterior.
4. Add your menu or room details, and keep prices and cuisine tags accurate.
5. Link your website and booking page.

## How Should You Respond to Tripadvisor Reviews?

Use the Management Response feature. According to Tripadvisor's [Management Response guidelines](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Trust-lpgT1CmsfQ3E.html), as shown in search results, replies must be:

- **Original and relevant:** no copied guest correspondence or off-topic content.
- **Non-commercial:** no promotions, and offers of gifts, services or money get removed.
- **Civil and private:** do not threaten the reviewer, accuse them of fraud, or guess their identity or share personal details.

Write replies in 2 to 4 sentences. Thank the guest, name one specific detail, and for a complaint state what you changed. Do not offer a free meal in the reply, because the guidelines treat offers as promotional.

## How Many Reviews Do You Need, and What Does Steady Look Like?

Tripadvisor publishes no minimum count. Use a rate instead.

Worked example, labelled as an assumption: a restaurant seats 1,200 guests a month, and 2% leave a Tripadvisor review. That is 24 new reviews a month, or about 288 a year. If you ask every table with a QR code on the receipt and lift the rate to 3%, you get 36 a month. Replace these percentages with your own count from the Management Center.

Decision rule: if your newest review is more than 60 days old, fix your ask process before changing anything else on the listing.

## What Do Fake Reviews Cost You?

More than before. CNBC's coverage of the [2025 Transparency Report](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/business/money-report/heres-how-many-fake-reviews-tripadvisor-found-on-its-website-in-2024/6277840/) reports that about 8% of the 31.1 million reviews submitted in 2024 were fake, roughly 2.7 million. Review boosting accounted for 54% of them, and Tripadvisor removed over 200,000 reviews it suspected were AI-written.

Penalties reported by CNBC: offending properties have their rankings penalized for a year, and repeat offenders get a red badge saying the property is trying to deceive travellers. Do not buy reviews or reward guests for positive ones. [PhocusWire's report](https://www.phocuswire.com/tripadvisor-fraudulent-reviews-2145480036) on the same data was also checked in search.

## What Are Travellers' Choice Awards?

Tripadvisor's [Trust and Safety page](https://www.tripadvisor.in/Trust-lAkEadpFVLyU.html) states, per search results, that winners are determined from the quality and quantity of traveller reviews and ratings over a 12-month period. Eligibility reported there includes being listed for at least 12 months, reaching a review threshold, and keeping a minimum bubble rating. The exact review count is not published.

Winners sit in the top 10% of listings, and Best of the Best are the top 1%.

Do not promise an award in marketing. Focus on the inputs you control: review rate and rating over the year.

## FAQ

**Q: What affects Tripadvisor ranking?**
A: Tripadvisor says its Popularity Ranking uses the quality, quantity and recency of traveller reviews. It has not published how much each counts.

**Q: Can I buy Tripadvisor reviews?**
A: No. Reported penalties include a ranking penalty for a year and a red warning badge for repeat offenders, and about 8% of 2024 submissions were fake.

**Q: Should I reply to every review?**
A: Reply to reviews you can answer helpfully, positive and negative, and follow the Management Response guidelines. Tripadvisor does not publish a response-rate ranking weight.

**Q: How long until my ranking improves?**
A: There is no published timeline. Because recent reviews carry more weight, expect movement only after several weeks of new reviews.

**Q: Tripadvisor or Google first?**
A: For a restaurant or hotel with tourist demand, run both from the same data. Keep details identical on each.
