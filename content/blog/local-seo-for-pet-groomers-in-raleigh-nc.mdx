A pet groomer in Raleigh, NC earns a place in the Google Maps local pack by keeping its Google Business Profile complete, accurate and active, then matching that information across the web. The local pack is the top three business listings in local search results ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-audit-checklist-15-things-to-fix-today), secondary), and those three listings are pulled directly from their Google Business Profile (GBP) records ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-my-lawn-care-business), secondary). This guide covers the profile checklist, citation rules, reviews, a worked timeline and how to measure progress.

## How Does the Google Business Profile Feed the Map Pack?

Google Business Profile is a free tool that lets a business manage its presence across Google Search and Maps ([Google](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/), primary). Because the map pack draws on those listings, the profile is the starting point for any Raleigh groomer.

Third-party analysis points to the profile as a major ranking input. Adwave reports that GBP signals represented 32% of local pack ranking weight in 2025 ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/multi-location-local-seo-audit), secondary). In a BrightLocal survey, 36% of SEOs named the profile the most important map pack factor in 2021 ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/seo/local-seo), secondary).

Category choice is where many groomers start. Adwave recommends reviewing the top three to five businesses in local map results for a service search to see which categories they use ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones), secondary). For more on this step, see [Google Business Profile for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-for-pet-groomers).

## What Should the Profile Contain Before You Compete?

Hours, phone number and address must be accurate. The vendor guidance on this point is direct: make sure hours, phone number and address are 100% accurate ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary). A mismatch between the profile and your other listings causes confusion for both customers and search systems.

The business description allows 750 characters ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary). Lead with the grooming services and the Raleigh neighbourhood you serve. Keep the wording natural rather than stuffed with repeated keywords. A deeper walkthrough is in [Google Business Profile optimization for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-for-pet-groomers).

Adwave recommends at least 15 high-quality photos, saying a robust gallery gets far more clicks and calls ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary). Posts also matter. Google reviews each post, and only posts with the Live status show publicly on Search and Maps ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en), primary). Adwave advises at least one new post a week ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary).

Mobile groomers face a specific choice. A business that works from home or serves customers at their location can hide its street address and is then designated a Service Area Business ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).

## Why Does Citation Consistency Matter in Raleigh?

Citations are mentions of your name, address and phone number on other websites. Adwave states that the NAP must be exactly the same on your website, your GBP and major directories such as Yelp, Apple Maps and Bing Places ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/voice-search-optimization-for-local-businesses), secondary).

Consistency includes small details. The guidance is that your NAP on the website must match the GBP listing exactly, including punctuation and whether you write "St." or "Street" ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary). A street address written two ways is a mismatch.

Before you add new listings, fix the ones you already have. Audit existing citations first, because new listings that repeat errors spread the problem. Apple Maps is one of the directories to check, and Apple Business Connect reports Profile Views, meaning how many people saw your business card in Apple Maps ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/apple-business-connect-the-google-business-profile-alternative-youre-ignoring), secondary).

## How Many Reviews Does a Pet Groomer Need?

An analysis by Blogging Wizard of two million GBP pages found that businesses with over 200 Google reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business), secondary). Another article claims that businesses with over 50 reviews and a 4.7+ average rating dominate top map pack spots, but it cites no source ([Accountability Now](https://accountabilitynow.net/ai-google-business-profile-ranking-for-landscapers/), secondary). Treat both as benchmarks rather than guarantees.

Google shows reviews next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122), primary). That means reviews work as both ranking input and a conversion tool at the moment a customer compares groomers.

Ask for reviews consistently after each appointment. Mentioning specific services in review text, such as mobile grooming or a particular breed, gives customers a natural way to describe what they booked. Respond to reviews as well, since your replies are public.

## Worked Example: How Long to Reach 200 Reviews?

The timeline below uses assumption-labelled inputs for a hypothetical Raleigh groomer. Replace them with your own numbers.

Inputs (assumption): current reviews 60, goal 200 (the threshold from the Blogging Wizard analysis), new reviews per week 3.

Gap: 200 minus 60 equals 140 reviews.

At 3 per week (assumption): 140 divided by 3 is about 46.7, so roughly 47 weeks, or about 11 months.

At 6 per week (assumption, double the pace): 140 divided by 6 is about 23.3, so roughly 24 weeks, or about 6 months.

## Should Paid Ads Sit Alongside the Map Pack?

Paid and organic work can run side by side. Google Ads location assets pull data directly from Google Maps and GBP, and corrections made there flow into linked Google Ads accounts ([Google](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en), primary). Fixing the profile therefore helps both channels.

A Marketing 360 case study about a mobile pet groomer describes choosing Facebook and Google as advertising channels in 2023 ([Marketing 360](https://blog.marketing360.com/case-studies/pet-groomer-marketing-case-study-driving-success-with-digital-ads/), secondary). The case study is a vendor example, so check it against your own budget. For a channel comparison, read [Google Ads vs Facebook Ads for local business](/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-local-business-which-wins).

## How Do You Track Whether the Map Pack Is Working?

Adwave lists six useful local search metrics: GBP impressions, map-pack views, clicks to call, direction requests, website visits from GBP, and branded search lift over time ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-search-intent), secondary). Record these monthly, starting before you change anything, so you have a baseline.

Pair the GBP numbers with your own search tools. Check your rank for your top keywords manually in private browsing, and note the date of each check. Changes in the profile take time to show up in rankings, so compare months rather than days.

Google's AI answers also draw on profile information. Adwave, citing Accrisoft's summary, says responses in Google's AI-driven answering are sourced from business profile data, website content and reviews ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-q-and-a), secondary). Read more in [AI for Google Business Profile optimization](/blog/ai-for-google-business-profile-optimization).
