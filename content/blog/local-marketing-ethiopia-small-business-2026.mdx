**What is the best way to market a small business in Ethiopia?** Start where the connected customers are: Addis Ababa and other cities. Use a Facebook page and local groups for discovery, a Telegram channel for opted-in followers, a complete Google Maps listing, and Telebirr for payment. Only 21.7% of Ethiopians were online in late 2025, so urban businesses get the most from digital channels.

This guide is for owners of cafes, salons, gyms and similar local businesses. Every number comes from a source listed at the end. Budget examples are labelled as assumptions. Telegram has no audience figure in the DataReportal report, so this article makes no claim about its size.

## How Big Is Ethiopia's Online Audience?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 136 million people, 29.5 million internet users and 93.2 million cellular connections (68.4% of the population). Many connections do not mean mobile internet use, as the report itself warns. The population is 75.7% rural and 24.3% urban, with a median age of 19.1.

Facebook's ad tools show 9.80 million reachable users, Instagram 1.55 million and LinkedIn 1.80 million. These are advertising audience estimates, not active users. For a local business the lesson is simple: the reachable audience is real but small, and it sits mostly in cities. Median fixed download speed was 9.37 Mbps, so keep images light and pages simple.

## Which Channels Should a Local Business Use First?

Pick two or three and run them well.

| Channel | Role for a local business |
|---|---|
| Google Maps and Search | People searching for a place near them |
| Facebook page, groups, Marketplace | Discovery and community selling |
| Telegram channel | Direct updates to followers who opted in |
| Instagram | Younger and international audiences in areas like Bole (small reach: 1.55 million) |
| WhatsApp and Messenger | Bookings and questions |

Telegram is widely used by Ethiopian businesses for promotions, but no figure for it appears in the DataReportal report, so treat its reach as something to measure yourself: count channel subscribers and replies. Telegram's own terms say sponsored messages run only in certain countries and in channels with over 1,000 subscribers, so check whether paid options exist for you before planning around them.

## How Do You Get Found on Google in Addis Ababa?

Claim and complete your Google Business Profile: correct name, category, hours, phone number and photos. Customers search by area, so mention the district in your description and services. Typical Addis Ababa areas are Bole, Kazanchis, Piassa, Megenagna, CMC and Sarbet.

Examples of local search phrases to cover: "hair salon Bole", "cafe Kazanchis", "gym CMC". There are no reliable published cost-per-click benchmarks for Ethiopia that this article could open, so start with a small test budget, read Google's Keyword Planner for your terms, and track cost per call or booking. See our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) for the full setup.

## How Should You Use Telegram?

Treat the channel as a notice board for people who already know you.

1. Create a channel and put the link on your Facebook page, Google profile, receipts and shop window.
2. Post in Amharic a few times a week: offers, new items, opening hours, photos.
3. Add a group only if you can answer questions daily.
4. Track which posts lead to calls, messages or visits.

Ask customers how they found you at the counter. A simple tally on paper is enough to compare Telegram, Facebook and Google.

## Why Does Telebirr Matter for Local Sales?

Paying should be easy. Ethio Telecom reported about 58.61 million Telebirr users in January 2026, with a later report citing 60.59 million for the 2025/26 fiscal year. The second number comes from a single secondary source, so treat it as approximate. Display your Telebirr merchant code at the till and on your Facebook page. Keep cash and bank transfer as options, since not every customer uses the wallet.

## What Does a Starter Budget Look Like?

All inputs below are **assumptions** for planning, not benchmarks. Suppose a salon sets aside 20,000 ETB a month.

| Item | Share (assumption) | ETB |
|---|---|---|
| Google Ads test | 40% | 8,000 |
| Facebook or Instagram boosted posts | 35% | 7,000 |
| Content (photos, Telegram posts) | 25% | 5,000 |

Run it for two or three months. Record calls, Messenger bookings and walk-ins per channel, then move money toward the channel with the lowest cost per booking. Convert prices at the current exchange rate, since the birr moves.

## When Are the Busy Seasons?

Ethiopia uses its own calendar. Enkutatash (New Year) falls on September 11, Meskel on September 27, Timkat on January 19-20, and Ethiopian Easter (Fasika) in April or May. Salons, restaurants and gift sellers often plan promotions before holidays. Check dates each year, as Easter moves.

## FAQ

### What language should I use for Ethiopian digital marketing?
Amharic for the broad local audience and English for expat, diplomatic and business customers, for example in Bole. Some businesses add Oromiffa for Oromo communities.

### Is Telegram or Facebook better for a local business?
They do different jobs. Facebook helps new customers find you through pages and groups. Telegram keeps people who already follow you informed. Start with Facebook and Google, and add Telegram once you have customers to invite.

### Is digital marketing practical with limited internet access?
Yes for urban businesses. With 21.7% internet penetration and a 75.7% rural population, online channels work best for city customers who search on phones. Rural customers are mostly outside their reach.

### How much should I spend?
Start with an amount you can lose while testing for two or three months, then track calls and bookings. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) explains how to set a share of revenue.

### Do I need a website?
Not on day one. A complete Google profile, a Facebook page and a phone number come first. A simple website helps later.
