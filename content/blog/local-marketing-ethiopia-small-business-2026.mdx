## Why Start with Local Search Instead of Broad Advertising?

Most customers of a cafe, salon or gym choose between a few places within walking or taxi distance. Local search is therefore the most direct channel. A person who types a service and an area name is close to booking, so the listing they see matters more than the number of followers a business has.

Broad campaigns reach people who may never visit. For a shop with one location, spending on reach before the listing is complete usually wastes money. Fix the basics first: name, hours, phone number, photos and services. Then test paid options with a small, fixed budget and compare the results after a set period.

## Which Channels Should a Small Business Use First?

Pick two or three channels and run them well rather than spreading a small budget across five.

| Channel | Role for a local business | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Google Maps and Search | Customers searching for a service near them | Low once set up |
| Facebook page and groups | Discovery, photos, community trust | Medium |
| Telegram channel | Updates for people who chose to follow | Medium |
| WhatsApp or Messenger | Bookings and questions | Low |

Google and Facebook usually handle discovery. Telegram and messaging apps suit repeat customers. ## How Do You Get Found on Google Maps in Addis Ababa?

Claim and complete your Google Business Profile. Use the correct business name, category, opening hours, phone number and recent photos. Customers search by area, so name your district in the description and services. Common Addis Ababa areas include Bole, Kazanchis, Piassa, Megenagna and CMC.

Write the phrases a customer would type, such as "hair salon Bole" or "cafe Kazanchis", and use them naturally in your services list. Ask satisfied customers to leave reviews, and reply to each one.

Paid search can support the listing. Start with a small test budget, check terms in Google's keyword tools, and measure cost per call or booking. ## How Should a Small Business Use Telegram Without Spamming?

Treat the channel as a notice board for people who already know the business. Adding strangers or posting every hour will lose followers quickly.

1. Create a channel and place its link on your Facebook page, Google profile, receipts and shop window.
2. Post a few times a week in Amharic: offers, new items, opening hours and photos.
3. Add a group only if you can answer questions every day.
4. Note which posts lead to calls, messages or visits.

Keep posts short and end each one with a clear next step, such as a phone number or booking link. Do not share customer details in any channel.

## How Do You Accept Payment Customers Prefer?

Payment habits differ between customers. Some will pay through a mobile wallet such as Telebirr, while others prefer cash or bank transfer. Offer the methods your customers ask for, and show them clearly at the counter and on your Facebook page.

Record each sale by channel, so you can tell which marketing brought the money in. A simple spreadsheet is enough to begin. ## FAQ

### What language should I use for marketing in Ethiopia?
Amharic reaches the broadest local audience. Add English if you serve visitors, expatriates or business customers. Choose the language your own customers use when they message you.

### Is Telegram or Facebook better for a local business?
They do different jobs. Facebook helps new customers find you through pages and groups. Telegram keeps people informed who already follow you. Start with Google and Facebook, then add Telegram when you have followers to invite.

### How much should a small shop spend?
Start with an amount you can afford to test for two or three months. Track calls, bookings and walk-ins by channel before increasing spending. The worked example above shows the method, but your own figures will differ.

### Do I need a website?
Not on day one. A complete Google profile, a Facebook page and a working phone number come first. A simple website can be added later, once you know which channels bring customers.
