Generative engine optimization (GEO) means making your business easy for AI tools such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode to find, trust and name. For a local business it is mostly local SEO done well: a complete Google Business Profile, consistent listings, real reviews and a website that answers customer questions. Google says there is no special markup or extra optimization needed for its AI features.

This guide covers what the 2026 data shows, which sources AI answers cite, and a short audit you can run in an afternoon. All figures come from the sources listed at the end.

## Are Customers Really Asking AI for Local Businesses?

Yes, and fast. BrightLocal's 2026 Local Consumer Review Survey (1,002 US adults) found 45% of consumers use generative AI such as ChatGPT or Gemini for local business recommendations, against 6% in 2025. It ranks AI as the third most popular source for local recommendations. Also, 42% said they trust AI platforms as much as traditional reviews.

One caution: this is a single survey of US adults and it asks about use, not about how often AI replaces Google. Treat it as a direction, not a share of your own traffic. Your own data (Search Console, "how did you hear about us" at checkout) is the real measure.

## Where Do AI Answers Get Local Business Information?

BrightLocal's August 2026 study ran 5 prompts per location across 1,355 business locations in the US, UK and Australia, on Google AI Overviews, AI Mode and ChatGPT. It collected 1,967,762 citations across 116,670 domains.

## What Does Google Say You Need to Do?

Google's documentation on AI features is short. A page must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet, and "there are no additional technical requirements." It also says there are no additional requirements for AI Overviews or AI Mode and no special schema.org markup, and that you do not need new machine-readable or AI text files.

So skip anyone selling a secret AI file. Make sure Googlebot can crawl your pages, keep your content text-based and accurate, and keep structured data consistent with what visitors see. AI traffic from Google is counted inside normal Web search traffic in Search Console, so you cannot separate it there.

## How Do You Appear in ChatGPT Search?

OpenAI documents three crawlers. OAI-SearchBot surfaces sites in ChatGPT search, and sites that block it will not appear in ChatGPT search answers. GPTBot crawls for model training, and you can block it without leaving search. ChatGPT-User visits a page when a person asks a question.

In practice: check your robots.txt (or ask your web host or developer) and confirm OAI-SearchBot is allowed. OpenAI says changes can take about 24 hours to apply. Many site builders and security plugins block AI bots by default, so a quick check is worthwhile.

## What Should a Local Business Do First?

Five steps, in order of effort versus payoff. They match the citation data above.

1. **Finish your Google Business Profile.** Categories, hours, services, photos and a description. See the [Google Business Profile optimization checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).
2. **Claim and complete Yelp.** Same name, address and phone as Google.
3. **Keep listings consistent.** Facebook, Apple Maps, Bing Places and any industry directory should match your Google details.
4. **Get detailed reviews.** Ask customers to mention the service they had. Review text is data that AI tools can quote. Never buy or fake reviews.
5. **Publish pages that answer real questions.** Prices, services, who you serve, how booking works. Plain text, one topic per page.

For how Google's own AI results change local rankings, read [Google AI Mode for local business](/blog/google-ai-mode-local-seo-2026) and [Google AI Overviews and local SEO](/blog/google-ai-overviews-local-business-seo).

## How Do You Check Your AI Visibility?

Run the same short test each month and write down the result. No paid tool is required.

Worked example, with every input an **assumption**: a hair salon in a mid-size city tests 5 prompts ("best hair salon near [neighbourhood]", "balayage near [neighbourhood]", "salon open Sunday in [city]", "cheap haircut [city]", "salon for curly hair [city]") on 3 tools: ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews.

| Step | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Prompts x tools | 5 x 3 = 15 checks |
| Times the salon is named | 4 of 15 |
| Visibility rate | 4 / 15 = 27% |
| Most-cited source in answers | Yelp (assumption) |

The action then is clear: if the answers cite Yelp, fix Yelp first. Repeat monthly and track the rate. Answers vary between runs and between users, so a single check proves little. BrightLocal also sells a Local AI Visibility Tracker if you want to automate this.

## FAQ

### Is GEO different from local SEO?

For a local business the overlap is large. The same Google profile, citations, reviews and useful pages feed both. The new work is checking AI bots are not blocked and testing what AI tools actually say about you.

### Do I need schema markup or an llms.txt file?

Google states that no special schema or AI text files are needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. Standard LocalBusiness markup is still fine if it matches the page. No primary source shows that an llms.txt file improves local visibility, so do not pay for one.

### Can I pay to appear in ChatGPT or Perplexity answers?

Organic answers cannot be bought. Advertising options in AI tools are changing, and we have not verified any local-business ad product, so this guide does not cover them.

### How long does it take to show up?

No source gives a timeline. Listing and review changes can show up as soon as the platform re-crawls. OpenAI says robots.txt changes take about 24 hours for ChatGPT search.

### Which AI tool matters most for my business?

Test the ones your customers use. BrightLocal's citation data covered Google AI Overviews, AI Mode and ChatGPT, and Google Business Profile led in the citations it counted.
