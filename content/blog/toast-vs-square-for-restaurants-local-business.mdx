## Can You Compare Toast and Square From Published Prices?

Not fully. The Square loyalty page cited below states terms rather than a complete rate card, and the Toast material this guide can cite gives no card rates or plan prices. A side-by-side table is only as reliable as its dates and sources, so check each figure against a current vendor page or a written quote before you rely on it.

Compare like with like. A headline in-person rate can sit beside an online or keyed-in rate that costs more, and monthly software fees, hardware and add-ons are often listed separately. A quote that leaves any of these blank is not finished. Ask for the total you would pay on your own card volume, not only a list of rates.

## What Does Square's Loyalty Program Commit You To?

Square states that its loyalty plan has no hidden fees or locked-in contracts and that customers can cancel or switch plans anytime, according to its [loyalty software page](https://squareup.com/us/en/software/loyalty). That is a statement about the loyalty product, not the point-of-sale agreement. Read the POS contract separately and confirm that the two sets of terms match.

Square's Future of Restaurants 2025 report, cited on its [café marketing page](https://squareup.com/ca/en/the-bottom-line/reaching-customers/marketing-plan-for-a-coffee-shop), says 82% of restaurant leaders find their loyalty program successful at driving up repeat visits and 83% say it boosts order size. The same page says over 80% of Canadian consumers are very or somewhat likely to engage with a loyalty program. These are vendor-reported survey results, so read them as direction rather than a forecast for your own room.

If a POS bundle includes loyalty, add its monthly cost to the card-fee total. A cheaper card rate can be cancelled out by a loyalty fee you did not plan for.

## How Do You Build a Like-for-Like POS Quote?

Use the same five steps for every vendor, and keep the answers in one sheet so the totals are comparable.

1. Write down your monthly card volume and average ticket, split into in-person, online and keyed-in payments.
2. Ask each vendor for the card rate for each channel, in writing.
3. Ask for the monthly software fee for the exact modules you need, including any loyalty or marketing tools.
4. Ask for hardware and installation costs, and whether any financing changes the total.
5. Ask for the contract length, the fee for leaving early, and what happens to your hardware when you exit.

A quote that cannot answer one of these five points is incomplete, however low its headline number looks.

## What Does a $40,000 Month Look Like on Two Quotes?

The inputs below are **assumptions**, not vendor prices. Monthly in-person card sales are $40,000 (assumption), the average ticket is $25 (assumption), which gives 1,600 tickets, and the two quotes are labelled A and B (assumption).

| Input or result | Quote A | Quote B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Card rate, in person (assumption) | 2.6% + 15¢ | 2.9% + 10¢ |
| Monthly software fee (assumption) | $0 | $49 |
| Card fees on $40,000 (1,600 tickets) | $1,280 | $1,320 |
| Monthly total | $1,280 | $1,369 |
| Annual total | $15,360 | $16,428 |

The math: Quote A charges 2.6% of $40,000, which is $1,040, plus 1,600 × 15¢, which is $240. Quote B charges 2.9% of $40,000, which is $1,160, plus 1,600 × 10¢, which is $160. Adding Quote B's $49 software fee gives $1,369 a month, $89 more than Quote A, or $1,068 a year. Each 0.1 percentage point on $40,000 is $40 a month under these assumptions. Swap in your own volume and the rates from each written quote to see which total is lower.

## Which Setup Fits Which Restaurant?

A low-volume single location should weight the monthly fee heavily, because a plan with no monthly charge can win even when its card rate is slightly higher. ## FAQ

### Is Toast more expensive than Square?

Published data cannot settle that. Toast's material available here gives no card rates or plan prices, so only written quotes for the same volume make the comparison fair.

### Does Square require a contract?

Square states that its loyalty plan has no hidden fees or locked-in contracts and that you can cancel or switch plans anytime. Check the POS agreement separately, because loyalty terms and POS terms are not the same document.

### Are loyalty programs worth paying for in a POS?

Square's Future of Restaurants 2025 report says 82% of restaurant leaders find loyalty successful at driving repeat visits. That is a vendor survey, so test the cost against your own repeat-visit numbers before you commit.

### How do I work out the true monthly cost?

Add the card fees on your expected volume, the monthly software fee, and any loyalty or add-on charges. Then run the same sum on each written quote and compare the totals.
