The fastest way to fill an empty salon slot is to message people who already know you: your waitlist first, then clients who are due for a visit. Send a short text or email with the exact time and a booking link, post the same opening on Instagram Stories, and offer an add-on instead of a discount. Paid ads are slower and costlier for same-day gaps.

This guide covers the tactics, what the main booking tools charge to send messages (checked October 2026), and the legal basics for texts and emails. No salon-wide average for lost revenue is published, so none is quoted. Worked numbers are labelled as assumptions.

## Who should you message first to fill a gap?

Start with the warmest list and work outward. Each step takes minutes, and you can stop when the slot is booked.

1. **Waitlist clients** who said they can take that day or time.
2. **Clients due for a repeat visit**, based on your usual interval for that service.
3. **Recent first-time clients** who have not rebooked.
4. **Followers and the public**, through Instagram Stories and your Google Business Profile.

Ask waitlist clients one question when they join: are you flexible on day and time? Someone who only wants Saturday afternoon should not get a Tuesday message. Our [rebooking script guide](/blog/salon-rebooking-script-that-gets-clients-back) has wording for the second group, and the [ghosting guide](/blog/why-salon-clients-ghost-you-and-how-to-stop-it) covers why gaps appear in the first place.

## What should the message say?

Name the time, the service and the booking link, and keep it to a few lines. Example: "A 3:00 PM cut opened up today. Reply YES or book here: [link]. Reply STOP to opt out." Send it only to people who agreed to receive marketing.

Avoid blanket "20% off everything" offers. If you need an incentive, attach a small add-on to the specific slot, such as a gloss or a scalp treatment. That limits the discount to the slot you are filling. For a no-discount way to bring in new clients, see the [referral program guide](/blog/salon-referral-program-without-discounts).

## Do you need permission to text or email clients?

Yes, for marketing messages. Under the FCC rule in 47 CFR 64.1200, autodialed or prerecorded telemarketing messages need prior express written consent: an agreement bearing the signature of the person called, which can be electronic, naming the phone number ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/64.1200)). A client can revoke consent by any reasonable method, including replying "stop", and you must honor it within a reasonable time not to exceed ten business days.

For email, the FTC says you must honor an opt-out within 10 business days, include a valid postal address and keep subject lines accurate; each violating email can carry penalties up to $53,088 ([FTC CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/CAN-SPAM-act-compliance-guide-business)). This is general information, not legal advice. Our [SMS guide for salons](/blog/sms-marketing-for-hair-salons) covers consent wording and carrier registration.

## Do reminders reduce empty slots?

Reminders cut no-shows, which are a common cause of gaps. The Cochrane review of mobile phone text reminders found attendance of 67.8% with no reminders and 78.6% with text reminders across eight randomised trials and 6,615 participants ([Cochrane](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD007458_mobile-phone-text-messaging-reminders-attendance-healthcare-appointments)). Those trials were healthcare appointments, not salons, so treat the size of the effect as indicative.

Turn on automatic reminders in your software and add a short cancellation policy. Some tools also let you take a deposit. Plans vary, so check which tier includes deposits and no-show fees before you choose.

## How should you post an opening on social media and Google?

Post the opening on Instagram Stories with a link sticker or a "message me" prompt, and keep the post time-specific: today, the time, the service. Add your booking link to your Google Business Profile so searchers can act at once. Posts reach people who already follow you, so they work best as a second wave after your direct messages. For paid reach, read the [Instagram story ads guide](/blog/instagram-story-ads-for-salons-bookings).

Do not buy ads for a single same-day slot unless you have already measured your cost per booking. No benchmark for a salon booking cost is published here, so track yours from your own data before scaling.

## What does a gap-filling message cost? (worked example)

All inputs are **assumptions**, not benchmarks.

- Assumption: 300 clients have consented to marketing texts
- Assumption: a $0.08-per-text plan like Fresha's
- Assumption: 2% of recipients book the open slot
- Assumption: average ticket of $70

Texting all 300 costs $24. Two percent of 300 is 6 replies, which is more than enough for one slot, so you would text a smaller group (for example the 50 most overdue clients, costing $4). If one client books at $70, the message returns about $66 after cost. The takeaway: send to the smallest list that can fill the slot, to avoid texting people for no reason and driving opt-outs.

## FAQ

### How do I fill a salon appointment slot the same day?

Text your waitlist and the clients most overdue for a visit, with the exact time and a booking link. Then post the opening on Instagram Stories and keep your Google Business Profile booking link live.

### Should I discount to fill empty slots?

A small add-on tied to that slot is safer than a broad percentage off, which can teach clients to wait for deals. Use it only for slots you could not fill otherwise.

### Is it legal to text my clients about openings?

Marketing texts need prior express written consent under FCC rules, and clients can revoke it by replying stop. Appointment reminders for a booked visit are a different category, but get legal advice if you send at volume.

### Which booking software has a waitlist?

Fresha lists waitlists in its features, and Booksy and Vagaro both say waitlists are included. Confirm on each vendor's current pricing page.

### How many text messages are free?

Booksy's plan includes 2,000 SMS marketing messages a month. Fresha charges $0.08 per marketing text. Vagaro shows no text price.
