## What Should a Fitness Studio Automate First?

Start where a delay loses money. Four sequences cover most of a studio's revenue risk:

- **New lead:** an instant reply with the class schedule and a booking link.
- **Trial or intro offer:** reminders before each class, then a check-in and an offer to join.
- **Missed visits:** a friendly nudge after a member skips a set period you define.
- **Lapsed members:** a win-back message with a reason to return.

Write each as a short sequence of two to four messages. ## What Rules Apply to Automated Emails and Texts?

For US email, the FTC's CAN-SPAM guide requires accurate headers, no deceptive subject lines, a clear statement that the message is an ad, your postal address and a working opt-out. You must honour opt-outs within 10 business days. Penalties go up to $53,088 per violating email.

Marketing texts are stricter. Under 47 CFR 64.1200(a)(2), advertising or telemarketing texts sent with an autodialer to wireless numbers need prior express written consent. That consent is a signed agreement that names the number, discloses that signing authorises autodialed marketing messages and states that signing is not a condition of purchase. Electronic signatures count. Opt-out requests, including words like STOP, must be honoured within a reasonable time not exceeding ten business days. This is not legal advice; ask a lawyer before launching text marketing.

On these assumptions the sequence returns about $1,040 a month net of tools. If only one member returns, revenue kept is $360, still above the tool cost, but staff time to reply to responses is extra. Measure returns for 90 days against a control month without the message.

## What Mistakes Should You Avoid?

Do not automate before you can answer replies. A message that invites a reply needs a human to read it. Do not text people who gave only an email address. Do not send promotions in a message people expect to be a reminder. Do not connect tools without checking where member data goes.

Keep the list of members and consent in one place, and test every sequence on your own phone before it goes live. If you use an AI assistant for replies, keep a person in the loop for cancellations and complaints.

## FAQ

### What is marketing automation for a fitness studio?

Software that sends emails or texts when something happens, such as a new lead, a first class or a missed week, so staff do not follow up by hand.

### How much does it cost?

Entry booking software is from $79 a month per location at Mindbody, and Zapier's paid plan starts at $19.99 a month billed annually. Mailchimp's free plan has no automation flows. Check each page for current prices.

### Do I need consent to text members?

For marketing texts sent with an autodialer, US rules require prior express written consent. Reminders and transactional messages have different rules, so check with a lawyer.

### Which sequence should I build first?

The new-lead reply, because speed matters most there, and then the trial follow-up.

### How do I know it works?

Compare return rates, trial conversion and cancellations before and after, over at least a quarter.
