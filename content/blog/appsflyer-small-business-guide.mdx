A small business needs AppsFlyer only if it has its own mobile app and pays for ads that send people to install it. If you do not run paid app-install campaigns, the free Google Analytics for Firebase is usually enough. AppsFlyer's pay-as-you-go Growth plan charges $0.07 per attributed conversion after a Welcome Package of 12,000 free conversions in the first 12 months, according to its pricing page checked on 1 November 2026.

Most local businesses, such as cafes, salons and gyms, do not need a mobile attribution tool. They need booking, call and direction tracking, which is a different job.

## What Does AppsFlyer Do?

AppsFlyer is a mobile attribution platform. AppsFlyer defines mobile attribution as "a method for determining which campaigns, media partners, and channels delivered specific app installs." It ties an install, and the actions that follow, to the ad that prompted it, so you can see which campaign produced users.

Attribution can credit a click or a view. AppsFlyer says click windows are typically 7 or 30 days and view-through windows typically 24 hours. Where device IDs are not available, for example iOS users who have not given tracking consent, it says it uses probabilistic methods that estimate performance at campaign level.

## Does a Local Business Need Mobile Attribution?

Usually not. Attribution answers one question: which paid campaign brought this app install? That matters when you spend on several networks, such as Meta, Google and Apple, to promote an app. It does not matter if customers book through your website, phone or Google Business Profile.

You may need it if you run a loyalty or ordering app, advertise it on more than one network, and want to compare cost per install by source. If your app has a few hundred users who came through word of mouth, the tool tells you little. ## How Much Does AppsFlyer Cost?

The pricing page lists three plans.

| Plan | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zero | Free | For owned-media apps; "Not suitable for apps with paid activities" |
| Growth | $0.07 per conversion | Pay-as-you-go, aimed at startups starting paid campaigns |
| Enterprise | Custom | Demo required to get a quote |

Zero and Growth include the Welcome Package: 12,000 free conversions within the first 12 months, core marketing analytics, SKAdNetwork support for iOS, and 30 days of free access to select premium add-ons. A conversion is an install or action your campaigns drove and AppsFlyer measured. Organic installs it does not attribute are not counted and are free. The page does not publish Enterprise prices or minimum fees.

## The Math: What Would Growth Cost a Small App?

All inputs below are **assumptions**, and the $0.07 rate is the only AppsFlyer figure.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Attributed installs from paid campaigns, per month | 500 |
| Attributed conversions in year one | 6,000 |
| Welcome Package allowance | 12,000 |
| Billable conversions in year one | 0 |
| Billable conversions per month after year one | 500 |
| Monthly AppsFlyer cost after year one | 500 x $0.07 = $35 |

On these assumptions the tool is free in year one and about $35 a month afterwards. The tool costs little compared with the ad spend it measures. If you also count in-app purchases or bookings as conversions, volume and cost rise, so count every event type you plan to measure.

## What Are the Free Alternatives?

Google Analytics for Firebase describes itself as "an unlimited analytics solution available at no charge", with reporting on up to 500 distinct events. It reports campaign performance across organic and paid channels. It is not a dedicated attribution network, so it does not offer the install-source matching that a specialist tool does.

Branch's pricing page lists Basics, Essentials and Enterprise plans with no dollar prices, and offers a free trial on Basics with no stated length. Adjust's pricing page returned a rate-limit error when checked, so it is not covered. For web tracking, see our [Google Analytics 4 guide for small business](/blog/google-analytics-4-for-small-business) and [local business analytics and tracking](/blog/local-business-analytics-and-tracking).

## What Does Setup Involve?

You add the vendor's SDK to the app, define which events to track, and connect each ad network. That is developer work on both iOS and Android, so budget for it if your app was built by an agency. AppsFlyer's pricing page does not give setup steps or hours, so ask your developer for an estimate.

If you advertise apps on Apple's network, see our guide to [Apple Search Ads for local business](/blog/apple-search-ads-local-business) and [Google App Campaigns](/blog/google-app-campaigns-local-business).

## FAQ

### Is AppsFlyer free?
The Zero plan is free but is for owned-media apps, not paid campaigns. Growth includes 12,000 free conversions in the first 12 months, then $0.07 per conversion.

### What counts as a conversion?
A successful install or user action that your campaigns drove and AppsFlyer measured. Organic installs it does not attribute are free.

### Do I need AppsFlyer if I only run Google Ads?
Not necessarily. Google Analytics for Firebase reports campaign performance at no charge, so start there.

### Can I use it for a website?
AppsFlyer is a mobile app attribution tool. For a website, use Google Analytics 4.
