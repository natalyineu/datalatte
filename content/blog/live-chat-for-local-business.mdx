## Which Live Chat Tools Are Free for Small Businesses?

Three tools offer a no-cost start. They differ in what is limited.

| Tool | Free plan | Paid example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tawk.to | Live chat software free; keeps a "Powered by tawk.to" label | Remove branding $29 a month; AI Assist from $29 a month; hired chat agents $1 an hour |
| Tidio | $0, live chat included, 50 billable conversations | Starter $24.17 a month (100 conversations); Growth from $49.17 a month |
| Crisp | $0, 2 agent seats, 100 customer profiles, website chat widget and shared inbox | Mini $45 a month; Essentials $95 a month; extra seats $10 each |

Each vendor sets its own definition of a billable conversation or customer profile, so read it before you rely on a limit. Tidio's page also says the first 50 Lyro AI conversations are free for life.

## Do You Need Live Chat or a Contact Form?

Choose live chat if someone can reply within a few minutes during opening hours. A chat widget with nobody behind it looks worse than no widget. If you are away from your desk for long stretches, a short form with a promised reply time works better.

Many local businesses use a hybrid: chat during open hours, a form after hours. Compare automated and human options in our [chatbot vs live chat guide](/blog/ai-powered-chatbot-vs-live-chat-local-business), and our [live chat widget guide](/blog/live-chat-widget-local-business-website) covers placement.

## Who Should Answer the Chat?

Name one person per shift and give them a short script. Typical first replies cover opening hours, prices, booking and directions. Save common answers as canned replies in the tool.

Tawk.to sells human chat agents at $1 an hour, per its pricing page. The quality of that service is not verified here, so test it on a small scale first. For a booking-heavy business, an [AI chatbot for booking and FAQs](/blog/ai-chatbot-for-local-business-booking-and-faqs) can handle after-hours questions.

## Where Should the Widget Go?

Put it on pages where a visitor decides: service or price pages, the booking page and the contact page. Keep the first message short and specific, such as "Questions about prices or availability?" rather than "How can we help?".

Then check whether it helps. Mark chat starts as a key event in GA4, as described in our [Google Analytics 4 guide](/blog/google-analytics-4-for-small-business), and compare lead counts before and after.

## Is It Worth the Time? A Worked Example

All numbers are **assumptions** for a service business with a small team.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Website visitors per month | 1,500 |
| Visitors who start a chat | 2% |
| Chats per month | 30 |
| Chats that become a booking | 20% |
| Extra bookings | 6 |
| Gross profit per booking | $50 |
| Monthly gross profit from chat | $300 |
| Staff time, 10 minutes per chat | 5 hours |
| Value of an hour | $20 |
| Monthly time cost | $100 |

On a free plan the tool costs $0, so the net gain in this example is about $200 a month. On Tidio Starter at $24.17 it is about $176. If chats arrive outside your hours and go unanswered, the gain drops to zero, so measure your real reply rate.

## FAQ

### Is live chat free for small businesses?

tawk.to describes its core live chat software as free. Tidio and Crisp both have free plans with limits on conversations or seats.

### How many chats can I handle on Tidio Free?

Tidio's Free plan lists 50 billable conversations. Paid plans start with Starter at $24.17 a month for 100.

### Should I use live chat or a chatbot?

Use live chat when a person can reply quickly. Use a chatbot or form for after-hours questions. A hybrid often fits small teams.

### Does live chat help with Google rankings?

No source in this guide says so. The benefit is more enquiries from existing visitors, which you measure with analytics.

### Can I run live chat from my phone?

Crisp lists mobile apps on its Free plan. Check each vendor for app support before choosing.
