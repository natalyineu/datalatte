The best Google Ads keywords for a bakery are specific, local and tied to something you can sell this week: custom birthday cakes, wedding cakes, gluten-free bread, cake delivery, pre-order pastry boxes. Generic words such as "bakery" bring browsers. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put the average click in Restaurants and Food at $2.05, against $5.42 across all industries, but there is no bakery category, so treat it as a range.

Google does not publish a keyword list or a CPC for bakeries. This guide gives you a method for building your own list, the settings that keep it clean, and a budget example whose inputs are labelled as assumptions.

## Which Keywords Should a Bakery Start With?

Group keywords by what the customer wants to order, not by the word "bakery". Each group gets its own ad group and its own landing page section, so the ad text matches the search. The phrases below are examples of intent to test, not a list with published volumes or prices.

| Intent group | Example phrases to test | Landing page should show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Custom cakes | custom birthday cake, cake for birthday [your town] | Order form, lead time, photos, starting price |
| Wedding and events | wedding cake bakery, tasting booking | Tasting slots, serving sizes, enquiry form |
| Dietary | gluten free bakery, vegan cakes | Menu, allergen note, pickup hours |
| Everyday walk-in | bakery near me, fresh bread [your town] | Address, hours, map, phone |
| Delivery and pre-order | cake delivery, pastry box pre-order | Delivery area, cut-off time, checkout |

Walk-in terms get the most searches but the least intent to pay a high price. Custom and wedding orders are worth more per customer, so a small budget usually does more there. Check each phrase in Keyword Planner, which per Google gives monthly search estimates, average cost to show, and a forecast of clicks and conversions based on your spend.

## How Do Match Types Change What You Pay For?

Google Ads Help describes three match types. Broad match shows ads on related searches that may not contain your words and, per Google, it is critical to use Smart Bidding with it. Phrase match, written in quotation marks, covers searches that include your keyword's meaning. Exact match, written in square brackets, covers searches with the same meaning or intent as the keyword and reaches the fewest searches.

For a new bakery account, start with phrase and exact match on the groups above. Move to broad match only once conversion tracking works and you have a negative list, because broad match gives you the least control over which searches trigger your ads.

## Which Negative Keywords Does a Bakery Need?

Negative keywords exclude search terms so your ads reach people more likely to buy. Google notes that negatives do not match close variants, so a negative for "recipe" will not block "recipes". You add singular, plural and synonym versions yourself. Misspellings and casing are handled for you.

A starting list for a bakery, to be checked against your Search terms report weekly:

- Recipe and home-baking intent: recipe, recipes, how to make, mix, from scratch
- Job and training intent: jobs, hiring, salary, course, school
- Trade intent if you do not sell it: wholesale, supplier, equipment, franchise
- Product types you do not make

Save the Search terms report each week and add anything irrelevant. Our guide to [negative keywords](/blog/google-ads-negative-keywords-stop-wasting-budget) walks through the process.

## How Much Should a Bakery Budget?

No official figure exists. The only published anchor is the category average: $2.05 per click, an 8.05% conversion rate and $30.57 per lead for Restaurants and Food. A bakery's own numbers will differ, mostly because custom-order enquiries are rarer than a restaurant booking click.

All inputs below are **assumptions** using the category averages, with a conversion defined as an enquiry or call.

| | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $500 |
| Cost per click | $2.05 |
| Clicks | about 244 |
| Conversion rate | 8.05% |
| Enquiries | about 19.6 |
| Cost per enquiry | about $25.5 |
| Enquiries that become orders | 25% |
| Orders | about 4.9 |
| Cost per order | about $102 |

If an average custom cake order brings in less than the cost per order, the campaign loses money on the first sale. That is the number to track, not clicks. If your local clicks cost more than the average, the maths gets worse in proportion. ## Should You Bid More for Mobile and Near Me Searches?

Bakery searches are often made on a phone, but no published source gives a bakery mobile conversion premium, so do not apply a fixed bid increase. Instead, compare device performance in your own account after a few weeks and adjust from that. Keep location targeting tight around the area you can deliver to or that customers will drive to.

## FAQ

### What are the best Google Ads keywords for a bakery?

Specific order-intent phrases such as custom birthday cake, wedding cake bakery, gluten-free bakery and cake delivery, each combined with your town. Test them in Keyword Planner and keep the ones that produce enquiries, not just clicks.

### How much does a click cost for a bakery?

There is no bakery figure. The 2026 LocaliQ average for Restaurants and Food is $2.05 per click, and $5.42 across all industries. Your own cost depends on your area and competition.

### Which match type should a bakery use?

Start with phrase and exact match. Google says broad match shows ads on related searches that may not contain your keywords and should be used with Smart Bidding.

### Do negative keywords block plurals automatically?

No. Google says negative keywords do not match close variants, so add singular, plural and synonym versions. Misspellings and casing are covered.

### Is the Keyword Planner volume reliable for a small town?

Treat it as an estimate. Google describes Keyword Planner as giving estimates and notes that very low-volume keywords are not discoverable or forecastable, so small-town terms may be missing.
