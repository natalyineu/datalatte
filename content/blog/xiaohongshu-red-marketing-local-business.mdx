A local business outside China can open a free Xiaohongshu (RED) account and post notes for Chinese-speaking visitors and residents, but it cannot do everything a Chinese business can. Xiaohongshu's paid Professional (Business Pro) upgrade costs RMB 600 according to Dragon Trail, and AppInChina reports that foreign entities cannot run ads through the Juguang ad platform. Treat the app as an organic channel and test it with a small time budget.

Platform rules change, and the evidence here comes from agencies and press rather than from Xiaohongshu's own help pages, which were not available to check. Confirm each step in the app before you pay anyone.

## Is Xiaohongshu Worth It for a Local Business?

Rest of World reported about 225 million monthly active users in late 2024, mostly in China, and about 7 million users outside China in December 2024, mostly of Chinese descent. In March 2025 the US had about 800,000 daily active users, down from a January peak of 1.3 million.

Those numbers describe a Chinese-speaking audience, not a general local audience. Use the platform if Chinese-speaking students, tourists or residents are a real share of your customers. If you cannot name a single customer who found you that way, start with Google Business Profile.

## How Do You Open a Business Account From Overseas?

A personal account needs only a phone number or an Apple, Weibo or QQ login, according to Dragon Trail's July 2024 guide. The Business Pro upgrade is manual: go to Settings, Account Security, Official Verification, upload your license and other documents, and pay RMB 600. The guide says verification takes no longer than one month and adds media-buy, giveaway and store functions.

Dragon Trail does not list the exact documents, and its guide is nearly two years old, so check the in-app form. Hotels and other businesses can upgrade directly, but tourism boards go through a separate process.

## What Can't Overseas Businesses Do on the App?

Rest of World reports that monetisation requires identity verification including a selfie holding a passport, and that livestreaming and online stores require a Chinese ID. Foreign influencers cannot withdraw commissions without a Chinese bank account, though they can be paid through overseas agencies. One US business owner found her livestreams reached mostly mainland viewers instead of her North American audience, with no paid option to adjust reach.

AppInChina states that a Juguang ad account can only be opened by a Chinese entity. Both are secondary sources, so ask a Xiaohongshu representative before planning an ad budget.

## What Should You Post?

Xiaohongshu content is posted as notes. Post what a visiting customer wants to know before a trip: the dish or service, the price range, the address in Chinese characters and how to pay. Photos of your menu or price list help more than slogans.

No source used here publishes how often to post or what click-through rates to expect, so set your own test: for example, a fixed number of notes in six weeks and a record of how new customers say they found you. Link notes to your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) so searchers can verify the address.

## How Does It Compare With Douyin and WeChat?

Xiaohongshu is a search and recommendation app for reviews. Douyin is the short-video platform; see [Douyin and TikTok for local business](/blog/douyin-tiktok-local-business-chinese-market). For timing, the [Chinese New Year marketing guide](/blog/chinese-new-year-marketing-local-business) covers the main travel peak.

## What Does Six Weeks Cost? (worked example)

All inputs are **assumptions**: a cafe posts 2 notes a week for 6 weeks, each note takes 1.5 hours including photos, the owner values time at $20 an hour, and the team does the Business Pro upgrade.

## FAQ

### Can a foreign business have a Xiaohongshu account?

Yes. A personal account needs a phone number or social login. Business Pro requires manual verification and a RMB 600 fee, per Dragon Trail.

### Can overseas businesses run Xiaohongshu ads?

AppInChina says Juguang accounts can only be opened by a Chinese entity. Confirm with Xiaohongshu before budgeting.

### Do I need to speak Chinese?

Notes are read by Chinese speakers, so your core details should be in Chinese. A native speaker should review them.

### How many people use Xiaohongshu?

About 225 million monthly users in late 2024 according to Rest of World, mostly in China, with about 7 million outside it.
