The best Google Ads keywords for a gym are local, ready-to-join searches: "gym near me", "gym membership [town]", "24 hour gym [town]" and "gym with [feature you have]". Bid on those first, keep them in phrase or exact match, and block job, equipment and free-workout searches. LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put Health and Fitness at $6.17 per click and $67.36 per lead.

No public source gives search volumes or bids for gym keywords in your town. Use Keyword Planner and your own Search terms report for those. Everything below is a method, and the numbers come from the sources at the end.

## Which Keyword Groups Should a Gym Build First?

Group keywords by what the searcher wants, and give each group its own ad group and landing page section.

| Group | Example searches | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Join now | gym membership [town], gym near me, 24 hour gym [town] | High: ready to sign up |
| Trial and price | gym day pass, gym free trial, cheap gym [town] | High, price sensitive |
| Feature | gym with pool, gym with childcare, women only gym | Medium to high |
| Service | personal trainer [town], strength training gym | Medium |
| Brand | your gym name | Defend your own name |

These are example phrasings, not measured volumes. Check each in Keyword Planner and delete any that do not apply to your gym. Our [fitness studio keyword guide](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-fitness-studios-in-2026) covers class-based studios, which search differently from open-access gyms.

## What Does the Benchmark Say About Cost?

Health and Fitness averages $6.17 per click, 5.81% click-through, 6.94% conversion and $67.36 per lead. The nearby Sports and Recreation category is cheaper at $2.77 per click and $44.26 per lead. The all-industry average is $5.42 per click.

Neither category is "gyms" exactly, and the benchmark does not say what a lead was. Treat the range as a planning guide and measure your own cost per joined member.

## Which Match Types Work for Gyms?

Google's help page describes three types. Broad match is the default and shows ads on related searches, even without the literal words. Phrase match shows ads on searches that carry your keyword's meaning. Exact match gives the most control but reaches the fewest searches.

For a small local budget, start with phrase and exact for the join-now group. Broad match works best with Smart Bidding, per Google, and needs enough conversion data to guide it, so wait until the campaign has some.

## Which Negative Keywords Should a Gym Add?

Negatives stop spend on searches that will not join. Google's help says negative keywords do not match close variants, so add singular and plural forms yourself.

- Job intent: jobs, hiring, salary, careers
- Equipment: home gym, dumbbells, treadmill, for sale
- Free intent: free workout, workout video, at home
- Wrong place: names of towns you do not serve
- Cancelling: cancel membership, refund

Review the Search terms report every week during the first month and add what wasted spend.

## How Do You Track Joins, Not Just Clicks?

Count calls with call assets and call reporting, and add a conversion for form or trial requests. Google says call reporting uses forwarding numbers and can count calls as conversions, which may affect auto-bidding.

Then record joins in your own system and tag them by source. Ask at sign-up how they found you. Your [Business Profile](/blog/fitness-studio-google-business-profile-optimization) is free and supports the ads.

## FAQ

### What are the best keywords for gym Google Ads?
Local, ready-to-join searches such as "gym membership [town]" and "24 hour gym [town]". Check volume in Keyword Planner because no public source gives it per town.

### How much does a click cost for gyms?
LocaliQ lists $6.17 for Health and Fitness and $2.77 for Sports and Recreation in 2026. Your cost depends on your town.

### Should a gym use broad match?
Start with phrase and exact. Broad match can help later, once Smart Bidding has conversion data.

### How many keywords do I need?
Fewer than most people think. Start with 10 to 20 tightly themed keywords across two or three ad groups.

### What is the difference between gym and fitness studio keywords?
Gyms attract price and access searches. Studios attract class and style searches. Our [Facebook ads guide for gyms](/blog/facebook-ads-for-gyms-fitness-studios-complete-guide) covers the social side.
