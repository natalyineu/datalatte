Google says there is no special optimization for AI Overviews. A page is eligible to be a supporting link if it is indexed and can be shown in Search with a snippet, and Google states there are no additional technical requirements. For a local business, that means the basics still decide your visibility: a correct Business Profile, reviews, and a clear site. What changes is the click, because fewer people follow links when a summary appears.

This guide covers what the data says about clicks, what Google confirms, and a short checklist. Every number has a source at the end.

## Do AI Overviews Reduce Clicks to Local Websites?

Pew Research Center tracked the browsing of 900 US adults in March 2025 and analyzed 68,879 Google searches, 12,593 of which produced an AI summary. Users clicked a traditional result link in 8% of visits with a summary and 15% without one. They clicked a link inside the summary in about 1% of visits. Sessions ended after the page in 26% of cases with a summary and 16% without.

Pew studied general searches, not local business queries. The figures show a direction, not what your own site will see.

## What Does Google Say About Getting Included?

Google's Search Central page on AI features says a page needs to be indexed and eligible to appear with a snippet. It says there is no extra requirement and no special schema.org markup to add, and structured data should match the visible page text. Sites that appear in AI features are counted in the Web search type of the Search Console Performance report.

So there is no AI-Overview-only tag to install. Be suspicious of anyone selling one.

## Does the Local Pack Still Matter?

Yes, and the two do not always agree. The Local SEO Data study, republished by MarTech Series in June 2026, ran 1,120 queries across seven verticals and 13 US cities in May 2026. It compared Google AI Mode with the Local Pack and found 28.5% of Local Pack businesses missing from AI Mode for the same query. Among the top three results, overlap was 48%.

That study covers AI Mode, not AI Overviews, and verticals such as plumbers and dentists rather than salons or cafes. Still, it suggests ranking in the map pack does not guarantee a mention. Our [AI Mode guide](/blog/google-ai-mode-local-seo-2026) goes deeper.

## What Should a Local Business Do?

Google ties local ranking to relevance, distance and prominence, and says verified, complete information helps. Work on the inputs it names:

1. Verify your Business Profile and complete hours, category and attributes.
2. Keep the name, categories and description within Google's guidelines. The name should match your real-world name, and the description should not include links or promotions.
3. Reply to reviews. Google says replies should be short and specific, and rewards for reviews are prohibited.
4. Put plain text answers on your site: prices where possible, services, area and hours, written for a person.
5. Check that Googlebot can crawl the pages and that you have no stray noindex tags.

Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers step 1 and 2 in detail.

## How Do You Measure Impact?

Google folds AI feature traffic into the Web search type, so you cannot separate it in Search Console. BrightLocal's September 2026 post says Google's reporting of AI impressions is limited and sells a tracker for ChatGPT, AI Overviews and AI Mode. Treat the vendor claim as marketing, not independent evidence.

A free approach: pick ten searches your customers use, run them monthly, and note whether you appear in the map pack and in any summary. Record calls, direction requests and bookings in your Business Profile and booking tool.

## FAQ

### Can I optimize for AI Overviews?

Google says no special optimization is needed beyond Search fundamentals. Being indexed, eligible for a snippet and useful is the requirement.

### Do I need new schema markup?

No. Google states there is no special structured data required, and markup must match the visible text.

### Will my Business Profile show in AI answers?

Not guaranteed. The Local SEO Data study found overlap between the Local Pack and AI Mode was 71.5% across all results and 48% in the top three.

### Is zero-click search a reason to stop SEO?

No. Pew found 8% of visits still clicked a result with a summary. See our [GEO guide for local businesses](/blog/generative-engine-optimization-local-business) for what else to track.

### How can I see AI traffic?

Search Console includes it within the Web search type. It is not reported separately.
