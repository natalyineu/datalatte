## What Do Meta Ads Cost for a Pet Groomer?

AdWave's channel table lists a standalone Meta buy at often $300-$1,000 per month ([AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-choose-advertising-channels)). Its veterinary guide, which is the closest pet-care comparison, says most veterinary practices can run effective Meta campaigns for $300 to $1,000 per month ([AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/veterinary-advertising)). That band is a reasonable starting point for a single grooming location.

Two numbers drive the cost. Cost per thousand impressions (CPM) is what you pay for every 1,000 times the ad shows. Cost per click is what you pay each time someone taps through. Typical Meta ranges are $5-15 per thousand impressions and $1-3 per click ([AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-small-business-guide)).

Barklead suggests starting Facebook ads for groomers at $10-$20 per day ([Barklead](https://barklead.com/post/using-facebook-ads-for-groomers), secondary). That works out to roughly $300-$600 per month over 30 days, which sits in the lower half of the range above.

## Which Ad Formats Work Best for Pet Groomers?

Video is the format to test first. AdWave cites Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, which says short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram ([AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/electrician-advertising-reaching-homeowners), secondary). A before-and-after clip or a dog leaving the salon suits Reels, which is where the engagement claim applies.

You do not need separate campaigns for each feed. AdWave notes that Instagram ads run through the same Meta Ads Manager as Facebook ads, and one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook, or both ([AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/instagram-advertising-for-local-business), secondary). For organic posting alongside paid, see [Instagram marketing for pet groomers](/blog/pet-groomers-guide-to-instagram-marketing).

Search can complement social. A mobile pet groomer in a Marketing 360 case study advertised on Facebook and Google, which shows the two channels working together in one business ([Marketing 360](https://blog.marketing360.com/case-studies/pet-groomer-marketing-case-study-driving-success-with-digital-ads/), secondary, 2023).

## How Should You Target Pet Owners Near Your Salon?

Meta lets advertisers identify detailed customer profiles and set a budget for each ad ([The Restaurant HQ](https://www.therestauranthq.com/operations/coffee-shop-marketing/), secondary). For a fixed grooming location, use that to narrow the audience to people who can realistically reach you.

Barklead suggests targeting users within a 10-15 mile radius and pet owners aged 25-60 who are likely to have disposable income ([Barklead](https://barklead.com/post/using-facebook-ads-for-groomers), secondary). Treat the radius as a starting test, not a rule. For a salon, drive time matters more than distance, so set the radius where clients will actually travel.

Keep search and social targeting separate in your plan. Search intent for grooming is covered in [Google Ads for pet groomers in Milwaukee](/blog/google-ads-for-pet-groomers-in-milwaukee-wi), while the Meta audience is built around who is scrolling, not who is searching.

## What Does a Worked Budget Look Like?

This calculation uses assumption-labelled inputs. The cost figures come from the sourced ranges above.

- Monthly budget: $600 (assumption, within the $300-$600 range from the daily-budget suggestion)
- Average client ticket: $80 (assumption)
- Click-to-booking rate: 3% (assumption)

At a $3 cost per click, $600 buys 200 clicks. At $1 per click, it buys 600. At 3%, that is 6 or 18 bookings. At $80 each, the revenue is $480 or $1,440.

| Scenario | Clicks | Bookings (3%, assumption) | Revenue ($80 ticket, assumption) | Return on $600 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Expensive clicks ($3) | 200 | 6 | $480 | -20% |
| Cheap clicks ($1) | 600 | 18 | $1,440 | +140% |

At the expensive end, a single booking does not cover the spend. Repeat visits, which this calculation leaves out, are what make the campaign pay back. Measure your real click-to-booking rate for 30 days before scaling.

## Which Ad Copy and Offers Should You Use?

Barklead's sample ad copy offers a discount on a first visit: "Book today and get 10% off your first grooming session" ([Barklead](https://barklead.com/post/using-facebook-ads-for-groomers), secondary). A 10% discount on an $80 ticket costs $8 per booking, so check that margin before you run it.

Two example hooks to test, with a city name in place of the placeholder:

"Book your dog's groom in [city] this week."

"Mobile or in-salon, your pet's next groom is one tap away in [city]."

These are examples, not tested results. Run one offer against one hook at a time so you know which change moved bookings.

## How Do You Set Up Tracking and Your Account?

Work through these steps before you spend the first dollar:

1. Create or confirm your ad account in Meta Ads Manager, which AdWave describes as the central tool for running Facebook and Instagram ads ([AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/instagram-advertising-for-local-business), secondary).
2. Install the Meta Pixel on your booking confirmation page and your contact form.
3. Set one conversion event, such as a booking form submission, so Meta optimises for bookings.
4. Add UTM tags to every ad URL so your analytics can separate Meta traffic from other sources.
5. Preview placements before launch. Meta Ads Manager covers Facebook, Instagram, and Messenger ([Turrboo](https://turrboo.com/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media), secondary).

Without the pixel and a conversion event, you will see clicks and impressions but cannot tell which ads brought in bookings.

## FAQ

### How much should a pet groomer spend on Meta ads per month?

AdWave's channel table puts a standalone Meta buy at often $300-$1,000 per month, and its veterinary guide uses the same band for most pet-care practices. Start near the bottom of that range and scale only after you measure your cost per booking.

### Should a groomer run Facebook or Instagram ads?

Meta Ads Manager can run both from one campaign, so the choice is about placement rather than separate budgets. Test video in Reels and Facebook Feed to see where bookings come from.

### Do video ads work better than photos for groomers?

AdWave cites Meta's 2025 report that short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images. Engagement is not the same as bookings, so judge video ads by the conversion events you set up.

### What radius should a Milwaukee groomer target?

Barklead suggests 10-15 miles, but that is a general guide. For a salon, base the radius on realistic drive time to your location and adjust it using your own booking data.
