A US local business can run CTV (connected TV) ads by buying streaming TV spots through a self-serve platform and targeting them locally. Two platforms publish a minimum: Roku Ads Manager requires a lifetime budget of $500 or more, and Disney Campaign Manager (Hulu and Disney+) lists a $500 minimum per campaign. Market CPMs run roughly $15 to $45 per 1,000 impressions.

This guide covers where a local business can buy, how targeting by DMA and ZIP code works, what a $500 test buys and how to measure it. Prices are benchmarks from a reseller and change often, so get a written quote before you commit. CTV suits awareness and works best next to Google Search, which captures people who go on to look you up. For the wider overview, see our [CTV advertising guide](/guides/ctv-advertising-guide).

## Which CTV platforms can a US local business use?

Start with the routes that need no sales call.

| Route | What the source says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Roku Ads Manager | Lifetime budget must be $500 or more | Roku help center |
| Disney Campaign Manager (Hulu, Disney+) | $500 per campaign, free sign-up, billing starts once the ad runs | Disney FAQ |
| Tubi self-serve (Universal Ads) | Announced May 6, 2025, for businesses of all sizes; no minimum published | Fox Corporation |
| YouTube on TV | Bought through Google Ads | Google Ads Help |

Premium and live-sports inventory, such as Amazon Prime Video, is usually bought through an account executive or a demand-side platform (DSP). ## How does DMA and ZIP code targeting work?

A DMA (Designated Market Area) is a Nielsen-defined region used to measure US television audiences. Precisely, which licenses the data, says there are over 200 of them. A DMA can include suburbs far from your shop, so a ZIP-level option matters for a local business.

Disney's FAQ lists "zip code, age, gender, interests, and beyond" among its audience options. Tubi's announcement mentions content and audience targeting but gives no geo detail, so ask before you plan around it. Roku's cost page does not mention ZIP or DMA targeting either. Confirm in writing which geographic options your account has and whether using them changes the CPM.

## What does CTV cost per 1,000 views?

AdWave, a CTV reseller, reports a blended US CTV CPM of about $26 for 2026, citing eMarketer. It puts the full market at about $15 to $45, and says Prime Video runs near $28, down from about $35 a year after its 2024 ad launch. It gives free ad-supported services such as Tubi and Pluto about $15 to $25 and premium services such as Hulu and Peacock about $25 to $45. These are reseller benchmarks, not rate cards.

Higher demand pushes CPMs up in some periods, and sports-adjacent inventory is usually priced above general content. Ask each platform how pricing changes by season and ZIP selection. Our [CTV cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data) explains how to convert a CPM into a cost per customer.

## What does a $500 test buy?

The budget and CPMs below come from the sources above. Everything else is an **assumption**: a new customer is worth $120 in gross profit over the first year, and no impressions are wasted.

| CPM | Impressions for $500 | Customers to break even | Impressions per break-even customer |
|---|---|---|---|
| $15 | about 33,300 | 5 | about 6,700 |
| $26 | about 19,200 | 5 | about 3,850 |
| $45 | about 11,100 | 5 | about 2,200 |

Break-even is $500 divided by $120, rounded up to 5 customers. If your new customer is worth $60, you need 9. A first-visit loss is fine only when repeat visits make up the difference. Compare this with what the same $500 would do in Google Search before you decide.

## Which local businesses should try CTV first?

Businesses with a clear local trade area and a repeat-purchase customer have the most to gain: gyms, cafes, salons and pet groomers. CTV does not capture someone ready to book today. Search does that. CTV builds familiarity, so people recognise your name when they later search or see your Google profile.

For a gym or salon, keep the creative simple: who you are, what you offer, where you are and one clear action. Specs and lengths are in our [CTV creative specs guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026), and the general question of whether a small business can advertise on streaming TV is covered in [CTV ads for local businesses](/blog/ctv-ads-for-local-businesses-can-you-advertise-on-streaming-tv).

## How should you measure a CTV campaign?

Give CTV its own tracking: a promo code, a vanity URL or a dedicated phone number. Compare branded search volume and bookings during the flight with the 30 days before. Impressions alone are not customers.

Common mistakes to avoid:

- Buying a whole DMA when only a few ZIP codes are your trade area.
- Judging CTV on last-click conversions like search.
- Running CTV without a branded Google Search campaign to catch the follow-up searches.
- Skipping the written quote on minimums, data fees and creative fees.

## FAQ

### Do I need a production company to make a CTV ad?
Not necessarily. Platforms set their own creative specs, so check the requirements first. Our [creative specs guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026) lists them. Costs for videographers vary widely, so get quotes.

### Can I run CTV ads in several cities at once?
Platforms that support geographic targeting let you set separate locations. Confirm per-market minimums before you split a budget, since Roku and Disney list their minimums per campaign.

### How long until I see results?
Awareness builds over repeated exposure. Compare branded search and bookings during and after the flight, and treat the first month as a calibration period.

### Is CTV worth it on a marketing budget under $1,000 a month?
Usually search comes first. A complete Google Business Profile and a Google Ads campaign capture people who already want your service. CTV makes more sense once those basics work.

### How much does CTV advertising cost for a local business?
Roku and Disney list $500 minimums, and CPMs run roughly $15 to $45 according to a reseller benchmark. Confirm the current price with each platform.
