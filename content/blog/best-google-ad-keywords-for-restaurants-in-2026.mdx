An independent restaurant should bid on a short list of high-intent searches: the cuisine plus a place, "near me" phrases, reservations, private dining and catering. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put the average click for Restaurants and Food at $2.05, with an 8.05% conversion rate and $30.57 per lead. Everything else, such as recipes, jobs and menus of other places, belongs in your negative keyword list.

## Which Keywords Should a Restaurant Bid On?

Start with searches where the person is ready to book or order. Group them by what the guest wants to do, because each group needs its own ad text and landing page.

| Intent | Example keyword pattern | Send them to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find a table now | "[cuisine] restaurant near me", "[cuisine] [neighbourhood]" | Page with hours, map, booking button |
| Reserve | "restaurant reservations [city]", "book table [cuisine]" | Booking page |
| Occasion | "private dining [city]", "birthday dinner [city]", "brunch [neighbourhood]" | Event or brunch page |
| Catering | "catering [city]", "office lunch catering" | Catering enquiry form |

These are patterns, not measured winners. Check each one in Keyword Planner and in your own Search terms report before you raise bids.

## How Do Match Types Change What You Pay For?

Google's help explains three match types. Broad match is the default and can show on searches related to your keyword without containing it. Phrase match, written in quotes, shows on searches that include the keyword's meaning. Exact match, written in square brackets, shows on searches with the same meaning or intent and gives the most control.

For a restaurant with a small budget, begin with phrase and exact match. Google also says it is critical to use Smart Bidding with broad match, so leave broad match until you have conversion tracking in place. Our [guide to negative keywords](/blog/google-ads-negative-keywords-stop-wasting-budget) explains how to clean up the search terms that slip through.

## Which Negative Keywords Save Money?

Negative keywords stop your ad appearing on searches that contain a term. Google notes that negatives do not match close variants, so add singular, plural and synonym forms yourself.

Typical negatives for a restaurant:

- Job intent: jobs, hiring, salary, careers
- Cooking intent: recipe, how to make, ingredients
- Free intent: free, coupon, if you do not run offers
- Other-business intent: franchise, supplier, wholesale, equipment

Review the Search terms report every week for the first month. Add anything that does not describe a guest.

## Should Opening Hours Control When Ads Run?

Yes, for table and takeaway keywords. Google says that with an ad schedule your ads are only eligible to show during the times you specify, and that schedules follow the account's time zone. You can set up to 6 schedule blocks per day for each campaign.

A restaurant can show reservation ads all day and pause takeaway ads when the kitchen closes. Add a call button as well. Google says clicks on a call asset cost the same as headline clicks.

## FAQ

### What is the average cost per click for restaurant keywords?

WordStream by LocaliQ lists $2.05 for Restaurants and Food in its 2026 benchmarks, against $5.42 for all industries. Your city and competition will move that number.

### How many keywords does a small restaurant need?

Ten to twenty well-grouped keywords are enough to start. Group them by intent (table, reservation, occasion, catering) so each group has matching ad text.

### Should a restaurant use broad match?

Google recommends Smart Bidding with broad match. If you do not yet track bookings or calls as conversions, start with phrase and exact match.

### Are search volumes published here?

No. Volumes depend on the city and month. Use Google Keyword Planner, which needs a Google Ads account with billing set up, to see estimates for your area.

### Do I need negative keywords?

Yes. Negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals and synonyms. Check the Search terms report weekly at first.
