A beauty clinic should start Google Ads with a small local Search campaign aimed at specific treatments, a booking or call goal, and a careful check of advertising rules. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put Beauty and Personal Care at $4.62 per click, a 10.35% conversion rate and $39.25 per lead. Medical-adjacent categories cost more: Dentists and Dental Services are $8.00 per click. Rules matter as much as cost, because some injectable treatments cannot be advertised in the UK.

This guide is for independent clinics offering facials, skin treatments, laser and similar services in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. Every figure is sourced at the end, and examples are labelled as assumptions.

LocaliQ's 2026 averages for all industries are $5.42 per click, a 6.64% click-through rate and $66.69 per lead. Beauty and Personal Care sits below that on cost: $4.62 per click, 6.75% click-through, a 10.35% conversion rate and $39.25 per lead.

## Which Treatments Can You Advertise?

Check the rules for each treatment and country before writing ads. Google's healthcare and medicines policy does not allow ads for products that imply they are as effective as prescription drugs, or for non-government-approved products marketed as safe or effective. It also restricts speculative and experimental treatments. If a clinic offers prescription drug services, Google says advertisers must apply to serve ads for them.

In the UK the ASA states that botulinum toxin injections are prescription-only medicines and "cannot be advertised to the public". It also says references to a prescription-only medicine, even indirect ones, are likely to amount to promotion of it. Do not use Botox names or coded phrases in ads, and ask the ASA's Copy Advice service before publishing. Rules in the US and other countries differ, so ask your regulator or insurer.

## What Keywords Should a Clinic Start With?

Start with treatment plus place, such as the service name with your suburb or "near me". Intent is higher than broad terms like "skin care". Group ads by treatment, with one landing page per treatment that shows price ranges, time, aftercare and a booking button.

Add negative keywords early. Google explains that negative keywords stop ads showing for matching searches and do not match close variants, so add plurals and synonyms yourself. Typical negatives for a clinic are "jobs", "course", "training", "diy" and "at home", plus treatments you do not offer. Review the Search terms report weekly.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local?

Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km. It recommends broad "Presence or interest" location targeting for Search, and says presence-only targeting suits sensitive verticals with strict limits or advertisers who want to avoid people outside the target area. Test presence-only if clicks come from places you cannot serve.

Also set ad schedules around reception hours if you want calls answered. Call asset clicks cost the same as headline clicks, and you can show the asset only on chosen days and hours.

## Can You Remarket to Past Clients?

Be careful. Google treats Health as a sensitive interest category. Advertisers promoting in these categories cannot use advertiser-curated audiences such as Customer Match or lookalike segments, according to Google's personalized advertising policy. Whether a cosmetic treatment is treated as health depends on the ad and page, so check your account for restrictions before building lists.

## How Do You Track Bookings?

Count calls and online bookings as conversions. Google says you set a minimum call length and every call lasting at least that long is counted, so choose a length that matches a real enquiry. ## FAQ

### How much should a beauty clinic spend on Google Ads?
There is no standard figure. At LocaliQ's $4.62 average click, $1,000 buys roughly 216 clicks. Start with an amount you can test for 60 to 90 days.

### Can I advertise Botox in the UK?
No. The ASA says botulinum toxin injections are prescription-only medicines and cannot be advertised to the public, and indirect references can count as promotion.

### Do calls count as conversions?
Yes. Set a minimum call length that reflects a real enquiry.

### Can I use customer lists for targeting?
Possibly not. Advertisers promoting in Google's sensitive categories, which include Health, cannot use Customer Match or lookalike segments.
