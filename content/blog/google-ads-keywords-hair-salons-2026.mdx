Hair salon Google Ads have one significant advantage over most other local businesses: the booking intent is explicit and recurring. Someone searching "balayage near me" isn't casually browsing — they want an appointment. And once you win that client, they come back every 6–8 weeks for years.

This means the cost to acquire a hair salon client through Google Ads is justified at a higher CPA than most business types. A new client worth $150–250/year for 3+ years should make you willing to spend $30–60 to acquire them.

Here are the keywords, campaign structures, and settings that make it work.

## The Landing Page Problem Most Salons Have

The most common reason hair salon Google Ads don't convert: traffic goes to the salon homepage instead of a dedicated booking page.

A converting hair salon landing page includes:
- A visible "Book Now" button above the fold that opens your booking system directly
- 6–10 photos of actual client results (not stock photos)
- Your top services with starting prices
- 5+ Google reviews embedded or quoted
- Your address, parking info, and hours
- A phone number that's click-to-call on mobile

If you use Booksy, Fresha, or StyleSeat — link directly to your booking page. If clients have to navigate through the website to find the booking button, you'll lose 40–60% of your conversions.

---

## Bidding Strategy: What to Use at Each Stage

**Month 1–2: Maximise Clicks**
You don't have conversion data yet. Maximise Clicks gets you the most traffic within your budget, which builds the data you need.

Set a maximum CPC bid cap at $8–10 to prevent overspending on individual clicks.

**Month 3+: Target CPA**
Once you have 30+ conversions tracked, switch to Target CPA. Set your target at a CPA you can afford — typically $40–70 for a hair salon new client.

Google's algorithm will then optimise toward your target, reducing wasted spend on clicks that don't book.

---

## Smart Features to Enable

**Lead form extensions:** Let people submit their name and phone number directly from the search results, without visiting your website. Works well for "book a consultation" offers.

**Location extensions:** Shows your address and distance. Essential for walk-in searches.

**Promotion extensions:** Use for specific offers — "New clients: 20% off first colour service" or "January: Free deep conditioning with any colour."

**Call reporting:** Enable call tracking so Google can see when ad clicks lead to phone calls. This dramatically improves Smart Bidding performance.

## Competitive Benchmarks for Hair Salon Google Ads

Across hair salon campaigns I manage, here are typical ranges for a well-optimised account:

- **CTR:** 4–10% (below 3% means ads or keywords need improvement)
- **Avg CPC:** $2.50–6 (higher in London, NYC, Sydney; lower in smaller markets)
- **Conversion rate:** 15–30% (from click to booking inquiry)
- **Cost per booked appointment:** $25–60
- **New client LTV (Year 1):** $300–600 for colour clients, $150–300 for cut-only

At a $40 cost per booking with a $400 first-year value, the ROI math works even at lower end conversion rates.
