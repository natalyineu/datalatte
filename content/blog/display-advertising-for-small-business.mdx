Display advertising for a small business means banner and image ads shown on websites and apps. Clicks are cheap, but leads cost more than from search. LocaliQ's 2026 display benchmarks, based on 7,074 of its customer campaigns, show click costs from $1.65 (Education and Instruction) to $5.58 (Beauty and Personal Care), a click-through rate between 0.12% and 0.40%, and cost per lead between $65.37 and $230.44. Use display to remind people who already know you, not as your first channel.

This guide compares display with search, lists what Google's own help says about setup and the Display-to-Demand Gen change, and works through an example with labelled assumptions.

## How Much Does Display Advertising Cost?

LocaliQ's display benchmarks (medians for standard display campaigns) are below, next to its search figures for the same categories from its 2026 search report. LocaliQ does not publish a display CPM or conversion rate, so none appears here.

| Category | Display CPC | Display CTR | Display cost per lead | Search cost per lead |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Animals and Pets | $3.29 | 0.19% | $115.58 | $31.50 |
| Beauty and Personal Care | $5.58 | 0.15% | $115.58 | $39.25 |
| Restaurants and Food | $4.18 | 0.12% | $97.51 | $30.57 |
| Education and Instruction | $1.65 | 0.40% | $106.70 | $77.48 |
| Home and Home Improvement | $3.49 | 0.19% | $65.37 | $90.92 |

In four of these five categories, a lead from search is cheaper than a lead from display. Home improvement is the exception in these numbers. A lead is whatever the advertiser counted, so definitions differ, and your results will too.

## When Does Display Make Sense for a Local Business?

Display fits when people already know who you are and need a reminder. Google's help says data segments let you reach people who previously visited your website or app, which is the use case most small businesses can afford. It is a weak fit for catching people who are ready to book today, which is what search ads do.

Build search and your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) first. Then add a small display budget aimed at website visitors. If you have no website traffic yet, there is nobody to remind.

## What Is Google's Display Network?

Google describes its Display Network as websites, apps and Google-owned properties such as YouTube and Gmail, citing 35 million websites and apps. Optimized targeting is the default and uses signals such as keywords on your landing page. Responsive display ads are the default format: you upload images, headlines, logos and descriptions and Google assembles them.

Google's best-practice page says Display campaigns are moving to Demand Gen. It states that from June 2026 eligible advertisers can move campaigns with a migration tool, and that new campaigns can later only be created in Demand Gen, with remaining campaigns migrated automatically. Check your account for the current state before building.

## What Creative Do Display Ads Need?

Google lists up to 15 images in three aspect ratios and recommends 5 to 10 images per ratio. Minimum is one landscape and one square image.

| Ratio | Recommended size | Minimum size |
|---|---|---|
| Landscape 1.91:1 | 1200 x 628 | 600 x 314 |
| Square 1:1 | 1200 x 1200 | 300 x 300 |
| Vertical 9:16 | 900 x 1600 | 600 x 1067 |

Files must be JPG or PNG and under 5120 KB. Use one clear offer, your logo and a plain call to action. Avoid several near-identical images.

## The Math: What Does $500 a Month Buy?

All inputs are **assumptions** built on LocaliQ's Animals and Pets display median. A "lead" is whatever you track, such as a call or booking form.

| Input | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly budget | $500 | Assumption |
| Cost per click | $3.29 | LocaliQ display median |
| Clicks | about 152 | Budget divided by CPC |
| Cost per lead | $115.58 | LocaliQ display median |
| Leads | about 4.3 | Budget divided by CPL |
| Same budget on search at $31.50 per lead | about 15.9 leads | LocaliQ search median |
| Value of a customer | $200 | Assumption |
| Leads that become customers | 50% | Assumption |

At these inputs display brings about two customers worth roughly $430, while search brings about eight worth roughly $1,590. Display could still pay if it lifts the conversion of people you already reached, but the benchmark gives no proof of that. Measure it with a before and after comparison.

## FAQ

### How much does display advertising cost per click?

LocaliQ's 2026 medians range from $1.65 to $5.58 by industry. Animals and Pets is $3.29 and Restaurants and Food is $4.18.

### Is display better than search ads?

For cost per lead, LocaliQ's numbers favour search in most of the categories above. Display builds familiarity and retargets visitors, which search cannot do on its own.

### What is a good click-through rate for display ads?

LocaliQ's industry medians run from 0.12% to 0.40%. The USA TODAY Network sample shows 0.26% for high-impact display and 0.28% for native display.

### Is Google Display being replaced?

Google's help page says Display campaigns are moving to Demand Gen, with a voluntary migration from June 2026 and later automatic migration. Check your account.

### How much should I spend on display ads?

No source sets a rule. Start below your search budget and judge by cost per lead.
