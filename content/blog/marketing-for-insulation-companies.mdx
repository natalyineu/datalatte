Insulation companies win leads by being the clear local answer to a specific problem: a cold room, a high energy bill, an attic that needs work. The channels that fit are Google search ads and Local Services Ads, a strong Business Profile with reviews, and proof of what the job includes. Do not advertise the federal 25C tax credit for 2026 jobs, because the IRS says it covers property placed in service before December 31, 2025.

This guide is for residential insulation and air-sealing contractors in the US. Costs come from LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks, claims from ENERGY STAR and the IRS. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Does Marketing Cost for an Insulation Company?

LocaliQ's 2026 Search benchmarks list no insulation category. Home and Home Improvement is the closest: $8.33 per click, 6.47% click-through, 8.05% conversion and $90.92 per lead, against $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead across all industries. Your real figures depend on your city and the job type.

Facebook's Home and Home Improvement lead campaigns in LocaliQ's 2026 report show a $2.18 click, 5.32% conversion and $42.95 per lead. A Facebook lead is usually less ready to buy than a searcher. Compare cost per signed job, not cost per lead.

## Can You Still Advertise the Federal Tax Credit?

Not for 2026 jobs. The IRS page on the Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit says the credit applies to qualifying property placed in service on or after January 1, 2023 and before December 31, 2025. Insulation and air-sealing materials that met IECC standards qualified under the overall $1,200 annual cap, but labor for installation did not.

So remove credit claims from your ads and site unless a customer's work was finished in 2025. Check state, utility and local rebate programs separately, and link to the program page instead of quoting amounts.

## What Can You Say About Savings?

ENERGY STAR's page says air sealing and insulation can provide up to 10% savings on annual energy bills. That is a ceiling from a government-backed program, with a methodology page behind it, not a promise for a given house. Quote it with the wording "up to" and link the source.

Do not publish a savings number or payback period you cannot support. If you give estimates, base them on an energy audit of that home and say so in writing.

## How Do You Get Found on Google?

Run Search ads for the service plus city and for problem phrases, and add negative keywords for jobs, DIY, courses and product shopping you do not sell. Google says negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals. Use a radius around your service area; Google's minimum is 1 km.

Check whether Local Services Ads are open to your trade in your area. Google says LSAs charge per valid lead, and are moving into Performance Max with pay-per-lead goals. See [Google Ads for contractors](/blog/google-ads-for-contractors) and our [Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-services-ads-complete-guide).

## How Do You Build Trust Before the Quote?

Homeowners are letting you into the attic and paying thousands, so proof matters. Show licence and insurance numbers where your state requires them, photos of before and after jobs with the home type, the product and R-value used, and the warranty terms. Add a short page per service: attic insulation, wall insulation, air sealing.

Reviews do the heavy lifting. Our [reviews strategy for home services](/blog/reviews-strategy-for-home-services) shows how to ask after each job, and our [Google Business Profile guide](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-for-local-seo) covers the profile setup.

## What Should the Offer Be?

A free or low-cost assessment is the usual first step: inspect, measure, quote. State what the assessment includes and how long it takes, and do not call a paid inspection free. Make the next step one tap: call, book a time, or send photos.

Run seasonal pushes before cold and hot weather, since interest in comfort and bills rises then. Test your own dates against your calendar of calls instead of relying on a general rule.

## FAQ

### How much should an insulation company spend on ads?

There is no standard. LocaliQ shows $8.33 clicks in Home and Home Improvement, so $1,500 buys roughly 180 clicks. Start at a level you can run for two months.

### Is the 25C credit still available?

Not for property placed in service after December 31, 2025, according to the IRS page. Do not promote it for new jobs.

### Can I promise energy savings?

No. ENERGY STAR says up to 10%. Use "up to" and cite the source.

### Are Facebook ads good for insulation?

They can generate leads for seasonal offers, but leads are less ready than search leads. Compare cost per job. See [Facebook ads for home services](/blog/facebook-ads-for-home-services).

### How important are reviews?

BrightLocal's 2026 survey finds 97% of consumers read local business reviews. Ask after every job.
