To speed up a small business website, aim for Google's "good" Core Web Vitals at the 75th percentile of real visits: Largest Contentful Paint within 2.5 seconds, Interaction to Next Paint of 200 milliseconds or less, and Cumulative Layout Shift of 0.1 or less. For most small sites the biggest wins are one smaller hero image, no lazy-loading on that image, reserved space for images and fewer third-party scripts.

Google says Core Web Vitals are used by its ranking systems, but also that good scores do not guarantee top rankings. Treat speed as a way to keep visitors, with a small ranking benefit.

## How do you measure your site's speed?

Use real-user data first and lab tests second. PageSpeed Insights shows field data from the Chrome User Experience Report over a rolling 28-day period, plus a Lighthouse lab test. Lab data is useful for debugging; field data shows what visitors actually experience. web.dev notes that lab measurement is not a substitute for field measurement.

Search Console has a Core Web Vitals report that groups URLs as Good, Need improvement or Poor, using the same field data. A group is rated by its slowest metric, and small sites may not have enough data to appear. If so, rely on PageSpeed Insights for your key pages.

## What slows down a small business site?

Google's speed guidance points to the same short list: large images, slow server response, render-blocking code, heavy JavaScript and third-party scripts such as chat widgets, trackers and booking embeds. For LCP, web.dev suggests a guide split of roughly 40% server response time, under 10% load delay, 40% download time and under 10% render delay. Those shares are guidelines, not rules.

Find which part of your own page is largest before changing anything. Our [website hosting guide](/blog/website-hosting-for-small-business) covers the server side.

## How do you fix a slow Largest Contentful Paint?

On most local sites the LCP element is the hero image or a headline. web.dev's advice:

- Never lazy-load the LCP image.
- Add fetchpriority="high" to it, on one or two images at most.
- Put the image in the page HTML so the browser finds it early, not only in CSS or scripts.
- Compress and modernise the image, and use a CDN.
- Reduce redirects to improve server response time.

Images are also the largest part of page weight according to Google's image guidance, which supports formats such as WebP and AVIF. See our guide to [hero sections](/blog/website-hero-section-examples-local-business).

## How do you fix layout shift and slow taps?

Layout shift comes from images or videos without dimensions, fonts that change size when they load, and ads or widgets that resize. Set width and height on images and reserve space for embeds. web.dev also advises animating with transforms instead of changing size or position.

For INP, long tasks block the main thread. Split heavy scripts, defer non-critical work and remove scripts you do not use. web.dev does not single out third-party scripts on its INP page, but its general speed guide says to identify slow ones and load them efficiently.

## Should you lazy-load images?

Lazy-load images below the fold and never the first screen. web.dev explains that loading="lazy" delays fetching until an image is near the viewport, and warns against using it on images visible at load, especially the LCP image, because it delays them.

## Worked example: what could a fix be worth?

All inputs are **assumptions**; no source here gives a conversion lift from speed.

| Input (assumption) | Value |

| Monthly mobile visitors | 1,500 |

| Rate after fixing LCP from 4.5 s to 2.4 s | 2.3% |

Today: 1,500 x 2% = 30 bookings, or $2,400. After: 1,500 x 2.3% = 34.5 bookings, or $2,760. The difference is $360 a month. If your rate does not move, you still gain a faster site, so test with your own analytics before paying for large rebuilds. ## What should you ask a developer or agency?

Ask for before and after PageSpeed Insights field data on your five main pages, a list of third-party scripts and what each one earns, and the image formats used. Add speed to ongoing [maintenance](/blog/website-maintenance-cost-small-business) so new plugins do not undo the work.

## FAQ

### What is a good page speed for a local business?

Google's thresholds are LCP within 2.5 seconds, INP of 200 ms or less and CLS of 0.1 or less, measured at the 75th percentile of visits.

### Does page speed affect Google rankings?

Google says Core Web Vitals are used by its ranking systems, but that good scores do not guarantee top rankings and that there is no single signal.

### Why does PageSpeed Insights show two different results?

One is field data from real Chrome users over 28 days; the other is a lab simulation using Lighthouse. They differ because real visitors use varied devices and networks.

### Is a free website builder fast enough?

It depends on the theme, images and add-ons, so test your own pages. No builder is quoted here because we did not verify any builder's speed data.

### What is the first thing to fix?

The hero image: right size, modern format, not lazy-loaded, and with dimensions set.
