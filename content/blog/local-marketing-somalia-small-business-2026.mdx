Local marketing in Somalia has to combine online and offline channels, because only 27.6% of people use the internet. DataReportal counts 5.47 million internet users among 19.8 million residents in October 2025, and 72.4% are offline. Facebook ad reach is 2.70 million and TikTok adults reach 3.51 million, so social media works for the connected minority, while shop signs, word of mouth and mobile money cover the rest.

## How many Somalis are online?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report lists 19.8 million people, up 3.4% in a year. Internet users grew by 178 thousand (+3.4%), but penetration did not change, so about 14.3 million people are offline. Social media user identities total 3.51 million, 17.7% of the population and up 14.1%.

There are 11.5 million mobile connections, 58.1% of the population. The report warns that some connections carry only voice and SMS, that social identities are not unique people, and that internet figures may lag real adoption. The World Bank lists GDP per capita of about US$661 for 2025, so budgets are small.

## Which platforms have the biggest ad reach?

These are advertising reach figures, not active users, and DataReportal warns they can be revised.

- TikTok: 3.51 million, adults 18+ only (37.7% of adults)
- Facebook: 2.70 million, 13.6% of the population
- Messenger: 1.40 million
- Instagram: 511 thousand, 2.6%
- LinkedIn: 420 thousand
- X: 143 thousand

Facebook and TikTok are the practical choices for a consumer business. Instagram reaches under a fifth of Facebook's audience. The report excerpt checked did not give a YouTube figure, so none is claimed.

## How does mobile money affect marketing?

Somali customers commonly pay by mobile money rather than cards. A 2018 World Bank report, as summarised by secondary sources, found almost three quarters of Somalis aged 16 and over used mobile money. Hormuud's own claims, which are company statements, say over two thirds of payments run through such platforms. Hormuud received the first mobile money licence from the central bank in February 2021.

No current central bank figure on mobile money use was found, so treat the old numbers as dated. In 2025, coverage reported a nationwide instant payment system with a QR standard (SOMQR). For a shop, show the accepted wallet number or QR on every post and sign.

## Can Somali businesses buy ads from Google?

Google Ads Help lists Somalia among the countries that do not support prepay (manual payments) for new accounts. A Somali advertiser would need automatic payments or monthly invoicing if eligible, which usually means a card or bank account that Google accepts. Whether a particular Somali business can open a billing profile was not verified, so test it in the account settings before planning a campaign.

No Somali cost-per-click or cost-per-thousand-impressions benchmark exists in the sources checked, so none is quoted. Meta ad tools show an estimated audience size before you pay, which is the safest guide.

## What about advertising rules and sanctions?

No Somali advertising regulator or consumer-protection code was found in the sources checked. Any legal, sanctions or payment-compliance item for Somalia is therefore unverified. A business that sells across borders or receives money from abroad should ask its bank or a local lawyer before launching campaigns.

## How could a Somali shop test channels? (worked example)

All numbers are **assumptions**, not benchmarks. A pharmacy in Hargeisa has 8 hours a week.

| Channel | Assumption | What to count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shop sign and printed price list with wallet number | 2 hours | Customers who mention it |
| Facebook page with opening hours, location pin and phone | 3 hours | Messages and calls |
| WhatsApp or Messenger replies | 2 hours | Orders taken by chat |
| Ask every customer how they found you | 1 hour | Tally in a notebook |

Because most people are offline, the tally decides where the next hour goes. Repeat customers can be reached by text, as in our [SMS marketing for retail](/blog/sms-marketing-for-retail) piece. If chat orders beat everything else, move the posting hours to answering messages faster. ## FAQ

### How many people use the internet in Somalia?
5.47 million, 27.6% of the population, in October 2025 (DataReportal).

### Is Facebook the best platform for Somali businesses?
Its ad reach is 2.70 million, below TikTok adults at 3.51 million. Both are ad-tool estimates.

### Should a small shop rely on social media alone?
No. With 72.4% of people offline, signs, word of mouth and mobile money also matter.

### How much do ads cost in Somalia?
No sourced price per click was found, so none is given here.

### Are there legal rules I must follow?
No Somali advertising or data rules were verified for this article. Check with a local lawyer.
