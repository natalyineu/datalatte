## How much should a Miami coffee shop spend on Meta Ads?

AdWave's coffee shop comparison lists Instagram plus Meta Ads at $300–$1,500 per month, with first results in 1–3 weeks ([AdWave coffee shop advertising guide](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-coffee-shops-cafes)). Its restaurant guide gives the same $300–$1,500+ monthly range for Instagram and Facebook ([AdWave restaurant advertising](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-restaurants)).

Share of total budget matters too. The same coffee shop guide allocates 45% of budget to Instagram and Meta Ads in the pre-launch and year-one mix, then 30% in years two to four.

A smaller total marketing budget means a smaller Meta line. Plan for several weeks of optimisation before judging results, because the vendor sources describe Meta as taking weeks to optimise.

## What does a Meta ad cost per click and per visit?

AdWave's how-to guide puts typical Meta costs at $5–$15 per thousand impressions and $1–$3 per click ([AdWave small business marketing guide](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-small-business-guide)). These are cost inputs, not outcomes.

The more useful planning number for a coffee shop is cost per first visit. AdWave estimates it at $5–$15 in dense urban markets and $10–$25 in suburban areas ([AdWave coffee shop advertising guide](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-coffee-shops-cafes)). A dense Miami neighbourhood may sit near the lower end, while a shop on a quieter corridor may sit higher.

Check your own ad account after two to three weeks and compare its real cost per visit against these ranges.

## Which Meta ad formats work for coffee shops?

Short-form video is the format to start with. Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, as cited by AdWave, says short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram ([AdWave electrician advertising page, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/electrician-advertising-reaching-homeowners)). Treat this as a directional finding from a vendor summary.

AdWave's coffee shop launch plan uses Instagram Reels ads at $30–$50 per day with a "first drink on us" offer, built from latte art video, morning atmosphere and customer photos ([AdWave coffee shop advertising guide](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-coffee-shops-cafes)). For production ideas, see [Instagram Reels for coffee shops](/blog/instagram-reels-for-coffee-shops).

## Should coffee shops run ads on Facebook, Instagram or both?

Instagram ads run through the same system as Facebook ads. Because Instagram is owned by Meta, one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook, or both ([AdWave Instagram advertising for local business](https://adwave.com/resources/instagram-advertising-for-local-business)).

Meta Ads Manager is the central tool for both platforms. Adobe describes it as a single point to run ads across Instagram and Facebook ([Adobe Express, secondary](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-advertising)). Turrboo lists Facebook, Instagram and Messenger as covered platforms ([Turrboo, secondary](https://turrboo.com/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media)).

For a step-by-step setup, read the [Instagram ads playbook for coffee shops](/blog/instagram-ads-for-coffee-shops-step-by-step-playbook).

## How should you target people near your shop?

AdWave's example for a local coffee shop runs Facebook and Instagram ads promoting a new fall latte to everyone within a five-mile radius of the store ([AdWave multi-channel marketing](https://adwave.com/resources/multi-channel-marketing-approach)). Use that as a starting radius, then narrow it in dense areas and widen it where foot traffic is thin.

Radius is a setting you test, not a rule. Compare cost per visit across two radii over the same weeks before deciding which one to keep.

Meta's apps reach an average of 3.56 billion daily active people, according to AdWave ([AdWave Facebook advertising for small business](https://adwave.com/resources/facebook-advertising-for-small-business)). That scale is reach across the world, so the local radius is what limits your audience.

## What does a worked Meta budget look like?

The following example uses assumed inputs. Assumption: total monthly marketing budget of $2,000. Applying AdWave's year-one share of 45% gives a Meta budget of $900 per month. The table divides that budget and other levels by AdWave's cost-per-visit ranges.

| Monthly Meta budget (assumption) | Dense urban first visits | Suburban first visits |
|---|---|---|
| $300 | 20–60 | 12–30 |
| $900 | 60–180 | 36–90 |
| $1,500 | 100–300 | 60–150 |

The visit ranges are simple divisions: budget divided by $15 and $5 for dense urban, and by $25 and $10 for suburban. They are planning estimates, not guarantees. At the $900 level, the click ceiling from AdWave's $1–$3 cost per click is 300–900 clicks, and actual spend will land somewhere within that range depending on what you buy.

## How do you track whether Meta Ads bring customers in?

Set up tracking before spending. Install the Meta Pixel on your website, fire it on the booking confirmation or contact form, and create a conversion event for each lead. Add UTM parameters to every ad URL so Google Analytics can separate Meta-sourced visits from other traffic.

Measure cost per booking or cost per first visit, not impressions or reach alone. If your Meta leads land in a list, a follow-up sequence matters, and the [marketing automation guide for local coffee shops](/blog/marketing-automation-for-local-coffee-shops) covers that next step.

## FAQ

### How much does Meta advertising cost for a coffee shop per month?

AdWave's 2026 coffee shop comparison lists Instagram plus Meta Ads at $300–$1,500 per month. Start at the lower end for a test and raise the budget only after you see cost per visit in your own account.

### What is a realistic cost per first visit from Meta?

AdWave estimates $5–$15 in dense urban markets and $10–$25 in suburban areas. Your real number depends on creative, radius and offer, so track it for at least two to three weeks.

### Should a coffee shop run ads on Facebook or Instagram?

Both can run in one campaign through Meta Ads Manager. Many small businesses start with Instagram for visual content and add Facebook where the audience responds.
