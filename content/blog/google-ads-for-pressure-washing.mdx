A pressure washing business should run a small, local Google Search campaign on high-intent phrases, send calls and quote requests to one landing page, and block job-seeker and do-it-yourself searches with negative keywords. No pressure washing benchmark is published, so the nearest one is Home and Home Improvement. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 data lists $8.33 per click, 8.05% conversion and $90.92 per lead for that category.

This guide is for owner-operators and small crews serving homeowners and commercial properties in the US, UK, Canada and Australia. Every figure comes from a source listed at the end. Anything illustrative is labelled as an assumption.

## How Much Does Google Ads Cost for Pressure Washing?

There is no pressure washing figure in the public benchmarks, so treat the numbers below as a range for planning. For all industries the 2026 average is $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead. Home and Home Improvement costs more at $8.33 per click and $90.92 per lead, with an 8.05% conversion rate. LocaliQ defines conversion rate as leads divided by clicks.

| Benchmark (US, 2026) | CPC | Conversion rate | Cost per lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All industries | $5.42 | 8.18% | $66.69 |
| Home and Home Improvement | $8.33 | 8.05% | $90.92 |

Your own cost will depend on your town, season and how many competitors bid. Read the first month of your account as the real benchmark.

## Should You Use Search Ads or Local Services Ads?

Start with Search ads, because you control keywords, location and landing page. Local Services Ads are a separate Google product with their own sign-up flow and eligibility. The Google help page we opened does not list service categories, so pressure washing eligibility is not confirmed here. Check whether your area and service are offered when you start sign-up in your own account.

If Local Services Ads are available, the main change is how you are charged and shown, not how you get jobs. Pricing for them varies by area and category, and no official figure was found. Do not budget from a vendor's blog post.

## Which Keywords Bring Pressure Washing Jobs?

Pick phrases that show someone wants a quote, then add a place. The list below is a starting set, not search volume data.

| Intent | Example phrases | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Residential | driveway cleaning near me, house washing, patio cleaning | Homeowner with a specific surface |
| Commercial | parking lot cleaning, storefront washing, dumpster pad cleaning | Property managers, repeat contracts |
| Specific surface | fence washing, deck cleaning, roof moss removal | Narrow intent, often cheaper |

Avoid single words such as "pressure washer". Those searches are mostly people buying or renting equipment. Use phrase or exact match to keep control, and review the Search terms report every week.

## How Do You Stop Wasted Clicks?

Negative keywords are your main defence. Google explains that a negative keyword stops your ad showing for searches that match it, and that negatives do not match close variants. You need to add singular, plural and synonym forms yourself.

Start with these groups:

- **Equipment:** "pressure washer for sale", "rent", "parts", "hose", "nozzle"
- **Jobs and training:** "jobs", "hiring", "course", "salary"
- **Do-it-yourself:** "how to", "diy", "tutorial" if you have no guide to show

Add them at campaign level, or build an account-level list so every campaign inherits it ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453972)).

## How Do You Keep Ads Inside Your Service Area?

Travel time is your biggest hidden cost, so limit the map. Google Ads lets you target regions, cities or postal codes depending on the country, or a radius around a location. A radius must be at least 1 km ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722043)).

Google recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting for Search, but says "Presence" fits when you only want people in specific locations. A crew that will not drive past a county line should test Presence. Then check which towns send clicks and drop the ones you do not serve.

## Should You Track Phone Calls?

Yes. Pressure washing jobs are often booked by phone after someone sees photos or reviews. Google says clicks on a call asset cost the same as headline clicks, and you pay when the number is clicked ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453991)).

Use a number that forwards to your mobile, and note on each call where the job is. A call from outside your area costs you the click even if you decline the work. Pair calls with a quote form for people who will not phone, and set both as conversions so you can compare them.

## Why Does Your Google Business Profile Matter Too?

People often compare a few results before they call, and your profile sits next to the ad. Google says local ranking depends on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)). Ask customers for a review with a link or QR code. Google prohibits offering a discount or gift in exchange for a review ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)).

Work through our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) before spending on ads, then add before-and-after photos to the profile.

## The Math: What Does a $600 Month Return?

All inputs are **assumptions** built on the Home and Home Improvement benchmark. A "lead" is a call or quote request.

| Input | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $600 | assumption |
| CPC | $8.33 | LocaliQ benchmark |
| Clicks | about 72 | 600 / 8.33 |
| Conversion rate | 8.05% | LocaliQ benchmark |
| Leads | about 5.8 | 72 x 8.05% |
| Cost per lead | about $103 | 600 / 5.8 |

Cost per job is roughly $260 here. At a $350 average job the month covers its cost, but only just, once labour and fuel are counted. Larger commercial contracts or repeat washes change the result more than click price does. ## FAQ

### How much should a pressure washing business spend on Google Ads?
There is no standard figure. At the $8.33 benchmark click, $600 buys roughly 72 clicks. Begin with an amount you can afford to lose for a month and scale once you know your cost per booked job.

### Are Local Services Ads available for pressure washing?
Not confirmed. The Google help page we opened did not list categories. Check the sign-up flow in your account for your area.

### Is Google Ads or SEO better for pressure washing?
Ads give faster results and a clear cost per lead. A strong Business Profile and reviews improve your free map listing. Most small crews do both, starting with the profile.

### What conversion rate should I expect?
The nearest benchmark is 8.05% for Home and Home Improvement. Your rate depends on your page, price and speed of reply, so use your own data after a month.

### Should I bid on "pressure washer" as a keyword?
No. It mostly attracts people buying or renting a machine. Use service phrases and add equipment terms as negatives.
