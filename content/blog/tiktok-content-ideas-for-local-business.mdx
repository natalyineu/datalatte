The best TikTok ideas for a local business show something a customer can come in and do: the drink being made, the haircut finishing, the dog leaving fluffy, the class starting. Below are 22 ideas for cafés, salons, groomers and gyms. They are suggestions, not tested results. No public source publishes view counts by video type for local businesses, so measure your own.

Pew's 2026 US survey found 31% of adults use TikTok, rising to 65% among adults 18 to 29. That makes it a strong place for younger local customers and a weaker one for older ones.

## What Music Can a Business Account Use?

TikTok's help page says the Commercial Music Library is a pre-cleared library of 1 million songs that is free for businesses to use. It also says businesses cannot use the general music library for commercial usage, and that for organic branded content, video ads and branded content they should use the Commercial Music Library instead.

If you reuse another user's original sound, TikTok advises getting proper licensing advice. The safe habit for a shop: pick music from the Commercial Music Library, or film with no music and add a voice-over.

## Which Ideas Work for Any Local Business?

These need no special equipment and suit any shop. Use them when you are unsure what to film.

1. Opening routine: the first ten minutes before the doors open.
2. Meet the team: one person, one fact, one thing they recommend.
3. A customer question answered in 30 seconds.
4. The price list explained: what is included and what is extra.
5. Behind the scenes of one task that takes skill.
6. A myth about your trade, corrected.

## What Should a Coffee Shop Film?

Make one product look good and show the sound of the work.

7. A single drink, start to finish, with no talking.
8. A new seasonal drink: what goes in it.
9. Latte art attempts, including the failures.
10. A regular's usual order, with permission.

## What Should a Hair Salon Film?

Salons have the strongest visual format: a result that appears on screen.

11. A before and after of a cut or colour, with the client's consent.
12. A styling tip for a hair type, in under a minute.
13. A day in the life of a stylist.
14. What a consultation covers, step by step.

Always ask for written permission before posting a client's face. See our [salon before-and-after TikTok guide](/blog/hair-salon-tiktok-strategy-before-after-content) for consent wording.

## How Do You Plan a Month of Posts? (Worked Example)

The numbers below are **assumptions**, not benchmarks. A salon plans one month of content.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Posts per week | 3 |
| Filming time per video | 15 minutes |
| Editing and captioning per video | 15 minutes |
| Videos in the month | 12 |
| Total time | 6 hours |
| Mix | 4 before and after, 4 tips, 2 team, 2 behind the scenes |

Review after 12 videos. Keep the formats that produced profile visits, bookings or direction requests. Drop the rest. Track "how did you find us" at the till to see whether TikTok is working.

## Can You Turn a Good Video Into an Ad?

Yes. TikTok's help says Spark Ads let you use organic posts in your advertising, and each ad account can run up to 10,000 Spark Ads. It also says you cannot edit a post's caption after it has been authorized as an ad, and a private video becomes public once it is used in a campaign. So finalize the caption before authorizing. ## FAQ

### How often should a local business post on TikTok?

No source gives one correct number. Three posts a week is a rhythm many small teams can keep. Pick what you can sustain for three months.

### Do I need a business account?

TikTok's music rules are different for businesses, so using a business account with the Commercial Music Library avoids licensing problems.

### How long should my videos be?

TikTok does not state an ideal length on the help pages checked. Test short and longer videos and compare your own results.

### Can I use trending songs?

Not from the general library for commercial use on a business account. Use the Commercial Music Library.

### Which businesses should skip TikTok?

If your customers are mostly over 50, check Pew: 31% of adults 50 to 64 and 11% of those 65 plus use TikTok.
