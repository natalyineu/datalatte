## How Many Small Businesses Compete for BC Customers?

ISED's key small business statistics for December 2024 list 173,246 employer businesses in British Columbia. Of these, 170,512 (98.4%) are small, defined as having 1 to 99 paid employees. The table counts businesses with paid employees, so sole operators with no staff are generally excluded, and the real field of competitors is larger.

## What Should a BC Local Business Fix First?

Start with your Google Business Profile. Google says local results are ranked on relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Prominence draws on links from other sites and on your reviews and ratings.

Practical steps from Google's own guidance:

- Choose a category that describes what you do.
- Keep hours accurate, including special hours.
- Add photos and videos of your work and space.
- Reply to customer reviews.

## Does Paid Search Make Sense in BC?

It can, if you control the area and the cost. Google Ads lets you target a country, a region, a city or postal codes, or a radius of at least 1 km around a point such as your address. For a business that serves one neighbourhood, a radius or a short list of cities keeps spend near the shop.

No official source publishes a BC average cost per click, so plan with your own data. Run a small test, read the search terms report, and add negative keywords for jobs and free-intent searches. If phone calls matter, Google says you pay for call asset clicks at the same rate as headline clicks, and calls can be counted as conversions when call reporting is on.

## How Should You Market in More Than One Language?

BC is multilingual. Statistics Canada's 2021 Census analysis reports that 17.1% of people in British Columbia speak a language other than English or French predominantly at home. In the Vancouver metropolitan area, Mandarin is the language spoken most often at home by the most people among those languages. Nationally, the largest groups are Mandarin (530,000), Punjabi (520,000) and Cantonese (390,000).

Those are census counts, not your customers. Use them to decide what to test. If your staff speak a language, add it to your Business Profile description and a page on your site, and watch whether calls and bookings follow before you spend on translated ads.

## What Does CASL Require for Email and Text Marketing?

Canada's Anti-spam Legislation (CASL) applies to commercial electronic messages. Under section 11, an unsubscribe request must be honoured without delay and no later than 10 business days after it is sent. Section 20 caps penalties at CA$1 million per violation for individuals and CA$10 million per violation for any other person, such as a company.

The Act's purpose clause says penalties aim to promote compliance, not to punish. This article is not legal advice. Get consent in writing at the point of sale or booking, record it, and read the government's CASL guidance before you send. ## FAQ

### How many small businesses are in British Columbia?

ISED Canada counted 170,512 small employer businesses (1 to 99 employees) in BC in December 2024, which is 98.4% of the province's 173,246 employer businesses. Businesses with no paid employees are generally excluded from that count.

### What is the average Google Ads cost per click in BC?

No official source publishes it. Use your own account data after a small test, and treat any number quoted without a source with caution.

### Do I need consent to email customers in BC?

CASL applies across Canada, including BC. It requires you to honour unsubscribe requests within 10 business days at most. Check the government's CASL guidance for consent rules before you send.

### Should I advertise in languages other than English?

Test it. Census data shows many BC residents speak other languages at home, with Mandarin the leading one in the Vancouver area. Start with your profile and a web page, then measure calls before paying for translated ads.

### Can I pay Google for a better map ranking?

No. Google states there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Complete your profile, keep hours accurate and reply to reviews.
