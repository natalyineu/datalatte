Local Services Ads are worth checking only if your country lists your category. Google's US help page lists "Pet grooming" among eligible services. The UK and Canada pages I opened list home and property services and no pet grooming. Where you can run them, you pay for each valid lead, not each click, and Google does not publish a typical price per lead.

This guide covers who can use the ads, how leads are charged, and a worked example with assumed numbers. It is for groomers in the US, UK, Canada and Australia. Everything here comes from the sources at the end.

## Can Pet Groomers Use Local Services Ads?

It depends on the country. Google says Local Services Ads are available only for certain categories. The US list includes "Pet adoption", "Pet boarding", "Pet grooming", "Pet training" and "Veterinarian". The UK list I opened covers home and property services plus legal and estate agent services in Greater London, with no pet entries. The Canada list names sixteen categories, from appliance repair to window repair, with none for pets.

I did not open the Australian list, so check it in your account. Google also says eligibility depends on your area, so the account setup is the final test.

## How Do Local Services Ads Charge?

Per lead. Google says you are charged for each valid lead received through the ad, and that prices vary by location, job type, lead type and bidding mode. Message leads usually cost less than phone leads. Google does not state an average price.

A phone lead counts when you answer or call back and speak with the customer. A missed call during business hours can count if the caller stays on the line for more than 20 seconds. Texts, emails and voicemails count too, and so do booking requests in the US and Canada.

Compared with a Search campaign, where you pay for each click (see [Google Ads for dog groomers](/blog/google-ads-for-dog-groomers-complete-2026-guide)), a lead-based price shifts the risk of clicks that go nowhere to Google.

## What Does Google Check Before Your Ad Runs?

Google says businesses must pass a series of checks to run a Local Services ad. Depending on the category and region, that can include business verification, background checks on owners or service professionals, licence checks and insurance. Providers who pass are marked as screened for their category.

For a groomer in a supported country, expect to prepare business documents and insurance details. The exact requirements for pet grooming are not on the pages I read, so see the setup flow in your account.

## What Happens With Poor-Quality Leads?

Google checks leads when the customer makes contact, and invalid or low-quality leads are not charged. Charged leads are reassessed over time and can be credited automatically. You can also report a lead in the Feedback Survey. Credits are not available for health care verticals, tax specialists or advertisers in EMEA.

That matters if you offer grooming plus anything vet-adjacent, and for UK or EU readers, where automatic credits do not apply to the EEA.

## How Are Budgets Set?

Google says the weekly budget is a target, not a hard daily limit. Some weeks you may spend less, and some more, depending on demand. The monthly cap is the weekly budget times 30.4 divided by 7. After you reach it, the ad does not appear for the rest of the month unless you change the budget.

Google also says Local Services Ads is moving into Google Ads as a Performance Max campaign type with pay-per-lead goals. The first phase starts in August 2026 for select US advertisers, and non-US accounts follow in 2027, so your screens may differ from older guides.

## What Does a Lead Budget Buy? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**. Google publishes no average price per lead, so pick your own and test.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $400 |
| Price per lead | $30 |
| Leads | about 13 |
| Leads that book | 50% |
| New clients | about 6.5 |
| Cost per new client | about $62 |
| First groom | $75 |

At $30 per lead the first groom covers the ad cost. At $50 per lead and 40% booking, cost per new client is $125, well above the first groom, and repeat visits decide whether it pays. Test the price Google actually charges you before scaling.

## FAQ

### Are Local Services Ads available for dog groomers?
In the US, Google's list includes "Pet grooming". The UK and Canada lists I opened do not include any pet grooming.

### How much does a lead cost?
Google does not publish a typical price. It varies by location, job type, lead type and bidding mode.

### Do I pay for every lead?
You pay for valid leads. Invalid or low-quality leads are not charged, and some can be credited later, except in a few verticals and regions.

### Is the weekly budget a hard limit?
No. It is a target, and the monthly cap is the weekly budget times 30.4 divided by 7.

### Do I need insurance or a licence?
Where applicable, yes. Requirements depend on the category and location. Check the setup flow.
