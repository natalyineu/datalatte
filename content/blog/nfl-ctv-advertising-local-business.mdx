Yes, a local business can advertise around NFL games on streaming TV, usually by buying geo-targeted sports CTV inventory through a programmatic platform rather than a single game package. Sports inventory carries premium CPMs, so plan on a low-to-mid four-figure monthly budget as a realistic starting point, and treat the figures below as rough ranges that change by season, market and platform.

NFL games are among the most-watched programs on US television, and a growing share streams on services such as Amazon Prime Video and Peacock. That gives local advertisers a way to reach football audiences in their own metro area without a national TV budget.

## Where NFL Games Stream

NFL rights are split across several broadcasters and streamers, and the arrangements change from season to season. Check the current schedule before you plan, but as a general picture:

- **Amazon Prime Video** has carried Thursday Night Football. Local geo-targeting is generally available through Amazon's DSP or a managed-service partner.
- **Peacock (NBCUniversal)** streams Sunday Night Football and has carried select exclusive games. Local access typically goes through NBCUniversal sales or programmatic intermediaries.
- **ESPN and ABC** carry Monday Night Football and some playoff games, with streaming access via Disney's ad platform or programmatic buying.
- **Live TV streaming services** (YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV, Sling) carry games through local affiliates and sell their own ad inventory.
- **NFL+** offers games on mobile, with limited third-party ad inventory.

Audience size per game varies by matchup and platform, so ask any seller for current figures instead of relying on a headline number.

## What NFL CTV Ads Cost

Live sports is among the priciest streaming content because the audience is large, attentive and hard to reach elsewhere. As a rough guide, premium sports CPMs commonly run well above general streaming CTV CPMs, and playoff inventory costs more than regular-season games. Exact rates depend on the platform, the market, the targeting and how competitive the auction is, so get quotes before you commit. Our [CTV advertising cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data) breaks down how CPM is calculated.

A quick way to estimate reach: divide your budget by the CPM and multiply by 1,000 to get impressions, then divide by your target frequency to estimate households. For example, a $2,000 budget at a $50 CPM buys about 40,000 impressions, which is roughly 10,000 households at a frequency of four. Higher CPMs mean fewer households for the same money.

| Buying route | Typical entry point | Control over games |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct platform or local sales team | Often several thousand dollars minimum | High |
| Managed Amazon DSP campaign | Often several thousand dollars minimum | Medium |
| Programmatic DSP sports package | Often low four figures per month | Lower, you buy audience and content category |

Minimums change frequently, so confirm them with each provider.

## Who You Reach

NFL audiences have broadened over time and skew toward adults in their prime earning years, but demographics differ by game, platform and market. The practical advantage for a local advertiser is geographic: in a market with a team, you can reach households in your own area during the games they care about most.

## How to Buy Local NFL CTV Ads

### Option 1: Amazon DSP

Buying through Amazon's DSP can let you target by metro area or zip code. It typically requires working with an Amazon Advertising partner, and minimums are higher than most small budgets.

### Option 2: NBCUniversal and Disney local sales

For Peacock or ESPN and ABC adjacency, ask the local sales team about streaming packages tied to game windows. Minimums tend to be higher, and packages vary by market.

### Option 3: Programmatic DSPs

For smaller budgets, a programmatic platform is usually the most accessible route. You buy a sports CTV package with geographic and demographic targeting, and your ads run in sports content that is available programmatically, which may or may not include a particular NFL game. Platforms commonly used for this include The Trade Desk, StackAdapt and Simpli.fi, though availability and minimums change, so verify before choosing. ## Season Timing

Prices generally rise as the season progresses and peak around the playoffs. Early-season games tend to have strong viewership before the highest-demand windows, which often makes them better value for local budgets. Preseason is cheaper but has smaller audiences. If your business depends on game-day traffic, consider concentrating spend on weekends and primetime windows.

## Creative Best Practices

- **Use 15 or 30 seconds.** Test both. Shorter spots can stretch a budget further, but check completion rates on your platform.
- **Do not use NFL marks.** Team names, logos and the NFL name require licensing. Use phrases such as "game day" or "this weekend's big game" instead.
- **Be locally specific.** Mention your neighborhood or city.
- **Add one clear action.** A QR code or short URL in the closing seconds works well because many viewers hold a phone while watching.

## FAQ

**Q: Can a small local business afford NFL CTV advertising?**
A: Often yes, through programmatic buying with geo-targeting, though sports inventory is pricier than general streaming. Start with a modest test, measure results, and avoid buying more game windows than your budget can support.

**Q: Do I need to advertise all season or only on key games?**
A: Concentrating spend on the games and weeks that matter most to your customers is usually more efficient than spreading thin all season. Playoff windows cost more, so decide whether the extra reach justifies the price.

**Q: Can I target specific zip codes?**
A: Most DSPs support geo-targeting at the zip code, city, DMA or radius level. Smaller radii reduce waste but also limit scale.

**Q: What is the difference between buying on Amazon, Peacock or a DSP?**
A: Platform buys give you specific games and more control, usually with higher minimums. A DSP aggregates sports inventory across several streamers, with lower minimums but less control over which games your ad appears in.

**Q: How do I measure whether it worked?**
A: Use a dedicated URL or QR code, a promo code, and a branded-search or foot-traffic comparison before and after the flight. Expect CTV to influence awareness, so judge it over weeks rather than a single game.
