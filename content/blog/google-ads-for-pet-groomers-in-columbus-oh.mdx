## Is There Enough Demand for Pet Grooming Ads in Columbus?

The Columbus metro area had an estimated 2,242,028 residents in 2025, up more than 21,000 in a year, according to a WOSU report on new Census numbers. Nationally, APPA says dog ownership reached 71 million households in 2025, or 53% of households.

There is a warning in the same APPA release: spending on boarding, salon grooming and mobile grooming all declined in 2025 as owners sought ways to save, and 51% of dog owners groom their dogs at home. That makes price clarity and first-visit offers more important than broad awareness. APPA does not publish a Columbus figure.

## Which Keywords Should a Columbus Groomer Use?

Start with searches that name the service and the place. The themes below are starting points; check volumes and costs for Columbus in Google Keyword Planner, because no source we opened publishes them.

| Theme | Example | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service plus city | "dog grooming Columbus" | Clear local intent |
| Near me | "dog groomer near me" | Mobile searchers ready to book |
| Neighborhood | "dog groomer Short North" | Narrow, lower waste |
| Specific service | "cat grooming", "puppy first groom" | Fewer competitors |
| Mobile | "mobile dog grooming Columbus" | Only if you offer it |

Add negative keywords early: jobs, school, course, DIY, "how to", and supplies. Google's help explains that negatives do not match close variants, so add singular and plural forms yourself. Our [pet groomer keyword list](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-pet-groomers-in-2026) has more themes.

## How Do You Keep Spend Inside Your Service Area?

Google says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km, and recommends Presence or Interest as the default for Search, with Presence only suited to sensitive verticals or to exclude people elsewhere. A groomer whose customers drive from nearby can test Presence if the Search terms report shows clicks from places you cannot serve.

Pick a radius that matches where your bookings really come from, using your own booking records. We did not find a source for a standard travel distance, so any figure like "three to five miles" is your own data to set. ## What Should You Track?

Set up conversion actions for calls and online booking requests. Google's call assets let people call from the ad, and you set the minimum call length that counts as a conversion, so choose a length that reflects a real inquiry. Also watch the Search terms report weekly and cut terms that bring no calls.

A strong Business Profile helps ads, because many searchers compare reviews and hours before they click. ## FAQ

### How much does Google Ads cost for a Columbus pet groomer?

No Columbus-specific groomer CPC is published in the sources we could open. LocaliQ's 2026 Animals & Pets average is $4.06 per click and $31.50 per lead.

### How much should I spend?

It depends on your capacity. Work backward from how many new clients you can take per week and your cost per request.

### Is Google Ads better than Maps for groomers?

They work together. Maps listings are free, and ads capture searchers who compare options. Start with your profile.

### Should I target all of Columbus?

Not at first. Start near your salon and widen only if the Search terms report shows strong calls from farther away.

### How long until I know if it works?

There is no standard timeline in the sources we opened. Judge by calls and bookings against cost over several weeks.
