## Who Are You Marketing To in Belarus?

Belarus had 8.98 million people in October 2025 (UN data), down 0.7% on end 2024. 81.6% live in urban areas and the median age is 41.3. Internet users number 8.47 million (94.3%), and social media identities total 7.64 million (85.0%).

Cellular connections number 11.4 million (127%). Because 81.6% of people live in cities, most of your reach sits in a few large urban areas.

## How Should Google Ads Language and Location Work in Belarus?

Google's Ads Help says that from September 2026 the campaign-level language setting is being removed for Search campaigns, and Search ads will match on the language of the ads themselves. For searches with a clear language, Google prioritises ads in that language. Decide which language your customers use and keep each ad group and landing page in a single language, so you can see which one converts.

For location, Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km and recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting as best practice for Search, while presence-only fits when you want to avoid people outside your target area. Set a radius around your premises, then read the report to see which areas convert.

No Belarus-specific click costs were found in the sources checked, so every cost figure below is an assumption.

## What Does a Test Budget Return? The Math

These rows are **assumptions** for illustration, in US dollars. They are not Belarus benchmarks. Replace each with your own data after a month.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget (assumption) | $300 | $300 | $300 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | $0.60 | $0.35 | $0.25 |
| Clicks | 500 | 857 | 1,200 |
| Click to enquiry rate (assumption) | 3% | 5% | 7% |
| Enquiries | 15 | 43 | 84 |
| Enquiry to booking rate (assumption) | 30% | 40% | 50% |
| Bookings | 4.5 | 17.1 | 42 |
| Ad cost per booking | $67 | $17 | $7 |
| First-visit value (assumption) | $25 | $25 | $25 |
| Revenue minus ad spend | -$188 | +$129 | +$750 |

Counts use unrounded values. Revenue is not profit: staff, product and rent come out of it. A first visit often does not pay back by itself, which is why repeat visits matter.

Your break-even click price is the first-visit value times the enquiry rate times the booking rate. At $25, 5% and 40%, that is 25 x 0.05 x 0.40 = $0.50 per click. If your clicks cost more on average, the first visit loses money and you need repeat bookings to justify it. The biggest lever is usually the booking rate: answer messages and calls within minutes.
