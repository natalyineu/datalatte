Real-time bidding (RTB) is an automatic auction in which a single ad impression is put up for bid at the moment a page or app loads. The IAB Tech Lab describes it as a way of transacting media that "allows an individual ad impression to be put up for bid in real-time." In Google's version, bidders have between 80 and 1,000 milliseconds to answer, depending on format and auction type.

You do not run an auction yourself. A platform bids for you, using the budget and audience you set. This guide explains the steps, the difference from Google Ads and Meta Ads, and how a small business can use RTB without a large budget.

## How Does Real-Time Bidding Work?

Four steps happen in the time it takes a page to load.

1. A person opens a page or app that sells ad space.
2. The seller's system sends a bid request describing the impression, such as the placement and available context.
3. Bidders, which are usually buying platforms acting for advertisers, answer with a bid and an ad.
4. The highest bid wins and its ad is shown.

Google's documentation describes the same flow: a bid request goes to the bidder, the bidder returns a response with a bid and creative, and the highest bid wins the impression. Responses after the deadline are excluded. The shared language for these messages is OpenRTB, maintained by the IAB Tech Lab; version 2.6 includes features for connected TV.

## Is RTB the Same as Programmatic Advertising?

Not exactly. Programmatic advertising is the wider term for automated buying. RTB is one way to buy programmatically, where each impression is auctioned. Our guide to [how programmatic buying works for local businesses](/blog/how-programmatic-buying-works-local-business) explains the other pieces.

## How Is RTB Different From Google Ads and Meta Ads?

With Google Ads Search, your bid is only one of six auction factors. Google says that even with a lower bid you can win a higher position through relevance and ad quality. Display campaigns reach people across more than 35 million sites and apps plus YouTube and Gmail, using optimized targeting by default.

Google Ads and Meta Ads are closed platforms where you buy from their own inventory. RTB through an independent platform lets you reach inventory on many publishers and TV apps in one buy. Neither is automatically better; the right choice depends on your goal.

## Which Platforms Can a Small Business Use?

Many enterprise platforms are not built for small accounts. Google says new Display & Video 360 accounts begin by contacting its sales team. Others state lower barriers: StackAdapt says it has no minimum advertising spend requirement, and AdRoll says it has no minimum spend and uses CPM-based dynamic pricing. Prices are not published as a fixed rate, so ask each vendor for a quote.

## The Math: How Many Impressions Does a Test Buy?

All numbers here are **assumptions**; no standard CPM applies to every campaign.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget | $500 |
| Assumed CPM | $10 |
| Impressions bought | 500 / 10 x 1,000 = 50,000 |
| Assumed click-through rate | 0.2% |
| Clicks | 100 |
| Cost per click | $5.00 |

If the same $500 were spent at a $20 CPM, you would get 25,000 impressions. This shows why CPM is the first number to ask a vendor about. Replace the figures with real quotes and your own conversion rate.

## Is RTB Right for Your Business?

It suits businesses that want to reach people before they search: a new shop, an event, a seasonal offer. It fits poorly when you depend on people who are ready to buy now, because Search ads capture that demand more directly. If your Google Business Profile and website are not ready, fix them first. ## FAQ

### What does RTB stand for?

Real-time bidding: an auction for one ad impression at the moment it is served.

### How fast is the auction?

In Google's system the bidder waits 80 to 1,000 ms for a response, depending on format and auction type. Late responses are excluded.

### Do I need a developer?

No. A buying platform handles the bidding. You set budget, audience, location and creative.

### Is there a minimum budget?

Some vendors state none (StackAdapt, AdRoll). Others require contact with sales. Ask before you commit.

### Can I do RTB on a TV screen?

OpenRTB 2.6 includes features for connected TV, so some TV inventory is sold this way. See our [CTV platforms comparison](/blog/ctv-advertising-platforms-compared-2026).
