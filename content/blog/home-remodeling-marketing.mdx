Paid clicks are expensive in this category. ## What Does Lead Generation Cost for Remodelers?

LocaliQ's [2026 search advertising benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/) (page last updated June 1, 2026) list Home & Home Improvement at $8.33 per click, a 6.47% click-through rate, an 8.05% conversion rate and $90.92 per lead. All industries average $5.42, 6.64%, 8.18% and $66.69.

LocaliQ sells ad management, and the page gives no sample size. The category mixes plumbers, roofers and remodelers, and a "lead" is any conversion action, including a call that never becomes a project. Your own cost per signed job is the figure that counts. Our [Google Ads guide for contractors](/blog/google-ads-for-contractors) covers campaign setup.

## Which Channels Fit a Remodeling Business?

Remodeling is a considered purchase, so homeowners compare several firms. Cover the places they look.

| Channel | What it does | Main cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | Appears in Maps and local results when people search nearby | Time |
| Google Search ads | Reaches people searching "kitchen remodel [town]" | Per click |
| Local Services Ads | Pay-per-lead listing for eligible home service categories | Per lead |
| Houzz and other directories | Visibility among renovation shoppers | Subscription or per lead |
| Referrals and past clients | Highest trust | Time and a thank-you |

Houzz and similar directories are compared in our [Houzz ads guide for contractors](/blog/houzz-ads-home-services-contractors). Check each platform's current pricing before you commit, since it changes.

## How Do Local Services Ads Work?

Local Services Ads are a separate Google product for eligible services. Google's [Verified badge help](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/7549288) says new advertisers must complete the Local Services screening and verification process to earn the Google Verified badge, which replaces the earlier advertiser badges. The page says ranking is not affected by the change, and that the money-back guarantee tied to the old badge was ending, covering services booked before December 7, 2025.

Which categories qualify depends on your country and trade, so check eligibility in your Google account. Our [Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-services-ads-complete-guide) explains the lead model.

## What Compliance Rules Affect Remodelers?

In the US, the EPA says anyone paid to perform work that disturbs painted surfaces in housing built before 1978, and in child-occupied facilities, must be certified in lead-safe work practices, including sole proprietorships. Details are on the EPA's [Renovation, Repair and Painting Program page](https://www.epa.gov/lead/renovation-repair-and-painting-program). The page does not describe advertising requirements.

Licensing and contract rules vary by state and country, so check yours before you advertise work you cannot legally perform. State your license number on your website where the law or your board expects it. In the UK, Australia and Canada, regulators differ again.

## How Do Reviews and Photos Win the Job?

A homeowner is hiring someone to work in their house, so proof matters. Collect photos of finished projects with permission, and ask each client for a Google review while they are happy with the result. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists what to complete.

Do not buy or invent reviews. Use real project photos on service pages, with the project type and town named. Ask clients whether you may show their house, and never claim work that was not yours.

## The Math: What Does a Signed Job Cost?

All inputs are **assumptions** built on the LocaliQ Home & Home Improvement benchmark. A "lead" here is any enquiry.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget (assumption) | $2,000 |
| Cost per lead (benchmark) | $90.92 |
| Leads | about 22 |
| Leads who get an estimate visit (assumption) | 40% |
| Estimates that become signed jobs (assumption) | 20% |
| Signed jobs | about 1.8 |
| Ad cost per job | about $1,136 |
| Average gross profit per job (assumption) | $9,000 |

At these inputs, ads cost about $1,136 for each signed job against $9,000 gross profit. If only 10% of estimates convert, the ad cost doubles to about $2,270, which still sits well below the assumed profit. The key variable is the close rate on estimates, which you control with fast replies and clear proposals. ## FAQ

### How much should a remodeler spend on marketing?

No official figure exists. At LocaliQ's $90.92 average cost per lead, $2,000 a month buys roughly 22 enquiries. Judge spend by cost per signed job.

### Are Local Services Ads better than Search ads?

They suit different situations. LSAs are a pay-per-lead listing with a verification process, while Search ads let you control keywords and pages. Many remodelers test both.

### Do I need lead-safe certification?

In the US, the EPA says anyone paid to disturb painted surfaces in pre-1978 housing must be certified. Check the EPA page for exceptions.

### How do I get more reviews?

Ask at project completion, send a direct link, and reply to every review. Never offer payment for positive reviews.
