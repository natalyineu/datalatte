## Where Are Argentine Customers Online?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report for Argentina, published 8 November 2025, lists 41.6 million internet users (90.6% penetration), 32.9 million social media user identities, and 66.6 million cellular connections. Its ad reach estimates are 31.1 million for Instagram, 29.3 million for Facebook and 29.2 million for TikTok among adults.

DataReportal notes these are advertising reach estimates, not monthly active users. They show where ads can be delivered, which is the number a business buying ads needs. Our [Latin America overview](/blog/small-business-marketing-latin-america-2026) puts Argentina in regional context.

## How Do Taxes Affect Google Ads in Argentina?

Google's Argentina tax page says it applies a 21% VAT perception to all manual payments, and also to payments on monthly invoicing. A further 3% VAT applies only to advertisers registered for VAT who are not withholding agents and have no exclusion certificate. Google also collects Ingresos Brutos perceptions in nine provinces, including Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Buenos Aires, Córdoba and Tucumán.

Google asks for tax documents when you create the account, such as AFIP registration. Without current documents it may charge the highest tax rates. Rates can change, so confirm with an accountant.

## Which Google Ads Setup Suits a Local Shop?

Target a radius around the shop. Google says a radius must be at least 1 km and that very small areas may show ads only intermittently. Use exact match for the few terms that matter, such as your service plus the neighbourhood in Spanish, and phrase match to test variations.

Google says negative keywords do not match close variants, so add plural and synonym forms yourself. Estimate costs in Keyword Planner, which needs a Google Ads account with billing set up. Do not use a foreign CPC as your Argentine number.

## Can You Run Sales Through WhatsApp?

Yes, and customers expect it. Meta's developer pricing page says each business phone number gets a free tier of 1,000 delivered service messages a month, and that messages after a Click to WhatsApp ad are free for a window. Meta's two pricing pages disagree on that window (up to 7 days on one, 72 hours on the other) and on utility message billing, which the developer page says changes on 1 October 2026.

Meta lists Argentina on its rate card with ARS pricing, but neither page shows the amounts. Read Meta's current rate card before sending paid template messages, and do not budget from this guide.

## FAQ

### Is Instagram or Google better for an Argentine small business?

Instagram reaches 31.1 million people by DataReportal's ad estimate, so it suits discovery. Google captures people already searching, so it suits bookings and visits. Most local businesses use both.

### Does Google charge VAT on ads in Argentina?

Google says it applies a 21% VAT perception to manual payments and to monthly invoicing, plus a 3% VAT in some registered cases and provincial Ingresos Brutos perceptions.

### Are WhatsApp messages free for businesses?

Meta lists 1,000 free delivered service messages per number each month and a free window after Click to WhatsApp ads. Its pages differ on details, so check the current rate card, which lists ARS pricing for Argentina.

### How much should I budget for ads in Argentina?

No benchmark was found for Argentine CPC. Start with an amount you can afford to lose, then measure cost per customer.

### Which cities should I target first?

Start with the area where your customers already live, using a radius around the shop, then widen it only if data supports it.
