A Newfoundland and Labrador small business should start with a complete Google Business Profile, run a small Google Ads Search campaign around its town, and build a consented email list. The province is small: Statistics Canada's Business Register counted 15,265 businesses with employees in December 2025, and half of them have 1 to 4 staff.

This guide covers St. John's, Corner Brook, Mount Pearl, Gander and the communities around them. No source checked publishes province-specific click or impression costs, so none are quoted. Every example input is labelled as an assumption.

## How Big Is the Newfoundland and Labrador Small Business Market?

The province's Statistics Agency, using the Statistics Canada Business Register, lists 15,265 businesses with employees in December 2025. By size: 7,707 have 1 to 4 employees (50.5%), 5,488 have 5 to 19 (36.0%), 1,707 have 20 to 99 (11.2%), 325 have 100 to 499, and 38 have 500 or more.

Nearly nine in ten employers have fewer than 20 staff. The counts are establishments, and a retailer with several stores is counted more than once. Sole operators with no employees are not included. In a small market, most customers already know the local names, so reviews and an accurate profile do much of the work.

## Which Marketing Channels Should You Use First?

Start with free local visibility, then add paid channels once you can measure results. The order below is a recommendation, not a ranked study.

| Step | Channel | Cost | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Google Business Profile | Free | Shows you on Search and Maps |
| 2 | Reviews and photos | Free | Helps customers choose between nearby options |
| 3 | Google Ads, local Search | Ad budget | Reaches people searching now |
| 4 | Meta Ads, small radius | Ad budget | Reaches locals who are not searching yet |
| 5 | Email and SMS list | Low | Repeat sales, with consent |

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to pay for a better local ranking. More reviews and positive ratings can help.

The closest public reference is LocaliQ's US 2026 benchmark: $5.42 average cost per click across industries, from $2.05 for Restaurants and Food to $8.33 for Home and Home Improvement, in US dollars. ## How Do You Keep Ads Local?

Google allows radius targeting from 1 km upward and recommends "Presence or interest" for most Search campaigns. It says "Presence" fits when you only want people physically in the area.

Outside St. John's, towns are far apart, so a radius can include large areas of ocean or empty land. Draw it around the communities your customers drive from, and add negative keywords for jobs, DIY and free.

## Can You Offer a Discount for a Google Review?

No. Google's contributed content policy lists offering "payment, discounts, free goods and/or services" in exchange for reviews as fake engagement. You may ask for a review with a direct link after a visit. See our [guide to getting more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop).

## What Does CASL Require for Email and SMS?

Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) prohibits sending a commercial electronic message unless the recipient has consented, expressly or impliedly (section 6). Subsection 20(4) sets the maximum penalty per violation at $1,000,000 for an individual and $10,000,000 for any other person.

Collect sign-ups with a clear checkbox at checkout or booking, keep a record of when and how each person agreed, and honour unsubscribes. A legal adviser can confirm what applies to your list.

## FAQ

### How many businesses are there in Newfoundland and Labrador?

The provincial Statistics Agency counted 15,265 businesses with employees in December 2025. Businesses without employees are not included.

### How much should I spend on Google Ads in Newfoundland and Labrador?

No source publishes a provincial figure. Pick a budget you can hold for two months and work backwards from the value of a customer.

### Does Meta advertising work in a small market?

It can for local offers and events. No published source gives provincial costs, so test a small daily budget with a tight radius.

### Is Google Business Profile free?

Creating and managing a profile costs nothing. You need to claim and verify the business to appear on Search and Maps.

### What is the CASL penalty?

Up to $10 million per violation for a business and $1 million for an individual, per subsection 20(4) of the Act.
