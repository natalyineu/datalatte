A small business can build a marketing dashboard for free in Looker Studio, which Google's docs now call Data Studio and describe as a no-cost tool for turning data into dashboards and reports. Connect Google Analytics 4 for site visits, Search Console for search impressions and Google Ads for paid results, then show 5 to 8 numbers on one page. Keep it to numbers you will act on.

## What Should a Small Business Dashboard Show?

Start from questions, not charts: Are people finding us? Are they contacting us? What did paid ads cost? Pick one number per question.

| Question | Number | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Are people finding us in search? | Search impressions and clicks | Search Console |
| Is the site being used? | Sessions and key actions | Google Analytics 4 |
| What did ads cost and return? | Cost, clicks, conversions | Google Ads |
| Are calls and bookings rising? | Calls, direction requests, bookings | Your own records (sheet) |

Our guide to [local SEO KPIs](/blog/how-to-measure-local-seo-success-kpis) explains the search numbers, and the [reporting guide](/blog/marketing-reporting-for-small-business) covers a monthly routine.

## How Do the Google Connectors Work?

A connector links Looker Studio to your data. Each has limits worth knowing before you build.

- **Google Analytics 4:** supports GA4 properties and needs at least Read & Analyze permission. Reports are subject to GA4 Data API quotas and may show an error if exceeded. GA4 segments and comparisons are not available through the connector.
- **Search Console:** has a Site Impression table and a URL Impression table, and one data source can use only one. Google notes Search Analytics does not show all data, so some queries are grouped under a blank query.
- **Google Ads:** one data source connects to one account you can access, or up to 50 sub-accounts under a manager account. Entities with zero impressions do not appear, and Auction Insights data has been removed.

If a Search Console property does not appear, sign in to both tools with the same Google Account and make sure it has at least view permission.

## How Do You Set It Up?

1. Make sure GA4 is installed. Google's setup guide says data can take up to 30 minutes to start appearing in Realtime after the tag is installed. See our [GA4 guide](/blog/google-analytics-4-for-small-business).
2. Create a new report in Looker Studio and add each data source.
3. Put the four questions across the top as number tiles, each with a month-on-month comparison.
4. Add one trend chart per source, with a date selector.
5. Share the report link with whoever reviews it.

Include a Google Sheet for numbers that no connector provides, such as bookings or calls counted by hand. A manual column is better than an empty tile.

## A Worked Example: A Salon Dashboard

All numbers are **assumptions** shown only to illustrate layout.

| Tile | This month | Last month | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Search clicks | 220 | 190 | +16% |
| Sessions | 640 | 600 | +7% |
| Ad spend | $300 | $300 | 0% |
| Bookings (sheet) | 42 | 38 | +11% |

Divide ad spend by bookings only if you can tie bookings to ads; otherwise do not show a cost per booking. The honest number here is bookings per month and ad spend, side by side. Review it on the first of each month and write one decision under it, such as "move the offer higher on the page".

## FAQ

### Is Looker Studio free?

Google's docs describe it as a no-cost tool for dashboards and reports. A Pro tier exists, but Google's page does not compare it.

### Do I need Google Ads to use a dashboard?

No. Start with GA4 and Search Console, and add ads later.

### Why do my Search Console numbers look incomplete?

Google says Search Analytics does not show all data, to protect privacy. Some queries are grouped under a blank row.

### Can I compare segments from GA4?

Not through the connector. Google states segments and comparisons are not available there.

### How often should I check it?

Monthly for decisions, weekly only while running a campaign. That is practice, not a Google rule.
