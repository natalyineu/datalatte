## What Reach Does Google's Display Network Offer?

Google's help page says Display campaigns can reach people across 35 million websites and apps, plus Google-owned properties such as YouTube and Gmail. That figure is a worldwide total. The page does not split out Mexico, so it cannot tell a Mexican business how many local viewers or readers a campaign would reach.

Display campaigns can be built around different goals. Google lists sales, leads, website traffic and awareness as options. For a local business, the chosen goal decides what the campaign is measured against, so it should be set before any budget is spent. A campaign built for awareness will not show lead numbers, even if it runs well.

The page also describes Display as reaching people at different stages of the buying cycle, from people with demonstrated interests to people close to buying. That describes how the network is designed. It is not a measured result, and it needs testing in the advertiser's own account.

Display is not the same as CTV. The sources describe ads on websites, apps and Google properties, not inventory on TV screens. A claim that a Display campaign runs on television would be unverified, so it should not appear in a client proposal or a budget request.

## Can a Small Business Test Display Without an Agency?

Google's help page says Display campaigns use responsive display ads by default. The advertiser uploads headlines, images and logos, and Google's AI combines them into the formats most likely to perform. Optimized targeting is the default targeting method, and another method can be selected instead.

Bidding can follow Smart Bidding, which Google recommends based on the campaign goal, or bids can be set manually. For conversion goals, Target CPA bidding can be paired with a "Pay for conversions" option, so the advertiser pays for results rather than clicks. Check which of these options the account allows before setting a budget.

## What Do Display Benchmarks Suggest a Test Costs?

WordStream's all-industry averages are a 3.17% search CTR, a 0.46% display CTR, a $2.69 search CPC and a $0.63 display CPC. The vendor says both CTR averages are higher than a couple of years earlier, and that CPCs rose only slightly from $2.32 and $0.58.

Industry figures vary widely. Legal search CPC is $6.75, while Travel and Hospitality is $1.53. In the display column, Dating and Personals reaches $1.49, the only industry above $1 there. Find the row closest to the business before using any number. These benchmarks come from a vendor dataset, not from Mexico, and they describe search and display clicks. They cannot price a CTV spot.

## How Does a Worked Test Look?

The inputs below are labelled as assumptions or taken from the benchmark. The calculation is a sanity check on a display test, not a CTV price.

| Input | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Test budget | $600 over 4 weeks | assumption |
| Display CPC | $0.63 | WordStream all-industry average |
| Display CTR | 0.46% | WordStream all-industry average |
| Clicks bought | 600 / 0.63 = about 952 | calculation |
| Impressions needed | 952 / 0.0046 = about 207,000 | calculation |
| Implied CPM | 600 / 207,000 x 1,000 = about $2.90 | calculation |

The implied CPM of about $2.90 is what the benchmark suggests for a display test with these inputs. If a CTV seller quotes a CPM far above that, the seller should explain what the extra cost buys. Keep the budget small and limit the test to one city or postcode area, so a weak result costs little. Any CTV plan should follow the same logic: write down the price, the impressions and the target, then compare.

For a wider budget view, a [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) usually costs less than any video buy and is worth fixing first.

## How Do You Run the Test Step by Step?

1. Set one goal in Google Ads, such as website visits or leads, and write down the number that would count as success.
2. Limit the campaign to the service area and check the location settings before adding any budget.
3. Upload several headlines, images and logos so responsive display has enough assets to combine.
4. Set the test budget written down in step one, with a time limit of four weeks.
5. Compare the result with the benchmark CTR and CPC for the closest industry, not the all-industry average alone.
6. Record any calls, form fills or coupon uses that fall inside the test window.
7. Decide before the test ends whether to scale, change the creative or stop.

## What Is Still Unverified?

The sources checked for this guide do not confirm several things that a Mexico CTV plan would need.

- The share of Mexican homes with smart TVs or streaming apps.
- Any streamer or TV rate card, in pesos or dollars.
- Whether TV-screen inventory is available to a small local campaign in Mexico.
- Mexican rules on TV or streaming ads, including sector limits.

Check these with the seller, a local adviser or official Mexican sources before committing money. This list marks gaps in the evidence and is not legal advice.

## FAQ

### Does Google's Display Network reach Mexico?

The help page gives a worldwide figure of 35 million websites and apps, but it does not break out Mexico. Check the location settings in the account to see what a campaign actually covers.

### What is the average display CPC?

WordStream's all-industry average is $0.63 for the Display Network. Industry figures vary, and Dating and Personals is the highest in the display column at $1.49.

### Can I buy CTV ads without an agency?

The sources do not confirm a self-serve CTV route in Mexico. Display campaigns can be set up inside Google Ads, but streamer deals usually need a sales contact. Ask for a written quote that states the format, audience and reporting.

### How current are the benchmarks?

The WordStream article was first published in 2016 and says it is updated regularly. Check the latest version of the report before relying on any figure in a plan.
