## How Many Mexican Homes Can Receive CTV Ads?

INEGI's household technology survey (ENDUTIH), published on 16 June 2026, counted 35.7 million homes with a television in 2025, or 89.9% of all homes. Of those, 75.6% had a smart TV, 6.6 percentage points more than in 2024. Smart TVs are the hardware that runs streaming apps and YouTube, so they are the base for CTV.

Internet access is high but uneven. INEGI says 78.3% of homes had internet in 2025, and 86.1% of people aged 6 and over used it. Mexico City had the highest home access at 90.5%, then Nuevo Leon at 89.9%. A smart TV does not guarantee a streaming subscription, and INEGI's press release does not give that figure.

## Which Platforms Can a Small Business Use?

Public information is thin, so here is what we could confirm and what we could not.

- **YouTube on TV screens.** Google Ads Help describes "TV screens" as devices that stream TV content, such as smart TVs, gaming consoles and Chromecast. DataReportal puts YouTube's ad reach in Mexico at 85.0 million people, 64.3% of the population, in late 2025.
- **Google TV network.** Google's help page lists Mexico among 28 countries where this network is available. It serves in-feed ads through Video reach campaigns with the "Efficient reach" subtype, with 1920 x 1080 px, 16:9 creative.
- **Prime Video.** Amazon Ads lists Mexico among markets where Prime Video ads run. The page does not mention self-serve access or minimum budgets.
- **ViX and other local streamers.** We could not open an official Mexican rate card or audience figure, so we make no claim about their reach or price.

## Can You Buy CTV Ads Yourself, or Do You Need an Agency?

YouTube on TV screens is the one route you can test yourself. It sits inside a normal Google Ads account, and Google says Video campaigns default to all eligible devices unless you narrow them. The device filter page notes that TV screen targeting applies to Display and Video campaigns.

Streamer sales teams and programmatic platforms are different. They usually work through a sales contact, and no public minimum spend for Mexico was found. Ask for a written quote that states the format, the audience, the delivery dates and the reporting you will receive.

One limit to know: Google's own TV screens announcement is from 2018, and its lab results (a 47% lift in ad recall and 35% in purchase intent) came from a US study. Do not treat them as Mexican results.

## What Does CTV Cost in Mexico?

No reliable public figure exists in the sources we could open. Earlier versions of this page listed CPMs in pesos for specific streamers. None of them could be traced to a rate card, so they were removed.

What you can do is calculate cost per thousand from a real quote. Divide the price by the number of impressions and multiply by 1,000. Then compare it with what the same money buys on Facebook or YouTube mobile, where you can see results in your own account.

## What Does a Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All numbers below are **assumptions** for the arithmetic, not market prices. The point is the method.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly test budget | MXN 6,000 |
| Quoted CPM (placeholder) | MXN 150 |
| Impressions bought | 6,000 / 150 x 1,000 = 40,000 |
| Frequency cap (assumption) | 4 views per household per week |
| Campaign length | 4 weeks |
| Unique viewers if the cap holds (assumption) | about 10,000 |

If your real quote is higher, divide again. The result shows how few people a small budget reaches, which is why a local business should limit CTV to a city or a postcode area. Measure it against a simple signal: branded searches, calls, or a coupon code shown only on the TV ad.

## What Do Mexican Advertising Rules Say?

Mexico's Federal Consumer Protection Law (last reformed 12 December 2025) is clear on content. Article 32 says information or advertising about goods or services, in any medium, must be truthful, verifiable and clear, and free of text, images or sounds that mislead or confuse. Article 32 also defines misleading advertising as presentation that is inexact, false, exaggerated, partial or tendentious.

That applies to a 15-second TV spot as much as to a flyer. Keep every claim, price and "free" offer provable. We could not open CONAR Mexico's self-regulation code, so we do not describe it here. Check it, and any rules for your product category, such as alcohol or food, before you publish. This is not legal advice.

## Is CTV Right for Your Business?

CTV fits businesses with a clear service area and a visual product: restaurants, clinics, gyms, and car dealers. It is a weaker fit for a shop that needs sales this week, because it builds awareness slowly and tracking is indirect.

Start with the cheaper step if you have not done it. Fix your Google profile, then run a small YouTube test and read the device report. If TV screens bring views at a cost you accept, scale. ## FAQ

**Q: Do most Mexican homes have a smart TV?**
A: Among homes with a television, 75.6% had a smart TV in 2025, according to INEGI. That is 6.6 points higher than in 2024.

**Q: Is the Google TV network available in Mexico?**
A: Google's help page lists Mexico among 28 countries. Ads there run through Video reach campaigns with Efficient reach and the Google TV network option.

**Q: How much does a CTV campaign cost in Mexico?**
A: No public rate card was found. Ask sellers for a written quote and calculate CPM yourself.

**Q: Can I target only my city?**
A: Google Ads supports location targeting. For streamer deals, ask the seller what geographic limits they can deliver and report.

**Q: Do advertising laws apply to streaming ads?**
A: Article 32 of the Federal Consumer Protection Law covers advertising in any medium and requires it to be truthful and verifiable.
