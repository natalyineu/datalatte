A local business can start video marketing with one phone, a Google Business Profile clip of up to 30 seconds and one social channel. Pew's 2026 survey found 84% of US adults use YouTube, 70% Facebook, 51% Instagram and 31% TikTok. Those are reach figures for adults overall, so check which platform your own customers mention before you commit.

## Which Platform Should You Start With?

Pew fielded its survey from January 30 to June 18, 2026, with 5,511 adults ([Pew social media fact sheet](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/fact-sheet/social-media/)). Age changes the answer: 79% of adults 18 to 29 use Instagram against 21% of those 65 and older, and 65% of 18 to 29s use TikTok against 11% of those 65 and older.

Facebook peaks at 78% among adults 30 to 49. So a salon with young clients may favor Instagram or TikTok, while a plumber serving homeowners may favor Facebook and YouTube.

## What Are the Rules on Your Google Profile?

Google's Business Profile guidelines allow videos up to 30 seconds, up to 75 MB, at 720p or higher ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6103862)). One phone clip fits those limits. The profile is where local searchers already look, so it is a sensible first home for a video.

Our guide to [photos and videos on Google Business Profile](/blog/google-my-business-photos-and-videos) covers what to show.

## How Long Can a YouTube Short Be?

YouTube's help page says Shorts can be up to 3 minutes long, with a maximum upload resolution of 1080p, and that you can upload vertical videos from a phone or record in the app ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070)). Shorter is easier to produce, so one 30-second clip can serve both your profile and Shorts.

For a comparison of formats, read [Shorts vs TikTok vs Reels](/blog/youtube-shorts-vs-tiktok-vs-reels-local-business).

## What Is a Simple Weekly Plan?

The plan below is an **assumption**, a time budget and not a result. A coffee shop spends 90 minutes a week:

| Step | Minutes |
| --- | --- |
| Film three clips in one batch | 25 |
| Trim and caption | 40 |
| Upload to the Google profile and one social channel | 25 |
| Total | 90 |

Track one thing: orders or bookings that mention the video, using a unique link or a till code. After eight weeks, keep the format with the most mentions and drop the rest.

## What Should You Film?

- **Cafes and restaurants:** product close-ups and process clips. See [video marketing for coffee shops](/blog/video-marketing-for-coffee-shops-complete-guide).
- **Salons:** before-and-after and short how-tos. See [video marketing for salons](/blog/video-marketing-for-salons-reels-tiktok-guide).
- **No time:** publish only to the Google profile, one clip a month.

Hands, products and the space work without anyone on camera. That is a practical choice, not a rule from any source.

## FAQ

### How long should a local business video be?

On Google Business Profile, at most 30 seconds. Other platforms set their own limits, so check each one.

### Do I need to be on camera?

No. Hands, products and the space all work.

### How do I know video works?

Use a unique link or code and count mentions. Views alone do not show customers.

### Which platform first?

Start with your Google profile, then the platform your customers already message you on.
