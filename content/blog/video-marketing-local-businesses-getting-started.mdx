## Where your audience is

Pew's 2025 survey (fielded February 5 to June 18, 2025) found 84% of US adults use YouTube, 71% Facebook and 50% Instagram ([Pew social media fact sheet](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/fact-sheet/social-media/)). Those are reach figures for adults overall, not local-buyer behaviour, so check which platform your own customers mention.

## The one place with hard rules: your Google profile

Google's Business Profile guidelines allow videos up to 30 seconds, up to 75 MB, at 720p or higher ([photo and video requirements](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6103862)). That makes a single phone clip enough, and it sits where local searchers already look. See [photos and videos on Google Business Profile](/blog/google-my-business-photos-and-videos).

## Worked example (assumption)

Weekly plan for a coffee shop, 90 minutes total: film three clips in one batch (25 minutes), trim and caption (40 minutes), upload to the profile and one social channel (25 minutes). Track one thing: bookings or orders that mention the video, using a unique link or a code at the till. After 8 weeks, keep the format with the most mentions and drop the rest. The numbers here are a time budget, not a prediction of results.

## Decision guide

- Restaurants and cafes: product close-ups and process clips; see [video marketing for coffee shops](/blog/video-marketing-for-coffee-shops-complete-guide).
- Salons: before-and-after and short how-tos; see [video marketing for salons](/blog/video-marketing-for-salons-reels-tiktok-guide).
- No time: publish only to the Google profile, one clip a month.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long should a video be?**
For Google Business Profile, at most 30 seconds. For other platforms, check each platform's current limits.

**Do I need to be on camera?**
No. Hands, products and the space all work. This is a choice, not a rule from any source.

**How do I know it works?**
Use a unique link or a code and count mentions; do not rely on views alone.

**Which platform first?**
Start with your Google profile, then the platform your customers already message you on.
