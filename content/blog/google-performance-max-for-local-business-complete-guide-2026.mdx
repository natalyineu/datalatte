Performance Max is a goal-based Google Ads campaign that serves YouTube, Display, Search, Discover, Gmail and Maps from one campaign. A local business can aim it at store visits, calls or directions, give it assets in an asset group, and let Google decide placement. Google advises running new campaigns for at least 6 weeks without frequent budget or bid changes. Google publishes no minimum budget or conversion count for this campaign type, so start with a budget you can leave alone.

## What Is Performance Max, and Where Do the Ads Show?

Google's help page says Performance Max "allows you to access all Google Ads inventory, including YouTube, Display, Search, Discover, Gmail, and Maps, from a single campaign." You choose an objective (Sales, Leads, Website traffic, or Local store visits and promotions, or no goal), add conversion goals, and supply text, images and videos.

An asset group holds the creative around one theme or audience, and Google's AI combines it into ads across inventory. Reporting includes asset-level metrics, asset group reporting, channel performance and placement reports, so you can see more than a single total.

## How Is It Different From a Search Campaign?

| | Search campaign | Performance Max |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where ads show | Google Search (and optionally partners) | YouTube, Display, Search, Discover, Gmail, Maps |
| What you control | Keywords, match types, ad text | Goals, assets, audience signals, location |
| Keyword targeting | You choose keywords | Search themes guide the AI, with the same priority as phrase and broad match keywords |
| Negative keywords | Account and campaign level | Campaign-level control for blocking queries |
| Landing page | The final URL you set | Final URL expansion may send people to a more relevant page |

Google says final URL expansion can also generate dynamic headlines and descriptions, and that you remain responsible for reviewing the landing page content. ## Can Performance Max Drive Store Visits and Calls?

Yes, through the "Local store visits and promotions" objective. Google's help page says store goals campaigns aim to maximize in-store value and conversions using store visits, store sales and local actions such as call clicks or direction clicks. You define the store locations by linking a Business Profile or choosing affiliate locations.

Location handling differs from Search. Radius targeting applies to Business Profile and affiliate location assets. You can set the radius in location targeting, or Google determines it automatically from the targeted locations and users' travel-distance preferences, plus vertical, population density and existing competitors. The page does not state a minimum number of locations, so check your account for eligibility. A well-kept profile matters here; start with our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## What Should You Put In the Campaign?

Google's optimization tips recommend an "Excellent" or "Good" ad strength rating, and at least these assets:

- 20 text assets: 15 headlines and 5 descriptions
- 7 image assets: 3 landscape, 3 square and 1 portrait
- 1 video asset, uploaded or made with Google's video tool

It also recommends relevant, high-quality audience signals such as remarketing lists, and keeping logos and business information grouped consistently within the same asset groups. Audience signals are a hint. They do not limit who sees the ads.

Brand exclusions can keep ads from showing for named brands, and campaign-level negative keywords block unwanted queries. Check the search terms and placement reports early, because Performance Max can place ads in more locations than a Search campaign.

## How Long Should You Wait Before Judging It?

Google's guidance is to run new campaigns for at least 6 weeks, and after significant changes to an existing campaign to allow a similar learning period of 1 to 2 weeks. It also advises avoiding frequent changes to budget, bid strategy or campaign status in the initial period.

Third-party sources quote various conversion minimums, such as 15, 30 or 100 or more conversions. Google's tips page does not state a conversion threshold, so treat those numbers as opinion. If your business generates only a handful of calls a month, a Search campaign is easier to read. ## Worked Example: A 6-Week Test Plan

All figures here are **assumptions** for planning, not benchmarks. A neighbourhood bakery with one location tests Performance Max:

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Objective | Local store visits and promotions |
| Daily budget | $20 |
| Test length | 6 weeks (42 days) |
| Maximum spend, at Google's 30.4-day rule | about $608 per 30.4 days |
| Conversions tracked | Direction clicks and call clicks |
| Decision point | Week 7 |

During the 6 weeks the owner changes nothing in budget or bids, checks search terms weekly, and adds negatives. At week 7 the owner compares cost per direction click and call click against a Search campaign run on the same budget. Google can overdeliver up to 2 times on a single day, so check the billing total rather than the daily figure. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) explains how to size such a test.

## When Is Local Services Ads a Better Fit?

If you offer a service and want to pay for leads, compare Performance Max with Local Services Ads. Our [Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-services-ads-complete-guide) explains eligibility and billing. For a business with one location and low call volume, a plain Search campaign is the easier test.

## FAQ

### What is Performance Max in Google Ads?
A goal-based campaign type that accesses YouTube, Display, Search, Discover, Gmail and Maps inventory from one campaign.

### How long should I run a Performance Max campaign?
Google advises at least 6 weeks for new campaigns, and 1 to 2 weeks of learning after significant changes.

### How much should I spend on Performance Max?
Google's tips page gives no minimum budget. Pick an amount you can leave unchanged through the learning period.

### Can I use negative keywords in Performance Max?
Google lists negative keywords as a campaign-level control to block unwanted queries.

### Does Performance Max work for stores with one location?
Google's store goals page does not state a minimum number of locations. Try it as a test against Search and compare results.
