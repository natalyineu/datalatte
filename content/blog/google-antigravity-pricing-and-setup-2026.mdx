Google Antigravity is free for individuals ($0 a month, with basic weekly rate limits). Higher limits come with Google AI plans: Google AI Pro, listed at $19.99 a month on Google's plan page, and Google AI Ultra, which Google's Antigravity blog says is $100 or $200 a month. Business use goes through Google Cloud, from $30 per seat a month for eligible Gemini Enterprise customers.

Prices and features checked on 7 November 2026.

This guide is for owners and marketers of cafés, salons, grooming studios and gyms who want to know what Antigravity is, what it costs and what the data terms mean. All facts come from Google's own pages, listed in Sources.

## What Are the Usage Limits?

Google's plans documentation gives no fixed message counts. It says all plans include Gemini models, unlimited Tab completions and access to all product features, including scheduled tasks and the CLI. Ultra gets the highest quota, refreshed every five hours, plus the highest weekly limits and access to third-party models. Pro gets a high quota, also refreshed every five hours until the weekly limit is reached. Other plans get a meaningful quota refreshed weekly.

Limits "depend on available capacity" and track the agent's workload, so simple tasks use less quota than complex ones. Limits may change to manage capacity.

Pro and Ultra users can use AI credits for overage once the baseline quota is gone. The setting offers Never or Always. Per Google's blog, credits "are now used solely as an overage mechanism." Credits are charged at standard Gemini Enterprise consumption pricing.

## What Is Antigravity and How Does It Work?

Google describes it as "our agentic development platform, allowing anyone to build in the agent-first era." The site says it is available on macOS, Windows, Linux and Googlebook, with a desktop download. The Antigravity App is a hub for running several agents at once, grouping conversations into projects and scheduling recurring messages. There is also an agentic IDE, and the site mentions browser-in-the-loop agents for repetitive UX tasks.

Setup is a download and a sign-in with a Google account. The pages opened do not list system requirements, so check the download page for your computer.

## Do You Need to Pay for Antigravity?

The pricing page says it is "Available at no charge" for individual developers, and the Individuals plan is $0. A paid Google AI plan raises limits and adds a credit pool. The Google plan page also mentions a free year of Google AI Pro for students with verification. It states no trial length for other plans.

## What Are the Risks and Limits?

- **Data use.** Google's Antigravity terms say Google records "Interactions" (user data, interaction data, metadata, feedback) and uses them to "evaluate, develop, and improve Google and Alphabet research, products, services and machine learning technologies."
- **Human review.** Google employees and contractors may access and review Interactions. The terms advise against submitting "confidential information or any data you wouldn't want a reviewer to see."
- **Telemetry toggle.** The terms say "Even when your Telemetry is toggled off, Google still uses your data to provide the service."
- **Third-party models.** If you pick one as the main agent model, that model's terms apply.
- **Work and Google accounts.** Access through Gemini Enterprise or Workspace falls under the administrator's terms.
- **Limits.** Quotas depend on capacity and may change. Bring-your-own-key is not currently supported.
- **Review.** Agent output needs checking before publication.

This guide did not open the Google Privacy Policy that the terms refer to.

## FAQ

### Is Google Antigravity free?

Yes for individuals. Google's pricing page lists an Individuals plan at $0 a month with unlimited Tab completions and Command requests and basic weekly rate limits.

### How much is Google Antigravity Pro?

Antigravity higher limits come through Google AI Pro. Google's AI plans page calculator lists $19.99 a month or $199.99 a year. The Antigravity pricing page itself shows no price for Pro.

### How much does Antigravity Ultra cost?

Google's 19 May 2026 blog post describes a $100 a month Ultra plan and a $200 a month top plan, down from $250. The Google AI plans page calculator shows $99.99 monthly for Ultra.

### What computers does Antigravity run on?

Google's site says macOS, Windows, Linux and Googlebook, with separate Apple Silicon and Intel downloads for Mac.

### Does Google use my code in Antigravity?

Google's terms say it uses Interactions to improve Google products and machine learning technologies, and that reviewers may see them. Even with telemetry off, it still uses data to provide the service. Avoid confidential data.
