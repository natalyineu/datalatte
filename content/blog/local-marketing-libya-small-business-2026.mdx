A small business in Libya should use Facebook and Messenger as its main marketing channels, because DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 6.62 million internet users (88.5% of 7.48 million people) and Facebook ad reach of 6.70 million. Paid ads are possible through Meta's tools, but payment, cash and legal conditions in Libya are less clear than in most markets, so test small and verify each step.

This guide uses DataReportal, the World Bank, Google's restriction page and the Central Bank of Libya. It gives no ad prices because none were found.

## How Many Libyans Are Online?

DataReportal lists 7.48 million people in Libya (UN, October 2025), 82.3% of them urban, with a median age of 27.7. Internet users number 6.62 million, which leaves 860,000 (11.5%) offline. Mobile connections total 14.9 million, 199% of the population.

DataReportal itself says the 199% mobile figure and the social media figures, which exceed 100% of internet users, are implausible and attributes them to data methods. Take the numbers as a sign of very high connectivity, not as exact audience sizes.

## Which Platforms Reach Customers in Libya?

Facebook reports ad reach of 6.70 million, equal to 89.6% of the population, though DataReportal flags that this is above the internet user count. Messenger reaches 4.90 million and Instagram 2.30 million, up 200,000 (9.5%) in the year to October 2025. TikTok's tools show 6.19 million users aged 18 and over, up 23.0% since the end of 2024. LinkedIn shows 700,000 members and X 534,000.

Adult audiences skew male on every platform DataReportal lists: 60.0% male on Facebook and 63.5% male on TikTok. If your customers are mostly women, check your own Page insights before assuming ads reach them. WhatsApp is not covered on the country page.

## Can a Libyan Business Run Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Google's country-restriction page lists Crimea, Cuba, the so-called Donetsk and Luhansk People's Republics, Iran and North Korea. Libya is not on it, and no update date is shown. That page covers Google's own sanctions-based blocks only. Whether other sanctions, banking or legal rules affect a given Libyan advertiser is **unverified** in this guide, so take legal advice before opening an account.

Meta's tools produce Libyan reach figures, which suggests audiences can be targeted, but this guide did not confirm Meta's billing options for Libya. Check inside the account. No Libyan price benchmark was found.

## What Is the Cash Situation, and Why Does It Matter?

The Central Bank of Libya reported on 10 December 2025 that its governor told commercial bank managers distribution of 4 billion Libyan dinars in cash would start that week, with ATMs to be kept stocked. He also told banks to expand electronic payment channels to reduce pressure on cash withdrawals. The post gives no figures for electronic payment volumes.

For a seller, this means cash can be hard to get and customers may prefer cards or transfers when offered. State your accepted payment methods in the post, and confirm availability with your bank rather than assuming it.

## What Does the Economy Look Like?

The World Bank lists Libya's 2025 GDP at US$48.1 billion, growth of 13.4% and GDP per capita of US$6,448.8. Growth of that size in a single year reflects a volatile economy, so build offers that you can change quickly and avoid long fixed-price promises. ## How Should a Libyan Business Use Facebook?

A Facebook Page often serves as a business's main online presence: photos, hours, location and customer comments. Fill it in completely and reply to messages quickly. Our [organic and paid social guide](/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media) explains the balance of free posts and ads, and our [WhatsApp Business guide](/blog/how-to-use-whatsapp-business-for-customer-service) covers handling chats.

Run one small ad with a message objective around Tripoli, Benghazi or your own city, and ask each new customer how they found you.

## FAQ

### How many people in Libya use Facebook?

DataReportal lists Facebook ad reach of 6.70 million in late 2025, 89.6% of the population, with a note that the figure exceeds internet users.

### Is Google Ads available in Libya?

Libya is not on Google's sanctions-based restriction list. Other legal or banking limits are unverified here.

### Are Libyans mostly on mobile?

DataReportal reports 14.9 million mobile connections, 199% of the population, but flags the figure as inflated by methodology.

### Does cash shortage affect small businesses?

The Central Bank of Libya announced new cash distribution and urged banks to expand electronic payments in December 2025.

### How much do ads cost in Libya?

No public benchmark was found. Run a small test.
