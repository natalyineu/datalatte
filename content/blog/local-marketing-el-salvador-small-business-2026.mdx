Local marketing for a small business in El Salvador rests on four levers: Facebook and Instagram ads for reach, WhatsApp for conversations and bookings, a Google Business Profile for local discovery, and Google Search for people already looking for a service. The figures here come from platform help pages and vendor pricing, and none is specific to El Salvador, so confirm local audience sizes inside each ad tool before committing a budget.

## Which channels should a San Salvador business start with?

Meta's apps, including Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp and Threads, reach an average of 3.56 billion daily active people, according to [Adwave's Facebook advertising guide](https://adwave.com/resources/facebook-advertising-for-small-business) (secondary, retelling Meta's figures). That reach is global, so it sets a ceiling rather than a local audience size.

For a shop in San Salvador, the practical question is which channel gets a customer to message you. Start with the channel your customers already use, then add search so that people ready to buy can find you.

Google Search matches intent, because a person who types a service into Google is already looking for it. Adwave's pricing pages list Google Search, Facebook and Instagram among channels that run from a $30 daily minimum, which gives a concrete starting point for a test. Run one channel for 30 days before adding a second, so that one budget and one set of conversations can be judged clearly.

## How do Facebook and Instagram ads send people to WhatsApp?

Click-to-WhatsApp ads connect the two. According to the vendor Crescendo Lab, its Click-to-WhatsApp Ads feature moves potential customers from Facebook and Instagram ads into personalized WhatsApp conversations ([Crescendo Lab's WhatsApp marketing guide](https://blog.cresclab.com/en/whatsapp-marketing), vendor).

Assign one person to answer every ad conversation before the campaign launches. If replies arrive late, the ad spend buys conversations that nobody closes, and the test result tells you nothing about the ad itself.

Count conversations rather than clicks. A WhatsApp message that started from an ad is a lead you can track against spend. For a comparable approach in another large WhatsApp-led market, see the [Brazil guide](/blog/local-marketing-brazil-small-business-whatsapp-meta).

## What should a Google Business Profile include?

Google's help page says businesses can add one social media link per platform to a Business Profile, for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, TikTok, X and YouTube ([Google Business Profile help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617), primary).

Because only one link is allowed per platform, link the account that gets the most replies. A business with two Instagram accounts links the one customers actually message.

The list does not include WhatsApp. Add your WhatsApp number through the profile's contact fields where the interface allows it, and check the live help page for current options. Keep the business name identical across the profile, Google Ads and Meta, so customers recognise one brand in Search, Maps and social feeds. For how Google Ads is set up in a different market, the [Germany guide](/blog/local-marketing-germany-small-business-google-ads) walks through the account structure.

## How much does a starting campaign cost?

Adwave, a vendor, publishes a $30 daily minimum for campaigns that include TV, Google Search, Facebook and Instagram, billed from a prepaid wallet ([Adwave garage door advertising page](https://adwave.com/resources/garage-door-company-advertising), vendor). Its pricing table shows $900 for 30 active days at that level.

That minimum is one vendor's floor, not a market rate. Check whether the service and its billing work for businesses in El Salvador before relying on it. Buying directly inside Meta and Google tools may have different minimums, so confirm those on each platform's live pricing pages.

## Can a $300 monthly budget run a café campaign?

The example below is a hypothetical café in San Salvador. Every input is an assumption except the vendor floor, which is sourced.

| Line | Amount | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly budget | $300 | assumption |
| Active days | 30 | assumption |
| Daily budget | $10 | calc: $300 ÷ 30 |
| Meta share (60%) | $180 | assumption split |
| Google Search share (40%) | $120 | assumption split |
| Vendor floor, $30/day × 30 | $900 | sourced, Adwave |
| Gap to vendor floor | $600 | calc: $900 − $300 |
| Ad conversations per month | 90 | assumption |
| Cost per conversation | $3.33 | calc: $300 ÷ 90 |

At $10 a day, the café sits at one-third of Adwave's $30 floor. A managed campaign at that level would need $900 a month. A direct campaign can fit the $300 budget, and the test then becomes whether ads produce conversations at a cost near $3.33 each. If they cost much more, shift budget toward Google Search, where the intent is clearer.

## Who owns the ad accounts?

Adwave's salon advertising page says its Facebook, Instagram and Reddit ads run on advertising accounts that Adwave owns, and that it sets up the Google Ads account for the customer ([Adwave salon advertising page](https://adwave.com/resources/salon-advertising-growing-your-beauty-business), vendor).

Before signing any managed service, ask who holds each ad account, whether you can export campaign and conversation data, and what happens to the accounts if you leave. Those answers decide whether your audience history stays with your business.

## Is TikTok worth adding to the mix?

Adwave's auto care example says TikTok ads run only after the user turns TikTok on in its Customize settings and connects a TikTok account ([Adwave auto care example](https://adwave.com/resources/auto-care-campaign-example), vendor). Its restaurant example repeats the same condition ([Adwave restaurant example](https://adwave.com/resources/restaurant-campaign-example), vendor).

Treat TikTok as a second-phase channel, added once you have a content routine and a connected account. No TikTok usage figure for El Salvador is included here, so check the platform's own audience tools for local reach before budgeting.

## FAQ

### Does this article include El Salvador usage statistics?

No. The platform and vendor figures here are global or vendor-published. Check local audience estimates inside Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads, which show reach for the location you target.

### Is WhatsApp on the list of Google Business Profile social links?

No. Google's help page lists Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, TikTok, X and YouTube. Add your WhatsApp number through the profile's contact fields where the interface allows it.

### What is the lowest budget to start?

Adwave's published minimum is $30 a day for campaigns that include TV, Google Search, Facebook and Instagram. Direct ads may have lower or different minimums, so check each platform's live pricing before deciding.

### Should a business own its ad accounts?

Ask every vendor. Adwave says it runs ads on accounts it owns, so confirm the ownership and exit terms before you commit.
