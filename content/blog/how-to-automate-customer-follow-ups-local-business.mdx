A local business can automate follow-ups in five steps: collect consent, send a thank-you after each visit, ask for a Google review, remind customers to rebook and honour opt-outs. The rules matter: the FTC says each email that violates CAN-SPAM can be fined up to $53,088, and marketing texts to mobile numbers need prior express written consent under FCC rules.

This guide explains each step with the published rules and gives an assumption-based example. It is not legal advice.

## Step 1: How Do You Get Permission to Follow Up?

Ask at booking or checkout, record the answer and keep the date. For marketing texts to cellphones, 47 CFR 64.1200 requires prior express written consent for autodialed or prerecorded advertising calls and texts, defined as a signed written agreement (an electronic signature counts) that names the phone number and says signing is not a condition of buying anything.

Email follows the CAN-SPAM Act: accurate header information, an honest subject line, a clear ad label where it is an ad, your valid postal address and a working opt-out. Transactional messages such as a booking confirmation are treated differently, so ask a lawyer how your messages are classified.

## Step 2: What Should the Thank-You Message Say?

Send it within a day: thank the customer, repeat what they had done and tell them how to reach you. Keep it short and plain.

An automated email platform can do this once. Mailchimp's free plan lists 250 contacts and up to 500 sends a month, but does not include marketing automation flows, so check which plan you need. See [cheap marketing ideas](/blog/cheap-marketing-ideas-small-business) for low-cost tools.

## Step 3: How Do You Ask for a Review?

Send the Google review link or QR code a day or two after the visit. Google's help says customers must be signed into a Google Account and that offering free or discounted goods in exchange for reviews is prohibited. BrightLocal's 2026 survey says 97% of consumers read online reviews.

Send the link to every customer, not only happy ones. Google says reviews must reflect a genuine experience. Our [guide to automating review requests](/blog/how-to-automate-google-review-requests) shows the flow.

## Step 4: When Should You Remind Customers to Rebook?

Pick the interval from your own service cycle: a haircut every six weeks, a dental clean every six months. Send a reminder two weeks before that point, with one booking link. Send it by email, or by text only if the customer gave written consent.

For retail repeat sales, see [SMS marketing for retail](/blog/sms-marketing-for-retail). WhatsApp can also work where customers use it; read [WhatsApp Business marketing: free vs paid](/blog/whatsapp-business-marketing-local-business).

## Step 5: How Do You Handle Opt-Outs?

Build it into every message. Under CAN-SPAM you must honour an opt-out within 10 business days. The FTC also says hiring another company to send your email does not transfer your legal responsibility. For texts, 47 CFR 64.1200 treats a "stop" reply as a reasonable revocation method and requires requests to be honoured within ten business days.

Stop all marketing messages to that person, not only one campaign.

## FAQ

### Can I text customers without permission?
Marketing texts to cellphones need prior express written consent under 47 CFR 64.1200. Ask a lawyer about transactional messages.

### How fast must I honour an unsubscribe?
CAN-SPAM requires it within 10 business days.

### Can I offer a discount for a review?
No. Google prohibits incentives in exchange for reviews.

### What is the penalty for breaking CAN-SPAM?
The FTC lists up to $53,088 for each separate email in violation.

### What tool should I use?
That depends on volume. Check each plan's automation features, as Mailchimp's free plan excludes automation flows.
