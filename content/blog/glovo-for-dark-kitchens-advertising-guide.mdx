A dark kitchen on Glovo pays two separate costs: a commission on every order and, if it advertises, a campaign budget on top. Glovo's advertising terms say the partner sets a monthly budget, pays only for the amount consumed, and gets no refund for spend already used. Glovo does not publish a public price list for ads. Commission rates vary by plan and country, and the only detailed breakdown found is a secondary blog, so check your own contract before planning a budget.

This guide covers delivery-only kitchens and virtual brands, not restaurants with a dining room. Anything Glovo has not published is marked as such.

## How Does Glovo Charge a Dark Kitchen?

Glovo's marketplace takes a commission on each order. The most detailed public breakdown found is a May 2026 blog post by Fooderise, which is secondary, mixes several European countries, and is internally inconsistent on the overall range. It lists Marketplace at 15-18%, Standard at 25-28%, Premium at 28-30% and Top at 30-35%, all before VAT.

The same post estimates all-in costs of 35-42% of revenue once VAT on the commission, service charges of about 1.5%, restaurant-funded promotions and refunds are included. Treat that as an indication only. The commission is agreed per contract, so ask your Glovo account manager for the current rate card in writing and use that figure in every calculation.

## What Do Glovo's Advertising Terms Say?

Glovo's terms for its promotion and self-service advertising tools, in the version dated August 2025, set out the money rules that matter most for budgeting:

- The partner pays for the amount effectively consumed by a campaign.
- Self-service campaigns run on a monthly budget, which is the maximum monthly spend.
- Glovo may stop a campaign at 90% of the agreed budget, and the partner can be charged for spend above the original budget.
- Flash Offers are charged per fulfilled order, based on order value.
- Costs already incurred are not refundable.
- Costs are deducted from Glovo invoices.

The terms give no minimum budget and no click or order prices. Launch coverage from 2023 describes Partner Ads as paid per click, but that coverage comes from a single non-European market and may not match current formats in your country. No source reviewed publishes average cost per click or per order for Glovo Ads, so any figure quoted without a source is a guess.

## Should a Dark Kitchen Advertise on Glovo?

Advertise only when you can show that an extra order still earns money after commission and food cost. A dark kitchen has no walk-in traffic, so ranking inside the app drives most orders, and paid placement is one way to buy visibility. A Fooderise post on dark kitchen marketing (December 2025, no figures) calls paid ads almost essential at launch. That is an opinion, not measured data.

Ads do not fix a weak menu, slow preparation or poor ratings. Fix those first, because a promoted listing that leads to a poorly rated page converts badly and burns budget faster. Check ratings, prep times and menu accuracy for two weeks before raising any ad budget.

## What Is the Break-Even Math?

All inputs below are **assumptions**, not Glovo figures. Replace them with your own numbers from your invoices.

| Per order | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Basket | 20.00 | Assumption |
| Commission at 28% | 5.60 | Assumption, inside the Standard range in the Fooderise post |
| Food and packaging at 32% | 6.40 | Assumption |
| Left before labour and rent | 8.00 | Basket minus the two lines above |
| Monthly ad budget | 500 | Assumption |
| Extra orders needed to break even | about 63 | 500 divided by 8.00 |

At these inputs, a campaign must bring about 63 orders a month that would not otherwise have arrived. Orders from regular customers who would have ordered anyway do not count. Glovo's reports may not separate new orders from existing ones, so compare order counts for the same weeks before and after the campaign.

To find your real platform cost, open your latest Glovo invoice, add up commission, service charges and marketing lines, and divide by gross sales. That percentage is the base for every ad decision.

## How Do Virtual Brands Change the Plan?

The Fooderise post (secondary) says Glovo runs a Glovo Kitchen program for delivery-only restaurants, with faster validation and the option to run several virtual brands from one kitchen. It gives no fee details for that program. More brands mean more listings and more chances to be found, but also more menus to keep accurate and more ratings to protect.

Split the ad budget by brand and track results per brand. A single combined budget hides which concept earns money. Start with the brand that has the best ratings, and add the others only after the first one covers its own budget in the break-even table above.

## Where Do Other Paid Channels Fit?

Glovo ads help with orders inside one app. If the goal is broader awareness, for example a new neighbourhood or a new virtual brand, compare the cost per order with other options. The [DOOH advertising complete guide](/blog/dooh-advertising-complete-guide-2026) covers out-of-home formats that reach people who are not yet using delivery apps, although a delivery-only kitchen will usually see a longer payback from that channel.

Operators in Canada can also review [local business marketing in Canada](/blog/local-business-marketing-canada-guide-2026) to see how small local channels compare with platform ads. Whichever channel you pick, use the same test: extra orders multiplied by margin after commission must exceed spend.

## FAQ

### How much do Glovo Ads cost?

Glovo does not publish a price list in the documents reviewed. Its terms say the partner sets a monthly budget and pays for what is consumed, and Flash Offers are charged per order. Ask your Glovo account manager for current terms in your country.

### What commission does Glovo charge dark kitchens?

A May 2026 Fooderise post lists 15-18% to 30-35% depending on plan, before VAT. It is a secondary source and its overall range is inconsistent. Your contract is the only reliable figure.

### Can I cancel a Glovo campaign?

Glovo's terms describe self-service campaigns that can be managed in the tools, but partners pay for the amount already consumed. Spent budget is not refunded.

### Does Glovo guarantee a return on ad spend?

No guarantee appears in the terms reviewed. Any return figure quoted in marketing material is Glovo's own claim and not an independent result, so build your plan on your own break-even numbers.

### Is advertising on Glovo worth it for a ghost kitchen?

It is worth it only if the extra orders cover the ad budget after commission and food cost. Run the break-even example above with your own basket size, commission and budget.
