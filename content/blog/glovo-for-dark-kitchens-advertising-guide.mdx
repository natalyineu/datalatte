A dark kitchen on Glovo pays two separate costs: a commission on every order and, if it advertises, a campaign budget on top. Glovo's own advertising terms say the partner sets a monthly budget, pays only for the amount consumed, and gets no refund for spend already used. Glovo does not publish a price list for ads. Commission rates in this guide come from a third-party blog and vary by plan and country, so check your contract.

This guide is for delivery-only kitchens and virtual brands, not for restaurants with a dining room. Glovo's ad products were described in 2023 launch coverage and its terms were updated in August 2025. Anything Glovo has not published is marked as such.

## How Does Glovo Charge a Dark Kitchen?

Glovo's marketplace takes a commission on each order. The only detailed public breakdown we could open is a May 2026 blog post by Fooderise (secondary, mixes several European countries, and contradicts itself on the overall range). It lists Marketplace at 15-18%, Standard at 25-28%, Premium at 28-30% and Top at 30-35%, before VAT.

The same post estimates all-in costs of 35-42% of revenue once VAT on the commission, service charges of about 1.5%, restaurant-funded promotions and refunds are included. Treat that as an indication. The fee is agreed per contract, so ask your account manager for the current rate card.

## What Does Glovo Say About Ads for Partners?

Glovo launched Glovo Ads in 2023. Launch coverage describes two products: Brand Ads for large brands, and Partner Ads for small and medium restaurants and shops, paid per click, with campaigns that can be paused at any time. That coverage is dated 2023 and comes from the Kenyan launch, so formats and pricing may differ in your market.

Glovo's own terms for its promotion and self-service advertising tools, last seen in the August 2025 version, say more about money:

- The partner pays "the amount effectively consumed" of a campaign.
- Self-service campaigns run on a monthly budget, the maximum monthly spend.
- Glovo may stop a campaign at 90% of the agreed budget, and the partner can be charged for spend above the original budget.
- Flash Offers are charged per fulfilled order, based on order value.
- Costs already incurred are not refundable.
- Costs are deducted from Glovo invoices.

The terms give no minimum budget and no click prices. No source we could open publishes average cost per click or per order for Glovo Ads, so any number you see quoted without a source is a guess.

## Should a Dark Kitchen Advertise on Glovo at All?

Advertise only when you can show that an extra order still earns money after commission. A dark kitchen has no walk-in traffic, so ranking inside the app is the main source of orders, and paid placement is one way to buy visibility. A Fooderise post on dark kitchen marketing (December 2025, no figures) also calls paid ads almost mandatory to get started, which is an opinion, not data.

Ads do not fix a weak menu, slow prep or poor ratings. Read our guides on [Glovo promotions and visibility](/blog/how-to-grow-on-glovo-marketing-tips) and [Glovo promoted listings](/blog/glovo-promoted-listings-restaurants-guide) to fix the basics first.

## What Is the Break-Even Math?

All inputs below are **assumptions**, not Glovo figures. Replace them with your own.

| Per order | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Basket | 20.00 | Assumption |
| Commission at 28% | 5.60 | Assumption, inside Fooderise's Standard range |
| Food and packaging at 32% | 6.40 | Assumption |
| Left before labour and rent | 8.00 | Basket minus the two lines above |
| Monthly ad budget | 500 | Assumption |
| Extra orders needed to break even | about 63 | 500 divided by 8.00 |

If the campaign brings fewer than about 63 orders that you would not have received anyway, it loses money at these inputs. Orders from regular customers who would have ordered anyway do not count. Glovo's reports do not necessarily separate the two, so compare order counts before and after on your own.

<Callout type="tip" title="Do This Today">
Open your latest Glovo invoice, add up commission, service charges and marketing lines, and divide by gross sales. That is your real platform cost, and the base for any ad decision.
</Callout>

## How Do Virtual Brands Change the Plan?

Fooderise (secondary) says Glovo has a Glovo Kitchen program for delivery-only restaurants, with faster validation and the option to run several virtual brands from one kitchen. It gives no fee details for the program. More brands mean more listings and more places to be found, but also more menus to keep accurate and more ratings to protect.

Split the budget by brand and keep records per brand. A single combined ad budget hides which concept earns money. Start with your best-rated brand, and add the others once the first one pays back. ## FAQ

### How much do Glovo Ads cost?

Glovo does not publish a price list in the documents we could open. Its terms say the partner sets a monthly budget and pays for what is consumed, and Flash Offers are charged per order. Ask your Glovo account manager for current terms in your country.

### What commission does Glovo charge dark kitchens?

A May 2026 Fooderise post lists 15-18% to 30-35% depending on plan, before VAT. It is a secondary source and its overall range is inconsistent. Your contract is the only reliable figure.

### Can I cancel a Glovo campaign?

Glovo's terms say partners can cancel at any time in the tools, but pay for the amount already consumed. Spent budget is not refunded.

### Does Glovo guarantee a return on ad spend?

No. Launch coverage repeats a Glovo claim of up to 5x return on ad spend for Brand Ads, which is Glovo's own marketing claim and not an independent result. Do not plan on it.

### Is advertising on Glovo worth it for a ghost kitchen?

Only if extra orders cover the ad budget after commission and food cost. Use the break-even example above with your own numbers.
