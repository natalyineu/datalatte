## How Big Is the Namibian Online Market?

DataReportal gives a population of 3.11 million, 2.00 million internet users (64.4%) and 2.80 million mobile connections (90.2%). About 1.11 million people, 35.6%, are offline. The country is small and spread out, so a Windhoek business and a coastal lodge reach different audiences.

Facebook reaches 44.4% of adults aged 18 and over and 37.9% of people aged 13 and over, according to the same report. LinkedIn shows 500 thousand members (16.1%), but DataReportal counts registered members there, not active users. Compare with neighbors such as [Zimbabwe](/blog/local-marketing-zimbabwe-small-business-2026) and [Mozambique](/blog/local-marketing-mozambique-small-business-2026).

## How Much Do Tourists Matter in Namibia?

The 2025 tourist arrivals report, published on 29 June 2026 and covered by The Brief, gives 1,345,168 international visitor arrivals, down 6.9%. International tourists numbered 1,217,108, down 3.2%. Holidaymakers were 48.4%, visiting friends and relatives 28.6%, and business travelers 18.5%.

African arrivals rose to 917,474 while overseas arrivals fell to 299,634. National park visits rose 5.4% to 994,780, and Etosha was the most visited. For a lodge, guide or restaurant near a park, visitors are a real audience, but overseas demand fell in 2025.

This is a secondary source reporting the ministry's figures; the ministry report itself was not opened.

## How Do You Show Up on Google Maps?

Google says local ranking uses relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Reviews and ratings can help prominence. For tourist-facing businesses, add photos, accurate hours and a response to each review, since visitors read reviews before booking. ## Worked Example: A Guesthouse Facebook Test

All numbers are **assumptions**.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget, 30 days | $150 |
| Enquiries from the ad | 10 |
| Share that become bookings | 20% |
| Bookings | 2 |
| Average booking margin | $120 |

Two bookings at $120 margin give $240 against $150 spend, a gain of $90. If only one enquiry in ten books, the margin of $120 is below the $150 cost and the test loses $30. Track enquiries by asking "how did you find us" at booking.
