## How much should a fitness studio in Fresno spend on Google Ads each month?

The same guide gives separate budget benchmarks by studio size. A small independent studio with under 200 members is pointed to $600–$1,200 a month on Google Ads, while a mid-size gym with 200–600 members is pointed to $1,500–$2,500. The guide is written for gyms, so a boutique studio may land at the lower end of either range while it tests.

| Studio profile | Monthly Google Ads benchmark |
|----------------|------------------------------|
| Small independent studio (under 200 members) | $600–$1,200 |
| Mid-size gym (200–600 members) | $1,500–$2,500 |
| Well-optimised fitness campaign | $1,000–$2,000 |

Google Ads is one line in a total marketing budget. [Mariana Tek](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/) notes that many boutique studios budget between 5 and 10 percent of monthly revenue for marketing. Assumption: a studio with $30,000 in monthly revenue would have $1,500–$3,000 for all marketing. At that size, the $1,000–$2,000 benchmark would take most of the allowance, so starting at the lower end makes sense.

## What does that budget buy in clicks and trial sign-ups?

The worked example below uses assumption inputs taken from the midpoints of the published ranges. It shows how far a single month sits from the cost target.

At 4.5%, reaching 50 trial sign-ups would require about 1,111 clicks. The gap closes only through a lower cost per click, a higher click-to-trial rate, or both. Tracking those two numbers separately shows which one needs work.

## Which keywords should a Fresno studio bid on first?

Start with the service-plus-location searches your members actually type, such as "gym near me" or "fitness studio Fresno," and add neighbourhood names your members use. Run exact match on the terms that already convert, then add phrase match for local variants. Broad match belongs later, once the tighter campaigns are producing trial sign-ups. The [keyword guide for fitness studios](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-fitness-studios-in-2026) covers how to build those lists.

## Which negative keywords stop wasted clicks?

Negative keywords block searches that rarely turn into members. Add these to every campaign from the start:

Job-related terms such as "jobs," "hiring," "career," and "fitness studio salary."

Do-it-yourself terms such as "how to," "course," and "certification," which signal someone who wants to train alone.

Cities outside your service area, so the budget stays within the radius you can serve.

Unrelated modifiers such as "franchise," "wholesale," and "supplier."

The [Google Ads guide for fitness studios](/blog/fitness-studio-google-ads-guide) includes a fuller setup walkthrough.

## Should the Google Business Profile come before ad spend?

Google Business Profile is a free tool that displays business hours, phone numbers, addresses, reviews, and photos directly in search results. The [Association of Fitness Studios](https://member.afsfitness.com/content/how-make-your-fitness-business-stand-out-google) describes how the Google Map Pack lists nearby businesses for queries such as "personal training studios near me." Someone who clicks an ad and then lands on a thin profile has less reason to book.

[Mariana Tek](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/) reports that Google review volume can start influencing visibility within a few weeks. The same source's table lists a 6 to 12 month payoff for local SEO and Google reviews. Ads and profile work should run side by side, not in sequence. The [profile optimisation guide](/blog/fitness-studio-google-business-profile-optimization) explains what to fill in first.

## How long before a Fresno studio sees results?

Paid clicks can bring in enquiries quickly, but the sources reviewed here do not give a Google Ads payback timeline for fitness studios. ## What should a studio track during its first month?

Follow these steps before judging the campaign:

1. Set the monthly budget against the benchmark for your studio size.
2. Record clicks and cost per click each week.
3. Set up call tracking, booking-form completions, and direction requests as conversions.
4. Log trial sign-ups and divide spend by them to get cost per trial lead.
5. Compare cost per trial lead with the under $30 target.
6. Track converting members and compare cost per member with the $150–$200 target.
7. Confirm that every trial enquiry gets a follow-up within 24 hours.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much should a fitness studio in Fresno spend on Google Ads?

The published benchmark for a well-optimised fitness campaign is $1,000–$2,000 a month. A small independent studio under 200 members is pointed to $600–$1,200. These are national benchmarks, so check current Fresno cost-per-click data before setting a bid.

### What cost per lead should a fitness studio aim for?

The guide sets a target cost per trial lead under $30. It also sets a cost per converting member of $150–$200. Treat both as targets to test against your own numbers, not guarantees.

### Is a 3–6% click-to-trial rate realistic?

The guide projects this rate for a well-optimised campaign. It is a planning assumption. Measure your own click-to-trial rate for at least one full month before deciding whether the campaign needs changes.

### Should a studio use broad match keywords?

Start with exact and phrase match on terms that already convert. Broad match can find new searches, but it works better once tighter campaigns are producing trial sign-ups and the negative keyword list is in place.
