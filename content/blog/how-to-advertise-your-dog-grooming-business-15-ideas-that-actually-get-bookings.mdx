To advertise a dog grooming business, fix your free Google Business Profile and reviews first, then add a small Google search campaign with a call button, then test local extras such as flyers, partnerships and email reminders. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put Animals and Pets search clicks at $4.06 on average, with a 16.22% conversion rate and $31.50 per lead. Those are industry averages, not a promise for your town.

This guide lists 15 ideas in the order worth trying them. Costs come from published pages. Where no figure is published, the text says so. Examples labelled as assumptions are illustrations.

## Which Free Ways to Advertise a Grooming Business Work First?

Start with channels that cost time, not money.

**1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile.** Google says local ranking rests on relevance, distance and prominence, and that complete, accurate information makes a business more likely to appear. It also says you cannot pay for a better local ranking. Add hours, services, photos and your phone number. Our [Google Business Profile guide for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-for-pet-groomers) has the field list.

**2. Ask for reviews every week.** BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US consumers found that 97% read reviews of local businesses, 47% will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews, and 74% only consider reviews from the last three months. It is not groomer-specific, but it shows why a steady flow matters. Reply to each review.

**3. Make booking and calling one tap.** Put a phone link and booking button at the top of every page and on the profile.

**4. Set up a referral offer.** A discount for both the referrer and the new client costs nothing until someone books. No published data shows the average result for groomers, so track your own.

**5. Partner with vets, pet stores and trainers.** Leave a stack of cards with a QR code and a tracked offer. Partners send you customers that match your service.

## What Does Paid Online Advertising Cost for a Groomer?

Paid ads reward a tight setup. Averages for Animals and Pets in WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 data: $4.06 CPC, 7.49% CTR, 16.22% conversion rate and $31.50 CPL. The all-industry averages are $5.42 CPC and $66.69 CPL. Pet services look cheaper than the average, but a "conversion" in these reports is usually a call or form, not a paid groom.

**6. Google Search ads.** People searching "dog groomer near me" are ready to book. Use a call asset so people can tap to call. Google says clicks on call assets on mobile cost the same as headline clicks. Our [Google Ads for dog groomers guide](/blog/google-ads-for-dog-groomers-complete-2026-guide) covers setup.

**7. Performance Max with a local goal.** Google lists "Local store visits and promotions" as an objective for Performance Max, which serves across Search, Maps, YouTube and Display. It needs conversion tracking and good images.

**8. Meta ads on Facebook and Instagram.** Before-and-after photos suit the format. Meta says its minimum budget depends on vertical, bid strategy and optimization, and most advertisers charged per impression should allow at least $1 per day. See our [Facebook ads guide for pet groomers](/blog/pet-groomer-meta-ads).

**9. Yelp Ads.** Yelp lists Yelp Ads from $150 per month and says you can cancel anytime. Test it only if Yelp shows up in your area's searches, and use a separate tracking number.

## What Are Cheap Offline Ways to Get Dog Owners to Notice You?

**10. Flyers and postcards with a QR code.** USPS lists EDDM Retail at $0.26 per piece, at least 200 pieces per mailing, so the minimum postage is $52. Printing is extra. Point the QR code at a tagged booking link.

**11. Local sponsorships.** A youth team or rescue event can put your name in front of pet families. Use a unique code to count results. Our guide to [local sponsorships](/blog/local-sponsorships-marketing) shows how.

**12. Spotify audio ads.** Spotify's help page says an Ads Manager campaign needs a minimum budget of 250 in your local currency. That is a lot for one groomer. Read what [Spotify ads cost for a local business](/blog/how-much-do-spotify-ads-cost-pricing-for-local-business) before you try it.

## How Do You Keep Clients Booking Again?

**13. Rebooking reminders by email or text.** Groomers rely on repeat visits. Mailchimp's free plan allows 250 contacts and 500 sends per month, but does not include automation flows or scheduling, so a paid plan or another tool is needed for automatic reminders. Our [guide to retaining grooming clients](/blog/how-to-retain-pet-grooming-clients-long-term) has message ideas.

**14. Before-and-after posts.** Post with the owner's permission, tag the breed and the service, and add a booking link. No platform publishes a reliable engagement average for groomers, so judge by bookings.

**15. Seasonal offers.** Shedding season, holidays and summer tidy-ups give people a reason to book now. Schedule the offer two weeks ahead so slots fill early.

## What Does a $400 Google Ads Month Look Like?

All inputs below are **assumptions** based on the Animals and Pets benchmarks. A "conversion" is a call or form, not a booking.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $400 |
| Cost per click | $4.06 (benchmark) |
| Clicks | about 98 ($400 / $4.06) |
| Conversion rate | 16.22% (benchmark) |
| Conversions (calls and forms) | about 16 |
| Cost per conversion | about $25 |
| Share that book a groom (assumption) | 40% |
| Bookings | about 6 |
| Cost per booking | about $63 |

At these numbers, six bookings bring about $450 on the first visit, which covers the $400. Profit comes from repeat visits, so rebooking reminders matter. If your real conversion rate is half the benchmark, cost per booking doubles. Run it for 60 days and compare your own numbers, not the benchmark.

## FAQ

### How much should a dog groomer spend on advertising?

No published standard exists. Search clicks average $4.06 for Animals and Pets, so $400 a month buys roughly 98 clicks. Start with an amount you can test for 60 days. See the [budget math in our groomer Google Ads guide](/blog/google-ads-for-dog-groomers-complete-2026-guide).

### What is the best free way to get dog grooming clients?

A complete Google Business Profile with photos, hours, a phone number and regular reviews. Google says it cannot be bought, only earned through relevant, complete and well-known listings.

### Should a groomer use Yelp Ads?

Only after checking that your customers use Yelp. Yelp Ads start at $150 per month. Use a separate phone number and review the calls after 60 days.

### Are Facebook ads worth it for pet groomers?

They can work for showing your results and local offers, but the data to prove it for groomers is not published. Test a small budget with one clear offer and track bookings.

### How do I track which ad brought the booking?

Ask on the phone and in your booking form, use a different QR code per flyer, and tag links with campaign parameters. Google Business Profile shows a Calls count of taps on the call button.
