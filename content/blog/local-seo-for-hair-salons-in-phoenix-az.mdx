## What Is the Google Map Pack and Why Does It Matter for a Salon?

The map pack is the block of three local listings that appears above regular results when someone searches for a service nearby. [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-audit-checklist-15-things-to-fix-today) defines it as the top three business listings in local search results. A salon outside those three gets far fewer taps, even if its website is strong.

Booking is the reason this matters. [Booksy](https://biz.booksy.com/blog/salon-marketing) states that Reserve with Google lets clients book directly from Search and Maps in under 30 seconds. A salon that has booking connected through its profile captures that intent at the moment it forms.

Phoenix has no special ranking rule. The same GBP and citation signals apply in Phoenix as in any other city, so the checklist below is the starting point for a Phoenix salon, with neighbourhood names added where customers actually live.

## Which Google Business Profile Settings Should a Salon Get Right First?

Google states that its Business Profile is free to set up and manage. [Google](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/) describes it as a free listing on Search and Maps, so cost is not a barrier to starting.

Category is the first decision that affects ranking. [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones) recommends reviewing the top three to five businesses in local map results for your service search and noting which category they use. Adwave also states that the primary category is the number one ranking factor for getting into the local pack, though the analysis behind that claim is not named.

Accuracy comes next. [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers) advises making sure hours, phone number, and address are 100% accurate. A business description allows 750 characters, so use the space to name your services and your neighbourhood in plain language. If you are a home-based or mobile stylist, Adwave notes you can hide your street address and be designated a Service Area Business.

## How Many Photos and Posts Does a Salon Profile Need?

Adwave recommends at least 15 high-quality photos, and says a profile with a robust gallery gets far more clicks and calls. A separate [Adwave to-do list](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack) suggests at least 10 as a minimum. Use before-and-after colour work, a clean interior, and stylists at work.

Posts need the same discipline. Adwave advises at least one new Google Post a week to keep the profile fresh. Google's [post help page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en) says a post is public on Search and Maps only once its status is Live, and Pending or Not approved posts do not show. Check the status after each publish.

Q&A is worth seeding. Google launched Q&A in Maps in 2017, and [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-q-and-a) reports that answers in Google's AI-driven answering draw on business profile data, website content, and reviews. Write your own answers to common questions so the information is accurate.

## How Many Reviews Does a Hair Salon Need to Compete?

A third-party analysis by Blogging Wizard, cited by [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business), examined 2 million Google Business Profile pages and found that businesses with over 200 Google reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack. Treat 200 as a target to build toward, not a guaranteed result.

Google states that reviews show next to a business's profile in Maps and Search. [Google's review guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122) confirms that reviews are visible at the point of decision, so the text and star rating shape clicks as well as ranking.

## What Does Reaching 200 Reviews Take? A Worked Example

The timeline depends on how many reviews you collect each month. The inputs below are assumptions for illustration, not benchmarks.

- Current reviews (assumption): 40
- Target (from the 200-review benchmark above): 200
- New reviews per month (assumption): 10

Reviews needed: 200 minus 40 equals 160. At 10 per month, 160 divided by 10 gives 16 months. At 20 per month (assumption), the same gap closes in 8 months. The calculation shows why a steady request process matters more than a single burst. If your salon runs 60 appointments a month and 1 in 6 clients leaves a review after a prompt, that is roughly 10 reviews a month, which matches the first scenario.

## Why Must Citations Match Exactly Across Directories?

Adwave states that a business's name, address, and phone number must be exactly the same on the website, the GBP, and major directories such as Yelp, Apple Maps, and Bing Places. Small differences count. Adwave's [local SEO guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack) says the address must match right down to the comma and to whether you write "St." or "Street."

Start with the listings you already have. Fix conflicts before adding new ones, because duplicate or conflicting listings make the record harder for Google to trust. Check Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing Places, and any booking platform your salon uses, including Booksy and StyleSeat.

## Should a Salon Also Claim Apple Business Connect?

Adwave describes Apple Business Connect as an alternative to a Google profile and says it gives access to over 1.5 billion affluent iOS users across Maps, Siri, and Wallet. That figure is not attributed to a source on the page, so treat it as a claim.

The same page reports that 88% of people use Google Maps and 12% use Apple Maps, with no named source. In a maps-only comparison that excludes web search, Adwave says Apple Business Connect has a 7% share and is growing. Apple's dashboard reports Profile Views, the count of people who saw your business card in Apple Maps. Claiming it costs little, and it extends the same accurate NAP to another map.

## What Local SEO Steps Should a Phoenix Salon Take, in Order?

Work through this checklist in sequence. Each step depends on the one before it.

1. Claim or verify your GBP, then confirm the name, address, and phone match your storefront or service area.
2. Choose your primary category by checking the top three to five local results for your main service.
3. Write a business description of up to 750 characters naming your services and neighbourhoods.
4. Upload at least 15 high-quality photos and keep adding fresh ones.
5. Publish one post a week, then confirm each shows Live status.
6. Seed Q&A with accurate answers to common questions.
7. Audit citations on Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing Places, and booking platforms against your GBP record.
8. Set up a review request after each appointment and reply to every review.
9. Track monthly results and compare against your baseline.

Adwave also notes that a business may start ranking for its own name a few weeks after verification. That is a vendor estimate, so use it as a check, not a deadline.

## How Do You Measure Whether Map Pack Work Is Working?

Adwave lists six metrics it considers useful: GBP impressions, map-pack views, clicks to call, direction requests, website visits from GBP, and branded search lift over time. Record these monthly so you can separate real change from noise.

Pair GBP data with rank checks. Search your top keywords in a private window and note where your listing appears. If you also run paid search, the [Google Ads guide for Phoenix hair salons](/blog/google-ads-for-hair-salons-in-phoenix-az) covers how paid and local efforts fit together.

## FAQ

### Does a Phoenix salon need a separate Google profile for each neighbourhood?

Not for a single location. Google Ads location assets pull data directly from Google Maps and Google Business Profile, and up to 10 locations can be linked to an ads account when the standard options do not work, but those locations must belong to your business. Check the current rules on Google's [location assets help page](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en).

### Are reviews more important than photos for ranking?

Both feed the same profile. Blogging Wizard's analysis, cited by Adwave, links 200+ reviews to better local pack odds, while Adwave recommends 15 photos for clicks and calls. Reviews drive ranking, and photos drive the click once someone sees the listing.

### Can a salon rank without a storefront?

Yes, if it hides its street address and is designated a Service Area Business, as Adwave describes. Confirm the setting in your profile before you change it.

### Where do the category choices come from?

Review the top three to five businesses that appear in local map results for your service and note the category each uses. This is Adwave's recommended method.
