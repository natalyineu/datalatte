Connected TV (CTV) advertising lets a Jacksonville, FL business place short video ads in front of households streaming shows on platforms such as Hulu and Peacock. Targeting can be narrowed by ZIP code and demographics. The practical approach is a small, time-limited test with a fixed budget, followed by a review of results after 60 to 90 days. This guide covers how the channel works, how it compares with search and display using published benchmarks, a worked budget example, and how to measure whether it is working.

## What Is CTV Advertising for a Jacksonville Business?

CTV ads run on the television screen through streaming services, so they reach households rather than individual phones. A viewer sees a video ad in a TV-style environment, which is a different experience from a search result or a social feed. The format is built for brand familiarity rather than immediate clicks.

That difference affects how the spend should be judged. Search ads capture people who already want what a business sells, while CTV builds recognition before that need appears. Measure it on that basis, not on click volume.

## How Do Local Businesses Target CTV Audiences?

Most local CTV buys start with geography. A business can enter its address and choose a radius, or pick specific ZIP codes where its customers live. Demographic filters such as age, income, or household composition can narrow the audience further, but each filter reduces reach and usually raises cost. Add them one at a time so the effect of each is visible.

Frequency is the second setting to control. Too many exposures to the same household wear out the message, and too few may not register. Set a cap in the platform and review it weekly during the first month.

Google's own display product follows a similar logic of broad reach with targeting controls. According to [Google's Display campaign help page](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190), Display campaigns can reach people across 35 million websites and apps, including YouTube and Gmail. That is a different channel from CTV, but it shows how a platform can extend reach beyond search.

## What Do Published Benchmarks Say About Search and Display Costs?

Benchmarks help set expectations before spending begins. WordStream's published Google Ads averages across all industries give a search click-through rate of 3.17% and a display click-through rate of 0.46%. The average search cost per click is $2.69 and the display cost per click is $0.63 ([WordStream, secondary](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks)).

These figures are averages across many advertisers, not local businesses in Jacksonville specifically. Use them as a sanity check rather than a forecast. If search metrics sit far from these averages, investigate the cause before adding budget to any channel.

## How Much Should a Jacksonville Business Budget for a CTV Test?

The sources used for this guide do not publish current CTV rates, CPMs, or platform minimums for Hulu, Peacock, or other services. Check each platform's rate card before committing, and use the calculation below to test different budgets against the quoted numbers.

The worked example for a 4-week test uses the following inputs, all labelled as assumptions for illustration:

- Test budget: $500 over 4 weeks (assumption)
- Cost per thousand impressions (CPM): $25 (assumption)
- Target frequency: 4 impressions per household per month (assumption)

Impressions = $500 ÷ $25 × 1,000 = 20,000 impressions.

Households reached = 20,000 ÷ 4 = 5,000 households.

Cost per household reached = $500 ÷ 5,000 = $0.10.

If the platform's real CPM is higher, the same budget reaches fewer households, so rerun the numbers with the quoted CPM before launch. A test that reaches only a few thousand households is still useful for checking creative and response, but it will not show a large brand effect.

## How Should You Measure a CTV Campaign?

Track signals that CTV can plausibly move. Direct searches for the business name, branded search clicks if search ads run, and website direct traffic are the usual starting points. Compare these during the flight with the weeks before it, and keep the same tracking settings throughout.

Add a simple question to every new-customer intake: how did you hear about us? Record the answers weekly. Self-reported attribution is imperfect, but it offers a view that platform reporting cannot provide.

Allow 60 to 90 days before judging the channel. A four-week test can show whether creative and targeting work, but brand effects take longer to appear in search and direct traffic.

Often yes, because the two channels do different jobs. CTV introduces the business, and search captures people who look for it later. ## FAQ

### Is CTV advertising worth it for a small local business?

It can be, if the goal is brand recognition in a defined area and slower results are acceptable. Start with a small test budget, define a measurement plan before launch, and review after 60 to 90 days. If the test shows no lift in direct or branded search, stop or change the approach.

### How is CTV different from Google Display ads?

CTV runs video on streaming services on the TV screen. Google Display ads appear across websites, apps, YouTube, and Gmail. Google's help page describes Display reach across 35 million websites and apps ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190)). Formats, targeting options, and cost structures differ, so compare them with separate tests.

### What ZIP-code radius should a local CTV campaign use?

The sources reviewed here do not name a correct radius. Start with the areas where current customers live, which can usually be seen in customer records or intake data. Expand only after the first test shows which areas respond.

### Which metrics should be trusted most for CTV?

Trends over time matter more than single-week spikes. Compare branded search and direct website traffic against the same weeks before the campaign, and record new-customer answers consistently. Platform-reported views show delivery, not business impact.
