A service business should start Google Ads with one Search campaign per core service, call tracking, a tight location radius, and a landing page that matches the search. Click costs differ by trade. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks show $8.33 per click for Home and Home Improvement and $5.87 for Business Services, against $5.42 across all industries. Plumbers and electricians should also test Local Services Ads, which charge per lead.

This guide is for local service businesses in the US, UK, Australia and Canada: trades, personal services and professionals. Every figure is sourced at the end, and examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Service Business?

LocaliQ's 2026 Search benchmarks (updated June 1, 2026) give averages by industry. Across all industries the average click costs $5.42, with a 6.64% click-through rate and $66.69 per lead. The closest categories for service businesses are below.

| Category | CTR | CPC | Conversion rate | Cost per lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home and Home Improvement | 6.47% | $8.33 | 8.05% | $90.92 |
| Business Services | 6.10% | $5.87 | 4.85% | $93.69 |
| Beauty and Personal Care | 6.75% | $4.62 | 10.35% | $39.25 |
| Health and Fitness | 5.81% | $6.17 | 6.94% | $67.36 |
| Real Estate | 7.61% | $3.22 | 3.70% | $102.51 |

These are averages across advertisers, not forecasts for your city. Emergency trades in big metros often differ. Use them to sanity-check the range, then replace them with your own cost per lead after a month.

## Should You Use Google Ads or Local Services Ads?

Many trades should test both. Local Services Ads charge per valid lead, not per click. Google's help says you pay for each valid lead, set an average weekly budget, and prices vary by location, job type, lead type and bidding mode. Invalid or low-quality leads are not charged, and charged leads are reassessed over 30 days for credits.

LSAs are also changing. Google says they are moving into Performance Max campaigns with pay-per-lead goals, starting in August 2026 with US home and storefront service advertisers. New LSA campaigns still start at g.co/localservices. Read our [Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-services-ads-complete-guide) for eligibility and setup.

## How Do You Structure the Campaign?

Keep it simple while the budget is small. One campaign per main service, one ad group per intent, and a short list of keywords in phrase and exact match. Group "emergency" and "repair" terms apart from "installation" or "quote" terms, because they need different pages and offers.

- Service plus location, such as the service and your city
- Problem phrases customers type when something breaks
- Qualifiers such as "same day" or "licensed", only if true

Our [best Google Ad keywords for contractors](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-contractors-in-2026) lists starting keywords, and [Google Ads for contractors](/blog/google-ads-for-contractors) covers the trade angle.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local and Stop Wasted Clicks?

Set a radius around your service area. Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km, and it recommends broad geo targeting (Presence or interest) as best practice on Search. Choose Presence if you only want people physically in your locations.

Then add negative keywords. Google explains that negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals and synonyms yourself, such as jobs, careers, free, DIY and training. Check the Search terms report weekly. Our guide on [negative keywords](/blog/google-ads-negative-keywords-stop-wasting-budget) has a starter list.

## How Do You Track Phone Calls?

Calls are often the main lead for trades, so track them. Google says clicks on a call asset cost the same as a headline click, and call reporting uses Google forwarding numbers on the Search Network. For call conversions you set a minimum call length, and every call at least that long counts. Google's page does not state a default length.

Choose a length that filters wrong numbers but keeps real inquiries, and review call details before trusting the number. Also track form submissions on the thank-you page. See [how to get more phone calls from your website](/blog/how-to-get-more-phone-calls-from-your-website).

## What Should the Landing Page Include?

Send each ad group to a page about that service, not the homepage. The page should repeat the service and city from the ad, show a tap-to-call number at the top, and give a clear next step: call, request a quote or book.

Add proof that you can back up: licence number if your trade has one, review count from your Business Profile, and the area you serve. Remove menus that lead away from the action. Test one change at a time, and let each test run long enough to collect calls.

## FAQ

### How much should a service business spend on Google Ads?

There is no standard budget. LocaliQ shows clicks of $5.87 to $8.33 in the closest categories, so $1,000 buys about 120 to 170 clicks. Start with what you can run for at least a month and judge by cost per job.

### Do I need an agency?

Not necessarily. The work is weekly: review search terms, add negatives, check calls. If you cannot spend that time, a specialist or a managed service may be cheaper than wasted clicks.

### How long before Google Ads brings leads?

Leads can come in the first week, but judge results over a month or more. Early data is thin. Wait for enough calls before changing bids.

### Are Local Services Ads cheaper than Search ads?

Google does not publish one price. Lead prices vary by location, job type and bidding mode. Compare cost per valid lead in your own account.

### Should I also run Facebook ads?

It can help for retargeting and offers, but start with the channel where people are actively searching. See our [Google Ads vs Facebook Ads comparison](/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-local-business).
