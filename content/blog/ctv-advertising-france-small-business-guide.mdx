A small French business can now buy streaming TV ads without an agency. According to trade press, TF1 Pub opened a self-service tool in April 2026 that starts at 1,000 euros and targets by catchment area, data segment or programme. This guide covers what is confirmed, what is the broadcaster's own claim and what we could not find, such as a public CPM.

The sources are French trade press and Google help pages, listed at the end. TF1 figures are the sales house's own numbers, so treat them as claims, not independent measurement.

## Can a Small Business Buy TV Ads in France Without an Agency?

Yes, on at least one platform. Le Media Leader reported on 15 April 2026 that access to a self-service campaign tool inside TF1 AdManager opened on 1 April. The article says a business can create and broadcast a video campaign on premium inventory in under ten minutes, without an agency, from a 1,000 euro entry ticket.

This comes from one trade article describing the broadcaster's announcement. We could not open TF1 Pub's own pricing page, so check current terms with TF1 before you plan around it. Other French broadcasters may offer similar tools, and we did not verify them.

## How Does Targeting Work on TF1+?

The same article lists three levels. Geographic targeting goes down to the catchment zone. Data targeting uses 1,400 partner segments. Contextual targeting works by programme. The inventory combines TF1+ streaming with addressable linear TV, which TF1 Pub says reaches 50 million French people a month.

For a shop or salon, the geographic level is the one that matters. A campaign limited to the neighbourhoods you can actually serve avoids paying for viewers in another city. Ask whether the minimum budget still applies once the zone is small, because the article does not say.

## What Do Local Businesses Get Through Mediaposte?

In a second report dated 25 September 2026, Le Media Leader says TF1 Pub signed a partnership with Mediaposte, which becomes its local sales house. The offer is aimed at SMEs, local businesses and multi-location brands. It combines mailbox and SMS campaigns in chosen zones with addressable TV and TF1+ streaming.

The figures quoted there are the partners' own: 24 million mailboxes reachable, 41.8 million monthly TF1+ streamers in the first half of 2026 and 5.7 million daily streamers. No campaign price is given in that article.

## Who Checks Your Ad Before It Airs?

French TV ads are reviewed before broadcast. The Le Media Leader article on TF1 AdManager lists ARPP rules among the compliance steps, and says human validation of a spot currently takes five days, with a target of 48 hours. Plan your creative timeline around that delay, not around the ten minutes it takes to set up the campaign.

We could not open ARPP's 2026 price grid, so we give no fee for the review. ARPP's own page says members get a preferential rate on review credits before broadcast. Ask ARPP or TF1 what applies to you.

## Can You Make the Video Without a Studio?

TF1's tool includes an AI generator. Per the same article, you enter your website address and it builds a spot from your images and keywords at no charge. The only proof point reported is 750 geolocated spots for a real estate network produced in 48 hours from a national spot.

An AI spot built from a website will look generic. A cafe or salon gains more from a ten-second clip of its own premises, a clear offer and a street name. Keep any music and images licensed, because the human check applies to them too.

## Is Google's YouTube on TV an Option?

Possibly, but the picture is limited. Google Ads Help says device targeting includes TV screens (smart TVs, consoles and streaming devices such as Chromecast), and that this option is only available for Display and Video campaigns. A separate help page says Demand Gen and Performance Max can show QR codes on connected TV, and that older TVs will not display them.

Neither page lists countries, so we cannot confirm availability for French accounts. Test it in your own account. Our [guide to starting with connected TV ads](/blog/connected-tv-ads-local-business-how-to-start) explains the basics.

## What Does It Cost to Break Even? A Worked Example

The sources give no public CPM for CTV in France, so this example avoids one. Every input except the 1,000 euro minimum is an **assumption**.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign budget (reported minimum) | €1,000 |
| Average sale per new customer (assumption) | €60 |
| Gross margin on that sale (assumption) | 50% |
| Profit per new customer (assumption) | €30 |
| New customers needed to recover the budget | about 34 |

If a first visit is the only sale, 34 extra customers in a month is the bar. Repeat visits lower it. Measure with a code or landing page unique to the campaign, and compare against a search ad budget using our [guide to Google Ads and Meta budgets](/blog/digital-marketing-budget-guide).

## What Does the French Audience Look Like?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 66.7 million people in France and 63.4 million internet users, 95.2% of the population. It also counts 51.5 million social media user identities. YouTube ad reach is 51.5 million, Facebook 31.5 million, Snapchat 29.8 million and Instagram 28.2 million.

The report has no TV viewing figures, so it cannot tell you how many households use connected TV. For a local business, social ads remain far cheaper to test first. ## Which Businesses Should Try It First?

Businesses with a wide catchment area and a visual offer are the best fit: restaurants, fitness studios and multi-location brands. Our posts on [CTV ads for restaurants](/blog/ctv-ads-for-restaurants) and [CTV ads for coffee shops](/blog/ctv-ads-for-coffee-shops) cover the creative side, with US examples.

Skip it if your whole market is a few streets and you have no way to track visits. A 1,000 euro minimum is a lot for a business that earns 30 euros per customer.

## FAQ

### Is there a TV ad price list for small businesses in France?

Not one we could verify. TF1 reports a 1,000 euro entry ticket, but no CPM or rate card was available to us. Ask TF1 or Mediaposte for a quote.

### Do I need an agency to run CTV ads in France?

Not on TF1 AdManager, according to the April 2026 report. The platform is described as self-service, with card or bank transfer payment via Stripe.

### How long does ad approval take?

The reported human validation time is five days, with a stated target of 48 hours. Allow for that before any seasonal promotion.

### Are the TF1+ audience numbers independent?

No. The 40 million and 41.8 million monthly streamers are figures from TF1 Pub or its partners, as reported by trade press.

### Can I target only my city?

The tool is reported to target down to the catchment zone. Confirm minimum spend and zone size with TF1 before you buy.
