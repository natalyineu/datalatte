All four cost time, not money. BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses, which is why reviews come first.

## What Should You Do First With No Budget?

Claim your Google Business Profile. Google's help says you can add or claim your business at no charge, then follow the on-screen verification steps. Google also states that more reviews and positive ratings can help local ranking, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Fill in every field you can: categories, hours, phone, website, services and photos. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists the fields, and the [local pack guide](/blog/how-to-rank-in-google-maps-local-pack) explains what Google weighs.

## How Do You Get Reviews for Free?

Google says you can ask customers to leave reviews by sharing a Google link or QR code. Customers need a Google Account to post. Print the QR code on a counter card or receipt, and ask in person while the customer is happy.

Do not offer discounts, free items or any reward for a review. Google's help treats free or discounted goods or services in exchange for reviews as fake or misleading content, and its contribution policy bans selectively soliciting only positive reviews. Reply to every review: BrightLocal found 89% of consumers expect owners to respond. Our [coffee shop Maps guide](/blog/coffee-shops-dominate-google-maps) has a full review routine.

## Which Free Channels Reach Nearby Customers?

| Channel | What is free | Limit or catch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | Listing, photos, replies | Verification needed |
| Nextdoor Business Page | Page, posts, recommendations | Ads are separate; some providers need documents to verify |
| Mailchimp free plan | 250 contacts, 500 emails a month, 250 a day | No scheduling, automation flows or SMS |
| Your own social accounts | Posting and replying | Reach is not guaranteed |

Nextdoor describes its Business Page as free and says free posts show up in the most visible part of the app. Use it to collect recommendations from neighbors. See our [list of free marketing tools](/blog/free-marketing-tools-for-small-business) for more options.

## How Do You Build an Email List With Nothing?

Collect an email at the point of sale or booking, with permission, and send a short note once or twice a month. Mailchimp's free plan covers 250 contacts and a maximum of 500 emails a month or 250 a day. It does not include scheduling or automation flows, and sending pauses if you exceed the limits.

An email list is a channel you own, unlike a social account. Keep each message to one offer or one update. Ask customers on the spot rather than hoping they find a sign-up form.

## What Free Habits Do Not Need Tools?

Ask for referrals, show up at local events, trade cross-promotion with a neighboring business, and put a flyer on a community board. None of these are measurable by a platform, so keep a tally: ask each new customer how they found you and write it down.

That tally tells you which free activity to continue. With no ad budget, the cheapest data you have is a question at the till. Our [budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows when it is worth moving from free to paid.

## What Does a 30-Day Plan Look Like? A Worked Example

All items are **assumptions** for a café or salon with five hours a week to spare.

| Week | Task | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Claim and complete the Business Profile; print a review QR code | 3 hours |
| 2 | Ask every customer for a review; reply to all existing reviews | 2 hours |
| 3 | Create a Nextdoor page; start an email sign-up sheet | 2 hours |
| 4 | Send the first email; review the tally of how customers found you | 2 hours |

At the end of the month, compare new reviews, profile calls and directions, and the tally. If you cannot tell what worked, the tracking is the first thing to fix. Our [KPI guide](/blog/how-to-measure-local-seo-success-kpis) shows what to count.

## FAQ

### Can you market a business with no money?

Yes, with free tools and your time: a Business Profile, review requests, a Nextdoor page and a free email plan. Results depend on your location and effort, and no source promises a number.

### Is a Google Business Profile really free?

Yes. Google says you can add or claim your business at no charge.

### Can I give a discount in return for a review?

No. Google's help says offering rewards for reviews is prohibited.

### Is Mailchimp free?

It has a free plan with up to 250 contacts and 500 emails a month, per its pricing page. Check the page before relying on it, as plans change.

### When should I start paying for ads?

When you can state what a new customer is worth and your free channels bring steady inquiries. Start small and track results.
