The best way to market a small business in Minnesota is to fix your Google Business Profile first, run a small Google Ads Search campaign aimed at a few miles around your location, and keep customers with email or SMS. Minnesota had 547,493 small businesses in the SBA's 2024 profile, 99.5% of the state's firms.

This guide is for coffee shops, studios, salons, groomers and service firms in Minneapolis, Saint Paul, Rochester, Duluth and St. Cloud. Cost figures are US-wide benchmarks, because none of the sources checked publishes Minnesota-specific click costs. Anything unverified is marked.

## What Marketing Works for a Small Business in Minnesota?

Rank channels by how directly they reach someone ready to buy. Search ads and Google Maps reach people who are already looking. Meta ads and email reach people who are not yet looking or who already know you.

| Channel | Best for | Benchmark (US-wide, 2026) | Start with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | Every local business | Free | Complete profile, real photos, review replies |
| Google Ads Search | Services people search for | CPC $2.05 (Restaurants) to $8.33 (Home improvement) | $300 to $600 per month test |
| Meta lead ads | Studios, salons, offers | Lead CPL $27.11 (Fitness), $50.91 (Beauty) | One offer, small radius |
| Email and SMS | Repeat customers | Cost of the tool | Collect addresses at checkout |

All CPC and CPL figures come from LocaliQ's 2026 benchmark pages and describe the whole US. None of the sources checked for this guide publishes Minnesota-specific click costs.

## What Does the Minnesota Market Look Like?

BLS lists Minnesota's September 2026 unemployment rate at 4.5% (preliminary, seasonally adjusted), with 3,065,000 nonfarm jobs, of which 277,100 are in leisure and hospitality.

The SBA's 2024 Minnesota profile lists 547,493 small businesses, 99.5% of Minnesota businesses, and 1.2 million small-business employees (45.4% of employees), per a search summary. Other secondary sites quote different counts for the same series, so use the number as approximate. The 2025 profile PDF returned 403.

## What Does $600 a Month Buy? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** built on the LocaliQ Restaurants and Food benchmark, not Minneapolis data. A lead is a call, form fill or booking request.

| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget (assumption) | $600 |
| Cost per click (benchmark, Restaurants and Food) | $2.05 |
| Clicks | about 293 |
| Conversion rate (benchmark) | 8.05% |

| Cost per lead | about $25 |

For a café in Minneapolis, the test pays if the revenue line beats $600 plus your cost of serving the customers. If it does not, fix the offer, the landing page or the call handling before adding budget. Track results for at least two months before you judge them.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local in Minnesota?

Set a radius around the business and exclude places you cannot serve. Google's help page says radius targeting needs at least 1 km. It recommends "Presence or interest" as the best practice for Search, and says "Presence" fits when you only want people physically in the area.

For a Minneapolis café, a 2 to 4 mile radius is a sensible first test, which is an assumption to check against your own customer addresses. Outside the Twin Cities, drive distances grow, so radius and customer base look different in Rochester or Duluth.

Add negative keywords weekly from the Search terms report: jobs, free, DIY and categories you do not offer.

## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Minnesota?

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help local ranking. It also says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Practical steps, in order:

- Fill every field: categories, hours, services, service area and website.
- Upload real photos and refresh them each season.
- Reply to every review, good or bad.
- Keep name, address and phone identical on your website, Yelp and Bing Places.

Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes through each field.

## Can You Offer a Discount for a Google Review?

No. Google's contributed content policy lists offering "payment, discounts, free goods and/or services" in exchange for reviews as fake engagement. You may ask customers for a review. You may not reward them for one.

Ask at the right moment with a direct link, for example right after a visit or pickup. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for scripts.

## Do Meta Ads Work for Minnesota Small Businesses?

They can, if you sell something visual or have a clear first-visit offer. LocaliQ's 2026 Facebook benchmarks (page updated September 23, 2026) list lead-campaign results of $27.11 per lead for Health and Fitness and $50.91 for Beauty and Personal Care, with $1.84 and $2.97 average CPC. Restaurants and Animals and Pets have no lead benchmarks on that page.

Use Meta for people who are not searching yet. Run one offer, one small radius and a booking link. Show the same offer to people who visited your site. Track cost per booked visit, not likes.

## When Do Minnesota Businesses Plan Seasonal Offers?

The Minnesota State Fair ran August 27 to September 7, 2026, with total attendance of 1,923,325, below 2025's 1,940,869 (Fox 9 and MPR, secondary). The 2027 fair is set for August 26 to September 6 (secondary).

Businesses near the fairgrounds in Saint Paul can plan fair-week hours and offers. No source checked gives winter foot-traffic data for Minnesota shops, so compare your own December to February sales before changing budget.

## Where Can Minnesota Owners Get Free Advice?

Minnesota's SBDC network is a partnership between the SBA and the state's Department of Employment and Economic Development, with nine regional centers. DEED says consulting is confidential and free, with a nominal fee for some training. In 2022 it reports 32,000 consulting hours for more than 5,200 businesses.

## What Mistakes Do Minnesota Owners Make?

- **Targeting the whole state.** If customers come from 10 miles away, statewide ads waste budget.
- **Stopping in slow months.** Compare cost per booking monthly, then lower spend instead of pausing.
- **Not tracking calls.** Phone calls are often the real conversion for service firms. Set a minimum call length that counts, so wrong numbers do not inflate results.
- **Spreading thin.** Test one channel for 60 days before adding a second. See our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide).

### What is the best marketing for a small business in Minnesota?

Google Business Profile and local SEO first, then Google Ads for high-intent searches, then Meta and email to bring people back. This order is a recommendation, not a ranked study.

Not verified. No source checked for this guide compares Minnesota click costs with other states, so test with a small budget.
