## How Much Does Booking Software Cost for a Small Team?

Per-seat pricing grows with each stylist or trainer, flat plans do not. Check whether the number you pay is per user, per calendar or per location.

Setmore charges per user, Acuity per plan with a calendar limit (1, up to 6 or up to 36), SimplyBook.me per plan with a provider limit (1, 5, 15 or 30), and Vagaro per bookable calendar after the first. Fresha charges per bookable team member and adds payment fees when you take card payments through it.

## Do You Need Deposits and SMS Reminders?

Both are gated on paid plans at several vendors. Acuity includes email reminders from Starter but text reminders only from Standard, at $27 a month billed annually. Setmore puts SMS reminders and deposits on Pro. SimplyBook.me puts payments, deposits and tips on Basic. Fresha gives 100 texts a month before charging $0.02 per text.

No source used here publishes how much reminders reduce no-shows, so test them with your own bookings before assuming a number.

## What Are the Payment Fees?

Fresha lists 2.79% + $0.20 for online payments and 2.29% + $0.20 in person. Calendly and Cal.com connect to Stripe or PayPal, which set their own fees, and Calendly lists Stripe and PayPal payments on its paid Standard plan at $10 per seat a month billed yearly. Read the fee table before you compare monthly prices, because processing costs often exceed the subscription.

## FAQ

### What is the best free online booking software?

Setmore Free allows up to four users with unlimited appointments, and SimplyBook.me Free allows 50 bookings a month with one provider. Both vendors list these on their own pages.

### Is online booking software worth it for a small business?

It is if people already ask to book outside opening hours. The tools above cost from $0 to about $50 a month for a small team, which you can compare with your hourly rate.

### Should I pick monthly or annual billing?

Annual billing is cheaper on every vendor that lists both, for example Acuity Starter at $16 versus $20. Pay monthly for the first two months while you test.

### Can a scheduling tool take payments?

Yes on paid plans. Check the processing rate and whether deposits need a higher plan.
