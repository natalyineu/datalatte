For an independent coffee shop, an AI loyalty program mostly means using the purchase data your point-of-sale system already records to send the right reward to the right customer. You do not need your own AI model. Start with a visit-based or points program, collect a phone number or email at checkout, and add simple rules such as a nudge to guests who have not visited in a while.

Starbucks is the large-scale example. In February 2024 Marketing Dive reported that the chain uses its Deep Brew data analytics and AI platform to find Rewards member groups and offer them relevant incentives. That is a company with 34.3 million active US members, not a single-site cafe.

## What Does AI Actually Do in a Loyalty Program?

In practice it does three jobs: it recognises returning customers, it groups them by behaviour, and it chooses an offer for each group. Starbucks describes the second and third jobs at scale. A small shop can do a manual version with the reports its POS gives.

Be careful with figures about AI loyalty results. Widely repeated numbers, such as retention gains of 30% or sales gains of 15%, were in the previous version of this page without a primary source, so they have been removed. Starbucks' own earnings materials and the Marketing Dive summary give member counts, not a proven uplift from AI.

## What Do You Need Before Adding AI?

You need an identifier for each customer and a program that records every purchase against it. Toast's support documentation shows the building blocks that most POS loyalty tools share:

- **Two earning models:** visit-based, where guests earn at a flat rate per visit, or amount-based, where they earn points per dollar spent.
- **A sign-up identifier:** points are recorded through a phone number, email address or linked card.
- **A reward menu:** a Toast program can hold up to five rewards in any combination.
- **Gaps:** Toast says points cannot be earned or redeemed on DoorDash, Grubhub or Uber Eats orders, so delivery-app customers stay invisible.

Square documents a similar flow: a customer with a card linked to their Loyalty account sees a reward redemption screen at payment. Square's help page does not state pricing, and third-party price listings disagree, so check your own dashboard before budgeting.

## Which Simple Rules Work Like AI?

Three rules cover most of what a small cafe needs, and you can run them from a spreadsheet export or your POS reports. These are suggestions to test, not proven results.

| Rule | Data needed | Offer to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lapsed guest | Date of last visit | A small reward after a set number of weeks without a visit |
| Usual order | Item purchased most often | A free upgrade on that item |
| Slow daypart | Time of purchase | Bonus points in the quiet hours |

Review each rule after a month. Keep it if redemptions rise and the discount does not erase your margin. ## How Do You Handle Customer Data Responsibly?

Tell customers what you collect and why, at sign-up. Privacy law may apply. The California Attorney General says the CCPA gives consumers rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of the sale or sharing of their data, and it applies to for-profit businesses that meet a threshold: over $25 million in annual revenue, handling data of 100,000 or more California residents or households, or earning 50% or more of revenue from selling such data. Many single cafes fall under those thresholds, but UK, EU, Australian and Canadian rules differ, so check your local law.

## The Math: Is a Lapsed-Guest Offer Worth It?

All numbers below are **assumptions** for one shop, not benchmarks.

| | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Members who have not visited in 6 weeks | 200 |
| Offer | Free drink, cost to you $1.20 |
| Members who redeem | 10% |
| Redeemers | 20 |
| Extra spend per redeemer, beyond the free drink | $4.00 |
| Cost of free drinks | $24 |
| Extra revenue | $80 |

On these inputs the offer returns $80 in extra sales for $24 in drink cost. If only 3% redeem, the revenue drops to $24 and the offer only breaks even. Measure your real redemption rate before scaling. ## FAQ

### Do I need AI to run a coffee shop loyalty program?

No. A visit-based or points program tied to a phone number or email already records what each customer buys. AI adds automatic grouping and offer selection, which you can approximate with simple rules.

### What does Starbucks use?

Marketing Dive reported in February 2024 that Starbucks uses Deep Brew to identify Rewards member cohorts and offer relevant incentives. The article gives member counts but no figure for how much the AI itself adds.

### How much does a loyalty tool cost?

It varies by vendor and plan. Toast's pricing is not published on its support pages, and Square's help page gives no price. Check your vendor's current pricing page.

### Can delivery-app orders earn points?

Not in Toast. Its support page says points cannot be earned or redeemed on DoorDash, Grubhub or Uber Eats orders. Other vendors may differ.

### Is collecting customer data legal?

Usually yes with notice, but rules differ by country and state. The CCPA, for example, gives California residents rights to know, delete, correct and opt out. Check which law covers your customers.
