Google Ads is not available to advertisers in Iran. Google's help page says it must comply with US sanctions and names Iran among the places it cannot serve. DataReportal counts 73.8 million internet users in Iran, 79.6% of 92.6 million people, so the audience is large. This guide separates what could be verified from what could not, because several platform and payment questions had no reliable public answer.

It is written for shops, cafes, salons and service firms in Iran. Figures come from the sources at the end, checked on 1 November 2026. Nothing here is a workaround for platform restrictions.

## How Many Iranians Are Online?

Most of them. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report lists 92.6 million people, 73.8 million internet users (79.6%) and 18.9 million offline (20.4%). It counts 48.0 million social media user identities, 51.8% of the population, and 151 million cellular connections, 163% of the population. Median mobile download speed was 57.64 Mbps (Ookla, to August 2025).

| Measure | Iran, late 2025 | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Population (UN) | 92.6 million | n/a |
| Internet users | 73.8 million | 79.6% |
| Social media user identities | 48.0 million | 51.8% |
| Cellular mobile connections | 151 million | 163% |
| Platform ad reach (Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp, YouTube) | Not in the report | n/a |

DataReportal gives no platform-by-platform ad reach for Iran, so this guide cannot rank platforms by size. It also warns that mobile connections can include voice and SMS plans and that social identities may not equal unique people.

## Can an Iranian Business Run Google Ads?

No, according to Google. Its country restrictions page says Google must comply with sanctions imposed by the US Office of Foreign Assets Control, that Google Ads is not available to advertisers in certain places, and that those places are not able to be targeted. Iran is named alongside Crimea, Cuba, North Korea and the self-declared Donetsk and Luhansk People's Republics.

Iran also did not appear in Google's list of billing countries when it was checked, and that page says a country missing from the list means Google Ads is not available there. Do not try to open an account through a false address. Google says sanctions-related suspensions allow no grace periods.

News coverage from the period of protests reported that authorities blocked Instagram and WhatsApp. In a 2023 briefing Meta said tens of millions of people in Iran still reach Instagram despite the block. Both come from secondary reports, and the current position was not confirmed.

## What Can a Small Business in Iran Do Without Paid Ads?

Work from the channels your own customers already use, and measure them yourself. That is a recommendation, not a finding. Ask new customers how they found you, keep a simple count by source, and spend time on the channel that produces most enquiries.

Organic content, a clear address and phone number, and prompt replies cost no ad money. Whether Google Business Profile features work for businesses located in Iran was not verified, so do not plan around them without checking in your own account.

For general ideas on organic content and customer follow-up, see the [salon marketing guide](/blog/best-marketing-for-salons-in-2026-what-actually-works-with-real-examples) and the barbershop Instagram guide. Neither is specific to Iran, and Instagram's current status there was not verified.

## What Does a Break-Even Check Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for a salon that sources clients from referrals and social posts. They show the method for any paid or unpaid effort, not Iranian results. Currency is "units" of your local money.

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Gross profit per new client over a year (assumption) | 1,000 units |
| Time spent on content and replies per month (assumption) | 20 hours |
| Value you place on an hour (assumption) | 25 units |
| Monthly cost of the effort | 20 x 25 = 500 units |
| New clients needed per month to break even | 500 / 1,000 = 0.5 clients |

If the effort brings one new client a month, it pays for itself in this example. If it brings none for three months, change the content, the offer or the channel. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) explains how to compare unpaid time with paid spend.

## What Mistakes Do Small Businesses Make in Restricted Markets?

These are recommendations, not study findings.

- Assuming a platform's ad tools work in Iran because its app is popular.
- Paying intermediaries who promise ad access without checking their terms.
- Quoting audience sizes from other countries' reports.
- Not tracking where customers come from.

## FAQ

### Is Google Ads available in Iran?

No. Google's country restrictions page names Iran among places where Google Ads is not available because of US sanctions, and Iran is missing from Google's list of billing countries.

### How many people use the internet in Iran?

DataReportal reports 73.8 million internet users, 79.6% of the population, in October 2025.

### Can Iranian businesses advertise on Instagram?

Not verified. No Meta policy page on Iranian advertisers was found, and DataReportal does not report Instagram ad reach for Iran.

### Can Iranian businesses use Telegram Ads?

Not verified. No official Telegram page on availability or payment for Iran was found.

### What payment methods work for online ads in Iran?

Not verified for any platform. Google does not serve the country, and no reliable public source was found for others.
