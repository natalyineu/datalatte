A Myanmar small business should test TikTok and Facebook side by side. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report shows Facebook ad reach of 13.7 million people (24.9% of the population) and TikTok ad reach of 21.0 million users aged 18 and over, 53.6% of adults. Instagram is tiny at 929 thousand (1.7%).

## How Big Is the Online Market in Myanmar?

DataReportal counts 54.9 million people, 39.8 million internet users (72.5%, up 4.9%) and 62.5 million mobile connections (114%). About 15.1 million people, 27.5%, are offline. Social media user identities total 21.0 million, 38.2% of the population.

Connections are not people, and some are voice-only. In the same region, see our guides to [Laos](/blog/local-marketing-laos-small-business-2026) and [Vietnam](/blog/local-marketing-vietnam-small-business-2026).

## Which Platforms Reach People in Myanmar?

| Platform | Ad reach | Share | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok (aged 18+) | 21.0 million | 53.6% of adults | up 16.5% |
| Facebook | 13.7 million | 24.9% of population | up 7.9% |
| LinkedIn (members) | 1.10 million | 2.0% of population | up 10.0% |
| Instagram | 929 thousand | 1.7% of population | up 9.7% |

TikTok's reach is larger than Facebook's in the data. DataReportal flags a TikTok decline of 7.0% from July to October 2025 that may reflect account corrections, so watch the trend. Instagram is too small to be a main channel.

## What Should a Myanmar Business Test First?

Run a 30-day test on each of TikTok and Facebook with the same offer, and compare cost per enquiry. Short video suits food, beauty and fitness businesses, while Facebook suits businesses that take orders by message.

Our [local business Meta ads guide](/blog/local-business-meta-ads) covers Facebook setup. Keep records of where each customer came from, so the comparison is real.

## How Do You Show Up on Google Maps in Myanmar?

Google says local ranking uses relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Reviews and ratings can help prominence. Add photos and hours and answer reviews. The fields to complete are in our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## Worked Example: A Two-Channel Test

All numbers are **assumptions**.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $80 on TikTok, $80 on Facebook |
| Enquiries, TikTok | 40 |
| Enquiries, Facebook | 24 |
| Cost per enquiry | $2 and $3.33 |
| Share of enquiries that buy | 10% |
| Margin per sale | $15 |

TikTok yields 4 sales and $60 margin against $80 spend, a $20 loss on the first sale. Facebook yields 2.4 sales and $36 margin against $80. At these inputs both lose on the first purchase, so repeat sales or higher margin decide whether to continue.
