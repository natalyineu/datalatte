An Indian small business in an English-speaking city market should start with Google, because Statcounter shows Google at 96.69% of search in India in September 2026. Next come Instagram, with an advertising reach of 481 million, and WhatsApp for customer chats. Food businesses also need to understand the cost of delivery apps such as Swiggy and Zomato.

No source we could open publishes Indian click prices by trade, so this guide gives channels and rules, not invented rupee figures. The budget example is labelled as an assumption.

## Which Channels Reach Customers in Indian Cities?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report for India counts 1.03 billion internet users in October 2025, 70.0% of the population, and 500 million social media user identities. Ad reach is an audience estimate, not monthly active users, and Meta itself says estimated audience size is not a proxy for active users.

| Channel | Figure, late 2025 | Use for a local business |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Search and Maps | 96.69% of search, Sept 2026 | Customers searching for a service nearby |
| Instagram | 481 million ad reach | Salons, cafes, fitness, visual products |
| Facebook | 403 million ad reach | Community groups, older audiences |
| YouTube | 500 million ad reach | Tutorials, longer video |
| WhatsApp | No figure on the DataReportal page | Enquiries, bookings, reminders |

The report does not print WhatsApp numbers, so claims about its user base need a separate source. DataReportal figures are national, so they do not describe a single city.

## How Do You Set Up Google for a Local Business?

Adding or claiming a Google Business Profile is free, according to Google's help. Fill in hours, categories, phone and photos, and ask customers for reviews with a Google link or QR code. Do not offer discounts or gifts for reviews, which Google's contribution policy prohibits.

For ads, Google says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km and recommends Presence or Interest for Search. In a dense city, a small radius around your outlet keeps spend near customers. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists every field.

## What Can WhatsApp Do for an Indian Small Business?

The free WhatsApp Business app lets a shop greet customers and reply when away, according to WhatsApp's business page. The Business Platform adds automation and per-message pricing that Meta publishes in rate cards by country, not on its general pricing page.

Marketing messages need the customer's opt-in, and automation is allowed inside the 24-hour customer service window with a clear route to a human. ## Are Swiggy and Zomato Worth It for Restaurants?

They bring orders, but the cost is contested. Inc42 reported on August 7, 2026 that more than 250 Bengaluru restaurants threatened to delist from Swiggy and Zomato over inflated commissions, extra charges and forced promotional levies. Swiggy reportedly agreed to refund disputed promotional charges. The article gives no commission percentages.

The Ken, in a summary seen only in search results, says restaurants pay commissions of 20% to 30%, but we did not find an official rate card. Ask each platform for a written statement of commission, ad charges and payout terms, and compare the net margin on a delivery order to a dine-in order before joining or buying ads.

## What Data Rules Apply to Customer Lists?

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Rules were notified in November 2025 and roll out in phases over about 18 months, according to law-firm summaries. Those secondary sources say they emphasise express user permission and breach reporting within 72 hours.

None of the sources we read explained how the rules apply to promotional messages. Collect permission when you take a phone number, say what you will send, and make opting out simple. Check with a local adviser, as this summary is not legal advice.

## What Does a Starter Budget Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**, not Indian benchmarks. A salon tests Google Ads with 20,000 rupees a month and a click price of 25 rupees.

| Item | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | Assumption | 20,000 rupees |
| Click price | Assumption | 25 rupees |
| Clicks | 20,000 / 25 | 800 |
| Enquiry rate | Assumption | 4% |
| Enquiries | 800 x 4% | 32 |
| Enquiries that book (assumption) | 50% | 16 |
| Cost per booking | 20,000 / 16 | 1,250 rupees |

Replace the click price with Google Keyword Planner data for your city and service. If the value of a booking is below the cost, improve the profile and landing page before spending more. See our [budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) for splitting spend.

## FAQ

### What is the best marketing channel for a small business in India?

A complete Google Business Profile, because Google has about 96.69% of search per Statcounter. Instagram and WhatsApp complement it.

### How many Indians use Instagram?

DataReportal lists an advertising reach of 481 million in late 2025. That is an audience estimate, not active users.

### Can I use WhatsApp for marketing?

Yes, with opt-in. The free app suits low volume. Automation needs the Business Platform.

### Are food delivery apps worth the commission?

It depends on your net margin. No official commission rate card was found, so request one in writing.

### How much do Google Ads cost in India?

No Indian benchmark by trade was available in the sources we opened. Use Keyword Planner.
