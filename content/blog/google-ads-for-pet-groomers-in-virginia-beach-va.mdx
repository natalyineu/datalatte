## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Pet Groomer?

LocaliQ, updated 1 June 2026, lists Animals and Pets at $4.06 average cost per click, 7.49% click-through rate, 16.22% conversion rate and $31.50 cost per lead. The all-industry averages are $5.42 per click, 6.64% click-through, 8.18% conversion and $66.69 per lead.

"Animals and Pets" is the nearest category to grooming and includes other pet businesses, so your own account will differ. Your actual cost per click depends on how many local groomers bid on the same searches. Judge the first month by your own booking cost, not by the benchmark.

## How Should You Target Locations?

Use radius targeting around your shop. Google says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km, and very small radii may show ads intermittently or not at all. Google recommends the "Presence or interest" setting for Search, but says "Presence" fits when you only want people physically in your locations.

For a groomer who needs customers to drive in, start with a radius that matches a realistic drive and widen only if booking cost stays low. Check the location report weekly and exclude areas that send clicks but no bookings.

## Which Keywords and Match Types Work?

Start with service plus place terms such as "dog grooming near me" or "dog groomer Virginia Beach", and add breed or service words you offer. Google describes three match types: broad, phrase and exact. Exact gives the most control; broad reaches the most searches, and Google calls Smart Bidding with broad match "critical" for it to work well.

For a small account, begin with phrase and exact and add broad later. Add negatives for jobs, courses, grooming supplies and "do it yourself". Negatives do not match close variants, so list plurals yourself.

## How Do You Track Calls and Bookings?

Track the actions that become appointments: calls, form submissions and booking clicks. Google says clicks on a call asset cost the same as headline clicks, and you can count calls as conversions by turning on call reporting. Set a minimum call length that reflects a real inquiry.

Google Business Profile also reports calls, directions and website clicks, so compare ad numbers against the free listing. ## How Does Daily Budget Work?

Google defines average daily budget as the amount you set per campaign per day. On a given day it can spend up to two times that amount, and the monthly limit is 30.4 times the daily budget for most campaigns. A $15 daily budget can therefore spend up to $30 on a busy day, but not more than about $456 in a month.

## FAQ

### How much should a Virginia Beach groomer spend on Google Ads?
No source sets a figure. At $4.06 per click, $450 buys roughly 111 clicks. Start there, then raise the budget only when cost per appointment is steady.

### Is Google Ads better than Google Business Profile?
They work together. The profile is free and drives calls and directions. Ads add visibility for urgent searches. Fix the profile first.

### Should I target the whole city?
Usually not. The city covers 244.72 square miles of land, so start with a radius near the shop.
