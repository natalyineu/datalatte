A business coach should start Google Ads with a small Search campaign on narrow keywords that name the buyer and the problem, send clicks to one landing page with a single call or booking action, and measure cost per signed client, not cost per click. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmark for Business Services is $5.87 per click, a 4.85% conversion rate and $93.69 per lead. Coaching is not a listed category, so use it as a rough range.

This guide is for coaches who sell to owners of local businesses such as cafés, salons and studios. Prices are US dollars. Any figure not tied to a source is an assumption and is labelled.

## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Business Coach?

LocaliQ's page, updated 1 June 2026, lists several categories. Business Services costs $5.87 per click with a 6.10% click-through rate, 4.85% conversion and $93.69 per lead. The all-industry average is $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead. Legal sits much higher at $9.87 per click and $131.63 per lead.

## Which Keywords Should a Coach Bid On?

Pick words a buyer types when they already want help. Keep them narrow: the type of business plus the outcome. Examples to test are "business coach for salon owners", "coffee shop business consultant" and "fitness studio growth coach". Check volume and estimated cost in Google Keyword Planner, because no source we opened publishes them.

Block junk early. Google says negative keywords do not match close variants, so add singular and plural forms and synonyms yourself. Start with "free", "jobs", "course", "certification", "salary" and "training", then review the Search terms report weekly. Broad terms like "business coach" attract students and job seekers.

## How Should the Ad and Landing Page Work?

Responsive search ads take up to 15 headlines of 30 characters and up to 4 descriptions of 90 characters, and need at least 3 headlines and 2 descriptions. Write several that state who the coach helps and what the first step is, such as a free call. Google says pinning is not recommended for most advertisers, because it cuts the combinations tested.

Do not promise a result. Google's misrepresentation policy bans claims that "entice the user with an improbable result", even if that result is possible. "Turn your salon into a $200k business in 12 months" is that kind of line. Describe the process and the offer instead.

The landing page needs one action: book a call. Put the niche, the method, the price range or how pricing works, and a short form above the fold. Do not send traffic to your homepage.

## How Do You Measure a $10,000 Client?

Cost per lead is not the number that pays you. Most coaches close the sale on a call days or weeks after the click. Google's offline conversion import lets you send a closed deal back to the click that caused it by saving the Google click ID (GCLID) with each lead. Google's help page also says that from 15 June 2026, offline imports migrate to the Data Manager API and are blocked in the Google Ads API, so ask your CRM or developer which route they use.

Until that is set up, log each lead in a spreadsheet with its source, call date and outcome. Compare monthly spend to signed clients. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) helps you split spend across channels.

## What Could $1,500 a Month Produce? A Worked Example

Every input beyond the LocaliQ benchmark is an **assumption**.

| Item | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | Assumption | $1,500 |
| CPC | LocaliQ Business Services | $5.87 |
| Clicks | $1,500 / $5.87 | about 255 |
| Conversion rate | LocaliQ Business Services | 4.85% |
| Leads | 255 x 4.85% | about 12 |
| Leads who take a call (assumption) | 50% | 6 |
| Calls that become clients (assumption) | 25% | about 1.5 |
| Cost per client | $1,500 / 1.5 | about $1,000 |
| Package price (assumption) | One client | $10,000 |

On these numbers the campaign returns about ten times its cost per client, but only if you close one in four calls. At 10% the cost per client rises to $2,500. At zero signed clients in three months, fix the offer or page before raising the budget. Do not add budget until you have at least a month of lead data.

## How Do You Improve Results Over Time?

Change one thing per week. Google describes Quality Score as a diagnostic from 1 to 10 at keyword level, built from expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience. It is not a goal in itself, but a low score on a keyword tells you which of the three to fix.

Keep a short test log: what changed, the date and the result. Retargeting visitors who did not book is an option once the base campaign works. ## FAQ

### How much does Google Ads cost for a business coach?

LocaliQ's 2026 Business Services average is $5.87 per click and $93.69 per lead. Coaching is not listed separately, so treat the figure as a range.

### Is Google Ads worth it for high-ticket coaching?

It can be, if you close enough calls. In the example above, a 25% close rate on a $10,000 package returns about ten times the ad cost per client. The close rate is an assumption, so test with your own.

### What keywords should a business coach avoid?

Broad terms such as "business coach" without a niche, plus free, course, certification and jobs words. Add them as negatives and review the Search terms report weekly.

### Should I promise results in my ads?

No. Google's policy bars claims that present an improbable outcome as the likely result. Describe your process and a clear next step.

### How do I track a sale that happens on a call?

Save the Google click ID with each lead and import the closed deal back into Google Ads, or log it manually in a spreadsheet until that is set up.
