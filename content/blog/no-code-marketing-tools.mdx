The cheapest no-code stack for a small business is usually one email tool with built-in automations plus, if needed, one connector such as Zapier or Make. On current pricing pages, Zapier Free gives 100 tasks a month, MailerLite Free gives 250 subscribers and 3 automations, and Mailchimp Free gives 250 contacts but no automation flows. Paid plans start around $12 to $20 a month.

This guide lists what the free plans actually allow, which paid step you are likely to hit first and a worked example. Prices were read from each vendor's page on 1 November 2026 and change often. An older version of this page had a price table I could not match to any vendor, so it was removed.

## What Are No-Code Marketing Tools?

They are apps that automate marketing tasks through forms and visual builders instead of code: an email tool that sends a welcome sequence, or a connector that copies a new booking into a spreadsheet and sends a text. There are two groups. Email and CRM tools run automations inside one product. Connector tools such as Zapier and Make link separate apps.

For local businesses the common jobs are booking reminders, review requests, lead follow-up and list building. See [10 Zapier automations that save time](/blog/zapier-for-small-business-10-automations-that-save-time) and [local business marketing automation](/blog/local-business-marketing-automation).

## What Do the Free Plans Include?

| Tool | Free plan | Automations on free |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zapier | $0, 100 tasks a month | Two-step Zaps only, 15-minute polling |
| Make | $0, 1,000 credits a month | Plan limits for scenarios not stated on the page I opened |
| Mailchimp | $0, 250 contacts, max 500 sends a month or 250 a day | Automation flows not included |
| MailerLite | $0, 250 subscribers, 2,500 emails a month | 3 active automations, up to 5 steps each |

The free tiers are for testing. A salon with 200 appointments a month will pass Zapier's 100-task limit in the first week if each booking triggers one task.

## What Do the Paid Plans Cost?

Zapier Pro is priced by tasks: $19.99 a month for 750 tasks and $39.00 for 1,500 on annual billing, or $29.99 and $58.50 billed monthly. Pro adds multi-step workflows with 2-minute polling.

Mailchimp lists Essentials from $13 a month and Standard from $20 a month, both as a 12-month promotional price (15% off at 10,000+ contacts), without showing the price after the promotion. Essentials allows up to 4 flow steps and Standard up to 200 flows. MailerLite's paid plans start at $12 a month (Comfort) and $25 a month (Power); the page does not state subscriber counts or billing period. Make's page did not show paid prices; it offers a 50% discount on Business for companies under 20 employees.

## Which Tool Fits Which Job?

| Job | Simple choice | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome email and newsletters | MailerLite or Mailchimp | Automation sits inside the email tool |
| Copy form leads to a sheet or CRM | Zapier or Make | Connects separate apps |
| Appointment reminders | Your booking software | Many include them; check yours first |

## Worked Example: A Salon With Three Automations

All inputs are **assumptions**. The salon books 200 appointments a month and wants a booking reminder, a review request after each visit and a form-lead alert. Check how your connector counts tasks, since one run can use several.

| Automation | Runs per month | Assumed tasks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reminder | 200 | 200 |
| Review request | 200 | 200 |
| Lead alert | 30 | 30 |
| Total | | 430 |

430 tasks exceed Zapier Free's 100, so the salon needs the 750-task Pro tier at $19.99 a month on annual billing, or $29.99 monthly. If the booking tool already sends reminders, the need drops to about 230 tasks, still above free. Add a MailerLite or Mailchimp plan only if you send newsletters.

## What Should You Automate First?

Start with the task that loses money when it is missed. For appointment businesses that is usually the reminder, because a no-show costs a slot. I did not find a source I can cite for how much reminders reduce no-shows, so I give no percentage. Measure your own no-show rate for a month before and after.

Build one automation, test it with your own phone and email, and only then add the next. Keep a list of what each one does so a staff member can pause it.

## FAQ

### Are no-code tools really free?
Free plans exist but are small: 100 Zapier tasks, 250 Mailchimp contacts, 250 MailerLite subscribers. Plan on paying once you automate real volume.

### Which is cheapest for a small business?
It depends on the job. MailerLite includes 3 automations on its free plan, while Mailchimp's free plan has no flows. For connecting apps, compare task counts against Zapier's tiers.

### Do I need a developer?
Usually not for simple flows. Complex logic, such as custom data checks, may be easier with a consultant.

### Is Google Tag Manager a marketing automation tool?
No. It manages tracking tags. I did not check its pricing for this update.
