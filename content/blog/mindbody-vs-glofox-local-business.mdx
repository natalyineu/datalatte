## What Does Mindbody Cost per Location?

Mindbody's related education content states that plans start from $79 per month per location. That is a starting figure, not a plan-by-plan price, so the cost for the tier you need should be confirmed in a written quote. Ask whether the quote is per location or per account, because a multi-site studio multiplies the base fee.

Mindbody's customer story on Combine Air shows how that multiplication works in practice. The page describes Combine Air as a simulated altitude training studio in New South Wales, Australia, founded in 2020, operating 12 locations. At the published starting figure, 12 locations would start at $948 a month before add-ons. That is a calculation from a starting price, not a quote for that business.

Mindbody's blog also features five business owners, including Rise Pilates, Training Collective, Grouse Studio, Peaches Pilates and Combine Air. Those are vendor testimonials, so read them as descriptions of how the platform is used, not as evidence of results.

## How Does Glofox Pricing Compare?

No Glofox price is stated in this guide. Pricing for software of this type is often shown only through a quote form, so the lack of a public number is not a reason to rule the vendor out. It does mean you cannot compare list prices directly, and any comparison has to rest on a written quote.

Request the same details from each vendor so the totals line up. Use this checklist:

1. Write down your member count, location count and expected monthly card volume.
2. Ask for the base software fee per location, with the plan named.
3. Ask for the card processing rate and any per-transaction fee, in writing.
4. Ask whether using a third-party processor is allowed, and what it changes.
5. Ask for any setup, data migration or export fee, with the amount.
6. Ask whether the quote holds if you add a location or staff.
7. Ask for the contract length, renewal terms and cancellation notice.

## How Much Do Card Fees Change the Total?

Card fees can outweigh the base software fee. The worked example below uses assumed inputs, not vendor rates. Assume a studio with 150 members paying $99 a month by autopay on one location. The assumed card rate is 2.9% plus $0.30 per payment.

| Input or result | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Members on autopay | 150 | Assumption |
| Monthly membership price | $99 | Assumption |
| Monthly membership revenue | $14,850 | Calculation |
| Card cost per payment | about $3.17 | Assumption: 2.9% plus $0.30 |
| Card cost per month | about $476 | Calculation |
| Mindbody base fee, one location | $79 | Published starting figure |

On these assumptions, card processing costs about six times the $79 starting fee. A difference of half a percentage point on $14,850 is about $74 a month, close to the whole base fee. That is why the quote should be built as one monthly total per vendor, not as a headline price.

## Which Software Should a Small Studio Choose?

Choose Mindbody if you want a published starting point and can accept a sales call to confirm the plan price. Its starting figure gives you a baseline to test other quotes against.

Choose Glofox only after its written quote, with the same member count and card volume, beats the Mindbody total. A branded app or lower fees are only useful if the total is lower.

If neither quote includes a clear cancellation clause or export terms, keep shopping. A cheaper monthly fee is not a saving if moving your member data costs more later. ## FAQ

### Is Mindbody cheaper than Glofox?

This guide cannot say, because no Glofox price is stated here. Mindbody's starting figure is $79 per location per month. Compare written quotes with the same member count, location count and card volume before deciding.

### Does Mindbody have a setup fee?

Mindbody's guidance in this guide does not list a setup fee. Data migration or export may carry a fee, so ask for the amount in writing before signing.

### How do card fees affect the monthly cost?

Card fees scale with payment volume. In the worked example, 150 autopay members at $99 would pay about $476 a month in card costs at an assumed 2.9% plus $0.30 rate, which is about six times the $79 base fee. Get the vendor's actual rate.

### What should I ask a software vendor in writing?

Ask for the plan price per location, card processing rate, per-transaction fee, setup or export fee, contract length, renewal terms and cancellation notice. Also ask whether the quote holds when you add a location or staff member.

### Does software alone fill classes?

No. Mariana Tek advises posting short videos three to five times a week and following up with new contacts within 24 hours. Software supports that work, but the follow-up has to happen.
