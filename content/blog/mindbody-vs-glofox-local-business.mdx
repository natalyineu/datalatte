Mindbody publishes a starting price of $79 per month per location, while Glofox shows no price and sends you to a quote form. So on cost alone, only one of the two can be compared in public.

Both sell booking, memberships and payments to fitness studios, so the real choice for a small studio is which quote is lower once card fees and add-ons are included, and which contract you can leave.

This guide is for owners of yoga, pilates, boutique fitness and small gym studios choosing studio software. We opened both vendors' pages on 2026-10-24. Where a page did not show a number, we say so and do not fill the gap. Examples with invented inputs are labelled as assumptions.

## What Does Mindbody Cost a Small Studio?

Mindbody's pricing page lists three plans: Starter, Accelerate and Ultimate. The page states a starting price of $79 USD per month per location for North America and Asia. It does not give a separate price for each plan. Each plan links to a "Let's talk" sales conversation, so the price for the plan you actually need is not public.

Staff logins are not a cost driver: the page says all plans include unlimited users per location. The Ultimate plan includes a monthly text-message bundle, but the page does not say how large it is. Mindbody also states there is no onboarding fee on any plan. Moving data from a previous system may carry fees, and the amount is not stated.

## What Does Glofox Cost?

We could not verify a Glofox price. The pricing page is a "Get a price quote" form with four steps: business type, locations, details and pricing. It lists no plans, monthly prices, setup fees, member limits or payment rates. Glofox's home page also states no fee amounts, although it mentions "fees" as a revenue tool for studios, such as charging for late cancellations.

That is a normal way to sell studio software, but it means you cannot compare list prices. Request a written quote and ask the questions in the checklist below before you compare it with Mindbody.

## How Do Payment Fees Compare?

Studio software often costs less than the card fees it processes. Mindbody's page says every transaction carries a processing rate plus a fixed fee of $0.10 to $0.30 in North America and Asia. The rate itself is not shown, and it varies by card-present or card-not-present payment and payment type. Mindbody Payments is optional, and you can use a third-party processor.

Glofox's home page says it offers built-in payment processing, flexible billing, point-of-sale tools and cardless in-app purchases. It names no rates. Treat the processing rate as the first number to get in writing from both vendors, because on a recurring membership base it repeats every month.

## What Contract Terms Should You Expect?

Neither page gives a contract length. Mindbody says terms depend on the plan and billing terms you sign, including promotions. Cancellation is handled through your account or Customer Support and may need advance notice, for example before the next billing date. Glofox's pages contain no statement on contract length, cancellation or renewal.

So ask both vendors in writing:

- Is the term monthly, annual or multi-year, and does it auto-renew?
- How much notice is needed to cancel, and what happens to the data?
- Is there a fee to export or migrate members and payment tokens?
- Does the quote hold if you add a second location or a staff member?

## Which One Should a Small Studio Pick?

Use these rules, then confirm with written quotes.

- **Pick Mindbody if** you want a published starting price, you want to be listed in a consumer app where people search for classes, and you can live with a sales call to learn your plan price.
- **Pick Glofox if** your members already find you directly, you care most about a branded app and in-app purchases, and its written quote beats Mindbody's after card fees.
- **Pick neither yet if** you run a few classes a week and a simpler booking tool covers it. Our [guide to using Mindbody for fitness studio marketing](/blog/how-to-use-mindbody-for-marketing-fitness-studio) shows what the platform adds beyond booking.

If neither quote includes a clear cancellation clause, that is a reason to keep shopping.

That is why the quote comparison should be built as one monthly total per vendor: software, card fees and any text or app add-ons. Glofox's software line stays blank until it quotes you.

## FAQ

### Is Mindbody cheaper than Glofox?

We cannot say, because Glofox publishes no price. Mindbody states a starting price of $79 per location per month, but the price of each plan is only given on a sales call. Compare written quotes with the same member count and payment volume.

### Does Mindbody charge a setup fee?

Mindbody's FAQ says there is no onboarding fee on any plan. Moving data from another system may carry fees, and the page does not state the amount.

### Do Mindbody or Glofox lock you into a contract?

Neither page states a contract length. Mindbody says terms depend on the plan and billing terms signed. Ask both for the term, renewal and cancellation notice in writing.

### Can I use my own card processor?

Mindbody says its payments service is optional and that third-party processors can be used. Glofox's pages describe built-in payments and do not say whether outside processors are allowed, so ask.

### Do both offer a branded app?

Yes, both pages describe one. Mindbody offers a custom app for your brand, and Glofox offers a branded app and customizable web experience. Costs and limits of the app are not shown.
