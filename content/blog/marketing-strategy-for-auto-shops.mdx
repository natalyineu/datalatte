An auto repair shop's marketing strategy should rest on search: a verified Google Business Profile with steady reviews, then a small Google Search campaign for repair intent. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put Automotive Repair, Service and Parts at $4.35 per click, a 15.51% conversion rate and $29.96 per lead, among the cheaper local categories. Those numbers are US averages, not a forecast for your shop.

This guide is for independent shops in the US that want more booked repairs and regular customers. Every figure has a source at the end, and examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Does Marketing Cost an Auto Repair Shop?

The category is cheap by home-services standards. All-industry averages in the same report are $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead, so auto repair sits below both. The conversion rate of 15.51% is nearly double the 8.18% all-industry average.

A high conversion rate means many clicks end in a call or form. It does not say how many become paying jobs. Track your own calls and bookings for a month before you set a budget. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to split spend.

| --- | --- |
| Cost per click | $4.35 |
| Click-through rate | 5.56% |
| Conversion rate | 15.51% |
| Cost per lead | $29.96 |

## How Do Customers Choose a Repair Shop?

Reviews and proof drive the decision. BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses, 74% only consider reviews from the last three months, and 68% require four or more stars. Also, 89% expect owners to reply.

Google says local ranking depends on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help. It also advises verifying your profile, adding every detail such as categories and hours, and keeping holiday hours current.

Ask every customer for a review at pickup. The FTC's rule on fake reviews took effect on 21 October 2024 and bars offers of rewards that require a positive review. Read [how to manage online reviews](/blog/managing-online-reviews-small-business) and [Google review basics](/blog/google-business-profile-reviews).

## Should an Auto Shop Use Local Services Ads?

Possibly. Google's US eligible list names Auto Repair Shop, Auto Body Shop, Oil Change Service, Tire Shop, Brake Shop, Car Wash and Towing. Go-live needs a licence or business registration, billing setup and a budget, plus any category review. Google says you pay only for leads related to your business.

Compare them with Search ads, where you pay per click and choose your own keywords. Our [auto shop keyword list](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-auto-shops-in-2026) covers terms to target and block.

Google Ads lets you set an ad schedule, showing ads only on chosen days and hours. Ads will not show at times with no searches, and the schedule affects budget pacing. Use it to avoid paying for clicks when no one can answer.

## How Do You Win Repeat Customers?

Repair work recurs: oil changes, tire rotations, inspections. A phone number, email or text consent collected at drop-off is your retention channel. Send a reminder at the interval the work needs. In the UK or EU, check consent rules before you send.

Keep the message plain: vehicle, service due, how to book. Do not offer rewards in exchange for positive reviews. Our [mobile optimization guide](/blog/mobile-optimization-local-business) explains why the booking link must work on a phone.

## The Math: What Does $900 a Month Buy?

All inputs below are **assumptions** built on the category benchmark.

| Step | Assumption or source | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget | Assumption | $900 |
| Cost per lead | $29.96 (LocaliQ benchmark) | about 30 leads |
| Leads who book a job | 50% (assumption) | about 15 jobs |
| Average first ticket | $350 (assumption) | about $5,250 |
| Cost per booked job | $900 divided by 15 | about $60 |

If only 25% book, the cost per booked job doubles to about $120. Calls missed during busy hours are the usual leak, so route ad calls to someone who can answer or to a call-back text.

## FAQ

### How much should an auto repair shop spend on marketing?
No source sets a standard share. The LocaliQ benchmark of $29.96 per lead lets you size a test budget, such as $900 for about 30 leads, then adjust to your close rate.

### Do reviews matter more than ads for a repair shop?
They work together. Reviews influence both rankings and trust, and 47% of consumers will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews.

### Is a Google Business Profile enough?
It is the best start. Google says complete, verified profiles with reviews are more likely to appear, but you still need a way to turn views into calls.

### Can I advertise my shop's hours?
Yes. Use an ad schedule and keep your profile hours accurate, including special hours.

### Are Local Services Ads available for auto shops?
Google's US list includes several auto categories, such as auto repair shop and tire shop. Check your own country's list.
