A Singapore small business should start with Google Maps and search, then add Instagram, Facebook or TikTok ads for the audience that fits. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 5.78 million internet users (98.4% of the population) and ad reach of 3.80 million on Facebook, 3.35 million on Instagram and 3.80 million on TikTok among adults 18 and over. Google does not publish Singapore click prices by industry, so budget from your own test.

This guide covers where Singapore customers are reachable, how to set up a first campaign, what to check about marketing messages, and a worked example in SGD. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## How Many People Can You Reach Online in Singapore?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 Singapore report (data mostly from late 2025) lists a population of 5.88 million, 5.78 million internet users and 5.33 million social media user identities (90.6% of the population). Ad reach was 3.80 million on Facebook (64.6%), 3.35 million on Instagram (57.0%), 5.33 million on YouTube (90.6%) and 3.80 million on TikTok (75.4% of adults 18 and over).

These are ad reach estimates from the platforms, not unique active users, and they can overlap. DataReportal reports Instagram reach up 9.8% and TikTok up 11.5% on a year earlier. Use them to size the audience, not to forecast sales.

## Why Does Google Maps Come First?

People searching for a nearby business see map results. Google says local ranking depends on relevance, distance and prominence, that complete and detailed business information helps relevance, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help ranking. You cannot pay for a better local ranking.

So verify the profile, fill in hours, category and attributes, add photos, and ask for reviews. Prominence in Singapore is also helped by listings on sites your customers use, so keep name, address and phone consistent. See our [Google Business Profile optimization guide](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization) for the steps.

## What Does Google Ads Cost in Singapore?

Google publishes no Singapore average click price by industry, and the US figures from LocaliQ do not carry over. For reference only, LocaliQ's 2026 US benchmarks show $5.42 per click across industries, $4.62 for Beauty and Personal Care and $2.05 for Restaurants and Food. Singapore costs can be higher or lower.

Use Keyword Planner in your own account. Google says it shows estimated searches and average cost per keyword from the last 7 to 10 days of data, adjusted for seasonality, and that these are estimates. Our [Google Ads setup guide](/blog/how-to-set-up-google-ads-for-your-small-business-in-2026-step-by-step-with-screenshots) walks through the first campaign.

## Which Social Platform Fits Your Business?

Match the platform to the buyer and the product. Instagram and TikTok suit visual products such as hair, beauty, food and fitness classes. Facebook still reaches a broad adult audience. YouTube has the widest reach, but the format costs more to produce.

DataReportal's reach figures say all three are well populated, and nothing in them ranks one as the best ad performer. Compare cost per booking in your own account. Our comparisons of [Instagram and Facebook ads](/blog/instagram-ads-vs-facebook-ads) and [TikTok and Instagram ads](/blog/tiktok-vs-instagram-ads-local-business-2026) explain how to test them.

## What Should You Check Before Messaging Customers?

Singapore runs a Do Not Call Registry under the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC), and its rules cover marketing messages sent to Singapore numbers. The PDPC page with the detailed requirements could not be read in full when this article was checked, so confirm the current rules on the PDPC site: whether you must check the registry, what counts as consent, how long a check stays valid, and what an opt-out must look like.

Keep a record of when and how each customer agreed to hear from you, and make unsubscribing easy. See our [SMS marketing best practices](/blog/sms-marketing-best-practices).

## Worked Example: A S$1,000 Month

Inputs are **assumptions**, because Google publishes no Singapore click cost and no benchmark is used.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | S$1,000 |
| CPC (assumption) | S$2.00 |
| Clicks | 500 |
| Booking-request rate (assumption) | 4% |
| Requests | 20 |
| Requests that become customers | 50% |
| New customers | 10 |
| Cost per customer | S$100 |
| First-visit value (assumption) | S$80 |
| Visits per customer in a year (assumption) | 4 |

At these numbers each customer costs S$100 and brings S$80 at the first visit, so the ad only pays if customers return. At four visits that is S$320 per customer, or S$3,200 from ten. At S$4.00 clicks the cost per customer doubles. Run a small test before you commit, then use your own data.

## FAQ

### What is the best first channel for a Singapore small business?

Google Maps and search, because customers looking for a nearby service use them. Add social ads once your profile and website are in order.

### How much should I spend on Google Ads in Singapore?

There is no published benchmark. Start with a budget you can run for a month and use Keyword Planner for estimates.

### Is TikTok worth trying?

DataReportal shows TikTok ad reach of 3.80 million adults in Singapore, up 11.5% over a year. That says the audience exists, not that ads convert for you. Test with a small budget.

### Do I need to follow Do Not Call rules?

The PDPC runs the registry for marketing messages. Check its current pages or ask an adviser before you send marketing texts or calls.

### Can I use US benchmarks for Singapore?

Only as a rough reference. Replace them with Singapore data from your own campaigns.
