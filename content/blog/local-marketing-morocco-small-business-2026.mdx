## How Big Is Morocco's Online Audience?

DataReportal reports 38.5 million people in Morocco in October 2025 and 57.1 million cellular mobile connections, equal to 148% of the population. About 2.99 million people (7.8%) were offline. Internet users grew 2.2% in a year, so the market is close to saturated and growth now comes from time spent, not new users.

Social media user identities rose 7.8% to 22.8 million. DataReportal warns these are identities, not unique people, and that ad reach figures come from platform ad tools and are revised often.

## Which Platforms Reach Moroccan Customers?

DataReportal lists these ad reach figures for late 2025:

| Platform | Ad reach | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 22.8M | 59.1% |
| YouTube | 21.6M | 56.1% |
| TikTok (18+) | 16.7M | 62.3% of adults |
| Instagram | 15.1M | 39.2% |
| Snapchat | 7.64M | 19.8% |
| Messenger | 7.10M | 18.4% |
| LinkedIn (members) | 6.90M | 17.9% |

Instagram grew fastest among the big Meta apps (+20.8% in a year) and TikTok grew 19.1%. Facebook still has the widest reach. For a salon, café or gym, the practical start is a Facebook page and an Instagram profile, with a Google Business Profile for map searches. ## Can You Run Google and Meta Ads From Morocco?

Google lists Morocco as a billing country for Google Ads, with all account currencies and payment methods shown as available. Its terms of service for Morocco are offered in Arabic and English. That page confirms you can open an account. It does not say what a click costs.

Meta's published table of ad account currencies lists the Turkish lira but not the Moroccan dirham, Malagasy ariary or Maldivian rufiyaa. So a Moroccan advertiser may need to pay in another currency such as USD or EUR. Check the currency offered in Ads Manager before you fund an account.

## What Does Advertising Cost in Morocco?

We could not find a public source for average click costs, CPM or conversion rates in Morocco. Tables with such numbers circulate online, but none cites a method, so we do not repeat them. The only reliable price is the one inside your own ad account.

Run a small test, then read the cost per message, call or booking. A forecast from Google Ads Keyword Planner for your own keywords is a better guide than any country average.

## How Do You Know if a Test Budget Pays Back?

Work backwards from the sale. All figures below are **assumptions** for an imaginary barbershop, not Moroccan benchmarks.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly test budget | MAD 1,500 |
| Average ticket | MAD 150 |
| Gross margin on a ticket | 50% |
| Enquiries that become bookings | 40% |
| Bookings needed to cover the budget | 20 |
| Enquiries needed | 50 |
| Maximum cost per enquiry | MAD 30 |

If your ads produce enquiries for more than MAD 30 each under these inputs, the test loses money in month one. Repeat customers change the picture, so track who returns. Change the inputs to your own numbers.

## What Data Rules Apply to Marketing Messages?

Morocco's personal data law is Law 09-08, enforced by the CNDP. A search summary of the CNDP's direct-marketing fact sheet says prospecting requires prior consent and a prior declaration to the CNDP, with an exception when you collected the contact details directly from the person. We could not open that PDF ourselves (the server returned an error), so treat this as **unverified** and read the fact sheet before you send bulk SMS or email.

Penalty amounts reported in third-party summaries were not checked against the statute, so we do not quote them.

## FAQ

### How many people in Morocco use Facebook?

DataReportal reports a Facebook ad reach of 22.8 million in late 2025, equal to 59.1% of the population. This is ad-tool reach, not a count of unique active users.

### Is Instagram worth it for a Moroccan small business?

Instagram reached 15.1 million people (39.2%) and grew 20.8% in a year. It suits visual businesses such as salons and cafés. See our [Instagram guide for barbershops](/blog/barbershop-instagram-marketing) for content ideas that work anywhere.

### What does Google Ads cost in Morocco?

No public source we could open gives a Moroccan average. Use Keyword Planner and a small test to find your own cost per enquiry.

### Can I pay for Google Ads in Morocco?

Google lists Morocco as a billing country with all currencies and payment methods available, per its billing-country page. Confirm the options in your account.

### Do I need consent before sending marketing SMS?

Morocco's CNDP requires prior consent for direct prospecting according to secondary summaries. This is unverified here, so check the CNDP fact sheet or a Moroccan lawyer.
