## Why does a Google Business Profile need work before ads?

Ads send people to your listing, so the listing has to answer their questions. Google verifies a business before it goes live, usually by mailing a postcard with a code to the business address, though some businesses can verify by phone or email ([Per Diem](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). Allow time for this step before launch, and check Google's live help page for current verification rules.

Per Diem recommends answering common questions in the Q&A section in advance, such as whether you offer free Wi-Fi, dairy-free options, or dogs on the patio. It also advises choosing "Coffee shop" or a related term as the category rather than something broad like "Restaurant" (Per Diem). BrewRound notes that you can select one category at setup and add more later ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary, 2020).

Google lets users message businesses directly through their listings, so an unanswered message is a lost lead that the ad already paid for. Bellwether Coffee estimates that a coffee shop needs about 5 minutes a day on Google Business ([Bellwether Coffee](https://bellwethercoffee.com/blog/social-media-marketing-for-coffee-shops), secondary). For the full setup, see [coffee shop Google Business Profile optimization](/blog/coffee-shop-google-business-profile-optimization).

## Which local keywords should a Miami coffee shop bid on?

Localized phrases tend to be less crowded than generic ones. seoplus+ states that a phrase such as "coffee shop downtown Phoenix" is likely to have less competition than a generic term like "coffee shop", which can lead to a lower cost per click ([seoplus+](https://seoplus.com/paid-ads/the-ultimate-guide-to-multi-location-ppc-advertising-on-google-ads/), vendor). The same logic applies in Miami, where customers often search with a neighbourhood name in mind, such as Wynwood, Brickell or Little Havana.

Group your keywords into three sets:

- Near-me and open-now phrases, matched to a tight radius around your address
- Service plus neighbourhood, such as "coffee shop Wynwood"
- Specialty terms you actually stock, such as cold brew or single-origin espresso

Hold off on broad generic terms until you have conversion data. The [best Google Ad keywords for coffee shops](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-coffee-shops-in-2026) guide covers how to build these sets in more detail.

## How much budget does a Miami coffee shop need to test?

No Miami-specific cost-per-click figure is used here. The inputs below are assumptions. Replace them with numbers from your own account or Google's Keyword Planner before deciding anything.

Assumed inputs:

- Monthly budget: $300 (assumption)
- Average cost per click: $2.50 (assumption)
- Click-to-customer rate: 4% (assumption)
- Average spend per visit: $6 (assumption)
- Visits per new customer over 90 days: 4 (assumption)

| Step | Calculation | Result |
|------|-------------|--------|
| 1. Clicks | $300 ÷ $2.50 | 120 clicks |
| 2. New customers | 120 × 4% | about 4.8 |
| 3. Cost per new customer | $300 ÷ 4.8 | about $62.50 |
| 4. Value per new customer | $6 × 4 visits | $24 |
| 5. Break-even visits | $62.50 ÷ $6 | about 10.4 visits |

On a 90-day view, this budget loses money: $24 of value does not cover $62.50 of acquisition cost. The useful question is how many visits a new customer makes, not whether the first visit covers the ad. Test the budget, measure the result in the table above, and adjust the budget or the keywords before scaling.

## Which negative keywords stop wasted clicks?

Negative keywords block searches that rarely turn into visits. Common groups for coffee shops include:

- Job terms such as "jobs", "hiring", "career" and "coffee shop salary"
- DIY and training terms such as "how to", "course" and "certification"
- Trade terms such as "wholesale" and "supplier", unless you sell that way
- Franchise terms, unless you are actually franchising
- Other cities outside your service area

Add these before the campaign goes live, then review the search terms report each week in the first month. Irrelevant clicks are easiest to stop early, while the account has little data.

## How should a Miami coffee shop track results beyond clicks?

Clicks show interest, not customers. Set up conversion tracking for the actions that lead to a visit:

1. Phone calls from the ad, using a call tracking number
2. Booking or contact form completions on your website
3. Direction requests from your Google Business Profile
4. "Book now" or contact page clicks as a secondary signal

Connect these to the cost-per-new-customer calculation above. A conversion that does not lead to a visit is only a signal, not a result.

## What order should a first campaign follow?

Launch in a fixed order so that each step has something to measure:

1. Verify the Google Business Profile, set the category, and answer the common questions in advance.
2. Build a short list of tight keywords in the three groups above.
3. Write ads that name your neighbourhood, and point them to a page that matches the search.
4. Add negative keywords before the first click.
5. Set up call, form and direction tracking, then test a small budget.
6. Review after roughly three months of data, using the break-even method before increasing spend.

<Callout type="tip">
Keep the first campaign narrow. A small set of tight keywords with a clear neighbourhood focus is easier to measure than a broad campaign spread across the metro area.
</Callout>

## FAQ

### How much should a Miami coffee shop spend on Google Ads?

Start with the break-even method above. Work out what a new customer is worth over several visits, then set a budget where the cost per new customer stays below that value. A budget that fails this test should be adjusted before it is increased.

### Should a Miami coffee shop run Spanish-language ads?

Choose the language your customers use. Check the search terms and calls your own account receives before writing Spanish ads. No sourced figure for Miami's language mix is used in this guide.

### Why does the Google Business Profile matter for Google Ads?

Clicks from ads lead to your listing, where people read answers, check the category, and message you. An incomplete profile makes a paid click less likely to become a visit.

### How long before results show?

Judge a campaign on enough clicks and visits to fill the worked example, not on the first week. A first review after about three months of data gives a more reliable picture than early results.
