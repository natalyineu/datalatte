Most Haitians are not reachable by online ads. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 4.69 million internet users, or 39.3% of the population, and Facebook ad reach of 2.95 million people, 24.7% of the population. A Haitian small business should treat Facebook as one channel next to word of mouth, a Google Business Profile and a simple phone number or message link.

This guide uses published audience data only. No source checked gives Haitian ad prices, so there are no CPC figures; the budget example is an assumption.

## How Many People Can You Reach Online in Haiti?

DataReportal reports 10.1 million cellular mobile connections, 84.6% of the population, and 4.69 million internet users, up 1.1% in a year. About 7.25 million people, or 60.7%, were offline at the end of 2025. Mobile connections are not people: one person can hold several, and some carry only voice and SMS.

So a salon in Port-au-Prince cannot assume that most customers see ads. Online ads reach a minority, usually the younger and urban part of the market. Use them to complement the signs, referrals and walk-in traffic that reach the rest. See our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) for splitting spend.

## How Big Is Facebook in Haiti?

DataReportal lists Facebook ad reach of 2.95 million, up 13.5% from October 2024. That equals 33.7% of people aged 13 and over, 38.4% of adults, and 62.9% of internet users. The number of social media user identities is also 2.95 million, which DataReportal says may reflect only the largest platform.

| Measure | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Facebook ad reach | 2.95 million |
| Share of total population | 24.7% |
| Share of adults 18+ | 38.4% |
| Share of internet users | 62.9% |

Among people who are online, Facebook is therefore the widest paid channel in the data. Meta says audience size is an estimate and not a count of active users, so treat these as upper bounds.

## What Should a Haitian Business Do Offline and Online?

Because most people are offline, start with what works without a screen: a clear sign, consistent opening hours, and a phone number. Then add a free Google Business Profile so people who do search can find you. Google says local ranking uses relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Reviews and ratings can help prominence. Claim and complete your profile first; it costs nothing.

Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers the fields. The sources checked for this guide give no figures for WhatsApp use or mobile money in Haiti, so this guide does not claim any.

## Worked Example: Is a Facebook Test Worth It?

All numbers are **assumptions**. A hair salon in an urban area spends a small test budget. The example uses dollars to avoid guessing an exchange rate.

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget, 30 days | $60 |
| New clients from the ad | 4 |
| Cost per new client | $15 |
| Profit per first visit | $10 |
| Repeat visits per client over 6 months | 3 |

At these inputs, each client costs $15 and returns $10 on the first visit plus about $30 over three repeats, so the test pays back only if clients return. If only 1 client arrives, cost per client is $60 and the test fails. Ask each client how they heard about you, and judge the test on that count.

## FAQ

### How many people use Facebook in Haiti?
DataReportal reports Facebook ad reach of 2.95 million in late 2025, which is 24.7% of the population.

### Is online advertising worth it in Haiti?
It reaches a minority: 39.3% of people are online. It can still work for urban businesses, but test with a small budget and track where customers come from.

### How much do ads cost in Haiti?
No source checked publishes Haitian ad prices. Read the cost per result in Ads Manager after a small test.

### Do I need a Google Business Profile?
It is free and helps people who search on Google find you. Google says you cannot pay for a better local ranking.

### How reliable are the audience figures?
They are estimates. DataReportal and Meta both caution that ad reach is not the same as active users.
