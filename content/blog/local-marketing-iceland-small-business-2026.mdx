## How Many People Can You Reach Online in Iceland?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report puts the population at 399 thousand in October 2025, with 395 thousand internet users. Penetration is 99.0%, a figure Kepios caps at 99%. Social media user identities total 335 thousand (83.9%).

Ad reach in late 2025: YouTube 335 thousand (83.9%), Facebook 238 thousand (59.5%), Instagram 203 thousand (50.8%), Messenger 215 thousand (53.7%), TikTok 162 thousand adults, X 131 thousand. DataReportal stresses these are ad reach estimates, not active users.

## Is Facebook Still the Main Platform?

It is still large but shrinking in the data. Between October 2024 and October 2025 Facebook ad reach fell by 21.3 thousand (8.2%), Instagram by 3,650 (1.8%) and Messenger by 24.3 thousand (10.2%). YouTube rose 7.0% and TikTok 11.4%.

With roughly 238 thousand people reachable on Facebook, audiences are tiny, so narrow targeting can leave an ad set too small to deliver. Test broad audiences and a YouTube video before assuming Facebook is the best channel.

## How Big Is the Visitor Market?

The Icelandic Tourist Board says the total number of foreign overnight visitors was just under 2.3 million in 2025. About 2.27 million (98.7%) came through Keflavík airport and about 18,100 by the Norröna ferry. The board warns that departing passengers are counted manually before security, so the figures need to be viewed with caution.

Its January 2026 report puts the 2025 airport total at 2,267,638, up 0.3% on 2024. For a business serving tourists, visitors can outnumber residents several times over across a year, though that varies by season and region.

## What Does the Economy Look Like for a Small Business?

The World Bank lists Iceland's 2025 GDP per capita at US$98,323.5, GDP growth at 1.3%, unemployment at 3.6% and inflation at 4.1%. Population is listed at 392,404, which differs from DataReportal's 399 thousand because the sources use different dates and methods.

On company size, a EURES labour market page lists about 42,736 microenterprises with up to nine employees, 95% of all enterprises, and says they employ 33.9% of workers. The page's size data sits in a section on 2022, so treat the year as probable, not stated. Most competitors are very small.

## What Advertising Rules Apply?

Iceland's Act No. 57/2005 on Supervision of Unfair Commercial Practices and Transparency of the Market, as translated on WIPO Lex, says it is not permissible to do anything "in conflict with good commercial practices" (Article 5) and not permissible "to grant incorrect, unsatisfactory or misleading information in advertisements" (Article 6).

The Act on Electronic Commerce, No. 30/2002, Article 7, requires that the person responsible for marketing is readily identifiable and that offers, competitions and games are clearly presented as such, along with their conditions. Rules on unsolicited email and SMS were not verified for this guide. The rule may well require a prior request, but that is unconfirmed. Confirm with Persónuvernd or the Post and Telecom Administration before sending campaigns.

## Worked Example: A Reykjavík Guesthouse

All inputs are **assumptions**, not benchmarks. A guesthouse in Reykjavík tests two channels for four weeks.

| Step | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly test budget | ISK 100,000 |
| Split | 60% Search aimed at visitors, 40% YouTube video |
| Margin per booking | ISK 25,000 |
| Bookings needed to break even | 100,000 / 25,000 = 4 |
| Decision rule | Fewer than 4 tracked direct bookings, rework the offer |

Track bookings with a tagged booking link and a question at check-in. Whether the budget delivers four bookings depends on your account data, which the sources cannot predict.

## Where Should a Small Budget Go First?

In order: a complete [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist), a website in Icelandic and English, and local Search. Then test one social channel. ## FAQ

### How many people in Iceland use Facebook?

DataReportal estimates Facebook ad reach at 238 thousand (59.5% of the population) in late 2025, down 8.2% from a year earlier. It is an ad reach estimate.

### How many tourists visit Iceland?

The Icelandic Tourist Board reports just under 2.3 million foreign overnight visitors in 2025, counted by departing passengers, with caution advised.

### What are the advertising rules in Iceland?

Act No. 57/2005 bars misleading advertising, per WIPO Lex. Rules on unsolicited email were not verified here, so check with Persónuvernd.

### How much do ads cost in Iceland?

No Icelandic ad price data was found. Set a small cap in your own account and review it weekly.

### Should I advertise in English or Icelandic?

It depends on who you serve. Residents expect Icelandic, and visitors need English. Keep separate pages and track each audience.
