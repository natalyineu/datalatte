## What Does Google Ads Cost for a UK Hair Salon?

Nobody publishes an audited UK figure. Whito's May 2026 page cites Keyword Planner data. Its ranges are: "hairdresser near me" £1 to £4, "barber near me" £1 to £3, "balayage" plus a city £1 to £4, "bridal hair" and "hair extensions" plus a city £2 to £5, men's haircut £0.50 to £2. The page does not explain how the ranges were derived and shows inconsistent update dates, so use them only to plan.

For a US reference, WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks list Beauty and Personal Care at $4.62 per click, 10.35% conversion rate and $39.25 per lead. Those are US advertisers, not UK.

## How Do You Get a Real Cost for Your Town?

Use Google's Keyword Planner. Its help page says it shows monthly search estimates, plan forecasts of clicks and conversions based on spend, and average cost for an ad to show on a keyword. Google also states that performance depends on bid, budget, ad quality and location targeting.

Set the location to your town or postcode radius, not the whole UK. Check the cost range for your five main services, then use those figures in your plan. Our [salon Google Ads cost guide](/blog/how-much-should-a-hair-salon-spend-on-google-ads) covers budgeting in more detail.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local in the UK?

Clients rarely travel far for a cut. Google's help says radius targeting requires a radius of at least 1 km, and recommends broad geo targeting ("Presence or interest") as a best practice on Search. "Presence" alone suits cases such as a sensitive vertical.

For a salon, start with a radius around the shop and read the location report. If clicks come from places you will not serve, switch to "Presence" and exclude those areas.

## Which Keywords Should a Salon Start With?

Pick services where one booking is worth the most, and put the town in the keyword:

- Colour and balayage, bridal and occasion hair, extensions
- "Hairdresser" or "barber" plus your area
- Specific treatments you actually offer

Add negatives. Google says negative keywords exclude search terms and do not match close variants, so add plurals yourself. Common ones: "jobs", "courses", "training", "wholesale", "products", "free", "diy".

See our [mistakes guide for salons](/blog/google-ads-mistakes-hair-salons-make-and-how-to-fix-them) for what typically wastes budget.

## What Should You Track?

A booking is the goal. Google says clicks on a call asset on mobile cost the same as headline clicks, and that for call conversions you set a minimum call length. Every call that lasts at least that long is counted. Pick a length that reflects a real booking enquiry.

Also track your online booking link or form, and ask new clients how they found you. The [complete guide for salons](/blog/google-ads-for-hair-salons-complete-guide) and [email and SMS marketing for UK salons](/blog/email-sms-marketing-uk-salons) cover the free side that brings clients back.

## What Does a Budget Buy? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**. The cost per click is the midpoint of Whito's £1 to £4 range for "hairdresser near me" (secondary), and the conversion rate is a planning figure, not a UK benchmark.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | £300 |
| Cost per click | £2.50 |
| Clicks | 120 |
| Conversion rate (call or booking) | 8% |
| Enquiries | about 9.6 |
| Cost per enquiry | about £31 |
| Enquiries that book | 60% |
| Cost per new client | about £52 |
| Average first visit | £70 |

At these inputs a first visit covers the ad cost with some margin. Raise the click cost to £4 and cost per new client rises to about £83, which is more than the assumed first visit. Repeat visits then decide whether the ads pay off, so measure rebooking too.

## FAQ

### How much does Google Ads cost for a hair salon in the UK?
No audited UK figure is published. Whito lists £1 to £5 per click for hairdresser keywords (secondary, May 2026). Check Keyword Planner for your own area.

### Can I advertise on a budget of £300 a month?
You can run a small local campaign. At an assumed £2.50 per click that is about 120 clicks, so keep the keyword list short.

### Is the LocaliQ benchmark valid for the UK?
No. It is based on US advertisers. Use it only as a rough comparison.

### Do phone calls count as conversions?
Yes, if they last at least the minimum call length you set.

### Is a radius under 1 km possible?
No. Google requires a radius of at least 1 km.
