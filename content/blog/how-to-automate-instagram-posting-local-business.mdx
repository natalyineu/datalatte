## What Do You Need Before Automating Posts?

Meta's content publishing documentation says the Instagram account must be a professional account connected to a Facebook Page. If the Page requires Page Publishing Authorization, publishing is blocked until that is completed. Images must be JPEG for API publishing, and a carousel can hold up to 20 items.

Two limits matter for tools built on the API. Shopping tags and filters are not supported, and the alt text field applies to image posts only, not reels or stories. If you rely on product tags, publish those posts by hand. Our [Later vs Planoly comparison](/blog/later-vs-planoly-instagram-scheduling-local-business) covers more tool differences.

## Can You Schedule Posts Without a Paid Tool?

Yes. Shopify's guide to scheduling Instagram posts (a secondary source) says a professional account can schedule natively: after creating a post, open Advanced settings, choose Schedule this post, and pick a date and time up to 75 days ahead. It also names Meta Business Suite for scheduling Instagram and Facebook together.

Meta's own help pages were not readable when checked, so confirm the 75-day figure in the app. Native scheduling suits one account and a simple calendar. Paid tools add team approval, a visual grid and bulk upload.

## How Many Posts Can You Automate?

For most local businesses the limit is time and content, not the platform. Meta's documentation states a limit of 100 API-published posts in a rolling 24-hour period, and it also gives 50 for carousels in a different section without reconciling the two. A local business posting a few times a week is far below either.

Batch the content instead. Plan a month of themes, take photos in one session, write captions in a spreadsheet, and upload in one sitting. ## What Should You Not Automate?

Do not automate replies, community management or time-sensitive posts. Scheduling far ahead makes it harder to react to trends or stock changes, as Shopify's guide notes. Check scheduled posts weekly and edit or delete any that are out of date.

Also keep a human review step before posting, because an automated post about a closed day or a sold-out item hurts trust. Our [Instagram growth strategy](/blog/instagram-growth-strategy-local-business-0-to-1000-followers) shows which interactions to handle manually.

## FAQ

### Can I schedule Instagram posts for free?

Yes. Buffer's free plan allows 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts each. Instagram also has a native scheduler for professional accounts, per Shopify's guide.

### Do I need a business account?

Meta's documentation requires a professional account connected to a Facebook Page for API publishing.

### How far ahead can I schedule?

Shopify's guide says up to 75 days with Instagram's native scheduler. This is a secondary source, so check in the app.

### Can scheduling tools add product tags?

No. Meta's documentation lists shopping tags and filters as not supported for API publishing.

### Is it safe to automate all my posting?

Keep a weekly review. Out-of-date posts or missed replies hurt more than the time you save.
