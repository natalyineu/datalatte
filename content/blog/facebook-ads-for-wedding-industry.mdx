Facebook and Instagram ads can put a wedding venue, photographer, florist or planner in front of couples who are planning now. The difficulty is timing. [Adwave's guide to wedding advertising](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-wedding-venues-event-planners) says couples research venues and vendors for 9 to 18 months before they book, and often touch 6 to 12 advertising sources before they inquire. Target couples in your service area, send them to a dedicated page with a short enquiry form, follow up quickly, and judge campaigns by cost per enquiry and cost per booking rather than likes or clicks.

This guide covers who the platform suits, how to set up campaigns in Meta Ads Manager, what to show couples and how to measure results.

Search still matters. [Adwave cites research](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-track-local-seo-rankings-with-free-tools) showing 73% of couples use Google to find wedding vendors, so Facebook and Instagram ads work best alongside search listings rather than replacing them.

Planners reach a smaller group. [According to The Knot, as reported by SaleGenie](https://www.salesgenie.com/resources/marketing-to-wedding-planners/), about 27% of couples hire a professional wedding or event planner. Of all couples, 37% only need a day-of coordinator and 31% choose a full-service or all-inclusive planner. Planners should therefore target a narrower audience than a venue would.

Spending is high for some vendors. [Adwave states](https://adwave.com/resources/photography-advertising) that couples spending $3,500 to $12,000 on a wedding photographer are making a decision they cannot redo, which favours creative that shows real work.

## Which couples should you target on Facebook and Instagram?

Start with location. Use a radius around your venue or studio, or the towns you serve, because most couples choose vendors within travelling distance of the wedding location or their home town. Venues often draw from a wider area than a florist does, so test a larger radius for them.

Next, check life-event options. [Adwave notes](https://adwave.com/resources/real-estate-marketing-ideas) that ad platforms allow targeting based on life changes such as recently engaged, expecting a baby, newly married or a recent job change. Availability varies by country and changes over time, so confirm what your account offers before building a plan around one option.

Interest and behaviour audiences around weddings and bridal topics can extend reach. Lookalike audiences built from past clients' emails can help, provided you have consent and follow Meta's rules on customer lists. Retargeting people who visited your site or engaged with your Instagram is often the most efficient group, because they already know your work.

Avoid implying you know personal facts about someone. "Planning a spring wedding near [town]?" speaks to a situation. "You are newly engaged" makes an assumption about the viewer.

## How do you set up campaigns in Meta Ads Manager?

1. Connect a Facebook Page and an Instagram profile to a Meta Business account.
2. Install the Meta Pixel on your website and define a Lead event for enquiry form submissions. Many website platforms and tag managers offer an integration.
3. Build a dedicated landing page for each offer, with strong photos, a short form, what is included and clear contact details. Avoid sending traffic to a busy homepage.
4. Choose the objective. Use Leads for direct enquiries, or Conversions optimised for the Lead event once the pixel is firing. Use Awareness or Engagement only if you have a plan to retarget those people later.
5. Add one qualifying question to any instant form, such as wedding date or guest count, to filter out casual enquiries.

Meta changes its menus and targeting options, so confirm the current names inside Ads Manager before launching.

## What ad creative gets couples to enquire?

Show real work first. A venue can show the space in use, a florist can show finished arrangements, and a photographer can show complete galleries. Short vertical video suits Reels and Stories: a venue walkthrough, a time-lapse of a table being set, or a client reaction recorded with permission. Get written permission before using clients' photos or faces.

Write copy that opens with the couple's concern, adds one or two concrete facts such as capacity, location or what is included, and ends with one action: book a viewing, check your date or book a tasting. Avoid stock images that do not match what couples will see on the day.

Test two to four variations of image or video and primary text. Change one element at a time and let each variation gather enough results before deciding. ## Worked example: what does a month of ads cost per booking?

The figures below are illustrative. Only the cost-per-inquiry range comes from Adwave's estimate.

| Input | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly ad budget | $1,500 | Assumption |
| Cost per inquiry | $60 | Assumption, inside the $35 to $120 venue range Adwave estimates |
| Inquiry to booking rate | 10% | Assumption |
| Average booking value | $4,000 | Assumption |

- Inquiries: $1,500 ÷ $60 = 25
- Bookings: 25 × 10% = 2.5 a month on average
- Cost per booking: $1,500 ÷ 2.5 = $600
- Booked revenue: 2.5 × $4,000 = $10,000
- Ad spend as a share of booked revenue: 15%

At $120 per inquiry with the same budget, cost per booking doubles to $1,200 (assumption-based). Treat results as averages across months, since you cannot book half a wedding in a single month.

Seasonality matters too. [Adwave lists](https://adwave.com/resources/slow-season-marketing-strategies) January to March and November as the wedding industry's slow months. Consider a lower budget in those months, or keep retargeting running while new enquiries slow down.

## How should you follow up enquiries?

Couples often contact several vendors, so reply the same day with a personal message rather than a template. Offer one clear next step: a call, a viewing, a consultation or a tasting. Send a pricing guide, a sample timeline or an FAQ.

Keep leads in an email sequence over months. With research running 9 to 18 months, many couples will not book on the first conversation. Record each enquiry's source and outcome so you can see which ads lead to bookings.

## How do you measure whether the ads are working?

Meta's reporting shows cost per enquiry. Add enquiry to consultation rate, consultation to booking rate and cost per booking from your CRM or spreadsheet. Ask every new couple how they found you and compare their answers with the ad data, because platform attribution and self-reported sources often differ.

Review results in monthly blocks rather than daily snapshots. A long sales cycle makes daily numbers misleading, and likes or clicks alone do not show whether a campaign produces bookings.

## What mistakes waste wedding ad budgets?

- Boosting single posts instead of running Ads Manager campaigns with clear objectives
- Sending traffic to a generic homepage
- Slow or template replies to enquiries
- Stock images that do not match your real work
- Targeting so narrowly that the audience is too small to deliver results
- Overlooking Meta's advertising policies, including special category rules that can limit targeting; check current guidance before launching

## FAQ

### How much should a wedding business spend on Facebook ads?

No single figure applies. Start from your average booking value and the cost per booking you can afford, then test numbers with the worked example above. Adwave's estimate of $35 to $120 per venue inquiry gives a starting range to check against.

### Can I target recently engaged couples?

Ads Manager may offer life-event options such as recently engaged, but availability varies by country and over time. Check your own account, and combine any such option with location and retargeting audiences.

### Do Facebook ads work better than Google for wedding vendors?

They reach couples at different points. Adwave cites research showing 73% of couples use Google to find vendors, so search captures couples who are actively looking. Facebook and Instagram can reach couples earlier in a research period that can run 9 to 18 months. Using both is the practical approach.

### How long before ads produce bookings?

Enquiries can arrive quickly, but bookings often take a long time because couples compare vendors. Plan for a full booking cycle and review results in monthly blocks.
