Local marketing in Chad works best with a few channels you can run well: an accurate Facebook Page, WhatsApp Business for questions and orders, and word of mouth. Paid search is not a dependable option for most small businesses here, so spend should go where replies come in. This guide covers the realistic channels, how WhatsApp's free messaging window affects ad costs, a worked budget for an N'Djamena café, and what to check before committing money.

Readers comparing markets across Africa may also find the [Nigeria small business guide](/blog/local-marketing-nigeria-small-business-guide) useful for context.

## Which channels are realistic for a Chadian small business?

Start with a Facebook Page that shows your location, opening hours and clear photos. Keep it current, because customers will check it before they visit. Then set up WhatsApp Business so people can ask questions and place orders in one place. Neither channel needs a large budget, which matters when marketing money is limited.

Click-to-WhatsApp ads connect the two. According to Crescendo Lab, a vendor, the feature moves potential customers from Facebook and Instagram ads into personalized WhatsApp conversations ([secondary](https://blog.cresclab.com/en/whatsapp-marketing)). Only run these ads if someone can answer the inbox quickly, because a late reply loses the customer.

This guide does not assume a working local paid-search market. If you want to see how search ads fit a market where they do operate, the [Germany Google Ads guide](/blog/local-marketing-germany-small-business-google-ads) shows that setup. For Chad, confirm Google's current country availability on its help pages before you spend anything.

## How does the 72-hour WhatsApp window change ad costs?

WhatsApp Business Platform pricing says that when a customer messages after a Click-to-WhatsApp ad or a Facebook Page call-to-action button, every message you send in the following 72 hours is not charged ([primary](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/)). The page's wording is "for the following 3 days (72 hours), all of your messages are not charged."

For a small shop, this means the main cost of a conversation is the ad spend, as long as your replies fall inside that window. Plan your follow-up so that the reply and the offer both happen within those 72 hours.

Outside the window, the pricing rules apply differently. Check the live pricing page before planning longer follow-up sequences, because pricing terms change and this is a snapshot of the current rule.

## Should you target N'Djamena first or the whole country?

Start with N'Djamena. A café, shop or salon can only win customers who can physically reach it, so the capital is where a boosted post is most likely to lead to a visit. Set the audience radius around your address in Facebook's ad tools, and keep it tight at first.

Widen the area only after you see messages from other places. Track where each enquiry comes from by asking one simple question in WhatsApp: "Where are you coming from?" Keep the answers in a notebook or spreadsheet.

Write captions in French and, where your customers read it, Arabic. The cost of posting is the same either way. Test which version gets more replies over four weeks and keep the better one.

## Does mobile money belong in your marketing?

Mention mobile money only if you can actually accept it. Adoption may still be limited, so many customers will expect to pay in cash. An advertised payment option you cannot process costs more trust than it gains.

Ask your regular customers which payment methods they use, and list only those on your Facebook Page and in WhatsApp replies. Check any mobile money provider's current terms before you promise a payment method in an advertisement.

## What does one month look like for a café in N'Djamena?

This worked example uses only assumptions, not sourced figures. Every input below is labelled as an assumption so you can replace it with your own numbers.

Assumptions: a monthly boost budget of $15; one boosted Click-to-WhatsApp post per month; 20 customer conversations starting from the ad; all replies sent within 72 hours, so no WhatsApp message charges apply; 1 in 4 conversations becomes an order (5 orders); average order value of $6.

Ad spend is $15. Cost per conversation is $15 divided by 20, which is $0.75. Cost per order is $15 divided by 5, which is $3.00. Order revenue is 5 times $6, which is $30. Gross return on ad spend is $30 divided by $15, or 2.0 times.

Gross revenue ignores ingredients, staff time and rent, so it is not profit. Run the same calculation with your real margin before deciding whether the campaign pays. If conversations do not start in the first test month, stop and reassess the offer or the post.

## How much does Meta's reach matter for a small Chad business?

Adwave reports that Meta's apps, including Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp and Threads, reach an average of 3.56 billion daily active people ([secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/facebook-advertising-for-small-business)). That is a global figure. It does not tell you how many people in Chad use these apps, so treat it as background, not a local forecast.

Ownership is also context. Salesforce states that Facebook (now Meta) acquired WhatsApp in 2014 for $22 billion ([secondary](https://www.salesforce.com/marketing/whatsapp-marketing/?bc=OTH)). That explains why the ad and messaging tools connect, but it does not change how your own customers respond.

Check your own numbers. The Facebook and WhatsApp reply counts in your inbox are more useful than any global reach estimate. If you want a structured review of your current setup, you can [request a free audit](/free-audit).

## What should you do if the internet goes down?

Any digital plan needs an offline fallback. Keep clear signage, a phone number printed on the door, and regular customers who recommend you to others. Word of mouth does not depend on connectivity.

Keep the Facebook Page and WhatsApp profile complete with hours, location and a current phone number. When access returns, customers who check your profile should find accurate information rather than an outdated page.

## FAQ

### Why does this guide skip Google Ads cost figures?

This guide has no sourced local price for paid search in Chad. Check Google's current country and pricing help pages before assuming a search budget will return anything.

### Should a Chadian business use Instagram or TikTok?

Start with Facebook and WhatsApp. Add another platform only when your own customers show they use it, and measure replies before you expand.

### Is a Click-to-WhatsApp ad worth it on a small budget?

It can be, if someone can answer messages quickly and the spend fits your budget. Test it with the $15 example above, and stop if conversations do not start during the test month.

### How should a business plan for connectivity problems?

Keep an offline presence through signage, consistent quality and word of mouth, and keep your online profiles complete so customers find accurate information whenever access returns.
