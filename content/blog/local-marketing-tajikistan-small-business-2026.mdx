## How do you stop chat and call leads from going cold?

The most concrete operational point in the sources is about follow-up. The vendor says a missed call becomes a task, so nobody forgets to call the client back, and that the manager handles the chat and the call in one window ([shamCRM](https://shamcrm.com/en/tajikistan?code=tajikistan)). The same page suggests starting with messengers and the sales pipeline before adding a warehouse or store.

For a salon, café or clinic, that order makes sense. A lead from an Instagram direct message should land on one client record with the next action attached. Without that record, a WhatsApp question and a missed call from the same customer can be handled by two people who do not know about each other.

## What does a monthly budget look like in TJS?

The sources do not give Tajik prices, so the example below uses assumed inputs throughout. The aim is to show the arithmetic, not to predict results.

Assumptions for a hypothetical Dushanbe beauty salon:

- Monthly marketing budget: TJS 2,000 (assumption)
- Instagram content production: 40% of budget, TJS 800 (assumption)
- Google search ads: 35%, TJS 700 (assumption)
- Telegram and WhatsApp tools and promotions: 25%, TJS 500 (assumption)
- Qualified leads per month from all channels: 25 (assumption)

Cost per lead = TJS 2,000 ÷ 25 = TJS 80 (assumption-based). If the salon converts 1 in 5 leads into a booking (assumption), that gives 5 bookings and an effective cost of TJS 400 per booking. Replace these numbers with your own tracked results after one quarter. If a channel produces leads at a clearly higher cost than others, move budget before adding new channels.

Pricing for the CRM itself is listed on the vendor's price page in TJS ([shamCRM pricing](https://shamcrm.com/en/price)). The amounts are not in the sources used here, so they are excluded from this calculation.

## What is a practical launch sequence?

1. Pick two messaging channels your customers already use, usually WhatsApp and Telegram, and publish the same phone number and hours on each.
2. Set up one client record per lead in a CRM or shared inbox, with a rule that each call or chat creates a follow-up task.
3. Post on Instagram weekly with before-and-after work, prices in TJS, and a booking link or number.
4. Run a small search test with a fixed daily limit and log every lead source.
5. After one quarter, compare cost per lead by channel and shift money toward the cheapest qualified source.

## FAQ

### Which social platform should a Tajik small business start with?

The vendor page lists Instagram, Telegram and WhatsApp as lead sources in Tajikistan, so starting with Instagram for content and one messaging app for conversations matches the sourced picture ([shamCRM](https://shamcrm.com/en/tajikistan?code=tajikistan)). Choose based on where your current customers already write to you.

### Do Tajik businesses need Google Ads?

Google search is offered as a service by local agencies, but the sources give no Tajik cost-per-click or conversion figures ([AAMAX](https://aamax.co/blog/top-10-best-digital-marketing-companies-in-dushanbe)). Run a small, capped test and measure cost per lead before committing a larger budget.

### How quickly should a business respond to a chat lead?

The sources do not set a response-time standard. The practical rule from the CRM vendor is that every missed call or chat should become a task with an owner, so nothing is forgotten ([shamCRM](https://shamcrm.com/en/tajikistan?code=tajikistan)).
