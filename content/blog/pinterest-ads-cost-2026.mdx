Pinterest ads are priced by auction, so there is no fixed price list: your cost per click (CPC) or cost per thousand impressions (CPM) depends on your objective, targeting, creative and competition.

Pinterest publishes no official average CPC or CPM, and third-party benchmarks mix industries, countries and objectives, so treat them as rough context only. Pinterest sets a bid floor per campaign type, country and format and, per its help page, uses only what is needed to win the auction ([Pinterest Help](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/set-your-bid), checked 2026-11-03).

The numbers that matter for budgeting come from your own account after a two to four week test.

## How Does Pinterest Ads Pricing Work?

Pinterest runs an auction. You choose an objective, such as awareness, traffic, conversions or catalog sales, and Pinterest charges accordingly.

- **Custom bids:** You set the maximum you are willing to pay for the action, such as a click, and update it manually.
- **Pinterest Performance+ bids:** Pinterest adjusts bids several times a day to get the most results for your budget. It is the default for consideration, leads, sales and brand awareness campaigns ([Pinterest Help](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/set-your-bid), checked 2026-11-03).
- **Budgets:** You can set a lifetime, Performance+ daily or fixed daily budget. With Performance+ daily, a single day can reach up to 125% of the daily budget ([Pinterest Help](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/set-up-campaign-budgets), checked 2026-11-03). Pinterest's budget page states no minimum daily amount, so check the minimum shown in Ads Manager when you create an ad group.

Your real price depends on five things:

- **Targeting.** Narrow keyword and interest targets can cost more per impression than broad ones, but may convert better.
- **Competition.** Seasonal events (holidays, weddings, new-year fitness) tend to raise auction prices.
- **Creative quality.** Ads that get saved and clicked usually earn lower costs over time.
- **Objective and bid strategy.** Automatic bidding typically gives the system more room to optimize.
- **Location.** Prices differ by country and metro area.

<Callout type="warning">Published CPC and CPM averages often mix industries, countries and objectives. Your own numbers after a two to four week test are the only ones that matter for budgeting.</Callout>

## What Could $300 a Month Buy on Pinterest?

Pinterest does not publish an average CPC, so no one can tell you in advance what you will pay. The table shows what happens to your cost per booking when the numbers move. Every figure below is an assumption for illustration, not a benchmark and not a real result.

Example: $10 a day for 30 days is $300. Assume a salon with a $70 average first-visit ticket. Compare four combinations of CPC and the share of clicks that become a booking request.

| Scenario (assumed) | Clicks | Bookings | Revenue | Cost per booking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: $1.00 CPC, 3% book | 300 | 9 | $630 | $33.33 |
| B: $2.00 CPC, 3% book | 150 | 4.5 | $315 | $66.67 |
| C: $1.00 CPC, 1.5% book | 300 | 4.5 | $315 | $66.67 |
| D: $2.00 CPC, 1.5% book | 150 | 2.25 | $157.50 | $133.33 |

Read it like this. Clicks = $300 / CPC. Bookings = clicks x booking rate. Revenue = bookings x $70. Cost per booking = $300 / bookings.

Two things stand out. First, a worse click price and a worse landing page multiply: scenario D costs four times as much per booking as scenario A. Second, the landing page is half the equation. Doubling your booking rate does as much as halving your CPC, and it is usually cheaper to fix.

## Is Pinterest good for local businesses?

Pinterest users often plan ahead, saving ideas for weddings, home projects, haircuts, food and fitness. That makes it a fit for visual businesses that people research before they buy: hair salons, bakeries and cafes, pet grooming, fitness and wellness, and home or event services.

It is typically weaker for urgent, search-driven needs such as emergency repairs, where Google Ads is usually the better choice. See our [Google Ads service](/services/google-ads) and compare with Pinterest ads for small business.

## How Can You Lower Your Pinterest Ads Cost?

- **Use specific targeting.** Combine location with relevant keywords and interests instead of broad terms like "beauty".
- **Match location to your service area.** Do not pay for impressions in cities you cannot serve. Pinterest lets you target by country, region, metro area or postal code ([Pinterest Help](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/location-language-device-and-gender-targeting), checked 2026-11-03).
- **Test video and idea formats.** Short before-and-after clips can perform well for visual services. Test them against static pins.
- **Exclude people who already converted** and retarget warm visitors using the Pinterest Tag.
- **Time your spend.** Start campaigns weeks before a season or event, and review performance by day.
- **Send clicks to a focused page.** One offer and one action beats a generic homepage. A pin that links to your homepage is a wasted pin.

## How Do You Measure Pinterest Ad Results?

Judge cost per acquisition (CPA), such as cost per booking or cost per lead. Clicks and impressions are not the goal. Install the Pinterest Tag, add UTM parameters to your links and connect Google Analytics so you can see what happens after the click. Pinterest influence is often slow, so give campaigns time before judging them. Our [guide to Pinterest ads setup](/blog/how-to-set-up-pinterest-ads-local-business) walks through tracking.

## Pinterest Ads or Google Ads for Local Services?

| | Pinterest Ads | Google Ads |
|---|---|---|
| Intent | Planning and inspiration | Active search |
| Best for | Visual, planned purchases | Urgent or high-intent services |
| Typical timeline | Longer, people save then book later | Shorter, people book quickly |
| Creative | Visual pins and video | Text ads, plus optional images |

Capture existing demand with Google first. Test Pinterest second, and only if your service looks good in a photo.

## When Should You Run Pinterest Ads?

Wedding, holiday, back-to-school and new-year fitness content tends to be searched weeks or months before the event. Auction prices usually rise in peak seasons, so build audiences and test creative in the quieter period before. Use Pinterest's own trend tools to see what people are searching for in your category and region.

## Which Ad Formats Are Worth Testing?

- **Standard image pins:** A good default for a first test.
- **Video pins:** Useful for showing a transformation, a process or a menu item in action.
- **Carousel pins:** Helpful when you want to show several services or products.
- **Shopping or catalog ads:** Relevant if you sell products online.

Test one change at a time, such as image versus video or two headlines, so you know what moved your cost per result.

## What Mistakes Raise the Cost?

- **Broad targeting for a local business.** Pay for people you can actually serve.
- **Sending traffic to a homepage.** Use a page with one offer and one action.
- **Judging too early.** Pinterest results build over weeks. Do not change everything after three days.
- **No tracking.** Without the Pinterest Tag and UTM links, you cannot tell whether spend produced bookings.

## What Should You Do Today?

1. Open Ads Manager and note the minimum shown when you create an ad group. Write it down.
2. Pick a test budget you can hold for 4 weeks. $10 a day is $300 over 30 days.
3. Make 3 pins: one image, one short video, one carousel. Same offer on all three.
4. Build or pick one landing page with one booking button.
5. Install the Pinterest Tag and tag every link with UTM parameters.
6. Calendar a review at day 14 and day 28. Work out cost per booking using the table above.

## FAQ

**Q: How much do Pinterest ads cost in 2026?**
A: It varies by objective, targeting and competition, and Pinterest publishes no official average prices. Test with a small budget for several weeks and use your own cost per click and cost per booking.

**Q: What is the minimum budget for Pinterest ads?**
A: Pinterest sets minimum bids by country and format, and you set ad group budgets in Ads Manager. Pinterest's budget page states no minimum daily amount, so check the figure shown when you create an ad group. Very small budgets limit learning.

**Q: Is Pinterest advertising cheaper than Facebook or Instagram?**
A: Sometimes, but it depends on your audience and objective. Compare cost per booking or lead across channels rather than CPC alone.

**Q: Can I target a specific location with Pinterest ads?**
A: Yes. You can target by country, region, metro area or postal code ([Pinterest Help](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/location-language-device-and-gender-targeting), checked 2026-11-03). Choose places your business can actually serve.

**Q: How long until Pinterest ads show results?**
A: Plan for several weeks. Pinterest users often save ideas and act later, so judge performance over a longer window than you might for search ads.
