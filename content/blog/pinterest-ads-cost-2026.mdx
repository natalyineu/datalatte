Pinterest ads are priced by auction, so there is no fixed price list: your cost per click (CPC) or cost per thousand impressions (CPM) depends on your objective, targeting, creative and competition. We found no official average CPC or CPM figures from Pinterest, and third-party benchmarks mix industries, countries and objectives, so treat them as rough context only. Pinterest sets a minimum bid per country and format and charges you only what is needed to win the auction, up to your bid ([Pinterest Help](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/set-your-bid), checked Oct 2026). The only numbers that matter for budgeting are the ones from your own account after a two to four week test.

## How Pinterest Ads Pricing Works

Pinterest runs an auction. You choose an objective, such as awareness, traffic, conversions or catalog sales, and Pinterest charges accordingly.

- **Custom bids:** You set the maximum you are willing to pay for the action, such as a click, and update it manually.
- **Pinterest Performance+ bids:** Pinterest adjusts bids several times a day to get the most results for your budget. It is the default for consideration, leads, sales and brand awareness campaigns ([Pinterest Help](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/set-your-bid), checked Oct 2026).
- **Budgets:** You can set a lifetime, Performance+ daily or fixed daily budget, and ad group budgets are still required ([Pinterest Help](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/set-up-campaign-budgets), checked Oct 2026). We could not confirm a current minimum daily amount, so check the minimum shown in Ads Manager when you create an ad group.

Your real price depends on:

- **Targeting.** Narrow keyword and interest targets can cost more per impression than broad ones, but may convert better.
- **Competition.** Seasonal events (holidays, weddings, new-year fitness) tend to raise auction prices.
- **Creative quality.** Ads that get saved and clicked usually earn lower costs over time.
- **Objective and bid strategy.** Automatic bidding typically gives the system more room to optimize.
- **Location.** Prices differ by country and metro area.

## What Should a Local Business Budget?

Start with what you can afford to test, not what the platform minimum allows.

1. **Decide the goal.** For local businesses, that is often bookings, calls, store visits or email signups.
2. **Pick a daily budget you can sustain** for at least a few weeks. A common approach is a modest daily amount per campaign, then scaling the winners.
3. **Estimate outcomes.** Take your expected CPC, divide your budget by it to estimate clicks, then apply your landing page conversion rate to estimate leads. If the resulting cost per lead is above what a customer is worth to you, adjust before spending.

Example math (a made-up scenario, not a benchmark): at a $10 daily budget and a $1.00 CPC, you would get roughly 10 clicks a day. If 3 in 100 clicks become a booking request, that is about one booking every 3 to 4 days. Your numbers will differ.

## Is Pinterest Good for Local Businesses?

Pinterest users often plan ahead, saving ideas for weddings, home projects, haircuts, food and fitness. That makes it a good fit for visual businesses that people research before they buy: hair salons, bakeries and cafes, pet grooming, fitness and wellness, and home or event services. It is typically weaker for urgent, search-driven needs such as emergency repairs, where Google Ads is usually the better choice. ## How to Lower Your Pinterest Ads Cost

- **Use specific targeting.** Combine location with relevant keywords and interests instead of broad terms like "beauty".
- **Match location to your service area.** Do not pay for impressions in cities you cannot serve.
- **Test video and idea formats.** Short before-and-after clips can perform well for visual services. Test them against static pins.
- **Exclude people who already converted** and retarget warm visitors using the Pinterest Tag.
- **Time your spend.** Pinterest users plan ahead, so start campaigns weeks before a season or event, and review performance by day.
- **Send clicks to a focused page.** One offer and one action beats a generic homepage.

## Measuring Results

Focus on cost per acquisition (CPA), such as cost per booking or cost per lead, instead of clicks or impressions. Install the Pinterest Tag, add UTM parameters to your links and connect Google Analytics so you can see what happens after the click. Pinterest influence is often slow, so give campaigns time before judging them. Our [guide to Pinterest ads setup](/blog/how-to-set-up-pinterest-ads-local-business) walks through tracking.

## Pinterest Ads vs Google Ads for Local Services

| | Pinterest Ads | Google Ads |
|---|---|---|
| Intent | Planning and inspiration | Active search |
| Best for | Visual, planned purchases | Urgent or high-intent services |
| Typical timeline | Longer, people save then book later | Shorter, people book quickly |
| Creative | Visual pins and video | Text ads, plus optional images |

Most local businesses do best by capturing existing demand with Google first and testing Pinterest as a second channel if their service is visual.

## Seasonal Timing

Pinterest users plan ahead, so launch earlier than you would on other platforms. Wedding, holiday, back-to-school and new-year fitness content tends to be searched weeks or months before the event. Auction prices usually rise in peak seasons, so consider building audiences and testing creative in the quieter period before. Use Pinterest's own trend tools to see what people are searching for in your category and region.

## Ad Formats Worth Testing

- **Standard image pins:** A good default for a first test.
- **Video pins:** Useful for showing a transformation, a process or a menu item in action.
- **Carousel pins:** Helpful when you want to show several services or products.
- **Shopping or catalog ads:** Relevant if you sell products online.

Test one change at a time, such as image versus video or two headlines, so you know what moved your cost per result.

## Common Mistakes

- **Broad targeting for a local business.** Pay for people you can actually serve.
- **Sending traffic to a homepage.** Use a page with one offer and one action.
- **Judging too early.** Pinterest results often build over weeks, so avoid changing everything after a few days.
- **No tracking.** Without the Pinterest Tag and UTM links, you cannot tell whether spend produced bookings.

## FAQ

**Q: How much do Pinterest ads cost in 2026?**
A: It varies by objective, targeting and competition, and we found no official average prices from Pinterest. Test with a small budget for several weeks and use your own cost per click and cost per booking.

**Q: What is the minimum budget for Pinterest ads?**
A: Pinterest sets minimum bids by country and format, and you set ad group budgets in Ads Manager. We could not confirm a current minimum daily amount, so check the figure shown when you create an ad group. Very small budgets limit learning.

**Q: Is Pinterest advertising cheaper than Facebook or Instagram?**
A: Sometimes, but it depends on your audience and objective. Compare cost per booking or lead across channels rather than CPC alone.

**Q: Can I target a specific location with Pinterest ads?**
A: Yes. You can target by country, region, metro area or postal code ([Pinterest Help](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/location-language-device-and-gender-targeting), checked Oct 2026). Choose places your business can actually serve.

**Q: How long until Pinterest ads show results?**
A: Plan for several weeks. Pinterest users often save ideas and act later, so judge performance over a longer window than you might for search ads.
