Wolt does not publish how it ranks venues, so no one outside the company can promise a ranking trick. What its Merchant Learning Center does say is that venues which stay online, keep a strong menu with good photos and give customers a reliable experience are more likely to improve visibility. It also says menu pricing is only one factor among many.

This guide lists the steps Wolt itself recommends, which numbers are Wolt's own claims, and how to test whether a change helped. Nothing here is a secret ranking factor. Examples labelled as assumptions are illustrations.

## Does Wolt Publish How It Ranks Venues?

No. The pages opened for this guide describe what Wolt recommends, not the formula. One page says pricing is "a factor among many" in visibility and does not name the others. Treat any article that gives a ranking percentage as invented, including the ones that claim a single review raises visibility by a fixed share.

Wolt's guidance for new partners groups its advice into availability, opening hours, menu and photos, pricing, promotions and reliability. It contains no numbers. The one number on ranking comes from a different Wolt page: the Ads guide says 70% of customers buy from the top 10 venues, which is Wolt's reason for selling sponsored positions.

## How Much Do Uptime and Opening Hours Matter?

Wolt says "venues that stay online consistently tend to get more orders over time." It advises keeping the Merchant App open during listed hours, avoiding temporary closures, checking tablet, printer or POS integration, and updating unavailable items quickly in busy periods.

It also says to keep hours current, set holiday hours in advance and not close earlier than advertised. Opening during busy meal times can reach more customers. These are the cheapest changes: they cost nothing and are fully in your control.

## What Menu Photo Rules Does Wolt Enforce?

Wolt's photo guide sets firm rules: horizontal 16:9 images, the whole dish visible and centered, only the listed item, no people, text, borders or AI-generated images. Cropped, partial, vertical or blurry photos are rejected, and you can attach up to five images per item.

Wolt claims good photos of main dishes can lift order volume "by up to 5x". That is Wolt's own upper figure, not a guaranteed result. Start with your ten best sellers. ## What Else Does Wolt Recommend for the Menu?

Add photos to as many items as you can, use clear item names, write helpful descriptions, group items into easy-to-browse categories and remove unavailable items promptly. The Merchant Portal also shows personalised growth recommendations. The first two are adding photos and adding item descriptions, each with an estimated sales effect over 90 days, though the page gives no figures.

Price consistency matters for trust. Wolt says prices should match in-store prices and be updated when they change. Check the fee side too: [delivery platform fees explained](/blog/delivery-platform-fees-explained) shows how commission affects price decisions.

## Should You Pay for Visibility?

Wolt Ads are sponsored listings in top positions on the Restaurants, Stores, Categories, Search and Discovery tabs. Wolt says you pay only for ads that lead to a sale, not for impressions or clicks. Promos, which are free delivery, basket discounts and buy-more-save-more offers, drive traffic but are not described as changing rank.

Wolt advises starting with a small ad budget and adjusting on results. Our [Wolt Ads guide](/blog/wolt-ads-restaurant-guide-europe) covers setup and billing in detail, and [Glovo vs Wolt](/blog/glovo-vs-wolt-which-is-better) compares the two platforms.

## How Do You Test Whether a Change Worked? A Worked Example

The inputs are **assumptions** chosen for easy arithmetic.

| Input (assumption) | Before | After 4 weeks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Items with photos | 10 of 60 | 40 of 60 |
| Hours offline during opening times per week | 6 | 1 |
| Orders per week | 50 | 60 |
| Average basket | 22 EUR | 22 EUR |
| Weekly sales | 1,100 EUR | 1,320 EUR |

In this illustration, weekly sales rise by 220 EUR. You cannot tell which change caused it, so change one thing at a time where you can, and keep a log with dates. Check the Merchant Portal weekly, as Wolt suggests, and compare the same weekdays. ## FAQ

### Can I pay Wolt to rank first?

Wolt sells sponsored listings in top positions, charged per attributed sale. It does not say the organic ranking can be bought. See the Ads guide for details.

### How many photos should each menu item have?

Wolt allows up to five images per item. It asks for at least one clear 16:9 photo on as many items as possible, starting with main dishes.

### Do reviews improve my ranking?

Wolt's pages opened for this guide do not state how ratings affect rank, so no figure is given. The same pages advise monitoring reviews weekly.

### Does lowering prices raise visibility?

Wolt says pricing is one factor among many and asks for consistency with in-store prices. It gives no threshold or markup target.
