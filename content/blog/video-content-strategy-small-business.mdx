A small business video strategy comes down to four decisions: one main platform, one repeatable video format, a fixed weekly time budget and one booking-based metric. Pew Research's 2025 survey found 84% of US adults use YouTube, 71% Facebook, 50% Instagram and 37% TikTok. Reach is not the same as where your customers are, so choose by who buys from you.

## Which Platform Should a Local Business Pick First?

Start where your customers already look for you, then add a second platform only after four steady weeks.

| If your customers are... | Start with | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Searching "near me" for a service | Google Business Profile and YouTube | Intent is already there |
| Local families and older adults | Facebook | 71% of US adults use it |
| Browsing for style, food or fitness | Instagram Reels | 50% of US adults use it |
| Younger and trend-led | TikTok | 37% of US adults use it |

Do not run all four at once. One channel done weekly beats four done monthly.

## What Video Formats Work for Local Businesses?

Choose one format you can repeat in under 30 minutes. These suit most local niches:

- **Before and after:** hair color, a groomed dog, a refreshed room. Get written permission from the customer.
- **How we make it:** a drink, a dish, a cut, in one take.
- **Answer one question:** "How often should a dog be groomed?" or "What is included in a first class?"
- **Meet the person:** 15 seconds of the owner or a staff member saying what they do.
- **Customer story:** a short quote read aloud, with consent.

Pick the format that answers the question your customers ask most before booking. Film vertically, add captions, and put the offer or the action in the first sentence.

## How Do You Use Video on Your Google Business Profile?

Start here because it costs nothing and sits next to the searches that send people to you. Google's [Business Profile media help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6103862) says videos can be up to 30 seconds long, no larger than 75 MB, and 720p or higher.

Upload clips of the entrance, the work in progress, a staff introduction and a finished result. Keep file names descriptive and match the content to what your profile says. Our [guide to Google Business Profile photos and videos](/blog/google-my-business-photos-and-videos) covers more, and a clip made for Reels can be cut to 30 seconds for the profile.

## How Much Time and Money Does Video Take?

Plan time first. Worked example, labelled as an assumption: you record two 30-second videos a week.

| Task | Minutes per week |
| --- | --- |
| Plan the two ideas | 20 |
| Film both clips | 40 |
| Edit and add captions | 40 |
| Post and reply to comments | 20 |
| Total | 120 |

That is about 2 hours a week, or 8 to 9 hours a month. If your time is worth $30 an hour, that is $240 to $270 a month before any ad spend. Replace these inputs with your own.

Decision rule: if two hours a week is unrealistic, make one video a week and drop the second platform. Paid promotion comes after you have organic videos that already get replies and calls.

## How Do You Know If Video Is Working?

Views are cheap to produce and weak as proof. Track actions that lead to revenue:

- Bookings or orders that mention the video, collected by asking "How did you hear about us?"
- Calls and direction requests from your Google Business Profile.
- Clicks on the link in your bio, using a tagged link or a separate landing page.
- Replies and saves on posts, as a signal of what to repeat.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the best video platform for a small local business?**
A: Pew's 2025 survey found YouTube used by 84% of US adults, Facebook 71%, Instagram 50% and TikTok 37%. Choose the one your customers use, and start with Google Business Profile video.

**Q: How long should my business videos be?**
A: Google Business Profile accepts videos up to 30 seconds, 75 MB, 720p or higher. For social platforms, check each platform's current help page, because limits change.

**Q: How often should I post video?**
A: Pick a rate you can keep for 12 weeks. Two short videos a week is about 2 hours of work in the plan above.

**Q: Do I need expensive equipment?**
A: No. A phone, daylight and captions are enough to start. Spend money later, on ads, once a video proves it brings bookings.

**Q: Should I outsource video?**
A: Only if the person films your real staff, premises and answers. Generic stock-style videos do not show customers what to expect.
