The most common Google Ads mistakes for a dog groomer are no negative keywords, no conversion tracking for calls and bookings, a radius that is too wide, and sending clicks to a home page. Each is fixed with a setting. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks list Animals and Pets at $4.06 per click and a 16.22% conversion rate, so a fixed budget runs out fast when clicks are wasted.

This article covers seven mistakes in the order they usually cost money. The numbers come from the sources at the end, and examples are labelled as assumptions.

## Mistake 1: Are You Paying for Searches That Cannot Book?

Without negative keywords, you pay for people who want a job, a course, a product or a free tip. Google says negative keywords let you exclude search terms from campaigns. They do not match close variants, so add singular and plural forms yourself.

Fix: add negatives such as "jobs", "school", "course", "training", "supplies", "diy", "free" and "recipe" before launch. Then use the Search terms report. Google describes it as the list of search terms that led to your ad being shown, and advises adding irrelevant terms as negatives. Check it weekly for the first two months.

## Mistake 2: Is Your Radius Too Wide?

A groomer serves a drive-time area, not a county. Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km, and recommends broad geo targeting ("Presence or interest") as a best practice on Search. If your search terms or location report show clicks from towns you do not serve, test "Presence" and exclude those areas.

Mobile groomers should set the service area to match the vans' real routes, and say so in the ad text.

## Mistake 3: Are You Counting Clicks Instead of Bookings?

Clicks tell you little. A booking or a call does. Google says that for call conversions you set a minimum call length, and every call that lasts at least that long counts as a conversion. Google also says clicks on a call asset on mobile cost the same as headline clicks.

Fix: track calls, the booking form and your online scheduler link as conversions. Without them, Google cannot show which keywords produce appointments. Log the source of each new client at the front desk too.

## Mistake 4: Do Ads Run When Nobody Can Answer?

If a call goes to voicemail, the click is wasted. Google's ad schedule lets you show ads only on certain hours or days and set bid adjustments for specific times. A caveat from Google: if nobody searches at the scheduled time, your ads will not show.

Fix: schedule call-focused ads for opening hours. Keep the booking link ads running outside them, because pet owners often book in the evening.

## Mistake 5: Where Do Your Clicks Land?

A home page forces the visitor to hunt. Send each ad group to a matching page: a "dog grooming near me" ad to a page with services, prices or starting prices, area, hours and a booking button. Show the call number at the top on mobile.

Fix: one landing page per main service, such as full groom, puppy first groom, de-shedding and nail trim. Build pages only for services you offer.

## Mistake 6: Are You Ignoring the Free Listing?

Paid clicks work better when your Business Profile shows reviews, photos and hours. See our [Business Profile guide for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-for-pet-groomers) and the [25-point checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## Mistake 7: Have You Set a Budget Without a Plan?

Many groomers set a budget by feel. Work backwards from what a new client is worth. Our [guide on how much a pet groomer should spend](/blog/how-much-should-a-pet-groomer-spend-on-google-ads) shows the method. The example below uses assumptions.

## FAQ

### What is the biggest Google Ads mistake for dog groomers?
Paying for irrelevant searches. Without negative keywords, jobs, courses and product searches use the same budget as bookings.

### What is the average cost per click for pet services?
WordStream by LocaliQ lists Animals and Pets at $4.06 per click in its 2026 benchmarks. It does not publish a dog grooming row.

### How do I track phone calls?
Set up call conversions and choose a minimum call length. Every call that lasts at least that long counts.

### Should I use a smaller radius?
Test it. Google requires at least 1 km. Check the location report to see where bookings really come from.

### How long before I judge the campaign?
Google does not set a number. Review the Search terms report weekly and judge cost per booking once you have a month of conversion data.
