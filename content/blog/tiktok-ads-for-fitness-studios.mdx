A fitness studio can run TikTok ads, but the platform sets a floor: TikTok's help center says ad group daily budgets must exceed $20 and campaign budgets must exceed $50. One ad group at just over $20 a day is roughly $600 a month. The ad policy also limits weight-loss and muscle-gain claims, so lead with classes, coaches and a trial offer.

This guide is for studio owners and managers in the US and similar markets. It covers budget math, local targeting, ad rules and a worked example. TikTok does not publish average CPM or CPC for fitness on its help pages, so none is given here. Sources are listed at the end.

## How Much Do TikTok Ads Cost for a Fitness Studio?

TikTok sets minimums, not prices. Per its daily budgets page, both daily and total campaign budgets must exceed 50 USD, ad group daily budgets must exceed 20 USD, and a lifetime ad group budget is calculated as 20 USD times the scheduled days. Over 30 days, one ad group therefore needs more than $600.

What you pay per click or per thousand views is set by auction and is not published on TikTok's help pages. Third-party sites quote figures, but none is verified here. Read our [TikTok ads cost guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-guide) for how the auction works, then use your own results after the first test. If $600 a month is too much, put the money first into your Google Business Profile and reviews.

## Can You Target People Near Your Studio?

Yes, within limits. TikTok's location targeting page lists city, county, DMA region, state, ZIP or postal code and other location types, up to 3,000 selections per ad group where available. It says targeting finds people located in or regularly in the selected place, and that delivery and accuracy are not guaranteed.

The page also says TikTok does not support excluding locations or ZIP codes, and that narrow geographic targeting can noticeably reduce impressions and clicks and may raise cost per action. It does not mention radius targeting, so use ZIP codes around the studio. See our [TikTok ads targeting guide](/blog/tiktok-ads-targeting-guide) for more.

## What Are TikTok's Rules for Fitness Ads?

TikTok's weight management policy matters most. It says weight-loss or muscle-gain claims must be restricted to people 18 or older and promote a healthy lifestyle. It bars references to unrealistic muscle gain or weight loss, claims that a product alone leads to results, suggestions that results are easy or guaranteed, shaming users about their bodies, and implying an ideal body type.

The policy page does not clearly address gym or class advertising, and its before-and-after rules were not visible when read. Avoid body-transformation promises, use age targeting, and review the full policy before publishing.

## What Should the Ad Show?

Show the studio, the coach and a real class, with an offer a stranger can accept: a first class, a trial week or an intro rate. Keep the call to action single, such as booking the trial. Send people to a page that works on a phone and has the class schedule, address and price.

## A Worked Example: What Does a Month Buy?

All inputs are **assumptions**. TikTok does not publish fitness CPC, so the click cost is a placeholder that you replace with your own number after a test.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly spend, one ad group | $620 ($20.67 x 30 days) |
| Cost per click (placeholder) | $1.00 |
| Clicks | 620 |
| Click to trial sign-up | 3% |
| Trial sign-ups | about 19 |
| Trial to member | 25% |
| New members | about 5 |
| Cost per new member | about $124 |

If a member is worth $60 a month and stays six months (assumption), lifetime value is $360, so five members return $1,800 against $620. If the click costs $2 or sign-up is 1%, the cost per member rises to about $800 and the test loses money. Run it for a fixed period, then compare.

## FAQ

### Do I need many followers to run TikTok ads?

No source checked here ties ad delivery to follower count. You need a business account and Ads Manager access.

### Is the $20 daily minimum strict?

TikTok says ad group daily budgets must exceed 20 USD. Exactly $20 does not meet the wording, so set slightly more.

### Can I show weight-loss results?

Claims must be restricted to adults and avoid unrealistic or guaranteed results. Do not promise outcomes. Check the policy page before using transformation content.

### Is TikTok better than Meta or Google?

Not proven. Google captures people already searching for a gym, while TikTok shows ads to people scrolling. Test with a fixed budget.
