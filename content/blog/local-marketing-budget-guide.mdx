There is no single right local marketing budget. Size it backwards: work out what a new customer is worth to you, divide by the share of leads that become customers, and that is the most you can pay per lead. Then check it against 2026 benchmarks. LocaliQ puts average Google Search clicks at $2.05 for restaurants, $4.06 for pet businesses, $4.62 for beauty and $6.17 for health and fitness.

This guide is for coffee shops, salons, pet groomers and fitness studios in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. Benchmarks are industry averages, not promises, and examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Should a Local Business Spend on Marketing?

Start with the most you can afford to pay for one lead, not a percentage of revenue. Percent-of-revenue rules circulate widely, often attributed to the US Small Business Administration, but that attribution could not be verified on sba.gov, so no percentage is used here.

Three inputs set your ceiling:

1. **Profit per new customer** over the period you count, for example the first six months.
2. **Lead-to-customer rate**, meaning the share of calls and forms that book.
3. **Monthly lead target**, based on the capacity you have.

Maximum cost per lead equals profit per new customer times lead-to-customer rate. Budget equals that ceiling times the leads you want.

## What Do Clicks and Leads Cost by Industry?

LocaliQ's June 2026 Google Search benchmarks and its September 2026 Facebook benchmarks give a starting range. Coffee shops fit Restaurants and Food, the nearest category, because there is no cafe row.

| Industry | Google CPC | Google conversion rate | Google cost per lead | Meta lead-campaign cost per lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Restaurants and Food | $2.05 | 8.05% | $30.57 | not reported |
| Animals and Pets | $4.06 | 16.22% | $31.50 | not reported |
| Beauty and Personal Care | $4.62 | 10.35% | $39.25 | $50.91 |
| Health and Fitness | $6.17 | 6.94% | $67.36 | $27.11 |
| All industries | $5.42 | 8.18% | $66.69 | $27.39 |

A "lead" in these reports is whatever the advertiser counted as a conversion, such as a call or form. Meta traffic campaigns are far cheaper per click, at $0.45 to $0.64 in these categories, but clicks are not leads.

## How Far Does $250, $500 or $1,000 a Month Go on Google?

The click volume is simple division: budget divided by cost per click. Google also says daily spending can reach twice your average daily budget on a given day, with a monthly cap of 30.4 times the daily figure.

| Monthly budget | Restaurants and Food | Animals and Pets | Beauty and Personal Care | Health and Fitness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $250 | about 122 clicks | about 62 | about 54 | about 41 |
| $500 | about 244 clicks | about 123 | about 108 | about 81 |
| $1,000 | about 488 clicks | about 246 | about 216 | about 162 |

At $500 and each category's conversion rate, that is roughly 20 leads for restaurants, 20 for pet businesses, 11 for beauty and 6 for health and fitness. Local competition changes every figure, so treat your first month as a measurement, not a forecast.

## Is Meta or Google Better for Your Business?

Google reaches people who are searching now. Meta reaches people who match an audience but are not searching. LocaliQ's data suggests Meta lead forms cost less per lead for fitness ($27.11) but more for beauty ($50.91) than Google's $39.25 average. Those are averages across all advertisers.

A reasonable first split follows the buying moment:

- **Coffee shops and pet groomers:** search-led. Start with Google Search and a complete Google Business Profile.
- **Salons:** test both, because the services are visual and also searched.
- **Fitness studios:** Meta lead forms for trial offers, plus Google Search for "gyms near me" intent.

## What Does Free and Low-Cost Marketing Include?

Google states that creating a Business Profile and listing your business on Google is free. That makes it the first line item for any budget, including a $0 one. Complete the categories, hours, photos and services, then ask every customer for a review.

No authoritative source used here publishes typical prices for local SEO retainers or freelance ad management, and quotes vary widely. Ask for a written scope and a monthly report before paying anyone, and compare against the clicks your ad spend buys. ## What Does a First $500 Month Look Like?

**Worked example (all inputs are assumptions):** a salon with a $500 monthly test budget, split $300 Google Search and $200 Meta lead ads. Profit per new customer over six months is $120, and 25% of leads become customers.

| Step | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum cost per lead | $120 x 25% | $30 |
| Google clicks | $300 / $4.62 | about 65 |
| Google leads | 65 x 10.35% | about 7 |
| Meta leads | $200 / $50.91 | about 4 |
| Total leads | 7 + 4 | about 11 |
| New customers | 11 x 25% | about 3 |
| Profit from new customers | 3 x $120 | about $360 |

Result: spending $500 to earn about $360 loses money in month one. Benchmark cost per lead ($39.25 on Google, $50.91 on Meta) is above the $30 ceiling. To make it work the salon needs a higher lead-to-customer rate, a higher value per customer, or a cheaper source such as reviews and its Business Profile. ## What Should You Track Before Raising the Budget?

Track calls, form fills and bookings by source before adding money. Google Ads can count calls from a call asset or website forms as conversions. Without tracking, you cannot compare channels or compute your real cost per customer.

Review monthly: spend, leads, customers and profit. Raise a channel's budget only when its cost per customer stays below your ceiling for two months. ## FAQ

### How much should a small local business spend on marketing?

No source used here supports a fixed percentage. Calculate your ceiling from profit per new customer and lead-to-customer rate, then test with a budget you can afford to lose, such as $250 to $500 a month.

### Is $200 a month enough for Google Ads?

It buys limited volume. At LocaliQ's 2026 average of $5.42 per click, $200 is about 37 clicks. In a cheaper category like Restaurants and Food ($2.05) it is about 98 clicks.

### Which is cheaper, Meta or Google?

Meta clicks are cheaper in LocaliQ's data ($0.50 to $0.64 for traffic in these categories), but cost per lead depends on the objective and industry. Compare cost per lead, not cost per click.

### Should a new business with no reviews run ads?

Ads can work without reviews, but a fuller Google Business Profile and a few real reviews help conversion. The sources used here do not quantify the effect.

### How long until I see results from local SEO?

No source used here publishes a reliable timeline. Treat claims of guaranteed rankings in 30 days with caution and track your own Business Profile calls and direction requests.

### Should I hire an agency or do it myself?

Do it yourself if you can check the account weekly and track results. Hire help if you cannot. Ask for a written scope and monthly reporting.
