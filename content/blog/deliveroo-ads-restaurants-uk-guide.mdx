Deliveroo sells restaurant advertising as Marketer Adverts, a cost-per-click service. Bids start at £0.25 per click, you pay only when a customer taps your ad, and Deliveroo's help centre states no minimum budget. Ads appear in the app homepage, cuisine pages, search results and restaurant listings, and are labelled as ads. Deliveroo does not publish an average click price, so test with a small budget first.

## How Do Deliveroo Ads Work?

Marketer Adverts is a pay-per-click product. You set a budget, choose when the ad runs, and bid a price per click. Each time a customer taps your ad and reaches your menu, a set amount comes out of the budget. Unused budget is not charged, and charges for budget spent appear on your next invoice.

Placements are the homepage, cuisine pages, search results and restaurant listings. Deliveroo says paid placements are tagged as sponsored. Ad technology for Deliveroo's wider advertising platform, which also sells slots to consumer brands, is provided by Criteo, according to Deliveroo's 2022 launch announcement. Restaurant adverts are a separate, self-serve product.

## How Much Do Deliveroo Ads Cost?

Bids start at £0.25 per click in the UK. Deliveroo says higher bids generally mean more exposure and faster budget use. It does not publish an average winning bid, so the real price in your area depends on competing restaurants.

You can choose a total campaign budget that Deliveroo says it will not exceed, or a weekly budget that renews every Monday. Campaigns can be one-off, such as a bank holiday weekend, or run continually until you switch them off. A campaign ends on its end date or when its budget runs out, whichever comes first.

## Automatic or Manual Bidding: Which Should You Pick?

Deliveroo offers two modes. With automatic bidding it adjusts your bid across the campaign to reach customers likely to engage. With manual bidding you set the exact amount per click. Deliveroo's marketing page says an auto-bidding tool can suggest bids for new advertisers.

For a first campaign, automatic bidding saves guesswork. Move to manual once you see your cost per order and want to cap it. Because bids compete with other restaurants nearby, a bid that works on a quiet Tuesday may not win on Friday evening.

## Who Can You Target?

Targeting is by site and by customer group. You can advertise one site or all of them and vary bids by site. Deliveroo advises running a site on its own if you want to lift sales there, because combined campaigns spend budget faster in busier areas.

| Customer group | Typical use (our suggestion) |
| --- | --- |
| New customers | Win first orders |
| Existing customers | Repeat orders |
| Deliveroo Plus subscribers | Customers with free delivery |
| Deliveroo Students subscribers | Student areas |
| Menu browsers | Customers who looked but did not order |
| Customers who have not ordered recently | Win-back |

You can also schedule ads always on, on set days, or during meal occasions and custom hours. Up to five campaigns can overlap on one site per day.

## What Results Does Deliveroo Report?

After a campaign ends, Hub shows taps on your ad, customers who ordered after tapping, new customers attracted and extra orders generated. Deliveroo lists downloadable reports, view-based attribution and performance by time of day as coming soon, so check Hub for current features.

Deliveroo's own marketing page claims a 40-70% rise in menu views, a 20-40% rise in order volume and £5.80 to £9.90 returned per £1 spent. These figures are not independently verified and the page gives no method, sample or period. Treat them as the platform's pitch, not a forecast for your kitchen.

## The Math: What Could a £200 Test Return?

All inputs are **assumptions**, not Deliveroo data, so replace them with your own numbers after the first campaign.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget | £200 |
| Average cost per click | £0.40 |
| Clicks | 500 |
| Share of clicks that become an order | 10% |
| Orders | 50 |
| Average order value | £22 |
| Gross sales | £1,100 |
| Food and packaging cost | 32% of sales |
| Deliveroo commission | 30% of sales (assumption; check your contract) |
| Left after costs and ads | £1,100 x 38% - £200 = £218 |

At these assumptions, the test makes about £218 before labour and overheads. If only 5% of clicks ordered, orders halve, gross sales fall to £550 and the same arithmetic gives £550 x 38% - £200 = £9. That is why conversion rate and your commission rate matter more than the click price. Before spending, work out your own margin per order.

## FAQ

### How much do Deliveroo ads cost?
Bids start at £0.25 per click in the UK, and you pay only for taps on your ad. Deliveroo does not publish an average price, and its help centre states no minimum budget.

### Can I set a spending limit?
Yes. You can set a total campaign budget or a weekly budget that renews every Monday. Deliveroo says unused budget is not charged.

### Where do Deliveroo ads appear?
On the homepage, cuisine pages, search results and restaurant listings, labelled as ads.

### Can I target new customers only?
Yes. Customer groups include new customers, existing customers, Plus and Students subscribers, menu browsers and customers who have not ordered recently.

### Are Deliveroo's ROI claims reliable?
They come from Deliveroo's own marketing page, with no published method. Run a small test and calculate your own return.
