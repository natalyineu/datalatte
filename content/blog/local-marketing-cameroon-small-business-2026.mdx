A Cameroonian small business cannot assume its customers are online: only 41.9% of the population uses the internet, according to DataReportal's Digital 2026 report. Facebook reaches 5.90 million people, 19.6% of the 30.1 million population. Online marketing works for urban, younger customers; the rest need offline channels and mobile money.

This guide covers what those numbers mean for shops, salons and cafes in Douala and Yaounde. No Cameroon ad price data was found, so none is quoted.

## How Many Cameroonians Are Online?

DataReportal (Kepios data, October 2025) counts 12.6 million internet users out of 30.1 million people. That leaves 17.5 million offline. Mobile connections total 29.0 million, 96.4% of the population, up 11.7% in a year. The median age is 18.0 and 60.6% of people live in towns.

The World Bank agrees on the direction: 46% internet use in 2024, GDP per capita of 1,972.4 US dollars in 2025 and 72.0% electricity access in 2024. Each source measures internet use differently, so pick one for your plan and stay consistent.

## Which Social Platforms Reach Customers?

Facebook dominates. It has 5.90 million ad reach, 46.8% of internet users, and grew 15.7% over the year. Messenger reaches 631,000 and Instagram 611,000. LinkedIn shows 1.60 million registered members, and X 185,000.

The audience leans male: 59.0% of Facebook's adult audience is male, and Instagram is 60.2% male. A business selling mainly to women should not assume Facebook reaches them as well as it reaches men.

DataReportal notes that ad reach is not active users and that platforms revise the numbers. It lists no TikTok or YouTube audience for Cameroon, so those are not compared here.

## How Do Cameroonians Pay?

By mobile money. Business in Cameroon, citing the central bank of the region (BEAC) 2023 report, says Cameroon had 24.86 million mobile money accounts, of which 11.7 million were active in the last 30 days. Mobile money transactions totalled about CFA 22.1 trillion that year, compared with about CFA 12.6 trillion in cheques.

Mobile money is also the more common route than banks: the same article says banking, including microfinance, does not exceed 40%. These are 2023 data reported in a news article, so they are secondary.

For a small seller, accepting Orange Money and MTN MoMo is a basic requirement, and the number to put on a Facebook page should be one customers can pay to.

## What Should a Business Do With Only 19.6% on Facebook?

Use Facebook where your customers are, and use something else where they are not. A neighbourhood business with walk-in customers gains little from a national audience. A seller in Douala or Yaounde, the two cities with most of the urban population, has a better chance of reaching customers online.

Put the same name, address and mobile money number on every channel: a Facebook page, a Google Business Profile, a shopfront sign and printed cards. If a customer finds you offline, the online listing should confirm what they saw.

## What Would a Cautious Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**, not Cameroonian benchmarks.

- Test budget (assumption): 25,000 CFA francs
- Goal (assumption): conversations on Messenger
- Test length (assumption): 3 weeks
- Radius (assumption): the neighbourhood you can deliver to

Count the conversations, the orders that came from them and how many paid by mobile money. Divide the spend by the orders to get your own cost per order. If you get no orders, check the photo, price and reply speed before raising the budget.

## FAQ

### How many people use Facebook in Cameroon?

DataReportal counts 5.90 million Facebook ad-reach users in late 2025, 19.6% of the population and 46.8% of internet users.

### Is most of Cameroon online?

No. Internet penetration is 41.9%, so 58.1% of the population, 17.5 million people, are offline (DataReportal, October 2025).

### How common is mobile money?

Cameroon had 24.86 million mobile money accounts and 11.7 million active ones in 2023, per a BEAC report cited by Business in Cameroon.

### How much do ads cost in Cameroon?

No verified cost-per-click or CPM data was found for Cameroon, so none is given. Test with a small budget and measure your own cost per order.

### Who sees my Facebook ads?

Mostly men: 59.0% of the adult Facebook ad audience is male, so test creative aimed at women separately.
