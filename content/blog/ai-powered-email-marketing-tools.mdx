The useful question is narrower than most comparison tables suggest: which repeated writing task disappears? For a local list that task is usually the monthly newsletter draft, review-request wording, or a lapsed-customer message. Everything else is decoration on the invoice.

## Which AI email features are actually useful?

Subject-line and send-time suggestions are the safest features because they stay inside measured systems. The platform tests variants against real opens and bookings, so a weak suggestion costs one send, not a reputation. Accept the suggestion, keep the measurement.

Draft generation helps blank-page tasks: a first version of a win-back message or an event announcement the owner then edits for prices and dates. [Email marketing for yoga studios](/blog/email-marketing-for-yoga-studios) shows that edit gate in a real send calendar.

Predictive segmentation is the feature to distrust at small scale. A list of 800 contacts does not contain enough behaviour to train reliable predictions, whatever the dashboard implies. Manual segments (new, active, lapsed) outperform black-box scores until lists reach the thousands. ## What should a local business pay for AI email?

Pay for the platform, not the adjective. Mailchimp Essentials from $13 a month at 500 contacts, Brevo Starter from $9, and ActiveCampaign Starter from $15 at 1,000 contacts all bundle drafting help at entry level, on November 2026 vendor pricing. A separate AI writer adds a second subscription for work the platform already drafts.

**Assumption example.** Assume an owner spends 3 hours a month writing a newsletter and reminders, valued at $30 an hour, or $90. If built-in drafting halves that, the saving is $45 against a $13 platform cost. A separate $20 writing tool only wins if it saves a further 40 minutes. The inputs are assumptions; time one real newsletter before buying either.

General assistants cover overflow writing at $0 to $20 a month. [Best AI tools for small business marketing](/blog/ai-tools-for-small-business-marketing-2026) compares that route, including when a chatbot or agent becomes the better purchase.

## What can go wrong with AI-written email?

Accuracy, then voice. Generated copy invents plausible details: a class time, a discount nobody approved, a stylist who left in March. Every AI draft needs a facts pass against the booking system and the current price list before scheduling.

Voice fails quietly. Three months of unedited generated newsletters sound like every other business using the same tool, and regulars notice. Keep one human sentence in every send: the specific thing that happened in the business this week. That sentence is the retention mechanism.

Compliance is not optional decoration. Consent, a working unsubscribe and a real address remain the sender's legal problem regardless of who drafted the text. AI tools do not check any of them.

## FAQ

### Do AI email tools improve open rates?

They can improve subject lines through testing, which is a platform feature older than the AI label. No tool guarantees opens, and Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates open counts anyway. Judge AI help on drafting time saved and bookings produced, the two numbers a local business can verify in its own account.

### What is the cheapest AI email setup for a local business?

A free tier first: Mailchimp covers 250 contacts and Brevo allows 300 emails a day, on November 2026 vendor pricing. Draft with the built-in assistant or a free general assistant, and edit every send. Paid tiers from $9 to $15 a month make sense once automation steps or list size pass the free caps.

### Should email copy be written entirely by AI?

No. Draft with AI, then edit for facts, prices, dates and one human sentence. Unedited generated email risks invented offers and a generic voice that regulars can spot. The edit takes minutes and is the difference between a faster newsletter and a trust problem delivered monthly.

### Which AI email tool fits a very small list?

At under 500 contacts, choose on automation and deliverability basics, not AI branding: Brevo from $9 or Mailchimp Essentials from $13, on November 2026 pricing. Lists that small gain more from a welcome, a reminder and a win-back send than from predictive features trained on too little data.
