A Vapi AI phone agent answers your business line, responds to common questions, and takes bookings when your staff cannot pick up. For a salon or cafe, that means a caller who rings during a cut or a morning rush can reach a live-sounding agent instead of voicemail. Setup needs a phone number, a voice, a system prompt built from your real menu and hours, and a webhook that checks your booking calendar. ElevenLabs, a voice AI vendor, [states its voice agents handle calls 24/7 for small businesses](https://elevenlabs.io/agents/small-business-answering-service). This guide covers the setup, telephony, and a cost example with every input labelled.

## What Can a Phone Agent Handle for a Salon or Cafe?

Most calls to a salon or cafe fall into a small set of types: opening hours, prices, whether you take walk-ins, and whether a particular stylist has a gap. A phone agent works best where the answers are fixed and the next step is a booking. The same pattern drives [appointment booking agents](/blog/ai-agent-for-appointment-booking-local-business) in other local trades.

Vapi is a voice AI platform for live phone calls. Callers can interrupt, change their minds, and restart a question, and the agent has to keep up. Vapi also lets you choose the transcriber, language model, and voice component, so you can change one part without rebuilding the whole flow.

Calls that need judgment should go to a person. Complaints, refund requests, and callers who sound upset are better handled by a human, with the agent taking a name and number for a callback. For a wider view of where voice tools fit, see the [comparison of AI agents and chatbots](/blog/ai-agent-vs-chatbot-whats-the-difference-local-business).

## How Do You Set Up a Vapi Phone Agent, Step by Step?

Work through these steps in order. Each one depends on the one before it.

1. Create a Vapi account and generate an API key. Store the key as an environment variable, never inside application files that others can read.
2. Build the assistant in the dashboard or through the API. Set the model, voice, transcriber, and the first thing the caller hears.
3. Write the system prompt from your real hours, service menu, staff list, and booking rules.
4. Connect a phone number. Buy one through Vapi or forward your existing line (see the routing section below).
5. Build a webhook server that answers availability and booking requests from your calendar or booking database.
6. Send the confirmation text from your webhook, using your SMS provider, rather than relying on the agent to do it.
7. Make at least ten test calls from a mobile phone before going live, including calls with background noise.

Keep a short list of what the agent can and cannot change. It should read availability and create bookings, but it should not edit prices or cancel appointments without a rule you wrote.

## What Belongs in the System Prompt?

The system prompt does most of the work. A vague instruction like "be helpful" gives the agent nothing to act on. Callers ask about prices on almost every call, so the full menu belongs in the prompt. Include the exact address and hours, with every exception: closed days, different weekend hours, and holiday closures. Add staff names and specialties, because callers often ask for a specific person. Include your deposit and cancellation rules, since those questions come up often.

Use a template like this and replace the bracketed text with your own details:

```text
You are the phone receptionist for [SALON NAME], a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] in [CITY].
Answer questions about hours, services, prices and staff using only the details below.
Hours: [EVERY DAY, INCLUDING CLOSED DAYS AND HOLIDAY EXCEPTIONS]
Services and prices: [PASTE FULL MENU]
Staff and specialties: [NAME: SPECIALTY]
Booking rules: [DEPOSIT, CANCELLATION NOTICE, BOOKING WINDOW]
Never quote an opening time before checking the booking tool.
If you cannot answer, offer a callback from a team member and take the caller's name and number.
If the caller is upset or asks for the owner, offer a transfer or a callback.
Tone: [WARM AND CASUAL, OR PROFESSIONAL AND APPROACHABLE]
```

Test the prompt by writing ten questions you have heard over the phone and checking whether the agent can answer each one without guessing.

## How Do You Route Calls From Your Existing Number?

You have two routes. You can buy a new number inside Vapi and point customers to it, or you can keep your current number and forward unanswered calls to the Vapi number. Forwarding keeps the number customers already know. Conditional forwarding codes differ by carrier and VoIP provider, so check your carrier's own instructions or your VoIP account's call-flow settings before you change anything.

ElevenLabs [states that connecting its conversational agent to phone calls requires integrating a telephony provider](https://elevenlabs.io/conversational-ai) such as Twilio, Vonage, or a SIP-compatible phone system. Whichever route you choose, the number has to work end to end before customers depend on it.

If you switch to a new number, update it everywhere your business appears. [AdWave states that a business's Name, Address, and Phone number needs to be 100% identical everywhere online](https://adwave.com/resources/voice-search-optimization-for-local-businesses) (secondary). A mismatched number on your profile or directory listing sends customers to the wrong line.

## How Do You Measure Whether the Agent Brings in Bookings?

Track bookings by source before you judge the agent. Your webhook can tag every booking with a source field, such as the phone agent, so you can count them separately from walk-ins and online bookings.

For calls that also come from ads, Google's guidance matters. [Google counts a call as a conversion only when it lasts longer than a minimum length you set](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6095883). [INSIDEA, a marketing agency, recommends counting only calls longer than 60 seconds as call conversions](https://insidea.com/blog/marketing/pest-control-companies/google-ads-strategy-for-exterminators) (secondary). Short calls to the agent, such as a question about hours, should not inflate your conversion count.

A site tracker does not replace a call log. [AdWave notes that a tracker ties later visits to a campaign but does not prove a closed sale that was never tracked](https://adwave.com/resources/campaign-workflow-across-channels). Keep the agent's call logs and booking records side by side. For an outside check on your setup, you can [request a free audit](/free-audit).

## What Mistakes Should You Avoid?

Several errors show up again and again in phone agent setups.

1. Skipping a human fallback. Every fallback should end with a promised callback, and someone has to make that callback.
2. Writing a thin prompt. If the agent says "I don't know" on common questions, the menu, hours, and policies are missing from the prompt.
3. Leaving out confirmation texts. Send the confirmation from your webhook from the first day.
4. Testing only in a browser. Background noise and cell compression change how speech is transcribed, so test on a phone.
5. Not reading call transcripts. Review them regularly for the first month and fix the prompt wherever callers get confused.

## FAQ

### How human does a Vapi phone agent sound?

ElevenLabs describes its agents as having [sub-second response times and studio-quality voices](https://elevenlabs.io/agents/ai-call-agent). Vendor descriptions are not the same as real caller tests. Make calls from your own phone, listen for pauses during lookups, and check whether callers ask if they are speaking to a machine. Give the agent a clear answer for that question in the prompt.

### Can the agent handle callers with accents or other languages?

ElevenLabs states that its voice agents [support customers in 90+ languages](https://elevenlabs.io/agents/ai-call-agent). The vendor pages used for this article do not publish accent accuracy figures. Test with callers who represent your customer base, and check transcripts for misheard names and services.

### What happens when the agent cannot answer a question?

It should offer a callback from a team member and take the caller's name and number. Add that instruction to the prompt, then make the callback in the promised window. A missed callback damages trust more than an unanswered question does.

### Who sends the booking confirmation text?

The agent handles the voice call, but your webhook sends the confirmation through an SMS provider after the booking is saved. Check that provider's messaging terms and any consent rules for your region before you send automated texts.
