## Where Do Weight Loss Clinics Get Patients Online?

Three channels do most of the work for a local clinic: map and organic search, paid search, and paid social. They answer different moments. Search captures someone who has already decided to act. Social ads reach people who have not searched yet and need a reason to start.

| Channel | What it does | Published cost anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile and local SEO | Shows the clinic in map results | No ad cost; needs reviews and accurate details |
| Google Search ads | Captures active searches | $6.17 CPC, $67.36 cost per lead (Health and Fitness) |
| Facebook and Instagram lead ads | Reaches people before they search | $1.84 CPC, $27.11 cost per lead (Health and Fitness) |

These are category averages across health and fitness, not weight loss clinics specifically. Clinic-only benchmarks are not published in the sources checked.

## How Does Local SEO Help a Weight Loss Clinic?

Google says local results are ranked mainly by relevance, distance and prominence. Prominence is influenced by links to your business and by review count and ratings. Google also states there is no way to pay for a better local ranking.

So the work is practical. Fill in every field of the profile, choose accurate categories and list each service. Keep hours correct. Ask every patient who is comfortable doing so for a review, and reply to each one without repeating private health details. Put the city and service in page titles on your website. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) lists the fields.

## What Are the Rules for Weight Loss Ads?

Rules differ by platform, and they change. Check the live policy before you build creative.

**Meta.** Its health and wellness standards say ads for dietary, health or weight loss products and services must be targeted to people aged 18 or older. They also list statements of inferiority about physical appearance as prohibited. The page shows a change-log entry dated July 22, 2026, and trackers disagree on what moved, so read the current text.

**Google.** The healthcare policy bans hCG products in relation to weight loss, and bans unapproved products marketed as safe or effective for treating a condition. Google also does not allow advertiser-curated audiences, such as remarketing lists and customer match, for sensitive interest categories.

**FTC.** Its "Gut Check" guide for media lists seven weight loss claims that cannot be true. One example is "causes permanent weight loss even after the consumer stops using product". The guide targets products such as supplements and devices, but it shows what regulators treat as red flags. Have a lawyer or compliance adviser review medical claims.

## Which Keywords and Offers Work for a Clinic?

Use service-plus-place searches first, because the searcher is local and ready: for example "medical weight loss [city]" or "weight loss clinic near me". Send each group to a page that names the service, the location, how to book and what a first visit includes. Do not promise an amount of weight lost.

Add negative keywords for jobs, courses, recipes and "free". Google notes that negative keywords do not match close variants, so add plurals yourself. Because Google blocks remarketing lists for sensitive health categories, plan to grow reach through search and Meta lead forms rather than retargeting on Google.

## FAQ

### What is the best way to market a weight loss clinic locally?

Start with a complete Google Business Profile and reviews, then add a small Search campaign on service-plus-city keywords. Add paid social once tracking works.

### How much do weight loss clinic ads cost?

No clinic-specific benchmark is published in the sources used. For Health and Fitness, LocaliQ lists $6.17 per click on Google Search and $1.84 per click on Facebook lead ads.

### Can weight loss ads use before and after photos?

Meta's rules on this have changed, and its page shows a July 22, 2026 update. Read the current Health and Wellness standard before you use transformation images.

### Can I target under-18s?

No. Meta requires weight loss ad targeting to be 18 or older.

### Can I retarget website visitors on Google?

Google does not allow remarketing lists or customer match for sensitive interest categories, which include health. Use search and platform lead forms.
