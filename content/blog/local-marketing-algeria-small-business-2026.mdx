DataReportal counts 47.6 million people and 79.5% internet penetration, with a young median age of 28.6, so a small Algerian business should plan Facebook first and test TikTok and Instagram next.

This guide is for shops, salons, cafes and service firms in Algeria. It uses published figures, labels every assumption, and ends with a break-even formula you can run on your own numbers. ## Who Are You Marketing To in Algeria?

Algeria had 47.6 million people in October 2025, up 1.3%. 76.3% live in urban areas and the median age is 28.6. Internet users number 37.8 million (79.5%), up 4.8%, which leaves 9.74 million offline.

Cellular connections number 55.6 million (117%), and 94.5% are broadband-capable. Social media identities total 27.5 million (57.7%), up 10.2%. A young, urban, mobile audience favours short video and message-based selling.

Facebook is the largest, and Messenger (16.2 million) is a natural route for enquiries. TikTok reaches 80.1% of adults and grew 22.6%, the strongest growth among the big three. Instagram is about half Facebook's size but growing at 16.9%. Facebook's audience is 58.4% male among adults, and TikTok's is 62.9% male, so check your audience mix before targeting women-focused services.

Local Algerian platforms and messaging apps are not covered by DataReportal's article, so this guide gives no figures for them. Payment and delivery rules for online selling are also not covered here.

## How Should Google Ads Language and Location Work in Algeria?

Google's Ads Help says that from September 2026 the campaign-level language setting is being removed for Search campaigns, and Search ads will match on the language of the ads themselves. For searches with a clear language, Google prioritises ads in that language. Customers may search in Arabic or French, so write a separate ad group for each language you serve, and keep each landing page in the same language as its ad.

For location, Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km and recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting as best practice for Search, while presence-only fits when you want to avoid people outside your target area. Set a radius around your premises, then read the report to see which areas convert.

No Algeria-specific click costs were found in the sources checked, so every cost figure below is an assumption.

## How Should You Split a Small Budget?

Start with Facebook and a message button, add Google Search for service plus town, and run TikTok as a small test of about 10 to 15% of paid spend (a suggestion, not a platform rule). Compare cost per booked appointment after four to six weeks.

## What Does a Test Budget Return? The Math

These rows are **assumptions** for illustration, in US dollars. They are not Algeria benchmarks. Replace each with your own data after a month.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget (assumption) | $300 | $300 | $300 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | $0.40 | $0.25 | $0.15 |
| Clicks | 750 | 1,200 | 2,000 |
| Click to enquiry rate (assumption) | 3% | 5% | 7% |
| Enquiries | 22 | 60 | 140 |
| Enquiry to booking rate (assumption) | 30% | 40% | 50% |
| Bookings | 6.8 | 24 | 70 |
| Ad cost per booking | $44 | $12 | $4 |
| First-visit value (assumption) | $12 | $12 | $12 |
| Revenue minus ad spend | -$219 | -$12 | +$540 |

Counts use unrounded values. Revenue is not profit: staff, product and rent come out of it. A first visit often does not pay back by itself, which is why repeat visits matter.

Your break-even click price is the first-visit value times the enquiry rate times the booking rate. At $12, 5% and 40%, that is 12 x 0.05 x 0.40 = $0.24 per click. If your clicks cost more on average, the first visit loses money and you need repeat bookings to justify it. The biggest lever is usually the booking rate: answer messages and calls within minutes.

## FAQ

### Which platform is biggest in Algeria?
Facebook, with 27.5 million in ad tools, ahead of YouTube at 25.4 million and TikTok at 24.8 million adults, in late 2025.

### How many Algerians are online?
37.8 million, 79.5% of the population, in October 2025.

### Is Instagram worth using?
Yes as a test: it reaches 13.5 million and grew 16.9%, but it is about half the size of Facebook.

### Do I need separate ads in Arabic and French?
Test both and let your own results decide. Keep each landing page in the language of the ad.
