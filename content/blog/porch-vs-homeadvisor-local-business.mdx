## How do Porch contractor leads work?

According to a secondary review published by Townsquare Interactive, Porch lets homeowners enter a timeline and budget for a project. Once the request is submitted, several pros in the area are alerted, so contractors compete to reach the homeowner first ([K2 Radio review of Porch Pro](https://k2radio.com/porch-pro-reviews/), secondary, March 2022).

Porch Group's corporate site describes Porch as a provider of software and services for several home service industries, including contractors, through both business-to-business and consumer offerings ([Porch Group](https://porchgroup.com/), primary). The corporate homepage does not explain how lead billing works. Treat the lead mechanics as the secondary account's description until the terms are visible inside your own account.

## Is there a published price for Porch leads?

No price per lead appears in the primary source reviewed for this article. The Porch Group site is a corporate homepage that lists companies, industries and news. It does not list pro lead prices ([Porch Group](https://porchgroup.com/), primary).

The secondary review calls Porch similar to other pay-per-lead companies, but it does not quote a per-lead cost ([K2 Radio review of Porch Pro](https://k2radio.com/porch-pro-reviews/), secondary). Any figure found elsewhere should be checked against the price shown inside your own account for your trade and service area before money is loaded. A price that appears for one trade or zip code may not apply to yours.

## Do contractors report consistent results from Porch?

The secondary review reports that Porch lead generation is portrayed as inconsistent in many Porch Pro reviews. Its author concludes that Porch is "not really" worth it for contractors ([K2 Radio review of Porch Pro](https://k2radio.com/porch-pro-reviews/), secondary). That verdict is one writer's opinion, not a measured result, and no sample size or methodology is given.

The same review notes a structural issue. Porch has a partnership with Lowe's, and Lowe's will try to sell the homeowner its own in-home services. Whether this affects your leads depends on your trade and region, so it belongs in the test plan. ## How do you track whether a lead platform pays back?

Use this checklist before adding budget to any lead marketplace:

1. Record the price shown for each lead, by trade and zip code.
2. Log the time from lead arrival to your first call or message.
3. Count homeowners reached, quotes sent and jobs signed.
4. Note every lead that was unusable, and request any credit the platform's written terms allow.
5. Divide net spend by signed jobs to get cost per signed job.

Keep the log in the same spreadsheet or job system used for quotes. [Jobber vs ServiceTitan for small business](/blog/jobber-vs-servicetitan-small-business-local-business) covers how field-service software records leads and jobs, which makes the log easier to maintain.

## What does cost per signed job look like in practice?

Every input below is an assumption for illustration. None is a Porch or HomeAdvisor price, and none describes any platform's credit rules.

Assumption: $500 is spent in one month on one platform. The average lead costs $50, so 10 leads arrive. Two leads are credited back at $50 each, leaving $400 net. The contractor reaches 4 homeowners, sends 2 quotes and signs 1 job worth $1,200. Materials and labor for that job total $700.

Cost per signed job is $400 net ($400 divided by 1 job). Profit on the job is $1,200 minus $700 minus $400, which equals $100.

Change one input at a time. If the same $400 net spend produces 2 signed jobs, cost per signed job falls to $200 and total profit across both jobs rises to $600. Win rate and reach usually move the result more than the sticker price does, so track those numbers alongside spend.

## FAQ

### Does Porch publish its lead prices?

Not in the primary source reviewed for this article. The Porch Group site is a corporate homepage and does not list pro lead prices ([Porch Group](https://porchgroup.com/), primary). Check the price shown in your own account before funding it.

### Are Porch leads exclusive to one contractor?

The secondary review says multiple pros in the area are alerted when a homeowner submits a request, so contractors compete to reach that homeowner first ([K2 Radio review of Porch Pro](https://k2radio.com/porch-pro-reviews/), secondary). Plan on sharing each request with other pros.

### Is Porch worth it for contractors?

That depends on your own cost per signed job. The secondary review concludes "not really," but that is one writer's verdict rather than a measured result ([K2 Radio review of Porch Pro](https://k2radio.com/porch-pro-reviews/), secondary). Test with a capped budget and log every lead.

### Which industries does Porch Group serve?
