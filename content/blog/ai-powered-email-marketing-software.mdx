For most local businesses, the best AI-powered email marketing software is the one that fits your size and workflow: Mailchimp or MailerLite for simple newsletters on a small budget, Brevo for email plus SMS, ActiveCampaign for deeper automation, and Klaviyo if you sell products online. All now include AI features for drafting copy and subject lines, but the value comes from automation and your list, not the AI label.

Below is a plain comparison, what AI features are actually useful, how to choose, and how to set up your first campaigns. Pricing and features change often, so confirm current plans on each vendor's site.

## What "AI-powered" usually means in email tools

Most platforms include some combination of:

- **Content generation:** draft subject lines, preview text and email bodies from a short prompt.
- **Send-time optimisation:** choose a delivery time per recipient based on past behaviour.
- **Predictive segments:** groups such as likely to buy or at risk of churning, in platforms with enough data.
- **Smart automation suggestions:** prebuilt journeys such as welcome, abandoned cart and win-back.
- **Reporting summaries:** plain-language readouts of campaign results.

For small lists, content generation and ready-made automations are the features that save you time. Predictive features often need thousands of subscribers or purchase history to work well.

Treat these prices as ballparks. Plans vary by country, contact count and add-ons, and many offer free trials.

## How to choose

Use this checklist:

- **List size and growth:** pricing usually rises with contacts. Estimate your list in a year.
- **Channels:** do you need SMS or only email?
- **Integrations:** does it connect with your booking system, POS or website (Square, Shopify, WordPress, Mindbody, Fresha)?
- **Automation complexity:** do you need simple welcome emails or multi-step journeys with conditions?
- **Ease of use:** can you or your staff build an email in 20 minutes?
- **Deliverability and support:** does the platform authenticate your domain and offer help when emails go astray?
- **Data ownership and compliance:** can you export contacts, and does it support consent records and unsubscribes under CAN-SPAM, GDPR or similar?

## Which tool for which business

- **Coffee shops and cafes:** simple newsletters plus a welcome and birthday flow. MailerLite, Mailchimp or Brevo are usually plenty. See [coffee shop email marketing with Mailchimp](/blog/coffee-shop-email-marketing-with-mailchimp).
- **Salons and groomers:** reminders and rebooking prompts, ideally connected to your booking tool. Brevo or Mailchimp, or the email features built into your booking software.
- **Fitness studios:** trial follow-ups, lapsed member emails and event promotions, where a CRM and automation matter. ActiveCampaign or the studio platform's built-in tools.
- **Product sellers:** abandoned cart and post-purchase flows, where Klaviyo is a common choice.

## Setting up your first AI-assisted campaign

### Step 1: Clean your list

Remove invalid addresses and anyone who has not given consent. Never buy lists.

### Step 2: Authenticate your domain

Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC records with your domain provider using your email platform's instructions. This improves deliverability, and some inbox providers now require it for bulk senders.

### Step 3: Create a template

Use a simple, mobile-friendly layout: logo, one image, short text, one button. Avoid heavy design that breaks on phones.

### Step 4: Draft with AI

Give the assistant context: your business, your customer, the offer and the tone. Ask for several subject lines. Edit the output for accuracy and personality.

### Step 5: Test and send

Send test emails to yourself on both phone and desktop. Check links, images, spelling and unsubscribe. Schedule at a time your customers read, such as mid-morning or evening, then refine using the platform's reports.

### Step 6: Add automation

Build a welcome series, a post-visit follow-up, and a win-back email. The [email automation sequences guide](/blog/email-automation-sequences) shows structures that fit local businesses.

## Using AI responsibly

- **Check facts.** AI can invent details, prices or claims. Verify everything before sending.
- **Protect customer data.** Avoid pasting customer lists into external AI tools. Use the platform's built-in features or anonymised information.
- **Avoid deceptive subject lines.** They harm trust and deliverability.
- **Keep it human.** Add real photos, names of staff and local references.
- **Mind consent.** Only email people who opted in, and honour opt-outs promptly.

## Measuring results

Watch:

- **Click rate and conversions**, which are more reliable than open rate because some mail apps pre-load images and inflate opens.
- **Bookings, visits or orders** traced to emails.
- **Unsubscribe and spam complaint rates**, to catch over-sending.
- **List growth**, to see whether sign-up efforts work.

Review monthly and make one change at a time, such as testing two subject lines.

## Questions to ask before you pay

- What happens to my price if my list doubles?
- Can I export my contacts and consent records at any time?
- Does the free plan limit automations, sending volume or branding?
- Will it connect to my booking system or point-of-sale without extra fees?
- How quickly does support reply?

Taking a free trial and building one real welcome email will tell you more than any feature table.

## When to switch tools

Stay with your first platform until something specific holds you back: a missing integration, deliverability trouble, or pricing that no longer fits your list size. Switching too early wastes weeks rebuilding templates and automations, and the grass is rarely greener. When you do move, export contacts and consent records first, rebuild only the automations that earn their keep, and warm up sending gradually so inbox providers learn to trust the new setup.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need AI features in my email software?**
A: Not strictly. Automation, good deliverability and easy list management matter more. AI writing helpers are a useful bonus for saving time.

**Q: What is the cheapest option?**
A: Mailchimp, MailerLite, Brevo and HubSpot all offer free tiers with limits. Free plans can change, so check current terms.

**Q: Is email still worth it compared with social media?**
A: Yes for many local businesses, because you own the list and reach people directly. Use social media to grow that list.

**Q: Can I switch platforms later?**
A: Yes, most tools let you export contacts. Keep clean records of consent and unsubscribed contacts before migrating.
