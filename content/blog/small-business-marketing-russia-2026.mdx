A small business in Russia reaches local customers through Yandex (search and Maps), VK (social and ads) and the Max messenger, with Telegram and WhatsApp now mostly blocked and reachable only by workarounds. Start with a free Yandex Business listing and a VK community, add Yandex Direct for search, and mark every online ad as the law requires. Instagram and Facebook ads are illegal to buy or sell.

This guide covers businesses operating inside Russia: cafes, salons, studios and shops. It is a marketing overview, not legal advice. Sanctions and legal items that could not be sourced are marked unverified. Figures were checked on 2026-10-24.

## Which Platforms Should a Russian Small Business Use?

Pick by what you can reach legally and cheaply. The table lists the main options.

| Platform | Job | Status in 2026 | Verified data |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Yandex Search and Maps | Local discovery | Main search engine | About 71% of search queries, StatCounter June 2026 (secondary) |
| VK (social, VK Ads) | Community, paid social | Active | 62.7M daily and 93.4M monthly users, Q1 2026 (VK, Mediascope) |
| Max | Messenger | Growing | 86.2M monthly audience, June 2026 (Mediascope via Meduza) |
| Telegram | Channels, messaging | Almost completely blocked | 75.7M monthly audience, June 2026, falling (Meduza) |
| WhatsApp | Messaging | Almost completely blocked | 63.5M monthly audience, June 2026 |
| Instagram, Facebook | Social | Blocked, Meta listed as extremist | Do not advertise |

Meduza, citing Kommersant and Mediascope, reports that WhatsApp and Telegram "remain almost completely blocked" even though many people still reach them through workarounds. A channel that depends on a workaround can disappear overnight, so keep a second contact route such as VK, Max or SMS.

## What Is Legal and What Is Not?

Three rules matter for a small business.

1. **Ads on banned networks are prohibited.** A law signed in April 2025 bans advertising on the sites of organizations designated "undesirable" or "extremist", with Instagram and Facebook given as examples. It took effect on September 1 and covers both advertisers and distributors (Novaya Gazeta). Fines run up to 2,500 rubles for individuals, 20,000 for officials and 500,000 for organizations.
2. **Online ads must be marked.** Under Article 18.1 of the advertising law, in force since September 2022, online ads carry the word for "advertising", the advertiser's name or a link to details, and a token (erid) from an advertising data operator (Gorodissky, Sostav). Unmarked ads draw administrative fines. Sostav lists up to 200,000 rubles for sole traders and 500,000 for companies; check the current Code of Administrative Offences.
3. **Costs of unmarked ads are not deductible.** Sostav reports a law from 2024 effective January 1, 2025 on this point.

Platforms and agencies usually handle the token for you in VK Ads and Yandex Direct. Confirm that the ad cabinet shows an erid before you pay.

## Do Sanctions Affect a Marketing Business?

For a business registered and paid inside Russia, local law applies. If you are a US, UK or EU company or person, sanctions and export rules can apply to services you provide or payments you receive. This guide found no verified OFAC determination that covers marketing services; the only OFAC item opened concerned petroleum services.

That does not mean no rule exists, so this point is **unverified**. Check the OFAC list and take legal advice before contracting with Russian platforms, banks or clients.

## How Do You Get Found on Yandex Maps?

Add and complete a Yandex Business listing, which is free. Third-party guides say to add the organization in Yandex Business rather than directly in Maps, and that Yandex may ask you to confirm you operate at the address. Shops without a physical office may appear in search only.

Fill in these fields:

- Name, address, categories and opening hours.
- Phone, website and booking link.
- Real photos of the interior and products.
- Replies to every review.

The same logic as a Google profile applies. Yandex's official help was not opened, so check current steps in the Yandex Business account.

## How Does Yandex Direct Work and What Does It Cost?

Yandex Direct is the pay-per-click platform for Yandex search and partner sites. A Russian blog citing Yandex's help says the minimum daily budget is 300 rubles and the minimum top-up is 1,200 rubles with VAT (secondary; the official help did not load). The same source notes that the technical minimum is not a statistically useful budget and suggests enough for roughly 10 target conversions a week.

No verified click prices per niche were found. Per-click price ranges seen in other guides could not be verified, so none are given here. Run a small test and read your own cost per call.

## How Do You Use VK for a Local Business?

VK is the largest social network in Russia: VK's Q1 2026 release says it reached 90% of the Russian internet audience monthly and 57% daily, with 82.6 million monthly users for VK Video (Mediascope, ages 12+). Create a business community, post offers and photos, and use VK Ads to show them to people near you.

Practical steps:

- Set a local geo target and test one offer at a time.
- Add a lead form or booking link.
- Put your VK community on receipts and packaging.
- Mark every ad and get the erid.

VK reported Q2 2026 advertising revenue of 26.7 billion rubles, up 14% year on year (secondary reports of VK results). That signals the platform is still funded by advertisers, not that ads will work for you.

## What About Max, Telegram and Messaging?

Max has overtaken Telegram in audience by Mediascope's June 2026 figures (86.2 million versus 75.7 million). Because Telegram is largely blocked, treat it as an extra channel, not your only customer list.

Collect phone numbers and consent for SMS or Max, and keep a plain customer list in your own CRM. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) helps you decide how much of your budget should go to owned channels.

## How Do You Take Payments?

Russian cards and the Faster Payments System (SBP) are the main routes. A Russian business article reports SBP commissions of about 0.2 to 0.7% depending on the merchant category (secondary; page returned 403, figures from a search summary). Compare with your bank's card acquiring tariff before choosing.

Do not rely on Visa, Mastercard or PayPal for Russian customers. Whether any foreign card works depends on the bank and is not verified here.

## FAQ

### What is the best marketing for a small business in Russia?

A free Yandex Business listing, a VK community and a tested Yandex Direct campaign, with Max or SMS for reminders.

### Is Telegram still usable for business in Russia?

It is described as almost completely blocked, with a 75.7 million monthly audience in June 2026 that is declining. Use it as a secondary channel.

### Can I advertise on Instagram in Russia?

No. A law effective September 1, 2025 bans advertising on resources of organizations designated extremist or undesirable, with fines up to 500,000 rubles for organizations.

### Do I need to mark ads?

Yes, online ads aimed at Russia must carry the advertising label, advertiser information and an erid token.

### How much does Yandex Direct cost?

The minimum daily budget is reported as 300 rubles, but no verified per-click prices were found. Test with a small budget.

### Does Google Ads work in Russia?

This guide could not verify the current status, so it is left out.
