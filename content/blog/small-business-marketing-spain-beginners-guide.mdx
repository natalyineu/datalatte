A small business in Spain should start with a complete Google Business Profile, a WhatsApp Business account and one social channel, then add paid ads once enquiries are tracked. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 46.1 million internet users in Spain (96.4% of the population), so almost every customer is reachable online. The question is which channel suits your business.

This guide uses published audience data and platform help pages. It does not invent Spanish ad prices: neither Google nor Meta publishes a Spain CPC for small businesses, so budget examples are labelled assumptions.

## How Big Is the Spanish Online Audience?

Spain had a population of 47.9 million in October 2025, according to DataReportal's Digital 2026 report published on 5 November 2025. The same report counts 56.1 million mobile connections and 39.0 million social media user identities, equal to 81.4% of the population.

DataReportal's ad reach figures come from platforms' own advertising tools. They are not monthly active user counts, and one person may appear on several platforms. Use them as a size guide, not a customer count.

## Which Channels Reach the Most People in Spain?

Ad reach by platform in late 2025, per DataReportal:

| Platform | Ad reach in Spain | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | 39.0 million | 81.4% |
| Instagram | 26.4 million | 55.0% |
| TikTok (adults 18+) | 20.9 million | 52.0% of adults |
| Facebook | 20.3 million | 42.3% |

Instagram grew from 24.8 million in early 2025 to 26.4 million. Facebook was flat at about 20 million (20.4 million, then 20.3 million). DataReportal's page did not give a WhatsApp figure in the sections reviewed, so this guide does not quote one.

Reach is not the same as fit. A hair salon and a legal adviser reach different people on the same platform. Pick the channel where your customers already look for you, then test.

## Why Start With Google Business Profile?

Local searches turn into visits through Google Maps and the local results, so your profile is the base for any local business. Fill in categories, services, photos and hours first. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) and [categories guide](/blog/google-business-profile-categories-guide) cover the steps, and the [local pack guide](/blog/how-to-rank-in-google-maps-local-pack) explains ranking.

Opening hours deserve care in Spain, where some businesses close in the middle of the day. Google's help pages describe three tools: main hours, special hours for holidays or temporary changes (up to 6 days in a row) and "more hours" for different features, such as delivery. BrightLocal reports you can add a second set of hours for the same day with the plus sign, which suits a midday break. That is a secondary source, so check it in your own profile. Mark local fiestas and August closures with special hours.

## How Should You Use WhatsApp Business?

WhatsApp Business is a separate app with tools for small businesses. WhatsApp's own onboarding guide suggests this order:

1. Complete the business profile: description, address, hours, email and website.
2. Write a greeting and an away message that states when you will reply.
3. Save at least five quick replies, such as prices, hours and location.
4. Add a catalog starting with your top 5 to 10 items or services.
5. Use labels such as New inquiry, Pending info and Follow up.

Put the number on your website, Instagram bio and Google profile. We cover the channel in more detail in our [WhatsApp Business marketing guide](/blog/whatsapp-business-marketing-local-business). Check consent and data rules before sending promotions, because EU privacy law applies in Spain.

## What Language and Tone Should You Use?

Write in Spanish for a Spanish audience. In regions with a second official language, such as Catalonia, ask your own customers which they prefer, and test both in ads before committing. Do not assume. No source reviewed here measures which language converts better, so treat any such claim as unproven.

## How Much Should a Small Business Spend? A Worked Example

Spanish ad costs are not published in a form we could verify, so this split is a planning **assumption**, not a benchmark:

| Item (assumption) | Monthly share | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Ads for local searches | 40% | 120 EUR |
| Meta Ads on Instagram and Facebook | 30% | 90 EUR |
| Photos and video content | 20% | 60 EUR |
| Tools (email, scheduling) | 10% | 30 EUR |
| Total | 100% | 300 EUR |

At this size, run one Search campaign on two or three local keywords and one Instagram campaign, and judge both by enquiries, not clicks. Track how many WhatsApp chats, calls and bookings each source produced. Raise the budget only for the channel that wins. ## Does Spain Need Different Marketing by Region?

Yes in practice, though no study reviewed here quantifies it. Madrid, Barcelona, Andalusia, the Basque Country and tourist coasts differ in competition, language and seasonality. A beach town with foreign visitors may need English listings and reviews on top of Spanish. A neighbourhood business may gain more from local content and partnerships.

Test with small budgets per city before scaling. Use local fiestas for content ideas, but confirm dates each year, since they vary by town.

## FAQ

### What is the first thing a Spanish small business should do online?

Complete the Google Business Profile with correct hours, then set up WhatsApp Business. Both are free.

### Is Instagram or Facebook better in Spain?

DataReportal shows Instagram with larger ad reach (26.4 million versus 20.3 million) in late 2025. Fit still depends on who your customers are.

### How much do Google Ads cost in Spain?

Neither Google nor DataReportal publishes a Spain CPC for small business categories. Use Keyword Planner for your city and keywords, then test with a small budget.

### Do I need a website?

Not to start, if your Google profile and WhatsApp are complete. A simple site helps with trust and tracking, and ranks for searches beyond your profile.

### Should I post in Catalan or Basque?

Ask your own customers and test. No source reviewed here proves one language converts better.
