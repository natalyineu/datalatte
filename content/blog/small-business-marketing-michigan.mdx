Fix your Google Business Profile first, run a small Google Search test aimed at a few miles around your shop, and track calls and bookings. Visitor-facing businesses can also use Michigan's tourism flow.

## What Does the Michigan Market Look Like Right Now?

Flat. BLS lists Michigan's September 2026 unemployment rate at 5.0% (preliminary, seasonally adjusted) with 4,487.4 thousand nonfarm jobs, unchanged over 12 months. Leisure and hospitality jobs number 430.4 thousand, up 0.4%, while trade, transportation and utilities, at 799.4 thousand, fell 0.5%.

The Census Bureau estimates Michigan's population at 10,127,884 in 2025, up 27,922 (0.28%) from 2024. Deaths exceeded births by 4,998, so the growth came from international migration of 30,706 and domestic migration of 1,796.

For a business owner this means a stable customer base that is not growing fast. Winning share, not riding growth, is the task, and a repeat-customer offer usually beats a broad awareness push.

## What Does Tourism Add for Michigan Businesses?

A second customer base in some places. A Tourism Economics study released by Travel Michigan on 29 July 2025 reported 131.2 million visitors in 2024 who spent $30.7 billion, up 4.9% from $29.3 billion in 2023. It estimated 351,292 jobs supported, about one in 17 Michigan jobs, including 96,000 in food and beverage.

The release does not break visitors down by season, region or Canadian share, so no source here says which towns benefit most. A 2025 report had not been found when this guide was written. If your shop sits on a visitor route, check your own sales by month before planning a seasonal budget.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local in Michigan?

Set a radius around the business and leave out places you cannot serve. Google's help page says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km. It recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting as the best practice on Search, and says "Presence" only suits cases where you want to exclude people who are elsewhere but interested in your offer.

Michigan has dense metro areas and long rural distances. A shop in a metro area might test a 2 to 4 mile radius, which is an assumption to check against customer addresses. In northern Michigan, customers may drive much farther, so a wider radius may fit.

Add negative keywords weekly from the Search terms report: jobs, free, DIY and services you do not offer. Google notes that negative keywords do not match close variants, so add plurals and synonyms yourself.

## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Michigan?

Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. It also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Fill every field: categories, hours, services, service area and website. Upload real photos and refresh them each season. Reply to every review and keep your name, address and phone identical elsewhere. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes through each field.

## Can You Offer a Discount for a Google Review?

No. Google's contributed content policy says merchants may not offer incentives, such as payment, discounts, free goods or services, in exchange for posting a review. You may ask customers for a review. You may not reward them for one. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for ways to ask.

## Where Can Michigan Owners Get Free Advice?

The Michigan SBDC says it provides consulting, business education and market research to help small businesses launch, grow, transition and innovate, at no cost thanks to federal and state funding and local hosts. Its site lists ten offices across the state.

Search results describe the lead center as hosted by Grand Valley State University and note that regional hosts include Western Michigan University, Eastern Michigan University and Michigan Technological University. Those host details are from search summaries, so confirm your nearest office on the SBDC site.

## What Mistakes Do Michigan Owners Make?

These are recommendations, not study findings.

- Targeting the whole state when customers come from a few miles away.
- Cutting spend to zero in a slow month. Compare cost per booking monthly and adjust instead.
- Not tracking calls. Set a minimum call length that counts, so wrong numbers do not inflate results.
- Spreading across four channels before one works. See our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide).
