## Which Social Platform Is Biggest in Jamaica?

TikTok, by the numbers DataReportal publishes. Its Digital 2026 report lists a TikTok ad reach of 1.81 million people aged 18 and over, 82.6% of adults. Instagram follows at 1.10 million (38.7% of the population), then LinkedIn members at 960 thousand and Facebook at 928 thousand (32.7%).

TikTok's share is measured against adults and the others against the whole population, so the percentages are not directly comparable. Instagram's reach is about 19% larger than Facebook's (1.10 divided by 0.928).

## How Should a Jamaican Business Split Its Effort?

Lead with the platform where your customers already scroll, and make Instagram the booking point. Barbers, salons, caterers and cafes can post finished work and a clear way to book by direct message. TikTok suits short clips of the same work.

Facebook still reaches 928 thousand people and is useful for events and community groups. Use it for an older or wider audience and for local pages, not as your only presence. Our barbershop Instagram guide covers the content side.

## What Do We Know About Small Businesses in Jamaica?

The Planning Institute of Jamaica's Economic and Social Survey 2023, reported by the Jamaica Information Service in January 2025, put micro, small and medium enterprise sales at J$207.4 billion in 2023, up 1.2% on 2022. It used own-account workers as a proxy for the sector and put them at 30.8% of the labour force.

Counts of MSMEs and their share of jobs vary by source and method, so this guide quotes none. The point for a shop owner is that many Jamaicans are self-employed, so your competitors are often small and active on the same apps.

## Can a Jamaican Business Run Google Ads?

Yes. Google's list of billing countries includes Jamaica. In that list the account currency and payment method columns read "All", but Google says the options you see depend on your billing country, currency and payment setting, and that prepay is not offered in many countries.

Open the billing page in your own account to see what is available. No source checked publishes Jamaican click prices, and DataReportal does not report Google ad reach for the country.

Search ads suit businesses with a clear need people type into Google, such as a plumber or a tyre shop. A salon may do better on Instagram. Compare both with a small budget and your own cost per booking. See the [Google Ads cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-google-ads-cost-for-small-businesses-real-2026-pricing-breakdown).

## What Does a First Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for a barbershop that takes bookings through Instagram messages. They show the method, not Jamaican results. Currency is "units" of Jamaican dollars.

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Gross profit per new regular client over a year (assumption) | 12,000 units |
| Enquiries that become regulars (assumption) | 20% |
| Most you can pay per enquiry and still break even | 12,000 x 20% = 2,400 units |
| Test budget for one month (assumption) | 24,000 units |
| Enquiries needed to break even | 24,000 / 2,400 = 10 enquiries |

If the video ads or boosted posts bring ten booking enquiries for 24,000 units, the month broke even on a one-year view. If a quarter of enquiries become regulars, results improve. Track the source of each booking by asking one question at the chair.

## How Do You Get Found on Google Maps in Jamaica?

Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. It also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Fill in every field, use real photos, reply to every review and keep the name, address and phone number identical everywhere. Add your Instagram handle to the profile so people who find you on Maps can see your work. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes through each field.

Do not offer a discount or a free item in return for a review. Google's contributed content policy says merchants must not offer incentives, such as payment, discounts, free goods or services, in exchange for posting a review. Asking is fine. Rewarding is not. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for ways to ask.

## What Mistakes Do Jamaican Small Businesses Make?

These are recommendations, not study findings.

- Treating Facebook as the main channel when Instagram and TikTok reach more people.
- Posting without a booking path. Every post needs a message button or link.
- Counting likes instead of bookings.
- Offering discounts for Google reviews, which the policy forbids.

## FAQ

### Is TikTok bigger than Facebook in Jamaica?

By ad reach, yes. DataReportal lists TikTok at 1.81 million adults and Facebook at 928 thousand people, though the two use different bases.

### Is Google Ads available in Jamaica?

Jamaica appears in Google's list of billing countries. No source checked publishes Jamaican click prices, so run a small test.

### How many Jamaicans use the internet?

DataReportal reports 2.54 million internet users, 89.5% of the population, in October 2025.

### Are MSMEs important to the Jamaican economy?

The Jamaica Information Service reported MSME sales of J$207.4 billion in 2023 and used own-account workers, 30.8% of the labour force, as a proxy for employment.

### Can I give a discount for a Google review?

No. Google's policy says merchants may not offer payment, discounts or free goods in exchange for a review.
