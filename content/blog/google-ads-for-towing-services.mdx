Towing has no row in the LocaliQ table. A driver stuck on a roadside searches with urgency, which can change both click price and conversion rate, so treat the proxy cautiously.

## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Tow Company?

Google does not publish a towing price. The all-industry 2026 averages from LocaliQ are $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead, and the automotive category sits below both. Competition in your city may push you either way, and the only reliable number is the cost per call from your own account.

Google's budget rules apply: spend can exceed the average daily budget by up to 2 times on a given day, and monthly charges are capped at 30.4 times the daily budget. For an emergency service where demand spikes at night or in bad weather, that overdelivery matters. Check how our [Google Ads cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-google-ads-cost-for-small-businesses-real-2026-pricing-breakdown) calculates a budget before you pick one.

## Should You Use Search Ads or Local Services Ads?

Both are options, and many service companies test them side by side. Google's list of Local Services Ads categories includes Towing, and the page gives that entry no country or state limit. Google also notes that Local Services Ads are available only for certain categories and places, so confirm eligibility for your location in your own account before planning around it.

| | Search ads | Local Services Ads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How people find you | Keywords they type | Service category and location |
| What you control | Keywords, ad text, bids, schedule | Profile, service areas, budget |
| Cost model | Pay per click | Differs from search, see our guide |
| Good for | Control over terms and landing pages | Appearing in lead-focused placements |

Our [Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-services-ads-complete-guide) explains how that model works. The Google Verified badge note on Google's page says it is currently unavailable for the auto, beauty and dining categories, and the page does not say whether towing falls under auto.

## How Do You Make Ads Easy to Call?

Add call assets. Google says clicks on a call asset cost the same as a standard headline click, and you can count calls as conversions by turning on call reporting (Search Network only). On desktop, a Call us button appears under the ad. You can set numbers to appear only when your business can accept calls, and schedule the asset for certain days and hours.

This matters for towing. A missed call from a stranded driver is a lost job and a paid click. If no dispatcher answers at night, either hire answering cover or schedule the asset so the number does not show when nobody picks up. Our [call-only campaigns guide for service businesses](/blog/google-ads-call-only-campaigns-service-businesses) goes deeper on call-driven campaigns.

## How Should You Set the Service Area?

Use location targeting that matches where you can dispatch a truck. Google says broad geo targeting ("Presence or interest") is a best practice on Search, and that "Presence" limits reach to people physically in the location. For towing, test Presence if you see clicks from people searching about a place but located far away. Radius targeting must be at least 1 km, and a very small radius may show ads only intermittently or not at all.

Draw the area around the distance a truck can reach quickly, not around the whole metro. Then add negative keywords for jobs, courses and unrelated vehicle services. Our [guide to negative keywords](/blog/google-ads-negative-keywords-stop-wasting-budget) lists match types and shows how to read the Search terms report.

## FAQ

### How much do Google Ads cost for a towing company?
No source publishes a towing figure. LocaliQ's 2026 automotive repair category averages $4.35 per click and $29.96 per lead, which can serve as a rough proxy.

### Are Local Services Ads available for towing?
Google's category list includes Towing, with no location note on that entry. Confirm eligibility in your account for your area.

### Do call assets cost extra?
Google says clicks on call assets cost the same as headline clicks, a standard CPC.

### Can I stop ads when nobody answers the phone?
Yes. You can set numbers to appear only when the business can accept calls and schedule the call asset by days and hours.

### What radius should I target?
Google requires at least 1 km. Pick a distance your trucks can reach quickly, and check whether Presence targeting reduces clicks from outside the area.
