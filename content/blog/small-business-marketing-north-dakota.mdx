## How Big Is the North Dakota Market?

North Dakota had a record 796,568 residents on 1 July 2024, according to the Census Bureau estimate published by the governor's office. Cass County, which includes Fargo, passed 200,000 for the first time at 200,945. Burleigh County, which includes Bismarck, reached 103,107. The two counties made up more than 58% of the state's estimated growth.

For your budget, that means volume sits in a few places. Fargo and Bismarck support larger ad budgets and more competitors. Smaller towns have fewer searches, so a single well-kept map listing often matters more than ads.

## Where Should a North Dakota Business Start: Maps or Ads?

Start with Google Business Profile. Google says local ranking rests on relevance, distance and prominence, and that you cannot pay for a better local ranking. Prominence depends partly on review count and ratings. So the free steps come first:

- Verify the profile and keep the address, hours, category and attributes complete.
- Add photos and videos of what you actually offer.
- Reply to reviews, good and bad.

Google states that businesses with complete and accurate information are more likely to appear in local results. In a small town where few competitors manage their profiles, this alone can separate you from the next listing. See [how coffee shops win Google Maps](/blog/coffee-shops-dominate-google-maps) for the full checklist.

## How Should You Target Ads Outside the Big Cities?

Google recommends radius targeting for a business whose customers live within a set distance of it, with a minimum radius of 1 km. It also lists "Presence or Interest" as a best practice on Search, which includes people who show interest in your area, such as a traveller researching a trip. Choose "Presence" only to reach people physically in the area.

For a shop in Dickinson or Grand Forks, a radius around the shop is a sensible first test. Widen it only if search terms show enough demand. Setup steps are in our [Google Ads setup guide](/blog/how-to-set-up-google-ads-for-your-small-business-in-2026-step-by-step-with-screenshots).

## Is Meta Advertising Worth It in North Dakota?

LocaliQ's 2026 Facebook benchmarks for the US: traffic campaigns average $0.60 per click with a 1.93% CTR, and lead campaigns average $1.80 per click, an 8.54% conversion rate and $27.39 per lead. Beauty and Personal Care has the highest traffic CTR at 2.73%. These are national figures; no North Dakota or CPM average was confirmed.

Meta suits awareness and retargeting, while Google captures people already searching. See [Meta Ads for local businesses](/blog/local-business-meta-ads).

## What About the Bakken and Oil Workers?

Western North Dakota has a workforce that moves between job sites, and some businesses there sell to them. Current workforce, rig or housing figures for the Bakken could not be verified for this update, so this page gives no numbers. If you serve that market, check Search terms and call tracking for demand instead of assuming it, and avoid building a plan on 2012-era boom assumptions.

## The Math: What Does $500 a Month Buy?

The CPCs and conversion rates are the LocaliQ US averages above. The budget and the two business types are assumptions.

- **Assumption:** $500 a month on Google search ads.
- **Assumption:** a cafe uses the Restaurants and Food averages; a studio uses Health and Fitness.

| | CPC | Clicks from $500 | Conversion rate | Leads |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cafe (assumption) | $2.05 | about 244 | 8.05% | about 20 |
| Studio (assumption) | $6.17 | about 81 | 6.94% | about 6 |

A "lead" here means whatever the benchmark counts as a conversion, such as a call or form. Decide what a lead is worth to you before you spend.

## How Should You Plan Around the Seasons?

North Dakota winters change what people want, but this page found no published North Dakota search-demand data by month. Look at your own Google Business Profile insights and Google Ads search terms from last year, then move budget to the months your own data shows. Keep a baseline budget in slow months so your account keeps its history.

## FAQ

### How much should a North Dakota small business spend on Google Ads?

No source gives a North Dakota figure. Use the table above: $500 a month buys about 244 clicks at a $2.05 CPC and about 81 at $6.17. Pick a budget that produces enough clicks to learn from.

### Is Google Business Profile free?

Yes to set up and manage. Google says you cannot pay for a better local ranking.

### Are ads cheaper in North Dakota than the national average?

Unproven. LocaliQ's figures are US averages with no state split, so test in your own account.

### Does Meta work for local businesses in North Dakota?

It can for awareness and retargeting. LocaliQ's US lead campaign average is $27.39 per lead, but there is no North Dakota figure.

### Should I market differently in Fargo and smaller towns?

Likely yes, since Cass County alone has over 200,000 residents while smaller towns have far fewer searches. Use a bigger radius and budget in cities, and put more effort into your map listing in small towns.
