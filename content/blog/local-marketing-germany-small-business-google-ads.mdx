## Why Does Google Matter So Much in Germany?

StatCounter's September 2026 data for Germany: Google 87.84%, Bing 6.5%, Yahoo 1.7%, Yandex 1.56%, DuckDuckGo 1.07% and Ecosia 1.03%. Almost nine in ten searches go through Google, so the Business Profile and Search ads reach most local demand.

Google says creating a Business Profile is free. Customers can find you on Search and Maps, read reviews, book and request quotes. Follow the [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) and keep name, address and opening hours identical across listings.

## What Does Google Require for Ads to EU Visitors?

For users in the European Economic Area, Google's help says you must collect consent for use of personal data to keep using tags for measurement, ad personalization and remarketing, and pass the consent signals to Google. Consent mode adds two parameters, ad_user_data and ad_personalization.

If a banner delays tags until the visitor responds, Google cannot verify consent, which may reduce data. Certified consent management platforms update automatically, while custom banners must implement consent mode v2. Check the Conversion diagnostics tab for consent status.

## How Do You Target a German Town?

Use location targeting around your service area. Google allows radius targeting from 1 km and warns that small areas may show only intermittently. It recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting for Search and says "Presence" fits when you want only people physically there.

Keep language and location consistent: Google advises, for example, targeting English speakers in England with English ads. For Germany, target German and write ads in German. ## What Does a Test Plan Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are an **assumption**, because no German CPC was published in sources checked here. Suppose a physiotherapy practice in a mid-sized city plans a 30-day test.

| Item | Assumption |

## FAQ

### Which search engine should German businesses focus on?
Google, which held 87.84% of German search in September 2026 per StatCounter.

### Do I need a cookie banner for Google Ads in Germany?
Google's help says advertisers must collect consent from EEA users to keep measurement, personalization and remarketing. Ask a lawyer how the rules apply to your site.

### What does Google Ads cost in Germany?
The sources checked here do not publish a German average click price. Use Google Keyword Planner and a small test budget.

### Can I target just my city?
Yes. Radius targeting needs at least 1 km, and you can pick named locations.

### Is a Google Business Profile free in Germany?
Google's FAQ says creating a Business Profile and listing your business is free.
