## Why Start With Google Ads in Germany?

Google Ads lets a local business control who sees a message, where they see it, and in which language. Search campaigns respond to what people type, which suits services such as physiotherapy, plumbing or dog grooming. A small budget is enough to start, and the campaign can be paused at any time.

Check the current search market before deciding how much weight to give Google. [StatCounter publishes monthly search engine share for Germany](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share/all/germany), and you can compare its latest month with the search traffic shown in your own analytics. This rewrite did not verify those figures, so treat them as a prompt to check, not as settled facts.

## What Benchmarks Can You Use for German Campaigns?

The only benchmark figures in the sources are all-industry averages from WordStream, a marketing vendor. They are not German, so use them as a rough sense check only. Across all industries, WordStream reports a 3.17% average search click-through rate and a $2.69 average search cost per click.

Your own numbers will differ by trade and by city. A plumber in a small town and a law firm in a major city face very different competition. Measure the first two weeks, then judge costs against your own margin rather than against an average from another market.

## How Does Consent Work for EEA Visitors?

Google's help pages describe consent requirements for users in the European Economic Area, covering measurement, ad personalisation and remarketing. Check how your consent banner passes signals to Google, because a banner that delays tags until a visitor responds can reduce the data Google can use.

This guide does not give legal advice. Read [Google's consent mode help page](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13695607) (not verified in this rewrite), then ask a German lawyer to confirm the cookie banner and imprint duties for your website. Also check consent status in your conversion reporting before relying on conversion counts.

## How Do You Target a German Town?

Location targeting reaches people in and around your service area. Start with the towns you actually serve, then widen only if enquiries justify it. Ad language should match the search language, so German campaigns need German keywords and German ad text.

Google's help pages explain radius and location options in detail. Check the current minimum radius in the location targeting help before you set one, because very small radii can show fewer impressions. Review the search terms report each week and add unrelated queries to the negative keyword list.

## Where Does Display and Social Media Fit?

According to Google's help, Display campaigns reach across 35 million websites and apps, including YouTube and Gmail, which extends reach beyond Search. For a small local business, Display usually works best after Search has produced enquiries, because it reaches broader audiences who are less ready to buy.

Social channels suit awareness and repeat customers. If you plan to run several accounts, compare scheduling tools first. The [Hootsuite and Sprout Social comparison](/blog/hootsuite-vs-sprout-social-small-business-local-business) shows which features matter for a small team. For most local services, keep Search as the first paid channel.

## How Do You Track Enquiries to Paying Jobs?

A click or form fill is not a customer. Track each enquiry to a booking, then to a paid appointment, so you know the real cost per customer. A spreadsheet is enough for the first month. When volume grows, a booking or field-service system can link leads to jobs, and the [Jobber and ServiceTitan comparison](/blog/jobber-vs-servicetitan-small-business-local-business) explains the trade-offs for trade businesses.

For services that take phone calls or quote requests, check whether a [Google Local Services Ads guide](/blog/google-local-service-ads-worth-it-local-business) fits your category. Local Services Ads have different pricing and eligibility rules, so read them before comparing them with standard Search ads.

## What Does a 30-Day Test Look Like? (Worked Example)

Every input below is an **assumption**. No German cost per click is published in the sources reviewed here. The example uses a physiotherapy practice in a mid-sized German city.

| Item | Base case (assumption) | Higher click cost (assumption) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | 600 EUR | 600 EUR |
| Cost per click | 2.50 EUR | 4.00 EUR |
| Clicks (budget / CPC) | 240 | 150 |
| Enquiry rate on landing page | 5% | 5% |
| Enquiries | 12 | 7.5 |
| Share of enquiries that book | 50% | 50% |
| New patients | 6 | 3.75 |
| Cost per new patient | 100 EUR | 160 EUR |

The base case gives 6 new patients for 600 EUR, which is 100 EUR each. Raising the click cost to 4 EUR cuts clicks to 150 and raises the cost per patient to 160 EUR. Replace each assumption with account data after two weeks, then decide whether the cost per patient fits your margin.

## What Steps Should You Follow in Order?

A simple order keeps the first month manageable:

1. Confirm your business details are identical across your website and listings.
2. Confirm the consent banner and tags with a German lawyer and your conversion reporting.
3. Set a location radius around the towns you serve, in German.
4. Launch Search ads with a fixed monthly test budget.
5. Review the search terms report weekly and block irrelevant queries.
6. After two weeks, compare cost per enquiry and cost per booked customer against your assumptions.
7. Add Display or social channels only after Search produces enquiries.

## FAQ

### Which search engine should a German business focus on?
Check current German search share on StatCounter, then compare it with your own traffic data. The figures were not verified for this rewrite, so do not rely on a number from this article.

### Do I need a cookie banner for Google Ads in Germany?
Google's help pages describe consent requirements for EEA users. Confirm your setup with a German lawyer and check consent status in your conversion reporting.

### What does Google Ads cost in Germany?
The sources reviewed here do not publish a German average cost per click. The all-industry WordStream average of $2.69 is not German and is only a rough reference. Use Keyword Planner and a small test budget to find your own figure.

### Can I target only my own town?
Yes. Location targeting lets you choose named areas or a radius. Start with your service area and widen only after the search terms report shows relevant demand.
