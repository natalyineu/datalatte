Hawaii businesses sell to two audiences at once, residents and visitors, and should decide which one each ad is for. In 2025 Hawaii had 9,642,991 visitors who spent $21.75 billion (DBEDT, preliminary data released January 29, 2026), so tourists are a large market, but they leave. Run Google Business Profile and Search ads for both, and use location settings to separate them.

## How Big Is the Visitor Market in Hawaii?

DBEDT reports that 2025 visitor arrivals were 9,642,991, down 0.6% from 9,701,499 in 2024, while spending rose 5.7% to $21.75 billion. Daily spending per person was $259.8, up 6.7%. Visitor numbers fell on Oahu (5,679,047, down 2.0%) and rose on Maui (2,516,163, up 7.0%).

Island counts overlap because some visitors go to more than one island. The state figures are preliminary. For a business, this means the mix of tourists and locals differs by island, so check your own sales before you assume.

## Should You Target Tourists, Residents or Both?

Pick per campaign. A restaurant near the beach may want visitors planning a trip, while a gym, groomer or salon depends on residents.

In Google Ads, "Presence or interest" is the default and recommended location option. It reaches people in or regularly present in a location plus those who showed interest in it. "Presence" limits ads to people physically there. Google says radius targeting is available with the first option, and that the old interest-only setting is no longer available for Search, Display or Shopping campaigns.

Use Presence for services that need the customer in town, and Presence or interest for businesses that sell to people researching a trip.

## What Can Meta Ads Do for Local Reach?

Meta's location targeting lets you set a radius around a place and choose people who live there, were recently there or are travelling there. That helps separate residents from visitors with two ad sets. Meta's 2026 benchmarks from WordStream put the US average traffic click at $0.60 and lead cost at $27.39. These are US figures, not Hawaii figures. Read our [Meta Ads for local business overview](/blog/local-business-meta-ads) for setup.

## Why Does Google Business Profile Matter More Here?

Visitors search on their phones, often for something open now and close by. A complete Business Profile with hours, photos and category gives you free visibility on those searches before you spend on ads. Follow our [Business Profile optimization checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) and keep hours accurate for holidays.

## FAQ

### How many tourists visit Hawaii each year?

DBEDT preliminary data show 9,642,991 visitors in 2025, down 0.6% from 2024, with $21.75 billion in spending.

### What do Google ads cost in Hawaii?

No Hawaii figure is published in the sources checked. The national average for all industries is $5.42 per click (LocaliQ 2026).

### Should I use Presence or Presence or interest?

Google recommends Presence or interest by default. Use Presence if your customers must be physically in the area.

### Is Meta or Google better for a Hawaii shop?

Search catches people looking now, and Meta can reach people nearby before they search. WordStream says the two complement each other.

### Do these figures cover every island?

DBEDT gives island totals. Oahu, Maui, Kauai and Hawaii Island differ in visitors and spending, so look at your own island.
