## How Big Is the Visitor Market in Hawaii?

The [Hawaii Department of Business, Economic Development and Tourism (DBEDT)](https://dbedt.hawaii.gov/?p=15180) reports that 2025 visitor arrivals were 9,642,991, down 0.6% from 9,701,499 in 2024. Spending rose 5.7% to $21.75 billion, and daily spending per person reached $259.8, up 6.7%. Visitor numbers fell on Oahu, to 5,679,047 (down 2.0%), and rose on Maui, to 2,516,163 (up 7.0%).

Island counts overlap because some visitors travel to more than one island. The state figures are preliminary, so a business should treat them as a directional guide. The mix of visitors and residents differs by island, which means each business should check its own sales records before relying on a statewide average.

## How Should a Business Split Residents and Visitors?

Many Hawaii businesses sell to two groups at once. A gym, groomer or salon usually depends on residents who return every week. A tour operator, a restaurant near a resort or a rental service often depends on visitors who are planning or already on a trip.

Write two short audience profiles before choosing any channel. For each one, note the search words they would likely use, the hours they need and the device they are most likely to use. Residents may search from a desktop at home, while visitors often search on a phone while walking somewhere.

Keep the two audiences in separate ad groups or campaigns. A single broad message rarely fits both, and separate groups show which audience is responding.

## Which Channels Should a Hawaii Business Test First?

Begin with the free listing. A complete Google Business Profile with accurate hours, a clear category, current photos and a phone number builds visibility before any clicks are paid for. Check holiday hours in advance, since a wrong closing time is an easy way to lose calls.

Then add one paid channel. Search ads suit people looking for a service now. Social ads suit businesses that need people to notice them before they search. The [comparison of social scheduling tools](/blog/hootsuite-vs-sprout-social-small-business-local-business) helps if posts need to run across several channels at once.

Test one channel for 30 days before adding another. Changing two things at once makes results hard to read, because no one can tell which change caused the difference.

## What Would a 30-Day Test Cost?

The table below is a worked example. Every input is an **assumption** because the sources checked do not publish Hawaii-specific advertising costs. Replace each input with your own account data after the first month.

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget (assumption) | $400 |
| Average cost per click (assumption) | $2.00 |
| Clicks (budget divided by cost per click) | 200 |
| Share of clicks becoming a call or booking (assumption) | 5% |
| Calls or bookings (clicks multiplied by share) | 10 |
| Cost per call or booking | $40 |

If the real cost per click is $3.50, the same $400 buys about 114 clicks. At the same 5% rate, that is about 6 bookings, and the cost per booking rises to about $70. The gap between those two results is why the first month should measure actual numbers.

Decide before the test what result would justify continuing. A simple rule is to keep the channel only if the cost per booking is at or below the profit earned from one booking.

## How Do the Islands Change the Plan?

Maui Marketing states that it covers Maui, Oahu, Big Island and Kauai. The vendor page does not publish island-level market data, so treat each island as its own test rather than assuming identical results across the state.

A shop on Oahu may draw much of its trade from residents, while a business on a neighbour island may depend more on visitors. Review sales by island, by month and, where possible, by customer postcode. Internal records give a more reliable picture than any general statement about the state.

Small island markets can be compared across regions. The [Bahamas local marketing guide](/blog/local-marketing-bahamas-small-business-2026) covers a similar island setting and is useful for thinking about how a small market behaves. It is not Hawaii data.

## FAQ

### What is the first thing a Hawaii small business should do?

Complete the Google Business Profile with accurate hours, categories and photos. Setup costs nothing, and it creates visibility before any ad spend.

### Are there Hawaii ad costs to use in a budget?

The sources checked do not publish Hawaii-specific advertising costs. Use the assumption-labelled example above, then replace it with your own click cost and conversion data after 30 days.

### Should a business target residents or visitors?

Choose based on your own sales records. Services that need regular local customers usually lean toward residents, while trip-related businesses lean toward visitors. Many businesses need both, run in separate campaigns.

### Does the island matter for marketing?

Yes. The four named islands differ in population and visitor patterns, so test each one separately. The vendor page does not give island-level figures, so your own sales data should guide the split.
