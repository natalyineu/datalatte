## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Pet Groomer?

LocaliQ's 2026 Animals and Pets row shows $4.06 per click, 7.49% click-through, 16.22% conversion and $31.50 per lead. The all-industry average is $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead. These are national averages. Costs in Nashville depend on how many groomers bid on the same searches, and Google does not publish that by city.

To see local estimates, use Keyword Planner. Google says it estimates searches and the cost to target them, and that you need a Google Ads account with billing set up.

## How Should a Nashville Groomer Set the Target Area?

Wikipedia, citing the 2020 census, lists 689,447 residents in the city of Nashville (Nashville-Davidson) and 2,150,553 in the metropolitan area. The metro is about three times the city, so a large share of potential customers live in surrounding towns.

Target a radius around your salon rather than the city name alone. Google says a radius must be at least 1 km. If bookings come from towns outside Davidson County, check the location report and add those areas as separate targets so you can see their cost per booking.

## Which Keywords Should a Nashville Groomer Use?

Start with terms that show a booking need: dog grooming near me, plus Nashville. Use exact match for your top terms. Google describes exact match as the type that gives the most control and reaches the fewest searches, and broad match as the type that can trigger related searches.

Add negative keywords such as jobs, courses and free. Google says negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals and synonyms yourself. Our [pet groomer keyword guide](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-pet-groomers-in-2026) has a longer starting list.

## How Do You Track Bookings?

Count phone calls. Google lets you set a minimum call length for a call to count as a conversion, and reports calls from ads that last at least that long. A grooming enquiry is usually short, so pick a length that excludes wrong numbers but keeps real bookings.

Also track online booking-form submissions. If you also run your Business Profile, keep it current, as covered in our [pet groomer profile guide](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-for-pet-groomers).

## FAQ

### How much does Google Ads cost for a pet groomer in Nashville?

No Nashville-specific average is published. LocaliQ's 2026 national Animals and Pets average is $4.06 per click. Your cost depends on competition and your settings.

### What budget should a Nashville groomer start with?

There is no standard figure. At $4.06 per click, $600 a month buys about 148 clicks. Start there and adjust once you know your cost per booking.

### Should I target the whole city or a radius?

A radius around your salon. Google says a radius must be at least 1 km, and small targets may deliver unevenly. Widen it only if bookings come from further away.

### How do I count phone calls as conversions?

Set up call tracking and choose a minimum call length. Google counts every call that lasts at least that long.

### Is Google Ads better than my Business Profile?

They work together. Ads cost money per click, while a profile is free. Our [pet groomer ads mistakes guide](/blog/pet-groomer-google-ads-mistakes) covers what to avoid.
