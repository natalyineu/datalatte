A Vermont small business that serves visitors should plan its marketing around the visitor calendar, keep its Google Business Profile current, and run a small, tightly targeted Google Ads campaign. Visitors are a big market: about 16 million people visited Vermont in 2024 and spent a record $4.2 billion, equal to 9% of state GDP, according to the state's tourism study as reported by WCAX.

No source publishes a Vermont click price. The WordStream by LocaliQ 2026 all-industry average is $5.42 per click. Use it as a starting range for cafes, studios, salons and service firms in Burlington, Montpelier, Stowe and smaller towns.

## How Important Is Tourism to Vermont Businesses?

WCAX reported in December 2025 that the visitor economy directly supports almost 32,000 jobs, about 10.5% of Vermont's workforce. The figures are for 2024; a 2025 edition had not been published when this article was checked.

For a small business this means two customer groups with different habits: residents who buy year round and visitors who arrive in peaks. No source here gives the seasonal split. Pull last year's monthly sales and mark the months visitors drive.

## How Should Visitor Seasons Shape Your Settings?

Google Ads lets you schedule ads and adjust bids by day and time. Once you have a few weeks of data, shift spend toward the days your phone rings. If visitors book ahead, run ads before their trip, not just during it.

Google's location help describes "Presence or interest" for reaching people interested in your area who are not there yet, and "Presence" for people physically in it. A Stowe massage studio might test the first for pre-trip planners. A Barre plumber needs only "Presence".

## How Do You Win the Map Listing in a Small Market?

Google says relevance, distance and prominence drive local results and that you cannot pay for a better ranking. Complete every field, add photos and respond to reviews.

Use special hours for holidays and seasonal closures rather than editing regular hours. Visitors read the hours before driving, so a wrong listing costs bookings. ## FAQ

### How much should a Vermont business spend on Google Ads?

No state benchmark exists. At the national average of $5.42 a click, $400 a month buys about 74 clicks. Begin small and measure cost per customer.

### Does tourism help a small local shop?

Only if visitors buy from you. Statewide visitor spending of $4.2 billion in 2024 does not show your share, so compare your own seasonal sales.

### Should I use Presence or Presence or interest targeting?

Use "Presence or interest" if you want pre-trip visitors. Use "Presence" if you only serve people who are already local.

### Is Google Business Profile worth it?

Yes. It is free and sits where people compare nearby options. Keep hours accurate for seasonal changes.
