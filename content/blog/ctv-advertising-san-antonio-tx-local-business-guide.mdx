San Antonio has 568,979 households, and the median household income in one ZIP code (78258) is almost double that of another (78216). That gap is the whole argument for buying streaming TV by ZIP code instead of by city. A $1,500 monthly test spread across the entire metro reaches a lot of people who will never book your chair, class or table. The same money pointed at three or four well-chosen ZIP codes can reach the same household several times a month.

This guide is for San Antonio owners of salons, studios, groomers, restaurants and similar local businesses who are weighing connected TV (CTV), meaning ads that play on streaming apps on a living-room screen. It covers what the city's data says about who you would be reaching, which events shape the calendar, what the self-serve platforms offer, and the arithmetic of whether a test can pay back. For a plain-English primer first, read [Connected TV Ads for Local Business: How to Start](/blog/connected-tv-ads-local-business-how-to-start).

A few more numbers that matter for an ad buyer:

- **Median age is 34.9** and the median household income is $66,176 (margin of error $1,375). A younger, mid-income city suits services with a monthly or weekly rhythm, such as grooming, haircuts, classes and casual dining.
- **48% of households rent.** 273,449 of 568,979 occupied homes are renter-occupied. Renters may switch providers more often than owners, but test that against your own data rather than assuming it.
- **96,163 residents are veterans**, or 8.3% of the city's population, which fits a city anchored by military bases.
- **11% work from home.** 82,573 of 742,336 workers worked from home in 2024, so a share of your customers are home on weekday daytimes when a streaming ad can reach them.

On language: 58.5% of residents aged 5 and over speak only English at home and 37.0% speak Spanish. That does not tell you what language people prefer for advertising, and plenty of Spanish speakers are fully bilingual. It does justify a controlled test: run the same 15-second spot in English and in Spanish to separate ZIP clusters and compare calls or bookings. Do not assume the answer.

## Why ZIP Codes Matter Here: Eight Compared

CTV platforms that support ZIP targeting let you choose where the ad plays. Below are eight San Antonio ZIP Code Tabulation Areas (ZCTAs, which approximate ZIP codes) from the ACS 2020 to 2024 five-year estimates, again via Census Reporter. Treat the income column as approximate: the margin of error on 78204 is more than $15,000.

| ZIP | Population | Median household income | Hispanic or Latino | Renter households |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 78204 | 11,983 | $60,255 | 80% | 55% |
| 78209 | 43,400 | $86,514 | 31% | 50% |
| 78212 | 27,437 | $62,801 | 56% | 53% |
| 78216 | 41,180 | $59,110 | 58% | 65% |
| 78230 | 41,387 | $73,529 | 52% | 51% |
| 78245 | 96,015 | $90,029 | 63% | 31% |
| 78253 | 69,282 | $106,373 | 47% | 23% |
| 78258 | 52,448 | $117,835 | 40% | 45% |

How to use this:

1. **A premium service** (a color specialist, boutique studio, higher-priced grooming) has a clearer fit in the higher-income ZIPs, such as 78253 and 78258, where the five-year median is above $100,000. The Census data does not say these households will respond to CTV; it only says where higher-income households live.
2. **A value or convenience offer** (walk-in cuts, a first-month gym deal) may fit better where income is lower and renter share is higher, such as 78216 and 78212.
3. **Large, family-heavy ZIPs** such as 78245, with 96,015 residents and a 31% renter share, give you the most households per dollar of geography. A big ZIP is not a better ZIP. Check your own booking data first.
4. **Bilingual creative tests** can be set up by assigning an English and a Spanish version to similar ZIP clusters, such as 78230 and 78216, and keeping everything else equal.

Your own customer list beats any demographic table. Export the ZIP code of your last 100 customers, count them, and start with the top three or four. The Census data is useful mainly for finding look-alike neighborhoods nearby.

## The San Antonio Calendar: Four Anchors

**Fiesta.** The Fiesta San Antonio Commission says Fiesta draws on more than 100 non-profit member organizations running more than 100 official events, and describes an economic impact of more than $340 million. The next Fiesta is April 15 to 25, 2027, according to the Commission's site. If you want recognition before Fiesta, a CTV flight in March is a reasonable test window (that timing is a suggestion, not a platform rule). Bear in mind that the $340 million is the organizer's own estimate.

**Spurs season.** The Spurs open the 2026-27 season at home against the Oklahoma City Thunder on October 20 at Frost Bank Center, according to Fox San Antonio and Texas Public Radio. TPR counts 34 nationally televised Spurs games, and three games are being played in Austin. For a local restaurant or bar, game nights are a natural dayparting test.

**Military.** The Texas Comptroller's 2025 analysis lists 74,713 direct employees at Joint Base San Antonio, including 33,256 active-duty personnel. It adds 7,132 contractors and an average daily student load of 23,867, and estimates total direct and indirect employment at 223,349 jobs and a contribution of at least $53 billion to the Texas economy. The student load matters to a marketer: trainees cycle through the area continuously, so there is steady demand from people who have just arrived and do not yet have a regular barber, vet or gym. No official source used here gives the timing of moves, so plan against your own booking history rather than an assumed moving season.

**Tourism.** Visit San Antonio reports a 2024 hospitality impact of $23.4 billion, with 39.2 million annual visitors, $284 million in tax revenue and 150,000 jobs. The figure excludes international visitors' impact. Visitor traffic matters for restaurants, cafes and experiences more than for service businesses that depend on repeat local customers. It also shapes the labor market: the Bureau of Labor Statistics reports leisure and hospitality employment in the San Antonio-New Braunfels area at 149,500 in August 2026 (preliminary, not seasonally adjusted), out of 1,191,700 total nonfarm jobs, about 12.5%. The same release puts the area's unemployment rate at 4.5%.

## What the Platforms Actually Offer

You do not need a sales rep to start. Platform details change, so confirm them inside each tool.

- **Tubi.** Fox announced a self-serve ad platform for Tubi, powered by Universal Ads, on May 6, 2025, aimed at small and medium-sized businesses. Fox's page says Tubi has over 97 million monthly active users. It does not state minimums or geo-targeting options, so ask before you plan around ZIP codes.
- **Roku Ads Manager.** Launched in September 2024 as a self-service tool for buying CTV video ads, per Advanced Television. The launch article mentions reaching "key geos and audiences" but does not describe ZIP-level targeting or spend minimums.
- **YouTube on TV.** Google says its video action campaigns were upgraded to Demand Gen as of May 2026, and ads can also appear in the YouTube app on TV. Google's page requires a video of at least 10 seconds and does not describe location targeting, so check geo options in your own account.
- **Premium streaming and live sports.** Usually bought through sales teams or demand-side platforms, with higher CPMs and larger budgets. Our [platform comparison](/blog/ctv-advertising-platforms-compared-2026) goes through each route.

On price, AdWave's Q2 2026 benchmark article reports a blended CTV CPM of about $26 (cost per 1,000 impressions, citing eMarketer), a typical range of $25 to $35, and a full-market range of about $15 to $45. By inventory type, it lists free ad-supported streaming at $15 to $25, standard programmatic on premium services at $25 to $45, and premium direct and live sports at $45 to $65. Vibe cites an estimate of $20 to $60 for Roku. These are vendor benchmarks, not rate cards, and neither source quantifies a ZIP-targeting premium. Our [CTV cost and CPM data guide](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data) covers this in more depth.

## The Math

Take a $1,500 one-month test. Every row marked assumption is an illustration, not a benchmark. The CPMs are the low end, blended average and top of the AdWave full-market range above.

| | Low CPM | Blended CPM | High CPM |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | $1,500 | $1,500 | $1,500 |
| CPM (AdWave low, blended, top of range) | $15 | $26 | $45 |
| Impressions | 100,000 | 57,692 | 33,333 |
| Households in target ZIPs (assumption) | 15,000 | 15,000 | 15,000 |
| Max impressions per household if all land (assumption: ignores waste) | 6.7 | 3.8 | 2.2 |
| Gross profit per new customer, first year (assumption) | $150 | $150 | $150 |
| New customers needed to break even | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Impressions per break-even customer | 10,000 | 5,769 | 3,333 |

How to read it: at a $26 CPM you need roughly one new customer for every 5,800 impressions to cover the spend with first-year gross profit. Real delivery will not be perfectly efficient, so actual frequency will be lower than the table shows. If your average new customer is worth $400 in the first year, break-even drops to 4 customers. If it is worth $60, you need 25.

Because CTV rarely produces clickable traffic, you will measure it indirectly. Compare branded searches for your business name in Google Search Console, calls and direction requests in your Google Business Profile, and the answer to "how did you hear about us" in the weeks before and during the flight. Allow 60 to 90 days of data and treat any lift as directional, since promotions and seasons move the same numbers. CTV also needs a place to send people who go looking, so make sure your [Google Business Profile and Google Ads](/services/google-ads) are ready before the first impression plays.

## Creative: Keep It Simple

Check the current specs inside each platform, but plan for a 16:9 landscape file at 1920 by 1080 and a 15-second spot as a default. Spend the first two seconds on your name and neighborhood, the middle on one clear benefit shown on screen, and the last few on one action: a phone number, a short URL or a QR code. Our [CTV creative specs guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026) has the details. Unlike mobile video, assume sound is on.

## Do This Today

1. Export the ZIP code of your last 100 customers and rank them. Pick your top three or four ZIPs.
2. Look those ZIPs up in the table above, or pull your own from the Census, and note household income and renter share.
3. Write down your break-even number: spend divided by the first-year gross profit of one new customer.
4. Ask two platforms (for example Tubi and Roku Ads Manager) in writing for minimum spend, ZIP-level targeting and a delivery forecast for your chosen ZIPs.
5. Set up your measurement before launch: a baseline of branded searches, calls and a "how did you hear about us" field on every booking.

## FAQ

**Q: How much does CTV advertising cost in San Antonio?**
A: There is no San Antonio rate card. AdWave's Q2 2026 benchmark puts the blended national CTV CPM at about $26, with a market range of $15 to $45. At $26, a $1,500 budget buys roughly 57,700 impressions. Ask platforms for a forecast for your ZIP codes.

**Q: Which streaming platform is best for a small San Antonio business?**
A: Start with a self-serve tool such as Tubi (through Universal Ads) or Roku Ads Manager, and confirm in the tool what ZIP-level targeting and minimums are available. Premium and live-sports inventory usually needs a larger budget.

**Q: Should my San Antonio CTV ads be in Spanish?**
A: Test it. About 37% of residents aged 5 and over speak Spanish at home (ACS 2024), but language preference for advertising varies. Run English and Spanish versions against comparable ZIP codes and compare results.

**Q: How long does CTV take to work?**
A: Plan for 60 to 90 days before judging branded search, calls and bookings, since CTV builds familiarity rather than driving immediate clicks.

**Q: Is CTV worth it before Google Ads and a Business Profile?**
A: Usually not. Search and your Business Profile capture people who are ready to book, and CTV works best when they are in place. See our [local SEO service](/services/local-seo).
