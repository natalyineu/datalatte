To advertise on connected TV (CTV) in San Antonio, buy by ZIP code, not by city. The median household income is $117,835 in ZIP 78258 and $59,110 in ZIP 78216, according to Census five-year estimates. Disney Campaign Manager lists ZIP code targeting with a $500 minimum per campaign, and Roku Ads Manager requires a $500 lifetime budget. A $500 to $1,500 test aimed at three or four ZIPs is a sensible start.

This guide uses Census data, local event calendars and platform pages, all checked in October 2026. Assumptions are labelled.

## Who lives in San Antonio, according to the Census?

The figures come from the U.S. Census Bureau's American Community Survey (ACS) 2024 one-year estimates for the City of San Antonio, read through the [Census Reporter API](https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/latest?table_ids=B01003,B19013,B25003,B01002,B03003&geo_ids=16000US4865000), a mirror of the Census data.

- **Median age is 34.9**, and median household income is $66,176 (margin of error $1,375).
- **48% of households rent:** 273,449 of 568,979 occupied homes.
- **96,163 veterans**, 8.3% of the 1,154,712 civilian residents aged 18 and over.
- **11% work from home:** 82,573 of 742,336 workers.

On language, 58.5% of residents aged 5 and over speak only English at home and 37.0% speak Spanish. That says nothing about which language people prefer for ads, and many are bilingual. It does justify a controlled test: run the same spot in English and in Spanish in similar ZIP clusters and compare calls or bookings.

## Which San Antonio ZIP codes should I target?

Below are eight ZIP Code Tabulation Areas (ZCTAs, which approximate ZIP codes) from the ACS 2020 to 2024 five-year estimates. The margin of error on 78204 income is more than $15,000, so treat that row as approximate.

| ZIP | Population | Median household income | Hispanic or Latino | Renter households |

| 78209 | 43,400 | $86,514 | 31% | 50% |
| 78212 | 27,437 | $62,801 | 56% | 53% |
| 78216 | 41,180 | $59,110 | 58% | 65% |
| 78230 | 41,387 | $73,529 | 52% | 51% |
| 78245 | 96,015 | $90,029 | 63% | 31% |
| 78253 | 69,282 | $106,373 | 46% | 23% |
| 78258 | 52,448 | $117,835 | 39% | 45% |

The Census says where households live, not who responds to CTV. Use it for these recommendations:

1. **Premium services** (color specialist, boutique studio) fit 78253 and 78258, where median income is above $100,000.
2. **Value offers** (walk-in cuts, a first-month deal) may fit 78216 and 78212, with lower income and more renters.
3. **Reach per dollar** is highest in a large ZIP like 78245, but a big ZIP is not a better ZIP.
4. **Bilingual tests** can pair similar clusters, such as 78230 and 78216.

Your own data beats any table. Export the ZIP codes of your last 100 customers and start with the top three or four.

**Fiesta.** The [Fiesta San Antonio Commission](https://fiestasanantonio.org/) lists more than 100 San Antonio non-profits, over 100 events and an economic impact of more than $340 million (the organizer's own figure). Its site gives April 15 to 25, 2027 as the next dates. A CTV flight in March is a suggested test window, not a platform rule.

**Spurs.** The Spurs open the 2026-27 season at home against the Oklahoma City Thunder on October 20 at Frost Bank Center. [Fox San Antonio](https://foxsanantonio.com/news/local/spurs-unveil-2026-27-schedule-home-opener-vs-thunder-on-october-20) and [Texas Public Radio](https://www.tpr.org/news/2026-08-13/nba-western-conference-champion-spurs-release-2026-2027-schedule) both report 34 nationally televised games and three games at Austin's Moody Center.

**Military.** The [Texas Comptroller](https://comptroller.texas.gov/economy/economic-data/military/2025/joint-base-sa.php) counts 74,713 direct employees at Joint Base San Antonio (including 7,132 contractors, and 33,256 active-duty personnel) and an average daily student load of 23,867. It estimates 223,349 direct and indirect jobs. Trainees keep arriving, which suits barbers, vets and gyms. No source here gives move timing, so use your own booking history.

**Tourism.** [Visit San Antonio](https://assets.simpleviewinc.com/sv-sanantonio/image/upload/v1/clients/sanantoniotx/VSA_ROI_rackcard_2025_FINAL_121025_bda74455-7f88-453f-80d6-228c6f1a833a.pdf) reports a 2024 hospitality impact of $23.4 billion, 39.2 million visitors and $284 million in tax revenue (international visitor impact excluded). ## How much does CTV cost per impression?

AdWave, a CTV reseller, reports a blended US CTV CPM of about $26 for 2026, citing eMarketer, with a full-market range of $15 to $45 ([AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/average-ctv-cpm-q2-2026), second-hand). It lists free ad-supported streaming at $15 to $25 and premium direct and live sports at $45 to $65. These are benchmarks, not rate cards, and none of them is specific to San Antonio. See our [CTV cost and CPM guide](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data).

## Does a $1,500 San Antonio CTV test pay back?

Worked example. The CPMs come from AdWave's range; every other row is an assumption.

| | Low CPM | Blended CPM | High CPM |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | $1,500 | $1,500 | $1,500 |
| CPM | $15 | $26 | $45 |
| Impressions | 100,000 | 57,692 | 33,333 |
| Target households (assumption) | 15,000 | 15,000 | 15,000 |
| Impressions per household if none are wasted | 6.7 | 3.8 | 2.2 |
| First-year gross profit per new customer (assumption) | $150 | $150 | $150 |
| Customers to break even | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Impressions per break-even customer | 10,000 | 5,769 | 3,333 |

At $26 you need about one new customer per 5,800 impressions. If a new customer is worth $400 in the first year, break-even falls to 4 customers. At $60 it rises to 25. Real delivery is never perfectly efficient.

CTV rarely produces clicks, so measure indirectly: branded searches in Google Search Console, calls and direction requests in your Google Business Profile, and "how did you hear about us". Allow 60 to 90 days and treat any lift as directional. Have your [Google Ads](/services/google-ads) and Business Profile ready first.

## What should my CTV creative look like?

Google's Demand Gen page requires video of at least 10 seconds and lists 15 seconds as recommended. Beyond that, check each platform's specs; our [CTV creative specs guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026) has details. A reasonable structure, as a recommendation: your name and neighborhood first, one benefit in the middle, one action at the end (phone, short URL or QR code).

## FAQ

**How much does CTV advertising cost in San Antonio?**
There is no San Antonio rate card. Roku and Disney each need at least $500 per campaign, and the blended US CPM is about $26 (AdWave, second-hand). At that CPM, $1,500 buys about 57,700 impressions.

**Which streaming platform is best for a small San Antonio business?**
Disney Campaign Manager is the one that documents ZIP code targeting. Roku, Tubi and YouTube on TV are alternatives; confirm geo options in each account.

**Should my San Antonio CTV ads be in Spanish?**
Test it. About 37% of residents aged 5 and over speak Spanish at home (ACS 2024), but ad language preference varies. Compare English and Spanish versions across similar ZIPs.

**How long does CTV take to work?**
Plan for 60 to 90 days before judging branded search, calls and bookings.

**Is CTV worth it before Google Ads and a Business Profile?**
Usually not, because search and your profile capture people ready to book. See our [local SEO service](/services/local-seo).
