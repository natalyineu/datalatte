## What Is a Good Landing Page Conversion Rate for a Local Business?

[Unbounce's 2024 Conversion Benchmark Report](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/) uses over 57 million conversions and 41,000+ landing pages. Its median across all industries is 6.6%, and the nine industry medians run from 3.8% to 12.3%. The page text does not give each industry's figure, and local services such as cafes or groomers are not one of the nine categories.

How to use it:

- Compare your page with your own past results first.
- Count one action per page, such as a booking or a call, not every click.
- Treat anything far below 3.8% as a sign the offer, the traffic or the page is off.

A page that converts 6% when the traffic is people searching "dog groomer near me" is not comparable to a page for cold social traffic.

## What Does Google Look at on Your Landing Page?

If you run search ads, Google's [landing page experience help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14086) says it is one of several factors in a keyword's Quality Score. Google describes it through the relevance and usefulness of the page, ease of navigation, the number of links on the page, and whether the page meets expectations set by the ad.

Practical rule: match the page headline to the ad or search phrase. If the ad says "Same-day cat grooming," the page should open with that phrase, not a general homepage.

For speed, Google's [Core Web Vitals guidance](https://web.dev/articles/vitals) lists good thresholds of 2.5 seconds or less for Largest Contentful Paint, 200 milliseconds or less for Interaction to Next Paint and 0.1 or less for layout shift, measured at the 75th percentile. Compress hero photos before anything else.

## What Goes Above the Fold, in What Order?

Use the same skeleton for every niche, then swap the content.

1. **Headline:** the service, place and result, such as "Haircuts in Leeds, booked in 30 seconds."
2. **One action button:** book, call or order. Repeat it after the proof section.
3. **Proof next to the button:** your Google rating and review count, with a link to the reviews.
4. **Real photo:** your team, shop or product, not a generic picture.
5. **Facts:** address, hours, price range and what to expect.

Remove the top navigation from ad pages, or cut it to two links. The fewer routes off the page, the more clicks reach the action.

## What Should a Coffee Shop, Salon, Groomer and Studio Page Include?

| Niche | Primary action | Proof | Page-specific block |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coffee shop | Order ahead or visit | Rating, a menu photo | Hours, parking, loyalty offer |
| Hair salon | Book appointment | Before and after photos with permission | Price list, stylist links |
| Pet groomer | Book a groom | Reviews that mention the pet's breed | Vaccine policy, size-based prices |
| Fitness studio | Claim a trial class | Class schedule, coach bios | Trial terms and cancellation rules |

Choose one primary action and demote the rest. A studio that offers a free trial, a call and an email form on the same screen splits attention. ## How Much Does a Better Page Change Your Cost per Lead?

Worked example, labelled as an assumption: a salon pays $2 per click and buys 500 clicks, or $1,000. At a 5% booking rate, that is 25 bookings, or $40 each. If a clearer page lifts the rate to 8%, that is 40 bookings, or $25 each.

The page change saved $15 per booking without spending a cent more on ads. Replace the $2 click cost with your real figure from your ad account, and use your own booking rate.

Decision rule: fix the page before raising the ad budget. Doubling spend on a 5% page buys twice as many of the same weak results.

## One Page or Several Pages?

Make one page per ad group or service when you run paid traffic. Someone who clicked "teeth whitening" should land on a page about that, not a list of every service. For organic search, a single strong service page per offer plus your Google Business Profile usually beats a single catch-all page.

Check each page weekly for three things: the call and book buttons work on a phone, the price and hours are current, and the headline matches the ad. ## FAQ

**Q: What is a good conversion rate for a local business landing page?**
A: Unbounce's 2024 median across all industries is 6.6%, with industry medians from 3.8% to 12.3%. Compare against your own baseline and traffic source first.

**Q: Do I need a separate landing page for each service?**
A: For paid ads, yes, because Google weighs relevance of the page to the ad. For organic search, one strong page per service is enough.

**Q: How fast should my landing page load?**
A: Google's guidance for a good Largest Contentful Paint is 2.5 seconds or less, measured at the 75th percentile of visits.

**Q: How many buttons should a landing page have?**
A: One primary action, repeated. Add a phone link for businesses where people prefer to call.

**Q: Should the page include navigation?**
A: For ad traffic, cut it to a minimum. Google's guidance mentions the number of links on the page as part of landing page experience.
