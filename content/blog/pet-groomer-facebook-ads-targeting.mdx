A pet groomer should set a tight location around the shop or mobile route and a minimum age, then put the targeting effort into the offer and the ad itself. In Meta's Advantage+ audience, location and minimum age are hard limits, while interests, custom audiences and lookalikes are only suggestions, according to a Meta-ads specialist's guide. Narrow interest stacks do less than they used to.

This guide is for salon and mobile groomers in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. Meta's own Help Center pages could not be read by our fetcher, so the targeting rules below come from a secondary source and are marked as such. Check Ads Manager before you rely on them.

## Which Targeting Settings Does Meta Treat as Hard Limits?

Jon Loomer, an independent Meta-ads educator, describes Advantage+ audience as having controls and suggestions. His guide (published 26 February 2024, modified 6 March 2025, so it may have changed) lists these as controls: locations, minimum age and excluded custom audiences. Custom audiences and lookalikes, he says, "will always be an audience suggestion", and Meta can deliver ads to people outside them.

| Input | Treated as | Source type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location (living in or recently in) | Control | Secondary |
| Minimum age | Control | Secondary |
| Excluded custom audiences | Control | Secondary |
| Custom audiences | Suggestion | Secondary |
| Lookalike audiences | Suggestion | Secondary |

For a groomer, that means two settings do real work: where people are, and the youngest age you want to reach.

## How Should a Groomer Set the Location?

Set the area to what you can serve. A salon groomer should use the towns or radius people will drive to. A mobile groomer should use the postcodes or towns on the route, not the whole metro. Wasted impressions in places you do not cover cost money and lower the quality of your results data.

Name the area in the ad. "Dog grooming in [your town], vans on the road Tuesday to Saturday" filters for you before anyone clicks. Our guide to [Meta ads for pet groomers](/blog/pet-groomer-meta-ads) covers goals, tracking and a budget example for this.

## Do Interests and Lookalikes Still Matter?

Less than before, if the sources are right. Treat them as hints. A groomer can add interests such as dog ownership or pet care as a suggestion, and upload a customer list as a custom audience, but delivery can go beyond them.

Two cautions. Upload a customer list only if your privacy notice allows it and Meta's terms are met. And small lists give Meta little to learn from. In practice, the creative carries the targeting: a photo of a doodle after a full groom speaks to doodle owners better than an interest label.

## What Creative Targets Better Than an Interest?

Name the dog and the problem. "Double coat shedding", "puppy first groom" and "anxious dog, quiet room" tell the right owner this ad is for them. Test three images against one offer and compare cost per booking, not likes.

Existing clients are a separate job: reminders suit email better than ads. ## What Does a Test Cost? A Worked Example

LocaliQ lists Animals & Pets only for traffic campaigns: a 1.85% click-through rate and $0.51 per click. It lists no lead or conversion figure for pets. The inputs below are therefore **assumptions**.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget (assumption) | $300 |
| Cost per click (LocaliQ, Animals & Pets traffic) | $0.51 |
| Clicks | about 588 |
| Share of visitors who book (assumption) | 2% |
| New bookings | about 12 |
| Cost per booking | about $25 |
| Average first groom (assumption) | $70 |

If 0.5% book instead, bookings fall to about 3 and cost per booking rises to $100. The click price is real; the booking rate is yours to measure. Related costs for beauty-type lead campaigns are higher, so see the table in our [Meta ads guide for groomers](/blog/pet-groomer-meta-ads). Search ads reach people who already want a groomer; see [best Google Ads keywords for pet groomers](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-pet-groomers-in-2026).

## How Do You Know Targeting Is Working?

Check where clicks come from in the breakdown by location, and ask each new client how they found you. If bookings come from outside your area, tighten the location. If clicks are cheap but no one books, the page or offer is the problem, not the audience.

## FAQ

### What is the best Facebook audience for a pet groomer?

People who live near you. Location and minimum age are the settings Meta treats as hard limits, per a secondary source. Everything else is a suggestion that Meta may go beyond.

### How much does a click cost for pet businesses?

LocaliQ's 2026 benchmark for Animals & Pets traffic campaigns is $0.51 per click and a 1.85% click-through rate. It lists no lead cost for pets.

### Should I use lookalike audiences?

You can add one as a suggestion, but delivery may go beyond it. A small customer list gives Meta little to learn from, so do not expect much.

### Can I target by dog breed?

The sources opened for this guide say nothing on breed targeting. Put the breed or coat type in the ad text and image instead.
