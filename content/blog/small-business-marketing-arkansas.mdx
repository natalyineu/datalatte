## How big is the Arkansas market for local businesses?

Arkansas had 3,114,791 residents on July 1, 2025, up 18,711 (0.60%) from 3,096,080 a year earlier, according to the Census Bureau's Vintage 2025 state file.

Growth came from people moving in. Domestic migration was +14,475 and international migration +5,470, while natural change was -1,224. Compared with the 2020 base of 3,011,530, the state has added about 103,000 residents. Local patterns differ by county, so check your ZIP codes.

## Which Arkansas metro areas are growing?

The Census Bureau publishes metro population estimates. Changes below are July 2024 to July 2025.

| Metro area | Population, July 2025 | Change | Change, % |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fayetteville-Springdale-Rogers | 622,177 | +14,744 | +2.43% |
| Little Rock-North Little Rock-Conway | 777,607 | +5,019 | +0.65% |
| Fort Smith (AR-OK) | 234,140 | +1,164 | +0.50% |

The Census release names Fayetteville-Springdale-Rogers among the metros with the fastest percentage growth. It gained 14,744 people, about four fifths of the state's total gain. Little Rock is bigger but grew more slowly. The Fort Smith metro includes Oklahoma counties.

## How do you keep Google Ads local in Arkansas?

Google Ads lets you target regions, cities or postal codes depending on the country, or a radius of at least 1 km around a location ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722043)). Little Rock and Northwest Arkansas are far apart, so run separate campaigns for each. A cafe in Bentonville should not pay for clicks from central Arkansas.

Google recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting for Search, but says "Presence" may suit advertisers who only want people physically in the area. A cafe or restaurant that depends on people already nearby may prefer Presence so it does not pay for clicks from people who are only interested in the area. Run a separate campaign for each town you serve so budget and results stay readable.

## Why does Google Business Profile come first?

A free profile appears in map results without a per-click cost. Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your ranking ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)).

Fill in every section, pick the most specific category and keep hours current. Ask customers for reviews with a link or QR code. Google prohibits offering free or discounted goods in exchange for a review ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)). Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers the full setup.

## The math: what does a $500 test return?

Inputs are **assumptions** built on the LocaliQ benchmark for this industry, applied to a restaurant or cafe (Restaurants and Food benchmark).

- Budget: $500 a month. CPC: $2.05. Clicks: 500 / 2.05 = about 244.
- Conversion rate: 8.05%, so about 19.6 leads. Cost per lead: about $25.
- Assumption: 50% of them become first orders at $18 each, giving about 9.8 first orders and $177 in first-sale revenue.

On first visits alone the test earns less than it costs. Repeat business decides the result, which is why a rebooking or reminder system matters more than click price. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to plan for that.

## How do you win new residents and keep customers?

Newcomers do not know the local favorites yet. List your business on Google Maps with photos and a menu, collect reviews, and offer a loyalty card or email list at the register. Our [coffee shop Google Maps guide](/blog/coffee-shops-dominate-google-maps) covers the profile steps.

There is no Arkansas standard. Start with free channels and a test budget you can afford to lose, then scale on cost per customer. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) covers the method.

### Are Google Ads cheaper in Arkansas?
Unknown. The benchmark source publishes US averages by industry, not by state. Check your own cost per click in the first month.

### Which Arkansas metro is growing fastest?
