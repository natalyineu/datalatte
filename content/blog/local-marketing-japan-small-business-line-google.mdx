A small business in Japan should start with two free channels: a LINE Official Account for repeat customers and a verified Google Business Profile for people searching on Google Search and Maps. LY Corporation reports about 100 million monthly active LINE users in Japan, roughly 80% of the population. LINE plans start at ¥0 a month, with paid plans at ¥5,000 and ¥15,000 (excluding tax).

This guide covers what LINE costs, what it can do for a shop, how Google Maps fits in and where the evidence runs out. Prices are in yen, before tax, as published on the dates shown in Sources.

## How Much Does a LINE Official Account Cost?

LY Corporation lists three plans, all excluding tax. The Communication plan is ¥0 and includes 200 messages a month. Light is ¥5,000 and includes 5,000 messages, with no extra messages allowed. Standard is ¥15,000, includes 30,000 messages and allows extra messages at a per-message charge starting at about ¥3.

The page says it revised extra-message pricing on 1 October 2026 and will revise Premium ID pricing on 1 December 2026. The tiered extra-message prices are shown as an image, so check them on the plan page before budgeting.

| Plan | Monthly (excl. tax) | Included messages | Extra messages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Communication | ¥0 | 200 | Not allowed |
| Light | ¥5,000 | 5,000 | Not allowed |
| Standard | ¥15,000 | 30,000 | From about ¥3 each |

## What Can a Local Shop Do With LINE?

LY Corporation describes sending messages to people who added your account, including images and video, with targeted and step (scheduled) delivery. You can create coupons that customers use in person and send them as messages. A rich menu is a fixed menu at the bottom of the chat that can link to coupons, shop cards or external pages such as a booking site.

One business ID can create up to 100 accounts. Booking is not a stand-alone feature on the page, but the rich menu can link to a reservation site, and a beauty package bundles a reservation mini app.

## How Do You Show Up on Google Search and Maps?

Claim and verify your Business Profile, then keep address, hours, category and attributes accurate. Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Reviews, replies, photos and links to your website feed prominence. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) walks through the setup.

If you serve customers at their location rather than at a shop, Google lets you set a service area and hide the address. For paid media, see our [CTV advertising in Japan guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-japan-small-business-guide).

## Which Channel Should Come First?

Use Google Business Profile to be found by new customers and LINE to keep them. A person searching "ramen near me" sees your profile; a person who has already visited can add your LINE account at the till through a QR code or coupon offer.

Market-share figures for Instagram, Tabelog and Hot Pepper Beauty, and Japan cost-per-click benchmarks, could not be verified from a primary source for this page. Treat those channels as options to test, not as ranked recommendations.

## The Math: Will a Salon Stay Inside the Light Plan?

All inputs are **assumptions**, not published results.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Friends who added the account | 600 |
| Broadcasts per month | 4 |
| Messages sent per month | 2,400 |
| Light plan allowance | 5,000 |
| Monthly fee | ¥5,000 |
| Fee per message sent | about ¥2.1 |

Six hundred friends receiving four broadcasts means 2,400 messages, under the Light allowance of 5,000. Headroom allows up to roughly 8 broadcasts to 600 friends. If the list grows past about 1,250 friends at four broadcasts a month, the Light allowance runs out, and Light does not allow extra messages, so the shop would need Standard.

## FAQ

### Is LINE free for businesses?
Opening an account is free, and the Communication plan costs ¥0 with 200 messages a month. Light and Standard are paid plans, priced before tax.

### Can I send unlimited LINE messages?
No. Each plan has a monthly message allowance. Only Standard allows extra messages, charged per message.

### Do I need Google Ads in Japan?
Not at first. A verified Business Profile is free. Google Ads costs in Japan were not verified for this page, so test with a small budget and your own conversion data.

### Can LINE take bookings?
The rich menu can link to a booking page, and some packages include a reservation mini app. Check the current package list for your industry.
