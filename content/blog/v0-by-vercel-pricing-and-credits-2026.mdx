## How Much Does v0 Cost?

The v0 pricing page shows four plans: Free, Plus, Business and Enterprise. The documentation also lists a fifth, Premium, that new users cannot join.

| Plan | Price | Credits and limits the vendor lists |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 a month | 7 messages a day, per the pricing page. New users get a one-time $5 credit. Includes deploying to Vercel, Design Mode and GitHub sync |
| Premium | $20 a month | $20 of credits a month. Being sunsetted and not open to new users |
| Plus | $30 per user a month | $30 of credits a month per user, $2 daily login credits, all models, extra credits can be bought, chats can be shared with a team |
| Business | $100 per user a month | Everything in Plus, $30 of credits a month per user, and training opt-out by default |
| Enterprise | Custom | Data never used for training, single sign-on, role-based access, priority access with no queues and support guarantees |

Prices are in US dollars and exclude taxes, which are calculated from the billing address. The pricing page text shows two figures next to Plus ($30 and $90), without saying which applies; the documentation lists $30. Check the figure shown at checkout before paying.

The pricing page names no annual price, so none is given here.

## What Is v0 and How Does It Work?

v0 describes itself as "an AI agent that helps anyone create real code and full-stack apps and agents". Its documentation says you can build anything from landing pages and dashboards to ecommerce stores and AI apps. You type what you want, v0 writes the code and shows a preview, and you can deploy "with one click" to infrastructure powered by Vercel.

The docs add that you can deploy straight to production or open a pull request for review. A Vercel account gives access to v0, so no separate sign-up is needed. For the full set of features such as environment variables and previews, a GitHub repository should connect to a Vercel project, though code can also be exported and deployed elsewhere.

In practice v0 sits close to [Lovable](/blog/lovable-pricing-and-credits-2026) and Bolt.new: you describe a page in plain English and get a working result. The difference on the pricing page is the unit. v0 shows dollars of credit rather than a count of credits or tokens.

## How Do v0 Credits Work?

According to the pricing documentation, credits are a prepaid balance and each generation draws from it. The cost depends on tokens:

- **Input tokens** cover your prompt, uploaded files and context v0 adds, such as chat history and source files.
- **Output tokens** are what v0 generates.
- **The model matters.** Tokens per credit vary by model. Hovering over a model in the selector shows its cost.

Longer prompts and larger outputs use more tokens. The pages give no price for a typical landing page, so none is assumed here.

Rules that matter for budgeting:

- **Daily login credits.** Premium, Plus and Business get $2 the first time they use v0 each UTC day. These expire at 00:00 UTC and do not accumulate. Free and Enterprise do not receive them.
- **Monthly credits roll over** to the next billing cycle and expire 65 days after they were granted.
- **Purchased credits** stay in the balance if a subscription lapses, but an active paid plan is needed to use them. They expire one year after purchase.
- **Shared Credit Pool.** On Plus, Business and Enterprise, pooled credits are shared across the team, are used after individual monthly credits and expire one year after purchase.
- **When credits run out**, the documentation says "generation pauses". Paid plans can buy more.

ChatGPT Plus and Pro subscribers can connect an account to cover eligible responses from supported OpenAI models. A v0 credit balance is still required, because each request carries a small platform fee and image work uses credits too.

## What Does the Free Plan Include?

The pricing page lists Free access to v0, deploying apps to Vercel, visual editing with Design Mode and GitHub sync, with a limit of 7 messages a day. The documentation describes Free as "No cost. For people looking to explore."

The sources also differ slightly on the allowance. The documentation says new users get a one-time $5 credit and that existing Free users keep a $5 monthly allowance, while the pricing page banner reads "$5 in one-time credits for new users". If you are a new user, treat the $5 as a one-time trial.

For a small business, Free is enough to test whether v0 can produce a page you like. It is a thin base for ongoing edits, since a 7-message daily cap ends sessions quickly.

## What Are the Rules and Risks?

From v0's own FAQ and documentation:

- **Who owns the code.** The FAQ says "Vercel doesn't own the code generated based on your queries and prompts." It also warns that output may resemble other users' output or third-party intellectual property, may be incomplete or buggy, and that you are responsible for evaluating whether it suits commercial or other purposes.
- **Data use.** By default, v0 may use prompts and content as inputs to models and learning systems from third-party providers. Enterprise customers and teams that opted out of model training in Vercel settings are excluded. The FAQ also says customer data from Vercel's platform services is not used to train the models used by v0. Business lists training opt-out by default on the pricing page.
- **Sensitive content.** Do not paste customer lists, staff records or private pricing into prompts on a plan with training on.
- **Credit surprises.** Long chats cost more because chat history counts as input tokens.
- **Account needed.** A Vercel account is required, and the full workflow assumes a GitHub repository.
- **Review.** Test forms, links and phone numbers before launch.

This guide did not open Vercel's full terms of service, so licence terms beyond the FAQ wording are not stated.

## FAQ

### Is v0 free?

Yes, the Free plan costs $0 and includes deploying to Vercel, Design Mode and GitHub sync, with a limit of 7 messages a day. New users also get a one-time $5 credit, per the documentation.

### How much does v0 cost per month?

Plus is $30 per user a month and Business is $100 per user a month, both with $30 of monthly credits and $2 of daily login credits. Premium at $20 a month is no longer open to new users.

### What happens when v0 credits run out?

The documentation says generation pauses. Paid plans can buy more credits at any time, and purchased credits expire one year after purchase. Monthly credits expire 65 days after they are granted.

### Can I use v0 for a business website?

v0 lists landing pages among what it builds, and Vercel says it does not own the code generated from your prompts. You are responsible for checking that the output suits commercial use. Review every page before it goes live.

### Is v0 worth it for a small business?

It can be for simple pages and tools if someone reviews the result. Test on Free first, then pay for one month of Plus ($30) to see how quickly the credits are used.
