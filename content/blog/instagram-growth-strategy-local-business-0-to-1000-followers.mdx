## Is Instagram Worth It for a Local Business?

Instagram is large. Adobe Express reports that at least 1.7 billion people use it, citing DataReportal. The largest age group is 25 to 34, followed by 18 to 24, and users are reported as 48% female and 52% male. Bookeo, a booking software vendor, says about 81% of people use Instagram to research a service, though it names no source for that figure.

Whether it fits depends on who buys from you. A business serving people under 35 is closest to the reported age profile. Bookeo also states that Facebook and Instagram can reach audiences and build trust without spending a dollar on the platform itself, so the starting cost is mainly time. If your customers are mostly older, test Instagram against your other channels before committing hours to it.

## What Should a Local Business Post Each Week?

Bellwether Coffee's guide recommends three to five feed posts a week on Instagram, plus daily Stories. It is written for coffee shops, so treat that range as a starting point. Stories are temporary: Associated Hair Professionals notes that top influencers use Instagram or Facebook stories to share insights into their lives, and those posts disappear after a set period. That suits quick behind-the-scenes clips and same-day offers.

Content about your own work tends to hold attention better than generic trends. Infegy reports that GRWM (Get Ready With Me) videos exploded in early 2023 across TikTok and Instagram, after which engagement and post volume declined as content shifted toward lifestyle storytelling. Mix service demonstrations, team faces and answers to customer questions. Trafft suggests using Instagram and Facebook to showcase your work, run targeted ads and offer promotions.

Start with a modest volume. Appointy advises against more than two or three posts a day at the beginning, because high volume can curb the reach of earlier posts when an account has few followers. ## How Much Daily Time Should Instagram Take?

Bellwether Coffee lists Instagram at about 30 minutes per day for a coffee shop, which is a useful planning figure for a small team. Bookeo says Instagram users reportedly spend an average of 33.1 minutes a day on the platform, without naming a source, and that Gen Z spends 53 minutes. Those are consumer habits, not a guide to how long your account needs.

Treat the 30 minutes as a fixed block: one filming session, one caption and keyword pass, and a daily check of comments and Stories. Choose a block you can keep for months, because a schedule you abandon after three weeks produces no data. Planning tools can reduce the admin. Sintra's example claims that a calendar which once took a full Sunday can be built in under an hour with an AI agent. ## How Do Collabs and Local Partnerships Help?

Marketing Together describes micro-influencers as having a smaller audience, usually between 1,000 and 100,000 followers, but a highly engaged one. Blackstone Infomatics recommends that pet shops start with micro-influencers who have 5,000 to 50,000 followers. Those ranges are useful for a local business, because a creator near you may reach people you already serve.

The simplest partnership is a neighbouring business with overlapping customers and no direct competition, such as a café and a bookshop on the same street. Plan one joint post or a shared giveaway, and agree in writing who posts what and when. If a partner receives a free product or payment, say so in the post so followers are not misled. ## What Should You Track Toward 1,000 Followers?

Engagement rate is the simplest way to compare posts. Groomsoft defines it as (number of engagements divided by number of followers) multiplied by 100. Bellwether Coffee sets a goal of 3% to 6% on Instagram. Choose which actions count as engagements, use the same definition every month, and compare posts only against your own history.

One of One Group, a London agency, sends clients a monthly report on followers, reach and enquiries. That is a sound model to copy. Followers show growth, reach shows distribution, and enquiries show business effect. Log each post with its date, format, topic, reach and enquiries. ## How Many Posts Does It Take to Reach 1,000 Followers?

Every input in this example is an assumption, not a benchmark.

1. Assumption: you publish 12 posts a month, which is three a week.
2. Assumption: each post reaches 1,500 non-followers on average.
3. Assumption: 1 in 100 of those people follows you, so 12 x 1,500 x 1% = 180 follows a month.
4. Assumption: customers you ask in person add 20 follows a month.
5. Total: 200 follows a month, so 1,000 followers takes about five months.

If average reach is only 500 per post, follows fall to 12 x 500 x 1% = 60, plus 20, which is 80 a month. Then 1,000 followers takes about 12.5 months. Reach per post is the number to improve first.

Check engagement too. Assumption: a 1,000-follower account gets 40 engagements on a typical post. Using Groomsoft's formula, (40 / 1,000) x 100 = 4%, inside Bellwether's 3% to 6% goal.

Paid reach is an option, though the cost data is dated. Adobe Express cites 2021 AdRoll figures of $0.20 to $2 per click. Assumption: a $100 test at $1 per click buys about 100 clicks, which is a trial rather than a follower plan. The [Instagram Reels ads guide](/blog/instagram-reels-ads-how-to-use-them-for-local-business-growth) explains how to set up that test. Purchased followers add a number without enquiries, so they will not show up in your results.

## FAQ

### How many times a week should a local business post on Instagram?
Bellwether Coffee's guide suggests three to five feed posts a week plus daily Stories for a coffee shop. Start inside that range, then adjust using your own reach and enquiry data.

### Should I post more than once a day to grow faster?
Not at the start. Appointy advises against more than two or three posts a day early on, because high volume can curb the reach of earlier posts when an account has few followers.

### Are Reels better than photos?
No source here compares Reels and photos on reach. Test both over a few weeks and compare reach and enquiries in your own Insights data.

### Should I buy followers or run ads?
Do not buy followers, since they add a number without adding customers. Ad costs vary, and the dated $0.20 to $2 per-click range is only a rough guide, so test with a small budget first.
