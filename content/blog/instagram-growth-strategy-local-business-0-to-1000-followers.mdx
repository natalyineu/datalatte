To reach 1,000 real followers, a local business should post original Reels and photos about its own products and people, use keywords in its bio and captions, run Collab posts with neighbouring businesses, and ask customers to follow. Instagram itself says one of the most effective ways to grow is to post original content consistently. No source publishes how long it takes, so set targets and review them monthly.

This guide separates what Instagram and Pew publish from what is a judgment call. The follower math at the end is an **assumption**, not a benchmark.

## Is Instagram Worth It for a Local Business?

It depends on who your customers are. Pew's 2026 survey found 51% of US adults use Instagram, including 79% of those aged 18 to 29, 64% of 30 to 49, 41% of 50 to 64 and 21% of those 65 and over. Women are at 56%, men at 45%.

If your customers are mostly under 50, the audience is there. If they are older, Instagram may help less than a good Google profile. Check the [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) alongside this guide.

## How Does Instagram Decide Who Sees Your Posts?

Instagram says each part of the app, Feed, Stories, Reels and Explore, ranks content differently. Reels are judged mainly on whether people are likely to watch to the end, reshare or like. Explore weighs information about the post itself more than Feed or Stories do. Instagram also says most Explore and Reels content comes from accounts people do not follow.

That is the opening for a small account: a good Reel can reach strangers without a following. Instagram's creators site also says to keep Reels to three minutes or less to be recommended and to avoid reposted content.

## What Should You Post to Get Followers?

Original content about what you actually do:

- Reels showing your work or product in action
- A "meet the team" post and a short tour of the place
- Answers to questions customers ask in person
- Offers and events with dates

Instagram advises you to avoid reposts, to expect less recommendation of watermarked clips, and to use the Remix tool to react to a popular reel. It also lists keywords in your content, caption, bio and hashtags as a way to be found in search. Use your service and town in plain words, such as "dog grooming in Leeds."

Instagram's January 2025 update also introduced Trial Reels, which show a reel to non-followers first so you can see how it does before sharing with followers.

## How Do Collabs and Local Partnerships Help?

Instagram's Collabs feature creates a single post shared to both accounts' followers. Pair with a neighbouring business whose customers overlap with yours but who is not a competitor, for example a café and a bookshop on the same street.

Do not offer free products to creators without disclosure. The FTC says free or discounted items are a material connection and must be disclosed in the content, using clear words such as "ad" or "sponsored." ## What Should You Track?

Instagram says to look for patterns in which content gets more reach. Check Insights weekly for reach among non-followers, profile visits, follows and link taps. Likes alone do not show business impact.

Keep a simple log: date, format, topic, reach, follows. After a month, repeat the top two formats. Also check Account Status, which Instagram says shows whether your content can be recommended to non-followers.

## A Worked Example: How Many Posts Reach 1,000 Followers?

All numbers are **assumptions** to show the arithmetic.

- You publish 3 Reels or posts a week, 12 a month
- Each reaches 1,500 non-followers on average
- 1 in 100 of those people follows you
- Monthly follows: 12 x 1,500 x 1% = 180
- Add 20 a month from customers asked in person, 200 total
- Time to 1,000: about 5 months

If the average reach is 500, it takes about 15 months, so reach per post is the number to improve. Do not buy followers. Bought followers do not visit or buy. ## FAQ

### How many times a week should I post?
Instagram gives no required number, only to post original content consistently. Choose a schedule you can sustain, such as three posts a week.

### Do hashtags still work?
Instagram's guidance names hashtags as one place to include relevant keywords. It does not publish an ideal count. Use a few that match your service and town.

### Should I buy ads to get followers?
Ads can promote posts, but no benchmark here shows ad cost per follower. See our [Instagram ads guide](/blog/instagram-ads-for-local-business-complete-2026-guide) for cost data.

### Are Reels better than photos?
Reels make up over 50% of time spent on Instagram, so they have more reach potential. Mix formats and compare results in Insights.

### Why is my reach low?
Check Account Status for recommendation limits, avoid reposts and watermarks, and test shorter, clearer Reels.
