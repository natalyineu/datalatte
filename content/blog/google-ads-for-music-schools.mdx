## How Much Does Google Ads Cost for a Music School?

LocaliQ lists Education and Instruction at $4.81 per click, a 7.56% click-through rate and $77.48 per lead. Arts and Entertainment is far cheaper at $1.63 per click and $26.84 per lead, with a 5.91% conversion rate. The all-industry average is $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead.

Which row is closer depends on the search. Someone typing "piano lessons near me" wants instruction, so Education is the safer planning range. LocaliQ does not publish a music school row, so your own account data after the first month is the real benchmark.

## Who Are You Actually Advertising To?

Parents and adults, not children. Google's ad-serving protections for teens state that ads personalization is not allowed for users under 18 on Google Accounts. You cannot build a campaign around interests or age targeting for young students, and you do not need to. Parents search for the lessons.

Split campaigns by buyer, because the words and the landing page differ:

- **Parents:** child lessons by instrument, with age ranges, trial lesson and trial instrument rental if you offer it.
- **Adults:** beginner lessons, evening or weekend slots, and online options if you teach them.

## How Do You Keep the Campaign Local?

Students travel a short distance to weekly lessons, so location settings decide your cost. Google's help says radius targeting requires a radius of at least 1 km around a location. It also says broad geo targeting ("Presence or interest") is recommended as a best practice on Search, and that "Presence" alone suits cases such as a sensitive vertical with strict targeting limits.

For a studio that only teaches in person, test "Presence" if the search terms report shows clicks from towns you cannot serve. For online lessons, wider targeting is reasonable.

## Which Keywords and Negatives Work for Lessons?

Start with instrument plus intent plus place: "guitar lessons" with your town, "piano teacher near me", "violin lessons for kids". Add one ad group per instrument so each ad names the instrument and links to a matching page.

Negative keywords stop wasted spend. Google explains that negatives exclude search terms and do not match close variants, so add singular and plural forms yourself. Likely negatives for a school:

- Free and DIY intent, such as "free", "tutorial", "online course" if you teach in person
- Job and training intent, such as "teacher jobs", "salary", "degree"
- Instruments you do not teach and products, such as "buy" or "sheet music"

Review the Search terms report weekly for the first two months.

## Should You Track Calls and Trial Bookings?

Yes. A trial lesson booking is the conversion that matters. Google says clicks on a call asset on mobile cost the same as headline clicks. For call conversions you set a minimum call length, and every call that lasts at least that long counts as a conversion. Pick a length that reflects a real enquiry, not a wrong number.

Track the booking form too, and ask each new student how they found you. Match both against the ads report so you know which instrument campaigns bring students who stay. ## What Does a Budget Buy? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions**, built on the Education and Instruction benchmarks. A conversion here is an enquiry (call or form), not an enrolled student.

At these assumed numbers a new student pays back the ad cost in under one month of lessons, and more if they stay a year. If only 10% of enquiries enrol, cost per student rises to about $366, over two months of fees. Your enrolment rate from enquiry is the number to measure first.

## FAQ

### How much should a music school spend on Google Ads?
There is no standard figure. At LocaliQ's Education and Instruction average of $4.81 per click, $500 a month buys roughly 100 clicks. Start small and scale once you know your cost per enrolled student.

### Can I target children with ads?
No. Google does not allow ads personalization for users under 18 on Google Accounts. Target parents and adult learners instead.

### Is a radius of 1 km possible?
Google requires a radius of at least 1 km around a location. In a dense city that can still be a tight area.

### Do phone calls count as conversions?
They can. You set the minimum call length that counts as a conversion, so choose one that reflects a real enquiry.

### Is there a published CPC for music lessons?
LocaliQ does not publish a music school row. The nearest are Education and Instruction ($4.81) and Arts and Entertainment ($1.63).
