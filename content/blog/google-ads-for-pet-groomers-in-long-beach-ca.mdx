## What Does Public Data Say About Long Beach Customers?

Census Reporter, which republishes American Community Survey data, lists Long Beach at 450,917 people and 170,773 households in its 2024 one-year estimates. Median household income is $91,318 (margin of error about $3,306), and the median home value is $849,500.

Mean travel time to work is 31.2 minutes. A long commute means many owners are away from home for most of the day, which is a reason to show hours, drop-off options and booking links clearly in your ads. That last point is a recommendation, not a finding from the data.

## How Big Is the Grooming Business Around You?

The US Bureau of Labor Statistics' Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages reports, for Los Angeles County in 2024, 737 private establishments in NAICS 812910, Pet Care (except Veterinary) Services, with an annual average of 4,475 employees. The average weekly wage in that category was $793, compared with $1,563 across all private industries in the county.

This is county data, not Long Beach alone, and it covers grooming, boarding, sitting and training together. Read it as a sign that many small pet businesses compete across the county, not as a count of Long Beach groomers. Check Google Maps for groomers within 10 minutes of your shop.

## How Should You Target Long Beach?

Google's help says that for most campaign types you can choose a radius around a location, and that location targeting is best effort, with no guarantee of perfect accuracy. For a shop, draw a radius around your address and check the location report to see where clicks come from.

For a mobile groomer, choose the neighborhoods or ZIP codes you will drive to. Avoid targeting all of Los Angeles County if you do not serve it. Ask new clients for their ZIP code for a few weeks, then draw the radius from that.

## Which Keywords Fit a Long Beach Groomer?

Google defines phrase match as searches that include your keyword's meaning and exact match as the most control with the fewest searches. With a small budget, start on phrase and exact.

| Ad group | Example keywords | Match |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core service | "dog grooming near me", [dog groomer long beach] | Phrase and exact |
| Cats | "cat grooming near me" | Phrase |
| Mobile | "mobile dog grooming long beach" | Phrase |
| Need-based | "senior dog grooming", "puppy first groom" | Phrase |

Add negative keywords such as `jobs`, `school`, `course`, `diy` and `supplies`. Google notes negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals yourself. See our [pet groomer keyword list](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-pet-groomers-in-2026) for more.

## FAQ

### How much do Google Ads cost for a Long Beach groomer?

No Long Beach figure is published. LocaliQ's 2026 national average for Animals and Pets is $4.06 per click and $31.50 per lead. Use those to plan, then replace them with your own numbers.

### Should I target all of Long Beach?

Only if you serve it all. A radius around your shop usually wastes less than a whole city, because most customers choose a nearby groomer. That is a rule of thumb, not a published statistic.

### Is county data useful?

It shows how many pet care businesses exist across Los Angeles County, but not how many compete with you. Count nearby groomers on Google Maps for a direct view.

### Do I need a website?

A landing page with prices, hours and a booking button gives clicks somewhere useful to go. Without one, calls and Business Profile actions can serve as conversions.
