A Virginia small business should treat visitor spending and residents as two separate audiences. Tourism Economics reports that 117.1 million visitors spent $36.2 billion in Virginia in 2025, up 3.1%, with food and beverage the largest category at $10.51 billion. 

Fix your Google Business Profile, run a small Search test aimed at a few miles around your location, and track calls and bookings. Virginia's population is also growing faster than most.

This guide is for restaurants, studios, gyms, retailers and service firms in Virginia. Cost figures are US-wide benchmarks. Anything unverified is marked.

## What Does the Virginia Market Look Like?

Growing. BLS lists Virginia's September 2026 unemployment rate at 3.6% (preliminary, seasonally adjusted), with 4,250.6 thousand nonfarm jobs, down 0.6% over 12 months. Leisure and hospitality jobs number 424.3 thousand, up 0.7%.

The Census Bureau estimates Virginia's population at 8,880,107 in 2025, up 60,465 (0.69%) from 2024, the fastest growth of the four states in this series. Births (92,070) exceeded deaths (78,253) by 13,817, and net migration added 40,436 international and 6,268 domestic. New residents need to find new dentists, gyms and cafes, which favours local search.

## Where Do Visitors Spend Money in Virginia?

Mostly on food and transport. The Virginia Tourism Corporation's 2025 study, produced by Tourism Economics and published in July 2026, puts visitor spending at $36.2 billion, about $99 million a day, and total economic impact at $54.8 billion. It supports 332,302 jobs, 8.0% of all Virginia jobs.

| Spending category, 2025 | Amount | Change on 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food and beverage | $10.51 billion | up 5.7% |
| Transportation | $10.5 billion | not stated here |
| Lodging | $6.80 billion | up 2.7% |
| Recreation | $4.78 billion | up 4.9% |
| Retail | $3.66 billion | up 2.5% |

International visitor spending fell 7.3% to $1.35 billion (preliminary), while domestic spending rose 3.5% to $34.86 billion. A restaurant, an attraction or a shop that markets to domestic travellers is aiming at most of the spending.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local in Virginia?

Set a radius around the business and leave out places you cannot serve. Google's help page says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km. It recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting as the best practice on Search, and says "Presence" only suits cases where you want to exclude people who are elsewhere but interested in your offer.

Virginia has dense northern suburbs, mid-sized cities and rural areas. In Northern Virginia a 2 to 4 mile radius may fit, which is an assumption to check against customer addresses. In rural areas, drive distances grow and a wider radius may be needed.

Add negative keywords weekly from the Search terms report: jobs, free, DIY and services you do not offer. Google notes that negative keywords do not match close variants, so add plurals and synonyms yourself.

## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Virginia?

Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. It also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Fill every field: categories, hours, services, service area and website. For visitor-facing businesses, keep seasonal hours current and add photos of the menu or entrance. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes through each field.

## Can You Offer a Discount for a Google Review?

No. Google's contributed content policy says merchants may not offer incentives, such as payment, discounts, free goods or services, in exchange for posting a review. You may ask customers for a review. You may not reward them for one. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for ways to ask.

## Where Can Virginia Owners Get Free Advice?

The Virginia SBDC network provides no-cost individualized consulting. The Virginia Highlands Community College center says it offers no-cost individualized consulting sessions for businesses at all stages, and the Shenandoah Valley SBDC page says its advisory services are no-fee and confidential. Search results say George Mason University leads the statewide network, but that detail is from search summaries and the Mason page returned an error.

## What Mistakes Do Virginia Owners Make?

These are recommendations, not study findings.

- Ignoring newcomers. A growing population searches for providers by map.
- Treating all visitors alike. Domestic travellers account for most spending.
- Targeting the whole state. Customers come from a few miles around.
- Spreading thin. Test one channel for 60 days. See our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide).
