Local SEO for a small business usually costs between $500 and $3,000 per month on a retainer, with a typical single-location price near $1,500. Ahrefs' survey of 439 SEO providers puts the average local SEO price at $1,557 per month ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-pricing/)). WebFX, surveying 250 US marketing and sales professionals, gives a retainer range of $501 to $3,000 ([WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/local-seo/pricing/)). Both are vendor surveys, so treat them as market signals, not quotes.

## What do the price surveys say local SEO costs?

Two vendor surveys give the clearest public numbers. Neither is independent research, and both list the figures as reported by providers or buyers.

| Source | Sample | Monthly retainer | Other prices |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-pricing/) | 439 SEO service providers | $1,557 average for local SEO; agencies $1,819; freelancers about $1,150 | Most common local hourly rate: $75 to $100 |
| [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/local-seo/pricing/) | 250 US marketing and sales professionals | $501 to $3,000 | One-time projects $500 to $5,000; consulting $75 to $200 per hour |

Caveats matter here. The Ahrefs page was last updated in August 2024 and does not state the year of the survey. The WebFX page does not state a date either. Prices have likely risen since, so use these as a floor for budgeting, not a ceiling.

WebFX also reports that micro businesses (1 to 10 employees) and small businesses (11 to 50) most often spend $100 to $500 per month. That is typically a lighter, tools-and-basics scope, not a full agency retainer.

## What does each price tier usually include?

Public surveys do not publish a standard menu, so the tiers below are a planning frame built from the price bands above. Ask any provider to confirm exactly what sits in their package.

- **Under $500 per month:** a one-location starter. Usually Google Business Profile upkeep, review requests and citation clean-up.
- **$500 to $1,500 per month:** adds on-page work for your service and location pages, plus regular profile posts and reporting.
- **$1,500 to $3,000 per month:** adds content, link outreach and multi-page strategy. Fits competitive cities or two to three locations.

Know what Google does not sell. Google's help page says: "There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google" ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)). It says local results rely on relevance, distance and prominence. A provider can improve the first and third. Nobody can change the second.

## Is local SEO worth the money for a small business?

It is worth it when one extra customer pays for a meaningful part of the fee. Test that before you sign anything.

Every figure below is an **assumption** for illustration. Replace them with your own.

| Assumption | Coffee shop | Hair salon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly local SEO fee | $600 | $600 |
| Average ticket | $9 | $85 |
| Gross margin on a ticket | 65% | 60% |
| Margin per visit | $5.85 | $51 |
| Extra visits needed to break even | 103 per month | 12 per month |

The coffee shop needs about 3.4 extra visits a day. The salon needs about 3 extra bookings a week. That difference is why local SEO suits higher-ticket and repeat-booking services more easily than low-ticket counters. A cafe leans on loyalty and repeat visits to make it work.

You can count real results for free. Google's [Business Profile performance report](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9918094) shows calls, directions, website clicks and bookings. Record last month's numbers before work starts, so you have a baseline.

## Should I do local SEO myself or hire someone?

Do it yourself when you have the time and your profile is not set up yet. Hire when time is your tightest resource or the market is crowded.

- **DIY:** costs your hours. Price them honestly. At an assumed $40 per hour of your time, 10 hours a month is $400 of time, close to a freelancer's starter rate.
- **Freelancer (about $1,150 per month on average in Ahrefs' local data):** good for one location with clear scope.
- **Agency (about $1,819 per month on average in the same data):** good for several locations or heavy competition, with account management.
- **One-off project ($500 to $5,000 per WebFX):** right for a site or profile fix, not for ongoing work.

## How do I avoid overpaying for local SEO?

Ask direct questions and get the answers in writing.

1. Which locations, pages and profiles are in scope, and what is excluded?
2. What will be reported each month? Ask for calls, direction requests and bookings, not only rankings.
3. Who owns the profile and the website content if we stop?
4. What is the minimum term, and can I cancel with 30 days' notice?
5. If you have more than one location, is each one priced and reported separately?

Be wary of any promise of a guaranteed rank or a fixed time to reach the top spot. Google's own page says there is no way to pay for a better local rank. ## FAQ

**Q: How much does local SEO cost per month for a small business?**
A: Surveys put it at roughly $500 to $3,000. Ahrefs reports a $1,557 average for local SEO, and WebFX reports $501 to $3,000 for retainers. Smaller owners often spend $100 to $500 on a lighter scope, per WebFX.

**Q: What is a fair hourly rate for local SEO?**
A: Ahrefs says the most common local SEO hourly rate is $75 to $100. WebFX gives a wider consulting range of $75 to $200 per hour.

**Q: Can I pay Google to rank higher in the map results?**
A: No. Google says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

**Q: Is a freelancer cheaper than an agency?**
A: In Ahrefs' local data, freelancers average about $1,150 per month and agencies $1,819. Freelancers fit a single clear scope. Agencies fit multiple locations.

**Q: How do I know if my local SEO spend is working?**
A: Compare calls, directions, website clicks and bookings in your Business Profile report before and after the work, over the same number of weeks.
