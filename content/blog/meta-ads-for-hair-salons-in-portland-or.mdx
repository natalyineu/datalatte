## How much should a Portland hair salon spend on Meta Ads?

Bookeo's [beauty salon guide](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/) suggests USD 750 to USD 1,500 per month as a starting point for Facebook and Instagram ad spend. AdWave puts Meta spend for [most single-location restaurants](https://adwave.com/resources/restaurant-advertising-complete-marketing-guide) at $500 to $2,000 per month, and for [most veterinary practices](https://adwave.com/resources/veterinary-advertising) at $300 to $1,000. These are category estimates, not salon benchmarks, so treat them as planning ranges.

No Portland-specific Meta benchmark is sourced here, so your own account data should set the final number. Start near the low end, then review results after the first few weeks. AdWave's channel comparison lists [1 to 4 weeks](https://adwave.com/resources/restaurant-advertising-complete-marketing-guide) before Meta results appear, so judge a campaign only after that window.

## Which ad formats work best for a hair salon?

Short-form video is the strongest format candidate. Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, as cited by AdWave in its [electrician advertising guide](https://adwave.com/resources/electrician-advertising-reaching-homeowners), says short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram. That is an engagement figure, not a booking figure, so check it against your own cost per booking.

Authentic footage tends to work better for salons than polished stock content. In Portland, stylist Jules Heron built an authentic brand around gender-affirming haircuts and shared consultations and transformations on TikTok, according to [Zeely's salon advertising article](https://zeely.ai/blog/hair-salon-advertising-ideas/). The same approach transfers to Meta: film real consultations, colour processes and finished results in your chair. For more on short video, see [TikTok marketing for hair salons](/blog/tiktok-marketing-for-hair-salons).

Stories are temporary by design, which suits same-week offers. One [hairstyling industry article](https://www.associatedhairprofessionals.com/updates/blog-posts/power-social-media-your-hairstyling-brand) describes Instagram and Facebook stories as temporary formats. Carousels can show a service menu. Run a test of authentic footage against polished images and compare cost per booking for each.

## Who should the ads reach, and how far?

Bookeo recommends a [5 to 10 mile radius](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/) around the salon for Facebook and Instagram ads. That is a sensible starting point for most Portland salons. Tighten it in dense neighbourhoods and widen it only if booking data supports the change.

Set age and gender filters around the services you sell. Colour, cuts and barbering attract different clients, so build a separate ad set for each. Meta Ads Manager can run campaigns across Instagram and Facebook from one place, as [Adobe Express explains](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-advertising), and [Turrboo lists](https://turrboo.com/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media) Messenger as a placement too. Past clients can be uploaded as a custom audience. For a fuller account setup, read [Facebook ads for hair salons](/blog/facebook-ads-for-hair-salons).

## Which campaign goal should a salon choose?

Restaurants can set Meta campaigns to drive traffic or reservations across Facebook, Instagram or both, as [The Fork Manager describes](https://www.theforkmanager.com/en/blog/introduction-paid-ads-restaurants). Salons can apply the same logic: pick a goal that leads to a booking action rather than one that counts impressions.

Awareness and retargeting have a role too. Bookeo states that a client who has seen a salon's brand on Facebook 5 to 10 times before searching "hair salon near me" converts at 2 to 3 times the rate of a cold searcher, though the page gives no source for this claim. Treat it as a vendor claim and test it on your own clients.

## How do you measure whether Meta Ads bring bookings?

Software Pro Digital says it [reports Meta ad results on bookings and orders rather than reach](https://softwareprodigital.com/industries/pet-marketing/meta-ads-for-pet-businesses). Use the same standard for your salon. A basic setup looks like this:

1. Install the Meta Pixel on your website, including the booking confirmation page.
2. Set a Lead or booking event that fires when a client completes a booking.
3. Add UTM parameters to every ad URL so Google Analytics can separate Meta traffic.
4. Review cost per booking each week, not impressions.

Once bookings arrive, [marketing automation for hair salons](/blog/marketing-automation-for-hair-salons-guide) can handle confirmations and rebooking reminders. Check peak follower activity in the Insights tab of Instagram or Facebook, which Bookeo notes requires a business account.

## How should Meta Ads fit with organic posting and Google Ads?

Bookeo says most salon owners find three to four Instagram posts and one to two Facebook posts per week sustainable. Organic posting keeps the page active between paid campaigns, which helps when someone checks your profile before booking. For Instagram planning, see [Instagram marketing for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons).

Google and Meta do different jobs. Google Search Ads reach people actively searching for services, while Meta Ads introduce your business to people browsing Facebook and Instagram, as [Pawfectly Digital explains](https://www.pawfectlydigital.com/blog/meta-ads.html). Run Google Ads to capture existing demand and Meta to build demand. For Portland search campaigns, see [Google Ads for hair salons in Portland, OR](/blog/google-ads-for-hair-salons-in-portland-or).

## FAQ

### How much should a hair salon spend on Facebook ads each month?

Bookeo suggests a starting range of USD 750 to USD 1,500 per month for Facebook and Instagram ads. Increase spend only after cost per booking is stable over several weeks.

### How large should the ad radius be for a Portland salon?

Bookeo recommends a 5 to 10 mile radius around the salon. Narrow it in dense areas and widen it only if your booking data supports the change.

### Are Meta Ads better than Google Ads for salons?

They serve different purposes. Google Ads reach people already searching for services, while Meta Ads introduce your salon to people browsing Facebook and Instagram. Most salons benefit from running both.

### Is video better than photos for salon ads?

AdWave, citing Meta's 2025 report, says short-form video generates 2.5 times the engagement of static images. Engagement does not always mean bookings, so compare cost per booking between formats before choosing one.
