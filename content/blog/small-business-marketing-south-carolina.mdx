## What Does the 2025 Tourism Data Mean for Small Businesses?

WRHI reported in February 2026 that the South Carolina Department of Parks, Recreation and Tourism put 2025 tourism impact at $31 billion, $1 billion above the previous record, with $6 billion in hotel and vacation rental revenue. The article also says Myrtle Beach and the Grand Strand reported a 3% decline in occupancy. Officials linked it to a lack of international travel and broader economic pressure. Inland and nature-based destinations are growing.

Takeaway: a record statewide total can hide weaker spots. Compare your own monthly sales before assuming visitor demand will fill your calendar.

## How Should You Target Visitors Versus Residents?

Google's location help says "Presence or interest" reaches people in or interested in your location, while "Presence" limits ads to people physically there. A Charleston tour-adjacent business may add pre-trip searchers. A Greenville dentist or plumber serves residents and should use "Presence".

Radius targeting needs a minimum radius of 1 km. Keep the radius to the area people will travel. For a hair salon example, see [Google Ads for hair salons](/blog/google-ads-for-hair-salons-complete-guide).

## How Do You Win the Map Listing?

Google says relevance, distance and prominence rank local results and that you cannot pay for better placement. Prominence includes links from other sites and review counts and ratings.

Keep hours accurate: Google says to use special hours for holidays and temporary changes. Storm closures and seasonal hours matter on the coast. Work through the [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## Where Does Social Media Fit?

Pew's 2026 survey found Facebook used by 70% of US adults and Instagram by 51%, with Facebook stronger in rural areas (76% against 67%). Pew does not publish South Carolina figures. Read [social media for local businesses](/blog/social-media-for-local-businesses) for a platform decision method.

## FAQ

### How much should a South Carolina business spend on Google Ads?

No state figure exists. At $5.42 a click, $600 a month buys about 110 clicks. Start with money you can afford to lose while you learn.

### Is coastal tourism safe to rely on?

Not on its own. The Grand Strand reported a 3% occupancy decline in 2025 while the statewide total hit a record. Track your own sales.

### Should I target visitors or locals?

Pick based on where your sales come from. Use "Presence or interest" for pre-trip visitors and "Presence" for locals.

### Is Google Business Profile worth it?

Yes. It is free and affects how prominent you are in local results.
