In Switzerland, nearly everyone is online, and the biggest ad audiences are YouTube and LinkedIn, not Facebook. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 8.89 million internet users (99% of 8.98 million people), a YouTube ad reach of 7.27 million, LinkedIn at 5.30 million members and Instagram at 3.80 million. Facebook reaches 2.95 million.

This guide uses only figures from pages that were opened and are listed in Sources. No verified Swiss ad-price benchmark was available, so no cost per click or per thousand views is quoted. Read prices from your own ad account.

## Which Platforms Reach Swiss Customers?

DataReportal reports ad reach, which is what each platform's ad tool says it can show ads to. It is not active users, and LinkedIn counts registered members.

| Platform | Ad reach | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | 7.27 million | 81.0% |
| LinkedIn | 5.30 million | 59.0% |
| Instagram | 3.80 million | 42.3% |
| Reddit | 3.50 million | 39.0% |
| Facebook | 2.95 million | 32.9% |
| Snapchat | 2.87 million | 32.0% |
| TikTok (18+) | 2.46 million | 27.6% of internet users |
| X | 2.15 million | 24.0% |

For a local shop, Instagram is the strongest social channel after YouTube. Facebook is smaller than in many countries, so do not assume it is the default. LinkedIn suits a business that sells to other businesses or professionals.

## How Strong Is Swiss Spending Power?

World Bank data for 2025 shows nominal GDP of about US$1.04 trillion, GDP per person of US$114,769, GDP growth of 1.3% and consumer price inflation of 0.2%. Stable prices and high income mean customers compare on quality and trust. They are less sensitive to a small price gap than customers in a high-inflation market.

Customers also have many alternatives nearby, so a weak profile loses to a strong one. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers the fields that help.

## What Do Swiss Rules Say About Advertising Messages?

Switzerland's Federal Act against Unfair Competition (UWG), Article 3, paragraph 1, letter o, makes it unfair to send mass advertising by telecommunications without a direct link to requested content, unless the sender first gets consent, names a correct sender and offers a simple, free way to decline. A narrow exception exists for similar offers to existing customers.

This text comes from justement.ch (secondary source); we could not open the official Fedlex page, so the wording is **unverified**.

The revised Federal Act on Data Protection took effect on 1 September 2023. According to a Webrepublic summary (secondary source), consent is not required for all processing, but data subjects must be informed before data is collected. Check both rules with a Swiss lawyer before you build an email or SMS list. For list-building basics, see our [coffee shop email list guide](/blog/coffee-shop-email-list-building).

## Does Language Change Your Ads?

Switzerland has several language regions, so one ad often does not fit all. The sources we opened gave no language split, so the figures here do not show how many people speak each language. Decide which regions you serve, then write ads and landing pages in the language of those customers rather than translating everything by default.

Test one language first. Add others when the first one produces enquiries at a cost you accept.

## How Do You Split a Test Budget? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for planning. No ad prices are used.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly test budget | CHF 1,000 |
| Google Ads local search | 40% |
| Instagram ads | 30% |
| YouTube short video | 20% |
| Facebook ads | 10% |
| Test length | 4 weeks |

Search gets the biggest share because people looking for a nearby business already want to buy. Instagram and YouTube follow because of their reach in the table. Facebook gets a small test because its reach is the smallest of the four. After four weeks, compare cost per call or visit, then move money. The split is a starting point and your data replaces it.

## FAQ

### Is Facebook worth using in Switzerland?

Yes, as a smaller test. Its ad reach is 2.95 million, below YouTube (7.27 million) and Instagram (3.80 million).

### Which channel suits a local shop first?

Google search and Instagram are sensible starting points. YouTube has the widest reach but needs video.

### Are there verified ad prices for Switzerland?

None could be verified. Use your own account data.

### Can I send promotional emails to a purchased list?

The UWG text we read points to consent, a correct sender and an opt-out. Verify with a Swiss lawyer. Our [SMS marketing guide](/blog/sms-marketing-for-retail) covers consent-based contact.
