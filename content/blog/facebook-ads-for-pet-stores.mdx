A pet store should run Facebook and Instagram ads as a small local campaign: one clear offer, a tight radius around the shop, and a way to count who comes in. WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks put traffic-campaign clicks in Animals and Pets at $0.51 on average, with a 1.85% click-through rate. Those are cheap clicks, which means the offer and the in-store follow-through decide whether the spend pays back.

This guide is for independent pet stores and pet supply shops. It covers what the benchmarks say, what to promote, how to keep ads local and how to measure visits. Meta's own help pages could not be read for this update, so interface details are left for you to confirm in Ads Manager.

## How Much Do Facebook Ads Cost for a Pet Store?

LocaliQ's 2026 Facebook benchmarks list traffic campaigns in Animals and Pets at $0.51 per click and a 1.85% click-through rate. The overall traffic average is $0.60 per click with a 1.93% click-through rate. Shopping, Collectibles and Gifts, the nearest retail category, is $0.59 per click.

The page reports no cost per lead or conversion rate for Animals and Pets on lead campaigns, so there is no published number for what a click is worth to a pet store. Clicks on Meta are also a weaker signal than clicks on Google search, because people are browsing, not searching. Judge the campaign on visits and sales, not clicks.

## Who Is the Audience?

Pet ownership is broad. The American Pet Products Association reports about 95 million US households own a pet in its 2025 National Pet Owners Survey. That means radius targeting alone reaches many owners, and your job is to be specific about the offer and the moment: a new puppy, a food change, a seasonal need.

Equivalent figures for the UK, Australia and Canada come from their own pet industry bodies and are not used here. Check local data before copying US assumptions.

## What Should a Pet Store Advertise?

Pick one thing per campaign and one reason to come in this week. Options that suit a store with a physical shop:

- A new-customer offer, such as a discount on a first visit, redeemed in store
- A single high-margin category, such as fresh or raw food, with a tasting or free sample
- An event, such as an adoption day or a nail-trim afternoon with a local groomer
- Free local pickup for heavy items like litter and large food bags

Keep ad text short and show the product or your own shop and staff. Photos taken in your own store show customers what they will actually find. For online sales, see our [ecommerce Facebook ads guide](/blog/facebook-ads-for-ecommerce).

## How Do You Keep Ads Local?

Set the location to a radius around the store or to named neighborhoods, and place the ad on both Facebook and Instagram. Meta offers choices about whether to reach people who live in an area or were recently there. Confirm the current labels and minimum radius in Ads Manager, since Meta's help center page was not readable when this guide was updated.

Choose a radius based on how far your customers actually travel, which your point-of-sale postcodes or a loyalty list can tell you. A shop in a dense city may only need a short drive-time area. A rural store may need a much wider one.

## How Do You Know It Worked?

Use a method you control. Meta can report clicks, but only your till can report sales.

1. Give each campaign its own offer or code, such as "mention the Facebook offer at checkout".
2. Ask at checkout how the customer heard about you and tally the answers.
3. Compare weekly sales for the promoted product against the weeks before.

## FAQ

### How much should a pet store spend on Facebook ads?
No standard exists. At the $0.51 benchmark, $300 a month buys about 588 link clicks. Start with an amount you can run for four weeks and judge it on redemptions.

### Are Facebook ads good for pet stores?
They can drive visits for a local offer, but the benchmark data only covers click costs, not sales. Test a small campaign before scaling.

### What is the best offer for a pet store ad?
A concrete, limited offer tied to something you stock, such as a first-visit discount or a sample. Avoid generic "shop now" ads without a reason to act.

### How do I track in-store sales from ads?
Use an offer code, ask at checkout, and compare weekly sales of the advertised item with earlier weeks.

### Should I advertise on Google instead?
Search ads reach people who are already looking, but clicks cost more. LocaliQ's 2026 Google Ads figure for Animals and Pets is $4.06 per click. Many stores use both in small amounts.
