## What Is the Google Maps Local Pack?

The local pack is the short list of businesses Google shows on a map for a local search such as "nail salon near me". Google does not publish a formula for who appears. Its help page names three factors: relevance, which is how well your profile matches the search; distance, which is how far you are from the searcher; and prominence, which is how well known you are.

Because distance depends on where the person stands, the same business can appear for one street and vanish two streets away. Test from several spots, not only from your shop.

## Which Steps Come First?

Start with what costs nothing and takes under an hour. Google's help says verified, complete and current profiles are more likely to appear in local results. Work through this order:

1. Verify the profile so Google treats you as the authorised owner.
2. Choose the primary category. Whitespark's experts scored it highest of all factors, at 227.
3. Check the address and the map pin. Google asks for the full and accurate address, and the pin placement scored 165.
4. Set regular and holiday hours. Being open at the time of search scored 189.
5. Add secondary categories that truly describe you (score 173).

Our [categories guide](/blog/google-business-profile-categories-guide) shows how to pick them.

## How Should You Name Your Business on the Profile?

Enter the name exactly as it appears on your signage, stationery and other branding. That is Google's rule. Experts rated keywords in the business title third (223), but adding words that are not in your real name breaks the guidelines, and edits are reviewed before they go live.

If your sign says "Harbour Cafe", the profile says "Harbour Cafe". The category does the keyword work.

## How Do Reviews Affect Local Pack Ranking?

Google says prominence includes review count and ratings, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. Whitespark scores a high star rating at 181 and the quantity of native Google reviews with text at 170. It also says review signals gained importance in the 2026 survey.

Shoppers read them closely. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults found 74% look for reviews from the last three months, and 31% will only use a business rated 4.5 stars or higher. Ask every customer, and use the link or QR code Google provides.

Do not offer discounts or gifts for reviews. Google calls rewards in exchange for reviews strictly prohibited. Our [review strategy guide](/blog/google-business-reviews-strategy) covers wording and timing.

## How Do Photos and Replies Help?

Google lists adding photos and videos, and responding to reviews, among its tips for improving local ranking. Neither is described as a ranking guarantee. They help the person deciding between three listings.

Google accepts JPG or PNG photos from 10 KB to 5 MB, recommends 720 by 720 pixels, and allows videos up to 30 seconds and 75 MB. Reply politely and briefly. Do not use replies to push deals. ## What Is a Realistic Plan for the First Month?

This is a worked example. All inputs are **assumptions**, not benchmarks: a salon with a verified profile, 30 Google reviews and an average of 4.3 stars, and about 60 customers a month.

| Week | Task | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Check primary category, extra categories, address, pin, hours | 1 hour |
| 2 | Add 10 real photos and a 20-second video | 1 hour |
| 3 | Print a QR code for the review link; staff ask every customer | 30 minutes |
| 4 | Reply to every review; check Business Profile calls and directions | 1 hour |

If 1 in 5 of the 60 monthly customers leaves a review, that is 12 new reviews a month. At that pace the profile would reach 54 reviews in two months (30 plus 24). The rate of asking, not the exact number, is what you control. Track calls and direction requests monthly in your Business Profile performance report.

## FAQ

### How do I get into the Google Maps 3-pack?

Verify your profile, pick the right primary category, keep your address and hours accurate, and earn genuine reviews. Google names relevance, distance and prominence as the factors and does not publish a formula or guarantee placement.

### Can I pay to rank higher in the local pack?

No. Google's help page says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Paid ads appear separately and are labelled.

### How long does it take to rank in the local pack?

Google gives no timeline. Profile edits are reviewed before going live, so allow time, then track calls and directions each month rather than rank alone.

### Do keywords in my business name help?

Experts scored them highly (223), but Google requires the name to match your real-world branding. Adding words you do not use risks rejected or reversed edits.

### Why do I rank differently in different places?

Distance from the searcher is one of Google's three factors, so results shift as the searcher moves.
