The strongest Mother's Day offers for a local business are the ones people buy as an experience or a gift card: a dinner, a brunch, a spa visit, a class. In the National Retail Federation's 2026 survey, 63% of shoppers planned a special outing such as dinner or brunch and 55% planned gift cards, while total spending reached a record $38 billion. The next US Mother's Day is Sunday 9 May 2027 (the second Sunday of May), so a plan started in March is not late.

This guide is for US cafes, salons, spas, studios and similar shops. The figures are from the NRF and Prosper Insights survey of 7,877 adults. The example is labelled as an assumption.

## What Do Shoppers Actually Buy for Mother's Day?

NRF's 2026 release says 84% of US adults planned to celebrate. Of those shoppers, 75% planned flowers, 74% greeting cards, 63% a special outing, 55% gift cards and 51% clothing or accessories. By projected dollars, jewelry led at $7.5 billion, special outings followed at $6.4 billion, and flowers were $3.2 billion.

For local service and food businesses, the useful numbers are the outing and gift card shares. Shoppers also spread across channels: 33% planned to shop online, 33% at department stores and 29% at specialty stores. The release does not say when people start buying, so launch early and watch your own data.

## Which Promotions Fit a Local Business?

Match the offer to what your business sells.

| Business | Offer idea | Why it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cafe or restaurant | Brunch set menu, pre-booked table | Outings are the second-largest category |
| Salon or spa | Gift card or bundled treatment | 55% of shoppers plan gift cards |
| Fitness studio | Class pass or a "bring mum" session | Experience gifts |
| Pet groomer or retail shop | Gift card, small gift bundle | Easy to buy online |

Keep the offer simple, with a clear price and expiry. A pre-paid bundle is better than a discount code, because it brings cash in before the day. Our [Mother's Day ideas for salons, spas and coffee shops](/blog/mothers-day-marketing-salons-spas-coffee-shops) goes deeper on those three.

## How Do You Promote It Without a Big Budget?

Start where people already find you. Google Business Profile posts can carry an Offer type with a title, dates, a "View Offer" button, and a coupon code; only live posts show on Search and Maps, and posts older than six months are archived unless you set a date range. Update your hours for the holiday weekend too.

Then use email and social. Send two emails, one to announce and one a few days before the cutoff, and post the offer on Instagram with the booking link. Our [holiday email campaign guide](/blog/holiday-email-marketing-campaigns) and [holiday marketing strategy for local businesses](/blog/holiday-marketing-strategy-local-business) cover the sequence, and the [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers hours and posts.

## What Does a Simple Timeline Look Like?

The dates below are a suggestion, not a research finding.

- **Early March:** decide the offer, set the price, build the booking or gift-card page.
- **Late March to April:** announce by email, Business Profile and social. Mention the last order date.
- **Early May:** reminder emails and posts; confirm stock and staff.
- **Mother's Day weekend:** update hours, post reminders for last-minute buyers.
- **Week after:** email buyers a thank-you with a rebooking offer.

## Worked Example: A Brunch Set for a Cafe

All inputs are **assumptions**.

| Item | Assumption or calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set menu price | Chosen | $38 per person |
| Food cost | Assumption: 30% of price | $11.40 |
| Seats offered | Assumption | 60 |
| Seats booked | Assumption: 80% | 48 |
| Gross margin | 48 x ($38 - $11.40) | about $1,277 |
| Promotion cost (email and Instagram, boosted post) | Assumption | $100 |
| Net before labour | $1,277 - $100 | about $1,177 |

If only half the seats sell (30), the margin falls to about $798 and net to about $698, before extra staff. Take pre-payments so empty tables do not cost you. See the [no-show guide](/blog/reduce-no-shows-appointment-business) for deposits and reminders.

## FAQ

### When is Mother's Day in 2027?

In the US, the second Sunday of May, which is 9 May 2027. The UK and some other countries celebrate on different dates, so check your own market.

### How much do people spend on Mother's Day?

NRF's 2026 survey projected a record $38 billion and an average of $284.25 per person. That is a national average across all gifts, not a figure for your business.

### Should I offer a discount or a bundle?

A pre-paid bundle or gift card brings revenue early and avoids cutting your price. Neither is tested against discounts in the source cited here.

### Is gift card marketing worth it?

55% of shoppers planned gift cards, per NRF's 2026 survey. Check your gift card margins and breakage rules locally.

### How early should I start promoting?

NRF's release does not give a start date for shopping. Announce at least four to six weeks ahead and measure when your own orders arrive.
