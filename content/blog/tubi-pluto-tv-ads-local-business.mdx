## What is confirmed

According to [Advanced Television's coverage of Tubi's August 2026 update](https://www.advanced-television.com/2026/08/06/tubi-hits-110m-monthly-active-users/), Tubi reached 110 million monthly active users (up 14% year over year), took an all-time high 2.3% of US TV viewing in April 2026 per Nielsen, and has an audience that is nearly 60% Gen Z or Millennial. Annual revenue passed $1 billion, though that is total revenue, not just ads. Note that "monthly active user" has no industry-standard definition, so treat it as a size signal, not a household count for your zip code.

On the buying side, Tubi announced in May 2025 that [businesses of any size can buy ads in a self-serve format powered by Universal Ads](https://www.foxcorporation.com/?p=6482), aimed at small and medium-sized businesses. The announcement does not state minimum spend, pricing or targeting options.

## What is not confirmed

- **Tubi pricing.** The $15-25 CPM range and the "$25,000+ per campaign" figure for buying directly come from [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/tubi-advertising-small-business), a reseller that sells Tubi inventory. Treat them as the seller's estimates.
- **Pluto TV.** No current Paramount-published user count or small-business self-serve product turned up in our research. Figures of "80 million" that circulate come from older or reseller sources. Until Paramount publishes current numbers, we do not build a plan around Pluto.
- **Results.** Neither platform publishes local-business conversion benchmarks.

## Worked example (assumption, not a benchmark)

A pet groomer in a mid-size city sets aside $1,500 for a six-week test. All inputs below are assumptions for illustration.

| Input | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Budget | $1,500 |
| CPM | $20 (middle of the reseller range) |
| Impressions | 75,000 |
| Average frequency | 3 per household |
| Households reached | about 25,000 |
| Gross profit per new customer, first visit | $45 |
| Customers needed to break even | about 34 |

Reaching 25,000 households and winning 34 of them is a 0.14% response, so the real question is whether you can measure it. Without tracking, you will not know. Use a dedicated landing page or phone number, a QR code on screen if the format allows, and a "how did you hear about us" prompt at booking.

## Decision guide

- **Try Tubi if** you have a clear offer (first-visit discount, grand opening), a service area you can target tightly, and at least roughly $1,500 you can leave alone for six weeks or more.
- **Skip it for now if** your Google Business Profile is thin or you are not yet capturing people already searching. Search demand converts faster; start with [Google Ads](/blog/how-much-does-google-ads-cost-for-small-businesses-real-2026-pricing-breakdown) and your [Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).
- **Compare platforms before committing.** Our [CTV platform comparison](/blog/ctv-advertising-platforms-compared-2026) and [CTV cost data](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data) show other ways to buy the same TV screens.

## Creative basics

Adwave lists 15 or 30 second spots, 16:9, 1080p MP4 as typical specs, with most ads non-skippable. Confirm the current requirements inside the platform before producing video. Our [creative specs guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026) covers the general CTV checklist: one offer, your business name in the first seconds, and a single way to respond.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Tubi advertising free?**

No. The viewers watch for free; advertisers pay, typically per 1,000 impressions.

**Can a small business buy Tubi ads without an agency?**

Tubi announced a self-serve platform for small and medium-sized businesses in May 2025. Check Tubi's advertiser site for current terms.

**What does Tubi cost?**

Tubi has not published pricing in the sources we checked. A reseller lists $15-25 CPMs, so a $1,500 budget buys roughly 60,000 to 100,000 impressions.

**What about Pluto TV?**

We could not verify a current self-serve option or audience size from Paramount, so confirm directly with the platform before budgeting.
