## Why Do Before-and-After Videos Suit a Salon?

The result is visual, and the change happens in seconds. A viewer sees the starting point, the work and the outcome without reading anything. That makes the format easy to understand with the sound off and gives the viewer a reason to watch to the end.

It also gives a salon proof without claims. Show the real hair, the service name and the approximate time it took. Avoid editing that changes the colour or length, because the client will compare the video with the result in the chair. Our guide on [before-and-after photos, legal and converting](/blog/hair-salon-before-after-photos-legal-and-converting) covers consent in more detail.

## How Do You Set Up the Account?

TikTok advises anyone promoting a business to use a Business Account. Business Accounts can put a website link on the profile, so use your booking page. TikTok also notes they only have access to Commercial Sounds, the library cleared for commercial use, instead of the wider music library.

Fill the profile: salon name, city, hours and what you do, such as colour, cuts and extensions. Pin your three best transformations. Then post consistently for a few weeks before you judge. ## What Rules Apply to Client Videos?

Get written permission before you post a client's face or hair. If you give a free or discounted service in return for a video, the FTC says that counts as a material connection and must be disclosed clearly. Its guidance says terms like "ad" or "sponsored" are generally enough, vague shorthand like "collab" is not, and the disclosure should be in the video itself, not only the description.

Keep a simple form: name, date, what may be posted and where, and how to withdraw consent. This protects you if a client later changes their mind.

## How Do You Structure a Transformation Video?

A repeatable structure saves time:

1. First second: the "before" shot with a one-line text overlay, such as "Level 5 to blonde".
2. Middle: two or three quick process shots.
3. End: the "after" from the same angle and light as the "before".
4. Caption: the service, the city and how to book.

Film in the same spot with the same light. Mention the real time the service took. If the result took two sessions, say so.

## When Should You Pay to Boost a Video?

After a video has performed well organically. TikTok says Spark Ads let you promote your own posts and credit the views, likes and follows to the original post. Ads Manager requires a daily budget above $20 for an ad group and above $50 for a campaign, and a lifetime ad group budget equal to days times that minimum, so a 31-day run needs at least $620.

The example is an **assumption**: a salon boosts its best video for 7 days at the $20 ad group minimum, so $140 in total. It sets a booking link and a promo code and counts bookings that use the code. If three bookings use the code, the cost per booking is about $47 before the client's later visits. The three bookings are invented for the arithmetic, not a forecast.

## Which Numbers Should You Watch?

TikTok defines a 6-second view as a play lasting at least 6 seconds or the full video if shorter, and a 100% view as watched to the end, excluding replays. Compare these with total views. A video with many plays but few 6-second views has a weak first moment. Also count profile link clicks and bookings with your code.

## FAQ

### Do I need a Business Account for my salon?

TikTok recommends it for anyone promoting a business. You get a website link on the profile but only Commercial Sounds.

### Can I post a client's before-and-after without asking?

No. Get written consent. If you gave a discount or free service in return, disclose it clearly in the video.

### How much does it cost to boost a video?

TikTok's minimums are above $20 a day for an ad group and above $50 for a campaign. Click and view prices are not published as averages here.

### How many videos should I post?

No source gives an exact number. Pick a rate you can keep for 8 to 12 weeks, then compare results.

### What are Spark Ads?

A format that promotes an existing organic post, with engagement credited to the original post.
