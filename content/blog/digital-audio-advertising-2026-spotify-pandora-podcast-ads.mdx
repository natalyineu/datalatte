Digital audio advertising for a local business starts at $250: that is the minimum campaign budget on Spotify Ad Studio ([Spotify Advertising](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/small-business-advertising/), checked Oct 2026) and on AudioGO, the self-serve platform that sells Pandora and other streaming inventory ([AudioGO](https://www.audiogo.com/how-to/what-is-audiogo), checked Oct 2026). Beyond the minimum, prices are not fixed: Spotify sells ads by bidding, so cost per thousand impressions (CPM) depends on demand and targeting. We found no official CPM ranges for the other platforms, so we do not quote any.

Audio reaches people when other formats can't: in the car, at the gym, walking the dog or cooking dinner. In 2026 it covers streaming music (Spotify, Pandora), radio-network streaming (iHeart), podcast ads and programmatic buys through ad platforms. Terms change often, so confirm current minimums in each tool before you commit.

## Why Audio Deserves a Place in Your Local Ad Stack

Audio's core advantage is reaching people in moments when they are not watching a screen or scrolling. The morning commute, a lunch break with earbuds in and a workout are all moments where display and social ads are easy to miss.

For local businesses, the most valuable use is **contextual timing plus geographic precision**: a 30-second ad for your coffee shop reaching listeners near your location in the morning, as they decide where to stop.

## Platform 1: Spotify Ad Studio (Self-Serve)

Spotify Ad Studio is the most accessible entry point for small businesses. Campaigns start at $250 and the platform uses a bidding system rather than a rate card ([Spotify Advertising](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/small-business-advertising/), checked Oct 2026).

- **Formats:** Audio ads between songs, video ads and podcast ads. Audio and podcast ads can be up to 30 seconds ([Spotify ad specs](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/ad-specs/audio-ad-specs/), checked Oct 2026).
- **Location targeting:** In the US you can target by country, state, city, postal code or DMA ([Spotify Help](https://ads.spotify.com/en-GB/help-center/location-targeting/), checked Oct 2026).
- **Other targeting:** Age, gender, platform, genre, interest and real-time context, such as a listener's current activity ([Spotify Help](https://adshelp.spotify.com/HelpCenter/s/topic/0TO7S000000Gt5BWAS/targeting?language=en_US), checked Oct 2026).
- **Frequency:** By default a listener hears an ad set at most 5 times per day. You can set a custom cap of 1 to 5 per day, 1 to 35 per week or 1 to 50 per month, though a low cap may reduce delivery ([Spotify Help](https://ads.spotify.com/en-AU/help-center/custom-frequency-cap/), checked Oct 2026).
- **Creative:** Spotify offers a free voiceover service for ads created from scratch ([Spotify Advertising](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/news-and-insights/getting-started-on-ad-studio-education-marketers/), checked Oct 2026). It recommends no more than 65 words for a 30-second ad.

**Audience:** Spotify reported 494 million ad-supported monthly active users in Q2 2026 ([The Desk](https://thedesk.net/2026/08/spotify-q2-2026-earnings-report/), checked Oct 2026). Only the free tier hears audio ads, so that is your targetable audience.

<Callout type="tip">Use Spotify's free voiceover if you don't have a professional recording. A script of about 65 words fits a 30-second spot. A tight radius around your business keeps spend on people who can actually visit.</Callout>

## Platform 2: Pandora (SiriusXM Media)

Pandora is the main alternative to Spotify. SiriusXM Media says advertisers can buy through direct insertion orders, programmatic channels or AudioGO, and that Pandora supports targeting by age, gender and geography including cities, MSAs and counties, with audio spots of 10, 15 or 30 seconds ([SiriusXM Media](https://www.siriusxmmedia.com/insights/getting-started-with-pandora), checked Oct 2026). It states no minimum budget, and SiriusXM Media says it cannot give one-size-fits-all pricing ([SiriusXM Media](https://www.siriusxmmedia.com/insights/pandora-ad-pricing-the-guide-to-planning-a-campaign-budget), checked Oct 2026).

For a small business, the practical route is AudioGO, which lets you start a Pandora campaign at $250 with no long-term commitment ([AudioGO](https://www.audiogo.com/pandora), checked Oct 2026). Because audiences differ by market, test Pandora and Spotify side by side rather than assuming one fits your customers better.

## Platform 3: iHeartMedia

iHeartMedia is best known for broadcast radio, with a digital streaming operation on the iHeartRadio app. Its AdBuilder tool is described as a self-service way to buy radio advertising ([iHeartMedia](https://share.iheartmedia.com/iHeartAdBuilder_YourGuideToRadioAdvertising), checked Oct 2026). We could not confirm a current minimum or CPM, so ask for a written quote. For a closer look, see our [iHeartMedia ads guide](/blog/iheartmedia-ads-cost-targeting-setup-small-business).

## Platform 4: AudioGO, the Multi-Platform Option

AudioGO is a product of AdsWizz that lets you buy streaming audio from one self-serve dashboard. It connects to Pandora, Spotify, SiriusXM, SoundCloud, iHeartRadio, TuneIn, Univision and others, and campaigns start at $250 ([AudioGO](https://www.audiogo.com/how-to/what-is-audiogo), checked Oct 2026). Pandora inventory through AudioGO is described as reaching over 49 million unique listeners ([AudioGO](https://www.audiogo.com/pandora), checked Oct 2026), which is AudioGO's own claim.

**Best use case:** a first-time audio advertiser who wants one dashboard and a low entry point. Run a small test first, then decide whether to move toward a specific platform.

## Podcast Advertising

Podcast ads are a separate buy from streaming music. There are three common models:

1. **Host-read ads:** You work with a podcast directly. The host reads your ad in their own voice, which can feel more like a recommendation than an ad. Prices and minimums are negotiated per show.
2. **Dynamically inserted ads:** A produced audio spot is inserted when an episode is downloaded or streamed. Spotify Ad Studio sells podcast ads this way, with a 30-second maximum ([Spotify ad specs](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/ad-specs/audio-ad-specs/), checked Oct 2026).
3. **Sponsorships:** Longer-form brand integrations that usually need larger budgets and lead times, so they rarely suit a small business.

## Programmatic Audio Platforms

Businesses spending more can use programmatic platforms for finer targeting, cross-channel frequency control and attribution. Minimums and fees vary by platform and by whether the buy is managed, so request current terms. ## Creative Best Practices for Audio Ads

Audio has no visuals, so your message has to work through sound, structure and voice.

**A 30-second script structure:**
1. **Hook (first few seconds):** Open with the problem or a local hook. "In [city]? Tired of slow Wi-Fi ruining your morning?"
2. **Body:** One benefit, delivered clearly. Don't try to say five things.
3. **Call to action (final seconds):** Tell them exactly what to do and say the business name twice.

**Production notes:**
- Keep background music low so it supports the voice.
- Match the voice to your brand: warm and local for food, energetic for fitness, calm for health.
- Avoid jargon, since listeners can't rewind.
- State your location early.

## Budget Guide

| Monthly budget | Suggested approach |
|---|---|

## FAQ

**Q: How much does digital audio advertising cost for a local business?**
A: The confirmed entry point is $250 per campaign on Spotify Ad Studio or AudioGO. Beyond that, pricing is auction-based, so check the CPM shown in the tool and divide your budget by it to estimate impressions. Managed buys on other platforms have their own minimums, so ask for a written quote.

**Q: Can I use digital audio ads instead of Google Ads?**
A: They serve different purposes. Google search ads capture people who are searching right now. Audio ads reach people before they search. Start with search to capture existing intent, then add audio to build awareness.

**Q: How do I know if anyone actually heard my audio ad?**
A: Not perfectly, but well enough to decide. Use a promo code ("mention AUDIO for $10 off"), a dedicated tracking phone number and a UTM-tagged landing page. Also watch branded search in Google Search Console for a lift while the campaign runs.

**Q: Is digital audio advertising worth it at a $300 monthly budget?**
A: It is tight. Spotify's $250 minimum leaves little room to learn, so run one tightly targeted test for several weeks and judge it by calls, visits or redemptions. If you cannot sustain it, put the money into Google Ads or Meta Ads first.

**Q: Is digital audio only for younger urban audiences?**
A: No. Different platforms reach different listeners, and radio-network streaming and podcasts reach more varied audiences. Check each tool's audience data for your area and test before assuming.

**Q: How long before digital audio advertising shows results?**
A: Plan for a few weeks of data on streaming audio, and a 60 to 90 day test before a final judgment. Host-read podcast ads with a promo code give faster signals.

**Q: Do I need a professional voiceover for audio ads?**
A: Not necessarily. Spotify offers a free voiceover service for ads created from scratch, and AudioGO offers professional production or free synthetic voice options ([AudioGO](https://www.audiogo.com/pandora), checked Oct 2026). A clear recording of your own voice often works well for local service businesses. Spend your effort on the script.

## The Simple Playbook

Set up your tracking. Pick one channel. Target tightly. Write something that doesn't sound like an ad. Run it for 60 to 90 days. Judge it by the numbers in your bank account and booking software, not the platform's impression counts.
