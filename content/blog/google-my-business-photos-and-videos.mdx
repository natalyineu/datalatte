## What Do Google's Guidelines Say About Business Photos?

Google's Business Profile guidelines do not treat photos as a separate rule set in the guidance reviewed for this article. They set principles that apply to all profile content. Content should highlight what makes a business unique, and it should be accurate, relevant and free of anything that breaks Google's prohibited content policies.

The guidelines also ask that a business be represented as it is recognised in the real world, across signage, stationery and other branding. A shopfront photo showing an old name or outdated branding works against that principle. Photos should match what a customer sees when they arrive.

The guidelines also say there should be only one profile per business, because duplicate profiles can cause problems with how information displays on Google Maps and Search. Add photos to that single profile rather than creating a second listing for a new set of images. Check each image for customer details, screens showing personal data or documents before uploading.

## Which Photos Should a Local Business Upload?

Start with the images a customer wants to check before visiting. That usually means the outside of the building so people can find the entrance, the inside so they know what to expect, and the products or finished work the business is known for. A café would show its counter and seating, a salon would show its chairs and a finished style, and a plumber would show a completed installation.

Keep promotional text out of the images. Google's business description guidance does not allow special promotions, prices or sale messages, and the same caution applies to photos that carry sale banners. Keep the focus on the product, place or team.

Show staff only if they have agreed to be photographed. Google's guidance on personal information applies to people in images as well as to written text, so a clear consent step protects both the business and the employee.

## Does Adding Photos Change Your Local Ranking?

The Google documents reviewed here do not state that photos raise local rankings. They give no figure linking a number of photos to better placement. Treat photos as a way to win the click after a listing appears, not as a ranking lever that can be measured precisely.

The job of a photo is to help a searcher choose one business over the one next door. A clear exterior, a clean interior and a close-up of a product can make the profile feel more trustworthy at the moment of decision. ## How Long Before Photo Changes Appear?

Google's SEO Starter Guide says that some changes take effect within a few hours, while others can take several months to be reflected. It advises waiting a few weeks before assessing whether a change had a beneficial effect, and it notes that not every change produces a visible result.

For photos, this means views and calls will not usually shift overnight. Upload a batch, leave it for a few weeks, then compare the profile's performance with the period before. If nothing changes, the photos may need more time, or another factor may be the real constraint on visibility.

The same guide says Google's crawler generally sees pages from a US location. Businesses that serve customers elsewhere should check that the information visible from the US matches what they intend to show.

## How Do You Check Photos Before Uploading?

Use a short checklist before each upload. Confirm the image shows the real business, uses current branding and contains no private information. Remove any promotion, price or link. Check that the image fits the category and services listed on the profile.

Review the profile a few days after uploading. Google's guidelines say that content breaking its policies can lead to changes or removal of business information, so act quickly if an image disappears or is flagged. Replace it with an image that meets the guidelines rather than leaving a gap.

## FAQ

### Can I have more than one Google Business Profile for my business?

Google's guidelines say there should be only one profile per business. Multiple profiles can cause problems with how information displays on Google Maps and Search.

### Does Google say how many photos to upload?

The guidance reviewed here does not give a number of photos or a file size. Focus on accurate, current images that show the real business.

### Can I use promotional text in my profile photos?

Google's business description guidelines do not allow special promotions, prices or sale messages. Keep promotional text out of descriptions and, where possible, out of images.

### How long should I wait to see if photo changes worked?

Google advises waiting a few weeks to assess a change. Some changes take effect within hours and others can take several months.
