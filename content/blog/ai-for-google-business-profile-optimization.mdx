AI can draft four parts of a Google Business Profile in about 30 minutes: the description, the service list, monthly posts and review replies. A person must still check every fact. Google caps the description at 750 characters, bans URLs and HTML in it, and wants it to focus on the business, not promotions or prices. AI helps with wording, not with ranking: Google says no one can pay for a better local ranking.

## What Can AI Do for a Business Profile, and What Should It Not Do?

Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence. AI can improve relevance by turning your real services into clear, complete text. It cannot change distance, and it cannot invent prominence, which comes from things like reviews and links.

Do not let AI invent facts. Hours, prices, awards, years in business and service areas must come from you. Do not ask it to write reviews. Google treats fake or incentivised reviews as a policy violation, including free or discounted goods in exchange for reviews. ## How Do You Write the Description With AI?

Give the tool facts, not adjectives. Google asks for your offerings, what sets you apart and how long you have operated. It says to leave out URLs, HTML code, promotions, prices and sales. It also notes that AI help for the description is available in select regions and languages.

```text
Write a Google Business Profile description of 650 characters or fewer for [business type] in [town]. Use only these facts: [services], [year opened], [what is different]. No prices, no offers, no links, no emojis. Plain English.
```

Count the characters in the result and trim it under 750. Read it aloud, and delete any claim you cannot prove, such as "best in town".

## How Do You Draft the Service List?

List every service customers ask about, each with one plain sentence. Do not copy a competitor. Ask the AI to turn your price sheet into short descriptions without the prices if the field does not need them, and keep names identical to what you call things in the shop.

```text
Here is my service list: [paste]. Write one factual sentence per service, under 25 words each, using the words customers use. Do not add services I did not list.
```

Check that categories and services match. A mismatch confuses both Google and customers. Our guide to [AI for local SEO](/blog/ai-for-local-seo) shows where else this wording helps.

## How Do You Plan a Month of Posts?

Business Profile posts come in three types: updates, offers and events. Offers need a title and dates, and include an automatic "View Offer" button. Events show as lasting 24 hours if you give no times. Google reviews each post against its content policy, and posts can be live, pending or not approved. Posts older than six months are archived unless you set a date range.

```text
Draft four Business Profile updates for [business], one per week, each under 80 words. Topics: [new service], [staff intro], [seasonal need], [answer to a common question]. No phone numbers, no regulated products, no exaggerated claims.
```

Schedule them in one sitting. Keep a plain log of which posts get calls or direction requests.

## How Do You Reply to Reviews With AI?

Google's guidance is to be brief, polite and specific, and to avoid identical thank-you messages. Replies should not be promotional, and should never share a reviewer's private details. For a complex complaint, invite the reviewer to contact you privately.

```text
Write a reply under 60 words to this review: [paste]. Thank the customer by first name, mention one specific detail from the review, and sign as [name]. For a negative review, acknowledge the issue and invite them to contact [phone]. No discounts or offers.
```

Read each reply before posting, because a wrong detail looks worse than a slow reply. For automation options, see [AI agents for Google reviews](/blog/ai-agent-for-google-reviews-management) and the [auto-reply script](/blog/ai-agent-google-reviews-auto-reply-script).

## FAQ

### Can I use ChatGPT or another AI tool to write my Google Business Profile?

Yes for drafting. Check facts, remove promotions and prices from the description, and keep it under 750 characters with no URLs.

### Does AI-written content hurt my ranking?

The Google pages read for this article do not say. They say relevance, distance and prominence drive local ranking, and that accurate, complete information helps.

### How often should I post?

Google gives no required frequency. Posts older than six months are archived unless you set a date range, so plan a steady monthly rhythm.

### Can AI reply to every review automatically?

Google asks for specific, non-generic replies. Draft with AI, but have a person approve negative reviews.

### Can I ask customers for reviews?

Yes, but you cannot offer free or discounted goods or services in exchange.
