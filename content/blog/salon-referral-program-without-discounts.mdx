A salon referral program without discounts rewards the person who refers a friend with something that costs you less than it looks like it is worth, such as a product sample, an add-on treatment or priority booking, and charges the new client full price. In the example below, a 20% discount on a $90 first visit costs $18, while a product reward with a $6 cost (an assumption) costs a third of that. The rules also matter: the FTC says a reward for a review must be disclosed and cannot depend on the review being positive, so keep referrals and reviews separate.

This guide covers reward options, the rules, how to track referrals in booking software and a worked example. No source opened gives an average referral rate for salons, so none is quoted.

## What Can a Salon Offer Instead of a Discount?

Rewards that cost you little and feel personal work best. The reward should fit the referrer, not just the new client.

- **A product sample or travel size** from your retail range, which also gets the product used.
- **An add-on service** such as a scalp massage or gloss on the next visit, when the chair is otherwise empty.
- **Priority booking** for popular slots.
- **A handwritten card or a small gift** at the next visit.
- **A donation** to a local cause in the referrer's name.

Avoid cash-value credit. It behaves like a discount and trains clients to wait for the offer. For the new client, a welcome extra such as a complimentary blow-dry finish keeps the service price intact.

## What Rules Apply to Rewards?

Referral rewards are not reviews, but they sit close. The FTC's endorsement guidance says that offering something of value for reviews requires disclosure, and that you cannot condition an incentive on the review being positive. It adds that asking only happy customers for reviews can mislead if it skews the results.

So never tie a reward to a review. Ask for reviews separately, from everyone, using your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) link. If a client tells friends about you on social media in return for a reward, they should disclose the reward. This is general information, not legal advice; check local rules where you operate.

## How Do You Track Referrals?

Use the simplest method that does not rely on memory. Ask every new client "How did you hear about us?" at booking and record the answer, and give referrers a card with their name or a code.

Software can help. Vagaro's pricing page lists customer loyalty programs under features included with every plan, but does not mention referral features. Fresha's page lists a one-time 20% commission, minimum $6, for new clients found via its marketplace, and no fee for clients who book through your direct link, Facebook or Instagram. A referred friend who books through your own link therefore avoids that marketplace fee. ## The Math: Discount or Product Reward?

All inputs are **assumptions**.

| Input | 20% discount | Product reward |
|---|---|---|
| First-visit price | $90 | $90 |
| Cost to salon of the incentive | $18 (forgone revenue) | $6 (product cost) |
| New client pays | $72 | $90 |
| Revenue from first visit minus incentive | $72 | $84 |
| Referred clients per month | 4 | 4 |
| Monthly incentive cost | $72 | $24 |

At these inputs, four referrals a month cost $72 as discounts or $24 as product rewards, and the product route also brings $48 more in first-visit revenue. The reward still has to be attractive enough to make people refer, which only your clients can tell you. Test it with a small group first.

## FAQ

### Do salon referral programs work without discounts?

No source opened measures that for salons. The logic is cost: a reward with a low cost to you protects your price, but you should test whether clients respond.

### What is a good referral reward for a salon?

Something that costs you little and the client values, such as a product sample or an add-on. Avoid credit that acts as a discount.

### Can I reward clients for Google reviews?

The FTC says rewards for reviews need disclosure and cannot depend on positive reviews. Safer to ask without a reward.

### How do I track who referred whom?

Ask every new client how they heard about you and record it. Cards or codes make it easier.

### Does Fresha charge for referred clients?

Fresha's page lists no fee for clients who book via your direct link, Facebook or Instagram. A one-time 20% commission, minimum $6, applies to new clients found via its marketplace.
