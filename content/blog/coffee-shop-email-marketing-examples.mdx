Five emails cover most of what a coffee shop needs: a welcome email, a new-drink announcement, a loyalty nudge, a win-back message and a short event invite. The first is automated and the rest go out when there is news.

Klaviyo's 2026 data shows automated flows get a 5.58% click rate against 1.69% for one-off campaigns. The sample emails below are drafts written for this guide, not results from real shops.

Each example lists the goal, a sample subject line and what to measure. Benchmarks come from Klaviyo and Mailchimp, and the legal basics from the FTC.

## Which Coffee Shop Email Should You Send First?

The welcome email. It goes to each new subscriber automatically and does one job: tell them what to do next. A sample draft is below, with subject line "Welcome. Your first coffee is on us" if you offer a first-visit treat, or "Here is what we pour" if you do not.

Keep the email short: one line on who you are, one offer or fact, your address and hours, and a button to order ahead if you offer it. Mailchimp's free plan does not include automation flows, so check your plan before you plan a welcome series.

## What Does a New-Drink Email Look Like?

Goal: bring regulars in for a limited release. Sample subject: "New on the menu: the cinnamon oat latte" (an invented drink name used only for illustration). Body: one photo, two sentences on what is in it, the dates it is available, and a single button.

Measure clicks and the number of redemptions if you attach a code. One-off campaigns like this sit near the Klaviyo campaign benchmark of a 1.16% order rate for Food and Beverage, so most recipients will not order.

## How Do You Write a Loyalty Nudge?

Goal: remind a customer how close they are to a reward. Sample subject: "You are two stamps from a free coffee". This needs visit data from your loyalty or POS system. Without it, send a general reminder with the reward rules.

Keep it factual, with the count and the expiry date if there is one. Our [coffee shop loyalty program ideas](/blog/coffee-shop-loyalty-program-ideas-without-an-app) show ways to track visits without an app.

## What Should a Win-Back Email Say?

Goal: bring back someone who has not visited for a while. Sample subject: "We saved your usual". Body: one friendly line, one reason to return, and a small offer you can afford. Decide the gap that counts as lapsed from your own visit data, such as twice a customer's normal interval.

Send it once or twice, then stop. If someone does not respond, remove them from regular sends.

## What Are the Legal Basics?

The FTC's CAN-SPAM guide lists eight requirements, including accurate sender details, non-deceptive subject lines, clear identification as an ad, a valid postal address and a working opt-out that you honour within 10 business days. The maximum penalty is $53,088 per violating email.

A "free coffee" subject line must match the offer inside. If the offer has conditions, state them. ## FAQ

### How many emails should a coffee shop send a month?
No source sets a number. Start with the welcome automation, then one campaign a month, and watch unsubscribes.

### Are open rates reliable?
Mailchimp notes that Apple's Mail Privacy Protection can affect open-rate accuracy. Use clicks and redemptions instead.

### Do I need an app for email?
No. You need an email platform and a way to collect addresses. Both Mailchimp and Klaviyo list free plans with 250 contacts and 500 sends a month on their pages.

### What is the biggest email mistake?
Sending offers you cannot honour. Match subject lines to the offer and state the conditions.
