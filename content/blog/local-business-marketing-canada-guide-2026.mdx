A Canadian local business should start with a complete Google Business Profile, then add Meta ads or Google Search ads for a defined area, and build an email list that follows Canada's anti-spam law. Two rules set Canada apart from the US: CASL governs email and text marketing, and Quebec requires French in advertising, websites and social pages. Neither is optional.

This guide uses published data from Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED), DataReportal, Google and Quebec's legal information service Éducaloi. Canadian ad prices are not published in any source checked for this guide, so no CPC figures appear here.

## How Big Is the Small Business Market in Canada?

ISED counts 1,099,521 employer businesses in Canada as of December 2024, and 1,079,188 of them (98.2%) have 1 to 99 employees. Small businesses employ about 5.8 million people, or 46.6% of private sector employees. Ontario has 410,154 small employer businesses and Quebec 228,622, more than half of the total between them.

These counts exclude businesses with no paid employees, such as sole traders. For a local shop, the point is crowding: in a big city you compete with many other small firms for the same search. A complete Business Profile and a clear offer matter more than volume of posting.

## Which Platforms Reach Canadians?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report for Canada (data from October 2025 and late 2025) puts the population at 40.2 million and internet penetration at 95.1%. Social media user identities total 33.0 million, or 82.1% of the population.

| Platform | Ad reach in Canada | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | 33.0 million | 82.1% |
| Facebook | 24.6 million | 61.2% |
| Instagram | 21.0 million | 52.2% |
| TikTok (18+ only) | 16.6 million | not stated as a share |

Ad reach is an advertising estimate, not a count of monthly users, and one person can hold several identities. Facebook and Instagram still reach most adults, so a local advertiser can start there. ## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Canada?

Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. You control relevance by filling in the profile completely, and prominence by earning reviews and good ratings. Google also recommends verifying the business, replying to reviews and adding photos.

For a Canadian business, add both English and French business descriptions if you serve a bilingual area, and keep hours current for statutory holidays. ## What Does CASL Require for Email and Text Marketing?

Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation covers commercial electronic messages, meaning messages that encourage participation in a commercial activity. ISED's guidance says you need consent, must identify yourself, and must give a working unsubscribe.

Express consent has no time limit unless withdrawn. Implied consent covers existing business relationships and lasts up to 2 years after the last purchase, or 6 months after an inquiry. Unsubscribe requests must be actioned within 10 business days, and contact details must stay valid for at least 60 days after sending. Keep records of consent. This is general information, not legal advice.

## What Are the Quebec French Rules for Advertising?

According to Éducaloi, public signs and commercial advertising in Quebec must generally be in French. Another language can appear if French is "markedly predominant", meaning at least twice the space and at least as visible. A business with a Quebec address that sells to Quebecers must have a French website, and social media pages must be available in French.

Replies to comments follow the commenter: reply in French to a French comment, and in the commenter's language otherwise. Rules changed in 2025, so check the Office québécois de la langue française (OQLF) for current detail. See also our [Quebec small business guide](/blog/small-business-marketing-quebec-canada).

## What Does a Starter Plan Look Like?

Both examples below use **assumptions**, not benchmarks.

**Consent timing under CASL.** A customer buys on October 3, 2026 and gives no express consent. Implied consent from that purchase would run to October 3, 2028 (2 years). A person who only sent an inquiry on that date would be covered until April 3, 2027 (6 months). Add an opt-in box at checkout so you do not depend on the shorter window.

**Monthly split for a single-location shop.** The following CAD budget is an assumption to test, not a recommendation from data:

| Item | Monthly CAD (assumption) |
| --- | --- |
| Google Search ads, small radius | 450 |
| Meta ads, local audience | 250 |
| Email tool and sign-up offer | 50 |
| Photos and video | 150 |
| Total | 900 |

Review after 60 days. Keep the channel with the lowest cost per booking or sale, and cut or rework the rest.

## Do You Need Different Marketing in Each Province?

Mostly the same tools, with different language and audiences. Quebec needs French content. Tourist areas in British Columbia and Atlantic provinces swing with the season. Smaller communities depend more on Facebook groups and word of mouth. ## FAQ

### How many small businesses are there in Canada?

ISED reports 1,079,188 small employer businesses (1 to 99 employees) in December 2024, 98.2% of all employer businesses.

### Which social platform has the biggest reach in Canada?

By DataReportal's advertising reach estimates, YouTube leads at 33.0 million, followed by Facebook at 24.6 million and Instagram at 21.0 million.

### Do I need consent to email customers in Canada?

Yes. CASL requires consent, identification and an unsubscribe. Implied consent after a purchase lasts up to 2 years.

### Must my website be in French?

In Quebec, if your business has a Quebec address and sells to Quebecers, a French version is required, according to Éducaloi.

### How much do Google Ads cost in Canada?

No verified Canadian benchmark was available. Use Google's Keyword Planner in your own account for estimates.
