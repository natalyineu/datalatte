CTV advertising in Portugal means placing video ads on internet-connected TVs, most simply through YouTube on the TV screen via Google Ads.

Streaming use is high: 52% of Portuguese aged 15 and over used streaming platforms in late 2024 (Marktest BStream, secondary), and YouTube's ad tools reached 7.59 million people (72.9% of the population) in late 2025 (DataReportal). No published Portuguese CTV price list was found, so this guide gives none.

## What Is CTV and How Does It Differ From Normal TV Ads?

Connected TV is a television that plays video over the internet, through apps, sticks or consoles. Ads are bought by audience and location through a self-serve tool, not as a block of airtime. That is why a local business can try it with a small budget.

Google's help page on CTV targeting says you must opt into Connected TV in your device targeting and include TV screens. Demand Gen and Performance Max campaigns that target CTV also get QR codes placed automatically, so a viewer can scan from the sofa. The page lists no regional restrictions.

## How Many Portuguese Watch Streaming?

Marktest's BStream study, as reported by Senal News, found 52% of people aged 15 and over used streaming in the latest 2024 wave, the highest since 2021. Paid subscriptions held at 41%. The sample was 6,000 people on the mainland.

ANACOM data reported by 4gnews puts pay TV in 89.4% of households and internet access in 91.1% in 2025. Both are secondary reports of the original studies, which were not opened directly. Neither figure counts smart TVs, and no source used here gives smart TV penetration.

## Which Platforms Can You Actually Buy?

For a small business, the practical option is YouTube through Google Ads. DataReportal counts 7.59 million people reachable on YouTube in Portugal, against 6.30 million on Facebook and 6.35 million on Instagram. Portugal has 10.4 million people and 9.26 million internet users.

Other channels, such as streaming-service ad tiers or broadcaster platforms, were not verified here. If a sales rep quotes a price or reach, ask for it in writing, along with the audience definition.

## What Rules Apply to the Ads?

Portugal has a self-regulation body, Auto Regulação Publicitária (ARP), whose jury judges complaints. According to ICAS, ARP also offers copy advice before a campaign runs, and all jury members are independent of the industry (secondary). The current code text and its digital provisions were not verified here, so read ARP's code before you write claims.

Keep claims you can prove and name your business clearly. Our [CTV advertising in Europe guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-europe-small-business-guide) covers the wider EU context.

## Is CTV Worth It for a Small Business?

Only after the basics work. If your Google Business Profile is thin and your website cannot take bookings, a TV screen ad will not fix that. Start with local search and maps, then test video to widen reach. Our [Portugal local marketing guide](/blog/local-marketing-portugal-small-business-2026) and [Europe GBP guide](/blog/google-business-profile-optimisation-europe) cover that foundation.

Worked example, with all inputs as assumptions: a Lisbon cafe sets aside EUR 300 for a four-week YouTube test targeted to a radius around the shop. Assumption: the aim is 30 extra visits. The CPM and visit rate are unknown until the campaign runs, so judge it on cost per visit from your own counts, such as a promo code or QR scans. ## FAQ

### What does CTV advertising cost in Portugal?

No published, verifiable price list was found. Google Ads shows an estimate when you set a budget; use that and your own results.

### Can I run TV-screen ads from Google Ads?

Yes, by including TV screens in device targeting for supported campaign types, per Google's help page. Check which formats your account offers.

### Do I need a production budget?

Not necessarily. Many small advertisers start with a short vertical or horizontal video made on a phone, but check Google's current format guidance.
