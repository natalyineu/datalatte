## How Big Is the Alabama Small Business Market?

Governor Ivey's May 2025 Small Business Week release cites more than 449,000 small businesses in Alabama, attributed to the SBA Office of Advocacy's profiles, and says small businesses are 99.4% of all businesses and employ 46.7% of private-sector workers. The release names no source for the last two figures.

The SBA's own PDF could not be opened for this article (it returned a 403), so these are secondary to the SBA. The numbers do not show how many competitors you have in your own category and town, so search for your service on Google Maps and count.

## What Does Google Ads Cost in Alabama?

No state-level figure is published. LocaliQ's 2026 US benchmarks give a range by category: Beauty and Personal Care $4.62 per click, Restaurants and Food $2.05, Health and Fitness $6.17, Animals and Pets $4.06. The all-industry average is $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead.

These are national averages, and your cost will depend on your city and competitors. Run a small test and read your own report. Google's help says its average daily budget can spend up to twice the daily figure on a given day, with a monthly ceiling near 30.4 times the daily budget.

## Why Start With the Google Business Profile?

It is free and shows in map results when people search for a local service. Google says you must add or claim the business and verify it before it appears in Search and Maps. Keep hours, services, photos and booking links current.

Reviews matter next. BrightLocal's 2026 US survey found 74% of consumers only consider reviews from the last three months, and 47% will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews. Google prohibits rewards in exchange for reviews. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) walks through each field.

## Where Can an Alabama Owner Get Free Help?

The Alabama SBDC offers free and confidential business advising, through 10 centres at university partners covering all 67 counties. Advisors can help build a marketing plan, and the network lists a Digital Marketing Bootcamp. The phone line is 1-877-825-7232, weekdays 8 AM to 5 PM.

Use an advisor to check your budget and goals before spending on ads. This is a service worth knowing because the advice costs nothing.

## Should You Use Local Services Ads?

For eligible categories, yes, test them. Google's help says you pay for leads related to your business, when a customer gets in touch from the ad. Availability depends on category and area, and the page has a "Check eligibility" link. New advertisers go through screening and verification before the Google Verified badge is issued.

If your category is not eligible, use regular Search ads with a tight radius. Our [US local marketing guide](/blog/local-marketing-united-states-small-business-2026) compares the main platforms.
