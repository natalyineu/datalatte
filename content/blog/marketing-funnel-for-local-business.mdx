A marketing funnel for a local business has four stages: be found, earn trust, get the booking, and bring customers back. Fix the stage that loses the most people first. Give each stage one number you can check every month. Then compare what you spend to win a new customer with what that customer is worth. Vendor data suggests bringing lapsed customers back is usually the cheapest growth, so it belongs in the plan from the start.

The four-stage model below is simpler than the classic awareness, interest, desire and action model. A local shop rarely needs five steps, and each stage here maps to a number you can track.

## What Are the Four Stages of a Local Marketing Funnel?

| Stage | Question the customer asks | What to measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Be found | Who is near me that does this? | Profile views, search impressions |
| Earn trust | Are they good, and is the price clear? | Inquiries, review count, reply rate |
| Book | How do I get started? | Calls, booking requests, bookings |
| Come back | Should I return or refer someone? | Repeat visits, referrals, lapsed reactivations |

Each stage has one number to watch. If you cannot count a stage, that gap is the first thing to fix. A simple spreadsheet with one row per month is enough to start.

## How Do Customers Find You Before They Book?

The first stage is about being visible when someone searches near you. Complete every field on your free business profile, keep hours and contact details current, and choose a category that matches what customers actually buy. Check your platform's live help pages for what currently affects local visibility, because those rules change and a fixed list goes stale.

Paid reach can add to this stage. Event promotion is one route for local businesses that want attention in a specific place and week. The [event marketing guide for local businesses](/blog/event-marketing-for-local-business) covers planning steps that suit a shop or studio. Measure paid reach by the inquiries it brings, not by impressions alone.

## How Do You Earn Trust Before the Booking?

Trust comes from proof: reviews, photos, clear prices and fast replies. A customer who sees a recent review and a visible price is more ready to call. Ask for reviews soon after a visit, reply to every one, and place the best ones on your website and booking page.

Objections are where trust is lost. The Winback Engine guide states that the gap between untrained and trained objection handling is often a 3x difference in revenue recovered. That is a vendor claim about its own method, so test it with your own call notes before relying on it. Write down the three objections you hear most, and prepare one short answer for each.

## How Do You Turn Interest Into a Booking?

Remove steps. A visitor who wants a haircut, a table or a quote should be able to call, tap a booking link or fill a short form from the first screen. Put the same contact details on the profile, the website and every ad, so the path is identical wherever the customer starts.

Calls are often the main booking route for trades and salons. The [voice AI guide for local business customer calls](/blog/elevenlabs-voice-ai-local-business-customer-calls) explains how automated answering fits into this step. A marketing-tools article from MindStudio reports that companies using AI for lead qualification cut customer acquisition costs by up to 30% (secondary source). Treat that as a general report, not a forecast for a single shop.

## Why Is Bringing Customers Back Cheaper?

Winback Engine states that reactivation campaigns typically cost 5–10x less per converted customer than new acquisition. Its comparison table puts new-customer conversion at 1–3% against 15–40% for reactivation, and time to first booking at 2–8 weeks against 3–14 days. These are vendor-reported figures for service businesses, so use them to set a test, not a target.

Acquisition costs are also rising for many owners. A Wellhub survey reported by the Health & Fitness Association found that 82% of gym and studio owners said their customer acquisition costs had increased this year. A follow-up sequence for lapsed customers can start small, for example with an [AI agent for customer service](/blog/ai-agent-for-customer-service-local-business) that sends reminders and collects rebooking requests. Check each message against your own records before it goes out.

## How Do You Check Your Acquisition Cost?

Acquisition cost is simple to calculate. GroomSoft defines it as total marketing spend divided by the number of new customers acquired (secondary source). Google Local Services Ads reporting vendor Ryze states that sustainable customer acquisition cost should be no more than 15–20% of average project value.

Worked example, using assumptions for a salon:

| Input or result | Value | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly marketing spend | $600 | Assumption: ads plus listing tools |
| New clients from that spend | 12 | Assumption |
| Average client spend | $250 | Assumption |
| Acquisition cost per client | $50 | $600 divided by 12 |
| Ceiling at 15% of average spend | $37.50 | Ryze rule of thumb |
| Ceiling at 20% of average spend | $50.00 | Ryze rule of thumb |

The result sits at the top of the range. At the 20% ceiling, $600 of spend is the limit for 12 clients. At the 15% ceiling, the cap drops to $450. Either cut spend, or raise repeat bookings so each client is worth more. A SaaS-focused rule from LivePlan says customer lifetime value should be more than three times acquisition cost. It was written for software companies, so use it only as a prompt to track repeat value.

## What Should You Fix First?

1. Write down the current count for each of the four stages, and mark any stage with no count.
2. Find the stage with the weakest result, which is often the step where people stop between interest and booking.
3. Make one change at that stage, such as a clearer booking link or a reply to every review.
4. Measure it across one full booking cycle before judging the result.
5. Divide your monthly marketing spend by new customers and compare it with the 15–20% ceiling.
6. Add one follow-up message for lapsed customers. The [ChatGPT prompts for local business marketing guide](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-local-business-marketing) includes drafting ideas for review requests and reminders.

Do not build all four stages at once. One working loop, such as profile to booking to review request, teaches more than a large plan that nobody measures.

## FAQ

### What is a marketing funnel for a local business?

It is the path from someone finding your business to becoming a repeat customer. A practical version has four stages: be found, earn trust, book, and come back.

### Which stage should I fix first?

Start with the stage that has the weakest measured result. If you have no count for a stage, measuring it is the first fix.

### What is customer acquisition cost?

GroomSoft defines it as total marketing spend divided by new customers acquired (secondary source). Divide your monthly spend by new clients to get your own figure.

### Is reactivation cheaper than finding new customers?

Winback Engine says reactivation typically costs 5–10x less per converted customer. That is a vendor figure, so track your own cost per returning customer to confirm it.

### What is a safe acquisition cost?

Ryze, a Google Local Services Ads reporting vendor, says sustainable acquisition cost should be no more than 15–20% of average project value. Compare that ceiling with your own average client spend.
