To get more phone calls from your website, put a tap-to-call number at the top of every page, make the number match your Google Business Profile, keep the page fast on a phone, and measure calls so you can see what works. No fix is magic. Each one removes a step between a visitor who wants to talk and the phone ringing.

This guide is for local businesses such as salons, groomers, cafes and trades that take bookings by phone. It uses Google's and MDN's documentation for the technical parts. Where no benchmark is published, the text says so, and the worked example is an assumption.

## Why Is Your Website Not Getting Phone Calls?

Usually the number is hard to find, does not work with a tap, or does not match what people see on Google. The three common causes are a number only in the footer, plain text that is not a link on mobile, and a slow page that visitors leave before they see the number.

Check your own site on your phone. Open it on mobile data, not Wi-Fi, and try to call within five seconds. If you cannot, a customer will not try. Then look at what Google shows about you, because many callers never reach your website at all.

## How Do You Make Your Number Easy to Tap?

Use a telephone link. MDN documents `tel:` URLs in the href of a link, and says cellular devices autodial the number while other devices may open a calling app or offer to save the contact. Write the number with the country code, and put it in a header button as well as the page body.

- Add a "Call now" button in the header on every page
- Show the number as text next to the button, so desktop visitors can read it
- Repeat the button after the pricing or services section
- List opening hours near the button so no one calls a closed shop

Keep the same number on every page. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) shows where else it must match.

## Does Your Google Business Profile Match Your Website?

It should, and fix it first. Google says local results rank on relevance, distance and prominence, and that complete, accurate information makes a business more likely to appear. It also says you cannot pay for a better local ranking.

Check the phone number, hours and category. Google's performance report counts Calls as the number of times a customer clicked the call button on your Business Profile, and requires a phone number on the profile. That figure counts taps, not completed calls. Google also removed chat and call history from Business Profile on July 31, 2024, so you can no longer see individual callers there.

## How Fast Should the Page Load?

Google's web.dev lists targets for a good experience: Largest Contentful Paint within 2.5 seconds, Interaction to Next Paint of 200 milliseconds or less, and Cumulative Layout Shift of 0.1 or lower. A page passes when it meets all three at the 75th percentile of visits, measured separately for mobile and desktop.

Test your page in PageSpeed Insights, which reports these numbers from real visits when enough data exists. Common fixes are smaller images, fewer scripts and a simple layout so the call button does not jump while the page loads. No published figure links these thresholds to call volume for local businesses.

## What Else Helps People Decide to Call?

Give visitors what they need before they dial. Put what you offer, where you are, your hours and a starting price near the top. A starting price filters out callers who cannot afford you, so you get fewer but better calls.

Add reviews and a short answer to the most common question. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US consumers found 97% read reviews of local businesses and 47% will not use a business with fewer than 20. Add LocalBusiness structured data, too. Google's documentation lists telephone as a recommended property, with the country and area code included. It does not guarantee a rich result.

## How Do You Track Calls From Your Website?

Track two things: taps and answered calls. Google Ads can count several kinds of call conversions (see our [Google Ads guide for dog groomers](/blog/google-ads-for-dog-groomers-complete-2026-guide) for a call-asset setup example). These include calls from call assets, calls to a number on your website after an ad click, and clicks on a number on your mobile website, which tracks the click and not the call. You set the minimum call length that counts as a conversion.

Calls from ads and calls to a website number use Google forwarding numbers. If you do not run ads, ask every caller how they found you and log the answer. A free spreadsheet works. Our pieces on [AI voice agents for local business phone calls](/blog/voice-ai-agent-local-business-phone-calls) and [AI phone answering with Bland](/blog/bland-ai-phone-answering-local-business-2026) cover answering after hours.

## What Does a Worked Example Look Like?

All inputs below are **assumptions** to show the maths, not benchmarks.

| Input | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mobile visits per month | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| Share of visitors who call | 1.5% | 2.5% |
| Calls | 15 | 25 |
| Calls that become booked jobs | 40% | 40% |
| Booked jobs | 6 | 10 |
| Average job value | $120 | $120 |
| Monthly revenue from website calls | $720 | $1,200 |

Under these assumptions, a tap-to-call button and a faster page add four jobs and $480 a month. The point is the method: count visits, calls and booked jobs for a month, change one thing, then count again. Do not change five things at once.

## FAQ

### What is the fastest way to get more calls starting tomorrow?

Fix your Google Business Profile. Confirm the phone number matches your website, add hours, photos and your category, and reply to reviews. Then add a tap-to-call button to your site header.

### Will showing my number everywhere bring spam calls?

No published data on this was found. The number is already public on your profile, and hiding it risks losing real calls. If spam becomes a problem, use your phone provider's call blocking.

### Do I need call tracking?

If you run Google Ads, yes, because you cannot judge the campaign without it. Google Ads can count calls from ads and calls to your website number through forwarding numbers. Without ads, a simple log of how each caller found you is enough to start.

### Do I need a new website to get more calls?

Not necessarily. Many of the fixes above, such as a tap-to-call header button, matching profile details and smaller images, can be made on an existing site. Replace the site only if it cannot be made fast on mobile.

### Why do I get calls that do not turn into customers?

That is usually a phone handling problem, not a website problem. Listen to a few calls with the caller's consent, note what was asked, and write a short script for pricing and booking.
