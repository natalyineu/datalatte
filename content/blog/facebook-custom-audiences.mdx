A custom audience is Meta advertising aimed at people a business already knows: past customers, lapsed clients, email subscribers or website visitors. The business uploads identifiers or installs the pixel, Meta matches them to accounts, and ads run only to that matched group. No minimum list size is published for creating an audience, but tiny lists deliver poorly.

For local businesses this is usually the highest-value audience in the account. These people have already paid once or nearly booked, so the ad needs a reason and a slot, not an introduction. That is a cheaper job than persuading a stranger, and the budget can stay at $5 a day.

## How do Facebook custom audiences work?

Two sources feed them. A customer list (emails or phone numbers exported from the booking or till system) is uploaded and hashed before matching, per Meta's Business Help documentation checked in November 2026. A website audience comes from the Meta pixel recording visits, with windows up to 180 days.

Match rates are partial. Only customers whose details match a Meta account appear, and work emails or old numbers reduce the overlap. Judge the matched size Meta reports after upload, not the row count in the spreadsheet. Lists of a few hundred matched people can still work inside a small trade area.

Consent is the gate. Customer data should only be uploaded where marketing use is lawful for those contacts, and unsubscribes must be removed before upload, not after complaints. Meta places responsibility for the data's lawful basis on the advertiser in its custom audience terms, checked in November 2026.

## Which custom audience should a local business build first?

Build the lapsed list first: customers past their usual return interval. A groomer, salon or café already owns this data, and the message writes itself (a named service, two slots, a small add-on). [Facebook ads for pet groomers](/blog/pet-groomer-meta-ads) runs exactly this play with interval numbers.

Build website intent second: visitors to price, menu or booking pages in the last 30 days who did not convert. [Instagram retargeting](/blog/retargeting-ads-for-local-business) covers that setup, including the tracking check most failed campaigns skip.

Build lookalikes last, if at all. A lookalike of a few hundred local customers inside a small radius often just returns the same postcodes with less certainty. At local scale, geography plus the real list usually outperforms the modelled expansion. [Meta ads for hair salons](/blog/using-meta-ads-for-hair-salon-marketing) shows the radius discipline that applies.

## What does a custom audience campaign cost?

The platform does not charge for the audience; media is auction-priced as usual. Practical local tests run $5 a day because the audience is small and warm, on November 2026 platform practice. Frequency climbs fast inside a tiny pool, so cap it and refresh creative monthly.

**Assumption example.** Assume a salon uploads 600 lapsed clients, Meta matches 60%, and the campaign reaches 360 people at $5 a day for 30 days, or $150. If 2% rebook at an assumed $90 ticket, that is about 7 bookings and $630 against $150 of media. Match and rebooking rates are assumptions; the booking system provides the real ones after one cycle.

Refresh the list monthly. A static upload decays as people rebook, move or unsubscribe, and advertising a win-back offer to someone who visited last week wastes money and goodwill. Booking systems that export on schedule turn this from a project into a habit.

## FAQ

### How big does a customer list need to be?

Meta publishes no minimum for creating a customer-list audience, but delivery needs enough matched accounts to run. In practice a few hundred matched people inside the trade area is a workable local floor. Below that, widen the window or combine lapsed and website audiences rather than forcing spend into a pool of dozens.

### Is it legal to upload a customer list to Meta?

It can be, where the business has a lawful basis to use those contacts for marketing and honours unsubscribes before upload. Meta's custom audience terms put that responsibility on the advertiser, checked in November 2026. Rules differ by country; this is general information, not legal advice, and purchased lists should never be uploaded.

### Why is the matched audience smaller than the list?

Meta only matches identifiers tied to active accounts. Old numbers, work emails, typos and customers without accounts fall out. Match rates well under the uploaded row count are normal. Use several identifier columns (email and phone) in the export to improve matching, and judge results on the matched figure Meta reports.

### Custom audiences or lookalikes for a local business?

Custom audiences first, almost always at local scale. The real lapsed and visitor lists carry intent no model can invent. Lookalikes help when entering a genuinely new area or when the source list numbers in the thousands. Inside one trade area, tight geography on the real list usually converts better per dollar.
