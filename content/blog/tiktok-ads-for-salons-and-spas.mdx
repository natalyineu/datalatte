## Facts worth knowing

- Pew reports 37% of US adults use TikTok, 63% of ages 18-29, 44% of ages 30-49, 30% of ages 50-64 and 12% of 65 and older ([Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/03/02/8-facts-about-americans-and-tiktok/)).
- TikTok requires campaign budgets above 50 USD and ad group daily budgets above 20 USD ([TikTok Business Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-daily-budgets)).

## Decision guidance for spas

A spa client base often skews older than a barbershop. If most of your treatment revenue comes from clients 50 and older, TikTok reaches a smaller share of them (30% and 12%). Start with [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) before paid social.
