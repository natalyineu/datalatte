A non-developer can use Cursor, an AI coding editor, to draft and edit a small business website by describing changes in plain English. But Cursor writes code, not a finished business. You still choose the content, check every page, host the site and keep it secure.

This guide explains what Cursor does, what it costs on its current plan list, and a hosting trap: Vercel's free Hobby plan is limited to personal, non-commercial use. Plans and prices below come from Cursor's and Vercel's own pages, opened on 6 November 2026. They change often, so re-check before you pay.

## What Does Cursor Cost Now?

Cursor's pricing documentation lists these individual plans: Free at $0 with limited usage, Plus at $8 a month (base usage), Premium at $30 (4x Plus usage), Super at $100 (15x Plus) and Ultra at $200 (50x Plus). It says usage allowances refresh weekly and that third-party models are billed separately at API list price on some plans.

The same page says older plans such as Hobby, Pro and Pro+ "continue for existing customers". Many articles still quote Pro at $20 a month. That is a legacy name, so do not rely on it for a new sign-up.

A site for one shop is a small project. Start on Free or Plus, and move up only if you hit the usage limit.

## How Do You Start a Project in Cursor?

Cursor's quickstart tells you to download the app for your system, log in, pick a folder and start with a small task. It says to open Agent with Cmd I (the guide lists macOS shortcuts, so check the keyboard settings on Windows). Agent can search your files, make the edits you ask for, and run checks such as a local build. Changes appear in a diff view that you review. For bigger jobs, Plan Mode (Shift+Tab) researches the code and asks questions before building.

Agent also creates checkpoints before significant changes. Cursor's docs say these are stored locally, separate from Git, and are for undoing Agent changes rather than permanent version history. Put the project in Git or another backup as well.

Write your first request as a brief, not a wish. Give the business name, address, phone number, opening hours and services as you want them printed. Tell Cursor not to invent anything:

```text
Create a one-page site for [business name] in [city]. Use only the facts below.
Do not invent prices, reviews, awards or staff names; leave a clear TODO where
information is missing. Sections: hero, services, hours and address, contact.
Facts: [paste your real text here]
```

## Where Should You Host It?

Vercel is one popular option. Its pricing page lists Hobby at $0 for "personal, non-commercial use" and Pro at $20 a month for professional developers, freelancers and businesses. Vercel's Hobby plan page repeats that its fair use guidelines restrict Hobby to non-commercial, personal use only. A business website is commercial. Plan for the $20 Pro price, or choose another host and read its terms first.

You also need a domain name. Registrars set their own prices, so check one directly. Point the domain at your host following the host's instructions.

## What Should You Check Before Publishing?

AI-written pages can look finished and still be wrong. Read every line against your real details.

- **Facts:** name, address, phone, hours, prices.
- **Reviews:** only publish genuine testimonials you have permission to use. The US Federal Trade Commission's final rule bans creating or buying fake reviews and testimonials, including AI-generated ones, when the business knew or should have known they were fake. Never ask Cursor for "sample reviews" and publish them.
- **Contact form:** send yourself a test message. A form that fails silently loses leads.
- **Mobile:** open the site on a phone and tap every button.
- **Secrets:** never paste passwords or API keys into prompts or into the code.

## When Is a Developer the Better Choice?

Cursor suits simple brochure sites you can review yourself. Hire help when you need online payments, accounts, bookings with a database, accessibility compliance or a site with many templates. ## FAQ

### Is Cursor free?

Cursor lists a Free plan with limited usage. Paid plans on the current list start at $8 a month (Plus).

### Can I use Vercel's free plan for my business website?

Vercel says Hobby is for personal, non-commercial use. A business site should be on a paid plan or another host whose terms allow commercial use.

### Do I need to know how to code?

You do not need to write code, but you must be able to check results. Review the preview and the diff, test forms and keep a backup. If that feels unmanageable, hire a developer.

### Can Cursor write my reviews or testimonials?

It can generate text, but publishing fake testimonials is banned by the FTC's rule when the business knew or should have known they were fake. Use real customer reviews only.

### Will Cursor make my site rank on Google?

No tool guarantees rankings. Cursor can help you build pages and fix technical issues, but rankings depend on content, links and your Google Business Profile.
