## How Many People in Portugal Can You Reach Online?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report gives a population of 10.4 million in October 2025. Of these, 9.26 million use the internet (89.0%) and 1.15 million are offline. Social media user identities total 7.59 million (72.9%).

Advertising reach is 7.59 million on YouTube (72.9%), 6.35 million on Instagram (61.0%) and 6.30 million on Facebook (60.5%). These are ad tool estimates, not active users. For comparison, the World Bank's page lists a 2025 population of 10,804,871, so population figures differ by source.

## What Does the Portuguese Economy Look Like for a Local Business?

The World Bank lists Portugal's 2025 GDP at US$346.64 billion, GDP per capita at US$32,081.8, GDP growth at 1.9%, unemployment at 6.2% and inflation at 2.3%. These are national averages and tell a single shop little, but they set the backdrop: customers are price-aware, and growth is modest.

On company size, APCMC, relaying a CCP summary of 2023 statistics, reports 1,526,926 enterprises, 96% of them microenterprises, with SMEs at 99.9% of the total. This is a secondary source, so check the original INE release. The practical point is that most of your competitors are tiny too.

## Which Customers Matter: Residents or Visitors?

For hotels, restaurants, tours and shops in tourist areas, visitors are a major segment. According to ECO's report of 9 July 2026 on INE's Estatísticas do Turismo 2025, tourist accommodation received 34.8 million guests and 89.7 million overnight stays. There were 29.9 million non-resident tourists, 3.3% more than in 2024, and foreign tourists made up 67.1% of overnight stays. The United Kingdom was the top source with a 17.3% share.

ECO adds that of the 25 municipalities where foreigners made up more than 75% of overnight stays, seven were in the Algarve and ten in Madeira. If you serve both groups, build separate ads and landing pages in Portuguese and in English.

## Where Should a Small Budget Go First?

The sources support a simple order, though they cannot tell you the cost of each step.

1. A complete [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist), because local searches start on Google and the profile is free.
2. Local Search ads for the services people look up, written in Portuguese, with an English version if tourists are a real segment.
3. Instagram and Facebook ads, since both reach more than 60% of the population by DataReportal's estimate.
4. Email to people who have given consent, covered in the next section.

Run one channel at a time for four weeks, then compare. Our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to split a small budget.

## What Are the Rules for Marketing Emails and Messages?

Electronic direct marketing to individuals is governed by Law 41/2004 of 18 August, with the GDPR applying alongside it, according to a Garrigues article on the CNPD guidelines of 25 January 2022. Two points from that law-firm summary:

- Consent must be express. Pre-ticked boxes and implied consent are invalid, and consent cannot be a condition for visiting a website or entering a contest.
- Existing customers can be marketed to on the basis of legitimate interest only for the same or similar products or services, if the right to object was offered when their data was collected. Every message must offer an easy, free way to refuse.

Abreu Advogados notes that the guidelines leave out some questions, such as messages to business email addresses. This is a secondary summary from 2022. Read the CNPD's current text, or ask a Portuguese lawyer, before you send a campaign. The advertising code (Decreto-Lei 330/90) and the self-regulator's rules were not verified for this guide.

## Worked Example: A Small Algarve Restaurant

All inputs here are **assumptions**, not benchmarks. A restaurant near Faro tests Instagram and Search over four weeks.

| Step | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly test budget | €500 |
| Split | €300 Search, €200 Instagram |
| Average table spend | €45, gross margin 60% |
| Margin per booking | 45 x 0.60 = €27 |
| Bookings needed to break even | 500 / 27 = about 19 |
| Decision rule | Fewer than 10 tracked bookings, change the offer before spending more |

Track bookings with a dedicated phone number, a booking link with a tag, or a question at the table. Whether €500 can deliver 19 bookings is not something the sources can answer.

## What Mistakes Cost Portuguese Small Businesses Money?

Four mistakes recur in local campaigns anywhere, and apply here:

- Sending tourists and residents to the same page.
- Running ads without a way to count calls and bookings.
- Buying an email list instead of collecting consent, which conflicts with the express-consent rule above.
- Judging a channel after a week.

## FAQ

### How many people in Portugal use Instagram?

DataReportal estimates Instagram ad reach at 6.35 million, 61.0% of the population, in late 2025. This is an ad reach estimate, not a count of active users.

### Do I need consent to send marketing emails in Portugal?

Generally yes for individuals, under Law 41/2004 as summarised by Garrigues, with a limited exception for similar products sold to existing customers. Verify with the CNPD or a lawyer.

### How much do Google Ads cost in Portugal?

No Portuguese price data was found in the sources opened for this guide. Check the keyword planner in your own account and set a small cap.

### Are tourists important for local marketing?

In tourist areas, yes. INE data reported by ECO puts non-resident tourists at 29.9 million in 2025 and foreign visitors at 67.1% of tourist overnight stays.
