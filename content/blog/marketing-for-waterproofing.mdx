A waterproofing company should market through search: a service-area Google Business Profile, reviews, and Google Search ads timed to wet-weather problems. The closest published benchmark is Home and Home Improvement from WordStream by LocaliQ's 2026 report: $8.33 per click, 8.05% conversion and $90.92 per lead. Waterproofing jobs are large, so a high cost per lead can still pay, but only if you measure booked jobs.

This guide is for basement, foundation and crawl-space waterproofing firms in the US. The benchmark covers home services in general, not waterproofing alone.

## What Marketing Works for Waterproofing Companies?

Customers search when water shows up, so be easy to find at that moment. Three pieces carry most of the work.

1. A verified Business Profile. Google says local ranking depends on relevance, distance and prominence, and that complete, verified profiles are more likely to appear.
2. Reviews. BrightLocal's 2026 survey found 97% read reviews, 47% will not use a business with fewer than 20, and 68% require four or more stars.
3. Search ads for urgent and planned work. Calls from a call asset cost the same as headline clicks, per Google.

## How Should You Set Up a Service-Area Profile?

Most waterproofing firms visit homes instead of hosting customers, which Google calls a service-area business. Google says to remove your address if you do not serve customers there. You can list up to 20 service areas and should stay within roughly 2 hours of driving. You cannot define the area as a radius, so list cities or postal codes.

If you also run a showroom with signage, you may be a hybrid business and can show the address. Pick categories that describe the work you do and add photos of finished jobs, with permission.

## Are Local Services Ads Available for Waterproofing?

Not by that name in Google's US list. The page lists "Foundations services" and "Water damage services" but no waterproofing category, so check the live list for your area before you plan around it.

Search ads remain the main paid route. Use location targeting for the towns you serve. Google says radius targeting needs at least 1 km, and "Presence or interest" is the recommended default for Search, though "Presence" fits when you only want people physically in your area. ## When Should You Increase Ad Spend?

Use an ad schedule and budget changes, not guesswork. Google Ads schedules let you choose days and hours, and bid adjustments raise or lower bids for specific times. Campaigns pace toward 30.4 times the average daily budget in a month.

No source here gives waterproofing demand by month, so check your own call log and Search Console data to find your busy weeks. Raise the budget before them, not after.

## The Math: What Does $2,000 Buy?

All inputs below are **assumptions** except the benchmark.

| Step | Assumption or source | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget | Assumption | $2,000 |
| Cost per lead | $90.92 (benchmark) | about 22 leads |
| Leads who book a job | 20% (assumption) | about 4.4 jobs |
| Average job value | $6,000 (assumption) | about $26,400 |
| Cost per booked job | $2,000 divided by 4.4 | about $455 |

The job value is not from a published source, so use your own. If the close rate is 10%, cost per job doubles to about $910, still small against a large job but worth tracking. Estimates need an on-site visit, so ask callers what they see and book the visit quickly.

## How Do Reviews and Proof Help?

Homeowners spend big money here, so show proof. Ask each customer for a review after the job and reply to all of them. The FTC's rule on fake reviews took effect on 21 October 2024 and bars offers of rewards that require a positive review. ## FAQ

### How much does a waterproofing lead cost?
No waterproofing-specific figure is published in the sources used here. The closest category, Home and Home Improvement, averages $90.92 per lead.

### Do I need a physical office on Google?
No. Service-area businesses can hide their address and list up to 20 areas.

### Are Local Services Ads worth trying?
Check whether Google lists a category for your work. The US list shows Foundations services and Water damage services but not waterproofing.

### What is the fastest way to get more calls?
Search ads with call assets, plus a complete profile. Reviews build more slowly.

### How many reviews do I need?
BrightLocal found 47% of consumers will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews.
