## Who Are You Marketing To in Czech Republic?

Czechia had 10.6 million people in October 2025, down 1.3% on end 2024. 75.0% live in urban areas and the median age is 43.8. Internet users number 9.96 million (94.2%), and social media identities total 8.08 million (76.4%).

Cellular connections number 14.7 million (139% of the population), 97.6% of them broadband-capable, so mobile-first pages are essential. A median age of 43.8 means an older audience than many markets, which favours Facebook and search.

## How Should Google Ads Language and Location Work in Czech Republic?

Google's Ads Help says that from September 2026 the campaign-level language setting is being removed for Search campaigns, and Search ads will match on the language of the ads themselves. For searches with a clear language, Google prioritises ads in that language. Write ads and landing pages in Czech for local customers. If you also serve English speakers, build a separate English ad group and page.

For location, Google's help says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km and recommends broad "Presence or interest" targeting as best practice for Search, while presence-only fits when you want to avoid people outside your target area. Set a radius around your premises, then read the report to see which areas convert.

No Czech Republic-specific click costs were found in the sources checked, so every cost figure below is an assumption.

## What Does a Test Budget Return? The Math

These rows are **assumptions** for illustration, in US dollars. They are not Czech Republic benchmarks. Replace each with your own data after a month.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly ad budget (assumption) | $300 | $300 | $300 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | $0.70 | $0.40 | $0.28 |
| Clicks | 429 | 750 | 1,071 |
| Click to enquiry rate (assumption) | 3% | 5% | 7% |
| Enquiries | 13 | 38 | 75 |
| Enquiry to booking rate (assumption) | 30% | 40% | 50% |
| Bookings | 3.9 | 15 | 37.5 |
| Ad cost per booking | $78 | $20 | $8 |
| First-visit value (assumption) | $30 | $30 | $30 |
| Revenue minus ad spend | -$184 | +$150 | +$825 |

Counts use unrounded values. Revenue is not profit: staff, product and rent come out of it. A first visit often does not pay back by itself, which is why repeat visits matter.

Your break-even click price is the first-visit value times the enquiry rate times the booking rate. At $30, 5% and 40%, that is 30 x 0.05 x 0.40 = $0.60 per click. If your clicks cost more on average, the first visit loses money and you need repeat bookings to justify it. The biggest lever is usually the booking rate: answer messages and calls within minutes.

## FAQ

### Is Facebook still bigger than Instagram in Czechia?
Yes by ad reach: 5.00 million against 3.95 million in late 2025, but Facebook fell 4.8% and Instagram grew 3.9% over the year.

### How many Czechs are online?
9.96 million, 94.2% of the population, in October 2025.

### Is TikTok big in Czechia?
TikTok reaches 2.13 million adults, 24.7%, per DataReportal, much lower than Facebook or Instagram.

### Are the reach figures reliable?
They are estimates from ad tools, and Meta revised its method, so DataReportal warns they may not compare with earlier years.
