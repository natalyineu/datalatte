## Is a voice agent worth it for a small business?

A voice agent pays off only if the calls it catches are worth more than it costs. Do the arithmetic before you pick a voice.

**Worked example (all inputs are assumptions, swap in your own).** A grooming shop or salon with a $55 average ticket.

| Line | Assumption | Arithmetic | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Missed calls caught | 25 missed calls a month, 40% would book, the agent converts 60% of those | 25 x 0.4 x 0.6 = 6 bookings | 6 x $55 = **$330** |
| No-shows saved | 120 appointments a month, 10% no-show, reminder calls prevent 1 in 4 | 120 x 0.10 = 12 no-shows; 12 x 0.25 = 3 saved | 3 x $55 = **$165** |
| **Monthly value** | | $330 + $165 | **$495** |

In this example the agent has to cost clearly less than $495 a month, all-in. All-in means the plan, the language-model usage, any outside telephony and your setup time. ElevenLabs publishes plan prices and the $0.08 minute rate, but not a flat per-call language-model cost, so that line stays an estimate until you run test calls.

If your real missed-call count is 5 instead of 25, the answer flips. Count your missed calls for two weeks first.

## What jobs can an AI voice agent do for a local business?

### Appointment reminders and confirmations

A reminder call can confirm, offer to reschedule or capture a cancellation so you can fill the slot. It matters most where a missed slot costs money: salons, groomers, clinics, studios. Test a plain SMS reminder first, since it is the cheaper experiment.

### Inbound receptionist

The agent answers when you are busy or closed: hours, services, parking, rough pricing, booking requests. For anything it cannot handle, it takes a message or transfers to a person. See the [AI receptionist setup guide for 2026](/blog/ai-receptionist-small-business-setup-guide-2026).

## How do you set one up?

1. Define one job: reminders, FAQs or booking.
2. Write the system prompt: role, tone, what it knows, what it must never do.
3. Choose a voice from the library, or clone your own voice (see the warning below).
4. Connect your tools: booking system, calendar or CRM through the platform's tool or webhook options.
5. Connect a phone number. Purchased Twilio numbers can take inbound and outbound calls, while a verified caller ID supports outbound calls only ([ElevenLabs docs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/agents-platform/phone-numbers/twilio-integration/native-integration)). Check support for your country first.
6. Test with real calls, including interruptions, accents and odd questions.
7. Review transcripts weekly and refine the prompt.

A simple prompt structure for a fitness studio (adapt it to your business):

```text
You are a friendly virtual receptionist for [Studio Name] in [City].
You help callers with:
- Class schedule questions
- Membership options
- Booking a free trial class
- Our cancellation policy

Be warm and concise. When someone wants a trial class, collect their name,
email and preferred class, then trigger the booking tool.
If you do not know an answer, say "Let me have our team call you back"
and take a message. Never make up prices or policies.
```

Automated outbound reminders need a developer or a no-code tool such as Zapier or Make to pass appointment data to the agent. Use ElevenLabs' current API documentation for endpoints and fields.

<Callout type="warning">ElevenLabs says you can only create a Professional Voice Clone of your own voice, and it verifies this ([ElevenLabs help](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/product/voices/voice-cloning/can-i-create-a-professional-voice-clone-of-someone-elses-voice)). In the US, the FCC ruled in February 2024 that calls made with AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices under the TCPA, so its robocall rules apply to them ([FCC](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-400393A1.pdf)). Disclose to callers that they may be speaking with an AI, and check call-recording and automated-calling rules in your country or state before launching outbound campaigns.</Callout>

## What should you do before you build?

1. For 14 days, tally every missed call and every voicemail. Note which ones were new customers.
2. Pull your no-show count for the last 30 days.
3. Fill in the math table with those two numbers and your average ticket.
4. Add up the all-in monthly cost from the current pricing pages.
5. If the value is not at least double the cost, use a missed-call text-back instead.
6. If it is, start with after-hours calls only, because the alternative is voicemail.

Rollout rules that apply to any voice platform: tell callers it is an AI assistant and give them a way to reach a person, set boundaries in the prompt (no medical, legal or pricing promises beyond what you list), keep a call-back path, and read transcripts weekly for the first month. SMS and email reminders, a card on file for high-value appointments and an easy reschedule link can cut no-shows at lower cost. ## FAQ

### Is ElevenLabs Agents different from its text-to-speech?

Yes. Text-to-speech converts text into audio. The agents platform runs live, two-way calls with reasoning and tool use. You can also use ElevenLabs voices inside other voice-agent platforms.

### How much does an ElevenLabs phone agent cost?

Plans run from $0 (15 call minutes) to $990 a month, extra minutes cost $0.08, and language-model usage is billed separately. A third-party number provider such as Twilio bills you directly. Source: the pricing page, 3 Nov 2026.

### Can I make outbound calls without coding?

ElevenLabs documents batch outbound calling through its telephony integrations. Pulling live appointment data into those calls generally needs the API or a no-code tool such as Zapier or Make, so plan for some setup.

### Is it legal to use AI voice calls to my customers?

Rules vary by country and state. In the US, the FCC treats AI-generated voices as artificial voices under the TCPA, which affects outbound calls. Get consent, disclose AI use where required, and ask a local professional about recording rules.

### Will an AI voice agent replace my receptionist?

For simple, repetitive calls it can take a lot of load. Complex or sensitive calls should go to a person.
