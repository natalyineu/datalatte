Yes, ElevenLabs can handle customer calls for a local business. Its agent platform (previously branded Conversational AI) lets you build a voice agent that answers inbound calls, places outbound calls and triggers actions such as bookings through tools and integrations, using synthetic voices. It suits businesses where voice quality matters and where someone can set up the integrations. Pricing, features and phone-number support change, so check ElevenLabs' current documentation before you build.

## What ElevenLabs Is

ElevenLabs started as a text-to-speech company. It now also offers a platform for real-time voice agents. According to its [agents documentation](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/agents-platform/overview) (checked Oct 2026), the platform includes:

- **A large voice library** across 31 languages, per the docs. Check that your market's language and accent are covered.
- **Low-latency speech recognition and generation** for real-time conversation.
- **Tools that call APIs**, so the agent can trigger actions such as booking or sending data to your CRM.
- **Document upload** (a knowledge base) so it can answer questions about hours, prices and policies.
- **Telephony options**: a native Twilio integration, SIP trunk connectivity and batch outbound calling.

## Two Core Use Cases

### 1. Appointment reminders and confirmations

Reminder calls from a friendly voice can reduce no-shows for businesses where each missed slot costs money, such as salons, groomers, clinics and studios. A reminder call can confirm, offer to reschedule or capture a cancellation so you can fill the slot. Compare it with a simple SMS reminder first, which is a lower-effort option to test. ### 2. Inbound receptionist

An AI voice agent can answer calls when you are busy or closed: hours, services, parking, rough pricing, and booking requests. For anything it cannot handle, it should take a message or transfer to a person. ## How to Set One Up

1. **Define the job.** Reminders, FAQs or booking. Start with one.
2. **Write the system prompt.** Describe the agent's role, tone, what it knows and what it must never do.
3. **Choose a voice** from the library, or clone your own voice (see the warning below).
4. **Connect your tools.** Booking system, calendar or CRM through the platform's tool or webhook options.
5. **Connect a phone number.** ElevenLabs documents importing a Twilio number in its dashboard. Purchased Twilio numbers can take inbound and outbound calls, while a verified caller ID supports outbound calls only ([ElevenLabs docs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/agents-platform/phone-numbers/twilio-integration/native-integration), checked Oct 2026). Check support for your country before you commit.
6. **Test with real calls,** including interruptions, accents and unexpected questions.
7. **Review transcripts weekly** and refine the prompt.

Here is a simple prompt structure for a fitness studio (adapt the details to your own business):

```text
You are a friendly virtual receptionist for [Studio Name] in [City].
You help callers with:
- Class schedule questions
- Membership options
- Booking a free trial class
- Our cancellation policy

Be warm and concise. When someone wants a trial class, collect their name,
email and preferred class, then trigger the booking tool.
If you do not know an answer, say "Let me have our team call you back"
and take a message. Never make up prices or policies.
```

For automated outbound reminder campaigns, you will need a developer or a no-code tool such as Zapier or Make to pass appointment data to the agent. Use the current API documentation from ElevenLabs for endpoints and fields instead of copying older code samples.

<Callout type="warning">ElevenLabs says you can only create a Professional Voice Clone of your own voice, and it verifies this; even with someone's consent you cannot clone their voice that way ([ElevenLabs docs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/product/voices/voice-cloning/can-i-create-a-professional-voice-clone-of-someone-elses-voice), checked Oct 2026). In the US, the FCC ruled in February 2024 that calls made with AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices under the TCPA, so its robocall rules apply to them ([FCC](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-400393A1.pdf), checked Oct 2026). Disclose to callers that they may be speaking with an AI, and check call recording and automated-calling rules in your country or state before launching outbound campaigns.</Callout>

## When ElevenLabs Fits and When It Does Not

**It is a good fit when:**

- Voice quality and brand feel matter (premium salons, spas, boutique studios).
- You serve customers in several languages.
- You mainly want outbound reminders or a polished receptionist.

**It may not be the best fit when:**

- You need very complex multi-step tool logic, where a developer-focused platform may be more flexible.
- Your budget is very tight and a missed-call text-back or simple voicemail would solve the problem.
- You want a no-code setup in under an hour.

## Costs to Check

ElevenLabs sets its own pricing and changes plans often, so read its current pricing page for how agent conversations are billed and what is included. Before committing, estimate your monthly call minutes (number of calls times average length), then compare the cost with your current cost of missed calls and no-shows. Also include telephony charges for your phone number, any separate language-model or add-on costs, and developer time for integrations.

## Rollout Tips for Small Businesses

- **Start with after-hours calls only.** This reduces risk because the alternative is usually voicemail.
- **Tell callers it is an AI assistant** and give them an easy way to reach a person.
- **Set clear boundaries** in the prompt: no medical, legal or pricing promises beyond what you list.
- **Keep a short call-back path.** If the agent cannot answer, it should take a name, number and reason for the call and send it to you by text or email.
- **Review transcripts weekly** for the first month, and update the prompt with new questions you see.
- **Measure outcomes:** missed calls recovered, bookings made, no-show rate and customer feedback.

These steps apply to any voice platform, not just ElevenLabs.

## FAQ

**Q: Is ElevenLabs Conversational AI different from its text-to-speech?**
A: Yes. Text-to-speech converts text into audio. Conversational AI runs live, two-way calls with reasoning and tool use. You can also use ElevenLabs voices inside other voice-agent platforms.

**Q: How realistic is ElevenLabs voice cloning?**
A: Quality depends on the audio you provide and the clone type you use. ElevenLabs offers Instant and Professional clones, and Professional clones require verification that the voice is yours ([ElevenLabs docs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-cloning/professional-voice-cloning), checked Oct 2026). Listen on a real phone line before launching.

**Q: Can I make outbound calls without coding?**
A: ElevenLabs documents batch outbound calling through its telephony integrations. Pulling live appointment data into those calls generally needs the API or a no-code tool such as Zapier or Make, so plan for some setup.

**Q: Is it legal to use AI voice calls to my customers?**
A: Rules vary by country and state. In the US, the FCC treats AI-generated voices as artificial voices under the TCPA, which affects outbound calls ([FCC](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-400393A1.pdf)). Get consent, disclose AI use where required, and ask a local professional about recording rules.

**Q: Will an AI voice agent replace my receptionist?**
A: For simple, repetitive calls it can take a lot of load, but it should hand off complex or sensitive calls to a person.
