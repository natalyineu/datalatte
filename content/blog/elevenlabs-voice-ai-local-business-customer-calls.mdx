## What can ElevenLabs Agents do on a phone call?

The call-agent page describes inbound and outbound calls with low-latency voice. It says agents can authenticate a customer, update a CRM, log the interaction and schedule an appointment through API and telephony integrations ([ElevenLabs AI call agents](https://elevenlabs.io/agents/ai-call-agent)).

The platform page adds a knowledge base built from uploaded documents, FAQs or a connected data source. It also describes workflows, actions and escalation paths, plus a dashboard for tracking conversations and spotting gaps ([ElevenLabs conversational AI](https://elevenlabs.io/conversational-ai)). For a salon or clinic, that could mean hours, service questions and booking requests handled without a person picking up.

These are the vendor's product claims. Test each one on your own calls before relying on it.

## Is the vendor's cost claim reliable?

The call-agent page states that its agents can reduce cost per call by up to 66% ([ElevenLabs AI call agents](https://elevenlabs.io/agents/ai-call-agent)). The page does not publish a method or name the human-staffed baseline it compares against, so treat the figure as a direction, not a forecast. The same page cites 60% more efficient collections for firms, which is a different workflow and does not transfer to salon bookings.

A better test is your own arithmetic, shown in the next section.

## Is a voice agent worth it for a small business?

A voice agent pays off only when the calls it catches are worth more than its all-in cost. All-in means the plan, language-model usage, any telephony provider and your setup time. The sources do not give plan prices or language-model costs, so those lines need figures from the pricing pages and your own bills.

**Worked example.** Every input below is an assumption for a salon with a $55 average ticket. Replace them with your own counts.

| Line | Assumption | Arithmetic | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Missed calls caught | 25 missed calls a month (assumption), 40% would book (assumption), agent converts 60% of those (assumption) | 25 x 0.4 x 0.6 = 6 bookings | 6 x $55 = $330 |
| No-shows saved | 120 appointments a month (assumption), 10% no-show (assumption), reminder calls prevent 1 in 4 (assumption) | 120 x 0.10 = 12; 12 x 0.25 = 3 saved | 3 x $55 = $165 |
| Monthly value | | $330 + $165 | $495 |

On these assumptions, the agent must cost clearly less than $495 a month, all-in, to justify itself. If the real missed-call count is 5 instead of 25, the value falls to a fraction of that, and a missed-call text-back may be the better buy. Count missed calls and no-shows for two weeks before committing to any platform.

## Which calls should the agent take first?

Start with one narrow job. The three most common in the vendor materials are reminders, inbound answering and booking. Launching all three at once makes failures hard to trace.

For appointment-based businesses, a reminder call can confirm a slot, offer to reschedule or capture a cancellation so the time can be resold. It matters most where an empty slot costs real money. A plain text reminder is the cheaper experiment to run first.

For after-hours inbound calls, the agent answers questions about hours, services and parking, then books or takes a message. Counter businesses can apply the same logic to orders and questions, as covered in [AI-driven customer service for local coffee shops](/blog/ai-driven-customer-service-for-local-coffee-shops).

## How do you set one up?

1. Define one job: reminders, FAQs or booking.
2. Write the system prompt: role, tone, what the agent knows and what it must never do.
3. Load the knowledge base with current hours, services and policies. For larger FAQ sets, a retrieval approach like the one in [LangChain RAG for customer FAQs](/blog/langchain-rag-customer-faq-local-business) keeps answers grounded.
4. Connect your booking tool or CRM. Developer help is usually needed. A no-code route such as [Zapier vs Make for small business](/blog/zapier-vs-make-small-business-local-business) can pass appointment data across.
5. Connect a phone number through the platform's telephony options, and check that your country and number type are supported.
6. Test with real calls, including interruptions, accents and unusual questions.
7. Review transcripts weekly and log failed calls. For that habit, see [monitoring and debugging AI agents](/blog/agentops-monitor-debug-ai-agents-local-business).

A simple prompt structure for a fitness studio (adapt it to your business):

```text
You are a friendly virtual receptionist for [Studio Name] in [City].
You help callers with:
- Class schedule questions
- Membership options
- Booking a free trial class
- Our cancellation policy

Be warm and concise. When someone wants a trial class, collect their name,
email and preferred class, then trigger the booking tool.
If you do not know an answer, say "Let me have our team call you back"
and take a message. Never make up prices or policies.
```

## What rules apply to AI voice calls?

Rules on AI-generated calls vary by country and state. They cover consent, disclosure and call recording. The sources reviewed for this update do not set those rules out, so treat this section as a checklist to bring to a local professional, not as legal advice.

Tell callers they are speaking with an AI assistant and give them a route to a person. Keep a call-back path. Set prompt boundaries: no medical, legal or pricing promises beyond what is listed. Get consent before outbound calls, and check recording rules before launch.

For broader agent safeguards, see [AI agents for customer service in local businesses](/blog/ai-agent-for-customer-service-local-business).

## FAQ

### Is ElevenLabs Agents different from its text-to-speech product?

Yes. The agents platform runs live two-way calls with a knowledge base, tools and workflows. Text-to-speech only converts text into audio. The agents pages describe voice agents that connect to CRMs and schedulers, which goes beyond voice generation ([ElevenLabs conversational AI](https://elevenlabs.io/conversational-ai)).

### How many languages does ElevenLabs support for calls?

The vendor states support for 90+ languages with consistent tone ([ElevenLabs AI call agents](https://elevenlabs.io/agents/ai-call-agent)). Test the specific language and accent your callers use before relying on it.

### Can an AI voice agent replace my receptionist?

It can take a large share of routine calls, such as hours, bookings and reminders. Complex, sensitive or emotional calls should still go to a person, with the agent taking a message or transferring the call.

### How do I know if the monthly cost is worth it?

Run the worked example with your own missed-call and no-show counts. If the monthly value is not clearly higher than the all-in cost, start with a cheaper fix such as SMS reminders or a missed-call text-back.
