## What does marketing automation mean for a small business?

It means messages that send themselves when something happens, so you do not remember each one by hand. For a local business that is usually four things:

- A welcome email or text when someone subscribes or books for the first time.
- A reminder before an appointment.
- A review request after a visit.
- A win-back message to customers who have not returned in a set number of days.

You do not need a complex funnel. You need those basics to work reliably. If you want the concept first, read [what marketing automation does, in plain English](/blog/what-does-marketing-automation-do-plain-english-guide-for-small-business-owners).

## Which one should you pick?

Match the tool to the first job, not the whole wish list.

- **Brevo:** cheapest way to start with email and SMS. Its free plan allows up to 300 emails a day once your account is approved for sending. SMS credit is sold separately.
- **Mailchimp:** the easiest start if you have fewer than 250 contacts, because the free plan covers that. Its page lists SMS as an add-on on paid plans in select countries.
- **ActiveCampaign:** the pick when a welcome series, reminders and win-back need branching logic. Multi-step automation is on every plan. Compare it in detail in [ActiveCampaign vs Mailchimp for local business](/blog/activecampaign-vs-mailchimp-local-business) and [HubSpot vs ActiveCampaign](/blog/hubspot-vs-activecampaign).
- **Klaviyo:** built for stores with an online shop. Use its free plan to test before paying.
- **HubSpot:** worth opening if you want contacts and a pipeline in one place. The Professional tier shows $800 a month with a one-time onboarding fee of $3,000, so it is not a small-shop starting point.
- **Keap:** the only entry in this list that bundles CRM, automation and text marketing in one price, at $320 a month. Its page also describes implementation services as required, so budget for them.
- **Constant Contact:** its pricing page returned a security block, so no price is stated here. See [Constant Contact vs Mailchimp](/blog/constant-contact-vs-mailchimp-local-business).

## Which tool sends welcome messages automatically?

Any tool above can, because a welcome message is the simplest automation: a trigger (someone joins your list or books) and an email or text sent straight away.

- Brevo's Starter plan includes forms that "automatically trigger follow-up emails as soon as someone signs up". Multi-step workflows begin on Standard.
- ActiveCampaign includes multi-step automation on every plan.
- Mailchimp's free plan lists flows of up to four steps, enough for a short welcome sequence.

A 5-email template and timing is in our [welcome email series guide](/blog/welcome-email-series-local-business-5-email-template).

## What should you automate first?

Start with three messages before anything complex:

1. **Welcome message**, sent immediately. Say who you are, what to expect and offer one useful thing.
2. **Appointment reminder**, sent before the booking. See [how to reduce no-shows](/blog/reduce-no-shows-appointment-business).
3. **Review request**, sent after the visit with a direct Google review link. See [how to automate Google review requests](/blog/how-to-automate-google-review-requests).

## FAQ

### What is the best marketing automation tool for a small business?
For most, Brevo or Mailchimp to start, and ActiveCampaign when you need branching sequences. There is no single winner, because the answer depends on your first automation and whether you sell online.

### Is there free marketing automation software?
Yes. Brevo has a free plan with up to 300 emails a day, Mailchimp is free under 250 contacts, Klaviyo's free plan covers 250 profiles and 500 emails a month, and HubSpot is free for up to 2 users. Free plans limit contacts, sends or automation steps.

### Do I need marketing automation with only 200 customers?
Yes if you repeat the same messages by hand. A welcome email, a reminder and a review request cost little to run once built. For 200 contacts, a free plan is enough to test.

### What is the difference between a CRM and email marketing?
Email tools send messages to lists. A CRM records each customer relationship: deals, notes and history. Keap and HubSpot lean CRM, Brevo and Mailchimp lean email, and ActiveCampaign sits between them.

### Can I switch tools later?
Yes. You can export contacts as a CSV and import them elsewhere. Automations, reports and integrations usually have to be rebuilt.

### Is SMS marketing allowed?
It needs consent, and rules differ by country. This page did not verify legal rules, so check your local law before texting. Use the platform's opt-in forms rather than importing numbers with no record of consent.

## Where does AI fit in?

Several tools now include AI for subject lines or drafts. Brevo's Starter page lists an AI content generator, and ActiveCampaign lists AI credits on every plan.
