A local business in Uzbekistan can reach most customers through Instagram, Google Search and Telegram, and can take card payments almost everywhere. DataReportal counts 14.1 million Instagram ad-reach in a population of 37.2 million, StatCounter gives Google 76.31% of search, and the central bank reports 68.27 million bank cards in circulation. This guide shows what each number means for a cafe, salon, studio or shop in Tashkent or another city.

Every figure comes from a source listed at the end. Where a source is secondary or a point is unconfirmed, the text says so.

## How many people can you reach online in Uzbekistan?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report lists a population of 37.2 million in October 2025, with 33.1 million internet users (89.0% penetration). It counts 14.1 million social media user identities (37.9% of the population) and 33.9 million cellular connections.

Most adults are online, but fewer than four in ten are counted as social media users. Social media is a strong channel, not a universal one.

## Is Instagram the main social channel?

In DataReportal's data it is the largest platform it lists for Uzbekistan: 14.1 million ad reach, equal to 53.7% of adults aged 18 and over. Facebook reaches 2.30 million (6.2% of the population) and TikTok 2.59 million adults.

DataReportal warns that ad-reach figures are not monthly active users and may not compare with earlier reports. Its Uzbekistan page does not include Telegram or YouTube, so this guide quotes no numbers for them.

## Which search engine do customers use?

Google, with Yandex second. StatCounter shows Google at 76.31%, Yandex at 21.45%, Bing 1.72% and DuckDuckGo 0.24% for September 2026 across all devices.

A fifth of searches still go through Yandex, so a business that serves Russian-speaking customers should check that its site and listings appear there too. Run searches in both Uzbek and Russian to see which language your customers use for your service.

## Can small businesses advertise on Telegram?

Directly on the official platform, it is expensive. Spot.uz (a local business outlet, January 2025) says Telegram Ads has been available in Uzbekistan since 2021, needs a budget from 2 million euros, and holds 1 million euros of it as a deposit. This is a secondary source; confirm on Telegram's ad platform.

Spot.uz adds that third-party services run campaigns with smaller budgets for a commission, and that local advertisers more often buy placements inside channels. ## What about Google Ads and Meta ads?

Check the current rules before you plan a budget. This guide does not confirm from a Google or Meta source that Uzbekistan advertisers can pay for Google Ads or Meta Ads directly, so treat that as unverified.

Whatever the channel, start from your own cost per call or booking, as in our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide).

## Do customers pay by card?

Increasingly. The Central Bank of Uzbekistan data, reported by Kun.uz on 9 February 2026, shows more than 68.27 million bank cards on 1 January 2026, up from 62 million a year earlier. There were 430,762 payment terminals, and terminal payments reached UZS 460.25 trillion, up from UZS 326.7 trillion.

For a shop or cafe, a terminal and a visible QR or card option are expected. The data does not show which cards or apps your own customers prefer, so ask them.

## Is the economy growing enough to plan for?

The World Bank reports real GDP growth of 7.7% in 2025, up from 6.7% in 2024, and projects 7.9% for 2026, led by private consumption and investment. It also notes slower growth in Russia and China, Uzbekistan's main trading partners, as a drag on exports and remittances.

Growth supports demand, but it is national data. Your own city and neighbourhood may differ.

## Is 2GIS worth a listing?

Possibly. 2GIS launched in Tashkent in 2019 with a map of more than 217,000 buildings and 35,000 organisations, per Spot.uz (secondary, 2019). No current Uzbekistan user or listing counts, so check whether your customers use it before spending time.

## FAQ

### Which social network is biggest in Uzbekistan?

Among platforms in DataReportal's Uzbekistan page, Instagram: 14.1 million ad reach in late 2025. The page does not cover Telegram.

### Is Google or Yandex more used for search?

Google, at 76.31% versus Yandex at 21.45% in September 2026 (StatCounter).

### How much does Telegram Ads cost?

Spot.uz reports a 2 million euro minimum budget directly with Telegram, with 1 million euros held as a deposit. Intermediaries sell smaller packages. Verify before planning.

### Can I rely on card payments?

Central Bank data shows 68.27 million cards and 430,762 terminals at the start of 2026, so card acceptance is common.

### Can I run Google or Meta ads from Uzbekistan?

Unverified here. Check Google Ads and Meta billing and country support directly.
