A New Zealand local business should start with a complete Google Business Profile, then add Facebook and Instagram, and use paid search only where the job value justifies the click. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report counts 5.26 million people, 5.06 million internet users and a Facebook ad reach of 3.45 million (65.6% of the population). Email and SMS need consent under the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007.

No public source gives average Google Ads cost per click for New Zealand local categories, so this guide gives no CPC benchmark. Use your own account data.

## Which Channels Reach New Zealanders?

DataReportal reports 4.24 million social media user identities (80.6% of the population), Facebook ad reach of 3.45 million, Instagram 2.65 million and LinkedIn 3.30 million members. TikTok reaches 2.04 million users aged 18 and over, 49.6% of adults. These are advertising audience estimates, not counts of active users, and the report warns against comparing them with earlier years.

| Channel | NZ figure (DataReportal 2026) | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | 3.45 million, 65.6% of population | Ad reach |
| Instagram | 2.65 million, 50.4% | Ad reach |
| LinkedIn | 3.30 million, 62.7% | Members |
| TikTok | 2.04 million, 49.6% of adults 18+ | Ad reach |

Facebook has the widest reach, so it is the usual first paid social channel. Which one suits you depends on your customers: a barber or a plumber may do well on Facebook, a design studio on Instagram or LinkedIn.

## How Should You Set Up Google Business Profile in NZ?

If you visit customers rather than serve them from a shopfront, Google treats you as a service-area business. Google says to remove your address and enter service areas instead, with up to 20 areas by city, postal code or other area, not by radius, and an overall area within about two hours of driving.

Use a +64 phone number, state your suburb clearly, and update holiday hours. Employment New Zealand lists Waitangi Day on Friday 6 February 2026, Matariki on Friday 10 July 2026 and Auckland Anniversary on Monday 26 January 2026. For 2027 it lists Matariki on Friday 25 June, but warns that dates may contain errors, so check with your local council. ## Do Reviews Matter in New Zealand?

The best data found is US-based. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults found 97% read online reviews and 41% always do. No comparable New Zealand figure was found, so treat it as a signal, not a local statistic.

Ask every customer for a review at the point of service and reply to each one. Our [review guide](/blog/google-business-profile-reviews) and [review request automation](/blog/how-to-automate-google-review-requests) cover the steps.

## Can You Email and Text Customers?

Yes, with consent. Under the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007, the Department of Internal Affairs says recipients must agree to receive your messages, you must clearly identify yourself as sender, and there must be a clear and easy way to unsubscribe.

In practice that means a tick box on your booking form, your business name in every message and an unsubscribe link or reply word. Do not buy lists. The Act is enforced by DIA, so ask them if a case is unclear.

## Do You Need to Show GST-Inclusive Prices?

Check before you advertise. The Commerce Commission enforces the Fair Trading Act, which, according to its published releases, bans false or misleading claims about prices. Whether every ad must show GST-inclusive prices was not confirmed in the sources read here, so confirm with the Commission before you publish prices.

## Worked Example: A NZ$600 Monthly Test

Every number below is an **assumption**, not a benchmark: a NZ$600 monthly budget split NZ$300 to Google search and NZ$300 to Meta, a cost per click of NZ$3 on search, and 5% of clicks becoming enquiries.

| Step | Search | Meta |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | NZ$300 | NZ$300 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | NZ$3 | NZ$1 |
| Clicks | 100 | 300 |
| Enquiry rate (assumption) | 5% | 1.5% |
| Enquiries | 5 | 4.5 |
| Cost per enquiry | NZ$60 | about NZ$67 |

On these inputs the two channels are close, which is why the first month should measure, not scale. Replace each assumption with your own results after 30 days. If an enquiry becomes a customer worth more than your cost per enquiry, keep spending.

## FAQ

### How big is the New Zealand market for local marketing?

DataReportal counts 5.26 million people and 5.06 million internet users in October 2025. Facebook ad reach is 3.45 million.

### Is Facebook or Google better for a NZ local business?

Neither wins on a source. Facebook has the widest social reach, while search catches people who are ready to buy. Test both with a small budget.

### What does Google Ads cost in New Zealand?

No source read for this guide publishes NZ category CPCs. Check Keyword Planner and your own account.

### Can I send marketing texts and emails?

Only with consent, a clear sender identity and an easy unsubscribe, per the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007.

### Do I need an address on my Google profile?

Not if you serve customers at their location. Google says to remove the address and set service areas.
