## Who Can Use Yandex Direct for a Local Business?

Yandex Direct is the self-serve tool for running ads in Yandex Search, the Yandex Advertising Network, or both at once. Yandex says the network includes Yandex services, websites of businesses and media organisations, Telegram channels, mobile apps and smart TV apps. The company states that both large corporations and small local businesses can reach their audiences through it.

The practical test is the customer. Search ads answer specific queries, so the question is whether buyers type those queries into Yandex. Display and video placements reach people by interest and behaviour, which helps only if those people use Yandex. For a business serving English-speaking customers in North America, the UK or Australia, that link is usually weak. Check where your own customers search before spending anything.

## Does the Yandex Advertising Network Reach Your Customers?

Yandex reports an average audience of 65 million users a day, which it describes as nearly half of the Russian population. The network also includes about 50,000 partner sites, and Yandex says that number keeps growing. These are reach figures for Russia, not for the English-speaking markets this blog covers.

Yandex offers three formats. Text and image ads appear in search and on network sites. Banners build brand awareness across a wide audience. Video ads run on network sites, video hosting platforms and mobile apps. Targeting covers location, age, gender and interests. A local business can use these tools, but only after confirming that its own customers are on the platform.

## What Do Sanctions Mean for a US, UK, Australian or Canadian Business?

The sources do not answer this question. Yandex's own material describes its formats, payment models and targeting, but says nothing about whether a foreign business may fund an account. A Russia-based agency page (secondary) says Google Ads is unavailable for paid promotions in Russia because of sanctions, which explains why Yandex is used there. It does not describe rules for buyers in other countries.

Treat eligibility as an open question. Before topping up an account, ask a lawyer who handles sanctions compliance in your country. Ask your bank whether it will process the payment. A blocked payment or compliance problem costs more than any test budget.

## How Do Payments and Budgets Work?

Yandex says there is no budget threshold. Advertisers bid in an auction, and all campaigns draw from one shared account that must be topped up before ads launch. A daily or weekly budget limit controls spend, which suits a small test.

Yandex Direct offers two payment models. Pay-per-click charges for every click. Pay-per-conversion lets you set a goal, such as a submitted request or a view of specific product pages, and charges only when that action happens. Clicks that do not lead to the goal are free under that model.

Google Play reviews of the Yandex Direct app include complaints that payment is available only in Russian rubles. The developer replied by directing those users to its support email. Confirm the currency and payment options in your own account before committing money.

## How Do You Test Yandex on a Small Budget?

Run a test only after the landing page and tracking are ready. Yandex's guidance lists these steps, which apply to any paid channel:

1. Build a landing page where a visitor can find contact details, view your catalogue or services, and place an order, with your main selling point stated at the top.
2. Add a Yandex Metrica tag to the site, so you can see which pages visitors view and which actions they take.
3. Set a specific goal, such as increasing sales by a stated percentage within two months, rather than a vague aim to sell more.
4. Choose your KPIs, such as click-through rate and number of conversions, before launch.
5. Pick pay-per-click or pay-per-conversion, and set a daily or weekly budget limit.

The table below uses **assumptions** for planning only. Yandex publishes no cost-per-click figures in these sources, so replace every input with your own data.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget (assumption, USD) | $300 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | $0.50 |
| Clicks | $300 / $0.50 = 600 |
| Conversion rate to booking (assumption) | 3% |
| Bookings | 600 x 3% = 18 |
| Cost per booking | $300 / 18 = about $16.67 |
| Margin per booking (assumption) | $40 |

In this example each booking earns $40 and costs about $16.67, so the test clears the margin on paper. If your real cost per booking is above your margin, stop. Compare the result with other paid options before spending more, such as [Bing Ads for local business](/blog/bing-ads-microsoft-advertising-local-business), or with [Google Local Service Ads](/blog/google-local-service-ads-worth-it-local-business) for lead-based services.

## Is the Yandex Direct App Enough to Run Campaigns?

The Google Play listing describes the app as a way to create, launch and manage campaigns from a phone. It offers Easy Start setup, campaign editing, performance tracking, budget top-ups and notifications when campaigns start or stop. The developer says more features are coming.

The reviews are mixed. Some users praise the support team. Others say advertised features are missing or that only part of the functionality works, and they point to the full account for the complete toolset. Use the app for monitoring, and do the setup and targeting work in the full account.

## FAQ

### Is Yandex Direct available outside Russia?

Yandex describes its ad network as reaching users and partner sites across Russia. The sources here do not say whether foreign businesses can fund accounts, so check sanctions rules with a lawyer before starting.

### Can a small business run Yandex ads with no minimum budget?

Yandex says there is no budget threshold. You set a daily or weekly budget limit, and all campaigns draw from one shared account that you top up before launch.

### Is Yandex better than Google Ads for a local business?

Google Ads is the normal choice for English-speaking customers. Yandex matters only if your customers search or browse on Yandex. A Russia-based agency says Google Ads is unavailable for paid promotions in Russia.

### What should I measure in a Yandex test?

Track click-through rate, conversions and cost per conversion against goals set before launch. Yandex's guidance recommends Metrica to see which pages visitors view and which actions they take.
