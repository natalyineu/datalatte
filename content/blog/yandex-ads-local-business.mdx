Yandex Direct is Yandex's self-serve advertising platform, and it only makes sense for a local business whose customers use Yandex. Yandex's help center says payment methods are available in almost any country, but cards from Russian and Belarusian banks are not accepted. Whether a business in the US, UK or EU may legally buy ads there is a sanctions question that this article could not verify. Ask a lawyer and your bank before you spend.

## Can a Non-Russian Business Advertise on Yandex Direct?

The help center lists three payment groups. One covers Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan and Turkmenistan. One covers Azerbaijan, Armenia, Georgia and Moldova. The third, "Other countries", is described as "available in almost any country". Wire transfers can be made in euros, pounds sterling, US dollars, Swiss francs, Turkish liras, yuan or dirhams.

Cards are Visa and Mastercard "except for cards issued by banks in Russia or Belarus". The help center says nothing about sanctions on this page.

## What About Sanctions?

This could not be confirmed from an official source. Yandex's pages describe payments, not legal eligibility. A secondary guide (Daily Intel Service, updated May 13, 2026) says Yandex's payment terms bar customers located in or listed under sanctions regimes. It also says US and EU rules restrict specific services, such as marketing consulting, rather than consumer-facing media buying, and that you should check your own bank and passport first. It cites law-firm analyses but is itself a blog.

Treat that as a prompt to ask, not as clearance. If you are a US, UK or EU business, get written advice before you fund an account.

## How Do You Pay and Verify?

On the first payment, individuals in the "Other countries" group upload a passport, residence permit or tax residency certificate. Yandex says document checks can take up to three days. Companies use a similar process with registration documents.

Currency is chosen when you register and stays fixed for the life of the account, according to the secondary guide. The same guide gives a minimum payment of 300 RUB, roughly 15 USD, EUR or GBP, and says international cards clear only in USD or EUR. Refunds need a written request.

## Who Is Yandex Direct Actually For?

It suits a local business that serves people who search on Yandex: for example a shop or studio in a country where Yandex is a main search engine, or one that targets Russian-speaking customers. This article has no verified market-share figure, so do not assume one. Check where your own customers search before you test it.

If you serve an English-speaking local market, Yandex has little to offer compared with Google. Start with [Google Ads for coffee shops](/blog/google-ads-for-coffee-shops) or the [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist), which cost less to set up and are far easier to verify.

## What Does Yandex Direct Cost?

Yandex does not publish a rate card by vertical, according to the secondary guide, which also found no verified 2026 CPC or CPM figures. Prices move with the ruble and with auction changes, so confirm numbers inside a live account. Do not trust any blog that quotes a single average.

The secondary guide describes one account that covers search placements and the Yandex Advertising Network, which runs display, native and video. It advises running search and network campaigns separately so you can see which one converts.

## Do You Need Special Labels for Russian Audiences?

If ads target Russian users, Russian law requires an erid token on internet ads, according to the secondary guide, which cites law 347-FZ with fines from September 1, 2023 and a 3% quarterly levy on internet advertising revenue aimed at Russian users from April 1, 2025. The guide is secondary. If this applies to you, confirm it with a Russian advertising specialist.

## FAQ

### Is Yandex Direct available outside Russia?

The help center lists payment methods for almost any country, with wire transfers in seven currencies. Whether you may legally use it depends on your country's sanctions rules, which this article could not verify.

### Can I pay Yandex Direct with a US or UK card?

The help center accepts Visa and Mastercard except those issued by banks in Russia or Belarus. A secondary guide says international cards clear in USD or EUR only. Your bank may still block the payment.

### How much does Yandex Direct cost?

Yandex publishes no rate card by vertical, and no verified 2026 CPC figure was found. Test with a small budget and measure cost per booking.

### Is Yandex better than Google Ads for local business?

For customers who search in English, Google is the normal choice. Yandex matters only if your audience uses it.

### How long does account verification take?

Yandex says document checks can take up to three days on the first payment.
