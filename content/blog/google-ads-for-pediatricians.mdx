The best way to use Google Ads for a pediatric practice is a tightly targeted Search campaign on high-intent terms such as "pediatrician near me" and "pediatrician accepting new patients", with call and booking tracking from day one. Budget depends on your market, and healthcare advertising has its own policy and privacy rules you need to follow.

Parents search the moment they need care or are choosing a new practice. If your clinic does not appear, those families may book the practice that does. Here is how to set it up.

## Which Google Ads Campaign Type Works Best for a Pediatric Office?

Search is the core campaign type, because parents type specific terms like "pediatrician near me" or "child doctor open Saturday".

- **Search:** Text ads on Google results for intent-driven queries. Start here.
- **Local Services Ads:** Available only for certain categories and regions. Check Google's current list of eligible business types and whether pediatric care is included for your area before planning around it.
- **Display and YouTube remarketing:** Use sparingly to remind site visitors to book. Healthcare advertisers face limits on audience targeting and remarketing, so review Google's healthcare and sensitive-category policies first.

Set geo-targeting to the area your patients can realistically reach, often a handful of miles, and add call assets so parents can phone from the ad. Start with one tightly themed keyword list for appointment-seeking searches and expand only after you see which terms convert.

<Callout type="warning" title="Privacy and policy">
Medical practices should be careful about what data they send to ad platforms. Do not pass patient information into tags or audiences, and talk to your compliance or legal adviser about HIPAA (or the local equivalent) before using remarketing or conversion tracking on pages where patients enter health details.
</Callout>

## How Much Should You Spend and How Do You Bid?

Costs vary a lot by city, specialty terms and competition. Healthcare keywords are often more expensive than average, so check Keyword Planner and your own account data before setting a budget. A better approach than guessing:

1. Decide what a new patient family is worth to the practice over time.
2. Decide how much you can pay to win one.
3. Start with a daily budget large enough to collect data within a few weeks, and adjust.

Avoid very small budgets that cannot gather meaningful data. Start with manual bidding or Maximize Clicks with a cap while you learn, then move to Maximize Conversions or Target CPA once you have conversion data (for example, phone calls over a set length and booking form submissions).

| Cost area | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Ad spend | Clicks from Search campaigns |
| Landing page | A fast, mobile-friendly page with hours, insurance info and a booking option |
| Tracking and reporting | Call tracking, conversion setup and a monthly review |
| Management | Your time or an agency's fee |

## Choosing Keywords

Group keywords by intent:

- **New patient:** "pediatrician near me", "pediatrician accepting new patients", "[city] pediatrician".
- **Convenience:** "pediatrician open Saturday", "same-day pediatric appointment".
- **Services:** "well-child visit", "school physical", "vaccinations for kids".
- **Insurance:** "pediatrician that takes [insurer]" if you want to attract those families.

Use phrase and exact match, and add negatives such as "jobs", "salary", "residency", "free", "online course" and symptoms-based searches you do not want to answer with an ad. Review the Search Terms report weekly.

## Writing Ad Copy That Gets Parents to Call

Parents care about trust, convenience and how their child will be treated. Only make claims you can support and that match your credentials.

- Headline ideas: "Pediatrician in [City]. Accepting New Patients", "Same-Day Sick Visits Available", "Evening and Weekend Hours".
- Description ideas: "Caring pediatric team. Book online or call today. Most insurance accepted."
- Assets: call, location, sitelinks (new patients, services, insurance, hours) and structured snippets listing services.

Do not use "Google Guaranteed" language unless you are actually in that program. Avoid unverifiable superlatives such as "best" or "#1". Make the booking path short, since a simple booking button or click-to-call can reduce friction.

## Tracking, Reporting and Optimizing

Set up conversion tracking for phone calls from ads, calls from your website, and booking form submissions, without capturing health details. Use Google Analytics to see which campaigns bring families who actually book. Our [analytics service](/services/analytics) covers this.

Weekly routine:

1. Review search terms and add negatives.
2. Add high-performing queries as exact match keywords.
3. Compare cost per call or booking by campaign and ad group.
4. Test at least two or three headlines and two descriptions.
5. Check call recordings or logs, where permitted, to see what callers ask and whether phones are answered.

Missed calls waste ad spend. Make sure your front desk or answering service can pick up during ad hours.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I start with a small budget, like $200 a month?**
A: You can, but a very small budget collects little data and gives few clicks in a competitive market. If you start small, use tight geo-targeting and exact-match, high-intent keywords, and be realistic about volume.

**Q: Should I use Local Services Ads?**
A: Only if your category and area are eligible. If they are, test them alongside Search and compare cost per booked appointment, not just cost per lead.

**Q: Why am I getting clicks but few calls?**
A: Check your search terms report, landing page speed and clarity, ad schedule and whether the phones are answered. Informational searches, such as symptoms, rarely turn into bookings.

**Q: Can I bid on competitor names?**
A: Google generally allows bidding on competitor keywords, but you cannot use their trademarks in ad text in most cases. Weigh the cost against more specific angles such as hours, languages spoken and new-patient availability.

**Q: How long until Google Ads brings patients?**
A: You can see clicks and calls within days, but expect several weeks of testing and optimization before results stabilize.
