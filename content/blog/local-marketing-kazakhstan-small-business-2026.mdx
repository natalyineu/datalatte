A small business in Kazakhstan should put its main social effort on Instagram, not Facebook. DataReportal lists Instagram ad reach at 13.1 million, 62.7% of the population, against 2.50 million for Facebook. Google Ads is listed as available, and 2GIS lets anyone add a company for free. Payment methods for Meta and Telegram ads could not be verified, so check them in your account before planning a budget.

This guide is for salons, cafes, shops and service firms in Kazakhstan. Figures come from the sources at the end, checked on 1 November 2026.

## Which Platforms Reach the Most People in Kazakhstan?

Instagram and TikTok. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report lists 20.9 million people, 19.5 million internet users (93.4%) and 27.3 million mobile connections, 131% of the population. Social media user identities total 16.9 million, 80.8%.

| Platform | Ad reach, late 2025 | Change reported |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok (adults 18+ only) | 16.9 million | up 12.4% |
| Instagram | 13.1 million (62.7% of population) | up 9.6% year on year |
| Facebook | 2.50 million (12.0% of population) | down 3.8% year on year |
| LinkedIn (members) | 1.90 million | up 18.8% |
| Snapchat | 795 thousand | down 7.0% |
| Messenger | 354 thousand | down 8.1% |
| YouTube, Telegram | Not reported | n/a |

Instagram's reach is about 5.2 times Facebook's (13.1 divided by 2.50). DataReportal warns that some values exceed 100% of a base, including TikTok at 122.8% of adults, and that platform corrections can distort trends, so use the table as a ranking.

## What Does That Mean for Where You Put Budget?

Build your offer and bookings around Instagram and TikTok, and keep a Facebook Page only as a listing. A Facebook-first plan copied from another country would reach roughly one fifth of the Instagram audience on the numbers above.

Telegram and YouTube are absent from the DataReportal page, so their size in Kazakhstan was not verified here. If your customers use them, test with a small budget after checking that the ad tools accept your account and payment method.

## Can a Kazakh Business Run Google Ads?

Yes, on paper. Google's list of billing countries includes Kazakhstan. In that list the account currency and payment method columns read "All", but Google says the options you see depend on your billing country, currency and payment setting, and that prepay is not offered in many countries.

Open the billing page in your own account to see what is available. No source checked publishes Kazakh click prices, and DataReportal does not report Google ad reach for the country.

## How Do You Use 2GIS?

2GIS is a city map and business directory. Its Kazakhstan site offers "Add organization", and the company's help centre says reference data about companies are published for free, that anyone can report a missing business, and that at least one contact detail is needed so staff can verify it. The site also links to paid advertising for businesses.

Claim your listing first and check the address, hours and phone number. Paid placement terms and Kazakh prices were not found, and a 2026 Russian-language trade article reports complaints from small businesses about 2GIS advertising, which could not be read in full. Weigh that before paying.

## How Big Is the Small Business Sector in Kazakhstan?

The government reported on 13 July 2026, as carried by Qazinform, that small and medium businesses produced 40.9% of GDP in 2025, up from 32.9% in 2020. It also said there were about 2.2 million active SMEs out of 2.4 million registered on 1 January 2026.

That is a large crowd of competitors, many of them sole traders. The implication for marketing is simple: a clear niche and fast replies matter more than extra reach.

## What Does a First Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for a beauty salon taking bookings by Instagram message. They show the method, not Kazakh results. Currency is "units" of tenge.

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Gross profit per new client over a year (assumption) | 40,000 units |
| Booking enquiries that become clients (assumption) | 25% |
| Most you can pay per enquiry and still break even | 40,000 x 25% = 10,000 units |
| Test budget for one month (assumption) | 100,000 units |
| Enquiries needed to break even | 100,000 / 10,000 = 10 |

Ten booking enquiries from 100,000 units would pay for the month on a one-year view. Record which enquiries mention Instagram, 2GIS or Google. The [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to split spend after the first month.

## How Do You Get Found on Google Maps in Kazakhstan?

Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. It also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Fill in every field, use real photos, reply to every review and keep the name, address and phone number identical everywhere. Keep the name, address and phone number identical on Google, 2GIS and Instagram. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes through each field.

Do not offer a discount or a free item in return for a review. Google's contributed content policy says merchants must not offer incentives, such as payment, discounts, free goods or services, in exchange for posting a review. Asking is fine. Rewarding is not. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for ways to ask.

## What Mistakes Do Kazakh Small Businesses Make?

These are recommendations, not study findings.

- Building a Facebook-first plan when Instagram reaches about five times as many people.
- Paying for a directory placement before the free listing is correct.
- Assuming a payment method works for ads without checking billing in the account.
- Judging by followers instead of booked clients.

## FAQ

Instagram, by a wide margin. DataReportal lists 13.1 million Instagram ad reach and 2.50 million for Facebook in late 2025.
