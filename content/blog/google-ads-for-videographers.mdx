A videographer should start Google Ads with a small Search campaign aimed at one service in one area, such as corporate video or event filming, with call and form tracking and a long negative keyword list. No public benchmark covers videography itself. The nearest, Arts and Entertainment in LocaliQ's 2026 search benchmark, shows a $1.63 median click cost and a 5.91% conversion rate, against $5.42 and 8.18% for all industries. Treat that as a rough range, not a forecast.

This guide covers what to bid on, how to keep ads local, what to block and how to calculate whether the ads pay.

## What Do Google Ads Cost for a Videographer?

The figures come from Digital Applied's summary of the WordStream and LocaliQ 2026 search benchmark (US search campaigns on Google and Microsoft Ads, April 2025 to March 2026, medians). WordStream's own page returned an access error, so these are secondary.

| Median, search campaigns | CPC | CTR | Conversion rate | Cost per lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arts and Entertainment | $1.63 | 12.75% | 5.91% | $26.84 |
| All industries | $5.42 | 6.64% | 8.18% | $66.69 |

Arts and Entertainment is broad. It does not isolate video production, and a corporate video buyer searching for a supplier may be searched and priced differently from a person looking for entertainment. Treat the numbers as a planning range and use your own data after the first month.

## Which Service Should You Advertise First?

Pick one lane. Corporate clients, weddings and events search with different words, expect different proof and spend different amounts. A narrow campaign makes keywords, ads and landing page match.

- **Corporate and brand video.** The landing page needs two or three relevant clips, a clear process and a way to request a quote.
- **Events and weddings.** Date availability, packages and reviews matter most.
- **Real estate or local business video.** Fast turnaround and a price range help.

Keywords should name the service and place. Write several close variants as phrase and exact match. ## How Do You Keep Ads Local?

Google says radius targets must be at least 1 km and that "Presence or interest" is recommended as a best practice on Search, while "Presence" fits when you want only people actually in your locations. Location targeting is best effort, so check reports.

If you travel to clients, set the campaign to the cities you will actually serve. Pair it with your Business Profile: Google lets service-area businesses list up to 20 service areas by city or postal code, does not allow radius-based areas, and says the whole area should be no more than about 2 hours' driving from your base. If you serve clients at your address, show it. If you only visit clients, Google says to remove the address. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) covers the rest.

## What Should You Block With Negative Keywords?

Video searches attract many people who will never hire you: students, hobbyists and viewers. Add negatives for words like "free", "download", "software", "template", "course", "jobs" and "how to". Google says broad match negatives block a search only when it contains all the negative terms, phrase negatives need the terms in order, and negatives do not match close variants such as plurals, so add those yourself.

Check the Search terms report weekly for the first month. See our guide to [negative keywords](/blog/google-ads-negative-keywords-stop-wasting-budget) for a starting list.

## How Should You Track Enquiries?

For a service business, calls and quote forms are the results. Google says clicks on a call asset on mobile cost the same as headline clicks. The number must be in strict international format and visible on your site or verified through Search Console. For form submissions, set a conversion action with the Google tag and count each quote request once. If you want calls from your website counted, Google needs a phone snippet, and you set a minimum call length that counts as a conversion.

Track what happens next, too: how many enquiries become paid jobs and the average job value.

## What Does the Math Look Like?

Every input below is an **assumption**, using the benchmark ranges.

| Input (assumption) | Arts and Entertainment-like | All-industry-like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $600 | $600 |
| Cost per click | $1.63 | $5.42 |
| Clicks | about 368 | about 111 |
| Conversion rate | 5.91% | 8.18% |
| Enquiries | about 22 | about 9 |
| Enquiries that become jobs | 20% | 20% |
| Jobs | about 4 | about 2 |
| Cost per job | about $150 | about $300 |

If an average job pays $1,500 (an assumption), both ranges are profitable, but only if the 20% close rate holds. If your jobs pay $400, the higher cost range loses money. Calculate your break-even before you spend: monthly budget divided by jobs won must be below your profit per job. ## FAQ

### How much should a videographer spend on Google Ads?

Choose a test you can afford, such as $600 for a month in the example above (an assumption). The benchmark suggests clicks may cost from about $1.63 to $5.42, so that buys between roughly 110 and 370 clicks.

### Are Google Ads worth it for wedding or event filming?

They can be if you have a clear package, reviews and a quick way to check dates. No source in this article measures wedding videographers specifically.

### Should I use a Smart campaign?

A Smart campaign shows ads on Search and Maps from a linked Business Profile and charges per click. It gives less control over search terms, which matters for a trade where many searches are not buyers.

### How do I stop paying for clicks from outside my area?

Use location targeting for the cities you serve, check the "Presence" setting and review the locations report. Google notes location targeting is best effort.
