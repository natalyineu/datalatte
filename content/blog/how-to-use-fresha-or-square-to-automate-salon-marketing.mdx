Both Fresha and Square Appointments can automate booking reminders and send marketing messages to past clients. Fresha publishes its prices: reminders by email are free, 100 texts a month are free, marketing emails cost $0.02 after 50 free, and marketing texts cost $0.08 each. Square's own pages gave conflicting marketing prices when checked, so confirm them in your Square dashboard before you commit.

This guide compares what each tool automates, what it costs, and which messages are worth turning on first. It is written for salon owners already using, or choosing, one of the two. For processing fees and subscriptions, see our separate [Fresha, Square and Vagaro fee comparison](/blog/fresha-vs-square-vs-vagaro-salon-booking-software).

## What Does Fresha Automate, and What Does It Cost?

According to Fresha's pricing page, the Independent plan is $19.95 a month for one bookable team member, and Team is $14.95 per bookable team member. The page shows dollars without naming a currency, and prices exclude local sales taxes. Email and app notifications are free and unlimited.

Texts and WhatsApp messages include 100 free a month (per team member on Team), then $0.02 per text. Marketing emails include 50 free a month, then $0.02 each, and marketing texts cost $0.08 each. Meta Pixel and Google Analytics integrations are free. Paid add-ons include Client Loyalty at $59.95 a month and Google Rating Boost at $14.95 a month per location.

## What Does Square Offer, and What Is Unclear?

Square Appointments lists three tiers: Free, Plus and Premium. The Free tier includes online booking and appointment reminders. Plus adds cancellation policy with no-show fees, multi-staff booking and a waitlist, and Premium adds reports and resource management. Square's US pricing page returned no monthly prices when read for this guide.

Square also sells a separate marketing product that describes automated campaigns such as welcome messages, birthday offers and win-backs for lapsed customers. Prices for its email and text features differed between Square pages seen in search results, and the marketing pages could not be loaded directly, so no figures are quoted here. Open Pricing and subscriptions in your Square Dashboard to see what applies to your plan.

## Which Automations Should You Turn On First?

Start with messages tied to a booking, because they do not need a campaign calendar.

1. **Reminder before the appointment.** It is the simplest message to automate and needs no campaign planning.
2. **Thank-you and review request after the visit.** Send the Google review link we explain in our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).
3. **Rebooking nudge** a few weeks after the visit, timed to the service cycle. Our [salon rebooking script guide](/blog/salon-rebooking-script-that-gets-clients-back) has wording.
4. **Win-back message** to clients who have not visited in a while.

Segment by service when you can, since a colour client and a cut client rebook on different cycles. See [email segmentation for hair salons](/blog/email-marketing-segmentation-for-hair-salons).

## What Are the Rules for Marketing Emails and Texts?

For email in the US, the FTC's CAN-SPAM guide requires a clear opt-out, a valid physical postal address, honest subject lines and an ad disclosure, and says opt-outs must be honored within 10 business days. Each violating email can carry a penalty of up to $53,088. Texting rules require consent and vary by country, and the FCC page could not be read for this guide, so check your local rules before sending marketing texts. UK salons should read our [UK email and SMS guide](/blog/email-sms-marketing-uk-salons).

## FAQ

### Is Fresha or Square cheaper for salon marketing?
Fresha publishes message prices, so you can model it exactly. Square's marketing prices conflicted across its pages, so compare using your own dashboard quote.

### Can I send automated texts for free?
On Fresha, 100 texts a month are free (per team member on Team), then $0.02 each. Marketing texts are $0.08 each from the first one.

### Does Fresha charge for clients who book through Instagram?
Fresha's pricing page says clients booking through your own link, Facebook or Instagram are free, while new clients from its marketplace carry a 20% one-time commission with a $6 minimum.

### Do I need consent to text clients?
Yes in most places, and rules vary by country. Check your local requirements before sending marketing texts.

### Which message should I automate first?
The appointment reminder, then a review request, then a rebooking nudge.
