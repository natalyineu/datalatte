A coffee shop can start Google Ads at about $300 a month ($10 a day) and should raise it only when tracked calls, direction requests or orders pay back. At the 2026 benchmark for Restaurants & Food, which is the closest published category, $300 buys roughly 146 clicks at $2.05 each, or about 10 leads at $30.57 each. Below about $200 a month you collect too few clicks to learn what works, so treat the first two to three months as a test.

These numbers come from an industry average, not from your shop. Costs depend on your **location**, **competition** and **goals**, so the rest of this guide shows how to set a budget you can defend with your own numbers.

## The Ideal Coffee Shop Google Ads Budget in 2026

There is no one-size-fits-all number, so start from what a budget buys. The table below divides each monthly budget by the 2026 Restaurants & Food averages: $2.05 per click and $30.57 per lead. It is arithmetic on a category average, so your own cost per click will differ by city and by how many cafés bid on the same searches. Confirm local costs in Google's Keyword Planner.

| Monthly budget | Average daily budget | Clicks at $2.05 | Leads at $30.57 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $200 | about $6.60 | about 98 | about 7 |
| $300 | about $9.90 | about 146 | about 10 |
| $500 | about $16.45 | about 244 | about 16 |
| $1,000 | about $32.90 | about 488 | about 33 |

Here a "lead" is a conversion your account tracks, such as a call, a direction request or an order. Daily figures divide the month by 30.4, the multiplier Google uses for its monthly spending limit.

> Pro tip: start with at least $300 a month. Much less than that often fails to produce enough data to optimize.

## 1. How Much Does Google Ads Cost for a Coffee Shop?

For Restaurants & Food, WordStream by LocaliQ lists an average cost per click of $2.05, a click-through rate of 6.83%, a conversion rate of 8.05% and a cost per lead of $30.57. The all-industry averages are $5.42, 6.64%, 8.18% and $66.69. Food businesses are cheaper per click than most categories, but these are averages across many advertisers, not a quote for your street.

### What Drives Cost?

* **Competition:** how many nearby cafés and chains advertise on the same searches
* **Quality Score:** Google rewards relevant ads and landing pages with lower costs
* **Device:** mobile searches dominate local coffee intent
* **Time of day:** morning and lunch searches are often more competitive
* **Your Google Business Profile:** an incomplete listing with few reviews can weaken results, so keep it strong with our [Google Business Profile service](/services/google-business-profile)

### How Does Google Charge Against Your Budget?

You set an average daily budget. On a given day a campaign might spend up to twice that amount, and Google's monthly spending limit is 30.4 times the average daily budget, so a $10 daily budget cannot cost more than $304 in a month. You are not billed above that limit.

## 2. How to Set Your Coffee Shop Google Ads Budget

### a. Define Your Goal

* **In-store visits:** Search campaigns with location extensions, plus a strong Google Business Profile
* **Online orders or catering:** Search ads pointing to an order page, or Performance Max if you have conversion tracking in place (see our [Performance Max guide](/blog/google-performance-max-for-local-business-complete-guide-2026))
* **Awareness:** Display or YouTube, but only after search is working

### b. Work Backwards From Value

A simple formula:

1. Decide how many new regulars you want per month.
2. Estimate what a regular is worth over a few months (average ticket times visits).
3. Decide what you can pay for each new customer and still be profitable.
4. Divide by your expected conversion rate, using your own data once you have it.

Percentage-of-revenue rules vary widely, so treat any such rule as a starting point, not a target.

### c. Start Small, Test, Then Scale

Start at a level you can sustain for 8 to 12 weeks. If tracked results are profitable, raise the budget gradually, for example by 10 to 20 percent at a time, and watch cost per customer as you do.

## 3. Example Budget Split for $500 a Month

**Illustration with assumption inputs, not a case study.** Take a mid-sized-city café with $500 a month. At the benchmark $30.57 per lead, that is about 16 leads. Assume 40% of leads become a first-time customer, and assume a new regular spends $7 a visit across 12 visits in three months.

| Step | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Leads (benchmark) | $500 / $30.57 | about 16 |
| New customers (assumption: 40%) | 16 x 40% | about 6 |
| Value per new regular (assumption) | $7 x 12 visits | $84 |
| Revenue from new regulars | 6 x $84 | about $504 |
| Spend | monthly budget | $500 |

On these assumptions the ads roughly return their spend in revenue within three months, before the cost of coffee and labour. That is a break-even test, not a profit, so improve targeting, ad copy and the landing page before adding money. You could divide the budget like this:

| Campaign (assumption) | Monthly spend | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Search ads for "coffee near me" style terms | $300 | Drive in-store visits during morning and lunch |
| Search ads for specific offers (catering, breakfast specials) | $100 | Capture higher-value orders |
| Retargeting or testing reserve | $100 | Bring back website visitors and test new ideas |

## 4. Is $10 a Day Enough for Google Ads?

It can be enough to start, but it is thin for a busy area. A $10 daily budget caps the month at $304. At the Restaurants & Food average of $2.05 per click, that is about 148 clicks and roughly 10 leads, so you need tight location and time targeting and a patient test period.

## 5. How to Make Your Coffee Shop Google Ads Budget Go Further

### a. Use Location Targeting

If you are in a walkable neighborhood, target a small radius around the shop. If you rely on office workers, target the surrounding business district. Add location extensions so your address appears in the ad.

### b. Schedule Ads for Peak Hours

Many cafés see the most demand in the morning and around lunch. Use ad scheduling to concentrate spend on those windows, and check the "Hour of day" report to see what your account actually does.

### c. Keep Your Profile Strong

Photos, hours, reviews and accurate categories improve both organic visibility and ad performance.

### d. Retarget Website Visitors

If your site gets steady traffic, remarketing can bring back people who looked at your menu or catering page but did not order.

### e. Choose Keywords Carefully and Add Negatives

Broad match with no negative keywords is a classic budget leak. Start from the lists in [best Google Ads keywords for coffee shops](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-coffee-shops-in-2026) and the wider [Google Ads for coffee shops guide](/blog/google-ads-for-coffee-shops-complete-2026-guide).

## FAQ

### Can I run Google Ads with only $200 a month for my coffee shop?

You can, but it is tight. At $200 a month, about $6.60 a day, the Restaurants & Food average of $2.05 per click buys roughly 98 clicks and about 7 leads. Use tight location targeting and peak hours only, and treat it as a small test.

### How long until I see results from Google Ads?

You will see clicks within hours. Calls, direction requests and orders can show up in the first week or two if tracking is set up correctly. Reliable insight into which ads and keywords work typically takes several weeks.

### Should I hire an agency or do it myself?

With a small budget and time to check the account weekly, you can manage it yourself. Once spending grows, or if you are too busy to review the account, a specialist can help avoid wasted spend. Compare fees against how much budget they can realistically save.

### Do Google Ads work for a small-town coffee shop?

Yes, though differently. Competition and cost per click may be lower, but search volume is lower too, so a budget can run out quickly. Targeting terms like "coffee near me" and visitors passing through can add volume.

### How do I know my ads are bringing people into the shop?

Use call tracking, direction-request conversions, a promo code redeemable in-store, and store-visit conversions where Google makes them available. Also ask new customers how they found you.
