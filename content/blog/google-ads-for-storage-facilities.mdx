A storage facility should run Google Ads as a small local Search campaign aimed at people who need a unit now, with call and direction tracking from day one. No public source gives a self-storage click price, so plan with the all-industry average from WordStream by LocaliQ for 2026: $5.42 per click and $66.69 per lead. Replace those numbers with your own after 30 days.

This guide is for independent facilities in the US, UK, Australia and Canada that compete with national chains. Every figure has a source at the end. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Storage Facility?

LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks do not list self-storage as an industry. The report covers 13,474 search campaigns across all industries, so treat its averages as a rough starting range, not a forecast. The categories closest in behavior are Real Estate ($3.22 per click, $102.51 per lead) and Business Services ($5.87 per click, $93.69 per lead).

Searchers for storage often compare two or three nearby facilities and decide within a day. That makes cost per rented unit more useful than cost per click. A trade group report on UK self-storage Google Ads exists (SSA UK, Q2 2025), but its click and lead prices are not public, so they are not used here.

## Which Keywords Bring Renters?

Group keywords by the problem the renter has, then give each group its own ad and landing page section.

| Renter need | Example keywords |
| --- | --- |
| Unit type | climate controlled storage, 10x10 storage unit, vehicle storage |
| Location | storage units in your city, storage near a named neighborhood |
| Life event | storage while moving, student storage, renovation storage |
| Price | cheap storage units, first month free storage |

Phrase and exact match give more control than broad match. Google says broad match can show for related searches that do not share your keyword's exact meaning, and that Smart Bidding is critical when you use it. Add negative keywords for "jobs", "auction", "storage wars", "lock" and "container for sale" if you do not sell them. Negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals yourself.

## How Do You Keep Ads Within Your Trade Area?

Storage is a short-drive decision, so set a tight radius around the facility. Google Ads allows a minimum radius of 1 km. Google recommends broad geo targeting ("Presence or interest") as best practice on Search, but if you see clicks from places you cannot serve, test "Presence" instead.

Link your Business Profile to Google Ads as a location asset. Google says a location asset can show your address, a map, travel distance and a Call button, and that the data comes from your Business Profile. Keep hours, photos and unit sizes there up to date. See our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).

## Should You Count Phone Calls?

Yes. Many renters call to ask about availability. Google says clicks on a call asset cost the same as headline clicks, and call reporting lets you count calls as conversions. A call-only format is covered in our [call-only campaigns guide](/blog/google-ads-call-only-campaigns-service-businesses).

Set a minimum call length that reflects a real inquiry, then compare booked reservations with the call log each week. Track online reservation clicks as a second conversion alongside calls.

## What Does the Math Look Like?

All inputs below are **assumptions** built on the all-industry averages. A lead is a call or reservation request.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $1,500 |
| CPC | $5.42 |
| Clicks | about 277 |
| Conversion rate | 8.18% |
| Leads | about 22.6 |
| Cost per lead | about $66 |
| Leads that rent a unit | 25% |
| New renters | about 5.7 |
| Cost per new renter | about $265 |
| Monthly rent (assumption) | $120 |
| Months a renter stays (assumption) | 8 |
| Rent per renter over the stay | $960 |

On these assumptions, 5.7 renters bring about $5,400 in rent over the stay, so the first month does not pay back but the stay does. The result depends on how long units stay occupied and how well you convert calls. Change those two inputs first. Facilities with many empty units may also prefer a [moving-company partnership](/blog/google-ads-for-moving-companies) to paid clicks.

## Should You Bid on Competitors or Your Own Name?

Your own name usually costs little and protects the top of the page from competitors. Test it with a small budget and check whether those clicks would have come free. Bidding on large chains' names often brings expensive clicks and poor ad relevance, so test it only after your core campaign works.

## How Do You Beat the Big Chains?

Chains buy broad terms. A local facility wins on specifics: a named neighborhood, drive-up access, extended gate hours, unit sizes in stock, and reviews that mention your location. Put those in headlines and on the landing page. Our [Google Ads guide for contractors](/blog/google-ads-for-contractors) shows how another location-based business sets radius and ad schedules.

## FAQ

### How much should a storage facility spend on Google Ads?

No public source sets a standard. With the $5.42 all-industry click price, $1,500 a month buys roughly 277 clicks. Start lower if you have few units to fill, and scale once you know your cost per rented unit.

### Are there storage-specific Google Ads benchmarks?

LocaliQ's 2026 report does not list self-storage. A UK trade association has published a Q2 2025 report, but its prices were not available to check.

### Should I bid on my own business name?

Test it. It protects the listing from competitors, but check whether you would get those clicks free from organic and Business Profile results.

### Do phone calls count as conversions?

Yes, if you set up call reporting. Choose a minimum call length that matches a real inquiry.

### How long until Google Ads work?

Clicks start within hours of approval. A fair test of rented units takes at least a month of data, because rentals follow calls by days.
