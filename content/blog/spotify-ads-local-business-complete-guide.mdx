A local business can run Spotify ads through Spotify Ads Manager, a self-serve tool with a minimum campaign budget of 250 in your local currency. Pricing is an auction, so there is no fixed rate card and Spotify does not publish a CPM. You set a daily or lifetime budget, pick a location and audience, upload a 30-second audio ad or use Spotify's free voiceover service, and wait about 24 to 48 hours for approval.

This guide covers what Spotify publishes, what it does not, and how to test the channel cheaply. Every figure comes from a source listed at the end. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## How Big Is Spotify's Ad-Supported Audience?

Spotify's second-quarter 2026 report, dated 4 August 2026, lists 777 million monthly active users, up 12% on the year, and 300 million Premium subscribers. Ad-supported monthly active users were 494 million. Premium subscribers do not hear ads, so the ad-supported figure is the pool you can reach.

These are global numbers. Spotify does not split the ad-supported audience by city in that report, so you cannot read a local audience size from it. Ads Manager shows impression forecasts for the audience you select, and that forecast is the number to trust for your area.

## What Ad Formats Can a Local Business Run?

Spotify's ad pages list audio, video, display and carousel formats. The Ads Manager help page says you can build an audio or video campaign and reach listeners across music, podcasts, or both. For most local businesses, audio is the simplest start because the creative is a script and a short companion image.

Spotify's specs for audio ads:

| Item | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Length | Up to 30 seconds (music and podcast) |
| File types | MP3, WAV or OGG, 50 MB maximum |
| Script length | No more than 65 words for 30 seconds (recommended) |
| Companion image | 1:1, at least 600x600 px, JPEG or PNG, 200 KB maximum |
| Advertiser name | 25 characters maximum |
| Tagline | 40 characters maximum |

Spotify also says that adding video and display to audio campaigns can drive up to 90% more incremental sales than audio alone. That is Spotify's own claim, with no method given on the page, so treat it as a vendor figure.

## What Targeting Does Spotify Offer?

Spotify lists five targeting groups: demographic, location and device; interest and behavioural; contextual (music genres and podcast topics); custom and lookalike audiences; and daypart targeting by the listener's local time. Device targeting includes connected TVs and in-car listening.

For a shop or studio, the useful settings are location, age range and daypart. A coffee shop can limit delivery to morning hours. A gym can test evening hours. Spotify says changes to age targeting and location targeting need re-approval, so choose them carefully before launch.

## How Much Do Spotify Ads Cost?

Spotify says Ads Manager runs on an auction and "doesn't come at a fixed cost." You choose a daily or lifetime budget and a bid strategy: a maximum bid, a cost-per-result bid, or automated bidding. The pricing page gives no CPM, no price range and no cost benchmark.

Some third-party blogs quote CPMs for audio. Those are estimates from writers, not Spotify rates, and this guide does not use them. For the cost of the channel in your market, create a draft campaign and read the forecast in Ads Manager. ## What Does a First Test Look Like?

The inputs below are **assumptions**, not Spotify rates. They show how to turn a CPM into reach so you can compare it with your forecast.

| | Assumption A | Assumption B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | 250 | 250 |
| CPM (assumption) | 15 | 25 |
| Impressions | about 16,700 | 10,000 |
| Cost per 1,000 | 15 | 25 |

The formula is budget divided by CPM, times 1,000. If Ads Manager forecasts a very different number for your city, trust the forecast. Then decide in advance what a success looks like: a promo code redeemed, a landing page visit, or a booking. Audio is hard to attribute, so a unique offer or a short URL helps.

## How Do You Set Up the Campaign?

Spotify's own steps are: set an objective, define the audience, choose a delivery goal, set the budget with bid cap, pacing and frequency, and create the ad. Spotify offers a free audio ad creation service as part of this.

1. Register for Ads Manager. Registration is free.
2. Pick an objective and your city or region.
3. Add age and daypart settings if they match your customer.
4. Set a budget of at least 250 and a flight date.
5. Write a script of 65 words or less, or request a voiceover.
6. Upload a 600x600 companion image with your name and offer.
7. Submit and wait for approval.

The free voiceover service lets you write the script, choose a language and voice profile, and pick background music. Spotify says you are notified within 48 hours that the ad is recorded and ready to review. The page marks the free offer as subject to change.

## How Long Does Approval Take?

Spotify says ad approval is usually 24 to 48 hours, including requests made with its free creative tools. Edits to a live ad's audio file, companion image or click-through URL also need re-approval, and the ad is paused for 24 to 48 hours while it is reviewed.

Plan for that delay. Do not schedule a campaign to start the same morning as a promotion, and avoid editing a live ad during a busy week.

## Is Spotify Better Than Other Local Channels?

It depends on your customer and goal. Spotify reaches people while they listen, so it works for awareness and for reminders near your hours. It is weaker for people who are searching right now. ## FAQ

### What is the minimum budget for Spotify ads?

Spotify's Ads Manager help page says you can launch a campaign with a minimum budget of 250.00 in your local currency. The pricing page does not state a minimum, so confirm it inside your account.

### Does Spotify publish its ad prices?

No. Spotify says Ads Manager runs on an auction with no fixed cost, and it does not publish a CPM. Use the forecast in Ads Manager for your targeting.

### How long can a Spotify audio ad be?

Music and podcast ads in Ads Manager can run up to 30 seconds. A 60-second format exists only in select markets for Direct campaigns.

### Can I make the ad without a studio?

Yes. Spotify says a voice actor will produce your audio ad free of charge, though the offer is subject to change. You supply the script and choose a voice profile.

### Can I target by time of day?

Yes. Spotify lists daypart targeting, with days and times based on the listener's local time.
