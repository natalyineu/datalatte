Hire a digital marketing consultant after you have a clear goal, such as booked appointments, and a monthly ad budget. Before signing, check three things.

You should own every ad and analytics account. The consultant should report on leads or sales, not only clicks. Any badge they show should mean what they say it means. Fees for freelancers and agencies are not published by any neutral source, so compare written quotes.

This guide covers what to check, what a Google Partner badge does and does not prove, how to share account access safely and how to compare cost against an in-house hire.

## Freelancer, Agency or In-House: Which Fits a Local Business?

A freelancer suits a clear, narrow task such as a Google Ads setup. An agency suits a business that needs several channels run together. An in-house marketer suits a business with enough work for a full role. No neutral source publishes typical freelancer or agency fees, so treat any "average price" you read with caution and ask for quotes.

The one price reference available is wages. The US Bureau of Labor Statistics reports median pay of $166,790 a year for marketing managers and $133,660 for advertising and promotions managers (May 2025). Those are employee wages, without benefits or tools, and cover a senior role, not a small shop's first hire.

## What Does a Google Partner Badge Prove?

Less than many owners assume. Google says a Partner needs a minimum 70% optimization score on its manager account, $10,000 in ad spend over 90 days across managed accounts, and certification for at least 50% of account strategists (counted up to 100 users). Premier Partner adds a place in the top 3% of participating companies in a country.

These measure the agency's account management at scale. They do not show results for a business like yours. Ask for the agency's Partner status in your country, and ask which of its current clients have a similar size and budget.

## Who Should Own the Ad Accounts?

You should. Create the Google Ads, Google Business Profile, Analytics and Meta accounts yourself, then give the consultant access. Our [Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) shows what to set up first. Google Ads access has three levels: Read-only, Standard and Admin. Only Admins can give account access, change access levels and cancel invitations.

Google also recommends having at least one additional admin, so you do not lose access if one admin becomes unavailable.

Meta works the same way: you add a partner to your Business Portfolio and choose which ad accounts and Pages they can reach. If a consultant insists on running ads from their own account under a single invoice, you may lose history and audiences when you leave.

## What Should You Ask Before You Sign?

Ask for these in writing:

- Which numbers will be reported monthly, and who sets up conversion tracking
- Whether ad spend is billed by the platform to you or marked up
- The notice period and what you receive on exit, including account access and creative files
- What the consultant will not do, so you can fill the gaps

## Which Red Flags Should Make You Walk Away?

Guaranteed rankings or guaranteed lead counts, because no one controls Google's results; Google states there is no way to pay for a better local ranking. Refusal to share account access. Selling reviews: the FTC's rule bans creating or buying fake reviews and testimonials, including AI-generated ones.

Also treat vague reports with suspicion. The FTC says advertising claims must be truthful and evidence-based, and that applies to claims made on your behalf.

## FAQ

### How much does a marketing consultant cost?
No neutral source publishes this. Get at least three written quotes for the same scope and compare what each includes.

### Is a Google Partner always better?
No. The badge reflects account-level management criteria, not results for your type of business. Ask for relevant, current client work.

### Should I give a consultant admin access?
Give the access level the work needs. Keep yourself as an Admin, and add a second admin you trust.

### How long before I see results?
Not published. Agree on a review date, such as 90 days, and on the numbers you will judge by.
