## How much do Google Ads cost for family law?

[LocaliQ's 2026 search advertising benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/) (updated June 1, 2026, based on thousands of customer campaigns) list these figures for legal services versus all industries:

| Metric | Attorneys & Legal | All industries |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per click | $9.87 | $5.42 |
| Click-through rate | 5.87% | 6.64% |
| Conversion rate | 5.55% | 8.18% |
| Cost per lead | $131.63 | $66.69 |

A lead here means a call or form fill, not a signed client. The report does not break out family law, so expect large cities and divorce-heavy keywords to cost more than these averages, and smaller markets to cost less.

## What will $3,000 a month realistically produce?

This is an assumption-based example, not a result. Use your own numbers.

1. Budget: $3,000 a month at the legal average of $131.63 per lead gives about 22 leads.
2. Assume 30% of leads become consultations that sign: about 7 clients.
3. Assume an average retained fee of $4,000: roughly $28,000 in fees against $3,000 in ad spend, or about $430 in ad cost per signed client.

If your real sign rate is 10%, the same budget yields about 2 clients and about $1,360 ad cost per client. The sign rate, driven by how fast you answer the phone, matters as much as the bid.

## Which keywords should a family law firm bid on?

Bid on people ready to hire, and exclude people researching or looking for free help.

- **Core intent:** "divorce lawyer near me", "child custody attorney [city]", "family law attorney [city]".
- **Specific services:** "uncontested divorce attorney", "modify child support".
- **Negative keywords to add:** "free", "forms", "pro bono", "how to file myself", "jobs", "salary".

Informational searches such as "how much does a divorce cost" usually cost less per click but can bring readers who are not yet ready to hire. Test them with a small budget and compare cost per signed client, not cost per click.

## What should the ad and landing page include?

- A phone number that works from mobile with one tap, plus call tracking.
- The city, your practice area and the consultation offer in the headline.
- Credentials, review count and a short intake form.
- No guarantees of outcomes. Lawyer advertising is regulated by each state bar, so check your state's rules before running copy.

## Are Local Services Ads worth testing?

Google Local Services Ads for lawyers are a separate product from search ads, with a verification step. Google's own help page notes its [Google Verified badge](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/7549288) program, and its Money Back Guarantee is being discontinued. We could not confirm from a primary source which legal practice areas are eligible or what a lead costs, so check eligibility in your Google Ads account and compare the cost per signed client against search ads before shifting budget.

## How do I set up a campaign step by step?

1. Create a campaign limited to your service area (city or radius), not the whole state.
2. Start with one ad group per practice area (divorce, custody, support).
3. Add negative keywords on day one and review the search terms report weekly.
4. Set up call tracking and form conversion tracking before launch.
5. Review after 30 days: leads, consultations booked, clients signed, cost per client.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I target specific neighborhoods or zip codes?

Yes. Google Ads supports location targeting by city, radius and postal code. Start with the areas you can actually serve in court.

### How long until I see results?

Calls can arrive within days of launch, but plan for about 30 days of data before judging the campaign and changing bids or keywords.

### Should I run display ads too?

Start with search, where people actively look for a lawyer. Consider display retargeting only after search campaigns have enough data.

### Can I manage Google Ads myself?

You can, but legal keywords are expensive, so mistakes cost more. Review the search terms report weekly and keep negative keywords updated.

### Is the average cost per lead the same for my city?

No. The $131.63 figure is a US average across legal services. Your own cost depends on competition, practice area and landing page.
