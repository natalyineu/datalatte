## What Marketing Works for a Small Business in Missouri?

Rank channels by how directly they reach someone ready to buy. Search ads and Google Maps reach people who are already looking. Meta ads and email reach people who are not yet looking or who already know you.

| Channel | Best for | Benchmark (US-wide, 2026) | Start with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | Every local business | Free | Complete profile, real photos, review replies |
| Google Ads Search | Services people search for | CPC $2.05 (Restaurants) to $8.33 (Home improvement) | $300 to $600 per month test |
| Meta lead ads | Studios, salons, offers | Lead CPL $27.11 (Fitness), $50.91 (Beauty) | One offer, small radius |
| Email and SMS | Repeat customers | Cost of the tool | Collect addresses at checkout |

All CPC and CPL figures come from LocaliQ's 2026 benchmark pages and describe the whole US. None of the sources checked for this guide publishes Missouri-specific click costs.

BLS lists Missouri's September 2026 unemployment rate at 3.6% (preliminary, seasonally adjusted). Nonfarm employment was 3,007,300, up 1.0% from a year earlier, and leisure and hospitality employment was 321,200, up 4.5%.

## How Do You Keep Ads Local in Missouri?

Set a radius around the business and exclude places you cannot serve. Google's help page says radius targeting needs at least 1 km. It recommends "Presence or interest" as the best practice for Search, and says "Presence" fits when you only want people physically in the area.

Kansas City and St. Louis each straddle a state or river line, so check that your radius does not reach customers you cannot serve. A groomer with a van or a drop-off site should draw the radius around where clients actually are.

Add negative keywords weekly from the Search terms report: jobs, free, DIY and categories you do not offer.

## How Do You Rank on Google Maps in Missouri?

Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help local ranking. It also says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Practical steps, in order:

- Fill every field: categories, hours, services, service area and website.
- Upload real photos and refresh them each season.
- Reply to every review, good or bad.
- Keep name, address and phone identical on your website, Yelp and Bing Places.

Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes through each field.

## Can You Offer a Discount for a Google Review?

No. Google's contributed content policy lists offering "payment, discounts, free goods and/or services" in exchange for reviews as fake engagement. You may ask customers for a review. You may not reward them for one.

Ask at the right moment with a direct link, for example right after a visit or pickup. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for scripts.

## Do Meta Ads Work for Missouri Small Businesses?

They can, if you sell something visual or have a clear first-visit offer. LocaliQ's 2026 Facebook benchmarks (page updated September 23, 2026) list lead-campaign results of $27.11 per lead for Health and Fitness and $50.91 for Beauty and Personal Care, with $1.84 and $2.97 average CPC. Restaurants and Animals and Pets have no lead benchmarks on that page.

Use Meta for people who are not searching yet. Run one offer, one small radius and a booking link. Show the same offer to people who visited your site. Track cost per booked visit, not likes.

## Where Can Missouri Owners Get Free Advice?

Missouri SBDC centers offer no-fee one-on-one consulting, according to Missouri State University's center, with a nominal fee for some training. Eligibility follows SBA size standards and some businesses are excluded by federal rules, so ask your nearest center first.

## What Mistakes Do Missouri Owners Make?

- **Targeting the whole state.** If customers come from 10 miles away, statewide ads waste budget.
- **Stopping in slow months.** Compare cost per booking monthly, then lower spend instead of pausing.
- **Not tracking calls.** Phone calls are often the real conversion for service firms. Set a minimum call length that counts, so wrong numbers do not inflate results.
- **Spreading thin.** Test one channel for 60 days before adding a second. See our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide).

## FAQ

### How much does Google Ads cost in Missouri?

No Missouri-specific figure could be verified. US-wide, LocaliQ's 2026 average is $5.42 per click, ranging from $2.05 for restaurants to $8.33 for home improvement. Your own account data after a month is the real number.

### What is the best marketing for a small business in Missouri?

Google Business Profile and local SEO first, then Google Ads for high-intent searches, then Meta and email to bring people back. This order is a recommendation, not a ranked study.

### Is Kansas City or St. Louis cheaper than other markets for ads?

Not verified. No source checked for this guide compares Missouri click costs with other states, so test with a small budget.

### Can I give a discount for Google reviews?

No. Google's policy treats discounts or free goods in exchange for reviews as fake engagement.

### Where can I get free marketing advice in Missouri?

Missouri SBDC centers provide no-fee one-on-one consulting to eligible for-profit businesses. Book through the center nearest you.

Ads can produce calls within weeks. Google says SEO changes "might take effect in a few hours, others could take several months." Maps rankings and reviews have no published timeline.
