Measure local SEO by actions, not rankings alone: calls, direction requests, website clicks and bookings from your Google Business Profile, plus review count and rating, and clicks from Google Search to your site. Google shows the first group in your profile performance report and the second in Search Console. Add a short monthly scorecard and compare it with the month before.

No source publishes a standard "good" number for these KPIs, so this page gives definitions and a tracking method, with a labelled example.

## Which Local SEO Metrics Matter Most?

Start with the customer actions Google reports for a verified profile. Google's help lists these metrics:

| KPI | What Google counts | Caveat from Google |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calls | Taps on the call button | Add your phone number to track it |
| Directions | People who asked for directions | Counting method filters repeat taps and spam |
| Website clicks | Taps on your website link | Counts clicks, not visits you can measure in Analytics |
| Bookings | Completed bookings | Only if booked through a booking provider |
| Views | People who viewed your profile on Search and Maps | Unique per day, so lower than total views |
| Searches | Search terms that found your business | Updated at the start of each month |

Performance data covers both organic results and Google Ads, so mixed traffic appears in the same totals. Only metrics that apply to your business type are shown.

## How Do You Track Website Results?

Search Console's Performance report shows clicks, impressions, click-through rate and average position. Google defines clicks as the number of times a user clicked your site from Search results, and impressions as how many times your site appeared. Its default view shows the past three months.

In Google Analytics, a key event is an event that measures an action important to your business, such as a booking form submission or a tap on your phone number. Mark those events so you can see how many users completed them and which channels brought them. ## Are Reviews a KPI?

Yes, because customers use them to decide. BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults found 97% read reviews for local businesses and 41% always do. 31% will only use a business with 4.5 or more stars and 68% want four or more. 74% only consider reviews from the last three months, and 47% will not use a business with fewer than 20 reviews.

The same survey says 89% expect owners to respond, and 50% are unlikely to choose a business that uses templated replies. Track review count, average rating, number of new reviews each month and your response time. It is a US consumer survey, so use it as direction for other markets.

## Should You Track Rankings?

Rankings are a diagnostic, not the goal. Local results vary by the searcher's location, so a single rank check from your office can mislead. Use a grid or a few checks from nearby streets, or rely on profile views and searches as a proxy. Search Console's average position is a site-wide average and is useful for trend only.

Pair ranking checks with the action metrics above. If your position improves but calls fall, the problem is the profile, the reviews or the offer. ## What Should a Monthly Scorecard Look Like?

Keep one row per month and these columns: profile views, searches, calls, directions, website clicks, bookings, new reviews, average rating, Search Console clicks, and the number of new customers who said they found you on Google. Ask the last one at checkout or booking.

Add one note column for what you changed that month, such as new photos or a profile category fix. Changes that you cannot link to a number are the ones to drop. ## Worked Example: Reading a Month of Numbers

All numbers below are **assumptions** for a salon, not benchmarks.

| Metric | Last month | This month | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Profile views | 1,200 | 1,350 | +12.5% |
| Calls | 40 | 52 | +30% |
| Directions | 25 | 27 | +8% |
| Website clicks | 90 | 85 | -5.6% |
| New reviews | 4 | 9 | +5 |

Views rose 12.5% and calls rose 30%, so more of the people who saw the profile acted. Website clicks dipped slightly, which could be noise at that volume. The next step is to check what changed (for example, the nine new reviews) and keep doing that. Do not read a trend from one month.

## FAQ

### What is a good local SEO KPI?

Calls, directions and bookings from your profile, because they are customer actions. Google publishes no target number.

### How often should I check?

Monthly is enough for a small business. Check reviews weekly so you can reply promptly.

### Do reviews affect rankings?

This page does not claim a ranking effect. BrightLocal's survey shows reviews affect which businesses customers choose.

### Can I measure local SEO without Google Analytics?

Yes. Your Business Profile report and Search Console are free and cover the main actions. Analytics adds what visitors do on your site.

### Why do my profile views differ from email notifications?

Google says views count unique people, once a day, so the total can be lower.
