Pinterest is worth a small, time-boxed test for local businesses with visual, planned purchases such as weddings, hair, interiors, baking and pet styling. It is a weak fit for urgent, same-day needs where people search Google Maps instead. Pinterest reported 619 million monthly users at the end of 2025, and its automated ad type lists no minimum budget. That makes a $150 test cheap enough to run before you decide.

## Is Pinterest worth it for a local business?

It depends on how your customers decide. Pinterest is built around saving and planning, so it suits businesses where the customer plans ahead. A wedding florist, a hair colorist or a bakery that does custom cakes fits. A drive-through coffee stand does not.

The platform is large. Pinterest says it reached 619 million monthly active users in Q4 2025, up 12% year over year ([Pinterest Q4 2025 results](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260212059914/en/Pinterest-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Results-Delivers-14-Revenue-Growth-and-Record-Users)). That is global, though. The number of people near your shop is far smaller, which is why local targeting matters.

## Who should skip Pinterest and who should try it?

Use this quick filter.

- **Try it:** your service looks good in photos, customers book days or weeks ahead, and you already have 15 or more strong images.
- **Try it:** you sell products people can buy online or order for pickup, so a click can end in a sale.
- **Skip it for now:** you have no website or booking page to send clicks to.
- **Skip it for now:** your busiest channel is urgent search, such as "emergency plumber", where Google Business Profile does more work. See our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) for where to put the first dollars.

If you are unsure, test with free Pins first, using our guide to [Pinterest SEO for small businesses](/blog/pinterest-seo-free-traffic-small-business).

## Can you target ads to people near your business?

Yes, with limits. Pinterest's help center says you can target by country, region, metro area or postal code, with US metro and zip-code targeting listed as supported. That help article is several years old, so confirm what you see in Ads Manager ([Pinterest location targeting help](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/location-language-device-and-gender-targeting)).

Local inventory ads are a separate product for retailers with product feeds. Their default radius around a store is 10 miles, and Pinterest warns that excluding zip codes inside a store's radius can hurt performance ([Pinterest local inventory ads help](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/local-inventory-ads)). Service businesses without a product catalog will use standard campaigns with postal-code targeting instead.

## What does Pinterest advertising cost?

Pinterest does not publish a single average cost, so budget by rules, not rumors. These are from Pinterest's own pages.

## What does a $150 test look like?

This is an assumption-based example, not a forecast. A cake studio spends $5 a day for 30 days, a total of $150, sending clicks to a custom-order page.

- Assumed cost per click: $0.50 (the real number will vary; this is not a Pinterest figure)
- Clicks: $150 / $0.50 = 300
- Assumed order-request rate: 2% of clicks = 6 requests
- Assumed value of an order: $90 gross profit

Six requests that close at half would still be three orders, or $270 of gross profit, against a $150 spend. If your clicks cost twice as much or your page converts half as well, the test loses money, and that is the signal to stop. Track clicks with a UTM tag and ask every order request where they found you.

## How do you decide after 30 days?

Set the pass mark before you start. Pick a cost per request you can afford and compare.

1. Calculate gross profit per customer.
2. Divide by your close rate to find the most you can pay per request.
3. If the test beats that number, raise the budget in small steps.
4. If not, fix the landing page and images first, then run one more test.

Keep posting free Pins in parallel. A broader comparison of the channels lives in our article on [Pinterest vs Instagram ads for local business](/blog/pinterest-vs-instagram-ads-local-business).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a Pinterest business account?**
Set one up before you advertise, so ads and analytics are in one place. Check Pinterest's help center for current requirements.

**What is the minimum Pinterest ad budget?**
Pinterest's campaign budget help page states no minimum, and its Performance+ page says there is no minimum budget. Bid floors apply, for example $0.10 for a CPC campaign in the example Pinterest gives.

**Does Pinterest work for service businesses?**
It can, if the service photographs well and customers plan ahead. Send clicks to a booking or inquiry page, not your home page.

**How long before I know if it works?**
Plan on 30 days of ads and a fixed pass mark. Smaller budgets collect data slowly.
