## Is Pinterest worth it for a local business?

It depends on whether your customers plan before they buy. Pinterest works like a visual search engine for ideas people are about to act on, so it suits businesses where the decision takes days or weeks. A wedding photographer, a holiday let, a furniture maker, or a bakery that takes custom orders fits that pattern. A drive-through coffee stand or an emergency plumber does not.

The platform is large. Pinterest reported 631 million monthly active users in early 2026, up 11% year on year ([Pinterest Q1 2026 results, secondary via Outcome Digital](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260504431866/en/Pinterest-Announces-First-Quarter-2026-Results)). That figure is global. The number of people near your shop is far smaller, so the platform's size matters less than whether your local customers use it for planning.

Check your own audience too. Pew's 2026 survey does not report a Pinterest figure, so the most recent US number is from 2024, when 36% of US adults said they use the platform ([Pew Research Center, social media fact sheet](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/fact-sheet/social-media/)). Instagram, by comparison, was used by 51% of adults in 2026.

## Does Pinterest actually drive sales?

Pinterest's strongest sales claim comes from a UK study using Tesco Clubcard purchase data. Shoppers exposed to Pinterest spent 26% more over a year and visited stores 25% more often ([Pinterest and Dunnhumby study, secondary via Outcome Digital](https://business.pinterest.com/en-gb/blog/liveramp-study-pinterest-sales-lift/)). This was a platform-commissioned study, so treat it as a directional signal rather than a benchmark for your business.

Intent is the other half of the case. Pinterest reports that 96% of its top searches are unbranded, meaning people look for ideas rather than a company they already know ([Pinterest Business, secondary via Outcome Digital](https://business.pinterest.com/audience/)). A small business that matches a planning search can be found by someone with no prior brand loyalty.

Pins also last longer than most social posts. Because people search pins over time, a useful image can keep bringing clicks for months, which makes Pinterest closer to slow search than to a feed.

## Who should skip Pinterest?

The fit depends on the purchase, not the industry label. Use this table to sort your business before spending anything.

| Business type | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Wedding florist, photographer, caterer | Strong | Planned weeks ahead, highly visual |
| Interior designer, furniture maker | Strong | Mood-board behaviour matches the platform |
| Holiday let, travel operator | Strong | Trip planning is a core use case |
| Custom bakery, food with photogenic products | Good | Visual, but orders can be urgent |
| Hair salon, beauty studio | Good | Looks matter, bookings are often local searches |
| Plumber, electrician, emergency trades | Weak | Urgent needs go to Google |
| Accountant, B2B service firm | Weak | Decisions are rarely made from images |

## Can you target Pinterest ads to people near you?

The sources available for this article do not cover Pinterest's current location targeting options, so this section does not state what the platform supports. Check the options directly in Ads Manager before you build a plan around local targeting. Radius and postal-code choices change, and a help page you find online may be out of date.

Whatever targeting you use, keep the test clean. Meta's guidance on paid social testing, which applies to ad testing in general, says to run comparisons with equal budgets and to avoid switching campaigns on and off manually, because overlapping audiences make results unreliable ([Meta, About A/B testing for Meta Ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1738164643098669)). Apply the same discipline to any Pinterest test.

## What does a $150 test look like?

This is a worked example with assumed inputs, not a forecast. Replace each assumption with your own numbers from a first test.

A cake studio spends $5 a day for 30 days, a total of $150, sending ad clicks to a custom-order request page. Its inputs are all assumptions:

- Cost per click: $0.50 (assumption)
- Clicks: $150 divided by $0.50 equals 300
- Order-request rate: 2% of clicks, so 6 requests (assumption)
- Close rate on requests: 50%, so 3 orders (assumption)
- Gross profit per order: $90 (assumption)

Three orders at $90 produce $270 of gross profit against $150 spent, a return of 1.8 times the outlay. If the cost per click is double the assumption, or the request rate is half, the same test loses money. That is the point of setting the pass mark first.

Track each step with a UTM tag on the landing link, and ask every order request where the customer found you. Without the tag, you cannot tell which clicks produced the orders.

## How do you decide after 30 days?

Set the pass mark before the first ad runs. Work backwards from profit, not from clicks or impressions.

1. Calculate gross profit per customer, after materials and direct costs.
2. Divide by your close rate to find the most you can pay per order request.
3. Compare the test's actual cost per request to that ceiling.
4. If the test beats the ceiling, raise the budget in small steps and keep measuring.
5. If it misses, fix the landing page and images first, then run one more test with the same budget.

Keep the free Pins going alongside paid tests, since they build up over time. Pinterest is rarely a small business's main channel, so judge it against the other channels you already use, not against the platform's headline reach. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Pinterest good for small businesses?

It is good for small businesses that sell visual, planned purchases, such as weddings, interiors, food, and design-led services. Pew's 2024 figure of 36% of US adults using the platform suggests a sizeable audience, but your own customers need to match. A business with nothing visual to show is a weaker fit.

### How is Pinterest different from Instagram?

Pinterest is closer to a search engine than a social feed. People arrive to plan something, so the intent is higher and posts keep getting found for months. Instagram is built around following accounts and sharing moments, and posts tend to fade faster. The two can work together, but they serve different stages of a buying decision.

### Do Pinterest pins really drive sales?

The strongest evidence is a UK study using loyalty-card data, which found higher spend among shoppers exposed to Pinterest. It was commissioned by the platform, so it shows potential rather than guaranteed results. Your own test, with tracked clicks and order requests, will show whether pins drive sales for your business.

### How long should a Pinterest test run?

Plan on about 30 days with a fixed budget and a pass mark set in advance. Smaller budgets collect data slowly, so a very low spend can leave you with too few clicks to judge. Stop or adjust based on the numbers you set beforehand, not on how the campaign feels.
