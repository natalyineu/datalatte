Yes, a local business can advertise on Peacock, but usually not through a simple self-serve dashboard like Google Ads. Most small advertisers reach Peacock inventory through a programmatic platform (a demand-side platform, or DSP), through NBCUniversal's advertising sales team, or through a local NBC affiliate package. Costs are CPM-based and vary by content, season and market, so get current quotes before you plan a budget.

Peacock is NBCUniversal's streaming service, with NBC shows, Bravo, movies and a lineup of live sports that has included NFL games, Olympic coverage and Premier League soccer. Subscriber numbers and ad-tier availability change over time, so check NBCUniversal's current figures rather than relying on older ones.

## Who Watches Peacock

Peacock's audience reflects its NBC heritage. Compared with some other streamers it tends to include a lot of sports viewers and established households, and the live sports content attracts engaged viewing. Demographic details change as the service grows, so ask your rep or DSP for the current audience profile in your market before you buy.

For local businesses targeting established households, such as home services, restaurants, salons, fitness studios and healthcare practices, that profile can be a reasonable match.

## What Makes Peacock Different

### Live Sports

Live events can make for attentive viewing, and the content context can suit sports-adjacent brands such as gyms and trainers. Live sports packages are usually sold through NBCUniversal's sales team with higher minimums and higher prices than regular streaming inventory.

### Premium Content Environment

NBC content comes with established brand-safety standards. For businesses where trust matters, such as dental practices, medical spas and home services, a premium environment can be a plus.

### Local Market Targeting

Local TV advertising has long been planned by DMA (designated market area), and many CTV platforms support targeting at the DMA level or smaller geographic units such as ZIP codes. Confirm which options your buying route supports.

<Callout type="tip">
If you have run local TV ads before, your existing 30-second spot may already meet streaming technical specs. Ask your rep or DSP for the current creative requirements before you re-produce anything.
</Callout>

## Peacock Ad Formats

- **Standard video (typically 15 or 30 seconds):** pre-roll and mid-roll in streaming content on the ad-supported tiers.
- **Live event packages:** sponsorships and ad packages around specific events, usually managed through a sales team.
- **Interactive and enhanced formats:** sometimes available through managed buys.

Formats and availability change, so ask which are open to your budget level.

## How Small Businesses Can Access Peacock Advertising

**1. Programmatic through a DSP.** Peacock inventory has been available through major programmatic platforms. If you already work with an agency or a DSP, ask whether Peacock can be included in your CTV mix. This is often the most flexible route for a small budget.

**2. NBCUniversal advertising sales.** Direct buys can come with higher minimums than programmatic. Ask what is currently required for a local campaign.

**3. Local NBC affiliate sales reps.** In many markets, the local NBC station sells packages that combine local broadcast with streaming. For a true local business, this can be the most approachable way in, because you deal with a local rep.

<Callout type="warning">
Peacock is harder to buy than a fully self-serve platform. If you have no agency or DSP relationship, start with the local affiliate route or a CTV agency that already buys Peacock.
</Callout>

## What Peacock Ads Cost

Streaming TV is priced by CPM, the cost per thousand ad impressions. Typical CTV CPMs are much higher than social media CPMs, and live sports content generally costs more than library or general entertainment content. Exact Peacock CPMs depend on the content, time of year, targeting and your buying route, so request current quotes. As a general rule of thumb:

| Content type | Relative cost |
|---|---|
| Live sports and major events | Highest |
| Current NBC primetime and popular series | Higher |
| Library, movies and general entertainment | Lower |

To estimate reach, divide your budget by the quoted CPM and multiply by 1,000. For example, a $2,000 budget at a hypothetical $30 CPM would deliver roughly 66,000 impressions, which is spread across households and frequency. Your actual numbers will differ.

## When Peacock Makes Sense for Local Business

- **Sports-adjacent businesses:** gyms, trainers and sports retailers match well with live sports content.
- **Home services:** older, established households are often your core customers.
- **High-consideration healthcare and wellness:** a premium environment helps trust.
- **Restaurants and local brands:** good for awareness when you have a large service area.

If your average sale is small and you need immediate, trackable leads, test search and social first, then add CTV for awareness.

## Peacock vs Hulu

| | Peacock | Hulu |
|---|---|---|
| Content strength | Live sports, NBC shows | Broad entertainment library |
| Typical audience | Sports viewers, established households | Often younger and more varied |
| Access for small budgets | Programmatic, local affiliate | Often more approachable |
| Best use | Brand awareness with sports context | Awareness and response-oriented tests |

**Choose Peacock if** you want sports adjacency, you already work with NBC local, or your customers are likely to watch NBC content. **Choose Hulu if** you want an easier path to buying or a more varied audience. ## How to Measure Peacock Campaigns

CTV impressions are rarely clicked, so measure with:

- A dedicated landing page or vanity URL and phone number for the campaign
- Branded search volume and Google Business Profile calls in your target area before and during the campaign
- A simple "how did you hear about us?" question at booking
- A comparison with a similar period without the campaign

Do not rely on a single platform's reported attribution.

## FAQ

**Q: Can small businesses advertise on Peacock?**
A: Yes, usually through a programmatic platform, a CTV agency or a local NBC affiliate package rather than a fully self-serve dashboard. Minimums vary, so ask for current terms.

**Q: Can I target a specific city or ZIP code?**
A: Many CTV buys support geo-targeting by DMA and in some cases ZIP code or similar units. Ask which level your route supports and whether it affects pricing.

**Q: Do I need a professional video ad?**
A: CTV ads appear on a living room screen, so quality matters. Clear audio, good lighting and a simple offer are more important than a big production budget. Ask for the current technical specs.

**Q: How do I know if Peacock ads are working?**
A: Compare cost per new customer against your other channels, using a unique phone number, landing page or offer code, rather than judging by impressions.

**Q: What kinds of businesses should skip Peacock?**
A: Businesses with very low-priced products, or those that need instant tracked clicks, may be better served by search ads first. CTV is better suited to awareness for higher-value local services.
