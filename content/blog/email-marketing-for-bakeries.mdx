A bakery should build a small email list at the counter and online, send a few seasonal campaigns a year and automate a welcome email. Klaviyo's 2026 benchmarks put the order rate for Food and Beverage at 1.16% for campaigns and 1.79% for automated flows. Free plans from Mailchimp and Klaviyo stop at 250 contacts and 500 sends a month, which matters once you email weekly.

## What Results Can a Bakery Expect From Email?

Klaviyo's 2026 report, drawn from over 183,000 customers, gives Food and Beverage an order rate of 1.16% for campaigns and 1.79% for flows. Across all industries, campaign open rate is 31% and flow open rate 32.2%. Click rate is 1.69% for campaigns and 5.58% for flows.

Klaviyo does not publish open or click rates for Food and Beverage on that page, and it has no bakery category. Mailchimp's overall figures (35.63% opens, 2.62% clicks, 0.22% unsubscribes, December 2023) have no food category either, and it warns that Apple's Mail Privacy Protection can affect open rates.

## Which Emails Should a Bakery Send First?

Start with one automated welcome email, then add seasonal campaigns. Klaviyo's data shows flows with a higher click rate (5.58% against 1.69%) and a higher Food and Beverage order rate (1.79% against 1.16%). Build the welcome once and it runs without effort.

Then plan campaigns around your ordering deadlines, such as holiday pre-orders, custom cake lead times and wedding season. Send the pre-order reminder before the cut-off, not after. ## What Do the Free Plans Allow?

Mailchimp's free plan covers 250 contacts and a maximum of 500 sends a month or 250 a day, and marketing automation flows are not included. Klaviyo's free plan covers up to 250 active profiles and 500 emails a month, with email support for the first 60 days.

Do the sending arithmetic early. A list of 300 people receiving one email a week sends about 1,300 messages a month, over both free limits. Check the current plan pages before you commit, as prices change.

## How Do You Collect Email Addresses?

Offer something specific at the counter: a QR code on the pastry case and a line on receipts. Collect consent when the person signs up, and record where each address came from. Online, add a sign-up box on the order page.

Do not buy lists. Under CAN-SPAM, hiring another company to send does not transfer your legal responsibility, so you remain accountable for the content and for honouring opt-outs.

## What Does the Law Require?

The FTC's CAN-SPAM guide lists eight requirements: accurate header information, non-deceptive subject lines, a clear ad identification, a valid physical postal address, a clear opt-out explanation, honouring opt-outs, no delay beyond 10 business days, and responsibility for third-party senders.

Each separate email in violation can carry a penalty of up to $53,088. This guide covers US rules only; if you email people in the UK or EU, other rules apply and were not checked here.

## The Math: What Could a Holiday Pre-Order Email Earn?

All inputs are **assumptions** for an illustrative bakery. The 1.16% order rate is the Klaviyo Food and Beverage campaign figure.

| Input | Value |

| Subscribers | 600 |
| Order rate | 1.16% |
| Orders from one campaign | about 7 |
| Average pre-order value (assumption) | $45 |
| Revenue from one campaign | about $313 |

Seven orders at $45 is about $313 per campaign. Four campaigns a month means 2,400 sends, far over the free limits of 500, so budget for a paid plan. Whether that pays depends on your margin; compare plan cost with the extra orders you can credit to email.

## FAQ

### How often should a bakery email its list?
No source gives a fixed number. Send when you have a reason, such as a seasonal menu or a pre-order deadline, and watch unsubscribes.

### Is a free plan enough?
For a small list and occasional sends, yes. Weekly campaigns to hundreds of people exceed the 500-send limit, and Mailchimp's free plan does not include automation flows.

### What is a good open rate?
Klaviyo shows 31% across industries for campaigns, but Apple's privacy features can inflate opens. Judge by clicks and orders.

### Do I need consent to email customers?
In the US, CAN-SPAM requires an opt-out and your address in each email. Check local rules if you email outside the US.
