Uber Eats ads (sponsored listings) work on pay-per-click. A restaurant sets a weekly budget in Uber Eats Manager, picks an audience and a bid, and pays only when someone clicks into its menu. When the weekly budget is used up, the campaign pauses until the next week. When Uber launched the feature in France in 2020, the weekly budget started at €35.

This guide covers how to create an ad, what the UK marketplace commissions are, how to read the results, and a worked return-on-ad-spend example. Uber's terms differ by country and some features may not be offered to every restaurant.

## How do Uber Eats ads work?

According to Uber's merchant material, you create the ad in the Marketing tab of Uber Eats Manager by choosing "Create an ad", then set a budget, an audience and a bid. A custom bid lets you set the price per click. An automatic bid adjusts over time.

Uber's Australian blog describes the model as pay-per-click, with charges only for clicks into your menu, not for impressions. Uber Eats Manager can show predicted sales before you launch. Ads can be paused, edited or resumed. Uber's help also says ads run each week automatically at the budget you set, and performance data can take up to 48 hours to appear.

## What did the first European launch look like?

Uber's French newsroom says sponsored listings reached France on 19 October 2020, the first country in the Europe, Middle East and Africa region after the US launch in August 2020. Sponsored restaurants appeared at the top of the app's home page, with one sponsored restaurant shown per customer session.

Restaurants chose three settings: audience (new or existing customers), a weekly budget starting at €35, and a maximum cost per click. The cost was deducted from Uber Eats' weekly payouts to the restaurant. These are launch-day details from 2020. Check the current options in your own Uber Eats Manager before you plan around them.

## What do Uber Eats commissions look like in the UK?

Ad spend sits on top of commission. Uber's UK merchant pricing page lists these marketplace fees, excluding VAT:

| Option | Commission |
| --- | --- |
| Uber Delivery | 30% (33% on Uber One member orders) |
| Self-delivery | 13% |
| Pickup | 13% |
| One-off activation fee | £650 excl. VAT |

The UK page does not cover sponsored listings. A different regional page, for Japan, ties sponsored listing funding to the pickup tier, but its terms apply only there and do not transfer to Europe. ## How do you read the results?

Uber's Australian blog defines return on ad spend (ROAS) as the sales returned for each unit spent. The Marketing tab shows ROAS, sales from ads, conversion rate and amount spent in real time. Sales are those from customers who ordered within 7 days of seeing the ad. The blog suggests trying ads for three to four weeks, and Uber's help says the weekly figure renews automatically.

ROAS counts revenue, not profit. After the commission, food cost and packaging, a ROAS of 3 can still lose money. Compare it with your own margin on delivery orders.

## Worked example: is a €35 weekly ad worth it?

All numbers are **assumptions** for illustration, not Uber figures or benchmarks.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly ad spend | €35 |
| Reported ROAS | 4 |
| Sales attributed to ads | €140 |
| Commission (self-delivery tier, UK-style 13%) | €18.20 |
| Food, packaging and labour cost | 55% of sales = €77 |
| Left after costs | €140 − €18.20 − €77 = €44.80 |
| Minus ad spend | €9.80 |

At ROAS 4 the ad earns about €9.80 a week in this example. At ROAS 2, sales would be €70 and the ad would lose money: €70 − €9.10 − €38.50 − €35 = −€12.60. The break-even ROAS on these costs is about 3.1. Use your own costs to find yours. ## What should you fix before spending on ads?

A click only pays if the menu converts. Check photos, item names, prices and opening hours first. Then start with the smallest weekly budget, one audience, and run it for the three to four weeks Uber suggests before changing it.

## FAQ

### How are Uber Eats ads charged?

Per click. Uber's help describes a weekly budget that is only spent when customers click into your menu.

### What is the minimum budget for Uber Eats ads?

Uber's help pages checked do not state a minimum. The 2020 French launch started at €35 a week. Check Uber Eats Manager for your market.

### How long before I see results?

Uber says performance data can take up to 48 hours to appear.

### Can every restaurant run Uber Eats ads?

Not necessarily. Uber's help says that if you cannot see the feature in Uber Eats Manager, you should contact support.

### Are ads available in every European country?

Uber has not published a country list on the pages checked. The first European country was France in 2020. Check Uber Eats Manager for your country.
