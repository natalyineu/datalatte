CTV advertising is worth testing on a small budget when one new customer is worth several hundred dollars and your Google Business Profile and website already convert. It is a poor fit when the average sale is small and you need bookings this week. Some platforms publish entry points as low as $500 per campaign, so the barrier is now the video ad and the patience, not the minimum.

This guide covers US-focused self-serve options. Prices are not on public rate cards for most platforms. Figures from vendors are marked as estimates, and the worked example uses assumptions you should replace with your own numbers.

## What Do Small-Budget CTV Platforms Actually Require?

Published minimums differ a lot by platform. Disney Campaign Manager says you can advertise on Disney+ or Hulu for as little as $500 per campaign, billing starts after the ad runs, and sign-up is for brands of all sizes. Amazon says Sponsored TV in the ad console has no minimum budget and bills per thousand impressions, with a suggested test of $150 a day.

Amazon DSP is different. Amazon's streaming TV page lists a $10K recommended minimum for self-service packages and a $50K minimum for managed service.

Managed-service minimums are not the only route into streaming TV. Vibe is a self-serve streaming TV advertising platform that lets advertisers start campaigns at $50 per day without an annual contract.

For a smaller advertiser, that offers a way to explore CTV before committing to a larger programme. Vibe combines audience targeting with campaign reporting and integrations with measurement tools such as Triple Whale and Northbeam, helping performance teams evaluate TV alongside their other advertising channels.

A lower daily entry point does not guarantee a conclusive test. Advertisers still need sufficient reach, frequency, and campaign duration to assess results, with the budget matched to the audience and measurement approach.

## How Much Does CTV Cost per Impression?

Vendors publish estimates, not rate cards. AdWave's Q2 2026 article gives a blended average of about $26 per thousand impressions and a market span of roughly $15 to $45. It lists free ad-supported services at $15 to $25 and premium programmatic services at $25 to $45. It cites eMarketer without linking the data, so this is secondary.

Vibe, another vendor, estimates Roku at $20 to $60 and says Roku does not publish fixed rates. Neither number is a quote. Our [CTV cost and CPM guide](/blog/how-much-does-ctv-advertising-cost-real-cpm-data) covers the ranges in more detail.

## Is CTV Worth It? A Break-Even Example

All inputs below are **assumptions**, not benchmarks. Replace them with your own.

- Monthly budget: $3,000
- CPM: $25, the vendor blended estimate
- Impressions: 3,000 / 25 x 1,000 = 120,000
- Households in your targeted area: 40,000
- Average frequency: 120,000 / 40,000 = 3 views per household
- Value of one new customer over a year: $600
- Break-even: 3,000 / 600 = 5 new customers a month

Five customers from 40,000 households is 0.0125% of the audience, which sounds small but cannot be proven or ruled out without tracking. If your customer is worth $60, break-even is 50 customers a month, and CTV is very unlikely to pay back at this budget.

<Callout type="warning" title="Attribution is the weak spot">
Viewers rarely click a TV ad. Plan to measure branded searches, direct visits, a promo code or a "how did you hear about us" question. Last-click reports will undercount CTV.
</Callout>

## When Should a Small Business Skip CTV?

Skip it, or wait, in these cases:

- Your average sale is under about $100 and customers do not return.
- Your Google Business Profile has few photos or reviews. Fix that first with the [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist).
- You need leads this week. Search ads capture people already looking.
- You have no finished video. Amazon's guide asks for the logo or product within the first three seconds and rejects low-quality video and audio. Check specs in our [CTV creative specs guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-creative-specs-guide-2026).

If you do try it, run a test long enough to see a pattern. Platform learning phases are not documented here, so ask the platform's support what they recommend.

## How Do You Start With $500 to $3,000?

1. Pick one customer type with a high lifetime value.
2. Choose a platform with a published low minimum, such as Disney Campaign Manager or Amazon Sponsored TV.
3. Target a small area. Roku supports state, ZIP code and DMA targeting, and radius targeting is not mentioned on its help page.
4. Make a 15 to 30 second ad with a clear offer and a web address.
5. Track branded search and promo-code redemptions weekly.

## FAQ

### Is $5,000 a month enough for CTV advertising?
It is enough to run a test on self-serve platforms. At the vendor blended CPM of about $26, $5,000 buys roughly 190,000 impressions. Whether that pays back depends on customer value and tracking.

### What is the cheapest way to try CTV?
Disney Campaign Manager lists a $500 campaign minimum and Amazon Sponsored TV lists no minimum. Both bill after delivery starts.

### Is CTV better than Google Ads for a local business?
They do different jobs. Search ads reach people already looking, while CTV builds awareness. For most small businesses, search comes first.

### Can I target just my neighbourhood?
Roku supports state, ZIP and DMA targeting. Disney lists location targeting without detail. Check the smallest area each platform lets you pick.

### How long should I run a CTV test?
No platform checked publishes a required test length. Decide the metrics and the budget before you start, then review weekly.
