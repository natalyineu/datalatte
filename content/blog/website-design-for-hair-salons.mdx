A hair salon website needs five things: a booking button visible without scrolling, a price list, real photos of your work, your address and hours, and a page that loads fast on a phone. Google's "good" thresholds for Core Web Vitals are a Largest Contentful Paint of 2.5 seconds or less, Interaction to Next Paint of 200 milliseconds or less and Cumulative Layout Shift of 0.1 or less, measured at the 75th percentile. Booking software can also cost you money per client, so choose it carefully.

## What Must a Salon Booking Page Include?

Put the essentials where a visitor on a phone sees them first. Someone looking at your site usually wants to know whether you do their service, what it costs and when you have a slot.

- **A Book button at the top** that opens your booking tool, repeated at the bottom of the page.
- **Services with prices or "from" prices.** If prices vary by hair length, say so.
- **Photos of real work** by your stylists, with names.
- **Address, hours and a map link**, matching your Google Business Profile.
- **Phone number** that can be tapped to call.
- **Cancellation terms** in one or two sentences.

Keep the number of booking steps as low as the software allows. Our guide to [online booking systems for salons](/blog/online-booking-system-for-salons) covers the tools.

## How Fast Should a Salon Site Load?

Google's web.dev defines three Core Web Vitals. A page passes when it meets all three at the 75th percentile of page loads, split by mobile and desktop.

| Metric | Measures | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Largest Contentful Paint | Loading | 2.5 seconds or less |
| Interaction to Next Paint | Responsiveness | 200 milliseconds or less |
| Cumulative Layout Shift | Visual stability | 0.1 or less |

Salon sites are image heavy, so the usual culprit is oversized photos. Compress images, size them to the space they fill and test your home page in Google's PageSpeed Insights. No published figure was located linking page speed to salon bookings, so treat speed as a basic requirement, not a promised lift.

## What Do Booking Platforms Cost?

Many salons use the booking tool's own page or embed it on their site. The price model differs, so compare before you commit.

| | Fresha | Square Appointments |
|---|---|---|
| Subscription | Independent $19.95 a month for one bookable team member; Team $14.95 per team member a month | Free plan for solo professionals; Plus and Premium are paid, prices not shown on the page read |
| Online payments | 2.79% + $0.20 online, 2.29% + $0.20 in person | Not shown on the page read |
| New clients from the marketplace | One-time 20% commission, minimum $6 per client | Not applicable |
| Clients booking via your own link, Facebook or Instagram | No fee | Booking website, Book Now button, QR code or embedded widget |

Fresha's fee applies to new clients found through its marketplace, not to people who book through your own link. A website that sends visitors to your direct booking link keeps those clients fee-free. ## Should the Site Say More Than the Booking Tool Does?

Yes. A booking tool takes the appointment, but your site is where Google reads who you are. Add a page for each main service, your location and your team. Make sure the name, address and phone match your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) exactly. ## FAQ

### What should a hair salon website include?

A top Book button, services with prices, photos of real work, address, hours, phone and a short cancellation policy.

### How fast should my salon website load?

Google's good thresholds are 2.5 seconds or less for LCP, 200 ms or less for INP and 0.1 or less for CLS, at the 75th percentile.

### Does Fresha charge for bookings?

Fresha's pricing page lists a one-time 20% commission, minimum $6, on new clients found through its marketplace. Returning marketplace clients and clients who use your direct link, Facebook or Instagram are free.

### Do I need a website if I use a booking app?

The app gives you a booking page, but a website lets you rank for local searches and present your services your own way. Link the two.

### How much does a salon website cost?

No source opened publishes an average price. Ask for a fixed quote that lists hosting, booking integration and who owns the domain.
