Instagram retargeting shows paid ads to people who already visited your profile, engaged with a post or saved one. Those audiences can be used in Instagram or Facebook ads, so the budget reaches people who already know the business instead of hoping a free post gets past your followers. Setup takes a business account, four audience types and a campaign that places them in the Custom Audience field. This guide covers each audience, the steps, and a worked budget example with every input labelled as an assumption.

## How does Instagram retargeting work for a local business?

Retargeting means paying to show ads to people who have already interacted with a business. [Power Digital's guide to Instagram retargeting](https://powerdigitalmarketing.com/blog/monetize-your-instagram-following-using-retargeting/) describes the approach for accounts with an active following. Its core argument is that organic reach keeps shrinking as the algorithm changes, so an audience you already have is worth paying to reach again.

The same logic suits a local business. A salon, plumber or tutor with a steady profile has a warm audience already, but not everyone in it is a likely customer. [BrightLocal's guide to Instagram for local businesses](https://www.brightlocal.com/learn/instagram-for-local-businesses/) reports that 90% of users follow brands and 81% use the app to research a product or service. Those figures are retold from a Hootsuite study, so treat them as directional rather than exact.

## Which Instagram audiences can a local business build?

The Power Digital guide lists four audience types. Each one signals a different level of interest, so each should get a different message.

| Audience | Built from | Level of interest | Suggested use (this guide's suggestion) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Profile visitors | People who opened the profile | Lower | Proof posts, staff introductions, reviews |
| Engagers | People who commented, liked, clicked or shared | Moderate | Service posts with clear booking details |
| Messengers | People who messaged the profile | Higher | A direct reply path, quote or call prompt |
| Post savers | People who saved a post | Higher | Price or service detail posts |

Keep these as separate audiences rather than one blended pool. Visitors are the widest group, so they give the largest reach at the lowest intent. Savers and messengers are smaller but closer to booking.

Engagers only grow when posts keep earning responses, so consistency matters. [Scheduling tools compared for Instagram](/blog/later-vs-planoly-instagram-scheduling-local-business) can keep a local posting calendar steady, which feeds these audiences over time.

## How do you set up Instagram retargeting step by step?

BrightLocal recommends a business account first, because it gives better analytics and recognition as a business. The steps below combine that advice with the audience workflow in the Power Digital article.

1. Open Settings, select Account and choose Switch to Professional Account. Pick the category and Business Account.
2. Link the profile to a Facebook Page if that is relevant to the business.
3. In Meta Business Manager, go to Audiences, click Create Audience and choose Instagram Business Profile.
4. Create one audience for each type: visitors, engagers, messengers and post savers.
5. In the campaign's ad set, place the audience in the Custom Audience field.
6. Launch as normal. The Power Digital guide refers to Power Editor, which may no longer match current screens, so check the menu names in Ads Manager before you start.

## What does Instagram retargeting cost for a local business?

The example below uses a hypothetical salon. Every input is an assumption, so replace each one with figures from your own Ads Manager before setting a budget.

- Profile visitors who could be retargeted each month: 400 (assumption)
- Monthly retargeting budget: $100 (assumption)
- Cost per click: $1.20 (assumption)
- Booking rate from clicks: 8% (assumption)
- Average booking value: $65 (assumption)

Clicks come to $100 divided by $1.20, about 83. Bookings are about 83 multiplied by 8%, roughly 6.7. Booked revenue is about 6.7 multiplied by $65, or about $433, roughly 4.3 times the spend. That is revenue, not profit, and it excludes materials and staff time.

If only 100 people match the audience, the same $100 reaches them more often, and results thin out. [Facebook ads budgets for local businesses](/blog/facebook-ads-budget-local-business-how-much-to-spend) explain how to size a test before committing a full month.

## What should retargeted posts say?

The Power Digital guide frames retargeting as a way to put budget behind content that already performs. The audience is only useful if it responds, so start with posts that already earn comments and saves.

BrightLocal argues that concise information paired with an image helps users take the next step, and that personal referrals are more valuable than advertising. For a local business, that points to staff introductions, real work examples and clear booking details rather than generic logos. [ChatGPT prompts for local business marketing](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-local-business-marketing) can help draft captions, but the facts in them must come from the business itself.

## FAQ

### Do you need Meta Business Manager for Instagram retargeting?

Yes. The Power Digital guide starts in Business Manager, where the Audiences menu and Create Audience option let you build an Instagram Business Profile audience. A personal account without a business setup does not give the same options.

### Do all Instagram followers become customers?

No. The Power Digital article says plainly that a large following does not mean every follower is a potential customer. Engagers and profile visitors are a better starting point than the total follower count.

### Is Instagram worth it for local service businesses?

BrightLocal notes that plumbers, electricians and tutors have used Instagram to reach local audiences, though it is not a fit for every business. Canadian readers can compare channels in the [local business marketing guide for Canada](/blog/local-business-marketing-canada-guide-2026) before deciding where to spend.

### Should a brand-new business start with retargeting?

Probably not first. Retargeting needs a pool of profile visitors or engagers, so a new account needs posts and visits before the audiences are useful. Build a consistent posting habit, then add retargeting once the audiences start to form.
