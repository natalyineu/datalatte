## How much does Buffer cost for a coffee shop?

These figures come from [Buffer's pricing page](https://buffer.com/pricing). Prices are per channel, so every account you connect adds to the bill.

| Plan | Price per channel | Scheduled posts per channel | Analytics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 10 | 30 days of Insights history |
| Essentials | $5 a month or $60 a year | Unlimited (fair use cap of 5,000) | Unlimited history, performance overview, reports |
| Team | $10 a month or $120 a year | Unlimited (fair use cap of 5,000) | Same, plus branded reports and unlimited users |

Essentials also adds first-comment scheduling and unlimited AI replies in the community inbox. The Free plan allows 5 AI reply suggestions per week.

Worked example (assumption): a cafe posts 4 times a week on Instagram and Facebook and once a week to Google Business Profile. It needs 3 channels. On Free, that costs $0, and a two-week batch of 8 posts per channel fits under the 10-post limit. Essentials would cost $15 a month and lifts the limit.

## Which channels can a cafe schedule in Buffer?

According to [Buffer's supported channels page](https://support.buffer.com/article/567-supported-channels), it publishes to Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, Google Business Profile, TikTok, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Threads and more. Facebook personal profiles cannot be connected.

For most cafes, start with three:

- **Instagram** for photos, Reels and carousels.
- **Facebook Page** for opening hours, events and local groups.
- **Google Business Profile** for offers and updates on Maps and Search.

Add TikTok or Pinterest only after the first three run smoothly.

## What are the Instagram scheduling limits to know?

Per [Buffer's Instagram help article](https://support.buffer.com/articles/scheduling-instagram-posts-reels-stories-and-notifications-3XA98S9Q5p), automatic publishing works only on Instagram professional (Business or Creator) accounts. Adding music, stickers, Story links or product tags switches the post to "Notify Me" mode. Buffer then sends a push notification and you finish the post in the Instagram app.

Other limits from the same article:

- Hashtags: 5 in the caption and 30 in the first comment.
- Reels: 3 seconds to 15 minutes.
- Story video: 3 to 60 seconds.
- First comments are not supported on notification posts.

Practical rule: schedule plain photos, carousels and Reels for automatic publishing, and add trending audio on the phone.

## How often should a coffee shop post?

[Buffer's analysis of 2.1 million Instagram feed posts](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/) from more than 102,000 accounts shows rising returns with frequency. It excludes Stories and comes from Buffer users, and Buffer sells scheduling tools, so treat it as directional.

| Posts per week | Reach per post vs 1-2 posts | Follower growth rate |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Baseline | +0.12% |
| 3-5 | About 12% higher | +0.26% |
| 6-9 | About 18% higher | +0.44% |
| 10+ | About 24% higher | +0.66% |

The article stresses that quality matters more than volume and that returns diminish. For a cafe with one owner, 3 to 5 posts a week is a realistic target.

## What does a 4-post weekly plan look like?

This plan is a suggested template, not a measured result. Batch it in one 60 to 90 minute session.

1. **Monday: a drink or food photo** with the day's special.
2. **Wednesday: a short Reel** of latte art, roasting or prep.
3. **Friday: weekend offer or event** with a clear call to action.
4. **Sunday: a customer photo**, reposted only after the customer says yes.

Use Buffer's queue to fill these slots, then spend 10 minutes a day answering comments. Pair this with ideas from [coffee shop social media content ideas](/blog/coffee-shop-social-media-content-ideas) and [Instagram Reels for coffee shops](/blog/instagram-reels-for-coffee-shops).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is Buffer free for a coffee shop?**

Yes, up to 3 channels and 10 scheduled posts per channel, with 30 days of analytics history. Essentials removes the post limit for $5 per channel per month.

**Q: Can Buffer post to Instagram automatically?**

Yes, for Instagram Business or Creator accounts, if the post meets Instagram's requirements. Posts with music, stickers or Story links use notification mode instead.

**Q: Does Buffer post to Google Business Profile?**

Yes. Google Business Profile is on Buffer's list of supported channels.

**Q: How many hashtags should I use?**

Buffer's Instagram help article caps captions at 5 hashtags and first comments at 30. Pick a few relevant local tags rather than filling the limit.

**Q: Can I connect my personal Facebook profile?**

No. Buffer supports Facebook Pages, not personal profiles.
