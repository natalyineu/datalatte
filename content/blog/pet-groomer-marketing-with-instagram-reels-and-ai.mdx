## Is Your Customer on Instagram?

The US pet market is big and everyday. The American Pet Products Association (APPA) reports about 95 million US households own a pet, based on its 2025 National Pet Owners Survey. Its category called "Other Services", which it defines as boarding, grooming, insurance, training, pet sitting, pet walking and all services outside veterinary care, totals $14.3 billion, with a projection of $14.9 billion for 2026 ([APPA industry stats](https://www.americanpetproducts.org/industry-trends-and-stats)). APPA does not break grooming out on that page, so do not read $14.3 billion as a grooming figure.

On Instagram itself, Pew Research Center's 2025 survey of 5,022 US adults (fielded February to June 2025) found 50% use it overall, 80% of 18 to 29-year-olds and 19% of those 65 and older ([Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/11/20/americans-social-media-use-2025/)). ## What to Film (and What to Leave Out)

Grooming is visual, but "before and after" alone gets repetitive. Build a repeating set of formats, so each Reel is quick to make:

| Format | Example | Why it works for grooming |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reveal | Matted coat to neat trim, with the owner pickup moment | The transformation is the product |
| 15-second process | Bath, dry, trim, finish in quick cuts | Shows skill without a long explainer |
| Care tip | "Why we do not shave double coats" | Builds trust with nervous owners |
| Breed or coat explainer | "What a doodle coat needs between grooms" | Matches what owners search for |
| Behind the scenes | Opening up, a calm dog on the table | Shows how you handle animals |
| Rebooking nudge | "Your dog's next groom is due in 4 weeks" | Turns viewers into repeat clients |

Pick two or three and repeat them for a month. A series is easier to produce and easier to measure than ten different ideas.

### Animal welfare comes first

Never keep a stressed dog on the table for a better shot. Film short clips at natural pauses and stop when the animal is uncomfortable. It is ethics and business: owners judge how you handle their pet, and one clip that looks rough can undo months of work.

### Ask the owner before you post

The pet belongs to a client, and the owner may appear in the frame or be heard on the audio. Put a separate tick box on your booking form: "I am happy for [Salon] to share photos and videos of my pet on social media." Offer a "pet only, no people or voices" option, record who agreed, and delete on request. Treat paid ads as a separate permission. Rules for recognisable people differ by country, so have a local adviser check your wording. ## Filming With a Phone

You do not need special kit. Shoot vertically in window light, keep the background tidy, open with the messy coat or a line like "Wait for the reveal", and use the same angle for before and after clips so the comparison is honest.

Instagram's ranking explainer says it aims to make "low-resolution or watermarked reels, reels that are muted or contain borders" and "reels that are majority text" less visible ([Instagram: Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)). For a groomer: good light, sound on, no watermark, full frame.

## Music: Use What Your Account Is Cleared For

Trending songs are a trap for business accounts. Meta's guidance for organisations lists three safe routes for music in Reels: public domain tracks, Meta's Sound Collection (it describes over 14,000 tracks of royalty-free music), or music you license from the rights holders for the right territories ([Meta: Music for videos on Facebook and Instagram](https://www.facebook.com/government-nonprofits/blog/music-for-videos-on-facebook-and-instagram)). Which tracks show up in your in-app picker depends on your account type and country, so check yours rather than assuming. The simplest groomer option is original audio: your voice explaining the groom over the sound of the salon.

## Where AI Genuinely Helps

AI is best at the work around the video. It does not replace real footage of real dogs, and it cannot know your pricing, coat types or town. Here is a practical split:

| Task | How AI helps | Your check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly idea list | A general AI assistant drafts 12 ideas for the season | Delete anything you cannot film this month |
| Hooks and captions | Draft five hook lines per Reel | Edit to sound like you; confirm every fact |
| Reply drafts | Draft answers to common DMs (hours, prices, vaccines) | Read before sending; never guess on health |
| Editing | Instagram's Edits app lists tools such as green screen, AI image animation and high-quality exports with no watermark | Do not let effects hide the dog |
| Review requests | Draft a short message after each groom | Personalise it with the dog's name |

Instagram's Edits page ([Edits by Instagram](https://edits.instagram.com/)) also advertises real-time insights, and gives no pricing, so check the app store. Whatever editor you use, make sure it exports without a watermark.

### A prompt you can adapt

> "I run a dog grooming salon in [town]. Give me 12 short Reel ideas for [month] that I can film on my phone during normal grooms. For each, write a 3-second hook, 3 to 5 shots, and a caption ending with one clear booking step. Friendly, practical tone. Do not make health claims."

Always edit the result. AI will happily write confident-sounding coat-care advice that is wrong for a specific breed. Anything about skin, parasites or behaviour should come from you or the vet, not from a draft.

### Label AI visuals honestly

If you use AI to animate a photo or generate an image, say so. Meta says it applies "AI info" labels to content flagged through industry-shared AI signals or through self-disclosure, and puts the label in the post's menu when AI tools were only used to edit ([Meta: Our approach to labeling AI-generated content](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/)). More importantly for a groomer: never show an AI-generated "after" for a real dog. Clients will compare it with the dog they collect.

## Automating Replies Without Annoying Anyone

Bookings often start in the DMs. Meta's developer documentation for the Instagram messaging API says an app has 24 hours to respond to a message an Instagram user sends to your account, and that, where the law requires it, automated chats must disclose that the person is talking to an automated service. Meta recommends disclosure as good practice even where it is not required, and gives examples such as "You are talking to a bot" ([Meta: Instagram messaging API](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api)).

What to do with that:

- **Answer fast.** A same-day reply to "Do you have Saturday slots?" matters more than a clever bot.
- **Automate only the repetitive part:** hours, prices, vaccine requirements, the booking link. Our Instagram DM automation guide covers the set-up.
- **Tell people when a bot is replying,** and give an easy route to a human.
- **Hand health, behaviour and complaint messages to yourself.** For wider automation ideas, see AI agents for pet groomers.

## Make Every Reel Point to a Booking

Views from another country do not fill your diary. Say your town out loud and in the caption: Instagram's creator guidance says to include relevant keywords in your content, caption, bio and hashtags to be found in search ([Instagram Creators: Tips for improving your reach](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/tips-for-improving-your-reach)). End every Reel with one step, such as "Link in bio for spring slots". Keep your [Google Business Profile](/blog/pet-groomer-google-business-profile-optimization) in step with your Instagram, because many owners search "dog groomer near me" after seeing you.

## The Math

Take one month of 12 Reels. Everything below is an **assumption** for illustration; replace it with your own after a month of counting.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reels per month | 12 | 12 | 12 |
| Hours (12 x 25 min filming/editing, plus 1.5 h planning and replies) | 6.5 | 6.5 | 6.5 |
| Time cost at $30/hour | $195 | $195 | $195 |
| New clients who say Instagram sent them | 0 | 2 | 5 |
| Average groom price | $85 | $85 | $85 |
| Visits in first year (every 6 weeks) | 8 | 8 | 8 |
| First-year revenue per client | $680 | $680 | $680 |
| Profit margin on that revenue | 50% | 50% | 50% |
| Profit from new clients | $0 | $680 | $1,700 |
| Net after time cost | -$195 | +$485 | +$1,505 |

Break-even is time cost divided by profit per client: $195 / $340 = 0.57, so on these assumptions one new regular in a month covers the work. Two caveats. The Strong column assumes you have chair time for five new regulars; most groomers have a fixed number of slots, so a full book is a reason to raise prices or start a waitlist, not to film more. And the Tough column is a useful result, not a failure: if after two months nobody mentions Instagram, change the offer, the booking path or the local signals before you change the video style.

## Do This Today

1. Add a social media consent tick box (with a "pet only" option) to your booking form.
2. Check which music sources your account is cleared for, or plan to record original audio.
3. Ask an AI assistant for 12 ideas using the prompt above, then cross off any you cannot film in normal grooms.
4. Film three 10 to 20 second clips in one session at natural pauses, with good light, sound on and no watermark.
5. Add "How did you find us?" to your booking form and start a monthly tally against your $195 break-even.

## FAQ

**Q: How often should a pet groomer post Reels?**
A: Pick a pace you can keep for 90 days. Three a week works for the maths above; Instagram does not publish a required frequency in the pages we reviewed.

**Q: Can I use AI to write my captions?**
A: Yes, for hooks and drafts. Edit the result, keep health and behaviour claims out unless you can stand behind them, and be open when you use AI to create or animate an image.

**Q: Is it safe to automate DMs?**
A: For simple, repetitive questions, yes. Disclose that a bot is replying, answer within Meta's 24-hour window and hand anything about a pet's health or a complaint to a person.
