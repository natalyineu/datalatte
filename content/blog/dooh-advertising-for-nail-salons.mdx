DOOH (digital out-of-home) advertising shows your nail salon's offer on digital screens near where clients walk, shop or wait. It is usually bought by CPM, the price per thousand impressions, and StackAdapt states CPMs range from $2 to $15 depending on screen type and campaign scope. No source read publishes a nail salon booking rate or a US minimum budget, so treat DOOH as a small test next to search, not a replacement.

This guide covers cost, screen choice, creative limits and how to measure bookings. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## How Much Does DOOH Cost for a Nail Salon?

StackAdapt lists three pricing models: CPM, a flat rate for a set period, and auction-based buying common in programmatic DOOH. It says CPMs range from $2 to $15, with large iconic screens at the top and smaller screens in busy spots lower. Location, audience, campaign length and holiday periods all move the price.

StackAdapt states no minimum budget. A 2020 UK IAB guide says many media owners had minimum orders of about 2,500 to 5,000 pounds for a few weeks. That is old and British, so use it only as a warning to ask for a written minimum before you commit.

## Is DOOH Big Enough to Matter?

The Out of Home Advertising Association of America reports that US out-of-home ad spend grew 3.6% to $9.46 billion in 2025. DOOH was 36% of sales, programmatic DOOH was 30% of DOOH, and DOOH grew 10.5%. Local advertisers made up 65% of total OOH sales.

Those are industry totals. They show screens are a normal channel, not proof it will pay for one salon.

## Which Screens Suit a Nail Salon?

Pick screens clients pass on the way to an appointment. The UK IAB guide says you can target by environment (rail, retail, roadside), by hour of day and by geography, down to a business 100 meters from a screen. It also says ads usually show for about 10 seconds.

| Screen type | Reach | Fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shopping center or retail screens | Shoppers with spare time | Walk-in and same-week offers |
| Street-level or bus-stop screens | People near your block | Awareness and a QR code |
| Roadside digital billboards | Drivers | Name, location, one line |
| Venue screens (offices, gyms) | A routine audience | Targeting by habit |

Ask your next ten new clients how they got to the salon and where they saw you. That tells you which screens to ask about.

## What Should the Ad Say?

One message, readable in a glance: salon name, one service with a price or offer, and the location. Add a QR code only where people stand still. Google Ads already captures people who search "nail salon near me", so use DOOH for the offer search cannot create, such as a first-visit price.

Design the ad to the media owner's specs. The IAB UK guide says media owners usually need the creative at least five working days before launch, though same-day starts are possible if the ad is ready.

## How Do You Measure Whether It Worked?

The IAB's July 2025 measurement guide separates impression types. An opportunity to see counts people in a screen's exposure zone, while likelihood to see requires them to be there when the screen is active and viewable. Neither proves a client noticed you.

For results, the IAB recommends matched market tests, which compare a test area with a similar area that has no ads. A salon can do a rough version: compare new-client bookings near the screens with a similar neighborhood over the same weeks. Add "how did you hear about us" to your booking form and a QR code with its own link or offer code.

## How Does DOOH Compare With Search and Facebook?

DOOH is priced per thousand impressions, search and Facebook per click, so compare cost per booking. LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks for Beauty and Personal Care show Google Ads at $4.62 per click, 10.35% conversion and $39.25 per lead. Facebook lead campaigns show $2.97 per click, 5.63% conversion and $50.91 per lead, and traffic campaigns $0.50 per click.

## FAQ

### How much does DOOH cost for a nail salon?

StackAdapt states CPMs range from $2 to $15. Total cost depends on screens, hours and any minimum order, which StackAdapt does not state.

### Is DOOH better than Instagram for a nail salon?

They do different jobs. Instagram shows your work to followers and ad audiences; DOOH reaches people near the salon. Our [Instagram guide for nail salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-nail-salons) covers the first.

### Can a small nail salon try DOOH?

Possibly, if screens are close and the minimum is low. Keep it a short test.

### How do I know if DOOH brought clients?

Use a QR code or offer code and a booking question, then compare against a similar area without ads.
