A small business in Belgium can reach almost everyone online, so the question is which audience, not whether. DataReportal counts 11.3 million internet users (96.3%), a Facebook ad reach of 6.20 million and an Instagram reach of 5.10 million. Start with Google Business Profile and a small Google Search campaign, add Facebook or Instagram for people not yet searching, and check cookie consent before you track visitors.

This guide is for shops, cafes, salons and service firms in Belgium. Figures come from the sources at the end, checked on 1 November 2026. Google Ads click prices for Belgium were not found in any source, so none are quoted.

## How Many Belgians Can a Small Business Reach Online?

Nearly all of them. DataReportal's Digital 2026 report lists 11.8 million people in Belgium, 11.3 million internet users and 12.0 million mobile connections, or 102% of the population. Social media user identities stand at 9.08 million, 77.2% of the population.

| Platform | Ad reach, late 2025 | Share of population |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | 9.08 million | 77.2% |
| Facebook | 6.20 million | 52.7% |
| LinkedIn (registered members) | 6.20 million | 52.7% |
| Instagram | 5.10 million | 43.4% |
| Messenger | 4.55 million | 38.7% |
| Snapchat | 4.29 million | 36.4% |
| TikTok (adults 18+ only) | 4.03 million | 42.5% of adults 18+ |

Compared with a market such as Mali or Uganda, reach is no longer the limit. Competition for attention is. Ad reach is an ad-tool estimate, and platforms revise it, so use the table as a ranking, not an exact count.

## Which Platform Should a Belgian Business Start With?

Google Search and Maps first, because someone typing "kapper" or "boulangerie" plus a town is already looking to buy. Then Facebook and Instagram for visual trades and offers. Snapchat's 4.29 million suits younger audiences but needs creative that fits the format.

LinkedIn's 6.20 million are registered members. It suits business-to-business firms, not a bakery. YouTube's 9.08 million is a reach figure for ads, and short videos of your premises or your work can help local searches, but plan this after the basics.

## Do You Need Different Ads for Each Language?

Probably, in two of the three regions. Belgium has three official languages: Dutch, French and German, according to a secondary reference page. DataReportal does not split platform reach by language, so no sourced figure shows how your town divides.

Build separate ad groups and landing pages for each language you serve and write them natively, not as translations. If your town has one dominant language, start there. Check your own customer list for the language of names and phone replies before choosing.

## Can a Belgian Business Run Google Ads?

Yes. Google's list of billing countries includes Belgium. In that list the account currency and payment method columns read "All", but Google says the options you see depend on your billing country, currency and payment setting, and that prepay is not offered in many countries.

Open the billing page in your own account to see what is available. No source checked publishes Belgian click prices, so read cost per click from your own account after a small test.

The [Google Ads cost guide](/blog/how-much-does-google-ads-cost-for-small-businesses-real-2026-pricing-breakdown) shows how clicks, conversion rate and cost per lead combine, using US benchmarks you should not copy to Belgium.

## What Does Cookie Consent Change for Your Marketing?

It decides what you can measure. Law-firm summaries of the Belgian Data Protection Authority's cookie guidance say prior consent is required for non-essential cookies, including marketing and audience measurement, and that further browsing does not count as consent. These are secondary summaries, and the authority's English page was not available.

In practice, set up a consent banner that offers a choice by category before you load ad or analytics tags. Expect fewer tracked visitors than your web statistics showed before, and judge campaigns on calls, bookings and sales as well as clicks. Check the authority's current text with your web developer or adviser.

## What Does a First Test Look Like? A Worked Example

All inputs are **assumptions** for an independent bakery with click-and-collect orders. They show the method, not Belgian results. Amounts are in euros.

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Gross profit per new customer over a year (assumption) | EUR 90 |
| Visitors who place a first order (assumption) | 4% |
| Orders per 100 website visitors | 4 |
| Gross profit per 100 visitors | 4 x 90 = EUR 360 |
| Most you can pay per visitor and still break even | 360 / 100 = EUR 3.60 |

If a click costs more than EUR 3.60 in this example, the campaign loses money on first orders unless customers come back. Your real figures will differ, so replace each assumption with numbers from your till and web statistics. ## How Do You Get Found on Google Maps in Belgium?

Google says local results rest mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking. It also states that there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.

Fill in every field, use real photos, reply to every review and keep the name, address and phone number identical everywhere. Fill in the profile in the language your customers search in, and add the second language in the description if you serve both. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) goes through each field.

Do not offer a discount or a free item in return for a review. Google's contributed content policy says merchants must not offer incentives, such as payment, discounts, free goods or services, in exchange for posting a review. Asking is fine. Rewarding is not. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop) for ways to ask.

## What Mistakes Do Belgian Small Businesses Make?

These are recommendations, not study findings.

- Running one set of ads in one language for a mixed area.
- Loading tracking tags before consent, then trusting incomplete statistics.
- Paying for reach on every platform when the shop serves a few streets.
- Buying LinkedIn space for a consumer product, where Instagram or Snapchat fits better.

## FAQ

### How many people use Facebook in Belgium?

DataReportal reports a Facebook ad reach of 6.20 million in late 2025, 52.7% of the population. It is an ad-tool estimate, not a count of active users.

### Is Instagram or TikTok bigger in Belgium?

Instagram's ad reach is 5.10 million. TikTok shows 4.03 million, but that covers adults 18 and over only, so the two are not directly comparable.

### What does Google Ads cost in Belgium?

No source checked publishes Belgian click prices. Run a small Search test and read your own cost per click and cost per call.

### Do I need cookie consent for Google Ads tracking in Belgium?

Secondary summaries of the Belgian Data Protection Authority's guidance say yes for marketing and audience measurement cookies. Confirm with the authority's current guidance.

### Should I advertise in Dutch, French or both?

Match the language of your customers. DataReportal gives no language split, so use your own customer data and test one language first.
