To run YouTube ads for a local business, create a Google Ads video campaign, choose a goal, upload a short video to YouTube, limit the location to your service area, set a small daily budget and track calls or visits. Google does not publish typical prices for local advertisers, so this guide shows how to work out your own cost from a test instead of quoting a rate.

Everything below comes from Google Ads Help pages opened in October 2026. Where a number is not published, the text says so. Examples that use made-up numbers are labelled as assumptions.

## Which YouTube Ad Format Should a Local Business Use?

Pick the format by what you want the viewer to do. Google describes five formats, and each bills differently.

| Format | Length | How you are charged |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skippable in-stream | No maximum; under 3 minutes recommended | CPV: when a viewer watches 30 seconds (or the whole video if shorter) or interacts. With Target CPM, Target CPA or Maximize conversions: per impression |
| Non-skippable in-stream | 15 to 60 seconds depending on campaign subtype | Target CPM (per impression) |
| Bumper | Up to 6 seconds (5 seconds minimum) | Target CPM |
| In-feed video | No maximum listed | When someone clicks to watch |
| YouTube Shorts | Under 60 seconds recommended | CPV: 10 seconds watched, or an interaction. Target CPM: when the ad starts to play |

A bumper repeats a name and a place. A skippable in-stream ad can carry a message, because you pay on CPV only when a viewer stays or interacts. Our [YouTube ad formats guide](/blog/youtube-ad-formats-explained-skippable-non-skippable-bumper-discovery) goes deeper.

## Step 1: Which Goal Should You Pick in Google Ads?

Start a new campaign and choose the objective that matches the outcome. Google lists these for Video campaigns:

- **Sales, Leads or Website traffic:** bid with Target CPA or Maximize conversions.
- **YouTube reach, views, and engagements:** for awareness. Video views uses Maximum CPV and you pay only for TrueView views. Video reach uses Target CPM.
- **Local store visits and promotions:** aimed at businesses such as restaurants and dealerships. Google's page gives no further detail on bidding for this one.

Google also states that all Video action campaigns were upgraded to Demand Gen as of May 2026. If you want conversions rather than views, you will likely set up a Demand Gen campaign, which can serve on YouTube, Gmail, Maps, Discover and the Display Network. Conversion tracking should be in place first. Google suggests waiting for about 50 conversions before making big changes.

## Step 2: How Do You Set Up a Reach Campaign?

For brand awareness in your area, Google documents the Efficient reach route. The steps are: choose the YouTube reach, views, and engagements objective, pick the Video campaign type, choose Auction as the buying method, pick Video reach, then Efficient reach.

Next, select ad formats (Google recommends bumper and skippable in-stream, in-feed and Shorts together), then enter budget, locations and languages. Add your video, final URL and call to action. Finally, set a Target CPM bid and create the campaign.

Google advises against narrow audience and content targeting on reach campaigns, because it can restrict reach. For a local business, let location do the narrowing.

## Step 3: What Video Should You Make?

Make one video that works in the first five seconds, because skippable ads can be skipped and bumpers end at six. State the business type and area, show the result or the place, and give one action such as a call, a booking or a visit.

For non-skippable reach, Google sets the length bands: up to 6 seconds for bumpers and 7 to 15 seconds for standard non-skippable ads. Upload the video to your YouTube channel first, since the campaign uses a YouTube video. Keep a vertical and a horizontal cut if you plan to use Shorts.

No source gives a length that performs best for local businesses, so test two versions.

## Step 4: How Do You Target Only Local Viewers?

Use location settings. Google says radius targeting needs a radius of at least 1 km around a location. Its location targeting page lists two options: "Presence or interest" reaches people in or interested in the area, and "Presence" limits delivery to people physically there.

For a business people visit, test "Presence" if you see views from places you cannot serve. In Demand Gen you can set campaign-level location targeting, including radius, but Google notes you cannot switch back to ad group level after publishing.

## Step 5: How Much Should You Spend?

Google does not publish average YouTube prices by industry or city, and this guide does not quote a rate. Use a test. Google says the monthly charge will not exceed 30.4 times your average daily budget, and daily spend can exceed the daily budget but never reaches twice the average.

**Worked example (all numbers are assumptions, not benchmarks).** Say you set $10 a day, about $304 a month. If your cost per view turned out to be $0.05, you would get about 6,080 views. At $0.10, about 3,040. At $0.20, about 1,520. Your real cost per view will show in the campaign report after a week.

Compare it with [our YouTube ad cost guide](/blog/how-much-do-youtube-ads-cost-real-cpv-cpm-data) and your own break-even.

## Step 6: How Do You Know It Worked?

Match the measure to the goal. For views campaigns, check view rate and cost per view. For conversion goals, count calls, form fills or direction requests, and compare cost per conversion with what a customer is worth.

Add a question at checkout or on the phone: "How did you hear about us?" Some customers will act later, not on the first view, so the ad report may not capture every result. Judge over at least a month.

## FAQ

### How much do YouTube ads cost for a local business?
Google does not publish an average. You set a budget and a bid, and what you pay depends on the format. Run a small test and read your own cost per view or per impression from the report.

### What is the cheapest way to start?
A small daily budget, one location radius, one video and one goal. Views campaigns charge only for TrueView views, and reach campaigns charge per thousand impressions at a Target CPM you set.

### How long should a YouTube ad be?
Bumpers are up to 6 seconds, standard non-skippable ads 7 to 15 seconds, and Google recommends skippable in-stream ads stay under 3 minutes.

### Can I target only people near my shop?
Yes. Radius targeting needs at least 1 km. Choose "Presence" to limit delivery to people physically in the area.

### What happened to Video action campaigns?
Google states that all Video action campaigns were upgraded to Demand Gen as of May 2026.
