A local shop should use Instagram ads to put specific products and an in-store reason to visit in front of people nearby, then count visits and sales, not likes. The closest public benchmark is LocaliQ's Facebook traffic figure for Shopping, Collectibles and Gifts: a $0.59 click and a 1.88% click-through rate. It is a Facebook benchmark, not Instagram-only and not retail-specific, so use it only to plan a first test.

This guide is for independent shops that sell products to people in their own area. It covers what to promote, how to target, a $300 test and how to know whether it paid.

## What Should a Local Shop Promote on Instagram?

Promote what a nearby person can act on this week. Good candidates are a new arrival, a seasonal range, an event in the shop or a clear introductory offer. Avoid ads for your whole catalog, because they give no reason to come in now.

Show the real product in a real setting. A phone video of the shelf or a staff member holding the item is enough. Add the street or neighborhood in the first line so locals recognize the ad is for them. If you sell online as well, our [Instagram Shopping guide](/blog/instagram-shopping-guide) covers tagging products.

## Which Campaign Type Fits a Shop?

LocaliQ reports two kinds of Facebook campaign benchmarks, and the choice matters for a shop.

- **Traffic campaigns** send people to a page. Across industries they cost $0.60 per click with a 1.93% CTR. LocaliQ notes that traffic campaigns report no conversion rate or cost per lead.
- **Lead campaigns** collect a sign-up or form. Across industries they average $1.80 per click and $27.39 per lead.

For a shop, a traffic campaign pointing to a page with opening hours, a map and the featured product is the simplest start. A lead campaign suits a shop with a list-building offer, such as a launch-day notification. Meta is also changing its objective names, so match the goal to what your Ads Manager shows.

## How Do You Target People Who Can Visit?

Use a radius around the shop. Customers rarely travel far for a single purchase, so start with the area you could walk or drive to in 10 to 15 minutes (an assumption, check against your own customer postcodes). Keep age and interests broad at first.

Ask a second question: what do people do after clicking? If they land on a slow page without hours and a map, the cheap click is wasted. Put opening hours, address and the product first. ## What Does a $300 Test Look Like?

Every input below is an **assumption** built on the LocaliQ category CPC. A Facebook benchmark is a rough guide for Instagram.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $300 |
| Cost per click | $0.59 |
| Clicks | about 508 |
| Click to store visit | 2% |
| Store visits | about 10 |
| Visitors who buy | 50% |
| Buyers | about 5 |
| Average first order | $40 |
| First-order revenue | about $200 |

On those inputs the first month does not cover the $300 on first orders alone. It pays only if repeat purchases or higher baskets follow. That is the honest test: run it, then replace each line with your numbers. If your click-to-visit rate is higher, the result improves quickly, which is why a coupon or a dated event helps.

## How Do You Know It Worked?

Clicks are not sales. Track:

1. **Ad-driven visits.** Give the ad its own code or ask at the till, "How did you hear about us?"
2. **Sales from those visits.** Log them for 30 days.
3. **Repeat purchases.** A shop wins on the second visit.

Compare cost per sale with your gross profit per sale. For wider planning, our [local marketing budget guide](/blog/local-marketing-budget-guide) shows how to size spend against revenue, and our [Reels ads guide](/blog/instagram-reels-ads-local-business-guide) covers video formats.

## FAQ

### Can a small shop get foot traffic from Instagram ads?

Possibly. Ads can put a product in front of nearby people, but no public study proves foot traffic results for shops. Test with a small budget and count visits at the till.

### What budget should a local shop start with?

The example uses $300 for 30 days as an assumption. Choose an amount you can lose, and run it long enough to see at least a few hundred clicks.

### Should I use traffic or lead campaigns?

Use traffic if the goal is visits. Use leads if the goal is a list. LocaliQ reports lead cost only for lead campaigns.

### Is Instagram or Google better for shops?

Google is better for people searching for something now, while Instagram suits visual products and new arrivals. See our [Instagram versus Facebook ads comparison](/blog/instagram-ads-vs-facebook-ads) for the placements.
