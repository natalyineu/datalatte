## Is NotebookLM Now Called Gemini Notebook?

Yes. On 16 July 2026 Google's blog post by Josh Woodward said: "We're renaming NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook." Google calls it "the same standalone product, now doing more across the Google ecosystem". Its help pages and plans page now use the new name only, and some still show the notebooklm.google address.

The blog post does not address pricing or the free plan. Google's plans help page still lists a free Standard plan, which is what this guide relies on. The article keeps the old name in its title because most searches still use it.

The rename also brought code execution: "Gemini Notebook to write and execute code natively", with a secure cloud computer for each notebook. Google said it was available for Google AI Ultra and Workspace business customers on launch, and rolling out to Pro on the web.

## What Are the Usage Limits?

Limits on chats, audio and video overviews are not given as numbers. Google's usage page describes them as "compute-based", depending on prompt complexity, the models and features used, and chat length. Plans are expressed as multiples: no plan has standard limits, AI Plus two times, AI Pro four times, and AI Ultra 5x or 20x AI Pro.

Quota refreshes every 5 hours until you reach a weekly limit. Google says limits can change. Your own usage and weekly limit show under Settings, then Usage, in the app.

Because no daily count is published, a plan comparison on "how many audio summaries" is not possible from official pages. The notebook and source counts are the hard numbers.

## What Is Gemini Notebook and How Does It Work?

Google describes it as "an AI-powered research assistant designed to help you refine and organize your ideas". You add sources such as PDFs, websites, YouTube videos, audio files, Google Docs and Google Slides. It answers questions grounded in those sources with inline citations, and can create study guides, briefings, audio overviews and mind maps.

Because answers come from the sources you add, it suits internal material better than open-ended writing. It can still make mistakes, and Google says to consult a qualified professional for medical, legal or financial advice.

## What Are the Rules and Risks?

From Google's Gemini Notebook help pages:

- **Training.** "Your data is protected, and is not used to train Gemini Notebook unless you provide feedback." Sending feedback lets Google review the full context of that interaction.
- **Work and school accounts.** For Workspace and Workspace for Education, uploads, queries and responses "will not be reviewed by human reviewers".
- **Ownership.** The help page says Google will not claim ownership of content you generate. It asks you to respect copyright and not share copyrighted material without the necessary rights.
- **Sharing.** Notebooks are private by default (lock icon); a globe icon means shared publicly. Check before sharing a notebook that contains staff or customer data.
- **Age and region.** Users must verify their age in the Google Account, and availability depends on country.
- **Accuracy.** It can make mistakes, so check citations against the source file.
- **Commercial terms.** The pages opened do not state commercial-use rules for outputs beyond the ownership line, and they do not cover Gemini app chat storage settings, so check them before processing customer data.

## FAQ

### Is NotebookLM free?

Yes. The Standard plan is free with a Google account and allows 100 notebooks per user and 50 sources per notebook. Higher limits come with paid Google AI plans.

### What is the difference between NotebookLM Plus and Pro?

Plus (Google AI Plus) allows 200 notebooks and 100 sources each. Pro (Google AI Pro) allows 500 notebooks and 300 sources, higher model access and watermark removal.

### How much does NotebookLM Pro cost?

Google AI Pro is $19.99 a month or $199.99 a year on Google's plans page. It is free for a year for eligible students.

### Is NotebookLM now Gemini Notebook?

Yes. Google renamed it on 16 July 2026 and describes it as the same standalone product.

### Is NotebookLM safe for business documents?

Google says data is not used to train Gemini Notebook unless you give feedback, and Workspace uploads are not reviewed by humans. Check sharing settings and avoid uploading data you are not allowed to process.
