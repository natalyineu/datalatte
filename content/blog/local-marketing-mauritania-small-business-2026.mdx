Local marketing in Mauritania works best when a small business starts with a Facebook Page and WhatsApp Business, publishes accurate hours and contact details, and makes payment options clear. Meta's apps reach billions of people each day, so a presence there is a practical starting point. WhatsApp handles the conversations that turn interest into orders and bookings. Google Ads can supplement this for specific searches, but it should not replace the social and messaging foundation. The sections below cover each step and include a budget calculation you can adapt.

## Why do Facebook and WhatsApp come first?

Meta's apps, which include Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp and Threads, reach an average of 3.56 billion daily active people, according to [Adwave's summary of Meta's apps](https://adwave.com/resources/facebook-advertising-for-small-business) (secondary). That reach is across the whole platform, not Mauritania alone, so treat it as context rather than a local forecast.

Meta also owns WhatsApp. [Salesforce reports](https://www.salesforce.com/marketing/whatsapp-marketing/?bc=OTH) (secondary) that Facebook, now Meta, acquired WhatsApp in 2014 for $22 billion. For a small business, the practical result is that the two apps are built to work together.

Country-level user numbers change quickly. Check the audience estimate in Meta Ads Manager for your city and radius before you plan a budget, because that tool reflects current local reach better than any general figure.

## How does the free WhatsApp window work for ad leads?

The [WhatsApp Business Platform pricing page](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/) (primary) says that when a customer messages you after a Click-to-WhatsApp ad or a Facebook Page call-to-action button, all of your messages sent in the following 3 days (72 hours) are not charged.

This matters for a salon or café that receives a burst of enquiries from a boosted post. Replying quickly and completing the conversation inside that window keeps those messages within the free period. The same vendor-level feature is described by [Crescendo Lab](https://blog.cresclab.com/en/whatsapp-marketing) (secondary), which says Click-to-WhatsApp Ads move people from Facebook and Instagram ads into personalized WhatsApp conversations.

Pricing terms change, and the page describes the Business Platform. Confirm which WhatsApp setup you use and read the current terms before relying on the free window in your budget.

## Should your content be in Arabic, French or both?

The sources available for this guide do not measure language preference in Mauritania, so decide from your own evidence. Look at the language your customers use in their first WhatsApp message and in comments on your Facebook posts. Then write the main post in that language and test a second version in the other language.

Keep prices, opening hours and booking instructions identical across languages. Customers who switch between Arabic and French should see the same facts, which reduces mistakes and repeat questions.

For a comparison of how a WhatsApp-led market structures its channel mix, the [Brazil guide to WhatsApp and Meta marketing](/blog/local-marketing-brazil-small-business-whatsapp-meta) shows a similar sequence of Facebook discovery followed by WhatsApp conversion.

## How should mobile money fit into your marketing?

Customers are more likely to buy when they can see how they will pay before they message you. Put accepted payment methods on your Facebook Page information, in your WhatsApp greeting message, and on any printed signage. Name only the providers your customers actually use, and confirm each provider's current acceptance rules directly with them.

Train staff to answer payment questions in one standard sentence. A short, consistent answer removes a common source of hesitation without adding any cost.

Mobile-first checkout and payment habits are discussed in the [Kenya guide to mobile-first local marketing](/blog/local-marketing-kenya-small-business-mobile-first). Its advice on keeping the purchase path short applies directly to a WhatsApp order.

## What does a realistic monthly budget look like?

The calculation below is a hypothetical salon in Nouakchott. Every input is an assumption you should replace with your own numbers. The purpose is to show how to test whether a small ad budget is paying back, not to predict results.

| Item | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly ad budget | $30 | assumption |
| WhatsApp conversations started from ads | 40 | assumption |
| Bookings per 10 conversations | 3 | assumption |
| Average booking value | $12 | assumption |
| Bookings per month | 12 | 40 × 3 ÷ 10 |
| Booking revenue | $144 | 12 × $12 |
| Cost per booking | $2.50 | $30 ÷ 12 |
| Revenue per $1 of ads | $4.80 | $144 ÷ $30 |

Revenue is not profit. Subtract product costs and staff time before deciding whether the campaign worked. The example also assumes no messaging charges for replies inside the 72-hour window, which you should verify against the current WhatsApp pricing terms.

Convert the dollar figures to ouguiya using your bank's current rate. Run the test for at least one full month, then change only one input at a time, such as the offer or the boosted-post audience, so you can see which change moved the result.

Plan extra promotions around the periods when your own sales records show customers spend more, including religious holidays if those affect your category. Use your sales history rather than a general seasonal assumption.

## Is Google Ads worth trying for a Mauritanian business?

Google Ads is best treated as an optional supplement for a narrow set of search terms, such as a specific service and neighbourhood, where a customer is already close to booking. Start with a small daily budget and a short list of exact-match keywords, then review the search terms report weekly to remove irrelevant queries.

If you want to see how a larger market structures its paid search, the [Germany guide to Google Ads for small businesses](/blog/local-marketing-germany-small-business-google-ads) covers campaign structure in more depth. Treat that as a process reference, not a forecast for Mauritania.

## FAQ

### Is Mauritania a good market for early Facebook and WhatsApp marketing?

Facebook and WhatsApp are the platforms most small businesses can set up without a large budget. Start with a complete Facebook Page and a WhatsApp Business account, then measure how many conversations come from each. Whether that adds up to an advantage depends on your local competition, which you can check by reviewing competitor pages in your own city.

### Should marketing content be in Arabic or French?

Use the language your customers actually write in. Test a version in each language and compare replies. Keep prices and opening hours identical in both versions so customers get the same information regardless of language.

### How important is mobile money to a marketing strategy?

It can remove a payment obstacle, so show accepted methods clearly on your Facebook Page and WhatsApp greeting. Confirm each provider's current rules with the provider and with your customers, because acceptance terms can change.

### Is Google Ads worth it for a small Mauritanian business?

Only as a small, targeted supplement. Use exact-match keywords for specific services and check the search terms report each week. Keep Facebook and WhatsApp as the main channels unless your own data shows search delivering more bookings.
