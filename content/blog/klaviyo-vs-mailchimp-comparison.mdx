Klaviyo and Mailchimp cost about the same for a small list and drift apart as it grows. Both official pricing pages show a free plan for 250 contacts and 500 emails a month. A third-party tracker (secondary) lists both at $100 a month for 5,000 contacts, then Klaviyo at $400 against Mailchimp's $310 at 25,000 contacts.

## What Do the Free Plans Include?

Klaviyo's pricing page lists a free plan with up to 250 active profiles, 500 email sends a month and $5 of mobile messages a month, which it says depends on region and carrier ([Klaviyo pricing](https://www.klaviyo.com/pricing)).

Mailchimp's pricing page lists Free with 250 contacts and a cap of 500 emails a month or 250 a day. Older articles say 500 contacts, so check the date of any source you read ([Mailchimp pricing](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/)).

## How Do Mailchimp's Paid Plans Start?

Mailchimp's page shows Essentials from $13, Standard from $20 and Premium from $350 a month, as first-year offers. It adds 15% off the first 12 months for lists of 10,000 or more. It notes that going over a plan's contact or send limit can trigger overage charges.

Those headline figures are for the smallest list. The monthly price depends on how many contacts you have, so use the page's contact slider with your own number.

## What Does Each Tool Cost as Your List Grows?

Stack Reviewer (secondary, verified October 2026) lists Klaviyo's Email plan at about $150 for 10,000 contacts, $400 for 25,000, $720 for 50,000 and $1,380 for 100,000. For Mailchimp Standard it lists $100 at 5,000 contacts, $310 at 25,000 and $450 at 50,000.

Other trackers disagree on Mailchimp. At 50,000 contacts, one lists $350 or more and another $480. Treat every figure here as a range, and read your own quote.

| Contacts | Klaviyo Email | Mailchimp Standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5,000 | about $100 | about $100 |
| 25,000 | about $400 | about $310 |
| 50,000 | about $720 | about $450 (other trackers: $350 to $480) |

## What Can Raise the Bill?

Several billing rules matter more than the base price. These come from secondary sources, so confirm them before you rely on them.

- **Klaviyo SMS** is an add-on. Stack Reviewer lists $0.01 per SMS credit plus carrier fees.
- **Klaviyo profiles:** PricePulse says unsubscribed and suppressed profiles still count toward your tier.
- **Mailchimp audiences:** PricePulse says a contact in two audiences counts as two contacts.
- **Mailchimp overages:** extra contacts or sends beyond a plan's limit can cost more.

A clean list is the cheapest saving: remove invalid and long-inactive addresses before you move up a tier.

## Is Klaviyo Worth the Higher Price?

The tracker's own rule is that Klaviyo pays for itself in ecommerce stores with Shopify or WooCommerce that use abandoned cart, post-purchase and browse flows. For a local service business with no online store, a cheaper tool usually covers the basics.

The example below uses **assumption** inputs, not benchmark data. A shop has 25,000 contacts. Klaviyo costs about $90 more a month than Mailchimp, or $1,080 a year. At an assumed 40% margin, Klaviyo needs about $225 of extra monthly sales to cover the gap.

If your flows will not clearly do that, stay with the cheaper tool. Our guides on [abandoned cart emails](/blog/abandoned-cart-email-strategy) and Klaviyo for local business show what the flows involve.

## FAQ

### Is Klaviyo more expensive than Mailchimp?

At 5,000 contacts the tracker lists them equal. At 25,000 and 50,000 it lists Klaviyo higher. Mailchimp's own page shows lower entry prices, but exact monthly prices depend on contact count.

### Do both have a free plan?

Yes. Both pricing pages list 250 contacts and 500 emails a month on the free tier.

### Does SMS cost extra?

On Klaviyo, yes, as an add-on billed per credit plus carrier fees, per a secondary source. Compare channels in our [SMS vs email guide](/blog/sms-vs-email-marketing-local-business-which-is-better).
