## What does each tool actually charge a small business?

Semrush sells breadth. Pro covers keyword tracking, site audits and competitor research for a handful of projects, and local listing tools sit in higher tiers or add-ons. A salon tracking itself and two competitors fits Pro; a chain tracking twenty locations does not.

Ahrefs sells depth in links and keyword data. Starter at $29 a month suits occasional checks, while Lite at $129 a month is the realistic working tier, on Ahrefs pricing checked in November 2026. Its free Webmaster Tools covers a verified site's own data at no cost.

The honest comparison for a local owner is not Semrush against Ahrefs. It is either tool against $0 of Google Search Console plus a local rank tracker. Search Console shows the queries already bringing impressions, which is the list that matters first.

## Which one fits local SEO work?

Local rankings turn on the Google Business Profile, reviews and location pages more than on national keyword volume. Neither suite manages reviews or listings as well as a dedicated local tool. Semrush helps more when content planning is the bottleneck: topic research, content briefs and PPC data sit in one login, alongside the writing tools priced in [Best AI tools for small business marketing](/blog/ai-tools-for-small-business-marketing-2026). Ahrefs helps more when the question is links: which sites link to competitors, and which local directories or partners are realistic targets.

Agencies feel the difference sooner than owners. Tracking fifty clients makes per-project limits the real price, and both vendors price that scale in the hundreds per month. ## What does the maths look like before subscribing?

**Assumption example.** Assume a café owner spends 2 hours a month checking rankings by hand and values that time at $30 an hour, or $60 a month. Ahrefs Lite at $129 only breaks even if it also replaces other paid checks or finds one fix worth $69 or more a month. Those inputs are assumptions; substitute real hours and one named task before subscribing.

A simpler test runs for one month. List the three questions the tool must answer, such as which five pages to rewrite. Buy monthly billing, not annual. If the answers do not change a decision by the renewal date, cancel and return to Search Console.

## FAQ

### Is Semrush or Ahrefs better for a local business?

For most single-location businesses, neither is the first purchase. Google Search Console is free, and a local rank tracker or profile tool costs less than either suite. Choose Semrush when content and PPC planning dominate; choose Ahrefs when link research dominates and the budget already exceeds $129 a month.

### What is the cheapest way to use Ahrefs?

Ahrefs Starter lists at $29 a month and free Webmaster Tools covers a verified site's own backlinks and keywords, on Ahrefs pricing checked in November 2026. Starter suits monthly checks. Regular competitor tracking pushes most users to Lite at $129 a month, so buy Starter only with a short question list.

### Can a small business share one account?

Both vendors charge for extra seats, with Semrush Pro adding $45 per user a month on secondary 2026 pricing checks. Sharing one login informally risks lockouts and messy billing. A freelancer reporting monthly usually makes one seat enough; a team of three should price seats into the comparison from the start.

### When is Semrush worth $139.95 a month?

When one subscription replaces several others: rank tracking, content research, PPC spying and reporting for multiple services or locations. A business using two of those weekly likely recovers the fee. A business logging in twice a month to check five keywords does not, and should stay on free tools.
