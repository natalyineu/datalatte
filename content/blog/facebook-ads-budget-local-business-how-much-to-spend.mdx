There is no single right Facebook ads budget for a local business. Work backward instead: leads you want per month multiplied by your cost per lead. LocaliQ's September 2026 benchmarks put the average Facebook lead campaign at $27.39 per lead, so 10 leads is roughly $274 a month, with beauty and personal care at $50.91 per lead costing about $509 for the same 10. Start with that number, not a round figure.

## How Do You Calculate Your Budget?

Use this chain: customers wanted, divided by the share of leads that book, multiplied by cost per lead.

1. Decide how many new customers you want per month.
2. Estimate the share of leads that book. This is your number, not Meta's. Track it for a month.
3. Leads needed = customers / booking share.
4. Monthly budget = leads needed x cost per lead.
5. Daily budget = monthly budget / 30.

Check the result against profit. If a new customer is worth less than your cost per customer, the budget is too high for that offer, however good the ad looks.

## How Do Daily and Lifetime Budgets Work?

Meta's developer documentation defines a daily budget as "the average amount you're willing to spend on an ad set or campaign each day", and says on days with better opportunities up to 25% more than your daily budget may be spent. A $10 daily budget could spend up to $12.50 on such a day. A lifetime budget is "the amount you're willing to spend over the entire run of an ad set or campaign".

The page states no minimum budget amount, only that amounts are set in the smallest unit of your currency. Secondary guides quote different overspend percentages and minimums, so rely on what Ads Manager accepts for your account. For a steady local campaign, a daily budget is simpler to read.

## A Worked Example: Hair Salon and Fitness Studio

| --- | --- | --- |
| New customers wanted per month (assumption) | 8 | 8 |
| Leads that book (assumption) | 40% | 40% |

| Cost per lead (LocaliQ) | $50.91 | $27.11 |
| Monthly budget | about $1,018 | about $542 |
| Daily budget | about $34 | about $18 |
| Cost per new customer | about $127 | about $68 |

Compare cost per customer with what a new customer is worth over a year. If a salon client is worth $300 in first-year profit (assumption), $127 is fine. If it is worth $90, you lose money until repeat visits show up. When the numbers do not work, change the offer or follow-up speed before you raise spend.

## How Much Should You Start With?

Start with the smallest amount that can produce a readable result in 30 days, and no more than you can lose. Using LocaliQ's average, even 10 leads costs about $274, and a booking share of 40% turns that into only 4 customers. That is thin data.

Raise the budget in steps while cost per booking stays below profit per customer, and avoid editing the ad daily. ## When Should You Scale or Pause?

Judge by cost per booking after about 30 days, not by likes or clicks. Scale when the booking cost sits below your profit per customer for two weeks. Pause or fix when it sits above, or when leads arrive but few book.

A simple rule of thumb, not a Meta rule: change the budget by about 20% at a time so you can see the effect. Frequent large edits make results harder to read. Make sure you reply to leads fast; a slow reply wastes the most expensive part of the budget. ## FAQ

### How much should a small local business spend on Facebook ads?

Calculate it: leads you need times your cost per lead. At LocaliQ's all-industry average of $27.39 per lead, 20 leads is about $548 a month. Your own booking data should replace the averages after the first month.

### Is $5 a day enough?

At $5 a day (about $150 a month) and the $27.39 average, you would get roughly 5 leads a month. That is enough to see whether leads arrive, not enough to judge profitability. This is arithmetic on a benchmark, not a Meta guarantee.

### Should I use a daily or a lifetime budget?

Meta defines a daily budget as an average, with up to 25% more spent on some days. A lifetime budget covers a fixed run. Use daily for ongoing campaigns and lifetime for a dated event.

### What is a good cost per lead?

LocaliQ's 2026 average is $27.39 across industries, with $50.91 for beauty and $27.11 for fitness. Compare with your profit per customer, not only with the average.

### How long before I know if it works?

Plan on about 30 days of data before judging cost per booking. That is a planning window, not a Meta rule.

### Does the budget include ad creative and a landing page?

No. The ad spend is only what Meta charges. Photography, a booking page and tracking are separate costs.
