## What Does a Google Ads Click Cost for Animals & Pets?

The cleanest published figure is $4.06 per click for the Animals & Pets category in LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks. That is the only per-click number this article relies on, and it comes from a secondary source that reports the benchmark.

Animals & Pets is a broad category. It covers many businesses beyond grooming, so the average shows what the category pays for clicks in general. It does not show what a cat groomer will pay in a particular town. Competition for a search like "cat groomer near me" may be higher or lower than the category average.

Treat the average as a starting point. Once your account has enough clicks to compare, replace it with the cost per click it reports. A quiet suburb and a dense city centre can produce very different numbers, so your own figure should drive the final budget.

## How Do You Build the Budget From Bookings?

Work from the result you want back to the spend it requires. The steps below use only the cost per click and your own tracked rates.

1. Set the number of new cat clients you want each month.
2. Track the share of enquiries that become bookings. Until you have data, use an assumption and label it as one.
3. Divide clients by that booking share to get the enquiries needed.
4. Divide enquiries by the share of clicks that become enquiries to get the clicks needed.
5. Multiply clicks by cost per click to get the monthly budget.
6. Divide the monthly budget by the number of days in the month for a daily figure.

Cost per lead is less useful here. A "conversion" can mean a call, a form or a booking, and each platform counts them differently. Count the same events every month, and check the definition in your account. ## What Does a Six-Client Month Cost in Ads?

All inputs are assumptions except cost per click. The table shows each step so you can swap in your own numbers.

| Input or result | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New cat clients wanted per month | 6 | Assumption |
| Share of enquiries that book | 40% | Assumption |
| Share of clicks that become enquiries | 25% | Assumption |
| Enquiries needed | 15 | 6 ÷ 40% |
| Clicks needed | 60 | 15 ÷ 25% |
| Cost per click | $4.06 | Animals & Pets average |
| Monthly ad budget | about $244 | 60 × $4.06 |
| Daily budget, 30-day month | about $8.12 | Assumption: 30 days |
| Ad spend per new client | about $40.60 | $243.60 ÷ 6 |

The result is a plan, not a forecast. If the booking share falls to 20 percent, enquiries needed double to 30, clicks rise to 120, and the monthly budget becomes about $487. Recalculate the moment your tracked rates differ from the assumptions. The per-client cost is the number to compare with what a client is worth to you over a year, using your own price list.

## Is a $200 Budget Enough to Learn Anything?

At $4.06 per click, $200 buys about 49 clicks in a month. With the assumed 25 percent enquiry rate, that is roughly 12 enquiries. That is enough to see whether people in your area contact you and whether your phone number and booking page work.

It is not enough to judge profit. Twelve enquiries is a small sample, so one quiet week can shift the picture a lot. Use a test budget to check the basics: are the right searches triggering your ads, and do the enquiries turn into bookings at a rate you can track? Once that works, increasing the budget is a far safer decision than increasing it on day one.

## How Do Agency Fees Change the Budget?

Agency pricing often adds a second cost on top of the ads. [Performance Pets](https://performpets.com/how-much-does-grooming-salon-marketing-cost/) (secondary) reports that one agency program requires at least $600 a month in ad spend, and that ad spend is not included in the published agency fees. Its worked example assumes a $2,000 monthly fee plus $600 in ads, a total of $2,600 a month.

[TailWerks](https://tailwerks.com/industries/pet-grooming-marketing-agency/) (vendor) states that ad spend is paid separately from its management fee. A UK guide from [Bonsai & Frazer](https://bonsandfrazer.com/insights/facebook-ads-on-a-budget/) (vendor) says that at £500 a month of ad spend, most professional management costs as much as the ads again, or more.

Set against a $244 plan, a $600 minimum spend already exceeds the whole budget. At this scale, a managed program can cost more than the ads it buys. Ask any provider for the total monthly cost, including ad spend, in writing before comparing.

## How Should You Set the Budget Controls?

The sources here do not cover Google Ads budget settings directly. Check Google's live help page for current controls before you change anything. The general principle from the platforms that are covered is that you decide the spend, not the vendor.

For Facebook, [WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2020/09/08/facebook-local-advertising) (secondary) suggests setting the budget at campaign level for simplicity, so you tell the platform how much to spend per day. [Impossible](https://www.impossible.sg/a-guide-to-facebook-advertising-in-singapore-how-to-get-started/) (secondary) explains that a daily budget suits ads meant to run all day, while a lifetime budget suits campaigns with a set end date. If you test Facebook alongside search, compare costs with the [Facebook ads budget guide for local businesses](/blog/facebook-ads-budget-local-business-how-much-to-spend).

## Where Should a Cat Groomer Start on a Near-Zero Budget?

If the budget is close to nothing, start with work that needs time more than money. [Rozhair](https://rozhair.com/blogs/education/hair-salon-advertising-ideas) (secondary), writing about salons, recommends starting with your Google Business Profile, reviews, service descriptions and portfolio.

For very small operations with a tight budget, the messaging apps listed in [Builder Growth Engine's](https://buildergrowthengine.com/top-client-communication-tools-for-pet-grooming-businesses/) comparison, such as WhatsApp Business and Google Messages, are presented as the best fit for quick replies. That article gives no prices, so check each app's current terms yourself.

If you have no budget at all, the [zero-budget marketing guide](/blog/local-business-marketing-when-you-have-zero-budget) covers free options in more detail. Once your profile is complete and you have reviews, a small paid test becomes easier to measure.

## How Wide Should the Ad Radius Be?

Radius matters because a cat groomer usually serves a limited area. [Barklead](https://barklead.com/post/using-facebook-ads-for-groomers) (secondary) suggests targeting users within a 10 to 15 mile radius of the groomer's location.

A 2016 article from [Groomer to Groomer](https://www.groomertogroomer.com/reaching-more-customers-with-facebook-ads/) (secondary) describes an author whose customers lived within about 12 miles. The same article warns that a broad audience of 150,000 pet owners in a 20-mile radius spreads a limited budget thin, with ad frequency typically below 1.5. Start narrower than you think you need, then widen only if enquiries fall short and your budget can support it. ## FAQ

### What is the average Google Ads budget for a cat groomer?

No source publishes one for cat groomers. The example above uses an assumed $244 a month for six new clients, built from the $4.06 Animals & Pets average cost per click.

### Is $200 a month enough to start?

It buys about 49 clicks at $4.06. That is enough to test whether searchers contact you, but not enough to judge profit.

### Do agencies include ad spend in their fee?

Not in the examples reviewed here. Ad spend is billed separately, and one program requires at least $600 a month in ads.

### Should a cat groomer start with Google or Facebook?

The sources here do not compare the two for cat groomers. Start with the channel your clients use to search for a groomer, and track which one produces bookings.
