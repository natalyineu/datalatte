To set up Google Analytics 4 for a local business, create an account and a property, add a web data stream for your site, install the Google tag, and check the Realtime report. Google says data can take up to 30 minutes to begin. Then mark your booking, call and form actions as key events, and exclude your own visits so your numbers are not inflated.

This guide follows Google's own help pages. It is for owners of cafes, salons, studios and clinics with a website. Examples are labelled as assumptions.

## How Do You Create the Account and Property?

Go to analytics.google.com and click Start Measuring if it is your first time. In Admin, click Create and choose Account, name it and set data-sharing options. Then create a property: name it and choose the reporting time zone and currency. Google also asks for an industry category and business size.

Use the time zone and currency of your business, because reports use them. Google's page describes setup as free ("at no cost"), and says one account can hold up to 2,000 properties. A local business needs one.

## How Do You Add a Data Stream and Install the Tag?

In Admin, open Data Streams under Data collection and modification, click Add stream and choose Web. Enter your primary URL and a stream name, decide whether to turn on enhanced measurement, and click Create stream. You get a Measurement ID that starts with "G-".

Install the Google tag in one of three ways, per Google:

- **CMS integration:** paste the Measurement ID into your website builder's Google Analytics field.
- **Manual install:** paste the tag snippet immediately after the head tag on every page.
- **Google Tag Manager:** add the Google Analytics configuration tag. Our [Tag Manager guide](/blog/google-tag-manager-for-local-website-analytics) covers that route.

## How Do You Check It Works?

Open the Realtime report and visit your own site on your phone. Google says data collection may take up to 30 minutes to begin, so wait before you troubleshoot. If nothing appears, check that the ID is the G- ID of this stream and that the tag is on every page, including the booking page.

If your booking page sits on another domain, such as a third-party scheduler, the tag must be installed there too, if the vendor allows it. Schedulers differ, so ask the vendor.

## Which Actions Should Be Key Events?

Google defines a key event as an event that measures an action particularly important to the success of your business. Any event you collect can be marked as one. Key events then show in a Key events column in reports such as Landing pages and User acquisition, and can be turned into Google Ads conversions.

For a local business, the actions that matter are usually a booking, a form submission, a click on the phone number and a click on directions. Create the event first, then mark it. Enhanced measurement sends events such as page views, scrolls, clicks and file downloads, but Google's page does not say any of them are key events by default.

## How Do You Stop Counting Your Own Visits?

On a small site your own visits can distort the numbers. Google lets you define internal traffic by IP address or range (Admin, Data streams, your web stream, Configure tag settings, Define internal traffic) and then create an Exclude data filter.

Start the filter in Testing. Google says matching data is labelled with the "Test data filter name" dimension and that a filter can take 24 to 36 hours to apply. Active filters change incoming data permanently, and excluded data is never processed. Home and mobile connections change IPs, so this works best for a fixed shop network.

## Should You Link Google Ads and Check Data Retention?

If you run ads, link the property in Admin, under Product links, Google Ads links. You need to be an editor or administrator on the property and have administrative access in Google Ads. Google says Ads data appears in Analytics reports within 48 hours of linking.

Retention matters for Explorations. Google's options for user-level and standard event data are 2 months or 14 months, and the setting does not affect standard aggregated reports. Check the setting once.

## FAQ

### Is Google Analytics 4 free?
Google's setup page describes it as at no cost. Paid versions exist but are not needed for a local site.

### How long until data shows up?
Google says collection may take up to 30 minutes to begin. Check the Realtime report.

### What is a key event?
An event that measures an action important to your business. It can also be turned into a Google Ads conversion.

### Can I exclude my own visits?
Yes, with an internal traffic rule and a data filter. Test it first, because an active filter permanently changes incoming data.

### How long does GA4 keep my data?
User-level and event data in Explorations can be kept for 2 months or 14 months on a standard property. Standard reports are not affected.
