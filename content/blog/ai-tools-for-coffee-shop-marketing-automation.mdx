## Which Coffee Shop Tasks Can AI Handle?

Drafting is the safe use: first versions of email copy, social captions, menu descriptions and replies to reviews. A person should check facts such as prices, hours and allergens before anything is published. AI cannot know that you sold out of oat milk today.

Scheduling and triggers are the other use. A welcome email that sends on sign-up is automation, not AI, and it is the single task with the clearest benchmark. Our [coffee shop marketing automation guide](/blog/coffee-shop-marketing-automation) covers the wider setup.

## What Can You Automate Without Paying?

Mailchimp's free plan covers 250 contacts and up to 500 sends a month or 250 a day, and its marketing automation flows are not included. Klaviyo's free plan covers up to 250 active profiles and 500 emails a month, with email support for 60 days.

Square's pricing page lists unlimited email marketing under its Plus tier, with the first 500 texts free and then 3 cents each, though part of that page did not render when checked.

The limits are tight. A list of 300 people emailed weekly sends over 1,000 messages a month. Plan for a paid tier early.

## Can AI Reply to Google Reviews?

It can draft, but you post. Google requires a verified business to reply, says replies are checked against content policies, and notes that most checks finish within about 10 minutes but some take up to 30 days. The reviewer is notified when you reply.

Do not automate a reply to a complaint without reading it. For setup, see our guides on [Google review auto-reply scripts](/blog/ai-agent-google-reviews-auto-reply-script) and [how to get more Google reviews for a coffee shop](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-coffee-shop).

## Is It Safe to Use AI to Create Reviews?

No. The FTC's final rule bans creating or selling fake or false reviews and testimonials, including AI-generated ones, and buying or spreading them when the business knew or should have known they were fake. It also bans paying for reviews that express a set sentiment.

Ask real customers for reviews and share a review link or QR code, which Google's help page supports. The FTC page did not state a dollar penalty, only that it can seek civil penalties against knowing violators.

## How Does Google Ads Use Automation?

Google describes Smart Bidding as setting a separate bid for each search query using signals at the time of the auction, and calls pairing it with broad match "critical". This is automation inside the ad platform, and it needs conversion data to work. Google's conversion tracking page lists website, phone call and offline conversions.

For a small café, that means tracking calls and orders before you hand bidding to the system. Otherwise it optimises for clicks that may never become customers.

## The Math: How Much Time Could Drafting Save?

All inputs are **assumptions** for an illustrative café, not measured results.

| Weekly task | Minutes by hand | Minutes with AI draft and check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social captions (5) | 50 | 25 |
| Review replies (6) | 30 | 15 |
| Email newsletter | 60 | 30 |
| Total | 140 | 70 |

That is 70 minutes saved a week, about 5 hours a month at four weeks. At an assumed $20 an hour the time is worth about $100 a month, so a paid AI subscription should cost well under that to be worth it. Your own timings matter more than these.

## FAQ

### Which AI tool is best for a coffee shop?
This guide does not rank tools. Pricing pages for several were not accessible, so compare current prices yourself and test with one task for two weeks.

### Will AI write my posts for me?
It will draft them. Check prices, hours and claims, and post under your name.

### Is automation worth it for a small shop?
It is worth it for repeat tasks like a welcome email. Klaviyo's data shows flows outperform campaigns on click rate.

### Are AI-generated reviews allowed?
No. The FTC's rule bans fake reviews, including AI-generated ones.
