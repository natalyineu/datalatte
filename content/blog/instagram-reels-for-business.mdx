For a local business, Reels work best as short, original videos that show real work or real products, filmed on the phone and posted without watermarks. Instagram's own ranking page says Reels are mostly shown to people who do not follow you, ranked by how likely they are to watch to the end, reshare, like or visit the audio page. Reach is earned per video, so the plan is a steady rhythm you can sustain, measured by reach and shares.

This guide uses Instagram and Meta documentation only. Engagement benchmarks are left out because the public ones come from vendor samples of unknown size. Examples labelled as assumptions are illustrations.

## How Does Instagram Decide Who Sees a Reel?

Instagram's [ranking explainer](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained) says Reels are meant for discovering new things, with an emphasis on entertainment, and that most come from accounts people do not follow. Instagram first picks Reels it thinks a person may like, then orders them by how interesting they seem, partly from surveys asking whether a Reel was worth their time.

The page names four signals, roughly in order of importance: the viewer's own activity, their history with the poster, information about the Reel (audio, visuals, popularity) and information about the poster. The predictions it calls most important are reshare, watching all the way through, liking and visiting the audio page.

## What Does Instagram Say It Shows Less?

The same page says Instagram aims to make some Reels less visible: low-resolution or watermarked videos, muted videos or those with borders, mostly-text Reels, and Reels already posted on Instagram. It also says it is testing notices that tell creators when a watermark may limit reach.

For a business this has practical consequences. Film natively on the phone instead of reposting a TikTok with its logo. Keep sound on and the frame full. Post original footage of your own place, product or process, and avoid recycling the same clip across accounts.

## What Should a Local Business Film?

Pick formats that show the work, because completion and shares are the signals that count. Short clips of a real process hold attention: a before and after, a product being made, a day-in-the-shop sequence or a customer-question answer. Our guides to [coffee shop Reels ideas](/blog/coffee-shop-instagram-reels-ideas-that-get-views) and [salon Reels and TikTok](/blog/video-marketing-for-salons-reels-tiktok-guide) give niche examples.

Put the hook in the first seconds. Meta's developer documentation defines skip rate as the percentage of views from people who skipped during the first 3 seconds, so the opening frame is the one that matters most. End with a clear next step, such as booking, a map pin or an order link.

## Which Numbers Should You Track?

Meta's [Instagram media insights documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/reference/instagram-media/insights/) defines the metrics you will see.

| Metric | Meta's definition | What to do with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reach | Unique users who saw the Reel at least once (estimated) | Compare Reels against each other |
| Average watch time | Average time spent playing the Reel | Compare with the Reel length |
| Skip rate | Share of views skipped in the first 3 seconds | Rework the opening |
| Shares | Number of shares | Strongest sign people value it |
| Saves | Times the media was saved | Useful content signal |

Instagram publishes no benchmark for these, so compare each Reel with your last ten. Check whether reach turns into profile visits, link taps and bookings in your Business Profile and booking tool, as our [analytics guide](/blog/data-analytics-for-small-business) describes.

## How Often Should You Post?

Instagram does not state a recommended posting frequency, so treat any "post daily" rule as someone's opinion. Choose a rhythm you can keep for three months, such as one or two Reels a week, and review it after twelve posts. Consistent small batches beat a burst of ten followed by silence.

Batch filming saves time. Film four clips in one session while the shop is quiet, then edit and schedule them across the following weeks. If a Reel does well, make a follow-up on the same theme rather than a copy of it.

## FAQ

### Do Reels get more reach than normal posts?

Instagram says most Reels are shown to people who do not follow the poster, which gives them the chance to reach new audiences. It publishes no comparison of reach against photos.

### Should I repost my TikToks to Reels?

Instagram's ranking page lists watermarked videos and reposts among content it aims to show less. Export a clean copy and post original footage when you can.

### How long should a Reel be?

Instagram's ranking page does not set a length. Make it as long as the idea needs, then check average watch time against the video length.
