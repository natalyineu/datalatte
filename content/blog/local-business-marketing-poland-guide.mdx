Then add Meta, where DataReportal estimates Facebook ads reach 18.7 million people and Instagram ads 12.4 million. Poland's click prices are not published in a source this guide could verify.

This guide is for English-speaking owners and managers in Polish cities. It gives the market numbers, the channel order and a budget example. Where a figure could not be verified, it says so, and the worked example labels every input as an assumption.

## How Big Is the Polish Market Online?

DataReportal's Digital 2026 report for Poland, using data mostly from late 2025, counts 38.0 million people and 34.1 million internet users, a penetration of 89.8%. It lists 27.1 million social media user identities and mobile connections equal to 142% of the population.

Ad reach figures are Meta and TikTok estimates of audiences advertisers can reach, not monthly users. Facebook ad reach fell 3.6% year on year to 18.7 million, while Instagram rose 6.9% to 12.4 million. TikTok reaches 12.4 million adults, up 11.5%. So Instagram and TikTok are growing, while Facebook is large but flat.

## How Many Businesses Will You Compete With?

Statistics Poland (GUS) counted 2,896,481 active enterprises in the second quarter of 2026, up 1.6% on a year earlier. Micro enterprises with up to nine people employed were 96.0% of the total, and their number rose 1.8%. Trade and repair of motor vehicles was the largest section at 16.5%.

For a local café, salon or studio this means most rivals are tiny owner-run firms. Many will have a thin Google profile. Complete information and regular reviews can stand out quickly.

## Which Channel Should You Start With?

Match the channel to how customers decide. Search captures people who already want the service. Social reaches people who do not know you yet.

## What Should Your Google Business Profile Include?

Google says local results are based on relevance, distance and prominence, and that prominence is influenced by links and reviews. It also says there is no way to pay for a better local ranking.

Fill in the profile in Polish, add English if you serve visitors, and keep the name, address and phone number identical everywhere. List services, hours and a booking link, and add real photos. Ask each customer for a review and reply to all of them. See our [Europe GBP guide](/blog/google-business-profile-optimisation-europe) and [online reviews strategy for Europe](/blog/online-reviews-strategy-europe).

## How Do You Run Google Ads in Poland?

Use radius or district targeting. Google lets you target a radius of at least 1 km around a location, and recommends broad geo targeting (Presence or interest) on Search, with Presence fitting when you want only people physically there. Write ads in Polish unless you serve tourists, and add negative keywords for jobs, courses and free offers. Negatives do not match close variants, so add plurals yourself.

No Polish click price from an official source was found. Check Keyword Planner for your own keywords before you set a budget. If you email customers, check consent rules first.

## FAQ

### Is Google or Facebook better for a local business in Poland?

Start with Google. It handles 89.56% of search, and searchers are ready to act. Add Meta ads to reach people who are not searching yet.

### Should ads be in Polish or English?

Use Polish for local customers. Add English only if you serve tourists or expats, and compare results between the two.

### How much do Google Ads cost in Poland?

No official figure was found. Use Keyword Planner for your own keywords.

### How many businesses are there in Poland?

GUS counted 2,896,481 active enterprises in Q2 2026, and 96.0% were micro firms.

### Is TikTok worth trying in Poland?

TikTok reaches 12.4 million adults and is growing 11.5% a year. Test short video once your profile and offer are ready.
