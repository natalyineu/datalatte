## How Does Google Pick Pet Groomers for the Map Pack?

The top three businesses in the map pack are pulled directly from their Google Business Profile listings, according to [Adwave's local SEO guide](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-my-lawn-care-business) (secondary). That makes the profile the main place where map pack work happens.

Adwave also states that the primary category is the number one ranking factor for getting into the Local Pack, citing analysis of millions of profiles without naming the source ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones), secondary). A 2025 benchmark cited by [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/multi-location-local-seo-audit) puts GBP signals at 32% of local pack ranking weight (secondary).

A BrightLocal survey cited by [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/seo/local-seo) found that 36% of SEOs rated the Google Business Profile as the most important map pack factor in 2021, up from 23% in 2013. Treat these percentages as directional rather than exact weights, since they come from vendor and survey estimates.

## Which Google Business Profile Fields Should Pet Groomers Fill In First?

Start with the fields that affect matching and relevance. Google describes its [Business Profile as free to set up](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/), so the cost of getting these right is time, not fees.

- Primary category: search your main service in Pittsburgh and look at the top three to five businesses in the map results to see which category they use ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones), secondary).
- Hours, phone number and address: Adwave says these must be 100% accurate ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary).
- Description: the profile allows 750 characters, so lead with the grooming service, the Pittsburgh neighbourhood you serve and one clear differentiator ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).
- Photos: one Adwave article recommends at least 15 high-quality photos, and another suggests at least 10. Aim for 15.
- Mobile or home-based groomers: if you work from home or travel to clients, you can hide your street address and be designated a Service Area Business (same Adwave guide, secondary).

For a field-by-field walkthrough, see [Google Business Profile optimization for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-for-pet-groomers).

## How Often Should Pet Groomers Post and Update Their Profile?

Google shows a Business Profile post publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts do not appear ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)). Build posting time into your week, and check that posts have gone live.

Adwave advises posting at least one new Google Post a week to keep the profile fresh ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary). It describes posts as free mini-billboards that appear on the profile in Search and Maps.

Google launched Q&A in Maps in 2017 ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-q-and-a), secondary). Seed the Q&A with questions clients actually ask, such as drop-off times and whether you handle anxious dogs. Google's AI-driven answers draw on business profile data, website content and reviews, according to an Accrisoft summary cited by Adwave (secondary). Keep your website and profile saying the same things.

## Do Reviews Change Where a Pet Groomer Ranks?

Reviews matter for visibility in their own right. Google states that reviews show next to a business's profile in Maps and Search ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)). Reviews also feed relevance: the text customers write tells people and search systems what you do.

A Blogging Wizard analysis of 2 million profile pages found that businesses with over 200 Google reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business), secondary). That is a correlation, so it shows where successful businesses tend to sit rather than proving cause.

Ask every client for a review after the visit, and invite them to mention the service they booked. Reply to each review with your service and city in plain language. For how AI answers use this information, see [AI for Google Business Profile optimization](/blog/ai-for-google-business-profile-optimization).

## Why Must Name, Address and Phone Match Across Directories?

Consistency is the basic requirement. [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/voice-search-optimization-for-local-businesses) (secondary) states that the name, address and phone number must be exactly the same on your website, your Google Business Profile and major directories such as Yelp, Apple Maps and Bing Places.

Small formatting differences count. Adwave notes that the listing must match right down to punctuation and whether the address says "St." or "Street" (same [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack) guide, secondary). Pick one format and use it everywhere.

Apple is also worth checking. Adwave reports that Apple Business Connect provides Profile Views, which count how many people saw your business card in Apple Maps ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/apple-business-connect-the-google-business-profile-alternative-youre-ignoring), secondary). The same page puts Google Maps at 88% of map users and Apple Maps at 12%, but names no source, so treat that split as unverified. Your website's location page can also carry a [Google Maps embed](/blog/google-maps-embed-local-business-website) to reinforce the address.

## What Could a Map Pack Listing Be Worth? A Worked Example

The figures below are illustrative. Every input is an assumption to replace with your own numbers.

- Assumption: 30 extra calls or direction requests per month from map pack searches.
- Assumption: 40% of those contacts book an appointment.
- Assumption: $80 average grooming ticket.
- Assumption: 3 hours per month on profile upkeep, valued at $30 per hour.

Bookings: 30 × 40% = 12 bookings per month. Revenue: 12 × $80 = $960. Upkeep cost: 3 × $30 = $90. Net monthly value: $960 − $90 = $870.

## Should Pet Groomers Also Run Google Ads?

Local SEO and paid search draw from the same listing data. Google states that location asset data in Google Ads is sourced directly from Google Maps and Google Business Profile, and that successful corrections made there flow into linked Google Ads accounts ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)).

If Business Profile and chain store options do not work for linking, you can select up to 10 locations from Google Maps, but they must be yours or they may be disapproved ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)). Paid channels are a separate budget decision. A 2023 Marketing 360 case study describes a mobile pet groomer that chose Facebook and Google to advertise (vendor case study, secondary). For a channel comparison, read [Google Ads vs Facebook Ads for local businesses](/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-local-business-which-wins).

## How Do You Track Whether Local SEO Is Working?

Adwave lists six local search metrics it considers useful: Google Business Profile impressions, map pack views, clicks to call, direction requests, website visits from the profile, and branded search lift over time ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-search-intent), secondary).

- Record these numbers monthly from your profile dashboard before you change anything.
- Check your main searches in a private browser window each week and note where you appear.
- Compare calls and direction requests to the worked example above to see whether your inputs hold.

## FAQ

### Does a Google Business Profile cost anything?

Google describes its Business Profile as a free tool for managing your presence on Search and Maps ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)).

### How many photos should a pet groomer upload?

Adwave recommends at least 15 high-quality photos in one article and at least 10 in another. Starting with 15 gives you the higher bar.

### Can a home-based groomer hide the street address?

Yes. Adwave says you can hide your physical street address and be designated a Service Area Business ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).

### How soon will map rankings change?

Adwave's estimate is that a business may begin ranking for its own name a few weeks after verification (vendor estimate). Check your platform's live help pages for current guidance on timing.
