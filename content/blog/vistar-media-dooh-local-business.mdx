Vistar Media is a programmatic out-of-home platform that lets advertisers buy digital screens, such as billboards and venue displays, by audience and location instead of booking one billboard. Its published terms offer two ways in: self-service, with no minimum budget stated, and co-managed service, where media cost must exceed $10,000 per month. Vistar publishes no CPM figures.

This guide explains what that means for a local business. Facts come from Vistar's own pages and a deal announcement checked on 1 November 2026. Where a number is an example, it is labelled as an assumption.

## What Is Vistar Media?

Vistar describes itself as a programmatic OOH platform with a "DSP, SSP & Ad Server" login on its site, plus traditional OOH planning tools and enterprise tools for media owners. Its homepage says it facilitates connections between advertisers and screens "hundreds of thousands of times a day" but gives no screen count.

A January 2025 announcement says T-Mobile agreed to acquire Vistar for about $600 million in cash, and described a network of more than 1.1 million screens from around 370 media owners worldwide, with closing expected in the first quarter of 2025. Check Vistar's site for any change in ownership or terms since.

## How Does Programmatic DOOH Differ From a Billboard Contract?

With a traditional contract you choose a location and a flight, and pay for that placement. With programmatic you set audience, location and schedule, and the platform serves your ad on qualifying screens.

| | Traditional billboard | Programmatic DOOH on a platform like Vistar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you buy | A specific screen or face for a set period | Impressions on qualifying screens in a target area |
| Targeting | Location only | Location, time and other signals the platform offers |
| Pricing basis | Quoted by the media owner | Dynamic CPM, per Vistar's terms |
| Delivery | Fixed for the booking | Not guaranteed for self-service or co-managed campaigns |

## What Does Vistar Charge?

Vistar's services overview gives the structure but not the prices. For self-service, you are solely responsible for planning, trafficking, execution and reporting. Impression delivery and CPM are non-guaranteed, and you are invoiced monthly at a dynamic CPM inclusive of media cost, targeting, measurement and platform fees. No minimum budget is stated.

For co-managed service, Vistar adds inventory pulls, setup, optimisation and creative upload for an additional fee set in an order form, and media total cost must exceed $10,000 per month. Co-managed fees are not published.

Third-party pages quote CPM ranges for programmatic DOOH, but the one checked returned an error, so this guide gives none. Ask Vistar or an agency for a current CPM for your market.

## Is Vistar Right for a Single-Location Business?

It depends on your capacity and budget. Self-service suits a business that can plan, upload creative and read reports itself. The co-managed floor of $10,000 a month is out of range for most single locations.

Weigh it against other options such as search ads, which capture people already looking, and local screens at venues you can negotiate directly. ## How Should You Plan a Small Test?

Run one campaign, one geography and one creative for a fixed period, and decide the question first: footfall, visits, or promo code uses. DOOH does not carry a click, so use a unique offer, a QR code with a short URL or a branded search lift. No source verified for this article gives a typical lift, so judge by your own result.

## Worked Example: Cost Per Redemption

All inputs are **assumptions**. The CPM is invented for arithmetic; Vistar does not publish one.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Budget | $1,500 |
| Dynamic CPM, all fees included | $12 |
| Impressions | $1,500 / $12 x 1,000 = 125,000 |
| Redemptions of a screen-only offer | 60 |
| Cost per redemption | $25 |
| Average sale | $18 |

At $25 per redemption against an $18 average sale, the campaign loses money on the first visit. It works only if repeat visits or higher baskets close the gap. Test with the lowest budget your platform allows and use your own CPM quote.

## FAQ

### Is there a minimum spend on Vistar?

For self-service, none is stated on Vistar's terms page. For co-managed service, media total cost must exceed $10,000 per month.

### What does Vistar cost per thousand impressions?

Vistar does not publish a CPM. Billing is at a dynamic CPM.

### Can I choose a specific billboard?

The platform buys by audience and area, not by one screen, and delivery is not guaranteed.

### Who owns Vistar?

A January 2025 announcement says T-Mobile agreed to acquire it for about $600 million. Confirm current ownership with Vistar.

### How do I measure results?

Use a unique code, URL or offer.
