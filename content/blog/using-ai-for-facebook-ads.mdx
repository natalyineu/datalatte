On Facebook and Instagram, "AI" mostly means Advantage+: Meta's system chooses who sees your ad, where it shows and how the budget is spread. Per Meta's developer documentation, a campaign counts as Advantage+ only when budget, audience and placement automation are all enabled. For a local business, the practical move is to let Meta widen the audience, but keep hard limits on location and age, and put your effort into the ad creative and the offer.

## What Does Advantage+ Actually Automate?

Meta's developer documentation says Advantage+ sales, app and leads campaigns are updated versions of the older Advantage+ shopping and app campaigns. A campaign needs budget, audience and placement automation all set to enabled, or it does not count as Advantage+. Budget is set at campaign level, and placements are not chosen by hand.

In plain terms, you give Meta the objective, a budget, your creative and your location. The system decides who sees what and where. You lose some control and gain simpler setup. For a shop with a small budget, simpler setup often matters more than fine targeting.

## How Does Advantage+ Audience Treat Your Targeting?

Meta's documentation recommends opting in to Advantage+ audience. Age and gender can be set as suggestions, which the system may go beyond, instead of hard limits. Individual targeting options are allowed with relaxation.

Two cautions for local businesses. First, keep location strict, because a café 3 miles away cannot serve a customer 40 miles away. Second, for lists of existing customers, Meta's docs say to make sure the setting is not a suggestion or relaxed, using the relaxation value 0 for custom audiences. ## Can AI Write the Ads for You?

AI tools can draft text variations, but you must check each one. Meta's generative features are described in third-party coverage, which disagrees on counts, and Meta's own pages could not be read, so no feature list is given here.

A safe workflow: write two or three headlines yourself, ask a chatbot for variants, delete anything with a claim you cannot prove, and keep the offer and price accurate. A made-up discount or an outdated opening hour costs more than a weak headline. Our [video ads guide](/blog/facebook-video-ads-guide) shows what to test.

## The Math: What Does $600 Buy in Leads?

All inputs below are **assumptions** built on the all-industry benchmark.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $600 |
| Cost per lead (benchmark) | $27.39 |
| Leads | 600 / 27.39 = about 22 |
| Share of leads who book (assumption) | 30% |
| Bookings | about 6.6 |
| Cost per booking | about $91 |
| Average first-visit value (assumption) | $80 |

Under these inputs the first visit does not cover the ad cost, so the campaign pays only if clients return. With the beauty benchmark of $50.91 per lead, the same budget gives about 12 leads. Know your repeat rate before you scale. Change one thing at a time so you can tell what worked.

## FAQ

### Does AI make Facebook ads cheaper?
No neutral source publishes a savings figure. Benchmarks vary by industry, from $27.11 to $50.91 per lead in LocaliQ's 2026 data for fitness and beauty.

### What is Advantage+?
Meta's automation for budget, audience and placements. All three must be enabled for a campaign to count as Advantage+, per Meta's developer documentation.

### Should a local business use Advantage+ audience?
Usually yes for broad offers, with strict location limits. Use custom audiences carefully, and check the relaxation setting.

### Can I let AI write all my ad copy?
Draft with it, but review every claim, price and hour before publishing.

### How much should I spend?
There is no standard figure. See our [budget guide](/blog/facebook-ads-budget-local-business-how-much-to-spend) for a way to decide from your cost per booking.
