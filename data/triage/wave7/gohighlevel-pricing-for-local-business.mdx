## How Much Does GoHighLevel Cost Each Month?

Two monthly prices are on record for GoHighLevel's paid plans. Unlimited costs $297 a month, and the review notes that it removes the sub-account limit, which suits people who manage multiple studios or coach other instructors. Founder costs $497 a month and adds exclusive rebilling features and partner network access, according to the [SMMcalc class booking review](https://smmcalc.com/blog/best-class-booking-system/) (secondary).

These are plan fees only. The review's examples are about class businesses, but the same automation features apply to any appointment-based shop. It states that workflows can send SMS reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before a class starts. That describes what the platform can do, not a measured result for any business. Before you budget, confirm the entry-level tier and annual rates on the vendor's current pricing page.

## What Does the Founder Plan Add for Resellers?

Founder's extra features are rebilling and partner network access. Rebilling only matters if you charge other businesses for the platform or for usage that runs through it. A salon owner paying for their own reminders and review requests has no client to rebill.

The review does not describe SaaS mode in detail, so it is not clear from that source whether SaaS mode is tied to Founder or included elsewhere. Check the live pricing page and ask the vendor directly before you assume either way. If you do not plan to resell, the answer rarely changes your decision.

## Which GoHighLevel Plan Should a Local Shop Choose?

Work through these steps before you sign an annual contract:
