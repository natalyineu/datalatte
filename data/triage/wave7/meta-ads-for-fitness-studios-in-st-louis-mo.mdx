## How Much Should a St. Louis Fitness Studio Spend on Meta Ads Each Month?

Budget guidance varies by vendor and business type. For a fitness or wellness business, [Adwave's connected TV versus social comparison](https://adwave.com/resources/connected-tv-vs-social-media-ads-comparison) recommends 45-55% of budget on Meta, 30-40% on connected TV, and the balance on local platforms. Those shares only mean something once you know your total paid budget.

For a standalone Meta buy, [Adwave's channel guide](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-choose-advertising-channels) puts the typical cost at $300 to $1,000 per month. That range is a planning band, so start at the low end and raise spend only after bookings from Meta show up in your tracking.

Many boutique studios budget between 5 and 10 percent of monthly revenue for all marketing, according to [Mariana Tek](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/). Meta spend should sit inside that overall figure, not on top of it.

## Which Meta Ad Formats Work Best for Fitness Studios?

Short-form video is the format to build around. [Adwave, citing Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report](https://adwave.com/resources/electrician-advertising-reaching-homeowners), states that short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram. That is a vendor's summary of Meta research, so treat it as directional rather than a guaranteed result.

Real footage from your own classes, coaches and member moments is the practical version of that advice. [Mariana Tek advises studios](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/) to post short-form video three to five times a week at a steady pace rather than in bursts.

For booking, [Instagram and Facebook both let a business add a Book Now button to its profile](https://blog.appointy.com/2020/12/11/social-media-marketing-for-fitness-studio-gym/). Choose the destination based on the first step you want: a free trial class, an intro offer, or a full membership sign-up. For Reels-specific creative, see [Instagram Reels for fitness studios](/blog/fitness-studio-marketing-with-instagram-reels).

## How Should a Studio Target Its Meta Ads Audience?

Meta's targeting controls let you choose location, interests and custom audiences. Start with a radius that matches how far members will realistically drive to your studio, then test a second radius against cost per booking. Keep the test to one variable at a time so the results are readable.

Meta Ads Manager covers [Facebook, Instagram and Messenger](https://turrboo.com/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media), and [Instagram ads run through the same system as Facebook ads](https://adwave.com/resources/instagram-advertising-for-local-business) because Meta owns both. One campaign can run on either platform or both, which makes placement tests simple to set up.

For a wider look at audience setup across Facebook campaigns, see the [complete Facebook ads guide for gyms and studios](/blog/facebook-ads-for-gyms-fitness-studios-complete-guide).

## How Do You Track Meta Ads Results Before Spending Heavily?

Install the Meta Pixel on your website and confirm it fires on the booking confirmation page and the contact or trial form. Without that event, Ads Manager cannot connect ad spend to real bookings, and you end up optimising for clicks.

Speed matters after the click. [Mariana Tek advises following up within 24 hours](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/) with an automated welcome sequence, since interest fades quickly after a pop-up or trial offer. A setup guide is in [marketing automation for local fitness studios](/blog/marketing-automation-for-local-fitness-studios).

If your team is new to paid social, [Meta's Blueprint courses on Facebook and Instagram advertising are listed as free, with certification on completion](https://sascom247.com/digital-marketing-for-beginners-in-nigeria/) (secondary source). Check Meta's own Blueprint page for current course details.

## How Do Meta Ads Fit Alongside Google Ads in St. Louis?

Meta ads reach people who are scrolling and have not yet started looking for a studio. Search captures people already typing a need. Many studios use Meta to build awareness and retargeting, and search to catch people ready to book this week.

Because Facebook and Instagram share a single system, the Meta side of your plan is easy to manage alongside search. For the search half, see [Google Ads for fitness studios in St. Louis](/blog/google-ads-for-fitness-studios-in-st-louis-mo). Compare the two channels on cost per booking rather than on reach.
