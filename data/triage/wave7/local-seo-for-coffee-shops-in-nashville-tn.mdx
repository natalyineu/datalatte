## Why does the Google Business Profile come first?

Google describes its Business Profile as a free tool for managing your presence across Search and Maps, and its setup page leads with the phrase "Stand out on Google with a free Business Profile" ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/), primary). Free access is the reason the profile is the first step, not a reason to skip it.

Verification is the step most owners leave unfinished. BrewRound's 2020 guide says Google mails a letter with a code, and that the profile does not appear in results until it is verified ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary). Verification methods change, so confirm the current option in Google's [verification help page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7107242?hl=en) before you start.

A survey cited by Ahrefs found 36% of SEOs rated the profile the most important ranking factor for the map pack in 2021, up from 23% in 2013 ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/seo/local-seo), secondary).

## How should a coffee shop choose its category?

Pick "Coffee shop" or a closely related term, not a broad category such as "Restaurant" ([Per Diem](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). BrewRound notes that you can select only one category at creation and add more later ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary, 2020).

Start with the primary category, finish verification, then add secondary terms such as café or espresso bar once the listing is live. The [Google Business Profile tips for coffee shops](/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-tips) cover naming and description details in more depth.

## Which posts and Q&A content keep the profile active?

Google shows a post publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts do not show ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en), primary). After you publish, check the status, because a post stuck in review does nothing for customers.

For posting frequency, The Restaurant HQ recommends coffee shops post on social media at least once per week, ideally one to two times per day ([The Restaurant HQ](https://www.therestauranthq.com/operations/coffee-shop-marketing/), secondary). That guidance is about social media, so use it as a benchmark for the profile, not a proven rule for Google rankings.

Answer common questions yourself in the Q&A section. Per Diem suggests covering free Wi-Fi, dairy-free options and whether dogs are allowed on the patio ([Per Diem](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). Google also lets users message businesses through their listings, so check that messages are answered.

For a full profile walkthrough, see the [coffee shop Google Business Profile optimization guide](/blog/coffee-shop-google-business-profile-optimization).

## How do reviews affect map pack visibility?

Google states that reviews show next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122), primary). A review that names a drink, a seating feature or a service gives customers and search results more context about your shop.

Some agency pages claim that 50 or more reviews and a 4.7+ average rating are needed for top map pack spots. The claim cites no source, so test it against your own local results rather than treating it as a fixed target ([Accountability Now](https://accountabilitynow.net/ai-google-business-profile-ranking-for-landscapers/), secondary).

## Worked example: how many reviews could a request routine add?

The inputs below are assumptions for illustration, not measured results.

- Review requests sent per day by text after a paid order: 4 (assumption)
- Share of requests that produce a review: 25% (assumption)
- Trading weeks in the period: 13 (assumption)

Reviews per day: 4 × 0.25 = 1. Reviews per week: 1 × 7 = 7. Reviews over 13 weeks: 7 × 13 = 91 (assumption-based). Walk-in reviews are excluded, so any real count may be higher. Keep requests steady rather than sending a burst, and follow Google's review policies on how requests can be made.

## Does the website still matter for map rankings?

Yes, because customers check your website after they find you on the map. Add a Google Map embed on your contact or location page so visitors can see the pin next to your address ([Google Maps embed guide](/blog/google-maps-embed-local-business-website)). Keep your name, address and phone number identical to the profile.

Paid search can run alongside the profile. Google says location asset data for Google Ads comes directly from Google Maps and Google Business Profile, and corrections there flow into linked accounts ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en), primary). If you want to test paid terms, see the [best Google Ads keywords for coffee shops](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-coffee-shops-in-2026).

## What is the Nashville profile checklist?

1. Sign in with a Google account. Registration is free ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary).
2. Enter the shop name, address and phone exactly as they appear on your signage and website.
3. Choose "Coffee shop" as the primary category, not "Restaurant".
4. Complete verification using the method Google currently offers.
5. Add secondary categories, such as café or espresso bar, after the listing is live.
6. Answer the Q&A questions customers ask most, including Wi-Fi, dairy-free options and dog policy.
7. Publish a post each week and confirm it shows as Live.

After the checklist is done, add a review request routine and track profile views monthly. [AI tools for profile optimization](/blog/ai-for-google-business-profile-optimization) can help draft posts and answers, but review every output for accuracy before publishing.

## FAQ

### Is a Google Business Profile free for a coffee shop?

Yes. Google describes its Business Profile as free to set up, and BrewRound says creating a listing is free ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/), primary; [BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary).

### Why does a new post not appear publicly?

Google shows posts on Search and Maps only when the status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts stay hidden ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en), primary).

### Should a coffee shop use one category or several?

Start with one primary category at creation. BrewRound says more categories can be added later ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary).

### What share of Google searches are local?

BrewRound states 46% of Google searches are for local businesses, but the figure has no cited source and dates from 2020, so use it only as a rough indicator ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary).
