For most local businesses, connected TV (CTV) is the better place to start, because it can be aimed at a few zip codes, sized to a small test budget and measured more closely than a broadcast spot. Traditional TV still makes sense when a business needs broad reach across a whole market and can absorb production and airtime costs. Forecasts point the same way: eMarketer projects CTV ad spending to pass traditional TV around 2028. Get written quotes from both before you decide, because rates vary by market, platform and season.

The main differences come down to targeting, cost structure and measurement. This guide compares them honestly, including where traditional TV still has an edge, and shows how a local business can test CTV without a large commitment.

## Is Connected TV Overtaking Traditional TV?

eMarketer projects that connected TV ad spending will surpass all traditional TV ad spending around 2028, at roughly $47 billion against $45 billion ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/ctv-advertising-market-size-q2-2026), secondary). Advertise In Tampa's CTV page cites the same projection ([Advertise In Tampa](https://advertiseintampa.com/services/advertising/ctv-ott/), secondary). Treat it as a forecast, not a result.

The shift is also visible in spending that has already happened. OTT and CTV ad revenue grew from 5.9% of traditional TV ad spend in 2020 to roughly 21.5% by 2024 ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/what-is-ott-advertising), secondary). Budgets are moving toward streaming, so the question for a local business is whether a test makes sense now, not whether the channel exists.

## How Does CTV Targeting Differ From Traditional TV?

Amazon describes OTT as the way content is delivered over the internet without traditional satellite or cable providers, while CTV is the device used to watch it ([Amazon Ads](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/connected-tv-advertising), primary). For advertisers, the practical point is that the ad runs inside streaming content on the TV screen.

Targeting is where the two channels differ most. Adwave says CTV offers digital-like targeting by demographics, interests and behaviors ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/what-is-linear-tv-advertising), secondary). StackAdapt reports that 84% of advertisers believe CTV delivers better targeting capabilities than linear TV ([StackAdapt](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/connected-tv-stats), secondary).

Traditional TV ads run in scheduled commercial breaks, so the audience is set by the program and the time slot ([Priority Media Agency](https://prioritymediaagency.co.uk/services/tv-tvod-streaming), secondary). VOD ads appear on streaming services, catch-up TV and connected devices instead.

## What Can You Measure on CTV Compared With Traditional TV?

Measurement is the main reason many buyers still hold back. Teads notes that CTV measurement is still catching up, and that 53% of marketing decision-makers would increase CTV spend with better ROI measurement ([Teads](https://www.teads.com/blog/what-is-connected-tv-advertising/9858/), secondary).

Adwave states that CTV offers better measurement than linear TV ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/what-is-linear-tv-advertising), secondary). Check your platform's live help page for the exact reports it provides, such as delivery, completion and site-visit data.

Because people rarely click a TV ad, judge the channel by indirect signals. Use a unique URL, a promo code or a dedicated phone number, then compare branded searches and direct visits before and during the flight. Traditional spots rarely offer that link, so the comparison is harder to make.

## How Do the Costs Compare?

StackAdapt says CTV gives small businesses access to premium content at a lower cost and with less minimum ad spend than linear TV ([StackAdapt](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/integrate-connected-tv), secondary). That is a vendor claim, so confirm it with written quotes for your own zip codes.

Traditional TV is typically built from 15, 30 or 60-second video ads ([StackAdapt](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/connected-tv-marketing-strategies), secondary). Filmkraft's guide quotes Chris Chen of Keynes Digital describing a CTV model that eschews traditional agency fees ([Filmkraft](https://www.filmkraft.tv/guides/pioneering-connected-tv-advertising), secondary). Agency structure varies, so ask every vendor for a full fee breakdown.

## What Does a CTV Test Cost in Numbers?

The table below is an original worked example. Every input is an **assumption**, not a market price: a $2,000 budget, a $25 CPM, a target of 4 impressions per household, a $40 average ticket and a $500 price for one traditional 30-second spot.

| Step | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| CTV impressions | $2,000 / $25 x 1,000 | 80,000 |
| Households reached | 80,000 / 4 | 20,000 |
| Customers needed to cover the spend | $2,000 / $40 | 50 |
| Share of reached households that must buy | 50 / 20,000 | 0.25% |
| Traditional spots for the same money | $2,000 / $500 | 4 spots |

Under these assumptions, the CTV test needs one in 400 reached households to buy. Four traditional spots give you no household-level data to check that break-even point. Replace the CPM and spot price with your own quotes before you rely on the result.

## How Do CTV and Traditional TV Compare Side by Side?

| Feature | Connected TV (CTV) | Traditional TV |
|---|---|---|
| Targeting | Demographics, interests and behaviors on many platforms | Mostly by market, channel and program |
| Ad length | Commonly 15, 30 or 60 seconds | Commonly 15, 30 or 60 seconds |
| Placement | Streaming services, catch-up TV and connected devices | Scheduled commercial breaks |
| Minimum commitment | Often lower, per vendor claims | Often higher once production and airtime are included |
| Measurement | Platform reporting and optional site-visit tracking | Estimated audience measurement, harder to link to sales |
| Best fit | Trade-area awareness and testing | Broad market reach and community presence |

Traditional TV is not a poor choice. Priority Media Agency describes its ads as reaching millions in real time during scheduled breaks ([Priority Media Agency](https://prioritymediaagency.co.uk/services/tv-tvod-streaming), secondary). The honest question is whether you need broad reach or precise local reach.

## Is CTV Right for Your Local Business?

CTV suits businesses whose customers live within a short drive, such as salons, coffee shops, fitness studios and pet groomers. Zip-code targeting lets you pay for your trade area rather than an entire metro.

CTV works best as awareness that search later captures. Pair it with [Bing Ads for local businesses](/blog/bing-ads-microsoft-advertising-local-business) or Google search so people who saw the ad can find you when they look you up. ## How Do You Run a Small CTV Test?

1. Set one goal, usually more branded searches or walk-ins from your trade area.
2. Ask each platform for a delivery forecast and a written CPM quote for your zip codes.
3. Start with location targeting and add no more than one or two audience filters.
4. Write two or three short ads that read without sound and end with a clear call to action. For drafting variants, see [ChatGPT prompts for local business marketing](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-local-business-marketing).
5. Track with a unique URL, promo code or phone number, and review delivery weekly.
6. Give the test a fixed window, such as 60 to 90 days, before you judge it. This is a planning window, not a benchmark.

## What Mistakes Should You Avoid?

- Buying CTV like a cable buy, with a large budget and no early review.
- Skipping a frequency cap, so the same household sees the ad too often.
- Using generic stock footage instead of your real business and neighborhood.
- Judging the channel after two weeks, before the test has had time to build awareness.

## FAQ

### Is CTV cheaper than traditional TV for a local business?

Often the entry cost is lower, because a campaign can be sized to a few zip codes, and StackAdapt describes lower cost and minimum spend as an advantage. Treat that as a vendor claim. Compare written quotes for your own market.

### Can you track sales from CTV ads?

Usually you track indirect signals. Platform reporting covers delivery and completion, while promo codes, dedicated phone numbers, branded searches and "how did you hear about us?" answers show whether people responded. Direct clicks are uncommon.

### Does a small business need professional video?

Not always. Clear, well-lit footage with readable on-screen text can work. Your real business and customers, shown with permission, often matter more than expensive production.

### How long should a CTV test run?

Plan for 60 to 90 days so there is enough exposure to judge the results. Shorter tests rarely show whether awareness is turning into searches or visits.
