## What monthly budget should a Columbus fitness studio set?

Budget benchmarks depend on membership size. [Vertical Impression](https://www.verticalimpression.com/guides/how-to-market-health-wellness-business-2026) lists $600–$1,200 per month for Google Ads at a small independent studio with under 200 members, and $1,500–$2,500 for a mid-size gym with 200–600 members. The same guide projects that a well-optimized $1,000–$2,000 monthly campaign can generate 300–600 clicks, with 3–6% converting to trial sign-ups.

[Mariana Tek](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/) notes that many boutique studios budget between 5 and 10 percent of monthly revenue for marketing overall. Ads sit inside that total rather than on top of it. Start at the low end of your tier, and scale only after trial sign-ups are tracked. The [fitness studio Google Ads guide](/blog/fitness-studio-google-ads-guide) covers account setup in more detail.

## Which keywords should Columbus studios bid on first?

Start with searches that signal intent to attend: near-me service terms, city-plus-service phrases, and class-format searches. Google Maps matters here too. The [Association of Fitness Studios](https://member.afsfitness.com/content/how-make-your-fitness-business-stand-out-google) describes the Google Map Pack as listing relevant local businesses for queries such as "personal training studios near me", so local phrasing drives both paid and organic visibility.

| Search type | Example | Starting match type | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Near-me service | gym near me | Exact | Start here |
| City plus service | fitness studio Columbus | Exact | Start here |
| Class format | yoga classes near me | Phrase | Test second |
| Trainer | personal trainer Columbus | Phrase | Test third |

Neighborhood names such as Short North or German Village are reasonable modifiers to test in phrase match. Local search volume and cost per click shift by season and area, so check Keyword Planner in your own account before setting bids. Keep broad match off until conversion data exists.

## How does Google Business Profile affect ad results?

The Association of Fitness Studios describes Google Business Profile as a free tool that shows business hours, phone numbers, addresses, reviews, and photos directly in search results. People who click your ad and people who find your listing organically see the same facts, so mismatched hours or phone numbers waste paid clicks.

[Mariana Tek](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/) states that Google review volume can start influencing visibility within a few weeks, while its payoff table gives a 6–12 month typical timeline for local SEO and Google reviews. Paid results can fill the gap while reviews build. The [Google Business Profile optimization guide](/blog/fitness-studio-google-business-profile-optimization) walks through the profile fields to check before launch.

At these inputs, cost per trial sits above the under-$30 target and cost per member sits above the $150–$200 target. Reaching $30 per trial at the same budget and conversion rate needs about 33 trials, or roughly 740 clicks at a cost per click near $1.35. Trial-to-member conversion matters as much as clicks. At a 40% trial-to-member rate with the other inputs unchanged, cost per member falls to about $139.

## Which negative keywords and tracking steps cut waste?

Add negative keywords for searches that rarely book. These include job searches such as "jobs", "hiring", and "career"; do-it-yourself terms such as "how to" and "certification"; city names outside your service area; and unrelated terms such as "franchise" and "wholesale".

Set up conversion tracking before scaling spend:

1. Phone calls from ads, using a tracked number.
2. Booking or trial form completions on your website.
3. Direction requests from your Google Business Profile.
4. "Book now" or contact page clicks as micro-conversions.

## How fast should studios follow up on ad leads?

[Mariana Tek](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/) advises following up within 24 hours with an automated welcome sequence, because interest fades quickly. The advice is written about pop-up events, but the same timing applies to ad-generated trial sign-ups. Set the sequence to trigger on sign-up rather than relying on staff to send a manual reply.

## FAQ

### How much should a fitness studio in Columbus spend on Google Ads?

Vertical Impression benchmarks $600–$1,200 per month for a small independent studio with under 200 members, and $1,500–$2,500 for a mid-size gym with 200–600 members. Start at the low end of your tier and scale once trial tracking is in place.

### Is a $1,000 monthly budget enough to test?

It can be, if the campaign is tightly targeted. In the worked example above, an assumed $2.50 cost per click gives about 400 clicks and roughly 18 trial sign-ups. Replace the cost per click with the figure from your own Keyword Planner estimates before deciding.

### How long until Google Ads produce results?

Paid clicks can produce trial sign-ups soon after a campaign goes live. Local SEO typically takes 6–12 months to pay off, according to the timeline in Mariana Tek's guide, so run ads and profile work side by side.

### Should Columbus studios target the whole metro area?

No. Radius targeting around your address keeps spend on people who can reasonably reach the studio. Set the radius from your actual member catchment rather than the full metro.
