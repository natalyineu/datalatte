## What is the Google map pack, and why does it matter for a fitness studio?

The Google Maps local pack is the group of top three business listings that appear in local search results. For a query such as "personal training studios near me", Google lists relevant local businesses in the Map Pack section of search results. A prospect who searches on a phone often sees these listings before any studio website.

The Google Business Profile is the free tool that feeds those listings. It displays business hours, phone numbers, addresses, reviews and photos directly in search results. Google states that a Business Profile is free to set up on Search and Maps.

A step-by-step walkthrough of profile setup is available in [the fitness studio Google Business Profile guide](/blog/fitness-studio-google-business-profile-optimization).

## Which Business Profile fields should a San Diego studio fill in first?

Start with the primary category. Adwave advises checking the top three to five businesses in local Maps results for a service search to see which categories they use. The same vendor states that the primary category is the number one ranking factor for the Local Pack, although it does not name the analysis behind that claim.

Next, complete the description. Adwave notes that a Business Profile allows 750 characters. Lead with the service and the neighborhood the studio serves, and write for a reader rather than a search engine.

Hours, phone number and address must be exact. Adwave says they should be 100% accurate. Upload photos as well: Adwave recommends at least 15 high-quality photos, while a different guide suggests at least 10. Studios that work from home or travel to clients can hide the street address and be designated a Service Area Business.

## Is an unclaimed listing a real problem for a studio?

Adwave states that 11.1% of Business Profiles are unclaimed, without naming a source for that figure. An unclaimed listing can show details the owner never entered, and only the verified owner can change hours, photos and posts.

Claiming and verifying the profile is therefore the first task, before any review or citation work. Google displays reviews next to the Business Profile in Maps and Search, so the owner should also confirm that the listing shows the right business name and category.

## How many reviews does a fitness studio need, and how long will it take?

Reviews carry weight in the map pack. A Blogging Wizard analysis of 2 million Business Profile pages, cited by Adwave, found that businesses with over 200 Google reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack. A separate Adwave piece on gyms claims that 100+ reviews with a 4.7+ rating dominate the local map pack, and that a rating below 4.5 meaningfully suppresses conversion. Both are vendor claims rather than controlled studies, so treat them as targets to measure against.

The timeline depends on review pace. The table below uses assumed inputs, not studio data.

Inputs (all assumptions): the studio has 38 Google reviews today, and the target is 200 reviews.

| Pace | New reviews per month (assumption) | Reviews still needed | Months to reach 200 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slow | 5 | 162 | 33 (32.4, rounded up) |
| Steady | 10 | 162 | 17 (16.2, rounded up) |
| Active | 15 | 162 | 11 (10.8, rounded up) |

Formula: months = (200 minus current reviews) divided by new reviews per month. Doubling the pace from 5 to 10 a month cuts the wait from 33 months to 17. A steady request routine matters more than a one-off push. ## Why must name, address and phone match everywhere?

Adwave states that the name, address and phone (NAP) must be exactly the same on the website, the Business Profile, and major directories such as Yelp, Apple Maps and Bing Places. Another Adwave guide adds that the match must be exact down to punctuation, so "St." and "Street" count as different entries.

Check existing listings before adding new ones. Inconsistent details split the studio's history across duplicate entries, which weakens the signals Google uses to match a business to a search. A map embed on the contact page is a minor signal, and the [Google Maps embed guide](/blog/google-maps-embed-local-business-website) explains how to add one.

## Should a studio also claim Apple Business Connect?

Apple Business Connect is the Apple counterpart to the Business Profile. Adwave reports that in a maps-only comparison that excludes web search, Apple Business Connect has captured a 7% share and is growing. The page does not name a source for that share, so treat it as directional.

The tool reports Profile Views, which counts how many people saw the business card in Apple Maps. Apple Maps Actions can also link third-party booking or reservation services to a profile, so customers can act without leaving the app. Adding the same verified name, address and phone used on Google avoids a fresh inconsistency.

## How often should a studio post, answer questions and reply to messages?

Adwave advises at least one new Google Post per week, describing posts as free mini-billboards that appear on the profile in Search and Maps. Google's help page adds an important detail: a post appears publicly only when its status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts do not show.

Q&A is also part of the profile. Google launched the Q&A feature in Maps in 2017. According to an Accrisoft summary cited by Adwave, responses in Google's AI-driven answering draw on business profile data, website content and reviews. Adwave also notes that Business Profile messages can be managed from a desktop dashboard, not only from the Maps mobile app.

## Which metrics show whether local SEO is working?

Adwave lists six local search metrics worth tracking: Business Profile impressions, map-pack views, clicks to call, direction requests, website visits from the profile, and branded search lift over time. Track them monthly and compare the same months year over year to account for seasonal demand.

Set expectations on timing. A vendor estimate says a business may begin ranking for its own name a few weeks after verification. Competitive map pack movement for category searches usually takes longer, so judge progress over a multi-month window.

## FAQ

### How long before a fitness studio sees map pack movement?

A vendor estimate says ranking for the business's own name can start a few weeks after profile verification. Category rankings, such as "yoga studio near me", take longer. The same vendor says organic channels take 6-12 months to gain real traction.

### Does a Google Business Profile cost anything?

No. Google describes the Business Profile as free to set up on Google Search and Maps.

### Can a home-based or mobile studio hide its address?

Yes. A business that works from home or serves customers at their location can hide its street address and is then designated a Service Area Business, according to Adwave.

### Do Google Ads campaigns use the same profile data?

Location asset data in Google Ads comes directly from Google Maps and Google Business Profile, and corrections made there flow into linked accounts. When Business Profile linking does not work, advertisers can select up to 10 Maps locations, provided they belong to the business.
