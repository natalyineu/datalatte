## What Should a Plumber Do in the First 30 Days?

Plumbing is a search-driven trade, so the profile comes first. A [local digital marketing strategy can include Local SEO, Google Business Profile optimization, website SEO, social media marketing, content marketing, online reviews, paid advertising and lead generation](https://creatorspk.com/digital-marketing-for-local-businesses.php) (secondary). A plumber does not need all of these in month one.

The vendor's week-one plan asks for a fully optimized Google Business Profile with a primary category, at least 30 photos and current hours. Then the business needs a website that shows the phone number clearly and loads quickly on a mobile phone. Reviews come next, since requests can start once jobs are finished and the customer is satisfied.

Keep the first month focused on setup. Paid ads should start only after the profile is verified and the phone is answered reliably.

## How Much Should a Plumber Budget per Month?

Vendor guidance gives a range rather than a single number. A plumber should [budget $500-2,000 a month to start with Google Local Service Ads, depending on market size](https://adwave.com/resources/plumbing-advertising-local-lead-generation) (secondary). For Google search ads, the same source advises [expecting to spend $1,000-3,000 a month for meaningful results](https://adwave.com/resources/plumbing-advertising-local-lead-generation) (secondary).

A second vendor example works from revenue. A solo plumber with $500,000 a year in revenue [should plan $25,000-$60,000 in annual ad spend, or $2,000-$5,000 per month](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-for-plumbers) (secondary). That is a planning example, not a rule. Start at the low end, measure cost per booked job for 60 days, then raise spend only if jobs are being booked at an acceptable cost.

## Should a Plumber Use Local Services Ads or Google Search Ads?

Local Services Ads and search ads answer different needs. The vendor's 30-day plan suggests [launching Google LSAs at $30-$50 per day once verified](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-for-plumbers) (secondary). For urgent work, it also suggests [setting up Google Search Ads on emergency keywords with a $20-$40 per day budget](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-for-plumbers) (secondary).

Search ads are the more flexible option for keywords like "burst pipe repair." Local Services Ads suit a plumber who wants leads without managing keywords. Many plumbers test both for 30 days and keep the one with the lower cost per booked job.

For homeowners searching for help, the [AI agent for lead generation](/blog/ai-agent-for-lead-generation-local-business) approach can sort inbound enquiries, but it does not replace a fast phone answer.

## What Does a Plumbing Lead Cost, and What Does It Return?

The vendor puts [LSA cost per lead at $25-$100, with 35-60% lead-to-booked-job rates for well-run plumbers](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-for-plumbers) (secondary). Those are ranges, so run your own numbers. The example below uses assumptions inside those ranges.

Worked example. Every input is an assumption unless noted:

1. Monthly budget: $1,500 (assumption, inside the $500-2,000 starting range).
2. Cost per lead: $50 (assumption, inside the $25-$100 range).
3. Leads per month: 30 (budget divided by cost per lead).
4. Lead-to-booked-job rate: 35% (assumption, low end of the 35-60% range), giving 10.5 jobs.
5. Average job value: $400 (assumption), giving about $4,200 in revenue.
6. Cost per booked job: about $143.

If the rate falls to 20% (assumption), the same spend books 6 jobs at $250 each. The gap is driven by how calls are answered, not by ad setup. ## How Should the 90 Days Be Sequenced?

The plan works in four stages. Each stage has a clear check before moving on.

1. Month 1: audit current marketing and fix the profile. Verify the Business Profile, add photos and confirm hours. The vendor's framing for a 90-day test starts with [auditing current marketing in Month 1](https://adwave.com/resources/brand-awareness-vs-lead-generation) (secondary).
2. Once verified, launch LSAs and emergency-keyword search ads at the daily budgets above. Review cost per lead weekly.
3. Month 2: add a brand awareness element. Social advertising for plumbers is [a 60-90 day play before results such as phone calls and brand-search increases appear](https://adwave.com/resources/plumbing-advertising-local-lead-generation) (secondary), so judge it only after day 60.
4. Month 3: add a direct response element and compare calls, booked jobs and cost per booked job against the Month 1 baseline.

Seasonal demand changes the timing. A [holiday marketing strategy for local business](/blog/holiday-marketing-strategy-local-business) can help plan promotions around peak months for heating or drain work.

The answer depends on how much time the owner can spend on the account. Running ads, reviewing call logs and replying to reviews takes several hours a week. If that time is not available, a [comparison of AI and human marketing for local businesses](/blog/ai-vs-human-marketing-local-business-honest-comparison) lays out the trade-offs.

That result is not specific to plumbers, so treat it as a sign that consistent content work can help, not as a forecast.

## FAQ

### How much should a plumber spend on marketing?
Vendor guidance suggests $500-2,000 a month to start with Local Services Ads, and $1,000-3,000 a month for search ads to get meaningful results. Start low and judge spend by cost per booked job.

### Are Local Services Ads worth it for plumbers?
They can be, because cost per lead runs $25-$100 according to one vendor, and well-run plumbers report 35-60% of leads becoming booked jobs. Your own rate will vary, so track it from the first month.

### How long until plumbing marketing shows results?
Ads can produce calls quickly once live. Social advertising for plumbers is described as a 60-90 day play. Check your platform's live help pages for current timing rules.

### Do I need a website if I have a Google Business Profile?
Yes. A [local digital marketing strategy](https://creatorspk.com/digital-marketing-for-local-businesses.php) (secondary) lists website SEO alongside profile optimization, and the website gives customers a place to confirm your work before they call.

### Should a plumber track calls or booked jobs?
Both, but booked jobs matter more. Calls show interest, while booked jobs show whether the spend pays. Compare cost per booked job each month.
