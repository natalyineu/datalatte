## Why Do Categories Matter for Local Search?

Categories tell Google what kind of business a profile is, which helps it match the profile to searches. The Adwave guide says that weak performance on simple local searches points back to profile accuracy, reviews, categories and proximity. Categories are one part of that picture, not the whole thing.

Scale matters too. Google processes billions of searches per day across all categories, according to the [Keepers Digital comparison](https://keepersdigital.com/en-ca/resources/local-search/yelp-vs-google-business-profile/) (secondary). A salon competing for "hair salon near me" is competing with many profiles, so a clear, accurate category set is a basic fix, not a bonus.

## Which Category Should Be the Primary One?

The primary category should describe the main thing a client pays for. For most salons that is a hair salon category, but check the names in your own profile editor, because labels differ by country and change over time. Open the category search, type "hair", and read every option that appears.

A practical test: ask which phrase a customer would type when they need your main service. If the closest option is a broad "salon" label and a narrower hair option exists, the narrower one is usually the better fit. Keep the primary choice honest. A category you cannot back up with real services will not help a booking.

## How Many Secondary Categories Should a Salon Add?

A profile can hold up to 9 secondary categories alongside the primary, according to the [Keepers Digital comparison](https://keepersdigital.com/en-ca/resources/local-search/yelp-vs-google-business-profile/) (secondary). The limit is a ceiling, not a target. Each extra category should match a service that is really offered to the public and brings in regular revenue.

Keep the list short and current. Remove categories for services you have stopped, and add one when a new service becomes a regular part of the menu. Once services are accurate, they can connect to booking: [Booksy](https://biz.booksy.com/blog/salon-marketing) says Reserve with Google lets clients book from Search and Maps in under 30 seconds. Clients can only do that if the profile describes what you sell.

## How Do You Map Services to Categories? (Worked Example)

The salon below is an **assumption** built to show the method. It sells cuts, colour, extensions, short barber-style cuts and waxing, and has no separately run barbershop. The revenue shares are also assumptions.

| Service | Offered to the public? | Revenue share (assumption) | Category action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Women's and men's cuts | Yes | 45% | Primary: the closest hair salon option |
| Colour | Yes | 30% | Check the dropdown; add only if a separate option fits |
| Extensions | Yes | 15% | Add as secondary if a matching option exists |
| Short barber-style cuts | Yes, from the same chairs | 5% | Skip a barber category unless it is a separate business |
| Waxing, sold twice a month | Yes | 5% | Skip unless a matching option exists and the service is listed |

Use this checklist to apply the same method to your own salon:

1. List every service you sell and its rough share of revenue.
2. Drop any service that is occasional, unpublicised or not sold on its own.
3. Pick the primary category that matches the largest share.
4. Search "hair salon near me" in your area and open the top three profiles. Note their categories, then copy the idea rather than the list.
5. Set a reminder to review the list when your services change.

## What Mistakes Slow Salons Down?

The most common mistake is treating categories as a substitute for an accurate profile. The [RŌZ guide](https://rozhair.com/blogs/education/hair-salon-advertising-ideas?srsltid=AYCX9vIdNXXuxGV-rKdglolA2QOQJbEGs8irFigzpePpcaBIjnjcFKs6) (vendor) says that when a salon's budget is close to zero, it should start with the Google Business Profile, reviews, service descriptions and portfolio, because these take time more than media spend. Categories belong in that same profile work.

Other errors are easy to fix. Do not add a category for every service you have ever offered, and do not use a category as a keyword for a hairstyle. The RŌZ guide also says a salon can start correcting common missing or inconsistent profile details in about ten minutes, so a quick accuracy pass is a good first step.

## How Long Does Profile Work Take to Show Results?

Profile changes do not show up overnight. The [Adwave guide](https://adwave.com/resources/salon-advertising-growing-your-beauty-business) (vendor) says salons that complete and keep active their Google Business Profile usually see better local visibility within 4 to 8 weeks. Treat that as a vendor estimate, not a guarantee, since results depend on the market.

Measure what you change. Note the date you update categories, then watch your Search and Maps views and calls over the following weeks. If you run other tactics at the same time, such as [Google posts for hair salon marketing](/blog/hair-salon-marketing-google-my-business-posts), your data will mix effects, so keep one change at a time where you can. ## FAQ

### Does the primary category matter more than the others?

The sources here do not give a ranking weight for the primary category. The profile model separates one primary category from up to 9 secondary ones, so the primary is the main signal for what the business is ([Keepers Digital, secondary](https://keepersdigital.com/en-ca/resources/local-search/yelp-vs-google-business-profile/)).

### Can I create my own category name?

Choose from the options Google offers in the category search. If a niche service has no exact match, use the closest broader option that is honest for your business.

### How many categories should a hair salon have?

Up to 9 secondary categories plus one primary is the ceiling. Most salons need far fewer, limited to services they really sell.

### Should a salon and a barbershop under one roof use both categories?

Only if both services are real and sold to the public under the same business. If the barbershop is run as a separate business, give it its own profile.

### Will changing my category hurt my ranking?

No source here measures that. Change categories when your services change, and track calls and Search and Maps views for several weeks afterward.
