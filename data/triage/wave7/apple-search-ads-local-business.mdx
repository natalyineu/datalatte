Apple Search Ads places your app listing at the top of App Store search results. For a local business, it only makes sense when the app is the product, such as a booking, ordering or loyalty app. Typical costs run $0.50 to $2.00 per tap for most categories, and $1.50 to $8 per install for category terms. Whether that pays off depends on what one new app user is worth to you. A business without an app should skip it and focus on Google Business Profile and local search.

## Should a Local Business Use Apple Search Ads?

Only if customers already use your app, or would download it for a reason. A booking, ordering, loyalty or class-schedule app gives the ad a clear destination. If customers book by phone or through a third-party marketplace, the ad has nowhere useful to send them. Apple Search Ads also does not promote a shop's location. It promotes an app listing.

Search is where most installs start. Adwave reports that, according to Apple, 65% of App Store downloads come directly from search inside the store ([Adwave, best advertising mobile apps](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-mobile-apps), secondary). That makes the placement relevant for a booking app, but only if the app already works well for new users.

If you have not built an app yet, start with the groundwork in our [app marketing guide for local businesses](/blog/app-marketing-for-local-business). Check your listing before spending anything: screenshots, the short description and ratings all affect whether a tap turns into an install.

## What Does Apple Search Ads Cost Per Tap and Per Install?

Costs depend heavily on category. Adwave gives a typical cost per tap of $0.50 to $2.00 for most categories, and $3.00 to $5.00 or more for competitive categories such as finance and gaming ([Adwave, best advertising mobile apps](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-mobile-apps), secondary). Cost per install is usually higher than cost per tap. Adwave's fitness guide gives $1.50 to $8 per install for category terms and $0.50 to $3 for branded terms ([Adwave, fitness app advertising](https://adwave.com/resources/fitness-app-advertising), secondary).

Adwave's pages do not agree. One places cost per install in the mid tens to low hundreds of dollars for competitive categories ([Adwave, best advertising for mobile apps](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-for-mobile-apps), secondary). Treat every range as a starting point and measure your own cost per install in the first weeks.

Branded terms, meaning your own app name, usually cost less. Search for your app name first, because competitors may bid on it.

## How Much Budget Do You Need to Test?

For a pre-launch paid test, Adwave says a few hundred dollars a day split between Meta or Apple Search Ads and creative production is enough to learn ([Adwave, best advertising for mobile apps](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-for-mobile-apps), secondary). Its fitness guide recommends early-stage apps budget $1,000 to $5,000 per month on Apple Search Ads, while scaling apps run $10,000 to $100,000 or more ([Adwave, fitness app advertising](https://adwave.com/resources/fitness-app-advertising), secondary).

A single-location business is more likely to sit near the early-stage range. Set a monthly figure you can afford to spend while you learn, and do not scale until the numbers show it pays.

Adwave's startup framework, for apps from pre-launch to 10,000 installs, puts 40% of the budget into App Store search ads ([Adwave, best advertising mobile apps](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-mobile-apps), secondary). That share is for app businesses in general, so use it as a reference point, not a rule for a salon or gym.

## How Do You Work Out If Apple Search Ads Pays Off?

Apple's pricing does not tell you what a booking is worth to your business, so calculate it yourself. The inputs below are all assumptions for a salon with a booking app. The 50% install rate matches the average conversion rate Adwave attributes to Apple for Search Ads ([Adwave, best advertising mobile apps](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-mobile-apps), secondary). Replace every figure with your own data.

| Input | Value (assumption unless noted) |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $1,500 |
| Cost per tap | $1.50, inside Adwave's $0.50 to $2.00 range |
| Installs per tap | 50% |
| Cost per install | $3.00 ($1.50 divided by 0.50) |
| Installs that book a first visit | 10% |
| Cost per booking | $30.00 ($3.00 divided by 0.10) |
| First-visit revenue | $60 |
| Gross margin | 50%, so $30 per visit |

At $1,500 spend you get about 1,000 taps, 500 installs and 50 first bookings. Those bookings earn 50 times $30, or $1,500 in margin, so the campaign breaks even on first visits. Every repeat visit is profit.

Now change one input. If only 5% of installs book, you get 25 bookings and $750 in margin against $1,500 of spend, a $750 loss. The booking rate matters more than the tap price. Measure what share of new installs book or order within 14 days before you scale spend.

## What Should a Small Test Look Like?

Run a test with a clear limit and a short list of checks:

1. Search your own app name and confirm competitor bids before you start.
2. Use Search Match to find which queries drive downloads, since Adwave recommends starting there ([Adwave, how to get users to a mobile app](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-users-mobile-app), secondary).
3. Track cost per install for branded and category terms separately.
4. Log installs, first bookings and margin each week in one sheet.
5. Refresh creative every 4 to 8 weeks, since Apple's ad placements rotate ([Adwave, fitness app advertising](https://adwave.com/resources/fitness-app-advertising), secondary).
6. Scale only while cost per booking stays below margin per visit.

If installs stay expensive, compare the cost of search clicks on other platforms before adding budget. ## FAQ

### How much does Apple Search Ads cost per install?
Adwave's fitness guide gives $1.50 to $8 per install for category terms and $0.50 to $3 for branded terms ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/fitness-app-advertising), secondary). Other Adwave pages give different ranges, so measure your own figure.

### Do I need an app to use it?
Yes. Apple Search Ads promotes an app listing in the App Store. A business without an app cannot use it to promote a location or service.

### Can I target customers near my shop?
Location options change, so check your current Apple Ads account settings before planning around a radius. Do not assume a location filter exists until you see it in the account.

### How often should I update my ads?
Adwave's fitness guide recommends updating Apple Search Ads creative every 4 to 8 weeks, because placements rotate ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/fitness-app-advertising), secondary).

### Is it worth it for a salon with a booking app?
It can be, but only when the booking rate justifies the cost. In the worked example above, a 10% booking rate roughly breaks even on the first visit, and a 5% rate loses money. Run the test with a monthly limit before deciding.
