## How does the Google Maps local pack decide which salons appear?

Adwave says the top three businesses in the map pack are pulled directly from their Google Business Profile listings, so the profile is the starting point for any salon that wants to appear there ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-my-lawn-care-business)). The same publisher states that the primary category is the number one ranking factor for getting into the Local Pack, citing analysis of millions of profiles without naming that source ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones)). For a salon, choosing the right category is the first decision to get right.

Adwave also estimates that Google Business Profile signals represented 32% of local pack ranking weight in 2025, citing a multi-location benchmark ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/multi-location-local-seo-audit)). Treat that figure as directional rather than a fixed formula. Competitor patterns are easier to read in another market, such as the [Denver hair salon review benchmarks](/blog/denver-hair-salons-google-maps-review-benchmarks-2026).

## Which Google Business Profile settings should a Lexington salon fix first?

Work through the profile in this order, then check each item again after the first month:

1. Set the primary category. Look at the top three to five businesses in local map results for your main service and note the category they use ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones)).
2. Write the description within 750 characters, leading with your service and your Lexington neighbourhood ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack)). Name the city once or twice in plain sentences.
3. Upload at least 15 high-quality photos, the figure Adwave recommends for a strong gallery ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers)). A separate Adwave to-do list sets the floor at 10 ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack)).
4. Publish at least one post a week. Google reviews each post, and a post shows publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts do not show ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)).
5. Make hours, phone number and address 100% accurate ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers)).
6. Claim the listing. Adwave states that 11.1% of profiles are unclaimed, without naming a source, so check whether yours is one of them ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers)). Google describes its Business Profile as free to set up ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)).

If your booking software supports it, Booksy states that Reserve with Google lets clients book from Search or Maps in under 30 seconds ([Booksy, secondary](https://biz.booksy.com/blog/salon-marketing)).

## Do reviews and citations change map pack position?

Google states that reviews show next to a business's Business Profile in Maps and Search ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)). Adwave cites an analysis of 2 million profiles by a third party, which found that businesses with over 200 Google reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business)). The page does not give an exact threshold, so use 200 as a benchmark rather than a guarantee.

Citations need matching details. The name, address and phone number must be exactly the same on the website, the Business Profile and directories such as Yelp, Apple Maps and Bing Places ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/voice-search-optimization-for-local-businesses)). Small differences count, including whether the page says "St." or "Street" ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack)). Google states that location data used in Google Ads comes directly from Google Maps and Business Profile, so a fix made there carries through ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)). Adwave also says Apple Business Connect reaches over 1.5 billion affluent iOS users, a figure the page does not attribute, so check the Apple Maps listing too ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/apple-business-connect-the-google-business-profile-alternative-youre-ignoring)).

## What does a 200-review target look like in numbers?

The example below uses assumption inputs for one salon. Replace them with your own counts.

Assumptions: the salon has 60 reviews today, and it adds reviews at either 3 or 6 per week. The target of 200 comes from the benchmark above.

Reviews needed: 200 minus 60 equals 140.

| Scenario (assumption) | New reviews per week | Weeks to reach 200 |
|---|---|---|
| Steady follow-up after each visit | 3 | 47 (140 divided by 3, rounded up) |
| Stronger follow-up plus a card at checkout | 6 | 24 (140 divided by 6, rounded up) |

At 3 per week, a 13-week quarter adds 39 reviews. The gap between the two rows shows why a fixed follow-up routine matters more than occasional pushes. Adwave also recommends responding to reviews, which keeps the profile active between new reviews ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business)).

## Which website signals support the Google Business Profile?

The website should repeat the same name, address and phone number as the profile, and the service pages should name the services that customers search for, such as balayage or men's haircuts. Adwave's guidance on keeping details matched applies to the website as well as directories ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/voice-search-optimization-for-local-businesses)).

A Google Maps embed on the contact page is a simple way to show the same location on your site. ## How long before a salon sees map pack movement?

Adwave states that a business may start ranking for its own name in the Map Pack a few weeks after its profile is verified, though it presents this as its own estimate ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack)). Map pack position for other searches usually takes longer and depends on competitors.

Track the metrics that show whether the profile is working. Adwave lists profile impressions, map-pack views, clicks to call, direction requests, website visits from the profile, and branded search lift over time as the useful ones ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/local-search-intent)). Check these monthly and compare against the same month the year before, so seasonal changes do not hide real gains.

## FAQ

### How many photos should a Lexington salon upload to its Google Business Profile?

Adwave recommends at least 15 high-quality photos for a gallery that gets more clicks and calls ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers)). Another Adwave to-do list sets the minimum at 10 ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack)). Use 15 as the target and refresh the gallery regularly.

### Are reviews enough to get into the map pack on their own?

No. Adwave places the primary category first among ranking factors for the Local Pack and reports profile signals as a large share of ranking weight ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/multi-location-local-seo-audit)). Reviews are one input alongside the category, accurate details and consistent citations.

### Can a salon that works from home hide its address?

Yes. Adwave says a business that works from home or serves customers at their location can hide its street address on the profile and is then designated a Service Area Business ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack)). Check whether this applies to your setup before changing the listing.

### Does a Google Business Profile cost anything?

Google describes its Business Profile as free to set up ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)). Costs come only from optional paid tools, such as ads or review software.
