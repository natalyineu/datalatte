## How Much Does Meta Advertising Cost for a Fitness Studio?

A standalone Meta buy often costs $300–$1,000 per month, according to [Adwave's channel comparison](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-choose-advertising-channels) (vendor). Treat that as a planning range for a test, not a promise of bookings. The same vendor family lists Meta cost per click at $1–$3 and CPM at $5–$15 in its [small business marketing guide](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-small-business-guide), which gives you a way to convert any budget into expected clicks.

For fitness and wellness businesses, [Adwave's CTV comparison](https://adwave.com/resources/connected-tv-vs-social-media-ads-comparison) recommends a 45–55% Meta share of budget, with 30–40% on connected TV and the balance on local platforms. That is a multi-channel mix. A studio running only Meta should not read it as a direct target.

Many boutique studios budget between 5 and 10 percent of monthly revenue for marketing, according to [Mariana Tek](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/) (secondary). Assumption: a studio with $20,000 in monthly revenue would then have $1,000–$2,000 for all marketing, with Meta taking a share of that. For account setup steps, see [Facebook ads for gyms and fitness studios](/blog/facebook-ads-for-gyms-fitness-studios-complete-guide).

## What Does a $600 Monthly Meta Budget Deliver?

The inputs below are assumptions for illustration, not sourced figures. Swap in your own numbers after the first two weeks of data.

Monthly budget: $600 (assumption)
Cost per click: $2.00 (assumption, inside the $1–$3 benchmark)
Trial booking rate from click: 5% (assumption)

Calculation: $600 ÷ $2.00 = 300 clicks. 300 × 5% = 15 trial bookings. $600 ÷ 15 = $40 per booking.

| Monthly budget (assumption) | Clicks at $2.00 CPC (assumption) | Trial bookings at 5% (assumption) | Cost per booking |
|---|---|---|---|
| $300 | 150 | 7.5 | $40 |
| $600 | 300 | 15 | $40 |
| $1,000 | 500 | 25 | $40 |

Under these assumptions, cost per booking stays flat as spend rises, so the real test is your booking rate, not the budget. A lower rate from clicks to trial visits raises your cost per booking quickly. Adwave notes that Meta results typically arrive in one to four weeks in its [restaurant guide](https://adwave.com/resources/restaurant-advertising-complete-marketing-guide) (secondary), so judge a campaign on at least a month of data.

## Which Ad Formats Should a Fitness Studio Use?

Short-form video is the format to start with. Adwave cites Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report as showing that short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/electrician-advertising-reaching-homeowners), secondary). Mariana Tek advises studios to post three to five short-form videos a week at a steady pace, rather than in bursts ([Mariana Tek](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/), secondary).

Reels suit class energy and trainer introductions. Stories suit time-limited trial offers and schedule reminders. For specifics, read [fitness studio marketing with Instagram Reels](/blog/fitness-studio-marketing-with-instagram-reels) and [Instagram Stories ads for fitness studios](/blog/instagram-stories-ads-fitness-studios).

## How Should You Target Fitness Studio Ads in Fresno?

Instagram ads run through the same system as Facebook ads, Meta Ads Manager, and one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook, or both ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/instagram-advertising-for-local-business), vendor). Meta Ads Manager covers Facebook, Instagram, and Messenger ([Turrboo](https://turrboo.com/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media), secondary), so one account handles placements.

Radius and age range are not covered by the sources used here. Assumption: start with a radius of 3 to 5 miles around your studio, then tighten it toward the postcodes where current members live. Build your age range from your member list rather than a generic band, and test one change at a time.

Retargeting people who visited your booking page but did not book is usually the cheapest audience to reach. Adwave lists typical Meta retargeting CPMs at $5–$15 in its [retargeting guide](https://adwave.com/resources/retargeting-ads-small-business), a benchmark you can compare against your own reach costs.

## How Do You Set Up Tracking Before Spending?

Set tracking up before the first dollar goes out. Without it, you can only see impressions and reach, not bookings.

1. Install the Meta Pixel on your booking confirmation page and your trial sign-up page.
2. Configure a Lead event that fires when someone submits a booking or trial form.
3. Add UTM parameters to every ad URL so your analytics can separate Meta traffic from other sources.
4. Add a Book Now button to your Facebook and Instagram profiles, which both platforms support ([Appointy](https://blog.appointy.com/2020/12/11/social-media-marketing-for-fitness-studio-gym/), secondary).
5. Consider Meta's free Blueprint courses on Facebook and Instagram advertising, which include a certificate on completion ([SASCOM](https://sascom247.com/digital-marketing-for-beginners-in-nigeria/), secondary).

## Which Mistakes Waste Fitness Studio Meta Budgets?

The most common mistake is asking cold traffic for a full membership. A free trial or first class asks far less commitment and gives you a booking you can follow up. The worked example above shows why: a low cost per booking only matters if those bookings turn into members.

Broad fitness interest targeting is the second mistake. Narrow your offer to the modality you actually teach, such as Pilates, HIIT, or strength classes, so the ad matches the person reading it.

Follow-up is the third. Mariana Tek advises following up with pop-up or trial contacts within 24 hours using an automated welcome sequence, since interest fades quickly ([Mariana Tek](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/), secondary). ## FAQ

### How much should a Fresno fitness studio spend on Meta ads per month?

Adwave's channel comparison puts a standalone Meta buy at $300–$1,000 per month. Pick a budget you can sustain for at least four weeks, because results can take one to four weeks to appear.

### Do video ads outperform static images for studios?

Adwave cites Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report as showing short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images. Test video against your best still image before deciding.

### Can I run one campaign on both Facebook and Instagram?

Yes. According to Adwave, one Meta Ads Manager campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook, or both.

### Are these benchmarks specific to Fresno?

No. The CPM, cost-per-click, and budget figures come from national vendor benchmarks. Use your own Ads Manager results from the first few weeks as the Fresno baseline.
