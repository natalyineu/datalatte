A Custom GPT is ChatGPT configured with your own instructions, business facts and tone rules, so it answers customer questions the way your business would. For a local business it works best on prices, hours, services and policies, with a clear handoff to a person for bookings and complaints. This guide gives copy-ready system prompts for a hair salon, a coffee shop and a fitness studio, plus setup steps, a knowledge file layout and a simple cost check. Replace every bracketed placeholder with your own details before publishing.

## What Can a Custom GPT Handle for a Local Business?

A well-configured Custom GPT can answer the questions customers ask most often. For a service business, that usually means prices, hours, location, service descriptions, cancellation rules and a link to your booking page. The more of these facts you put in the instructions, the fewer answers the model has to guess.

It should not handle everything. A Custom GPT can point customers to a booking link, but it cannot book an appointment on its own unless you connect it to a booking system. Complaints that need empathy and a decision from a manager should go straight to a person. So should anything involving sensitive personal data, such as payment details or medical notes.

Treat the GPT as a first responder, not a replacement for staff. It handles the repeat questions so your team can focus on the customers in front of them and the calls that need judgment.

## How Do You Set Up a Custom GPT Step by Step?

Setup takes an afternoon if your prices and policies are already written down. Open ChatGPT, go to My GPTs, and choose Create a GPT. Use the Configure tab rather than the chat-based builder, because it gives you direct control over the instructions and the knowledge file.

The checklist below covers the order that works best for most small businesses.

| Step | What to enter | Check before publishing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name and short description | Matches how customers search for you |
| 2 | Instructions (system prompt) | Every price, hour and policy is filled in |
| 3 | Knowledge file upload | Prices match the instructions exactly |
| 4 | Fallback line | Phone number or booking link is included |
| 5 | Test run | Answers match your current menu and policies |

Run the test with real questions you have received over email, text or the front desk. If the GPT invents a price or policy during testing, add that item to the instructions and test again.

## What Should the Hair Salon System Prompt Include?

A salon prompt needs four blocks: the business details, a services and price list, stylist specialties, and the cancellation and deposit rules. Stylist specialties matter because customers often ask who does balayage or men's fades, and the GPT should answer that without guessing.

The prompt below uses placeholders. Fill in your own figures and remove any service you do not offer.

```
You are the virtual assistant for [SALON NAME], a hair salon at [FULL ADDRESS].
Answer customer questions accurately, warmly and concisely.

ABOUT THE SALON:
- Phone: [PHONE]
- Hours: [HOURS FOR EACH DAY]
- Booking: [BOOKING URL]
- Instagram: [HANDLE]

SERVICES AND PRICES (use these exact figures, never invent prices):
- Women's haircut: [PRICE RANGE]
- Men's haircut: [PRICE RANGE]
- Balayage: [PRICE RANGE], consultation required for exact quote
- Full highlights: [PRICE RANGE]
- Blow dry: [PRICE RANGE]
- Deep conditioning: [PRICE]

STYLISTS:
- [NAME]: [SPECIALTIES]
- [NAME]: [SPECIALTIES]

POLICIES:
- Cancellation: [NOTICE PERIOD] required. [LATE FEE POLICY].
- Color services: [DEPOSIT POLICY].

TONE: Warm and professional. Not overly casual, not corporate.
If you do not know the answer, say so and give the phone number or booking link.
Do not make up prices or policies that are not listed above.
For scheduling, always say: "You can book online at [BOOKING URL] or call us at [PHONE]."
```

The last line matters most. It gives the GPT one fixed way to close every scheduling conversation, so customers always get the same next step.

## How Should a Coffee Shop Prompt Handle Menu Changes?

A coffee shop menu changes with the seasons, so the prompt should separate the stable facts from the menu section. Hours, address, Wi-Fi rules and dairy-alternative policies rarely change. The menu block changes every season, and it is the part you will edit most often.

Keep the tone neighborhood-friendly. A short prompt with three or four common questions and answers helps the GPT handle the wording customers actually use, such as asking about oat milk or wholesale orders.

```
You are the virtual assistant for [SHOP NAME], a coffee shop in [CITY].

ABOUT US:
- Address: [ADDRESS]
- Hours: [HOURS FOR EACH DAY]
- Phone: [PHONE]
- Online orders: [ORDER URL]
- Wi-Fi: [FREE OR PAID], password [WHERE CUSTOMERS FIND IT]

MENU (current season, update each season):
- Espresso: [PRICE]
- Latte: [PRICE] for each size
- Oat or almond milk: [EXTRA CHARGE]
- Seasonal drink: [PRICE]
- Food: [ITEM AND PRICE]

COMMON QUESTIONS:
Q: Do you have dairy alternatives?
A: [YOUR ANSWER AND EXTRA CHARGE]
Q: Do you take catering or bulk orders?
A: Please call [PHONE] for event and catering orders.

TONE: Friendly and relaxed. Enthusiastic about coffee without lecturing.
If you do not know something, say so and direct the customer to [PHONE].
```

Put the seasonal menu in the instructions, not only in the knowledge file. Instructions are read more reliably for specific prices, so a price change there takes effect straight away.

## What Does a Fitness Studio Prompt Need?

A fitness studio prompt has a different job. Customers ask about the class schedule, what to bring, whether a class suits their level, and how to book or cancel. Schedules change weekly, so the prompt should link to the live booking page rather than hard-code every class time.

Cancellation rules are where studios lose money, so state the window clearly. The prompt should also tell the GPT how to handle a first-time visitor, because new students often ask the same orientation questions.

```
You are the virtual assistant for [STUDIO NAME], a [STYLE] studio in [CITY].

ABOUT THE STUDIO:
- Address: [ADDRESS]
- Phone: [PHONE]
- Schedule and booking: [LIVE BOOKING URL]
- Instagram: [HANDLE]

TODAY'S SCHEDULE: Direct customers to [LIVE BOOKING URL] for current class times.

PRICING: [CLASS PRICES, PACKS AND MEMBERSHIP, AS PUBLISHED]

WHAT TO BRING: [MAT, WATER, FOOTWEAR, CLOTHING RULES]

POLICIES:
- Booking: [HOW FAR IN ADVANCE]
- Cancellation: [CANCELLATION WINDOW AND WHAT HAPPENS AFTER]
- First class: [ARRIVAL TIME AND ORIENTATION]

TONE: Energetic, encouraging and inclusive. Make every fitness level feel welcome.
Never suggest a class is too advanced for someone without checking the class description.
```

The schedule line is deliberate. Sending customers to the live booking page avoids the GPT quoting a class that was cancelled last week.

## What Belongs in the Knowledge File?

The knowledge file holds the detail that is too long for the instructions. Upload a PDF or text file that covers the full service or menu list, a set of frequently asked questions, staff bios, location details such as parking and transit access, and any seasonal information like holiday hours.

Write the FAQ section from questions you have actually been asked. A list of guesses is less useful than a log of what customers asked on the phone last month. Keep the file in plain sentences so the model can match a customer's wording to the right answer.

Update the file whenever prices change or a service launches. Keep prices identical across the instructions and the file. If they disagree, the GPT may quote whichever one it picks up first.

## Can a Custom GPT Connect to Live Data or Book Appointments?

By default, a Custom GPT answers only from its instructions and knowledge file. To pull live data, such as stock levels or open appointment slots, you need a connection. OpenAI's custom MCP server support has plan limits. According to Adwave's summary of OpenAI's documentation, custom MCP servers get full write access only on Business and Enterprise workspaces, while Pro users get read and fetch permissions only. On Business, only admins and owners can create and publish custom servers.

For a single-location business, that usually means the static Custom GPT is enough, with a live booking link doing the scheduling. If you need the assistant to check availability or write to your system, plan the plan tier first. The technical side is covered in [how to build an MCP server with custom data for local business](/blog/build-mcp-server-custom-data-local-business).

Booking itself should stay with your existing tool until you have a tested integration. A wrong booking costs more than a slow answer.

## What Does a Custom GPT Cost to Run? A Worked Example

The subscription is only part of the cost. The real question is how much staff time the GPT gives back. The example below uses a coffee shop with assumed inputs. Replace them with your own figures before you decide.

Assumptions (all assumption, not sourced): 40 repeat questions a week that a staff member answers by hand; 3 minutes per answer; staff time valued at $18 per hour; ChatGPT subscription at $20 per month. Check the current subscription price on OpenAI's site.

Step 1: 40 questions × 3 minutes = 120 minutes a week, or 2 hours.
Step 2: 2 hours × 4.33 weeks a month (52 weeks ÷ 12) = about 8.7 hours a month.
Step 3: 8.7 hours × $18 = about $156 of staff time a month.
Step 4: $156 − $20 subscription = about $136 a month in staff time freed up.

This is not cash in the bank. The gain only shows up if the staff reuse those hours for in-shop service, follow-ups or a promotion. Setup time and your own review time sit outside this sum. If your repeat questions are fewer, the case weakens, and a simple FAQ page may do the same job.

## How Do You Keep the GPT Accurate Over Time?

Accuracy is a maintenance task. Set a fixed review point, such as the first day of each month or the start of each season, and check every price, hour and policy against the live version in your system. A quick review once a season catches most drift.

Log the questions the GPT could not answer. Each gap is a line you should add to the knowledge file. Over a few review cycles the file becomes a record of what your customers actually ask.

## FAQ

### Do customers need a ChatGPT account to use my Custom GPT?

Access rules depend on how you share the GPT and on ChatGPT's current sharing settings, which change over time. Check the sharing options in your GPT settings before you publish a link. If you want a fully branded chat window with no OpenAI interface, you will need to build your own widget on the API, which takes more technical setup.

### How do I update the GPT when prices change?

Edit the Instructions section in My GPTs, then open the Configure tab for that GPT. If prices also appear in the knowledge file, upload the new version of that file as well. Keep the two in step so the GPT never quotes conflicting figures.

### What happens when the GPT gets a question it cannot answer?

Write an explicit fallback line into the instructions. It should say that the GPT does not know, then give your phone number or booking link. Do not leave this to default behavior, because a guessed answer about a price or policy creates problems at the counter.

### Can the GPT handle complaints?

It should not. Complaints need empathy and a decision from a person. Tell the GPT to acknowledge the issue briefly and pass the customer to your phone line or a named contact. Keep this rule visible in the instructions so it applies to every conversation.

### Is a Custom GPT better than a traditional chatbot?

A traditional chatbot follows a decision tree you build in advance, so it is predictable but breaks on unexpected questions. A Custom GPT handles natural wording and context, but it can occasionally misread an edge case. For most local business FAQ use, the flexibility is worth the extra review.
