## What Does Google Ads Cost for a Pet Groomer in Baltimore?

AdTurbo puts dog groomer clicks at about $2 to $8 and warns that a few wasted clicks add up fast at that price. These are vendor figures, not an audited average, so treat them as a range to test in your own account. Your first month of data will show where your market sits within it.

Competition is real. Scrap.io's database contains 59,322 US pet groomers, though that is a contact list, not a measure of demand. Baltimore groomers also compete for attention on the same searches, so a clear service area and booking step matter more than a low click price alone.

Local rivals show what customers see. Zoomin Groomin, a mobile groomer, says it serves Southeast Baltimore, Canton and Essex. Its booking process has customers request an appointment, then a groomer contacts them to set a time. Your ad landing page should make that next step equally clear.

## Which Keywords Should a Baltimore Pet Groomer Bid On?

AdTurbo lists three starting keywords: "dog groomer near me", "dog grooming near me" and "puppy grooming near me". Build from those, then add your Baltimore area names and the services you actually sell. Zoomin Groomin's page lists mobile spa and grooming, nail trimming, bathing and washing, and deshedding, which gives a ready list of service themes.

Job seekers also search for grooming work. The Pet Business Marketing Guide from PSPrint cites an average dog groomer salary of $40,000, so add terms such as "jobs" and "salary" as negative keywords. One vendor reports a shared list of more than 500 groomer negatives. Build your own list from your search terms report, since that shows what your ads actually matched.

## How Do You Keep Ads Inside Your Service Area?

Location settings control spend more than any other setting. Barklead suggests targeting users within a 10 to 15 mile radius, but that advice is for Facebook ads, not Google. Use it as a starting test for a mobile groomer, then tighten the radius for a fixed shop once you see where bookings come from.

Zoomin Groomin describes its service area as named neighbourhoods: Southeast Baltimore, Canton and Essex. Mirror that approach by listing the areas you really reach. Check Google Ads' location targeting help page inside your account for the current radius options before you save the setting.

At the low cost per click, the budget returns $1,400 in first visits. At the high cost per click, it returns $350, less than half the $800 spent. A booking must cost less than $70 for one visit to cover its ad cost, so that number is your break-even test. Repeat visits and product costs will change the result.

## What Should You Track?

AdTurbo names booking a grooming appointment as the primary conversion to track. Use that as your main goal. A form submission alone is not a booking, because Zoomin Groomin's process has a groomer call back to set a time. Count an appointment only once a time is confirmed.

Calls and direction requests can be tracked as secondary actions, but they should not replace bookings in your reports. Reporting booking counts next to spend makes the break-even test easy to check each month. ## When Should a Baltimore Groomer Push Ads?

Adwave, citing Groomer to Groomer, notes that November and December are traditionally a very busy time for groomers, sometimes too busy. If your calendar is already full in those months, a lower spend protects your service quality. If slots are open, that is the time to test a higher budget.

A 2023 Marketing 360 case study of a mobile pet groomer that advertised on Facebook and Google reported a 696% rise in clicks and a 682% rise in conversions against the previous six months. Those are percentage changes, not a cost per booking, so use them as direction only. ## FAQ

No fixed figure fits every shop. AdTurbo says most dog groomer advertisers spend around $800 to $5,000 a month. Start at the low end, then scale only after your cost per booked appointment passes the break-even test.

### What does a click cost for dog groomers?
