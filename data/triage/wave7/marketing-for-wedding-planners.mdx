## How Long Does a Wedding Booking Take?

According to [Adwave's wedding venue guide](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-wedding-venues-event-planners), couples research venues and vendors for 9 to 18 months before they book. That window is longer than most small-business campaigns, so a planner who appears only in the final month before a wedding misses most of the decision.

The same guide advises planning for a year of investment before judging a new channel's contribution. Use that as the test window for search, social or any new referral routine, and write the start date down so the review happens on time.

The guide also says most venues need 4 to 8 inquiries to produce one booked event. It is a venue figure, so treat it as a benchmark for your own funnel, not a rule. Track every enquiry by source, because that shows which channels need several touches before a booking.

## Which Vendor Referrals Bring Bookings?

A 2024 WeddingWire vendor survey, cited by [Adwave's florist advertising guide](https://adwave.com/resources/florist-advertising) (secondary), reports that 58% of wedding florists say referrals from photographers, venues and wedding planners drive more than half their bookings. The figure describes florists, but it shows that the same network of vendors can send each other work.

Build a routine with the vendors who meet the same couples at the same stage. Give each partner a one-page summary of the weddings you plan, the budget range you serve and the simplest way to reach you. A specific introduction is more useful to a partner than a generic one.

Keep a referral log with two columns: referrals you send out and referrals that arrive. Review it each quarter. Community events can also put you in front of those partners, as covered in [event marketing for local business](/blog/event-marketing-for-local-business). If you consider paying partners a fee for introductions, read the [affiliate marketing guide for local business](/blog/affiliate-marketing-for-local-business) first, because payment changes the rules.

## How Far Ahead Should You Plan Content?

Adwave's [three-month content calendar guide](https://adwave.com/resources/content-calendar-template-plan-three-months-in-30-minutes) says a quarter usually gives about 12 weekly opportunities, enough to plan one anchor theme per week. It recommends building the calendar around four horizons and says brands often work from 3 to 5 content pillars per quarter, with 8 to 15 related posts for each pillar.

The same guide says a planning sprint of about 30 minutes can settle the quarter's goal, themes, weekly topics, channel mix and paid amplification. A planner might set three pillars, such as venue sourcing, wedding timelines and real weddings shared with permission, then fill each one with posts.

Lead times vary by channel. [Boltic's marketing calendar guide](https://www.boltic.io/blog/marketing-calendar-template) (secondary) advises starting 90 days or one month before execution. Adwave's [seasonal TV calendar guide](https://adwave.com/resources/seasonal-tv-advertising-calendar) advises planning seasonal campaigns 4 to 6 weeks ahead, and its [CTV guide](https://adwave.com/resources/ctv-advertising-examples) suggests 3 to 6 months for a CTV calendar. ## What Does Paid Search Return per Booking?

The table below uses assumptions, not measured results. Replace each assumption with your own numbers after the first month.

| Cost per enquiry | $120 | assumption |
| Enquiries per month | about 6.7 | $800 ÷ $120 |
| Enquiries per booking | 6 | assumption, within the 4 to 8 venue range |
| Bookings per month | about 1.1 | 6.7 ÷ 6 |
| Planner fee per booking | $6,000 | assumption, within the $4K to $25K range |
| Booked revenue per month | about $6,700 | 1.1 × $6,000 |
| Revenue per ad dollar | about 8.3 | $6,700 ÷ $800 |

Revenue is not profit. Subtract your delivery costs before deciding anything. The monthly average also hides timing, because bookings follow a research window of up to 18 months. Rerun the table with a cost per enquiry of $200: that gives 4 enquiries, about 0.67 bookings and roughly $4,000 of revenue a month, or 5 per ad dollar. The gap shows why cost per enquiry matters more than the headline budget.

## How Should You Track Enquiries Before Judging Ads?

Add one question to every enquiry form: how did you hear about us? Record the answer in a spreadsheet with the date of first contact, the channel, and whether the enquiry became a consultation or a booking. Over a year, that sheet shows which referrals, pages and ads produce bookings rather than only clicks.

Count enquiries per booking as well as cost per enquiry. If the venue benchmark of 4 to 8 enquiries per booking holds for your own leads, the table above is a usable starting point. If it does not, replace the assumption with your own ratio.

Free layouts can help. [Adwave's planning templates guide](https://adwave.com/resources/planning-templates-for-every-season) lists downloadable calendars for Excel and Google Sheets, including Hootsuite's social media calendar template, which it describes as free to download.
