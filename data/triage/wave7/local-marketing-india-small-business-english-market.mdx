An Indian small business in an English-speaking city should start with a complete Google Business Profile and a small Google Ads test aimed at local searchers. Meta and WhatsApp come next, once you can count enquiries. The sourced figures here are global or come from other markets, so this guide sets out the shape of a plan rather than rupee benchmarks. Meta's apps reach an average of 3.56 billion daily active people worldwide, according to [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/facebook-advertising-for-small-business). That shows the size of the audience, not what one Indian city will deliver. For a similar English-speaking Asian city market, see the [Singapore guide](/blog/local-marketing-singapore-small-business-guide).

## Which Channels Should an Indian Small Business Test First?

Start with the channels where a customer shows intent. For a local service, that usually means Google Search, because people type a need and a town into the search box. Google Ads lets you target by language and location at once. Adwave gives the example of English or Spanish speakers in Mexico City, and the same logic applies to English speakers in an Indian city ([LianLian Global](https://www.lianlianglobal.com/post-us/5-tips-for-using-google-ads-to-expand-your-ecommerce-businesses-into-international-markets)). Check the live Google Ads help for current targeting options before you build a campaign.

Meta suits businesses that sell something visual, such as salons, cafes or fitness studios, because people browse Facebook and Instagram without a search in mind. Its apps include WhatsApp, which matters for enquiries (see the WhatsApp section below). Choose one primary channel for the first test. Two channels split a small budget too thinly to show what works.

## What Do Meta Ads Cost, and How Big Should a Test Be?

Adwave's guide puts typical Meta costs at $5 to $15 per thousand impressions and $1 to $3 per click ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-small-business-guide)). These are US dollar figures from a vendor, not Indian prices, so treat them as a sanity check rather than a forecast. Adwave's Stage 1 budget for a pre-product-market-fit business in its first year is $1,500 to $5,000 a month, with Meta and Instagram at 50% of that total ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/shopify-store-advertising)). That is an ecommerce plan, and a local business may need far less.

Adwave's budget ladder starts at $30 a day across channels including Google Search and Facebook and Instagram ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/ctv-advertising-market-size)). A small test should run long enough to collect enquiries, not for a day or two. Decide the number of enquiries that would justify more spend before you start.

## How Should You Set Up Google for a Local Business?

A Google Business Profile is the base for local discovery. Fill in your opening hours, services, phone number and photos, and keep them accurate. Google's Business Profile posts feature is limited. Google's help page says featured posts are available in English only, for single-location food and drink businesses in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)). An Indian restaurant should not assume that feature is available, so check the live help page before you plan around it.

For a European example of the same Google-first setup, read the [Germany guide](/blog/local-marketing-germany-small-business-google-ads). Keep ads tied to one service and one area. A broad campaign spends on people who cannot reach your door.

## How Do You Use WhatsApp Without Losing Trust?

Click-to-WhatsApp Ads move people from a Facebook or Instagram ad into a WhatsApp conversation. According to the vendor Crescendo Lab, the feature "seamlessly transitions potential customers from Facebook and Instagram ads to personalized WhatsApp conversations" ([Crescendo Lab](https://blog.cresclab.com/en/whatsapp-marketing)). That gives a small shop a direct route from ad to enquiry, and it works well for bookings and questions.

If you move beyond the free app to a business platform, compare providers closely. Message Central's guide for Brazil recommends submitting Meta verification early and comparing the timelines a provider promises with the ones it delivers ([Message Central](https://www.messagecentral.com/blog/best-whatsapp-business-api-platform-brazil)). The same test applies in India. For a Brazilian view of WhatsApp and Meta, see the [Brazil guide](/blog/local-marketing-brazil-small-business-whatsapp-meta). Only message customers who have agreed to hear from you.

## Which Steps Should You Take in the First 30 Days?

1. Claim or create your Google Business Profile and complete every field.
2. Pick one core service and one area you can serve well.
3. Write a test budget in rupees, and label every input you have not measured yet.
4. Set up call and message tracking so each enquiry has a source.
5. Run one channel for at least a few weeks before you add another.
6. Record enquiries, bookings and the value of each booking in one sheet.
7. Read the platform help pages again before you change settings, because policies change.

## Are Food Delivery Apps Worth the Commission?

Delivery apps bring orders, but the cost depends on your margin, which no general guide can calculate for you. Ask each platform for a written statement of commission, listing fees, advertising charges and payout timing. Then compare the profit on a delivery order with the profit on a dine-in order, after packaging and staff time. Only join if the net figure still works. Avoid signing up for paid promotion until you know the margin on a single order.

## What Should You Check on Customer Data Rules?

Customer lists carry legal duties, and the rules in India change over time. Collect a clear opt-in when a customer gives you a phone number, say what messages they will receive, and make it easy to opt out. Keep a record of the permission you hold. This guide does not summarise the law, so ask a local adviser to confirm what applies to your business before you send a campaign.

## What Does a Starter Budget Look Like?

The table below is a worked example. Every input is an assumption, not an Indian benchmark. A salon tests Google Ads with 20,000 rupees a month and an assumed click price of 25 rupees.

| Item | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | Assumption | 20,000 rupees |
| Click price | Assumption | 25 rupees |
| Clicks | 20,000 / 25 | 800 |
| Enquiry rate | Assumption | 4% |
| Enquiries | 800 x 4% | 32 |
| Share of enquiries that book | Assumption | 50% |
| Bookings | 32 x 50% | 16 |
| Cost per booking | 20,000 / 16 | 1,250 rupees |

Replace the click price with figures from Keyword Planner for your city and service before you decide anything. If the value of one booking is below the cost per booking, improve your profile and landing page before you raise spend. ## FAQ

### What is the best marketing channel for a small business in India?

Start with a complete Google Business Profile and Google Search, because people look for local services there. Add Instagram or WhatsApp once you can track enquiries from the first channel.

### How many people use Meta apps?

Adwave reports that Meta's apps reach an average of 3.56 billion daily active people. That is a global figure, not an Indian one.

### Can I use WhatsApp for marketing?

Yes, but only with customer permission. Click-to-WhatsApp Ads can send ad clicks into a chat. Compare platform providers before you pay for one.

### Are food delivery apps worth the commission?

It depends on your margin on each order. Request the commission and ad charges in writing, and compare net profit with dine-in orders before you join.

### How much do Google Ads cost in India?

The sources in this guide do not give Indian click prices. Use Keyword Planner for your city and service, and treat the worked example above as an assumption.
