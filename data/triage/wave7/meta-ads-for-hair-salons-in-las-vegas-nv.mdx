## How Much Should a Las Vegas Salon Spend on Meta Ads?

Bookeo's starting range of USD 750 to USD 1,500 a month is the most direct guidance for salons. Other Adwave guides give different bands for other industries. Its channel table lists a standalone Meta buy at $300 to $1,000 a month, and its restaurant guide puts most single-location restaurants at $500 to $2,000 a month. Those figures come from other verticals, so use them only to gauge scale.

A sensible first test for a single salon is the lower end of the salon range, run for several weeks. Adwave notes that Meta takes weeks to optimize, so a two-day test will not show whether the audience is right. Keep the spend steady while you learn, and change one variable at a time.

## What Does a Worked Budget Look Like for a Salon?

The table below shows how a $1,000 monthly budget translates into bookings. Every input is an assumption, so replace them with your own Ads Manager data after the first two weeks. The CPM range and cost-per-click range come from Adwave's typical Meta figures.

| Line | Figure | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly budget | $1,000 | Assumption, inside Bookeo's USD 750–1,500 range |
| CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) | $10 | Assumption, inside Adwave's $5–15 range |
| Impressions | 100,000 | $1,000 ÷ $10 × 1,000 |
| Click-through rate | 0.5% | Assumption |
| Clicks | 500 | 100,000 × 0.5% |
| Cost per click | $2.00 | $1,000 ÷ 500, inside Adwave's $1–3 range |
| Booking rate from clicks | 10% | Assumption |
| Bookings | 50 | 500 × 10% |
| Average ticket | $80 | Assumption |
| Revenue from ads | $4,000 | 50 × $80 |
| Revenue per ad dollar | 4× | $4,000 ÷ $1,000 |

If the booking rate is 5% instead, bookings fall to 25, revenue to $2,000, and the return to 2×. Before scaling, check that the return still covers product, staff time and the discounts you offer. The model ignores those costs on purpose, because they differ from salon to salon.

## Which Ad Formats Work Best for Hair Salons?

Adwave, citing Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, says short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram. For a salon, that usually means a short Reel showing a cut, color or styling process, filmed in the actual salon. Test one Reel against one static image with the same offer and budget.

Organic posting supports paid ads. Bookeo says most salon owners find three to four Instagram posts and one to two Facebook posts weekly sustainable. Your ads will point people to a profile that already looks active. For a deeper look at the organic side, read our [Instagram marketing guide for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons).

## How Should You Set the Radius and Targeting?

Bookeo advises a 5 to 10 mile radius around the salon for Facebook and Instagram ads. Start at the wider end if you are new to paid ads, then tighten the radius once you can see which postcodes or neighborhoods book. Reducing the radius too early can hide demand that you would have captured.

Decide whether your offer is for people who live nearby or for visitors. If both matter, build separate ad sets so you can compare them. Meta Ads Manager covers Facebook, Instagram and Messenger, and one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook or both. For a fuller walkthrough of the Meta setup, see [using Meta ads for hair salon marketing](/blog/using-meta-ads-for-hair-salon-marketing).

## What Tracking Do You Need Before Spending?

Set up tracking before the first dollar is spent. Install the Meta Pixel on your booking confirmation page and your contact form, and set a conversion event for each booking or form submission. Add UTM tags to every ad URL so your analytics can separate ad traffic from other sources. Without these, Ads Manager can only show clicks and impressions, not bookings.

Adobe describes Ads Manager as a central place to run ads across Instagram and Facebook, which makes it the right place to check cost per booking each week (secondary). Meta also offers free Facebook and Instagram advertising courses with certification on completion, according to one secondary summary, which can help staff learn the basics. Once a lead arrives, a follow-up sequence matters too. Our [marketing automation guide for hair salons](/blog/marketing-automation-for-hair-salons) covers that step.

If you hire an agency, ask for reporting on outcomes. One of One Group, a London agency, offers a monthly report on followers, reach and enquiries, and says enquiries are the most important part of that report. Ask any provider to report bookings, not just reach.

## FAQ

### How much should a hair salon spend on Meta ads?

Bookeo suggests starting Facebook and Instagram ad spend at USD 750 to USD 1,500 a month. Adwave's channel table lists a standalone Meta buy at $300 to $1,000 a month. Test one level for several weeks before changing it.

### Should a salon run ads on Facebook or Instagram?

One Meta Ads Manager campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook or both. Start with the platform where your current clients are most active, then add the second once you have results to compare.

### How long before Meta ads show results?

Adwave says Meta campaigns take weeks to optimize. Judge performance on cost per booking over several weeks rather than on the first few days of delivery.

### Do I need a business account to see when followers are online?

Yes. Bookeo says the peak-activity view in Instagram or Facebook Insights requires a business account. Use that data to schedule posts and to choose ad delivery times where the platform allows it.
