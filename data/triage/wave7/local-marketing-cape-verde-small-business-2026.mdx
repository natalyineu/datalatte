## Which channels should a Cape Verde small business start with?

Start with the channels a customer can find without paying for reach. A Google Business Profile lets you add one social media link per platform, including Facebook and Instagram, so your listing and your feeds point to each other ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617)). Set that up first, because it costs nothing and it is where many people check a business before they call.

Instagram is worth testing for local reach. In a Canadian survey cited by Square, three in five small business owners said Instagram helps them connect with customers in their cities ([Square, Cafe Marketing Ideas](https://squareup.com/ca/en/the-bottom-line/reaching-customers/marketing-plan-for-a-coffee-shop), secondary). That is one market's result, so use it as a reason to test in your own islands, not as a Cape Verde benchmark.

Add paid Google Search once you know which services people search for by name. For German-language search aimed at European visitors, the [Germany Google Ads guide](/blog/local-marketing-germany-small-business-google-ads) covers the same mechanics in another market. If most of your customers browse on phones, the [Kenya mobile-first guide](/blog/local-marketing-kenya-small-business-mobile-first) shows how a mobile-first approach changes the order of work.

## What does a test campaign cost at the minimum?

Adwave says campaigns that include TV, Google Search, Facebook and Instagram start at $30 a day from a prepaid wallet ([Adwave, garage door advertising](https://adwave.com/resources/garage-door-company-advertising)). Its pricing table lists the same $30 a day covering Google Search, Facebook, Instagram, mobile web and apps, with 30 active days totalling $900 ([Adwave, garage door advertising](https://adwave.com/resources/garage-door-company-advertising)).

Vendor minimums change, and they apply to one vendor's packages. Check the live pricing page before you commit. Ask who owns the ad accounts too. Adwave's salon page says Facebook and Instagram ads run on advertising accounts it owns, and that it sets up the Google Ads account for the user ([Adwave, salon advertising](https://adwave.com/resources/salon-advertising-growing-your-beauty-business)). Some businesses want their own accounts so that the history stays with them.

## How do you build a worked budget for a local business?

The table below uses a hypothetical restaurant in Praia. Every input is an assumption except the $30 daily minimum, which comes from the vendor pricing above. Replace the assumptions with your own numbers before you spend anything.

| Line | Figure | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Daily budget | $30 | Vendor minimum (Adwave) |
| Active days | 30 | Assumption |
| Total spend | $900 | $30 × 30 |
| Cost per click | $1.20 | Assumption, close to the $1.19 to $1.37 range in the ARC salon case |
| Clicks | 750 | $900 ÷ $1.20 |
| Enquiry rate | 4% | Assumption |
| Enquiries | 30 | 750 × 4% |
| Cost per enquiry | $30 | $900 ÷ 30 |

The ARC figures are for Facebook and Instagram clicks in one New York salon campaign in 2023 ([ARC](https://arccreativeco.com/what-a-salon-booking-costs-on-google-ads/)). They are a calibration point, not a Cape Verde price. At $1.37 per click, the same $900 buys about 657 clicks, which is roughly 26 enquiries at the same 4% rate, or about $35 each. Use two weeks of real data to replace both the click cost and the enquiry rate.

## How should WhatsApp fit into the plan?

Many enquiries for small service businesses arrive as messages rather than form fills, so decide early who answers them and how fast. Track the source of each message: a Facebook ad, an Instagram post, a Google listing, or a referral. Without that label, the cost per enquiry in the table above cannot be checked.

The [Brazil guide on WhatsApp and Meta](/blog/local-marketing-brazil-small-business-whatsapp-meta) is a useful comparison for another Portuguese-speaking market where messaging and paid social work together. Read it for the workflow, and then test the same steps with your own customers in Praia, Sal, or Mindelo.

## How do you judge a 30-day test?

Compare the cost per enquiry with what one paying customer is worth to you. If a $30 enquiry leads to a booking worth several times that, the test has earned a second month. If the cost per enquiry is close to your margin, change the offer or the landing page before you raise the budget.

Keep the test narrow. Change one variable at a time, such as the service being promoted or the language of the ad, and keep the daily budget fixed. Otherwise you will not know which change produced the result.

## What setup checks should come first?

1. Claim or update the Google Business Profile and add a Facebook link and an Instagram link.
2. Write the service names customers actually use, in Portuguese and English where both are needed, and check that the ad text matches them.
3. Confirm who owns the Facebook, Instagram and Google Ads accounts before the first payment.
4. Check which channels are live. Adwave says TikTok runs only after you turn it on and connect a TikTok account ([Adwave, auto care](https://adwave.com/resources/auto-care-campaign-example)).
5. Set a daily budget you can keep for 30 days, so the test is not cut short.

## FAQ

### Is $30 a day enough to test local marketing?

It is the minimum on one vendor's published pricing, and that vendor lists it as covering Google Search, Facebook and Instagram ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/garage-door-company-advertising)). Whether it is enough depends on your click cost and enquiry rate. The worked example above shows how to test that with your own numbers.

### Who should own the ad accounts?

Ask before you sign. Some vendors run Facebook and Instagram ads on accounts they own, and set up Google Ads for the client ([Adwave, salon advertising](https://adwave.com/resources/salon-advertising-growing-your-beauty-business)). Having your own accounts keeps your campaign history and audience data with your business if you change vendors.

### Does TikTok run automatically with a vendor package?

Not on Adwave. Its auto care page says TikTok runs only when you turn it on in the settings and connect a TikTok account ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/auto-care-campaign-example)). Check whether any package you buy includes it before you assume it is running.

### Can a Google Business Profile link to my Instagram?

Yes. Google's help page says you can add one social media link per platform to your profile, including Facebook and Instagram ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617)). It is a quick check that makes your listing easier to verify and to follow.
