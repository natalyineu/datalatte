Local SEO for a pet groomer in Chicago, IL means getting your Google Business Profile into the Maps local pack, which [Adwave defines as the top three business listings in local search results](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-audit-checklist-15-things-to-fix-today). Those three listings are [pulled directly from Google Business Profile listings](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-my-lawn-care-business) (secondary), so the work starts there. Accurate details, a primary category that matches your main service, photos, posts, reviews and consistent citations all feed the same listing. The steps below follow that order.

## Why does a Chicago pet groomer's map pack ranking depend on the Business Profile?

Google Business Profile is a [free tool that lets businesses manage their presence across Google Search and Maps](https://keepersdigital.com/en-ca/resources/local-search/yelp-vs-google-business-profile/) (secondary), and [Google states it is free to set up](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/). Because the profile is free and public, most competitors in a dense city already have one. The question is how complete it is.

One vendor reports that [Google Business Profile signals represented 32% of local pack ranking weight in 2025](https://adwave.com/resources/multi-location-local-seo-audit) (secondary). Treat that as a benchmark, not a rule. Its underlying source is not named in the excerpt, so use it to prioritise profile work rather than to forecast rankings.

Your profile also feeds other places. Google says [data for location assets used in Google Ads is sourced directly from Google Maps and Google Business Profile](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en), so corrections you make there flow into linked ad accounts.

## Which Business Profile fields should a pet groomer complete first?

Start with the facts customers check before they book. One vendor says businesses should make [their hours, phone number, and address 100% accurate](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers) (secondary). A wrong opening time on a Saturday grooming slot costs bookings even when your ranking is strong.

The business description allows [750 characters](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack) (secondary). Name your service, your Chicago neighbourhood and one differentiator, such as mobile grooming or anxious-dog handling. Write it for owners, not for search engines.

If you groom from home or travel to clients, the profile offers an option to [hide your physical street address](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack) (secondary). Google then treats you as a service area business. Check that your service area matches the neighbourhoods you actually drive to.

## How many photos and posts does a pet groomer need?

Vendor advice varies. One Adwave page recommends [at least 15 high-quality photos](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers) (secondary), while another suggests [at least 10](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack) (secondary). Aim for the higher number. Show clean dogs after grooming, the workspace, and the van if you run a mobile service. Get owner consent first.

Posts need a status check. Google says a post [shows publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en), and Pending or Not approved posts do not show. Publish an offer, a seasonal reminder or a service update, then confirm it reads as Live. Adwave advises [at least one new post a week](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers) (secondary).

Seed the Q&A section too. Google's Q&A launched in Maps in 2017, and a [transition summary reports that AI-driven answers draw on business profile data, website content, and reviews](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-q-and-a) (secondary). Your own answers become part of that input.

## Which Google Business Profile category should a Chicago pet groomer choose?

Pick the category by checking the competition. Adwave recommends reviewing [the top 3-5 businesses in the local map results for a service search](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones) (secondary) and noting which category each uses. Search your main service the way a client would, then compare.

The same vendor says in-depth analysis shows [the primary category is the #1 ranking factor for getting into the Local Pack](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones) (secondary), though the analysis source is not named. Set one primary category that matches your core service. Add secondary categories only where they describe services you deliver.

## How many reviews does a pet groomer need to compete in Chicago?

An analysis of [2 million Google Business Profile pages found that businesses with over 200 Google reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business) (secondary). That is a third-party analysis, so use 200 as a long-term benchmark. Another guide claims [over 50 reviews and a 4.7+ average rating dominate top map pack spots](https://accountabilitynow.net/ai-google-business-profile-ranking-for-landscapers/), but it cites no source.

Google says reviews [show next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122), so every review also works as a trust signal at the moment a client compares you with another groomer.

The table below shows what a steady request system produces. All inputs are assumptions for a typical shop.

| Item | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Grooms per month | 120 | assumption |
| Clients who leave a review after a text request | 10% | assumption |
| Reviews per month | 12 | calculated (120 × 10%) |
| Reviews per week | about 3 | calculated (12 ÷ 4.3) |
| Text tool cost | $30 per month | assumption |
| Staff time, 15 min per week at $25/hour | about $27 per month | assumption |
| Total monthly cost | $57 | calculated |
| Cost per review | about $4.75 | calculated ($57 ÷ 12) |
| Months to add 200 reviews at this pace | about 17 | calculated (200 ÷ 12) |

At this rate the review program costs under $5 per review but takes well over a year to reach the 200-review benchmark. Raise the request rate or the number of grooms to shorten that timeline.

## Which citations should a Chicago pet groomer fix first?

Citations are mentions of your name, address and phone number on other sites. One vendor says your NAP [must be exactly the same on your website, your GBP, and on major directories like Yelp, Apple Maps, and Bing Places](https://adwave.com/resources/voice-search-optimization-for-local-businesses) (secondary). Fix those listings before adding new ones.

Consistency includes small details. The same vendor's beginner guide says the NAP should [perfectly match your GBP listing, right down to the comma and whether you use "St." or "Street."](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack) (secondary). Pick one format for suite numbers, phone punctuation and street abbreviations, then use it everywhere.

Apple Maps also matters. One vendor reports that [Apple Business Connect has captured a 7% share in a maps-only comparison and is growing](https://adwave.com/resources/apple-business-connect-the-google-business-profile-alternative-youre-ignoring) (secondary). Claim your Apple listing and check that its details match your Google profile.

## How do you track whether local SEO is working?

Track the metrics that map to bookings. One vendor lists six useful local search metrics: [Google Business Profile impressions, map-pack views, clicks to call, direction requests, website visits from GBP, and branded search lift over time](https://adwave.com/resources/local-search-intent) (secondary). Record them monthly so you can compare the same period before and after changes.

Use Google Business Profile insights for profile activity and Search Console for website pages. Check your position for your top keywords with private-window searches or a rank tracker, and log the result. Expect the work to show first in profile views, then in map position.

## What should a 90-day local SEO plan look like?

Work through the profile before the website, because the profile drives the map pack. Use this sequence:

1. Check hours, phone and address on Google, Yelp, Apple Maps and Bing Places, and correct any mismatch.
2. Confirm your primary category against the top 3-5 map results for your main service.
3. Write a 750-character description that names your service and neighbourhood.
4. Upload at least 10 to 15 real photos, with owner consent for any dog shots.
5. Publish one post a week and confirm each shows as Live.
6. Set up a text or email request 24 hours after each appointment.
7. Reply to every review within 24 hours, naming your service and city.
8. Record the six tracking metrics monthly from day one.

Paid search can run alongside this. A [Marketing 360 case study of a mobile pet groomer](https://blog.marketing360.com/case-studies/pet-groomer-marketing-case-study-driving-success-with-digital-ads/) describes advertising on Facebook and Google (vendor case study). For a side-by-side view of those channels, read [Google Ads vs Facebook Ads for local businesses](/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-local-business-which-wins).

## FAQ

### Does a new Business Profile start ranking straight away?

Not necessarily. One vendor estimates that [you'll often start ranking for your own business name in the Map Pack a few weeks after getting your Google Business Profile verified](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack) (secondary). That is an estimate, not a guarantee. Broader map pack movement usually takes longer, so keep tracking monthly.

### Is a Google Business Profile free for a pet groomer?

Yes. Google describes its [Business Profile as free to set up](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/). You can add paid tools later, but the listing itself costs nothing.

### Do reviews appear on the profile or only on the website?

Reviews appear on the profile. Google states that reviews [show next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122). Respond to each one, because the replies also show next to the listing.
