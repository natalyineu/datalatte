## How Often Should Scheduled Posts Go Out?

Hootsuite's research, as summarised by [Salon Biz Software](https://salonbizsoftware.com/blog/daily-salon-social-media-marketing-ideas-how-to-engage-your-audience/) (secondary), recommends posting to Instagram 2-7 times per week. The [Bellwether Coffee guide](https://bellwethercoffee.com/blog/social-media-marketing-for-coffee-shops) suggests three to five feed posts a week for a coffee shop, plus daily Stories. Either range is manageable with a monthly batch.

Consistency matters more than volume. [Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/) states that salons posting consistently attract 3 to 5 times more inquiries than those posting sporadically. A queue keeps the rhythm steady when the week gets busy.

Do not start too fast. [Appointy's fitness guide](https://blog.appointy.com/2020/12/11/social-media-marketing-for-fitness-studio-gym/) advises against more than 2-3 posts a day early on, because high volume can curb the reach of earlier posts while the follower count is small.

## Which Times Should Scheduled Posts Go Live?

Bookeo cites platform research listing the best Instagram posting times as Mondays 2 to 4 p.m., Tuesdays 1 to 7 p.m., Wednesdays 12 to 9 p.m., and Thursdays 12 to 2 p.m. A scheduler lets you hit these windows without being online at the time.

Treat these as starting points rather than rules. Check your own audience data after a month of posts, and compare reach by day and hour. [Our guide to social media analytics for local businesses](/blog/ai-powered-social-media-analytics-for-local-businesses) shows how to read those numbers without a spreadsheet.

## What Do Scheduling Tools Cost?

Predis.ai's Rise plan is the only price confirmed in the sources for this guide. At $40 a month it includes auto-posting and publishing to 20 channels, with a set number of AI credits. Sintra also notes that Soshie produces platform-specific posts for Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn, which saves rewriting captions for each network.

Other schedulers publish their own prices, which change often. Check each vendor's pricing page before you commit, and compare the free tier first. Organic posting carries no platform fee: Bookeo states that Facebook and Instagram can reach audiences and build trust without spending a dollar on the platform itself. [The Small Business Expo](https://www.thesmallbusinessexpo.com/blog/best-local-advertising-for-small-business-are/) makes the same point, describing Instagram as a free stage for showing your business every day.

Context helps when budgeting. [The Restaurant HQ](https://www.therestauranthq.com/operations/coffee-shop-marketing/) notes that posting on social media costs only time, while other marketing options can run upwards of $1,500 a month.

## Can You Schedule Posts Without a Paid Tool?

Often yes. Instagram and Meta publish their own scheduling options, and the details change with account type and app version. Check Instagram's help pages for whether your account can schedule posts in the app before paying for anything.

Ads are a separate job. Adobe Express notes that Meta Ads Manager (formerly Facebook Ads Manager) can run ads across Instagram and Facebook from one place. Scheduling organic posts and running paid ads are different tasks, and a free scheduler will not run ad campaigns.

## What Should You Not Automate?

Do not automate replies. Direct messages, comments and bookings need a person, and [our Instagram DM automation guide](/blog/instagram-dm-automation-local-business) covers where automation helps and where it creates risk. Keep an eye on time-sensitive posts too. A scheduled post about a closed day or a sold-out item damages trust fast.

Location tags need a manual check. [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/marketing/local-marketing/?bc=OTH) notes that Instagram offers geotagging to tag a location when posting, so confirm the tag on each post before it goes out. Link the account from your Google Business Profile as well. Google allows one social media link per platform, including Instagram, in the [Business Profile help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617).

For content ideas that fit a batch, see [our guide to local business social media content creation](/blog/local-business-social-media-content-creation).

## What Does Automation Save? A Worked Example

All inputs below are **assumptions** for one local business with one Instagram account.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Posts per week | 4 (assumption) |
| Manual time per post, including photo edit and caption | 15 minutes (assumption) |
| Weeks per month | 4.3 (assumption) |
| Batch session per month | 2 hours (assumption) |
| Scheduling tool price | $40 per month (Predis.ai Rise, sourced) |
| Value of one hour of owner time | $25 (assumption) |

Manual time: 4 posts × 4.3 weeks × 15 minutes = 258 minutes, or about 4.3 hours per month. Batched time is 2 hours, so about 2.3 hours are saved. At $25 an hour, that is about $57.50 of time value. Against a $40 tool, the net gain is about $17.50 a month, so the tool pays back only if those saved hours are real.

At two posts a week, manual time falls to about 2.15 hours a month. The batch session of 2 hours then saves only 0.15 hours, worth about $3.75, which does not cover a $40 tool. In that case a free option or native scheduling is enough. Run the same sum with your own posting frequency and hourly value before deciding.

## FAQ

### Can I schedule Instagram posts for free?

Often yes, through Instagram's own options or a free tier from a scheduling vendor. Check the current help pages, because free limits change.

### How many posts should a local business schedule each week?

Hootsuite's research, as summarised by Salon Biz Software (secondary), recommends 2-7 Instagram posts a week. A coffee shop guide suggests three to five feed posts plus daily Stories.

### What does a paid scheduling tool cost?

Predis.ai's Rise plan costs $40 a month, according to Sintra (secondary). Other vendors' prices change, so check each pricing page.

### Should I automate replies to comments and messages?

No. Replies need a person, and automated responses can damage trust. Keep a weekly review of scheduled posts and check messages daily.
