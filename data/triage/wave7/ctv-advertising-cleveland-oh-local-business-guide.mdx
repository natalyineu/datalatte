Connected TV (CTV) advertising lets a Cleveland, OH business run video ads inside streaming TV content on the screen in the living room. According to [Paramount Ads Manager's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions), CTV ads are non-skippable video advertisements that typically run 15 or 30 seconds and appear full-screen. Inventory is usually bought programmatically, so an automated system matches your targeting criteria to available streaming audiences. This guide explains the formats, platforms, pricing, a worked test budget, and how to measure whether the campaign is working.

## What makes a CTV ad different from other local video?

CTV stands for Connected TV, a television that connects to the internet and allows viewers to stream content. [Simpli.fi](https://simpli.fi/latest/what-is-ctv) defines CTV advertising as video ads delivered through streaming content on internet-connected screens, including smart TVs and devices such as Roku, Fire TV and gaming consoles. [Teads](https://www.teads.com/blog/what-is-connected-tv-advertising/9858/) (secondary) describes Connected TV as any television that connects to the internet to stream digital content.

The main difference from social or search video is the viewing environment. [Priority Media Agency](https://prioritymediaagency.co.uk/services/tv-tvod-streaming) notes that VOD ads appear on streaming services, catch-up TV and connected devices, unlike traditional TV. Because many streaming services do not allow ads to be skipped, [Amazon Ads](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/connected-tv-advertising) reports that the video completion rate of CTV ads tends to be very high.

## Which streaming platforms should Cleveland businesses consider?

The platforms named for this market are Hulu, Peacock, Tubi and Paramount+ (which includes Pluto TV). They differ in audience, inventory and ad options, so compare them before committing budget.

Two inventory types matter when you read platform pages. Ad-supported video-on-demand (AVOD) services are, in [Paramount's definition](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions), free or lower-cost streaming services supported by ads. Free ad-supported streaming TV (FAST) channels are free for viewers and supported entirely by advertising, with Pluto TV given as an example. Amazon's streaming TV ads are also currently bought programmatically, which means an automated system buys and sells them.

Check each platform's current ad products and minimums on its live help page, because these change and this guide does not list them.

## How is CTV priced and bought?

Most CTV buying is programmatic. [Programmatic advertising](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions) uses automated technology to buy and serve ads in real time, with the platform purchasing inventory that matches advertiser-set criteria. [The TV Agency](https://thetvagency.com/insights/connected-tv-ads-in-the-uk/) (secondary) states that Sky's programmatic platform, Sky Advance, works on a CPM basis, meaning you pay per thousand impressions.

Two other metrics appear in CTV pricing. Cost per completed view (CPCV) measures the price of each completed ad, and it is used alongside video completion rate (VCR), according to [Amazon Ads](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/connected-tv-advertising).

For a sense of scale, [Adwave's Netflix guide](https://adwave.com/resources/netflix-advertising-small-business) gives one illustration: at programmatic CPMs of $40, a $200 to $400 monthly test delivers approximately 5,000 to 10,000 impressions across Netflix and other streaming platforms. That is one vendor's example, not a rate card, so request current CPMs from any platform you consider.

## What should a 15-second CTV ad include?

Open strong. [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions) recommends a strong opening hook within the first 3 to 5 seconds to engage streaming viewers. Your brand name, the benefit and the offer all need to land quickly, because a viewer who is watching a show will not rewind to catch your message.

Specs vary by platform. [Beasley's CTV page](https://advertiseintampa.com/services/advertising/ctv-ott/) (secondary) notes that every streaming giant sets its own rules for resolution, frame rate, bitrate and format. Confirm the spec sheet for each platform before you produce the file.

Interactive formats also exist. [Division D](https://divisiond.com/interactive-ctv/) describes interactive CTV advertising as connected TV ad formats that let viewers engage directly with ad messages on the screen. Check whether your chosen platform offers these before building a creative around them.

## How should a Cleveland business set up targeting?

Targeting in programmatic CTV is a set of criteria you define in the buying platform. The system then purchases inventory that matches those criteria, as [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions) explains. For a local business, the practical question is where your customers live and what they are likely to watch.

Start with a narrow geographic and audience definition, then widen only if the test shows reach without response. If you want to compare location-based approaches, the [geofencing guide](/blog/geofencing-advertising-local-business-guide) covers how location targeting works in other formats.

Write your criteria down before launch. A short document listing geography, audience assumptions and the single action you want viewers to take makes it easier to judge results later.

## What does a test budget look like?

The example below uses assumptions, not verified Cleveland rates. The CPM is set to the $40 figure from the Adwave example, and the completion rate is an assumed value because no source gives one.

Inputs (all assumptions):
- Monthly budget: $200 or $400
- Programmatic CPM: $40
- Completion rate: 90%

Calculation: impressions = budget ÷ CPM × 1,000. Completed views = impressions × completion rate. Cost per completed view = budget ÷ completed views.

| Monthly budget (assumption) | Impressions at $40 CPM (assumption) | Completed views at 90% (assumption) | Cost per completed view |
|---|---|---|---|
| $200 | 5,000 | 4,500 | $0.044 |
| $400 | 10,000 | 9,000 | $0.044 |

Replace the assumed CPM and completion rate with the platform's actual quotes and reporting. The same formula then shows whether a proposal is priced reasonably before you spend.

## What steps should you take before launch?

1. Confirm the current ad specifications on the platform's help page, since requirements differ by platform.
2. Script a 15-second or 30-second ad with the brand and hook in the first 3 to 5 seconds.
3. Define geography and audience criteria inside the buying platform and save them.
4. Get current CPM and minimum spend in writing from each platform you are considering.
5. Set a test budget and decide how you will track results before the first impression runs.
6. Put a review date on the calendar and do not judge the campaign in the first week.

## How do you measure whether CTV is working?

Track two layers. Campaign metrics include VCR and CPCV, which [Amazon Ads](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/connected-tv-advertising) describes as core CTV pricing measures. Business metrics show whether people are responding. Watch branded search volume, direct website traffic during the flight, and a simple "how did you hear about us?" question on calls and forms.

CTV tends to build recognition rather than clicks, so do not expect the same direct response as a search ad. For a fuller framework on connecting spend to outcomes, see the [marketing ROI guide](/blog/how-to-track-marketing-roi-local-business-guide).

## Where does CTV fit alongside search and mobile?

CTV works best as one part of a local plan. It introduces your business to a household, and search or map listings capture the customer when they look for a provider. Keep your search ads and Google Business Profile accurate so people who saw the TV ad can find you quickly.

Mobile placements are a separate channel with their own rules. The [in-app advertising guide](/blog/in-app-advertising-local-business-guide) covers that option, so you can compare it with CTV before choosing where to spend.

## FAQ

### Is CTV advertising skippable?

No. [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions) describes CTV ads as non-skippable video advertisements that typically run 15 or 30 seconds.

### What is the difference between AVOD and FAST?

AVOD services are free or lower-cost streaming services supported by ads. FAST channels are free for viewers and supported entirely by advertising, with Pluto TV as an example, according to [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions).

### How much does CTV cost per thousand impressions?

CTV is commonly bought on a CPM basis, as [The TV Agency](https://thetvagency.com/insights/connected-tv-ads-in-the-uk/) (secondary) describes for Sky Advance. Rates vary by platform and audience, so ask each seller for a current quote instead of relying on a single figure.

### How long should a CTV test run?

Set the test window before launch and measure against it. This guide does not give a fixed duration because no source supports one. Use your calendar and budget to choose a window long enough to collect the data you planned to track.
