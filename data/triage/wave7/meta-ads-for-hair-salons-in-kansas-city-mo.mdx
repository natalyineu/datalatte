## How Much Should a Kansas City Hair Salon Spend on Meta Ads?

Bookeo, a vendor that sells salon software and publishes marketing guides, suggests starting Facebook and Instagram ad spend at USD 750 to USD 1,500 monthly. Adwave's channel comparison lists a standalone Meta buy at $300 to $1,000 per month for small businesses in general. The two ranges overlap at the lower end, so a salon testing paid social can reasonably begin around $750 and treat $300 as a floor.

Neither source publishes figures for Kansas City specifically. Use these as national planning ranges and replace them with your own cost data after the first month. Bookeo also notes that Facebook and Instagram can reach audiences and build trust without spending a dollar on the platform itself, so organic posting remains a separate, free option alongside paid spend.

## Which Meta Ad Formats Work Best for Hair Salons?

Short video is the format with the strongest support in the sources. Adwave cites Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, as quoted by the vendor, saying short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram. Film real services in your own chair, and keep each clip short enough to make the point in a few seconds.

Stories are a second format to consider. Associated Hair Professionals describes Instagram and Facebook stories as temporary content that top influencers use to share parts of their lives. Bookeo says most salon owners find three to four Instagram posts and one to two Facebook posts per week sustainable, which gives you a workable rhythm for organic content. For that side of the plan, see [Instagram marketing for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons).

## How Do You Target Local Clients Within a Radius?

Set the radius first. Bookeo advises a 5 to 10 mile radius around the salon for Facebook and Instagram ads. Start inside that range and narrow it only after your bookings show which areas respond. Avoid widening it past the range without a reason you can measure.

Meta Ads Manager is the tool for this. Adobe Express describes it as a central point to run ads across Instagram and Facebook, and Turrboo notes it also covers Messenger. Because Instagram is owned by Meta, one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook, or both. Check the placements you select in the setup screen. For a broader look at Facebook setup, see [Facebook ads for hair salons](/blog/facebook-ads-for-hair-salons).

## What Should You Check Before Launching Meta Ads?

Work through these steps in order before spending:

1. Confirm you have a business account. Bookeo says the peak times when followers are online appear in the Instagram or Facebook Insights tab only with a business account.
2. Use those peak times to schedule your posts, starting from three to four Instagram posts and one to two Facebook posts a week.
3. Set the location radius to 5 to 10 miles around the salon.
4. Choose one service to promote and one clear booking action for the ad.
5. Confirm the placements in Ads Manager, which covers Facebook, Instagram, and Messenger.
6. Record every booking by ad, so you can compare the worked example with your real click-to-booking rate.

Check Meta's live help pages for current setup screens, since menu names change.

## Should a Salon Use Meta Ads or Google Ads?

Meta reaches people while they browse, while Google reaches people already searching. Trafft gives "hair salon near me" and "nail salon in [city]" as examples of the searches people run on Google. A practical approach is to run both at the same monthly budget and compare bookings by source after a few weeks.

Search intent is a different buying moment, so the ad copy should match it. ## FAQ

### Is a USD 750 monthly budget enough for a salon?

It is the starting point Bookeo suggests, and Adwave's standalone Meta range runs from $300 to $1,000. The worked example above shows what that spend could return under assumed rates. Measure your own results before increasing it.

### Does brand awareness on Facebook lead to more bookings?

Bookeo claims a client who has seen a salon's brand on Facebook 5 to 10 times converts at 2 to 3 times the rate of a cold searcher, but the page gives no source. Treat that figure as unverified. Test it by tracking bookings from people who saw your ads before.

### Is there free Meta advertising training?

A secondary source, a Nigerian digital marketing blog, states that Meta's Blueprint certification covering Facebook and Instagram advertising is offered at no cost. Confirm current terms on Meta's own site before you plan around it.

### Can one campaign run on both Facebook and Instagram?

Yes. Adwave says Instagram ads run through the same Meta Ads Manager system as Facebook ads, and one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook, or both.
