## What Are Shoppers Planning to Spend on Mother's Day?

NRF's 2023 survey found 84% of Americans planned to celebrate Mother's Day, and consumers planned to spend a record $35.7 billion. The expected average was a record $274.02, and consumers planned to spend $28 more on Mother's Day purchases, according to the NRF figures reported by [StackAdapt](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/mothers-day) (secondary).

For a local shop, two parts of the survey matter most. Almost 31% planned to take mom out to brunch, dinner or another special outing. Also, 44% planned to use promotions to fund their gifts, while 34% planned to cut back in other areas, so a visible offer has a chance to win some of that budget.

A separate EMARKETER figure, cited by StackAdapt, says 89% of consumers want to see more specific deals or promotions over the holidays. These are national US figures from 2023. They are not a forecast for your area, and readers in other markets should check local data, such as the [New Zealand small business guide](/blog/local-marketing-new-zealand-small-business-guide).

## Which Offers Suit a Local Business?

Match the offer to what you sell and to what a customer would buy as a gift. A cafe or restaurant can build a brunch set menu with a pre-booked table, since 31% of shoppers planned an outing. A salon or spa can sell a gift card or a bundled treatment, and a fitness studio can sell a class pass that someone else can use.

| Business | Offer to test | Check before launch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cafe or restaurant | Brunch set menu with pre-booked table | Seats, prep time, deposit rules |
| Salon or spa | Gift card or treatment bundle | Expiry date, price per treatment |
| Fitness studio | Class pass for a family member | Class capacity, booking limits |
| Pet groomer or retail shop | Gift card or small gift bundle | Stock levels, gift wrap |

Keep the offer simple, with a clear price and a clear end date. A pre-paid bundle or gift card brings money in before the day, which can matter more than a percentage off. Test one offer first rather than five.

## How Early Should You Start Promoting?

AdWave advises booking Valentine's Day and Mother's Day promotions 4 to 6 weeks out, according to its [restaurant advertising guide](https://adwave.com/resources/restaurant-advertising-complete-marketing-guide) (secondary). Its [2026 small business trends piece](https://adwave.com/resources/2026-summer-advertising-trends-small-business) (secondary) says retail and service businesses are starting holiday promotions 7 to 14 days earlier than they did in 2024, so attention is being contested sooner.

The sequence below is a suggestion built on that four-to-six-week window. It is not a research finding. Count back from your own Mother's Day date.

- Six weeks out: choose the offer, set the price and build the booking or gift card page.
- Four weeks out: announce by email, on your listings and on social, with a last order date.
- One to two weeks out: send reminders and confirm stock and the staff rota.
- The weekend: post last-chance reminders and update your opening hours.
- The week after: send a thank-you email with a rebooking offer.

## How Do You Promote It Without a Big Budget?

Start with email to people who have already booked or bought from you, then add your listings. According to [iCatch Marketing](https://www.icatchgroup.com/google-ads-vs-yelp-ads/) (secondary), Yelp Connect lets businesses publish seasonal promotions directly to followers and prospective customers, so a Yelp presence can carry the offer too.

Put the details in every post: name the item, the price and the cutoff date. Google's [guidance on reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122) (primary) says businesses should avoid using review replies to offer deals or promotions, so keep offers out of your replies and place them in posts or on your own channels.

If you also run paid search, the [Germany guide on small business Google Ads](/blog/local-marketing-germany-small-business-google-ads) shows how the same kind of offer can be set up in a different market. Compare the cost of each channel against the bookings it brings in.

## What Could a Brunch Promotion Earn?

This worked example uses invented inputs, all labelled as assumptions, to show how to test an offer before you commit. Replace each input with your own costs.

| Item | Assumption or calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set menu price | Assumption | $38 per person |
| Food cost | Assumption: 30% of price | $11.40 per person |
| Seats offered | Assumption | 60 |
| Seats booked | Assumption: 80% of seats | 48 |
| Gross margin | 48 × ($38 − $11.40) | $1,276.80 |
| Promotion cost (email and boosted post) | Assumption | $100 |
| Net before labour | $1,276.80 − $100 | $1,176.80 |

If only half the seats sell (30 seats), gross margin falls to $798 and net to $698 before any extra staff. Taking pre-payments or deposits means empty tables cost you less of the margin, and it is the same logic a salon or spa would apply to a treatment booking.

## FAQ

### Are these figures for this year?

No. The NRF figures used here come from its 2023 survey. They describe national US spending in that year and do not forecast your sales.

### How much do people spend on Mother's Day?

In NRF's 2023 survey, consumers expected to spend a record average of $274.02 per person, and planned spending totalled $35.7 billion. Those are national figures across all gifts, not a benchmark for a brunch or salon booking.

### Should I offer a discount or a bundle?

A pre-paid bundle or gift card brings money in before the day, while a percentage off lowers the price each customer pays. Test one approach at a time and compare the results against your own bookings.

### Can I mention a deal in my Google review replies?

Google advises against it. Its [review guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122) (primary) says to avoid using your response to offer deals or promotions, so put offers in posts or on your own channels.

### How early should I start?

AdWave recommends booking Mother's Day promotions 4 to 6 weeks out. Track when your own orders arrive and adjust the timing for next year.
