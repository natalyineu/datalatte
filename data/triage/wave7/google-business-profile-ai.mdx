AI helps most with drafting: post text, first-pass review replies and summaries of what customers say. It cannot confirm your business details, and it only works from the facts you give it. Accuracy matters more than speed here. A 2026 index from SOCi found that only 68% of business information shown by AI tools matches the Google Business Profile, so a wrong hour or address can spread through AI answers. Keep the facts under your control, read every draft, and keep your website accurate too.

## Where Do AI Answers Get Business Information?

Business websites are the largest source. In the [Cheers Market Baseline](https://www.cheers.tech/geo-academy/is-google-business-profile-enough-for-ai-visibility), business websites supplied 68.1% of pooled AI citations over the 28 days ending September 2, 2026, while Google surfaces supplied 3.3%. The figures cover 49,677 local-intent runs and 356,019 citations across 100 metros and four engines. Cheers corrected them on September 23, 2026, so check the current version before you quote them.

These numbers do not make the profile unimportant. They show that AI tools lean on your website as well, so the name, hours and services on the site must match the profile exactly. Google's Search Central guidance on keeping Business Profile information current for AI search was updated July 10, 2026, according to Cheers. For a site-level plan, read the [generative engine optimization guide for local businesses](/blog/generative-engine-optimization-local-business).

## Which Profile Fields Should You Keep Under Control?

Keep the factual fields in your own hands: business name, address, phone number, opening hours and services. These are the details AI tools repeat, and the SOCi figure shows how often they drift from the profile. Write them once in a master sheet, enter them in the profile yourself, and give AI only that sheet when it drafts copy. AI should never fill a gap with a guess.

Google renamed [Google My Business](https://www.pilateseducationinstitute.com/optimizing-your-pilates-studios-local-listing-and-google-business-profile/) to Google Business Profile in 2021, so older guides and prompts may still use the old name. Search for "GMB" in every draft before it goes live. Retailers in the US, Canada, the UK and Ireland can showcase in-store products on the profile, so product lists belong on your checklist. Google's Canadian profile page also says businesses can enable 24/7 online booking from the profile. ## How Can AI Draft Posts Without Losing Control?

Posting is the easiest task to automate. A public n8n template, for example, reads topics from a Google Sheet and uses OpenAI's GPT-4 to write a local-business post. Its sheet has Topic, Status, Date and Notes columns. The Notes column holds internal information that the automation does not use. That is a useful pattern: keep private context out of anything AI reads, and add a status column of your own so a person marks each draft approved before it goes live.

Write the topic list yourself, because a [coffee shop](/blog/coffee-shop-google-business-profile-optimization) and a [fitness studio](/blog/fitness-studio-google-business-profile-optimization) need very different post topics. Ask the AI for a draft using only the topic, your approved facts and the tone you want, then edit it. Check every price or offer in the draft against your own records, because a published post is public immediately.

## Do Review Replies Affect Local Visibility?

Reviews carry real weight. Mailchimp describes reviews on a Google My Business profile as especially influential and says they play a role in local search ranking. Google says local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, and that review count and rating can factor into local ranking, as Cheers reports. The sources do not name the reply itself as a ranking factor, so treat replies as customer service first.

Local Services Ads is a separate case. A 2026 [Ryze guide](https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/google-local-services-ads-reporting-tool) states that the program penalises businesses with poor responsiveness by reducing profile visibility. If you run those ads, a slow reply has a direct cost. AI can draft replies in seconds, but a person should check tone and facts before posting, and the live Google help pages should set the rules for your team.

## FAQ

### Do the sources here ban AI-written profile text?

They do not address AI-written text. Check Google's live Business Profile help pages before setting a policy, and make sure every AI-written line matches your verified facts.

### Can AI reply to reviews automatically?

Automation is possible, but an unread reply can repeat wrong details or read like a template. Review each draft before it posts.

### Does AI improve local ranking?

None of the sources used here show that. Google says relevance, distance and prominence drive local results, and review count and rating can factor in.

### What should you never automate?

Keep the factual fields, such as name, address, phone number, hours and services, under your own control and check them against your website. Also check the live Google help pages before setting any review rule.
