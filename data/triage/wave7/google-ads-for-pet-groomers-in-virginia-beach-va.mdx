## What Does a Dog Groomer Click Cost?

AdTurbo puts dog groomer clicks at about $2–$8 and warns that a few wasted clicks add up fast. That spread is wide. A groomer in a quiet area and one competing with several shops on the same searches can pay very different prices, so the range is a planning input, not a quote.

The same vendor says most dog groomer advertisers spend $800–$5,000 a month. A small local account usually sits well below the top of that range, and that is fine. Spend should follow bookings, not a benchmark.

A 2023 case study from Marketing 360 reports what one mobile pet groomer saw after advertising on Facebook and Google. Compared with the previous six months, impressions rose 134% and clicks rose 696%. The study gives percentage changes only, with no spend or booking totals, and it covers a mobile business, so read it as a direction.

## Where Should a Virginia Beach Groomer Target Ads?

Radius targeting keeps spend near the customers who can drive to the salon. A [Barklead guide on Facebook ads](https://barklead.com/post/using-facebook-ads-for-groomers) suggests targeting users within a 10–15 mile radius. That advice is for Facebook, so use it as a Google test starting point and widen only when cost per booking stays steady.

Nextdoor pages show how other Virginia Beach groomers present themselves. [Canine Cloud 9](https://nextdoor.com/pages/kanine-cloud-9-virginia-beach-va/) lists hours of 8:00 AM–6:00 PM on Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday, and 8:00 AM–4:00 PM on Sunday. [Blue Dog Pet Grooming](https://nextdoor.com/pages/blue-dog-pet-grooming/) lists 8:30 AM–6:00 PM on Tuesday and Wednesday. Check your own listed hours against what customers see.

A 2023 Nextdoor post from a couple who had just moved to Kempsville asked for a mini goldendoodle groomer. One post does not measure demand, but it suggests newcomers search soon after moving, so a "new to the area" ad variant is worth testing. The same radius approach is covered for another city in the [Long Beach pet groomer guide](/blog/google-ads-for-pet-groomers-in-long-beach-ca).

## Which Keywords Should a Pet Groomer Bid On?

AdTurbo lists "dog groomer near me", "dog grooming near me" and "puppy grooming near me" as keywords worth bidding on. These phrases combine the service with local intent, which is what a salon needs. Add only the services you actually offer, and keep the list short until the first bookings arrive.

Negative keywords need the same attention. GroomerGenie says its proprietary list holds more than 500 groomer negatives. That is a vendor claim, but it shows how much unrelated traffic a broad account can attract. Build your own list from searches for jobs, training courses, supplies and do-it-yourself grooming, and review the search terms report weekly.

## Which Conversion Should You Track?

AdTurbo names booking a grooming appointment as the primary conversion for dog groomer Google Ads. A click or a page view says little about revenue, so make the online booking the main conversion. Keep calls and contact forms as secondary signals so you can see where the phone rings.

Timing matters as well. AdWave, citing Groomer to Groomer, notes that November and December are traditionally a very busy time for groomers, sometimes too busy. If a shop is already full, lower the budget before the rush rather than paying for clicks it cannot serve. You can compare lead-based options in the [Google Local Services Ads guide for pet groomers](/blog/google-local-services-ads-for-pet-groomers).

## What Should the First 30 Days Look Like?

1. Confirm the online booking page is set as the primary conversion, with calls and forms tracked separately.
2. Set a radius around the salon and test one location before widening it.
3. Start with the three search phrases from AdTurbo plus the services you offer.
4. Add negatives for jobs, courses, supplies and do-it-yourself grooming.
5. Set a daily budget of about $13 (assumption) and review the search terms report every week.
6. Compare cost per booking with the table above, and raise spend only when that figure holds.

## FAQ

### How much should a Virginia Beach pet groomer spend on Google Ads?
Most advertisers in the AdTurbo benchmark spend $800–$5,000 a month, a wide range. Start with a test budget such as the $400 example above, and raise it only when cost per booking stays steady.

### Which conversion should a groomer track first?
Booking a grooming appointment. AdTurbo names it as the primary conversion for dog groomer ads. Track calls and contact forms as secondary signals.

### Should I target all of Virginia Beach?
Usually not at first. Start with a radius around the salon. The 10–15 mile figure comes from a Facebook guide, so treat it as a test point for Google, not a rule.

### Are negative keywords necessary?
For a small budget, yes. GroomerGenie claims a list of more than 500 groomer negatives, but a list built from your own unrelated search terms will do more for your shop.
