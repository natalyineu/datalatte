## What does a Meta Ads budget look like for a Dallas salon?

Vendor ranges differ by source. Bookeo's starting range for Facebook and Instagram is $750 to $1,500 per month. [AdWave's channel guide](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-choose-advertising-channels) puts a standalone Meta buy at often $300 to $1,000 per month, and its [restaurant guide](https://adwave.com/resources/restaurant-advertising-complete-marketing-guide) gives $500 to $2,000 per month for single-location businesses. None of these figures is set for hair services in Dallas.

AdWave also notes that Meta [takes weeks to optimize](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-small-business-2026), so judge a budget over several weeks, not days. For a wider comparison of platform spend, read [Facebook ads for hair salons](/blog/facebook-ads-for-hair-salons).

## How do you check whether a Meta Ads budget pays back?

The example below uses inputs labelled assumption, plus one sourced range. AdWave gives typical Meta costs of $5 to $15 per thousand impressions and $1 to $3 per click, which are the sourced inputs.

Assumptions: monthly budget of $750 (assumption, the low end of Bookeo's range), average client ticket of $60 (assumption), and a 5% click-to-booking rate (assumption).

Break-even is $750 ÷ $60 = 12.5, so about 13 bookings a month. Clicks at $1 to $3 each give $750 ÷ $3 = 250 clicks up to $750 ÷ $1 = 750 clicks. At 5%, that is 12.5 to 37.5 bookings, or $750 to $2,250 in revenue at $60 per visit.

| Monthly budget | Impressions at $15 CPM | Impressions at $5 CPM |
|---|---|---|
| $300 | 20,000 | 60,000 |
| $750 | 50,000 | 150,000 |
| $1,500 | 100,000 | 300,000 |

The table is arithmetic from the sourced CPM range, not a forecast. At the $3 click cost, the example barely covers its spend. At $1 per click, it returns about three times the budget. Your own click cost and booking rate decide which outcome you get, so measure both before you scale.

## Which ad formats work best for hair salons?

Short-form video earns more engagement than static images. Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, as cited by AdWave, puts short video at 2.5 times the engagement of static images on Facebook and Instagram. Film real work in your chairs: colour processes, cut steps and finished looks from your own team, not stock photography.

Stories are temporary by design, as [Associated Hair Professionals](https://www.associatedhairprofessionals.com/updates/blog-posts/power-social-media-your-hairstyling-brand) notes, so use them for time-limited messages such as same-week openings. Keep evergreen service menus in feed posts or carousels, where they stay visible longer.

## How wide should your Dallas targeting radius be?

Bookeo advises setting a 5 to 10 mile radius around the salon for Facebook and Instagram ads. That is a sensible starting point across a metro as large as Dallas, and it keeps the audience within a realistic travel distance for a haircut or colour appointment.

If your salon sits in a dense part of the metro, test a tighter radius against the 5 to 10 mile default and compare cost per booking. Use the audience estimate in Ads Manager to check how many people your radius reaches before you set the budget.

## Who should you target with Meta Ads in Dallas?

Start with the local radius and broad targeting, then let booking data narrow the audience. Age ranges and interest choices should follow your own client records, not a generic segment, because the right mix depends on which services fill your chairs.

Once you have a client list, upload it to build a custom audience of past clients, and use it to build a lookalike audience of similar people nearby. Meta Ads Manager runs ads on Facebook, Instagram and Messenger from one place, as [Turrboo](https://turrboo.com/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media) and [Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-advertising) both describe, so one campaign can cover several placements.

## Which ad copy should you test first?

Test copy that names your area and a specific service. Three drafts to adapt: "Book your balayage in [city] this week," "New cut, new week: book a first visit in [city]," and "See why [city] clients keep coming back to our chair." Replace the bracketed parts with your own details before you publish.

Calls to action are worth testing too. Compare "Book Now" against "Learn More" and a free first-visit consultation against a direct booking link, then keep the version with the lower cost per booking.

## How should you track Meta Ads bookings?

Before spending, install the Meta Pixel on your website and set a conversion event on the booking confirmation page. Add UTM parameters to every ad URL so your analytics can separate Meta traffic from other channels. Without these, you can only see clicks and impressions, not bookings.

Bookeo also advises checking peak follower activity in the Insights tab of Instagram or Facebook, which requires a business account. Use those peak times to schedule ads and posts. For follow-up after a booking request, [marketing automation for hair salons](/blog/marketing-automation-for-hair-salons) covers reminder and rebooking sequences.

## Should Meta Ads run alongside organic posting?

Yes, because paid and organic work best together. Bookeo says Facebook and Instagram can reach audiences and build trust without spending on the platform itself, and suggests most salon owners find three to four Instagram posts and one to two Facebook posts weekly sustainable. Use the ads to amplify your best organic posts, such as a client transformation that performed well. The [Instagram guide for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons) covers content planning in more detail.

## What mistakes should Dallas salons avoid with Meta Ads?

The most common error is spending without tracking bookings, which leaves you judging the campaign by impressions. A second error is running one ad creative for months, which lowers response over time, so refresh creative on a regular schedule. Third, targeting everyone within the metro rather than the radius and services you actually offer.

## FAQ

### What is a realistic starting budget for Meta Ads for a Dallas hair salon?

Bookeo suggests $750 to $1,500 per month, and AdWave lists a standalone Meta buy at often $300 to $1,000 per month. Start at the lower end until tracking is working, then increase spend only if cost per booking holds.

### How much does Meta advertising cost per 1,000 impressions?

AdWave gives a typical range of $5 to $15 per thousand impressions, and $1 to $3 per click. These are national vendor figures, so check your own delivery data in Ads Manager for Dallas.

### Do Instagram and Facebook ads run through the same system?

Yes. Instagram is owned by Meta, so its ads run through Meta Ads Manager, the same system as Facebook ads, and one campaign can run on either platform or both, as [AdWave's Instagram guide](https://adwave.com/resources/instagram-advertising-for-local-business) explains.

### Is there free training for Meta advertising?

A secondary source describes Meta's Blueprint courses for Facebook and Instagram advertising as free, with a certificate on completion. Check Meta's own Blueprint page for current course details before you enrol.
