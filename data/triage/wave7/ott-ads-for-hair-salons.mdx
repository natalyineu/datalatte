## Is Streaming TV Worth Testing for a Salon?

Streaming ads reach people while they watch shows, not while they look for a stylist. That makes them a reach tool rather than a booking tool. A single-chair barbershop with a full diary gains little from awareness it cannot convert. A salon opening a new location, or one with spare chairs for a specialist service, may gain more from being seen in a new area.

Before testing, check that the offer suits the screen. Colour transformations and other visible results work well in video. A standard trim is harder to show in a short clip. Prices vary by platform, season and audience, so get a live quote from each seller rather than relying on any single published figure.

## Why Start With Search and Your Profile?

Local search has the highest intent. The vendor RŌZ reports Google Ads keyword data from April 2026 estimating about 1.5 million U.S. searches per month for "hair salon near me", and about 110,000 for "hair stylist near me". These are people ready to book. RŌZ's comparison table lists a Google Business Profile and Google reviews as free, and says a salon owner can begin correcting missing or inconsistent profile details in about ten minutes.

Timing matters. A guide from The Local Gem, hosted on AdWave, says salons that complete and keep active their profile usually see better local visibility within 4 to 8 weeks. Set this up before any paid streaming test, because paid ads send people to the profile they find. Regular updates help, as explained in [Google Business Profile posts for hair salons](/blog/hair-salon-marketing-google-my-business-posts).

## Can a Campaign Stay Within Your Neighbourhood?

Not every platform offers it, so ask. Delivery that reaches viewers far outside your area is wasted spend for a salon that serves a few miles around its door. Ask every seller three things: whether targeting works by zip code, postcode or radius, what the minimum spend is, and whether they report where impressions were actually delivered.

Radius targeting is common on social platforms. Booksy's guide gives an example of a Meta ad aimed at newly engaged women within a 10-mile radius of a salon. For search, an Indian agency guide recommends a local radius of about 5 to 10 km around the salon, a starting point to adapt to your own catchment. If a streaming seller cannot limit delivery in a similar way, skip that seller.

## What Should the Ad Show?

Start with the work itself. Booksy says high-impact before and after videos capture attention in the first two seconds, so the opening frame must show the result. Zeely's guide recommends service demo videos of 15 to 30 seconds showing a treatment in action. End with the salon name, the area and one booking route, such as a short link or phone number used only for this campaign.

Get written permission before showing a client's face, and keep claims to what the work shows. Short clips can also run on social channels at lower cost, as covered in [Instagram marketing for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons).

## How Do You Measure and Keep New Clients?

Track each new client by asking how they heard about the salon, and give the campaign its own booking link or phone number. Compare bookings in the weeks before and after launch. Seasonal swings can distort that comparison, so note any holidays or promotions that ran at the same time.

Retention is where a new client pays back. Booksy's guide says engaged loyalty members visit salons 2.4x more often. It also states that email returns an average of £38 for every £1 spent in the beauty industry, a vendor claim rather than an independent study. ## FAQ

### Are OTT ads worth it for a hair salon?

Sometimes. They suit salons with a visual offer, spare capacity and a way to limit delivery to the local area. For most single-chair businesses, a complete Business Profile and local search come first.

### Can I limit OTT ads to one zip code?

Not on every platform. Ask each seller in writing whether targeting works by zip code, postcode or radius, and what the minimum spend is before you commit any budget.

### What creative works best for a salon?

Short clips that show a result early. Booksy says before and after videos capture attention in the first two seconds, and Zeely recommends service demos of 15 to 30 seconds. End with a clear booking route.

### How do I know if a streaming test worked?

Use a booking link or phone number that only the campaign uses, ask new clients how they found you, and compare new bookings before and after launch. Divide the spend by new clients to get a cost per client.
