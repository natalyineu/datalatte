## What decides whether a fitness studio appears in the Oklahoma City map pack?

The Map Pack shows the top three business listings in local search results ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-audit-checklist-15-things-to-fix-today), secondary). Those listings draw on the Business Profile, so the profile is the first place to work. One vendor analysis says the primary category is the top ranking factor for entering the Local Pack, though it does not name its data source ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones), secondary).

Google also draws on your website and reviews. Responses in Google's AI-driven answers, referred to as Ask Maps, are sourced from business profile data, website content, and reviews ([Accrisoft via Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-q-and-a), secondary). That means your profile, site, and review text should describe the same services in the same words.

Regional ranking data for Oklahoma City is not included here. Test your own searches in a private window and record where you appear, then compare that against the steps below.

## How do you set up a fitness studio's Google Business Profile?

Work through these steps in order:

1. Claim and verify the listing. One vendor reports that 11.1% of profiles are unclaimed, without naming a source ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary).
2. Choose the primary category by reviewing the top three to five businesses that appear for your service search and noting their categories ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones), secondary).
3. Write a business description of up to 750 characters that names your services and Oklahoma City neighbourhood ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).
4. Make your hours, phone number, and address 100% accurate ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary).
5. Upload photos. Vendor guidance ranges from at least 10 to at least 15 high-quality images ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary; [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary).
6. Publish at least one post a week. Posts show publicly only when their status is Live ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)).
7. Seed the Q&A section with the questions clients ask most. Google launched the feature in Maps in 2017 ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-q-and-a), secondary).
8. Use the Business Profile dashboard on a desktop to manage customer messages, not only the mobile app ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-messaging-should-you-turn-it-on), secondary).

For a fuller walkthrough of profile fields, see [our fitness studio Google Business Profile guide](/blog/fitness-studio-google-business-profile-optimization).

## How consistent should your name, address, and phone be across citations?

Your name, address, and phone (NAP) must match exactly on your website, your Business Profile, and major directories such as Yelp, Apple Maps, and Bing Places ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/voice-search-optimization-for-local-businesses), secondary). Small differences count. Google's own guidance is to match what appears on your listing, so an abbreviation such as "St." on one site and "Street" on another creates a mismatch ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).

Fix existing listings before adding new ones. Location data used in Google Ads comes directly from Google Maps and Business Profile, and corrections made there flow into linked accounts ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)). A wrong address therefore affects paid campaigns as well as organic results.

If you embed a map on your contact page, that adds a location signal to your site. See [how to embed Google Maps on a local business website](/blog/google-maps-embed-local-business-website).

## How many reviews does a fitness studio need in Oklahoma City?

Google states that reviews appear next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)), so each review is visible at the point of decision. A third-party analysis of 2 million Business Profile pages found that businesses with over 200 reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business), secondary).

A separate vendor claim says gyms with 100+ reviews and 4.7+ ratings dominate the map pack, and that a rating below 4.5 suppresses conversion ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-gyms-fitness-centers), secondary). Treat that as a vendor claim, not a measured threshold.

Ask for reviews that describe specific services and experiences. Review text is visible to readers, and the [Denver fitness studio review benchmarks](/blog/denver-fitness-studios-google-reviews-benchmarks-2026) show how review counts compare across a comparable market.

## What does a worked budget for fitness studio local SEO look like?

The example below uses assumption inputs throughout. Replace them with your own figures.

- Assumption: 60 Business Profile actions per month (calls, direction requests, website visits).
- Assumption: 1 in 10 actions becomes a trial class, giving 6 trials.
- Assumption: 1 in 3 trials joins at $120 per month, giving 2 new members.
- Assumption: an average member stays 6 months, so each member is worth $720 in revenue.
- Assumption: 4 hours per month of staff time on profile upkeep at $40 per hour, or $160.

Calculation: 2 members × $720 = $1,440 in revenue per month of acquired members. Dividing by the $160 time cost gives a 9x revenue-to-time ratio. This is revenue, not profit, and it ignores the free listing setup, which Google offers at no charge ([Google](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)).

Your own figures will change the result. The useful step is to replace each assumption with a tracked number after a month of data.

## How do you track whether local SEO is working?

Adwave names six useful local search metrics: Business Profile impressions, map-pack views, clicks to call, direction requests, website visits from the profile, and branded search lift over time ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-search-intent), secondary). Record these monthly, using the same definitions each time, so changes are comparable.

Pair the profile metrics with a fixed list of tracked searches. Check the same five to ten terms each week in a private window and log where your studio appears.

## FAQ

### How long does local SEO take to work for a fitness studio?

No timeline in this guide is sourced for Oklahoma City. Track your monthly profile metrics and map-pack position from the start, and judge progress against your own baseline rather than a fixed period.

### Do I need a physical address to appear in the map pack?

Not always. A business that serves customers at their location can hide its street address and be designated a Service Area Business ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary). A studio with a storefront should keep its address visible.

### Which is more important, reviews or the profile?

Both are signals. The profile sets your category and details, and reviews show the quality of your service. Vendors disagree on weighting, so treat any single percentage as an estimate.

### Should I use the Google Maps app or the desktop dashboard?

Use the desktop dashboard for managing customer messages, since it is available there and not only in the mobile app ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-messaging-should-you-turn-it-on), secondary).
