Local SEO for a fitness studio in Atlanta, GA comes down to appearing in the Google Map Pack for searches such as "personal training studios near me." The Map Pack lists relevant local businesses in the Map SERP features section, and your Google Business Profile is the free listing that feeds it, showing hours, phone numbers, addresses, reviews and photos directly in search results. This guide covers the profile, citations, reviews, Google Ads links and a worked budget example. The mechanics here come from Google's help pages and industry sources, none of which are specific to Atlanta, so test each step against your own profile data.

## How does the Google Map Pack decide which Atlanta studios appear?

The sources here do not publish a ranking formula. What they do show is the surface you compete on. The Map Pack appears for local queries and lists relevant businesses, as [How to Make Your Fitness Business Stand Out on Google](https://member.afsfitness.com/content/how-make-your-fitness-business-stand-out-google) describes. The article was published on May 10, 2024, so treat its details as a starting point and check Google's current pages.

Ahrefs, summarising a BrightLocal survey, reports that 36% of SEOs in 2021 rated Google Business Profile signals as the most important factor for the local pack, up from 23% in 2013 ([Ahrefs local SEO guide](https://ahrefs.com/seo/local-seo), secondary). That is an opinion survey, not a measured ranking weight. It is a reason to prioritise your profile, not a formula to optimise against.

## What belongs in a fitness studio's Google Business Profile?

Google describes its Business Profile as free to set up for appearing on Search and Maps ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)). Because it costs nothing, the bottleneck is completeness and upkeep, not budget. A full walkthrough for studios is in our [fitness studio profile optimisation guide](/blog/fitness-studio-google-business-profile-optimization).

Photos are the most concrete instruction the sources give. A vendor guide recommends uploading at least 15 authentic photos of the facility, classes in action and staff ([Vertical Impression](https://www.verticalimpression.com/guides/how-to-market-health-wellness-business-2026), secondary). Use real images from your own floor, not stock shots.

Posts need checking after you publish them. Google reviews each post, and it appears publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts do not show ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)). A post you assume is live may be invisible to searchers.

## How should you handle citations and NAP consistency?

Citations are mentions of your business name, address and phone number on other sites. The sources do not give a citation count or a directory list, so decide which listings to fix by checking where your studio already appears. Make the name, address and phone number identical everywhere, including abbreviations such as "St." versus "Street."

For multi-location operators, Google Ads uses data sourced directly from Google Maps and Google Business Profile. Corrections made there flow into linked Ads accounts ([About location assets, Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)). Fixing a wrong address once in the profile therefore helps both organic and paid results. Your site's map embed should match the same details; our [guide to embedding a Google Map](/blog/google-maps-embed-local-business-website) covers that step.

## How many reviews does a studio need to compete?

One vendor article states that businesses with over 50 reviews and a 4.7+ average rating dominate the top map pack spots, but it cites no source ([AI Google Business Profile Ranking for Landscapers 2026](https://accountabilitynow.net/ai-google-business-profile-ranking-for-landscapers/), secondary). Treat that threshold as a hypothesis to test in your own market, not a rule.

Reviews matter at the decision point. Google states that reviews show next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search ([Tips to get more reviews, Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)). A rating and review count are visible before a searcher taps through, so track both monthly. Check Google's current review guidance before running any request campaign. For ideas on drafting and organising responses, see our [AI for Google Business Profile optimisation guide](/blog/ai-for-google-business-profile-optimization).

## What does a worked budget example look like for a studio?

The table below uses assumed inputs to show how profile traffic can turn into revenue. None of the figures are benchmarks. Replace each assumption with your own profile insights and booking data.

| Step | Input | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly calls, direction requests and website clicks from the profile | 40 contacts (assumption) | 40 contacts |
| Contacts who book a trial | 25% (assumption) | 10 trials |
| Trials who join a membership | 40% (assumption) | 4 new members |
| Monthly membership fee | $150 per member (assumption) | $600 new monthly recurring revenue |
| Review-request texting tool | $200 per month (assumption) | $400 first-month net, before staff time |

The calculation is simple: 40 contacts times 25% gives 10 trials, and 10 trials times 40% gives 4 members. Four members at $150 equals $600 of new monthly recurring revenue. Subtracting the $200 tool cost leaves $400 in the first month. The result is only as good as the inputs, so measure the first two rows before trusting the rest.

## FAQ

### Is a Google Business Profile free for a fitness studio?

Yes. Google describes its Business Profile as free to set up on Search and Maps ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)).

### Do reviews appear on my profile in search?

Google states that reviews show next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)).

### Why is my Google Business Profile post not visible?

Posts appear publicly on Search and Maps only when their status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts do not show ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)).

### Can I link several studio locations to Google Ads?

You can select up to 10 Google Maps locations to link with your Ads account when Business Profile and chain store options do not work. The locations must be yours, or they may be disapproved ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)).
