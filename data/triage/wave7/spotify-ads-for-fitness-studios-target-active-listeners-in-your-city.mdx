## How Do Spotify Ads Target Listeners Near a Studio?

Ad Studio lets advertisers target by age and demographics, city and geography, devices and connectivity, genre, playlists and podcasts, predicted interests, off-platform behaviours, and past interactions. Spotify describes Ad Studio as its self-serve platform for building audio or video campaigns. For a studio, city and geography are the core settings, and the other options can narrow the audience once the local area is set.

Advertisers can also target by age, gender and location, according to a secondary vendor blog. Formats include audio, horizontal video and vertical video. The format choice is mainly a budget decision, since video needs more production work. For a wider look at how audio platforms compare, read [Spotify vs Pandora vs iHeart for local business](/blog/spotify-vs-pandora-vs-iheart-best-audio-ad-platform-local-business).

## How Many Listeners Can a Studio Reach, and Do They Pay Attention?

Spotify's podcast guide says it reaches over 115 million monthly podcast listeners in the US alone, and that more than 90% of US podcast listeners tune in through its network. Spotify also reports that 42% of its surveyed listeners trust their favourite host as much as their friends. A separate Spotify page says roughly one in four Americans, about 80 million people, are weekly podcast listeners.

Reach matters less than attention for a 30-second spot. Spotify says audio ads cannot be closed or swiped past, which helps recall. A secondary article citing Nielsen reports a 24% increase in ad recall from Spotify audio ads. A StackAdapt article citing Edison Research reports that 70% of monthly podcast listeners do nothing else while listening, but that figure comes from 2019 data and should be treated as dated.

## What Does a Spotify Audio Ad Cost?

Audio is usually priced as CPM, the cost per thousand impressions. A secondary guide puts Spotify audio CPM at $15 to $25, which it describes as higher than visual platforms. The same guide describes 15 to 30 second spots that play between songs and podcast episodes on the free tier.

Treat that range as an outside estimate, not a quote. The number to trust is the cost shown in your own Ad Studio draft campaign for your city or postal area. Set a test budget you can afford to lose for four weeks, then compare the result with the booking math below.

## Should a Studio Start with Spotify or Search?

Search reaches people who are already looking for a class. A fitness Google Ads guide sets a target cost per trial lead under $30 and a cost per converting member under $150 to $200. These are targets from a secondary source, not benchmarks, and a trial lead is not the same metric as a trial booking. Still, they give a starting point for comparing channels.

Audio tends to build awareness among people who have not searched yet, so most studios test it after search is working. Compare cost per booking across both channels over the same period. ## Which Formats and Offers Fit a Studio?

Spotify's sponsored session format gives mobile listeners 30 minutes of ad-free listening in exchange for watching a video ad on their phone. Because this is a video format, it needs more creative work than a 15 or 30 second audio spot. A studio that has a short video of a class is better placed to test it.

Keep the offer to one thing, such as one trial class and one URL. A longer offer or several calls to action makes the result harder to read. For a comparison of audio with other reach channels, see [audio ads vs display ads for local business](/blog/audio-ads-vs-display-ads-local-business).

## FAQ

### Can Spotify ads target only people near my studio?

Ad Studio supports city and geography targeting, so you can focus on the area around the studio. Check the location options your account shows for your country before you launch.

### Can listeners skip a Spotify audio ad?

Spotify states that audio ads cannot be closed or swiped past. The ad plays through in full.

### Is Spotify cheaper than search for trial bookings?

The sources here do not settle that. A secondary guide gives a search target under $30 per trial lead, which is a different metric. Compare cost per booking from each channel over the same period.

### How do I know whether the audio ad worked?

Use a unique URL or promo code that appears only in the audio ad. Match bookings to spend each month and compare with your search results.
