Most local businesses should start with a standard Search campaign and add Performance Max once conversion tracking is reliable. Search shows the exact queries you pay for, because you choose the keywords. Performance Max runs one goal-based campaign across Search, YouTube, Display, Gmail and Maps, and Google's AI decides where ads appear. It can add reach, but you give up keyword-level control. This guide compares the two using Google's own help pages and labels every example figure as an assumption.

## What Is the Difference Between Performance Max and Search Campaigns?

A Search campaign shows text ads on Google results when a query matches your keywords. Performance Max is a goal-based campaign. Google describes it as AI-powered, reaching customers across Search, YouTube, Maps and other Google surfaces based on the goals you choose, such as sales, leads or store visits ([Google Ads](https://business.google.com/en-all/google-ads/)).

| | Search campaign | Performance Max |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where ads show | Google Search results | Search, YouTube, Display, Gmail, Maps |
| What you choose | Keywords and ads | Goals and assets |
| Who decides placement | You, through keywords | Google's AI, based on goals |

The table shows the core trade-off. Search gives you the query list. Performance Max gives you wider reach, and Google's systems decide where the ads run.

## Which One Gives You More Control?

Search gives you more control over who sees the ad. You pick the keywords, so you know which searches trigger it. Performance Max gives you control over goals and creative assets, while Google chooses the placements. You influence the target rather than the exact query.

Google's own description says that in Merchant Center, Performance Max campaigns optimize for online sales, while campaigns managed in Google Ads can optimize to goals such as online and in-store conversions ([Google Marketing Tools Support](https://business.google.com/us/support/)). For a local business, the in-store goal matters. Deciding which numbers prove a campaign works is covered in [which marketing metrics matter for local business](/blog/which-marketing-metrics-matter-local-business).

## How Do Search and Performance Max Work Together?

Both can run in the same account. Google's account prompt states that you can create multiple campaigns in the same Google Ads account without creating a new account ([Google Ads announcement](https://business.google.com/us/accelerate/announcements/access-new-inventory-and-channel-performance-reporting-in-performance-max-campaigns/)). Keep Search on the queries you already know convert, and let Performance Max look for wider reach.

Location assets on Display inventory are only available through Local Campaigns or Performance Max with store goals ([About location assets](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)). If you want location-based ads on Display, that setup route matters. The full setup steps are in our [complete Performance Max guide for local business](/blog/google-performance-max-for-local-business-complete-guide-2026).

One agency guide recommends starting with Search campaigns, rather than Performance Max, to build conversion data before scaling ([Define Digital Academy](https://www.definedigitalacademy.com/blog/google-ads-for-local-service-based-businesses-5-step-strategy-for-2025), secondary, 2025).

## What About Reporting and Cost?

Google states that with Performance Max campaigns you choose the goals and you will never pay more than your monthly budget cap ([Google Marketing Tools Support](https://business.google.com/us/support/)). A budget cap is a spending ceiling, not a cost-per-lead target, so it does not tell you what a booking costs.

Reporting has improved. Google's November 2025 announcement says channel performance reporting is now available in all Performance Max campaigns, which gives more insight into where your ads are running ([Google Ads announcement](https://business.google.com/us/accelerate/announcements/access-new-inventory-and-channel-performance-reporting-in-performance-max-campaigns/)).

On cost, be careful with benchmarks. Adwave notes that its cost figures cover Search campaigns only, and that Display, YouTube and Performance Max costs are different ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-ads-cost-benchmarks), secondary). Search cost averages do not transfer to Performance Max.

## When Should a Local Business Use Each?

Start with Search when:

- Customers already search for what you sell, and you want to see each query.
- Your conversion tracking is new and not yet proven.
- Your monthly budget is small and every dollar needs a clear purpose.

Add Performance Max when:

- Conversion tracking works and counts the actions you care about.
- You want store visit or direction results. Google says Waze ads inventory is fully available for US advertisers using Performance Max for store goals, with no additional setup ([Google Ads announcement](https://business.google.com/us/accelerate/announcements/access-new-inventory-and-channel-performance-reporting-in-performance-max-campaigns/)).

Google's retail guidance reports that advertisers who moved from Standard Shopping to Performance Max saw a 25% increase in conversion value on average at a similar ROAS ([Google retailer best practices](https://business.google.com/us/accelerate/resources/articles/retailer-best-practices-for-ai-powered-performance-max-campaigns/)). That is Google's claim for retailers with a Merchant Center feed, not an independent test of local services. A UK restaurant agency says Performance Max often gives better results than its Search campaigns ([Taller Digital](https://www.tallerdigital.co.uk/post/a-guide-to-google-ads-for-restaurant-owners-in-2025), secondary), which is one agency's experience. If you are also weighing social platforms, compare [Google Ads and Facebook Ads for local business](/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-local-business-which-wins). Google's other reach format is covered in our [Demand Gen guide](/blog/google-demand-gen-campaigns-local-business).

## What Would a Fair Test Look Like?

Every number in this example is an assumption. Imagine a salon with $1,200 a month for ads. Assume it puts $900 into Search and $300 into Performance Max. The $300 sits inside the $200 to $350 range one source suggests for testing localized Performance Max ([WizerunekWSieci](https://wizerunekwsieci.com/local-advertising-on-google-for-small-businesses-ready-to-grow/), secondary). Both campaigns count the same conversion: a booking form submission.

| Campaign | Monthly spend | Assumed cost per booking | Assumed bookings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Search | $900 | $60 | 15 ($900 divided by $60) |
| Performance Max | $300 | $75 | 4 ($300 divided by $75) |

At these assumed costs, Performance Max costs 25% more per booking than Search ($75 against $60). Set your threshold before you start. For example, you might keep Performance Max only if its cost per booking is within 20% of Search. That threshold is your own rule, not a benchmark. Review the test monthly, as Dental Rank recommends setting aside time monthly to analyse performance and adjust campaigns ([Dental Rank](https://dentalrank.com.au/top-5-google-ads-mistakes-cost-dentists-thousands-how-to-fix-them/), secondary).

Do this today: open your Search campaign's query data and write down the five queries that bring bookings. Those become your core Search keywords before you add Performance Max.

## FAQ

### Is Performance Max better than a Search campaign?

Neither is better in general. Search gives you query-level control. Performance Max extends reach across Google surfaces. Results depend on your account, so test both with the same conversion.

### Can I run both campaigns in the same account?

Yes. Google states that you can create multiple campaigns in the same Google Ads account without creating a new account.

### What reporting does Performance Max give me?

Google says channel performance reporting is now available in all Performance Max campaigns. It shows where your ads ran. Check your own account, as reporting views can vary.

### How long should I test before deciding?

Review the test monthly, using your own booking data. Check Google's live help page for current guidance on learning periods, since the timing can change.
