## Do You Need a Mobile Ad Network Without an App?

Usually not. Apple Search Ads places ads for apps in the App Store, and Google App campaigns need an app that is published and available for download. A single-location cafe, salon or studio without an app gets more from Google Ads, which Google describes as appearing across Google Search, YouTube, Google Play and Discover. Those are the same Google properties a local business can reach from one account.

For a business that wants calls, directions and bookings, Google's store-focused campaign type is the more relevant tool. Performance Max for store goals replaced legacy Local Campaigns and uses machine learning to place ads across Search, Maps, YouTube, Gmail and the Google Display Network, according to a secondary summary. The full setup is covered in our [Performance Max guide for local business](/blog/google-performance-max-for-local-business-complete-guide-2026).

## How Does Apple Search Ads Charge?

Apple Search Ads charges per tap, so you pay when someone taps your ad. Adwave, a vendor, puts the average cost per tap at $0.50 to $2.00 for most categories, and $3.00 to $5.00 or more for competitive categories such as finance and gaming. These are vendor-published ranges, so check current bids in your Apple Ads account before you plan spend.

Adwave also says Apple reports that 65% of App Store downloads come directly from search inside the store. That makes keyword discovery the main job. Adwave recommends starting with Search Match to find which queries drive downloads, which is a sensible first step before you bid on fixed keywords. For more on setup, read our [Apple Search Ads guide for local business](/blog/apple-search-ads-local-business).

Creative also needs regular refreshing. Adwave recommends updating Apple Search Ads creative every 4 to 8 weeks, because Apple's ad placements rotate. Adwave also quotes Apple's average conversion rate for Search Ads at 50%, but the excerpt does not define which step that rate measures, so confirm the definition in Apple's current documentation before you use it in a forecast.

## How Do Google App Campaigns Work?

With Google App campaigns, you set a target cost per install, and Google's machine learning optimizes delivery across Search, Display, YouTube and Google Play, according to Adwave. You do not pick individual placements. You supply the app, the goal and the budget, and Google handles where the ads run. The campaign types, assets and bidding options are set out in our [Google App campaigns guide](/blog/google-app-campaigns-local-business).

Cost per install depends heavily on the category. Adwave gives a range of $0.50 to $3.00 per install through Google depending on category and geography. Its other page says subscription and finance apps can see $20 to $80 or more. Those are wide ranges from a vendor, so treat them as a starting point and measure your own cost per install during the first weeks.

## How Do Tap, Install and Click Costs Compare?

Taps, installs and clicks are different events, so they cannot be compared directly. The table below uses assumption-labelled inputs for a fitness studio with an app. It shows how many events the same budget buys under each price, which helps you decide what to measure next.

| Input | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget | $1,000 (assumption) | Your plan |
| Apple Search Ads cost per tap | $1.25 (assumption, inside the $0.50 to $2.00 range) | Adwave |
| Taps bought | 800 | $1,000 divided by $1.25 |
| Google App campaign cost per install | $2.00 (assumption, inside the $0.50 to $3.00 range) | Adwave |
| Installs bought | 500 | $1,000 divided by $2.00 |
| Google Search cost per click, auto repair benchmark | $3.90 (different category, dated data) | WordStream, secondary |
| Clicks bought at that benchmark | About 256 | $1,000 divided by $3.90 |

The 500 installs are not 500 paying members. A realistic next step is to multiply installs by the share who sign up and pay, which you must measure in your own app analytics. ## Which Network Fits Which Local Business?

The right network depends on whether you have an app and what the app does. The table gives a simple decision path. In most cases, the cheapest test is search ads, and app-install networks only come into play once the product lives in the app.

| Situation | Better fit | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No app, wants calls and bookings nearby | Google Ads search and store-goal campaigns | Intent-driven traffic, measurable calls and directions |
| App is the product, such as booking or loyalty | Apple Search Ads and Google App campaigns | Both promote app installs |
| Pre-launch app test | Apple Search Ads or Meta, plus creative | Adwave suggests a few hundred dollars a day covers a basic test |
| Reaching phones near a storefront | Location-based mobile campaigns | See our [in-app advertising guide](/blog/in-app-advertising-local-business-guide) for placement options |

Adwave describes a pre-launch test as a few hundred dollars a day split between Meta or Apple Search Ads and creative production. Google Ads can also start small. Adwave says even $10 to $15 a day can capture meaningful local search traffic for a food truck, though that is a vendor claim for one category.

## What Is the Smallest Useful Budget?

Vendor minimums are not network minimums, and they tell you little about what your business needs. Adwave publishes a $30 a day bundle covering TV, Google Search, Facebook, Instagram, mobile web and apps, which totals $900 across 30 active days. That is a service package from one agency, not a rule set by Google or Apple.

For a local app, a reasonable first test is the budget you can measure for at least a few weeks. Set one goal, such as a booking or a paid signup, count it in your analytics, and compare cost per goal across channels. Avoid judging a network on installs alone, because installs do not show who pays.

## FAQ

### Is a mobile ad network useful without an app?
Usually not for a local business. Apple Search Ads and Google App campaigns depend on an app being published. Google Ads search and maps campaigns are the usual starting point when you have no app.

### What does Apple Search Ads charge?
Apple Search Ads is priced per tap. Adwave gives a typical range of $0.50 to $2.00 per tap for most categories and $3.00 to $5.00 or more for competitive ones such as finance and gaming. Check current bids in your own account.

### How much does a Google App campaign install cost?
Adwave gives a range of $0.50 to $3.00 per install depending on category and geography, with subscription and finance apps higher. Treat these as vendor figures and measure your own cost per install.

### Should a local business start with Google Ads or Apple Ads?
Start with Google Ads if you have no app or want calls and bookings. Apple Ads suits a business whose app is the product, such as a booking or loyalty app. Many app businesses run both, as Adwave notes for fitness apps.
