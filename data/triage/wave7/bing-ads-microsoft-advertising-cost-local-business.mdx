Bing Ads now runs as Microsoft Advertising, and it has no fixed price. Microsoft does not require a minimum spend for search campaigns, so what you pay per click comes from an auction against other advertisers. Published figures give ranges, not prices. One agency reported Microsoft Advertising search CPCs from $0.82 to $7.71 across its managed accounts over twelve months. A 2025 agency benchmark placed average CPC around $1.50 to $2.80. For a local business, the practical answer is a short paid test with a fixed budget.

## What Does a Click Cost on Microsoft Advertising?

The figures above are not guarantees. The Hustle Marketers range comes from accounts that one agency manages, and the $1.50 to $2.80 range is a third-party average, not a platform rate. Microsoft does not present that benchmark as a guaranteed average. Your cost depends on your category, your competitors, and your bids.

Cross-industry numbers run higher. LocaliQ, working with WordStream, reports an average search CPC of $5.42 across all industries in 2026, covering Google Ads and Microsoft Ads together. A separate article quotes Microsoft Advertising CPC at around $1.54, citing WordStream data. Treat these as reference points, not forecasts for a plumber, dentist or café.

Category matters more than platform. One guide gives an example of a keyword that costs $8 per click on Google and might cost $5 to $6 on Microsoft. That is an illustration, not a rule. Check the bid and keyword estimates inside your own account before you commit budget.

## Who Does Microsoft Advertising Reach?

Microsoft Advertising places ads across the Microsoft Search Network, which includes Bing, Yahoo, AOL and other partner websites. The same source says ads can reach users of Windows PC, Microsoft Edge, Outlook, Office, Xbox and LinkedIn. That reach is broader than a search box, but it is not the same audience as Google's.

Microsoft's advertising site cites 109 million unique US PC users and a 38.1% US PC search market share. This is the vendor's own claim, based on Comscore desktop-only data from March 2023. A desktop-only figure says nothing about mobile searches, so it should not be read as a measure of your local customers.

Targeting options overlap. Both Microsoft Advertising and Google Ads allow segmentation by age, gender, income level and geographic location, according to a secondary guide. For a local business, the radius and location settings matter more than the platform name, so set them carefully before you launch.

## Is Bing Cheaper Than Google Ads for Local Businesses?

Microsoft says advertisers can get 19% lower CPCs and 6% higher CTRs than a leading ad platform, citing an eMarketer Q1 2026 forecast. This is a vendor claim comparing platforms in general. It is not a result for your category or your city.

The Hustle Marketers study draws on 16 accounts, which is a small sample from one agency. Other third-party pages reach different conclusions or none at all. Sources disagree, and none of them isolates local service businesses. The reliable comparison is cost per lead in your own accounts, measured over the same period.

## How Many Conversions Does Automated Bidding Need?

Microsoft's automated bidding guidance recommends at least 30 conversions in the previous 30 days before you evaluate Target CPA or Maximize Conversions. Many local accounts do not produce that many leads in a month, so judging automated bidding early can mislead you.

For a small account, count leads across the full test and compare cost per lead directly. Check the platform's live help page for current bidding options before you switch strategies.

## Worked Example: What Does a $300 Test Cost?

Microsoft does not publish click costs for your category, so every input below is an assumption except the benchmark range it references.

| Input or result | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Test budget | $300 | assumption |
| Average cost per click | $2.00 | assumption, inside the $1.50 to $2.80 benchmark range |
| Clicks | 150 | $300 divided by $2.00 |
| Click-to-lead rate | 5% | assumption |
| Leads | 7.5 | 150 times 5% |
| Cost per lead | $40 | $300 divided by 7.5 |
| Gross profit per new customer | $150 | assumption |
| Lead-to-customer rate | 27% | assumption |
| New customers | 2 | 7.5 times 27%, rounded |
| Result | Break-even | 2 customers times $150 equals $300 |

The $40 cost per lead sits below the 2026 all-industry average of $66.69 across Google and Microsoft Ads. That average covers every industry, so it is only a rough reference. Change one input and the result moves. At a $3.00 CPC, the same $300 buys 100 clicks, 5 leads and a $60 cost per lead, which is assumption-based arithmetic, not a forecast.

## How Should a Local Business Start the Test?

1. Confirm your Google Ads campaign is producing leads at a cost you can afford, so you have a baseline to compare against.
2. Set a monthly search budget. If it is $1,000 (assumption), a 10 to 20 percent share is $100 to $200 for Microsoft Advertising. Hustle Marketers suggests this as practitioner guidance, not a benchmark.
3. Start with one campaign and a small keyword set, and use the location settings to match your service area.
4. Check the account weekly so an unexpected budget or bid change does not run for days unnoticed.
5. Judge the test on cost per lead at the end of the full test period, not on the first few clicks.

## When Is Microsoft Advertising Not Worth Testing?

Skip the test if your Google Ads account is not yet producing profitable leads. Adding a second channel to a losing campaign multiplies the loss rather than fixing it.

Skip it, or test cautiously, if your customers mostly search on mobile. The audience figures above measure desktop PC users, so they will overstate reach for a mobile-heavy business. Also skip it if you cannot wait for enough lead volume, because automated bidding needs conversion history that a small account may take a long time to build.

## FAQ

### How much does Bing Ads cost per click?

There is no fixed price. Costs come from an auction. One agency reported $0.82 to $7.71 across its managed accounts, and a 2025 agency benchmark placed average CPC at $1.50 to $2.80.

### Is there a minimum spend for Microsoft Advertising?

Microsoft does not require a minimum spend for search campaigns, according to a secondary guide. Check the billing and budget pages inside the platform for current rules before you launch.

### Is Microsoft Advertising cheaper than Google Ads?

Microsoft claims 19% lower CPCs than a leading platform, but that is a vendor claim comparing platforms in general. Test cost per lead in your own accounts before deciding.

### How much budget should I give Microsoft Advertising?

Hustle Marketers suggests 10 to 20 percent of the search budget as a starting share when Microsoft Advertising is added to Google Ads. It is practitioner guidance, not a benchmark.
