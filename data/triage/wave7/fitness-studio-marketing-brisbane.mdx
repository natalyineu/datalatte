A Brisbane fitness studio should set its marketing budget as a share of revenue, then direct that money at the members it can realistically reach. Many boutique studios budget between 5 and 10 percent of monthly revenue for marketing, according to [Mariana Tek's group fitness marketing guide](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/). For a studio with AUD 40,000 in monthly revenue (assumption), that works out to AUD 2,000 to AUD 4,000 a month. Keep the spend local. West End, New Farm and Paddington are separate audiences, and Google, Instagram and paid ads should work as one system.

## How Much Should a Brisbane Studio Spend on Marketing?

The 5 to 10 percent range is a starting point, not a rule. A studio with strong retention may need less acquisition spend, while a newer studio in a crowded suburb may need more. The more useful question is how many new members the budget should produce each month.

The table below uses assumed inputs. Replace them with your own figures, because none of them are Brisbane benchmarks.

| Line item | Low case | High case |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly revenue (assumption) | AUD 40,000 | AUD 40,000 |
| Marketing share (5% or 10%) | 5% | 10% |
| Monthly marketing budget | AUD 2,000 | AUD 4,000 |
| New members per month (assumption) | 20 | 20 |
| Marketing cost per new member | AUD 100 | AUD 200 |

If the same studio wins 20 new members a month (assumption) at the higher budget, each member costs twice as much as at the lower budget. That gap is a reason to record which channel brought each sign-up before raising spend.

## Which Radius Should Your Ads Target?

A US guide to gym Google Ads settings suggests a 3 to 5 mile radius for a budget gym and 5 to 8 miles for a boutique studio, adjusted for how people actually drive locally ([Brandrocket](https://www.brandrocket.net/resources/gym-google-ads-settings-location), secondary). Using a rough conversion of 1.6 km per mile, a 5 to 8 mile radius is about 8 to 13 km. Treat that as a first test, not a setting to keep.

In Brisbane, drive time usually tells you more than a circle on a map. Build separate ad groups for West End, New Farm and Paddington, then compare cost per sign-up across them. Spotify Ad Studio also allows targeting by city and geography, which can help with audio campaigns ([Tinuiti](https://tinuiti.com/blog/tv-audio/spotify-ads/), secondary). For paid social setup, see [Facebook ads and AI for fitness studios](/blog/fitness-studio-marketing-with-facebook-ads-and-ai).

## How Should Google Business Profile and Instagram Work Together?

Start with the Google Business Profile. Put the suburb in the business description, such as "reformer Pilates studio in New Farm," so the listing matches the searches local residents actually make. Keep class schedules, opening hours and photos current, since out-of-date details are the quickest way to lose a new visitor.

Instagram suits relaxed, real-class content. Show the room, the instructor and the energy of a session. Use the river or local streets only where they appear naturally in your own footage. "Beat the humidity" can work as a caption for summer classes, but test it against plain class messaging before making it the main pitch. A wider plan is in the [social media marketing strategy guide](/blog/fitness-studio-social-media-marketing-strategy).

## Why Does Retention Matter More Than Intro Offers?

Second Measure reports, citing vendor data, that more than 80 percent of boutique customers do not spend in the last quarter of their first year, with one exception ([Second Measure](https://secondmeasure.com/?p=364), secondary). Acquisition alone does not solve that problem. A single boutique class is priced at $20 to more than $30 in the same reporting, so a new member who lapses is an expensive loss.

A vendor reports that Studio Svelte, a New York City boutique gym, saw a 40 percent increase in memberships within three months of integrating fitness CRM software ([Gleantap](https://gleantap.com/how-integrated-sales-crm-and-marketing-automation-can-drive-unprecedented-growth/)). That is one vendor's client result, not a forecast for a Brisbane studio. Follow-up emails are the simplest way to test retention. The approach is set out in [email marketing for fitness studios](/blog/email-marketing-fitness-studio).

## What Should a Brisbane Studio Do Each Month?

1. Set the marketing budget as a share of revenue, starting within the reported 5 to 10 percent range, and write the figure down.
2. Set a target cost per new member using your own intake records.
3. Split ad groups by suburb and compare cost per sign-up once enough sign-ups have come in to judge.
4. Check the Google Business Profile for suburb names, opening hours and the current class list.
5. Post regular real-class content on Instagram, tagged to the suburb where it was filmed.
6. Send a retention message to members in the weeks after their first class.
7. Review which channel brought each sign-up before changing the budget. Check timing against the [New Year membership guide](/blog/fitness-studio-new-year-membership-marketing) rather than assuming a single peak season.

## FAQ

### How much should a Brisbane fitness studio spend on marketing?

A commonly reported range is 5 to 10 percent of monthly revenue ([Mariana Tek](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/)). Use your own revenue and cost-per-new-member figures to check whether that range fits your studio.

### Should I target all of Brisbane or specific suburbs?

Suburb-level targeting is easier to measure. Start with West End, New Farm and Paddington as separate groups, and widen the radius only if cost per sign-up holds up.

### Is a Google Business Profile enough to generate local leads?

No. It is one channel. Pair it with paid search, social content and follow-up with existing members.

### Are the prices and budgets in this guide Brisbane-specific?

No. The class price and radius figures come from US sources, and the revenue and member numbers are assumptions. Check current Brisbane cost-per-click and suburb search volume in your own Google Ads account before setting budgets.
