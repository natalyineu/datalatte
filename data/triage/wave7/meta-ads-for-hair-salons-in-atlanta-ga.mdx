## What Do Meta Ads Cost for a Hair Salon?

Meta ads are priced two common ways: cost per thousand impressions (CPM) and cost per click (CPC). [AdWave's marketing guide](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-small-business-guide) puts typical Meta costs at $5–15 per thousand impressions and $1–3 per click. Those are cross-industry planning bands, not salon-specific figures, so replace them with your own Ads Manager numbers once a campaign has run for a while.

Monthly budget benchmarks also come from other local businesses. AdWave estimates that most single-location restaurants spend [$500 to $2,000 a month on Meta](https://adwave.com/resources/restaurant-advertising-complete-marketing-guide), and that [most veterinary practices can run effective campaigns for $300 to $1,000 a month](https://adwave.com/resources/veterinary-advertising). Bookeo's USD 750 to USD 1,500 starting range sits inside that spread, which makes it a reasonable first test for a salon.

## How Big Is Meta's Audience, and Where Do the Ads Run?

AdWave reports that Meta's apps reach an average of 3.56 billion daily active people across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp and Threads. Instagram and Facebook share one buying system. [Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-advertising) describes Meta Ads Manager as a central point for running ads across Instagram and Facebook.

[AdWave's Instagram guide](https://adwave.com/resources/instagram-advertising-for-local-business) adds that one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook, or both. A salon therefore does not need separate tools for each network. The platform setup is the same in Atlanta as anywhere else; the local part comes from the radius, the offer and the creative.

## Which Ad Formats and Posting Rhythm Suit a Salon?

Short-form video is the format to lead with. AdWave cites Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report as showing that short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram. Footage of real stylists, real chairs and finished looks gives the ad something concrete to show, which is more useful than a polished stock image.

Stories suit time-limited offers because, as [Associated Hair Professionals](https://www.associatedhairprofessionals.com/updates/blog-posts/power-social-media-your-hairstyling-brand) notes, they are temporary by design. For the organic side, [Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/) says most salon owners find three to four Instagram posts and one to two Facebook posts a week sustainable. The [Instagram marketing guide for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons) covers how to build that rhythm.

## How Should You Set the Radius and Targeting in Atlanta?

Bookeo advises setting a 5 to 10 mile radius around the salon for Facebook and Instagram ads. A salon in a dense neighbourhood can use the tighter end of that range to keep spend close to the chair. A salon drawing clients from further out can use the wider end. Keep the radius consistent for the first campaign so the results are easy to read.

Use the location and audience options in Ads Manager, but keep age and gender filters broad at first. None of the benchmarks used here set an age band for salon clients, so treat any age filter as a hypothesis to test. The [Facebook ads guide for hair salons](/blog/facebook-ads-for-hair-salons) walks through the Facebook-specific settings in more detail.

## Worked Example: What Could $750 a Month Buy?

The inputs below are labelled as assumptions where they are not sourced. The budget is the low end of Bookeo's starting range. The CPM and CPC bands come from AdWave. The 2% booking rate is an assumption.

Inputs: monthly budget $750 (from Bookeo's range); CPM $5–15 (from AdWave); CPC $1–3 (from AdWave); booking rate 2% of clicks (assumption).

Impressions: $750 ÷ $15 × 1,000 = 50,000 at the high CPM, and $750 ÷ $5 × 1,000 = 150,000 at the low CPM. Clicks: $750 ÷ $3 = 250 at the high CPC, and $750 ÷ $1 = 750 at the low CPC.

| Scenario | Assumed cost per click | Clicks from $750 | Assumed bookings (2% of clicks) | Cost per booking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Efficient | $1 | 750 | 15 | $50 |
| Expensive | $3 | 250 | 5 | $150 |

The booking rate matters as much as the cost per click. At an assumed 4% booking rate, the same spend yields 10 to 30 bookings. Replace the 2% assumption with your own booking rate as soon as the campaign reports one.

## What Should You Check Before Spending?

1. Confirm that Instagram and Facebook are on business accounts. Bookeo notes that peak follower times in Insights require one.
2. Set the radius to 5 to 10 miles around the salon.
3. Prepare two or three short videos per service group, plus a still image for each.
4. Point each ad to a booking page or booking form, not the homepage. Follow-up messages can then be automated, as covered in [marketing automation for hair salons](/blog/marketing-automation-for-hair-salons-guide).
5. Install the Meta Pixel and track a booking-confirmation event.
6. Add UTM parameters to ad links so Google Analytics records Meta-sourced visits.
7. Review results once enough impressions and clicks have accumulated, then change one variable at a time.

## How Do You Know Whether Meta Ads Are Working?

Measure bookings, not reach. Track clicks to booking confirmations, the cost per booking, and how many of those clients return. Impressions and reach show that the ad was seen; they do not show that a chair was filled.

## FAQ

### How much should a hair salon spend on Meta ads each month?

Bookeo suggests starting Facebook and Instagram ad spend at USD 750 to USD 1,500 monthly. AdWave puts a standalone Meta buy at often $300 to $1,000 a month across local businesses. Choose an amount you can sustain for several weeks, because AdWave notes that Meta campaigns take weeks to optimize.

### What is a realistic cost per click for salon ads?

AdWave's typical Meta range is $1 to $3 per click, with $5 to $15 per thousand impressions. Salon-specific figures are not broken out in these benchmarks, so use them as a starting point and replace them with your own Ads Manager data after the first campaign.

### Do Instagram and Facebook ads run in the same place?

They are managed in the same system. Instagram ads run through Meta Ads Manager, the same system used for Facebook ads, and one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook, or both.

### Does a salon need a business account for Meta ads?

Insights, which shows peak follower activity, requires a business account, according to Bookeo. A business account also keeps the page and ad accounts under the salon's control, which makes handover easier if staff change.
