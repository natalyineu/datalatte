## How Do You Get a Berlin Studio Found Before the First Visit?

Most first contact now starts with a search or an online profile. Google publishes guidelines for Business Profile names, categories and descriptions on its help pages. Check them before you edit, because the rules can change. Write the profile in the language your classes are taught in, and add German and English if you teach both.

Name the style of yoga you teach, the neighbourhood and the typical class size. Add photos of the real room, not stock images. Ask recent students to leave reviews, and reply to each one so that prospective students can see a teacher reading them.

## How Often Should a Yoga Studio Post Short Video?

Mariana Tek advises studios to post short-form video at a steady cadence, three to five times a week, rather than in bursts. The same guide suggests quick class highlight clips of 15 to 30 seconds. That length is enough to show the room, the pace of a class and the teacher's voice without asking the viewer for much time.

Consistency matters more than one busy week. Use a few repeatable themes, such as a teacher introduction, a class preview and a student question answered on camera. Post in the language of the class, with subtitles in the second language if you teach bilingually. ## What Should Happen in the 24 Hours After a Trial?

Mariana Tek advises following up with pop-up contacts within 24 hours using an automated welcome sequence, because interest fades fast once a pop-up ends. The same logic applies to a first yoga class. A student who has just tried your studio is at their most curious in the following day or two.

Greet the student by name in the first message, introduce the teacher, and offer one specific next step: a second class at a set time, or a membership trial. Keep the message short enough to read on a phone in under a minute.

Check the consent rules for marketing email in Germany with a local adviser before you send bulk messages. For sequence ideas, see our [email marketing template for fitness studios](/blog/fitness-studio-email-marketing-template).

## How Do You Cut No-Shows Without Chasing Students?

Mariana Tek advises sending an SMS reminder a few hours before class to reduce no-shows. Send one reminder per booking. Include the class name, start time, room and anything the student should bring. The message should be short enough to read at a glance on a phone.

Set the reminder up once in your booking system rather than sending it by hand. Track no-shows for four weeks before the change and four weeks after. That gives you a fair comparison in your own classes, which matters more than any general figure.

## When Should You Send a Win-Back Message?

Vertical Impression's marketing guide recommends automated win-back sequences for members who have missed three or more classes. That threshold gives you a clear trigger instead of a guess about who has drifted away. Check your booking data each week to see who has crossed it.

Name the class the student used to attend and the teacher who taught it. Offer one easy way back, such as a class at a time that suits them. Do not send win-back messages to anyone who has asked to stop receiving them. For sequence structure, see our guide to [email marketing for fitness studios](/blog/email-marketing-fitness-studio).

## How Do You Test Whether the Changes Work?

Change one thing at a time. Run a new Instagram theme for four weeks, then compare saves, replies and booking-link clicks against the previous four weeks. Do the same for the follow-up sequence and the SMS reminder, one at a time, so you can tell which change moved bookings.

Record where each first booking came from: the profile, Instagram, a trial visit or a referral. If you are choosing software to automate these messages, compare options in our guide to [AI-powered marketing tools for fitness studios](/blog/fitness-studio-ai-powered-marketing-tools).

## FAQ

### How do I market a yoga studio in Berlin?

Start with a complete Business Profile in the language your classes use. Post short class videos three to five times a week, follow up with every trial student within 24 hours, and send an SMS reminder before each class.

### How often should a yoga studio post short video?

Mariana Tek advises a steady cadence of three to five times a week, using highlight clips of 15 to 30 seconds. Consistency across weeks matters more than a single burst of posting.

### When should I send an SMS reminder?

Mariana Tek advises sending it a few hours before class to reduce no-shows. Keep it to one message per booking, with the class name, time and room.

### Can I send marketing email to students in Germany?

Check the consent rules with a local adviser before sending bulk marketing email. This article does not set out legal requirements, and rules should be confirmed before you send.

### Is a win-back message useful for lapsed students?

Vertical Impression recommends automated win-back sequences for members who have missed three or more classes. Use that as a trigger, and stop messages for anyone who opts out.
