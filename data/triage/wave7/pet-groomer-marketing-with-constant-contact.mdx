## What Does Constant Contact Cost for a Small Grooming Shop?

Adwave's auto body shop guide says [Mailchimp and Constant Contact cost $15-$50 per month for lists under 2,000 contacts](https://adwave.com/resources/auto-body-shop-advertising). Its pages for other trades quote different bands, such as [$20 to $80 per month for dog training](https://adwave.com/resources/dog-training-advertising) and [$20-$100 per month for restaurants](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-for-restaurants). These are agency figures, not platform prices, so the range is a starting point only.

A groomer with a few hundred clients will fall in the lowest band. Check the live pricing page before you buy, because plan limits and billing can change between the time a third party writes an article and the day you sign up.

## Is Constant Contact Good Enough for Automated Reminders?

Constant Contact suits a shop that wants simple, pre-built sequences. Adwave describes its automations as [straightforward, pre-built automations for a simple welcome series or a birthday greeting](https://adwave.com/resources/mailchimp-vs-klaviyo-vs-constant-contact-which-is-best-for-you), without complex conditional logic. That fits a welcome email and one rebooking reminder.

Branching is where other tools differ. The same article describes Mailchimp's Customer Journeys as [templated workflows with branching logic based on whether someone opens, clicks, or buys](https://adwave.com/resources/mailchimp-vs-klaviyo-vs-constant-contact-which-is-best-for-you). If you later want separate paths by coat type, [a Mailchimp setup for pet groomers](/blog/pet-groomer-email-marketing-with-mailchimp) shows how that works. Klaviyo is the most flexible option, and [its use in local business is covered here](/blog/using-klaviyo-for-email-and-sms-marketing-in-local-business).

## Which Emails Should a Groomer Build First?

Start with two. A welcome email goes to every new sign-up and explains how to book, what your cancellation rule is, and what to bring. A rebooking reminder goes to clients whose last visit is getting old.

For clients who stop booking, Constant Contact's guidance, as summarised by Adwave, identifies [a dedicated "We Miss You" message at the 90-day mark](https://adwave.com/resources/win-back-campaigns-re-engaging-customers-who-havent-bought-in-months) as the industry sweet spot. Set the rebooking delay to match each client's own visit pattern rather than one fixed interval for everyone. Your booking history will show that pattern within a few months.

## When Should a Groomer Send Reminders?

Timing matters more in some months than others. The Daily Groomer's Groomer to Groomer notes that [November and December are traditionally a very busy time for groomers, "sometimes too busy."](https://www.thedailygroomer.com/blog/Snip-and-Click-Mastering-the-Art-of-Pet-Groomer-Ads-Online) If your calendar fills fast in those months, send the reminder a few weeks earlier so clients book before slots run out.

Use the quiet months to re-engage lapsed clients, when a win-back message has less competition for your time. Track which month produces the most rebookings and repeat that timing the next year.

## How Do You Build a List Without Buying One?

Collect addresses where clients already are: at the front desk, on the booking form, and on a sign-up form on your website. Ask for the dog's name and coat type at the same time, so segmenting later needs no extra work.

Do not import a purchased list. Clients who gave you their address expect your emails, and people who did not will mark your messages as spam. Compare platforms before you commit; [Constant Contact vs Mailchimp for local business](/blog/constant-contact-vs-mailchimp-local-business) explains how each one counts contacts and bills them.

## Should You Add SMS Reminders Too?

SMS can reach clients faster than email, but it costs more to send and can annoy people who prefer email. Start with email and measure how many clients book after each reminder. If clients ignore the emails, test a text reminder for the same list. [Email versus SMS for local business](/blog/sms-vs-email-marketing-local-business-which-is-better) compares the two channels in more detail.

Drafting the copy is quicker with a template. [ChatGPT prompts for local business marketing](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-local-business-marketing) includes prompts for reminder emails, but edit every draft so it sounds like your shop.

## Worked Example: What Could a Rebooking Email Earn?

Every number below is an **assumption** chosen for easy arithmetic. None of them is a benchmark. Replace them with your own figures after two months of sending.

| Average ticket (assumption) | $70 |
| Plan cost per month (assumption, within the $15-$50 band) | $15 |

Under these assumptions, 100 reminders produce 8 bookings, or $560 in revenue. After the $15 plan cost, that leaves $545 before your time and card fees. At a lower response of 2%, you get 2 bookings and $140 in revenue, which still covers the plan with $125 to spare.

The lesson is about scale. A reminder to clients who already know you costs little to send. Paid ads reach strangers, which is a different job with a different cost.

## What Can You Skip at First?

You do not need a fixed newsletter calendar, a complex branching builder, or SMS on day one. A service shop needs one welcome flow and one rebooking flow. Add complexity only after the simple flows have produced bookings you can measure.

Ecommerce flows matter if you sell shampoos or accessories online, but they are a second step. Keep the first setup small so you can tell which email produced each booking.

## FAQ

### How many automations can a pet groomer run on Constant Contact?

Constant Contact's plan limits change, so confirm the current number on its live help pages before you choose a plan. A single-shop groomer usually needs only a welcome flow and a rebooking flow.

### How often should a groomer send rebooking reminders?

Set the delay to match each client's visit pattern. Your booking history will show whether clients come back every few weeks or every few months, and you can send the reminder just before that point.

### Is a purchased email list allowed?

Do not use one. Build your list from clients who give you their address at the front desk, on the booking form, or on your website sign-up form.

### What does Constant Contact cost?

Secondary sources put Mailchimp and Constant Contact at $15-$50 per month for lists under 2,000 contacts. Check the live pricing page, since plans and billing can change.

### Do I need SMS reminders too?

Not to start. Begin with email, measure the response, and add a text reminder only if clients ignore the emails.
