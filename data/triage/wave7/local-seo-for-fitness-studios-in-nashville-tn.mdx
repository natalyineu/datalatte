The pack draws its listings from Google Business Profile data ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-my-lawn-care-business), secondary), so most of the work starts on that profile. ## Why does the Map Pack depend so heavily on your profile?

A 2025 multi-location benchmark, cited by [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/multi-location-local-seo-audit) (secondary), reported that Google Business Profile signals represent 32% of local pack ranking weight. Adwave also states that, in analysis of millions of profiles, the primary category is the number one ranking factor for getting into the Local Pack ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones), secondary; the analysis is not named).

Older survey data points the same way. A BrightLocal survey, as reported by [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/seo/local-seo) (secondary), found 36% of SEOs rated the Business Profile as the most important map pack ranking factor in 2021, up from 23% in 2013.

These are national figures. They show where the ranking weight sits, not how a specific studio will rank in Nashville. The practical point is that a complete, accurate profile is the cheapest lever you control.

## What should the Google Business Profile say for a fitness studio?

Start with the primary category. Adwave suggests reviewing the top three to five businesses in local map results for your service and checking which category they use ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones), secondary). Choose the category that matches what customers search for, then add secondary categories for each class type you teach.

Write the description within the 750-character limit that [Adwave's beginner guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack) gives. Name your service, your neighbourhood and one differentiator in the first sentence. Make hours, phone number and address 100% accurate, as [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers) advises.

Upload at least 15 high-quality photos, since Adwave says that gallery size gets far more clicks and calls. Post at least once a week. Google shows a post publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live, so check that each post has been approved ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)). The full walkthrough is in our [fitness studio Google Business Profile guide](/blog/fitness-studio-google-business-profile-optimization).

## How many reviews does a Nashville studio need to compete?

An analysis of 2 million Google Business Profile pages by Blogging Wizard, reported by [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business) (secondary), found businesses with over 200 Google reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack. Another vendor claims that 100+ reviews and 4.7+ ratings dominate the map pack, and that a rating below 4.5 meaningfully suppresses conversion ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-gyms-fitness-centers), secondary; vendor claim).

Reviews also sit where buyers look. Google states that reviews show next to a business's profile in Maps and Search ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)).

Pace matters as much as the total. A steady request after each class builds the count over time. For a benchmark with a regional lens, see [Denver fitness studio review benchmarks](/blog/denver-fitness-studios-google-reviews-benchmarks-2026).

## Which citations and NAP details must match across the web?

NAP means name, address and phone number. Adwave says NAP must be exactly the same on your website, your Business Profile, and major directories such as Yelp, Apple Maps and Bing Places ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/voice-search-optimization-for-local-businesses), secondary). Its beginner guide makes the rule concrete: match punctuation exactly, and choose "St." or "Street" once and keep it everywhere ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).

Apple is a separate channel. Adwave says Apple Business Connect has captured a 7% share in a maps-only comparison and is growing, and describes Profile Views as the count of people who saw your business card in Apple Maps ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/apple-business-connect-the-google-business-profile-alternative-youre-ignoring), secondary; vendor figure). Treat that share as a claim to verify, not a benchmark.

Fix existing mismatches before you add new listings. Adding more inconsistent citations spreads the same error wider.

## Do posts, Q&A and messages still matter for the profile?

Google launched Q&A in Maps in 2017. Adwave reports, citing Accrisoft's transition summary, that responses in Google's AI answering, referred to as Ask Maps, draw on business profile data, website content and reviews ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-q-and-a), secondary). Your profile, website and reviews therefore feed the same answers, so the facts must agree across all three.

Google Posts act as free mini-billboards on your profile in Search and Maps ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary). Messages can be managed from the desktop dashboard as well as the mobile app ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-messaging-should-you-turn-it-on), secondary).

On the website side, a clear location page helps. You can see how to [embed a Google Map on a location page](/blog/google-maps-embed-local-business-website).

## How do you track whether the Map Pack work is paying off?

Adwave lists six useful local metrics: Business Profile impressions, map pack views, clicks to call, direction requests, website visits from the profile, and branded search lift over time ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-search-intent), secondary). Log them monthly in one sheet so you see trends rather than single-day swings.

Set expectations carefully. Adwave's beginner guide estimates that you may start ranking for your own business name a few weeks after verification (vendor estimate) ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary). Another vendor says organic channels such as Business Profile, referrals and community take 6 to 12 months to gain real traction ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-gyms-fitness-centers), secondary).

If you need visibility while organic work builds, the [keyword guide for fitness studios](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-fitness-studios-in-2026) covers which terms to test.

## FAQ

### Is a Google Business Profile free for a fitness studio?

Yes. Google describes its Business Profile as a free profile on Google Search and Maps ([Google](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)). Paid options such as ads are separate.

### How many photos should a fitness studio upload?

Adwave recommends at least 15 high-quality photos ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary). Another Adwave checklist suggests at least 10 ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary), so 15 is the safer starting point.

### Do Google reviews appear on the profile?

Yes. Google states that reviews show next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)).

### Why does a post not appear on my profile?

Google shows a post publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts do not show ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)).
