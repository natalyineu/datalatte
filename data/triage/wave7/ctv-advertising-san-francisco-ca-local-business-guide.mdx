Connected TV (CTV) advertising lets a San Francisco business run short video ads inside streaming TV content on internet-connected screens. According to [Paramount Ads Manager's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions), CTV ads are non-skippable, typically run 15 or 30 seconds, and appear full-screen. Most of this inventory is bought programmatically, meaning an automated system buys and serves the ads against criteria the advertiser sets. For a local business, the practical questions are whether the budget reaches households near the shop and how to tell whether it worked.

## What is CTV advertising and how is it bought?

Amazon Ads describes streaming TV as "the digital distribution of video content across devices with an Internet connection, rather than a cable signal." CTV covers smart TVs and external devices such as Roku, Fire TV and gaming consoles, as [Simpli.fi explains](https://simpli.fi/latest/what-is-ctv). [Seedtag](https://www.seedtag.com/blog/what-are-ctv-and-fast) adds that CTV ads run in streaming platforms and smart TV applications.

Buying is mostly programmatic. [Amazon Ads](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/connected-tv-advertising) states that its streaming TV ads are bought and sold digitally by an automated system. Pricing is based on metrics such as video completion rate (VCR) and cost per completed view (CPCV), and Amazon notes that CPCV "measures the price of each completed ad."

## Why does CTV suit a San Francisco local business?

CTV reaches the household, not just one phone, so a single ad can be seen by everyone watching in a home. Viewing is spread across many services. [Teads](https://www.teads.com/blog/what-is-connected-tv-advertising/9858/) reports, citing IAB Compass, that 61% of households subscribe to multiple streaming services. The same page cites EMARKETER for the finding that 71% of net new streaming subscriptions over the past nine quarters have been ad-supported.

CTV is different from other local channels. [Geofencing advertising](/blog/geofencing-advertising-local-business-guide) targets phones inside a physical area, and [in-app advertising](/blog/in-app-advertising-local-business-guide) targets people using specific apps. CTV puts a full-screen, non-skippable ad in the living room, but it builds familiarity rather than generating clicks.

## Which streaming formats can a local business buy?

The sources describe three broad formats. Subscription services with ad tiers are one. Ad-supported video-on-demand (AVOD) services are another, defined by Paramount as "free or lower-cost streaming services supported by ads." The third is free ad-supported streaming TV (FAST) channels, which are "free for viewers and supported entirely by advertising," with Pluto TV as Paramount's example.

Programmatic access can also reach premium services. [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/netflix-advertising-small-business) says programmatic access through platforms like its own opens the door to Netflix's audience alongside other premium streaming services. Platform minimums, targeting options and rates change, so check each platform's live ad manager rather than relying on a third-party summary.

## How should a 15 or 30 second CTV ad be built?

Paramount recommends a "strong opening hook in the first 3–5 seconds" to engage streaming viewers. Amazon notes that VCR for CTV ads tends to be very high because many streaming services do not allow ads to be skipped. A viewer who sees the ad is likely to watch it to the end, so the first five seconds carry most of the work.

Specifications vary by platform. [Beasley's CTV page](https://advertiseintampa.com/services/advertising/ctv-ott/) notes that streaming giants such as Amazon and Apple each set their own rules for resolution, frame rate, bitrate and format. Ask each platform for its current spec sheet before you export the file.

Interactive formats also exist. [Divisiond](https://divisiond.com/interactive-ctv/) defines interactive CTV as formats that "allow viewers to engage directly with ad messages appearing on a TV screen." Use these only if the platform supports them and you can track the response.

A simple script for a 15-second spot:

1. Seconds 0–5: business name and the single problem you solve, so the hook lands early.
2. Seconds 5–12: show the service or product in use.
3. Seconds 12–15: state the neighbourhood, phone number or website.

## What does a test budget buy? A worked example

The inputs below are assumptions for illustration. The $40 CPM is the programmatic rate used in Adwave's example, not a San Francisco benchmark.

- Monthly budget: $400 (assumption)
- Programmatic CPM: $40 (assumption, from Adwave's example)
- Test length: one month (assumption)
- Ad length: 15 seconds (assumption)
- Average impressions per household: 4 per month (assumption)

Impressions equal budget divided by CPM, multiplied by 1,000. For $400 at $40, that is 10,000 impressions. Dividing by an assumed frequency of 4 gives about 2,500 households reached.

## How do you measure a CTV campaign?

CTV does not produce clicks the way search does, so set expectations before launch. Start with the platform report: VCR and CPCV show how many viewers watched and what each completed view cost, as Amazon defines them.

Then track local signals that might change. Watch Google Business Profile searches for your exact name, direct website visits, and the answer to "how did you hear about us?" at the point of sale. Record a baseline for four weeks before the campaign so you can compare. The [ROI tracking guide for local businesses](/blog/how-to-track-marketing-roi-local-business-guide) explains how to set up this comparison.

Keep the test window fixed. The sources do not give a standard timeline for brand effects, so decide on one before launch and judge the campaign against that window rather than a few days of data.

## FAQ

### Is CTV advertising only for national brands?

No. Programmatic systems buy inventory that matches criteria the advertiser sets, so a local business can set ZIP-based or regional targeting where the platform supports it. Confirm geographic options and minimums in the platform's live ad manager.

### What is the difference between AVOD and FAST?

AVOD services are free or lower-cost streaming services supported by ads. FAST channels are free for viewers and supported entirely by advertising, with Pluto TV as an example. Both can be bought programmatically, and both fit a small test budget if the platform allows it.

### How long should a CTV ad be?

Paramount's glossary describes CTV ads as typically running 15 or 30 seconds. For a first test, a 15-second spot keeps creative simple. Check each platform's accepted lengths before you produce the file.

### How long before a CTV campaign shows results?

The sources do not give a fixed timeline. Set a test window before launch, compare against a four-week baseline, and review platform metrics alongside local signals rather than expecting clicks.
