To rank a coffee shop in Memphis, TN in the Google Maps pack, start with a verified Google Business Profile (GBP) set to the "Coffee shop" category. Then keep it active with posts, photos, answered questions and reviews. Setting up a profile is free, but it only works once Google verifies it: without verification, updates will not show publicly and customers may not find the business. Most of the work is ongoing, so this guide covers each step, a worked monthly example, and where to check live data.

## Which Google Business Profile category should a Memphis coffee shop choose?

Per Diem recommends choosing "Coffee shop" or a related term, rather than something too broad like "Restaurant" ([Per Diem guide](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). The category tells Google what searches your shop should match, so a precise choice matters more than a long list.

BrewRound's 2020 guide says you can select one category when creating a listing, and more can be added later ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary, dated 2020). Set your primary category first, then add secondary terms such as café or espresso bar once the profile is live.

For a complete field-by-field walkthrough, see [the coffee shop Google Business Profile optimization guide](/blog/coffee-shop-google-business-profile-optimization).

## How do you verify a coffee shop's Google Business Profile?

BrewRound's 2020 guide says Google sends a letter containing a code, and the profile does not appear in results until it is verified ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary). Verification methods can change, so check Google's current help pages before you begin.

Per Diem adds that without verification, your updates will not show publicly and customers may not find you ([Per Diem guide](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). Verify first, then spend time on posts and photos, because unverified work does not reach searchers.

Creating the listing is free. BrewRound says it takes little time to set up, but you will need a Google account, which can be registered for free ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary). Google's own Business Profile page also describes the service as free ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)).

## How often should a Memphis coffee shop post on Google?

Google's help page says a post appears publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts do not show ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)). Write posts with enough lead time for review, then check the status after publishing.

The Restaurant HQ recommends posting on social media at least once per week, ideally one to two times per day ([The Restaurant HQ](https://www.therestauranthq.com/operations/coffee-shop-marketing/), secondary). That guidance is for social media, not GBP, but it sets a practical floor: a weekly GBP post is the minimum habit to build.

Good post topics for a coffee shop include a seasonal drink, a new roast, an event, or a Memphis neighbourhood reference where it fits naturally. Keep each post specific, and avoid padding it with keywords.

## What should go in a coffee shop's Q&A and messages?

Per Diem recommends answering common questions proactively in the profile's Q&A section, such as whether you offer free Wi-Fi, dairy-free options, or allow dogs on the patio ([Per Diem guide](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). Seeding these answers yourself saves customers from guessing and keeps the wording consistent.

Google also allows users to message businesses directly through their listings ([Per Diem guide](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). Assign someone to check messages on a fixed schedule, since an unanswered message is a lost customer.

For more practical profile tips, read [Google Business Profile tips for coffee shops](/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-tips).

## How do reviews affect map pack rankings?

Google states that reviews show next to a business's profile in Maps and Search ([Google Business Profile tips on reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)). That means every review is visible at the moment a customer compares shops, so the review count and rating shape both ranking and click-through.

A BrightLocal survey cited by Ahrefs found that 36% of SEOs considered the Google Business Profile the most important ranking factor for the map pack, up from 23% in 2013 ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/seo/local-seo), secondary). Survey opinion is not a measured ranking formula, so treat it as a sign of where practitioners focus their effort.

Ask customers for reviews as part of the visit, and reply to each one with a short, specific response. For more on map visibility, see [how coffee shops dominate Google Maps](/blog/coffee-shops-dominate-google-maps).

## Does your website and map embed support local rankings?

Your website should match your GBP details exactly, including name, address and phone number. Inconsistent details across listings make it harder for Google to confirm that the business is real and where it sits. Check your name, address and phone on your site, GBP and the main directories before you add anything new.

Adding a Google map to your contact or location page helps visitors find you and reinforces your address. Follow the steps in [how to embed a Google Map on your local business website](/blog/google-maps-embed-local-business-website).

Keep page content natural. Mention Memphis and your neighbourhood where a visitor would expect it, not in repeated keyword blocks.

## What does a worked monthly ROI example look like?

The inputs below are illustrative assumptions, not benchmarks. Replace each one with your own data from GBP Insights and your point-of-sale system.

- Assumption: 60 visits per month come from Maps and Search (GBP actions).
- Assumption: $6 average spend per visit.
- Assumption: 2 hours per month for weekly posts and photo uploads.
- Assumption: 1 hour per month for review replies.
- Assumption: $25 value per hour of your time.

Monthly revenue from GBP-driven visits: 60 × $6 = $360. Time cost: 3 hours × $25 = $75. Net before product costs: $360 − $75 = $285. Setup costs nothing, so the main cost is time. Track the 60-visit figure for three months to see whether the trend holds.

## What should a Memphis coffee shop do in its first month?

Work through these steps in order:

1. Create or claim the Google Business Profile with a free Google account.
2. Choose "Coffee shop" as the primary category, then add secondary terms.
3. Complete verification, then confirm the profile shows publicly.
4. Add at least 20 photos: interior, drinks, menu board, storefront and staff at work.
5. Write a description that names your service, your Memphis location and your key difference.
6. Answer the common questions yourself in the Q&A section.
7. Publish the first weekly post, then check that its status is Live.
8. Set a review request routine and a reply schedule.

Once the profile is complete, run a short audit each month to compare your review count, post status and profile views.

## FAQ

### Is a Google Business Profile free for a Memphis coffee shop?

Yes. Google describes its Business Profile as free to set up ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)). You need a Google account, which is also free to register ([BrewRound](https://brewround.com/blog/coffee-shop-marketing/google-my-business-for-coffee-shops/), secondary).

### Why won't my coffee shop's updates show on Google?

Per Diem states that without verification, updates will not show publicly ([Per Diem guide](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary). Also check that each post has a Live status, since Pending and Not approved posts do not appear ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)).

### Can I use Google Ads with my Business Profile location?

Yes. Google states that location data in Google Ads is sourced from Google Maps and Google Business Profile, and corrections made there flow into linked Google Ads accounts ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)). For keyword choices, see [best Google Ads keywords for coffee shops](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-coffee-shops-in-2026).

### How many photos should I upload?

Upload a varied set covering the interior, drinks, menu, storefront and staff. The specific count is a practical target, not a Google requirement, so keep adding fresh photos as the shop changes.
