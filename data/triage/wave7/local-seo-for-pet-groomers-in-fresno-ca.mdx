## What does the Google Maps pack mean for a Fresno pet groomer?

The pack is the first thing a nearby customer sees when they search for a groomer on a phone. Adwave states that the top three businesses in the map pack are pulled directly from their GBP listings ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-market-my-lawn-care-business), secondary). That makes the profile itself the main thing you control.

Benchmarks on how much the profile matters vary. Adwave cites a 2025 multi-location benchmark that puts GBP signals at 32% of local pack ranking weight ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/multi-location-local-seo-audit), secondary). A BrightLocal survey cited by Ahrefs found 36% of SEOs in 2021 rated the GBP as the most important map pack ranking factor ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/seo/local-seo), secondary). Neither figure is specific to Fresno or to pet groomers.

Adwave also says a business may start ranking for its own name a few weeks after its profile is verified. This is the vendor's own estimate, so treat it as a rough expectation rather than a guarantee ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).

## Which Google Business Profile fields should a pet groomer fill in first?

Start with the category. Adwave recommends reviewing the top three to five businesses in the map results for your core search and noting which categories they use ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones), secondary). The same page says primary category is the number one ranking factor for the Local Pack, but it does not name the analysis behind that claim.

Next, complete the description. The profile allows 750 characters, so lead with the grooming services you offer, your Fresno location or neighbourhood, and what sets you apart ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary). Write for owners, not for search engines.

Photos and posts need regular upkeep. Adwave recommends at least 15 high-quality photos and at least one new post a week ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary). A different Adwave guide recommends 10 photos, so treat 10 as the floor and 15 as the target. Google shows a post publicly only when its status is Live. Posts marked Pending or Not approved do not appear ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)).

Hours, phone, and address must be accurate. Adwave says to keep them 100% accurate ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary). If you groom from home or travel to clients, you can hide your street address and be listed as a service area business ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).

Google's AI-driven answers draw on business profile data, website content, and reviews, according to one transition summary ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-q-and-a), secondary). For how this affects your listing, see [AI and Google Business Profile optimisation](/blog/ai-for-google-business-profile-optimization).

## How consistent must your name, address, and phone be?

Citations are listings of your name, address, and phone (NAP) on other sites. Adwave says your NAP must match exactly across your website, your GBP, and directories such as Yelp, Apple Maps, and Bing Places ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/voice-search-optimization-for-local-businesses), secondary). Small differences count, so check punctuation and abbreviations such as "St." versus "Street" ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).

Fix mismatches in existing listings before adding new ones. A landscaping article cites a case where clean-up led to more local pack appearances, but that is a single anecdote, so do not treat it as a benchmark ([AccountabilityNow](https://accountabilitynow.net/ai-google-business-profile-ranking-for-landscapers/), secondary).

Apple is worth a check. Apple Business Connect reports Profile Views, which count how many people saw your business card in Apple Maps ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/apple-business-connect-the-google-business-profile-alternative-youre-ignoring), secondary). Adwave also says Apple has a 7% share in a maps-only comparison and is growing. Check the live Apple help pages for current numbers before you decide.

If you run Google Ads, location data comes from Google Maps and your GBP. Corrections you make there flow into linked ad accounts ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)). Keeping the profile clean helps both your organic and paid listings.

## How many reviews do you need, and how do you ask for them?

Reviews show next to your profile in Maps and Search ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)). Their number and rating also matter for competitiveness. An analysis by Blogging Wizard of 2 million profile pages found that businesses with over 200 Google reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business), secondary). Most Fresno groomers will not reach 200 reviews quickly, so track your own progress against your nearest competitors.

One article claims that businesses with more than 50 reviews and a 4.7+ average rating dominate top pack spots, but it cites no source ([AccountabilityNow](https://accountabilitynow.net/ai-google-business-profile-ranking-for-landscapers/), secondary). Use it only as a rough reference.

Ask for reviews after each appointment, and keep the request simple. Respond to reviews promptly. For a step-by-step profile checklist, see [Google Business Profile optimisation for pet groomers](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-for-pet-groomers).

## Which local SEO metrics should you track each month?

Adwave names six local search metrics as the useful ones to track ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-search-intent), secondary):

- Google Business Profile impressions
- Map pack views
- Clicks to call
- Direction requests
- Website visits from your GBP
- Branded search lift over time

Record the same six figures each month, so you can compare trends. Run a manual check of your map pack position for your top keywords, using a private browser window. Do not rely on a single check.

Set a baseline before you change anything, so you can tell whether profile work is paying off. Give each change at least a month of data before you judge it.

## FAQ

### Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Google describes its Business Profile as free to set up on Google Search and Maps ([Google](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/)).

### Will a new profile rank for its own name quickly?

Adwave says a business may start ranking for its own name a few weeks after verification. This is the vendor's own estimate, not a guarantee ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).

### Do pending GBP posts show to customers?

No. Google shows a post publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live. Pending and Not approved posts do not show ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)).

### Should a pet groomer use Apple Business Connect?

It is worth checking, because Adwave reports Apple Maps as a growing share of maps-only traffic and notes its Profile Views metric ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/apple-business-connect-the-google-business-profile-alternative-youre-ignoring), secondary). Check the live Apple help pages for current figures.
