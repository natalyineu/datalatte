## How Does Louisiana's Market Shape Your Plan?

Louisiana has two customer groups that behave differently. Residents return often and expect a familiar face, while visitors may come once during a festival or a trip. New Orleans draws heavy tourist traffic, while Baton Rouge, Lafayette, and Shreveport lean more on local employers and neighborhoods. A single statewide campaign usually blurs both groups together.

Build separate messages for each. Residents respond to repeat-visit offers and neighborhood names. Visitors respond to "near me" searches and time-limited offers tied to the dates they are in town. The rest of this guide treats those two groups as distinct so your budget reaches the right person.

## Why Does Google Business Profile Come First?

Local Falcon notes that over 1 billion people use Google Maps every month and describes a Google Business Profile as a digital storefront ([Local Falcon guide](https://www.localfalcon.com/blog/effective-local-marketing-strategies-for-smbs), secondary). For a neighborhood shop, that listing is often where a customer first sees your hours, photos, and reviews. Claiming it costs nothing, so finish it before spending on ads.

Set your categories and service areas to match the places you actually serve. A salon near a parish line, for example, should list the nearby towns its customers come from, not just its own address. Check the platform's live help page for current profile rules, because listing features change.

## Which Ads Should a Louisiana Service Business Run?

Local Services Ads are one option for eligible service categories. Local Falcon says they charge advertisers only for actual leads such as calls, messages, or bookings, and that they appear above all search results ([Local Falcon guide](https://www.localfalcon.com/blog/effective-local-marketing-strategies-for-smbs), secondary). Eligibility depends on your category and location, so confirm it on Google's help pages before you plan around it.

Standard search ads suit businesses that want control over keywords and copy. Use a radius around your location rather than the whole state, and name the neighborhood in the ad text. Bid on "near me" variants with care, since they signal intent but can cost more per click. For a comparison of how bidding and ad rules differ in another market, the [Germany guide](/blog/local-marketing-germany-small-business-google-ads) covers Google Ads in detail.

| Channel | Best for | How you pay | First step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Map discovery and reviews | Free to claim | Complete every field and add photos |
| Local Services Ads | Eligible service categories | Per actual lead (calls, messages, bookings) | Check eligibility for your category |
| Google search ads | High-intent "near me" searches | Per click | Set a local radius and name neighborhoods |
| Meta ads | Residents who already know you | Per impression or click | Retarget website and profile visitors |

## What Does a Monthly Budget Look Like?

The example below is a hypothetical New Orleans hair salon. Every input is an assumption you should replace with your own numbers and current auction data.

Monthly ad budget: $500 (assumption). Average cost per click: $2.00 (assumption). Click-to-booking rate: 5% (assumption). Average visit value: $80 (assumption).

Clicks: $500 divided by $2.00 gives 250 clicks. Bookings: 250 times 5% gives 12.5, so round down to 12. Revenue: 12 times $80 gives $960. Revenue return on the $500 spend is about 1.9 times, before product, staff, and overhead costs.

Missed visits matter as much as new ones. Curogram's guide uses a hypothetical $200 average visit to show how five lost appointments a week add up to roughly $52,000 a year ([Curogram guide](https://curogram.com/blog/how-to-reduce-no-show-appointments), secondary). Using the salon's assumed $80 visit, five no-shows a week equal $400 weekly, or $20,800 over 52 weeks. Retention work can protect more revenue than the ad budget adds.

## How Do You Win Back Lapsed Clients and Grow Referrals?

Booksy describes a Message Blasts feature that sends an automatic message to customers who have not booked a visit in some time ([Booksy guide](https://biz.booksy.com/en-gb/blog/salon-clients-vanished-how-to-bring-them-back-v), secondary). Use a reminder like this for clients who have gone quiet, and set a clear window for when a client counts as lapsed.

Booksy also advises stopping messages to any client who says they are not interested in win-back offers ([Booksy guide](https://biz.booksy.com/en-gb/blog/salon-clients-vanished-how-to-bring-them-back-v), secondary). Respecting that request protects your sender reputation and your relationships in a community where word of mouth travels quickly.

Referral programs work best when both people benefit. Booksy suggests offering a lower price only when a client brings a referral, with the referred person receiving the same discount ([Booksy guide](https://biz.booksy.com/en-gb/blog/salon-clients-vanished-how-to-bring-them-back-v), secondary). Impact outlines six strategies for building a referral program if you want a fuller framework ([Impact](https://impact.com/referral/benefits-of-referral-marketing/), secondary).

## Should Video and Meta Ads Be Part of the Plan?

Video is useful for showing a shop's atmosphere, but it is not required in year one. One article claims that videos under 90 seconds retain 50% of viewers and recommends capturing attention in the first 10 seconds ([Elite Business Magazine](https://elitebusinessmagazine.co.uk/sales-marketing/social/item/video-marketing-for-small-businesses-proven-examples-and-strategies-for-2024), secondary). That retention figure is not cited to a study, so test it on your own audience.

Meta ads suit residents who already know your name. Retarget people who visited your website or profile rather than cold audiences. For messaging-led bookings, the [Brazil guide](/blog/local-marketing-brazil-small-business-whatsapp-meta) covers how WhatsApp and Meta can work together, which is a useful contrast for Louisiana shops that take bookings by message.

## How Should You Handle Festival Visitors and Regulars?

Festival traffic is seasonal, so build a calendar. Start paid search for event weekends before they arrive, and pause or shift budget once they end. Keep resident offers running year-round so your regular revenue does not depend on visitors.

Use separate ad groups for visitor keywords and resident keywords. A visitor might search for a salon near a specific street, while a resident might search for their usual stylist. Different phrasing needs different copy. For another market where visitors and residents both drive a small business, see the [Singapore guide](/blog/local-marketing-singapore-small-business-guide).

## What Is a 90-Day Checklist for Louisiana Shops?

1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile, including photos, hours, and accurate service areas.
2. Check whether your category is eligible for Local Services Ads, using Google's live help pages.
3. Launch one search campaign with a local radius (assumption: 5 to 10 miles) and neighborhood names in the copy.
4. Write a win-back message for clients who have not booked within a window you set, and honor opt-outs.
5. Track bookings by source each month and move budget toward the channels that produce visits, not clicks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need Google Ads if my Google Business Profile is complete?

Not always. A complete profile is free and should come first. Paid search helps when you need visits faster or want to reach people searching for a specific service near you. Run the worked example with your own numbers to see whether the return justifies the spend.

### Are Local Services Ads available for every business?

No. Eligibility depends on your category and location. Check Google's live help pages for the current list before planning a budget around them.

### How much should a small Louisiana business spend on ads?

There is no sourced benchmark for Louisiana. Use the calculation in this guide with your own assumed cost per click, booking rate, and visit value. Start with a budget you can measure for at least a few weeks.

### Is video worth making for a local service business?

It can help, but the retention claim we cite comes from a secondary article and is not independently sourced. Test short clips on your own audience before committing a large share of your budget to video.
