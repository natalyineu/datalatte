## What is the Google Map Pack, and why does it matter for a New Orleans studio?

The Google Maps local pack is the group of top three business listings that appears in local search results ([Adwave, local SEO audit checklist](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-audit-checklist-15-things-to-fix-today), secondary). For a studio, that group appears for queries such as "personal training studios near me," according to a trade association guide ([Association of Fitness Studios](https://member.afsfitness.com/content/how-make-your-fitness-business-stand-out-google), secondary).

Google describes its Business Profile as a free tool, and it can show your hours, phone number, address, reviews, and photos directly in search results ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/), primary). Treat vendor weightings as estimates. One benchmark cited by Adwave puts the profile at 32% of local pack ranking weight in 2025 ([Adwave, multi-location audit](https://adwave.com/resources/multi-location-local-seo-audit), secondary).

Check the live map results for your own category and neighborhood before setting priorities, because local competition varies from one area to the next.

## How do you set up the Google Business Profile for a New Orleans studio?

Start with the primary category. Adwave advises reviewing the top three to five businesses in local map results for your service search to see which category they use ([Adwave, categories](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-categories-choosing-the-right-ones), secondary). The same article says analysis of millions of profiles shows the primary category is the number one ranking factor for the local pack, though it does not name that analysis.

Next, write the description. Adwave notes that a profile description allows 750 characters ([Adwave, beginner's guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary). Lead with the service, the New Orleans neighborhood, and what sets the studio apart, and write it for a person reading it.

Hours, phone number, and address should be 100% accurate, according to Adwave ([Adwave, Google Maps marketing](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary). If you work from home or travel to clients, you can hide your street address and be designated a Service Area Business ([Adwave, beginner's guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).

## How often should a studio post, answer questions, and reply to messages?

Google Posts act as free mini-billboards on your profile in Search and Maps, and Adwave advises at least one new post a week ([Adwave, Google Maps marketing](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary). Google's help page says a post shows publicly on Search and Maps only when its status is Live. Pending and not-approved posts do not show, so check the status after publishing ([Google Help, posts](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en), primary).

Google launched Q&A in Maps in 2017 ([Adwave, Q&A](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-q-and-a), secondary). Seed the section with the questions customers ask most, such as whether there is a free trial, and answer them directly. A transition summary reported by Adwave says AI-driven answers draw on profile data, website content, and reviews, so keeping all three consistent matters.

Customer chats can be managed from the Business Profile dashboard on a desktop, not only in the mobile Maps app ([Adwave, messaging](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-messaging-should-you-turn-it-on), secondary).

## How consistent do your name, address, and phone details need to be?

NAP means name, address, and phone number. Adwave says your NAP must be exactly the same on your website, your Business Profile, and major directories such as Yelp, Apple Maps, and Bing Places ([Adwave, voice search](https://adwave.com/resources/voice-search-optimization-for-local-businesses), secondary). The same vendor's beginner guide says the match should hold right down to punctuation and whether "St." or "Street" is used.

Start with an audit of existing listings. Duplicate listings with different details create conflicting records, so correcting them usually comes before adding new citations. Booking platforms you already use, such as Mindbody or ClassPass, are worth checking for the same details.

Apple Maps also deserves a claimed listing. Adwave reports that Apple Business Connect holds a 7% share in a maps-only comparison that excludes web search, and that the figure is growing ([Adwave, Apple Business Connect](https://adwave.com/resources/apple-business-connect-the-google-business-profile-alternative-youre-ignoring), secondary).

## How many reviews does a New Orleans studio need, and how long will it take?

Google states that reviews show next to your Business Profile in Maps and Search ([Google Help, reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122), primary). A third-party analysis by Blogging Wizard of 2 million profile pages found that businesses with over 200 Google reviews are significantly more likely to appear in the local pack ([Adwave, reviews](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business), secondary).

Vendor thresholds disagree. One gym guide says 100+ reviews with a 4.7+ rating dominate the map pack and that ratings below 4.5 suppress conversion ([Adwave, gyms](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-gyms-fitness-centers), secondary). A landscaping guide claims 50+ reviews at 4.7+ dominate top spots ([Accountability Now](https://accountabilitynow.net/ai-google-business-profile-ranking-for-landscapers/), secondary). The same gym guide says organic channels take 6-12 months to gain real traction.

Worked example (all inputs are assumptions, not sourced data): a studio starts with 40 reviews and targets 200. At an assumed 3 new reviews per week, it needs 160 divided by 3, about 54 weeks, or roughly 12 months. At an assumed 5 per week, it needs 32 weeks, or about 7 months. Both paces fall inside the 6-12 month window.

## Where do Google Ads fit alongside the map pack?

Google's help page says location assets in Google Ads are sourced directly from Google Maps and Google Business Profile, and successful corrections made there flow into linked Ads accounts ([Google Ads Help, location assets](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en), primary). Keeping the profile accurate therefore helps paid campaigns too.

If the profile option does not work for your setup, you can select up to 10 Maps locations to link with your Ads account. Those locations must be yours, or they may be disapproved ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en), primary).

For the paid side, see the [Google Ads guide for fitness studios](/blog/google-ads-for-fitness-studios-complete-2026-guide), and for keyword choices, the [best Google ad keywords for fitness studios](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-fitness-studios-in-2026).

## Which metrics should a studio track each month?

Adwave lists six local search metrics worth tracking: Google Business Profile impressions, map-pack views, clicks to call, direction requests, website visits from the profile, and branded search lift over time ([Adwave, local search intent](https://adwave.com/resources/local-search-intent), secondary). Record them monthly so you can compare changes against the work you did.

Expect a delay before your name ranks. A vendor estimate says a business may begin ranking for its own name in the Map Pack a few weeks after verification ([Adwave, beginner's guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).

For a deeper profile checklist, read the [fitness studio Google Business Profile guide](/blog/fitness-studio-google-business-profile-optimization). Review benchmarks for another city are in the [Denver fitness studio review guide](/blog/denver-fitness-studios-google-reviews-benchmarks-2026), but local numbers differ by market.

## FAQ

### Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Google describes it as a free Business Profile on Google Search and Maps ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/ca-en/business-profile/), primary).

### How many photos should a studio upload?

Adwave recommends at least 15 high-quality photos and says a robust gallery gets far more clicks and calls ([Adwave, Google Maps marketing](https://adwave.com/resources/google-maps-marketing-getting-found-by-nearby-customers), secondary). A different vendor's to-do list suggests at least 10 ([Adwave, beginner's guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).

### Do Google Posts appear as soon as I publish them?

Not always. Google says a post is public on Search and Maps only when its status is Live. Pending and not-approved posts do not show ([Google Help, posts](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en), primary).

### How long before a studio ranks in the map pack?

Vendors give different answers. One gym guide says organic channels take 6-12 months to gain real traction ([Adwave, gyms](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-gyms-fitness-centers), secondary). Another estimates a few weeks before ranking for your own business name ([Adwave, beginner's guide](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-for-small-business-a-beginners-guide-to-the-map-pack), secondary).
