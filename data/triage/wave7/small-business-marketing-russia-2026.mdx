## Which Channels Matter Most for a Local Business in Russia?

Search comes first. Yandex is the local search engine that users in Russia prefer, so a Yandex Business profile and Yandex Direct are the core channels for a cafe, salon, studio or shop. The profile tells people where you are and when you are open, and a paid campaign puts your offer in front of people who are already searching.

Yandex's advertising network reaches further than search alone. According to the RMAA Agency page, the Yandex Advertising Network can show ads on hundreds of thousands of sites, mobile apps and video platforms. Yandex Direct's own Google Play description is narrower, saying its ads appear on Yandex and tens of thousands of partner sites.

This overview does not set out reach figures for social networks or messengers. Test those channels on a small budget and check their current rules before you commit money. ## Why Does Yandex Matter More Than Google in Russia?

Google is not a practical paid option for most Russian advertisers. The RMAA Agency page says that, due to sanctions, Google Ads is unavailable for paid promotions in Russia, and it positions Yandex Direct as the essential alternative. That is the position of one agency, so check Google's current help pages and your own account status before you plan.

The local search preference is the second reason. A marketing agency article on foreign-market campaigns says that in Russia and China users prefer local search engines, Yandex and Baidu. For a Russian shop, that makes Yandex the default place to test search ads, Maps-style listings and location targeting.

## How Does Yandex Direct Charge and Target Ads?

Yandex Direct works on a pay-per-click model, so advertisers pay for each click on their ad rather than for impressions. That makes the cost of a click the main number to track. Set a daily budget you can afford to lose while you learn which search phrases bring the people who actually book or visit.

Scheduling is one of the more useful controls. According to the RMAA Agency page, Yandex Direct lets you set specific time intervals for showing ads, such as only during working hours or on weekends. A salon can focus spend on the hours when bookings are taken, and a studio can pause ads outside the times it has staff available.

Payment is a practical limit. A developer reply on the Yandex Direct Google Play listing, dated to a 2022 review, says payment in the app is available only in Russian rubles. Plan your billing in rubles and check the current payment options in your Yandex Direct account before you top up.

## How Should You Set Up Your Yandex Business Profile?

A complete profile makes the paid campaign work harder, because people who click through will see accurate details. Work through these steps in your Yandex Business account:

1. Create or claim the business profile using the address customers visit, and check that the category matches what you sell.
2. Add the name, address and opening hours exactly as they appear on your website and signage.
3. Add a phone number and a booking link, and test both on a phone.
4. Upload real photos of the interior, products or work, not stock images.
5. Reply to every review, using a short script you can keep up every week.

Do not treat the profile as a one-off task. Review it monthly, update hours for holidays, and remove any listing that no longer matches your business.

## What Legal and Privacy Checks Come First?

The primary legislative act governing advertising in Russia is Federal Law No. 38-FZ "On Advertising", dated March 13, 2006, according to the RMAA Agency page. Check the current text and any amendments before you run campaigns, because this overview does not cover marking requirements, fines or the rules for specific ad formats.

Privacy is the second check. The developer's data safety section for the Yandex Direct app says the app may collect personal and financial information, and may share financial information and app activity with third parties. Read that section before you link accounts, and limit the customer data you store to what your bookings need.

If you are a foreign company or person, sanctions and export rules may apply to services you provide or payments you receive. Take legal advice before you contract with Russian platforms, banks or clients.

## Who Should Run the Campaign?

Many small owners can set up a profile and a first test, but the budget needs someone who checks the numbers every week. If you outsource the work, agree on the metrics first: cost per click, cost per booking, and the share of clicks that turn into calls or visits. Ask any candidate how they would handle the sanctions and legal questions above, and who in your business approves ads.
