## How much does Buffer cost for a coffee shop?

Buffer prices each plan per channel, so the bill grows with every account you connect. [Sintra's comparison of social media AI agents](https://sintra.ai/blog/ai-social-media/best-social-media-ai-agents) (secondary) lists the Free plan at up to 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts per channel, 100 AI ideas and one user. Essentials starts at $5 per channel per month billed annually with unlimited scheduled posts, and Team starts at $10 per channel per month billed annually and adds unlimited team members.

The table below applies those per-channel prices to a three-channel cafe. The channel count is an assumption.

| Plan | Cost for 3 channels (assumption) | What the source says |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 10 scheduled posts per channel, one user |
| Essentials | $15 a month, or $180 a year | Unlimited scheduled posts, 14-day free trial |
| Team | $30 a month, or $360 a year | Adds unlimited team members |

Compare these with the agency range. A coffee shop that pays for a social media agency faces $800 to $5,000 a month, while posting on social media costs only time, per the same guide. For a single-owner cafe, the Free or Essentials plan leaves most of the budget free for other work. The [marketing automation tools compared](/blog/best-marketing-automation-tools-small-business-compared) guide covers how other platforms price against Buffer.

## How much time does each platform take a coffee shop?

Bellwether Coffee's [social media guide for coffee shops](https://bellwethercoffee.com/blog/social-media-marketing-for-coffee-shops) (secondary) estimates the daily time each platform needs. Instagram takes 30 minutes a day, Facebook 15 minutes, Google Business Profile 5 minutes and TikTok 1 hour. The same guide recommends 2 to 3 hours each week for shooting and writing content.

Across the three core channels, that is 50 minutes a day, or about 5.8 hours a week before content work. Add the 2 to 3 hours of content creation and the total lands near 8 to 9 hours a week. Those are the guide's estimates, not measured results. Buffer's queue should cut the publishing part, since [repetitive, rule-based marketing tasks](https://www.ringcentral.com/us/en/blog/10-marketing-automation-tools-every-smb-needs/) such as social media publishing can be automated.

For a shop that cannot spare that time, the [AI tools for coffee shop marketing automation](/blog/ai-tools-for-coffee-shop-marketing-automation) article explains which steps can be handed off first.

## Which three channels should a coffee shop schedule?

Start with the channels your customers use. A [Pew Research figure cited by The Restaurant HQ](https://www.therestauranthq.com/operations/coffee-shop-marketing/) reports that 53% of urban social media users are on Instagram, and that 70% of rural users prefer Facebook. Choose Instagram for urban or younger customers and a Facebook Page for rural or older ones, or both if your area mixes them.

Google Business Profile matters for search. OneEveryMap, citing Backlinko's 2024 research, reports that 42% of local searchers click the Google map pack. It also cites Google's 2024 finding that a complete Business Profile makes customers 70% more likely to visit a location. Keep the profile's hours and offers current, because a posting tool cannot fix an outdated listing.

Check Buffer's live help page for the current list of supported channels before you connect accounts. The [Hootsuite comparison for coffee shops](/blog/coffee-shop-marketing-with-hootsuite) shows how another scheduler handles the same three channels.

## How often should a cafe post, and what should each post do?

The Restaurant HQ guide recommends that coffee shops post on social media at least once per week, ideally one to two times per day. Consistency matters more than volume for a small team. Bellwether sets a goal engagement rate of 3 to 6% on Instagram, which gives you a benchmark for judging whether a post works.

A 4-post weekly plan is a template for a shop with one owner, not a measured result. Batch it in one 60 to 90 minute session:

- Monday: a drink or food photo with the day's special.
- Wednesday: a short Reel of latte art, roasting or prep.
- Friday: a weekend offer or event with one clear call to action.
- Sunday: a customer photo, posted only after the customer agrees.

A 2021 marketing page on [social video engagement](https://www.yourdigitalresource.com/post/seo-for-restaurants-coffee) (secondary) claims that social videos get 1,200% more engagements than images and text combined. Treat that figure as dated and use it to justify one video a week, not a full video calendar.

If you run contests with a branded hashtag, [Square's cafe marketing guide](https://squareup.com/ca/en/the-bottom-line/reaching-customers/marketing-plan-for-a-coffee-shop) advises reviewing each platform's terms and conditions first. Build that check into the batching session.

## How do reviews fit into the posting plan?

Reviews reach more people than most posts. BrightLocal, as reported by OneEveryMap, found in 2026 that 97% of consumers read reviews for local businesses, and that 54% visit a business's website after reading positive reviews. Use the Sunday slot in your plan to share a customer photo, and ask for a review at the same time.

Buffer does not replace replies to reviews on Google. Set aside 10 minutes a day for comments and review responses. The [AI agent for social media posting](/blog/ai-agent-for-social-media-posting-local-business) article covers which replies you can draft in advance and which need your own voice.

## When is Buffer the wrong choice?

Buffer fits a one-owner cafe with two or three channels. Larger operations need more. OneEveryMap's roundup of multi-location tools, citing [SocioLocal's ranking](https://sociolocal.io/blog/8-best-multi-location-social-media-management-tools-2026-rankings) (secondary), describes SocioLocal as best for brands with 100+ locations. The same roundup gives Sprout Social's Standard plan at $199 per seat per month and Advanced at $399 per seat, with Enterprise custom-priced. Those seat prices dwarf Buffer's per-channel plans, so only pay for them if you run many locations.

## FAQ

### Is Buffer free for a coffee shop?

Yes, for up to 3 social channels with 10 scheduled posts per channel and one user, according to [Sintra's comparison](https://sintra.ai/blog/ai-social-media/best-social-media-ai-agents) (secondary). Essentials removes the scheduled-post limit and starts at $5 per channel per month when billed annually.

### How much does Buffer cost for three channels?

Using the per-channel prices, three channels cost $15 a month on Essentials or $30 a month on Team, both billed annually. That works out to $180 and $360 a year. These totals are a worked calculation, not a quoted price.

### How many times a week should a coffee shop post?

The Restaurant HQ guide recommends at least once a week, ideally one to two times per day. A 4-post weekly plan is a practical starting template for one owner, not a measured result.

### Does Buffer's free plan post to Google Business Profile?

Check Buffer's live help page before you rely on it. The sources reviewed here do not confirm the current channel list, so verify it before you connect the account.

### What should a coffee shop do before running a contest?

Review each platform's terms and conditions for contests, as Square's cafe marketing guide advises, especially when the contest uses a branded hashtag.
