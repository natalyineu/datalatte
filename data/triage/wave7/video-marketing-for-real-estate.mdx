## Do Buyers Actually Use Video and Virtual Tours?

Buyers search online. A vendor page citing the National Association of Realtors says 100% of buyers used the internet in their home search. That is a broad signal, not proof that a specific video lifts sales. It tells you that a listing without good online media is competing with listings that have it.

Be careful with the numbers. Adwave pages give 400% more inquiries in one place and 403% in another, which shows how loosely these figures travel. One Adwave page also quotes a 73% seller preference for video-using agents without naming a source, so this article does not use it. Use the 40% and 87% figures as a direction and measure your own enquiries.

## Which Videos Should an Agent Make First?

Adwave suggests filming a 90-second walkthrough of each new listing as the first video tour. Keep the order simple:

1. Film a vertical walkthrough of about 90 seconds, one room at a time, ending with price and how to book a viewing.
2. Produce a full prerecorded tour for the listing page that covers every room, the street and the outside.
3. Record a short neighbourhood video of you answering one question buyers ask, such as what the street is like on a weekday.

Adwave also says a buyer who spends 20 minutes clicking through a virtual tour has effectively given themselves a private showing. That is a qualitative claim, not measured data. Professional photography is still the baseline, since Adwave calls it mandatory for every listing.

If you want a fast first cut, Adwave's free plan for its Wavemaker tool includes 75 credits, which covers a roughly 30-second listing video at 480p with a watermark. The vendor says most videos generate in about 2 to 5 minutes after the script is approved. Check the live pricing page before relying on it.

## Where Should Each Video Go?

Give each video one job. The listing page converts because you control the contact form. Short vertical clips suit Instagram Reels and Facebook, where the [Facebook ads guide for real estate](/blog/facebook-ads-for-real-estate) covers paid reach. Email works for past enquiries and open-house follow-up, as explained in [email marketing for real estate](/blog/email-marketing-for-real-estate).

| Place | Best for | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listing page | Full prerecorded tour | You control the page and form |
| Instagram and Facebook | Vertical walkthrough | Paid boosts need a housing-ad policy check |
| YouTube | Searchable tours and area videos | Check the live YouTube Help page for length limits |
| Email | Past enquiries, open houses | Send the walkthrough link, not the full file |

StackAdapt describes lead ads as a way to let a target audience complete a form with just a few clicks, which makes it easy to book a virtual appointment. Pair the walkthrough video with a lead form when you run paid campaigns.

## How Fast Should an Enquiry Get a Reply?

Speed matters because a video can create interest that fades quickly. Adwave's lifecycle example triggers a CMA request email 15 minutes after a listing landing-page form submission. You can copy that timing for any listing enquiry: an automatic reply with the full tour link, then a personal follow-up the same day.

For open houses, geofencing is worth a look. A geofence is a virtual fence around a real-world location, and it can trigger a mobile ad when someone enters the area, according to StackAdapt. Check the platform's current targeting rules and housing-ad restrictions before setting one up. The [Google ad keywords guide for real estate agents](/blog/best-google-ad-keywords-for-real-estate-agents-in-2026) covers search intent if you want paid search alongside video.

## What Does a Video Budget Look Like?

Every input below is an **assumption** for a solo agent with a phone, except the 10% rule and the $6 price, which come from Adwave.

| Item | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expected commission per listing | $12,000 | Assumption, inside the $10,000 to $20,000 range Adwave states |
| Advertising budget at 10% | $1,200 | Vendor suggestion (Adwave) |
| Shoot and edit time | 3.5 hours | Assumption |
| Value of your time | $50 per hour | Assumption |
| Time cost per listing | $175 | 3.5 x $50 |
| Total cost per listing | $1,375 | $1,200 + $175 |
| Video-led enquiries per listing | 3 | Assumption |
| Cost per video-led enquiry | $458 | $1,375 / 3, rounded |

On these inputs, the full cost is about 11.5% of the $12,000 commission. If you outsource a vertical cut, Adwave lists $6 per social video, which adds $12 for two clips. If your real cost per enquiry is much higher than your target, cut the paid spend and keep only the free listing-page tour.

Ad wording rules differ by country and platform. Review narration and on-screen text against local housing advertising rules and platform policies before you boost any video.

## How Do You Run a 90-Day Test?

Adwave's real estate advertising plan splits into Month 1 (days 1 to 30), Month 2 (days 31 to 60) and Month 3 (days 61 to 90). Use that structure to test video on your own listings:

1. Film a 90-second walkthrough of your next new listing, as Adwave suggests.
2. Publish the full tour on the listing page and link the walkthrough in your social and email posts.
3. Log the source of every enquiry for all 90 days, so you can count video-led leads.
4. Compare enquiry numbers with a recent listing that had no video, over the same period.
5. At day 90, keep the formats that cost less per enquiry than your target, and drop the rest.

## FAQ

### What should a real estate agent film first?
Adwave suggests a 90-second walkthrough of each new listing as the first video tour, followed by a full prerecorded tour for the listing page.

### Do virtual tours increase clicks?
An Adwave page reports that listings with virtual tours get 40% more clicks than those without. It is vendor-reported, so measure it on your own listings.

### How long does an AI listing video take to make?
Adwave says most Wavemaker videos generate in about 2 to 5 minutes after the script is approved. Its free plan includes 75 credits for a roughly 30-second video at 480p with a watermark.

### How much should I spend on video ads for one listing?
Adwave suggests setting aside 10% of the expected commission for that property. The worked example above uses a $12,000 assumed commission, which gives a $1,200 budget.
