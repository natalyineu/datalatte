A coffee shop website can be one page: opening hours, an address with a map, a menu in plain text, one button for ordering or calling, and a few real photos. Build it for a phone first. Adwave's summary of third-party checklists says more than 60% of website traffic now comes from mobile devices ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/mobile-website-design-checklist-for-local-businesses)). This guide covers the page-by-page layout, the menu, local search basics and a worked example. No conversion lift is promised, because no neutral source publishes one for coffee shops.

## What Does a Coffee Shop Homepage Need?

Start with what a hungry person nearby wants to know. Put these items near the top of a phone screen, in this order: whether you are open now, your address with a tap-to-open map, one main action, a text menu, and two or three photos of your real shop and drinks.

Digital launch checklists treat the basics as the baseline. A [café opening checklist from TGP](https://www.tgpinternational.com/blog/the-ultimate-caf%C3%A9-opening-checklist-a-complete-pre-launch-guide) (secondary) lists a Google Maps listing, delivery platforms and website basics as digital presence items for launch. The site should match what those listings say, so one change does not leave a stale address somewhere else.

## How Should the Menu Appear on the Site?

Publish the menu as text on the page rather than only as an image or file. Text can be read on a small screen and updated in a minute. A photo of a chalkboard is harder to scan and cannot be searched.

Answer the questions customers ask most before they call. Per Diem recommends answering common questions in the Google profile's Q&A section proactively, such as whether you offer free Wi-Fi, dairy-free options, or dogs on the patio ([Per Diem, secondary](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide)). Repeat those answers in a short FAQ block on the homepage so the same facts appear in both places.

## How Do You Make the Site Show Up Locally?

Use the same name, address and phone number on the site and on the Google Business Profile. Inconsistent details make it harder for search engines to match the two. Per Diem also notes that skipping hours, categories or the business description can limit a shop's visibility in searches ([Per Diem, secondary](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide)).

Write page titles the way people search. Salesforce gives examples of long-tail local phrases such as "best coffee shop in [city]" ([Salesforce UK, secondary](https://www.salesforce.com/uk/marketing/local-marketing/?bc=OTH)). Use a phrase like that in the title and the first heading, but only where it describes your shop accurately. ## How Fast and Mobile-Friendly Should the Page Be?

Adwave's mobile checklist recommends at least 48px touch targets for buttons and links so they are easy to tap on a phone ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/mobile-website-design-checklist-for-local-businesses)). Make the order button large, keep it away from other links, and test it with your thumb on a real phone.

Adwave's local SEO audit places mobile-friendly and fast performance under a "Fix Now" heading ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/local-seo-audit-checklist-15-things-to-fix-today)). Its redesign guide says Google uses Core Web Vitals, including LCP, FID and CLS, as page-experience signals, and recommends testing components in tools like Lighthouse ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/website-redesign-checklist-when-and-how-to-refresh-your-site)). Oversized photos and auto-playing video are the usual causes of slow café pages, so compress images and set their dimensions.

## What Should a Site Redesign Keep?

A redesign can break traffic you already have. Adwave advises matching each old URL to the closest new destination and avoiding sending everything to the homepage. It also says a page with traffic, rankings, links or conversion history should not be removed without a replacement plan ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/website-redesign-checklist-when-and-how-to-refresh-your-site)).

Before launch, list every page on the current site and mark which ones get visits. Redirect those to their new equivalents. Adwave's booking page guide recommends three days of checks before sending paid traffic to a booking page ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/appointment-booking-page-design)), and the same discipline suits a menu or order page.

## What Is a Returning Customer Worth?

Use this worked example to decide how much a better order button might be worth. All inputs are **assumptions** you should replace with your own numbers.

| Input | Value | Label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly visitors from local search | 300 | assumption |
| Share who tap order or call | 5% | assumption |
| Average ticket | $12 | assumption |
| Order-ahead sales per month, current | 300 x 5% x $12 = $180 | calculated |
| Order-ahead sales per month, if share rises to 8% | 300 x 8% x $12 = $288 | calculated |
| Monthly difference | $288 - $180 = $108 | calculated |

Adwave's customer lifetime value example uses $5 per visit, four visits a week for three years, which gives $3,120 in total revenue from one person ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/customer-lifetime-value-small-business)). Its benchmark table puts coffee shop CLV at $1,200 to $7,000. Keeping one regular is worth far more than a single extra order, so the page should make returning easy too.

If you have few visitors, fix the Business Profile before the site. ## FAQ

### Does a coffee shop need a website if it has Instagram and Google Maps?
A website gives you a menu, hours and an order link you control. Social accounts and map listings are useful, but the site is the page you own. Keep all three consistent.

### What should the homepage show first?
Show whether you are open, your address, one main action and a text menu. Photos support these items but do not replace them.

### How fast should the site load?
Check Core Web Vitals in tools like Lighthouse before launch and after each big change. Compress photos and remove auto-playing video first.

### Do I need structured data?
It is optional for a one-page site. The priority is a consistent name, address and phone number across the site and the Business Profile.

### How much should I budget for advertising?
Adwave gives $500 to $1,000 a month as a reasonable starting budget for multi-channel advertising for a local shop ([Adwave, secondary](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-local-shops-2026)). That covers ads, not the cost of building the site, so compare builder plans separately.
