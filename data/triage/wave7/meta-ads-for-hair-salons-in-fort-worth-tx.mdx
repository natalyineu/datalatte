## How Much Should a Fort Worth Salon Spend on Meta Ads?

The starting point most sources give is a few hundred to about fifteen hundred dollars a month. Bookeo recommends USD 750 to USD 1,500 monthly for Facebook and Instagram ads for beauty salons ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). Adwave puts a standalone Meta buy at often $300 to $1,000 per month across local businesses ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-choose-advertising-channels)), so the salon range sits at the higher end of that band.

Spending at the low end of a range leaves fewer ads to compare. A budget near the bottom of the range is better for testing one offer, while a budget near the top gives room to run two or three ad sets at once.

## Which Ad Formats Work for Hair Salons?

Short video is the format to start with. Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, as relayed by Adwave, says short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/electrician-advertising-reaching-homeowners), secondary). That figure comes from a general report, not from salon accounts, so test it against your own static posts.

Stories have a different role. Associated Hair Professionals describes Instagram and Facebook stories as temporary content ([Associated Hair Professionals](https://www.associatedhairprofessionals.com/updates/blog-posts/power-social-media-your-hairstyling-brand)), which makes them a good fit for limited-time offers that should disappear after a set date. For organic posting, Bookeo says most salon owners find three to four Instagram posts and one to two Facebook posts weekly sustainable ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). A deeper look at Instagram content is in [Instagram marketing for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons).

## How Should You Set the Radius and Audience?

Bookeo advises setting a 5 to 10 mile radius around the salon for Facebook and Instagram ads ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). A tighter radius suits a salon that draws most clients from nearby neighbourhoods, while a wider one fits a salon with a strong reputation across the metro area. Adjust after the first two weeks using the reach and booking data in Ads Manager.

Peak activity times are useful for scheduling posts. Bookeo points to the Insights tab in Instagram or Facebook for this, which requires a business account ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). Audience setup for Facebook specifically is covered in [Facebook ads for hair salons](/blog/facebook-ads-for-hair-salons).

## Does Retargeting Bring Clients Back?

Retargeting is where Meta tends to pay off for returning clients. Adwave lists a typical Meta retargeting CPM of $5 to $15 ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/retargeting-ads-small-business)), which is a useful planning figure when you budget for a list of past clients who have visited your site or engaged with your posts.

Bookeo also claims that a client who has seen a salon's brand on Facebook five to ten times converts at two to three times the rate of a cold searcher. The page gives no source for that figure, so treat it as an unverified vendor claim and measure your own repeat-booking rate. For how retargeting fits a wider plan, see [using Meta ads for hair salon marketing](/blog/using-meta-ads-for-hair-salon-marketing).

## How Do You Set Up Meta Ads Manager?

Meta Ads Manager is the single place to run ads on both platforms. Adobe describes it as a centralised point for running ads across Instagram and Facebook ([Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-advertising)). Instagram ads go through the same system because Meta owns Instagram ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/instagram-advertising-for-local-business)), and one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook or both.

Meta's free Blueprint courses cover Facebook and Instagram advertising and include a certificate on completion ([SASCOM247](https://sascom247.com/digital-marketing-for-beginners-in-nigeria/), secondary). A short course is a reasonable first step before the first campaign goes live.

1. Confirm the salon has a business account on Instagram and Facebook.
2. Set the radius to 5 to 10 miles around the salon.
3. Choose one short video and one static image per ad set, then compare them.
4. Set a monthly budget that matches a planned test period, not a daily guess.
5. Send ad clicks to a booking page and track bookings, not likes.
6. Review cost per booking after two to four weeks, then adjust the budget.

Follow-up matters after a booking request. [AI-powered email marketing for hair salons](/blog/ai-powered-email-marketing-for-hair-salons) shows how to keep new enquiries warm, and [marketing automation for hair salons](/blog/marketing-automation-for-hair-salons) covers the steps that connect ads to reminders.

## FAQ

### What is a realistic first Meta ad budget for a hair salon?

Bookeo suggests starting Facebook and Instagram ad spend at USD 750 to USD 1,500 monthly. A smaller test is possible, but the first month should run long enough to compare two or three ads.

### Do I need a business account to see peak follower times?

Yes. Bookeo says the Insights tab that shows when followers are online requires a business account on Instagram or Facebook.

### Can one campaign run on both Instagram and Facebook?

Yes. Adwave notes that Instagram ads run through Meta Ads Manager, and a single campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook or both.

### Are the cost figures in this guide specific to Fort Worth?

No. The ranges come from national or vendor guides. Check live cost estimates in Meta Ads Manager for your own radius and audience before setting a final budget.
