## Which Channels Should a South African Business Test First?

Meta's apps (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp and Threads) reach an average of 3.56 billion daily active people, according to [AdWave's Facebook advertising guide](https://adwave.com/resources/facebook-advertising-for-small-business). That is a platform-wide figure, not a South African count, so it shows the scale of the audience rather than local demand.

Scale matters less than the match between your offer and the customer. A tradesperson or salon often gets enquiries from people who need a service nearby, while a shop selling products may suit Facebook ads better. Compare the two routes in [Google Ads vs Facebook Ads for local business](/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-local-business-which-wins) before you commit money to either.

## How Does Click-to-WhatsApp Work for Ads?

Crescendo Lab, a vendor, describes its Click-to-WhatsApp Ads feature as one that "seamlessly transitions potential customers from Facebook and Instagram ads to personalized WhatsApp conversations" ([Crescendo Lab's WhatsApp marketing guide](https://blog.cresclab.com/en/whatsapp-marketing)). In practice, a person taps the ad, opens a chat, and the first contact happens in WhatsApp rather than on a form.

This path suits businesses where customers already ask questions by message. Write a short greeting, prepare a few standard answers, and log every enquiry so you can count how many become customers. Check Meta's current help pages for how the ad format and messaging limits work today, since platform features change.

## Does Page Speed Change Ad Results?

Yes, according to the figures below. WasteNot, citing Google research, reports that the chance of a user bouncing rises by 32% when page load time goes from 1 to 3 seconds, and by 90% from 1 to 5 seconds ([WasteNot](https://www.wastenot.io/blog/retargeting-facebook-ads), secondary). Each extra second of delay can reduce retail conversions by up to 20%, according to Google's mobile speed guidance as summarised by [AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/mobile-first-website-design) (secondary).

A paid click that lands on a slow page is money spent on a visitor who leaves. Test your landing page on a mid-range phone over mobile data, not only on a office Wi-Fi connection. Compress images and remove anything that delays the first screen.

## Should You Hire an Agency or Learn In-House?

Sascom247 states that Facebook or Google Ads management typically earns 15% to 20% of the managed ad spend as a management fee ([Sascom247](https://sascom247.com/digital-marketing-for-beginners-in-nigeria/), secondary). On the R3,000 test budget above, that is an assumed R450 to R600 a month on top of the media spend. Ask for the fee in writing and confirm whether it is calculated on spend or on a flat rate.

The same page says Google's Digital Skills for Africa programme and the Google Digital Marketing and E-commerce Certificate are both free. Owners who want to run a small test themselves can start there. The [Google Ads management guide](/blog/google-ads-management-small-business-what-to-expect) lists what a manager should report, and the [affordable marketing help guide](/blog/affordable-marketing-help-small-business-2026) covers lower-cost options.

## What Should You Do in the First 30 Days?

1. Set a test budget you can afford to lose, such as the R3,000 assumed above, and split it across one campaign.
2. Load your landing page or Business Profile on a phone and fix anything that feels slow.
3. Set up a WhatsApp Business profile with a greeting, quick replies and a clear hours statement.
4. Log every enquiry with its source, so you know which ad produced it.
5. Review cost per customer weekly, and only increase the budget when the number holds.

Tools can help with follow-up and reporting. The [marketing automation tools comparison](/blog/best-marketing-automation-tools-small-business) and [AI marketing tools guide](/blog/ai-marketing-tools-for-small-business) show what each one does, so pick the simplest option that covers your enquiries.

## FAQ

### What is the best first channel for a small business in South Africa?

No single channel suits every business. Meta's apps have the largest audience in the figures above, and Click-to-WhatsApp ads suit businesses that already take enquiries by message. Test one channel with a small budget before adding another.

### How many people can Meta's apps reach?

According to AdWave, Meta's apps reach an average of 3.56 billion daily active people. This is a platform-wide figure, so it does not show how many of those people live in South Africa or need your service.

### Does page speed affect ad results?

The research cited by WasteNot says bounce chance rises 32% when load time goes from 1 to 3 seconds. Google guidance cited by AdWave links each extra second to up to a 20% drop in retail conversions. Test your own page on a phone to see where it stands.

### How much do agencies charge to run ads?

Sascom247 says management fees typically run from 15% to 20% of managed ad spend. On a R3,000 monthly budget, that is R450 to R600, though the exact fee depends on the agency's contract.
