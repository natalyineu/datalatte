## How Much Should a Lexington Hair Salon Spend on Meta Ads?

Bookeo recommends starting Facebook and Instagram ad spend at USD 750 to USD 1,500 monthly, a band aimed at salons that want a real test rather than a few boosted posts ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). Adwave's general guidance puts a standalone Meta buy at $300 to $1,000 per month ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-choose-advertising-channels)). Its restaurant comparison lists Meta at $300 to $1,500+ monthly, so the two figures bracket a typical starting point.

Timing matters as much as budget. Adwave's restaurant guide lists 1-4 weeks to results for Meta ads ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/restaurant-advertising-complete-marketing-guide)). Judge a campaign after that learning window, not after the first few days of delivery. Budgets too small to produce a steady flow of clicks make the results hard to read, so a month of consistent spend gives you more to act on than a week of heavy spend.

## Which Ad Formats Work Best for Hair Salons?

Short-form video is the format to start with. Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, as cited by Adwave, says short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/electrician-advertising-reaching-homeowners), secondary). That is an engagement figure, not a booking figure. Test a short clip of real chair work against a static photo of the same service and compare bookings.

Pair the creative with a clear action. Restaurants can set Meta campaigns with goals such as traffic or reservations across Facebook, Instagram, or both ([The Fork Manager](https://www.theforkmanager.com/en/blog/introduction-paid-ads-restaurants)). A salon can apply the same logic and choose a traffic or lead goal that points to its booking page.

Organic posting supplies the content. Bookeo says most salon owners find three to four Instagram posts weekly and one to two Facebook posts weekly sustainable ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). For the organic side, see [Instagram marketing for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons).

## Who Should Your Ads Reach Around the Salon?

Start with a 5 to 10 mile radius around the salon for Facebook and Instagram ads ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). Audience size is rarely the constraint, since Meta's apps reach an average of 3.56 billion daily active people ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/facebook-advertising-for-small-business)). Relevance and radius are the levers that matter.

Ask the front desk where existing clients live. That record is more reliable than a demographic guess, and it tells you whether a 5-mile or 10-mile radius fits your book. Build custom audiences from past clients where your setup allows it, then test lookalikes once the list is large enough for Meta to learn from.

Check when followers are online. Bookeo advises checking peak follower activity in the Instagram or Facebook Insights tab, which requires a business account ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). Schedule posts and boosts around those windows. For paid reach specifics, see [Facebook ads for hair salons](/blog/facebook-ads-for-hair-salons).

## Which Campaign Goal and Tracking Setup Should You Use?

Meta Ads Manager is a centralized point for running ads across Instagram and Facebook ([Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-advertising)). Instagram ads run through the same system because Instagram is owned by Meta, and one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook, or both ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/instagram-advertising-for-local-business)).

Set up tracking before the budget goes live. Install the Meta Pixel on the booking confirmation page and confirm the conversion event fires on a real test booking. Check the current pixel setup in Meta's help centre, since platform settings change. Meta's Blueprint certification covering Facebook and Instagram advertising is reported as free ([sascom247](https://sascom247.com/digital-marketing-for-beginners-in-nigeria/), secondary), which helps staff who will run the account.

Search captures people already looking for a salon, so pair Meta with search where it fits. See [Google Ads for hair salons in Lexington](/blog/google-ads-for-hair-salons-in-lexington-ky) for that side of the plan.

## Does Organic Posting Still Matter Alongside Ads?

Yes. Bookeo states that Facebook and Instagram can be used to reach audiences and build trust without spending a dollar on the platform itself ([Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/)). Paid ads widen reach beyond current followers, while organic posts give a new prospect a reason to trust the profile once they click through.

Bookeo also claims that a client who has seen a salon's brand on Facebook 5 to 10 times before searching for a hair salon converts at 2 to 3 times the rate of a cold searcher. The page gives no source for this figure, so treat it as a vendor claim rather than a benchmark.

Follow-up keeps new clients in the book. After a first booking, reminders and rebooking prompts do the work that ads cannot. See [AI-powered email marketing for hair salons](/blog/ai-powered-email-marketing-for-hair-salons) for that step.
