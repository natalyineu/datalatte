Fitness studio marketing in London depends mostly on proximity and consistency. [Vertical Impression's guide](https://www.verticalimpression.com/guides/how-to-market-health-wellness-business-2026) states that proximity is the number one factor in gym selection and that people leave gyms that require more than a 15 to 20 minute commute. [Mariana Tek](https://www.marianatek.com/blog/group-fitness-studio-marketing-strategies/) says many boutique studios budget 5 to 10 percent of monthly revenue for marketing. Start with a complete local profile, short video posted several times a week, and fast follow-up for every trial lead. Paid ads work best once those basics are running.

## How much should a London studio spend on marketing?

Mariana Tek's guidance is a planning range, not a London benchmark. Apply the 5 to 10 percent figure to your own monthly revenue, then review the spend against bookings every month.

The table below uses an assumed £30,000 of monthly studio revenue. The revenue figure and the split between channels are both assumptions for illustration, so replace them with your own numbers.

| Line item | Share of budget (assumption) | Monthly amount at £30,000 revenue (assumption) |
|---|---|---|
| Paid search (Google Ads) | 40% | £600 to £1,200 |
| Paid social and retargeting | 30% | £450 to £900 |
| Short-form video production | 20% | £300 to £600 |
| Follow-up email and welcome sequences | 10% | £150 to £300 |
| Total | 100% | £1,500 to £3,000 |

The total equals 5 to 10 percent of the assumed revenue. Hold the split long enough to read results, then move money toward the channel your bookings data supports. Pull cost-per-click from your own account by area rather than relying on a national average, since central and outer postcodes can differ.

## Is a Google Business Profile enough for a London studio?

A profile alone will not separate you in a dense postcode, but it sets the baseline for every other channel. It is usually the first page a nearby searcher reads, so it needs accurate hours, class types, and a current description. Check Google's own Business Profile help pages for the fields and post options available today.

[Brandrocket](https://www.brandrocket.net/resources/gym-google-ads-settings-location) suggests starting radii of 3 to 5 miles for a budget gym and 5 to 8 miles for a boutique studio, adjusted for local driving patterns. Those figures assume driving, and London members often walk or use the Tube. Set your targeting by travel time first, using the 15 to 20 minute threshold, and use mileage only as a cross-check. As a conversion, 5 to 8 miles is roughly 8 to 13 km.

- Name your area and nearest Tube or rail station in the business description.
- Reply to every review, including complaints about changing rooms or noise.
- Set ad geography by travel time, then confirm it on a map.

## What should a studio post on Instagram each week?

Mariana Tek advises posting at a steady cadence of three to five times a week rather than in bursts. Its guidance for highlight clips is 15 to 30 seconds, which is long enough to show a class without an elaborate edit. Keep the instructor in frame so a new visitor knows who teaches the session. For a broader channel plan, see [Fitness Studio Instagram Marketing Strategy](/blog/fitness-studio-instagram-marketing-strategy).

[Appointy's guide](https://blog.appointy.com/2020/12/11/social-media-marketing-for-fitness-studio-gym/), dated 2020, notes that Instagram and Facebook let businesses add a Book Now button to their profile. That removes a step between interest and a trial booking. Check Meta's current business help pages, since button options change. The same guide recommends creating at least 5 to 10 posts in each content category in advance for busy days, which makes a three-a-week schedule realistic for a small team.

## How fast should a studio follow up with trial and pop-up leads?

Mariana Tek advises following up within 24 hours with an automated welcome sequence, because interest fades quickly after a pop-up ends. Build the sequence before the event so it fires the same day, and include a direct trial booking link in the first message.

Email is a practical channel for that sequence. Loyalsnap cites an external Oberlo statistic that 4.4 billion people use email daily, which is a general indicator rather than a studio benchmark. For setup ideas, read [Fitness Studio Email Marketing Campaigns](/blog/fitness-studio-email-marketing-campaigns) and [Email Marketing for Fitness Studios](/blog/email-marketing-fitness-studio).

Gleantap reports that Studio Svelte, a New York City boutique gym, saw a 40 percent rise in memberships within three months of adding fitness CRM software, with a 50 percent open rate on personalised campaigns. That is one vendor-reported case in another city, so measure your own open rates before copying the approach.

## How should a studio time a pre-sale and re-engagement offer?

Health Club Consultants' guide, published in January 2018, gives a three-month pre-sale example and advises putting the best offer at the start of the pre-sale. It also advises changing pre-sale offers on a bi-weekly or monthly basis, so the studio can tell customers the price is going up. Because the article is from 2018, check current UK rules on pricing claims and pre-sale terms before reusing its wording.

For re-engagement, LoyalSnap recommends a six-week challenge, since it gives participants time to see results that encourage them to pay for a longer programme. Match the challenge to the audience. Beginners and experienced athletes need different entry points, as covered in [Fitness Studio Marketing to Different Personas](/blog/fitness-studio-marketing-to-different-personas-beginners-vs-athletes).

## FAQ

### How much should a small London studio budget per month?

Use 5 to 10 percent of monthly revenue as the planning range from Mariana Tek's guidance. At an assumed £30,000 monthly revenue, that is £1,500 to £3,000, split across search, social, video, and follow-up email.

### Does commute time matter more than distance in London?

Yes. Vertical Impression notes that proximity is the top factor in gym selection and that members leave when a commute exceeds 15 to 20 minutes. Set targeting by travel time, since Tube and walking routes do not match a straight-line radius.

### How long should a studio's challenge run?

LoyalSnap recommends a six-week challenge so participants see results that encourage them to commit to a long-term programme.

### Is a single vendor case study a reliable benchmark?

No. Gleantap's New York City result is a vendor-reported example from one studio. Use it as a prompt to test, then measure your own memberships and open rates over the same period.
