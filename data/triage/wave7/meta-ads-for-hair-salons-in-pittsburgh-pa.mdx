## How Much Should a Pittsburgh Salon Spend on Meta Ads?

Most salon guidance starts lower than agencies expect. Bookeo recommends starting Facebook and Instagram ad spend at $750 to $1,500 per month, which gives the platform enough volume to learn who responds to your offer. AdWave lists a standalone Meta buy as often costing $300 to $1,000 per month, so a budget below that range is workable for a single location but gives the system less data to optimise against.

Cost per thousand impressions for Meta sits in a $5 to $15 band, and cost per click in a $1 to $3 band, according to AdWave's small business guide. Those are national figures. A higher CPM in a dense urban market is possible, so treat the band as a planning range rather than a promise.

Set the budget for at least eight to ten weeks before judging results, because AdWave notes that Meta campaigns take weeks to optimise.

## Which Ad Formats Work Best for Hair Salons?

Short video is the format to start with. AdWave, citing Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, reports that short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram. For a salon, that means a 15 to 30 second clip of a real colour appointment or a finished cut, filmed in your own chair.

Instagram ads run through the same system as Facebook ads, Meta Ads Manager, because Instagram is owned by Meta. One campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook, or both, so you do not need separate setups. Bookeo also notes that Facebook and Instagram can reach audiences and build trust without spending a dollar on the platform itself, which is why organic posts still matter alongside paid ads. For a posting rhythm, see the [Instagram marketing guide for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons).

## How Should You Target a 5 to 10 Mile Radius?

Bookeo advises setting a 5 to 10 mile radius around your salon for both Facebook and Instagram ads. Start at the wider end, then narrow the radius once you see which postcodes or neighbourhoods produce bookings. Pulling the radius in too early can starve a new campaign of impressions.

Use the Insights tab to check when your followers are online. Bookeo points to Instagram or Facebook Insights for peak activity, but that tab only appears on a business account, so confirm your profile is set up as one before you plan posting times.

For paid search alongside Meta, the [Google Ads guide for Pittsburgh hair salons](/blog/google-ads-for-hair-salons-in-pittsburgh-pa) covers how the two channels split the job of capturing intent and creating demand.

## What Should You Set Up Before Launching?

Work through these steps in order before spending the first dollar:

1. Confirm your Facebook and Instagram profiles are business accounts so Insights data is available.
2. Open Meta Ads Manager, which Adobe Express describes as the centralised place to run ads across Instagram and Facebook.
3. Install the Meta Pixel on your booking confirmation page and contact form, so bookings are recorded as conversions.
4. Set a radius of 5 to 10 miles around the salon for the first campaign.
5. Start with a monthly budget between $750 and $1,500, or the $300 to $1,000 standalone range if that is what you can afford.
6. Schedule a creative refresh every few weeks so the same video does not fatigue.

Meta's Blueprint courses on Facebook and Instagram advertising are listed as free with certification, if your team wants structured training before running campaigns.

## FAQ

### How much does a Meta ad campaign cost for a Pittsburgh hair salon?

Bookeo suggests starting at $750 to $1,500 per month for Facebook and Instagram ads. AdWave lists a standalone Meta buy as often costing $300 to $1,000 per month. Start within that range and adjust once you have two to three weeks of data.

### What radius should I target around my salon?

Bookeo advises a 5 to 10 mile radius around your salon for Facebook and Instagram ads. Begin at the wider end and narrow the radius once you know which areas book.

### Is video better than photos for salon ads?

AdWave, citing Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, says short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram. Real footage of your own work is the practical choice.

### Do I need a separate account to run Instagram ads?

No. Instagram ads run through Meta Ads Manager, the same system as Facebook ads, and one campaign can run on either platform or both.
