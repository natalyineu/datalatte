Programmatic advertising means software buys and sells ad space, usually in an auction that runs as each web page loads. The advertiser sets the audience, budget and goal, and the platform bids on each ad view. StackAdapt, an ad platform vendor, says the selection and display process happens within a fraction of a second, faster than the page can fully load ([StackAdapt](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/what-is-programmatic-advertising), secondary). For a small business, the practical question is whether this buying style fits a modest budget and a clear goal, such as repeat visits or reaching people near a location.

## How Does Programmatic Advertising Work?

When someone opens a website or app, the page has empty ad slots. Software bids for each slot based on the targeting the advertiser set, and the winning ad loads. Adwave notes that these auction dynamics have operated in display and video on websites for over a decade ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/tv-media-buying), secondary).

Programmatic platforms give buyers access to a large pool of sites. StackAdapt says its platform provides access to hundreds of thousands of sites, including premium publishers ([StackAdapt](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/what-is-display-advertising), secondary). You do not bid on each slot yourself. You set the audience, creative, budget and goal, and the platform places bids within those limits. A walkthrough of the buying steps appears in our [display advertising vs programmatic guide](/blog/display-advertising-vs-programmatic-local-business).

## Is Google Display the Same as Programmatic?

Google Display and programmatic overlap, but they are not identical. Awarity compares the two. It says Google Display targeting mainly uses interests and keyword-based tactics, while programmatic can include behavioral and contextual targeting. It also says Google Display is limited to Google's ecosystem, while programmatic can access broader inventory across multiple ad exchanges ([Awarity](https://www.awarity.com/blog/google-display-network-vs-programmatic-display-ads), secondary).

| Factor | Google Display Network | Programmatic advertising |
|---|---|---|
| Targeting | Interests and keywords | Behavioral and contextual, among others |
| Inventory | Google's ecosystem | Multiple ad exchanges |

Search ads sit outside this comparison. StackAdapt defines digital advertising as covering display, social media and search engine marketing ([StackAdapt](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/what-is-programmatic-advertising), secondary). Programmatic is one part of online advertising, not all of it.

## How Big Is Programmatic Display Buying?

Spend has grown quickly. EMARKETER, cited by StackAdapt, puts US programmatic digital display ad spend at $156.82 billion in 2024, up from $4.99 billion in 2013 ([StackAdapt](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/what-is-programmatic-advertising), secondary). Adwave cites a 2025 industry report saying over 90% of digital display ads are now bought programmatically, and projects over 95% by 2027 ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/what-is-programmatic-advertising), secondary).

These figures are forecasts and industry totals, not benchmarks for a local campaign. Use them to see the direction of the market.

## What Does It Cost a Local Business?

Adwave lists programmatic CPM ranges, meaning cost per thousand ad views, by format. Standard display is $3-8, native display is $5-12, and high-impact formats are $8-15 ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/programmatic-political-advertising), secondary). A vendor blog says programmatic display makes sense when spend is $10,000 or more monthly ([ClicksGeek](https://clicksgeek.com/best-advertising-platforms/), secondary), which is well above most local test budgets. Check each platform's live minimums before committing.

The example below uses assumptions, not sourced prices. Assume a bakery spends $150 on a 30-day remarketing test aimed at 3,000 past website visitors.

| Scenario (assumption: $150 budget) | CPM used | Ad views bought |
|---|---|---|
| Low end of standard display | $8 | 18,750 |
| High end of standard display | $3 | 50,000 |

Assume the 18,750 views are spread evenly, which is about 6 views per visitor. Assume 5 orders at $35 come from the campaign. That is $175 in revenue against $150 in ad spend, before ingredients and labor. The bakery would only scale after it knew its margin and whether the orders repeat.

## Is Programmatic Worth It for a Small Business?

It fits best when you want to remind past visitors about your business, or reach a defined area with images or video. It is a weaker fit for people with clear search intent, such as "emergency plumber near me", where search ads usually come first.

## How Should You Measure a Programmatic Test?

The IAB, as cited by StackAdapt, says at least 50% of a display ad must be in view for at least one second to count as viewed. For video, the threshold is two seconds ([StackAdapt](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/programmatic-advertising-glossary), secondary). A served impression is not the same as a viewed one, so read impression counts with that in mind.

Judge the test on outcomes. Track calls or orders, then compare cost per action with your search campaigns. Our [ROI tracking guide](/blog/how-to-track-marketing-roi-local-business-guide) explains how to set that up.

## What Should You Check Before Starting?

1. Set up conversion tracking for calls or orders before spending the first dollar.
2. Ask each vendor for written fees, minimums and the CPM range for your ad formats.
3. Run a 30 to 60 day test with a fixed budget you can afford to lose.
4. Review placement reports weekly and exclude sites that do not fit your brand.
5. Compare cost per order or call with your search ads before expanding.

## FAQ

### Is programmatic advertising the same as Google Ads?

No. Google Ads includes Search and Display. Google Display overlaps with programmatic, but Awarity notes programmatic can reach broader inventory across multiple exchanges ([Awarity](https://www.awarity.com/blog/google-display-network-vs-programmatic-display-ads), secondary). Search ads are not usually described as programmatic.

### Do I need a big budget to start?

The sources here do not give a typical local spend. A vendor blog puts the threshold at $10,000 or more monthly ([ClicksGeek](https://clicksgeek.com/best-advertising-platforms/), secondary), but that is one opinion. Check each platform's live minimums.

### What counts as a viewed display ad?

According to the IAB as cited by StackAdapt, at least 50% of a display ad must be in view for one second, and two seconds for video ([StackAdapt](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/programmatic-advertising-glossary), secondary).

### Can a small business run programmatic without an agency?

Self-serve platforms exist. You need conversion tracking and time to review reports each week. Check each platform's live help pages for current setup steps.
