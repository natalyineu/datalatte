For a local business, Instagram Reels work best as short vertical videos that show one tip, one process or one product at a time. [Bookeo](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/) recommends 30- to 45-second clips covering one tip per video, and [Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-advertising) says videos under one minute are generally more impactful. Shoot in 9:16 format, post on a rhythm you can keep, and judge each Reel by whether it brings bookings, calls or visits, not by views alone.

## How Long Should Each Reel Be?

Bookeo's guidance is specific: short clips of 30 to 45 seconds, with one tip per video. A salon can film one technique per clip, and a coffee shop can film one brewing step. A longer lesson becomes a series, and each clip is easy to finish. Finishing a clip matters because it tells you whether the opening held the viewer.

Adobe Express says videos under one minute are generally more impactful, which agrees with the 30 to 45 second window. Treat both as starting points rather than rules. Test a 20-second cut against a 40-second cut on the same topic and keep the version your audience watches to the end. For planning several clips in one session, the [local business social media content creation guide](/blog/local-business-social-media-content-creation) sets out a batching routine.

## What Shape and Size Should the Video Be?

The [Bonsandfrazer guide to Facebook ads](https://bonsandfrazer.com/insights/facebook-ads-on-a-budget/) lists 1080×1920 at 9:16 for Stories and Reels, with text shown only in-video. [ADmanage](https://admanage.ai/blog/facebook-ads-for-construction-companies) recommends the 9:16 format for Reels, Stories and vertical project walkthroughs. Take 10 Media recommends a short vertical video at 9:16 for self-serve local digital screens, which confirms the same shape works across placements.

Because the Bonsandfrazer table lists text as in-video only, put the offer, price or booking step inside the frame itself. A caption adds context but cannot replace words on screen. Keep faces and key objects away from the bottom edge, where captions and buttons often sit.

## Where Do Reels Reach People Now?

Bonsandfrazer's 2026 guidance states that the standalone Instagram Explore feed was retired in January 2026, with delivery moving into Reels. For a small business, that makes Reels the main discovery format to plan around. The page does not say how much reach changes, so measure your own results before and after.

[Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-advertising) cites DataReportal as saying at least 1.7 billion people use Instagram (secondary). The same page reports users as 48% female and 52% male, with the largest age group at 25 to 34, followed by 18 to 24. Check those patterns against your own customers before choosing topics. A dog groomer and a bakery will find different audiences in the same feed.

## What Should a Local Business Film?

[Trafft](https://trafft.com/salon-ads/) names Instagram and Facebook as places to showcase salon work and run targeted ads. The most useful Reels show the work itself. [Infegy](https://www.infegy.com/blog/three-ways-to-find-beauty-insights) reports that GRWM (get ready with me) videos spiked across TikTok and Instagram in early 2023, then engagement and post volume declined as content moved toward lifestyle storytelling. Copying a format at its peak is a weaker plan than showing a real process from your own shop.

Good candidates are a before and after, a product being made, a single answer to a common customer question, or a short walk through one job from start to finish. Each one shows a result a customer can picture and then book.

## How Often Should You Post?

Hootsuite's research, as relayed on a salon marketing blog, recommends posting to Instagram 2 to 7 times per week (secondary). [Bellwether Coffee](https://bellwethercoffee.com/blog/social-media-marketing-for-coffee-shops) suggests a coffee shop post three to five feed posts a week plus daily Stories, and lists Instagram at about 30 minutes a day. Those are guidance figures, not platform rules.

Start at two Reels a week and hold that rhythm for twelve weeks. Thirty minutes a day is about 3.5 hours a week, so budget that time on the calendar. Scheduling tools can cut the hands-on time, and the [AI agent for social media posting guide](/blog/ai-agent-for-social-media-posting-local-business) compares the options.

## What Does a Reel Actually Cost?

The example below uses assumptions to show how to decide whether to keep going. Replace each assumption with your own numbers.

| Input or result | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Reels per month (assumption) | 8 |
| Staff hours per Reel (assumption) | 1.5 |
| Hourly staff cost (assumption) | $20 |
| Monthly cost (8 × 1.5 × $20) | $240 |
| New customers from Reels per month (assumption) | 6 |
| Margin per new customer (assumption) | $45 |
| Margin from new customers (6 × $45) | $270 |
| Net result | +$30 |

Break-even is 6 new customers a month, since $240 ÷ $45 is 5.3 and you cannot count a fraction. If only 3 customers come in, the month loses $105. Record how each new customer found you, because view counts cannot show whether Reels bring bookings. ## Should You Boost Your Best Reels?

Boost a Reel only after it has proven itself organically. ACT Global Marketing's comparison table lists an average cost per lead of $8 to $18 for Video Reels ads, though that is a vendor figure rather than a local benchmark. Adobe Express, citing AdRoll's 2021 figures, puts Instagram ads at $0.20 to $2 per click (secondary, and dated). Costs vary by market, so test a small budget first. The [Instagram Reels ads guide for local businesses](/blog/instagram-reels-ads-local-business-guide) covers setup.

## FAQ

### How long should a Reel be?

Bookeo recommends 30 to 45 seconds with one tip per video. Adobe Express says videos under one minute are generally more impactful. Test both lengths before settling on one.

### What video shape should I use?

Use 9:16 vertical. The Bonsandfrazer guide lists 1080×1920 for Stories and Reels, with text shown only in-video.

### Do Reels still appear in the Explore feed?

Bonsandfrazer states that the standalone Instagram Explore feed was retired in January 2026, with delivery moving into Reels. Check the platform's help center for any later changes.

### How often should a small business post Reels?

Hootsuite's research, relayed on a salon blog, suggests 2 to 7 Instagram posts a week (secondary). Start at two Reels a week, track the results for twelve weeks, then adjust.

### Should I pay to promote Reels?

Only after an organic Reel has shown it brings people in. ACT's vendor table lists $8 to $18 per lead for Video Reels ads, so compare that against your own margin per customer before spending.
