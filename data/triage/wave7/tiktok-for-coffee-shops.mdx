TikTok is worth the time for a coffee shop when most regulars are under 35. Bellwether Coffee estimates TikTok needs about 1 hour a day, compared with 5 minutes for Google Business, so the real cost is staff time. Start with organic videos for a trial month, measure visits with a code or a counter question, and pay for ads only after a video has shown what customers respond to.

These are Bellwether Coffee's time estimates for a coffee shop, not measured results.

## Is TikTok Worth It for a Coffee Shop Under 35?

Bellwether Coffee's guidance is direct: "If you're targeting customers under 35, yes." The same guide lists TikTok as reaching mainly people aged 18 to 34 and describes its strength as growth and a younger audience ([Bellwether Coffee](https://bellwethercoffee.com/blog/social-media-marketing-for-coffee-shops)). If most of your regulars are older, the guide points to other channels. It describes Facebook as skewing 30 and over and Instagram as reaching all ages.

For shops in Canada, Square cites Nordicity: 83% of Canadian small and medium businesses use TikTok for brand awareness and marketing ([Square](https://squareup.com/ca/en/the-bottom-line/reaching-customers/marketing-plan-for-a-coffee-shop), secondary). That figure shows how widely businesses use the app. It does not measure coffee shop sales.

## How Much Time Does Each Platform Take?

Bellwether lists the daily time for a coffee shop as 1 hour on TikTok, 30 minutes on Instagram, 15 minutes on Facebook and 5 minutes on Google Business. Its total for an active presence on one or two platforms is 5 to 7 hours per week ([Bellwether Coffee](https://bellwethercoffee.com/blog/social-media-marketing-for-coffee-shops)).

Posting frequency changes that total. The Restaurant HQ article says coffee shops should post at least once per week, ideally one to two times per day ([The Restaurant HQ](https://www.therestauranthq.com/operations/coffee-shop-marketing/), secondary). Those two targets need very different staffing. Pick the cadence you can keep for three months before judging results. Scheduling tools can cut the posting part of that time, as covered in [marketing automation for coffee shops](/blog/marketing-automation-for-local-coffee-shops).

## What Do the Results Data Show?

Adwave reports that 33% of small businesses used TikTok as of October 2025, up from 17% in September 2023, citing the SBE Council ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/tik-tok-for-small-business-is-it-worth-your-time-in-2026), secondary). The same page cites a 2024 Oxford Economics report: 88% of small businesses reported increased sales after TikTok activity. Both figures cover all small businesses, not coffee shops.

Shopify reports a median TikTok engagement rate of 1.73% in 2025, and that 51% of small businesses investing in TikTok advertising reported a positive return ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/tik-tok-for-small-business-is-it-worth-your-time-in-2026), secondary, citing Shopify). Engagement is not the same as a visit, so track footfall separately. Marketing Agent says watch time and completion rate carry 40% to 50% of TikTok's ranking weight, and that the first 3 seconds decide 71% of retention ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/tik-tok-for-small-business-is-it-worth-your-time-in-2026), secondary).

## Should a Coffee Shop Pay for TikTok Ads?

Restaurant HQ notes that some options, such as posting on social media, only cost time, while others can run upwards of $1,500 or more per month ([The Restaurant HQ](https://www.therestauranthq.com/operations/coffee-shop-marketing/), secondary). Paid ads sit in the second group, so test them only after an organic video has shown a clear response.

Adwave's guide suggests planning a loyalty platform at $50 to $300 per month plus $200 to $1,000 in incentive payouts, and recommends a 1 to 3 mile radius around the shop for Reels promotions ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/best-advertising-coffee-shops-cafes), secondary). It also says a cafe Reel paired with a "first drink free" offer routinely drives 50 to 200 first-time visitors. That is a vendor claim, so treat it as a target to test, not a guarantee.

## The Math: How Many Visits Cover the Time?

Only the 1-hour daily figure comes from a source. Every other input is an **assumption** for an illustrative café.

| Item | Daily TikTok habit (assumption) | 3 videos a week (assumption) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time per month | 30 hours (1 hour x 30 days) | 9.75 hours (3 x 45 min x 4.33 weeks) |
| Time cost at $20 per hour | $600 | $195 |
| Margin per $6 ticket at 65% | $3.90 | $3.90 |
| Extra visits needed to cover time | 154 | 50 |

The daily habit needs about 154 extra visits a month, or roughly 5 a day. Three videos a week need 50, or under 2 a day. Count visits with a TikTok-only code or a question at the counter. If you cannot reach the target within three months, drop the channel or cut the posting frequency.

## Where Does Google Business Fit?

Google Business takes about 5 minutes a day on Bellwether's estimate, and it is the cheapest local channel on the list. Per Diem recommends answering common questions in the profile's Q&A section before customers ask, such as whether you offer free Wi-Fi, dairy-free options or dogs on the patio ([Per Diem](https://www.tryperdiem.com/blog/google-business-profile-coffee-shop-guide), secondary).

BrewRound's 2020 guide says a listing is free, needs a Google account, and is verified by a letter containing a code. The profile does not appear in results until it is verified. The page is dated 2020, so check the live Google help pages for current steps. Answering the same questions every week is a good candidate for automation, as described in [AI-driven customer service for coffee shops](/blog/ai-driven-customer-service-for-local-coffee-shops).

## FAQ

### Is TikTok worth it if my customers are over 35?
Probably not as a first channel. Bellwether places TikTok mainly with 18 to 34 year olds, while it describes Facebook as skewing 30 and over and Instagram as reaching all ages.

### How much time should a coffee shop spend on social media each week?
Bellwether estimates 5 to 7 hours per week for an active presence on one or two platforms. Restaurant HQ says posting at least once a week is the minimum.

### Do TikTok ads pay off?
Shopify reports that 51% of small businesses investing in TikTok ads reported a positive return. That is a third-party figure across all small businesses, so test with a code before deciding for your shop.

### Should I answer common questions on my Google profile?
Yes. Per Diem recommends answering questions about free Wi-Fi, dairy-free options and dogs on the patio before customers ask.
