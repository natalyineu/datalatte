Local SEO in France comes down to one complete Google Business Profile, genuine reviews, a public reply to every review, and no incentives for feedback. Google's rules on reviews are strict: offering discounts or freebies in exchange for reviews is against its policies and can get a profile penalised. This guide covers those steps for independent shops, salons, cafés and trades in France. It keeps to figures that can be traced to a cited page, and the worked example is labelled as an assumption.

## What does a complete French Google Business Profile need?

Google Business Profile is a free tool. It shows business hours, phone numbers, addresses, reviews and photos directly in search results, according to [The Association of Fitness Studios](https://member.afsfitness.com/content/how-make-your-fitness-business-stand-out-google) (secondary). Every field should match what customers will find on your website and signage.

The business description allows 750 characters, and the primary category carries the most weight in Google's ranking algorithm, per [Adwave's review guide](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business) (secondary). Pick the category that matches your main paid service, then name the services and towns you serve in the description.

Do not list a country or state as a service area. Adwave reports that 2025 coverage says Google explicitly banned countries and states as service areas on Business Profiles ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/service-area-business-google-profile), secondary).

Google's AI answering, called Ask Maps, draws on profile data, website content and reviews, according to an Accrisoft summary cited on [Adwave's Q&A guide](https://adwave.com/resources/google-business-profile-q-and-a) (secondary). Keep the website and profile consistent. For a fuller walkthrough, see [AI for Google Business Profile optimisation](/blog/ai-for-google-business-profile-optimization).

## How should a French business ask for and answer reviews?

Google allows businesses to ask customers for genuine reviews and provides a direct review link to share, according to [dmly's automation guide](https://dmly.io/google-business-profile-automation-guide/) (secondary). Requests can go out by hand, or automatically after an appointment or payment, as a tracked link by WhatsApp, SMS or email.

The rules on incentives are strict. Adwave states that offering discounts or freebies for reviews is against Google's rules and can get a profile penalised ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-handle-fake-or-spam-google-reviews), secondary). [Keepers Digital](https://keepersdigital.com/en-ca/resources/local-search/yelp-vs-google-business-profile/) (secondary) adds that Google has explicit policies against soliciting reviews in exchange for compensation or in bulk.

Reviews matter. Mailchimp describes reviews on a Google Business Profile as especially influential in local search ranking and visibility ([Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/resources/local-marketing/), secondary). Reply to every review. Responses are public and appear in search results, so use them to show good service ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/responding-to-negative-reviews-templates-and-best-practices), secondary).

## What do you do about a fake or unfair review?

In your Google Business Profile, click the three-dot menu beside the review and select "Report review" ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-handle-fake-or-spam-google-reviews), secondary). Google's help page says you can report any review, but only those that violate Google policies are eligible for removal ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/4596773?hl=en-GB)). Do not report a review just because you disagree with it or dislike it.

Evaluation typically takes several days. Merchants in the European Economic Area, which includes France, can report third-party reviews directly on Google, per the same help page. A single one-time appeal in the Reviews Management Tool can cover up to 10 reviews.

Automated spam detection can occasionally remove legitimate reviews by mistake. Adwave notes that removals cannot be reversed unless shown to be an error ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-remove-negative-google-reviews), secondary). Do not post personal or business details in the public Google Business Profile Community forum, which warns against sharing private information there ([Google Community thread](https://support.google.com/business/thread/233288386/how-do-i-actually-get-in-contact-with-someone-about-fake-reviews?hl=en), community forum).

## What does a practical 30-day checklist look like?

1. Confirm the profile and make sure you hold the owner account. A Manager role can reply to reviews and create posts but cannot add users or delete the profile ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/managing-google-business-profiles-for-multiple-locations), secondary).
2. Set the primary category first, then write the description, using up to 750 characters.
3. Remove any country or state from the service area.
4. Send a review request by tracked link to recent customers. Do not offer any reward for a review.
5. Reply publicly to each new review.
6. Publish one post, then check it shows Live. Only Live posts appear on Search and Maps ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en)).
7. After 30 days, read the Performance tab and compare calls and direction requests with the month before.

## What does the time cost look like? (worked example)

All inputs are **assumptions** for a one-location hairdresser in a French town. They are not measured results.

- Assumption: 5 hours in month one to claim the profile, set the category, hours and service list, and write the description.
- Assumption: 20 appointments per week, with 1 in 4 customers agreeing to a review request, so 5 requests per week.
- Assumption: 2 minutes per request to send the link.
- Assumption: 5 minutes per public reply, so 5 replies per week.

Weekly time is 10 minutes for requests plus 25 minutes for replies, which is 35 minutes. Over about 4.33 weeks that is roughly 2.5 hours a month. Month one totals about 7.5 hours.

Adwave's salon guide advises that if the budget is close to $0, start with the Google Business Profile, reviews, service descriptions and portfolio ([Rozhair](https://rozhair.com/blogs/education/hair-salon-advertising-ideas), secondary). A paid step can wait until the free listing has data. ## What do these sources not cover?

The sources used here cover Google's own rules, review handling and profile features. They do not provide verified French market figures, such as search share or directory usage, so none are given in this article.

Cookie consent and French consumer law also need a separate check. Before you change tracking or review practices, read the current official text from the relevant regulator and check Google's live help pages for any change to the rules above.

## FAQ

### Can I offer a discount for a Google review?

No. Adwave states that discounts or freebies offered for reviews are against Google's rules and can get a profile penalised ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-handle-fake-or-spam-google-reviews), secondary).

### Can I remove a negative review I disagree with?

Only if it violates Google policies. Google advises merchants not to report a review just because they disagree with it or dislike it ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/4596773?hl=en-GB)).

### Is there a length limit for the business description?

Yes. Adwave states the business description allows 750 characters ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-get-more-google-reviews-for-your-local-business), secondary).

### Can a staff member help manage the profile?

Yes, with the Manager role. It can handle replies and posts but cannot add users or delete the profile ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/managing-google-business-profiles-for-multiple-locations), secondary).
