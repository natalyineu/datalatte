A Google Ads campaign for a gym usually costs more per new member than the price of a click suggests. Take an assumed $4 click: if 5 in 100 clicks become an enquiry and 1 in 5 enquiries joins, one new member costs $400 in ads. Vertical Impression, a secondary marketing guide, sets a target of $150 to $200 per converting member for fitness Google Ads and says a channel above $250 per member needs restructuring. The sections below set the numbers, build a search campaign, track members and work out payback.

The worked figures here are assumptions to replace with your own. The sources use US dollars; gyms in the UK, Australia and Canada can run the same formula with local click prices.

## What Does One New Member Cost From Google Ads?

The cost per member is the price of a click divided by the share of clicks that become members. Written out: cost per member = cost per click ÷ (click-to-enquiry rate × enquiry-to-member rate). Each step is a separate dial. A lower click price helps, but so does a better landing page, a faster reply to enquiries or a tighter offer.

Vertical Impression's guide sets a target of under $150 to $200 per converting member for fitness Google Ads. It also says that a channel with a cost per acquired member above $250 needs restructuring unless it is a new channel still building. Treat these as a yardstick from one secondary source, not a rule your local market must meet.

A cost above the yardstick does not mean the campaign has failed. It means one of the three dials needs work before you add budget.

## Which Benchmarks Are Worth Testing Against?

Vertical Impression also projects that a well-optimized $1,000 to $2,000 a month Google Ads campaign can generate 300 to 600 clicks, with 3% to 6% converting to trial sign-ups. That is a projection in a secondary guide, and the conversion is to trial sign-ups, not paid members. A trial sits earlier in the funnel than a member, so the figures cannot be read straight across to a member cost.

Reliable public click-price figures for gyms are scarce and vary by town and season. The practical approach is to run one month, count real clicks, enquiries and members, and compare them with the formula above. Check the platform's live help pages for current bidding guidance before you set a bid.

## Should a Gym Start With Search, Display or Video?

Search is the place to start for a single gym. Selworthy, a Google Ads vendor, states that search ads capture immediate interest from users actively searching for fitness services, while display and video ads build awareness and engagement. For a gym, that means a search ad meets someone at the moment they type a need, such as a class type or a town name.

Display and video have a role, but it is usually reminding people nearby who already know the brand. Once search leads are tracked and the cost per member sits inside your target, adding those formats becomes a separate test with its own budget and measurement. ## How Should Keywords and Match Types Be Set Up?

Selworthy notes that broad keywords like "fitness" can help attract general interest, while phrase match keywords such as "affordable gyms in [location]" target a more specific audience. On a small budget, the specific end is easier to control. Every irrelevant click is paid for, so tight keywords protect the money.

Group keywords by what the searcher wants, with one theme per ad group, so each ad can echo the search words:

| Ad group | Example keywords | Match type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generic gym | "gym near me", [gym in your town], "gyms open now" | Phrase + exact |
| Class type | "pilates classes [town]", [boxing gym near me], "spin class near me" | Phrase + exact |
| Offer-led | "gym free trial", "no contract gym" | Phrase |
| Specialty | "women only gym", "kids gymnastics classes" | Phrase + exact |
| Your brand | [your gym name] | Exact |

Keep each ad group to a handful of closely related keywords. An ad that mirrors the search is more relevant than a generic ad that covers every type of gym.

## Which Search Terms Should You Block?

Negative keywords stop spend on searches that will never become members. Add them on day one and review the search terms report every week. A starting list: jobs, careers, salary, equipment, home gym, DIY, workout plan, app, YouTube, franchise and second hand. Remove "free" from the list only if you offer a free trial. If you do, block plain "free gym" with exact-match negatives instead.

Selworthy recommends regularly reviewing search term reports to see which keywords perform well and which do not. The words real people type are the language your ads and landing page should use, so the report doubles as free market research.

## How Do You Track Enquiries and Members?

Without conversion tracking, you are paying for clicks alone. Set up at least three events: form or trial sign-ups, calls from the ad, and calls from the landing page. Set a minimum call length so misdials do not count. The right duration depends on how long a real enquiry takes at your front desk, so choose it from your own call logs.

Ad platforms count leads, but only the front desk knows which enquiries became paying members. Ask every new member how they found you, log the answer against the enquiry, and compare the totals monthly. ## What Does the Worked Budget Show?

The table uses a $1,000 monthly budget, the bottom of the range Vertical Impression projects. Every input except the budget is an assumption, labelled in the row names. The base case uses the same inputs as the opening example.

| | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly budget (assumption) | $1,000 | $1,000 | $1,000 |
| Cost per click (assumption) | $6.00 | $4.00 | $3.00 |
| Clicks | 167 | 250 | 333 |
| Click to enquiry rate (assumption) | 3% | 5% | 7% |
| Enquiries | 5 | 12.5 | 23 |
| Cost per enquiry | $200 | $80 | $43 |
| Enquiry to member rate (assumption) | 15% | 20% | 30% |
| New members | 0.75 | 2.5 | 7 |
| Ad cost per member | $1,333 | $400 | $143 |
| Monthly fee (assumption) | $55 | $55 | $55 |
| Payback months (cost per member ÷ fee) | 24 | 7.3 | 2.6 |

The base case lands at $400 per member, above the $150 to $200 yardstick and above the $250 restructuring line. The strong case reaches $143, inside the yardstick. The gap between the rows comes from the click price and the close rate, which is where the work goes.

Payback uses revenue, not profit, so real payback is slower once staff and rent are counted. To set a ceiling on bids, use this: maximum CPC = target cost per member × click-to-enquiry rate × enquiry-to-member rate. If you will pay $200 per member, at 5% and 20% the ceiling is $200 × 0.05 × 0.20 = $2.00 a click.

## How Long Do Members Stay, and Does Payback Hold?

Payback depends on how long a member stays. Secondmeasure reports that at Planet Fitness, 66% of the January 2017 new-member cohort was still active at 10 months. The author notes that "active" can mean an autopay account is still running. That is one chain and one cohort, so treat it as a rough picture of retention rather than a forecast for your studio.

The Health & Fitness Association's Wellhub-commissioned survey found that the most common member request was more flexible or shorter contracts, at 66%. If your payback model assumes a full-year commitment, test what a shorter, cancellable term does to the number of paying months. Then rerun the payback row with your own average length of stay, pulled from billing.

## Why Do Enquiries Dip After the New Year Rush?

Gyms see a surge in January. Spectrum News reports that a regular member at a Wisconsin YMCA says gym crowds start to taper off within a matter of weeks and months after Jan. 1. A budget set at the start of the year can buy a burst of enquiries from people who lapse before the first months are over.

Plan bids and budgets around that curve. Track enquiries by month, joins by month, and cancellations at three and six months. If the members who joined in January are gone by spring, the January cost per member is the wrong number to scale from.

## How Do Reviews and the Profile Affect Ad Results?

Marianatek, a fitness software vendor, states that Google review volume can start influencing visibility within a few weeks. The same article's table lists a 6 to 12 month typical payoff for local SEO and Google reviews. Reviews move more slowly than a campaign that can be switched on in a day, so they run alongside ads rather than replacing them.

A profile with current photos, accurate hours and recent reviews gives a click something to confirm before it converts. ## What Should You Do This Week?

The Health & Fitness Association's Wellhub survey found that 87% of gym and studio owners spend up to half their working week personally handling marketing and member acquisition. The steps below fit a short weekly slot.

1. Pull your last 50 new members and note the town or postcode of each. Draw the ad radius from that map, and set location targeting to people in or regularly in your area.
2. Write down your monthly fee, your average months of stay and the most you will pay for one new member. Calculate the maximum click price with the formula above.
3. Build one Search campaign for one location, with five tight ad groups in phrase and exact match, and load the negative list before launch.
4. Set up form, ad-call and landing-page-call tracking, then confirm each event fires with a test before the ads go live.
5. Set a 15-minute reply rule for enquiries. Each week, read the search terms report and add negatives, and compare enquiries with joins.

## FAQ

### How much should a gym spend on Google Ads per month?

Vertical Impression suggests $1,000 to $2,000 a month for a well-optimized fitness campaign. Start at a level your budget can sustain for a full month of tracked data, then use the break-even formula to decide whether to scale.

### Is "gym near me" worth bidding on?

It shows strong intent, but it can be expensive. Judge it by cost per member, not cost per click, and run it next to class-specific and offer-led keywords so you can compare results.

### Should a gym use display or video ads first?

Selworthy describes search as capturing immediate interest and display and video as building awareness and engagement. For a single studio, search is the usual starting point, with other formats tested once the search cost per member is known.

### How long before ads bring in members?

Enquiries can arrive within days of launch. A fair read on cost per member needs a month of tracked clicks, enquiries and joins, which is a practical judgement rather than a platform rule.
