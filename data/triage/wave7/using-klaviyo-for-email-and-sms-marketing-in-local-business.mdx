Secondary pricing reviews list the free tier at 250 contacts and 500 email sends a month, the Email plan at about $20 a month for 500 contacts and about $400 a month for 25,000. ## What Does Klaviyo Cost at Each List Size?

The Stack Reviewer's table lists the Email plan at about $20 a month for 500 contacts with 5,000 sends, about $100 a month for 5,000 contacts, about $400 for 25,000, about $720 for 50,000 and about $1,380 for 100,000 ([The Stack Reviewer](https://thestackreviewer.com/posts/klaviyo-pricing-review/), secondary). ## How Does Klaviyo Compare with Mailchimp on Price?

Mailchimp is the most common alternative for small shops. GetPricePulse reports that Mailchimp cut its free plan to 500 contacts in 2022, down from 2,000, and raised paid plan prices by 30 to 45 percent in 2024 ([GetPricePulse](https://www.getpricepulse.com/blog/email-marketing-tools-pricing-guide-2026.html), secondary). The same guide lists Mailchimp's Essentials plan at $13 a month for 1,000 contacts and Standard at $20 a month, with Premium starting at $350 a month or more.

The two price lists do not match on contact tiers, so a direct comparison needs the same list size on both. At 500 contacts, Klaviyo's listed Email plan is about $20 a month, close to Mailchimp's Standard price at 1,000 contacts. Check both live quotes at your real list size, and check whether a text-message add-on changes the total, since one secondary source lists Email and Email+SMS at the same $400 price for 25,000 profiles ([Tutorsbot](https://tutorsbot.com/blog/mailchimp-pricing-2026-cost-per-contact), secondary).

## Is the Free Plan Enough for a Small Local List?

Reviewers report that the free tier caps at 250 contacts and 500 email sends per month ([The Stack Reviewer](https://thestackreviewer.com/posts/klaviyo-pricing-review/), secondary). The send cap usually runs out before the contact cap, so the monthly send count matters more than the list size.

Assume a shop has 250 contacts and sends one email a week to each one. That is about 1,000 sends a month, which is double the 500 included. Assume instead that it sends one email a month to each contact. That is 250 sends, which fits. A small list with a regular newsletter needs a paid plan sooner than the contact cap suggests.

Compare the free plan with the paid tier that covers your real send volume. Do not assume the free plan will last after the first busy season, because a fixed contact count hides how quickly sends grow.

## Which Flows Should a Local Business Start With?

Automated flows tied to a customer action tend to outperform one-off blasts. AdWave, citing Klaviyo guidance, says post-purchase email sequences achieve open rates nearly 17 percent higher than the average email automation, and it recommends a sequence of 3 to 5 emails across 30 to 90 days ([AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/loyalty-email-sequences-keeping-customers-engaged-post-purchase), secondary). For a salon or studio, that maps to a thank-you after a visit, a reminder before a return appointment and a gentle check-in later.

For SMS, AdWave cites Klaviyo benchmarks showing that SMS flows account for just 7.6 percent of total SMS sends but generate 45.2 percent of total SMS revenue ([AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/sms-marketing-vs-email-when-to-use-each-channel), secondary). Behavior-triggered messages also reach click rates nearing 10 percent on average, with top performers above 16 percent. These are benchmarks across Klaviyo accounts, not results for a local shop. For a decision on which channel to lead with, read [SMS vs email marketing for a local business](/blog/sms-vs-email-marketing-local-business-which-is-better).

Build one flow at a time and keep each message to a single purpose. If you need first drafts of wording, [ChatGPT prompts for local business marketing](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-local-business-marketing) can speed up the writing, but a person should check every message before it sends.

## How Should You Measure Email and SMS Results?

Open rates are the easiest number to read and the weakest guide to revenue. AdWave, citing Klaviyo guidance, says Klaviyo recommends tracking business-impact metrics such as repeat purchase rate, customer lifetime value increase and email engagement rather than relying on open rates alone ([AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/loyalty-email-sequences-keeping-customers-engaged-post-purchase), secondary).

For a local business, repeat purchase rate is the most useful of these. Compare the share of customers who return within a set window before and after a flow goes live. Keep the comparison fair by using the same window and the same type of customer. Record any sale you can trace to a message, and count bookings taken outside the platform separately, since platform reporting only covers sales it can see.

## Worked Example: Does a Paid Plan Pay Back?

Every input below is an assumption for a hair salon, except the listed price, which comes from the secondary table.

| Input | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contacts on list | 5,000 | Assumption |
| Listed Email plan price | about $100 a month | The Stack Reviewer (secondary) |
| Average order value | $25 | Assumption |
| Orders per month traced to flows | 12 | Assumption |

At $100 a month, the plan costs $1,200 a year. At $25 an order, the salon needs 4 traced orders a month to cover the fee. The assumed 12 orders bring in $300 in revenue, which leaves $200 before staff time and product costs. If only 3 orders are traced, the plan does not cover its fee.

The result depends on the assumptions. Replace them with your own figures before you decide, and count the hours spent building and maintaining the flows as a cost too.

Reviewers report a free tier capped at 250 contacts and 500 email sends per month ([The Stack Reviewer](https://thestackreviewer.com/posts/klaviyo-pricing-review/), secondary). Check the live plan in your account, since prices and limits change.

### How much does Klaviyo cost for 25,000 contacts?

### Does the Klaviyo price include SMS?
