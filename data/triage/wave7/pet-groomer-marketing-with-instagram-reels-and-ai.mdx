Instagram Reels can pay for a pet groomer, but only when you count the bookings they create. On assumed inputs, 12 Reels a month take about 6.5 hours, roughly $195 at $30 an hour, and one new regular who books every six weeks at $85 a groom covers that cost. Film a repeating set of formats, get owner consent before posting, use AI for drafts rather than final footage, and tag every booking to its source.

This guide is for groomers in the US, UK, Australia and Canada who work from a salon, a van or home. It covers what to film, how long Reels should run, where AI saves real time, and how to count the bookings that make the effort worthwhile.

## Is Instagram Worth the Time for a Pet Groomer?

Reels reach people who do not follow you yet. A 2025 summary of Instagram Reels statistics from Teleprompter, republished by AdWave, says over 2 billion users interact with Reels monthly, and that Reels account for 35% of all time spent on Instagram ([AdWave, Instagram Reels for local business](https://adwave.com/resources/instagram-reels-for-local-business-a-quick-start-guide), secondary).

Those are platform-wide figures, not groomer results. Paid search gives a clearer cost benchmark: AdTurbo puts dog groomer clicks at about $2 to $8 ([AdTurbo](https://adturbo.ai/google-ads-for-dog-groomers)).

Reels are free to post but not free to make, so the real comparison is your hours against bookings. Timing also matters. Groomer to Groomer, quoted on AdWave's pet grooming advertising page, notes that November and December are traditionally a very busy time for groomers, sometimes too busy ([AdWave, pet grooming advertising](https://adwave.com/resources/pet-grooming-advertising), secondary). Plan a few Reels for the quieter months, when a full book is less of a risk.

## What Should a Pet Groomer Film?

Short clips are the safer bet. AdWave's guide to marketing videos recommends 15 to 30 seconds for TikTok and Instagram Reels ([AdWave, how to make marketing videos](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-make-marketing-videos)). A vendor guide on AdWave also says keeping Reels under about 90 seconds, often 15 to 60, keeps people watching to the end ([AdWave, Instagram Reels with AI](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-make-instagram-reels-with-ai)).

Film once and reuse. One vendor recommends filming a single session and posting the same clip to TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts ([Think Tank Ad Agency](https://thinktankadagency.com/marketing-ideas-for-barber-shop)). Reels, Shorts and TikTok all use the same vertical 9:16 format, so one shoot can serve all three ([AdWave, TikTok videos with AI](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-make-tiktok-videos-with-ai)).

Repeat two or three formats for a month: a before-and-after reveal, a 15-second process clip, or a short care tip. Stop filming when a dog is stressed. An anxious animal on camera hurts the clip and your reputation with the owner.

## How Can AI Speed Up Reel Production?

AI is strongest at the work around the video. AdWave's Reels guide describes a tool that produces a finished vertical 9:16 Reel with voiceover, music and captions in a few minutes, and says most videos come back in about 2 to 5 minutes ([AdWave, Instagram Reels with AI](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-make-instagram-reels-with-ai)). Those are vendor claims, so time one of your own before relying on them.

Export settings are worth fixing once. AdWave's lyric video preset for Instagram Reels is 9:16 at 1080 x 1920, H.264, 10 to 15 Mbps ([AdWave, AI lyrics video](https://adwave.com/resources/ai-lyrics-video)).

A prompt you can adapt:

> Give me 12 short Reel ideas for [month] that I can film on my phone during normal grooms. For each, write a 3-second hook, 3 to 5 shots and a caption ending with one booking step. Friendly, practical tone. Do not make health claims.

Always edit the output. AI can write confident coat-care advice that is wrong for a specific breed. Anything about skin, parasites or behaviour should come from you or the vet. Do not let AI create an "after" photo for a real dog; clients will compare it with the animal they collect. ## Where Do the Bookings Come From?

Track the booking, not the view. AdTurbo names booking a grooming appointment as the primary conversion to track for dog groomer Google Ads, and lists "dog groomer near me", "dog grooming near me" and "puppy grooming near me" as keywords worth bidding on ([AdTurbo](https://adturbo.ai/google-ads-for-dog-groomers)). The same booking goal works for Reels: every video should end with one clear step.

Location targeting matters too. Barklead suggests targeting users within a 10 to 15 mile radius of a groomer's location for Facebook ads ([Barklead](https://barklead.com/post/using-facebook-ads-for-groomers)). A mobile groomer may need a wider radius than a salon in a dense city.

Reels attract new people, but email keeps them. A simple list of past clients can announce a rebooking window, and our [Mailchimp guide for pet groomers](/blog/pet-groomer-email-marketing-with-mailchimp) explains how to set one up. For creators and local partners, the [influencer marketing guide for pet groomers](/blog/pet-groomer-instagram-influencer-marketing) covers a different route to the same audience.

## What Does Reel Time Cost You?

Every input below is an assumption, to be replaced with your own numbers after a month of tracking. It assumes 12 Reels, 25 minutes each, plus 1.5 hours of planning and replies.

| Line (assumption unless noted) | Tough | Base | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reels per month | 12 | 12 | 12 |
| Hours: 12 × 25 min, plus 1.5 h planning and replies | 6.5 | 6.5 | 6.5 |
| Time cost at $30 an hour | $195 | $195 | $195 |
| New clients who say Instagram sent them | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| Average groom price | $85 | $85 | $85 |
| Visits in first year (every 6 weeks, rounded down) | 8 | 8 | 8 |
| First-year revenue per client | $680 | $680 | $680 |
| Margin on that revenue | 50% | 50% | 50% |
| Profit from new clients | $0 | $340 | $680 |
| Net after time cost | -$195 | +$145 | +$485 |

Break-even is $195 divided by $340 profit per client, or about 0.57, so one new regular in a month covers the time on these inputs. The calculation counts only first-year profit, so it is a conservative check rather than a full lifetime value.

A second check uses a sourced figure. PSPrint's guide cites an average annual salary of $40,000 for dog groomers, attributed to Pet Groomer Magazine ([PSPrint](https://www.psprint.com/resources/definitive-pet-shop-marketing-guide/), secondary). Divided by an assumed 2,080 working hours, that is about $19 an hour, so valuing your time at that rate cuts the monthly time cost to about $125.

## How Often Should You Post?

AdWave's Instagram growth guide recommends 3 to 5 good feed posts or Reels per week, filled in with daily Stories to stay familiar ([AdWave, real followers on Instagram](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-gain-more-real-followers-on-instagram)). That pace fits the 12-Reel assumption above, which is roughly three a week.

Be careful with trend claims. A Metricool-based summary on AdWave reports that YouTube Shorts likes, engagement and reach declined, and Instagram Reels views also fell in the period it analysed ([AdWave, short-form video scripting](https://adwave.com/resources/short-form-video-scripting), secondary). Measure your own reach over 90 days rather than assuming growth.

If you run paid Reels ads, cap how often one person sees them. Epom suggests a starting cap of 2 to 4 per user per week for Facebook and Instagram Stories and Reels ([AdWave, ad frequency](https://adwave.com/resources/ad-frequency-small-business), secondary).

## Do This This Week

1. Add a social media tick box to your booking form, with a "pet only, no people or voices" option, and record who agreed.
2. Choose two or three repeating formats, such as a reveal, a process clip and a care tip.
3. Film three clips of 15 to 30 seconds in one session, in good light, with sound on and no watermark.
4. Use the prompt above to draft 12 ideas, then cross off any you cannot film in normal grooms.
5. Add "How did you find us?" to your booking form and track it against the $195 monthly break-even.

## FAQ

### How often should a pet groomer post Reels?

Choose a pace you can keep for 90 days. AdWave suggests 3 to 5 good feed posts or Reels per week, with daily Stories between them ([AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-gain-more-real-followers-on-instagram)).

### Can I use AI to write my captions?

Yes, for hooks and first drafts. Edit every result, confirm each fact about your services and keep health or behaviour claims out unless you can stand behind them.

### Which music can I use in Reels?

This guide does not list music rules. Check the music library available on your account and the platform's current help page before posting, because the options depend on your account type and country.
