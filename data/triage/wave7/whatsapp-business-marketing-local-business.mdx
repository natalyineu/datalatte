## Is the WhatsApp Business app free for marketing?

The free app works for a single owner or a small front desk that answers chats by hand. Message Central lists its limits plainly: one device, one agent, no serious automation and no integrations. Its best fit is a business with fewer than 100 conversations a day.

That makes the app a reasonable starting point for booking questions, opening hours and confirmations you send personally. It does not give you scheduled reminders, shared inboxes or broadcast tools, so anything that needs to run without someone typing will push you toward the paid platform.

Google also offers a free messaging route. The [Google Business Profile Help page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617) says chat options with text message or WhatsApp are available only in select regions, and that only businesses with a claimed and verified Business Profile can add a WhatsApp or text messaging option. Check whether your country is supported before you plan around it.

## When should you move to the paid API?

Message Central's threshold is simple: start directly on the API if you project more than 200 daily messages or need system integration. Once you pass that volume, the free app's manual workflow becomes a bottleneck, and the platform's automation starts to earn its cost.

Automation matters for booking because customers do not only write during opening hours. [Adwave's summary of Zippia statistics](https://adwave.com/resources/online-appointment-booking-making-it-easy-for-customers-to-schedule) (secondary) reports that 40% of appointments are booked outside standard business hours. A message sent at 9 pm that gets an automatic answer is a booking you keep. For a fuller setup, see [how a WhatsApp API helps with local marketing automation](/blog/whatsapp-business-api-local-marketing-automation).

Read the trial terms first. Message Central lists it as a red flag when platforms add monthly fees or auto-renewal during the free trial. Note the renewal date and the cancellation steps before you connect a single number.

## What does switching platforms cost?

Switching has a real delay, though not a fee. According to the vendor, template re-approval during a platform switch is free but takes 1 to 7 days. Every reminder and promotion template you rely on must be approved again, so a switch during a busy week can leave you without automated messages.

Some providers offer a faster route. Message Central says WhatsAppNow gives free credits without a credit card and managed onboarding in up to 5 business days. Treat that as a vendor claim and confirm the onboarding timeline before you commit.

A simple switching checklist:

1. Export your current template list and note which ones are still in use.
2. Schedule the move for a quiet week, not the week before a holiday rush.
3. Submit templates for re-approval and allow up to 7 days.
4. Test one reminder to a staff phone before going live with customers.
5. Keep the old app connected until the new platform confirms it is sending.

## Which free tools cover reminders and review requests?

Reminder timing matters more than most owners expect. A salon marketing author recommends texting between 24 and 48 hours before the appointment, and adds that 48 hours works best while texting the day before is too late. That is a [secondary source](https://www.lockhart-meyer.co.uk/a-salon-marketing-checklist-for-savvy-text-messaging/), but the timing advice is easy to test with your own clients.

Review requests are the next step. [DMly's automation guide](https://dmly.io/google-business-profile-automation-guide/) says it can send a customer a Google review request through WhatsApp or SMS after their appointment, and a delay can be added so the message arrives at a more appropriate time. Set that delay after the reminder, not on top of it, so one client does not receive two messages in an hour.

Bulk sending is a different job. [WASheet's Brazil page](https://washeet.com/whatsapp-bulk-sender/brazil/) lists a Free Plan that lets users run its core features without paying. Before you use any bulk sender, read the rules on consent and message content, and only message people who expect to hear from you. If your market is Brazil, the [local WhatsApp marketing guide for Brazil](/blog/local-marketing-brazil-small-business-whatsapp-meta) covers the context in more detail.

## How do you calculate the real cost per booking?

The cost that matters is cost per booking, not cost per message. [Bookeo defines it](https://www.bookeo.com/news/2026/09/social-media-marketing-beauty-salons-ultimate-guide-filling-appointment-book/) (vendor blog, secondary) as total monthly spend divided by the number of confirmed bookings from that channel. The count must be confirmed appointments. [TailWerks](https://tailwerks.com/industries/pet-grooming-marketing-agency/) makes the same point: a form submission is an inquiry until the booking process confirms the appointment, so a busy WhatsApp thread is not a booking until someone is in the diary.

Here is a worked example. Every input is an assumption, so replace them with your own numbers from your provider and the rate card for your country.

- Assumption: provider fee of $40 per month.
- Assumption: 300 utility reminders at $0.03 each, which is $9.
- Assumption: 300 promotional messages at $0.08 each, which is $24.
- Assumption: 25 confirmed bookings come from WhatsApp conversations in the month.

Total monthly cost is $40 + $9 + $24 = $73. Cost per booking is $73 divided by 25, which is $2.92. If only 10 of those conversations convert, the same spend becomes $73 divided by 10, which is $7.30. The fixed provider fee gets spread thinner as bookings rise, so conversion is the number to track.

The same arithmetic shows up in paid media. [ARC's illustrative example](https://arccreativeco.com/what-a-salon-booking-costs-on-google-ads/) puts the cost at $5.40 per action, and at a 50% booking rate each booked appointment costs $10.80. At a 25% booking rate it costs $21.60. Run that calculation for WhatsApp too: divide the cost per action by the booking rate before you decide whether a channel is worth funding. A service for paid traffic can help set this up, see [Google Ads services](/services/google-ads).

## How should you handle marketing opt-outs?

Promotions need a clear exit. [Bella Booking](https://bellabooking.com.au/use-cases/two-way-messaging) separates two responses on its SMS platform: clients reply NO PROMOS or STOP MARKETING for a marketing-only opt-out, while STOP stops all SMS to that client. That split is worth copying. A client who wants appointment reminders but not discounts should be able to say so without losing the reminders.

Check whether your WhatsApp provider supports a similar split before you launch a promotion. If it does not, keep promotions to a separate list and keep booking messages on a different track. Good opt-out handling protects the bookings you already have, which is where most of your revenue sits.

Bella Booking's free trial, for reference, runs 14 days with no credit card required, and its Messages Plus add-on includes 100 extra SMS credits a month that never expire. Compare trial terms across platforms the same way: what happens on day 15, and what do you pay if you forget to cancel?

## FAQ

### Is WhatsApp Business free to use?

The free WhatsApp Business app suits businesses with fewer than 100 conversations per day, one device and one agent, according to Message Central. Anything beyond that, including automation and integrations, runs through the paid platform and a provider. Check current provider pricing before you budget.

### When does the free app stop being enough?

Message Central advises starting on the API when you project more than 200 daily messages or need system integration. Automated reminders and several staff sharing one number also point toward the platform.

### Do free trials of WhatsApp tools cost anything later?

They can. Message Central says some platforms add monthly fees or auto-renewal during the free trial, and it treats that as a red flag. Read the renewal terms and set a reminder to cancel before the trial ends.

### Can Google Business Profile replace WhatsApp messaging?

Only where it is available. Google's help page says WhatsApp or text chat options are limited to select regions, and only a claimed and verified Business Profile can add them. Check your location and profile status first.

### Do I pay to switch WhatsApp platforms?

Template re-approval is free, according to Message Central, but it takes 1 to 7 days. Plan the switch around quiet weeks so reminders and promotions are not interrupted.
