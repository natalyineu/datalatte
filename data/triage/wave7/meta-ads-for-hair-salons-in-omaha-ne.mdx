## How much should a hair salon in Omaha spend on Meta ads?

Bookeo's starting range is USD 750 to USD 1,500 per month for Facebook and Instagram combined. [Adwave's channel comparison](https://adwave.com/resources/how-to-choose-advertising-channels) puts a standalone Meta buy at often $300 to $1,000 per month. The gap between those figures is a planning decision, not a rule. A lower budget works as a test. A higher one gives the platform enough delivery to show which audience responds.

Start near the lower end if you run one location with a small team and cannot yet tell which ads produce bookings. Move up once you can match ad clicks to appointments in your booking system. Adwave's ranges cover many industries, so use them as a band to test against rather than a promise for your chair count.

## Which radius and audience should Omaha salons target?

Bookeo advises setting a 5 to 10 mile radius around the salon for Facebook and Instagram ads. For a salon, that distance roughly matches how far most clients will drive for a cut or colour appointment. A wider area mostly buys clicks from people who will never book, so widen the radius only when your booking data shows clients arriving from further out.

Build your audience from the clients who already book with you before guessing at interests. Check the current audience, placement and pixel options in Meta's live help pages, since the menus change. Test the same offer on Instagram and Facebook separately so you can see which network brings the bookings rather than assuming one does.

## Which ad formats and posting rhythm work for salons?

Adwave cites Meta's 2025 Creative Best Practices report, as the vendor relays it, saying short-form video generates 2.5 times more engagement than static images on Facebook and Instagram ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/electrician-advertising-reaching-homeowners), secondary). Use that as a reason to shoot short clips of cuts, colour work and the styling chair, not as a guaranteed result for your ads.

Bookeo says most salon owners find three to four Instagram posts weekly and one to two Facebook posts weekly sustainable. That is an organic rhythm. Paid campaigns need a small set of strong creatives rotated over time, which is a different job from posting volume. For how to shape the organic side alongside ads, see [Instagram marketing for hair salons](/blog/instagram-marketing-for-hair-salons).

## Why run Meta ads for brand exposure before a client searches?

Bookeo claims a client who has seen a salon's brand on Facebook 5 to 10 times before typing "hair salon near me" into Google converts at 2 to 3 times the rate of a cold searcher. Bookeo gives no source for that figure, so treat it as a hypothesis to test, not a benchmark.

The underlying logic is still worth testing. A salon that appears in a client's feed before the moment of need competes differently from one that appears only in search results. Bookeo also states that Facebook and Instagram can reach audiences and build trust without spending a dollar on the platform itself, so organic posts and paid ads can run side by side. For a Facebook-first plan, read [Facebook ads for hair salons](/blog/facebook-ads-for-hair-salons).

## Where are Meta ads set up, and which apps do they cover?

Meta Ads Manager, formerly Facebook Ads Manager, can serve as a central point for running ads across Instagram and Facebook ([Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/social-media-advertising)). Turrboo lists Meta Ads Manager as covering Facebook, Instagram and Messenger ([Turrboo](https://turrboo.com/blog/organic-vs-paid-social-media)). Instagram ads run through the same system because Instagram is owned by Meta, and one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook or both ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/instagram-advertising-for-local-business)).

Adwave states that Meta's apps reach an average of 3.56 billion daily active people ([Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/facebook-advertising-for-small-business)). That reach describes the platform, not your neighbourhood. The radius setting is what keeps spend near the salon.

## How should a salon measure whether Meta ads book clients?

Measure bookings, not reach. Ads Manager shows delivery numbers, but only your booking system shows what happened after the click. Ask every new client where they heard about you and record the answer next to the appointment. Compare those answers with the campaign dates to see whether the ads or other channels are doing the work.

Confirm the tracking setup against Meta's current help pages before you spend, since setup steps change. Follow-up matters too. A client who books through an ad can be moved into reminders and rebooking messages, which is covered in [AI-powered email marketing for hair salons](/blog/ai-powered-email-marketing-for-hair-salons) and [marketing automation for hair salons](/blog/marketing-automation-for-hair-salons-guide). ## FAQ

### How much should a hair salon in Omaha spend on Meta ads each month?

Bookeo suggests starting Facebook and Instagram ad spend at USD 750 to USD 1,500 per month. Adwave lists a standalone Meta buy at often $300 to $1,000 per month. Test within the range that your booking data can support.

### What radius should a salon use for Meta ads?

Bookeo advises a 5 to 10 mile radius around the salon for Facebook and Instagram ads. Widen the radius only when your bookings show clients coming from further away.

### Can one campaign run on both Instagram and Facebook?

Yes. Adwave states that one campaign can run on Instagram, Facebook or both, because both are managed through Meta Ads Manager.

### Is the claim that brand views double conversions proven?

No. Bookeo's claim that a client who has seen the brand 5 to 10 times converts at 2 to 3 times the rate of a cold searcher carries no source. Test it with your own booking data before relying on it.
