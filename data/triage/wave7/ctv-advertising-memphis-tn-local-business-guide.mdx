Connected TV (CTV) advertising lets a Memphis, TN business run short video ads inside streaming content on internet-connected televisions. According to [Paramount Ads Manager's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions), CTV ads are non-skippable, typically run 15 or 30 seconds, and appear full-screen. Most are bought programmatically, meaning an automated system buys and serves them against targeting criteria the advertiser sets.

This guide explains the format, how buying and pricing work, how to open a short spot, what a small budget buys, and how to measure results. It does not publish Memphis-specific CPMs or platform minimums. Those change often, so confirm current rates on each platform's live help page before committing money.

## What Is Connected TV Advertising?

Connected TV refers to any television that connects to the internet to stream digital content, including smart TVs, external streaming devices, and gaming consoles, according to [Teads](https://www.teads.com/blog/what-is-connected-tv-advertising/9858/) (secondary). [Simpli.fi](https://simpli.fi/latest/what-is-ctv) describes CTV advertising as video ads delivered through streaming content on these screens, with Roku and Fire TV among the devices named.

The distinction that matters for a local business is the delivery method. [Amazon Ads](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/connected-tv-advertising) defines streaming TV as the digital distribution of video across devices with an Internet connection, rather than a cable signal. The ad runs inside the streaming session, not on a broadcast schedule.

Streaming services also differ in how they sell ads. [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions) explains that ad-supported video-on-demand (AVOD) services are free or lower-cost streaming services supported by ads. Free ad-supported streaming TV (FAST) channels are free for viewers and supported entirely by advertising, with Pluto TV given as an example.

## How Do CTV Ads Differ From Other Video?

The main difference is that viewers cannot easily skip the ad. Amazon notes that the video completion rate (VCR) of CTV ads tends to be very high, since many streaming services do not allow ads to be skipped. A message that reaches the end of the spot is more likely than one cut off after five seconds on a social feed.

Viewing is also different from a phone. [Priority Media Agency](https://prioritymediaagency.co.uk/services/tv-tvod-streaming) states that VOD ads appear on streaming services, catch-up TV, and connected devices, unlike traditional TV. Some formats go further. [Division D](https://divisiond.com/interactive-ctv/) defines interactive CTV advertising as formats that let viewers engage directly with ad messages on the TV screen.

Targeting is handled differently too. CTV buying is set up around criteria the advertiser chooses, so the available options depend on the platform or buying tool. Check which location and audience settings each platform supports before you plan a campaign around them.

## How Are CTV Ads Bought and Priced?

Programmatic buying is the common route. [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions) describes programmatic advertising as using automated technology to buy and serve ads in real time, with the platform purchasing inventory that matches the advertiser's criteria. [Clicksgeek](https://clicksgeek.com/best-advertising-platforms/) (secondary) describes platforms such as The Trade Desk as giving access to inventory across millions of websites, apps, and streaming services.

Pricing is usually expressed two ways. Cost per thousand impressions (CPM) is the most familiar. Cost per completed view (CPCV) measures the price of each completed ad, and Amazon lists it alongside video completion rate as a metric used in CTV programmatic pricing.

Some vendors publish example rates. [Adwave](https://adwave.com/resources/netflix-advertising-small-business) (secondary) uses a $40 programmatic CPM in its example and says programmatic access to Netflix inventory is available through platforms like Adwave. Treat that as one vendor's illustration, not a Memphis rate.

## How Should a 15-Second Spot Open?

The first few seconds decide whether a viewer stays. [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions) recommends a strong opening hook within the first 3–5 seconds of a CTV ad. For a local business, that means showing the service, the result, or a clear problem early, instead of holding the brand name until the end.

Technical specifications vary. A [Tampa-based CTV provider's page](https://advertiseintampa.com/services/advertising/ctv-ott/) (secondary) notes that every streaming giant has its own rules for resolution, frame rate, bitrate, and format. Ask the platform for its current spec sheet before you export the file, and do not reuse one export across every service without checking.

Keep the message simple. One offer, one location or phone number, and a single call to action work better on a television screen than a long list of services. Test two openings before committing a full budget, because the hook is the lever most under your control.

## What Does a Worked Budget Look Like?

The example below uses assumed inputs so you can replace them with your own numbers. Every input is labelled.

- Test budget: $400 per month (assumption)
- Test length: 4 weeks (assumption)
- CPM: $40 (example from Adwave, secondary)
- Completion rate: 90% of impressions finish the ad (assumption)

Impressions equal budget divided by CPM, multiplied by 1,000. That gives $400 ÷ $40 × 1,000 = 10,000 impressions for the month. At an even pace (assumption), that is about 2,500 impressions per week. Adwave's own range of 5,000–10,000 impressions for $200–400 per month matches this arithmetic.

Next, estimate completed views. At the assumed 90% completion rate, 10,000 impressions produce about 9,000 completed views. CPCV is then $400 ÷ 9,000, or about $0.044 per completed view. Replace the completion rate with the figure your platform reports, because a lower rate raises the real cost of each completed view.

Ten thousand impressions is a test scale, not a full brand campaign. Use the test to judge the opening hook and audience response before you extend the budget.

## How Do You Measure a CTV Campaign?

CTV rarely produces a click in the way a search ad does. It builds familiarity, which shows up later as people choosing your business when they need it. Measure the signals that follow that pattern, and set a longer window than you would for a search test. The [ROI tracking guide for local businesses](/blog/how-to-track-marketing-roi-local-business-guide) covers how to set up those baselines before the campaign starts.

Useful signals include:

Google Business Profile searches for your exact business name, compared with the weeks before the flight. Branded search clicks in Google Ads, if you run it. Website direct visits during the flight window. A consistent "how did you hear about us?" question at the point of sale, logged by week.

Record the baseline before launch. Without it, a rise in branded searches cannot be attributed to the campaign.

If you also run search ads, the two channels work together. CTV introduces the business, and search captures the intent that follows. For businesses that want to reach people through app placements rather than television, the [in-app advertising guide](/blog/in-app-advertising-local-business-guide) compares that route. ## FAQ

### Are CTV ads skippable?

According to [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions), CTV ads are non-skippable video advertisements served within streaming TV content. Confirm the settings for each platform you buy on.

### What is cost per completed view?

Cost per completed view (CPCV) measures the price of each completed ad, according to [Amazon Ads](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/connected-tv-advertising). It is used alongside video completion rate in CTV programmatic pricing.

### How long should a local CTV ad be?

CTV ads typically run 15 or 30 seconds, per [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions). A 15-second spot is the shorter, cheaper test format, but check the length options each platform accepts.

### Do I need a programmatic platform to run CTV ads?

Programmatic buying is the common route, as described in [Paramount's glossary](https://adsmanager.paramount.com/insights/what-is-ctv-advertising-definitions). Some platforms offer self-serve ad managers. Compare minimum spend and targeting options on each platform's live help page before you choose.
