## How much does Bolt.new cost per month?

Bolt's pricing page lists four plans. Free is $0 and includes 1M tokens a month, capped at 300K a day, with Bolt branding, 10 MB uploads and limited hosting. Pro starts at $25 a month and includes 10M tokens a month with no daily cap. Its listed extras are no Bolt branding, custom domains, larger uploads and token rollover.

| Plan | Price | Tokens | Notable extras |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 1M a month, 300K a day | Bolt branding, 10 MB uploads, limited hosting |
| Pro | From $25 a month | From 10M a month, no daily cap | No Bolt branding, custom domains, larger uploads, token rollover |
| Teams | $30 per member a month | Per member, not pooled | Centralized billing, admin controls |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Contact Bolt |

Teams charges per person, so a two-person team pays for two seats. Enterprise is priced on request with a contact-sales process. Higher Pro tiers with bigger allowances exist, but their prices are not listed in the sources used here. ## How do Bolt tokens work?

Tokens are small pieces of text that AI uses to read and generate content. Short, common words are typically one token each, while longer or less common words can be several. Bolt consumes tokens when it reads your prompts and project files, thinks, responds to your prompts, and builds in your project. Every bit of that work is counted against your allowance.

The allowance rules differ by plan. Free tokens do not roll over to the next month, and the 1M monthly allowance is also limited by the 300K daily cap. Pro includes token rollover. Arithmetic on the listed figures: at the full 300K daily cap, the 1M monthly Free allowance would take more than three days of maximum use to spend.

Cancelling does not remove tokens immediately. According to Bolt, your plan and unused tokens stay active until the current billing period ends, and then the account moves to the Free plan. On Teams, a free member receives no tokens and cannot send prompts.

## How can a small business use fewer tokens?

Plan mode is the cheapest way to think a project through. In Build mode, every prompt you submit updates your code and uses tokens. Plan mode uses fewer tokens because it doesn't write code, so settle the page structure, wording and layout there first, then switch to Build for the changes you have agreed.

Keep the context small. Bolt processes fewer tokens when it has less context to process, so long, tangled chats make each request more expensive. Start a fresh project when a new page has nothing to do with the old one, and remove old pages you no longer need.

Choose the right agent. Bolt says its Standard agent gives accurate results for tasks of average complexity without the extra token cost of deeper reasoning. The Max agent uses more tokens to work through the full scope of complex tasks, so keep it for the hard jobs. A menu page or contact form rarely needs it. ## Is Bolt.new worth it for a local business?

It can be, for simple pages where a person checks every price, opening time and form before publishing. Start on Free and build a draft to see how much of the allowance it uses. Free sites are public on a bolt.host address with Bolt branding, and custom domains are listed under Pro, so a business that wants its own address will need a paid plan.

Move to Pro for the build month, then review what you actually used before the next billing date. Teams suits a business where an owner and a staff member both edit the site, as long as the extra seat is worth the cost. ## FAQ

### Is Bolt.new free?

Yes, there is a Free plan at $0. It includes 1M tokens a month with a 300K daily token limit, and it comes with Bolt branding and 10 MB uploads. Free plan tokens do not roll over to the next month.

### How much does Bolt.new cost per month?

Pro starts at $25 a month for 10M tokens with no daily cap. Teams is $30 per member a month, with tokens allocated per member. Enterprise is priced on a custom basis.

### What happens to my tokens if I cancel Pro?

According to Bolt, your plan and unused tokens stay active until the current billing period ends. After that, the account moves to the Free plan.

### Can staff members use a Teams plan without paying?

No. Free members on a Teams plan receive no tokens and therefore cannot send prompts. Each member needs an allocation, which is why Teams is priced per member.
