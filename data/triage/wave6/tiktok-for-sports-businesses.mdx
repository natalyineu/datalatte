A sports business should post short, real clips of training and classes on TikTok, keep music to what is cleared for business use, and test ads on a fixed budget before scaling. TikTok users spend an average of 95 minutes a day on the platform, according to [Solveo](https://www.solveo.co/post/11-ai-agents-for-social-media-growth-and-engagement-in-2025) and [ClicksGeek](https://clicksgeek.com/best-advertising-platforms/), both secondary sources. Whether your members are there depends mostly on age. Start with organic posts, then run a small paid test with a stop date.

## Is Your Audience on TikTok?

Salesforce's local marketing guide says TikTok has gained immense popularity among Gen Z users, particularly for its short-form video content ([Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/marketing/local-marketing/?bc=OTH)). That fits gyms, climbing walls and dance studios that attract younger adults. It is a weaker fit for an older membership base.

Bellwether Coffee gives a simple test: if your customers are under 35, TikTok is worth trying ([Bellwether Coffee](https://bellwethercoffee.com/blog/social-media-marketing-for-coffee-shops)). Square cites Nordicity's finding that 83% of Canadian SMBs use TikTok for brand awareness and marketing ([Square](https://squareup.com/ca/en/the-bottom-line/reaching-customers/marketing-plan-for-a-coffee-shop)). That figure describes businesses, not the people who buy from them, so use it as a sign of what other local firms are doing, not as proof your members are on the app.

## What Should a Sports Business Post?

No source gives a winning formula, so test a few formats and keep the ones that generate saves, comments and enquiries. Practical starting points that need only a phone include:

- A coach demonstrating one drill or correction in under 30 seconds.
- A class clip from warm-up to cool-down, with consent from everyone shown.
- A sports shop showing how to size or care for one piece of gear.

Authenticity appears to matter more than polish. Zeely describes a Portland stylist who shared consultations and transformations on TikTok as an example of authentic video marketing ([Zeely](https://zeely.ai/blog/hair-salon-advertising-ideas/), secondary). Formats also fade. Infegy reports that GRWM (Get Ready With Me) videos surged in early 2023 on TikTok and Instagram, then declined as content shifted toward lifestyle storytelling ([Infegy](https://www.infegy.com/blog/three-ways-to-find-beauty-insights)). Rotate formats rather than repeating one for months.

Film a new member only with their written permission, and a child only with a parent or guardian's consent. For edits, the [AI content creation tools for local businesses](/blog/ai-content-creation-tools-local-business-2026) guide covers options. For captions, an n8n template uses a prompt asking for a short, engaging local post with a subtle call to action ([n8n](https://n8n.io/workflows/6165-automate-google-business-profile-posts-with-gpt-4-and-google-sheets/)). You can adapt that brief for your Google Business Profile, and the [ChatGPT prompts for local business marketing](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-local-business-marketing) article has more.

## Which Music Can You Use?

Do not assume a song from the trending feed is cleared for a business account. Check TikTok's current Business Help pages for what commercial use allows before posting anything with music. Then pick a short list of cleared tracks and reuse them across the week. This keeps your posting routine simple and reduces the chance of a clip with an unlicensed sound going out by mistake.

## What Does a Test Budget Look Like?

TikTok's minimum budgets change, so check the live Business Help pages before you set anything. The example below uses your own assumed numbers. Every input is an assumption.

| Input | Value (assumption) |
| --- | --- |
| Ad group daily budget | $25 |
| Test length | 14 days |
| Total ad spend | $25 × 14 = $350 |
| Membership price | $60 a month |
| New paying members needed to cover spend | $350 ÷ $60 = 5.8, so 6 |

This is a break-even check for the first month only. It ignores retention, class packs and discounts. If the test brings fewer than six paying sign-ups, change the offer or the content before raising the budget. ## How Do You Know If It Works?

Track three things each week: video views, profile visits, and enquiries or trial bookings. Views alone do not pay for classes. Ask every new member how they heard about you and record the answer next to their sign-up date.

Set a review date before the test starts. Four to six weeks of organic posting is a reasonable checkpoint (assumption). If there are no enquiries by then, change the content or the audience before adding budget. ## When Should a Sports Business Push?

Sports advertising clusters around the season. A TNS Media Intelligence report found that football TV ad revenue comes 75 to 80% from the regular season and 20 to 25% from the post-season ([Sports Video Group](https://www.sportsvideo.org/2007/03/07/tns-media-intelligence-releases-march-madness-advertising-trends-report/), secondary, 2007). That report covers TV, not TikTok, but it suggests timing matters.

Major events can also carry large media value. Elevate Sports Ventures and Hive estimated more than $410 million in media exposure value for featured brands during 2022 March Madness telecasts ([Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/article/march-madness-reportedly-generates-410m-of-in-game-brand-exposure/), secondary). That is an estimate of exposure, not sales. For your own business, plan heavier posting before your sign-up windows, such as a new term or season, and lighter posting in quiet months.

## FAQ

### Is TikTok worth it for gyms and sports clubs?

It can be, especially if your customers are under 35, as Bellwether Coffee suggests. Test with organic posts first. No source guarantees bookings.

### How much should I spend on TikTok ads?

Check TikTok's live Business Help pages for current minimum budgets. Then run a fixed test like the worked example above, with a set stop date.

### Can I use popular songs in my videos?

Not without checking. Use only music that TikTok's business help pages say is cleared for commercial use.

### Should I film children in class?

Only with written consent from a parent or guardian, and after checking your club's safeguarding rules. This article did not review child-safety law.
