## How Does Angi Charge Contractors?

Angi is classed as a pay-per-lead company. A secondary review of Porch placed it alongside Angi and HomeAdvisor in that category ([K2 Radio](https://k2radio.com/porch-pro-reviews/), secondary). Under this model, you pay when a lead is sent to you, and the same request can reach other professionals.

Creating a business profile on Angi is free, according to a secondary guide to lead generation programmes ([Online Visibility Pros](https://onlinevisibilitypros.com/internet-marketing-for-contractors/homeadvisor-vs-thumbtack-vs-angis/), secondary). Paid advertising is the other route. That same guide says advertising starts at around $300 per month, with pricing customised by service and location. Get the exact figure for your trade and area from Angi itself.

For a broader view of how this model differs from another marketplace, see [Angi vs Thumbtack for local business](/blog/angi-vs-thumbtack-for-local-business).

## What Contract Terms Apply to Angi Ads?

Angi advertising reportedly requires a 1-year contract, and a 35% early termination fee applies if you try to end it early ([Online Visibility Pros](https://onlinevisibilitypros.com/internet-marketing-for-contractors/homeadvisor-vs-thumbtack-vs-angis/), secondary). At the starting price, a full year is about $3,600 in commitment before any fee. That is a floor, not a quote.

The secondary source does not say what the early termination fee is calculated on. Ask for the calculation in writing before signing, and budget for the worst case in your cash flow.

## How Does Houzz Compare on Price and Ranking?

The sources behind this guide do not give verified Houzz advertising prices, so no Houzz figure is quoted here. Before you spend, put these questions to Houzz directly and get the answers in writing:

- Is there a monthly price, and is it fixed or based on your category and location?
- Does ad spend change where your listing appears in search results, and is paid placement labelled to customers?
- What is the minimum term, and what is the cancellation fee?
- How are leads or enquiries delivered, and do other pros receive the same enquiry?

Thumbtack uses a different structure again. Each lead costs a number of credits that varies with the job's complexity, demand and location ([7ten Marketing](https://7ten.marketing/angie-vs-thumbtack/), secondary). If you are weighing a third option, compare it using the same cost-per-job method in the next section. See also [Thumbtack vs Angi for local business](/blog/thumbtack-vs-angi-local-business).

## How Do You Calculate Cost per Closed Job?

Use one formula for every channel: total spend divided by jobs closed. Every input below is an **assumption** chosen for illustration, not platform data. Replace them with your own numbers.

| Input (all assumptions) | Lead-fee platform | Advertising platform |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly spend | $1,000 | $1,000 |
| Leads or enquiries received | 20 | 8 |
| Share you reach and quote | 60% | 75% |
| Quotes sent | 12 | 6 |
| Close rate | 15% | 25% |
| Jobs closed | 1.8 | 1.5 |
| Cost per closed job | about $556 | about $667 |

The platform with more leads has the lower cost per job here, but the result is sensitive. If the advertising platform's close rate rises from 25% to 30%, it closes 1.8 jobs and lands at about $556, the same as the lead-fee platform. Small changes in close rate matter more than headline lead counts.

To run this test with your own business:

1. Log each lead with its source, date received, and whether you contacted it.
2. Record the date each quote went out and whether you won the job.
3. Add each channel's monthly spend, including any minimum fees.
4. After at least 60 days, divide spend by closed jobs for each channel.
5. Keep the channel only if its cost per job beats your average job margin.

## FAQ

### Is Houzz or Angi cheaper?
Neither platform's verified pricing is in this guide. Angi ads start at around $300 per month on a 1-year contract, according to a secondary source. For Houzz, ask for written pricing and run the cost-per-closed-job test on your own numbers.

### Does Angi have a free option?
Creating a business profile on Angi is free, according to a secondary source. Advertising is a separate, paid option with a contract.

### What happens if I end an Angi ad contract early?
A 35% early termination fee applies, according to a secondary source. The source does not explain how the fee is calculated, so confirm it before signing.

### How long should I test a new lead channel?
Track at least 60 days of leads, quotes and closed jobs before judging a channel. A few leads are not enough to compare cost per closed job.
