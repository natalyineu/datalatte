Geofencing advertising draws a virtual boundary around a real place, such as a shop, a competitor's location, or a neighbourhood, and serves ads to mobile users who enter that area. For a local business, it reaches people while they are close enough to visit. Results vary by campaign. Some reported campaigns show conversion lifts of up to 20% or 30%, but those figures come from secondary sources. Your results depend on geofence size, the offer, and how well you track visits.

## What is geofencing advertising and how does it work?

Geofencing is a location-based method. Google's developer documentation describes geofencing as creating virtual geographic boundaries that trigger an action when a device enters or exits the defined area ([Google Developers, Geofencing Overview](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/location/geofencing)). In advertising, that action is an ad delivered to the device through websites and apps.

Geofences can be drawn at very different sizes. One secondary source notes that geofencing can reach an area as small as a zip code, and that a message can follow a person for 30 days after they enter the boundary ([West Coast Media](https://www.westcoastmedia.us/how-effective-is-geo-fencing-for-local-advertising), secondary). For a small shop, the useful question is not how large the area can be but how close the audience needs to be to visit.

The practical difference from broad digital ads is the audience. A campaign can target selected business locations, venues, or geographic areas, then layer mobile, display, video, or connected TV tactics where they fit ([White Sands Marketing Group](https://whitesandsmarketinggroup.com/geofencing-advertising.php), primary vendor page).

## Which local businesses benefit most from geofencing?

The vendor page lists several fits: retail and restaurant traffic campaigns, auto, home services, healthcare, and professional services, events and tourism, and businesses competing within defined local trade areas ([White Sands Marketing Group](https://whitesandsmarketinggroup.com/geofencing-advertising.php)). These are cases where the customer's location and the business's catchment area line up.

Geofencing is less useful when customers are spread across a wide region and visit rarely. A plumber serving a whole county may get more from search advertising. A coffee shop on a clear commuter route, or a salon near an office block, has a stronger case because the people nearby are the people likely to buy.

Competitor targeting is also an option. Geofences can be placed around a rival's location, which is one reason the method is used for local trade-area campaigns ([White Sands Marketing Group](https://whitesandsmarketinggroup.com/geofencing-advertising.php)). Check your local advertising rules before running any comparative message.

## How do you set up a geofencing campaign step by step?

The vendor's four-stage model works as a checklist: discover, plan, launch, optimize ([White Sands Marketing Group](https://whitesandsmarketinggroup.com/geofencing-advertising.php)). For a small business, the steps look like this:

| Stage | What to decide | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | Goal (visits, sales, or awareness), audience, and the places they spend time | You can name the goal in one sentence |
| Plan | Geofence locations, one message per audience, daily budget, tracking method | Tracking is set up before launch, not after |
| Launch | Run at least two full weeks so weekday and weekend patterns show | Daily spend and visits are logged |
| Optimize | Compare visits and sales by location and by day, then shift budget | Weakest geofence is paused or changed |

Keep the message short and specific. Name one offer and one action, such as a free add-on with a visit or a time-limited drink deal. Test two or three versions rather than one large creative set.

The message should match the moment. Someone walking past at 8 AM needs a quick reason to step in, not a long brand story. The [ChatGPT prompts for local business marketing](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-local-business-marketing) guide shows how to brief an AI tool for short ad copy. Always review that copy before it goes live.

## How do you measure geofencing foot traffic and ROI?

Measurement needs a plan before launch. Without a control period, a rise in visits could simply be a busy week. A simple method is to run the campaign for two weeks, pause it for one week, and compare visits on matching days. Record ad spend and visits per day in a spreadsheet.

No single method captures everything. The table below compares the common options for a small shop.

| Method | What it shows | Effort | Main blind spot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pause-and-compare test | Visits lift on matching days | Low, a spreadsheet and a clicker | One unusual week can distort the result |
| Unique offer code | Customers who saw the ad and redeemed | Low, needs staff to ask and log | Misses visitors who skip the code |
| POS "source" field | Repeat visits from geofence-acquired customers | Medium, needs a consistent habit | Depends on staff recording answers |
| Location attribution platform | Ad-exposed devices that later enter your area | Medium, paid tool | Accuracy depends on the vendor's data |

Combine two or three of these rather than relying on one. Each covers a gap the others leave open.

**Worked example: a coffee shop, one month (all inputs are assumptions)**

- Monthly geofencing budget: $600 (assumption)
- Average ticket per visit: $8 (assumption)
- Incremental visits from the pause-and-compare test: 40 (assumption)
- Cost per incremental visit: $600 / 40 = $15.00
- First-visit revenue: 40 × $8 = $320
- First-visit ROAS: $320 / $600 = 0.53

On first visits alone, the campaign loses money. Break-even on first visits needs $600 / $8 = 75 transactions. If new customers average 2.5 purchases over 90 days (assumption), revenue becomes 40 × 2.5 × $8 = $800, and ROAS becomes $800 / $600 = 1.33. Judge the campaign on repeat purchases, not the first month alone.

Geofencing works best as one layer in a plan. The vendor describes layering mobile, display, video, or connected TV tactics when they fit ([White Sands Marketing Group](https://whitesandsmarketinggroup.com/geofencing-advertising.php)). ## FAQ

### What is the minimum budget for geofencing ads?

The sources do not give a minimum budget, so there is no sourced figure to quote. Start with an amount you can run for at least two weeks, then use the worked example above to judge whether the numbers justify a larger test.

### How long should a geofencing test run?

Run it for at least two full weeks so weekday and weekend patterns appear. Then pause it for a comparable period to measure the difference. Shorter tests can be distorted by one unusually busy or quiet day.

### Can geofencing be used near competitors?

Yes. The vendor describes competitor geofencing as one use of the method ([White Sands Marketing Group](https://whitesandsmarketinggroup.com/geofencing-advertising.php)). Check your local advertising and comparative-claim rules before running any message that names a rival.

### How accurate is the conversion lift figure?

The 20% and 30% figures come from a secondary article that does not name the underlying studies ([West Coast Media](https://www.westcoastmedia.us/how-effective-is-geo-fencing-for-local-advertising)). Treat them as a reason to test, not as a forecast for your shop.
