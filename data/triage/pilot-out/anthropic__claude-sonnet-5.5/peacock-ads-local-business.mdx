A local business can advertise on Peacock through three routes: Universal Ads self-service, a direct NBCUniversal plan, or a programmatic deal through an eligible DSP. A [2026 buying guide from Ad Geeks](https://adgeeks.agency/news/news-how-to-advertise-on-peacock) (secondary) says Universal Ads describes no platform minimum spend. Neither source opened for this article publishes a CPM, so price has to come from a written quote. Work out break-even cost per customer first.

## How can a local business buy Peacock ads?

The route decides which inventory, targeting, reporting and terms apply. The Ad Geeks guide (secondary) maps the options like this:

| Route | Best fit | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Universal Ads self-service | Smaller and mid-market advertisers wanting hands-on setup | Peacock availability, eligible placements, forecasting, account approval |
| NBCUniversal direct or One Platform | Brands needing premium content, live moments or cross-platform scale | Minimum commitment, exact Peacock allocation, cancellation terms, creative lead time |
| Programmatic guaranteed or PMP | Advertisers that want DSP workflow and deal controls | Approved DSP, seller, deal type, price, device coverage, whether the deal is Peacock-specific |

Universal Ads is Comcast's self-service TV platform. It offers two campaign types: Performance+, where the system optimizes delivery toward awareness or conversion goals, and Content Select, where the advertiser picks genres or allowlisted networks. Specific publisher access may require an account representative.

One warning applies to every route. "NBCUniversal inventory" does not prove that each impression runs on Peacock, so ask for network, app, content, device and deal-level detail in writing.

These figures come from [NBCUniversal's own Peacock advertising page](https://together.nbcuni.com/network/peacock/), so they are sales claims rather than independent audits. The page says Peacock generates more than 15 million hours of ad-supported viewing every day.

The 64% figure is Nielsen P2+ duplication, a three-month average across ad-supported competitors. The brand impact lift is a composite of ad awareness, favorability and purchase intent across 374 campaigns run from October 2024 to September 2025. The search lift covers 83 campaigns and 66 brands over the same period.

Two limits matter for a local advertiser. The figures are aggregated, and the page excerpt does not break them out by advertiser size or by single market. A café with a five-mile radius cannot assume it will see the same lift.

## Which Peacock ad formats exist?

NBCUniversal lists several formats beyond a standard in-stream spot. Three are first-view placements:

- Arrival Ad, an exclusive placement on the profile selection page
- Live in Browse, which shows a brand logo inside the live event preview on the homepage
- Spotlight, which delivers an ad to all Peacock viewers in the first standard ad break for 24 hours on a chosen day

The page also says Programmatic Pause Ads are coming in 2026, starting with PG deals. It reports that ad innovations drive 1.7x higher ROAS than standard media. Contextual Pause Ads show 23% higher memorability than pause ad norms in a Marketcast study of 1,316 people from early 2023. Engagement Ads with NBCU IP show a 114% higher interaction rate than units without it, using BrightLine norms that are not Peacock-specific.

The Ad Geeks guide (secondary) adds that availability depends on the product, content window and commercial agreement. Neither source says which formats a local advertiser can buy or at what price. Spotlight reaches all Peacock viewers, which reads as national scale rather than a neighborhood buy. Confirm before planning around it.

## What targeting can a local advertiser use?

Targeting depends on the route and the data permissions attached to it. The Ad Geeks guide (secondary) documents these Universal Ads controls:

| Layer | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Location | State, DMA and ZIP code inclusion or exclusion |
| Core demographics | Age and gender |
| Advanced demographics | Household income, education, homeownership, family status, shopper behaviors |
| Viewership behavior | Sports, news, comedy, drama, travel, food viewing signals |
| Genre or contextual alignment | Content genres such as sports, news, reality |

ZIP-level inclusion is the control that matters most to a business with a fixed service area. The guide recommends starting with 1-2 targeting layers and checking forecasted scale after each one. Every added layer shrinks the audience and can raise the price per thousand views.

Universal Ads also supports a pixel for conversion signals. Programmatic deals have their own rules, so audience permissions, frequency controls and log-level data need to be confirmed deal by deal.

## What does a Peacock test cost, and when does it pay back?

No source gives a CPM, so every input below is an assumption. The example shows how to judge a quote when one arrives, not what Peacock will deliver.

| Input or result | Lower CPM | Higher CPM |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Test budget (assumption) | $3,000 | $3,000 |
| CPM (assumption) | $30 | $60 |
| Impressions | 100,000 | 50,000 |
| Views per household (assumption) | 4 | 4 |
| Households reached | 25,000 | 12,500 |
| Households that become customers (assumption) | 0.05% | 0.05% |
| New customers | 12.5 | 6.25 |
| Cost per new customer | $240 | $480 |

Assume a new customer leaves $300 of gross profit (assumption). At the lower CPM, 12.5 customers return $3,750 against $3,000 spent, a gain of $750. At the higher CPM, 6.25 customers return $1,875, a loss of $1,125.

The rule that falls out is simple. Cost per customer equals CPM times frequency, divided by 1,000 times the conversion rate. If a quote pushes that figure above gross profit per customer, the test loses money unless it earns value elsewhere, such as branded search or referrals.

## What should be checked before a campaign launches?

The Ad Geeks guide (secondary) lists items to request before building a programmatic line item:

- DSP and advertiser seat eligibility
- Seller and supply path
- Deal ID, deal type and pricing model
- Peacock-only or broader NBCU inventory
- Supported devices and environments
- Geography, content and live-event restrictions
- Audience permissions and frequency controls
- Measurement and reporting fields

On creative, the guide says to submit exact-duration 16:9 video and leave time for platform and publisher review. Streaming ads are rarely clicked, so a vanity URL, a dedicated phone number and a "how did you hear about us" field at booking should exist before the first impression runs.

Branded search and Business Profile calls in the service area should be compared with the weeks before launch. Platform-reported results alone are weak evidence, since the platform grades its own campaigns.

## Who should test Peacock first, and who should wait?

A business with high customer value and a trade area wide enough to absorb a metro-scale buy has the best case. Home services, dental and auto fit that profile, particularly when search and a Google Business Profile already work. At $300 of gross profit per customer, the example above shows how thin the margin can get.

A single shop with a small radius and a low ticket has a harder case. Cheaper intent-based channels such as [Google Local Services Ads](/blog/google-local-service-ads-worth-it-local-business) give a benchmark to beat. Compare any Peacock quote against current cost per customer from search.

Other streaming and CTV options help calibrate price and reach. ## FAQ

### Can a small business advertise on Peacock?

Yes. Ad Geeks (secondary) describes Universal Ads as a self-service route with no platform minimum spend, plus direct NBCUniversal and programmatic routes. Peacock availability inside Universal Ads can depend on the account, so confirm it in the forecast or in writing before committing budget.

### How much do Peacock ads cost?

Neither NBCUniversal's Peacock page nor the Ad Geeks guide publishes a CPM. Pricing depends on route, format, targeting and deal terms. Request a written quote and run it through the break-even example above.

### Can Peacock ads be targeted by ZIP code?

Universal Ads documents state, DMA and ZIP code inclusion or exclusion, according to the Ad Geeks guide (secondary). Whether ZIP targeting applies to Peacock inventory in a given deal needs confirmation from the seller.

### Do Peacock ads work?

NBCUniversal reports a 36% brand impact lift and a 22% search activity lift versus CTV norms, based on its own aggregated campaign data. These are self-reported averages across many campaigns, not results for local businesses. A holdout area or period gives a cleaner read on a single campaign.
