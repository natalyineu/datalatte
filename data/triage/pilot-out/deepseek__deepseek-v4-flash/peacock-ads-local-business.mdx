Peacock has 48 million paid subscribers and reported its first profit in Q2 2026, but NBCUniversal publishes no minimum budget or cost per thousand views (CPM) for advertisers. A local business can buy Peacock ads through self-service, programmatic deals, or direct sales, but cannot price the platform from public information. The first step is to ask the right questions and calculate break-even before receiving a quote. This guide covers verified facts, buying routes, a test-budget calculator, and questions for sellers. Peacock is a US service, so buying routes described are US-based.

According to [TV Technology's report on Comcast's Q2 2026 results](https://www.tvtechnology.com/platform/streaming/peacock-posts-first-profits-adds-2-million-subs-in-q2-2026), Peacock had 48 million paid subscribers, adding 2 million in the quarter. The company's CFO stated advertising revenue increased nearly 70%. [Advanced Television](https://www.advanced-television.com/2026/07/24/peacock-turns-profitable/) reports Peacock turned profitable with segment revenue of $1.9 billion for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Peacock's [help page](https://www.peacocktv.com/help/article/how-much-does-a-peacock-subscription-cost) lists Select at $8.99 per month, Premium at $12.99, and Premium Plus at $19.99. The [price increase notice](https://www.peacocktv.com/help/article/price-increase) states new subscribers pay these prices from August 18, 2026, and existing subscribers from their first billing date on or after September 17, 2026. Premium Plus is described as having "no ads (limited exceptions)", with ads remaining in channels, live sports, events, and a few shows. No source states which cheaper plans carry ads or the ad-supported subscriber share.

## What Are the Buying Routes for Peacock Ads?

Three documented routes exist for buying Peacock advertising. Each has different requirements and verified details.

**Peacock Ad Manager (self-service):** NBCUniversal announced this on April 29, 2022, in partnership with tvScientific, as a self-service platform for performance, local, and small business advertisers. The [announcement](https://together.nbcuni.com/insights/news/nbcuniversal-partners-with-tvscientific-to-launch-peacock-ad-manager/) describes precision audience and geo targeting with built-in performance measurement. [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/373440/nbcu-unveils-self-serve-programmatic-ad-manager.html) quoted NBCU's Krishan Bhatia saying it serves advertisers "who don't have existing" DSP relationships. No source states a minimum spend. The Ad Manager website did not load when checked. [The Desk reported](https://thedesk.net/2025/12/pinterest-acquiring-tvscientific/) on December 11, 2025, that Pinterest agreed to acquire tvScientific, expected to close in first half 2026, with no mention of Peacock.

**Programmatic through a DSP:** Peacock inventory is reported to be sold through major demand-side platforms, but no NBCU source confirms which deal types are available. Availability is deal-specific.

**Direct NBCUniversal sales:** NBCU has run local products under its One Platform for years, but current local minimums are not published in verified sources.

## What Can You Safely Say About Peacock Advertising?

Peacock is a large, growing, profitable subscription streamer with a premium ad-supported offering and a self-serve tool announced for smaller advertisers. From verified sources, you cannot state the CPM, minimum budget, share of viewers who see ads, age or income profile of local viewers, or whether a local business can reach only its zip codes. Vendor pages quote minimums from $50 to $150,000 and CPMs of $20 to $44, but they cite no NBCU source and some sell the access they describe. Treat these as noise until you have a written quote.

## How to Calculate Break-Even for a Peacock Test

NBCUniversal publishes no CPM, so every price in this table is an **assumption**, as are frequency and response rate. Use it to evaluate a received quote, not to predict results.

| | Cheap | Mid | Expensive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Test budget (assumption) | $3,000 | $3,000 | $3,000 |
| CPM, cost per 1,000 views (assumption) | $25 | $40 | $60 |
| Impressions | 120,000 | 75,000 | 50,000 |
| Views per household (assumption) | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| Households reached | 30,000 | 18,750 | 12,500 |
| Households that become customers (assumption) | 0.05% | 0.05% | 0.05% |
| New customers | 15 | 9.4 | 6.3 |
| Cost per new customer | $200 | $320 | $480 |

Break-even is gross profit per customer, not revenue. If a new client leaves $120 profit over a year, the Cheap column loses $80 per customer. A high-value service like dental implants or a full-year gym membership could carry the Expensive column. Check household coverage against your trade area. If the buy covers a whole metro, most households are too far away to visit. Compare cost per customer from Google Ads as a benchmark.

## What Questions to Ask Any Peacock Seller

1. What is the minimum for a one-month test, and is it per campaign or per flight?
2. What is the CPM or flat price for my geography, and does narrowing the area raise it?
3. Which shows, sports, and channels will my ad run in, and which can I exclude?
4. What proportion of my targeted households will see ads rather than a no-ads plan?
5. What creative do you need, and who pays for production?
6. How will you prove delivery, and do I get household or zip-level reports?
7. What changes if the product changes hands or is renamed mid-flight?

Put answers in writing. If a seller cannot answer the first two, you do not yet have an offer.

## How to Measure a Peacock Test

Streaming ads are rarely clicked, so build measurement before launch. Use a vanity URL and dedicated phone number for the campaign. Add a "how did you hear about us" field at booking. Track branded search volume and Business Profile calls in your area, compared with the four weeks before. Create a holdout area or period where you did not run the ad. Do not accept a platform's own attribution as the only evidence.

## Who Should Test Peacock?

Reasonable fit includes businesses with high customer value like home services, dental, med spa, gyms, and auto, with a trade area big enough to justify a metro-scale buy, and search and Business Profile already working. Wait if you run a single café or salon with a small radius and low ticket. Roku offers a self-serve tool with published minimums for comparison.

## FAQ

### Can a small business advertise on Peacock?
Yes in principle. NBCUniversal announced a self-serve Peacock Ad Manager aimed at small and local advertisers in 2022. Other routes exist through DSPs and sales teams. Current minimums and availability are not published in verified sources, so confirm them directly.

### How much do Peacock ads cost?
NBCUniversal does not publish CPMs in verified sources. Vendor figures range widely and are unsourced. Ask for a written quote and use the break-even table above.

### Does everyone on Peacock see ads?
No. Peacock states Premium Plus has no ads, with limited exceptions for live sports and events. It does not state which other plans carry ads, so ask what share of your audience sees them.

### Can I target my city or zip codes?
NBCUniversal's announcement describes precision audience and geo targeting. Which geographic level is available and at what price needs confirmation from the seller.

### Is Peacock better than Hulu or Roku for a local business?
Not in any way that can be verified from published sources. Compare written quotes on cost per customer.
