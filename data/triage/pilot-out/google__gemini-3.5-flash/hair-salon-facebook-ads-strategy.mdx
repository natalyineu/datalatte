Hair salon Facebook ads generate new clients by targeting local users within a specific driving radius, presenting a compelling introductory offer, and tracking bookings directly through the Meta Pixel. Success requires setting up robust tracking, calculating the maximum acceptable cost per lead based on customer lifetime value, and following up with leads immediately.

By combining highly visual creative assets—such as before-and-after transformations—with localized targeting, salons can consistently acquire new clients. This strategy ensures ad spend directly translates into booked appointments rather than empty clicks.
