To buy Peacock ads for a local business, choose Universal Ads for self-service control, NBCUniversal direct or One Platform for a negotiated premium plan, or an eligible DSP for a programmatic deal. Before budgeting, confirm that Peacock itself is included, which locations and devices are reachable, how reporting is broken down, and what creative approval requires.

A [secondary buying guide from Ad Geeks](https://adgeeks.agency/news/news-how-to-advertise-on-peacock) maps these routes and their verification points. The sources reviewed publish neither a Peacock-specific local CPM nor a universal Peacock minimum. The practical next step is a written inventory and price quote, followed by break-even math using gross profit per customer rather than revenue.

## What does Peacock offer advertisers?

NBCUniversal positions Peacock as a premium streaming destination for franchises, originals, films and live cultural events. Its [Peacock advertising overview](https://together.nbcuni.com/network/peacock/) provides scale and aggregated performance indicators, but not a local price card.

The viewing figure represents more than 15 million ad-supported hours each day. The 64% duplication finding is a Nielsen three-month average from December 2025 through February 2026 across ad-supported competitors, with ad-supported estimates based on Antenna and eMarketer data.

Brand impact draws on 374 campaigns from October 2024 through September 2025 and is compared with CTV norms. Search activity lift draws on 83 campaigns across 66 brands in the same period, measured through EDO. These are historical aggregates, not guaranteed results for a new local campaign.

For a local advertiser, the useful conclusion is narrower: Peacock has substantial ad-supported viewing, and NBCU reports lifts in brand and search outcomes. The figures do not reveal reachable households in a particular trade area, the cost of reaching them, or likely customer volume. Request a location-specific forecast rather than applying platform percentages to a service radius.

## Which buying route fits a local business?

Universal Ads is the hands-on route. Ad Geeks describes it as Comcast’s self-service TV platform, with NBCUniversal and other premium publishers and no platform minimum spend. Performance+ optimizes across eligible publishers toward awareness or conversion goals. Content Select lets advertisers choose genres or allowlisted publisher networks.
