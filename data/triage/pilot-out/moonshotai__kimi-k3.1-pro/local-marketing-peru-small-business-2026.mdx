## What should be fixed before promoting the business?

Onza’s diagnostic copy describes a brand that no longer reflects the business, a website that attracts visits but produces few enquiries, and social posting without a clear strategy. Its website discussion asks whether visitors understand the offer, find important information and manage to make contact ([Onza Marketing](https://onzamarketing.com/)). These prompts help separate a message problem from a traffic problem.

Manya similarly connects branding with recognition and trust, while its web service emphasizes UX/UI and contact generation. It also lists technical support for improving an existing website ([Manya](https://manya.pe/)). That distinction matters: unclear positioning, weak page structure and technical faults may require different work.

A practical brief should name the target customer, service area, offer and desired action. It should also record what currently happens after an enquiry. A campaign cannot be assessed properly if calls are missed, forms are not routed or nobody records whether an enquiry became a sale.

Avoid commissioning every available service by default. If the offer is difficult to understand, clarify the message and page content before increasing promotion. If the site already converts enquiries reliably, search or campaign testing may be a more sensible scope. The agency pages provide diagnostic categories, not evidence that a redesign or brand refresh will produce a particular commercial result.

## Which channels fit the business problem?

Search work is relevant when the business wants to be found for what it offers. Onza describes SEO as optimization of a website and its content, while Manya presents an SEO service addressing both conventional search and newer AI-oriented discovery ([Onza](https://onzamarketing.com/agencia-seo/), [Manya](https://manya.pe/seo-en-la-era-de-la-ia/)). A proposal should identify the pages, queries and measurable enquiries involved.

Paid media can be scoped as a controlled campaign test, but these sources do not show that advertising outperforms search or social publishing. Onza emphasizes reaching the intended audience and using investment effectively. Manya describes performance marketing around leads and sales. Ask how tracking will distinguish qualified enquiries from accidental clicks, spam and existing customers.

Social media needs an operational purpose beyond posting frequency. Onza links its service to consistent communication aligned with the brand. Ecommerce becomes relevant when products, payments and orders must be managed online. Manya’s automation service suggests another possible layer, but automation should follow a defined enquiry process rather than substitute for one.

The [Marketing Branding case index](https://www.marketing-branding.pe/casos-de-exito-marketing-peru-y-latinoamerica/) includes tags for Google Ads, Shopping, SEO, local SEO, analytics, ecommerce and web services. Those tags illustrate available capabilities; they do not rank channels by effectiveness.

## How should agency evidence be evaluated?

Agency scale indicators are not interchangeable. Onza reports activity since 2013, more than 1,000 projects, more than 15 countries and more than 10 sectors on its homepage. [Manya states](https://manya.pe/) that it has more than 800 happy clients. [Marketing Branding says](https://www.marketing-branding.pe/casos-de-exito-marketing-peru-y-latinoamerica/) that Google tools helped it serve more than 400 companies across Peru, Chile and Colombia.

Projects, clients and companies served are different units. The supplied text also does not provide a common reporting period or independent audit. These figures can support questions about experience, but they cannot establish which agency is largest or which will generate the best return.

Case labels need the same scrutiny. Marketing Branding tags Socoimport with advertising, analytics, web design and hosting, while Viosculpt is tagged with SEO and local SEO. The supplied entries do not state baselines, costs, lead volumes, revenue or timeframes. They demonstrate claimed project scope, not a quantified outcome.

Request evidence that matches the proposed work. A useful case explanation identifies the starting problem, work delivered, measurement period and business result. It should also explain how results were attributed. A recognizable logo, a testimonial or a large portfolio may be relevant context, but none replaces a measurement definition and verifiable outcome.

## How could a small business run a measured local test?

Worked example: every input and result in this subsection is an assumption for illustration. It is not a client case, agency quotation or Peruvian benchmark. Assumption: a Lima home-repair business has a functioning website and wants qualified enquiries that can become completed jobs.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test window | 4 weeks |
| Media spend | 600 currency units |
| Setup, creative and tracking | 300 currency units |
| Qualified enquiries during the test | 30 |
| Completed jobs attributed to those enquiries | 12 |
| Gross profit per completed job | 80 currency units |

| Assumption-based calculation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Total test cost: 600 + 300 | 900 currency units |
| Cost per qualified enquiry: 900 ÷ 30 | 30 currency units |
| Gross profit from completed jobs: 12 × 80 | 960 currency units |
| Gross profit less test cost: 960 − 900 | 60 currency units |

The remaining 60 currency units are not net profit; overhead, tax and other costs are excluded. This assumption-based result leaves little room for error and does not support automatic scaling.

A real test should define a qualified enquiry before launch, deduplicate calls and forms, and distinguish new from returning customers. If job completion or gross profit changes, recalculate the economics before changing spend.

## How should agency proposals be compared?

Onza organizes its homepage around business needs, while Manya emphasizes personalized diagnosis. A small business can use the same problem-led approach when writing a brief. Instead of requesting a generic marketing package, specify the obstacle, intended customer action and evidence that would show progress.

Ask each bidder to provide:

- The exact deliverables and the work explicitly excluded.
- Account ownership, administrator access and export arrangements.
- Definitions for enquiries, qualified leads and attributed sales.
- Separation of media spend from fees and production costs.
- Relevant case evidence with its baseline and measurement method.

Also identify who supplies copy, approves creative work, implements tracking and maintains the website. Responsibilities that remain unassigned can weaken an otherwise sensible campaign.

Compare proposals against the same brief rather than against the number of services listed. Broader capability may be useful, but a small engagement still needs a clear scope, accessible data and a decision about what happens after the test.

## FAQ

### Should a Peruvian small business start with SEO or paid media?

The sources provide no comparative performance data. Repair obstacles to understanding or contact first. Search work can address discoverability, while paid media can support a controlled test. In either case, define qualified enquiries and record whether they become sales.

### Do client and project counts prove agency performance?

No. The reviewed figures use different units and are self-reported. They may help frame questions about experience, but they do not demonstrate campaign return. Relevant outcomes, measurement methods and scope provide stronger evidence.

### Do the reviewed sources provide Peruvian advertising prices?

No. No market benchmark or agency price list is supplied. Request an itemized proposal and keep media spend separate from service and production costs. Use campaign economics from the business’s own tracked test.

### Do these sources establish which payment wallet to support?

No. They contain no comparative wallet-usage evidence. Confirm provider requirements directly and observe the payment methods customers actually request before making wallet adoption a campaign assumption.
