## Who Is Actually on Instagram?

Pew's 2026 survey of 5,511 US adults found that 51% use Instagram, up from 50% in its 2025 survey. Facebook reaches 70% of US adults, down from 71%, and YouTube reaches 84%. TikTok stands at 37%. These are national figures, not local ones, so they show where people spend time rather than whether the customers in your town use the app.

Check your own customer base before committing budget. Ask existing clients which apps they use, and review where your enquiries currently come from. If your clients skew older or live in areas where Facebook is the main channel, test Facebook placements alongside Instagram. The Pew figures are a starting point for that decision, not a substitute for it.

## What Are the 10 Rules?

1. Pick one objective per campaign. Choose the action you want, such as a booking, a call or a message, and build the campaign around it.
2. Send people somewhere that finishes the job. A booking page or lead form that loads quickly on a phone usually does better than a generic home page.
3. Keep delivery local. Set the area around the business and exclude places it cannot serve. Check the location options in Ads Manager before launch.
4. Lead with a real photo or short video. Show the work, the staff and the space.
5. State the offer in the first line. A price, a free consultation or a limited number of slots gives people a reason to act.
6. Test one variable at a time. Change the image, the headline or the audience, never all three together.
7. Give each test enough budget and time. Judge only after the ads have run long enough to produce a meaningful number of results.
8. Track outcomes you can count. Calls, bookings and form fills matter more than likes.
9. Reply to comments and messages quickly. A slow reply wastes the click that was paid for.
10. Cut weak ads and move budget gradually. Small changes are easier to read than a doubled budget overnight.

## How Should You Run a Clean Test?

Meta's own guidance is to test with a defined hypothesis and a controlled split. Its help page on A/B testing says each version is shown to a separate segment of the audience, so nobody sees both, and that the same budget should be used for both versions to keep the comparison fair. You can duplicate an ad set and change one variable, then compare cost per result.

Meta also advises against informal testing, such as switching ad sets on and off by hand. That approach can create overlapping audiences and unreliable results. Use the Experiments tool or the A/B test option in Ads Manager instead. If you manage several accounts or schedule many posts, a scheduling tool can help keep tests organised; this [comparison of social media tools](/blog/hootsuite-vs-buffer-vs-later-best-social-media-tool) covers the main options.

## Should Instagram Ads Replace Google Ads?

They do different jobs. Instagram ads reach people while they scroll, so they suit offers that create interest. Search and other Google campaigns reach people who are already looking for a service. Google's documentation describes its Display network as reaching people across 35 million websites and apps, including YouTube and Gmail, which shows how much wider that audience is than a single social feed.

Many local businesses start with search, since the intent is clear, and then test social as a second channel. ## How Do You Handle Video and Creative?

No source reviewed here ranks ad formats for local services, so the only reliable approach is to test. Run a short video, a single photo and a carousel against the same offer, then keep whichever format produces leads at the lowest cost. Keep text short and readable on a phone.

Assume many viewers watch with the sound off. Use captions or on-screen text so the offer is clear without audio. If AI tools are used to draft captions or variations, check every claim against the real service and prices before publishing. ## FAQ

### How much should a local business spend on Instagram ads?

There is no standard figure in the sources reviewed. Start with an amount that can be spent for a month, then compare the cost per booking with what a new customer is worth to the business.

### Which objective should I choose?

Choose the action that brings in revenue. For service businesses that is usually bookings or calls. Choose traffic only if the landing page is built to convert visitors.

### How long should I run an ad before judging it?

Wait until it has run long enough to gather a meaningful number of results. Editing or pausing ads every day makes it hard to read what is working.

### Do I need video to succeed?

No. Test a photo, a short video and a carousel with the same offer, and keep the format that produces the cheapest leads.

### Are Instagram ads better than Google Ads?

They serve different purposes. Google reaches people searching now, while Instagram reaches people who are browsing. Many local businesses begin with search and add social once they have reliable conversion data.
