## Do Waze ads still exist for local businesses?

Not as a standalone product. Google's wind-down FAQ says Waze would no longer provide Waze Ads after August 31, 2023, and that DV360 support for these campaigns ended the following day ([Waze Ads wind-down FAQ](https://support.google.com/wazeads/answer/14260169?hl=en), read 3 Nov 2026).

Many guides still describe the old platform. A French agency article, for example, lists a self-serve Waze Ads Starter option and an enterprise tier of up to $30,000 per month ([DeepReach](https://www.deepreach.com/media/how-waze-boosts-local-visibility-for-businesses), secondary). Treat that as history. Its pins, search placements and takeovers describe a product Google no longer sells.

If an agency offers Waze takeovers or a Waze minimum spend today, ask exactly what is being bought and where it will appear. Get the answer in writing before paying. Businesses in the US, UK, Australia and Canada can check current options directly in their Google Ads account, which is the only reliable source for what is live.

## How do Waze placements work inside Performance Max?

Google Ads Help says ads can appear as promoted places on Waze, and that Waze Ads are currently available only through Performance Max for store goals campaigns. The bidding options named there are Store Visits, Store Sales and Local Actions Directions ([Google Ads Help: Local Ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3246303?hl=en), read 3 Nov 2026).

Several points follow from that:

Waze is one surface inside a larger campaign. You cannot select it on its own.

Billing is CPM. The Local Ads page says these campaigns charge cost-per-thousand impressions, and no CPM figure is published there.

Location assets must be linked at the account or campaign level. Google recommends Google Business Profile as the source.

The campaign decides how to split spend across surfaces, so Waze gets no guaranteed share.

For background on how Performance Max allocates budget across channels, see the [Performance Max guide for local business](/blog/google-performance-max-for-local-business-complete-guide-2026). Google's Display help page also points advertisers toward Performance Max or Search for other automated campaign types ([About Display ads and the Google Display Network](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190), primary).

## Which local businesses should consider Waze placements?

Waze suits businesses where a driver can plausibly pull over on the way somewhere. A driver is already making small stop decisions, so the placement fits impulse visits.

Good fits include coffee shops and cafes on commuter routes, quick-service restaurants with lunch and dinner windows, fuel stations and auto services, and gyms or fitness studios that catch evening commuters.

Poor fits include city-centre shops where most customers walk, appointment-only services that need advance planning, and businesses whose customers do not drive.

If your customers book ahead or search first, spend on search and your Google Business Profile before any location campaign. ## What does a Waze test cost to break even?

There is no published Waze price, so no honest cost per visit can be quoted. You can instead work out in advance what a test must deliver. The inputs below are assumptions for a worked example, not market data.

Assumption: a cafe runs a store-goal campaign at $15 a day for 28 days. An average walk-in spends $7, and about 60% of that is gross margin.

| Step | Input | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Test budget | $15 x 28 days (assumption) | $420 |
| Margin per visit | $7 x 60% (assumption) | $4.20 |
| Visits needed to break even | $420 / $4.20 | 100 visits |
| Per day | 100 / 28 | about 3.6 extra visits a day |
| If 1 in 3 visitors returns once more | Extra $4.20 per 3 visitors (assumption) | about 75 visits, or 2.7 a day |

For context, WordStream's older benchmark puts the average Google search cost per click across industries at $2.69, and the display figure at $0.63 ([WordStream Google Ads benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks), secondary, dated). Those numbers are not Waze costs, but they show how small a test budget is in search terms.

If roughly four extra customers a day from a location campaign looks unlikely, the test will not pay back on first visits alone. Then the real question is whether repeat visits close the gap.

## How do you measure Waze ad results?

Google Ads Help says store visits can be measured and optimised where eligibility requirements are met, and that users clicking for directions is a trackable action. Those are the two signals built into the campaign.

Add three checks that do not depend on Google's modelling:

A unique offer code that customers mention at the counter.

A dedicated phone number or tracking link used only in the campaign.

A weekly comparison of calls and direction requests in Google Business Profile insights against the four weeks before the test.

Compare results at day 14 and day 28, not after a single week of data.

## What should a local business do this week?

1. Open your Google Business Profile and confirm the address, hours and phone number. Location campaigns rely on this data.
2. Write one offer in a single line, such as a free pastry with any large coffee before 10am.
3. Calculate break-even visits per day using the table above, with your own ticket size and margin.
4. Set reminders for day 14 and day 28 to compare visits and redemptions against the prior month.
5. Set ad hours to match opening hours, so you do not pay for visibility when the doors are shut.

## FAQ

### How do Waze ads work for local businesses?

Waze no longer sells ads directly. According to Google Ads Help, ads can appear as promoted places on Waze through Performance Max for store goals campaigns.

### How much do Waze ads cost?

There is no separate Waze price. Spend comes from your Google Ads campaign budget, and Google says these campaigns charge CPM. No CPM figure is published, so run a limited test and calculate break-even first.

### Can I still buy zero-speed takeovers or branded pins from Waze?

No. Google says Waze stopped providing Waze Ads after August 31, 2023. Offers of takeovers or branded pins today describe a retired product.

### Can I track visits from Waze placements?

Partly. Store visits may be reported where eligibility requirements are met, and directions are a trackable action. Promo codes, tracking links and dedicated phone numbers give more dependable measurement.

### Should I choose Waze placements or Local Services Ads?

They solve different problems. Local Services Ads reach people actively looking for a service. Waze placements reach drivers on the move, which suits walk-in and impulse businesses.
