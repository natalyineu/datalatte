## Which Platform Should a Gulf Small Business Use First?

Platform choice depends on the country. DataReportal's Digital 2026 country reports, published in November 2025 with data mostly from October 2025, show Saudi Arabia with 25.3 million Snapchat ad reach (72.9%) against 18.2 million for Instagram (52.4%). The UAE reverses that pattern, with Instagram at 8.05 million (70.5%) and Snapchat at 5.13 million (44.9%). Qatar also favours Instagram, at 1.70 million (54.3%) against 1.24 million (39.5%) for Snapchat.

Saudi Arabia is the clearest case for leading with Snapchat. Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman were not part of the figures reviewed for this update, so check their platform data directly in each platform's ad tool before choosing.

Google Business Profile and search ads reach people who are already looking for your service. For many local businesses they belong in the first test alongside one social channel. The [Google Ads setup guide](/blog/how-to-set-up-google-ads-for-your-small-business-in-2026-step-by-step-with-screenshots) walks through the account and campaign steps.

## What Should You Check Before Running Gulf Ads?

Advertising rules and paid creator rules vary by country and change over time. Before you publish paid content or hire a creator, confirm the current requirement with the regulator in each market. A news summary, a competitor's practice or a rule remembered from an earlier year is not enough.

In the UAE, Gulf News reports that an Advertiser Permit became mandatory on 1 February 2026 for individuals and businesses advertising on social media, websites or blogs, paid or unpaid, citing the UAE Media Council. The same report gives content-breach fines from AED 5,000 up to AED 1,000,000, depending on severity. It states that personal accounts promoting one's own products are exempt, but it is unclear whether a business account promoting its own products qualifies. Confirm that point in writing with the Media Council.

In Saudi Arabia, Arab News reported in October 2022 on the Mawthooq licence for content creators earning advertising revenue, at SR15,000 for three years. That report is several years old, so confirm the current authority, fee and scope with the regulator before paying an influencer.

| Step | What to confirm | Who to ask |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Whether the business or the creator needs a permit or licence | The national media or advertising regulator |
| 2 | Whether personal accounts promoting your own products are treated differently | The same regulator, in writing |
| 3 | Fee, validity period and penalties | The regulator's published fee schedule |
| 4 | Required disclosure wording for paid partnerships | The regulator's guidance |
| 5 | Date of the last update to the rule you rely on | The regulator's website |

Keep each written answer with the campaign file, so you can show what you relied on at the time if a rule changes later.

## Should You Write in Arabic, English or Both?

No source reviewed for this update gives a language split for Gulf audiences, so the choice is a test. Run the same offer in Arabic and in English to the same city over the same period, then compare cost per booking or cost per enquiry. Use a native speaker for each version, since literal translations often read poorly to local customers even when the words are correct.

Record the language of every enquiry in your booking form or CRM. One test month shows direction but not a precise answer. Repeat the test before shifting a large part of the budget to one language.

## How Do You Plan Around Ramadan Without a Fixed Date?

The UAE news outlet The National projects that Ramadan 2027 will begin around 8 February 2027, with Eid Al Fitr around 9 or 10 March. The actual start depends on moon sighting, so treat these dates as a planning anchor and confirm them against an official announcement before you schedule posts. Ramadan 2026 has already passed.

Build the calendar in relative weeks, so a one-day shift moves only the launch post and any countdown. The timeline below uses the projected start date and labelled assumptions for planning lead times.

Published figures on how much Ramadan shifts spending or posting times were not found, so this guide does not state them. Pull last year's weekly sales and enquiry volume, mark the Ramadan weeks, and compare them with the weeks either side.

## What Does a Ramadan Test Budget Look Like?

The example below is an illustrative salon or clinic. Every input is an assumption, so replace them with your own figures before you rely on the output.

Assumed total test budget: 2,000 units of local currency for the month. Assumed average cost per click on search ads: 1.50 units. Assumed click-to-enquiry rate: 5%.

Clicks = 2,000 ÷ 1.50 ≈ 1,333. Enquiries = 1,333 × 0.05 ≈ 67. Cost per enquiry = 2,000 ÷ 67 ≈ 30 units.

Suppose the Arabic version of the same ad produces an enquiry at 22 units and the English version at 38 units. Both figures are hypothetical. In that case the Arabic version is the stronger default for that city. If the gap is small, keep both running and test a second offer. ## How Do You Track Results Across Platforms?

Each channel needs its own tracking, so cost per enquiry is measured the same way everywhere. Add UTM parameters to every link, create a separate conversion for each enquiry type, and record the source in the booking form. Without this, platform dashboards will report results that do not match your calendar.

Once the data sits in one place, channels can be compared on the same measure. The [Google Analytics 4 guide for small business](/blog/google-analytics-4-for-small-business) covers the events and reports that support that comparison. ## FAQ

### Is Instagram or Snapchat better for Gulf small businesses?

It depends on the country. DataReportal's Digital 2026 figures show Snapchat with the larger advertising audience in Saudi Arabia and Instagram ahead in the UAE and Qatar. Confirm current figures in each platform's ad tool before you choose.

### When does Ramadan 2027 start?

The projected start is around 8 February 2027, but the actual date depends on moon sighting. Confirm it against an official announcement before scheduling posts.

### Do I need a permit to advertise online in the UAE?

Gulf News reports that an Advertiser Permit has been mandatory since 1 February 2026 for those advertising online, with a stated exemption for personal accounts promoting one's own products. Confirm your position with the UAE Media Council in writing.

### How much do Google Ads cost in the UAE or Saudi Arabia?

No published Gulf cost-per-click figures were found for this update. Use Keyword Planner for your city and keywords, then run a small test and read the real cost per click in your account.

### Should I write ads in Arabic or English?

Test both with the same offer and city, and compare cost per enquiry. Record the language of each lead so the result reflects your own customers.
