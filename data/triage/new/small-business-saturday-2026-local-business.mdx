Small Business Saturday 2026 falls on Saturday, November 28, the Saturday after Thanksgiving (November 26), according to [Vision Monday](https://www.visionmonday.com/business/the-independent-eye/biz-tactics/article/consumers-and-retailers-prepare-for-small-business-saturday-on-nov-28-2026/) (secondary). A coffee shop, salon, pet groomer or fitness studio can cover it with five moves: one clear Shop Small offer, a refreshed Google Business Profile, a message to existing customers, visible in-store signage, and a promo code that shows what worked. The countdown below starts November 16.

## What is Small Business Saturday and who runs it?

The day was created by American Express in 2010, and the U.S. Small Business Administration has co-sponsored it since 2011, according to the [SBA's November 2025 release](https://www.sba.gov/article/2025/11/25/sba-encourages-americans-shop-small-small-business-saturdayr-november-29th) (primary). The same release notes that SBA's participation is not an endorsement of any cosponsor's products or services. SBA also keeps an [observance page](https://www.sba.gov/about-sba/organization/observances/small-business-saturday) (primary), and American Express keeps a [Shop Small page](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/benefits/shop-small/) (primary).

A trade report from 2018 describes the promotion as a way to highlight locally owned businesses and also lift spending on American Express cards ([Digital Transactions](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/consumers-small-business-saturday-spending-rose-38-this-year-amex-reports/), secondary). A shop that wants to benefit from the card-linked side of the day should confirm that its register accepts those cards.

No 2026 results exist yet, because the day has not happened. Every figure below comes from earlier years.

## How much does Small Business Saturday matter to independent shops?

The numbers are large, but they are survey estimates, not sales receipts. The SBA release says its spending figures are self-reported by consumers in surveys commissioned by American Express and do not reflect actual receipts, sales or card spending.

Per the SBA release, American Express survey respondents who own physical-location businesses (including personal services and coffee shops) expected nearly 20% of annual revenue from the day. That is an expectation gathered September 29 to October 8, 2025, not a measured result. A shop's own point-of-sale report for last year's Saturday is a better planning number.

Earlier estimates swing. Amex-supplied data reported by [Digital Transactions](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/consumers-small-business-saturday-spending-rose-38-this-year-amex-reports/) (secondary) put 2017 at $12.9 billion and 2018 at $17.8 billion. Treat the totals as a direction, not a forecast.

## Which offer fits a coffee shop, salon, groomer or studio?

A good Shop Small offer is simple to say at the counter and cheap to deliver. It should also pull a customer back on a slower day, so the Saturday spike does not just borrow from the following week. The table lists starting points. Prices and limits are left open on purpose: the owner sets them from margin and capacity.

| Business | Offer idea | Track it with | Margin check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coffee shop | Free size upgrade or pastry add-on with the code; whole-bean bag plus drink bundle | Code said at the register, with a POS discount button or tally sheet | Upgrades cost milk and syrup, not full price; the bundle must still cover beans |
| Hair or beauty salon | Gift card with a bonus card valid only in a slower month | Gift card sold under the code; bonus card serial numbers logged | Bonus cards are redeemed later, so cost lands in a quiet week |
| Pet groomer | Free add-on (nail trim or brush-out) with a full groom booked for the weeks after the holidays | Code typed in the booking note | Add-on adds minutes to the appointment; cap how many per day |
| Fitness studio | Gift class pack with a guest pass attached, or a free guest week for a new neighbor | Code entered at pack checkout | Cap guest passes so peak classes do not fill with free spots |

The offer ideas are suggestions, not tested results. More campaign ideas are in the [holiday marketing strategy guide](/blog/holiday-marketing-strategy-local-business).

## What should go on Google Business Profile, signage and partner cards?

People looking for something to do on November 28 often search on a phone and land on a map listing. The profile should therefore match what the door says. A fuller walkthrough sits in the [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist). For this weekend, the short version is:

- Confirm holiday hours for November 26, 27, 28 and 29.
- Upload fresh photos of the storefront, the offer item and the team.
- Publish a post that names the offer, the date and the code.
- Reply to recent reviews so the page looks active.

In the shop, a window sign and a counter card carry the same wording as the post. A single sentence works best: the offer, the date, and "say the code" or "show this screen."

Partnerships need only a small ask. Two neighbors can hand each other's offer cards at checkout: a groomer with a pet store, a cafe with a bookshop, a studio with a juice bar. The [neighborhood marketing ideas](/blog/neighborhood-marketing-ideas) article covers more ways to make that swap work.

## How should email and SMS support the day?

Email and text reach people who already know the business, which makes them the cheapest channel for this day. The [email campaign ideas list](/blog/email-marketing-ideas-for-small-businesses-20-campaigns-that-drive-revenue-2026) and the [SMS versus email comparison](/blog/sms-vs-email-marketing-local-business-which-is-better) help with channel choice.

A workable rhythm is an announcement email the Monday before, a reminder the afternoon before the day, and a short text on Saturday morning. Text only customers who have agreed to receive messages.

Each channel gets its own code. If the email says SMALL28E, the Google post says SMALL28G and the window sign says SMALL28D, then Sunday's tally shows which channel brought people in. Codes also reveal a quiet truth: if nearly everyone uses the door code, the messages are not what moved them.

The day sits right after Black Friday on November 27. The [Black Friday and Cyber Monday guide](/blog/black-friday-cyber-monday-small-business-2026) covers whether to run one offer all weekend or a separate Shop Small offer on Saturday only.

## What is the countdown from November 16 to November 29?

The calendar below gives each task a date. Thanksgiving week is short, so the work that needs quiet focus happens before it. A longer view of the year is in the [12-month local marketing calendar](/blog/local-business-marketing-calendar-week-by-week-12-months).

### Week 1: Monday, November 16 to Sunday, November 22

- Mon Nov 16: Pick one offer from the table and check the margin on paper.
- Tue Nov 17: Create one code per channel and set up a POS discount button or tally sheet.
- Wed Nov 18: Photograph the storefront, the offer item and the team; update the profile photos and hours.
- Thu Nov 19: Ask two neighboring businesses about swapping offer cards.
- Fri Nov 20: Order or print the window sign and counter cards.
- Sat Nov 21 and Sun Nov 22: Draft the email and text; brief staff on the code script.

### Week 2: Monday, November 23 to Sunday, November 29

- Mon Nov 23: Send the announcement email and publish the profile post.
- Tue Nov 24: Put signs up and hand out the partner cards.
- Wed Nov 25: Confirm staffing, stock and holiday hours.
- Thu Nov 26: Thanksgiving. Nothing is scheduled.
- Fri Nov 27: Send the afternoon reminder email and text.
- Sat Nov 28: Text the code at opening, post a photo from the shop and log code counts at close.
- Sun Nov 29: Tally the codes by channel, send a thank-you message and save the numbers for next year.

## What could the numbers look like for a salon?

The example below is an illustration, not a forecast. Every input is an assumption and should be replaced with the salon's own figures.

| Input | Value (assumption) |
|---|---|
| Customers reachable by email and text | 900 |
| Share who buy a gift card with the code | 3% |
| Extra buyers from window sign and profile post | 8 |
| Gift card value | $50 |
| Bonus card, valid in January | $10 |
| Share of bonus cards redeemed | 70% |
| Service gross margin | 55% |
| Printing and signs | $60 |

Gift card buyers: 900 × 3% = 27, plus 8 = 35. Gift card sales: 35 × $50 = $1,750.

Per buyer, a redeemed $50 card brings $50 × 55% = $27.50 of contribution. The bonus card costs $10 × 70% × 45% (the cost share of a service) = $3.15 in expected delivery cost. Net per buyer is $24.35.

Across 35 buyers, that is 35 × $24.35 = $852.25. Minus $60 for printing, the net is $792.25. At $24.35 per buyer, the print cost is covered after three buyers (60 ÷ 24.35 is about 2.5).

This view assumes every gift card is eventually redeemed and that buyers would not have booked anyway. Neither is guaranteed, so the code tally and January bookings are what to check.

## FAQ

### When is Small Business Saturday 2026?

Saturday, November 28, 2026, the Saturday after Thanksgiving on November 26, per [Vision Monday](https://www.visionmonday.com/business/the-independent-eye/biz-tactics/article/consumers-and-retailers-prepare-for-small-business-saturday-on-nov-28-2026/) (secondary).

### Does the $22 billion figure mean shops actually made that much?

No. The figure is an estimate from a self-reported consumer survey of 2,601 adults, and the SBA release states it does not reflect actual receipts or sales ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/article/2025/11/25/sba-encourages-americans-shop-small-small-business-saturdayr-november-29th), primary).

### Does a business need to register to take part?

Registration and sign-up terms are not covered here. Check the [American Express Shop Small page](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/benefits/shop-small/) (primary) and the [SBA observance page](https://www.sba.gov/about-sba/organization/observances/small-business-saturday) (primary) for current details.

### Is paid advertising worth adding for the day?

It depends on budget and margin. Late-November ad prices are not documented in the material used for this article, so ask any platform or agency for current rates before committing.
