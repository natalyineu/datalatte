Instagram and Telegram are the two social channels with the strongest local data for Uzbekistan, and internet use is high. Secondary reporting puts internet users at 29.52 million, or 83.3% of the population, in January 2024, and more than 9 million Instagram users in December 2024. The sources do not verify a search-engine share for Uzbekistan, so a local business should check Google and Yandex results directly. The practical path is to choose one or two channels, run a small test, and count bookings.

## How many people can a business reach online in Uzbekistan?

Most of the population is online. The RMAA agency page states that over 87% of the population are internet users, citing official data without naming the source ([RMAA Agency](https://russia-promo.com/cis-advertising/uzbekistan/digital), secondary). A separate agency blog gives 29.52 million internet users and 83.3% penetration as of January 2024, and says internet speed rose 70% in 2024 compared with 2023 ([agency blog](https://russia-promo.com/blog/uzbekistan-smm-strategies-and-social-network-insights), secondary).

The figures differ by source and date, so a business should plan around a range. Online reach is large, but it still depends on a clear message for people who search or scroll on a phone. RMAA also estimates around 70% of the population uses mobile internet, or about 24 million people.

## Is Instagram the strongest social channel with local data?

It has the clearest local figure in these sources. The agency blog cites more than 9 million Instagram users as of December 2024, sourced to NapoleonCat ([agency blog](https://russia-promo.com/blog/uzbekistan-smm-strategies-and-social-network-insights), secondary). The same piece says 8.7 million people use social networks overall, which is lower than the Instagram figure. The two numbers come from different methods, so they should not be added together or compared directly.

Audience location matters. The blog notes that Instagram ads will be ineffective if the target audience is in Russia, where ad display is blocked. A business should confirm where its customers live before it buys ads that reach them.

## Does Telegram work for local business?

Telegram is widely used locally. The agency blog describes 174,000 Uzbek channels and 31,000 groups with local geographic ties, most of them in the national language ([agency blog](https://russia-promo.com/blog/uzbekistan-smm-strategies-and-social-network-insights), secondary). That shows a dense community, but it gives no price or reach figure for advertising.

Two routes are described: buying ads through Telegram's ad manager, directly or through an agency, or placing messages with large channel owners ([agency blog](https://russia-promo.com/blog/uzbekistan-smm-strategies-and-social-network-insights), secondary). A Telegram ad vendor claims more than 1 billion monthly users worldwide and sells its own service ([Telegram Ads vendor page](https://telegram-ads.com/telegram-ads-uzbekistan/), secondary). That is vendor marketing and refers to Telegram globally, not to Uzbekistan. Messaging-led selling works as a model; the [WhatsApp marketing guide for Brazil](/blog/local-marketing-brazil-small-business-whatsapp-meta) shows the same logic of tracking replies and bookings in another market.

## Is Google the main search channel?

The sources in this guide do not give a verified search-engine share for Uzbekistan, so no percentage is quoted. Search your service in both Uzbek and Russian, and note which results and map listings appear for your city. Russian-language search matters in a market where Russian is used alongside Uzbek. The [Belarus local marketing guide](/blog/local-marketing-belarus-small-business-2026) covers a neighbouring Russian-speaking market with similar questions about language and search.

Google listings are worth claiming because the cost is low. Check whether your customers actually use Google before you spend on paid search there.

## How should you choose between channels?

The table sets out what each channel offers a small business and what to verify first. It is a planning aid built from the sources, not a ranking.

| Channel | What the sources show | Verify before spending |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram | More than 9 million users (Dec 2024, secondary) | Ad display in your market; whether customers message you |
| Telegram | 174,000 Uzbek channels; ads via ad manager, agencies or channel owners | Channel owners in your niche; the actual placement price |
| Google search | No verified share in these sources | Your own searches in Uzbek and Russian |
| Mobile internet | Around 70% of the population (RMAA, secondary) | Whether your site loads quickly on a phone |

Scheduling is a separate decision. A small team that posts across several platforms can compare [Hootsuite and Sprout Social for small business](/blog/hootsuite-vs-sprout-social-small-business-local-business), though many salons and cafes can start with the native apps. Once leads arrive, booking software decides whether they become jobs; [Jobber and ServiceTitan](/blog/jobber-vs-servicetitan-small-business-local-business) differ mainly in team size and field-service needs.

## What does a first month look like?

The example below uses assumptions for a single-location salon in Tashkent. None of the inputs is a result.

- Assumption: a test budget of 1,000,000 UZS per month on one channel.
- Assumption: a target cost per booking of 100,000 UZS.
- Assumption: 2 hours per week for posts and message replies.
- Assumption: a question at checkout, "How did you find us?"

At the target cost, the budget needs 10 bookings to break even. If the month produces 4 bookings, the cost per booking is 250,000 UZS, so that channel needs a different offer or audience. After four weeks, replace the assumptions with real numbers and keep the channel that brings bookings.

## FAQ

### Which social network is biggest in Uzbekistan?

Among the sources here, Instagram has the most specific figure: more than 9 million users in December 2024, from a secondary agency blog. Telegram has far more channels, but the sources give no primary reach figure for it.

### Is Google or Yandex the main search engine?

The sources do not verify a share for Uzbekistan. Check results for your own service in Uzbek and Russian, and track which listings bring calls.

### How much does Telegram advertising cost?

No verified price appears in these sources. Ads are sold through Telegram's ad manager or agencies, and channel placements are negotiated directly. Request quotes and compare them with your target cost per booking.

### Can I run Google or Meta ads from Uzbekistan?

This guide does not confirm it. Check Google Ads and Meta billing and country support directly before you plan a budget.

### Should a small business start with Instagram or Telegram?

Start with the channel where your customers already reply. Test one channel for four weeks, record bookings, and add a second channel only after the first has a measured result.
