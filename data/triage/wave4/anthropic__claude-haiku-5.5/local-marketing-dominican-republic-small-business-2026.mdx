Local marketing for a Dominican Republic small business comes down to three channels: WhatsApp for direct customer contact, Facebook and Instagram for nearby reach, and Google Search with a Google Business Profile for people looking for a service close to them. A four-step plan covers most of it: fix the listing, pick one messaging channel, run a small search budget, and ask happy customers for reviews.

This article replaces an earlier draft. The source pack gathered for this rewrite held no Dominican Republic platform reach, advertising cost, or holiday data, so the earlier percentages, cost-per-click ranges and calendar dates were removed. Every figure left below is either a labelled assumption or is omitted.

## Which platforms should a Dominican business prioritise?

Platform choice should follow where your own customers already message you. Check your inbox, call log and direct-message history for one month and count which channel brings the most inquiries. That count is more useful than a national statistic copied from another market.

For most local service businesses, WhatsApp is the first channel to set up because it handles bookings, quotes and follow-ups in one thread. If appointments are scheduled through software rather than chat, compare options in [Jobber vs ServiceTitan for local businesses](/blog/jobber-vs-servicetitan-small-business-local-business). For a broader view of WhatsApp-led markets, the [Brazil WhatsApp and Meta guide](/blog/local-marketing-brazil-small-business-whatsapp-meta) shows how the same channel is used for customer messaging.

Facebook suits community groups and event announcements. Instagram and TikTok suit visual businesses such as salons, cafés and pet groomers that can show before-and-after work. Start with one social channel and add a second only after the first produces inquiries you can count. If posting volume grows, compare scheduling tools in [Hootsuite vs Sprout Social](/blog/hootsuite-vs-sprout-social-small-business-local-business) before paying for a tool.

## How should a Dominican business set up Google for local search?

Google Search is where people look for a service that is nearby and open now. Create or claim a Google Business Profile using the exact trading name, real opening hours, a full service list and photos of the actual premises. Choose the business category carefully, because it shapes which searches can show your listing.

Keyword wording matters. Write ad groups around the service plus the neighbourhood, in Spanish, using the place names customers actually use. A salon might target a specific district rather than the whole city. Keep the keyword list short, and review the search terms report weekly to remove queries that bring in people who will never book.

Track two things from the start: how many calls and direction requests the profile receives, and how many WhatsApp messages arrive from ads. These counts show whether the search budget is working before any revenue figure is available. Businesses that want help setting up and reviewing paid search can look at [Google Ads management](/services/google-ads).

The split itself is a planning choice. Some owners shift money toward content if the posts already attract enquiries, and shift it toward ads if the listing has few views.

## How should WhatsApp follow-up and reviews work in practice?

Reviews and messaging are low-cost parts of the plan that depend on routine rather than budget. The steps below turn them into a checklist you can run each week.

1. Claim or correct the Google Business Profile with real hours, services and photos.
2. Set one WhatsApp number for bookings and commit to a reply time you can keep every day.
3. Run a small search budget on named services and neighbourhoods, and review the search terms report weekly.
4. After each completed job, send the customer a WhatsApp message with a direct link to your Google review form.

Reviews are worth counting as a monthly metric. Record how many arrive, their average rating and how many mention a specific staff member or service. Reply to every review, positive or negative, within the same week. A short, polite reply to a critical review often matters more to a prospective customer than the review itself.

## How should a business plan around local calendars?

Holiday and festival dates vary by year and by city, and the source pack for this article did not verify any Dominican calendar. Build your own calendar from official government or municipal announcements each year, then add your peak periods from last year's booking records. Look at which weeks had the most inquiries and which had the fewest.

A common planning habit is to start a campaign one to two weeks before a known busy period, so the ads and listing have time to gather signals before demand peaks. Keep the budget for those weeks as a separate line in the monthly plan, so a strong period does not quietly drain the regular ad spend.

## FAQ

### Is Spanish or English better for Dominican Republic marketing?

Spanish suits most local customers. English may help businesses in tourist areas, but that choice should come from your own inquiry data. Test both on a small budget and keep the version that produces replies.

### Should a Dominican business run TikTok ads?

Only if the product photographs or demonstrates well and someone can post regularly. Start with organic posts, track which ones lead to inquiries, and add paid spend once one format consistently brings in enquiries.
