## Which WhatsApp AI Bot Option Fits a Small Business?

The three routes differ in cost, control and effort. The free app costs nothing beyond your phone plan, but it cannot answer open questions. Meta's Business Agent and third-party providers can, and they bill per message or through a platform fee. Choose by the questions customers actually send, not by the feature list.

| Option | Best for | What to check before starting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp Business app, greeting and away messages | Businesses with few messages and set hours | Whether the greeting covers hours, booking and how to reach a person |
| Meta Business Agent | Owners who want Meta's built-in assistant | Whether it is offered on your account and how it is billed |
| Third-party AI provider on the Business Platform | Businesses needing custom pricing, bookings or a CRM link | Provider fees, Meta message fees and who keeps answers current |

Start with the free option if the repeat questions are not yet known. A paid bot built on guesses will answer the wrong things quickly.

## What Should a WhatsApp Bot Be Allowed to Answer?

Limit the bot to facts that can be kept current. Hours, the price list, service area, parking and the booking link are good candidates. Anything that changes weekly, such as stock, special offers or staff availability, should go to a person or be checked by hand before the bot repeats it.

Write the ten questions customers ask most often, and mark each one as safe for the bot, needs a human, or needs a payment link. Keep that list in the same document as the price list, so one update changes both the website and the bot.

## How Do You Set Up a WhatsApp Bot Step by Step?

1. Write down the ten questions customers ask most, such as hours, prices, parking, booking and cancellation.
2. Set up the free app's greeting and away message first, then count how many questions still reach you.
3. Check whether Business Agent is available on your account, or whether a third-party provider is needed.
4. Load the bot only with facts that can be updated each week: price list, opening hours and service area.
5. Add a route to a person in every flow, such as a phone number or a "talk to a person" reply.
6. Read the first 50 real conversations, fix wrong answers, then repeat the check each month.

Step 5 matters most. A bot that leaves a customer with no way out loses the sale and the goodwill. Set the bot to hand over quickly when a message concerns a complaint, a refund or an urgent change.

## How Much Does a WhatsApp AI Bot Cost Per Reply?

Meta's pricing page gives Business Agent at $2.00 per million tokens, and estimates a typical message at 20,000 to 25,000 tokens. Using the midpoint of 22,500 tokens gives about 4.5 cents a reply. Meta's own page puts the typical figure at about 4 to 5 cents, which matches. Check the current page before budgeting, because rates change.

Third-party AI providers charge their own fees on top of Meta's message fees. Meta's pricing updates say that from October 1, 2026, each business phone number gets 1,000 free service messages a month, but this does not apply to AI providers. Ask the vendor how its fees are structured before signing.

## How Do You Keep a Human in the Loop?

Customers should always know how to reach a person. The bot can state this at the start of a chat and offer the route again whenever a customer types words such as "complaint", "refund" or "speak to someone". WhatsApp's business messaging policy requires automated responders to offer a prompt, clear and direct escalation path to a human, such as a phone number, email or an in-chat transfer.

Review the bot every month. Look for questions it answered wrongly, questions it could not answer, and conversations where a customer gave up. Each pattern points to one change in the answer list or the handover rule.

## What Should the Bot Never Collect?

Do not ask for card numbers in chat. Send a payment link from the booking or payment system instead, so card details never pass through the conversation. The same applies to passwords, ID numbers and health details. A bot that collects only names, preferred times and a phone number keeps the risk low.

The business messaging policy also limits outreach outside the 24-hour customer service window to approved templates, and it requires the customer's opt-in. Build the bot's follow-up messages around those limits. ## FAQ

### Is there a free WhatsApp AI bot?

The free WhatsApp Business app can send greeting and away messages without AI. An AI answer engine on your number usually carries a provider or platform charge, so check the current price before assuming it is free.

### Can I use ChatGPT on my WhatsApp Business number?

Check Meta's current business messaging terms before connecting a general-purpose assistant. A bot built for your own support and sales questions is a different case from an open-ended chat tool, and the rules can change.

### Do I have to tell customers it is a bot?

Saying plainly that a bot is answering is the safer choice. It also sets expectations, so customers know when to ask for a person.

### Can the bot take payments?

Do not take card details in chat. Send a payment link from the booking or payment system and let the customer pay there.

### How long does setup take?

The sources checked for this article give no setup time. It depends on how many questions there are and how quickly accurate answers can be supplied. Plan for the testing stage, when real conversations are read and errors fixed.
