CTV advertising in Chicago, IL lets a local business run video ads on streaming services such as Hulu and Peacock, with targeting by ZIP code and household. The sources checked for this guide cover Google Ads search and display benchmarks, not connected TV prices, so no CTV CPMs or platform minimums are quoted here. Where a number is an example, it is labelled as an assumption. Use the checklist and worked budget below to plan a test, then compare results against your own baseline.

## How do you set up a Chicago CTV test?

A small test comes before a larger budget. It answers three questions: does the ad reach the ZIP codes where customers live, does the creative hold attention, and does brand search rise during the flight.

1. List the ZIP codes, or a radius around your address, where most current customers live.
2. Pick one streaming inventory source to start. Compare minimums and reporting before buying.
3. Build one 15-second video with the logo in the first seconds, the core offer in the middle, and a clear call to action at the end.
4. Set a fixed test budget and a fixed flight length, such as four weeks.
5. Record a baseline first: branded search volume, Google Business Profile searches, and new customers who say how they heard about the business.
6. Review results at the end of the flight and decide whether to extend, change the creative, or stop.

Keep the test simple. Changing the creative, audience, and budget at the same time makes results impossible to read.

## What do the Google Ads benchmarks tell you about paid media costs?

The best-known public benchmark set is [WordStream's Google Ads industry data](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks). It reports an average search click-through rate of 3.17% and a display click-through rate of 0.46% across all industries. The average search cost per click is $2.69, and the display average is $0.63. These are search and display figures, not CTV figures, so they describe a different buying channel.

Costs vary widely by industry. In the same dataset, legal search clicks average $6.75 and consumer services average $6.40, while travel and hospitality average $1.53. A local business should compare against its own industry row, not the all-industry average.

## How should you split ZIP-code targeting in Chicago?

ZIP-code targeting is the main lever for local CTV. Chicago neighbourhoods differ a lot, so a business in one area may draw almost no customers from a suburb a short drive away. Rank ZIP codes by how many current customers they hold, using a customer list or delivery map.

Pick a radius the business can actually serve. A barbershop or pet groomer usually needs a tighter area than a multi-location fitness brand. Add demographic filters only after the geographic setup works, because each extra filter shrinks the audience and makes the test harder to judge.

Keep a separate control group if the platform allows it. Without a control, a rise in direct searches cannot be separated from ordinary seasonal demand. Chicago winters already push viewing indoors, so a test that runs in January needs a control more than one run in July.

## What does a worked CTV budget look like?

The example below uses assumed inputs, not sourced prices. Replace each assumption with the quote from the chosen platform before relying on the result.

Assumed monthly budget: $900. Assumed CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions): $25. Assumed frequency: 4 impressions per household per month.

Impressions = $900 ÷ $25 × 1,000 = 36,000 impressions per month (assumption-based).

Households reached ≈ 36,000 ÷ 4 = 9,000 households (assumption-based).

Cost per household reached ≈ $900 ÷ 9,000 = $0.10 (assumption-based).

If the actual CPM is higher, the same budget reaches fewer households, so the test should be resized before launch. The table below sets this example beside the sourced search figures so the two channels are not judged by the same yardstick.

## How do you measure whether CTV is working?

CTV mostly builds familiarity, so click-based reporting undersells it. Track the signals a viewer might act on later, and compare each week of the flight with the four weeks before it.

Google Business Profile searches for the business name are one useful signal. Branded clicks in Google Ads, if search campaigns run, are another. Ask new customers how they heard about the business, every time, and record the answers in the same format. Website direct traffic can also rise during the flight.

Give the test enough time to show a pattern. A four-week flight may be too short for a clear read on a local service business, so plan a second window before judging the channel. Results that look flat in week two may move once the household frequency builds.

## How does CTV fit with search and other paid channels?

CTV works best alongside search rather than instead of it. Television-style reach can raise awareness, and search captures the people who then look up the business name or category. A business that already runs Google Ads should check branded search before and after the CTV flight.

Other platforms can support the same plan. ## FAQ

### Does CTV advertising work for small businesses in Chicago?

It can, when the audience is limited to nearby ZIP codes and the goal is brand familiarity. The sources in this guide do not measure CTV results directly, so run a test with a baseline and judge it against your own numbers.

### What is a good budget to test CTV in Chicago?

No sourced CTV budget figure is available here. The worked example uses an assumed $900 monthly budget for illustration. Set the test budget from the platform's quoted minimum and a cost per 1,000 impressions that has been confirmed.

### How long should a CTV campaign run before judging it?

Plan at least one fixed flight, and compare it with the period before it. A four-week test is a reasonable starting point, with a longer window if results are unclear.

### Should a local business use CTV or Google Ads?

Most businesses that can afford both use them together. CTV builds awareness across households, and search captures people who then look up the business. The two channels answer different questions, so test each on its own terms.
