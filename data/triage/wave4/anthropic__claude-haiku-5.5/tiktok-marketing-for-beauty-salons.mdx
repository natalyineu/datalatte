## Does a Beauty Salon Need Paid Ads on TikTok?

No. A business account can post videos without spending anything. Organic posts reach followers and, sometimes, people who see the video in their feed. For a single salon, the first month is best spent learning which formats get watched to the end.

Paid ads are useful when you want reach in a specific area on a fixed schedule. TikTok Ads Manager sets minimum budgets for campaigns and ad groups, and the figures shown in your account are the ones to plan around. Check them before you set a budget, because they can change.

Treat any paid budget as a test with a clear end date. Decide in advance how many booking requests or paid visits would justify spending more, and stop if the test does not reach that level.

## Who Uses TikTok, and Does It Reach Local Clients?

Pew Research Center's 2026 survey of 5,511 US adults found that YouTube is used by 84%, Facebook by 70%, Instagram by 51% and TikTok by 37%. The survey was conducted between January 30 and June 18, 2026, with an overall margin of error of plus or minus 1.8 percentage points. These figures describe all US adults, not salon clients, and they do not show who lives near a given salon.

For a salon, that means TikTok is a real audience but not the only one. Many clients are on Instagram or Facebook, and some will search on Google before they book. Use the platform mix to decide where to spend time, rather than assuming one app covers everyone.

The Bureau of Labor Statistics notes that barbers, hairstylists and cosmetologists work mostly in barbershops or salons, often with evening and weekend hours. A short video that shows a stylist at work during those hours can connect the business to the people it serves. ## How Do You Target Nearby Clients on TikTok?

TikTok Ads Manager lets advertisers choose locations within a campaign. For a single salon, the practical unit is usually a city or district, because the goal is to reach people who can physically book a chair. Check the Location section of your own account to see which options your market offers, since they vary by country.

Location signals are imperfect. A viewer's location may be estimated from several sources, so some people who live nearby will be missed, and some who live further away may see the ad. Track this by asking new clients how they found you, not only by reading the ad dashboard.

Targeting also works on the content side. A video that names the neighbourhood, shows a local storefront or features a local stylist gives viewers a reason to act. If a platform's location controls are not precise enough for your market, put more effort into the content and the booking page instead.

## Is Trending Music Safe to Use in Salon Videos?

Trending sounds are a common way to get watched, but using them in business content can create licensing problems. Music that is licensed for personal accounts is not always licensed for promoting a business. Check TikTok's current guidance on commercial music before you post or boost a video that uses a popular track.

If a sound is not clearly cleared for business use, use original audio, your own voiceover or music from a library that allows commercial use. A salon video with a clear voice explaining a technique often works as well as a trending track, and it avoids the risk of a removed post.

## What Should a Salon Post on TikTok?

No source ranks which salon formats perform best on TikTok, so these are ideas to test, not proven winners:

- A short transformation, with the client's written permission
- One technique explained in under 30 seconds
- A stylist or technician introducing themselves and their specialism
- A quick look at a fully booked day, ending with the booking link
- A product in use, not only on a shelf

Post on a steady schedule for four weeks. Keep the three videos that held viewers longest and make more like them. For captions and scripts, [ChatGPT prompts for local business marketing](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-local-business-marketing) can speed up drafting, but check every claim about results and products before publishing. For influencer collaborations, see [how hair salons get free bookings from local creators](/blog/influencer-marketing-for-hair-salons-how-to-get-free-bookings-from-local-creator).

Meta's guidance on A/B testing is a useful model for comparing two videos or offers. Meta recommends using the same budget for both versions and splitting the audience evenly, and it warns against informal testing by turning ads on and off by hand, because that can produce unreliable results. The same discipline applies on TikTok: change one thing at a time and keep the spend equal.

## What Does a Small TikTok Test Cost?

The table below is a worked example. Every input is an **assumption** for illustration, not a benchmark. Replace them with your own numbers after a test.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly ad spend | $300 |
| Booking requests from ads | 10 |
| Cost per booking request | $30.00 |
| Requests that become paid visits | 60% (6 visits) |
| Cost per paid visit | $50.00 |
| Average first visit | $85 |
| Share who rebook once | 40% |

At these inputs, first visits bring in 6 × $85 = $510 against $300 of ads, so the test covers its cost before product or staff time. Rebookings add more: 40% of 6 is about 2.4 second visits, worth roughly $204. That brings total revenue to about $714, but it ignores product costs, staff hours and any clients who would have booked anyway.

Change the assumptions before deciding. If only 2 of 10 requests become visits, the cost per paid visit rises sharply and the test may not pay. Record the source of every booking, so you can see which assumption is wrong.

## FAQ

### Do beauty salons need paid ads on TikTok?

No. A business account can post for free. Paid ads add reach in a chosen area, and the minimum budgets shown in TikTok Ads Manager set the lowest spend for each campaign.

### How many people in the US use TikTok?

According to Pew Research Center's 2026 survey, 37% of US adults use TikTok. The survey covers all adults, so the share among salon clients in your area may be different.

### Can a salon target only people nearby?

You can choose locations in TikTok Ads Manager, usually at city or district level. Location signals are not perfect, so ask new clients how they found the salon to check results.

### Can I use any trending song in a salon video?

Not always. Music licensed for personal use may not be licensed for business promotion. Check TikTok's current guidance on commercial music before you boost a video.

### How do I know if a TikTok test is working?

Set a budget, a fixed end date and a target number of booking requests before you start. Compare cost per booking and rebooking rates against those targets, then decide whether to continue.
