Connected TV (CTV) advertising lets a small business place video ads on streaming services and smart TVs, and in the Netherlands it can work for local firms that target tightly and test with a capped budget. The sources behind this guide describe CTV in general terms and do not publish Dutch inventory, CPM or audience figures, so those numbers are left out. Where a calculation needs a number, it is labelled as an assumption. Use this guide to build a test plan, then confirm local prices and inventory with a vendor before committing budget.

## What counts as CTV advertising for a small business?

Connected TV refers to internet-connected devices such as smart TVs, gaming consoles and streaming sticks that carry video content. [IAS describes CTV advertising](https://www.internationaladvertisingsolutions.com/blog/connected-tv-advertising-everything-you-need-to-know/) as video ads delivered through these devices, with targeting based on viewer demographics and behaviour. [Streaming TV is often called OTT](https://www.internationaladvertisingsolutions.com/blog/connected-tv-advertising-everything-you-need-to-know/), which describes how content is delivered over the internet rather than by cable or satellite.

For a local business, the practical question is whether the audience sits inside your trading area. A café, salon or gym earns money from people who can walk or drive to it, so CTV only makes sense if the buy can be limited to the cities, postcodes or regions you serve. Ask vendors how geographic filtering works on each platform before you pay for impressions.

## How does CTV compare with Google Display for local businesses?

Google Display and CTV are separate buys. Display campaigns run across websites, apps, YouTube and Gmail, while CTV runs on the screen in the living room. Both can be targeted, but they are priced and measured differently, so they should not be judged by the same metric.

| Factor | Google Display | CTV |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reach | Websites and apps, plus YouTube and Gmail ([Google](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190)) | Streaming TV screens and devices ([IAS](https://www.internationaladvertisingsolutions.com/blog/connected-tv-advertising-everything-you-need-to-know/)) |
| Targeting | Optimized targeting is the default ([Google](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190)) | Demographics and behaviour, varies by platform ([IAS](https://www.internationaladvertisingsolutions.com/blog/connected-tv-advertising-everything-you-need-to-know/)) |
| Creative | Responsive assets, assembled by Google AI ([Google](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190)) | Interactive formats such as QR codes and shoppable ads ([Division-D](https://divisiond.com/interactive-ctv/)) |
| Bidding or buying | CPA and CPC bidding ([Google](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190)) | Programmatic buying, cost per thousand impressions (CPM) |
| Typical search-style cost | Search CPC benchmark only ([WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks)) | No sourced CPM here; get local quotes |

If your main goal is capturing people already searching, search or Display usually has clearer measurement. CTV is better used to build recall in a defined area before or alongside search. For many local firms, the [Google Ads service](/services/google-ads) is the stronger starting point, with CTV added once the search baseline is stable.

## What does a small CTV test budget look like?

The example below uses only assumed inputs. Replace each one with a quote from your vendor. Nothing in the sources confirms a Dutch CPM or household count.

Inputs (all assumption):
- Monthly budget: €1,000
- CPM: €25
- Target households in your postcode cluster: 20,000
- Campaign length: 1 month

Impressions = €1,000 ÷ €25 × 1,000 = 40,000 impressions.

Average frequency = 40,000 ÷ 20,000 = 2.0 impressions per household per month.

Two impressions per household is a light test, which is the point. It tells you whether the area responds before you spend more. The example does not assume any sales, visits or bookings, because those depend on your offer and tracking. Set a baseline for calls, bookings or walk-ins before the campaign starts so you have something to compare afterwards.

## How should a local business set up targeting and measurement?

Set up the campaign as a test with clear limits, not as an open-ended brand spend. The steps below follow the logic of the sources: define the audience geographically, track response, and compare against a baseline.

1. Draw the trading area on a map and list the postcodes or city districts inside it.
2. Choose one platform for the first test and set a monthly ceiling that you can afford to lose.
3. Write one message with one call to action, such as booking a visit or scanning a code.
4. Use a QR code or a trackable landing page so responses can be counted, as [Division-D describes QR codes](https://divisiond.com/interactive-ctv/) as a bridge from TV to phone.
5. Record a four-week baseline of bookings, calls or walk-ins before launch.
6. Compare results after four weeks and only scale if the baseline improves in the target area.

Be aware that CTV reporting can be stronger on engagement than on sales. Real-time insights are a vendor claim, so check which metrics your platform actually provides before relying on them.

## What consent and data questions should you ask first?

Privacy rules for targeted advertising differ by country and change over time, so this guide does not state them. Before buying, ask the vendor how they handle consent, which framework they use for consent signals, and whether their geographic targeting relies on device or IP data. Ask for the answers in writing.

Also ask whether the platform's audience data is first-party, aggregated or modelled. Vendors describe very different approaches, and the answer affects both compliance and how reliable your results will be.

## FAQ

### Is CTV advertising worth it for a very small local business?

It depends on whether your trading area can be targeted precisely and whether you can track responses. Start with a capped test and a baseline. If you cannot measure a response, the test will not tell you whether to continue.

### How is CTV different from Google Display?

Google Display covers websites, apps, YouTube and Gmail and is bought with bidding tools. CTV covers streaming screens and is usually bought per thousand impressions. The two are measured differently, so compare them against your own goals rather than one benchmark.

### Where can I find Dutch CTV prices?

The sources for this guide do not publish them. Request quotes from platforms and vendors active in the Netherlands, and use those figures in place of the assumptions in the worked example.
