## How Many US Adults Use TikTok Compared With Other Platforms?

Pew's 2026 survey of 5,511 US adults, [Americans' Use of Social Media in 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/fact-sheet/social-media/), puts YouTube at 84%, Facebook at 70%, Instagram at 51% and TikTok at 37%. TikTok's share was 33% in 2023 and 2024, then rose to 37% in 2025 and 2026, so the trend is upward.

For a salon, this means TikTok reaches fewer adults than Instagram or Facebook. A smaller pool makes cost per booking more important than reach. A cheap view count tells you little if nobody books.

## Should a Salon Post Organically Before Paying for Ads?

Post first. Ads amplify what already works, so find which videos earn saves, comments and booking questions before paying to show them. If you have only a few videos and a thin Google profile, fix those first. A viewer who searches your salon name after seeing an ad should find recent reviews, current hours and a booking link.

## How Do You Set Up a Booking Campaign?

Choose the Lead generation objective. Keep the form short, asking for name, phone number and the service wanted. Every extra question gives a person another reason to stop, so ask only what you need to book the appointment.

Phone each lead the same day. A slow reply wastes the ad spend, because someone who asked for a booking often books elsewhere within hours. If you need qualifying questions, send people to your own booking page instead, and track which route produces more completed bookings.

## How Do You Test Without Wasting Budget?

Meta's guidance on A/B testing is a useful model even though it covers Meta ads, not TikTok. Meta says to use the same budget for both versions of a test, and it advises against informal testing, such as switching ad sets on and off by hand, because that can create overlapping audiences and unreliable results. Change one variable at a time, such as the video hook or the service shown, and keep the budget equal across versions.

Test creative before audiences. Reuse your best organic videos with new opening lines, then compare cost per booking across the versions. Give each version enough days and bookings to judge fairly before changing anything else.

## How Do You Target Local Customers?

Target by city or postcode, and check which location options your ad account offers in your country before building the campaign. Start with a broad audience inside your area, then narrow it once you have results. Local search visibility matters too, so pair paid tests with a clear profile. | Cost per booking ($620 ÷ 10) | $62 |
| Average first ticket | $85 |
| First-visit revenue (10 × $85) | $850 |

At these inputs, first-visit revenue covers the ad cost, and repeat visits are profit. Now lower the bookings. If the cost per form rises to $40, only about 16 forms are bought and roughly 6 bookings follow. Cost per booking becomes about $103, and first-visit revenue of $510 falls short of the $620 spent. The lesson is to track how many forms you actually reach, not just how many you buy.

## What Steps Should You Follow Before Spending?

1. Check your Google Business Profile for current hours, services and a booking link.
2. Post three to five short videos organically and note which ones draw booking questions.
3. Set one daily budget you can sustain for the full test, and confirm the current minimum in Ads Manager.
4. Build one campaign with the Lead generation objective and a short instant form.
5. Assign one person to call every lead the same day, and log each booking against its source video.
6. Judge the test on cost per booking after the full month, then change one variable for the next test.

## What Should the Video Show?

Show the service and the result in the first few seconds, such as a finished style or a consultation. Name the service and your area on screen, keep one idea per video, and end with a clear booking action.

If you show before-and-after images, get written permission from every client whose face or hair appears. Do not use images that misrepresent the result.

## FAQ

### How much do TikTok ads cost for a hair salon?

TikTok sets minimum budgets, but the sources used for this article do not confirm the current figures. Check the minimums in Ads Manager, then set a test budget you can sustain for a full month. The worked example assumes $20 a day for 31 days.

### Are TikTok ads better than Instagram ads for salons?

No verified comparison exists for salons in these sources. Pew's 2026 survey shows Instagram reaches 51% of US adults and TikTok 37%, so Instagram reaches more people. Test both with equal budgets and compare cost per booking.

### Can I target only people near my salon?

You can target by city or postcode, but check which location options your ad account offers in your country. Do not assume a radius setting exists.

### How long before I see results?

Allow the full test month before judging. Early results can mislead, so compare cost per booking at the end rather than after the first week.

### Should I use TikTok ads or post organically first?

Post organically first. Ads amplify what already works, so identify your best-performing videos before paying to promote them.
