## Can Small Businesses in South Africa Buy Streaming TV Ads?

The sources checked do not settle this. Google's Display Network help page describes Display campaigns reaching people across 35 million websites and apps, plus Google-owned properties such as YouTube and Gmail. It says Display campaigns use AI for targeting, bidding and ad formats.

The page does not name a TV-screen placement, and it says nothing about eligibility by country. The answer therefore depends on the advertiser's own account. Open a new Display or Demand Gen campaign in Google Ads, check whether a TV or connected-screen option appears under placements, and record exactly what the screen shows.

If no such option appears, the inventory should not be assumed to be available. Ask Google support in writing. Streaming inventory from other sellers should be checked the same way, starting with the placement list, the audience source and the minimum spend.

## What Do Benchmarks Tell a Local Business About Budgets?

WordStream's industry benchmark is a vendor publication, not South African data. It reports an all-industry average search cost per click of $2.69 and a display cost per click of $0.63. Average click-through rate is 3.17% for search and 0.46% for display. These figures are in US dollars and reflect the vendor's own client sample.

Display clicks are cheaper but far less frequent in these figures. That pattern is a reason for caution with any new channel, because a low cost per impression is not the same as a lead. Connected TV is also not the same as Display, so these numbers describe channel behaviour in general, not streaming screens in South Africa.

For a local business, the more useful question is whether a channel produces calls, bookings or form fills at a cost the business can afford. Benchmarks can set a starting expectation, but a business's own search results give the better comparison.

The table below compares the three routes a South African small business is most likely to consider. Only the first two have any figure in the sources checked, and even those are not South African.

The table makes one gap clear. Search has the most usable benchmark, and streaming TV has none in the sources, so a test has to be judged against the business's own search results.

## How Should a Small Business Budget a Streaming TV Test?

The inputs below are **assumptions**, not South African prices or quotes.

- Monthly marketing budget (assumption): R5,000
- Share for a streaming TV test (assumption): 20%, so R1,000 per month
- Share for Google Search and a Business Profile (assumption): 80%, so R4,000 per month
- Search leads per month (assumption): 10, so about R400 per lead from search
- Goal (assumption): tracked phone calls or bookings from a dedicated page or number

If a seller's minimum spend is above R1,000 per month, the test does not fit this budget. In that case, keep spending on search this quarter and revisit streaming TV once search has produced a stable cost per lead.

A simple test plan follows these steps:

1. Get a written quote and minimum spend from each seller before committing.
2. Confirm whether the placement exists in the Google Ads account, and save a screenshot of the setup screen.
3. Create a separate landing page or tracking phone number for the test.
4. Run the test for at least one quarter (assumption), so that results do not rest on a few days of data.
5. Compare cost per lead with search. If streaming TV does not beat search on cost per lead, stop.

## When Is Streaming TV Worth Testing?

Streaming TV is usually a second-stage channel. Search captures people who already want what a business sells, and a Business Profile gives local customers a way to call or visit. Once those two channels produce a known cost per lead, a small test of a new channel can be judged against a real number.

## FAQ

### Is CTV advertising available in South Africa?

The sources checked do not confirm it. Check the Google Ads account for a TV-screen or connected-screen placement, and ask any other seller for a written placement list.

### How much does CTV advertising cost in South Africa?

No South African price data was found in the sources checked, so no price is quoted here. Ask each seller for a written quote and minimum spend.

### What are typical Display costs to compare against?

WordStream's all-industry average is $0.63 per display click and a 0.46% click-through rate. These are US-dollar vendor figures and do not describe streaming TV.

### Should a small business start with CTV?

Usually not. Start with Google Search and a Business Profile, then test streaming TV with a small share of the budget once the cost per lead is known.
