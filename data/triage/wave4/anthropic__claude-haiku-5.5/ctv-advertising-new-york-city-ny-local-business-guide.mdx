CTV advertising in New York City lets local businesses run short video ads on streaming services such as Hulu, Peacock and Amazon Prime Video, aimed at households in the ZIP codes where their customers live. The ads appear on the TV screen rather than a phone. The sources behind this guide do not include New York City CTV rates, so every budget figure below is a labelled assumption to replace with real quotes. The practical plan is to choose a few ZIP codes near the shop, run a four-week trial, measure branded search and direct visits against a baseline, then decide whether to scale.

## How does CTV differ from the display ads Google sells?

Connected TV ads run as full-screen streaming video. Google's Display Network places banner, image and video ads across websites, apps and Google-owned properties. Google describes the network as reaching people "across 35 million websites and apps, and on Google-owned properties (YouTube and Gmail)" ([Google Ads Help, primary](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190)). That reach figure helps with online planning, but it does not price CTV inventory.

## Which streaming platforms should New York City businesses consider?

Several platforms sell ad inventory to local advertisers, including Hulu, Peacock, Tubi, Amazon Prime Video and Paramount+. Each has its own ad manager, audience mix and rates. This guide does not publish sourced figures for any of them. Before committing, check each platform's current self-serve page for minimum spend, CPM and geographic targeting options.

Platform choice should follow audience fit rather than brand familiarity. A fitness studio near Park Slope and a family salon in Queens may need different viewing audiences, even inside the same city. Ask each platform whether it targets by ZIP code, because borough-level and neighbourhood-level targeting are not interchangeable in a city this dense.

## How should you target New York City ZIP codes?

Start with the customer list. Pull the ZIP codes of the last 100 customers, or the postcodes on booking forms, and count how many fall within a 2 to 5 mile radius of the business. That count shows whether a radius or a hand-picked list fits better. If customers come from several boroughs, a radius around one storefront will miss many of them.

Keep demographic layers simple. Age and household filters narrow reach, and each extra filter lowers the number of households available to buy. For most local shops, one demographic layer is enough. Testing different creative is usually a more useful use of the budget than stacking filters.

## What does a New York City CTV budget look like?

No sourced figure in this guide gives New York City CTV costs, so the example below uses assumptions throughout. Replace each one with the number from the platform's quote before approving spend.

The inputs for a four-week trial are all assumptions: a trial budget of $400, an effective CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) of $30, and an average frequency of 3 views per household over the four weeks. Impressions equal $400 ÷ $30 × 1,000, or about 13,333. Dividing by 3 views gives about 4,444 households. If the quoted CPM is $45, the same budget buys about 8,889 impressions and reaches about 2,963 households, roughly a third fewer. The table below runs the same sum across four scenarios.

| Scenario (all inputs are assumptions) | Budget | CPM | Impressions | Households at 3 views |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base trial | $400 | $30 | 13,333 | 4,444 |
| Higher CPM | $400 | $45 | 8,889 | 2,963 |
| Larger trial | $600 | $30 | 20,000 | 6,667 |
| Larger trial, higher CPM | $600 | $45 | 13,333 | 4,444 |

The trial should run long enough to show a pattern. Plan for four weeks, record a baseline for the four weeks before launch, and compare the two periods. A single week of data is too noisy to act on.

## How do you measure CTV results?

CTV builds familiarity, so the first signals are indirect. Track branded searches for the business name in Google Search Console or Google Ads, direct visits to the website, and Google Business Profile views during the flight. Compare each figure with the baseline period, and set aside weeks when another factor, such as a holiday, changed demand.

Add one question to every new-customer form: how did you hear about us? Record the answers weekly. This gives a low-cost check that does not need attribution software. Expect the effect to build over weeks rather than appear on day one.

<Callout type="tip">
Keep the CTV flight and the search campaign on the same calendar dates. If search ads are paused during the trial, branded searches will not show the same lift, and the comparison will mislead.
</Callout>

## Where do CTV and search advertising fit together?

CTV and search address different parts of the same purchase. A viewer who sees a short TV spot may later search for the business by name or by service. Search ads capture that intent when it happens, so a business with a search campaign already running is better placed to use CTV. Before the flight starts, confirm that branded keywords and the landing page are ready.

## FAQ

### Is CTV advertising worth it for a small New York City business?

It depends on whether customers live within a few ZIP codes and whether the budget covers a trial. A four-week test with a measured baseline is the most reliable way to judge fit before committing to a longer flight. The sourced data here gives no New York City return figure.

### What is the minimum budget to start?

This guide has no sourced minimum for any platform. Check each platform's self-serve page for the current minimum spend, then use the quoted CPM to calculate reach, as in the worked example above.

### How long before CTV shows results?

Plan for at least four weeks of flight and compare results with a matching baseline period. Brand effects build slowly, so one week of data is rarely enough to act on.

### Does CTV need separate creative?

Yes. CTV ads are video with sound on and full-screen viewing. Use 16:9 landscape, keep the message short, and include the business name, one clear benefit and a next step. Check the platform's current file specifications before production, since requirements change.

### Should a business choose CTV or Google Ads?

Search suits people already looking for a service. CTV suits building recognition in nearby neighbourhoods. Many small businesses test both at a modest scale. A [free audit](/free-audit) can compare the options against current numbers.
