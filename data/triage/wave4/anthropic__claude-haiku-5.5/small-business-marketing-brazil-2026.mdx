## Which Brazil marketing benchmarks can you trust?

Most published figures for Brazil come from tool vendors or unofficial estimates without a stated method, so they are hard to verify. The US figures above are useful for one reason: they show which metrics matter. [M.Wolf Media's 2026 guide](https://mwolfmedia.com/how-much-do-meta-ads-cost-small-business-2026/) separates cost per mille, cost per click, cost per lead and cost per booked customer, and argues that the last one decides whether a campaign is profitable.

[Adverge Media's 2026 benchmarks](https://advergemedia.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks-2026) report local lead-generation click-through rates of 0.7 to 1.2 percent, against 2.3 to 2.7 percent for ecommerce. Local services should judge campaigns on cost per booked customer, not click volume. Treat both sets as a starting hypothesis for a Brazilian account, not a forecast.

## How do you calculate a realistic Meta Ads budget?

The simplest test is cost per acquired customer. Divide monthly ad spend by the number of paying customers it produces, then compare that figure with what one customer is worth to the business.

At a one-in-five close rate the campaign is profitable, but only narrowly. If the close rate falls to one in ten, cost per customer doubles and the campaign loses money.

## What should a Brazilian business do on Google?

Google Ads and Google Business Profile should both target the neighbourhood where customers live, not only the city. Write the profile description and ad copy in Portuguese, and name the bairro explicitly. Search patterns vary by city, so check local keyword volumes in Google Keyword Planner before choosing terms.

Ask customers which payment and booking methods they actually use, then show those on the landing page. A simple test is to run two versions of the same page, one with payment details and one without, and compare booking rates over two weeks. Neither version should be assumed to win in advance.

Reply to reviews in Portuguese, using a personal tone rather than a template. A short, specific reply is quick to write and signals an active business. Track whether reply rate affects calls or direction requests. If it does not, adjust the time spent on it.

## Should Brazilian businesses run Meta Ads or Google Ads first?

For most local services, start with the channel customers already use to research. If people discover the business on Instagram, begin with a Meta campaign. If they search for a service when they need it, begin with Google. Running both at once with a small budget splits the data and makes results hard to read.

Use WhatsApp click-to-chat or a booking link as the call to action, and test it against a lead form. Compare cost per booked customer, not cost per message, because a cheap conversation that never books is not a win. Once results are clear, one channel usually carries most of the budget and the other supports it.

Creative needs regular refreshing. [M.Wolf Media reports](https://mwolfmedia.com/how-much-do-meta-ads-cost-small-business-2026/) that top creative can lose effectiveness after two to three weeks. Plan new short videos or photos each month, and state the offer clearly in the first seconds.

## How do you run WhatsApp, booking and follow-up?

WhatsApp can handle enquiries, bookings and reminders in one place, but only if someone answers quickly. Assign one person to check the inbox during opening hours, and set a response standard that can be measured, such as replying within one hour.

For service businesses with scheduling, a dedicated tool may be easier than managing everything by hand. The [Jobber and ServiceTitan comparison](/blog/jobber-vs-servicetitan-small-business-local-business) covers the trade-offs for field service teams. If content runs across Instagram and Facebook, a scheduler such as [Hootsuite or Sprout Social](/blog/hootsuite-vs-sprout-social-small-business-local-business) can keep posting consistent without daily effort.

## What are the most common mistakes?

The most common mistake is measuring activity instead of outcomes. Impressions, likes and message counts feel like progress but do not show whether a customer booked. Set one outcome metric per channel before launch.

The second is inconsistent information. If the Instagram bio, WhatsApp profile and Google Business Profile show different hours or addresses, customers hesitate. Check all three each month.

The third is changing too many things at once. Change one variable per test, such as the offer or the call to action, and keep everything else fixed for at least two weeks.

## What should you do in the next 30 days?

1. Week 1: Align hours, address and contact details across Google Business Profile, Instagram and WhatsApp. Set up WhatsApp Business with services or products listed.
2. Week 2: Launch a Portuguese Google campaign targeting the bairro and nearby areas. Record cost per click and calls or bookings from day one.
3. Week 3: Run a short-video Meta campaign with WhatsApp as the call to action. Compare it against a lead form for one week.
4. Week 4: Answer every outstanding review and comment, then calculate cost per booked customer for the month.

## FAQ

### How much should a small business spend on Meta Ads in Brazil?

No Brazil-specific budget benchmark was found in the sources reviewed. A US agency suggests about $1,500 per month as a minimum for the algorithm to learn reliably. Start there in a test, then scale based on cost per booked customer.

### Is Google Ads worth it for a local business in Brazil?

It depends on whether customers search for the service when they need it. Measure calls, bookings and direction requests for two to four weeks before deciding. Local search ads work best when the bairro and service are specific.

### Should a business use WhatsApp or a contact form?

Test both. Run one version with WhatsApp click-to-chat and one with a form, using the same offer, and compare cost per booked customer. Neither option can be assumed to win for every niche.

### Do I need a website if my business is mainly on Instagram?

A website helps with search visibility and trust, but Instagram can work as the primary presence for some businesses. Whichever is used, keep hours, contact details and offers identical everywhere.
