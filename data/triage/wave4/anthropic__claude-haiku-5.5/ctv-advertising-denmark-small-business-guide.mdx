## What do the benchmarks tell a Danish advertiser?

The best-known public benchmarks come from Google Ads search and display, not CTV. [WordStream's industry benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks) put the all-industry average search click-through rate at 3.17% and the display rate at 0.46%, with an average search cost per click of $2.69. The same report lists legal services at $6.75 average CPC, and dating and personals at a 6.05% average search CTR.

These figures are dated, come from a US-focused dataset, and measure clicks rather than household viewing. They are useful for sense-checking a Google Ads budget in Denmark. They should not be used as a CTV price estimate.

## How big is the Google Display Network compared with TV?

Google's documentation describes Display campaigns as reaching people across [35 million websites and apps](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190), plus Google-owned properties such as YouTube and Gmail. That figure covers the Display Network as a whole. It does not describe streaming TV inventory in Denmark, and it should not be read as a count of Danish households.

For a small business, the practical point is that reach depends on the platform used to buy it. Before spending on CTV, confirm which Danish streaming apps the buying platform can access and what audience data it supports. A broad reach number on a display network says little about how many local viewers a specific campaign will reach.

## What does a 6-week CTV test budget look like?

The table below shows how a local coffee shop could translate a test budget into impressions. Every input is an assumption, so replace them with the quotes received from the platform.

| Input | Value | Label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Test budget | 10,000 kr | Assumption |
| Test length | 6 weeks | Assumption |
| CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) | 150 kr | Assumption |
| Total impressions | About 66,700 | Calculated: 10,000 ÷ 150 × 1,000 |
| Impressions per week | About 11,100 | Calculated: 66,700 ÷ 6 |
| Target area | One city district | Assumption |

If the platform quotes a CPM of 220 kr instead, the same budget buys about 45,450 impressions. That gap is why the first step is a written quote from the platform, not a benchmark taken from another country. ## Which pre-launch checks reduce wasted spend?

Three checks cover most of the risk in a first campaign. First, ask the platform to list the Danish streaming apps in its inventory and their targeting options, and avoid buying packages without that detail. Second, set a frequency cap so one household does not see the same ad many times a week, which wastes budget without adding customers.

Third, agree on one measurable goal before the campaign starts, such as store visits tracked through a promotion code. Without a defined goal, a 6-week test cannot show whether the budget worked.

On creative and claims, check the rules for pricing statements and health or weight-loss claims with the relevant Danish regulator before launch. The sources used for this guide do not cover those rules, so they are not summarised here.

## How should the test be measured after launch?

Measurement works best as a short sequence. Each step is a decision point, and the test should not continue past step four without a result.

1. Record the baseline: weekly store visits or enquiries for the four weeks before launch.
2. Log the quoted CPM, the total spend and the impressions delivered each week.
3. Track the promotion code or booking link named in the creative, so responses can be linked to the campaign.
4. Compare the test period with the baseline, and with the benchmark CPC for search over the same weeks if Google Ads runs alongside.
5. Decide whether to scale, change the creative, or stop, based on the goal agreed before launch.

The comparison in step four is a rough guide, not a controlled experiment. Local demand shifts with seasons and weather, so note any unusual weeks in the log.

## How does CTV compare with search and social ads?

CTV is best understood as an awareness channel. It puts a video ad in a household setting, while search and social ads capture people who are already looking or who match an audience profile. Many local advertisers pair the two, using CTV to build recognition and [Google Ads](/services/google-ads) to catch people who search for the service.

Advertisers comparing how the same channel works in other countries can read the [Germany CTV guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-germany-small-business-guide) and the [Ireland CTV guide](/blog/ctv-advertising-ireland-small-business-guide). Each market has its own inventory and pricing, so the Danish quote should still be checked directly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does CTV advertising cost in Denmark?

This guide does not have a sourced Denmark CTV price. The worked example uses an assumed 150 kr CPM to show the arithmetic. Get a written quote from the platform, because CPMs vary by app, audience and targeting.

### Is CTV better than Google Ads for a local business?

They do different jobs. CTV builds awareness through video in a household setting, while Google Ads captures people searching for a service. Many small businesses test both, starting with the channel that fits their budget and goal.

### How long should a first CTV test run?

A 6-week test, as in the worked example, gives enough time to see whether store visits or enquiries move. Shorter tests are harder to read, and longer ones lock in budget before any results are available.

### Do I need Danish-language creative?

The sources used here do not address this. Test a Danish-language version against the current creative if possible, and compare results using the same platform and targeting setup.
