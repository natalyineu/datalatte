## What Verified Figures Are Available for This Guide?

The only verified sector data available for this rewrite comes from the US Bureau of Labor Statistics. Its page on barbers, hairstylists and cosmetologists reports a 2025 median pay of $35,880 and 670,800 jobs. Projected growth is 7% from 2025 to 2035, which the BLS describes as much faster than the average for all occupations ([BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/personal-care-and-service/barbers-hairstylists-and-cosmetologists.htm)).

These are US figures for one trade. They show that personal services are a sizeable, growing employment area. They do not show how Spanish customers search. Before setting targets, check the national statistics office for Spain and a current digital report covering Spain.

## Why Start With Google Business Profile?

Most local customers look for a business on Google Maps or in local results before they visit a website. A complete profile is therefore the base of local marketing, and it costs nothing to set up. Fill in the business category, services, photos, address, phone number and opening hours before spending on anything else.

Opening hours need extra care where businesses close in the middle of the day or for local fiestas. Set separate hours for a midday break if the profile tool allows it, and add special hours for August closures or regional holidays. Local dates vary by town, so check them each year.

Reviews matter as well. Reply to every review in Spanish, including negative ones, and keep each reply short and factual. For a comparison of how local listings work in another EU market, see the [Poland local business marketing guide](/blog/local-business-marketing-poland-guide).

## How Should a Business Set Up WhatsApp Business?

WhatsApp Business is a separate app with tools for small businesses. Setting it up in a fixed order helps customers get clear answers from the first message:

1. Complete the business profile: description, address, hours, email and website.
2. Write a greeting and an away message that states when you will reply.
3. Save several quick replies covering prices, hours, location, booking and parking.
4. Add a catalogue with your top five to ten services or products.
5. Use labels such as New enquiry, Pending information and Follow up, and review them daily.

Show the number on your website, Instagram bio and Google profile. Before sending promotional messages, check the consent rules that apply in Spain, and message only people who have agreed to hear from you.

## What Language and Tone Should Be Used?

Write in Spanish for a Spanish audience, using a friendly, direct tone that suits your sector. In regions with a second official language, such as Catalonia or the Basque Country, ask customers which language they prefer. Test both versions in ads before committing to one.

No source reviewed for this rewrite measures which language converts better in Spain, so any claim that one language wins should be treated as unproven. Your own enquiry data is the most reliable test.

## How Much Should a Small Business Spend on Ads in Spain?

Published Spanish ad prices were not available in the sources used here, so the split below is a planning exercise. Every input is an assumption to replace with your own numbers after a week of tests.

| Item (assumption) | Monthly share | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Ads for local searches | 40% | 120 EUR |
| Meta Ads on Instagram and Facebook | 30% | 90 EUR |
| Photos and short video | 20% | 60 EUR |
| Tools such as scheduling and email | 10% | 30 EUR |
| Total | 100% | 300 EUR |

At this size, run one Search campaign on two or three local keywords and one Instagram campaign. Judge both by enquiries rather than clicks, counting WhatsApp chats, calls and bookings from each source. Raise the budget only for the channel that brings paying customers at a cost the business can sustain.

## Does Spain Need Different Marketing by Region?

In practice, yes, although no source reviewed here quantifies the difference. Competition, language and seasonality vary between large cities, inland towns and coastal areas. A beach town with many visitors may need English listings and reviews alongside Spanish, while a neighbourhood business may gain more from local content and partnerships.

Test with small budgets in each city before scaling. ## FAQ

### What is the first thing a Spanish small business should do online?

Complete the Google Business Profile with accurate hours, photos and services. Then set up WhatsApp Business with a greeting, quick replies and a short service catalogue.

### Is Instagram or Facebook better for a small business in Spain?

This depends on your customers and cannot be answered from the sources used here. Run a small test on one platform, track enquiries for four weeks, and compare results before adding a second platform.

### How much should I budget for ads in Spain?

The worked example above uses 300 EUR per month as an assumption. Replace the figures with real click costs from your own keyword research, and test with a small budget first.

### Do I need a website to start?

Not necessarily. A complete Google profile and a working WhatsApp Business account can bring first enquiries. A simple website adds trust, gives a place to show services, and helps track where leads come from.

### Should I write in Catalan or Basque?

Ask your own customers and test both versions in small campaigns. The sources used here do not prove that either language converts better.
