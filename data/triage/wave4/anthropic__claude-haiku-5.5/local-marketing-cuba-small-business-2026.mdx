## How Large Is the Market for a Small Business?

Cuba has about 10 million inhabitants and is the third most populous country in the Caribbean, according to [Chandrawat & Partners, a consultancy](https://chandrawatpartners.com/where-we-can-help/doing-business-in-cuba/), which is a secondary source. The same page says the economy is dominated by tourism and exports of skilled labour, sugar, tobacco and coffee.

Private businesses have grown quickly. The same source reports over 7,000 new private businesses opening since 2021, after the government began legalising micro, small and medium enterprises. For a local business, a young private sector means many customers are still learning which firms exist, so a clear listing with location, hours and contact details has real value.

## What Limits Paid Advertising in Cuba?

Platform access is the first question. The sources reviewed for this guide did not confirm whether Google Ads or Meta Ads can be bought or targeted for customers in Cuba. Official country-restriction pages from each platform should be read before any money is spent.

Sanctions rules can change, and they can apply to the business owner as well as the audience. A foreign company selling to Cuba should take legal advice before relying on any ad account. Treat paid media as an unknown until a platform's current policy says otherwise.

## How Do Foreign Businesses Enter the Market?

For outsiders, the structure of a business affects how it can market itself. The consultancy source notes that foreign investors usually form joint ventures with state-owned enterprises, particularly in tourism, mining and oil. Private MSMEs of up to 100 employees, often set up as limited liability companies (SRLs), are another option described in the same source.

Marketing plans should match the legal vehicle. A joint venture may need its partner's approval for public messaging, while a local MSME owner controls its own profile. Confirm this with a Cuban adviser before launching campaigns.

## What Does a Content Plan Cost in Time?

The figures below are **assumptions** for illustration, not sourced data. Replace them with your own numbers.

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Posts per week (assumption) | 4 |
| Minutes to make one post (assumption) | 20 |
| Minutes spent answering messages per day (assumption) | 30 |
| Weeks per month (assumption) | 4 |

Posting time comes to 4 posts × 20 minutes = 80 minutes a week, or about 5.3 hours a month. Message replies come to 30 minutes × 30 days = 15 hours a month. Total work is about 20 hours a month. Cutting posts to two a week halves the posting time but leaves replies unchanged, so response speed usually matters more than post volume.

## How Should Prices Show Tax?

Sales tax is 10% on retail and services, and 2% on wholesale, according to the [Chandrawat & Partners overview](https://chandrawatpartners.com/where-we-can-help/doing-business-in-cuba/) (secondary). Price lists should state clearly whether tax is included. Customers compare prices quickly in chat, and an unclear total leads to repeated questions.

Social security and labour contributions are often around 14%, according to the same source. A business owner should check the exact rate with a local accountant, as the source describes it as approximate.

## What Should a Small Business Do First?

Start with a plan that needs little data and no paid media:

1. Write one profile on your main messaging app with products, prices, hours and the area you serve.
2. Put price lists in text, not image-only posts, so they load on small data plans.
3. Keep one phone number for orders and reply the same day.
4. Save the most common customer questions and turn them into fixed replies.
5. Ask satisfied customers to share your profile with friends.

## FAQ

### Can a Cuban business run Google Ads?

The sources reviewed for this guide did not confirm this. Check Google's country-restriction help page and your own account settings before planning any budget.

### Can a foreign company target customers in Cuba with ads?

This could not be verified from the sources. Read each platform's current advertising policy and seek legal advice on sanctions before spending.

### How many private businesses are there in Cuba?

The consultancy source reports over 7,000 new private businesses opening since 2021. This is an approximate figure, so use official statistics for an exact count.

### Do I need a joint venture to operate in Cuba?

Foreign investors usually form joint ventures with state-owned enterprises, particularly in tourism, mining and oil. Private MSMEs are another option described by the same source. Confirm the right structure with a Cuban adviser.

### What is the cheapest way to start marketing in Cuba?

Create a complete profile on the messaging app your customers already use, with text prices and compressed images. Its only cost is your time.
