## Why Test Facebook Before Spending Big?

Facebook is a sensible first paid channel for many small businesses, but the sources checked did not confirm how many Namibians it reaches or what an ad costs there. Ads Manager shows an estimated audience when a location is set, so the estimate for Namibia should be checked before any budget is committed.

A first campaign works best as a test rather than a promise. Set a fixed budget for 30 days, pick one offer, and send every click to a page that makes it easy to call, message or book. Such a test shows whether the audience responds, even when no outside benchmark exists for the market.

## How Should Visitor-Facing Businesses Think About Tourists?

A lodge, guide or restaurant near a park serves two audiences: visitors who plan ahead and residents who book locally. Keeping them separate in ads usually helps. A visitor audience may need a booking link and a clear location, while a local audience may respond better to a daily offer or a phone number.

Visitor enquiries also tend to arrive through several routes at once, so each route needs its own tracking. A booking form, a phone call and a chat message should each be logged with the source, so the business can see which channel produced which booking. ## What Should a Google Profile Include First?

A Google Business Profile is the base layer for local visibility, because it is where customers check location, hours and reviews before they call or visit. Fill in every field that applies: the exact address, opening hours including holiday changes, the phone number, the website, and the services offered. Incomplete profiles leave customers guessing, and guessing usually means a lost enquiry.

Add clear photos of the premises, the product or the team, and keep them current. Reply to each review, positive or negative, in a short and factual way. Visitors often read reviews before they book, so a business that answers reviews gives them a reason to choose it over a profile that never responds.

## How Do You Run a 30-Day Facebook Test?

Follow these steps in order. Each can be completed in an afternoon.

1. Set the ad location to Namibia and check the audience estimate in Ads Manager.
2. Choose one offer, such as a discounted night or a free consultation.
3. Set a fixed budget and do not change it mid-test.
4. Send traffic to a page with a phone number, booking link or chat button.
5. Ask every enquirer how they found the business and record the answer.
6. At day 30, divide total spend by bookings to get the cost per booking.

Keep the test small. A budget the business can afford to lose is the right size for a first run, because the goal is to learn which offer and audience respond, not to maximise reach in the first month.

## Worked Example: A Guesthouse Facebook Test

| Item | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Test budget, 30 days | $150 |
| Enquiries from the ad | 10 |
| Share of enquiries that book | 20% |
| Bookings | 2 |
| Average booking margin | $120 |

Two bookings at a $120 margin give $240 in margin against $150 spend, a gain of $90. If only one enquiry in ten books, the test produces one booking, which is $120 in margin, or $30 less than the cost of the campaign. The test should be repeated only when the margin from a booking covers the cost per booking with room to spare.

## Where Does WhatsApp Fit?

WhatsApp is often the easiest way for a customer to ask a question before booking, so a WhatsApp number should appear on the ad landing page.
