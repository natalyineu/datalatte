This guide walks through a worked example using an assumed EGP 10,000 monthly budget, shows how to compare cost per message across channels, and lists the data-protection checks to make before bulk messaging. ## Which Channels Should an Egyptian Shop Test First?

Start with the channels customers already use to find a business like yours. For most local shops, that means a Facebook page, a Google Business Profile for map searches and one short-video channel. Pick two to begin with, because each extra channel adds creative work and reporting time.

Write down the reason for each choice. Note where customers already send messages, where they search and which channel staff can answer fastest. A channel that brings enquiries nobody can answer within the day wastes money, however good the ad looks.

Keep the first test simple. One offer, one call to action and one contact point, such as a WhatsApp number or a map pin, make it clear which channel produced each enquiry. If the offer changes between channels, the comparison stops being fair.

## What Should Be Set Up Before the Test Starts?

Before spending anything, confirm that the contact point works. Send a test message to the WhatsApp number or call the phone line during opening hours, and check that someone replies. Slow replies lower results in every channel, so a test run against an unanswered phone tells you nothing about the ad.

Next, decide what counts as a result. For a shop, a result might be a message asking about stock, a booked appointment or a map direction request. Choose one definition and use it for every channel. Write it on the same sheet as the budget so the whole team reads it the same way.

Finally, set a fixed spend limit in each ad account. Daily or monthly caps stop a campaign from running past the planned budget during the weekend or a busy period. Check the caps once a week, because a budget that was right in week one may not suit week three.

A scheduler becomes useful only when you post on several platforms every week. ## How Should a Small Budget Be Split? A Worked Example

All inputs below are **assumptions** for planning. They are not benchmarks, and no ad-price data was verified for Egypt. Replace them with your own figures after the first month.

| Input | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly test budget | EGP 10,000 |
| Facebook and Instagram ads | 50% (EGP 5,000) |
| TikTok video ads | 30% (EGP 3,000) |
| Google Ads, local search | 20% (EGP 2,000) |
| Messages from Facebook and Instagram | 40 (assumption) |
| Messages from TikTok | 12 (assumption) |
| Calls or map actions from Google Ads | 16 (assumption) |
| Test length | 4 weeks |

Divide each channel's spend by its results to get the cost per message or call. With the assumed counts, Facebook and Instagram cost EGP 125 per message (EGP 5,000 divided by 40), TikTok costs EGP 250 (EGP 3,000 divided by 12) and Google Ads costs EGP 125 per call (EGP 2,000 divided by 16).

In this example TikTok is the most expensive channel, so the next month's money would move toward the two cheaper ones. TikTok keeps a smaller creative test so that its results are not judged on one week of ads.

The split is a starting point only. The account data from the first four weeks replaces it.

## How Do You Track Results Without a Messy Spreadsheet?

Track spend and results in one place, using the same date range for every channel. Record the ad spend, the number of enquiries and the number of paid jobs that followed. Bookkeeping software can hold the spend lines alongside other business costs. The [FreshBooks and QuickBooks comparison for local businesses](/blog/freshbooks-vs-quickbooks-small-business-local-business) covers how each handles expense categories.

Follow these steps each week:

1. Export spend by channel from each ad account.
2. Count new enquiries by source, using a question such as "where did you hear about us?"
3. Divide spend by enquiries to get cost per enquiry.
4. Note which enquiries became paid work.
5. Decide once a month whether to shift budget, pause a channel or keep the test running.

If the shop takes bookings or quotes, a job-management tool can link each enquiry to a completed job. The [Jobber and ServiceTitan comparison for local service businesses](/blog/jobber-vs-servicetitan-small-business-local-business) shows which fits a small crew and which suits a larger operation.

## What Should You Check Before Sending Bulk Messages?

Bulk SMS and WhatsApp campaigns carry more legal risk than a post on a page. Before sending one, confirm what permission you hold for each contact. Record where each phone number came from, and do not add people who only handed over a number for a receipt or a service.

Every message should show who is sending it and include a simple way to opt out. Honour opt-out requests quickly and keep a list of people who have asked to stop, so they are never messaged again by mistake.

This rewrite did not verify the current text of Egypt's data-protection rules, so it does not state specific requirements. Ask an Egyptian lawyer whether the campaign meets them before sending a bulk message, and keep that advice on file.

## What Should You Do Next?

Choose two channels, set a fixed test budget and record cost per enquiry for four weeks. Review the results against the worked example, then move money toward the cheapest source. Check permissions before any bulk message goes out.

## FAQ

### Is Facebook the best platform for a small business in Egypt?

The sources used for this rewrite do not confirm that. Test Facebook alongside one other channel, and let cost per enquiry decide which one gets the larger share.

### Are there verified ad prices for Egypt?

No. None were verified in the sources for this rewrite. Use the cost data inside your own Google Ads and Meta accounts.

### How long should a test run before the budget changes?

Four weeks is the period used in the worked example. Longer tests give steadier numbers, but a channel that produces nothing after two weeks may need changing sooner.

### Do the data-protection rules apply to a small shop?

They may, if the shop collects customer phone numbers or emails for marketing. Confirm the requirements with an Egyptian lawyer, because the legal text was not verified here.
