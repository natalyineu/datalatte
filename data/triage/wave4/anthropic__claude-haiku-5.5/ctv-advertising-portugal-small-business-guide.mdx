## What Is CTV Advertising in Portugal and How Is It Bought?

Connected TV is a television that plays video over the internet through apps, streaming sticks or consoles. Advertisers reach these screens through the same self-serve systems they use for online video. That is why the entry cost can be far lower than traditional TV airtime.

The practical question for a small business is not whether the screen exists, but whether the buying tool lets you target it. Google's Display campaign documentation describes a single campaign type that combines standard and AI-assisted options. Check the current campaign types in your account before planning formats.

## Does Google Give Access to TV Screens in Portugal?

Google's Display Network help page describes reach across 35 million websites and apps, plus Google-owned properties such as YouTube and Gmail. That is a network-wide figure, not a Portugal count, and the page does not break it down by country.

Connected TV inventory is a separate question. The help pages on CTV device targeting and QR codes were not opened for this guide, so confirm in your own account whether TV screens are available for your campaign type and country. Do not build a budget around TV placements until you have seen them listed.

## What Do Google Ads Benchmarks Suggest About Cost?

WordStream publishes average Google Ads figures by industry. Across all industries, its averages are a 3.17% search CTR, a 0.46% display CTR, a $2.69 average search CPC and a $0.63 average display CPC. The article notes these averages were slightly higher than an earlier period, but its publication date is not fully clear.

These are text and display benchmarks, not CTV benchmarks. Use them only as a rough starting point for a test budget. Replace them with your own click and conversion data as soon as the campaign runs.

## How Should a Small Business Set Up a Test?

Start with one goal, such as calls, bookings or visits, and one way to measure it. A promo code, a dedicated landing page or a QR code that leads to a booking form all work. Without a tracked action, a video test tells you little about return on spend.

Keep the test short and local. Set a fixed budget, one geographic radius around the business and one or two video versions. Review cost per click, cost per conversion and tracked actions after two weeks before increasing spend. ## What Would a Test Budget Return? A Worked Example

The inputs below are assumptions, not Portugal data. Assumption: a Lisbon cafe tests YouTube video for four weeks. Assumption: the $0.63 display CPC is a rough proxy, with the currency difference ignored. Assumption: 5% of clicks become a tracked visit. Under these inputs, the cost per visit is 0.63 ÷ 0.05 = 12.60 (in the proxy currency).

| Scenario | Budget (assumption) | Clicks at $0.63 proxy | Tracked visits at 5% | Cost per visit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low test | EUR 300 | about 476 | about 24 | about 12.60 |
| Mid test | EUR 600 | about 952 | about 48 | about 12.60 |
| High test | EUR 1,000 | about 1,587 | about 79 | about 12.60 |

Reaching a 30-visit target under these assumptions needs about EUR 378. The table shows the budget is the lever, not the click count. Replace the 5% rate and the proxy CPC with measured numbers after two weeks, because a different conversion rate changes every row.

## Which Rules Should a Business Check First?

Advertising rules vary by country and by product, and the sources used here did not cover Portuguese advertising codes. Before writing any claim, check the current rules for your sector and keep only claims you can prove. Name the business clearly in the ad, and keep a record of the evidence behind any price, result or comparison published.

If the business sells a regulated product or service, get advice from a local specialist before launch. This guide does not replace that check.

## Is CTV Worth Testing for a Local Business?

It is worth testing only after the basics work. If the Google Business Profile is incomplete, the website cannot take bookings, or no conversion can be tracked, a TV-screen ad will not fix those gaps. Start with local search and maps, then test video once results can be measured. Businesses weighing video against other paid formats may find the [native advertising guide for small businesses](/blog/native-advertising-guide-small-business) useful for comparing trade-offs.

## FAQ

### What does CTV advertising cost in Portugal?

No verified Portuguese price list was found for this guide. Use the budget estimate shown in Google Ads and judge the result by your own cost per tracked action.

### Can I run TV-screen ads from Google Ads?

It depends on the campaign type and what the account offers. Check the current Display and video options in the account before planning a TV-specific test.

### Do I need a professional video?

Not necessarily. A short, clear video can work for a test, but check the current format and size requirements in Google Ads before production.

### How long should a first test run?

Two to four weeks is a common range for a small test, long enough to collect tracked actions. Keep the budget fixed so results are comparable.
