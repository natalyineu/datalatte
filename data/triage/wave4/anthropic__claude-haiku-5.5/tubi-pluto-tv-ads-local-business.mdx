## What can you verify about Tubi and Pluto TV?

The source set for this update contains no Tubi or Pluto TV publication. Figures such as monthly user counts, TV viewing share, and reseller CPMs from earlier drafts were not matched to a checked source, so none appear here. Treat any streaming TV price or audience number as unverified until the platform's own advertiser site confirms it.

Google's Display Network is verifiable. Google's help page states that Display campaigns can reach people across 35 million websites and apps, plus Google-owned properties such as YouTube and Gmail. For a local business, that breadth matters less than whether the audience is near the business, so location targeting is the first setting to check.

TV-style reach works best when the audience matches the timing of the offer. For sports-driven audiences, [live sports advertising for local businesses](/blog/live-sports-advertising-local-business) explains how inventory and timing change the math.

## How far does a $1,500 test go?

WordStream's benchmark tables give average cost per click for search and display across industries. Using the all-industry averages, $1,500 buys about 558 search clicks at $2.69, or about 2,381 display clicks at $0.63. Industry averages vary widely. Legal search CPC is $6.75 and consumer services is $6.40, so a service business in a competitive category may buy far fewer clicks.

Clicks alone do not show whether a test pays. The table below adds an assumption-labelled conversion step.

| Input or result | Search (sourced CPC) | Display (sourced CPC) |
|---|---|---|
| Budget | $1,500 | $1,500 |
| Average CPC | $2.69 | $0.63 |
| Clicks bought | about 558 | about 2,381 |
| Conversion rate (assumption) | 2% | 0.5% (assumption) |
| Customers (assumption) | about 11 | about 12 |
| Gross profit per new customer (assumption) | $45 | $45 |
| Gross profit from test (assumption) | about $504 | about $536 |
| Net result after $1,500 spend (assumption) | about -$996 | about -$964 |

On these assumptions, a single $1,500 burst does not break even. Break-even requires about 34 customers at $45 each. At a 2% conversion rate, search would need roughly 1,700 clicks, which costs about $4,570 at $2.69. The test only makes sense if customer value is higher, the conversion rate is higher, or the campaign repeats so early learning carries forward.

## Is a streaming TV test worth it for your business?

A TV-style test fits the situations below. If none apply, search or the Display Network is the safer first step, because its cost per click is sourced.

- A specific offer exists, such as a first-visit discount, a grand opening, or a seasonal service.
- The service area is small enough to target by location.
- The budget can stay untouched for at least six weeks, since one or two weeks rarely show a clear result.
- Customer value is high enough that a few new customers cover the spend.

If the Google Business Profile is thin or people searching for the service are not yet reaching the business, fix that first. Search demand converts faster and its cost per click is documented. [Google Local Service Ads](/blog/google-local-service-ads-worth-it-local-business) cover pay-per-lead placements that some service businesses find easier to track than broad TV reach.

## How do you measure a local TV test?

Measurement is the step most small businesses skip. Without a plan, a streaming test produces impressions and no answer. Work through this checklist before launch:

1. Set a dedicated phone number or landing page used only for this campaign.
2. Add a "how did you hear about us" question to the booking or intake form.
3. Record the baseline: new customers per week for the four weeks before launch.
4. Set a break-even number in advance, using gross profit per new customer.
5. Compare new customers and calls during the test against the baseline and the break-even number.
6. Decide in advance whether a second flight runs, and only after the first is measured.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Tubi advertising free for small businesses?

No. Viewers watch free, but advertisers pay for the ads they run. Confirm pricing and minimum spend on the platform's own advertiser site, because none was verified for this update.

### What does a $1,500 test buy on Google?

At the all-industry average search CPC of $2.69, it buys about 558 clicks. At the average display CPC of $0.63, it buys about 2,381 clicks. Category averages differ, and legal and consumer services run higher.

### Should a business test Pluto TV first?

Not without a verified source. No current self-serve option or audience count was confirmed for this update, so check directly with Paramount before setting a budget.

### How long should a local TV test run?

Plan for at least six weeks and compare results against a baseline recorded beforehand. One or two weeks rarely produce enough new customers to judge.
