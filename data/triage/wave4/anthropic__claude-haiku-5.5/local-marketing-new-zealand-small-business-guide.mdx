## Which Channels Should a New Zealand Local Business Test First?

Channel choice depends on how customers decide. People searching for a plumber, electrician or dentist are usually close to booking, so paid search captures demand that already exists. People who have never heard of a business are reached through social platforms, where the job is awareness rather than an immediate call.

A barber, café or cleaning firm can often start with a Google Business Profile and one search campaign. A design studio or consultancy may find LinkedIn or Instagram more useful for reaching the right people. Pick the channel that matches how customers buy, and set up tracking before spending.

## How Should You Set Up Google Business Profile in New Zealand?

If you travel to customers rather than serving them from a shop, use Google's service-area option so a street address is not shown publicly. You then list the areas you cover. Check Google's current help page for the number of service areas allowed, because limits can change and older advice may be out of date.

Name your suburb or city clearly in the description and services. Update opening hours before each public holiday, using the official calendar from Employment New Zealand. A profile with wrong hours is a common reason customers arrive at a closed business, and it is among the cheapest problems to fix.

Check that the phone number and website match what appears on invoices and signage. Inconsistent details make it harder for both customers and search engines to trust the listing.

## Do Reviews Matter for Local Customers in New Zealand?

No New Zealand review survey was among the sources checked, so the effect of reviews on local decisions is untested for this market. Do not quote a percentage for New Zealand unless it comes from a dated, primary source.

The practical step is the same either way. Ask each customer for a review at the point of service, while the job is fresh, and reply to every review, positive or negative. Replies show prospective customers how problems are handled.

## Can You Email and Text Customers in New Zealand?

Only with consent. The Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act governs commercial electronic messages, and the Department of Internal Affairs publishes guidance on how it applies. That guidance was not among the sources checked for this rewrite, so read it directly before sending a first campaign.

In practice, use a clear tick box on the booking form, identify the business by name in every message, and include a working unsubscribe link or reply word. Do not buy contact lists. If a case is unclear, ask the Department of Internal Affairs before sending.

## Do Your Prices Need to Include GST?

This guide did not confirm whether every advertised price must include GST, so do not assume either answer. The Commerce Commission enforces the Fair Trading Act, which covers misleading price claims, but the specific GST display rule was not verified here.

Before publishing a price in an ad, a landing page or a Google listing, confirm with the Commerce Commission what must be shown. If prices change often, add a dated note so a figure does not mislead after an update.

## What Should You Do in the First 30 Days?

Work through these steps in order, and change only one thing at a time so each result has a clear cause.

1. Complete the Google Business Profile, including service areas, hours and a clear description.
2. Add a consent tick box to the booking or contact form before collecting any new contact details for marketing.
3. Set up call and form tracking so each enquiry is tied to a channel.
4. Launch one search campaign with a small daily budget, and one social campaign if customers use that platform.
5. Record cost per enquiry for each channel at day 30, then decide where the next NZ$600 goes.

## FAQ

### Is Facebook or Google better for a New Zealand local business?

Neither wins on any source checked here. Social platforms reach people who have not yet decided to buy, while search reaches people already looking for the service. Test both with a small budget and compare cost per enquiry.

### What does Google Ads cost in New Zealand?

No source checked for this guide publishes cost-per-click figures for New Zealand local categories. Check Keyword Planner and your own account history, and treat the NZ$3 search cost in this article as an assumption only.

### Can I send marketing texts and emails?

Only with consent. Read the Department of Internal Affairs guidance on commercial electronic messages, identify the business in every message, and include an easy way to unsubscribe.

### Do I need to show my street address on Google?

Not if you serve customers at their location. Use the service-area option and check Google's current help page for the rules that apply to the listing.
