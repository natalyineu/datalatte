## What do the verified benchmarks actually measure?

The benchmark figures come from [WordStream's Google Ads benchmark report](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks), which reports averages drawn from its own clients across twenty industries. They are not Thai data. The page carries a 2016 publication date and notes that it is updated regularly, so check the current PDF before relying on any figure.

Across all industries, search averages 3.17% CTR and $2.69 CPC, while display averages 0.46% CTR and $0.63 CPC. The same page says both averages were higher than in earlier years, and that search CPC had risen from $2.32 to $2.69.

Industry spread is wide. Legal search CPC averages $6.75, while e-commerce averages $1.16. A local service business should compare itself with its closest category rather than the all-industry average.

## Does Google's Display Network matter for a Thai CTV plan?

Google's [Display campaign help page](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190) says the Display Network reaches people across 35 million websites and apps, plus Google-owned properties such as YouTube and Gmail. It describes responsive display ads as the default, with Google AI selecting asset combinations, and optimized targeting as the default targeting method.

The page does not describe connected TV inventory in Thailand, device availability, or CTV pricing. Confirm with Google which CTV placements are available in your market before budgeting for streaming screens. For a wider view of programmatic formats, read the [native advertising guide for small business](/blog/native-advertising-guide-small-business).

## What compliance steps should come before launch?

Specific NBTC, Thai FDA health-claim, alcohol advertising, and VAT requirements are not restated in this guide because no verified source confirms them. Treat each one as an open question to answer directly with the regulator, the platform's ad review team, or a local adviser.

A practical order helps. First, check the platform's policy page against every claim in the creative. Second, confirm with the regulator any category-specific limits that apply to your product or service. Third, ask a local accountant how prices should be shown in the ad, including whether tax must be included.

Do not launch until each step is written down and signed off by the person responsible for the campaign.

## How big should a Thai CTV test budget be?

The example below uses assumed inputs, labelled as such. Replace them with real platform quotes before spending.

| Input | Value | Label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Test budget | USD 500 total | assumption |
| Test length | 6 weeks | assumption |
| CPM quoted by platform | USD 5 | assumption |
| Search CPC benchmark | $2.69 | sourced (WordStream) |
| Search CTR benchmark | 3.17% | sourced (WordStream) |

Impressions equal budget divided by CPM, multiplied by 1,000. So 500 ÷ 5 × 1,000 = 100,000 impressions under the assumed CPM.

For comparison, the sourced search CPC of $2.69 means the same $500 buys about 186 clicks (500 ÷ 2.69). At the 3.17% average search CTR, that implies roughly 5,860 impressions (186 ÷ 0.0317). The two channels price attention differently, so this comparison shows scale only, not performance.

Scale the budget only if the test reports a metric you can use and produces a cost per result you can accept.

## How should a local business read the test results?

Judge the test against your own goal, not the benchmark averages. Set a target cost per result before launch, then compare it with what the platform reports. If the platform cannot report results by location or device, test a different channel first.

Google's Smart bidding uses AI to optimize for conversions or conversion value, which may help once conversion tracking is set up correctly. Search and display answer different questions: search captures people already looking for a service, while streaming and display build awareness among people who are not searching yet.

## FAQ

### How much does CTV advertising cost in Thailand?

This guide does not give a verified Thai CTV price. Use the platform's CPM rate card for Thai inventory. The only sourced cost benchmarks are general Google Ads averages across industries: $2.69 per search click and $0.63 per display click.

### Are the Thai regulatory rules confirmed?

Not by the sources used for this guide. Check the regulator's published rules and each platform's ad policy pages directly before writing any creative.

### Is CTV better than search ads for a small business?

The sources do not compare the two for Thailand. Search captures existing intent, while streaming and display build awareness. Testing both against a clear goal is the practical route.

### What is a sensible minimum test?

The worked example uses an assumed USD 500 over six weeks. Actual minimums depend on each platform's terms, so confirm them in writing before committing.
