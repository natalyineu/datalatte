## How much does a Facebook ad cost a small business?

The cost of a Facebook ad is set by an auction, not by a published rate card. Your audience, ad creative, bidding approach and budget all influence what you pay per result. This means two businesses in the same town can pay very different amounts for the same click.

Because the price moves, the useful planning number is your monthly budget and the cost per lead it produces. Start with an amount you can sustain for two months, since Meta's testing guidance depends on running a fair comparison over time.

## How many people in the US use Facebook?

Pew Research Center's 2026 survey of 5,511 US adults found that 70% use Facebook, against 84% for YouTube and 51% for Instagram. Pew notes the survey represents all US adults and carries a margin of error of plus or minus 1.8 percentage points. [Pew's social media fact sheet](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/fact-sheet/social-media/) is the primary reference for these figures.

Reach matters for local businesses because the audience is broad, but local targeting does the narrowing. Nextdoor, which is hyperlocal, is used by 18% of US adults in the same survey, so it may suit some neighbourhood services while Facebook suits most others.

## How should you test ads without wasting budget?

Meta recommends A/B testing when you want to measure a change or compare two strategies. A/B testing splits the audience so that nobody sees both versions, and it can be set up by duplicating an existing campaign or ad set. Meta advises using the same budget for both versions so the comparison is fair.

Meta also advises against informal testing, such as switching ad sets on and off by hand. According to its help page, informal testing can cause overlapping audiences and unreliable results. Pick one variable to test first, such as the image or the offer, and leave everything else fixed. For the full setup steps, read [Meta's guidance on A/B testing](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1738164643098669).

## How do Facebook costs compare with Google Ads?

Google's own help pages describe Display campaigns as reaching people across 35 million websites and apps, plus Google-owned properties such as YouTube and Gmail. That is a separate channel from search, so it should be compared with Facebook on purpose, not assumed to be the same. [Google's Display network guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190) explains how those campaigns are set up.

## What should you measure in the first 60 days?

Track three things: cost per click, conversion rate on the page the ad sends people to, and cost per lead. Cost per click tells you whether people are interested enough to tap. Conversion rate tells you whether the page does its job. Cost per lead tells you what you pay for each person who asks for a quote, books, or calls.

Send ads to your website or booking page rather than your Facebook page, because a page with one clear action converts better than a feed full of other posts. Give every ad a unique offer so that customers who mention it at the counter can be counted. Do not judge the campaign on relevance score alone, since engagement is not the same as a booking.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much should a small business spend on Facebook ads per month?

There is no single correct figure, because cost depends on the auction. A practical method is to choose an amount you can sustain for 60 days, then measure cost per lead against what one customer is worth to you. The worked example above uses $300 as an assumed starting point.

### Why did my cost per click rise without a change on my side?

Costs can move with competition and with how often the same people see your ad. Check whether a small audience is seeing the ad repeatedly, and whether your tracking is firing correctly. Seasonal demand can also push prices up, so compare results across comparable periods.

### Is it worth running Facebook ads with few followers?

Ads are shown according to targeting, not according to your page follower count. A new business can reach people who have never heard of it. What matters more is whether the offer and the landing page give someone a reason to act.

### Should I run Facebook ads or Google Ads first?

It depends on how people look for your service. Google search suits people who already know what they need, while Facebook suits reaching people who may not be searching yet. Run one channel with a fixed budget for 60 days before adding the other, so you can compare results fairly.
