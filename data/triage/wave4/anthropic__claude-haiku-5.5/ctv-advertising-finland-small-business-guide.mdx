## How much does CTV advertising cost in Finland?

No verified Finnish CTV CPM appears in the sources, so any euro figure for Finland would be a guess. The closest reference is WordStream's all-industry average display cost per click of $0.63. That figure comes from US accounts and older data, and it sits alongside a search cost per click of $2.69 in the same [WordStream benchmark report](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks). Display cost per click and CTV cost per thousand impressions measure different things, so neither figure is a CTV price.

Request a current rate card from each platform you consider, then convert it into cost per thousand impressions (CPM) in EUR. That single unit makes platforms comparable. Build the test budget on that figure, not on a display click benchmark from another market.

## Which CTV platforms suit a small Finnish business?

The sources do not rank Finnish streaming apps by audience size or price, so this guide names none with figures. Start by listing the platforms your buyers already watch. Then ask each vendor for audience size in your target city, minimum spend, and reporting access. A vendor that cannot show city-level reach is harder to test fairly.

Google's [Display campaign documentation](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190) describes a verified option for businesses that want automated creative and bidding. Google says these campaigns use AI for targeting, bidding, and asset combinations, and that Smart Bidding can optimise for conversions. A business that cannot yet run a dedicated CTV buy can use Display to build awareness and track conversions in one account.

## How should a small business structure a test budget?

A test should be small enough to lose and large enough to read. The worked example uses assumption inputs only, because no verified Finnish CPM is available. Assume a 6-week test with a total budget of €1,200 (assumption) and a CPM of €20 per 1,000 impressions (assumption). That gives €200 a week, and €200 ÷ €20 × 1,000 = 10,000 impressions per week, or 60,000 over six weeks (calculated from assumptions).

Before launch, set one goal and one tracking method, and fix the budget and CPM assumption in a shared sheet. Keep the creative unchanged for the full 4-6 weeks. At the end, compare cost per action with your current channel, then decide whether to scale, change the audience, or stop. ## Does CTV beat Google Ads for local businesses?

They do different jobs. CTV and display build reach and recall, while search captures people already looking for a service. WordStream's all-industry search cost per click of $2.69 is higher than its display cost per click of $0.63, but search clicks usually sit closer to a purchase decision. These are US averages from older data, so check your own account figures.

Keep search running for demand you can already capture. Use display or CTV to widen awareness, then measure whether that spend lowers the cost of later search clicks. Some businesses also test [native advertising for small business](/blog/native-advertising-guide-small-business) as a further awareness layer, and [CTV advertising in Germany](/blog/ctv-advertising-germany-small-business-guide) shows how a different European market handles the same channel.

## Are there rules for CTV ads in Finland?

The sources do not cover Finnish advertising rules, VAT treatment of prices, or scheduling limits for product categories. Before launch, check current guidance from the relevant Finnish self-regulatory body and the platform's own ad policies. Confirm that any price in the creative matches how you quote it to customers.

Platform review teams enforce their own policies, which Google documents for Display ads. Read those policies before uploading creative, since a rejected ad delays the test and can distort the first week of data.

## FAQ

### What is a realistic starting budget for CTV advertising in Finland?

The sources do not verify a Finnish minimum. The worked example uses an assumed €1,200 over six weeks to show the arithmetic. Replace that assumption with the minimum spend and CPM your vendor quotes.

### Is the WordStream benchmark data current?

No. The WordStream averages cited here come from US-market data that is dated. Use them only as rough context, and replace them with your own account data as soon as you have it.

### Do I need Finnish-language creative?

The sources do not address language performance. If you can, test a Finnish-language version against your current creative and let the cost per action decide which one to keep.

### Which measure should I track first?

Track cost per action for the goal you set before launch. Impressions and reach show delivery, but only the action cost tells you whether the channel is worth scaling.
