## How Does Catch-Up TV Change Ad Timing?

Catch-up TV, also called BVOD, lets viewers watch programmes after they have aired. An [agency glossary](https://be.biggie.co/en-articles/catch-up-tv-22b4f) defines it as the temporary availability of a programme via streaming after broadcast, and notes that advertisers can run ads during these delayed programmes.

For a small business, this means an ad no longer depends on one fixed broadcast slot. Ads can appear before or during streamed content on a phone, tablet or smart TV. McKenzie Partners describes these ads as more personalised, using viewer data to match an ad to the audience watching.

Whether a given seller can target your suburb is a separate question. That depends on the platform, and it should be confirmed before any money is committed.

## Why Is CTV Different From Free-to-Air Ads?

Teads, a CTV vendor, explains that CTV ads are served digitally. They can use the targeting and measurement typical of digital media while still reaching the largest screen in the home.

Teads also notes that not all CTV inventory is equal. Premium broadcaster apps and curated streaming environments typically deliver stronger attention than long-tail inventory. It lists four inventory types: AVOD (ad-supported subscription platforms), FAST (free ad-supported channels), BVOD (broadcaster streaming apps) and home screen placements.

A seller offering BVOD only reaches one of these environments. Ask which ones a campaign would run in, because the audience and price can differ between them.

## How Strong Is Attention on Connected TV?

Teads cites industry benchmarks showing CTV ads achieve completion rates of 95% or more, compared with 20-40% on YouTube and under 30% on social video. Teads attributes this to full-screen, sound-on viewing with no skip button and no competing sidebar content.

This is a vendor figure, not a guarantee for any particular campaign. It does mean a CPM price should be compared against completed views rather than raw impressions. A higher CPM with near-full completion can deliver more attention than a cheaper impression that is scrolled past after three seconds.

## Which Targeting Options Can a Small Business Use?

Teads lists demographic and geographic targeting at household level, behavioural segments, contextual alignment with programming, and retargeting across devices. It reports that 64% of marketers use first-party data for CTV targeting, while 50% cite audience targeting inefficiencies as their top challenge. The capability exists, but execution varies between sellers.

Search and display work differently. Google states that Display campaigns can reach people across 35 million websites and apps, including YouTube and Gmail. Its optimised targeting uses information such as keywords on the landing page to find likely customers ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190)).

For a local business, a targeting rule is only useful if the seller states its smallest area and whether location is set by household or device. Regional planning context is in the [Australia local marketing guide](/blog/local-marketing-australia-small-business-2026).

## What Should a Business Ask Each Seller Before Buying?

Request these answers in writing before signing any commitment:

1. What is the minimum budget and the rate card for each inventory type?
2. What is the smallest geographic area you can target, and is location set by household or device?
3. Which environments will ads run in, such as BVOD, AVOD or FAST?
4. Does the report show where ads were delivered, and how completion is measured?
5. Can a campaign run for a fixed test period without a long contract?

If a seller can only target at state level, a single suburb café pays for reach it cannot use. Search ads allow a radius to be set directly, which makes them easier to control for a first test. ## How Should a First TV Test Be Budgeted?

The table below is a worked example. Every input is an **assumption** and should be replaced with real quotes.

| Test budget (assumption) | A$3,000 |
| CPM quoted by seller (assumption) | A$30 |
| Impressions (budget divided by CPM, times 1,000) | 100,000 |
| Average frequency (assumption) | 4 |
| People reached (impressions divided by frequency) | 25,000 |
| Redemption rate of a campaign code (assumption) | 0.1% |
| Redemptions (people reached times redemption rate) | 25 |
| Cost per redemption (budget divided by redemptions) | A$120 |

At these inputs, each sale would need a margin above A$120 to break even on the ad alone. If the margin is lower, reduce the target area or test search ads first.

If the CPM changes to a real quote, recalculate every row, because impressions and reach move with it.

## How Do You Measure a TV Test?

TV ads rarely produce direct clicks, so measurement needs a deliberate setup. A unique code, a vanity web address or a dedicated phone number that appears only in the TV creative makes it possible to attribute responses.

Compare redemptions in the test weeks against comparable weeks without the ad, and record spend for each week. Keep the same offer throughout so the comparison is fair.

For a reference point on search, WordStream's benchmark article gives an all-industry average search click-through rate of 3.17% and average cost per click of $2.69. These figures come from a dated 2016-era vendor report and are a rough guide only. Check current numbers in your own account before comparing.

## FAQ

### How much does streaming TV advertising cost in Australia?

No rates or minimum budgets were published in the sources checked. Ask each network or platform for a rate card and the exact inventory on offer.

### What is BVOD?

Broadcaster video on demand is the streaming service of a TV network, which lets viewers watch programmes after broadcast. Ads can run during these programmes.

### Can a small business target a single suburb?

It depends on the seller. Ask each platform for its smallest targetable area and how location is determined before planning a test.

### How do I know if a TV ad worked?

Use a unique code, vanity URL or dedicated phone number, then compare results in test weeks against weeks without the ad.
