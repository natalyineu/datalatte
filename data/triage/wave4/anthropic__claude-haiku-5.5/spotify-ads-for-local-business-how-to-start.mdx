To start Spotify ads for a local business, open Spotify's self-serve Ad Studio, set a budget (WebFX reports campaigns start at $250), pick a 15- or 30-second audio slot, target listeners near your business, and launch. Before you spend, decide how many new customers the campaign must bring in to pay for itself. Spotify ads work best as an awareness channel, so the break-even number is what turns a test into a decision.

## Is Spotify Right for a Local Business?

Spotify suits a business that wants people in its area to remember the name. It does not suit a business that needs someone to call today. People do not search on Spotify. They listen while commuting, exercising or working, so an ad interrupts the listening rather than answering a question.

Spotify says it has [more than 450 million global users each month](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/news-and-insights/types-of-advertising/). That reach is global, and most of it will fall outside your trading area. The value for a local business depends on how well you can narrow the audience to the streets you serve.

Good candidates are businesses with a clear, single offer in a dense area, such as a coffee shop, salon, studio or groomer. If local customers already search for you and cannot find you, fix Google first with a [Google Ads option](/blog/google-local-service-ads-worth-it-local-business) or your own listing before paying for audio.

## How Do You Set Up Spotify Ad Studio?

Create an Ad Studio account with your business details and a payment method. WebFX describes Ad Studio as a self-service tool where you set the budget, choose the objective, select the format, set targeting and schedule, then upload your audio and launch. [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/blog/ppc/spotify-advertising/) states that Spotify advertising starts at $250 through this route and that you pay based on impressions or clicks.

Prices differ by country and change over time, so check the live Ad Studio screens before you commit. Treat the $250 figure as a starting point from a secondary source, not a guaranteed price for your market.

## How Do You Target Local Listeners?

Use location targeting to keep the campaign close to your door. WebFX lists location, age, gender, music tastes, interests and real-time context as targeting options in Ad Studio. For a local business, location is the filter that matters most, because it decides whether your spend reaches people who can walk in.

Add age, interests or music tastes only when your customer profile is clear. Each extra filter narrows the audience, which can cut the number of listeners you reach faster than you expect. A narrow, clear audience usually beats a broad one for a single neighborhood business.

## What Should the Audio Ad Say?

Spotify's own materials say audio ads come in a 15- or 30-second slot, and audio ads cannot be closed or swiped past. That means the message is heard, but it also means you have a fixed window and listeners may skip after a few seconds in some formats. [Spotify](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/news-and-insights/types-of-advertising/) and a [secondary review of pros and cons](https://www.yourdigitalresource.com/post/should-your-business-use-spotify-ads) both describe this trade-off.

Use one structure: who you are, where you are, one offer, one action. Keep the web address short, because a listener cannot click while driving. WebFX also recommends high-quality visuals even for audio ads, so prepare a clear companion image with your name and offer.

## How Do You Track Whether It Works?

Give the campaign its own way to be counted. Use a dedicated landing page, a promo word at the till, or a phone number used only in the ad. Without this, new customers will say they "found you online," and you cannot separate Spotify from every other channel.

Run the first flight for several weeks rather than a few days, so listeners hear the ad more than once. Count new customers from the promo channel only, and compare that count with your break-even number.

## What Is the Break-Even Math?

Set the target before you spend. The example below uses assumed figures for a neighborhood salon. Replace every input with your own numbers.

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign budget (assumption) | $250 |
| Gross profit per new customer (assumption) | $30 |
| New customers to break even | 9 ($250 / $30) |
| New customers for a 2x return (assumption) | 17 |

If you cannot picture nine new customers coming from the areas you targeted, the test is not ready. Raise the offer, lengthen the flight or choose another channel. One test is a signal, not proof.

Spotify's own commentary on ROI is limited, and a [secondary review](https://www.yourdigitalresource.com/post/should-your-business-use-spotify-ads) notes that measuring return on audio spend can be difficult. The promo count is your best substitute for attribution.

## What Should You Do After the First Test?

Keep the offer and the location targeting the same, change only the script, and test again. This isolates whether the message or the channel is the problem. Change one variable per flight so the result means something.

If the first flight misses break-even, check the promo count before blaming Spotify. A weak offer, a hard-to-remember address or too few days can all look like a failed channel. ## Checklist Before You Launch

Work through these steps in order before the first flight goes live:

1. Write the break-even number from your real gross profit per customer.
2. Pick the location area within a short drive or walk of your door.
3. Write one offer and one action, and create a unique promo word or phone line.
4. Prepare a 15- or 30-second script and a companion image.
5. Set a multi-week schedule and a weekly check on promo redemptions.

## FAQ

### How much does it cost to start Spotify ads?

WebFX reports that Spotify advertising starts at $250 through Ad Studio, with payment based on impressions or clicks. Prices vary by country and change, so confirm the live figure in Ad Studio before launch.

### Can I target only the area around my business?

Yes, location targeting is part of Ad Studio's targeting options, according to [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/blog/ppc/spotify-advertising/). Use it to keep the audience close to your trading area, and check reach before you commit the full budget.

### Do I need to record the ad myself?

No, but you need an audio file and a companion visual. WebFX notes that audio ads should still have high-quality visuals. Plan the script in advance, because the spot is short.

### How long can the audio ad be?

Spotify's [audio ad format](https://ads.spotify.com/en-US/news-and-insights/types-of-advertising/) offers a 15- or 30-second slot. Write a script that fits the slot and includes one offer and one action.
