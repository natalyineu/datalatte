## Which documents does Google ask for in Brunei?

The documents depend on whether the account is registered as an Organisation or as an Individual. According to [Google's Brunei document requirements](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/9872280?hl=en&co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DBN), organisations may need two types of documentation: organisation registration documents and a government-issued photo ID from an authorised representative.

Accepted registration documents include a certificate of incorporation or registration, an extract from the commercial register, a business licence and a tax certificate. Only government departments and agencies can use an official letter showing full name, address and date.

The authorised representative is an admin of the Google Ads account or the payments profile paying for the ads. Their photo ID can be a passport, identification card, driving licence or permanent residence card, and it can be issued in any country.

Individuals must submit a Bruneian government-issued photo ID: a passport, national ID card, driver's licence or residence permit. In certain cases they may also need a second proof-of-address document that matches the full name and address already submitted. The address does not appear in ad disclosures.

## How do I check my account type before uploading?

Start by confirming the account type. Google states that it appears in the Google Ads payments profile, on the Settings page inside the Billing menu. The former Advertiser verification page is now called Account. It can be reached by selecting Admin, expanding the Policy drop-down and then selecting Account.

If the account type is Individual but verification must be as an organisation, or the reverse, Google asks the advertiser to contact its support team before beginning. The upload should not start until the type is correct, because the document list changes with it.

The submitted documents must also match the payments profile exactly, including the organisation name where one applies. If there is a mismatch, Google allows the payments profile name to be changed during the task. Correcting the name at this stage avoids a failed review. ## What does the Brunei verification checklist look like?

The table turns Google's requirements into a sequence. It is a planning aid built from Google's pages, not an official form.

| Step | Organisation | Individual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Confirm account type in Billing, Settings | Must read Organisation | Must read Individual |
| 2. Match names to payments profile | Organisation name identical | Full name identical |
| 3. Prepare registration documents | Certificate, commercial register extract, business licence or tax certificate | Not listed for this type |
| 4. Prepare photo ID | Authorised representative's passport, ID card, driving licence or residence card | Bruneian passport, national ID card, driving licence or residence permit |
| 5. Prepare second proof of address if asked | Not listed for organisations | Utility, phone or internet bill, bank statement or lease, dated within 60 days |
| 6. Photograph the ID correctly | Colour, clear, well lit, original | Colour, clear, well lit, original |
| 7. Upload through the Ads interface | Ads UI only, never by email | Ads UI only, never by email |

Some steps need extra care. Google says photos taken through third-party apps may not be accepted, so the phone's native camera app is the safer choice. When requested, the front and back of an ID must be submitted separately. Agencies must not upload documents for several advertisers in one file.

## How long is a proof-of-address document valid?

The 60-day rule is the one most likely to cause a rejected upload. Google requires a utility, phone or internet bill, or a bank statement, to carry a date within the last 60 days. A lease or mortgage agreement is also accepted. For a second government-issued photo ID, the side showing the address must be uploaded.

## Why do Brunei verifications fail, and how do I fix them?

Google lists three common reasons for failure: the document cannot be verified, the name does not match, or the document type is not supported for the region and account type. Each has a direct fix.

For an unverifiable document, retake the photo in colour, in good light, with the whole card visible. Photocopies and screenshots may not be accepted. For a name mismatch, compare the spelling on the ID with the payments profile before uploading, and check middle names, abbreviations and organisation suffixes. For an unsupported type, check the list above. An expired ID is another common miss, since Google requires a valid document.

Google also publishes a separate table of issues with submitting documents. That is the first place to look if a rejection message does not match the list above. Google states that personal data and documents are not shared with third parties under its data protection policy.

## What does verification change for my ads?

Google describes verification as a way to build trust and unlock more features. After verification, some information appears in ad disclosures and in the Ads Transparency Center, which gives context about an advertiser and its ads. A registered business name should be expected to be visible.

Google's page does not say whether ads are blocked until verification is complete, so check the status on the Account page before fixing a launch date. Verification is not a budget. ## Do This Today

1. Open Billing, then Settings, and confirm whether the account type is Organisation or Individual.
2. Compare the name on the payments profile with the name on the ID and registration papers, and fix any mismatch.
3. Gather the photo ID for the right person, checking that it is valid and not expired.
4. If the account type is Individual, check the date on each utility or bank statement and use only documents dated within 60 days.
5. Photograph each document in colour using the phone's native camera app, and upload through the Ads interface only.
6. Keep a copy of every document submitted, with the upload date, so a rejection can be answered quickly.

## FAQ

### Do I need the same documents as an organisation if I run a sole business?

Not necessarily. The account type decides the list. Check it in Billing, Settings. If unsure, contact Google before starting, since a mismatch can stop the process.

### Can I use a photocopy of my ID?

Google's requirements say the image should be a clear, original photo of a physical ID. Photocopies and screenshots may not be accepted, so photograph the card directly with the phone's native camera app.

### What if my bill is older than 60 days?

Upload a newer statement dated within the last 60 days. The rule applies to the date on the document, so an older bill does not qualify even if the address is correct.

### Will my address appear in public ads?

Google says the address on a proof-of-address document will not show in ad disclosures. Other verified information can appear in disclosures and the Ads Transparency Center, so check what is being registered before submitting.
