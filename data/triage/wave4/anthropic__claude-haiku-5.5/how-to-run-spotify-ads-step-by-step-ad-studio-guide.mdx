## How do you set up a Spotify Ads account?

Start by signing up for [Spotify Ad Studio](https://ads.spotify.com/) with your business details, then link the business to the account. A payment method must be on file before any campaign can run. Use the business name and address exactly as they appear on your Google Business Profile. Mismatched details can slow approval and make location targeting harder to check.

Before you build anything, decide the one action you want a listener to take: a store visit, a class booking or a phone call. A single goal gives you one number to track. Multiple goals split a small budget across objectives and blur the results, so pick one for the first test.

## Which Spotify ad format suits a local business?

Spotify offers audio, video and display ads, and each does a different job. Tinuiti quotes Spotify saying that audio-only campaigns tend to drive more foot traffic to stores at a more efficient cost per visit than multi-format campaigns ([Tinuiti, secondary](https://tinuiti.com/blog/tv-audio/spotify-ads/)). For a shop or service that depends on people arriving soon, audio is the sensible place to start.

The table below compares the three formats for a local business.

| Format | Best used when | What the sources say |
|---|---|---|
| Audio | Walk-ins or same-week bookings are the goal | Audio-only campaigns tend to drive more foot traffic at a more efficient cost per visit ([Tinuiti, secondary](https://tinuiti.com/blog/tv-audio/spotify-ads/)) |
| Video | The service is visual, such as a dish, a haircut or a renovation | Dentsu research found Spotify video ads drove 155% more attentive seconds than the platform average ([catapult°, secondary](https://www.catapultmymusic.com/mastering-spotify-advertising-a-beginners-guide)) |
| Display | You need a static message for brand recall | No Spotify-specific figure in the sources reviewed; test against audio before scaling |

The video figure is a platform-wide attention result, not a local sales result, so test video beside audio rather than replacing it. For more on how audio compares with other channels, see [digital audio advertising on Spotify, Pandora and podcasts](/blog/digital-audio-advertising-2026-spotify-pandora-podcast-ads).

## How should you target local listeners?

Start with geography. Spotify lets advertisers target users by location, so set a radius around your shop or service area that matches how far a customer would realistically travel. A wider radius spends money on people who will never visit ([catapult°, secondary](https://www.catapultmymusic.com/mastering-spotify-advertising-a-beginners-guide)).

Next, layer listening context on top of location. Spotify uses first-party data on the moods, activities and listening behaviour of its users, which advertisers can use to reach people at specific times ([Tinuiti, secondary](https://tinuiti.com/blog/tv-audio/spotify-ads/)). Test one interest segment at a time. A broad audience gives you data faster, while a narrow one can run out of people before you learn anything. Set frequency controls so one listener does not hear the same ad too often ([catapult°, secondary](https://www.catapultmymusic.com/mastering-spotify-advertising-a-beginners-guide)).

## What does a Spotify test campaign cost?

Spotify ads are bought against impressions, usually priced per thousand impressions, known as CPM. The live rate depends on targeting and competition, so check it inside Ad Studio before you commit. The sources reviewed do not give a reliable local CPM, so this guide does not quote one.

Set a test budget you can afford to run for a full month, then judge results on cost per customer rather than impressions. Impressions are easy to buy and easy to report, but they do not show whether anyone walked in. Plan for at least two months: one to learn and one to optimise.

## Worked example: a $500 monthly test for a coffee shop

The inputs below are assumptions for illustration only. Replace each one with a figure from your own Ad Studio dashboard after the first week.

- Monthly budget: $500 (assumption)
- CPM: $10 per 1,000 impressions (assumption)
- Click-through rate: 1% (assumption)
- Share of clicks that become a visit or order: 5% (assumption)
- Average spend per customer: $60 (assumption)

Impressions = $500 ÷ $10 × 1,000 = 50,000. Clicks = 50,000 × 1% = 500. Customers = 500 × 5% = 25. Cost per customer = $500 ÷ 25 = $20. Revenue = 25 × $60 = $1,500, which is 3× the spend.

If your real cost per customer is above your margin per sale, the test has failed, and the fix is the script or targeting rather than more budget. If it is below, extend the test. For a comparison with a paid social channel at a similar budget, see [TikTok ads for coffee shops](/blog/tiktok-ads-for-coffee-shops-step-by-step-guide).

## How do you measure whether the campaign works?

Track impressions, click-through rate and conversions in Ad Studio. Add a unique link or promo code to each ad so a visit can be tied back to the campaign. Spotify's attention measure, effective attentive seconds based on dwell time and outcome, helps compare creative versions ([catapult°, secondary](https://www.catapultmymusic.com/mastering-spotify-advertising-a-beginners-guide)).

Compare results against a baseline. If visits or bookings rise during the campaign, check whether they also rose in the same weeks last year, because seasonality can flatter or hide a result. The same tracking discipline applies to other channels, including [CTV advertising for small businesses in Canada](/blog/ctv-advertising-canada-small-business-guide) if that is your market.

## FAQ

### Can I run Spotify Ads without a website?

Yes, but send listeners to a page where they can act, such as a booking page, a map listing or a phone number. Without a clear destination, the click has nowhere to go and the test cannot show a result.

### What budget should I start with?

Start with a budget you can sustain for at least two months, and use the worked example above to estimate cost per customer before you commit. Check the current minimum and live rates inside Ad Studio, since they vary by account and market.

### Should I run Spotify Ads alongside Google Ads?

Often yes. Audio reaches people who are not searching yet, while search captures people who already are. Track branded search volume during the campaign to see whether the two channels reinforce each other. For channel selection, [Google Ads management](/services/google-ads) is a useful starting point.

### Does targeting by music genre work for local businesses?

It can, but it is less precise than location targeting. Test broad audiences first, then narrow to genre or activity segments only after you have seen results in the first month.
