CTV advertising in Virginia Beach, VA lets local businesses run video ads on streaming services such as Hulu and Peacock, aimed at households by location. Google describes its [Display Network](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190) as reaching people across 35 million websites and apps, but that is a different product from connected TV. The sources reviewed for this guide do not publish CTV prices for Virginia Beach, so every budget figure below is a labelled assumption. Start with a small test, measure branded search and direct visits, and treat CTV as a brand-building channel rather than a click generator.

## What do paid media benchmarks show for local businesses?

Benchmarks give you a reference point before you spend. WordStream's [Google Ads benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2016/02/29/google-adwords-industry-benchmarks) report an average search click-through rate of 3.17% and a display rate of 0.46% across all industries, with an average search cost per click of $2.69 and a display cost per click of $0.63. These are all-industry averages, and they vary widely by sector. Legal search clicks average $6.75 while advocacy averages $1.43, so your own category matters more than the headline number.

## How is CTV different from Google Display?

Google's display product places ads across partner websites, apps, YouTube, and Gmail, and it uses automated targeting, bidding, and creative selection. Its help pages explain that [Display campaigns](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404190) can optimise toward conversions and let Google AI pick the best asset combinations. That is useful context, but it is not the same as buying a spot on a living-room screen.

CTV is a separate buying environment with its own inventory, pricing, and measurement. Do not assume a Display CPC or CTR predicts CTV performance. Use the Display figures only as a reminder that reach-based formats are judged differently from search.

## Which streaming platforms should a Virginia Beach business test?

Most local advertisers start with one platform rather than several. Hulu, Peacock, Tubi, Amazon Prime Video, and Pluto TV are the names most often mentioned in CTV planning, and each has its own audience mix and self-serve tools. Check current ad-manager terms, minimums, and targeting options directly on each platform before you commit, because published terms change.

A simple rule: pick the platform whose audience matches your customer, then run a single test before adding others. Splitting a small budget across four platforms usually leaves none of them with enough data to read.

## How should you set up targeting step by step?

Use this checklist before launching:

1. Draw your service area by ZIP code, starting with the neighbourhoods where your current customers already live.
2. Set a test budget you can afford to run for at least four weeks.
3. Choose one or two demographic filters that match your buyer, such as household type or age band.
4. Set a frequency cap so the same household does not see the ad too often. The sources here do not give a recommended cap, so set one and review it.
5. Write one clear offer and one call to action, such as a phone number or a booking page.
6. Set up tracking before launch: a unique phone number or landing page, and a question at checkout asking how the customer heard about you.
7. Record a baseline for branded search and direct visits in the two weeks before the campaign starts.

## How should you measure CTV results?

CTV does not produce a click trail like search, so measurement relies on indirect signals. Compare the period before launch with the flight period and the weeks after it.

| Signal | How to check | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Branded search | Google Ads or Search Console branded queries | Rising searches for your business name |
| Direct visits | Website analytics, direct channel | Higher direct traffic during flight |
| Phone calls | Dedicated tracking number | Calls that rise without other channel changes |
| Self-reported source | Checkout or intake question | Customers naming the TV ad |

Assumption: allow about eight weeks before judging the campaign, since brand effects build slowly. Keep other channels steady during the test so the signals are easier to read.

## What creative and setup basics matter most?

Keep the message simple. One benefit, one clear call to action, and your location or phone number are enough for most local offers. Viewers on a TV screen are watching for entertainment, so the ad should identify your business quickly and name what to do next.

If you also run search ads, point the CTV audience to a landing page that matches the TV message. ## FAQ

### Is CTV advertising worth it for a small business in Virginia Beach?

It can be, if your goal is brand familiarity in specific neighbourhoods. The sources here do not measure CTV return for local businesses, so run a small test with a baseline and clear tracking before scaling up.

### How much should I budget for a first CTV test?

The worked example above uses an assumed $500 per month. Replace that with a budget you can run for at least four weeks and that matches the CPM quote from your chosen platform.

### Do I need to run Google Ads at the same time?

Not necessarily. Search ads capture people who are already looking, while CTV builds awareness. Running both can help you see whether TV exposure lifts branded searches, which is one of the signals in the measurement table.

### How long before I see results from CTV?

Assumption: about eight weeks, because brand effects tend to build gradually. Judge the test on the signals you recorded at the start, not on clicks.
