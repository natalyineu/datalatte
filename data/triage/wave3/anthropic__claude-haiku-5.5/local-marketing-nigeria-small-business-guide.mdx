## How Big Is the Online Audience in Nigeria?

A guide aimed at Nigerian business owners describes the country as one of Africa's largest internet markets, with over 100 million internet users. It credits smartphones and cheaper data plans for much of that growth. It also names Facebook, Instagram, X and WhatsApp as the widely used platforms ([ebrandpromotion, secondary](https://ebrandpromotion.com/the-ultimate-guide-to-digital-marketing-for-nigerian-businesses/)).

That is a secondary vendor source, and it gives no date for the figure. Treat it as a starting point and check a current national report before using it in a plan or a client pitch.

The size of the audience matters less than where your own customers spend time. A bakery in Ibadan and an accounting firm in Abuja will reach different people, so ask existing customers which app they message on before choosing a channel. A short question at the till or on the order form gives better local evidence than any national average.

## Which Channel Should a Small Business Start With?

Choose by what the customer does next. A customer who wants a price or a booking will send a message. A customer who browses for entertainment will watch a video. A customer who needs something nearby will search on a map.

| Goal | Channel | What to do first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Talk to customers | WhatsApp Business | Answer quickly and keep a record of consent |
| Reach many local people | Facebook and Instagram | Run one small boosted post or ad to a clear offer |
| Reach younger adults | TikTok | Post short videos that show the product or service |
| Be found nearby | Google Business Profile | Complete the profile with hours, photos and services |

Start with the free channels, then test one paid channel at a time. Changing three things at once makes it impossible to see what caused a result.

## How Should a Small Business Use WhatsApp Without Spamming Customers?

WhatsApp works best as a conversation, not a broadcast. When a customer writes in, reply the same day and keep the answer short and specific. A reply that names the product, the price and the next step converts better than a generic greeting.

Ask for permission before sending promotions. Put a consent line on the order form or in the first chat, note the date it was given, and explain how to stop messages. Sending offers to people who never asked for them damages the trust the channel depends on.

## What Does a Test Budget Look Like? A Worked Example

The inputs below are **assumptions**, not published prices. A salon has 300 opted-in customers and sends each one 4 reminder or offer templates a month, which is 1,200 messages.

| Item | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assumed cost per template | Assumption: $0.05 per message | $0.05 |
| Monthly template cost | 1,200 x $0.05 | $60 per month |
| Replies handled by staff | 300 replies, assumed 2 minutes each | 10 staff hours |

Replace the $0.05 figure with the rate shown for Nigeria on the platform's current pricing page. The total changes in proportion to the rate, so a rate twice as high doubles the $60.

A practical test plan follows from this:

1. Set a fixed monthly test budget you can afford to lose, for example one-tenth of your marketing spend.
2. Run one channel for four weeks with one clear offer.
3. Track replies, bookings and sales from each channel separately, using a unique code or question for each.
4. Compare cost per booking, not likes or reach.
5. Keep the channel that produced bookings at a cost you can repeat, and drop the rest.

## How Do Customers Find a Business Nearby?

A Google Business Profile is free to create and gives a small business a place in local search and maps. Fill in the opening hours, service list, photos and a short description of what the business does. Keep the phone number and address identical to the website and other listings, because inconsistent details make it harder for search systems and customers to confirm the business.

Ask satisfied customers to leave a review, and reply to every review, including the negative ones. Replies show future customers that someone reads the feedback and acts on it.

## FAQ

### Is Facebook or TikTok better for a Nigerian small business?

Neither is better in general. Test a small budget on each for four weeks, and compare the cost per booking or sale. The better channel is the one your customers respond to, which may differ by town and by product.

### Do I need the WhatsApp Business API to start?

No. Small businesses can start with the free WhatsApp Business app for direct replies. The API is for larger volumes and automation, and it has its own pricing, so check the current terms before switching.

### Is limited internet access a problem for this market?

It can be. Combine online channels with referrals, a clear phone number and visible signage at the shop. A customer without a data plan may still respond to a call or a printed offer.

### What do ads cost in Nigeria?

The sources reviewed for this article do not publish average Nigerian click or impression costs. Set a small test budget in the ad platform's own tools and use the results from your own campaign.

### Do Nigerian data protection rules apply to small businesses?

Assume they do. Get consent before sending marketing messages, keep a record of that consent, and make it easy for customers to opt out. For the exact legal obligations, check the current law with a qualified adviser.
