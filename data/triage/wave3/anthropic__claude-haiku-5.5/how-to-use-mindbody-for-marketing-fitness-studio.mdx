Mindbody's marketing tools for fitness studios sit inside its Marketing Suite, which Mindbody says helps owners retain clients, write emails, personalize offers and automate messages. Mindbody's own guide and related resources link the marketing tools to the Ultimate plan, which it describes as one plan covering marketing, lead management, booking, payments and analytics. Its listed starting price is $79 per month per location. Mindbody does not publish Ultimate pricing in the pages reviewed, so a written quote is needed before budgeting for automation.

This guide covers yoga, pilates and boutique fitness studios that need to decide what to run inside Mindbody and what to run elsewhere. Mindbody's claims are attributed to Mindbody, and every example figure is labelled as an assumption.

## What Does Mindbody's Marketing Suite Include?

Mindbody's guide says its Marketing Suite is built into the software and covers six jobs: keeping customers coming back, writing emails that get seen, personalizing to specific groups, keeping content conversational, using clear calls to action, and setting up automations. The guide is a downloadable resource, so it describes goals rather than a feature list with settings.

The related Ultimate plan video describes marketing, lead management and analytics as part of the same plan. That makes Ultimate the plan to check first if campaigns and automation are needed in one place. Mindbody's pricing tables were not part of the sources reviewed for this rewrite, so feature availability for each tier should be confirmed in the studio's own account.

## How Do Fitness Studios Use Mindbody to Find New Clients?

Mindbody's customer story on Combine Air describes a 12-location fitness business in New South Wales, Australia, that uses Mindbody's marketing tools. Mindbody says its integration with ClassPass brings in new clients. The owners in Mindbody's "Built to Grow" article also describe on-the-go reporting and an app that helps people discover their businesses.

These are vendor-reported experiences, not independent results. Combine Air's story gives no client numbers, so it is best read as a reason to test the ClassPass and app channels, not as a forecast. Tracking where each new client came from, starting with the first booking, shows which channel is working for a given studio.

## How Do You Set Up a Win-Back Message?

Two messages are a sensible starting point: a welcome message after a first class, and a win-back message for clients who have stopped booking. Each one should carry a single action, such as booking a second class or using a class pack offer.

Mindbody's guide recommends simple calls to action and short, conversational copy. Changing one thing at a time, such as the offer or the subject line, makes it clear what caused any difference. The guide does not publish timing or offer benchmarks, so the first month works as a baseline.

## The Math: What Does a Win-Back Campaign Return?

All inputs below are assumptions. Mindbody and the other sources reviewed do not publish open, click or rebooking rates for studios.

| Input (assumption) | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Lapsed clients emailed | 200 |
| Rebooking rate | 3% |
| Clients who rebook | 6 |
| Class pack price | $120 |
| Revenue from the campaign | $720 |
| Assumed Ultimate cost per location per month | $150 |

On these inputs the campaign brings $720 in one month, which is 4.8 times the assumed $150 plan cost. If the result holds for three months, the campaign covers the plan cost without counting staff time. If the rebooking rate is 1% instead of 3%, the same campaign brings $240 and does not cover the assumed plan cost. Every assumption should be replaced with the studio's own numbers before a decision is made.

## What Should You Check Before Buying a Plan?

The steps below are worth completing before committing to a plan that includes automation.

| Step | What to check | Where to find the answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Written Ultimate price per location | Mindbody sales team |
| 2 | Whether campaigns and automations are included | Plan feature list in your account |
| 3 | Text message rate, if texts are sent | Mindbody pricing or support |
| 4 | Number of lapsed clients in the data today | Mindbody client reports |

If the monthly cost is higher than the revenue from step 5, the campaign should be tested on a smaller group first. ## Can Mindbody Handle Ads and Outside Channels?

The pages reviewed for this rewrite do not show a built-in ad tool. Reaching past visitors or lapsed members with paid ads requires building those audiences in an ads platform directly. Mindbody's role, based on these sources, is the client list and the message, not the ad spend.

## FAQ

Mindbody's guide and related pages connect its marketing tools to the Ultimate plan. The pricing tables were not part of the sources reviewed, so confirm which automations a tier includes before relying on them.

### How much does Mindbody cost for a fitness studio?

Mindbody lists plans from $79 per month per location. Ultimate pricing is not published in the sources reviewed, so a written quote should be requested.

### Can I measure which email led to a booking?

Mindbody's sources do not confirm how booking attribution works. A unique offer code in each email, counted against the number of people emailed, gives a simple check.
