## Which channels should a small Peruvian business test first?

Start with the free listings a customer sees when searching for a nearby business. A complete Google Business Profile, with accurate hours, photos and a phone number, gives a shop a place to be found without an ad budget. The [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist) is a practical starting point for that work.

Social channels come second. A shop that already posts on Facebook or Instagram can test whether short videos of products or the workshop bring messages and visits. Keep one channel as the main test and track it for at least four weeks before adding another, so the results are readable.

Agency homepages often describe channels in broad terms. Onza's Lima homepage lists branding, web design, SEO, paid media and social media as services, which shows how agencies bundle work, but it does not show which channel works best for a given shop ([Onza Marketing](https://onzamarketing.com/), secondary). Test the channels yourself rather than relying on an agency's description of them.

## How should a shop show payment options?

A customer who wants to buy should not have to guess how to pay. Show the payment methods you accept on your profile, in your posts and on a printed sign at the counter. The goal is to remove one reason a message turns into no order.

Ask customers which payment method they prefer, and write the answers down. A tally of five or six replies is enough to see whether a wallet or a card matters more for your products. Update the profile and the posts once the pattern is clear, rather than assuming a method is popular because a news story says so.

## What does Google Ads involve in Peru?

Google Ads puts a paid listing in front of people who search for your service. Its setup needs a conversion goal, a budget and a location target so that money is not spent on customers outside your service area. Those settings matter more for a local shop than the headline reach figure of any platform.

No Peruvian price-per-click or cost-per-thousand-impressions benchmark from a primary source was used for this article, so none is quoted. If an agency quotes a price for Peru, ask for the source and the date. Agency homepages can list credentials, such as Onza's Google Partner badge ([Onza Marketing](https://onzamarketing.com/), secondary), but a credential is a claim to verify, not a cost benchmark.

## What consent rules apply before messaging customers?

Before sending promotional messages or calling a list of contacts, confirm that each person agreed to receive them. A contact collected at the counter is not automatically agreed to receive offers by message. Keep a record of how and when consent was given.

This article does not summarise Peruvian personal data law, because the sources used here do not cover it. Read the current rules on the official government portal, or ask a Peruvian lawyer before running bulk messages or calls. Treat any rule you read in a news story as unverified until you check the primary text.

## How could a Peruvian bakery test channels? (worked example)

All figures below are assumptions for illustration, not benchmarks. A bakery in Arequipa (assumption) has 10 hours a week (assumption) and a small test budget (assumption).

## FAQ

### How many people use the internet in Peru?
This article has no sourced figure for internet use in Peru. Check the latest DataReportal report for Peru directly before using any number.

### Is Facebook worth using for a Peruvian shop?
It can be, but test it. Track orders or messages that mention Facebook for four weeks, then decide whether to keep it as a main channel.

### Should I accept Yape or Plin?
Ask your customers which methods they use, show the ones you accept, and track the answers. Do not assume a method is popular without checking.

### Do I need consent to message customers?
Confirm consent before sending promotional messages and keep a record of it. Check the current rules with a Peruvian lawyer, because this article does not cover the legal text.
