Peacock ads can be purchased through three routes: Universal Ads self-service with no platform minimum spend, NBCUniversal One Platform for direct cross-platform deals, or programmatic guaranteed and private marketplace deals through an eligible DSP. Each route changes the inventory, targeting, reporting, and approval process available. Local businesses should confirm Peacock inventory availability before committing, match targeting to available scale, submit exact-duration 16:9 video, and leave time for platform and publisher review. Peacock generates more than 15 million hours of ad-supported viewing every day [S1], creating opportunities for brands to engage audiences at scale.

## What buying routes exist for Peacock ads?

Three documented routes provide access to Peacock inventory. Universal Ads, described as Comcast's self-service TV advertising platform, offers access to NBCUniversal and other premium publishers with no platform minimum spend [S2]. It supports two campaign types: Performance+, which optimizes delivery toward awareness or conversion goals, and Content Select, which lets advertisers choose genres or allowlisted publisher networks for greater placement control [S2].

The second route is NBCUniversal One Platform, which brings linear and streaming inventory into a broader planning framework [S2]. This suits brands needing premium content, live sports, sponsorships, or cross-platform scale. Direct plans can support strategic audiences, contextual alignment, and negotiated measurement [S2].

The third route uses programmatic guaranteed or private marketplace deals through an eligible DSP seat [S2]. Availability is deal-specific. Do not assume Peacock is present in every DSP or open auction. Ask for the seller, supply path, deal ID, deal type, pricing model, whether the deal is Peacock-only or broader NBCU inventory, supported devices, and geography restrictions before building the line item [S2].

## What targeting can a local business use on Peacock?

Targeting depends on the buying route and data permissions attached. Universal Ads documents location targeting by state, DMA, and ZIP code inclusion or exclusion, useful for local availability and store footprints [S2]. Core demographics cover age and gender, while advanced demographics include household income, education, homeownership, family status, and shopper behaviors [S2].

Viewership behavior targeting reaches people based on what they watch, such as sports, news, comedy, drama, travel, or food, rather than the content beside the ad [S2]. Genre or contextual alignment targets specific content genres like sports, news, reality, comedy, or drama [S2].

The platform recommends beginning with one to two targeting layers and a simple structure, then checking forecasted scale after each layer [S2]. Adding too many filters can shrink the reachable audience below what a local campaign needs. Universal Ads also supports a pixel for conversion signals, letting advertisers track outcomes beyond impressions [S2].

For a local business, ZIP code targeting is the most relevant layer. Confirm with the seller what geographic granularity is available on each buying route and whether narrowing the area changes the price.

## What ad formats and innovations does Peacock offer?

Peacock offers several ad innovation products. Arrival Ad places a brand on Peacock's profile selection page, introducing the brand before browsing begins [S1]. Live in Browse surfaces a brand logo within the live event preview on the Peacock homepage [S1]. Spotlight delivers an ad to all Peacock viewers across all content in the first standard ad break on a chosen day, maximizing reach for 24 hours [S1].

Coming in 2026, Programmatic Pause Ads will allow clients to buy Peacock's premium Pause Ads programmatically, starting with PG deals [S1]. Mindful Moments is another format in development [S1].

Ad innovations function as a performance multiplier, driving 1.7x higher ROAS than standard media [S1]. Contextual Pause Ads show 23% higher memorability compared to pause ad norms [S1]. Engagement Ads with NBCU IP show 114% higher interaction rate than those without [S1].

These formats may require direct NBCUniversal deals rather than self-service. Ask which products are available on each buying route and what minimum commitments apply.

## How much do Peacock ads cost for a local business?

NBCUniversal does not publish CPMs or minimum spend figures in the sources available. Universal Ads states no platform minimum spend [S2], but that does not mean campaigns are free. Actual costs depend on geography, targeting layers, content alignment, and deal type.

The only verified performance figures are relative. Peacock drives 36% brand impact lift compared to CTV norms across 374 campaigns measured from October 2024 through September 2025 [S1]. It also drives 22% search activity lift compared to CTV norms across 83 campaigns and 66 brands [S1]. These are aggregate platform averages, not guarantees for any individual advertiser.

Because no public price exists, the only way to cost a campaign is to request a written quote. Ask for the CPM or flat price for the geography, whether narrowing the area raises it, and what minimum commitment applies for a one-month test.

## What creative does Peacock require?

Peacock requires exact-duration 16:9 video creative [S2]. Confirm length and technical specifications with the seller before production, as requirements can vary by buying route and ad format.

A streaming spot plays on a large screen with sound on and no click. Lead with the business name and what it does in the first few seconds. Show the real location and people. Keep one message and one action. Put a short web address or QR code on screen long enough to use.

Leave time for platform and publisher review approval [S2]. This review period can add days or weeks to the timeline, so submit creative early. Ask the seller who pays for any production and what revisions are allowed.

## How to measure a Peacock ad test?

Streaming ads are rarely clicked, so build measurement before launch. Use a vanity URL and a dedicated phone number for the campaign. Add a question at booking asking how the customer heard about the business. Track branded search volume and Business Profile calls in the area, compared with the four weeks before the campaign.

A holdout area or period where the ad did not run provides a comparison point. Do not accept platform attribution as the only evidence. The 22% search activity lift figure [S1] suggests Peacock can drive incremental search, but that is a platform aggregate, not a promise for any single business.

## How does the break-even math work for a Peacock test?

Since no CPM is published, every figure below is an assumption. Use this table to evaluate a quote, not to predict results.

| | Low | Mid | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Test budget (assumption) | $3,000 | $3,000 | $3,000 |
| CPM, cost per 1,000 views (assumption) | $25 | $40 | $60 |
| Impressions | 120,000 | 75,000 | 50,000 |
| Views per household (assumption) | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| Households reached | 30,000 | 18,750 | 12,500 |
| Households that become customers (assumption) | 0.05% | 0.05% | 0.05% |
| New customers | 15 | 9.4 | 6.3 |
| Cost per new customer | $200 | $320 | $480 |

Break-even depends on gross profit per customer, not revenue. A business with $120 profit per customer loses money in every column. A high-value service such as roofing, dental work, or a year-long gym membership could carry the high column.

Check household coverage against the trade area. If the business serves a 10-mile radius containing 40,000 households and the mid column reaches 18,750, nearly half the trade area sees the ad. If the buy spreads across a whole metro, most households are too far away to visit.

Yes. Universal Ads offers self-service access with no platform minimum spend [S2], and NBCUniversal designed it for performance, local, and small to medium-sized advertisers. Confirm current availability and terms directly, as product details can change.

### How much do Peacock ads cost?

NBCUniversal does not publish CPMs in the available sources. Universal Ads states no platform minimum spend [S2], but actual costs depend on geography, targeting, and deal type. Request a written quote and compare it against the break-even table above.

### Can I target my city or ZIP codes on Peacock?

Universal Ads supports location targeting by state, DMA, and ZIP code [S2]. Which geographic level is available on each buying route, and at what price, requires confirmation from the seller.

### Does Peacock advertising drive results?
