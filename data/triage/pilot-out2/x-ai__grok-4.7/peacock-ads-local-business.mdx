A local business buys Peacock ads through Universal Ads self-service, an NBCUniversal direct or One Platform plan, or a programmatic deal on an eligible DSP. [Ad Geeks’ 2026 guide](https://adgeeks.agency/news/news-how-to-advertise-on-peacock) says each route changes inventory, targeting, reporting, approval and commercial terms. Universal Ads describes access to NBCUniversal and other premium publishers with no platform minimum spend. That is not a published cost per thousand. [NBCUniversal’s Peacock page](https://together.nbcuni.com/network/peacock/) lists audience and format results, not a local rate card. Confirm the network, app and deal before spend. NBCUniversal inventory is not proof every impression runs on Peacock.

## How does a local business buy Peacock ads?

The first decision is the buying route, not the creative. [Ad Geeks](https://adgeeks.agency/news/news-how-to-advertise-on-peacock) maps Universal Ads for smaller and mid-market advertisers that want hands-on setup, NBCUniversal direct or One Platform for premium content and cross-platform packages, and programmatic guaranteed or private-marketplace deals for teams that already buy in a DSP. A managed partner can compare those paths, but fees, account ownership and inventory proof still need a written check.

Universal Ads follows a campaign, ad set and ad structure. Advertisers choose Performance+ or Content Select. Performance+ optimizes across eligible premium publishers toward awareness or conversion. Content Select lets the advertiser pick genres or allowlisted networks. A network-level choice can exist, yet specific publisher access may require an account representative. If Peacock is the goal, confirm it in the forecast or in writing before the media plan is locked.

Direct buying fits when the plan needs live moments, sponsorships or formats beyond standard in-stream video. Ask the seller to separate Peacock delivery from the rest of the NBCU portfolio, and to report impressions, spend and outcomes at the lowest available level. Programmatic access is deal-specific. Do not assume Peacock sits in an open auction or in every DSP seat.

## What must be confirmed before a local buy?

[Ad Geeks](https://adgeeks.agency/news/news-how-to-advertise-on-peacock) lists the checks that change a local plan: approved DSP and seat, seller and supply path, deal ID, deal type, pricing model, whether the deal is Peacock-only, supported devices, geography limits, live-event restrictions, audience permissions, frequency controls, and which reporting fields come back. Missing any one of those leaves a metro buy that may never hit the trade area.

Universal Ads documents location controls at state, DMA and ZIP, including inclusion or exclusion. Core demographics cover age and gender. Advanced layers can include household income, education, homeownership, family status and shopper behaviors. Viewership behavior targets people by what they watch, not by the show beside the ad. Genre alignment is a separate control. The same guide says to start with one or two targeting layers and to recheck forecasted scale after each layer. A ZIP list plus income plus a sports behavior can shrink delivery until a small budget never spends.

Creative submission is part of access, not an afterthought. The guide’s quick answer calls for exact-duration 16:9 video and time for platform and publisher review. Universal Ads can support a pixel for conversion signals. That pixel does not replace a local booking question or a tracked phone number.

## Which ad products does Peacock publish?

[NBCUniversal’s Peacock page](https://together.nbcuni.com/network/peacock/) describes a first-view suite and newer units. Arrival Ad is an exclusive placement on the profile selection page, before browsing starts. Live in Browse puts a brand logo in the live-event preview on the homepage. Spotlight delivers an ad to all Peacock viewers across all content in the first standard ad break on a chosen day, for 24 hours. The page marks that 24-hour claim with an asterisk, so the commercial conditions belong in the insertion order.

Pause ads are part of the innovation set. NBCU says clients will be able to buy Peacock Pause Ads programmatically, starting with programmatic guaranteed deals. Contextual Pause Ads are tied to a memorability comparison, covered below. Engagement Ads are tied to an interaction comparison when the unit uses NBCU intellectual property.

None of these product names is a local rate. Availability depends on the product, the content window and the agreement. A 24-hour Spotlight across all Peacock viewers is a national reach tool. A shop that serves a few ZIP codes should ask whether a narrower geo exists, whether narrowing it changes price, and whether the unit can be excluded from markets the business does not serve. Live sports and cultural events are part of the pitch, which is why a live-sports plan should be compared with a city guide such as [CTV advertising in Phoenix](/blog/ctv-advertising-phoenix-az-local-business-guide) before a national sports package is treated as local media.

## What performance figures does NBCUniversal cite?

[NBCUniversal](https://together.nbcuni.com/network/peacock/) says Peacock generates more than 15 million hours of ad-supported viewing every day. It also says 64 percent of Peacock viewers are not reached on competitors’ ad-supported tiers. The footnote cites Nielsen Streaming Platform Ratings, persons 2+, a three-month average from December 2025 through February 2026, averaged across AVOD competition, with ad-supported estimates based on Antenna and eMarketer.

The same page reports a 36 percent brand-impact lift versus CTV norms and a 22 percent search-activity lift versus CTV norms. Brand impact is a composite of unaided brand awareness, favorability and purchase intent across 374 campaigns from 1 October 2024 to 30 September 2025, compared with KMB CTV norms. Search lift is online engagement across 83 campaigns and 66 brands in the same window, measured via EDO.

These are national campaign composites, not a promise for one salon or one contractor. Ad innovations are described as a performance multiplier, driving 1.7 times higher ROAS than standard media. Contextual Pause Ads showed 23 percent higher memorability versus pause-ad norms in a Marketcast study of adults 18+ (n=1316, 17 February 2023 to 27 April 2023). Engagement Ads with NBCU IP showed a 114 percent higher interaction rate versus units without that IP, from BrightLine campaigns October 2024 to September 2025. BrightLine norms are not Peacock-specific. Use the figures to write better questions, not to forecast a ZIP code.

## How can a shop judge a quote without a public CPM?

NBCUniversal’s page does not publish a CPM or a local minimum. Universal Ads describes no platform minimum spend, which still does not price a Peacock impression. Every dollar below is an assumption, used only to test a written quote.

Assume a one-month test budget of 3,000 dollars. Assume a CPM of 40 dollars. Impressions equal 3,000 divided by 40, times 1,000, or 75,000. Assume four views per household. Households reached equal 18,750. Assume 0.05 percent of those households become customers, or about 9.4 customers. Cost per new customer is about 320 dollars, calculated only from those assumptions.

Judge that 320 dollar figure against gross profit per customer, not against ticket price. If an assumption of 150 dollars gross profit is the real margin, the test loses money on last-click math even when the ad runs. If an assumption of 600 dollars gross profit fits a roof, implant or annual membership, the same quote can be a test rather than a loss. Ask whether the 18,750 households are inside the trade area. A DMA-wide buy can spend most of the budget on homes that will never visit.

Frequency is a planning choice, not a measured local result. Four views to fewer homes is a tighter test than one view spread across a whole metro, but that is a planning judgment, not an NBCU finding. Compare the quote with search already in the account. [Google Local Services Ads](/blog/google-local-service-ads-worth-it-local-business) and [Microsoft Advertising](/blog/bing-ads-microsoft-advertising-local-business) answer a different job: capture demand that already exists. Peacock’s published 22 percent search-activity lift is a national composite, so any local search lift still needs a before-and-after read on branded queries.

## When should a local advertiser wait?

A reasonable test is a business with high gross profit per customer, a trade area large enough for DMA or ZIP delivery, and search plus a Business Profile that already convert. Home services, dental, auto and memberships fit that pattern more often than a single low-ticket counter. Waiting is rational when the seller cannot name the minimum, the geography, or proof that impressions are Peacock rather than a broader NBCU package.

Live inventory needs the same skepticism. Peacock’s page sells cultural and live moments, and Spotlight is a 24-hour, all-viewer unit. That is a different buy from a weekend flight around one sport. Read [college football CTV advertising](/blog/college-football-ctv-advertising-local-business) and [ESPN+ advertising](/blog/espn-plus-advertising-local-business) as route comparisons, not as proof that one streamer wins on cost. No source in this article ranks Peacock against those networks on CPM.

Put seven questions in writing: one-month minimum, CPM or flat price by geography, shows and sports included or excluded, share of targeted homes on an ad-supported plan, creative specs and who pays production, proof of delivery at household or ZIP level, and what happens if a product is renamed mid-flight. If the first two answers are missing, there is not yet an offer. Set a vanity URL, a campaign phone number and a booking question before the first impression.

## FAQ

### Can a small business advertise on Peacock?

Yes, in principle. [Ad Geeks](https://adgeeks.agency/news/news-how-to-advertise-on-peacock) describes Universal Ads as a self-service route for smaller and mid-market advertisers, with no platform minimum spend stated on that platform’s site. Direct NBCU plans and DSP deals are separate routes. Peacock inclusion, local geo and price still have to be confirmed in the forecast or the order.

### Does Universal Ads guarantee Peacock inventory?

No. Performance+ optimizes across eligible premium publishers. Content Select can include a network choice, but specific publisher access may need an account representative. The guide’s warning is direct: NBCUniversal inventory is not proof that every impression runs on Peacock.

### What targeting can a local advertiser use?

On Universal Ads, documented layers include state, DMA and ZIP, age and gender, and advanced demographics such as income, education, homeownership, family status and shopper behaviors. Viewing behavior and genre alignment are separate. Start with one or two layers and check scale. Other routes depend on the deal’s data permissions.

### Are Peacock’s lift numbers a local forecast?

No. The 36 percent brand-impact figure covers 374 campaigns, and the 22 percent search figure covers 83 campaigns and 66 brands, both versus CTV norms. The 1.7 times ROAS line compares ad innovations with standard media. None of those studies is a ZIP-level result for one local advertiser.

### What creative does a Peacock buy require?

[Ad Geeks](https://adgeeks.agency/news/news-how-to-advertise-on-peacock) says to submit exact-duration 16:9 video and to leave time for platform and publisher review. Custom units such as Arrival Ad, Spotlight, Pause Ads and Engagement Ads depend on the product and the agreement. Confirm length, file specs and who pays production before production starts.
