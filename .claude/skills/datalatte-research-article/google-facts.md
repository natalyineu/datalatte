## Tips to improve your local ranking on Google (https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)
- "Local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and popularity."
- Prominence: "how well-known a business is"; also "based on info like how many websites link to your business and how many reviews you have"; "More reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking."
- "Businesses with complete and accurate info are more likely to show up in local search results."
- "There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google."
- Complete-info list: address, hours (including special hours), category, attributes. The page does **not** list website or phone number as ranking factors: never claim they are.

## Tips to get more reviews (https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)
- Customers can be asked to "visit a Google link or scan a QR code".
- Replies: "Be conversational, not promotional"; avoid offering deals in a reply.
- Incentives: offering free or discounted goods or services in exchange for reviews "is considered fake & misleading content and is strictly prohibited" (also to change or remove negative reviews).
- A business can flag reviews it believes violate the content policies.

## Manage customer reviews (https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474050)
- A business must be verified before replying; replies are reviewed and then posted publicly; the reviewer is notified.

## Edit your Business Profile (https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617)
- Main hours or closed; special hours for holidays; more hours for different features.
- Primary category "that best matches what your business does"; up to 9 more; "Do not use categories solely as keywords or to describe features of your business."
- Attributes (e.g. Wi-Fi, outdoor seating); photos (an exterior photo helps customers recognise the business); edits are reviewed before they go live.

## Local business structured data (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business)
- Required: `name`, `address`. Recommended include `telephone`, `url`, `geo`, `menu` (food), `openingHoursSpecification`, `priceRange`, `servesCuisine`.
- Examples use JSON-LD; use the most specific sub-type (e.g. `CafeOrCoffeeShop`); test with the Rich Results Test.
